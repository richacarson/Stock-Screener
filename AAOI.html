Company Profile
Technology ·Communication Equipment ·NASDAQ ·United States
Applied Optoelectronics designs, manufactures, and sells fiber-optic networking products including optical transceivers, lasers, and laser components for data center, CATV, and telecom markets. The company is vertically integrated, fabricating its own laser chips in Sugar Land, TX and Taipei, Taiwan. Q2 2026 delivered record revenue of $191.9M (+86% YoY) with 800G revenue more than doubling sequentially and record CATV revenue of $80.6M. The company returned to non-GAAP profitability with $0.06 EPS. Q3 2026 guidance of $255-290M implies 130% YoY growth at midpoint. Full-year 2026 revenue expected at approximately $1.1B. Market cap ~$12B.

The Q3 guidance of $255-290M requires the new Texas facility to ramp on schedule — any production delays would create a significant earnings miss.
