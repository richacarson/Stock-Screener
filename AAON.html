Company Profile
Industrials ·Building Products & Equipment ·NASDAQ ·United States
AAON, Inc. designs, manufactures, and sells premium commercial and industrial HVAC equipment including rooftop units, chillers, air handling units, condensing units, and data center cooling solutions through three segments: AAON Oklahoma, AAON Coil Products, and BASX. The BASX brand provides advanced liquid cooling systems for hyperscale AI data centers, with Q2 2026 BASX sales surging 216% YoY. The company raised 2026 revenue guidance to 55-60% growth after Q2 revenue doubled to $627M. Founded in 1987, headquartered in Tulsa, Oklahoma.

Investment Thesis
AAON is riding the most powerful secular tailwind in industrials: AI data center cooling demand. Q2 2026 delivered $627M in revenue (up 101.2% YoY) with BASX segment sales surging 216%, and management raised full-year 2026 guidance to 55-60% growth — the third consecutive guidance raise from the original 18-20% [1]. Record backlog of $2.1B+ provides multi-year revenue visibility. The $174.5M liquid cooling data center order and Applied Digital AI factory contract validate AAON's position as a leading provider of custom-engineered cooling for AI infrastructure that requires 15-30x traditional power density [2]. Forward PEG of 0.98 suggests the stock is fairly valued for its growth trajectory, D/E of 44.7% is manageable, and ROE of 17.3% exceeds target. The Inspire ALIGNED score of 64 and 16.7% insider ownership reinforce the quality thesis [4].
The primary risk is margin compression and execution at unprecedented scale. Profit margin has declined from 14% (FY2024) to 8.2% (TTM) as BASX production scales at lower margins than legacy AAON products, and gross margin guidance of 25-26% for 2026 represents significant compression from historical 30%+ levels [1]. FY2025 net income actually declined to $107.6M from $168.6M despite 20% revenue growth, demonstrating that BASX scaling is currently dilutive to profitability. Free cash flow is negative at -$214M due to $213M+ in capacity investment, and cash on hand is essentially zero ($13K) against $452M in total debt — creating balance sheet vulnerability if data center demand cycles or capacity investments take longer to generate returns [4]. At 23.7x forward earnings and 43.6x trailing PE, the stock prices in substantial execution success — any hyperscaler order delay, margin disappointment, or cooling technology disruption (e.g., immersion cooling competitors) could trigger meaningful de-rating from the $82.76 level. Short interest of 7.1% reflects some skepticism about the sustainability of the growth trajectory.
Key Catalysts
  1. Record $2.1B+ backlog and three consecutive guidance raises (from 18-20% to 55-60% growth) provide exceptional revenue visibility as AI data center cooling demand accelerates across hyperscaler infrastructure buildout [1]
  2. Gross margin recovery toward 27-28% as BASX production scales and manufacturing efficiency improves across four facilities, potentially restoring profit margins toward historical 12-15% levels and converting revenue growth into earnings growth [1]
  3. AI infrastructure buildout represents a multi-decade secular trend — hyperscaler capital expenditure plans of $200B+ annually through 2030 imply sustained demand for advanced liquid cooling solutions well beyond the current order cycle [2]
Key Risks
  1. Profit margin compression from 14% to 8.2% demonstrates that BASX revenue growth is currently dilutive to earnings — FY2025 net income declined 36% to $107.6M despite 20% revenue growth, and the 2026 gross margin guidance of 25-26% remains well below historical levels [1]
  2. Near-zero cash ($13K) against $452M total debt with negative free cash flow of -$214M creates balance sheet vulnerability during aggressive capacity expansion — any demand disruption could force dilutive capital raising at compressed valuations [4]
  3. Data center cooling technology risk: immersion cooling and direct-to-chip liquid cooling competitors could potentially disrupt AAON/BASX's air-cooled and indirect liquid cooling solutions, requiring significant R&D investment to maintain competitive relevance [2]
