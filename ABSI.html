Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Absci Corporation is a Vancouver, Washington-based clinical-stage biotech that uses generative AI and wet-lab data to design antibody therapeutics. Founded in 2011 by CEO Sean McClain, it has moved from a partnered drug-creation model to an internal pipeline led by ABS-201 (anti-PRLR) for androgenetic alopecia and endometriosis. ABS-201 showed a favorable interim Phase 1 safety profile and an estimated half-life of at least 65 days, with interim proof-of-concept data expected in H2 2026, full data in early 2027, and a Phase 2 endometriosis trial planned for Q4 2026. A $100M June 2026 offering that included a $40M strategic investment from Eli Lilly lifted cash to $201.1M, which the company says funds operations into H2 2028. Market cap approximately $1.9B at $11.13.

Investment Thesis
Absci now has a clinical asset that tests the AI drug-creation thesis directly. ABS-201 was well tolerated in blinded interim Phase 1 data and showed an estimated half-life of at least 65 days, which could support infrequent dosing, and interim proof of concept in hair loss is expected in H2 2026 with a Phase 2 endometriosis study starting in Q4 2026 [1]. Eli Lilly's $40M strategic investment in the June 2026 $100M offering adds big-pharma validation, and $201.1M of cash funds operations into H2 2028 [1][3]. The stock has risen from a 52-week low of $2.24 to $11.13, and analysts carry a strong-buy consensus with a mean target of $14.82 [7].
The bear case is binary clinical risk and dilution. Revenue was only $0.3M in Q2 2026 and the quarterly net loss widened to $33.2M, so the roughly $1.9B market cap rests almost entirely on ABS-201 [1][7]. The June offering sold 13.5M shares at $7.41, and the 10-Q warns of further dilution from future issuances [3]. A weak interim efficacy readout in a crowded hair-loss market, or a slip in the endometriosis Phase 2 timeline, could reverse most of the recent gain.

Key Risks
  1. Binary clinical risk: valuation depends almost entirely on ABS-201 efficacy data, and revenue is under $3M a year [1][5]
  2. Ongoing dilution: $33.2M quarterly net loss and a June 2026 offering of 13.5M shares, with more issuance likely before any product revenue [1][3]
  3. AI drug-design platforms commoditizing as larger AI biotechs and pharma in-house teams scale, eroding Absci's partnership value
