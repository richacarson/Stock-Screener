Investment Thesis
Abivax is approaching a potentially transformative inflection point with obefazimod, the first oral small molecule targeting RNA splicing for ulcerative colitis, with Phase 3 maintenance trial results expected and NDA submission planned for Q4 2026 [1][2]. The $10.89B market cap reflects substantial market confidence in the program's commercial potential in the $25B+ IBD therapeutics market, where oral small molecules offer convenience advantages over injectable biologics. The July 2026 $920M public offering provides capital for commercial infrastructure build-out and eliminates near-term dilution risk. CEO Marc de Garidel brings proven commercialization experience from his $1.8B CinCor Pharma exit [5]. The 13-analyst consensus Buy rating with $148.38 price target suggests 19% upside from current levels.
The SELL recommendation reflects a risk profile that is unfavorable relative to the current valuation. The $10.89B market cap prices in successful commercialization despite a safety signal that detected malignancies in patients receiving the higher obefazimod dose — this could result in label restrictions, black box warnings, or FDA concerns that narrow the addressable market [4]. Revenue of just $5.4M in FY2025 (down 58.5% YoY) and expected EPS of -$4.33 for 2026 mean the company is burning approximately $200M annually with cash runway only through Q4 2027 absent the recent offering [1][3]. The IBD market is intensely competitive: AbbVie's Skyrizi, J&J's Tremfya, and Takeda's Entyvio have established commercial infrastructure and physician relationships.
