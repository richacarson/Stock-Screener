Company Profile
Communication Services ·Publishing & Broadcasting ·NYSE ·United States
ACCESS Newswire Inc. (formerly Issuer Direct Corporation) provides cloud-based press release distribution, investor relations, and compliance communication services to public and private companies. Founded in 2006 by CEO Brian Balbirnie, the company is transitioning from a transactional press release model to a subscription-based SaaS platform with AI-powered tools including ACCESS Verified (content verification) and ACCESS Insights & Analytics (AI-scored media engagement dashboards). Q2 2026 revenue was $5.6M (flat YoY), with average ARR per subscription customer growing 15% to $12,718 and customer retention at 94%. Market cap is approximately $20M, down 54% over the past year.

Investment Thesis
ACCESS Newswire is a micro-cap founder-led company executing a subscription SaaS transformation with early AI integration at a deeply depressed valuation — Forward P/E of 8.05 and Price-to-Sales of 0.87 on a $20M market cap that has declined 54% in the past year [4]. The subscription model transition is gaining traction: average ARR per customer grew 15% YoY to $12,718, customer retention improved to 94%, and net revenue retention reached 124% — metrics that suggest growing wallet share despite flat headline revenue [1]. AI product launches (ACCESS Verified, ACCESS Insights) position the company to benefit from management's thesis that AI disruption will return the PR industry to double-digit growth by 2027 [2]. CEO Balbirnie's 20-year founder tenure and recent open-market stock purchase of 10,000 shares signal personal conviction at depressed prices [3]. The Inspire ALIGNED label (score: 48) and nearly debt-free balance sheet (D/E 9%) add to the foundation case.
The bear case is dominated by critical liquidity constraints and competitive disadvantage. Average daily turnover of just $127K is far below IOWN's $1M liquidity floor, making position entry and exit at scale impossible — the $20M market cap means any institutional-sized position would represent a significant ownership stake [4]. Revenue has stagnated at ~$22M TTM with flat-to-negative YoY growth, and EPS of -$0.09 in Q2 shows the company cannot cover its cost structure at current scale [1]. Profit margin of -7.63% and ROE of -5.07% reflect ongoing losses despite healthy 74.86% gross margins — the fixed cost base is too large for the revenue level [4]. The company competes against PR Newswire (Cision), Business Wire (Berkshire Hathaway), and GlobeNewsWire — competitors with 50-100x ACCS's scale, distribution networks, and R&D budgets. AI-driven disruption of press release distribution is a real threat to the core business even as the company attempts to ride the same wave. The Altman Z-Score of 2.46 indicates elevated financial distress risk for a company burning cash on a subscription transition that has not yet translated to revenue growth.
