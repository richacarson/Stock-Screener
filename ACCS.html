ACCESS Newswire Inc. (formerly Issuer Direct Corporation, renamed January 2025) provides cloud-based press release distribution, investor relations, and compliance communication services to public and private companies. Founded in 2006 by CEO Brian Balbirnie, the company offers ACCESS Newswire distribution, media database/pitching, media monitoring, webcasting platforms, IR content networks, and whistleblower hotline services. Q2 2026 revenue was $5.6M (flat YoY) with adjusted EBITDA of $642K (11% margin, down from 15% a year prior). The company has 91 employees and trades on NYSE American under ACCS with a $17.6M market cap.

The bear case is dominated by critical liquidity constraints and competitive disadvantage. Average daily dollar volume of approximately $100,590 is catastrophically below IOWN's $1M liquidity floor, making position entry and exit at scale impossible [1]. Revenue has stagnated at ~$22.6M with flat-to-negative YoY growth for three consecutive years ($24.5M in 2023 to $22.6M in 2025), while profit margin of -7.63% and ROE of -5.07% reflect ongoing losses despite healthy 74.9% gross margins [1].
