Company Profile
Industrials ·Engineering & Construction ·NYSE ·United States
AECOM is a global infrastructure consulting firm providing planning, consulting, architectural and engineering design, construction management, and program management services. Under CEO Troy Rudd's Think and Act Globally strategy, the company has refocused on higher-margin professional services and advisory, exiting construction management. Serves transportation, water, environment, energy, and facilities markets worldwide. Q3 FY2026 reported revenue of $3.59B impacted by a $337M Construction Management charge from a delayed legacy project; excluding the charge, adjusted EBITDA margin reached 17.4% with adjusted EPS of $1.49 (up 11% YoY). Long-term guidance reaffirmed: 5-8% organic growth, 20%+ margin by FY2028, 15%+ EPS CAGR through FY2029.

Investment Thesis
AECOM is executing a compelling professional services transformation and trades at a significant valuation discount following the Q3 FY2026 $337M Construction Management charge. At Forward P/E of 9.69x and PEG of ~0.69, the stock is deeply discounted relative to professional services peers like Jacobs (~14x) and WSP (20x+) despite reaffirmed long-term guidance of 5-8% organic growth, 20%+ margins by FY2028, and 15%+ EPS CAGR through FY2029 [1][5]. Excluding the one-time charge, Q3 delivered adjusted EBITDA margin of 17.4% (raised from 17.0%), adjusted EPS of $1.49 (up 11%), and NSR of $1.95B — the underlying business is performing well. Secular tailwinds from IIJA infrastructure spending, energy transition, and AI data center construction provide multi-year demand visibility. AECOM AI and the SMU partnership position the company to capture AI-driven infrastructure design demand [3].
The bear case centers on the $337M Q3 Construction Management charge, which exposed that AECOM's exit from legacy construction is incomplete and carries residual tail risk [1]. Reported revenue declined 14.2% YoY and profit margin compressed to 1.87%, temporarily destroying the margin expansion narrative even as adjusted metrics improved. D/E of 138.9% is elevated for a professional services firm and limits capital allocation flexibility during a period when the stock has declined from $71 to $62 [5]. Revenue Growth 5yr CAGR of ~4% on total revenue understates NSR growth quality but creates headwinds in quantitative screening. Net insider selling by COO Poloni ($4.3M+) weakens the conviction signal. Post-IIJA infrastructure spending deceleration after 2028 could compress the secular growth tailwind, and government budget pressures from deficit reduction may slow project awards. The FY2026 reported EPS guidance of $3.95-$4.15 (including the charge) versus adjusted $5.90-$6.10 creates investor confusion about the true earnings power [1].
Key Catalysts
  1. Valuation re-rating as the market digests the one-time $337M CM charge and refocuses on adjusted fundamentals — Forward P/E of 9.69x for a company guiding 15%+ EPS CAGR through FY2029 is significantly below professional services peers [1][5]

  2. Reported profit margin of 1.87% and -14.2% revenue growth make AECOM screen poorly on quantitative metrics despite strong adjusted performance, potentially limiting institutional buying interest until reported and adjusted metrics converge [5]
