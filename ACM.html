Company Profile
Industrials ·Engineering & Construction ·NYSE ·United States
AECOM is a global infrastructure consulting firm providing planning, consulting, architectural and engineering design, construction management, and program management services. Under CEO Troy Rudd's Think and Act Globally strategy, the company has refocused on higher-margin professional services and advisory, exiting construction management. Serves transportation, water, environment, energy, and facilities markets worldwide with a record $26.2B backlog.

Investment Thesis
AECOM is executing a compelling professional services transformation, delivering record 16.5% adjusted EBITDA margins in Q2 FY2026 with net income up 19% to $184M and EPS up 22% to $1.42 [1]. The stock trades at a forward P/E of 10.47 and PEG of 0.71 — a significant discount to professional services peers like Jacobs (~14x) and WSP (20x+) — despite record $26.2B backlog (up 8% YoY), 1.5x book-to-burn ratio sustained for 21 consecutive quarters, and a clear path to 20%+ margins by FY2028 through AECOM AI and advisory growth [1][3]. Secular tailwinds from IIJA infrastructure spending, energy transition, and AI data center construction provide multi-year demand visibility, with management raising FY2026 guidance to 14% earnings growth at midpoint.
The bear case centers on AECOM's structurally thin net profit margin (3.2% on total revenue including pass-throughs), which amplifies any revenue weakness or cost inflation — even modest increases in labor costs or project overruns can disproportionately impact earnings [3]. D/E of 135% is elevated for a professional services firm and limits capital allocation flexibility. Total revenue grew only 1% YoY in Q2 FY2026 despite record backlogs, and while NSR growth is stronger (4% reported, 8% Americas constant-currency), the gap between headline revenue and the quality metric creates investor confusion and potential undervaluation [1]. Net insider selling ($4.3M by COO Poloni) weakens the conviction signal [7]. Post-IIJA infrastructure spending deceleration after 2028 could compress the secular growth tailwind, and government budget pressures may slow project awards. The stock's 15% decline from its April highs suggests the market is pricing in execution risk on the margin expansion narrative.
Key Catalysts
  1. Advisory business accelerating toward 20%+ margin target by FY2028 as AECOM AI proprietary capabilities drive automation of lower-value design tasks and shift mix toward higher-margin consulting engagements
  2. AI data center construction boom creating a multi-year demand pipeline — AECOM is designing major projects including the €1.9B Nostrum Evergreen campus and benefits from every hyperscaler capacity expansion globally
  3. Record $26.2B backlog with 1.5x book-to-burn ratio for 21 consecutive quarters and double-digit pipeline growth providing 6+ quarters of revenue visibility, with FY2026 guidance raised to 14% earnings growth at midpoint [1]
Key Risks
  1. Net profit margin of 3.2% on total revenue means any slowdown in pass-through project activity, labor cost inflation, or project execution issues could disproportionately impact earnings and dividend coverage
  2. D/E of 135% and elevated net debt limits financial flexibility for M&A or shareholder returns, and rising interest rates increase debt servicing costs on the leveraged capital structure
  3. Post-IIJA infrastructure spending deceleration in the US after 2028 could compress the secular growth tailwind, and government budget pressures from deficit reduction may slow project awards in AECOM's core public-sector end markets
