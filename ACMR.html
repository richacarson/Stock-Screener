Company Profile
Technology ·Semiconductor Equipment & Materials ·NASDAQ ·United States
ACM Research develops, manufactures, and sells single-wafer wet cleaning equipment and other semiconductor process tools. Products include SAPS, TEBO, and Ultra C cleaning tools, electrochemical plating (ECP) systems, ACM Planetary furnace family, and advanced packaging equipment. Founded by Dr. David Wang in 1998, headquartered in Fremont, California with principal operations through subsidiary ACM Research (Shanghai). Named 2025 Innovator of the Year by Portland Business Journal.

Investment Thesis
ACM Research is a high-growth semiconductor equipment company riding the dual tailwinds of China's domestic chip localization push and global AI-driven capacity expansion. Q1 2026 delivered $231.3M revenue (up 34.2% YoY, beating estimates by 6.2%) with shipments of $241M (up 54%), and management confirmed FY2026 guidance of $1.08-$1.175B (25% growth at midpoint) [1]. The 5yr revenue CAGR of 46.6% is exceptional, and the balance sheet is pristine with D/E of just 20.7% and ample liquidity. At ~20.6x forward P/E and PEG of ~0.82, the stock is attractively valued for 25%+ revenue growth with expanding margins. Gross margin of 46.5% exceeded the midpoint of the 42-48% target range, and the product portfolio is broadening into higher-margin categories — furnace, ECP, and advanced packaging [1][3]. R&D intensity of 16-18% of sales and Dr. Wang's 100+ patents provide a deep innovation moat.
The bear case centers on existential geopolitical risk. ACM Research generates the majority of its revenue through its Shanghai subsidiary serving Chinese semiconductor fabs, and ACM Shanghai and ACM Korea were added to the BIS Entity List in December 2024, restricting access to items subject to U.S. export controls [4]. Escalating U.S.-China restrictions could limit the company's ability to serve advanced-node customers in China or restrict component sourcing from Western suppliers, threatening the core growth thesis. Insider selling is a material red flag — CEO Wang sold $9.5M in shares over the last 6 months with zero purchases, despite publicly guiding 25%+ growth [5]. Profit margin of 9.5% and ROE of ~10% remain below target thresholds, reflecting heavy reinvestment and margin pressure from product mix shifts — Q1 2026 gross margin of 46.5% was down from 47.9% prior year as cleaning revenue fell 5.5% while lower-margin ECP and furnace grew [1]. Competition from established Western incumbents and emerging Chinese competitors could pressure market share gains as the geopolitical environment evolves.

  1. FY2026 revenue guidance of $1.08-$1.175B (25% growth at midpoint) driven by ACM Planetary furnace ramp, Ultra C Tahoe, and continued single-wafer cleaning market share gains in China and expanding international markets [1]

Key Risks

  2. CEO David Wang sold $9.5M in shares over the last 6 months with zero purchases under a pre-arranged 10b5-1 plan, creating a material disconnect between public guidance of 25%+ growth and personal capital allocation decisions [5]
  3. Customer concentration in Chinese semiconductor fabs creates geographic and political risk — any slowdown in China's semiconductor investment cycle, policy shifts, or increased competition from domestic Chinese equipment startups could materially impact growth
