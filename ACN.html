Investment Thesis
Accenture at 13.3x forward P/E remains attractively valued for the world's largest IT services company, still trading at a ~40% discount to its 5-year average multiple despite recovering from $127 to $184 post the June 2026 crash [6]. Q3 FY2026 delivered reassuring results — revenue grew 6% to $18.7B, EPS rose 9% to $3.80, and operating margin expanded 20bps to 17% — demonstrating that the AI transformation is not yet eroding the top line [1]. The company's $5.5B+ in generative AI bookings, 77,000 AI specialists, and $9B acquisition push position Accenture as the 'picks and shovels' provider for enterprise AI adoption, and the reorganization under Reinvention Services gives the company a structural platform for the next growth cycle [1][3]. The ~3.5% dividend yield at a 47% payout ratio with ~20 consecutive years of growth provides reliable income support [6].
The fundamental bear case remains that AI structurally compresses consulting billable hours — the $5.5B in AI bookings cannot yet offset the secular erosion of the $72B revenue base built on headcount, and the $923M restructuring charge is only the beginning of transformation costs [3]. FY2026 local currency revenue growth guidance of 3-4% is well below the historical 8-12% growth trajectory, and DOGE-driven federal spending cuts continue removing 1-1.5 percentage points of growth with limited visibility on recovery [1]. The stock's recovery from $127 to $184 prices in some improvement, limiting the margin of safety if AI cannibalization accelerates or macro conditions deteriorate. Net insider selling continues, with executives not buying the 'generational value' narrative at current prices.
