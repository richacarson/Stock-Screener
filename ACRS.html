Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Aclaris Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing novel drug candidates for immuno-inflammatory diseases, with a pipeline anchored by dual kinase inhibitor platform technology targeting ITK, JAK3, and TXK pathways. Lead candidates include bosakitug (ATI-045, anti-IL-13 bispecific antibody) in Phase 2 for atopic dermatitis with topline results expected October 2026, ATI-2138 (dual ITK/JAK3 inhibitor) in Phase 2a, ATI-052 in Phase 1b for asthma and atopic dermatitis, ATI-9494 (oral dual ITK/TXK inhibitor), and lepzacitinib for atopic dermatitis. As of Q2 2026, cash and marketable securities totaled $170.6M with runway through 2028, supplemented by $40.2M raised via at-the-market offering post-quarter. Q2 2026 net loss was $21.5M on TTM revenue of $7.8M. Market cap is approximately $633M.

AI Risk Protection (7/9): Aclaris's core business — developing novel small and large molecule therapeutics through clinical trials — is a physical and biological process that AI structurally cannot replace [2].
