Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Acrivon Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company discovering and developing precision oncology medicines using its proprietary Acrivon Predictive Precision Proteomics (AP3) platform. The AP3 platform uses AI-driven phosphoproteomics and machine learning to create drug-specific OncoSignature companion diagnostics that predict patient response. Lead candidate ACR-368 targets CHK1/CHK2 for endometrial and ovarian cancers with a 39% overall response rate in Phase 2b. Second program ACR-2316 (WEE1/PKMYT1 inhibitor) targets lung cancers. Third candidate ACR-6840 targets CDK11. Global team operates from Greater Boston and Medicon Valley (Southern Scandinavia). Founded in 2018, headquartered in Watertown, Massachusetts.
