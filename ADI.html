Company Profile
Technology ·Semiconductors ·NASDAQ ·United States
Analog Devices designs, manufactures, and markets high-performance analog, mixed-signal, and digital signal processing integrated circuits used in industrial, automotive, communications, and consumer applications. Under CEO and Chair Vincent Roche, ADI has grown from $2.7B revenue (FY2012) to record Q2 FY2026 revenue of $3.62B (+37% YoY) through a combination of organic innovation and transformative acquisitions (Hittite Microwave 2014, Linear Technology 2017, Maxim Integrated 2021). TTM revenue reached $12.74B with 30% YoY growth as the semiconductor cycle recovery accelerates. ADI has raised its dividend for 22 consecutive years and is approaching Dividend Aristocrat status.

Investment Thesis
Analog Devices at 23.4x forward earnings is a premier analog semiconductor franchise riding a multi-year AI infrastructure supercycle, with record Q2 FY2026 revenue of $3.62B (+37% YoY) demonstrating the strongest growth trajectory in the company's history [1]. The conservative D/E of 27%, profit margin of 26%, and 22 consecutive years of dividend growth (11% CAGR) provide a rare combination of growth and income durability. ADI's products are essential AI infrastructure -- power management, data conversion, and signal processing chips required by every GPU cluster, autonomous vehicle, and AI-enabled factory -- making the company a direct beneficiary of the secular AI buildout [1]. The Maxim integration is complete, unlocking cross-selling synergies and operational leverage that should drive margin expansion as the cycle matures. Management expects FY2027 to be a 'brisk growth year,' and 5-year revenue CAGR of 15.17% validates the compounding trajectory.
The bear case centers on semiconductor cyclicality, valuation, and the sustainability of AI demand. Trailing P/E of 44.3x reflects cycle-trough earnings now rapidly recovering -- the risk is that the current 37% revenue growth is the peak of a cyclical rebound rather than the beginning of sustained secular growth, and the forward P/E of 23.4x prices in continued double-digit growth [4]. ROE of 12.23% remains below the 15% target, depressed by $32B+ in goodwill from three major acquisitions that inflate the equity base without generating proportional returns -- if revenue growth slows, the return profile looks less compelling. The payout ratio of 74% is elevated relative to the 40% target, creating less margin for error on the dividend. Semiconductor inventories across the supply chain may be rebuilt ahead of tariff uncertainty, creating pull-forward risk. Customer concentration in industrial, automotive, and communications means a macro slowdown in any of these end markets could compress revenue rapidly. The Inspire Impact Score of -49 (MISALIGNED) due to LGBT Activism may limit faith-based allocation despite strong ESG practices [3].
