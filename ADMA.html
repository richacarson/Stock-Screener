Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
ADMA Biologics is a commercial-stage biopharmaceutical company manufacturing, marketing, and developing specialty plasma-derived biologics for immunodeficient patients. Flagship products ASCENIV and BIVIGAM are intravenous immunoglobulin (IVIG) therapies for primary humoral immunodeficiency, and Nabi-HB treats acute hepatitis B exposure. The company operates plasma collection centers and a manufacturing facility in Boca Raton, Florida. Q2 2026 revenue was $124.4M with ASCENIV growing 24% YoY to $102.9M. FY2026 guidance reiterated at $530-560M revenue and $170-200M adjusted net income. Market cap approximately $2.15B.

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
8
AI Risk Protection (8/9): Plasma-derived biologics manufacturing is a physical-world operation that AI structurally cannot displace. Collecting human plasma from donors, fractionating it into immunoglobulin products through complex biochemical processes, and navigating FDA regulatory requirements for biologics involve deeply regulated, capital-intensive processes with multi-year barriers to entry [3]. The immunoglobulin market faces chronic supply constraints — AI cannot manufacture human plasma. ASCENIV is differentiated by clinical outcomes data in actual patients (statistically significant reductions in infection-related hospitalizations), not by information processing that AI could replicate [1]. The primary competitive threats are other plasma fractionators (CSL Behring, Grifols, Takeda), not AI tools. AI may even increase demand for specialty biologics as healthcare systems expand. AI Opportunity (0/1): ADMAlytics is an internal operational AI tool for manufacturing optimization, not a customer-facing AI product generating revenue. No AI-related revenue is separately disclosed [3].
