Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
ADMA Biologics is a commercial-stage biopharmaceutical company dedicated to manufacturing, marketing, and developing specialty plasma-derived biologics for immunodeficient patients. Its flagship products ASCENIV and BIVIGAM are intravenous immunoglobulin (IVIG) therapies for primary humoral immunodeficiency, and Nabi-HB treats acute hepatitis B exposure. The company operates plasma collection centers and a manufacturing facility in Boca Raton, Florida. Founded in 2004 by CEO Adam Grossman.

Investment Thesis
ADMA Biologics has completed a remarkable 12x revenue transformation from $42M (2020) to $510M (2025), demonstrating the commercial power of ASCENIV in the $8B+ U.S. immunoglobulin market. Q1 2026 showed resilience despite flat headline revenue — ASCENIV grew 28% YoY, gross margins expanded to 71%, adjusted net income grew 22% to $40.7M, and operating cash flow reached $58M [1]. At 10.84x forward earnings and a PEG of 0.36, the stock is deeply discounted relative to its fundamentals — ROE of 43.3%, profit margin of 32.4%, and free cash flow of $108M confirm the underlying business quality [6]. Management guided FY2026 revenue of $530-560M with adjusted net income of $170-200M and adjusted EBITDA of $265-300M, implying continued profitable growth even in a challenging competitive environment [1]. The yield-enhancement technology and McKesson Specialty distribution agreement provide structural growth levers.
The bear case is significant and explains the 49.6% stock decline. In March 2026, Culper Research published a short report alleging channel stuffing, an undisclosed related party distributor, and -3% real growth in 2025, triggering a 29.1% two-day stock decline and a securities fraud class action lawsuit covering the period August 2024 through March 2026 [5]. Revenue guidance was revised sharply downward from the initial $635M/$255M targets to $530-560M/$170-200M, confirming at minimum a significant growth deceleration [1]. Glassdoor reviews (2.8/5) describe a toxic work culture with CEO outbursts and fear-based management, raising governance and human capital risks [4]. BIVIGAM revenue declined 54% YoY in Q1 2026, indicating product-line vulnerability. Product concentration remains a structural risk — substantially all revenue depends on two IVIG products in a single therapeutic area. Sustainalytics rates ADMA's ESG risk management as 'Weak,' which could limit institutional investor appetite [7].

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
8
AI Risk Protection (7/9): Plasma-derived biologics manufacturing is a physical-world operation that AI structurally cannot displace. Collecting human plasma, fractionating it into immunoglobulin products, and navigating FDA regulatory requirements for biologics involve deeply regulated, capital-intensive processes with multi-year barriers to entry [3]. The immunoglobulin market faces chronic supply constraints — AI cannot manufacture human plasma. ASCENIV is differentiated by clinical outcomes data in actual patients, not by information processing that AI could replicate. The primary competitive threats are other plasma fractionators (CSL Behring, Grifols, Takeda), not AI tools. AI Opportunity (1/1): ADMA's proprietary ADMAlytics platform uses AI-driven process improvements to reduce manufacturing variability and improve production forecasting, demonstrating measurable AI value in biomanufacturing operations [3]. This directly supports the yield-enhancement program driving gross margin expansion to 71%.
