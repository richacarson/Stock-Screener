Company Profile
Industrials ·Security & Protection Services ·NYSE ·United States
ADT Inc. provides security, interactive, and smart home solutions to residential and small business customers in the United States. The company offers burglar and life safety alarms, smart security cameras, smart home automation, video surveillance, and personal emergency response systems. ADT serves over 6 million customers through its monitoring centers, field service network, and the ADT+ technology platform developed in partnership with Google. In February 2026, ADT acquired Origin AI for $170M, adding 200+ patents in Wi-Fi ambient sensing for whole-home intelligence. Founded in 1874, headquartered in Boca Raton, Florida.

Investment Thesis
ADT is the dominant U.S. home security provider with over 6 million customers, generating $1.3B quarterly revenue with 80%+ gross margins and rapidly growing free cash flow — adjusted FCF surged 48% YoY to $406M in Q2 2026, prompting management to raise full-year guidance [1]. At just 7.52x forward earnings, ADT trades at a deep value multiple for a business with high recurring revenue, powerful brand moats, and a 3.0% dividend yield. The February 2026 Origin AI acquisition for $170M adds 200+ patents in ambient Wi-Fi sensing technology, positioning ADT to evolve from security monitoring into whole-home intelligence with applications in aging-in-place and health monitoring [4]. The Verisure licensing deal generates a minimum $30M over five years plus activation fees, validating the technology's commercial value. Revenue payback of 2.3 years on new subscribers demonstrates efficient customer acquisition, and the company returned $684M to shareholders through buybacks and dividends in H1 2026 [1].
The primary risk is ADT's leveraged balance sheet — $7.4B net debt at 2.8x adjusted EBITDA constrains financial flexibility and consumes significant cash flow in interest payments, with management flagging $50-100M each in higher cash taxes and interest expenses anticipated for 2027 [1]. Revenue growth of just 2% YoY with monitoring and services revenue declining 1% suggests the mature security market offers limited organic expansion, and gross subscriber additions of 190,000 with RMR additions down 17% YoY signal slowing customer acquisition momentum [1]. Trailing 12-month attrition of 13.1% represents meaningful subscriber churn that must be offset by new additions. The dividend was cut from $0.84 to $0.14 during the 2020 pandemic, and at approximately $0.23 annually remains well below pre-cut levels with only 2 consecutive years of growth — undermining the income investment thesis. DIY security competitors (Ring, SimpliSafe) continue to pressure the professionally-installed market with lower-cost AI-powered alternatives [2].
Key Catalysts
  1. Origin AI ambient sensing integration into ADT+ platform enabling new revenue streams from aging-in-place, health monitoring, and whole-home intelligence use cases beyond traditional security, with 200+ patents providing competitive moat [4]
  2. Adjusted free cash flow growth of approximately 20-30% in 2026 (raised guidance) enabling accelerated debt repayment and shareholder returns that could rerate the equity from current deep-value 7.5x forward earnings
  3. Google partnership evolution delivering differentiated AI-powered security and home automation features, combined with Origin AI sensing capabilities, that widen the competitive moat against DIY alternatives
Key Risks
  1. Heavy debt burden at 206% D/E ($7.4B net debt) with management warning of $50-100M each in higher cash taxes and interest expenses in 2027, constraining financial flexibility and creating vulnerability to refinancing risk
  2. Slowing subscriber growth — gross RMR additions down 17% YoY and gross unit additions down 22% in Q2 2026, with trailing 12-month attrition of 13.1%, suggesting the professionally-installed security market faces structural headwinds from lower-cost DIY competitors [1]
  3. Dividend credibility gap — the dramatic cut from $0.84 to $0.14 in 2020 and current annual payout of approximately $0.23 with only 2 consecutive years of growth undermines ADT's positioning as a reliable income investment
