Company Profile
Industrials ·Security & Protection Services ·NYSE ·United States
ADT Inc. provides security, interactive, and smart home solutions to residential and small business customers in the United States. The company offers burglar and life safety alarms, smart security cameras, smart home automation, video surveillance, and personal emergency response systems. ADT serves over 6 million customers through its monitoring centers, field service network, and the ADT+ technology platform developed in partnership with Google. In February 2026, ADT acquired Origin AI for $170M, adding 200+ patents in Wi-Fi ambient sensing for whole-home intelligence. Founded in 1874, headquartered in Boca Raton, Florida.

Investment Thesis
ADT is the dominant U.S. home security provider with over 6 million customers, generating $1.3B quarterly revenue with 80%+ gross margins and rapidly growing free cash flow — adjusted FCF surged approximately 30% YoY in 2026, prompting management to raise full-year guidance [1]. At just 6.3x forward earnings, ADT trades at a deep value multiple for a business with high recurring revenue, powerful brand moats, and a 3.5% dividend yield. The February 2026 Origin AI acquisition for $170M adds 200+ patents in ambient Wi-Fi sensing technology, positioning ADT to evolve from security monitoring into whole-home intelligence with applications in aging-in-place and health monitoring [4]. The Verisure licensing deal generates a minimum $30M over five years plus activation fees, validating the technology's commercial value.
The primary risk is ADT's leveraged balance sheet — approximately $7.4B net debt at 224% D/E constrains financial flexibility and consumes significant cash flow in interest payments, with management flagging $50-100M each in higher cash taxes and interest expenses anticipated for 2027 [1]. Revenue growth of just 2% YoY with monitoring and services revenue declining suggests the mature security market offers limited organic expansion, and trailing attrition of approximately 13% represents meaningful subscriber churn that must be offset by new additions. The stock's 18% decline from $7.65 to $6.25 since August 2026 reflects market skepticism about growth prospects despite improving cash flows. The dividend was cut from $0.84 to $0.14 during the 2020 pandemic and remains at just $0.22 annually with no growth since 2024, undermining the income investment thesis [5].
