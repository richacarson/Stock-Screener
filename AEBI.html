Company Profile
Industrials ·Specialty Vehicles ·NASDAQ ·Switzerland
Aebi Schmidt Holding AG is a global specialty vehicles leader formed through the July 2025 merger of the Swiss-headquartered Aebi Schmidt Group (founded 1883) with The Shyft Group. The combined company operates across snow removal and de-icing equipment, street and runway sweepers, truck and RV chassis, truck bodies, walk-in vans, vehicle upfitting, and steep-terrain cultivation equipment. Serves commercial, municipal, and airport fleets across Europe and North America with approximately $2B in annual revenue.

Investment Thesis
Aebi Schmidt is a compelling turnaround story with clear catalysts. The Shyft merger created a global specialty vehicle leader with $1.95-2.15B 2026 revenue guidance, a $1.3B order backlog (up 23%), and order intake growing 9% YoY [1]. Q1 2026 revenue of $455.6M beat estimates, and management expects sequential acceleration through 2026 with a significantly stronger second half, driven by Walk-in-Van revenue conversion and European winter maintenance equipment demand. The forward P/E of 13.99 reflects reasonable valuation for a company with strong order visibility, and EPS is expected to grow 65.8% as merger synergies and cost rationalization take hold [3][4]. At $11.71 per share versus a 52-week high of $15.96, the stock offers meaningful upside if integration executes as planned.
The bear case centers on razor-thin profitability — net income of just $0.7M in Q1 2026 (0.15% net margin) and ROE of 1.64% signal that the combined company has not yet achieved the cost synergies promised by the merger [1][3]. Integration risk remains elevated with a D/E of 93% reflecting acquisition debt. The specialty vehicle industry is cyclical, and municipal budgets (a key customer segment) are vulnerable to fiscal tightening. The stock has declined 27% from its 52-week high, suggesting the market is skeptical about near-term profitability improvement. Revenue growth of 7% on a like-for-like basis is modest for a Growth-sleeve stock, and the 0.85% dividend yield signals limited income appeal. The company must demonstrate meaningful margin expansion in Q2-Q4 2026 to validate the merger thesis [4][5].
Key Catalysts
  1. Walk-in-Van revenue conversion expected to accelerate in Q2 2026, driving sequential revenue growth and margin improvement through higher-margin product mix shift
  2. Full-year 2026 guidance reaffirmed at $1.95-2.15B revenue with management expecting a significantly stronger second half as integration synergies materialize and order backlog converts

Key Risks

  2. Cyclical exposure to municipal government budgets for snow removal and sweeping equipment creates vulnerability to fiscal austerity or delayed procurement decisions
  3. Post-merger cultural integration risk between Swiss-headquartered Aebi Schmidt and Michigan-based Shyft operations could disrupt productivity and talent retention during the critical 12-24 month integration window
