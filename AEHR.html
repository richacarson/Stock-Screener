Company Profile
Technology ·Semiconductor Equipment & Materials ·NASDAQ ·United States
Aehr Test Systems provides semiconductor test and burn-in solutions for wafer-level, singulated die, and packaged part applications. The company's FOX-XP, FOX-NP, and Sonoma systems serve customers testing AI accelerator processors, silicon photonics, gallium nitride power semiconductors, silicon carbide power devices for EVs, and data storage devices. Aehr is the only provider of wafer-level turnkey test and burn-in solutions for AI processors. Enhanced Sonoma handles up to 2,000W per device for next-generation GPUs and CPUs.
136 Employees ·www.aehr.com

Investment Thesis
Aehr Test Systems has completed a transformative pivot from SiC/EV to AI infrastructure, with Q4 FY2026 delivering record bookings of $60.7M (up 5x YoY), a return to GAAP profitability ($1.4M net income), and AI+silicon photonics now comprising over 80% of quarterly revenue [1]. FY2027 guidance of $130-$150M (160-200% growth) with 18-22% pre-tax margins signals a step-function revenue inflection as the AI data center buildout accelerates demand for wafer-level test and burn-in [1]. Effective backlog of $100.6M provides strong near-term visibility. Aehr remains the only provider of wafer-level turnkey test and burn-in solutions for AI processors, creating a monopolistic position in a critical supply chain bottleneck [2]. The pristine balance sheet (D/E 7%, $37M cash) provides zero financial risk during the ramp.
The bear case centers on extreme valuation and execution risk for a 136-employee company attempting to scale revenue from $50M to $130-$150M in one year. At ~$93 and forward PE of ~130x estimated FY2027 EPS, virtually all future growth is priced in with no margin of safety — any execution miss, booking delay, or customer concentration issue could trigger severe multiple compression [5]. FY2026 revenue of $50M declined 15.3% from FY2025 with a -$7.1M net loss, meaning the stock prices in growth that has not yet materialized in financial statements [1]. Customer concentration in hyperscale AI creates binary dependency — the $60.7M record Q4 bookings included large orders from a small number of customers. Insider selling of $4.2M+ by directors near the 52-week high signals insiders view current prices as an opportunity to take profits [4]. The semiconductor equipment industry is cyclical, and Aehr's revenue history (from $82M peak to $50M trough to $130-$150M guided) demonstrates extreme volatility that could reverse if AI spending moderates.
