Company Profile
Technology ·Semiconductor Equipment & Materials ·NASDAQ ·United States
Aehr Test Systems provides semiconductor test and burn-in solutions for wafer-level, singulated die, and packaged part applications. The company's FOX-XP, FOX-NP, and Sonoma systems serve customers testing AI accelerator processors, silicon photonics, gallium nitride power semiconductors, silicon carbide power devices for EVs, and data storage devices. Aehr is the only provider of wafer-level AND package-level turnkey test and burn-in solutions for AI processors. Enhanced Sonoma handles up to 2,000W per device for next-generation GPUs and CPUs. FY2026 (ended May 2026) revenue of $50.0M declined 15.3% from prior year, but Q4 delivered record $60.7M bookings (5x YoY), effective backlog of $100.6M, and FY2027 guidance of $130-$150M (160-200% growth).

Investment Thesis
Aehr Test Systems has completed a transformative pivot from SiC/EV to AI infrastructure, with Q4 FY2026 delivering record bookings of $60.7M (up 5x YoY), a return to GAAP profitability ($1.4M net income), and AI+silicon photonics now comprising over 80% of quarterly revenue [1]. FY2027 guidance of $130-$150M (160-200% growth) with 18-22% pre-tax margins signals a step-function revenue inflection as AI data center buildout accelerates demand for wafer-level and package-level test and burn-in [1]. The lead hyperscaler customer continues placing multiple follow-on orders for Sonoma, and a $10M AI customer order in August 2026 confirms sustained momentum [3]. Effective backlog of $100.6M provides strong near-term visibility. Aehr remains the only provider of both wafer-level AND package-level test and burn-in solutions for AI processors, creating a monopolistic position in a critical supply chain bottleneck [3]. Pristine balance sheet (D/E 4.5%, $37M+ cash) provides zero financial risk during the ramp.
The bear case centers on extreme valuation and execution risk for a 136-employee company attempting to scale revenue from $50M to $130-$150M in one year. At $93.49 and forward PE of 67.58x, the market prices in substantial future growth with minimal margin of safety — any execution miss, booking delay, or customer concentration issue could trigger severe multiple compression [5]. FY2026 revenue of $50M declined 15.3% from FY2025 with a -$7.1M net loss, meaning the stock currently prices growth not yet visible in trailing financial statements [1]. Customer concentration in a small number of hyperscale AI companies creates binary dependency — the record bookings rely heavily on one world-leading hyperscaler. Insider selling of $4.2M+ by directors near the 52-week high signals insiders view current prices as an opportunity to take profits rather than add to positions [4]. The semiconductor equipment industry is cyclical, and Aehr's revenue history (from $82M peak to $50M trough to $130-$150M guided) demonstrates extreme volatility that could reverse if AI capital spending moderates. Competition from larger players developing competing wafer-level solutions represents a longer-term structural risk.
