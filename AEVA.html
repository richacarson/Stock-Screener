Company Profile
Technology ·Electronic Equipment, Instruments & Components ·NASDAQ ·United States
Aeva Technologies designs, develops, and manufactures Frequency Modulated Continuous Wave (FMCW) 4D LiDAR sensing systems for autonomous driving, ADAS, industrial automation, and smart infrastructure. The company's proprietary technology measures depth, reflectivity, and instantaneous velocity simultaneously on a single chip, providing superior perception compared to conventional time-of-flight LiDAR. Founded in 2016 by former Apple engineers Soroush Salehian (CEO) and Mina Rezk (CTO/President), Aeva has secured production programs with Daimler Truck and Bendix, strategic partnerships with NVIDIA (physical AI) and LG Innotek (manufacturing), and is expanding into smart city infrastructure via its CityOS platform. FY2025 revenue doubled to $18.1M with 2026 guidance of $30-36M. Market cap ~$1.05B.

Investment Thesis
Aeva's proprietary FMCW 4D LiDAR technology is genuinely differentiated — simultaneously measuring range, reflectivity, and instantaneous velocity on a single chip — and the company has translated that differentiation into credible commercial traction. FY2025 revenue doubled to $18.1M, 2026 guidance of $30-36M implies another 70-100% growth year, and the production pipeline spans automotive (Daimler Truck, Bendix, European passenger OEM), industrial, and smart infrastructure (CityOS) applications [1]. Selection as the reference sensor for NVIDIA DRIVE Hyperion and the LG Innotek manufacturing partnership provide validation from industry leaders [3]. Available liquidity of $246.9M ($121.9M cash plus $125M undrawn credit facility) provides multi-year runway, while non-GAAP operating expenses declined 12% YoY in FY2025, showing improving cost discipline [1].
The bear case is severe. Aeva is deeply unprofitable with -150% profit margin, D/E of 372%, and ROE of -516%, reflecting a company burning cash roughly 6x faster than it generates revenue [4]. The $18.1M annual revenue base is trivial relative to the ~$1B market cap, demanding years of flawless execution to justify valuation. Tesla's camera-only FSD approach fundamentally challenges the premise that autonomous systems require LiDAR — if AI vision proves sufficient, the entire addressable market contracts. Competition from better-capitalized LiDAR peers (Luminar, Hesai, Innoviz) creates pricing pressure. The Glassdoor rating of 2.9/5 with reports of quarterly layoffs, micromanagement, and blame culture raises serious concerns about talent retention in a company where engineering talent is the primary asset [5]. Path to profitability remains distant, and the stock has declined ~28% from its July 2026 levels as the market reassesses LiDAR sector valuations.

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience MODERATE
7
AI Risk Protection (6/9): Aeva manufactures physical LiDAR sensor hardware that AI systems depend upon — not hardware that AI can replace. The FMCW 4D LiDAR is essential enabling infrastructure for autonomous vehicles, robotics, and smart infrastructure, and as AI adoption accelerates, demand for high-quality perception sensors should increase [1][3]. The NVIDIA DRIVE Hyperion integration positions Aeva as a core supplier to the dominant AV computing platform. However, Tesla's camera-only Full Self-Driving approach — which relies entirely on AI neural network vision processing without LiDAR — poses a legitimate substitution risk. If camera-plus-AI perception proves sufficient for L3+ autonomy, the entire LiDAR industry's addressable market shrinks materially. Additionally, competitive pressure from well-funded LiDAR rivals and potential sensor commoditization represent meaningful threats. AI Opportunity (1/1): Aeva generates 100% of its revenue from products that directly enable AI-powered autonomous systems — sensors feeding autonomous perception stacks and the AI-powered CityOS smart infrastructure platform [1][4]. The co-packaged optics opportunity for AI data center networking further ties the company's future to AI infrastructure growth.
