Company Profile
Financial Services ·Insurance - Life ·NYSE ·United States
Aflac Incorporated is the world's largest provider of supplemental health insurance, operating primarily in the United States and Japan. The company offers voluntary insurance products including accident, cancer, critical illness, hospital indemnity, dental, and vision coverage sold at the worksite and directly to consumers. Aflac Japan accounts for approximately 70% of total premium revenue. The company reported TTM revenue of $18.1B with net income of $4.6B and has raised its dividend for 43 consecutive years.

Investment Thesis
Aflac at 15.57x forward earnings with 26.91% profit margin, 16.47% ROE, and D/E of just 26% represents one of the most financially disciplined companies in the insurance sector [1][4]. The 43-year consecutive dividend growth streak with a 15.68% 5-year dividend growth CAGR and only 28% payout ratio provides substantial runway for continued increases — Dividend Aristocrat quality at a reasonable valuation. H1 2026 total revenue of $8.5B demonstrates recovery momentum with Q1 up 27.9% YoY and Q2 delivering $825M net income [1]. Aggressive share repurchases (reducing shares from 600M+ to ~495M over the past decade) amplify per-share value creation. The Inspire Impact Score of 51 (ALIGNED) with zero negative attributions and strong ESG positioning makes AFL suitable for faith-aligned portfolios [3].
The bear case centers on Japan's structural demographic decline — approximately 70% of Aflac's premium revenue comes from a market where the population is shrinking by ~500,000 annually, creating persistent headwinds for organic growth [1]. The 5-year revenue CAGR of -4.54% quantifies this challenge: despite U.S. growth initiatives, total company revenue has contracted over the medium term. The Forward P/E of 15.57 exceeding the trailing P/E of 12.82 suggests the market prices in earnings deceleration. Currency risk from yen/dollar translation adds volatility — a weakening yen can materially reduce dollar-denominated revenue from Aflac Japan. Japan's aging population also increases claims frequency on Aflac's cancer and medical products, potentially pressuring loss ratios even as the premium base erodes. The 2.08% dividend yield, while supported by a strong growth trajectory, is modest for income-focused investors relative to higher-yielding alternatives in the insurance and financial sectors.
Key Catalysts
  1. Aggressive share repurchase program ($1.0B+ in Q1 2026 alone) driving EPS growth above underlying earnings growth, with potential for acceleration if the stock retreats from current levels [1]

  1. Japan demographic decline — population shrinking ~500K annually — creates persistent headwinds for Aflac Japan's premium base, which represents ~70% of total premium revenue and cannot be fully offset by U.S. growth [1]
  2. Yen/dollar currency translation adds significant volatility to reported results; a weakening yen can materially reduce dollar-denominated revenue and earnings from the Japan segment, as reflected in the -4.54% 5-year revenue CAGR
  3. Insurtech competitors leveraging AI for streamlined underwriting and claims processing could erode Aflac's traditional distribution advantages in the U.S. worksite market, particularly among smaller employers adopting digital-first benefits platforms
