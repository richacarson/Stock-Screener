Company Profile
Energy ·Specialty Chemicals - Renewable Fuels ·NYSE American ·United States
Abundia Global Impact Group, Inc. (formerly Houston American Energy Corp.) develops scalable, commercially proven pathways that convert waste plastics and certified biomass into drop-in fuels and low-carbon chemical feedstocks using pyrolysis technology. Partners with BTG Bioliquids for fast pyrolysis and Alterra Technology for waste plastics processing. Acquired RPD Technologies Americas for pilot plant operations. Focused on renewable diesel and Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF) production. Rebranded December 2025.

Investment Thesis
Abundia Global Impact Group is positioned in the attractive waste-to-fuel market, converting waste plastics and biomass into renewable diesel and SAF using commercially demonstrated pyrolysis technologies from BTG Bioliquids and Alterra [1]. The $53M market cap reflects speculative optionality on successful commercialization. CEO Ed Gillespie has assembled technology partnerships and acquired RPD Technologies for pilot plant capabilities, and the appointment of veteran executive Keith Berger as CCO signals commercial readiness preparation [2].
Abundia is a pre-revenue development-stage company with $654K TTM revenue, -$22M annual free cash flow, and less than one year of cash runway [4]. The pivot from Houston American Energy in December 2025 occurred because the oil company was failing, not because of a strategic epiphany. Commercial-scale revenue is not expected until 2028-2029, requiring years of dilutive capital raises — the $20M direct financing in Q1 2026 demonstrates this dependency [4]. Competitors including Plastic Energy and Brightmark already have operating facilities, and the technology remains unproven at Abundia's planned commercial scale. The $53M market cap on $654K revenue represents extreme speculative premium.
Key Catalysts
  1. Phase 1 Renewable Energy Complex completion could validate operational capability and demonstrate commercial-scale pyrolysis processing
  2. RPD Technologies acquisition provides in-house R&D, pilot plant operations, and engineering capabilities to accelerate technology development
  3. Growing demand for Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF) and renewable diesel driven by airline mandates and LCFS credits could provide premium pricing if commercial production is achieved
Key Risks
  1. Pre-revenue with -$22M annual cash burn and less than one year of cash runway, requiring continuous dilutive financing that erodes shareholder value
  2. Commercial revenue not expected until 2028-2029, creating 2-3 years of execution risk with no operating income to validate the business model
  3. Rebrand from failed Houston American Energy raises management credibility concerns, and the waste-to-fuel market already has established competitors with operating facilities
