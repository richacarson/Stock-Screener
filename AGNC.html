Company Profile
Real Estate ·REIT - Mortgage ·NASDAQ ·United States
AGNC Investment Corp. is a leading internally managed real estate investment trust (REIT) that invests primarily in Agency residential mortgage-backed securities (Agency MBS) guaranteed by Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac, or Ginnie Mae. Founded in 2008, AGNC is one of the largest agency mREITs with an investment portfolio of approximately $73B, generating returns through the net interest spread between MBS yields and repo financing costs. Market cap ~$8.7B.
60 Employees ·www.agnc.com

Investment Thesis
AGNC offers one of the highest dividend yields in public markets at 13.5%, supported by a ~$73B portfolio of government-guaranteed Agency MBS with zero credit risk [1]. Q1 2026 EPS of $0.42 beat the $0.37 consensus with revenue of $1.05B exceeding the $988.6M estimate, and Q2 2026 delivered $654M net income ($0.52 diluted EPS) with a 6.7% economic return and book value increasing $0.20 to $8.58 per share [2]. The internally managed structure aligns costs with shareholders (no external management fees), and the leadership team's 12+ year tenure and 90+ combined years of MBS experience provide deep sector expertise [3]. With the Federal Reserve on an easing trajectory and Agency MBS spreads historically wide, AGNC is positioned to benefit from spread compression and book value appreciation. Forward P/E of 7.3x and the Inspire Insight score of +54 (ALIGNED) add value and governance credibility [4].
The bear case centers on structural risks inherent to the mREIT model. AGNC's 7.2x debt-to-equity leverage means small moves in interest rates or MBS spreads produce outsized P&L swings — the 2022-2023 rate shock destroyed approximately 40% of book value [4]. The $0.12/month dividend has been flat since mid-2020 with zero growth, meaning investors receive high current income but no inflation protection — the 5-year dividend CAGR is 0% [5]. Payout ratio of ~77% of GAAP earnings leaves limited margin for book value rebuilding, and the ratio approached 98% just months ago under less favorable conditions. Agency MBS carry zero credit risk but significant prepayment and duration risk, and AGNC's returns are highly correlated to interest rate volatility and yield curve shape. The business model has not evolved since 2008, offering no diversification against a sustained adverse rate environment. Innovation (1/10) and Infinite Game (2/10) scores are inherently low for a financial vehicle, limiting the IOWN composite despite strong AI resilience and faith alignment.
