Company Profile
Consumer Staples ·Farm Products / Agricultural Technology ·NASDAQ ·Malaysia
Agroz Inc. is a Malaysian investment holding company operating as a vertically integrated agricultural technology provider, specializing in designing, developing, building, operating, and managing large-scale commercial indoor CEA (Controlled Environment Agriculture) vertical farms. The company grows leafy greens (butterhead, coral, oak leaf, kale, arugula, rocket) and is expanding into Japanese strawberry cultivation. Agroz IPO'd on NASDAQ in October 2025 at $4.00/share and has since declined approximately 93% to $0.28. The company was placed on Nasdaq's non-compliance list in May 2026 for late Form 20-F filing. Market cap is approximately $6M.
