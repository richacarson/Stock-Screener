Company Profile
Consumer Defensive ·Farm Products ·NASDAQ ·Malaysia
Agroz Inc. is a Malaysian vertically integrated agricultural technology company that designs, builds, operates, and manages large-scale commercial indoor CEA (Controlled Environment Agriculture) vertical farms. The company grows leafy greens (butterhead, coral, kale, arugula, rocket) and is expanding into Japanese strawberry cultivation using AI-powered technologies. Agroz IPO'd on NASDAQ in October 2025 at $4.00/share, has experienced extreme volatility (52-week high $144.00, current $2.84), and faces ongoing Nasdaq compliance issues including a proposed reverse stock split. Market cap is approximately $5.2M with 26 employees.
