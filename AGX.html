Company Profile
Industrials ·Engineering & Construction ·NYSE ·United States
Argan is a holding company operating through Power, Industrial, and Teledata segments, primarily through its wholly-owned subsidiary Gemma Power Systems — a leading EPC contractor for natural gas-fired power plants with nearly 20 GW of installed capacity. The 'Gas-to-Data' strategy positions the company to build power plants supplying electricity to hyperscale AI data centers. FY2026 (ending Jan 31, 2026) revenue of $944.61M, net income $137.8M ($9.74 EPS), with a virtually debt-free balance sheet holding $973.56M in cash. Record project backlog of $2.9B at year-end with $2.5B in new contracts added during the year. Q1 FY2027 revenue surged 50% YoY to $291M. Headquartered in Rockville, Maryland. CEO David Watson since August 2022.
1,410 Employees ·arganinc.com

Investment Thesis
Argan is a pure-play beneficiary of the AI-driven power infrastructure buildout, with its Gas-to-Data strategy generating a record $2.9B backlog (+115% from FY2025 opening) that provides 9-10 quarters of revenue visibility into 2028 [1]. FY2026 revenue of $944.61M with net income of $137.8M ($9.74 EPS) demonstrates profitable execution, and Q1 FY2027 accelerated to $291M (+50% YoY) with stronger margins and cash generation [2]. The fortress balance sheet — $973.56M cash against $9.58M debt (D/E 2%) — provides exceptional financial flexibility and eliminates balance sheet risk entirely. ROE of 38.52% reflects efficient capital deployment, and the $200M buyback authorization signals confidence in sustainable cash generation [2][4]. As hyperscalers race to deploy AI capacity, the structural need for baseload gas-fired power to supplement intermittent renewables creates a multi-year demand tailwind that Gemma Power Systems is uniquely positioned to capture with its 20 GW of installed capacity experience [3][5].
The bear case centers squarely on valuation and concentration risk. At Forward P/E 50.63 and PEG 4.95 (well above the 3.0 floor), Argan is priced as a hypergrowth company on FY2026 revenue that grew only 8.06% — the stock's +193.54% 52-week return has dramatically outpaced fundamental improvement [4]. The implied long-term earnings growth rate of ~10.2% embedded in the PEG does not support the current multiple, creating significant downside if AI data center capex growth moderates. Heavy concentration in natural gas-fired plants (~70%+ of backlog) exposes Argan to regulatory risk from faster decarbonization mandates or shifts toward distributed generation, nuclear, or advanced renewables that bypass gas-fired baseload [5]. The $2.8B backlog at Q1 FY2027 (down from $2.9B at FY2026 year-end) hints at potential moderation in new contract wins after the FY2026 intake surge. CEO David Watson has been in role only since August 2022, and the company's 1,410-employee workforce creates execution risk on billion-dollar EPC contracts where project delays or cost overruns can materially impact profitability. The 0.32% dividend yield provides negligible income support during any share price correction. Inspire Impact Score of 53 (ALIGNED) is positive for faith-based investors, but the company's fossil fuel focus may create screening tension for ESG-oriented mandates despite the energy transition framing.
