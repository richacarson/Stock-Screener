AH Realty Trust, Inc. (formerly Armada Hoffler Properties) is a self-managed real estate investment trust focused on the ownership and operation of high-quality retail and mixed-use office assets in the Mid-Atlantic and Southeastern United States. The company completed a strategic transformation in 2026, executing $565M+ in asset sales (11 multifamily properties), paying down $460M in debt, and exiting construction and multifamily to become a pure-play retail/office REIT [1]. Q2 FY2026 FFO as adjusted of $0.14/share with full-year guidance raised to $0.53-$0.57. Weighted leased occupancy of 95.4%. Founded in 1979, rebranded as AH Realty Trust in March 2026, headquartered in Virginia Beach, Virginia.

Investment Thesis
AH Realty Trust delivered a transformative Q2 2026 with the strategic restructuring largely complete: $565M+ in multifamily asset sales executed, $460M in debt paid down, and the company now operating as a focused retail/office REIT with same-store NOI growth of 2.9% retail and an impressive 8.3% office YoY [1]. Management raised FY2026 FFO as adjusted guidance to $0.53-$0.57/share (up from $0.51-$0.55), and AFFO of $0.18/share in Q2 covers the $0.14/quarter dividend with a 77% payout ratio [1]. The 8.86% dividend yield at $6.32 provides substantial income while the deleveraging continues toward the 5.5x-6.5x net debt/EBITDA target. Analyst consensus is Buy with a $7.50 average price target (+18.7% upside) [2]. Insider conviction is evident: Director A. Kirk purchased 40,000 shares at $7.64 in March 2026, a 58% increase in his personal holdings [3].
The bear case remains substantial. D/E of approximately 200% (even after $460M in debt paydown) represents severe financial leverage that constrains growth and creates refinancing risk [2]. ROE is negative at -0.14%, meaning the company is currently destroying equity value on a GAAP basis. Revenue declined 48% in Q2 2026 due to the transformation, and TTM revenue of $288.84M grows at only 3.2% [2]. The dividend has been cut from $0.88 to $0.56 annual (-36%), the 5-year dividend growth CAGR is approximately -8%, and FFO payout ratio of ~102% barely covers the current $0.56 dividend — any FFO shortfall could trigger a further cut that would compress the already-distressed stock price [4]. The Inspire Impact Score of 0 (MIXED) with no positive or negative attributions reflects minimal ESG visibility. The 52-week range of $5.13-$7.71 and price decline from $6.90 to $6.32 since July suggest investors are not yet pricing in recovery. With only 98 employees and a $628M market cap, AH Realty Trust lacks the scale, diversification, and financial resources to compete effectively with larger REITs for the best properties and tenants.
