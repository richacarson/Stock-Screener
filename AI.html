C3.ai, Inc. operates an enterprise AI software platform that enables organizations to develop, deploy, and operate AI applications at scale. Products include the C3 Agentic AI Platform, industry-specific SaaS applications for manufacturing, energy, defense, and financial services, and C3 Generative AI. Founded by Tom Siebel (founder of Siebel Systems/Oracle CRM), who resumed the CEO role on May 8, 2026 after a health-related absence. The board has initiated a search for a successor CEO. FY2026 revenue was $250.3M (-35.7% YoY) with $673M in cash. Market cap ~$1.5B.

Investment Thesis
C3.ai sits at the epicenter of the enterprise AI megatrend with a pure-play platform (C3 Agentic AI, C3 Generative AI) generating $227.1M in subscription revenue (94% of Q4 total), a fortress balance sheet with $673M in cash ($4.16/share) against minimal debt, and founder Tom Siebel's resumed leadership bringing the vision and urgency the company needs [1][3]. At $9.84/share with $673M cash, the market values the operating business at less than 1x revenue — a potential deep value entry if enterprise AI adoption accelerates and the company can stabilize revenue under Siebel's direct leadership. The Inspire Impact Score of 66 (ALIGNED) with zero negative attributions makes this a clean holding for faith-based investors. The enterprise AI market is forecast to grow at 30%+ CAGR through 2030.
C3.ai's FY2026 revenue collapsed 35.7% to $250.3M with a GAAP loss of $498.5M, burning through $1.3M per employee annually while competing against Microsoft, Google, AWS, Salesforce, and Palantir — companies with 100-1000x C3.ai's resources and existing enterprise relationships [1][4]. The board's initiated CEO succession search implicitly acknowledges that 73-year-old Siebel's leadership is not a long-term solution, creating governance uncertainty during a critical period [3]. Revenue has declined for four consecutive quarters, and Q4 FY2026 revenue of $51.6M suggests no near-term inflection point [1]. The history of insider selling, including Siebel's $288M post-IPO sale and continued CFO selling, undermines the alignment narrative [5]. At current burn rates, the $673M cash balance provides approximately 3 years of runway, but without a return to growth, the value destruction is accelerating.
