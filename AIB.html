Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Infrastructure / Data Centers ·NYSE American ·United States
AIB Data Centers Inc. (formerly BlockchAIn Digital Infrastructure, Inc., rebranded June 2026) develops and operates digital infrastructure for AI hosting and high-performance computing workloads. The company operates a 40 MW data center at its CLT-01 site, reports 65 MW of contracted power, approximately 140 MW under development, and a total identified pipeline of 570 MW across six active sites. CEO Jerry Tang leads the company's pivot from cryptocurrency mining to AI/HPC-grade colocation. The company raised $63.25M in a public offering in 2026 and carries no debt, with $52.8M in cash at June 30, 2026.

Investment Thesis
AIB Data Centers is a speculative micro-cap play on the AI infrastructure buildout, offering ground-floor exposure to one of the strongest secular demand trends in technology. The 570 MW total identified pipeline across six active sites, 65 MW contracted at CLT-01, and 140 MW under development provide a roadmap for significant capacity expansion [2]. The clean balance sheet ($52.8M cash, zero debt, $82.7M equity) and $63.25M capital raise provide runway to execute. If the company can convert pipeline to signed leases and fill capacity with AI/HPC tenants, the current ~$85M market cap could re-rate significantly — 570 MW at industry colocation rates would generate substantial revenue. The AI Resilience score of 8 (LOW RISK) reflects the structural positioning on the enabling side of the AI value chain.
The bear case is severe. Q2 2026 revenue declined 39% YoY to $2.92M with a net loss of $3.48M, and management acknowledged at the Emerging Growth Conference that the company has no signed lease — the 570 MW pipeline is identified capacity, not contracted revenue [1][2]. The June 2026 rebrand from BlockchAIn Digital Infrastructure raises questions about whether leadership is building enduring capability or chasing the next hot market theme. Gross margin deteriorated from 27% to 12% in Q1 2026 on higher energy costs, and Q2 adjusted EBITDA was -$3.1M. AIB competes against hyperscalers building their own facilities and established operators like Equinix (>$85B market cap) and Digital Realty (>$50B) with vastly superior scale, capital, and customer relationships. At $1.81/share, the stock prices in significant execution risk, but the lack of signed leases, declining revenue, and pre-profitability status make this suitable only for highly risk-tolerant investors with speculative allocation sizing.

  1. Conversion of the 570 MW identified pipeline into signed lease agreements — the first signed lease would represent a transformative de-risking event and validate the AI data center buildout thesis [2]

  3. Competitive pressure from hyperscalers (AWS, Azure, GCP) self-building AI data centers and established colocation operators (Equinix, Digital Realty) with 100x+ scale advantages in capital, relationships, and operational expertise [2]

AI Risk Protection (7/9): AIB Data Centers is a direct beneficiary of AI adoption rather than a company threatened by it. The entire business model is predicated on providing physical infrastructure — power, cooling, and connectivity — for AI training and inference workloads [2]. As AI compute demand grows exponentially, purpose-built data center capacity becomes a critical bottleneck, positioning AIB on the enabling side of the AI value chain.
