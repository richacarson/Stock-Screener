Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NASDAQ ·United States
Firefly Neuroscience is an AI-powered medical technology company developing neuroscientific solutions for brain health assessment and neurological disorder diagnosis. Its FDA-510(k) cleared Brain Network Analytics (BNA) technology analyzes EEG data using machine learning to objectively assess cognitive function across conditions including depression, dementia, anxiety, concussions, and ADHD. Following the May 2025 Evoke Neuroscience acquisition, the company has surpassed 200,000 standardized EEG/ERP brain scans deployed across 85+ US clinical sites. CEO Jon Olsen leads the 17-person team. Q1 2026 revenue of $485K. Market cap approximately $17.6M.

Investment Thesis
Firefly Neuroscience at $1.12 with a $17.6M market cap operates at the intersection of AI and brain health with an FDA-510(k) cleared BNA platform that has surpassed 200,000 standardized brain scans across 85+ US clinical sites [2]. Revenue growth of 71.9% YoY reflects rapid expansion from a near-zero base, with Q1 2026 revenue of $485K up from $43K a year earlier, and customer brain scan volumes surging 291% at TMS center partners [1]. The company holds $9.2M cash against zero debt, providing near-term runway. The NVIDIA Connect partnership to build a foundational model of the human brain positions Firefly at the frontier of neuroscience AI [2]. The Inspire Impact Score of 27 (ALIGNED) with zero negative attributions makes this one of the cleanest holdings for faith-based investors. At $1.12/share, successful commercialization of BNA at scale could generate significant multiples from the current micro-cap base.
The bull case is overwhelmed by existential risks. Firefly has approximately $1.8M in TTM revenue against an estimated $7M+ in annual losses with going concern risk flagged by auditors — the company loses approximately $4 for every $1 in revenue [1]. The $17.6M market cap has declined significantly from higher levels, reflecting sustained market skepticism about the company's ability to achieve profitability without additional dilutive capital raises that erode shareholder value. The 17-person company faces acute execution risk where a single key departure could derail operations, and leadership changes (Executive Chairman resignation in November 2024) signal ongoing organizational instability [3]. Revenue growth from $43K to $485K is encouraging in percentage terms but the absolute numbers are pre-revenue-scale — the company must demonstrate it can scale from 85 clinical sites to hundreds while maintaining data quality. The NVIDIA brain foundation model initiative, while visionary, requires resources far beyond what a 17-person company can independently deploy. Competing against established neuroimaging companies and big tech AI platforms with 100-1000x resources creates a structural disadvantage in commercialization and distribution [1].
