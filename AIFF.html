Company Profile
Healthcare ·Medical Devices ·NASDAQ ·United States
Firefly Neuroscience, Inc. is an AI-powered medical technology company developing neuroscientific solutions for brain health assessment and neurological disorder diagnosis. Its FDA-510(k) cleared Brain Network Analytics (BNA) technology analyzes EEG data using machine learning to objectively assess cognitive function across conditions including depression, dementia, anxiety, concussions, and ADHD. Following the May 2025 Evoke Neuroscience acquisition, the company has surpassed 200,000 standardized EEG/ERP brain scans deployed across 85+ US clinical sites. Aims to build the world's first EEG/ERP-based foundation model of the human brain through NVIDIA Connect. Market cap ~$16.7M.
17 Employees ·fireflyneuro.com
