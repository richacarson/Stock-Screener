Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NYSE American ·United States
AIM ImmunoTech Inc. is a clinical-stage immuno-pharma company focused on developing Ampligen (rintatolimod), a first-in-class investigational dsRNA and TLR3 agonist, for treatment of late-stage pancreatic cancer, ovarian cancer, and immune-deficiency disorders. The company also explores biodefense applications. CEO Thomas K. Equels leads a 21-person team from Ocala, Florida. Q2 2026 reported revenue of $26K, operating loss of $3.5M, and H1 net loss of $6.8M. Cash of $9.9M as of June 30, 2026, but with $7.3M H1 operating cash burn and going concern warning. Shares outstanding increased 894% YoY due to serial dilutive financings. Market cap approximately $7.6M. Ampligen has been in development for approximately 60 years without FDA approval.
