Company Profile
Real Estate ·REIT — Residential Development ·NYSE ·United States
Apartment Investment and Management Company (Aimco) is a diversified real estate company that was primarily focused on value-add and opportunistic multifamily investments in the U.S. The company operated a development-focused REIT with significant exposure to the Miami and Chicago markets. In February 2026, stockholders approved a Plan of Sale and Liquidation, and the company is now actively selling all remaining assets, winding down operations, and distributing proceeds to shareholders. Estimated total liquidating distributions are $5.75-$7.10 per share.

Investment Thesis
AIV offers a liquidation arbitrage opportunity: the stock trades at $2.97 against estimated liquidating distributions of $5.75-$7.10 per share, representing 94-139% potential upside to full realization [1]. Shareholders overwhelmingly approved the Plan of Sale and Liquidation in February 2026 (119M votes in favor vs 109K against). Execution is progressing — the $455M sale of seven Chicago properties (1,495 units) in March 2026 demonstrated ability to realize asset values, and a $1.30/share liquidating distribution was paid in June 2026 [2][3]. The remaining portfolio includes Miami and Fort Lauderdale properties in strong multifamily markets with institutional demand. If management executes the remaining asset sales competently, remaining distributions could significantly exceed the current stock price.
The liquidation timeline and realization risk are the primary concerns. The plan targets completion within 24 months of adoption (by February 2028), but real estate dispositions are subject to market conditions, interest rates, and buyer demand [1]. D/E of 238.61% means the company must sell assets at adequate prices to repay development debt before shareholders receive distributions — if property values decline or credit markets tighten, shareholder recoveries could fall below the $5.75 low-end estimate [4]. The company reported a $95.6M net loss on $35.1M revenue in Q3 2025, indicating significant ongoing operating costs during the wind-down. Revenue declined 34% YoY and will continue declining as assets are sold. Transaction and dissolution costs (legal, advisory, severance) consume a portion of proceeds. The $1.45/share already declared reduces the remaining distribution to approximately $4.30-$5.65 per share — still above the current price, but with meaningful execution and timing risk.
Key Catalysts
  1. Completion of remaining Miami and Fort Lauderdale property sales, which comprise 6,000+ units in high-demand South Florida multifamily markets with institutional buyer interest
  2. Additional liquidating distributions beyond the $1.45/share already declared, with potential for distributions near the $7.10/share high-end estimate if property sales exceed expectations
  3. Orderly dissolution within the 24-month target (by February 2028), with management demonstrating competent execution as evidenced by the $455M Chicago sale
Key Risks
  1. Liquidation realization risk — property values depend on interest rates, credit markets, and multifamily sector demand; a downturn could push distributions below the $5.75/share low-end estimate
  2. D/E of 238.61% means development debt must be repaid from asset sale proceeds before shareholders receive distributions, creating a leverage trap if property values decline
  3. Wind-down costs (legal, advisory, severance, ongoing operating expenses) consume proceeds during the 24-month liquidation timeline, reducing the net amount available for shareholder distributions
