Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Akebia Therapeutics, Inc. is a biopharmaceutical company focused on developing and commercializing therapeutics for patients with kidney diseases. The company markets Vafseo (vadadustat), an oral HIF-PHI therapy for anemia due to chronic kidney disease in adult patients on dialysis, and Auryxia (ferric citrate) for iron deficiency anemia and hyperphosphatemia. Akebia aims to become a leading, fully integrated nephrology-focused biopharmaceutical company. Founded in 2007, headquartered in Cambridge, Massachusetts. Vafseo revenue grew 32% YoY to $15.8M in Q1 2026, but Q2 2026 total revenue fell to $49.1M (vs $62.5M year-ago) as Teva's generic Auryxia entered the market in March 2026 [1][2]. FY2026 revenue guidance revised down to $206.6M from $222.5M [3].

Investment Thesis
Akebia is navigating a critical product transition. Vafseo revenue grew 32% YoY to $15.8M in Q1 2026 and reached $45.8M in FY2025, demonstrating commercial traction as the first oral HIF-PHI therapy for dialysis anemia — a genuine therapeutic improvement over burdensome injectable ESAs for 550K+ U.S. dialysis patients [1]. CEO Butler's 12.67-year tenure through years of losses culminating in FDA approval demonstrates rare leadership persistence. At $0.91/share, the $276M market cap trades at roughly 1.2x FY2026 guided revenue of $206.6M, providing limited downside if Vafseo continues gaining share [3][5]. Cash of $157.3M and positive free cash flow of $60M TTM provide operational runway [2]. Analyst consensus is Strong Buy with a $4.20 average price target (362% upside) [5].
The dominant risk is the Auryxia revenue cliff. Auryxia generated 77% of FY2025 revenue ($181.5M) but lost market exclusivity, and Teva received FDA approval for a generic in March 2026 [3]. Q2 2026 total revenue fell 21% YoY to $49.1M (vs $62.5M year-ago), missing estimates by 5.8%, as generic competition accelerated Auryxia erosion [2]. FY2026 revenue guidance was revised down from $222.5M to $206.6M, and losses are expected to widen to $0.17/share from $0.056 in FY2025 [3]. Vafseo must ramp fast enough to offset Auryxia's decline — a demanding commercial execution challenge for a ~300-person company. D/E of 599% ($195.2M debt on $32.6M equity) creates balance sheet vulnerability. The stock has declined 64% over 52 weeks, reflecting the market's skepticism about the transition. The pipeline beyond Vafseo and Auryxia is thin, leaving the company as a two-product franchise with one product in terminal decline.
