Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Akebia Therapeutics is a Cambridge, Massachusetts nephrology company whose stated purpose is 'to better the lives of people impacted by kidney disease.' It markets Vafseo (vadadustat), an oral HIF-PH inhibitor for anemia due to chronic kidney disease in adult dialysis patients, and Auryxia (ferric citrate), which has faced generic competition from Teva since March 2026. Q2 2026 revenue was $49.1M (vs. $62.5M a year earlier) as Vafseo grew 60% YoY to $21.3M with more than 10,500 patients on therapy while Auryxia fell to $25.5M; the 2,116-patient VOICE trial stopped early in June 2026 after showing a statistically significant reduction in mortality and hospitalization versus ESAs. Vafseo's TDAPA add-on payment period ends December 31, 2026, after which Akebia plans to price it within the ESA range. The pipeline includes praliciguat (Phase 2, FSGS) and ebribafusp (Phase 2 basket in IgAN, lupus nephritis and C3G, data in 2027). Shares trade at about $0.71.

Investment Thesis
Vafseo is working: Q2 2026 net revenue of $21.3M was up 60% YoY and 34% QoQ with more than 10,500 patients on therapy, and the VOICE trial's early stop for a statistically significant benefit on mortality and hospitalization versus ESAs gives Akebia a differentiated outcomes claim to take to dialysis providers [1]. At ~$0.71 the company's market value is about $196M against $155.5M of cash at June 30, 2026, and management says cash, product revenue and a planned term-loan refinancing fund at least two years of operations [1][4]. Consensus models a return to profitability by 2030 (EPS ~$0.06 on ~$257M revenue) as Vafseo volume grows and pipeline programs in FSGS, IgAN, lupus nephritis and C3G read out from 2027 [2].
The bear case is a revenue cliff. Auryxia revenue nearly halved to $25.5M in Q2 2026 after Teva's generic launch, total revenue fell 21% YoY, and the net loss was $8.9M [1]. When TDAPA ends on December 31, 2026, Akebia plans to price Vafseo within the ESA range — far below today's price — so management expects Vafseo revenue to decline from 2026 levels even as units grow [3]. Consensus shows losses of about $36M in 2026 and continuing losses through 2029 [2]. Debt-to-equity is 216% with a term loan needing refinancing, book value is only $0.09 per share, and a sub-$1 share price exposes Akebia to Nasdaq minimum-bid compliance risk and dilutive financing (it sold $50M of stock at a 28% drawdown in March 2025) [2][4].
