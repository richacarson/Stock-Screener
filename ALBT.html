Avalon GloboCare Corp. (rebranded to Change Agents Corp. / CHGA in July 2026) is a nano-cap company that has pivoted through cell therapy, real estate, consumer health devices, and AI software. Its products include KetoAir, an FDA-registered Class I handheld nanosensor breath-based metabolic monitor, and the AI Nutritionist software program. With only 2 employees, negative TTM revenue of -$350K, and a market cap of ~$3.4M, the company faces going-concern risk flagged by both management and auditors.
