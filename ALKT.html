Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application (Digital Banking) ·NASDAQ ·United States
Alkami Technology provides a cloud-based, multi-tenant digital banking platform that enables community banks, credit unions, and regional financial institutions to compete with large national banks on user onboarding, engagement, and revenue generation. Founded in 2009 and headquartered in Plano, Texas, Alkami reported Q2 2026 revenue of $129.8M (+15.9% YoY) with annual recurring revenue of $511.7M (+21% YoY) and 50 clients at or above $2M in ARR. Management raised full-year 2026 guidance to $528-531M revenue with $96-98M adjusted EBITDA.
1,225 Employees ·www.alkami.com

Investment Thesis
Alkami exited Q2 2026 with $511.7M ARR growing 21% YoY and 96% subscription revenue mix, demonstrating high-quality recurring revenue in a mission-critical vertical — community banks and credit unions cannot function without a digital banking platform [1]. The expanding penetration ($1.6M average ARR per client, 50 clients at $2M+ up from 18 in 2021) and strong new logo acquisition (37 in LTM, including 15 banks) validate both land-and-expand execution and greenfield opportunity across the ~5,000 US community FIs still running legacy platforms. Adjusted EBITDA margins expanded to 14.9% in Q2, and management's raised guidance of $96-98M adjusted EBITDA for FY2026 shows a clear path to sustained profitability. At ~$2.2B market cap (~4.2x FY2026 revenue), valuation is reasonable for a 19% revenue grower approaching profitability.
The bear case centers on persistent GAAP unprofitability, revenue deceleration, and cultural instability. ROE of -12.74% and profit margin of -10.63% mean the company is still burning cash on a GAAP basis despite adjusted EBITDA improvement, with elevated stock-based compensation inflating the gap between GAAP and adjusted results [1]. Revenue growth decelerated sharply from 29% in Q1 to 15.9% in Q2 2026, raising questions about whether the fastest phase of digital banking migration is maturing and whether the raised FY2026 guidance ($528-531M) embeds sufficient conservatism [1]. Cultural concerns — significant C-suite turnover, HR leadership departure, and mixed Glassdoor sentiment including micromanagement and morale complaints — suggest organizational growing pains that could impair execution during a critical scaling phase [2]. AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
7
AI Risk Protection (6/9): Alkami's digital banking platform is deeply embedded in client financial institutions' core banking infrastructure, creating high switching costs buttressed by heavy banking regulation (KYC, AML, data privacy) that AI cannot circumvent [1]. Analysts at Seeking Alpha have specifically described Alkami's per-user SaaS model as 'insulated from AI disruption' given the deep integration required with core banking systems [2]. However, AI-native fintech challengers could potentially build competitive digital banking front-ends at lower cost over time, and generative AI tools are lowering the cost of building banking interfaces generally, creating long-term competitive pressure. AI Opportunity (1/1): Alkami's Engagement AI predictive model is shipping to financial institution clients with measurable value — enabling identification of at-risk account holders and optimization of retention spending — and fraud prevention AI is integrated into the platform pricing model, meeting the threshold of early-stage AI products generating value [2].
