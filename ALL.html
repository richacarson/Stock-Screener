Company Profile
Financial Services ·Insurance - Property & Casualty ·NYSE ·United States
Allstate is the third-largest personal lines property and casualty insurer in the United States, providing auto, homeowners, and other personal lines insurance through exclusive agents, independent agents, contact centers, and digital channels under the Allstate, National General, Direct Auto, and Answer Financial brands. Protection Services provides consumer product protection plans, identity protection, and Arity telematics analytics. Founded in 1931 and headquartered in Northbrook, Illinois, Allstate reported Q2 2026 revenue of $18.6B with 215.9 million policies in force across all segments.

Investment Thesis
Allstate at 9.36x forward earnings offers compelling value for a P&C franchise generating 46% ROE and delivering record profitability. Q2 2026 revenue of $18.6B (+11.8% YoY) and adjusted EPS of $8.99 — beating consensus by 70.6% — demonstrate the payoff of Tom Wilson's Transformative Growth initiative as underwriting discipline, rate adequacy, and investment income converge [1][2]. The 86.6% combined ratio reflects structural improvement from multi-year pricing actions, and net investment income surged 33.8% to $1B from a larger, longer-duration portfolio [1]. Policies in force grew 3.8% to 215.9M, showing Allstate can grow volume while maintaining pricing discipline. The multi-brand distribution model (Allstate, National General, Direct Auto) provides unique market coverage across customer segments, and the Arity telematics platform creates an emerging data monetization opportunity.
The bear case centers on catastrophe volatility, cyclical earnings normalization, and modest growth potential. P&C insurance earnings are inherently volatile — Allstate's 2022-2023 underwriting losses remind that a single severe hurricane season or wildfire event can erase a year of profitability, and climate change is increasing the frequency and severity of natural catastrophes [4]. The current combined ratio of 86.6% is historically excellent and likely unsustainable as competitive pressure on rates intensifies and loss trends normalize. EPS growth of 61.2% YoY reflects recovery from depressed prior-year earnings rather than a sustainable growth trajectory, and the PEG of 1.9 suggests the market expects mid-single-digit forward growth. The 1.67% dividend yield and single-digit forward P/E signal the market treats Allstate as a cyclical value stock, not a compounder. AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience MODERATE
7
AI Risk Protection (7/9): Insurance is structurally protected from AI disruption. Allstate's core business — underwriting and bearing physical-world risk for auto, property, and casualty losses — requires state-by-state regulatory compliance, billions in statutory capital reserves, established actuarial data spanning decades, and trusted brand relationships that AI tools cannot replicate or displace [3]. Switching costs for bundled policyholders are high, and new entrants face 3-5 year loss ratio seasoning before achieving profitability. InsurTech challengers like Lemonade and Root have failed to demonstrate sustainable profitability despite AI-first approaches, validating the incumbents' structural advantages. However, AI does enable more precise pricing by competitors (Progressive's Snapshot, GEICO's telematics), creating competitive pressure on pricing accuracy, and AI-automated claims could compress service differentiation over time. AI Opportunity (0/1): While Allstate uses AI internally for claims processing and Arity generates telematics-based analytics revenue, the company does not report specific AI-derived revenue figures. AI is an operational efficiency tool rather than a distinct revenue stream.
