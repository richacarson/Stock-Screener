Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Other Industrial Metals & Mining ·NASDAQ ·United States
Almonty Industries is the leading Western-world producer of tungsten concentrate, operating the Panasqueira mine in Portugal (136 years of production history) and the newly commissioned Sangdong mine in South Korea — one of the largest and highest-grade tungsten deposits globally. The company listed on NASDAQ in July 2025 and has experienced explosive revenue growth (+498% YoY in Q2 2026) driven by Sangdong commissioning and record tungsten prices ($3,075/MTU vs. $453/MTU prior year). Q2 2026 net income of C$181.8M included significant non-cash fair value adjustments. Market cap is approximately $2.8B.

Investment Thesis
Almonty is the Western world's largest tungsten producer at an inflection point — Sangdong mine commissioning (March 2026), record tungsten prices ($3,075/MTU, up from $453/MTU a year earlier), and bipartisan geopolitical support for critical mineral independence from China. Q2 2026 revenue of C$43.0M (+498% YoY) with mining income of C$26.1M and EBITDA of C$17.6M demonstrates the operational leverage as Sangdong ramps to full capacity [1]. Q1 2026 revenue of C$25.4M (+221% YoY) with positive operating cash flow of $9.7M confirms the trajectory [2]. The company holds C$1.2B in cash following the oversubscribed $800M convertible notes offering, providing a war chest for vertical integration. Forward P/E of 8.26 is deep value for a company positioned at the intersection of critical minerals, national security, and semiconductor supply chains [3].
The bear case centers on commodity cyclicality, governance concerns, and valuation distortions. Tungsten prices are at record highs driven partly by tariffs and geopolitical tensions — any normalization in China trade relations or demand softening would compress margins toward breakeven [1]. The 46% stock decline from $18.28 to $9.83 since August 2026 already reflects some market skepticism. The profit margin of 125.2% is severely distorted by non-cash fair value adjustments — sustainable mining margins are 40-60%, and the GAAP net income of C$181.8M on C$43M revenue cannot recur [1]. D/E of 147.4% reflects the $800M convertible notes offering that represents significant dilution risk if converted. Massive insider selling ($227.6M net over 90 days by CEO Black and co-founder D'Amato) directly contradicts the long-term commitment narrative. The sudden CFO departure and lawsuit against competitor Pure Tungsten raise governance red flags [4]. The company has operated primarily during a commodity supercycle and has not been tested by a tungsten price collapse.
Key Catalysts
  1. Sangdong mine ramp to full production capacity through H2 2026-2027, with potential to supply up to 40% of non-China global tungsten and drive revenue toward C$200M+ annualized [1][2]
  2. Sustained tungsten price strength driven by Western reshoring policies, defense spending increases, and semiconductor manufacturing expansion requiring tungsten tooling and components [3]
  3. Deep value re-rating at 8.26x forward earnings — if Sangdong execution continues and tungsten prices hold, the current valuation implies the market prices in significant operational or commodity risk that may not materialize
Key Risks
  1. Tungsten price cyclicality — current record prices of $3,075/MTU are partially driven by tariffs and geopolitical tensions; normalization of China trade relations could collapse margins from 60%+ toward breakeven, as the stock's 46% decline since August already signals [1]
  2. Governance concerns — massive insider selling of $227.6M net over 90 days, sudden CFO departure (effective immediately), and lawsuit against competitor Pure Tungsten create a pattern inconsistent with long-term stewardship [4]
  3. GAAP financial distortions — 125.2% profit margin and C$181.8M net income on C$43M Q2 revenue are inflated by non-cash fair value adjustments on convertible notes; sustainable mining margins are 40-60%, making current multiples potentially misleading [1]

AI Resilience LOW RISK

AI Risk Protection (8/9): Tungsten mining is a physical-world operation that AI structurally cannot displace. Extracting, processing, and shipping tungsten concentrate requires mines, processing plants, and logistics infrastructure that no software can substitute [1][3]. AI actually increases demand for tungsten, which is a critical material in semiconductor manufacturing equipment, electrical contacts, and hardened tooling used in chip fabrication. CEO Lewis Black has specifically noted tungsten's 'pivotal role in the AI era' given its use in chip production hardware. The geopolitical dimension — Western nations seeking supply chain independence from China — creates an additional structural moat impervious to AI disruption. The only downgrade from IMMUNE (9) is that AI could optimize competitors' mining operations, though this competitive effect is marginal. AI Opportunity (0/1): Almonty does not generate AI-related revenue. The company benefits indirectly from AI-driven tungsten demand in semiconductor manufacturing, but this is not AI revenue itself.
