Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Other Industrial Metals & Mining ·NASDAQ ·Canada
Almonty Industries is the leading Western-world producer of tungsten concentrate, operating the Panasqueira mine in Portugal (136 years of production history) and the newly commissioned Sangdong mine in South Korea, one of the largest and highest-grade tungsten deposits globally. The company listed on NASDAQ in July 2025 and has experienced explosive revenue growth (+498% YoY in Q2 2026) as Sangdong reaches commercial production alongside record tungsten prices. Almonty positions itself as the anchor of a secure, Western-aligned tungsten supply chain for defense, semiconductor, and industrial applications, targeting 40% of non-China global tungsten supply.
341 Employees ·www.almonty.com

Investment Thesis
Almonty is the Western world's largest tungsten producer at the perfect inflection point — Sangdong mine commissioning (March 2026), record tungsten prices, and bipartisan geopolitical support for critical mineral supply chain independence from China. Q2 2026 revenue of $43M (+498% YoY) with mining income of $26.1M and EBITDA of $17.6M demonstrates the operational leverage as Sangdong ramps to full capacity [1]. The company ended Q2 with $1.23B in cash (including $800M convertible notes proceeds), providing a war chest for vertical integration and potential acquisitions. With a Forward P/E of 23x and the only NASDAQ-listed pure-play Western tungsten producer, Almonty offers unique exposure to a structurally supply-constrained critical mineral essential for semiconductors, defense, and AI hardware [6].
The bear case centers on commodity cyclicality, valuation sustainability, and governance concerns. Tungsten prices are at record highs driven partly by tariffs and geopolitical tensions — any normalization in China trade relations or demand softening would compress margins significantly [1]. The $5.27B market cap on $60M TTM revenue (87x P/S) prices in flawless execution of Sangdong ramp and sustained record tungsten pricing. The $800M convertible notes at 2.25% represent significant dilution risk if converted, and share count has already expanded 44% YoY [5]. Massive insider selling ($227.6M net in 90 days by CEO Black and Daniel D'Amato) undermines the confidence narrative — insiders are monetizing aggressively at peak valuation. The profit margin of 125% is distorted by one-time fair value adjustments and is not sustainable; normalized mining margins are 40-60% [1]. Operating in South Korea and Portugal introduces geopolitical and regulatory risks across multiple jurisdictions.
Key Catalysts
  1. Sangdong mine ramp to full production capacity through H2 2026, with potential to become one of the world's largest tungsten operations and drive revenue toward $200M+ annualized [1]
  2. Sustained tungsten price strength driven by Western reshoring, defense spending increases, and semiconductor manufacturing expansion requiring tungsten tooling and components [6]
  3. Share buyback program launched August 2026 could provide price support and signal management confidence in valuation despite concurrent insider selling [4]
Key Risks
  1. Tungsten price cyclicality — current record prices are partially driven by tariffs and geopolitical tensions; normalization of China trade relations could collapse margins from 60%+ to breakeven [1]
  2. Extreme valuation at 87x TTM P/S and 64x trailing P/E requires flawless execution of Sangdong ramp and sustained commodity pricing; any disappointment triggers severe multiple compression [5]
  3. Massive insider selling of $227.6M net over 90 days by CEO Lewis Black and co-founder Daniel D'Amato raises governance concerns about whether leadership is building for the long term or monetizing a cyclical peak [4]

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
8
AI Risk Protection (8/9): Tungsten mining is a physical-world operation that AI structurally cannot displace. Extracting, processing, and shipping tungsten concentrate requires mines, processing plants, and logistics infrastructure — no software can substitute for the physical resource [2]. AI actually increases demand for tungsten, which is a critical material in semiconductor manufacturing equipment, electrical contacts, and hardened tooling used in chip fabrication. CEO Lewis Black has specifically noted tungsten's 'pivotal role in the AI era' given its use in chip production hardware [6]. The geopolitical dimension — Western nations seeking supply chain independence from China — creates an additional structural moat that AI cannot disrupt. AI Opportunity (0/1): Almonty does not generate AI-related revenue. The company benefits indirectly from AI demand driving tungsten consumption in semiconductor manufacturing, but this is not AI revenue in itself.
