Investment Thesis
AMC at $2.77 (market cap about $2.5B) is a high-beta turnaround recovering with the box office: Q2 2026 delivered record revenue of $1,596.7M (up 14.2%) and record Adjusted EBITDA of $321.4M (up 69.6%), with first-half Adjusted EBITDA of $359.7M versus $131.8M a year earlier. Domestic ticket revenue outgrew the industry (11.4% vs 10.7%) and food and beverage revenue rose 15.3%, showing strong operating leverage as attendance rises [1].
The bear case is balance-sheet driven. Trailing profit margin is -10.6%, equity is negative, forward P/E is negative, and the 5-year revenue CAGR is roughly -3%, so the company still has not demonstrated durable profitability. Earnings are hostage to the studio release slate and theatrical windows, and past financing relied on heavy share dilution. A weak slate or streaming-window compression would quickly erase the EBITDA recovery [1][3].
Key Catalysts
  1. Strong 2026-2027 studio slate sustaining attendance growth after 71M guests in Q2 2026 (up 13.5%) and Adjusted EBITDA up 69.6% [1]
  2. Premium-format and food-and-beverage mix (F&B and merchandise up 15.3%) lifting per-guest revenue and margins [1]
  3. Debt reduction or refinancing funded by record EBITDA, easing interest burden on a negative-equity balance sheet [1]
Key Risks
  1. Negative equity and a trailing -10.6% net margin leave little cushion; further dilution or refinancing stress would hurt equity holders [1]
  2. Dependence on studio release calendars and shrinking theatrical windows; a weak slate would reverse the 2026 EBITDA recovery [1]
  3. Structural competition from streaming and elevated ESG/regulatory disclosure requirements in the U.S. and Europe adding cost and compliance risk [3]
