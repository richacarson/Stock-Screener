Investment Thesis
AMC posted record Q2 2026 results: revenue of $1,596.7M (+14.2% YoY) and adjusted EBITDA of $321.4M (+69.6%), with U.S. attendance up 12% and European attendance up 18% [1]. Adjusted EPS of $0.14 crushed the $0.06 loss consensus. H1 2026 revenue reached $2,642.1M (+16.9%), and Adjusted EBITDA of $359.7M surged 172.9% — the highest quarterly revenue and EBITDA in AMC's 100+ year history [1]. The 2026 blockbuster slate (Avengers: Doomsday, Spider-Man: Brand New Day) is driving exceptional box office performance, and per-patron revenue continues setting records through premium format and food-and-beverage optimization. The recent $350M equity raises, while dilutive, extend debt maturities by redeeming 2027 notes, pushing the next major debt wall to 2029 [3]. Concert cinema (Taylor Swift, Beyonce) has opened a genuinely new revenue category beyond traditional movies.
The bear case is severe and structural. AMC carries $7.34B in long-term debt against negative equity of -$1.93B, with free cash flow of approximately -$175M in the latest quarter [3][4]. Despite record revenue, GAAP net income remains negative (-$11.4M Q2 2026), and profit margin is -10.6% — the company cannot generate enough operating profit to service its debt organically [4]. The ~200M shares issued in June-July 2026 diluted existing shareholders by roughly 25%, and the authorized share count was doubled to 1.1B, signaling more dilution ahead. At $2.66/share, forward P/E of 122x prices in profitability that has not yet materialized on a GAAP basis. AMC's fortunes are entirely dependent on Hollywood content cycles it cannot control — a weak movie slate in any quarter could reverse the attendance momentum. Streaming competition from Disney+, Netflix, and Amazon continues to compress theatrical windows and audience demand for all but tentpole releases [2]. The meme-stock premium has largely evaporated, and the stock trades 35% below its 2026 52-week high.
Key Catalysts
  1. Blockbuster 2026 movie slate including Avengers: Doomsday and Spider-Man: Brand New Day could sustain record attendance through H2 2026, building on the Q2 12-18% attendance surge [1]
  2. $350M equity raises successfully redeemed 2027 notes, pushing the next major debt maturity to 2029 and reducing near-term refinancing risk [3]
  3. Concert/event cinema category (Taylor Swift, Beyonce model) represents a genuinely new content type that diversifies AMC beyond Hollywood dependence and generates premium per-patron revenue [1]
Key Risks
  1. $7.34B debt load against -$1.93B negative equity creates persistent bankruptcy risk — any sustained box office downturn could trigger covenant violations or restructuring [3][4]
  2. Continued equity dilution (~200M new shares in June-July 2026 alone, authorized shares doubled to 1.1B) destroys per-share value for existing shareholders and signals more dilution ahead [3]
  3. Complete dependence on Hollywood content cycles AMC cannot control — a weak movie slate in 2027 could reverse the attendance recovery and re-expose the structurally unprofitable cost base [1]
