AMC Robotics Corporation manufactures and sells autonomous robots and AI-driven security and safety technology solutions. Products include YI smart cameras, autonomous inspection robots, Kyro quadruped security patrol robotics, and logistics automation systems. The company went public via SPAC merger with AlphaVest Acquisition Corp in December 2025, trading on Nasdaq under AMCI. Revenue of $4.9M TTM is declining 33% YoY as the company deliberately shifts from lower-margin product sales to recurring AI and cloud revenue-sharing arrangements with Kami Vision. Market cap ~$78M.

Investment Thesis
AMC Robotics offers exposure to the AI robotics megatrend through autonomous patrol robots, smart cameras, and AI-powered logistics automation at a micro-cap entry point (~$78M market cap). The strategic shift to recurring AI revenue-sharing arrangements is yielding improved unit economics — gross margins expanded from 49% to 80% and operating losses narrowed dramatically from $735K to $157K in Q2 2026 [1]. The $50M standby equity purchase agreement provides capital to build a dedicated robotics facility [3]. If the company successfully scales recurring AI revenue while maintaining 80%+ gross margins, the current valuation reflects maximum pessimism with the stock down 80% from its 52-week high.
The bear case is severe. AMCI is a pre-scale micro-cap with $4.9M TTM revenue declining 33% YoY, no profitability, no analyst coverage, and stock down from $15.97 to $3.26 — an 80% decline from its 52-week high [4]. The liquidity floor is breached with only ~$580K daily dollar volume, making position entry and exit extremely difficult for any institutional portfolio. The company went public via SPAC — a structure with a poor historical track record for long-term value creation. The competitive landscape is brutal: Amazon Robotics, Boston Dynamics, NVIDIA Isaac, and well-funded competitors operate at vastly greater scale with 100x+ R&D budgets. The board has 0.1 years average tenure and CEO was appointed only December 2025, offering zero governance track record [2]. The $50M equity facility would dilute existing shareholders by potentially 2-3x at current prices. Revenue has declined 67% cumulatively from FY2022 to FY2025 as legacy camera business collapses faster than robotics revenue materializes.
