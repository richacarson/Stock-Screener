Company Profile
Healthcare ·Medical Devices ·NASDAQ ·United States
Autonomix Medical, Inc. is a development-stage medical device company focused on advancing technologies for sensing and treating disorders of the peripheral nervous system. Its technology platform features a catheter-based microchip-enabled sensing array to detect and differentiate peripheral neural signals, combined with RF ablation capability for a 'sense, treat, verify' approach. The company is pursuing clinical trials for pancreatic cancer pain and broader visceral cancer applications. Incorporated in 2014, headquartered in The Woodlands, Texas.

Investment Thesis
Autonomix Medical's catheter-based neural sensing and ablation platform addresses a genuine unmet medical need in pancreatic cancer pain management, a condition with limited effective treatments. The 'sense, treat, verify' approach using microchip-enabled sensing arrays is differentiated technology that was recognized at CRT 2026's Best Innovation Competition [2]. If the U.S. pivotal trial succeeds and FDA approval is obtained, the addressable market could be significant given the expansion into broader visceral cancers beyond pancreatic pain [2].
However, the investment case is extremely speculative. The company has generated zero revenue in its 12-year history, with net losses widening from $11.4M to $16.7M in FY2026 and an accumulated deficit of $67.1M [1]. The 1-for-21 reverse stock split signals NASDAQ compliance risk, and the 10-K notes going concern doubts. With a $3.5M market cap, 18,000 shares average daily volume, and continued cash burn of $12.3M annually against $7M in cash, the company faces existential funding risk before reaching any commercial milestone. The massive 308% share dilution in FY2026 demonstrates the punishing cost of equity capital for pre-revenue device companies [1][4].
Key Catalysts
  1. Successful IDE submission and FDA approval to initiate U.S. pivotal trial for pancreatic cancer pain, which would de-risk the regulatory pathway and attract institutional interest
  2. Positive topline data from PoC 2 clinical trial in Uzbekistan covering broader visceral cancers, potentially expanding the addressable market beyond pancreatic pain
  3. Strategic partnership or licensing deal with a larger medical device company that would provide capital, commercial infrastructure, and validation of the technology platform
Key Risks
  1. Going concern risk with only $7M cash against $12.3M annual burn rate — the company will need additional dilutive equity financing within 6-12 months to continue operations
  2. Clinical trial failure or FDA rejection of the IDE application would effectively eliminate the company's only viable product pathway and likely trigger delisting
  3. Daily dollar volume of $116K makes the stock essentially untradeable for institutional investors, creating severe liquidity risk and no path to index inclusion
