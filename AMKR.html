Company Profile
Technology ·Semiconductor Equipment & Materials ·NASDAQ ·United States
Amkor Technology is one of the world's largest outsourced semiconductor assembly and test (OSAT) providers, operating advanced packaging and test facilities across South Korea, Vietnam, Japan, the Philippines, Portugal, and China. The company specializes in advanced packaging technologies including high-density fan-out (HDFO), 2.5D, system-in-package (SiP), and flip-chip, serving semiconductor companies across computing, communications, automotive, and industrial end markets. Strategic partnerships with TSMC (10-year advanced packaging agreement) and NVIDIA (multi-year AI infrastructure collaboration) position Amkor at the center of AI chip packaging. Q2 2026 revenue reached a record $1.9B (+26% YoY) with 30,800 employees.

The bear case centers on margin structure, customer concentration, and execution risk. Profit margin of 7.45% is thin for a company with 30,800 employees and massive capital requirements — OSAT companies structurally operate at low margins due to customer bargaining power (Apple 29.8% of revenue, Qualcomm 11.1%) and capital intensity [1][4]. Free cash flow is negative $172M despite record revenue, as $2.5-$3.0B capex absorbs cash flow. Customer concentration risk is significant: top 10 customers represent 72% of sales, making Amkor vulnerable to demand shifts from any single large customer. The planned migration of SiP production from South Korea to Vietnam is expected to disrupt results through Q4 2026 and into H1 2027, creating near-term earnings risk [1]. The 5-year revenue CAGR of only 2.3% reflects the semiconductor cyclicality that makes Amkor's revenue stream inherently volatile — the current AI-driven boom could moderate, as prior cycles (2022-2024 downturn) demonstrated. Beta of 2.25 means the stock amplifies market moves in both directions.
