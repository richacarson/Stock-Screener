Company Profile
Healthcare ·Drug Manufacturers - Specialty & Generic ·NASDAQ ·United States
Amylyx Pharmaceuticals is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on neurodegenerative diseases and endocrine conditions. Co-founded in 2013 by Joshua Cohen and Justin Klee at Brown University, the company previously marketed RELYVRIO for ALS but voluntarily withdrew it in April 2024 after a confirmatory trial failed. The lead candidate avexitide is a first-in-class GLP-1 receptor antagonist in Phase 3 (LUCIDITY trial) for post-bariatric hypoglycemia, with topline data expected Q3 2026 and a potential 2027 NDA submission. Pipeline includes AMX0035 (Phase 2, wolfram syndrome/PSP), AMX0114 (Phase 1, ALS), and a Gubra collaboration for a long-acting GLP-1RA. Cash of $250.8M at June 30, 2026 funds operations into 2028. Market cap ~$4.1B.
136 Employees ·amylyx.com

Investment Thesis
Amylyx offers a binary clinical catalyst with avexitide Phase 3 LUCIDITY topline data imminent in Q3 2026 for post-bariatric hypoglycemia — a condition affecting 200,000+ U.S. patients with no FDA-approved treatment [1][2]. If positive, a 2027 NDA submission positions avexitide as first-in-class with minimal competition and potential premium pricing. The stock has rallied from a $10.83 low to $32.39 on pipeline momentum, and $250.8M cash (runway into 2028) removes near-term dilution risk. The co-founders' voluntary RELYVRIO withdrawal demonstrates rare integrity that builds institutional credibility with regulators and the medical community. Platform optionality exists through AMX0318 (next-gen GLP-1 antagonist via Gubra), AMX0035 (PSP/wolfram syndrome), and AMX0114 (ALS) [3].
The bear case is dominated by binary Phase 3 risk: LUCIDITY failure would devastate the stock given zero revenue and the precedent of the RELYVRIO confirmatory trial failure that crashed shares ~85% [1]. At $4.1B market cap for a pre-revenue company, the stock prices in significant avexitide success probability — merely adequate results could disappoint. The company burns ~$46M per quarter with negative ROE of -74.7%, and the $250.8M cash runway extends only into 2028, requiring capital raises if approval is delayed [1][5]. The RELYVRIO failure damaged credibility with some investors, and a class action lawsuit over RELYVRIO disclosures adds governance overhang. The PBH addressable market, while unmet, is relatively niche ($500M-$1B estimated), limiting peak revenue even in the bull case. With only 136 employees, Amylyx lacks commercial infrastructure and would need to build or partner for sales and distribution ahead of any launch [4].
Key Catalysts
  1. Avexitide Phase 3 LUCIDITY topline data expected Q3 2026 — positive efficacy results would de-risk the pipeline, validate the GLP-1 receptor antagonist mechanism, and likely trigger significant stock appreciation given first-in-class status for PBH [1]
  2. Potential NDA submission and 2027 FDA decision for avexitide, targeting commercial launch into a 200,000+ patient underserved market with no approved competitors [2]
  3. Pipeline expansion through AMX0318 (next-gen long-acting GLP-1RA via Gubra collaboration) and AMX0035 (Phase 2b/3 for PSP) broadens therapeutic optionality beyond the binary avexitide catalyst [3]
Key Risks
  1. Binary clinical trial risk — avexitide Phase 3 LUCIDITY failure would eliminate the near-term revenue path and likely result in catastrophic stock decline, repeating the RELYVRIO PHOENIX failure scenario that crashed shares ~85% [1]
  2. Cash runway of $250.8M at June 30, 2026 provides approximately 5 quarters of operations at current $46M/quarter burn rate — any delay in avexitide approval or need for additional trials would require dilutive equity financing [1][5]
  3. Limited commercial infrastructure with 136 employees means Amylyx must build or partner for sales, marketing, and distribution capabilities it does not currently possess, adding execution risk and cost to any post-approval launch timeline [4]
