Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NASDAQ ·United States
Alpha Modus Holdings, Inc. is a micro-cap company focused on creating, developing, and licensing data-driven technologies to enhance consumers' in-store digital experience at the point of decision. The company describes itself as a vertical AI company with a patented closed-loop retail AI framework (Sense, Decide, Deliver, Attribute). Subsidiaries include Alpha Modus Financial Services, which is developing AlphaCash fintech kiosks. Founded in 2014, headquartered in Cornelius, North Carolina. The company has 9 employees and essentially zero revenue.

The 96% decline from the 52-week high reflects the market's assessment of execution probability.
