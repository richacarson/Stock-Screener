Company Profile
Industrials ·Electrical Equipment & Parts ·NYQ ·United States
Amprius Technologies develops, manufactures, and markets high-energy-density silicon anode lithium-ion batteries under the SiCore and SiMaxx product platforms for aviation, defense, drones, EVs, and AI robotics applications. Founded in 2008 from Stanford University research and headquartered in Fremont, California, Amprius reported Q2 2026 revenue of $34.0M (up 126% YoY) and raised its FY2026 revenue guidance to at least $140M, with a target of achieving positive adjusted EBITDA.

Investment Thesis
Amprius at $9.67 with a $1.4B market cap is a high-growth, pre-profitability silicon anode battery company with accelerating commercial traction — Q2 2026 revenue of $34.0M grew 126% YoY, FY2026 guidance was raised twice to at least $140M (nearly doubling 2025's $72M), and the company targets positive adjusted EBITDA of at least $4M and net loss below $10M [1]. Silicon anode technology delivering up to 500 Wh/kg energy density (2x conventional lithium-ion) addresses the critical bottleneck for drones, defense systems, EVs, and AI robotics, with a $75M defense grant validating the technology's strategic importance [3]. The balance sheet is clean with $74.5M cash against only $6.4M debt, and the 9 analyst consensus target of $23.00 implies 138% upside from current levels [4].
The bear case centers on credibility risk, competitive vulnerability, and path to profitability. A May 2026 Manatee Research short seller report alleged that Amprius exaggerated customer orders (claiming at least $29M in announced LEV orders appear 'illusory'), overstated manufacturing quality through a contract manufacturing network including a Chinese entity (Berzelius) connected to former CEO/current Director Dr. Kang Sun, and used aggressive bill-and-hold revenue recognition [3]. A securities fraud investigation was subsequently announced. Even if allegations are unfounded, the reputational damage introduces investor uncertainty. Insiders are selling aggressively — CEO Stepien ($324K), Director Sun ($642K), CTO Stefan ($307K), and Director Dixon ($1.46M) all sold shares in 2026 [4]. At 97 employees and $72M annual revenue, Amprius is dwarfed by CATL, LG Energy Solution, and Samsung SDI, who could develop competing silicon anode technology with vastly greater resources. The stock is down 60% from its $24.23 52-week high, and Glassdoor reviews citing toxic culture and favoritism raise organizational concerns for a company at a critical scaling inflection [2][4].
