Company Profile
Materials ·Building Materials ·NYSE ·Switzerland
Amrize AG is a North American building solutions company spun off from global leader Holcim on June 23, 2025. The company delivers advanced branded solutions from foundation to rooftop — cement, aggregates, ready-mix concrete, asphalt, and building envelope products (roofing, insulation, waterproofing, coatings, adhesives) — serving infrastructure, commercial, and residential construction markets. Amrize operates over 1,000 sites across the U.S. and Canada. Q2 2026 revenue grew 8.6% YoY to $3,494M driven by strong mega-project demand from data centers, energy, advanced manufacturing, and infrastructure modernization, with net income up 14.4% to $476M [1]. Registered in Zug, Switzerland with operational headquarters in Chicago.

Investment Thesis
Amrize is a well-capitalized North American building materials leader with strong Q2 2026 results: revenue of $3,494M (+8.6% YoY, +6.7% organic), net income of $476M (+14.4%), and adjusted EBITDA of $986M (+5.8%) [1]. The company benefits from structural tailwinds in mega-project demand — data centers, energy infrastructure, advanced manufacturing, and infrastructure modernization are driving volume growth that management raised FY2026 revenue guidance to reflect [1]. CEO Jenisch's $57M+ in personal insider buying and 23 executive purchases with zero sales signal extraordinary conviction [5]. D/E of 54% and Forward P/E of approximately 15x at the current $44.29 stock price provide attractive valuation entry [6]. The $502M returned to shareholders in Q2 alone ($1.0B buyback program plus dividends) demonstrates capital return commitment. Free cash flow of $1.42B (12% FCF margin) supports ongoing shareholder returns and growth investment.
The primary concern is execution risk under a leadership culture that Glassdoor reviewers describe as problematic, with high turnover and top-down decision-making [4]. ROE of 10.08% and profit margin of 10.14% are below peers (CRH, Vulcan Materials, Martin Marietta), suggesting operational efficiency improvements are needed — though Q2 net income growing 14.4% on 8.6% revenue growth shows margin leverage is emerging [1][6]. EBITDA guidance was revised downward despite raised revenue guidance due to oil price-driven cost inflation in freight, diesel, and materials — exposing the company to commodity input cost volatility that management cannot fully control [1]. The stock has declined from $53.64 to $44.29 (-17%) since the July screen, and the Altman Z-Score of 2.42 flags elevated financial risk for a company carrying $7.1B in debt [6]. As a newly independent company with only 14 months of standalone history, Amrize lacks the track record to justify a premium valuation. The Inspire Impact Score of 0 (MIXED) provides no differentiation for faith-based investors.
