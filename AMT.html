Company Profile
Real Estate ·REIT - Specialty ·NYSE ·United States
American Tower Corporation is a global real estate investment trust and owner/operator of approximately 224,000 wireless and broadcast communications sites worldwide. The company also operates a growing data center business through its CoreSite subsidiary, which delivered record leasing activity in Q2 2026 with data center revenue growing 13.4% YoY to $297M. CEO Steven Vondran leads the company's positioning at the intersection of 5G densification and AI-driven compute demand. Q2 2026 total revenue reached $2.749B (+4.7% YoY), and the company raised full-year guidance for the second time, increasing data center growth expectations to approximately 15%.

Investment Thesis
American Tower is a high-quality digital infrastructure REIT positioned at the intersection of two secular growth trends: 5G network densification and AI-driven data center demand. Q2 2026 results validated the dual-engine thesis with total revenue of $2.749B (+4.7% YoY), data center revenue of $297M (+13.4%), and the company's second guidance raise of the year — property revenue midpoint up $110M, EBITDA up $45M, and data center growth raised to ~15% [1]. CoreSite achieved record leasing activity, adding more new business in Q2 than all of 2021, confirming the AI compute demand tailwind is accelerating. Adjusted EPS of $1.86 beat estimates, driving a 6% post-earnings rally to $176.73 [1]. The 4.05% dividend yield, 14-year growth streak, and ~8% 5yr dividend CAGR provide compelling income alongside the growth story. ROE of 29.95% and profit margin of 26.8% reflect improving capital efficiency [3].
The bear case centers on valuation, leverage, and structural tower headwinds. Forward P/E of 26.80x is a premium multiple for a REIT with only 4.7% top-line growth, relying on the data center narrative to justify the valuation [3]. D/E of 312% creates interest rate sensitivity — rising rates compress REIT valuations and increase refinancing costs on AMT's substantial debt stack. The Sprint/T-Mobile tower decommissioning cycle, while improving, continues to create a 200-300 bps drag on organic growth and isn't expected to fully resolve until mid-2027 [4]. The GAAP payout ratio of ~108% (on normalized earnings) exceeds 100%, leaving limited margin for dividend growth if AFFO disappoints. International operations in 25 countries expose revenue to FX headwinds (2.5pp drag in recent quarters). The Inspire Impact Score of -49 (MISALIGNED) limits the faith-based investor universe despite extensive positive ESG attributions. Over the medium term, LEO satellite constellations (Starlink, Kuiper) represent a speculative but real alternative connectivity risk to the tower model.
Key Catalysts
  1. CoreSite data center revenue accelerating beyond 15% FY2026 guidance as AI workload demand intensifies — Q2 2026 record leasing with more new business than all of 2021 suggests the growth rate could surprise to the upside [1]
  2. Sprint/T-Mobile churn cycle completion by mid-2027, removing a 200-300 bps organic growth drag and revealing underlying tower demand strength from 5G densification [4]
  3. Third guidance raise potential in H2 2026 — the company has already raised guidance twice in FY2026, and continued data center momentum plus stabilizing international operations could drive further upward revisions [1]
Key Risks
  1. Elevated leverage (D/E 312%) increases interest rate sensitivity — rising or sustained high rates compress REIT valuations and increase refinancing costs on AMT's substantial debt, limiting capital allocation flexibility [3]
  2. Forward P/E of 26.80x prices in sustained growth acceleration that depends on the data center narrative — any CoreSite leasing slowdown or data center oversupply concerns could trigger multiple compression [3]
  3. International FX headwinds (~2.5pp revenue drag) and carrier consolidation risk in emerging markets could offset domestic tower demand strength, while LEO satellite proliferation (Starlink, Kuiper) presents long-term alternative connectivity risk [4]
