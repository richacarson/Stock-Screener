Investment Thesis
Amentum is a newly formed $14B+ revenue government services platform trading at an exceptionally attractive 8.26x forward P/E and 0.56 PEG ratio, with a record $47.8B backlog providing multi-year revenue visibility unusual for a $5B market cap company [1][3]. Q2 FY2026 delivered $3.5B revenue with book-to-bill of 1.2x and $54M net income, demonstrating improving execution. FY2026 guidance of $13.95-14.30B revenue, $1.1-1.14B adjusted EBITDA (8% margin), and $525-575M free cash flow (>10% FCF yield on market cap) represents compelling value for a company with diversified government contract revenue across defense, nuclear, environmental, and digital solutions [1]. The nuclear energy opportunity ($1B+ in Q1 awards alone) provides secular growth tailwind from energy transition. Analyst consensus target of $33.73 implies 66% upside from $20.36.
The bear case focuses on merger integration risk and razor-thin GAAP profitability. Profit margin of 1.04% and ROE of 2.89% reflect a business that has not yet demonstrated profitability commensurate with its $14B revenue base — net income was just $148M TTM on that revenue [3]. Total debt of $3.93B against only $428M cash creates financial fragility if integration synergies fail to materialize or government spending is cut. Revenue declined 5.2% YoY due to contract transitions from consolidated to unconsolidated joint ventures, masking true organic growth of ~3% — modest for a company that must prove the merger's strategic rationale [1]. The government services sector faces secular headwinds from the current administration's DOGE cost-cutting initiatives and potential budget sequestration. Shares outstanding increased 46% YoY reflecting merger dilution, and no open-market insider buying suggests executives may be waiting for clearer integration progress. The 5.62% short interest reflects market skepticism about the near-term earnings trajectory [3].
