Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas Refining & Marketing ·NASDAQ ·United States
Aemetis, Inc. is a renewable natural gas and biofuels company focused on below-zero carbon intensity products. Operates through three segments: California Ethanol (60M gallon/year facility near Modesto), California Dairy Renewable Natural Gas (biogas collection from dairy farms), and India Biodiesel (50M gallon/year facility). Produces ethanol, renewable natural gas, biodiesel, and carbon credits under LCFS and RFS programs. Pursuing Carbon Zero production and Sustainable Aviation Fuel. Founded 2006, headquartered in Cupertino, California.

Investment Thesis
Aemetis grew Q1 2026 revenue 27% to $54.6M across all three operating segments, with dairy RNG volumes reaching 110,000 MMBtu and India biodiesel generating $10.5M [1]. Operating loss improved 60% to $6.3M, and 7 new LCFS pathway approvals averaging -380 carbon intensity dramatically increase credit revenue per MMBtu. Section 45Z Production Tax Credits contributed $4.0M in Q1, creating a new federal revenue stream. CEO McAfee's 20-year bioenergy track record and 2026 Holmberg Lifetime Achievement Award validate industry expertise, and the $144M market cap against $267M annual revenue prices the stock at just 0.54x sales [3][4].
The going concern warning is the dominant risk factor: management explicitly states substantial doubt about the company's ability to continue as a going concern [2]. Total debt of $404.7M includes $247.9M due on demand to Third Eye Capital, and the $5M cash position provides days, not months, of operating runway. The $639.9M accumulated deficit and -$263.9M negative equity represent two decades of losses that have destroyed all shareholder capital. Net loss of $77M in FY2025 (narrowing from $87.5M) still vastly exceeds operating cash generation. Any disruption to credit markets, LCFS pricing, or regulatory support (45Z tax credits are politically vulnerable) could trigger immediate liquidity failure. The stock trades at $1.60 — Nasdaq compliance is not immediately at risk, but the equity is effectively a call option on refinancing success.
Key Catalysts
  1. Seven new LCFS pathway approvals averaging -380 carbon intensity score could double credit revenue per MMBtu of RNG production, materially improving gross margins
  2. Section 45Z Production Tax Credits ($4M in Q1 2026) provide federal revenue support that reduces dependency on volatile LCFS credit pricing
  3. Successful debt refinancing with Third Eye Capital ($247.9M on-demand) could eliminate going concern warning and re-rate the equity from distressed to operational
Key Risks
  1. Going concern warning with $5M cash, $404.7M debt ($342.6M current), and $247.9M due on demand creates existential refinancing risk — any lender hesitation could force liquidation
  2. Accumulated deficit of $639.9M and negative equity of -$263.9M mean existing shareholders have been fully diluted and any recovery accrues first to creditors
  3. Political vulnerability of Section 45Z tax credits and LCFS program — regulatory rollback would eliminate the credit revenue that provides Aemetis's path to profitability
