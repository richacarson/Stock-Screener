Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NYSE American ·United States
Amaze Holdings is a creator-commerce software company that enables creators and brands to develop products, operate e-commerce storefronts, grow audiences, and access managed services across social and commerce channels. Formerly Fresh Vine Wine, the company pivoted to creator commerce in 2024 through its acquisition of Amaze Software. The platform includes Amaze Commerce and Moments AI, an AI-powered tool that converts creators' social content into sellable products. The company trades on NYSE American with a market cap of approximately $9.4M and reported Q2 2026 revenue of $619K with a net loss of $4.4M. CEO Aaron Day was removed in August 2026, with CFO Joel Krutz serving as interim CEO [1][3].

Investment Thesis
Amaze Holdings offers exposure to the rapidly growing creator economy through its commerce platform and Moments AI content-to-product tool, with a faith-aligned Inspire score of 33 (ALIGNED). The company showed sequential improvement in Q2 2026, narrowing net loss to $4.4M from $5.6M in Q1 while growing revenue 32% sequentially to $619K [1]. The 2024 pivot from wine to creator commerce was strategically sound given the $250B+ creator economy opportunity, and the Moments AI tool provides a genuine differentiator in converting social content into sellable products [4]. The new board additions, including TikTok Shop's former GM Sandie Hawkins, bring relevant e-commerce expertise [3].
However, the investment case is deeply challenged on nearly every fundamental dimension. The company trades at $0.20 with a $9.4M market cap and fails IOWN's liquidity floor — daily dollar volume of ~$70K makes position sizing impossible [5]. Revenue of $619K in Q2 2026 was down 28.8% YoY despite the acquisition-driven base, and the $4.4M quarterly net loss against $2.4M cash on hand creates immediate going-concern risk without additional financing [1][2]. The CEO removal in August 2026 introduces leadership vacuum risk during a critical period. The creator commerce space is dominated by Shopify ($100B+ market cap), TikTok Shop, and Amazon — Amaze has no scale, no data moat, and no switching costs to defend against these incumbents. Infrastructure scores 0 due to the liquidity floor, and every financial metric (ROE -220%, profit margin -710%) confirms the company is in survival mode, not a viable IOWN investment candidate.
