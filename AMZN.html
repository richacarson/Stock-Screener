Investment Thesis
Amazon at $251.52 (market cap about $2.7T) is executing a broad reacceleration: Q2 2026 net sales hit $200.6B (up 20%, $4B above consensus) and AWS growth accelerated for a fifth straight quarter to 37%, its fastest in 18 quarters, at a 39.4% operating margin ($16.6B). AWS AI and its custom chips each exceed $25B annualized revenue, and ROE of 30.6% with D/E of 45.6% shows capital-efficient scale [1].
The bear case is capital intensity and culture. Forward P/E of 24 is not demanding, but the AI capex cycle compresses free cash flow and any slowdown in AWS demand would hit the highest-margin segment. Corporate layoffs of about 14,000, poor morale, and AI talent leaving to rivals threaten execution, while ESG and labor scrutiny (warehouse injury rates nearly double the industry average in 2021) and antitrust attention add regulatory risk [2][3].
Key Catalysts
  1. AWS growth acceleration (17% to 37% over five quarters) and a $25B+ AI and chip run rate expanding as capacity comes online [1]
  2. Total revenue crossing $200B in a quarter with AWS operating income up from $10.2B to $16.6B, driving margin expansion [1]
  3. Cost discipline and a leaner management structure under Jassy lifting retail and advertising profitability [2]
Key Risks
  1. AI capex intensity and AWS reliance: AWS generates $16.6B of quarterly operating income, so any demand deceleration or share loss to Microsoft and Google hits earnings disproportionately [1]
  2. Culture and talent risk: roughly 14,000 corporate cuts, reported poor morale, and top AI talent leaving for rivals and startups [2]
  3. Labor, safety, and regulatory scrutiny, including warehouse injury rates and antitrust and ESG pressure that could raise costs [3]
