Company Profile
Technology ·Computer Networking ·NYSE ·United States
Arista Networks designs and sells cloud networking solutions including switches, routers, and software for data centers, campus, and AI/ML environments. The company is the dominant Ethernet networking supplier to hyperscale cloud titans including Microsoft, Meta, Google, and Oracle. Q2 2026 marked the first quarter surpassing $3B in revenue ($3.036B, +37.7% YoY), and management raised full-year guidance for the third time to $12.6B (40% growth). CEO Jayshree Ullal is in her 18th year leading the company. Founded in 2004, headquartered in Santa Clara, California.
5,115 Employees ·www.arista.com

Investment Thesis
Arista Networks delivered its first $3B quarter in Q2 2026 ($3.036B, +37.7% YoY) with non-GAAP operating margin of 49.9% and raised full-year guidance for the third time to $12.6B representing 40% growth [1]. The company is THE backbone of AI networking, with AI traffic exceeding 30% of revenue and growing as hyperscalers deploy increasingly larger GPU clusters requiring Arista's Ethernet fabric [3]. The fortress balance sheet ($12.35B cash, zero debt), dominant position with hyperscale titans (Microsoft and Meta each >10% of revenue), and expanding TAM into campus and AI networking create a compounding growth engine [2]. CEO Ullal's 18-year tenure has produced a 5-year revenue CAGR exceeding 30% — among the highest sustained growth rates in technology — with world-class 38.3% profit margin and 31.7% ROE. The Inspire Impact Score of 53 (ALIGNED) adds faith-based investor compatibility.
The primary risk is valuation. At 39.6x forward earnings and a PEG of 2.49, Arista is priced for sustained 30%+ growth, leaving limited margin of safety if AI capex spending decelerates or competitive dynamics shift [2]. Customer concentration remains a structural vulnerability — Microsoft and Meta together account for over 40% of revenue, exposing Arista to any single hyperscaler's procurement decisions. Broadcom's custom networking silicon and NVIDIA's Spectrum-X Ethernet platform could erode Arista's AI networking share if hyperscalers shift to vertically integrated solutions. While revenue growth reaccelerated to 40%, the stock's 52-week range of $114-$215 reflects momentum-driven trading that amplifies drawdown risk on any guidance miss. Insider selling of $1B+ in the last 90 days (under 10b5-1 plans) is notable, though typical for executives at a $234B company. CEO succession — while mitigated by COO Nightingale's appointment — remains a long-term consideration as Ullal enters her 19th year [5].
