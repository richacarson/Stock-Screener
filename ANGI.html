Company Profile
Communication Services ·Internet Content & Information ·NASDAQ ·United States
Angi Inc. operates a digital marketplace connecting homeowners with home service professionals for projects including plumbing, roofing, HVAC, and remodeling across the U.S. and internationally under brands including Angi, Angie's List, HomeAdvisor, and Handy. The company transitioned from a lead-generation network model to a homeowner-choice proprietary platform in January 2025 and is pivoting to an AI-native platform strategy under CEO Jeff Kip. Spun off from IAC in March 2025. Q2 2026 revenue of $248M declined 11% YoY, and the company recorded a $225-235M non-cash goodwill and intangible asset impairment. Adjusted EBITDA was $28.2M. Market cap ~$222M. Headquartered in Denver, Colorado.
2,300 Employees ·www.angi.com

Investment Thesis
Angi trades at a deeply discounted Forward P/E of 6.3x with $189M cash, reflecting extreme market skepticism about the AI-native platform pivot and turnaround prospects [4]. The company's 95.7% gross margin demonstrates inherent platform economics — the path to profitability requires operational leverage, not a business model fix. Adjusted EBITDA of $28.2M in Q2 2026 shows the underlying business is still generating cash despite revenue headwinds [1]. Revenue per lead increased 1% YoY from favorable Pro mix shift, and marketing ROI improved by reallocating $6M from inefficient TV spend to higher-ROI channels. The $138M in share buybacks (retiring 19.9% of shares) and $91.9M in discounted debt repurchases concentrate value on a smaller base if the turnaround materializes [2]. If the AI-native platform delivers and revenue stabilizes, the stock is priced for a meaningful re-rating.
The bear case is severe and compounding. Revenue declined 11% YoY to $248M in Q2 2026 following a 13% decline in FY2025, and the $225-235M goodwill impairment acknowledges massive value destruction [1]. The company's market cap of $222M is now below its $422M total debt, creating balance sheet risk as $500M in senior notes come due in 2028 [4]. Trailing ROE of -26.3% and profit margin of -22.3% (distorted by impairment but still reflecting losses) demonstrate fundamental deterioration. The AI-native pivot lacks tangible deliverables — no AI products shipping, no specific launch dates, and analyst downgrades specifically citing 'AI software transition is likely to flop' [3]. AI assistants from Google, Apple Intelligence, and ChatGPT pose existential threat to Angi's marketplace matching function by enabling direct homeowner-contractor discovery. With 2,300 employees in a toxic culture environment and no formal guidance reinstated, Angi risks becoming irrelevant before its AI pivot materializes [5].
