Company Profile
Communication Services ·Internet Content & Information ·NASDAQ ·United States
Angi Inc. operates a digital marketplace connecting homeowners with home service professionals for projects including plumbing, roofing, HVAC, and remodeling. The company transitioned from a lead-generation network model to a homeowner-choice proprietary platform in January 2025 and is pivoting to an AI-native platform strategy. Spun off from IAC in March 2025. Headquartered in Denver, Colorado.
2,500 Employees ·www.angi.com

Investment Thesis
Angi trades at an attractive 0.74x Forward PEG and 15x forward earnings with $244.6M cash, reflecting deep market skepticism about the AI-native platform pivot [4]. The company's 95.7% gross margin demonstrates inherent platform economics — the path to profitability requires operational leverage, not a business model fix. Proprietary Revenue grew 7% in Q1 2026 even as total revenue declined 3.2%, suggesting the new model is gaining traction [1]. If the AI-native platform delivers and Proprietary Revenue acceleration continues, Angi could return to overall revenue growth in H2 2026 as guided. The $138M in share buybacks and $91.9M in discounted debt repurchases signal management conviction at current valuations [2].
The bear case is severe. Revenue has declined for two consecutive years ($1.19B to $1.03B in FY2025, -13%) and Q1 2026 showed continued 3.2% decline to $238.2M [1]. Operating loss of $9.5M in Q1 2026 versus $20M operating income a year ago and net loss of $9.0M versus $15.1M net income demonstrate rapidly deteriorating fundamentals, exacerbated by a $14.9M restructuring charge. The $500M in senior notes due 2028 create refinancing risk if the turnaround stalls [1][2]. AI assistants from Google, Apple, and OpenAI pose an existential threat to Angi's core marketplace matching function — homeowners can increasingly ask an AI to find, vet, and schedule contractors directly. With a $229M market cap, -64.6% stock decline over 52 weeks, and toxic employee culture per Glassdoor reviews, Angi risks becoming irrelevant before its AI pivot materializes [4][5].

  3. $500M senior notes due August 2028 create refinancing risk; with $229M market cap below the debt load and near-breakeven profitability, credit market access could tighten if the turnaround does not accelerate materially

AI Resilience MODERATE

AI Risk Protection (5/9): Angi's core value proposition — matching homeowners with local service professionals for physical tasks like plumbing, roofing, and HVAC — has a physical-world moat that AI cannot substitute; someone still needs to fix the pipes [1]. However, Angi's actual business is the marketplace matching layer, not the physical services themselves, and that matching function is directly exposed to AI disruption. AI assistants from Google, Apple Intelligence, and ChatGPT could enable direct homeowner-contractor discovery without a platform intermediary, bypassing Angi entirely. The company's own pivot to an 'AI-native platform' implicitly acknowledges that its legacy matching model is vulnerable [3].
