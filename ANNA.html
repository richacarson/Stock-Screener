Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas Exploration & Production ·NASDAQ ·United States
AleAnna, Inc. is a natural gas company developing conventional natural gas and renewable natural gas (RNG) in Italy's Po Valley. It holds a 33.5% working interest in the Longanesi field and has two further discoveries (Gradizza and Trava), plus an RNG portfolio of more than 100 projects of which three are operational. Construction of Gradizza, its first wholly owned and operated production asset, began in Q2 2026. Q2 2026 revenue was $10.2M (vs. $4.0M) with net income of $3.8M. The stock trades near $2.44 (market cap about $100M), down from a 52-week high of $10.64.

Investment Thesis
AleAnna has become profitable quickly. H1 2026 revenue rose to $19.6M from $4.7M, net income reached $7.2M versus a $2.7M loss, Q2 adjusted EBITDA was $4.1M, and it was the fifth straight quarter of positive adjusted EBITDA [1]. The balance sheet holds $32.6M of cash and essentially no funded debt, TTM ROE is 21.4% and the net margin is 19.8% [1][2]. At about $100M market cap and a 12.8x trailing P/E, the stock prices in little value for Gradizza (first operated asset, under construction) or the 100+ project RNG funnel [3][5].
The bear case is concentration and tradability. Nearly all revenue ($9.5M of Q2's $10.2M) comes from a 33.5% non-operated stake in Longanesi, so any reservoir underperformance, operator delay or drop in Italian gas prices hits results directly [1]. The stock is down 77% from its $10.64 52-week high to $2.44, trades only about $485K a day (below IOWN's liquidity floor), and carries $27.4M of contingent consideration liabilities [1][3]. A 10% holder sold heavily in early 2026, which signals sponsor exits rather than insider conviction [6].
Key Catalysts
  1. Gradizza field reaching first gas: construction began in Q2 2026 and it would be AleAnna's first 100%-owned, operated production asset, diversifying away from the minority Longanesi stake [1]
  2. Conversion of the RNG funnel (8 projects in negotiation, 9 in due diligence) into operating plants that receive Italian biomethane incentives [5]
  3. Longanesi permanent production facility completion, which management says is 'on track to outperform our initial expectations' [1]
Key Risks
  1. Single-asset concentration: about 93% of Q2 revenue ($9.5M of $10.2M) came from AleAnna's 33.5% share of Longanesi, which is operated by a JV partner [1]
  2. Extreme illiquidity: roughly 198,600 shares per day (about $485K) makes institutional entry and exit impractical and fails the IOWN liquidity floor [3]
  3. Continued selling by 10% holder C. John Wilder (about 325,000 shares sold in Feb-Mar 2026) could pressure a stock already down 77% from its 52-week high [3][6]
