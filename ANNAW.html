Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas Exploration & Production ·NASDAQ ·United States
AleAnna, Inc. is a development-stage natural gas exploration and renewable natural gas (RNG) company focused on Italy, formed via a December 2024 SPAC business combination and listed on Nasdaq (common stock: ANNA) in January 2025. ANNAW represents publicly-traded warrants exercisable into AleAnna common stock at $11.50/share. The company holds a 33.5% stake in the Longanesi field — the most significant onshore Italian natural gas discovery in over two decades — and is developing 14 additional gas projects this decade, positioning itself as a contributor to European energy security amid the EU's Russian gas phase-out. Headquartered in Dallas, Texas, with regional operations in Rome and Milan, Italy.

Investment Thesis
ANNAW represents warrants on AleAnna, Inc., a development-stage Italian natural gas company that achieved its first material commercial revenue in 2025 ($22.4M gas plus $2.7M electricity) after a December 2024 SPAC combination and January 2025 Nasdaq listing [1]. The company holds a 33.5% stake in the Longanesi field — described as Italy's most significant onshore gas discovery in over two decades — and has received Italian regulatory approval for its first fully-owned gas field, targeting 2027 production, alongside a pipeline of 14 additional projects this decade [1]. AleAnna's mission of reducing European dependence on Russian gas gives it a genuine strategic rationale tied to EU energy-security policy, and the underlying business benefits from a physical, AI-immune resource-extraction model.
As a warrant instrument, ANNAW carries structurally different risk than the underlying ANNA common equity: at a $0.308 price against an $11.50 strike and an underlying stock trading around $8.16, the warrants are meaningfully out-of-the-money and derive value primarily from time and volatility rather than current intrinsic worth. Critically, average daily dollar volume of roughly $11,000 is more than 90x below the $1,000,000 IOWN liquidity floor, triggering an automatic Infrastructure score of 0 regardless of the underlying company's fundamentals — this security is effectively untradeable at any meaningful scale. Combined with a young, unproven public track record (18 months), no available culture or governance evidence, and thin overall data, ANNAW screens as a highly speculative, low-liquidity instrument appropriate only for a WATCH designation, if considered at all.

  1. Continued ramp of natural gas and electricity production from the Longanesi field toward its targeted 50+ MMcf/d rate, building on the 2025 revenue base

  3. As an early-stage E&P operator with limited public track record, AleAnna faces concentrated execution, regulatory, and geological risk across a small number of Italian assets
