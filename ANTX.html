Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
AN2 Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on discovering and developing novel small molecule therapeutics derived from its proprietary boron chemistry platform. Lead candidate epetraborole is a once-daily oral treatment in Phase 2/3 clinical trials for non-tuberculous mycobacterial (NTM) lung disease, and entering Phase 2 for polycythemia vera (PV). The pipeline also includes AN2-502998 (AN153) for Chagas disease. Founded by Eric Easom, former Anacor Pharmaceuticals scientist.
21 Employees ·www.an2therapeutics.com

Investment Thesis
AN2 Therapeutics offers speculative exposure to a differentiated boron chemistry platform targeting rare diseases with significant unmet need. Epetraborole is in Phase 2/3 for NTM lung disease — a condition with no FDA-approved therapy and a growing patient population — with Phase 2 PV expansion beginning dosing in August 2026 and initial data expected Q4 2026 [1][2]. CEO Easom's 29-year track record in infectious disease drug development, including the Anacor platform acquired by Pfizer for $5.2B, provides credibility for the boron chemistry approach [3]. The $40M March 2026 private placement extended cash runway to 2029, and the $85.3M cash position provides substantial runway to reach multiple clinical data readouts without near-term dilution [1].
The bear case centers on clinical and financial risk inherent to a 21-person pre-revenue biotech. Q1 2026 net loss was $10.0M ($0.29/share), missing analyst estimates, and consensus expects -$0.93 EPS for full-year 2026 with $0 revenue [1][4]. Phase 2/3 NTM data carries binary risk — failure would be devastating for a single-platform company. Competition from Insmed's established NTM franchise and potential AI-accelerated drug discovery entrants could limit commercial opportunity even with regulatory approval. The PV expansion, while scientifically interesting, represents a completely new therapeutic area requiring substantial additional investment and clinical validation. The company's focus on rare and neglected diseases, while socially admirable, limits total addressable market size and may constrain peak revenue potential.

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience MODERATE
7
AI Risk Protection (7/9): Small-molecule drug development via boron chemistry is a deeply scientific, physical, and regulatory process that AI cannot replicate. AN2's core value creation — designing novel boron-based compounds, conducting wet-lab synthesis, running clinical trials, and navigating FDA regulatory pathways — involves complex organic chemistry and biological testing requiring physical experimentation [2][5]. The FDA mandates physical clinical trials regardless of computational advances, and no amount of AI modeling can substitute for Phase 3 human safety and efficacy data. The boron chemistry platform represents proprietary chemical expertise that AI tools alone cannot replicate. However, AI-enabled drug discovery is accelerating competitor timelines for alternative NTM and PV treatments, potentially narrowing AN2's first-mover window. AI Opportunity (0/1): AN2 does not generate AI revenue or deploy AI-powered products. The company operates as a traditional drug development organization focused on wet-lab chemistry and clinical development [3].
