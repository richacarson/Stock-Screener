Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas E&P ·NMS ·United States
APA Corporation is an independent energy company that explores for, develops, and produces natural gas, crude oil, and natural gas liquids across the Permian Basin, Egypt, and the North Sea, with frontier exploration in Suriname (Block 58 with TotalEnergies). APA completed the $4.5B Callon Petroleum acquisition in April 2024. Q2 2026 delivered net income of $747M ($2.11/share diluted) on approximately $2.4B in revenue, and the company projects $2.3B of free cash flow for full-year 2026 at strip pricing. Incorporated in 1954, headquartered in Houston, Texas.
