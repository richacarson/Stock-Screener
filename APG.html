Company Profile
Industrials ·Specialty Business Services ·NYSE ·United States
APi Group Corporation is a global business services provider of safety, specialty, and industrial services, including fire protection, life safety, security, elevators, escalators, and HVAC-related work. The company operates through Safety Services and Specialty Services segments across more than 500 locations worldwide. The 2019 acquisition of Chubb Fire & Security transformed APi from a U.S.-centric contractor into a global safety services leader. Founded in 1926, headquartered in New Brighton, Minnesota.

Investment Thesis
APi Group delivered record financial results with full year 2025 revenue of $7.91B (+12.72%), adjusted EBITDA margin of 13.2% (above the 13% target), and free cash flow conversion of 80%, followed by record Q1 2026 revenue of $2.0B (+15.3% YoY, 10.4% organic) [1][2]. The company's safety services business provides essential, non-discretionary, and regulatory-mandated services that generate highly recurring revenue. The growing data center vertical (10-11% of revenue) positions APi as a direct beneficiary of AI infrastructure spending. Raised 2026 guidance to $8.475-8.675B revenue and $1.15-1.21B adjusted EBITDA demonstrates management confidence in continued momentum [2]. The 'Building Great Leaders' culture and 100-year operating history support a durable franchise.
The valuation premium is the primary concern. A trailing P/E of 125x (depressed by GAAP charges) and forward P/E of 27x with PEG of 2.41 price in substantial further earnings growth, leaving limited margin of safety [5]. Profit margin of 3.96% is below industrial peers, and while margin expansion is the core thesis, execution risk is real — the company must continue converting from lower-margin installation work to higher-margin inspection and service contracts. Net insider selling of $6.3M over the past year is a negative signal for a company whose culture narrative centers on leadership conviction. D/E of 88% reflects the leverage from the Chubb acquisition, and ROE of 10% is below the 15% target due to the goodwill-heavy balance sheet. Integration risk from the Chubb deal is largely behind, but maintaining cultural cohesion across 500+ locations and 29,000 employees as growth accelerates remains an ongoing management challenge.
Key Catalysts
  1. Data center safety services growing to 10-11% of revenue as hyperscale AI infrastructure buildout accelerates, creating a high-growth vertical within the recurring services portfolio

  2. Profit margin of 3.96% and ROE of 10% are below industrial peers, and the margin expansion thesis depends on successfully transitioning from installation to recurring service work across a fragmented 500+ location organization
  3. Net insider selling of $6.3M in the past 12 months contradicts the 'Building Great Leaders' conviction narrative and may signal leadership concerns about near-term valuation

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
9
AI Risk Protection (9/9): APi Group's core business — installing, inspecting, and servicing fire protection systems, life safety equipment, security systems, elevators, and HVAC infrastructure — is essentially immune to AI disruption [1][2]. These services require licensed technicians physically present at customer facilities, adherence to local building codes and fire marshal regulations, and hands-on work with physical systems that AI cannot replicate. Regulatory requirements mandate periodic in-person inspections that cannot be automated. Furthermore, AI is a demand driver: the explosion of AI data center construction (expected to reach 10-11% of APi's revenue in 2026) directly increases demand for fire suppression, power distribution, and cooling systems that APi installs and services [2]. The fragmented nature of safety services also limits AI-driven competitive disruption — local relationships, regulatory expertise, and licensed labor create defensible positions. AI Opportunity (0/1): APi does not generate revenue from AI products or services. The company is a beneficiary of AI-driven infrastructure spending rather than an AI technology provider.
