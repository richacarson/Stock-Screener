Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Apollomics is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on the discovery and development of oncology therapies to address difficult-to-treat and treatment-resistant cancers. The company's lead candidate, vebreltinib (APL-101), is a selective c-MET inhibitor in Phase 2 clinical development (SPARTA study) across NSCLC, brain tumors, and other solid tumors with MET alterations. Pipeline includes nine product candidates, six in clinical development. CEO Howard Chen (appointed September 2025) holds 42% of Class A shares. Market cap approximately $51M with trailing revenue of $8.5M.
