Investment Thesis
Digital Turbine is executing a compelling operational turnaround, with the stock up 162% over 52 weeks from its post-restatement nadir. FY2026 delivered $565.3M revenue (+15% YoY), $122.5M adjusted EBITDA (+69% YoY), and non-GAAP EPS of $0.56 (+51% YoY) [1]. At $10.88 and 14.32x forward earnings, the stock offers value for a company guiding FY2027 to $630-650M revenue (+12-15%) and $135-145M adjusted EBITDA (+10-18%) [1]. The App Growth Platform segment grew 21% to $185.7M, validating the AdColony/Fyber acquisition thesis. The appointment of a former Microsoft AI Copilot CTO signals serious AI investment in first-party data monetization, and device-level OEM/carrier integration provides a structural moat that pure-play ad tech competitors lack [3]. Analyst consensus has upgraded to 'Strong Buy' with a $17.17 price target implying 58% upside [6].
The bear case centers on structural industry risks and financial leverage. D/E of 187% reflects $361M acquisition debt against only $37.7M cash, severely constraining financial flexibility — free cash flow of just $11.8M in FY2026 leaves minimal margin for error on debt service [1][6]. GAAP net loss of -$37.7M persists despite adjusted profitability, driven by intangible amortization from acquisitions that inflated the balance sheet. The mobile advertising market is consolidating around AI-powered platforms from Google and Meta, with conversational AI and on-device AI assistants threatening to bypass traditional app discovery and distribution channels entirely — a secular risk to DT's core On Device Solutions model [3]. The SEC penalty for lax internal controls and unresolved securities fraud class action from the 2022 restatement remain governance overhangs [5]. Glassdoor ratings of 3.2/5 with concerns about executive communication and CEO leadership fit suggest cultural challenges as the company scales. The Infinite Game score of 3/10 (FINITE) reflects a company optimizing for financial outcomes without a deeper purpose.
