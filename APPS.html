Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NASDAQ ·United States
Digital Turbine, Inc. operates a mobile growth platform for advertisers, publishers, carriers, and device OEMs. It operates through two segments: On Device Solutions (app preloading via carrier/OEM partnerships) and App Growth Platform (programmatic advertising, DSP, offer wall, and ad monetization). The company's platform enables brand discovery, user acquisition, and engagement across the mobile ecosystem. FY2026 revenue of $565M with 15% growth and $106M adjusted EBITDA. Headquartered in Austin, Texas.

Investment Thesis
Digital Turbine is executing a compelling operational turnaround from its 2022 nadir. FY2026 delivered $565M revenue (15% growth), $106M adjusted EBITDA (70% YoY growth), and the App Growth Platform segment surged 57% in Q4, validating the AdColony/Fyber acquisition strategy [1]. At $10.39, the stock trades at just 9.8x forward earnings and 0.69x PEG — deep value territory for a company guiding FY2027 revenue to $630-650M with $135-145M EBITDA [1]. The appointment of a former Microsoft AI Copilot veteran as CTO signals serious AI investment in first-party data monetization, and the company's unique device-level integration with carriers and OEMs provides a moat that pure-play ad tech competitors lack [3].
The bear case centers on structural risks that the market is pricing in. D/E of 192% reflects $369M acquisition debt that constrains financial flexibility, and the company remains in net loss (-$37.7M TTM) despite adjusted profitability improvements [5]. The 2022 revenue restatement and associated securities fraud lawsuit remain a governance overhang, and analyst coverage has thinned to just 2 analysts with a $8.75 median price target below current market [4][5]. The mobile advertising market is consolidating around AI-powered platforms from Google and Meta, threatening Digital Turbine's differentiation. Volume of 5.7M daily shares provides adequate liquidity, but the beta of 2.77 means extreme volatility on any earnings miss.
Key Catalysts
  1. FY2027 guidance of $630-650M revenue and $135-145M adjusted EBITDA represents 14-15% revenue growth and 27-37% EBITDA expansion, with potential for margin inflection as the turnaround matures
  2. New CTO from Microsoft AI Copilot team driving AI-powered first-party data optimization across $1.5B addressable market, with potential to differentiate against larger ad tech competitors
  3. App Growth Platform segment growing 57% YoY in Q4 FY2026 demonstrates platform-level demand acceleration that could sustain double-digit consolidated growth
Key Risks
  1. D/E of 192% with $369M debt against only $38M cash leaves minimal financial cushion; any revenue miss could trigger covenant pressure and force dilutive capital raise
  2. Secular risk from AI-native app discovery (conversational AI bypassing traditional app stores) and Google/Apple platform AI integration could structurally reduce demand for third-party device-level app distribution
  3. Outstanding securities fraud class action from 2022 revenue restatement remains unresolved, with only 2 analyst coverage and median price target of $8.75 below current market price signaling limited institutional conviction
