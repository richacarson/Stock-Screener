Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology / Specialty Pharmaceuticals ·NASDAQ ·United States
Aquestive Therapeutics is a specialty pharmaceutical company focused on developing and commercializing differentiated products using its proprietary PharmFilm orally dissolving film drug delivery technology. The company has 6 FDA-approved products including Sympazan (clobazam oral film) and is advancing Anaphylm (sublingual epinephrine film) through late-stage development with an NDA resubmission planned for Q3 2026. Aquestive reported Q2 2026 revenue of $13.8M (+38% YoY) and maintains 2026 revenue guidance of $46-50M. The company has a net cash position of $67.15M ($110.73M cash vs $43.58M debt) following an Oaktree refinancing.
150 Employees ·www.aquestive.com

Investment Thesis
Aquestive Therapeutics at a $628M market cap offers a binary catalyst play on the Anaphylm NDA resubmission in Q3 2026 — if approved, a needle-free sublingual epinephrine film addressing the $2.5B+ anaphylaxis treatment market could transform the company from a sub-$50M revenue specialty pharma into a meaningful commercial-stage company [1]. Q2 2026 revenue growth of 38% YoY to $13.8M demonstrates accelerating base business momentum from manufacturing and royalty revenue, and the Oaktree refinancing eliminated the punitive 13.5% interest rate, extending runway. The net cash position of $67.15M provides ~2 years of operating runway at current burn rates [4]. The PharmFilm platform's 6 FDA-approved products validate the technology, and CEO Daniel Barber's 19-year tenure provides leadership stability through the critical regulatory period [3].
The bear case is financial fragility and regulatory binary risk. Aquestive burns ~$30-35M annually (guided adjusted EBITDA loss of $30-35M for 2026) and has negative stockholders' equity — the company's survival depends on either Anaphylm approval driving commercial revenue or raising additional capital [1][4]. The FDA has already issued deficiency letters on the Anaphylm NDA, and a second rejection would severely impair the company's viability and likely trigger significant equity dilution. Profit margin of -137% reflects that current revenue does not come close to covering operating expenses. With only ~150 employees and $46-50M in guided 2026 revenue, Aquestive lacks the commercial infrastructure to independently launch Anaphylm without a partner or significant investment [3]. The 2025 revenue decline of -22.6% shows base business volatility that undermines the growth narrative. The stock's $4.66 price with a market cap of $628M implies significant optionality value already priced in for Anaphylm approval.
