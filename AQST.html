Company Profile
Healthcare ·Drug Manufacturers - Specialty & Generic ·NMS ·United States
Aquestive Therapeutics is a specialty pharmaceutical company focused on developing and commercializing differentiated products using its proprietary PharmFilm orally dissolving film drug delivery technology. The company has 6 FDA-approved products including Sympazan (clobazam oral film) and is advancing Anaphylm (sublingual epinephrine film) through late-stage development with an NDA resubmission planned for Q3 2026. H1 2026 revenue of $28.3M (+51% YoY) with Q2 revenue of $13.8M (+38% YoY). Full-year 2026 revenue guidance of $46-50M. The company secured a $150M Oaktree term loan facility in Q1 2026 with principal repayment pushed to 2031.
150 Employees ·www.aquestive.com

Investment Thesis
Aquestive Therapeutics at $3.78 ($474M market cap) offers a binary catalyst play on the Anaphylm NDA resubmission in Q3 2026 — if approved, a needle-free sublingual epinephrine film addressing the $2.5B+ anaphylaxis treatment market could transform the company from a sub-$50M revenue specialty pharma into a meaningful commercial-stage business [1]. H1 2026 revenue surged 51% to $28.3M with Q2 at $13.8M (+38% YoY), demonstrating accelerating base business momentum from manufacturing and Zevra royalty revenue, and management reaffirmed 2026 guidance of $46-50M [1]. The $150M Oaktree term loan (principal deferred to 2031) with milestone-linked additional capital provides runway through the Anaphylm regulatory period. The FDA conditionally approved the Anaphylm trade name, suggesting engagement with the commercial pathway. CEO Barber's 19-year tenure provides continuity through the critical regulatory window, and the Inspire Impact Score of 28 (ALIGNED) makes AQST suitable for faith-based portfolios [3].
The bear case centers on financial fragility and binary regulatory risk. Aquestive burns ~$30-35M annually (guided adjusted EBITDA loss) and has negative stockholders' equity — survival depends on either Anaphylm approval driving commercial revenue or raising additional capital [1][4]. The FDA has already issued deficiency letters on the Anaphylm NDA, and a second rejection would severely impair the company's viability and likely trigger significant equity dilution at the $3.78 price level. Profit margin of -144.6% reflects that current revenue does not come close to covering operating expenses. Shareholder litigation from 2021 alleging securities violations related to FDA disclosure practices remains an overhang [4]. The stock has declined 20% from its 50-day SMA of $4.71, reflecting negative sentiment ahead of the NDA decision. With only ~150 employees and $46-50M in guided revenue, Aquestive lacks the commercial infrastructure to independently launch Anaphylm without a partner or significant additional investment in sales and marketing [3]. The Q2 2026 net loss of $22.9M included a non-recurring $11.7M debt-extinguishment charge, but even excluding it, the $11.2M adjusted loss demonstrates ongoing cash consumption.
