Company Profile
Technology ·Scientific & Technical Instruments ·NASDAQ ·United States
Arrive AI Inc. (formerly Arrive Technology Inc.) designs and implements smart mailbox and platform systems for autonomous logistics. Products include the AP3 Plus autonomous delivery platform, Arrive Points smart locker network, and Arrive OS software layer enabling secure, asynchronous exchange points connecting drones, robots, delivery providers, retailers, and consumers. The company focuses commercialization on healthcare, manufacturing, and specialty pharmacy sectors through partnerships with Nexus AMR and DXC. Founded 2020, headquartered in Fishers, Indiana. TTM revenue of $52K with going concern warning. Market cap ~$11M.
