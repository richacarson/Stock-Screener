Company Profile
Healthcare ·Medical Devices ·Nasdaq ·United States
Accuray Incorporated, headquartered in Madison, Wisconsin, develops, manufactures, and sells radiation oncology systems including the CyberKnife, TomoTherapy, and Radixact platforms for treating complex cancer cases. FY2025 net revenue grew 3% to $458.5M with Adjusted EBITDA of $28.3M, but FY2026 guidance was cut from $471-485M to $440-450M in revenue amid geopolitical delays to system installations in Middle East markets, and the company installed new CEO Steve La Neve in October 2025 to lead a performance turnaround.
