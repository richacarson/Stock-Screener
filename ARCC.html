Company Profile
Financial Services ·Asset Management — Business Development Company ·NASDAQ ·United States
Ares Capital Corporation is the largest publicly traded business development company (BDC) in the United States, providing direct lending and other financing solutions to middle-market companies. Managed by Ares Management, ARCC invests primarily in first and second lien senior secured loans, mezzanine debt, and equity to private companies across a variety of industries.

Investment Thesis
Ares Capital remains the gold standard among BDCs, offering a ~10% dividend yield supported by a $27B+ diversified portfolio of middle-market loans, dominant origination scale, and the backing of Ares Management's $450B+ AUM platform [1][2]. Q2 2026 net investment income of $0.50/share comfortably covers the $0.48 quarterly dividend, core earnings remain stable, and non-accrual rates are at historically low levels. The Commercial Paper program launch reduces funding costs and demonstrates ARCC's unique ability to access capital markets unavailable to smaller BDCs [1].
The bear case centers on interest rate compression squeezing net interest margins as the rate cycle turns — Q1 2026 core EPS already declined 6% YoY from $0.50 to $0.47 [1]. The $1B in AI-exposed software portfolio holdings creates indirect credit risk if AI disruption accelerates defaults among borrowers [7]. The structurally high payout ratio (~119%) leaves minimal cushion for dividend coverage in a sustained downturn, and BDC regulatory requirements limit strategic flexibility. At 9x forward earnings, the market is pricing in execution risk around the rate cycle transition.
Key Catalysts
  1. Commercial Paper program reducing funding costs by an estimated 50-100bps vs. traditional BDC credit facilities, expanding net interest margins
  2. Potential for increased deal flow as private equity sponsors resume M&A activity in the middle market, driving origination volume growth
  3. Dividend sustainability supported by Q2 2026 NII of $0.50/share exceeding $0.48/share payout, with 67 consecutive quarters of stable or growing distributions
Key Risks
  1. Interest rate compression in 2026-2027 could squeeze net investment income by 10-15% as floating-rate loan yields decline faster than funding costs adjust
  2. Approximately $1 billion in software portfolio investments face medium AI disruption risk, creating potential credit deterioration in an estimated 5-8% of the portfolio
  3. BDC regulatory structure requires 90%+ income distribution, leaving minimal retained earnings buffer for credit losses during a recession cycle
