Company Profile
Consumer Cyclical ·Restaurants ·NYSE ·Uruguay
Arcos Dorados is the world's largest independent McDonald's franchisee, operating approximately 2,400 restaurants across 21 countries and territories in Latin America and the Caribbean. The company holds exclusive rights to own, operate, and sub-franchise McDonald's restaurants in the region. Digital channels account for 64% of sales with a loyalty program of 30.4 million members. Q2 2026 set a record with $1.31B revenue (+14% YoY) and adjusted EBITDA of $126.8M (+15.2%). CEO Luis Raganato assumed leadership July 2025 after 34 years with the company. Market cap is approximately $1.6B.

Investment Thesis
Arcos Dorados at 8.76x forward earnings with a PEG of 0.54 offers exceptional value for the world's largest independent McDonald's franchisee delivering record revenue growth. Q2 2026 set an all-time record with $1.31B revenue (+14% YoY) and adjusted EBITDA of $126.8M (+15.2%), following Q1's $1.22B revenue (+12.9%) with EBITDA surging 29.3% [1][2]. Net income and EPS both doubled YoY in Q2, reflecting operational leverage as digital transformation (64% digital sales, 30.4M loyalty members) and restaurant expansion drive scale. ROE of 36.4% demonstrates capital-efficient execution. Plans for 105-115 new restaurants in 2026 with $275-325M capex target an underpenetrated Latin American market with 650M+ consumers. The Inspire Impact Score of 65 (ALIGNED) with 17 positive attributions and zero negatives makes this one of the strongest faith-aligned profiles in the coverage universe [3].
The bear case centers on leverage, FX risk, thin margins, and franchise dependency. D/E of 264.1% with $2.2B total debt against only $270M cash creates meaningful refinancing risk as Latin American interest rates remain elevated [4]. Profit margin of just 5.2% — structural for QSR franchisees — leaves minimal cushion for cost inflation, FX headwinds, or commodity price spikes. Systemwide comparable sales growth of 16% was described as 'in line with blended inflation,' suggesting limited real volume growth beyond currency pass-through in high-inflation markets like Argentina [1]. As a franchisee, Arcos Dorados lacks control over menu pricing strategy, brand direction, and royalty rates — McDonald's Corp could unilaterally change franchise terms or economics. The $1.6B market cap and $7.53 share price reflect small-cap status with limited institutional coverage, and forward EPS of $0.86 (vs. trailing $1.23) suggests potential earnings normalization ahead [4].
