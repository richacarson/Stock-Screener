Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Ardelyx discovers, develops, and commercializes innovative first-in-class medicines for gastrointestinal and cardiorenal diseases. The company markets IBSRELA (tenapanor) for irritable bowel syndrome with constipation (IBS-C) and XPHOZAH (tenapanor) for hyperphosphatemia in chronic kidney disease patients on dialysis. Q2 2026 revenue reached a record $118.1M (+31% YoY) driven by IBSRELA ($86.2M, +33%) and XPHOZAH ($31.9M, +27%). FY2026 revenue guidance of $520-$550M with path to sustainable profitability in 2027. Market cap approximately $954M with $281.8M in cash.
489 Employees ·www.ardelyx.com

Investment Thesis
Ardelyx is executing a compelling commercial ramp with Q2 2026 record revenue of $118.1M (+31% YoY), driven by IBSRELA's record-breaking IBS-C market share ($86.2M, +33%) and XPHOZAH's strong adoption as the only NHE3 inhibitor for hyperphosphatemia ($31.9M, +27%) [1]. FY2026 guidance of $520-$550M with a clear path to sustainable profitability in 2027 establishes a trajectory from zero to over half a billion in annual revenue within 4 years. Patent protection through 2042 and the Phase 3 ACCEL trial for CIC extend the tenapanor franchise runway. The PEG ratio of 0.70 suggests the market undervalues the earnings growth trajectory relative to the high forward PE, and the Inspire Impact Score of 29 (ALIGNED) makes the stock accessible for faith-based investors [4].
The bear case centers on valuation, profitability timeline, and balance sheet pressure. The stock has declined 37% over the past 52 weeks despite strong revenue growth, and the forward PE of 215x reflects near-breakeven earnings with no guarantee the profitability inflection arrives on schedule [4]. D/E of 190% with $233.8M in debt creates balance sheet leverage, and net losses of $16.7M in Q2 2026 continue to consume cash ($281.8M remaining). The June 2026 D.C. Circuit Court loss on XPHOZAH Medicare reimbursement was the final legal defeat, removing a potential revenue uplift catalyst [3]. Net insider selling of $1.7M over recent months creates governance concerns during the critical commercialization scaling phase. IBSRELA concentration risk is meaningful — 73% of revenue depends on a single product in the competitive IBS-C market, and any payer pushback or generic entry would disproportionately impact the company.
