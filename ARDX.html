Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Ardelyx discovers, develops, and commercializes innovative first-in-class medicines for gastrointestinal and cardiorenal diseases. The company markets IBSRELA (tenapanor) for irritable bowel syndrome with constipation (IBS-C) and XPHOZAH (tenapanor) for hyperphosphatemia in chronic kidney disease patients on dialysis. Q2 2026 revenue reached $118.1M (+31% YoY) driven by IBSRELA ($86.2M, +33%) and XPHOZAH ($31.9M, +27%). FY2026 IBSRELA guidance reduced to $350-370M due to payer restrictions; XPHOZAH guidance maintained at $110-120M. Market cap approximately $798M.
489 Employees ·www.ardelyx.com

Investment Thesis
Ardelyx at $3.09 with a $798M market cap is executing a compelling commercial ramp with Q2 2026 record revenue of $118.1M (+31% YoY), driven by IBSRELA's first-in-class IBS-C dominance ($86.2M, +33%) and XPHOZAH's strong adoption as the only non-phosphate binder for hyperphosphatemia ($31.9M, +27%) [1]. Despite the IBSRELA guidance reduction to $350-370M from payer restrictions, total FY2026 product revenue of ~$460-490M still represents transformative growth from pre-revenue status just 4 years ago. Patent protection through 2042 and the Phase 3 ACCEL trial for CIC extend the tenapanor franchise runway. The estimated PEG ratio of ~0.60 suggests the market undervalues the earnings growth trajectory, and the Inspire Impact Score of 29 (ALIGNED) with zero negative attributions makes the stock accessible for faith-based investors [4].
The bear case centers on the guidance cut, profitability timeline, and balance sheet pressure. The IBSRELA guidance reduction to $350-370M (from higher levels) due to payer restrictions on new patient starts signals commercial headwinds, and the stock has declined ~44% from its 200-day moving average [1][2]. Forward PE of ~190x reflects near-breakeven earnings with no guarantee the profitability inflection arrives on schedule in 2027. D/E of ~190% with ~$234M in debt creates balance sheet leverage, and net losses of $16.7M in Q2 2026 continue to consume cash. Net insider selling over recent months creates governance concerns during the critical commercialization phase. IBSRELA concentration risk remains meaningful — ~73% of revenue depends on a single product in the competitive IBS-C market, and the guidance cut demonstrates vulnerability to payer pushback. The 56% Glassdoor recommend-to-friend rate and only 56% positive business outlook on Glassdoor suggest mixed organizational sentiment despite the CEO's strong ratings [5].
