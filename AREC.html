Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Critical Minerals / Rare Earth Elements ·NASDAQ ·United States
American Resources Corporation is a development-stage company that has completed a multi-year transformation from legacy coal mining to a rare earth and critical minerals technology platform. Through its ReElement Technologies subsidiary, AREC operates a chromatography-focused separation facility for rare earth and critical elements in Indiana, using proprietary purification technology. The company targets the domestic magnet, battery, and semiconductor supply chains for defense and commercial applications. Recently hired seven new operational leaders for the ReElement campus from the US Navy, large-scale manufacturing, and chemical engineering backgrounds. Founded in 2015, headquartered in Fishers, Indiana.

Investment Thesis
AREC at $2.51 represents a speculative option on domestic critical minerals reshoring backed by bipartisan policy support and growing defense demand. The balance sheet improved through deconsolidation of legacy coal assets, producing clean D/E of ~5% and minimal debt [1]. ReElement Technologies' proprietary chromatography separation technology (developed with Purdue University) is validated by NSF Innovation Engines finalist status and a Mitsubishi Materials partnership [5]. The recent hiring of seven operational leaders from the US Navy and large-scale manufacturing signals progression toward commercial operations [2]. The Inspire Impact Score of 60 (ALIGNED) with zero negative attributions makes AREC attractive for faith-based investors seeking critical minerals exposure. U.S. policy environment aggressively incentivizes rare earth supply chain independence from China.
The bear case remains severe despite balance sheet improvements. FY2024 revenue was just $383K — a 97.7% collapse — and the company continues to burn cash with negative EPS [1]. Prior Nasdaq non-compliance notices for delayed financial filings signal governance breakdowns that could lead to delisting risk. The company lacks the scale and human capital to compete against well-funded peers like MP Materials ($3B+ market cap) and Lynas ($5B+) that are already at commercial-scale production. Market cap of ~$235M trades at an extraordinary multiple of near-zero revenue, pricing in substantial optionality that may never materialize. Forward P/E and PEG ratios are unavailable due to negative earnings forecasts through 2026, with analysts projecting approximately -$36M in losses [1]. Any additional equity raises to fund operations would dilute existing shareholders at the $2.51 price level.
