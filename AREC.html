Company Profile
Industrials ·Waste Management ·NASDAQ ·United States
American Resources Corporation is an Indiana-based holding company that has moved away from Central Appalachian coal toward critical-mineral recycling and refining. It holds minority stakes in ReElement Technologies (about 17%, rare earth and critical element refining), Willcox International Holdings (about 9%) and Royalty Management Holding (about 8%), and wholly owns Electrified Materials Corporation (magnet and battery recycling). Continuing-operations revenue was $0 in FY2025. Q1 and Q2 2026 10-Qs remain unfiled while its 2025 financials are re-audited, under a Nasdaq exception that runs to November 16, 2026.

Investment Thesis
The bull case is optionality on U.S. critical-mineral onshoring. AREC holds stakes in ReElement (~17%), which has begun weekly rare earth oxide shipments, received August 2026 air permits for its Marion, Indiana refinery, and reportedly won a $25M U.S. Department of War award in July 2026 [3][6]. At 12/31/2025 AREC had $72.2M of cash and short-term investments against $8.1M of debt, roughly half its $136M market cap, and the September 2026 $40.0M Wyoming County Coal settlement added West Virginia refuse-pile mineral rights as possible feedstock [2][4]. Analyst targets of $5-$7 compare with a $1.27 share price [4].
The bear case is about reliability and dilution. The FY2025 net income of $55.4M came entirely from a $73.2M discontinued-operations gain on deconsolidation, while continuing operations lost $17.8M on zero revenue [2]. The prior auditor's 2025 opinion was withdrawn, both 2026 10-Qs are unfiled, and the listing depends on meeting a November 16, 2026 Nasdaq deadline [4]. The stock is down about 73% over a year from a $4.94 high, a 10% holder sold about 500,000 shares in November 2025, and the all-stock Blackion deal plus capital-intensive refining buildouts point to more dilution [1][4][9].
Key Catalysts
  1. Filing the re-audited FY2025 10-K and the delinquent Q1/Q2 2026 10-Qs before Nasdaq's November 16, 2026 exception deadline, which would remove the delisting overhang [4]
  2. Confirmation of ReElement's germanium production at the Marion, Indiana campus (targeted Q3 2026) after August 2026 air permits, which would support the value of AREC's ~17% stake [3][6]
  3. Monetizing the West Virginia property, equipment and refuse-pile mineral rights received in the September 2026 $40.0M Wyoming County Coal settlement [4]
Key Risks
  1. Delisting risk: failure to file the overdue 10-Qs and the re-audited 2025 financials by November 16, 2026 could cost AREC its Nasdaq listing [4]
  2. Financial statement reliability: GreenGrowth CPAs' opinion on 12/31/2025 statements was withdrawn in July 2026, so the reported $55.4M FY2025 profit and $94.8M equity may be restated [2][4]
  3. Dilution: the ~$13.2M all-stock Blackion acquisition and capital-intensive refining buildouts at affiliates likely require further share issuance, with zero continuing revenue to fund them internally [2][4]
