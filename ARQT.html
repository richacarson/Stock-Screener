Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Arcutis Biotherapeutics is a commercial-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on developing and commercializing treatments for dermatological diseases. Its lead product ZORYVE (roflumilast) is FDA-approved as a topical cream for plaque psoriasis and atopic dermatitis, and as a foam for seborrheic dermatitis and scalp/body psoriasis. Pipeline includes ARQ-234 (CD200 receptor agonist, Phase 1a/1b), ZORYVE label expansions for pediatric and new indications, and the Ducentis collaboration. Founded in 2016, headquartered in Westlake Village, California.

Investment Thesis
Arcutis delivered Q1 2026 revenue of $105.4M (+65% YoY), reiterated full-year guidance of $480-495M, and generated positive operating cash flow of $2.2M — demonstrating the company's transition from money-losing biotech to self-sustaining commercial franchise [1]. ZORYVE is the only FDA-approved topical PDE4 inhibitor across four dermatological indications, commanding an uncontested first-mover position in a $10B+ market. At 25x forward PE with 30%+ revenue growth, 90%+ gross margins, and a clean balance sheet ($224M cash), the risk/reward is compelling for a category-creating biotech with a deep pipeline [4]. ARQ-234 entered Phase 1a/1b (first-in-human), ZORYVE sNDA for infants is under FDA review (target date June 29, 2026), and Phase 2 trials are underway for vitiligo and hidradenitis suppurativa — creating multiple pipeline catalysts [1]. The Ducentis acquisition adds further optionality.
The bear case centers on single-product concentration, earnings quality, and growth deceleration. ZORYVE accounts for substantially all revenue, and any safety signal, formulary exclusion, or competitive launch could materially impact the company [1]. Q1 2026 EPS of -$0.09 missed consensus of -$0.05, causing an 11.78% after-hours decline, demonstrating the stock's sensitivity to earnings quality [1]. R&D spending surged 75% to $30.6M (including a $10M Ducentis milestone), and SG&A climbed 16% to $74.1M from sales force expansion — profitability depends on these investments moderating relative to revenue growth [1]. Revenue growth has already decelerated from 91% (FY2025) to 65% (Q1 2026) to guided 29-33% (full year 2026), and this trajectory will continue as ZORYVE penetration matures. Net insider selling — CEO Watanabe ($941K) and Director Leonard ($997K) in March 2026 — suggests leadership may be de-risking personal exposure [4]. ARQ-234 is early-stage with uncertain clinical outcomes and the Ducentis collaboration adds milestone payment risk.
