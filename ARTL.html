Investment Thesis
Artelo Biosciences is a speculative micro-cap biotech ($3.07M market cap) with a differentiated three-candidate pipeline targeting cancer cachexia (ART27.13), neuropathic pain (ART26.12), and anxiety (ART12.11) through lipid-signaling pathway modulation [2]. Cash position of $10.27M from recent capital raises provides approximately 14 months of runway at the current $8.5M annual operating cash burn [1]. Analysts project potential $71.9M in revenue by 2028 if the pipeline advances successfully [3].
The bear case is severe: the stock has declined 98% from $42.42 to $0.88 over the past year, reflecting the market's loss of confidence in the company's ability to deliver [1]. Seven years of operation with zero revenue, only 7 employees, and a $3M market cap create existential risk. Zero revenue is projected through 2027, meaning multiple additional dilutive offerings are inevitable before any potential commercialization [3]. Daily dollar volume of only $114K makes the stock effectively untradeable for institutional investors, and the Nasdaq minimum market value deficiency creates ongoing delisting risk. This is a binary lottery ticket with asymmetric downside risk from further dilution versus speculative upside contingent on clinical success in a competitive therapeutic landscape.
