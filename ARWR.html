Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Arrowhead Pharmaceuticals is a commercial-stage biopharmaceutical company developing medicines based on RNA interference (RNAi) using its proprietary Targeted RNAi Molecule (TRiM) platform. The company received its first FDA approval in 2025 for Redemplo (plozasiran) to treat familial chylomicronemia syndrome (FCS) and has a broad pipeline targeting cardiometabolic, pulmonary, hepatic, and neuromuscular diseases. Strategic collaboration with Sarepta Therapeutics for ARO-DUX4 generated significant milestone payments. Headquartered in Pasadena, California.

Investment Thesis
Arrowhead Pharmaceuticals has reached a pivotal inflection point with its first FDA-approved product, Redemplo (plozasiran), for familial chylomicronemia syndrome, and the stock has surged 418% over the past year in response [1][4]. The Sarepta collaboration generated $750M+ in milestone payments validating the TRiM platform's breadth, and the pipeline includes multiple Phase 3 candidates targeting larger markets including severe hypertriglyceridemia and obesity-related cardiometabolic disease [2]. Q1 FY2026 revenue of $264M beat estimates of $228M, with EPS of $0.22 exceeding the $0.12 forecast, demonstrating the commercial trajectory is gaining momentum [1].
However, Arrowhead remains deeply unprofitable with a -48% profit margin, -42% ROE, and D/E of 231%, reflecting the capital intensity of transitioning from research to commercial operations [4]. Revenue is extremely lumpy due to milestone-driven collaboration payments, making financial projections unreliable: Q2 FY2026 revenue dropped to $74M from $264M the prior quarter [2]. The consensus EPS estimate for FY2026 remains at -$3.46 per share with no profitability expected in the next 3 years. Heavy insider selling by management including $11.7M by CEO Anzalone, combined with toxic culture reports on Glassdoor, raise governance concerns. The $12.25B market cap on $622M TTM revenue with no clear path to profitability makes the risk-reward profile speculative despite the promising pipeline.
Key Catalysts
  1. Redemplo commercial launch ramp in the US and EU, with peak sales estimates for the FCS indication of $500M-1B plus potential label expansion to severe hypertriglyceridemia addressing a significantly larger patient population
  2. Phase 3 clinical data readouts for plozasiran in obesity-related hypertriglyceridemia and ARO-APOC3 in 2026-2027, which could open multi-billion-dollar cardiometabolic markets
  3. Sarepta partnership milestones for ARO-DUX4 in facioscapulohumeral muscular dystrophy, with additional development and regulatory milestone payments expected
Key Risks
  1. Consensus FY2026 EPS of -$3.46 with no profitability expected in the next 3 years, combined with D/E of 231% and -48% profit margin, creates significant dilution and financing risk if pipeline readouts disappoint
  2. Revenue extreme volatility driven by one-time milestone payments rather than recurring product sales makes financial modeling unreliable and creates quarter-to-quarter expectations risk
  3. Heavy insider selling (50 sales, 0 purchases in 6 months) and Glassdoor culture concerns suggest governance and talent retention risks during the critical commercial launch phase

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience MODERATE
7
AI Risk Protection (6/9): Arrowhead's core business of developing RNAi therapeutics is well protected from AI disruption. Drug development requires physical molecule design, preclinical studies, multi-year clinical trials, and regulatory approval processes that AI cannot substitute [3]. The proprietary TRiM platform involves complex biology-chemistry interactions that require wet-lab validation. However, AI-accelerated drug discovery by competitors, particularly large pharma with dedicated AI units, could compress Arrowhead's competitive lead in RNAi target identification and molecule optimization. The company does not appear to be deploying AI defensively in its own discovery pipeline, creating a modest vulnerability relative to AI-enabled competitors. AI Opportunity (1/1): The broader pharmaceutical industry's adoption of AI for clinical trial design, patient identification, and drug interaction modeling creates opportunities for Arrowhead to leverage AI to accelerate its own pipeline development and reduce clinical trial costs [3].
