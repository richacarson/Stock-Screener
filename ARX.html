Company Profile
Financial Services ·Insurance Brokers ·NYSE ·Cayman Islands
Accelerant Holdings, headquartered in George Town, Cayman Islands and founded in 2018 by CEO Jeff Radke, operates a data-driven specialty insurance risk exchange connecting MGAs/MGUs with third-party risk capital. FY2025 revenue grew 51% to $913M with Exchange Written Premium up 35% to $4.19B and Adjusted EBITDA up 149% to $282M, and on August 13, 2026 the company agreed to be acquired by Thoma Bravo in an all-cash deal valuing shares at $20.25 (a 49% premium to the pre-announcement price), expected to close in the first half of 2027.

Investment Thesis
Accelerant has built a genuinely differentiated data-driven risk exchange model in specialty insurance, delivering FY2025 revenue growth of 51% to $913M, Exchange Written Premium growth of 35% to $4.19B, and Adjusted EBITDA growth of 149% to $282M, with third-party capital participation reaching 40% of Exchange premium as the model becomes more capital-efficient [1]. On August 13, 2026, Accelerant agreed to be acquired by Thoma Bravo in an all-cash transaction at $20.25 per share — a 49% premium to the pre-announcement price — with Altamont Capital Partners (holding ~82% of voting power) already committed to vote in favor and no financing condition attached, making deal completion reasonably likely by the expected first-half-2027 close [3].
The bear case is less about fundamentals than about opportunity cost and mission continuity: with shares trading at $19.75 against a $20.25 deal price, the stock offers only a modest ~2.5% spread to the pending acquisition, capping upside for new capital ahead of a first-half-2027 close, while remaining exposed to deal-completion risk (regulatory and shareholder approvals) [3]. The company is not yet profitable on a GAAP basis (ROE of -222%, profit margin of -127.5%), and Thoma Bravo's private-equity ownership model, once the deal closes, will likely prioritize a defined-horizon financial exit over the perpetual 'low-trust industry reform' mission Radke has articulated, undercutting the long-term Infinite Game thesis even if near-term financial execution remains strong [1][2][3].
Key Catalysts
  1. Closing of the Thoma Bravo acquisition at $20.25/share, with ~82% of voting power already committed in favor and no financing condition, would lock in value near the current price by H1 2027 [3]
  2. Continued Exchange Written Premium growth (>20% guided for 2026) and EBITDA scaling ahead of deal close could support the current share price relative to the deal terms [1]
  3. Expanding third-party capital participation (40% of Exchange premium) continues to reduce Accelerant's own balance-sheet risk exposure [1]
Key Risks
  1. The pending Thoma Bravo acquisition caps upside to roughly the $20.25 deal price, and any delay or failure to close (despite no financing condition) would introduce material downside risk to the current $19.75 share price [3]
  2. The company remains unprofitable on a GAAP basis with ROE of -222% and profit margin of -127.5%, reflecting the cost of rapid scaling [1]
  3. Private-equity ownership under Thoma Bravo, once the deal closes, is likely to prioritize a defined-horizon financial exit over the long-term 'low-trust industry reform' mission articulated by CEO Radke [2][3]
