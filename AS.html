Investment Thesis
Amer Sports is executing one of the most compelling brand-building stories in global sporting goods, with Q2 2026 revenue surging 32% YoY and adjusted gross margin expanding an extraordinary 710 basis points to 65.8%, while all segments, geographies, and channels achieved strong double-digit growth [1]. Arc'teryx continues as the breakout driver with Technical Apparel leading the portfolio, Salomon crossed $2B in annual sales, and DTC reached a record 55% of Q2 revenue. At a PEG of 0.89 on a forward P/E of 22x, the stock offers rare growth-at-a-reasonable-price among premium consumer brands, with FY2026 guidance raised to ~24% revenue growth and adjusted EPS of $1.27-$1.30 [4]. Asia Pacific revenue surged 60% in Q2 with Greater China up 36%, leveraging ANTA's strategic advantage in the world's second-largest consumer market [1].
The primary risks center on governance, insider selling, and cyclicality. CEO Zheng sold $17.2M in shares and CFO Page sold $3.9M in 2026, with no insider purchases identified — meaningful conviction concerns for a stock trading at 32x trailing earnings [4]. The ANTA consortium's 52% controlling stake creates governance asymmetry, and the appointment of ANTA executive Wei Lin to the board heightens related-party transaction concerns. The $64.3M tariff refund that boosted Q2 gross margin is a one-time benefit creating a tough comparison for future quarters. ROE of 9.15% and profit margin of 7.35% remain well below targets, reflecting the company's ongoing investment phase [4]. Heavy Asia Pacific exposure (60% Q2 growth) introduces geopolitical and consumer sentiment concentration risk. The stock's 24% decline over the past year despite strong operational results suggests the market already prices in execution risk.
Key Catalysts
  1. Asia Pacific revenue acceleration — Q2 2026 growth of 60% with Greater China up 36% indicates a long runway for premium outdoor brand penetration in the world's second-largest consumer market, leveraging ANTA's distribution expertise [1]
  2. DTC mix expansion toward 60%+ of revenue driving sustained margin improvement as Amer Sports captures more brand premium through owned retail and e-commerce versus wholesale channels [1]
  3. Adjusted operating margin expansion from 12.8% in Q2 toward the mid-teens long-term target as brand scale, DTC penetration, and supply chain optimization compound — management has raised guidance in each of the last four quarters [1][2]
Key Risks
  1. Macro sensitivity to consumer discretionary spending — premium outdoor apparel and sporting goods face demand compression in downturns, and the 32x trailing PE provides limited downside cushion if growth decelerates from the 30%+ pace [4]
  2. ANTA Sports majority ownership and governance structure limits minority shareholder influence; CEO and CFO insider selling totaling $21.1M in 2026 signals leadership may view the current valuation as full rather than an investment opportunity [4]
  3. Tariff policy and trade volatility — while the Supreme Court IEEPA ruling provided a $64.3M Q2 refund benefit, future trade policy changes could reverse this tailwind, and the global supply chain remains exposed to cross-border manufacturing costs [1]
