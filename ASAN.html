Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NYSE ·United States
Asana operates a work management platform that helps teams organize, track, and manage their work across projects, programs, and portfolios. Co-founded by Facebook co-founder Dustin Moskovitz, the company serves 150,000+ customers in nearly 200 countries. Asana reported FY2026 (ended Jan 2026) revenue of $790.81M (+9.25% YoY) with $18.2M non-GAAP operating income (9% margin) in Q4. Moskovitz transitioned from CEO to Chair in July 2025 to focus on philanthropy and AI safety, with Dan Rogers succeeding as CEO. The company has launched AI Studio and AI teammates as part of its transition toward an 'agentic enterprise' platform.

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience HIGH RISK
4
AI Risk Protection (4/9): Work management SaaS faces real AI substitution risk. AI agents are increasingly capable of automating project management tasks — creating task lists, tracking progress, assigning work, sending reminders, and generating status reports — which are core Asana functions. Multiple AI-native project management tools are emerging, and horizontal AI assistants (ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini) can perform many work coordination tasks without a dedicated platform [4]. Asana's enterprise integrations and workflow data create switching costs, but the competitive moat is narrowing as AI reduces the value of purpose-built project management tools. Revenue growth deceleration from 50%+ to 9% may partially reflect early AI disruption effects on customer acquisition. Asana is actively building AI features (AI Studio, AI teammates) to stay relevant, but the outcome is uncertain — the company is racing to transform from a tool that organizes work into a platform that does work, and it's unclear whether incumbents or AI-native entrants will win. AI Opportunity (0/1): Asana's AI Studio and AI teammates are shipping to customers but no specific AI revenue contribution has been quantified — the features are included in existing subscription tiers rather than priced separately.
