Company Profile
Financials ·Banks - Regional ·NYSE ·United States
Associated Banc-Corp is a Wisconsin-based regional bank holding company with approximately $42B in assets, offering commercial and retail banking, wealth management, and insurance services primarily across Wisconsin, Illinois, and Minnesota. Led by CEO Andy Harmening since 2021, the company completed the transformative acquisition of American National Corporation (ANC) in 2026, driving YTD revenue growth of 29% to $833.5M and loan growth guidance of 17-19% [1]. Q1 2026 net income of $117M ($0.70 EPS) and Q2 2026 adjusted EPS of $0.73 demonstrate strong post-acquisition earnings momentum. The company has paid dividends for 52 consecutive years.

Investment Thesis
Associated Banc-Corp at 9.9x forward earnings offers compelling value for a regional bank executing a transformative acquisition — the ANC deal drives 17-19% loan growth guidance and 29% YTD revenue growth to $833.5M, with Q2 2026 adjusted EPS of $0.73 demonstrating successful integration economics [1]. The 52-year dividend streak and 32% payout ratio provide significant income safety, while the 3.16% yield offers above-market income with room for future increases given the low payout. Net interest margin expansion from higher commercial lending yields and acquisition synergies position ASB to deliver sustained earnings growth. The $100M share buyback authorization further supports capital returns to shareholders.
The bear case centers on acquisition integration risk and structural growth limitations. The 29% revenue growth is almost entirely acquisition-driven — organic growth for a Midwest regional bank is likely low single digits, and the 7% five-year revenue CAGR reflects this reality [1]. ROE of 9.7% is below the 15% target, constrained by the capital-intensive banking model and integration costs. D/E of 113% is elevated relative to the 70% target, though manageable for a well-capitalized bank. The 5-year dividend growth CAGR of 3.7% trails the 6% target, suggesting modest income growth despite the long streak. Credit quality risk is elevated in a potential economic slowdown, particularly with the expanded commercial loan book from ANC. Geographic concentration in Wisconsin and the upper Midwest creates cyclical exposure to the region's manufacturing and agricultural economy.
Key Catalysts
  1. ANC acquisition synergies driving cost savings and revenue growth as integration progresses through 2026-2027, with 17-19% loan growth guidance and expanded commercial banking capabilities [1]
  2. Net interest margin expansion from higher commercial lending yields and favorable rate environment, supporting earnings growth beyond the Q2 2026 adjusted EPS of $0.73 [1]

  3. Compressed net interest margins if the rate environment reverses, reducing the spread between commercial lending yields and deposit costs that currently drive earnings growth [1]

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience MODERATE
7
AI Risk Protection (6/9): Associated Bank's core business — relationship-driven commercial and retail banking in the upper Midwest — is protected by regulatory moats (banking charter requirements, FDIC oversight, state licensing), high switching costs for commercial deposit relationships, and the physical branch network serving communities where digital-only banks have limited penetration [1]. AI cannot originate FDIC-insured deposits or hold a banking charter. However, AI-powered fintech lenders (SoFi, LendingClub) and neobanks are competing for consumer lending and checking accounts, and AI automation could reduce the need for back-office banking employees and loan processing staff. The commercial banking relationship model is more defensible than consumer banking against AI disruption. AI Opportunity (1/1): Associated Bank has invested specifically in AI capabilities with dedicated C-suite leadership — Chief Analytics Officer Chun Schiros leading data and AI innovation, and Andy Miner directing AI strategy for Corporate & Commercial Banking [2]. While no specific AI revenue figures are available, the bank is actively deploying AI tools for credit risk assessment and commercial lending operations, positioning it ahead of many regional bank peers in AI adoption.
