Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Other Precious Metals & Mining ·ASE ·Canada
Avino Silver & Gold Mines Ltd. is a Canadian mining company focused on silver and gold production in Mexico. The company operates the Avino Mine in Durango and is advancing its growth strategy to become a Mexico-focused mid-tier primary silver producer with inaugural mineral reserves of 127 million silver equivalent ounces. Q1 2026 set company records with $39.4M revenue (up 109% YoY) and $15.9M net income. Q2 2026 revenue was $26.8M (up 23% YoY) with $10.9M net income. Silver averaged $86.42/oz realized price in Q1. 2026 production guidance is 2.4-2.7M silver-equivalent ounces.

Investment Thesis
Avino Silver at $5.20 offers compelling value with a forward P/E of just 10.1x against 22.8% revenue growth, 38.2% profit margins, and near-zero debt (D/E 3.0%) [1][3]. The stock has declined 31% from $7.57 since August 2026 despite improving fundamentals — Q1 2026 set company records with $39.4M revenue (up 109% YoY) and $15.9M net income, while the inaugural mineral reserve estimate of 127M silver equivalent ounces provides multi-decade mine life visibility [1]. Silver's dual demand from industrial applications (AI/data center buildout, solar energy) and monetary demand provides structural price support. Forward EPS of $0.52 implies accelerating profitability. The $883M market cap offers asymmetric upside if Avino executes its mid-tier producer transition through La Preciosa advancement.
The bear case centers on Avino's extreme leverage to silver prices — with 54-60% of revenue from silver, a sustained price correction from the current ~$70-86/oz range to the historical $20-25/oz average would collapse margins and revenue by 50%+ [1][2]. Q2 2026 revenue of $26.8M was already 32% below Q1's record $39.4M, reflecting silver price volatility and production sequencing. The 31% stock price decline since August may signal market anticipation of normalizing commodity prices [3]. Single-country (Mexico) concentration creates regulatory, political, and labor risk — the Mining Journal reported a strike at Avino operations. The beta of 3.0 means the stock amplifies market movements by 3x, making it unsuitable for conservative portfolios.   1. Silver price supercycle driven by AI infrastructure buildout, solar energy expansion, and monetary demand could sustain prices above $60/oz through 2027, directly expanding Avino's margins on 2.4-2.7M silver-equivalent ounce production [1]

Key Risks

  3. Transition from junior to mid-tier producer requires significant capital deployment and execution on La Preciosa, with construction and permitting risk in Mexican regulatory environment where tighter ESG rules could raise compliance costs [2][6]
