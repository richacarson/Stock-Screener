Company Profile
Financial Services ·Asset Management ·NGM ·United States
Strive, Inc. is an asset management and Bitcoin treasury company founded in 2022 by Vivek Ramaswamy and Anson Frericks in Dallas, Texas. Originally launched as an anti-ESG ETF provider pursuing 'excellence capitalism,' the firm crossed $1B in AUM within 18 months and subsequently pivoted to a Bitcoin-focused treasury strategy. TTM revenue is approximately $8.5M with a -84.8% operating margin, reflecting deep unprofitability as the company scales its asset management and Bitcoin treasury operations.

Investment Thesis
Strive at $25.54 with a $2.6B market cap represents a highly speculative Bitcoin treasury and asset management play trading at over 300x TTM revenue of $8.5M. The company grew revenue 94.6% YoY and crossed $1B in AUM within 18 months of launch, demonstrating strong demand for anti-ESG investment products [1][3]. The pivot to Bitcoin treasury operations provides leveraged exposure to Bitcoin price appreciation, and near-zero debt with $188M in cash offers financial flexibility. The anti-ESG proxy voting model is differentiated in a market dominated by ESG-aligned managers, and Strive's political brand recognition through founder Vivek Ramaswamy provides organic marketing advantages [3].
The bear case is severe: Strive is deeply unprofitable with operating margins of -84.8%, operating cash outflow of $72.6M, and free cash burn of $455M against just $8.5M in revenue [1][5]. Q1 2026 EPS of -$5.19 missed the -$2.62 consensus by nearly 100%, and forward EPS of -$0.96 projects continued losses [1][2]. The Bitcoin treasury strategy concentrates risk in a single volatile asset class, and the $2.6B market cap is almost entirely Bitcoin-valuation-dependent rather than earnings-supported. The founder's departure to politics raises leadership continuity concerns, employee lawsuits signal governance risks, and the anti-ESG thesis depends on sustained political demand that could reverse with policy shifts [3][4]. With only 28 employees and $8.5M in revenue supporting a multi-billion-dollar valuation, the risk-reward skew is extreme.
Key Catalysts
  1. Bitcoin price appreciation — as a Bitcoin treasury company, Strive's market cap is directly leveraged to BTC price movements, with any sustained rally driving outsized valuation gains on the treasury holdings
  2. AUM growth acceleration — continued demand for anti-ESG investment products and potential new ETF launches could drive the asset management revenue base above the current $8.5M TTM run rate [1][3]
  3. Regulatory or political tailwinds — growing anti-ESG sentiment among state pension funds and Republican-led legislatures could channel institutional mandates toward Strive's product suite [3]
Key Risks
  1. Catastrophic cash burn — operating cash outflow of $72.6M and free cash burn of $455M against $8.5M revenue is unsustainable without continued capital raises, which will dilute existing shareholders at premium valuations [1][5]
  2. Bitcoin concentration risk — the treasury strategy creates binary exposure to Bitcoin price volatility; a sustained BTC decline would erode both the balance sheet and the market cap thesis simultaneously
  3. Governance and leadership instability — founder departure to politics, employee lawsuits alleging securities law violations, and a 28-person organization with no profitable track record present meaningful execution and reputational risks [3][4]
