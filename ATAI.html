Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
AtaiBeckley Inc. (formerly atai Life Sciences) is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing rapid-acting psychedelic and psychedelic-inspired therapies for treatment-resistant mental health conditions. Formed through the November 2025 combination of atai Life Sciences and Beckley Psytech, the company's lead asset BPL-003 (mebufotenin benzoate) is Phase 3-ready for treatment-resistant depression. Pipeline includes VLS-01 (DMT film, Phase 2), EMP-01 (R-MDMA, Phase 2), and multiple preclinical compounds. Eli Lilly has agreed to acquire AtaiBeckley for $6.75/share plus up to $2.50 in CVRs, with closing expected Q3 2026. CEO Srinivas Rao, M.D., Ph.D. leads from New York.
99 Employees ·www.ataibeckley.com

Investment Thesis
AtaiBeckley at $7.35 is effectively a merger arbitrage position with the pending Eli Lilly acquisition at $6.75/share cash plus up to $2.50/share in contingent value rights tied to BPL-003 and VLS-01 clinical milestones [1]. The stock trades above the cash component, reflecting market expectation of CVR value. The pipeline validation is significant — Lilly's willingness to pay up to $3.8B for a pre-revenue psychedelic therapy company validates the BPL-003 Phase 3 program for treatment-resistant depression, a massive unmet-need market estimated at $10B+ globally. The company has $169M cash providing runway into 2029 if the deal fails, and multiple clinical readouts (VLS-01 Phase 2 topline data H2 2026) provide near-term catalysts [3].
The primary risk is deal completion uncertainty — the Ademi LLP shareholder investigation into deal fairness, while typical for plaintiff-side law firms, adds governance noise [4]. If the Lilly deal fails, AtaiBeckley would need to fund BPL-003 Phase 3 trials independently with $169M cash, likely requiring additional financing. The company is deeply unprofitable with -$127M operating cash flow and a $660M GAAP net loss in 2025 (driven by non-cash items from the Beckley combination) [1][5]. Clinical-stage biotech risk is inherent — BPL-003 Phase 3 could fail, and the psychedelic therapy regulatory pathway remains novel with limited precedent. The 99-person company being absorbed into Eli Lilly means the original team's ability to execute their vision depends on a large pharma parent's priorities and bureaucracy.
