Company Profile
Technology ·Information Technology Services ·NASDAQ ·United States
Alpha Compute Corp. (NASDAQ: ALP, formerly ATON / AlphaTON Capital / Portage Biotech) is a micro-cap technology company that has undergone three identity changes in under two years. Originally a clinical-stage oncology biotech (Portage Biotech), it rebranded as AlphaTON Capital in September 2025 to pursue TON blockchain token treasury management, then rebranded again to Alpha Compute Corp. in April 2026 pivoting to AI GPU-as-a-service and confidential computing. The company operates NVIDIA GPU clusters (H200, B200, B300) for AI inference processing and maintains a TON digital asset treasury. CEO Brittany Kaiser leads a 4-person team. Market cap approximately $8M.

Investment Thesis
Alpha Compute (formerly AlphaTON/Portage Biotech) is a micro-cap speculative vehicle that pivoted from failed oncology biotech to AI compute infrastructure and TON blockchain treasury management [4]. The bull case rests on early revenue generation from Cocoon AI inference on NVIDIA GPU clusters since December 2025, a $46M GPU expansion commitment, partnerships with Telegram and Animoca Brands, and the $44M private placement demonstrating capital access [1][3]. At ~$8M market cap, any successful scaling of GPU-as-a-service revenue could generate outsized returns from a deeply depressed base. The confidential computing angle — privacy-preserving AI inference — addresses a real enterprise need.
The bear case is overwhelming. A 4-person company with a micro-cap market cap and going concern disclosure is committing to $46M in GPU infrastructure — a funding gap requiring massive dilution or favorable crypto markets [3][5]. The stock has lost over 90% from its highs with daily liquidity below $1M. Three identity changes in under two years (Portage Biotech to AlphaTON to Alpha Compute) and connections to promotional figures raise serious credibility concerns [4]. The GPU-as-a-service market is dominated by AWS, Azure, and GCP with effectively infinite balance sheets, making sustainable competitive advantage nearly impossible for a 4-person operation. TTM EPS of -$5.72 reflects deep operating losses with no clear path to profitability. This is a highly speculative situation with significant risk of permanent capital loss and potential NASDAQ delisting exposure.
