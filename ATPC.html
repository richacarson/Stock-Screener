Agape ATP Corporation is a Malaysia-based investment holding company supplying health and wellness products, skin care, and health solution advisory services through a network-marketing model. The company also provides renewable energy products, installations, and maintenance services through subsidiary ATPC Green Energy. Listed on NASDAQ in 2023, it serves approximately 56,000 distributors and 72,000 members. Market cap is approximately $2.6M.
