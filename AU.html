Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Gold ·NYSE ·South Africa
AngloGold Ashanti is a global gold mining company with operations and projects spanning four continents. Following its redomicile to the UK and NYSE primary listing in 2024, the company operates mines in Africa (Ghana, Guinea, Tanzania), the Americas (Argentina, Brazil, Colombia), and Australia. In Q1 2026 the company produced 724,000 ounces of gold and generated record free cash flow of $1.2 billion, benefiting from elevated gold prices above $3,000/oz.

Investment Thesis
AngloGold Ashanti represents a compelling value opportunity in the gold mining sector, trading at just 7.8x forward earnings despite delivering record free cash flow of $1.2 billion and EBITDA of $2.3 billion in Q1 2026 alone [1][2]. The company's transformed portfolio -- anchored by the Obuasi redevelopment, Siguiri expansion, and the world-class Merlin deposit in Nevada -- provides a multi-year production growth runway within 2026 guidance of 2.8-3.17 million ounces.
With gold prices sustained above $3,000/oz, the combination of operational leverage, conservative balance sheet (D/E of 38.8%), and exceptional capital returns (28.2% ROE) positions AU for continued outperformance. The record Q1 dividend of $585M signals management's commitment to shareholder returns, while the low 20% payout ratio preserves ample reinvestment capacity [2]. Primary risks center on gold price cyclicality, geopolitical exposure in West Africa, and execution on the Merlin development timeline.
Key Catalysts
  1. Sustained gold prices above $3,000/oz driving record margins and free cash flow generation through 2026-2027 [1]
  2. Obuasi Phase 3 and Siguiri Block 1 underground development ramping production toward the upper end of 2.8-3.17M oz guidance [2]
  3. Merlin project in Nevada advancing through permitting with potential to become a Tier 1 asset in a low-risk jurisdiction
Key Risks
  1. Gold price reversion below $2,500/oz would compress margins significantly given AISC guidance of $1,780-$1,990/oz [2]
  2. Geopolitical risk in West Africa (Ghana, Guinea, Tanzania) including regulatory changes, community disputes, or political instability
  3. Execution risk on Merlin development timeline and capital budget in a period of mining cost inflation

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience STRONG
8
AI Risk Protection (7/9): Gold mining is a physically intensive extraction industry with minimal direct AI substitution risk -- the core business of finding, extracting, and processing gold ore requires capital-intensive infrastructure, geological expertise, and regulatory relationships that AI cannot replicate [3]. The commodity nature of gold means demand is driven by macroeconomic factors, central bank policy, and geopolitical uncertainty rather than technology disruption. AI cannot substitute for physical gold production or replace the safe-haven asset class. The only risk vectors are AI-optimized exploration by competitors shifting discovery advantages, but AngloGold's own AI deployment mitigates this. AI Opportunity (1/1): AngloGold actively deploys AI for orebody modeling (improving resource estimation accuracy), predictive maintenance on mills and processing plants, and autonomous drilling programs at Australian operations [3]. These generate measurable cost savings by reducing unplanned downtime and improving ore recovery rates, contributing to industry-leading AISC performance.
