Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Gold ·NYSE ·United Kingdom
AngloGold Ashanti is a global gold producer with operations in Africa (including Obuasi in Ghana and Geita in Tanzania), Australia (Sunrise Dam, Tropicana) and the Americas. It is UK-domiciled with a primary NYSE listing. FY2025 revenue reached $9.89B (+71%) and net income $2.64B as gold prices surged. In Q2 2026, free cash flow rose 36% to $727M (H1 2026 FCF $1.9B), the company declared a 72c interim dividend, proposed a $2.0B share buyback and held $991M of net cash; 2026 production guidance is 2.80-3.17Moz at total cash costs of $1,315-$1,430/oz. CEO Alberto Calderon has led the company since 2021.

Investment Thesis
AngloGold Ashanti is converting high gold prices into exceptional cash generation with a clean balance sheet. FY2025 revenue rose 71% to $9.89B and net income reached $2.64B; H1 2026 free cash flow more than doubled to $1.9B, the company held $991M of net cash at June 30, 2026, and it declared a 72c interim dividend plus a proposed $2.0B buyback [1][6]. Mechanically the financials are near-ideal — ROE 44.7%, profit margin 32.2%, D/E 16.7% and a forward P/E of just 9.0 [5]. With the stock down to $82.26 from ~$121 in September and below its 50-day average of $98, the trailing yield is 5.5%, and 2026 guidance of 2.80-3.17Moz at $1,315-$1,430/oz total cash costs was reaffirmed [1][2][5]. The Inspire Impact Score of +30 (ALIGNED) is supportive.
The bear case is that nearly all of the improvement is gold-price leverage. The share price has fallen ~32% since September, signaling how quickly sentiment can reverse, and dividends are variable — annual payouts fell in 2022 and 2023 and were suspended entirely in 2014-2016 [5][7]. Q2 2026 EPS of $1.98 missed the $2.04 consensus and production fell to 744koz from 804koz after the Serra Grande sale [1]. Operations are concentrated in jurisdictions with elevated sovereign and security risk: Ghana's EPA ordered relocation of residents near the Binsere BIOX tailings facility pending a health study due in 2026, Tanzanian regulators raised concerns over Geita tailings-water trials, and a January 2025 security clash at Obuasi left nine illegal miners dead [4].
Key Catalysts
  1. Q3 2026 results (due November 2026) showing continued free cash flow growth after H1 2026 FCF of $1.9B, supporting further variable dividends [1]
  2. Execution of the proposed $2.0B share repurchase at a forward P/E of ~9x after a ~32% share-price decline since September [1][5]
  3. Delivery within 2026 guidance of 2.80-3.17Moz at $1,315-$1,430/oz total cash costs, protecting margins if gold prices soften [2]
Key Risks
  1. Gold-price reversal: revenue growth of 27% and the 87% 5-year dividend CAGR are driven by bullion prices, and AU's variable dividend would fall quickly in a downturn (as in 2022-2023) [5][7]
  2. ESG and community risk in Ghana and Tanzania — EPA-ordered resettlement near the Binsere BIOX tailings facility, Geita tailings-water concerns, and fatal security incidents at Obuasi [4]
  3. Production decline from asset sales (Q2 2026 production 744koz vs 804koz) and cost inflation compressing all-in margins [1]
