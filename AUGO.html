Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Gold ·NASDAQ ·United States
Aura Minerals Inc. is a mid-tier gold and copper production company operating five mines across the Americas: Minosa (Honduras), Apoena, Almas, and Borborema (Brazil), and Aranzazu copper-gold-silver mine (Mexico). Founded in 1946 and headquartered in Coconut Grove, Florida, the company operates under a 360-degree Mining concept integrating stakeholder value across shareholders, employees, and communities.

Investment Thesis
Aura Minerals delivered a record H1 2026 with US$718.6M revenue (up 104% YoY), driven by 27% production growth and sharply higher gold (+35%) and copper (+41%) prices [1]. Q2 2026 net income of US$217.7M and diluted EPS of $2.57 demonstrate exceptional operating leverage. With Era Dorada and Matupa projects targeting more than double current output, production guidance for 20-40% growth in 2026, and a 4.12% dividend yield supported by 17.38% average annual dividend growth, Aura offers a compelling combination of income and growth in a structural gold bull market. The Automet AI initiative delivering US$45.6M in efficiency savings shows operational discipline beyond commodity price tailwinds [3].
The primary risk is commodity price dependence: gold at record highs (+35% YoY) and copper at elevated levels (+41% YoY) are the dominant drivers of revenue and earnings growth, and a mean reversion in metals prices would compress margins significantly [1]. D/E of 142.54% is elevated for a cyclical business, and the TTM profit margin of 7.80% (pre-Q2 update) suggests underlying profitability remains modest despite headline revenue growth. Execution risk on the Era Dorada and Matupa development pipeline is meaningful, as new mine construction in Latin America faces permitting, environmental, and community challenges. The PE of 50.54x (TTM) is optically expensive, though it reflects stale earnings data that does not yet capture Q2 2026 results [5].
Key Catalysts
  1. Era Dorada and Matupa mine developments expected to more than double 2025 output over the coming years, providing multi-year production growth visibility
  2. Sustained gold price strength above $2,500/oz driven by central bank buying, geopolitical risk, and AI-driven copper demand supporting both revenue lines
  3. Automet AI and operational efficiency programs expanding across all five mines, targeting additional cost savings beyond the US$45.6M achieved to date
Key Risks
  1. Commodity price concentration risk: gold and copper together drive nearly all revenue, and a 20%+ pullback in metals prices would compress margins given the 142.54% D/E and capital-intensive operations
  2. Execution risk on Era Dorada and Matupa development projects, where construction delays, permitting challenges, or cost overruns in Latin American jurisdictions could defer production growth
  3. TTM payout ratio exceeds 100% based on trailing earnings; if Q2 2026 hedge gains prove non-recurring, the $2.25 annual dividend may require reassessment relative to normalized cash flows
