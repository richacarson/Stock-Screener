Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Gold ·NASDAQ ·United States
Aura Minerals Inc. is a mid-tier gold and copper production company operating five mines across the Americas: Minosa (Honduras), Apoena, Almas, Borborema, and Serra Grande (Brazil), and the Aranzazu copper-gold-silver mine (Mexico). Founded in 1946 and headquartered in Coconut Grove, Florida, the company operates under CEO Rodrigo Barbosa's 360-degree Mining concept integrating stakeholder value across shareholders, employees, and communities. Aura delivered record H1 2026 revenue of $718.6M (up 104% YoY) and is targeting 340k–390k GEO for full-year 2026 with a path toward 600k GEO annual production.

Investment Thesis
Aura Minerals at 9.7x forward earnings offers compelling value for a gold and copper producer delivering record results — H1 2026 revenue of $718.6M (up 104% YoY) with adjusted EBITDA of $440.5M (up 135% YoY) represents 12 consecutive quarters of EBITDA growth [1]. Production growth from ~130k GEO in 2018 to a 2026 target of 340k–390k GEO, with a clear path toward 600k GEO annually via Era Dorada and Matupa, provides multi-year volume growth. ROE of 100.2%, profit margin of 23.2%, and free cash flow of $403M support both the 3.35% dividend yield and continued development investment. The Automet AI initiative delivering $45.6M in efficiency savings demonstrates operational discipline beyond commodity price tailwinds [3][5].
The bear case centers on commodity price dependence and execution risk. Gold prices above $2,400/oz drive the exceptional current profitability — 2026 AISC guidance of $1,720–$1,865/oz leaves limited margin cushion at lower gold prices, and the company posted net losses in both FY2024 (-$30.3M) and FY2025 (-$79.3M) when gold was lower [1][5]. D/E of 100.3% represents meaningful leverage for a cyclical commodity producer. The 170% production growth story requires continued successful mine development — Borborema ramp-up, Serra Grande cost optimization, and Era Dorada permitting each carry project-specific risks across three Latin American jurisdictions. The stock trades at a 29% discount to its 52-week high of $110.32, suggesting the market may be pricing in commodity price reversion risk. Beta of 0.36 belies the cyclical nature of the underlying commodity exposure [5].
Key Catalysts
  1. Path to 600k GEO annual production through Era Dorada, Matupa, and continued Borborema ramp-up provides multi-year volume growth beyond the 2026 target of 340k–390k GEO [1]

Key Risks
  1. Gold price dependence — AISC guidance of $1,720–$1,865/oz means a decline to $1,800/oz gold would compress margins to near-breakeven, and the company posted net losses in FY2024 and FY2025 at lower gold prices [1][5]
  2. Execution risk across concurrent development projects — Era Dorada permitting with water treatment requirements, Matupa construction, and Serra Grande cost reduction face geological, regulatory, and community risks in three countries [1][4]
  3. D/E of 100.3% with $458M in total debt is elevated for a cyclical commodity producer, limiting financial flexibility if gold prices correct or project timelines slip [5]
