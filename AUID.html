Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Infrastructure ·NASDAQ ·United States
authID Inc. (formerly Ipsidy Inc.) provides a cloud-based biometric identity verification and authentication platform. Products include Proof (identity verification via government-issued IDs and facial biometrics), Verified (biometric authentication for passwordless login and transaction validation with anti-spoofing detection), and PrivacyKey (biometric authentication without storing any biometric data). Annual recurring revenue reached $1.9M in Q1 2026, up 58% YoY. CEO Rhon Daguro leads from Denver, Colorado with 46 employees.
