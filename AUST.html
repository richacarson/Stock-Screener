Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Gold ·NYSE American ·Canada
Austin Gold Corp. is a pre-revenue gold exploration company focused on district-scale discoveries in the southwestern United States, holding a 70% interest in the Kelly Creek Project (Humboldt County, NV) plus the Lone Mountain (Elko County, NV) and Stockade Mountain (Malheur County, OR) projects [1]. CEO Dennis Lyle Higgs leads a small technical team advancing RC drilling, geological mapping, and geochemical/gravity surveys across the three properties [2]. The company has not booked revenue or defined a mineable resource, and reported a 2025 net loss of $1.18 million with less than one year of cash runway against negative free cash flow of roughly $5.0 million, leaving it dependent on further equity or debt financing [1].

Investment Thesis
Austin Gold offers pure exploration optionality across three Nevada/Oregon gold targets (Kelly Creek, Lone Mountain, Stockade Mountain) at a sub-$16 million market cap, meaning any high-grade discovery could produce outsized returns for risk-tolerant speculators [1][2]. The company benefits from an experienced technical team, including 45-year exploration veteran Bob Hatch, and a rising gold price environment that has historically supported junior explorer financing and M&A activity in the district-scale discovery space [2].
The bear case dominates here: Austin Gold has less than a year of cash runway against roughly $5.0 million in negative free cash flow, virtually guaranteeing near-term dilutive financing or debt [1]. Average daily dollar volume of roughly $108,000 fails the liquidity floor by a wide margin, meaning the stock is effectively uninvestable for any allocator requiring meaningful position sizing or exit liquidity. No project has advanced to a defined resource, profit margin is zero, and return on equity is deeply negative at -32.98% [1][4]. This is a speculative, pre-revenue exploration vehicle appropriate only for high-risk capital explicitly earmarked for junior mining speculation, not a core holding.
Key Catalysts
  1. Positive assay results from the 2025-2026 RC drill programs at Kelly Creek or Lone Mountain could materially re-rate the stock given its sub-$16M market cap and thin share count [1]
  2. A sustained high gold price environment continues to support financing availability and M&A interest in Nevada-focused junior explorers, which could provide a strategic partner or takeout premium [2]
  3. Advancement of the Stockade Mountain (Oregon) RC program following up on 2023-2024 winter drilling could open a fourth distinct value driver beyond the Nevada assets [1]
Key Risks
  1. Less than one year of cash runway against ~$5.0 million in negative free cash flow means near-term shareholder dilution or debt financing is highly likely, and financing may not be available on favorable terms [1]
  2. Average daily dollar volume of roughly $108,000 falls far short of the $1,000,000 liquidity floor, exposing holders to wide bid-ask spreads and difficulty exiting meaningful positions [4]
  3. No project has a defined mineral resource or production timeline; continued negative drill results at any of the three targets could eliminate a key part of the investment thesis and further pressure the ability to raise capital [1]
