Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Gold ·NYSE ·Canada
Austin Gold Corp. is a gold exploration company focused on acquiring, exploring, and evaluating mineral resource properties in the southwestern United States. The company holds interests in three projects: the Kelly Creek Project (Humboldt County, Nevada), the Lone Mountain Project (Elko County, Nevada), and the Stockade Mountain Project (Malheur County, Oregon). Founded in 2020 and based in Vancouver, Canada, the company is pre-revenue and in the exploration stage with no proven reserves.

Investment Thesis
Austin Gold Corp. offers leveraged exposure to gold price appreciation through three exploration-stage properties in Nevada and Oregon, led by a management team with proven track records building mining companies — Pretium Resources (acquired by Newcrest for $3.5B) and Uranerz Energy (acquired by Energy Fuels) [2]. RC drilling has commenced at the Stockade Mountain Project in 2026, providing the first drill results that could validate or disprove the geological thesis [1]. The Kelly Creek Project sits on the prolific Battle Mountain-Eureka (Cortez) gold trend, and Lone Mountain is on the Independence-Jerritt Canyon trend — both world-class Nevada gold districts. With gold prices near all-time highs, zero debt, and $7.9M in total assets, a significant discovery at any project could generate multiples of the current $14.5M market cap [4].
The bear case is fundamental and severe. Austin Gold is pre-revenue with zero production, no proven reserves, no resource estimates, and no revenue timeline — Q1 2026 net loss was $564K against $7.9M total assets [1]. The company filed a $100M mixed securities shelf in June 2026, representing nearly 7x the current market cap and signaling imminent dilutive capital raises [1]. The stock has declined 72% from its 52-week high of $3.92 to $1.10, and daily dollar volume of ~$807K fails the $1M liquidity floor, making positions difficult to exit in a downturn [4]. Gold exploration has extreme binary outcomes — most junior miners destroy shareholder value through repeated dilutive financings that never lead to economically viable deposits. The CEO change at the May 2025 AGM (Dennis Higgs replacing prior leadership) may reflect strategic differences. Even if exploration succeeds, the $100M shelf positions the company for massive dilution of existing shareholders before any value can be realized.
