Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United Kingdom
Autolus Therapeutics plc is a clinical-stage-turned-commercial biopharmaceutical company developing next-generation, precisely engineered T-cell therapies for cancer and autoimmune disease. Its lead product, AUCATZYL (obecabtagene autoleucel), received FDA approval in November 2024 for adult relapsed/refractory B-cell acute lymphoblastic leukemia and subsequently gained UK and EU conditional marketing authorizations. Headquartered in London, UK, with manufacturing and clinical operations spanning the US and Europe.

Investment Thesis
Autolus has crossed the inflection from clinical-stage to commercial-stage biotech: AUCATZYL generated $74.3M in FY2025 net product revenue and $26.2M in Q1 2026 (versus $9.0M a year earlier), with 2026 guidance of $120-135M implying 62-82% growth, and gross margin turned positive for the first time in Q1 2026 as manufacturing efficiency and treatment-center volume both scale [1]. The company has activated 67 treatment centers with a target of 80+ by year-end 2026, and a four-program pipeline (CATULUS, LUMINA, BOBCAT, CARLYSLE) spanning oncology and autoimmune indications provides multi-year clinical and commercial catalysts beyond the core B-ALL indication [2]. The physical, biologically-grounded cell-therapy manufacturing and regulatory process is structurally immune to AI disruption.
The financial profile remains that of an early-commercial biotech: FY2025 net loss of $287.5M against $75.4M of revenue, negative ROE of -29.6%, and a negative forward P/E reflecting continued unprofitability. An April 2026 reduction in force affecting 13% of the workforce underscores ongoing capital discipline pressure even as the commercial ramp proceeds, and thin trading liquidity (~$2.0M/day dollar volume) adds volatility risk. With Inspiration (2/10) and Infinite Game (4/10) scores reflecting a short public track record and limited culture evidence, Autolus screens as a WATCH — a name whose commercial execution and pipeline readouts over the next several quarters will determine whether the recent gross-margin inflection is durable.
Key Catalysts
  1. Continued AUCATZYL commercial ramp toward the $120-135M 2026 guidance range as treatment centers expand from 67 toward the 80+ year-end target
  2. Sustained positive gross margin (achieved for the first time in Q1 2026) as manufacturing volume and efficiency scale further
  3. Pipeline readouts from the CATULUS, LUMINA, BOBCAT, and CARLYSLE trials, any of which could expand Autolus's addressable indications beyond adult r/r B-ALL
Key Risks
  1. Continued cash burn (FY2025 net loss of $287.5M) against a still-small revenue base creates ongoing capital-raise or dilution risk despite the recent 13% workforce reduction
  2. Thin trading liquidity (~$2.0M/day dollar volume) leaves the stock vulnerable to sharp price moves on clinical or commercial news
  3. Competitive pressure from established CD19-directed CAR-T therapies with larger commercial infrastructure could limit AUCATZYL's share gains in relapsed/refractory B-ALL
