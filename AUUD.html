Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NASDAQ ·United States
Auddia Inc. is an artificial intelligence technology company focused on audio and podcast engagement through its faidr app (AI-powered ad-free FM radio and podcast personalization) and Discovr Radio (B2B SaaS platform for artists and labels launched January 2026). The company reported Q2 2026 first product revenue of $7.6K from a Discovr Radio pilot, a net loss of $2.98M, and an accumulated deficit of $102.6M. Management expects $9.56M in cash to fund operations only into Q2 2027. A special committee is evaluating a business combination to restructure as an AI-native holding company.

Investment Thesis
Auddia's first product revenue of $7.6K in Q2 2026 from the Discovr Radio B2B SaaS pilot represents the earliest commercial traction in the company's 14-year history [1]. The pivot from B2C audio subscriptions to B2B SaaS targeting artists and labels could offer a more viable go-to-market path, with self-serve subscriptions launched in June 2026. The faidr app's proprietary AI ad-removal and personalization technology demonstrates genuine technical capability. The evaluation of a business combination as an AI-native holding company could unlock new revenue streams through centralized AI capabilities across portfolio companies [2]. With $9.56M in cash and low debt (D/E ~3%), the balance sheet provides at least 12 months of runway. The $4.4M market cap means any commercial success would generate outsized percentage returns from a deeply depressed base.
The bear case is overwhelming. Auddia has accumulated $102.6M in losses over 14 years with only $7.6K in quarterly product revenue — a ratio that defies any reasonable investment thesis [1]. H1 2026 net loss of $5.26M and widening quarterly losses ($2.98M in Q2 vs $2.28M in Q1) demonstrate accelerating cash burn. Management explicitly warns that $9.56M in cash funds operations only through Q2 2027, creating existential going concern risk within 9 months [1]. The proposed AI-native holding company restructuring introduces additional uncertainty — the special committee is still evaluating, and any deal would likely require dilutive equity issuance. Competition from Spotify, Apple Music, and Google in AI-powered audio, and from hyperscalers in AI compute, renders Auddia's offerings commercially irrelevant at current scale. No analyst coverage, no institutional ownership, and a stock that has declined from a split-adjusted $56+ to $1.22 reflect the market's near-unanimous verdict. The company is not investable under the IOWN framework.
