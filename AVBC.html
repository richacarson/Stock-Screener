Company Profile
Financial Services ·Banks - Regional ·NYSE ·United States
Avidia Bancorp, Inc. is the holding company for Avidia Bank, a Massachusetts-chartered community bank founded in 1869 and headquartered in Hudson, MA. The bank provides consumer and commercial banking services including savings, checking, CDs, mortgages, home equity, commercial loans, HSA accounts, and payment processing services. Avidia completed its mutual-to-stock conversion and IPO in July 2025 at $10.00/share, raising capital and establishing an $11.5M charitable foundation. Q2 2026 net income was $7.2M ($0.39 EPS), up 85% YoY, with net interest income of $24.0M (+16% YoY). Total assets of $2.84B.

Investment Thesis
Avidia Bancorp is a recently public Massachusetts community bank with 157 years of operating history and rapidly improving financials after normalizing from the IPO conversion. Q2 2026 net income rose 85% YoY to $7.2M ($0.39 EPS) with net interest income up 16% to $24.0M, NIM of 3.64%, and ROA of 1.04% [1]. First half 2026 net income of $13.2M represents a dramatic turnaround from a $7.7M net loss in H1 2025 (driven by a one-time $18.8M credit loss). Forward P/E of 14.79x represents a discount to regional bank peers. Management raised the quarterly dividend 20% to $0.06/share and authorized a $30M stock repurchase program, signaling confidence in sustained earnings power [1]. Unanimous insider buying by CEO ($90K), CFO ($20K), directors ($97.5K), and officers (~$60K) with no insider sales reinforces leadership conviction [4]. The $11.5M charitable foundation and 157-year heritage provide authentic community alignment [3].
The bear case centers on scale disadvantage, modest returns, and community bank structural challenges. ROE of 6.06% TTM is well below the 15% target and cost of equity for most investors, reflecting the dilutive impact of the large IPO capital raise that must be deployed into earning assets over time [1]. While revenue growth of 16.9% YoY is encouraging, the 5-year revenue CAGR of 8.9% is below the 10% target, and total loans declined 1% QoQ to $2.26B suggesting lending growth may be stalling. Community banks face secular headwinds from fintech competitors automating loan origination, branch consolidation trends, and AI-driven automation at larger institutions. As a newly public company (IPO July 2025), the operating track record under public market scrutiny is limited — the Q1 2025 $18.8M credit loss (primarily from one commercial loan charge-off) demonstrates the concentration risk inherent in a $2.84B asset community bank where a single loan can materially impact results. D/E data is unavailable, limiting balance sheet visibility. Dividend yield of only 0.73% does not qualify for the Dividend sleeve.
