Company Profile
Healthcare ·Medical Instruments & Supplies ·NASDAQ ·Australia
Anteris Technologies is a structural heart company developing the DurAVR transcatheter heart valve (THV) system, the world's first biomimetic TAVR valve incorporating proprietary ADAPT anti-calcification technology, designed to mimic the performance of a healthy human aortic valve for the treatment of severe aortic stenosis. Dual-listed on NASDAQ and ASX. Running the 1,000-patient PARADIGM global pivotal trial.
174 Employees ·anteristech.com

Investment Thesis
Anteris Technologies offers exposure to a potentially disruptive innovation in the $9.9B TAVR market through its biomimetic DurAVR heart valve, the first designed to mimic natural aortic blood flow using proprietary ADAPT anti-calcification technology. Major catalysts are materializing: FDA IDE approval secured, U.S. Medicare reimbursement eligibility confirmed under existing TAVR coverage in April 2026, and first U.S. patients treated in PARADIGM trial in May 2026 [3][4]. The Medtronic strategic investment of up to $90M validates the technology platform. Stock has rallied from $2.99 to $7.84 (52-week range) as clinical milestones de-risk the thesis, with analyst targets at $15.33 implying 96% upside [6].
The bear case remains substantial: Anteris is a pre-commercial company with $1.85M TTM revenue, $22.9M net loss in Q1 2026 alone, and $28.7M quarterly cash outflows [3]. The $320M capital raise provides runway but represents massive dilution across 97.46M shares. PARADIGM trial results are inherently binary — negative data would likely trigger catastrophic share price decline. The TAVR market is dominated by Edwards Lifesciences and Medtronic, both with global commercial infrastructure that Anteris would need to build from scratch even with positive trial results. The stock at $764M market cap prices in significant probability of commercial success, leaving limited margin of safety if the trial timeline extends or data disappoints [6].
Key Catalysts
  1. PARADIGM trial enrollment advancing with first U.S. patients treated May 2026 following FDA IDE approval and CMS Medicare reimbursement eligibility — topline readout data will be the definitive inflection point
  2. Medtronic partnership deepening beyond $90M strategic investment into potential co-commercialization, distribution, or acquisition at a premium to current valuation
  3. ADAPT anti-calcification tissue technology demonstrating superior durability versus bovine pericardial valves at multi-year follow-up, differentiating DurAVR for younger patient populations
Key Risks
  1. Binary clinical trial risk — negative PARADIGM trial results versus established TAVR devices would eliminate the investment thesis and likely trigger >50% share price decline from the $764M market cap
  2. Capital depletion risk with ~$100M annual cash burn against $1.85M revenue requiring continued capital market access; the $320M raise provides runway but additional dilutive financing may be needed
  3. Commercialization execution risk as a 174-person company attempting to build global sales infrastructure to compete with Edwards Lifesciences' and Medtronic's established TAVR commercial platforms
