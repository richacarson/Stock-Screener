Company Profile
Healthcare ·Medical Devices ·NASDAQ ·Australia
Anteris Technologies is a structural heart company developing the DurAVR transcatheter heart valve (THV) system, the world's first biomimetic TAVR valve incorporating proprietary ADAPT anti-calcification technology, designed to mimic the performance of a healthy human aortic valve for the treatment of severe aortic stenosis. Dual-listed on NASDAQ and ASX. Running the 1,000-patient PARADIGM global pivotal trial with U.S. enrollment begun in May 2026 and Medicare reimbursement eligibility secured. H1 2026 revenue $1.5M from regenerative tissue products, net loss $52.1M. Cash position $256.9M. Market cap ~$794M.
174 Employees ·anteristech.com

Investment Thesis
Anteris Technologies offers exposure to a potentially disruptive innovation in the $9.9B TAVR market through its biomimetic DurAVR heart valve, the first designed to mimic natural aortic blood flow using proprietary ADAPT anti-calcification technology. Major catalysts are materializing: FDA IDE approval secured, U.S. Medicare reimbursement eligibility confirmed under CMS NCD 20.32, and first U.S. patients treated in the PARADIGM pivotal trial in May 2026 [3][4]. The Medtronic strategic investment of up to $90M validates the technology platform. The stock has rallied from a $3.30 52-week low to $8.15 as clinical milestones de-risk the thesis. Cash position of $256.9M at June 30, 2026 provides funding runway for the pivotal trial [1]. If PARADIGM demonstrates superior durability over existing TAVR valves, DurAVR could capture meaningful share of a large, growing market with pricing power.
The bear case remains substantial for this pre-commercial company. H1 2026 revenue was just $1.5M against a $52.1M net loss, R&D expenses of $40.8M, and Q2 2026 cash outflows of $20.8M [1]. The $320M capital raise provides runway but represents massive dilution. PARADIGM trial results are inherently binary — negative data would likely trigger catastrophic share price decline given the $794M market cap prices in significant probability of commercial success. The TAVR market is dominated by Edwards Lifesciences and Medtronic, both with global commercial infrastructure that Anteris would need to build from scratch even with positive trial results. ROE of -74.7% and the absence of any meaningful revenue stream mean the company is entirely dependent on capital markets for survival [6]. Forward P/E of -8.15 reflects continued expected losses. Any delay in the trial timeline or disappointing interim data could require additional dilutive financing.
Key Catalysts
  1. PARADIGM pivotal trial enrollment advancing with U.S. patients being treated since May 2026 following FDA IDE approval and CMS Medicare reimbursement eligibility — topline readout data will be the definitive inflection point for a $9.9B market opportunity [3][4]
  2. Medtronic partnership deepening beyond the $90M strategic investment into potential co-commercialization, distribution, or acquisition at a premium, leveraging Medtronic's global TAVR commercial infrastructure [1]
  3. ADAPT anti-calcification tissue technology demonstrating superior durability versus bovine pericardial valves at multi-year follow-up, differentiating DurAVR for younger patient populations needing lifetime valve solutions [2]
Key Risks
  1. Binary clinical trial risk — negative PARADIGM results versus established TAVR devices from Edwards Lifesciences and Medtronic would eliminate the investment thesis and likely trigger >50% share price decline from the $794M market cap [1]
  2. Capital depletion risk with ~$100M+ annual cash burn against $1.5M H1 2026 revenue requiring continued capital market access; the $256.9M cash position provides runway but additional dilutive financing may be needed if the trial timeline extends [1]
  3. Commercialization execution risk as a 174-person company attempting to build global sales and distribution infrastructure to compete with Edwards' and Medtronic's established TAVR commercial platforms, even with positive trial results [2]
