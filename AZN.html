Company Profile
Healthcare ·Drug Manufacturers - General ·NYSE ·United Kingdom
AstraZeneca is a global biopharmaceutical company focused on the discovery, development, manufacturing, and commercialization of prescription medicines across oncology, cardiovascular/renal/metabolic, respiratory/immunology, and rare disease therapeutic areas. Under CEO Pascal Soriot's leadership since 2012, the company has transformed from a declining patent-cliff company into a growth leader, more than doubling revenue from $26.6B (2020) to $60.4B+ (TTM). H1 2026 revenue grew 6% at constant exchange rates (11% excluding Farxiga/Brilinta generics), with oncology up 15% to $14.1B. Named TIME Magazine's World's Most Sustainable Companies for a third consecutive year. Headquartered in Cambridge, England, with ADR trading on NYSE.

Investment Thesis
AstraZeneca delivered H1 2026 revenue growth of 6% at constant exchange rates (11% excluding generic competition), with oncology up 15% to $14.1B and core EPS up 11% [1]. The company guided for mid-to-high-single-digit revenue growth and low double-digit EPS growth for full-year 2026. Under Soriot's 14-year transformation, revenue has more than doubled from $26.6B (2020) to $60.4B+ TTM, with the $80B 2030 revenue ambition providing a clear compounding path [2]. Forward P/E of 14.42x with D/E of 64% and ROE of 21.96% represents attractive value for a diversified pharma compounder with leadership positions in oncology and rare disease [5]. The proactive AI adoption strategy, with a dedicated CDO and AI unit deploying generative AI across R&D and operations, positions AstraZeneca ahead of pharma peers in operational efficiency [3].
The primary concern is execution risk on ambitious growth targets and approaching patent cliffs. The $80B 2030 revenue ambition requires sustained high-single-digit growth from a $60B base, implying material contribution from pipeline products not yet launched [2]. Patent cliffs for Tagrisso (2032) and other key assets create medium-term headwinds that must be offset by pipeline replenishment. Profit margin of ~17% remains well below the mid-30s target, and the path to margin expansion depends on operational leverage and mix shift toward higher-margin products. H1 2026 revenue growth of just 6% (before excluding generics) signals the drag from Farxiga and Brilinta generic competition [1]. The Inspire Insight score of -75 (MISALIGNED) due to Embryonic Stem Cells and LGBT Activism attributions constrains allocation for faith-aligned investors. China market exposure (10-15% of revenue) creates ongoing regulatory, pricing, and geopolitical risk.
