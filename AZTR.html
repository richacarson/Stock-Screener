Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·ASE ·United States
Azitra is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company engineering living bacteria and proteins as precision dermatology therapeutics, leveraging a proprietary microbial library platform and a Bayer partnership. Lead candidate ATR-12 (engineered S. epidermidis) targets Netherton syndrome with Phase 1b topline data anticipated H2 2026. ATR-04 completed Cohort 1 of its Phase 1/2 trial for EGFR-inhibitor-associated rash with positive safety review and is proceeding to Cohort 2. Pre-revenue with Q2 2026 net loss of $3.3M and $6.7M in cash. Headquartered in Branford, Connecticut.

Investment Thesis
Azitra is a micro-cap clinical-stage biotech with a scientifically distinctive platform — engineered living bacteria delivered topically for precision dermatology — pursuing genuine unmet needs in Netherton syndrome (zero approved therapies) and EGFR-inhibitor rash in oncology patients [1][3]. Two binary catalysts are imminent: ATR-12 Phase 1b topline data expected H2 2026, and ATR-04 Phase 1/2 Cohort 1 completed with positive safety review and Cohort 2 now dosing at MD Anderson Cancer Center [2]. The Bayer partnership for S. epidermidis strain characterization adds credibility to the platform technology. A clean balance sheet with minimal debt (D/E 5.6%) at least means the company carries no leverage risk atop clinical and financing risk.
The bear case dominates. Azitra fails the IOWN liquidity floor — daily dollar volume of approximately $230,000 is a fraction of the $1,000,000 minimum required for institutional tradability, driving Infrastructure score to a hard zero. The company is pre-revenue with an accelerating cash burn: $6.7M in cash at June 30, 2026 (down from $10.1M at March 31), implying approximately two quarters of runway at the $3.3M quarterly burn rate [1]. Further financing at increasingly dilutive terms is near-certain, and prior dilution has been extreme. The CEO's 'strategic reorientation' letter signals survival-mode decision-making. Any clinical setback on ATR-12 or ATR-04 would likely be existential given the depleted cash position. With an $8M market cap, 13 employees, zero revenue, negative ROE of -256.7%, and a penny stock share price, this is a name suitable only for investors with very high risk tolerance who understand binary clinical outcomes [1][4].
