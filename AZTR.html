Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NYSE American ·United States
Azitra is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company engineering living bacteria and proteins as precision dermatology therapeutics, leveraging a proprietary microbial library platform. Lead candidate ATR-12 (an engineered strain of S. epidermidis) targets Netherton syndrome, a rare chronic skin disease with no approved treatment, with Phase 1b topline data anticipated in the second half of 2026. ATR-04 targets EGFR-inhibitor-associated skin rash in oncology patients and added MD Anderson Cancer Center as a clinical trial site in February 2026. The company is pre-revenue, reported a Q1 2026 net loss of $3.9 million against $10.1 million in cash, and CEO Francisco Salva issued a shareholder letter in 2026 outlining a 'strategic reorientation' of the business.
