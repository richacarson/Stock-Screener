Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas Midstream ·NASDAQ ·Hong Kong
CBL International Limited is a global marine fuel logistics provider established in 2015, operating a bunkering network spanning 70+ ports across the Asia Pacific and beyond. Under CEO Dr. Teck Lim Chia, the company distributes conventional marine fuels and is expanding into sustainable marine fuels through its April 2026 acquisition of a 50.5% stake in Green Marine Energy Holdings Limited. H1 2026 revenue reached $395.59M (+49.2% YoY), driven by geopolitical oil price escalation and 10.9% sales volume growth. The company returned to profitability with $1.5M net income (vs. $992K loss in H1 2025) and declared a special cash dividend of $0.10/share. CBL is a micro-cap ($9.4M market cap) with ultra-thin gross margins of 1.65% — a fuel intermediary rather than a refiner, earning spread on volume rather than product margin.
