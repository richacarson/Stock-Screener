Company Profile
Consumer Cyclical ·Specialty Retail — Pet Products ·NYSE ·United States
BARK, Inc. is a vertically integrated, omnichannel dog products company offering monthly subscription boxes (BarkBox and Super Chewer), e-commerce, retail partnerships, consumables including BARK in the Belly premium dog food, and BARK Air pet travel service. The company designs and manufactures proprietary dog toys, treats, and wellness products. Q1 FY2027 (June 2026) revenue of $78.8M was at the high end of guidance but down 23.4% YoY, with DTC at $66.7M, Commerce at $12.1M (-11% YoY), and BARK Air at $3.2M (+37% YoY) [1]. FY2027 guidance: $325-340M revenue, $7-10M adjusted EBITDA. Founded in 2012, headquartered in New York.

Investment Thesis
BARK offers a recognizable pet brand with a genuine dog-obsessed culture, and Q1 FY2027 showed encouraging operational improvements — subscriber retention improved 170bps YoY, average order value increased $0.45/unit, and BARK Air revenue grew 37% YoY to $3.2M with 90%+ of Q2 seats sold [1]. FY2027 adjusted EBITDA guidance of $7-10M is a significant step up from FY2026's $0.2M, indicating the cost restructuring is gaining traction [1]. Insider ownership of approximately 30.63% aligns management with shareholders. The Inspire Impact score of +52 (ALIGNED) with zero negative attributions makes this a faith-positive holding. If management can stabilize revenue, achieve FY2027 EBITDA targets, and BARK Air scales to a meaningful revenue contributor, the stock offers speculative upside from deeply depressed levels.
The bear case dominates the investment profile. BARK fails the IOWN liquidity floor with approximately $640K in daily traded value, making the stock effectively uninvestable for institutional portfolios [6]. Revenue has declined for four consecutive fiscal years — from $535M (FY2023) to $484M (FY2025) to $395M (FY2026) — and FY2027 guidance of $325-340M implies a fourth year of contraction with Q1 FY2027 already down 23.4% YoY [1][3]. The company has never achieved sustained net profitability, and even with improved EBITDA guidance, FY2027 net losses will likely continue. Cash of $21.7M against $38.3M debt with improving but still negative operating results creates a narrow financial runway [6]. Commerce revenue reversed course in Q1 FY2027, declining 11% YoY to $12.1M after growing 50% in FY2026, raising questions about the retail diversification narrative [1]. Employee reviews describe a toxic culture under CEO Meeker — fear-based management, constant layoffs, 29% employee recommendation rate — which undermines the dog-loving brand promise and erodes the creative capabilities that differentiate BARK's products [4].
