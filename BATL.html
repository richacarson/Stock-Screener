Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas E&P ·NYSE American ·United States
Battalion Oil Corporation is a micro-cap independent energy company focused on the acquisition, production, exploration, and development of onshore oil and natural gas assets in the Delaware Basin of West Texas. The company operates approximately 12,400 BOE/d of production (45% oil, 70% liquids) and reported Q2 2026 revenue of $48.1M (+12.4% YoY) with net income of $15.5M. Under CEO Matt Steele (appointed April 2023), the company has pursued cost reductions, balance sheet deleveraging, and joint exploration agreements. Headquartered in Houston, Texas with approximately 40 employees and a market capitalization of ~$30M.

Investment Thesis
Battalion Oil's Q2 2026 results show early signs of a turnaround: revenue of $48.1M (+12.4% YoY), net income of $15.5M, LOE reduced 21% to $8.69/BOE, and leverage improved from 1.79x to 1.36x [1]. The forward P/E of 5.08 prices the stock at deep value levels, and the joint exploration agreement for up to eight wells provides production growth optionality. Total liquidity of $88.4M against $162.5M term loan debt gives the company runway, and the new credit facility (maturity December 2029) reduces near-term refinancing risk. The Inspire Impact Score of 42 (ALIGNED) makes the stock accessible to faith-based mandates. At $1.35 per share with a $30M market cap, any sustained operational improvement could drive meaningful percentage returns [2].
The bear case is substantial: Battalion Oil is a micro-cap E&P ($30M market cap) with a 5-year revenue decline of -12.1% CAGR (from $285M to $166M), deeply negative TTM profitability, and no competitive moat in the Delaware Basin [2]. The company has diluted shareholders by over 32 million shares through ATM offerings at ~$1.73/share, and the 52-week low of $1.00 reflects genuine equity-wipeout risk. With only 12,400 BOE/d of production and commodity-price-dependent revenue, Battalion has no pricing power and is one sustained oil price downturn away from covenant stress on its $162.5M term loan at SOFR + 6.50% — a high-yield rate reflecting credit risk [1]. The Q1 2026 net loss of $64.8M (including $46.9M unrealized derivative loss) demonstrates the extreme earnings volatility. The absence of any dividend, R&D, innovation, or strategic vision beyond near-term survival makes this a speculative turnaround play unsuitable for IOWN's Return on Intention framework.
