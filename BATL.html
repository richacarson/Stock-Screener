Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas E&P ·NYSE American ·United States
Battalion Oil Corporation is a small-cap independent oil and natural gas company focused on the acquisition, production, exploration, and development of oil and natural gas properties in the Delaware Basin of West Texas. Formerly known as Halcon Resources, the company emerged from restructuring in 2020 and rebranded. Battalion operates approximately 25,000 net acres in the Delaware Basin with production of approximately 6,000-7,000 boe/d. In September 2026, Battalion announced a strategic equity investment in Collide Industrial Technologies for AI-powered oil and gas operations. Headquartered in Houston, Texas.

Investment Thesis
Battalion Oil trades at just 4.5x forward earnings with a $59M market cap, offering deep value exposure to Delaware Basin oil production if commodity prices hold. Q2 2026 delivered a strong recovery with $48.1M revenue (up from $42.8M YoY) and $15.5M net income ($0.34/share), driven by a $6.48/Boe increase in average realized prices [1]. Total liquidity of $88.4M and positive equity of $203.1M provide balance sheet stability despite the microcap scale. The September 2026 strategic partnership with Collide Industrial Technologies for AI-powered operations could drive meaningful cost reductions for a 40-person operation managing 100+ terabytes of data [3]. D/E of 78.2% is manageable for an E&P company, and the forward P/E of 4.48 offers substantial upside if profitability normalizes after the Q1 2026 impairment charges.
The primary risk is Battalion's microcap scale ($59M market cap, 40 employees) which creates concentration risk, limited access to capital markets, and vulnerability to single-well production disruptions. Q1 2026 delivered a devastating net loss of $64.81M on just $39.17M in revenue, likely from impairment charges, demonstrating the earnings volatility inherent in a small E&P [2]. Trailing profit margin of -24.5% and ROE of -20.2% reflect this instability. Production declined approximately 582 Boe/d in Q2 despite higher realized prices, suggesting operational challenges that may offset pricing tailwinds [1]. Battalion has no dividend, no innovation moat, no brand, and no purpose beyond commodity extraction — it is purely a bet on Delaware Basin oil prices and management execution. The stock's decline from $1.30 (50-day SMA) to $1.03 reflects ongoing market skepticism about the company's ability to sustain profitability.
