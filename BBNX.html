Company Profile
Healthcare ·Medical Devices ·NASDAQ ·United States
Beta Bionics is a commercial-stage public benefit medical device company that designs, develops, and commercializes the iLet Bionic Pancreas, the first FDA-cleared insulin delivery device that autonomously determines every insulin dose for type 1 diabetes in adults and children six and older. Founded by Dr. Ed Damiano, whose son has T1D. Q2 2026 revenue $32.0M (+38% YoY), full-year guidance $121-126M (revised down from $131-136M due to ~$10M Q4 deferral ahead of Mint launch). The company faces a securities fraud class action alleging concealment of 18,000+ unreported iLet safety complaints and abandonment of a built-in dosing safety algorithm. Developing Mint patch pump (expected Q2 2027), Bihormonal iLet, and T2D pivotal trial. Market cap ~$1.16B.
420 Employees ·www.betabionics.com

Investment Thesis
Beta Bionics is executing a strong commercial ramp with the only FDA-cleared fully autonomous insulin delivery system, generating $119M TTM revenue growing 38% YoY in Q2 2026 [1]. The pharmacy channel (153% YoY growth) is broadening patient access, and gross margins improved to 58.3%. The stock has recovered from its April low of $8.80 to $22.04, and the company's $180.5M cash position provides runway despite ongoing losses [7]. Major upcoming catalysts include Eversense 365 CGM integration (Q4 2026), Mint patch pump launch (Q2 2027), and T2D pivotal trial advancement — each representing meaningful TAM expansion. The iLet's proprietary autonomous dosing algorithm is a rare example of AI-powered medical device technology generating real commercial revenue, and the device's radical simplicity (body weight only input) differentiates strongly against Medtronic and Insulet [2].
The bear case is dominated by the securities fraud class action (lead plaintiff deadline November 3, 2026), which alleges concealment of 18,000+ unreported safety complaints including life-threatening hypoglycemic events, abandonment of a dosing safety algorithm, and failure to report adverse events to the FDA [8]. If the allegations are substantiated, enforcement could escalate to product recalls, consent decrees, or sales restrictions that would halt commercial momentum. Full-year revenue guidance was reduced to $121-126M from $131-136M, with ~$10M of Q4 revenue deferred as users await the Mint launch, creating near-term growth deceleration risk [1][3]. The company remains deeply unprofitable with -61.3% profit margin and -26.2% ROE, and the $1.16B market cap prices in significant future success despite material regulatory and legal uncertainty [7]. Revenue growth decelerated from 52.5% TTM to 37.8%, and the stock's 72% decline from $31.99 to $8.80 and subsequent volatile recovery to $22.04 demonstrates the binary risk profile. Net insider selling in early 2026 adds concern about management conviction [6].
