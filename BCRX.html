Company Profile
Healthcare ·Drug Manufacturers - Specialty & Generic ·NASDAQ ·United States
BioCryst Pharmaceuticals is a biotechnology company focused on rare diseases, principally hereditary angioedema (HAE). Lead product ORLADEYO (berotralstat) is the first and only oral once-daily prophylactic treatment for HAE, generating $158.2M in Q2 2026 revenue. The company acquired Astria Therapeutics to add navenibart subcutaneous HAE treatment and closed its Birmingham R&D facility in June 2026 to pivot to external innovation. CEO Charlie Gayer succeeded founding CEO Jon Stonehouse in January 2026. FY2026 total revenue guidance raised to $690-715M with ORLADEYO guidance of $625-645M.
