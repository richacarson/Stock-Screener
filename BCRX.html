Company Profile
Healthcare ·Drug Manufacturers - Specialty & Generic ·NASDAQ ·United States
BioCryst Pharmaceuticals, Inc. is a biotechnology company focused on rare diseases, principally hereditary angioedema (HAE). Lead product ORLADEYO (berotralstat) is the first and only oral once-daily prophylactic treatment for HAE, generating $158.2M in Q2 2026 revenue (+10% YoY). The company acquired Astria Therapeutics and its navenibart subcutaneous HAE treatment to build a comprehensive franchise. In June 2026, BioCryst closed its Birmingham R&D facility and ended internal drug discovery, appointing its first CSO (David Jenkins) to lead external innovation and partnerships. CEO Charlie Gayer succeeded founding CEO Jon Stonehouse in January 2026. 2026 total revenue guidance raised to $690-715M.

Investment Thesis
BioCryst has transformed from a pre-revenue biotech into a rare disease franchise generating $690-715M in raised 2026 total revenue guidance, with ORLADEYO's Q2 2026 revenue of $158.2M (+10% YoY) and total revenue up 45% YoY excluding the European divestiture [1]. Non-GAAP operating profit of $113.2M in Q2 2026 (up from $60.9M prior year) demonstrates accelerating profitability and operating leverage as the company scales [1]. Forward P/E of ~25.8x on $0.39 consensus EPS provides reasonable valuation for a rare disease franchise with 20%+ comparable revenue growth, 30% 5-year CAGR, and 98% gross margins. The analyst consensus of $21.86 target price implies ~117% upside. ORLADEYO's oral convenience moat, pediatric approval expanding the addressable market, and navenibart adding a second HAE franchise product create durable growth visibility [2].
The primary risk is single-franchise concentration — ORLADEYO represents essentially all commercial revenue, creating vulnerability to competitive entry, patent challenges, or safety events [1]. The closure of internal R&D and shift to external innovation reduces pipeline visibility and makes BioCryst dependent on licensing and M&A for future growth beyond HAE. GAAP profitability remains elusive with approximately -35% TTM net margin due to Astria acquisition costs and stock compensation, and $858M in total debt against -$554M equity represents significant leverage [4]. The CEO transition from founding CEO Stonehouse to Gayer, while orderly, introduces execution risk during the external innovation pivot. If ORLADEYO growth decelerates from the current ~10-20% comparable trajectory, the 25.8x forward multiple could compress significantly. The company's $261M cash position may be insufficient to fund both pipeline expansion and debt service if operating cash flows disappoint.
