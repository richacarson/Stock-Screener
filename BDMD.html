Company Profile

Baird Medical Investment Holdings engages in the research, development, manufacturing, and sales of microwave ablation medical devices. The company offers microwave ablation apparatus and needles for treating benign and malignant tumors including thyroid nodules, liver cancer, lung cancer, and breast lumps. Headquartered in New York City (relocated from China in 2026), with expanding US commercial operations under new US CEO Mark Saxton.

The AI Tumor Ablation Surgical Robot (awarded 'Most Valuable Investment Award' in China) and cross-platform AI surgical software in regulatory registration across US, EU, and China represent genuine innovation that could redefine the company's competitive position [2].
