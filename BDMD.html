Company Profile
Healthcare ·Medical Devices ·NASDAQ ·China
Baird Medical Investment Holdings Limited is a medical technology company specializing in advanced microwave ablation technologies for minimally invasive treatment of solid tumors, primarily benign thyroid nodules. Established in 2012 and headquartered in New York City (relocated from China in June 2026), Baird Medical is the market leader for thyroid microwave ablation devices in China and is expanding its commercial footprint across 20+ countries. The company's FDA-cleared microwave ablation system is being launched in the U.S. for treatment of benign thyroid nodules under newly appointed U.S. CEO Mark Saxton. H1 2026 revenue grew 25% to $9.9M with gross margin of 87% and the company's first positive net income.
