Company Profile

Biodesix develops and commercializes blood-based diagnostic tests for lung disease, combining multi-omics data through its proprietary Diagnostic Cortex AI platform. The company offers eight non-invasive tests including the Nodify Lung nodule risk assessment strategy and the IQLung treatment decision support platform. Q2 2026 revenue reached $26.9M (+34% YoY) at 82% gross margin, following Q1's $25.6M (+42%). FY2026 guidance was raised to $108-114M. The stock has surged 95% over 52 weeks as the company approaches profitability. Based in Louisville, Colorado with 334 employees.

Investment Thesis
Biodesix is at a compelling inflection point — Q2 2026 revenue reached $26.9M (+34% YoY) at 82% gross margin, following Q1's $25.6M (+42%), with FY2026 guidance raised to $108-114M (25% growth at midpoint) [1][2]. The company achieved its first positive Adjusted EBITDA in Q4 2025, and forward EPS of -$0.22 (vs -$3.10 trailing) signals dramatic loss narrowing toward profitability. The proprietary Diagnostic Cortex AI platform and eight non-invasive lung disease tests create a defensible market position, strengthened by the 1,100-patient Nodify CDT validation study (91-97% specificity) [4]. Director Jack Schuler's $2.08M in share purchases and 47% insider ownership signal strong conviction [5]. At $308M market cap, the stock has nearly doubled in 52 weeks as the market re-rates from 'pre-profit biotech' to 'approaching-profitability AI diagnostics,' with significant additional upside if BDSX achieves and sustains profitability.
The bear case centers on financial fragility, valuation, and competitive risk. D/E of 785% reflects $71.8M debt against deeply negative equity, and cash of $30M provides limited runway — the company may need dilutive equity raising despite improving operations [6]. Profit margin of -27% means BDSX is still burning cash at a meaningful rate, and the stock at $29.20 (price-to-book of 33.7x) prices in substantial future profitability that has not yet materialized. Average daily volume of just 98K shares creates micro-cap illiquidity risk and limits institutional access. Competition from larger diagnostic companies (Guardant Health at $8B+ market cap, Foundation Medicine backed by Roche) with deeper pockets, established oncology relationships, and broader liquid biopsy platforms could pressure Biodesix's market position as they expand into lung diagnostics. Reimbursement risk is real — changes in Medicare/insurance coverage for blood-based diagnostics could slow revenue growth. The deceleration from 42% Q1 growth to 34% Q2 growth may signal normalizing growth rates as revenue scales past $100M.
