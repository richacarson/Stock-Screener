Company Profile
Healthcare ·Health Information Services ·NASDAQ ·United States
HeartBeam develops a credit-card-sized, 3D ECG signal-acquisition platform designed to detect and monitor cardiac disease, including heart attacks, outside the hospital, with FDA clearance for arrhythmia assessment (December 2024) and 12-lead ECG synthesis software (December 2025) [1]. Founder and President Branislav Vajdic, Ph.D. became principal executive officer after CEO Robert Eno's departure in June 2026, coinciding with a strategic pivot toward licensing the 3D ECG platform to established partners rather than pursuing direct device sales [2]. The company is pre-revenue, initiating a limited 2026 commercial introduction with concierge and preventive cardiology groups while running the ALIGN ACS pilot study assessing heart-attack detection in emergency department patients in Belgrade, Serbia [1][2].

Investment Thesis
HeartBeam holds genuine technical differentiation -- a credit-card-sized 3D ECG platform with two FDA clearances (arrhythmia assessment and 12-lead synthesis) targeting the large, underserved out-of-hospital heart-attack detection market [1]. The June 2026 pivot to a licensing model, rather than capital-intensive direct sales, is a rational response to a difficult financing environment for small-cap medtech and could materially reduce cash burn while still monetizing the core IP through established partners [2]. Positive Inspire Insight attributions for Customer Welfare and Product Access and Affordability align with the company's core mission of expanding access to life-saving cardiac diagnostics.
The bear case is dominated by financing risk and execution uncertainty. With $4.4 million in cash against $17-19 million in projected 2026 cash outflows, HeartBeam will almost certainly need additional dilutive financing, and average daily dollar volume of roughly $274,000 fails the liquidity floor, making the stock difficult to trade in size [1][3]. The June 2026 CEO departure occurred at a critical commercialization juncture, and the shift to licensing is unproven -- no partner agreements have been disclosed. Zero revenue through Q1 2026, a -328% ROE, and a negative forward P/E underscore that this remains a speculative, pre-revenue story dependent on both clinical validation (ALIGN ACS) and successful partner monetization [1][3].
Key Catalysts
  1. Successful completion and positive results from the ALIGN ACS pilot study (120 ED patients in Belgrade) could provide the clinical validation needed to attract a licensing partner for heart-attack detection [2]
  2. Execution of the strategic pivot to licensing the 3D ECG platform to an established medical device or diagnostics partner would meaningfully reduce cash burn and de-risk the balance sheet [2]
  3. Early real-world performance data from the 2026 concierge and preventive cardiology launch partnerships could validate the platform ahead of broader commercialization [1]
Key Risks
  1. Cash of $4.4 million against $17-19 million in projected 2026 operating outflows creates near-certain need for additional dilutive financing, which could severely pressure the already-depressed $0.41 share price [1]
