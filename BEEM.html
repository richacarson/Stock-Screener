Company Profile
Technology ·Solar ·NASDAQ ·United States
Beam Global designs, develops, manufactures, and sells renewably energized infrastructure products and battery solutions including EV ARC solar-powered autonomous EV charging stations, BeamBike solar e-bike chargers, AllCell energy storage systems, drone battery systems, and wildfire detection technologies. The company pivoted from 62% government revenue (2024) to 78% commercial revenue (Q1 2026) after federal fleet electrification orders ceased under the new administration. FY2025 revenue declined 42.8% to $28.2M but Q2 2026 showed 21% YoY growth and 174% sequential improvement. Headquartered in San Diego, deployed in 30+ cities across five countries. Market cap ~$30M.

Investment Thesis
Beam Global is showing meaningful revenue recovery and commercial diversification after its devastating 2025. Q2 2026 revenue grew 21% YoY and 174% sequentially with 26% gross margin, while Q1 2026 saw 78% of revenues from nongovernment commercial entities (up 48% YoY) [1]. Revenue is now generated across six product categories — drone batteries, defense/military, AI robotics, wildfire detection, energy storage, and EV charging — deployed in 30+ cities across five countries [1]. CEO Wheatley's 15-year leadership tenure and deep patent portfolio in autonomous solar infrastructure provide differentiated positioning. At $30M market cap with minimal debt (D/E 9.47%), Beam offers high-risk exposure to the clean mobility infrastructure buildout. The Inspire Impact Score of 71 (ALIGNED) with zero negative attributions and 17 positive ESG categories makes BEEM one of the highest-scoring faith alignment stocks in the universe, with a business model inherently tied to clean energy and sustainability.
The bear case remains severe despite the liquidity improvement. FY2025 posted a net loss roughly matching its $28.2M revenue, and Q1 2026 revenue of $3.1M (down 51% YoY) with $6.9M net loss shows the burn rate remains unsustainable absent continued revenue acceleration [2]. Profit margin of -64.6% and ROE of -72.69% reflect a company far from self-sustaining operations [4]. Even with Q2's strong sequential improvement, the company needs sustained quarterly revenue above $10M with positive margins to approach breakeven. Cash reserves remain minimal — a $30M market cap company with heavy losses faces existential dilution risk from any capital raise. Competition from well-capitalized EV charging players (ChargePoint, Blink, Tesla) with broader product portfolios and deeper customer relationships threatens to marginalize Beam's niche position. The stock at $1.32 remains near penny stock territory, limiting institutional participation, and daily dollar volume of $1.49M barely clears the liquidity floor.
