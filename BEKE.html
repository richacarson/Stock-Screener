KE Holdings Inc. (Beike) is China's largest integrated online and offline platform for housing transactions and services, operating through Lianjia (owned brokerage), Beike (open platform connecting agents and developers), and expanding home renovation/furnishing and rental services. Founded in 2001 by Zuo Hui, headquartered in Beijing, led by co-founder CEO Stanley Yongdong Peng. Q2 2026 revenue was RMB24.5B (-5.7% YoY) while net income doubled to RMB2.6B on cost optimization and margin improvement, with existing home GTV rising 8% YoY. FY2025 revenue RMB94.6B. Dual-listed on NYSE and HKEX.

Investment Thesis
KE Holdings at $18.07 with a $19.8B market cap offers compelling value at 14.3x forward earnings with a PEG of 0.42 for China's dominant housing platform, which processed RMB888B in combined GTV in Q2 2026 with existing home transactions growing 8% YoY [1]. The profitability transformation is the key narrative — net income doubled in Q2 2026 to RMB2.6B with adjusted operating margin reaching a 3-year high of 14.6% and adjusted net margin of 13%, demonstrating structural operating leverage through cost optimization and AI-driven efficiency gains (60% of rental tasks automated) [1][3]. The fortress balance sheet with D/E of just 22% and RMB47B in cash exceeding total debt provides resilience through China's property cycle. KE Holdings' platform model with 45,000+ connected stores, 107K employees, and expanding home renovation/rental diversification creates structural moats from network effects.
The fundamental risk is China's structural property market headwinds. Revenue declined 19% in Q1 and 5.7% in Q2 2026, and while management attributes this partly to revenue recognition changes in rental/renovation services, the pattern reflects underlying demand uncertainty in China's real estate market [1][2]. The 5-year revenue CAGR of approximately 5% reflects a property sector that may face secular decline as China's demographics shift (aging population, urbanization saturation). As a China-domiciled ADR, BEKE carries geopolitical and regulatory risk including potential delisting concerns and VIE structure vulnerability — a Muddy Waters short report in 2021 alleged inflated metrics, triggering a securities class action [4]. Profit margin of 5.32% is thin for a technology platform, and ROE of 7.01% indicates modest returns on equity. Founder Zuo Hui's 2021 death removed the visionary leader, and current leadership has demonstrated effective cost management but not yet proven it can drive the next phase of growth innovation.
