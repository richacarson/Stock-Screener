Company Profile
Utilities ·Utilities - Renewable ·NYSE ·United States
Brookfield Renewable Corporation owns and operates one of the world's largest publicly traded portfolios of renewable power and sustainable solution assets, with approximately 13,396 MW of installed capacity across hydroelectric, wind, utility-scale solar, distributed generation, pumped storage, carbon capture and storage, cogeneration, biomass, and eFuels. Incorporated in 2019 as a subsidiary of Brookfield Renewable Partners L.P. (managed by Brookfield Asset Management), the company operates across North America, South America, and Europe. Under CEO Connor Teskey, Brookfield Renewable reported record 2025 results with FFO per unit of $2.01 (+10% YoY), commissioned a record 8,000 MW of new capacity, signed a 3,000 MW hydro deal with Google, and launched a $100B AI Infrastructure Program backed by Nvidia and the Kuwait Investment Authority.
