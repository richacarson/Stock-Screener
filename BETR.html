Company Profile
Financial Services ·Mortgage Finance ·NASDAQ ·United States
Better Home & Finance is a digital mortgage origination platform offering home loans, refinancing, and insurance products through a technology-driven, largely online process. Founded by Vishal Garg in 2016, the company went public via SPAC in 2023 after a deeply troubled IPO that saw shares drop 93% on the first day. Garg was removed as CEO on August 3, 2026 by unanimous board action, replaced by interim CEO Daniel Lewis, following years of governance controversy including the viral 2021 Zoom layoff of 900 employees and Garg calling employees 'dumb dolphins' and 'mortgage monkeys.' The company is now facing a proxy fight with a filing to remove 5 of 8 directors, and Holzer & Holzer has announced an investor investigation. Q2 2026 revenue was $54.7M with a loss of $1.12/share, and the stock has declined over 90% since its 2023 SPAC listing. Market cap is approximately $222M.

Investment Thesis
Better's bull case rests on a cyclical recovery play — if mortgage rates decline meaningfully, Better's digital-first platform could capture disproportionate refinancing volume with its low-cost, fully online origination model. Q1 2026 loan volume of $1.64B (+89% YoY) and Q2 revenue of $54.7M show the business recovering from its 2023 trough [1]. Analyst consensus is 'Buy' with a $25.67 price target (+89% upside), reflecting the view that Better is deeply undervalued relative to a normalized rate environment. The removal of Vishal Garg as CEO in August 2026 could be a governance inflection point — new leadership under interim CEO Daniel Lewis may restore institutional credibility and employee morale. At $222M market cap, the stock prices in near-terminal risk, meaning any positive development in rates, governance, or profitability could drive substantial upside.
The bear case is overwhelming and multifaceted. Better has accumulated over $1.5B in net losses, the stock has declined over 90% since its SPAC listing, and the company continues to generate deeply negative operating cash flow (-$156M TTM) [4]. The governance situation is chaotic — Garg was removed as CEO, a proxy fight seeks to remove 5 of 8 directors, and Holzer & Holzer has announced an investor investigation [3]. D/E of 1143% and ROE of -250% reflect a balance sheet in severe distress, with $662M in debt against only $106M in cash. Revenue growth of ~40% is from a base that represents a 95% decline from 2021 peak levels — 'recovery' is misleading when the company generates $193M in annual revenue compared to $1.6B three years ago. The digital mortgage space has become crowded with well-capitalized competitors (Rocket Mortgage, traditional banks deploying AI), and Better has no demonstrated technological moat. Q3 guidance of $49-52M revenue and -$18M to -$15M adjusted EBITDA shows the company remains unprofitable with no clear path to breakeven. The Infinite Game score of 1 (FINITE) reflects a company with no organizational trust, no cause-driven leadership, and no strategic flexibility — a distressed asset rather than an enduring business.
