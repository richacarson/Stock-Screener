Company Profile

Bluerock Homes Trust is an externally managed REIT that owns and operates a portfolio of institutional residential properties including single-family homes, build-to-rent communities, and other residential communities in Sunbelt and Western U.S. knowledge-economy markets. Formed in December 2021 and incorporated in Maryland, the company is managed by Bluerock Homes Manager, LLC. Q2 2026 rental revenues were approximately $19.3M (~15% YoY growth), and TTM revenue reached $83.1M. The company remains unprofitable with a TTM net loss of approximately -$13.6M. Market cap is approximately $33M.
