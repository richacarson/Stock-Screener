Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NYSE ·United States
Biohaven Ltd. is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on discovering and developing therapies for neurological, immunological, and other diseases. Key pipeline assets include taldefgrobep alfa (myostatin inhibitor for obesity), opakalim (Kv7 activator for epilepsy with Phase 2/3 RISE3 topline results expected H2 2026), BHV-1300 (Graves' disease, Phase 3 initiated), and BHV-1400 (IgA nephropathy). Spun off from original Biohaven after Pfizer's $11.6B acquisition in 2022. Pre-revenue with Q2 2026 net loss of $137.3M and $270.5M cash. Led by CEO Vlad Coric, M.D.
400 Employees ·www.biohaven.com

Investment Thesis
Biohaven offers a high-risk, high-reward pipeline play with multiple H2 2026 clinical catalysts that could significantly re-rate the stock from $15.95. Opakalim Phase 2/3 RISE3 pivotal topline results in epilepsy (selective Kv7 activator with differentiated mechanism), BHV-1300 Phase 3 initiation in Graves' disease, and taldefgrobep Phase 2 obesity data (targeting high-quality weight loss with lean muscle preservation) represent binary events with significant upside potential [1][2]. Q2 2026 net loss narrowed 31% YoY to $137.3M from $198.1M, showing improving cost discipline [1]. The analyst consensus price target implies meaningful upside, and the decentralized innovation culture under CEO Coric has rebuilt a competitive pipeline just 3-4 years after the Pfizer spin-off. Inspire Impact Score of 26 (ALIGNED) with zero negative attributions reflects responsible pharma practices.
The fundamental challenge is existential: Biohaven has $270.5M in cash at June 30, 2026 against quarterly R&D expenses of $100.8M and an H1 2026 total loss of $267.8M — implying approximately 2 quarters of runway without dilutive capital raises [1][2]. The D/E of 221% reflects accumulated losses that have severely eroded equity, and ROE of approximately -333% underscores the depth of the pre-commercial cash burn. The troriluzole FDA Complete Response Letter eliminated the most advanced pipeline program, and the obesity pivot places Biohaven against Lilly and Novo Nordisk — incumbents with vastly superior commercial infrastructure and financial resources [4]. The binary nature of H2 2026 catalysts means negative data from opakalim or taldefgrobep could trigger severe declines in a $2.3B market cap company with zero revenue cushion. Even with positive data, commercialization will require years and significant additional capital.
Key Catalysts
  1. Opakalim Phase 2/3 RISE3 pivotal topline results in H2 2026 for drug-resistant epilepsy — positive data would validate a differentiated Kv7 mechanism and create a path to the first novel anti-seizure mechanism in over a decade [1][2]
  2. BHV-1300 Phase 3 initiated in Graves' disease using novel IgG MoDE degrader platform, expanding the extracellular protein degrader franchise into autoimmune indications with high unmet need [1]
  3. Taldefgrobep Phase 2 obesity data in H2 2026 — myostatin inhibitor targeting fat reduction plus lean muscle preservation could differentiate against GLP-1 agonists that cause muscle wasting [2]
Key Risks
  1. Cash runway of approximately 2 quarters ($270.5M cash against ~$134M quarterly burn) makes dilutive equity raises imminent, likely compressing per-share value at a critical juncture before pivotal data readouts [1][2]
  2. Binary clinical risk across all H2 2026 catalysts — negative opakalim or taldefgrobep data would eliminate key value drivers with zero commercial revenue to cushion the impact, in a company with $2.3B market cap
  3. Obesity market competition against Lilly's tirzepatide and Novo Nordisk's semaglutide incumbents with $50B+ combined commercial infrastructure makes taldefgrobep commercialization path uncertain even with positive Phase 2 data [4]
