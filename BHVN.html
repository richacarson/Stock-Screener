Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NYSE ·United States
Biohaven Ltd. is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on discovering, developing, and commercializing therapies for neurological, immunological, and other diseases. Key pipeline assets include taldefgrobep alfa (myostatin inhibitor for obesity), opakalim (Kv7 activator for epilepsy), BHV-1400 (Gd-IgA1 TRAP degrader for IgA nephropathy), and BHV-1300 (IgG MoDE degrader for Graves' disease). Spun off from the original Biohaven Pharmaceutical after Pfizer acquired the migraine franchise for $11.6 billion in 2022. Led by Chairman and CEO Vlad Coric, M.D.
400 Employees ·www.biohaven.com

Investment Thesis
Biohaven offers a high-risk, high-reward pipeline play with multiple H2 2026 clinical catalysts that could significantly re-rate the stock from its current $16.90 level. Taldefgrobep Phase 2 obesity data (myostatin-activin pathway inhibitor targeting high-quality weight loss with lean muscle preservation) and opakalim pivotal epilepsy data represent binary events with significant upside if positive, and the BHV-1400 (IgA nephropathy) and BHV-1300 (Graves' disease) pivotal initiations add depth [2]. The analyst consensus price target of $21.76 implies 29% upside, and the $347.8M cash position with net cash of $61.6M provides near-term funding. The stock's beta of 3.31 amplifies upside potential in a positive data scenario.
The fundamental challenge is that Biohaven is a pre-revenue, deeply loss-making clinical-stage biotech burning $594M annually in operating cash flow with only $347.8M in cash — funding sufficiency beyond 12-18 months is uncertain without additional dilutive capital raises [5]. The troriluzole FDA Complete Response Letter in November 2025 represents a material clinical setback that eliminated the company's most advanced program, and the pivot to obesity places Biohaven in direct competition with Lilly's tirzepatide and Novo Nordisk's semaglutide — entrenched incumbents with massive commercial infrastructure [4]. Infrastructure scores reflect the reality of a pre-commercial company: ROE of -333%, no profit margin, and EPS of -$5.57. The binary nature of the H2 2026 catalysts means negative data from taldefgrobep or opakalim could result in severe stock price declines given the 3.31 beta and limited revenue diversification.
Key Catalysts
  1. Taldefgrobep Phase 2 obesity topline data in H2 2026 evaluating high-quality weight loss (fat mass reduction plus lean muscle preservation) via once-weekly or once-monthly autoinjector delivery
  2. Opakalim pivotal epilepsy trial topline results in H2 2026 as a selective Kv7 ion channel activator with a differentiated mechanism from existing anti-seizure medications
  3. BHV-1400 and BHV-1300 pivotal trial initiations by mid-2026 in IgA nephropathy and Graves' disease respectively, expanding the clinical pipeline beyond neuroscience
Key Risks
  1. Cash burn of $594M annually against $347.8M in cash creates funding risk beyond 12-18 months, likely requiring dilutive equity raises that would compress per-share value
  2. Troriluzole FDA Complete Response Letter in November 2025 eliminated the most advanced pipeline program, and the obesity pivot places Biohaven against Lilly and Novo Nordisk incumbents with vastly superior commercial and financial resources
  3. Binary clinical risk across all H2 2026 catalysts — negative data from taldefgrobep or opakalim would eliminate key value drivers with no commercial revenue to cushion the impact
