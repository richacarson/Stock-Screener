Company Profile
Healthcare ·Diagnostics & Research ·NMS ·United States
bioAffinity Technologies develops non-invasive diagnostic tests for the early detection of lung cancer and other lung diseases. The company's lead product, CyPath Lung, uses flow cytometry to detect cancer-associated changes in lung cells collected from sputum samples. Headquartered in San Antonio, Texas, the company generated approximately $6.2M in revenue in FY2025, a 34% decline from $9.4M in FY2024, and carries a market capitalization of approximately $4.7M.

Investment Thesis
bioAffinity Technologies at $3.62 with a $4.7M market cap is a micro-cap diagnostics company with a single commercial product — CyPath Lung — that has failed to gain commercial traction, with FY2025 revenue declining 34% to $6.2M and Q1 FY2026 missing consensus at $1.35M vs $1.5M expected [1][4]. The bull case rests on the clinical validity of non-invasive lung cancer screening as an alternative to invasive biopsy, pipeline expansion into asthma and COPD companion diagnostics, and the fundamental unmet medical need for early lung cancer detection. The company's low D/E of 38% provides balance sheet flexibility, and flow cytometry-based testing occupies a regulatory-moated niche [1][2].
The bear case is severe: revenue is declining, the company is deeply unprofitable with -257% profit margin, and the $3.2M dilutive offering in June 2026 signals ongoing cash burn with no path to profitability visible [1][3]. The $4.7M market cap places bioAffinity below institutional investment thresholds, limiting access to capital and analyst coverage. Competing diagnostic approaches — AI-powered CT scan analysis, liquid biopsy from Guardant and Grail, and Exact Sciences' multi-cancer early detection platform — are better-funded and further along in clinical validation. The company's single-product dependency and micro-cap scale create existential risk if CyPath Lung fails to achieve commercial adoption, and leadership changes including CFO turnover add operational uncertainty [2].
Key Catalysts
  1. Potential FDA clearance for CyPath Lung could expand the addressable market beyond CLIA-regulated lab testing and enable broader commercial distribution through hospital systems and integrated diagnostic networks [1]
  2. Pipeline expansion into asthma and COPD companion diagnostics could diversify revenue beyond lung cancer screening, leveraging the existing flow cytometry platform for multiple respiratory conditions [2]
  3. Growing emphasis on lung cancer screening following expanded CMS coverage guidelines for low-dose CT creates a broader market context that could benefit non-invasive alternatives like CyPath Lung
Key Risks
  1. Revenue declining 34% YoY to $6.2M in FY2025 with -257% profit margin and ongoing dilutive capital raises ($3.2M offering June 2026) indicate unsustainable cash burn and existential funding risk for a $4.7M market cap company [1][3]
  2. Competition from well-funded AI-powered diagnostics platforms (Guardant Health liquid biopsy, Exact Sciences multi-cancer detection, AI-enhanced CT screening) that offer potentially superior convenience and accuracy versus sputum-based flow cytometry
  3. Single-product dependency on CyPath Lung with no diversified revenue base — any clinical, regulatory, or commercial setback to this one product would eliminate the company's entire value proposition [1]
