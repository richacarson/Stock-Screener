Company Profile
Healthcare ·Diagnostics & Research ·NASDAQ ·United States
bioAffinity Technologies develops non-invasive diagnostic tests to detect early-stage lung cancer using proprietary flow cytometry and artificial intelligence. Its flagship product, CyPath Lung, uses a fluorescent porphyrin preferentially absorbed by cancer cells combined with AI-driven analysis to identify malignancy in patient sputum samples. Q2 2026 test volume surged 216% YoY with 1,097 tests delivered in H1 2026. The company is expanding into companion diagnostics for asthma and COPD, and developing gene silencing therapeutics. Headquartered in San Antonio, Texas. Market cap $3.24M, facing Nasdaq delisting risk.
57 Employees ·bioaffinitytech.com

Investment Thesis
bioAffinity Technologies is a mission-driven, AI-enabled diagnostics company with a differentiated product in CyPath Lung — a non-invasive sputum test with 92% sensitivity and 87% specificity for detecting early-stage lung cancer in patients with indeterminate pulmonary nodules [3]. Commercial traction is accelerating: Q2 2026 test volume surged 216% YoY, CyPath Lung testing revenue grew 159% in H1 2026 to $835K, ordering physician locations increased 122%, and existing client orders grew 71% [1]. The addressable market is large and AI-driven: 2.9M indeterminate nodules detected annually in the U.S., projected to reach 4.7M by 2030 as AI-enhanced CT screening expands, representing an estimated $4.7B opportunity [1]. The 2,000-patient longitudinal study across 17 medical centers could provide the clinical evidence needed for guideline inclusion and broad payer coverage. Pipeline expansion into asthma/COPD diagnostics and gene silencing therapeutics leverages the core flow cytometry and AI platform [3].
The bear case is existential survival risk across every dimension. bioAffinity has a $3.24M market cap, $2.43M cash (down from $6.45M six months earlier), going concern disclosure, and $15.19M annual net loss against only $5.9M TTM revenue [5]. The stock has collapsed 94.65% over the past year to $0.40, and Nasdaq has notified the company that it is out of compliance with the $1.00 minimum bid price requirement — having already executed a 1-for-30 reverse stock split in September 2025, the company is ineligible for the standard 180-day grace period and faces potential delisting [6]. The June 2026 public offering raised $3.2M in gross proceeds but further diluted shareholders (shares outstanding grew from 4.5M to 8.1M in H1 2026). Total company revenue actually declined 8% in H1 2026 despite CyPath Lung's growth because discontinued pathology revenue disappeared. CyPath Lung alone generates only ~$1.67M annualized testing revenue — grossly insufficient to cover the $11M+ annual cash burn. Without rapid payer coverage expansion, dramatic volume scaling, and additional capital, insolvency is a near-term risk. The Altman Z-Score of -17.01 is among the worst in any public company. AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
8
AI Risk Protection (7/9): bioAffinity's diagnostic business is structurally immune to AI disruption in its core value chain. CyPath Lung requires physical patient sputum samples processed through laboratory-grade flow cytometry equipment — AI cannot replace the specimen collection, processing, and cell analysis that form the product's foundation [3]. The diagnostic operates in a regulated environment requiring CLIA laboratory certification and clinical validation studies, creating high barriers. AI actually expands the addressable market: AI-improved CT imaging detects more indeterminate pulmonary nodules requiring further workup, growing the U.S. market from 2.9M nodules annually to a projected 4.7M by 2030 — an estimated $4.7B opportunity for CyPath Lung [1]. The physical sample requirement, regulatory barriers, and proprietary training data create meaningful AI disruption protection. AI Opportunity (1/1): AI is integral to CyPath Lung's diagnostic algorithm — proprietary machine learning models analyze flow cytometry data to identify cancer cell populations with 92% sensitivity [3]. The company is an AI-enabled healthcare product at its core, and the same AI platform is being extended to asthma/COPD companion diagnostics, demonstrating technology leverage across disease areas.
