Company Profile
Communication Services ·Internet Content & Information ·NASDAQ ·China
Baidu is China's dominant search engine with 80%+ market share and the country's leading AI company. Under CEO Robin Li, the company has pivoted aggressively toward artificial intelligence, investing $16.4B+ cumulatively in AI R&D. The company operates Baidu Core (search, AI Cloud, ERNIE large language model) and iQIYI (streaming). Q2 2026 revenue of RMB 31.3B was down 4% YoY as search advertising erosion outpaces AI growth, but AI Cloud Infra revenue grew 50% YoY and GPU Cloud surged 283%. Finite vs Infinite Game — Sinek (25%)
Mindset: MIXED Overall: 5 /10
Baidu under Robin Li demonstrates a mix of infinite and finite game characteristics. The company has shown impressive existential flexibility in pivoting from search to AI, and Li's willingness to invest $16.4B+ in AI R&D before the market validated the thesis reflects genuine courage. However, the top-down innovation mandate, declining workforce, growing financial losses, and mission framed around competitive positioning rather than a Just Cause limit the infinite game score. Revenue declining 4% while AI grows 50%+ creates a painful but potentially necessary transition.

Investment Thesis
Baidu's AI transformation is generating extraordinary growth within the company: GPU Cloud revenue surged 283% YoY in Q2 2026 (accelerating from 184% in Q1), AI Cloud Infra grew 50%, and Core AI-powered business revenue of RMB 12.5B now accounts for half of total revenue [1][2]. At forward P/E of 14.25x, $32B market cap, and $7.85B net cash ($23.16/share), Baidu trades at a significant discount to global AI peers — the market effectively values the AI business at roughly $24B net of cash, a fraction of what comparable US AI infrastructure companies command [5]. Robin Li's vision of the shift from model competition to AI agent competition positions Baidu to benefit from the next phase of enterprise AI adoption in China. Apollo Go autonomous driving at commercial scale represents additional optionality not reflected in current valuation.
However, Baidu's total revenue declined 4% YoY in Q2 2026 (worsening from -1% in Q1), demonstrating that AI Cloud growth cannot yet offset accelerating search advertising erosion [1][2]. The company swung to a net loss of $679M TTM with -2.90% profit margin and -1.39% ROE — a significant deterioration from the already thin 1% margins six months ago [5]. The 5-year revenue CAGR of 3.8% is deeply disappointing for a technology company, and the PEG of 2.30 reflects market skepticism about profitability recovery. DeepSeek, Alibaba Cloud, and ByteDance are rapidly closing the AI capability gap, threatening pricing power and early-mover advantage. China-specific risks remain elevated: content censorship requirements (60,000+ unique guidelines), data security regulations, VIE structure uncertainty, US-China geopolitical tensions, and potential ADR delisting concerns create a structural valuation discount that may persist indefinitely.
