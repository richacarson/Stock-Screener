Company Profile
Financial Services ·Asset Management & Custody Banks ·NYSE ·United States
The Bank of New York Mellon Corporation (BNY), founded in 1784 by Alexander Hamilton, is the world's largest custodian bank and now trades on the NYSE under the ticker BNY (formerly BK). It provides custody, asset servicing, clearing, collateral management, treasury services, investment management and wealth management to institutional clients worldwide. Q3 2026 total revenue rose 12% to $5.68B, fee revenue rose 12% to $4.08B, net interest income rose 15% to $1.42B and diluted EPS rose 24% to $2.34, with ROTCE of 29.0% and assets under custody/administration of $63.4 trillion (+10%). CEO Robin Vince's platform operating model and Eliza enterprise AI platform, with about 140 'digital employees', anchor the transformation.

Investment Thesis
BNY is compounding at an unusual rate for a mega-cap custodian. Q3 2026 total revenue grew 12% to $5.68B, fee revenue grew 12%, net interest income grew 15%, and diluted EPS rose 24% to $2.34, with ROTCE of 29.0%, a 38.5% pre-tax margin and 389bps of positive operating leverage [1]. AUC/A reached $63.4 trillion (+10%), and the platform operating model plus the Eliza AI program (about 140 digital employees, roughly 220 AI solutions in development) are lifting margins structurally [1][3]. At 14.1x forward earnings and a 0.58 forward PEG, with a 19% dividend increase and $937M of quarterly buybacks, investors pay a modest price for double-digit earnings growth [1][6][7].
Risks include rate sensitivity: net interest income (+15%) would reverse if rates fall faster or deposit mix shifts, and fee growth is tied to market levels and AUM, where growth was only 2% to $2.2 trillion [1]. D/E of 152% and a capital return of 86% of earnings year-to-date leave less of a buffer in market stress [1][7]. The 5yr revenue CAGR of about 5% shows that the recent acceleration is new and needs to persist [7]. AI-driven efficiency could eventually be passed to clients through fee compression, and recurring severance points to ongoing workforce reshaping [1]. The 2022 SEC ESG-misstatement settlement ($1.5M) is a governance reminder [5]. Key Catalysts
  1. Continued positive operating leverage from the platform operating model and the Eliza AI program (389bps in Q3 2026) driving pre-tax margin expansion toward 40%+ [1][3]
  2. AUC/A growth ($63.4T, +10%) and new platform services such as digital-asset custody and collateral driving fee revenue growth of 10%+ [1]
  3. Capital return of 86% of earnings year-to-date, including $937M of Q3 buybacks and a 19% dividend increase to $0.63 quarterly, reducing share count and supporting EPS [1]
Key Risks
  1. Interest-rate and deposit sensitivity: a faster easing cycle would compress net interest income, which grew 15% in Q3 2026 and helped drive EPS growth [1]
  2. Fee compression and competition from State Street, JPMorgan and tech-enabled custody platforms as AI lowers the cost of servicing and clients seek to share the savings [3]
  3. Operational, cyber or AI-governance failure at a systemically important custodian holding $63.4T, with heavy regulatory and reputational consequences [1][5]
