Company Profile
Industrials ·Aerospace & Defense ·NYSE ·United States
BlackSky Technology Inc. is a real-time geospatial intelligence and global monitoring company. It owns and operates a constellation of high-revisit, high-resolution small satellites and an AI-enabled analytics platform (Spectra) that provides real-time intelligence to government defense and intelligence agencies and commercial customers. The Gen-3 satellite constellation integrates on-board AI processing for automated target recognition, change detection, and battle damage assessment at 35cm resolution. Q2 2026 revenue reached $33.3M (+50% YoY) with first positive quarterly adjusted EBITDA of $4.7M. Headquartered in Herndon, Virginia.
321 Employees ·www.blacksky.com

Investment Thesis
BlackSky is at a genuine inflection point — Q2 2026 revenue surged 50% YoY to $33.3M with first positive quarterly adjusted EBITDA of $4.7M, while Space-Based Intelligence & AI Services reached a $100M annual run rate [1]. FY2026 guidance of $130-150M revenue (+30% at midpoint) with $12-24M adjusted EBITDA signals the transition from pre-profit buildout to profitable scaling. The Gen-3 constellation integrating on-board AI processing at 35cm resolution is driving accelerating customer adoption, with international revenue up 150% YoY and multi-year contracts representing 85% of funded backlog [1][2]. The NRO eight-figure AROS contract validates BlackSky as a critical commercial alternative for foundation imagery. The balance sheet has strengthened considerably — $234M cash against $221M debt and D/E improved to 105% — reducing dilution risk. At $887M market cap, the stock trades at ~6x FY2026 revenue guidance midpoint, reasonable for a 50% growth AI-defense platform approaching profitability.
The bear case centers on profitability durability, government concentration, and capital intensity. GAAP profit margin of -61% and ROE of -45% reflect a company still burning significant cash despite the adjusted EBITDA inflection — free cash flow was -$71M TTM, and the $234M cash position could be consumed within 3 years at current burn rates if GAAP profitability doesn't materialize [5]. The dramatic back-half revenue acceleration needed to hit $130-150M guidance (Q1 was $20.8M, Q2 $33.3M) raises execution risk, as Gen-3 deployment must proceed on schedule and contract conversions must close. Government budget uncertainty under evolving defense priorities, concentration risk in a small number of large contracts, and competition from larger primes (Maxar, Planet Labs) with deeper resources represent meaningful downside risks. The stock has risen significantly from its SPAC-era lows and the $887M market cap prices in substantial continued growth and margin expansion that remains unproven beyond a single quarter of positive adjusted EBITDA.
