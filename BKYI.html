Company Profile

BIO-key International develops and markets identity and access management (IAM) solutions and biometric authentication technologies. Products include the PortalGuard IAM platform, Identity-Bound Biometrics (IBB), and Passkey:YOU passwordless authentication. The company was delisted from Nasdaq in May 2026 due to non-compliance with filing requirements and bid price rules, and now trades on OTC Markets. Headquartered in Holmdel, New Jersey.

Investment Thesis
BIO-key is attempting a turnaround with Q1 2026 revenue growing 34% to $2.15M and the company targeting Q2 profitability, with H1 2026 revenue projected at $5M versus $3.3M in H1 2025 [1]. The strategic shift from lower-margin third-party software to core PortalGuard IAM and identity-bound biometrics products is improving unit economics, and a $280K Defense Ministry contract signals potential government vertical expansion [1]. With only $664K in debt against $7.4M equity and $2.2M cash, the balance sheet provides some runway. The passwordless authentication market is growing as enterprises adopt Zero Trust security frameworks.
The bear case is severe: BIO-key was delisted from Nasdaq in May 2026 for failing to meet bid price and filing compliance requirements, destroying institutional investor access and credibility [4]. FY2025 revenue of $6.1M was down 12% with a net loss of $4.59M, and the company has only 52 employees competing against Microsoft, Okta, and CyberArk — companies with thousands of engineers and billions in R&D budgets. The 1-for-10 reverse stock split reduced shares to 1.08M, creating severe illiquidity on OTC Markets with estimated daily traded value below $1M. Even if the turnaround succeeds, BIO-key operates in a market where scale determines winner-take-most outcomes in enterprise software [3].
