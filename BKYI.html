Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Infrastructure ·NASDAQ ·United States
BIO-key International sells biometric and passwordless identity and access management (IAM) software, including the PortalGuard IAM platform, MobileAuth (PalmPositive, FacePositive), and Passkey:YOU FIDO2 authentication, plus fingerprint hardware. Its customers are mainly government, defense, and financial institutions [1][2]. Q2 2026 revenue rose 13% to $1.92M (1H 2026 +23% to $4.07M). The company did a 1-for-10 reverse split on April 30, 2026 and regained Nasdaq compliance in July after being flagged for late filings [1][3]. Market cap is about $5.5M [5].

Investment Thesis
The bull case is operating leverage in a niche with tailwinds. 1H 2026 revenue rose 23% to $4.07M, the mix shift to licenses lifted gross margin to 87%, and the 1H net loss improved 59% to $(782K) [1]. National-scale wins (Central Bank of Jordan, a Portuguese national security agency, a Saudi education partnership) and an ~$0.8M hardware order slipped into 2H 2026 support management's target of growth and profitability in the second half [1]. AI-driven phishing and deepfakes are raising demand for phishing-resistant, passwordless authentication, and the ~$2.5M warrant inducement lifted cash above $4.5M [1][2].
The bear case is scale, governance, and dilution. Revenue of about $8M a year has declined at a -5.4% derived 3-year CAGR, ROE is -107.9%, and profit margin is -90.1% [5]. In 2026 the company missed its 10-K and Q1 10-Q deadlines, received multiple Nasdaq delinquency and bid-price notices, faced a delisting hearing, and needed a 1-for-10 reverse split to stay listed [1][3]. Q2 revenue came in about 30% below the ~$2.8M guidance, and repeated warrant-based financing dilutes holders of a company with about a $5.5M market cap [1][5]. Much larger platform IAM vendors (Microsoft, Okta, Ping) bundle passkeys and biometrics, which limits pricing power [2].
