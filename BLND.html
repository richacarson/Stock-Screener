Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NYSE ·United States
Blend Labs provides cloud-based origination software that powers the digital interface between banks, credit unions and mortgage lenders and their consumers, covering mortgages, home equity, personal loans, credit cards and deposit accounts. Its Autopilot agentic-AI orchestration layer automates lending workflows. Founded in 2012 by CEO Nima Ghamsari, a former Palantir employee, and headquartered in San Francisco, Blend reported Q2 2026 revenue of $33.8M (+7%), including $31.4M of software platform revenue, with non-GAAP operating income above guidance. Q3 2026 guidance of $31.5-33.5M implies roughly flat year-on-year revenue amid higher mortgage rates. Market cap is about $265M at $1.12 per share.

Investment Thesis
Blend has a debt-free balance sheet (D/E 2.2%), 75% gross margins and positive non-GAAP operating income, and it is one of the first mortgage-software vendors to put agentic AI into production [1][7]. Autopilot reached 65 activated lenders and more than 7,000 applications before paid pricing began in mid-2026, and management sees AI agents adding 10-15% incremental growth in 2027 [3]. At about $265M market cap, roughly 2x sales and 18.7x forward earnings, a mortgage-rate decline that revives origination volumes, combined with Autopilot monetization, could drive meaningful upside [7].
The bear case: growth is stalling, with Q2 2026 revenue up 7% and Q3 guidance of $31.5-33.5M implying roughly flat year-on-year revenue, below consensus, and the stock fell about 10% on that outlook [1][2]. GAAP profitability is barely breakeven (0.02% margin, 2.1% ROE), the forward PEG is negative, and revenue is highly sensitive to mortgage rates and refinance volumes [6][7]. Competition from ICE Mortgage Technology, which has a far larger installed base, and AI-native entrants could compress pricing, and at $1.12 with about $2.3M of daily traded value, liquidity is thin. The Inspire Impact Score of +35 (ALIGNED) is a modest positive for faith-based investors.
Key Catalysts
  1. Autopilot paid conversion: moving the 65 activated lenders to paid contracts could add the 10-15% incremental 2027 growth management has outlined [3]
  2. Mortgage-rate relief driving a refinance and purchase-origination rebound that lifts Blend's volume-based software revenue [2][3]
  3. Q3 2026 results and 2027 outlook showing renewed growth from top-20 bank wins and Consumer Banking Suite expansion [1][2]
Key Risks
  1. Revenue stagnation: Q3 2026 guidance of $31.5-33.5M implies roughly flat year-on-year revenue and missed consensus, reflecting mortgage-rate headwinds and customer volume roll-off [2]
  2. Competitive and AI commoditization pressure from ICE Mortgage Technology (Encompass) and AI-native document and decisioning startups on a roughly $124M revenue base [6]
  3. Thin profitability and liquidity: a GAAP margin near 0%, a negative forward PEG and about $2.3M of daily traded value at $1.12 per share heighten volatility and capital-raising risk [7]
