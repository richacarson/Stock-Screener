Company Profile
Communication Services ·Internet Content & Information ·NASDAQ ·United States
Bumble Inc. operates the Bumble and Badoo online dating apps and the Bumble For Friends community app, built on a 'women make the first move' model. On October 13, 2026, Bumble agreed to be acquired by Ithaca Holdings in an all-cash take-private at $6.05 per share (about $1.3B). Affiliates of Blackstone and founder-CEO Whitney Wolfe Herd have provided the required shareholder approval, and closing is pending. Q2 2026 revenue fell 15.2% to $210.5M and paying users fell 16.4% to 3.2M.

Investment Thesis
For public shareholders the thesis is now essentially merger arbitrage. Ithaca Holdings agreed on October 13, 2026 to buy Bumble for $6.05 per share in cash (about $1.3B), a 138% premium to the unaffected price, and Blackstone and Whitney Wolfe Herd have already delivered the required shareholder approval [4]. The business still generates cash: Q1 2026 adjusted EBITDA rose 28.3% to $82.6M, and TTM free cash flow yield is about 32% at the current price [1][2]. The stock at $5.98 trades about 1% below the deal price [1].
The bear case is that the standalone business is shrinking and the upside is capped. Q2 2026 revenue fell 15.2% to $210.5M, paying users fell 16.4% to 3.2M, and a $169.3M impairment produced a $127.9M net loss [1][4]. Q3 guidance of $205-$213M implies continued decline [1]. If regulators, financing or other closing conditions derail the deal, the shares could fall back toward the roughly $2.54 unaffected level implied by the 138% premium [4]. Long-term owners give up any recovery to the buyers.
Key Catalysts
  1. Closing of the $6.05/share all-cash Ithaca Holdings take-private, already approved by majority holders Blackstone and Whitney Wolfe Herd [4]
  2. Q3 2026 revenue landing within the $205-$213M guidance range, confirming the member-base reset is stabilizing ahead of closing [1]
  3. Continued adjusted EBITDA strength (Q1 2026 +28.3% to $82.6M) supporting deal financing [1]
Key Risks
  1. Deal-break risk: if the Ithaca transaction fails, the shares lose the 138% takeover premium over the October 9, 2026 unaffected close [4]
  2. Ongoing user decline: paying users fell 16.4% YoY to 3.2M in Q2 2026 and revenue fell 15.2%, eroding the standalone value [4]
  3. AI-native dating and companion apps could further compress paid engagement as rivals experiment with AI-driven matching [5]
