Company Profile
Healthcare ·Drug Manufacturers - General ·NYSE ·United States
Bristol-Myers Squibb is a global biopharmaceutical company focused on discovering, developing, and delivering innovative medicines across oncology, hematology, immunology, cardiovascular, and neuroscience. Founded in 1887 and headquartered in Princeton, NJ. Q2 2026 revenue reached $12.97B (+6% YoY), with the Growth Portfolio (Camzyos, Breyanzi, Reblozyl, Opdualag, Cobenfy) up 15% to $7.56B and now nearly 60% of total revenue, offsetting loss-of-exclusivity pressure on legacy products. The company raised full-year 2026 revenue guidance to $49.0-$50.0B and non-GAAP EPS guidance to $6.75-$7.00. CEO Chris Boerner leads the company, having taken the role in November 2024.

Investment Thesis
Bristol-Myers Squibb's Growth Portfolio transition is accelerating -- Q2 2026 revenue of $12.97B (+6% YoY) was driven by 15% growth in the Growth Portfolio to $7.56B, now nearly 60% of total revenue, successfully offsetting legacy loss-of-exclusivity pressure [1]. Eliquis alone is guided for 20-25% global revenue growth in 2026 with quarterly revenue of ~$4.5B. Management raised full-year 2026 guidance to $49.0-$50.0B in revenue and $6.75-$7.00 in non-GAAP EPS. The stock trades at a Forward P/E of just 9.1x with a 4.1% dividend yield backed by 94 consecutive years of payments and 17 consecutive years of increases -- an unusually inexpensive valuation for a $122B market cap pharma with a genuinely accelerating growth portfolio [4]. ROE of 46.6% and $8.1B in annual free cash flow support both the dividend and continued R&D investment in a late-stage pipeline (Admilparant, Arlo-cel, Iberdomide, Milvexian, Sotyktu) with multibillion-dollar peak sales potential.
The bear case centers on balance sheet leverage, patent cliff execution risk, and organizational health. D/E of 201.9% is elevated, a legacy of the $74B Celgene acquisition, and while ROE of 46.6% looks strong, it is partly a function of that leverage rather than pure operating efficiency [4]. Revenue 5yr CAGR of just 1.2% reveals that years of Revlimid generic erosion have barely been offset by the Growth Portfolio -- the market needs sustained proof that the inflection is durable. Two pipeline readouts were delayed in 2026 (Milvexian AFib to Q1 2027, Cobenfy ADEPT to early 2027) due to slower enrollment, reminding investors that clinical execution risk persists [1]. Glassdoor reviews describing 'fear-based leadership' and deteriorating culture under CEO Boerner raise questions about organizational health during a period requiring sustained commercial and R&D execution [2]. The PEG ratio of 17.0 suggests the market prices in very modest growth, and the Inspire Impact Score of -67 (MISALIGNED) due to Abortion Activism and LGBT Activism may limit faith-based allocation despite strong healthcare mission.
Key Catalysts
  1. Continued Growth Portfolio momentum (currently +15% YoY, ~60% of total revenue) sustaining the offset against legacy loss-of-exclusivity pressure through H2 2026 and into 2027 [1]
  2. Pivotal pipeline readouts expected by year-end 2026 across Admilparant, Arlo-cel, Iberdomide, and Sotyktu, with Milvexian (AFib) and Cobenfy (ADEPT) readouts in early 2027 -- any positive result could unlock multibillion-dollar peak sales and re-rate the stock [1]
  3. Eliquis global revenue growth of 20-25% guided for 2026, providing a $18B+ annual cash-flow bridge while the Growth Portfolio scales toward eventual replacement of all legacy revenue [1]
Key Risks
  1. Elevated leverage (D/E 201.9%, $45B total debt) constrains balance sheet flexibility for further pipeline-building M&A and creates vulnerability if revenue growth disappoints [4]
  2. Pipeline execution risk exemplified by 2026 delays -- Milvexian AFib readout pushed to Q1 2027 and Cobenfy ADEPT to early 2027 due to slower enrollment -- could recur across the remaining late-stage portfolio [1]
  3. Five-year revenue CAGR of just 1.2% reflects persistent legacy loss-of-exclusivity drag; the market requires sustained multi-quarter proof that Growth Portfolio acceleration is durable before re-rating from the current 9.1x forward P/E [4]
