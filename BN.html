Company Profile
Financial Services ·Asset Management ·NYQ ·Canada
Brookfield Corporation is a global alternative asset manager with over $1 trillion in assets under management across infrastructure, renewable power and transition, private equity, real estate, credit, and insurance solutions. The company operates and invests in long-life, high-quality assets in more than 30 countries. CEO Bruce Flatt (30+ year tenure) has positioned Brookfield as a leading enabler of AI-driven infrastructure buildout through data centers, renewable energy, and the Radiant compute platform. Q2 2026 revenue was $19.4B (+7.3% YoY) with distributable earnings per share growth of 15.1% to $0.61 [1]. Market cap is $81.7B.

Investment Thesis
Brookfield at $36.62 with an $81.7B market cap offers compelling value for a $1T+ AUM alternative asset manager trading at just 6.2x forward earnings, reflecting the market's struggle to value BN's complex holding company structure [4]. Q2 2026 distributable earnings per share grew 15.1% to $0.61, annualizing to ~$2.44, while the company targets 15%+ annual DE growth through 2028 driven by $150B+ in deployable capital, accelerating realizations from mature vintage funds, and the structural tailwind of institutional capital shifting into real assets [1]. Brookfield Renewable (~34 GW) and Brookfield Infrastructure are direct beneficiaries of the AI buildout through power generation and data center ownership, positioning BN as a leveraged play on AI infrastructure demand without semiconductor valuation risk. The $100B AI infrastructure program with NVIDIA and partnerships with OpenAI and Anthropic provide specific investment catalysts [2].
The bear case centers on structural opacity and financial complexity. BN's consolidated revenue of $80.7B produces thin GAAP profitability (1.8% margin, 2.3% ROE) that obscures the underlying economics of the asset management and operating businesses — distributable earnings, management's preferred metric, excludes mark-to-market losses, stock compensation, and other items, creating a significant gap between GAAP and adjusted profitability [1][4]. D/E of 161% with $269B in consolidated debt creates leverage risk if sustained high interest rates pressure portfolio company refinancing and distribution capacity. The stock price has fallen 6.7% below its 50-day SMA of $39.25, suggesting near-term momentum weakness. ESG credibility risk persists: critics cite over 215 fossil fuel assets across the portfolio despite net-zero pledges, and the company's sustainability ambition applies only to operationally managed investments [5][6]. Execution risk on the $100B AI infrastructure program is material — deploying capital of that scale requires sustained demand for AI compute over a multi-decade horizon, and any cyclical slowdown in hyperscaler capex could delay returns.
