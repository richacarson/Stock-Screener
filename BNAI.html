Company Profile

Brand Engagement Network, Inc. provides enterprise-grade conversational AI solutions built on proprietary natural language processing, multisensory awareness, sentiment analysis, and real-time personalization capabilities. Its Engagement Language Model (ELM) and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architecture power AI agents across text, voice, and avatar-based experiences for automotive, healthcare, financial services, and other industries. The company completed its $19.5M acquisition of Cataneo GmbH on June 30, 2026, adding the MYDAS advertising technology platform and approximately $5.3M in H1 2026 revenue. Formerly known as Blockchain Exchange Network Inc. Interim CEO Janine Grasso (appointed July 2025). Market cap approximately $100 million.

The Inspire Impact Score of 38 (ALIGNED) makes BNAI accessible to faith-based investors, and the ~$100M market cap provides significant upside potential if the Cataneo integration succeeds and organic AI revenue scales.
BNAI remains a pre-revenue (organically) company competing in one of the most crowded and well-funded technology markets in history. Organic quarterly revenue of $160K (Q2 2026) with 29 employees means the core BEN product has no commercially validated market, no meaningful customer base, and no competitive moat against OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, and Amazon [2]. The $100M market cap at approximately 9x Cataneo's annualized revenue is aggressive for an unproven integration. The risk of further dilution through equity raises is substantial given the cash constraints.
