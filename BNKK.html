Company Profile
Financial Services ·Asset Management ·NASDAQ ·United States
Bonk, Inc. (formerly Safety Shot, Inc.) is a digital infrastructure company bridging traditional public markets and the decentralized economy. The company manages revenue-generating assets within the Solana blockchain ecosystem, including a 51% revenue-sharing interest in the BONK.fun DeFi platform, and operates the BONK.trade prediction markets and social betting platform through a partnership with dYdX. The company also markets Sure Shot, a patented wellness beverage that reduces blood alcohol content. Rebranded in October 2025, headquartered in Scottsdale, Arizona.

Investment Thesis
Bonk, Inc. achieved a stunning Q1 2026 revenue inflection, reporting $4.34M in revenue — up 10,200% YoY from $42K — and its first-ever operating profit of $1.37M, validating the October 2025 pivot from failing beverage company to digital asset infrastructure [1]. Operating expenses were slashed 60% to $2.22M, demonstrating improved capital discipline. Founder Mitchell Rudy's appointment as president and open-market share purchase signal insider alignment with the digital strategy [2][3]. The three-pillar mandate (sustained profitability, BONK asset accumulation, direct business incubation) provides strategic focus, and the debt-free balance sheet with only 8M shares outstanding creates leverage if the Solana/BONK ecosystem continues to grow.
The bear case is overwhelming. Despite the Q1 revenue surge, the stock has declined from $2.83 to ~$1.05 (63% decline since prior screen), and market cap collapsed from $22M to $8.4M, indicating the market deeply discounts the sustainability of meme-coin revenue [4]. TTM net loss of -$68.2M dwarfs any quarterly improvement. Daily dollar volume of ~$202K makes the stock untradeable for any institutional portfolio. The entire business model depends on the BONK meme-coin ecosystem — a decline in Solana network activity, regulatory crackdown on meme-coins, or loss of community interest would eliminate the primary revenue stream overnight. The company's serial rebranding history (three names in three years), 10-person headcount, and Nasdaq compliance warnings signal structural governance deficiencies that should disqualify it from any fundamental investment framework. GAAP net loss was still $1.83M in Q1 2026 due to $3.83M unrealized digital asset adjustments, underscoring the volatility embedded in the balance sheet [1].
