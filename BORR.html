Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas Drilling ·NYSE ·Bermuda
Borr Drilling Limited is an international offshore drilling contractor operating one of the youngest modern jack-up fleets in the industry. Founded in 2016, the company provides drilling services in water depths up to 400 feet, with operations in the Middle East, Southeast Asia, West Africa, Mexico, and Europe. Fleet expanded to 34 rigs following the BC Ventures Mexico JV acquisition of 5 premium jack-ups for $287M. Q2 2026 reported a $241.4M net loss (including $176.3M debt extinguishment charge) on $232.3M revenue. New CEO Bruno Morand (September 2025) succeeded Patrick Schorn, who became Executive Chairman. Market cap $1.39B.
2,030 Employees ·www.borrdrilling.com

Investment Thesis
Borr Drilling operates one of the youngest and highest-specification jack-up fleets in the industry (34 rigs post-Mexico JV acquisition) in a structurally undersupplied market — limited new rig orders since 2015 mean existing modern fleets command premium dayrates [1]. TTM revenue of $1.02B with FY2025 adjusted EBITDA of $470.1M demonstrates the underlying business generates strong cash flow when normalized for the one-time $176.3M debt extinguishment charge [1]. The fleet expansion is aggressive: 21 contract commitments year-to-date adding $541M and approximately 4,350 days of backlog, with 73% contract coverage for 2026 at $134K average dayrate [1]. Director Tor Olav Troim's $6.0M share purchase (1.5M shares in August 2026), taking his stake to 10% ownership, signals strong insider conviction despite the weak Q2 headline loss [5]. The refinanced debt structure ($2.03B senior secured notes due 2032/2034, $300M convertible due 2033) extends maturities and reduces near-term refinancing risk. Analyst consensus is 'Buy' with a $5.09 target implying 13% upside [6].
The bear case is dominated by balance sheet risk, earnings deterioration, and cyclical vulnerability. D/E of 259% with $2.03B senior secured notes, $300M convertible, and $250M RCF against $1.39B market cap creates severe financial leverage — any sustained dayrate decline could compromise the company's ability to service debt [6]. Q2 2026 adjusted EBITDA collapsed 51% to $43.8M amid Odin rig startup costs, transition expenses, and a $10.8M credit loss provision from a West African customer [1]. The $241.4M Q2 net loss included the debt extinguishment charge but also revealed rising operating costs: rig operating and maintenance costs surged 21% QoQ to $175.1M, and insurance premiums spiked $2.2M due to Middle East conflict risk [1]. H2 2026 contract coverage of only 70% leaves meaningful revenue uncertainty. The energy transition creates secular headwinds as ESG-conscious capital limits valuation multiples for hydrocarbon exposure. Glassdoor's 3.1/5 rating and 41% recommendation rate raise operational execution risk in a safety-critical industry [4]. The Inspire Impact Score of 54 (ALIGNED) is a positive for faith-based investors despite the fossil fuel business model.

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK

AI Risk Protection (8/9): Offshore jack-up drilling is a physical-world operation requiring massive capital equipment ($50M+ per rig), specialized crews, regulatory permitting, and real-time subsea engineering that AI structurally cannot substitute [1][3]. The core revenue model — deploying rigs under multi-year dayrate contracts — involves physical asset utilization in harsh marine environments where human judgment and mechanical execution are irreplaceable. AI could modestly improve drilling efficiency through predictive maintenance and wellbore optimization, but this enhances rather than threatens the drilling contractor business model. Furthermore, AI-driven data center expansion is increasing global energy demand, which supports offshore exploration and production activity and thus demand for jack-up rigs. The regulatory environment (offshore safety, environmental permitting) creates additional barriers to disruption. AI Opportunity (0/1): No evidence of AI-generated revenue. Borr has not announced AI-powered services or products generating measurable revenue, and the company does not appear to be monetizing digital drilling optimization capabilities, unlike some larger peers.
