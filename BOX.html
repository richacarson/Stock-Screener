Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Infrastructure ·NYSE ·United States
Box, Inc. provides a cloud-based Intelligent Content Management platform for enterprises, enabling organizations to manage, secure, govern, and derive intelligence from unstructured data. Key products include Box Drive, Box Sign, Box Shield, Box Relay, Box Agent (unified AI engine), and Box Automate (agentic workflow solution). Enterprise Advanced, the AI-powered tier, now accounts for 10% of revenue with a 30-40% pricing uplift. Founded in 2005 by CEO Aaron Levie, Box completed FY2026 (ended January 2026) with $1.18B in revenue (+8% YoY), record RPO of $1.711B (+17%), and non-GAAP operating margin of 30.6%. Q1 FY2027 showed accelerating growth at 11% YoY.

Investment Thesis
Box is executing a successful AI-native transformation that is beginning to inflect the growth trajectory — Q1 FY2027 revenue growth accelerated to 11% YoY (from 4% in Q1 FY2026), Enterprise Advanced accounts for 10% of revenue with a 30-40% pricing premium, and record RPO of $1.711B (+17%) provides strong forward visibility [1][2]. FY2026 delivered $1.18B revenue with GAAP profit margin reaching 10.35%, non-GAAP operating margin of 30.6%, and record free cash flow of $313M. ROE of 66.96% reflects exceptional capital efficiency from the lean 1,980-person organization. Box Agent and Box Automate position the platform as the AI-native enterprise content layer — where organizations connect unstructured data to AI agents — a potentially large and underpenetrated market. The partnership with Google Cloud (Box Agent for Gemini Enterprise) extends reach without diluting Box's core value proposition.
The critical risk is competitive intensity from bundled alternatives: Microsoft's Copilot integration with SharePoint/OneDrive and Google's Gemini with Drive offer AI-native content capabilities packaged into platforms enterprises already pay for, potentially commoditizing Box's core value proposition [4]. D/E of 356% from aggressive share buybacks creates balance sheet fragility — while sustainable given $313M annual free cash flow and 80%+ gross margins, it limits acquisition capacity and financial flexibility in a downturn. The PEG of 2.21 at 20.52x forward earnings prices in significant AI-driven growth acceleration that remains unproven at scale. CEO Levie's consistent insider selling (13 sells, 0 buys over 5 years, including $365K in June 2026) does not inspire confidence in insider conviction [5]. Revenue growth of 8% for full-year FY2026, while accelerating to 11% in Q1 FY2027, remains below the SaaS sector average, and Box must demonstrate that Enterprise Advanced adoption can sustain growth acceleration rather than producing a one-time repricing event.
