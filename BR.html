Company Profile
Technology ·Information Technology Services ·NYSE ·United States
Broadridge Financial Solutions is a global fintech leader providing investor communications (proxy processing, annual reports, prospectuses), securities processing, and data-driven technology solutions for banks, broker-dealers, asset managers, wealth managers, and corporate issuers. Spun off from ADP in 2007, the company processes over 80% of U.S. proxy communications, settles approximately $15 trillion in trades daily, and serves over 10,000 public companies with a $17.8B market cap.

Investment Thesis
Broadridge delivers consistent, utility-like financial performance backed by regulatory moats — Q3 FY2026 showed $1,954M revenue (8% YoY growth), $2.72 adjusted EPS (11% growth), and $591M nine-month free cash flow with >100% conversion [1]. The company raised full-year guidance to 7%+ recurring revenue growth and 10-12% adjusted EPS growth, while the pipeline surged past $1 billion (up 20% YoY) with deal origination up 25% [2]. At 15.4x forward earnings with a 2.53% dividend yield, 18 consecutive years of dividend growth at an 11.1% CAGR, ROE of 42.3%, and near-monopoly proxy processing market share, BR offers a compelling value proposition as a defensive fintech holding with embedded position growth tailwinds (equity positions +15%, fund positions +6% in Q3) [5].
The bear case centers on structural disruption risks that drove a 43% share price decline over 52 weeks: AI potentially eroding technology franchises and tokenized equities disintermediating the governance business [3]. While SouthernSun argues these fears are overstated given regulatory moats and compliance liability, the lowered closed sales guidance ($240-$290M vs. prior $290-$340M) signals sales-cycle elongation. GTO segment recurring revenue grew only 3% in Q3 vs. 8% in ICS, revealing divergent growth trajectories. Profit margin of 15% lags the 20% Infrastructure target, and D/E of 121% with $3.2B total debt against $2.8B equity limits acquisition firepower [1]. The acquisitive growth strategy has inflated goodwill to $3.7B. A low Infinite Game score of 4 reflects the absence of a compelling purpose beyond efficient infrastructure operation, and Inspiration scores at 4 given a functional mission, no identified insider buying, and mixed employee reviews below the C-suite.
Key Catalysts
  1. Pipeline exceeding $1 billion with 25% higher deal origination and $430M recurring revenue backlog suggests accelerating revenue conversion in FY2027 as larger platform deals close [2]
  2. Equity position growth of 15% and internal trade growth of 16% provide structural tailwinds to recurring communications revenue tied to capital markets activity [1]
  3. CQG acquisition integration and DeepSee AI partnership expanding capital markets and agentic AI capabilities could unlock cross-selling opportunities across the trading technology and investor communications platforms [1][3]
Key Risks
  1. AI disruption risk to fund management software and GTO technology services — segment recurring revenue grew only 3% in Q3 vs. 8% in ICS, and analyst concerns contributed to a 43% 52-week share price decline [1][3]
  2. Tokenized equities could fundamentally alter the proxy communications chain if blockchain-native governance bypasses traditional intermediaries, despite current SEC regulatory protections requiring identical governance obligations [3]
  3. Closed sales guidance reduced to $240-$290M from $290-$340M signals sales-cycle elongation that could weigh on FY2027 revenue growth, with larger platform deals extending conversion timelines [2]

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience MODERATE
6
AI Risk Protection (6/9): Broadridge's core proxy communications business is well-insulated from AI disruption due to SEC-mandated regulatory requirements, extreme switching costs, and compliance liability that prevents clients from internalizing these functions [3]. SouthernSun Asset Management argues these fears are overstated, noting 'clients won't internalize proxy processing due to asymmetric risk-reward dynamics and compliance liability.' However, shares declined 43% over 52 weeks partly on analyst fears that AI will erode technology franchises, and fund management software faces real AI substitution risk [3]. Tokenized equities pose additional disintermediation risk, though the SEC has affirmed tokenized securities carry identical governance obligations. The regulatory moat protects the largest revenue stream, but meaningful exposure in GTO technology services (where recurring revenue grew only 3% in Q3) warrants a PROTECTED rather than IMMUNE rating. AI Opportunity (0/1): No quantified AI revenue reported. BondGPT on LTX and the DeepSee minority investment are exploratory; no AI-powered products generate measurable revenue contribution yet [3].
