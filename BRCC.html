Company Profile
Consumer Defensive ·Packaged Foods ·NYSE ·United States
BRC Inc., operating as Black Rifle Coffee Company, is a veteran-founded premium coffee and beverage company serving consumers through Wholesale, Direct-to-Consumer, and Outpost (brick-and-mortar) channels. Q1 2026 revenue surged 21.4% to $109.2M and Q2 2026 grew 12.8% to $107.0M, with adjusted EBITDA more than doubling year-over-year in Q2 to $6.3M. Wholesale distribution has expanded to 56.5% all-commodity volume (ACV), and the company recently entered the $20B+ energy beverage category with Black Rifle Energy. Full-year 2026 guidance targets at least 8% revenue growth and 35% adjusted EBITDA growth.
468 Employees ·www.blackriflecoffee.com

Investment Thesis
Black Rifle Coffee is executing a credible growth trajectory: Q1 2026 revenue surged 21.4% to $109.2M, Q2 grew 12.8% to $107.0M, and adjusted EBITDA more than doubled year-over-year in Q2 to $6.3M [1]. The 'land-and-expand' wholesale strategy has reached 56.5% ACV with significant whitespace remaining, DTC revenue grew 13.6%, and the Black Rifle Energy launch opens a transformative $20B+ category growth vector [1]. At forward P/E of 21.5 and a $129M market cap, BRCC offers meaningful upside if the company achieves sustainable GAAP profitability and energy beverages gain traction. The distinctive veteran brand identity creates genuine customer loyalty with high repeat purchase rates.
The bear case is formidable. BRCC remains GAAP unprofitable with -0.9% profit margin and -18.4% ROE, and the stock has declined roughly 90% from its 2022 SPAC-era highs [4]. The $129M market cap and daily dollar volume of barely $1M make this an illiquid micro-cap with execution risk far exceeding typical IOWN holdings. Glassdoor reviews reveal internal culture problems — fear-based decision-making, founder control, layoffs — that could impair talent retention and operational execution [2]. The class action alleging misleading 'America's Coffee' branding creates legal overhang, and the explicitly political brand positioning limits the total addressable market [3]. Full-year guidance of 'at least 8%' revenue growth implies meaningful H2 deceleration from H1's 17% blended rate.
