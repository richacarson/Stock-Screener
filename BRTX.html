Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
BioRestorative Therapies, Inc. is a clinical-stage biotechnology company developing regenerative medicine products using adult (non-embryonic) stem cell protocols. Its lead candidate BRTX-100 targets chronic lumbar disc disease using autologous cultured mesenchymal stem cells, with a fully enrolled Phase 2 trial of 99 patients and FDA endorsement of Phase 3 design. The company also operates a biocosmeceutical platform targeting the $28B regenerative aesthetics market and develops ThermoStem for metabolic disorders. Headquartered in Melville, New York, with 14 employees and a market cap of approximately $6 million.
