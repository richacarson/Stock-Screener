Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NasdaqGS ·United States
Braze, Inc. operates a cloud-based customer engagement platform that enables brands to deliver personalized, real-time marketing messages across email, mobile push, SMS, in-app, and web channels. The platform leverages first-party data, AI-powered automation, and cross-channel orchestration to help companies build stronger customer relationships at scale, reaching 8 billion monthly active users across 2,713 customers. Co-founded by CEO Bill Magnuson in 2011, headquartered in New York City. FY2026 (ended January 2026) revenue was $738.2M (+24.4% YoY), reaching $800M ARR. Q1 FY2027 revenue accelerated to $211.0M (+30.2% YoY), and FY2027 guidance was raised to $895-899M.

Investment Thesis
Braze is executing one of the most compelling growth stories in enterprise SaaS — Q1 FY2027 revenue of $211M grew 30.2% YoY (accelerating from 24.4% in FY2026), FY2027 guidance was raised to $895-899M, and dollar-based net retention improved to 110% [1][2]. The AI product suite (BrazeAI Operator, Agent Console, Creative Studio) drove a 50%+ increase in quarterly bookings and is accelerating enterprise adoption, with 333 large customers at $500K+ ARR (up 29% YoY) [2][3]. The platform's first-party data infrastructure and compliance capabilities (ISO 27001, SOC 2, HIPAA) create meaningful switching costs in regulated industries. D/E of only 14% with $387.5M cash provides financial flexibility, and non-GAAP net income of $42M in FY2026 (guided to $0.63 EPS in FY2027) confirms emerging operating leverage. $1B+ RPO provides strong forward visibility.
The primary concern is the path to GAAP profitability — the $122M net loss persists despite 30%+ revenue growth, largely driven by stock-based compensation that dilutes existing shareholders [1][6]. The non-GAAP forward PE of ~46x prices in meaningful expectations for sustained 20%+ revenue growth and margin expansion — any deceleration could trigger significant multiple compression. Competitive pressure from Salesforce Marketing Cloud and Adobe Experience Cloud, both deploying AI marketing suites with deeper resources and existing enterprise relationships, could erode Braze's differentiation if larger platforms match its AI capabilities [3]. Net insider selling across the C-suite (CRO, CTO, CBO, and General Counsel all selling in 2026) is a negative signal during a critical scaling phase [5]. Glassdoor reviews suggesting VP/management turnover and cultural tensions with high performers could constrain the talent acquisition and retention needed to execute the AI strategy.
