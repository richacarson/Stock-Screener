Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NasdaqGS ·United States
Braze, Inc. operates a cloud-based customer engagement platform that enables brands to deliver personalized, real-time marketing messages across email, mobile push, SMS, in-app, and web channels. The platform leverages first-party data, AI-powered automation, and cross-channel orchestration to help companies build stronger customer relationships at scale, reaching 8 billion monthly active users across 2,600+ customers. Co-founded by CEO Bill Magnuson in 2011, headquartered in New York City. The company reached $800M in annual recurring revenue in FY2026.

Investment Thesis
Braze delivered strong FY2026 results with revenue of $738.2M (up 24%), Q4 revenue of $205M (up 28%), and $800M in annual recurring revenue, demonstrating four consecutive quarters of accelerating organic growth in a competitive martech landscape [1]. The AI product suite — BrazeAI Operator, Agent Console, and Creative Studio — represents genuine product differentiation that drove a 50%+ increase in quarterly bookings and is accelerating enterprise adoption, with 333 large customers at $500K+ ARR (up 29% YoY) [1][3]. Non-GAAP net income of $42M (up from $18M) confirms emerging operating leverage, PEG of 1.24 suggests reasonable valuation for the growth rate, and $1B+ remaining performance obligations provide strong forward visibility [1][5].
The primary concern is the path to GAAP profitability — the $130.8M net loss widened from $104.0M despite 24% revenue growth, largely driven by stock-based compensation that dilutes existing shareholders [1][2]. CFO Isabelle Winkles' departure in May 2026 adds execution uncertainty during a critical transition period, and net insider selling across the C-suite (CFO, CTO, General Counsel all selling in Q1 2026) is a negative signal [3][5]. Forward P/E of 25.9 prices in meaningful expectations for both growth and margin improvement. Competitive pressure from Salesforce Marketing Cloud, Adobe Experience Cloud, and AI-native marketing startups could erode Braze's differentiation if larger platforms match its AI capabilities. Dollar-based net retention declining from 111% to 109% suggests some customer spending compression. The Inspire faith alignment score of -67 (MISALIGNED) may concern values-based investors, though faith alignment does not affect the IOWN overall score [5].
