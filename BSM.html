Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas E&P ·NYQ ·United States
Black Stone Minerals, L.P. is one of the largest owners of oil and natural gas mineral interests in the United States, with approximately 16.9 million gross acres of mineral interests, 1.8 million gross acres of nonparticipating royalty interests, and 1.6 million gross acres of overriding royalty interests across 41 states [2]. As a pure-play mineral and royalty company founded in 1876, BSM collects royalty income from E&P operators without bearing drilling or operating costs. Q2 2026 oil and gas revenue was $115.4M (+13% YoY), net income surged to $106.4M, Adjusted EBITDA was $91.3M, and the board increased the quarterly distribution 7% to $0.32/unit ($1.28 annualized) [1].

Investment Thesis
Black Stone Minerals offers a high-yield (8.22%) income stream from one of the largest mineral rights portfolios in the U.S. — approximately 16.9 million gross acres across 41 states [2]. The royalty model produces 66.9% profit margins with minimal capital requirements and pristine leverage (D/E 17.6%), while Q2 2026 net income surged to $106.4M with Adjusted EBITDA of $91.3M [1][3]. Forward P/E of 13.2 is well below the market average, and the business is structurally immune to AI disruption — mineral rights ownership cannot be replicated or displaced by software. The Q2 2026 distribution increase of 7% to $0.32/unit signals improving fundamentals, and Director DeWalch's $1.4M+ insider purchases demonstrate strong leadership conviction [3]. The Inspire Impact Score of 62 (ALIGNED) with 4 positive attributions and zero negative flags supports faith-based allocation.
The bear case centers on commodity price sensitivity, distribution volatility, and limited growth levers. Revenue declined from $784M peak (2022) to $422M (2025) as commodity prices normalized, though Q2 2026 oil and gas revenue grew 13% YoY to $115.4M [1]. Distributions were cut from $1.90/year (2023) to $1.35 (2025), and the payout ratio of approximately 85% provides limited cushion if commodity prices weaken — consecutive years of distribution growth stands at 0 [3]. BSM has no ability to control its revenue trajectory — income depends entirely on commodity prices and third-party operator drilling decisions on its acreage. The partnership structure limits access to capital markets. Mineral/royalty production declined 9% QoQ to 32.5 MBoe/d in Q2 2026, signaling near-term production softness [1]. Innovation and Inspiration each score just 2/10, as the company lacks any strategic optionality, purpose statement, or community engagement beyond collecting royalties — BSM is a pure commodity bet dressed as an income vehicle.
