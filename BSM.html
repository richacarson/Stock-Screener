Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas E&P ·NYSE ·United States
Black Stone Minerals, L.P. is one of the largest owners of oil and natural gas mineral interests in the United States, with approximately 16.9 million gross acres of mineral interests, 1.8 million gross acres of nonparticipating royalty interests, and 1.6 million gross acres of overriding royalty interests across 41 states [2]. As a pure-play mineral and royalty company founded in 1876, BSM collects royalty income from E&P operators without bearing drilling or operating costs. Q2 2026 net income surged to $106.4M with Adjusted EBITDA of $91.3M, and the board increased the quarterly distribution 7% to $0.32/unit [1].

Investment Thesis
Black Stone Minerals offers a high-yield (8.57%) income stream from one of the largest mineral rights portfolios in the U.S. — ~16.9 million gross acres across 41 states [2]. The royalty model produces 66.95% profit margins with minimal capital requirements and pristine leverage (D/E 18%), while Q2 2026 net income surged to $106.4M with Adjusted EBITDA of $91.3M [1][3]. Forward P/E of 12.95 is well below the market average, and the business is structurally immune to AI disruption. The Q2 2026 distribution increase of 7% to $0.32/unit signals improving fundamentals, and mineral/royalty production grew 16% QoQ in Q1 2026 to 35.9 MBoe/d [1]. Director DeWalch's $1.4M+ insider purchases in May 2026 demonstrate strong leadership conviction [3]. The Inspire Impact Score of 62 (ALIGNED) with 4 positive attributions and zero negatives reflects faith-alignment compatibility.
The bear case centers on commodity price sensitivity, distribution volatility, and limited growth levers. Revenue has been highly cyclical — declining from $784M peak (2022) to $422M (2025) as commodity prices normalized, though TTM revenue is now growing 36.6% [1][3]. Distributions were cut from $0.475/quarter to $0.30 between 2024-2025, and the payout ratio of 76.2% provides limited cushion if commodity prices weaken again [3]. BSM has no ability to control its revenue trajectory — income depends entirely on commodity prices and third-party operator activity on its acreage. The partnership structure limits access to capital markets. The 5-year distribution growth CAGR of -3.0% reflects the fundamental income instability of a commodity-driven royalty business, and consecutive years of distribution growth stands at 0 despite the recent increase [3]. Innovation and Inspiration scores are weak (2 each) as the company lacks any strategic optionality, purpose statement, or community engagement beyond collecting royalties — BSM is a pure commodity bet dependent on oil and gas prices.
