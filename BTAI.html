Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
BioXcel Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company that utilizes artificial intelligence approaches to develop medicines in neuroscience and immuno-oncology. The company's lead product IGALMI is a sublingual film for acute treatment of agitation associated with schizophrenia or bipolar I or II disorder in adults, with an sNDA pending for at-home use (PDUFA target November 14, 2026). Pipeline includes BXCL501 for agitation in Alzheimer's disease and BXCL701 for prostate and pancreatic cancer. The stock has collapsed to $0.07 with market cap of approximately $2.3M, 29 employees, and going concern risk disclosed in SEC filings.
