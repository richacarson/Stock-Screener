Company Profile
Healthcare ·Drug Manufacturers - Specialty & Generic ·NASDAQ ·United States
BioXcel Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company that utilizes artificial intelligence approaches to develop medicines in neuroscience and immuno-oncology. The company's lead product IGALMI is a sublingual film for acute treatment of agitation associated with schizophrenia or bipolar I or II disorder in adults. Pipeline includes BXCL501 for agitation in Alzheimer's disease and BXCL701 for prostate and pancreatic cancer. Market cap approximately $24M with only 29 employees.

Investment Thesis
BioXcel Therapeutics offers a binary bet on the November 14, 2026 PDUFA decision for IGALMI at-home use in acute agitation associated with bipolar disorders or schizophrenia [1]. Approval could dramatically expand the addressable market from in-hospital only (where IGALMI generated a mere $206K in Q1 2026) to outpatient settings reaching millions of patients. The company's AI-driven drug re-innovation platform was pioneering as the first AI drug discovery company to go public (2018), and the pipeline includes Phase 3 BXCL501 for Alzheimer's agitation and BXCL701 for immuno-oncology [2]. At a $24M market cap, even modest at-home IGALMI commercial success would generate substantial upside. The Inspire score of 39 (ALIGNED) reflects the social value of developing treatments for underserved neuropsychiatric conditions.

Cash of only $17.2M against $100.3M total debt with going concern risk means the company likely needs massive dilutive capital raises that could destroy shareholder value [1].
