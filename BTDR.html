Company Profile
Technology ·Bitcoin Mining & AI Cloud Infrastructure ·NMS ·Singapore
Bitdeer Technologies Group is a vertically integrated Bitcoin mining and AI cloud infrastructure company operating across three business segments: self-mining (63.2 EH/s hashrate), proprietary ASIC chip design (SEALMINER series), and AI Cloud services (4,184 GPUs deployed). Founded by Jihan Wu (co-founder of Bitmain), the company reported Q2 2026 revenue of $228.8M and Q1 2026 revenue of $188.9M (+170% YoY), though it posted net losses in both quarters ($92.3M and $39M respectively). The AI Cloud business reached an annualized run rate exceeding $69M by April 2026. Headquartered in Singapore with mining facilities in the U.S., Norway, and Bhutan.

Investment Thesis
Bitdeer Technologies at $9.21 with a $2.5B market cap is executing one of the most ambitious transformations in crypto infrastructure — evolving from a mining-as-a-service company into a vertically integrated platform spanning self-mining (63.2 EH/s, up 6.5x YoY), proprietary ASIC chip design (SEALMINER A4 launched April 2026), and AI Cloud infrastructure (>$69M annualized revenue, 4,184 GPUs at 92% utilization) [1][2]. Revenue surged 170% YoY to $188.9M in Q1 and reached $228.8M in Q2 2026, driven by the hashrate expansion and SEALMINER sales. Jihan Wu's pedigree as Bitmain co-founder provides unmatched domain expertise in mining ASIC design, and the AI Cloud pivot leverages existing power and cooling infrastructure to capture growing demand for GPU compute — the computational infrastructure convergence thesis positions Bitdeer at the intersection of crypto and AI megatrends.
The bear case is anchored in severe unprofitability and execution risk. Q2 2026 produced a gross loss of $8.5M (cost of revenue $237.3M exceeded revenue of $228.8M) and net loss of $92.3M, with $70M in quarterly non-cash depreciation pressure from the expanded mining fleet [2]. Profit margin of -28.1% and ROE of -25.3% indicate the company is destroying capital despite explosive revenue growth, and negative forward P/E signals analysts expect continued losses. The business remains fundamentally dependent on Bitcoin price — any sustained decline below mining cost would make the 63.2 EH/s fleet unprofitable. D/E of 177% reflects aggressive leverage for infrastructure buildout. A securities class action covering June 2024-November 2025 introduces litigation risk and potential management credibility concerns [4]. The AI Cloud business, while growing, is competing against massive hyperscalers (AWS, Azure, GCP) and well-funded GPU cloud providers (CoreWeave, Lambda) with deeper resources and established enterprise sales channels.

AI Risk Protection (8/9): Bitdeer's core business involves physical mining infrastructure — data centers, power infrastructure, cooling systems, and specialized ASIC hardware — that AI structurally cannot displace [1].
