Company Profile
Technology ·Blockchain & Cryptocurrency Infrastructure ·NASDAQ ·Singapore
Bitdeer Technologies is a vertically integrated blockchain and high-performance computing company spanning ASIC chip design (proprietary SEAL series), Bitcoin mining operations (86.1 EH/s hash rate under management, 289,000 mining rigs), data center infrastructure, and a growing AI Cloud business. Founded by Jihan Wu, co-founder of Bitmain Technologies, Bitdeer has pivoted from a mining hosting platform to a full-stack computing infrastructure company. Q2 2026 revenue reached $228.8M (+47% YoY), though the company remains unprofitable with a net loss of $92.3M. Market cap is approximately $3.1B with $496M in cash and $1.8B in total borrowings [1].

Investment Thesis
Bitdeer at $11.37 is a high-risk, high-optionality bet on the convergence of Bitcoin mining and AI infrastructure. Q2 2026 revenue of $228.8M (+47% YoY) demonstrates the self-mining scale-up is working — hash rate tripled to 86.1 EH/s while the SEAL01 chip delivers 67% energy efficiency improvements over prior-generation hardware [1][3]. The AI Cloud business ($14M Q2 revenue, +284% QoQ) adds a second growth vector leveraging existing data center infrastructure and providing diversification away from Bitcoin price dependency. With $496M in cash and adjusted EBITDA improving to $31.1M from $4.6M a year ago, the financial trajectory is improving even as GAAP losses persist [1]. Proprietary chip design (SEAL01, SEAL02 in development) creates a structural advantage most competitors lack, positioning Bitdeer as a vertically integrated computing platform rather than a commodity mining operator.
The bear case is substantial. The $92.3M Q2 net loss (-$0.37 EPS) shows the company is burning cash at scale, and $1.8B in total borrowings against $1.1B in equity creates significant financial risk if Bitcoin prices decline or the AI Cloud business fails to scale [1][4]. Gross margin turned negative (-3.7%) in Q2 due to rising electricity and depreciation costs from rapid expansion, raising questions about unit economics at current scale. The stock dropped 20% after Q2 earnings, and the Altman Z-Score of 0.87 signals elevated bankruptcy risk [4]. CEO Jihan Wu's leadership stability was undermined by dual C-suite departures (COO, CBO) in 2026 [2]. Bitcoin mining remains exposed to halving events, energy regulation, and cryptocurrency price volatility — risks no amount of operational efficiency can eliminate. The Inspire Impact Score of 43 (ALIGNED) is a positive for faith-based investors, though Bitcoin mining's energy consumption remains a broader ESG concern [3].
Key Catalysts
  1. SEAL02 chip development could further improve energy efficiency and mining economics, potentially achieving profitability at current Bitcoin prices if deployed at scale across the 289,000+ rig fleet [1][3]
  2. AI Cloud revenue trajectory ($14M Q2, +284% QoQ) could reach $100M+ annual run rate within 12-18 months, diversifying revenue and potentially re-rating the stock as an AI infrastructure play [1]
  3. Bitcoin price appreciation above $100,000 would dramatically improve mining economics and potentially push the company to GAAP profitability as self-mining scales with hash rate growth from 86.1 EH/s [1]
Key Risks
  1. Persistent unprofitability with $92.3M Q2 net loss and negative gross margin (-3.7%) raises going-concern questions, especially with $1.8B in debt and an Altman Z-Score of 0.87 signaling elevated bankruptcy risk [1][4]
  2. Bitcoin price volatility and halving cycle dynamics could compress mining margins further — the next halving (expected 2028) will cut block rewards by 50%, requiring either significant price appreciation or efficiency gains to maintain economics [1]
  3. C-suite departures (COO and CBO in 2026) and aggressive capital deployment ($1.8B debt) in an unprofitable company create execution and governance risk if chip design or AI Cloud strategies underperform [2]
