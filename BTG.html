Company Profile
Materials ·Gold ·NYSE ·Canada
B2Gold is a low-cost international senior gold producer headquartered in Vancouver, British Columbia, operating gold mines in Mali (Fekola), Namibia (Otjikoto), the Philippines (Masbate), and Canada (Goose Mine, Back River Gold District, Nunavut). The company produced 979,604 oz of gold in FY2025 and achieved record annual revenue of over $3 billion. FY2026 production guidance is 820,000-920,000 oz with AISC of $2,370-$2,550/oz. Q2 2026 net income reached $417M ($0.31/share) on gold revenue of $789.4M, driven by elevated gold prices above $3,400/oz. Mike Cinnamond succeeded founder Clive Johnson as CEO in mid-2026. Market cap is approximately $6.7 billion.

Investment Thesis
B2Gold at 5.48x forward earnings is one of the cheapest gold producers in the IOWN universe, with record FY2025 revenue exceeding $3 billion and Q2 2026 net income of $417M ($0.31/share) demonstrating exceptional profitability at gold prices above $3,400/oz [1]. The balance sheet is pristine with D/E of just 11%, ROE of 22.63%, and profit margin of 21.33% — all above scoring targets. The Goose Mine ramp-up provides organic production growth, and the company's low-cost operating model (AISC guidance $2,370-$2,550/oz) creates substantial margin expansion potential at current gold prices. The forward P/E of 5.48 and payout ratio of just 14.27% suggest the market is pricing in significant gold price decline or production risk that may not materialize given global macro uncertainty and central bank gold accumulation.
The bear case centers on commodity cyclicality, production decline risk, and organizational weakness. Gold prices above $3,400/oz are historically elevated and driven partly by geopolitical uncertainty — any normalization toward $2,500-$3,000 would compress margins and potentially push the company below current profitability levels [1]. FY2026 production guidance of 820-920K oz represents a potential decline from FY2025's 979K oz, suggesting Goose Mine ramp-up is not fully offsetting mature mine depletion. The CEO transition from founder Clive Johnson to CFO Mike Cinnamond introduces succession risk at a critical operational moment. Glassdoor reviews (2.7/5 overall, 2.0/5 culture) reveal a concerning cultural environment that could manifest in operational execution risk, particularly at remote mine sites where employee engagement directly affects safety and productivity [2]. The dividend was cut 20% despite record revenue, signaling management prioritization of capital preservation over income commitment. The Inspire Impact Score of 0 (MIXED) with no attributions suggests insufficient ESG data coverage, which may limit allocation for faith-based mandates. B2Gold's single-commodity focus provides no diversification against a gold price correction.

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
9
AI Risk Protection (9/9): Gold mining is a physical-world extraction business that AI structurally cannot displace. B2Gold operates open-pit and underground mines in Mali, Namibia, the Philippines, and Canada — removing gold-bearing ore from the earth, processing it through mills and leach circuits, and producing dore bars requires heavy equipment, geological expertise, processing infrastructure, and regulatory compliance that no AI system can substitute [1]. The gold mining industry is among the most AI-immune sectors: the product is a physical commodity, production requires massive capital infrastructure, and demand is driven by macroeconomic factors (inflation hedging, central bank purchases, jewelry) entirely independent of AI trends. If anything, AI data center buildout increases electricity demand, which could increase gold's correlation with energy-intensive industrial activity. AI Opportunity (0/1): B2Gold generates no AI-related revenue. The company's product is physical gold, and its operations do not include AI-powered services or AI infrastructure. Mining automation exists but is not a revenue-generating AI product.
