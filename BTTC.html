Company Profile
Financial Services ·Digital Assets & Blockchain ·NASDAQ ·Malaysia
Black Titan Corporation is a digital asset treasury company formed in July 2024 from the merger of Titan Pharmaceuticals Inc. and TalenTec Sdn Bhd. Headquartered in Petaling Jaya, Malaysia, the company deploys a Digital Asset Treasury Plus (DAT+) strategy focused on strategic digital currency investments, direct token acquisitions, blockchain infrastructure, and fintech M&A. Trades on NASDAQ with an $8M market cap.

Investment Thesis
Black Titan Corporation is a nano-cap ($8M) digital asset treasury company deploying a DAT+ strategy through a $200M convertible note facility secured in January 2026, with expansion into Indonesia and Singapore announced in July 2026 [1][2]. The zero-debt balance sheet and access to institutional capital provide runway for the strategy. Co-CEO Czhang Lin brings 8 years of blockchain expertise since 2017, and growing institutional adoption of digital assets could create tailwinds if the treasury strategy is executed properly [3].
The bear case is overwhelming. BTTC has declined 97% from its $39 IPO high to $0.75, destroying shareholder value in under a year [2]. Daily dollar volume of ~$56K makes the stock essentially untradeable for any institutional investor. Three co-CEO appointments in 8 months raise serious governance concerns. The $200M convertible note facility represents potential dilution of 15x+ the current $8M equity value if fully drawn and converted [1]. Revenue of $2.68M TTM with uncertain profitability, no analyst coverage, and no track record of successful digital asset treasury management make this a speculative nano-cap with no margin of safety. DeFi yield strategies carry catastrophic risk including smart contract exploits, stablecoin de-pegging, and regulatory crackdowns in Southeast Asian jurisdictions.
