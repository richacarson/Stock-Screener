Company Profile
Technology ·Digital Assets / Blockchain ·NASDAQ ·United States
Black Titan Corporation is a digital asset technology company formed from the October 2025 merger of Titan Pharmaceuticals and TalenTec. The company pursues a Digital Asset Treasury Plus (DAT+) strategy, using its corporate balance sheet to support, govern, and provide liquidity to decentralized protocols. BTTC has a co-CEO structure with Czhang Lin (blockchain veteran from LBank Labs/Binance Labs) overseeing cryptocurrency strategy. The company secured a $200M convertible note facility and signed an MOU with PyratzLabs for blockchain partnership. Revenue TTM is approximately $2.68M with a market cap of ~$10.9M. The stock has declined 97% from its 2025 IPO high of $39.00.

Investment Thesis
Black Titan offers speculative exposure to the digital asset ecosystem through a publicly traded vehicle with a $200M convertible note facility and a co-CEO (Czhang Lin) with blockchain credentials from LBank Labs and Binance Labs [1][2]. The DAT+ strategy of providing liquidity and governance to decentralized protocols could theoretically generate returns if DeFi adoption accelerates. The MOU with PyratzLabs suggests a partnership-driven approach to building blockchain infrastructure. At a sub-$11M market cap, any meaningful business development could generate outsized returns on a percentage basis.
The bear case is overwhelming. BTTC has declined 97% from its IPO high of $39.00 to $0.80, destroying virtually all shareholder value in under a year [3][4]. Revenue of $2.68M TTM and market cap of $10.9M place this firmly in penny stock territory. All core financial metrics are unavailable from standard data providers, the company was formed from a three-way merger less than a year ago, and the DAT+ strategy remains conceptual with no demonstrated revenue generation from digital asset operations. The $200M convertible note facility creates massive dilution risk for existing shareholders. Regulatory uncertainty about whether active DeFi engagement classifies BTTC as an unregistered investment company under the Investment Company Act of 1940 is an existential governance risk. No institutional coverage, no analyst estimates, and no track record of execution.
Key Catalysts
  1. Successful deployment of capital from the $200M convertible note facility into DeFi protocols or digital assets that generate measurable returns, validating the DAT+ strategy [2]
  2. PyratzLabs partnership moving from MOU to a binding agreement with specific revenue-generating activities or technology development milestones [2]
  3. Broader cryptocurrency market rally lifting digital asset valuations and creating favorable conditions for BTTC's treasury holdings and DeFi participation
Key Risks
  1. 97% stock decline from IPO suggests the market has already priced in severe execution failure, and the $200M convertible note facility creates massive dilution risk that could further compress per-share value [3]
  2. Regulatory classification risk — if the SEC determines BTTC's active DeFi engagement constitutes operating as an unregistered investment company, the company faces potential enforcement action [1]
  3. No established revenue model, no institutional coverage, and sub-$11M market cap create existential viability risk — the company may not generate sufficient operating income to sustain itself as a public entity [4]
