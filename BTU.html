Company Profile
Energy ·Thermal Coal ·NYSE ·United States
Peabody Energy Corporation is the world's largest private-sector coal company, mining and marketing coal for electricity generation and steelmaking. Operations span the United States (Powder River Basin, Illinois Basin, Appalachia) and Australia (Queensland, New South Wales). The company produces both thermal coal for power generation and metallurgical coal for steel production. Emerged from Chapter 11 bankruptcy in 2017. Headquartered in St. Louis, Missouri.
6,800 Employees ·www.peabodyenergy.com

Investment Thesis
Peabody offers a compelling deep-value proposition at 5.5x forward earnings with a 4.9% dividend yield, zero net debt, and $900M+ liquidity — the market is pricing terminal value risk that may be premature given AI-driven electricity demand extending coal plant lifetimes [1][2]. The Centurion premium met coal ramp (targeting 3.5M tons in 2026 with sevenfold shipment increase) should boost met coal realizations to ~80% of benchmark and become the lowest-cost met coal mine in the portfolio, driving significant FCF expansion. The 65-100% FCF return framework and 27% payout ratio provide substantial room for dividend increases and buybacks. At $22.68, investors are paid 4.9% to wait for the met coal ramp to deliver.
The bear case is structural: coal is a secularly declining energy source in developed markets as renewables achieve cost parity and governments tighten emissions regulation [1]. Revenue declined -15.0% YoY in the most recent period, and seaborne thermal volumes face long-term headwinds from the global energy transition. Profit margin of 11.9% reflects commodity pricing pressure with limited ability to differentiate. The bankruptcy history (emerged 2017) creates governance and trust overhang, and the short dividend track record (~4 years) offers limited evidence of sustainability through a full commodity cycle. Peabody's refusal to diversify beyond coal means the company's entire future is a single-commodity bet [2].
Key Catalysts
  1. Centurion premium met coal ramp targeting 3.5M tons in 2026 — expected to become the lowest-cost met coal mine in the portfolio and boost met coal realizations to ~80% of benchmark, driving material FCF expansion [2]
  2. AI data center electricity demand extending operational life of U.S. coal plants beyond current retirement schedules, supporting thermal coal volumes at the 82-88Mt PRB guidance level [1][2]
  3. 65-100% FCF return framework with a 27% payout ratio and zero net debt providing significant capacity for dividend increases, buybacks, or special distributions as Centurion ramps [1]
Key Risks
  1. Secular decline of thermal coal as renewables achieve cost parity globally — revenue already declining -15.0% YoY, and accelerating coal plant retirements could outpace AI-driven demand additions [1]
  2. Commodity price sensitivity with limited pricing power — met coal benchmark volatility and seaborne thermal pricing are driven by global supply/demand dynamics Peabody cannot control [2]
  3. Short dividend history (~4 years post-bankruptcy) provides no evidence of sustainability through a full commodity down-cycle, and the 2017 bankruptcy demonstrates the existential risk inherent in a single-commodity business model [1]

AI Risk Protection (8/9): Peabody's core business — extracting coal from the earth through surface and underground mining operations — is entirely physical-world and structurally immune to AI substitution [1]. Coal mining requires heavy equipment, geological expertise, safety management, regulatory compliance (MSHA), land access, and logistics infrastructure that AI cannot replace. Furthermore, the growing electricity demand from AI data centers is actually increasing demand for baseload power generation, including coal-fired electricity in regions where coal plants remain the marginal provider [2]. Peabody's management has stated that U.S. coal plants are 'here for the long term' partly due to AI-driven power demand [2]. Metallurgical coal for steelmaking is even more immune — no AI substitute exists for the coking process in blast furnace steel production. AI Opportunity (0/1): No AI-generated revenue.
