Webull Corporation operates a digital investment platform enabling self-directed retail investors to trade equities, options, ETFs, cryptocurrencies, futures, and corporate bonds across 15+ countries. The company deployed its AI-powered Vega platform (1.2M weekly users) and launched Vega Portfolio for agentic portfolio construction. Q2 2026 revenue reached $198.8M (+51% YoY) with $62.6M adjusted operating profit. Founded in 2016 by Anquan Wang, went public via SPAC merger in April 2025, headquartered in Saint Petersburg, Florida. Group President Anthony Denier leads operations. Market cap ~$3.2B.

Investment Thesis
Webull at $5.95 with a ~$3.2B market cap offers a high-growth fintech platform accelerating on multiple vectors: Q2 2026 revenue surged 51% YoY to $198.8M with adjusted operating profit tripling to $62.6M, customer assets grew 79% to $28.5B, and funded accounts reached 5.13M [1]. At ~4.8x TTM revenue ($672M) with a forward P/E of 19.2x, Webull trades at a discount to Robinhood while demonstrating comparable or superior growth rates. The AI strategy is tangible — Vega serves 1.2M weekly users, Vega Portfolio and MCP server shipped, and the FINRA Pattern Day Trader rule change on June 4 was 'the defining event' expanding the active trader addressable market [1][2]. The nearly debt-free balance sheet ($1.93B cash, D/E 7.5%) provides substantial runway for investment and global expansion across 15+ countries.
The bear case centers on governance risk, cultural weakness, and competitive intensity. Founder Wang Anquan's 79.2% voting control on 16.4% economic ownership creates a dual-class structure limiting public shareholder influence and board independence [3]. Glassdoor rating of 2.3/5 with 26% CEO approval signals deeply problematic workplace culture that could impair talent retention and execution quality [2]. Payment for order flow constituted 53%+ of 2025 revenue, creating existential risk if regulatory action eliminates PFOF — a scenario under active SEC consideration [3]. The stock has declined 19% from $7.32 since August 2026 (and substantially more from post-SPAC highs), reflecting market skepticism about governance and profitability sustainability. Trailing P/E of 74.4x indicates that GAAP profitability remains thin despite the strong Q2. Past FINRA fines and compliance deficiencies in options approval and customer complaint handling highlight systemic regulatory risk for a rapidly growing brokerage [3].
