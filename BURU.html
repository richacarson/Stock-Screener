Company Profile
Industrials ·Specialty Industrial Machinery ·NYSE American ·United States
NUBURU, Inc. develops high-power, high-brightness blue laser technology originally targeted at welding and 3D printing applications for EV batteries, medical devices, and displays. Since late 2025 the company has pivoted toward a Defense & Security ecosystem combining laser dazzler counter-drone systems, directed-energy platforms, and AI-enabled mission software, anchored by a pending acquisition of a 70% stake in Italian defense firm Tekne S.p.A. Headquartered in the United States, the company operates under a dual-CEO structure installed in October 2025 and faces active NYSE American delisting proceedings.

Investment Thesis
Nuburu is a micro-cap laser technology company attempting a survival pivot from industrial blue-laser welding into defense and security, anchored by directed-energy and counter-drone laser dazzler programs and a pending 70% acquisition of Italian defense firm Tekne S.p.A. [1][2]. TTM revenue grew 595.9% to $932.57K, reflecting early defense-related contract wins, and the Lyocon portable directed-energy platform reached proof-of-concept in March 2026, giving the pivot some technical credibility [1][2].
