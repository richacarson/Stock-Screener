Company Profile
Industrials ·Specialty Industrial Machinery ·NYSE American ·United States
NUBURU, Inc. develops high-power, high-brightness blue laser technology originally targeted at welding and 3D printing applications for EV batteries, medical devices, and displays. Since late 2025 the company has pivoted toward a Defense & Security ecosystem combining laser dazzler counter-drone systems, directed-energy platforms, and AI-enabled mission software, anchored by its completed Lyocon acquisition and pending 70% stake in Italian defense firm Tekne S.p.A. The company operates under a dual-CEO structure (Alessandro Zamboni and Dario Barisoni) and recently completed a reverse stock split. Former National Security Advisor John Bolton serves on the board. TTM revenue is approximately $932K with Q2 2026 revenue of $525K.
