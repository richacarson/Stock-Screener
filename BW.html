Company Profile
Industrials ·Specialty Industrial Machinery ·NYSE ·United States
Babcock & Wilcox Enterprises is a 157-year-old technology-based provider of steam generation, environmental, and power generation equipment and services for power and industrial markets worldwide. The company operates through B&W Renewable (sustainable power), B&W Environmental (emissions control), and B&W Thermal (steam generation) segments. B&W is pivoting into AI data center power infrastructure with a $2.4B contract with Base Electron and a global pipeline exceeding $14B. Q2 2026 revenue surged 130% to $319.7M with $100.7M from Base Electron. Headquartered in Akron, Ohio.

Investment Thesis
Babcock & Wilcox is a 157-year-old industrial company executing a dramatic transformation into AI data center power infrastructure. Q2 2026 delivered 130% revenue growth to $319.7M — including $100.7M from the $2.4B Base Electron AI data center contract — and adjusted EBITDA of $21.8M [4]. FY2026 EBITDA guidance was raised to $80-105M, nearly doubling from FY2025's $43.7M. Total backlog exceeds $2.7B (up 483% YoY) with the global pipeline exceeding $14B [1][3]. The stock has re-rated from $11.74 to $17.04 as the market recognizes the pivot's commercial traction. At forward P/E of 33.79 with a $14B+ pipeline and AI data center power demand accelerating, B&W offers significant upside if execution continues. The Inspire Impact Score of 45 (ALIGNED) with zero negative attributions supports faith-based investor interest [6].
The bear case centers on execution risk, governance concerns, and balance sheet fragility. The class action lawsuit (Cho v. B&W, April 2026) alleges management failed to disclose that BRC Group Holdings' co-CEO was a Base Electron director and that Base Electron was not incorporated until seven weeks after the preliminary deal announcement — raising serious related-party transaction concerns [5]. BRC subsequently sold its entire B&W stake at 140% above pre-announcement levels. B&W carries deeply negative stockholders' equity of approximately -$232M with accumulated losses exceeding $1.7B, and GAAP profit margin remains at -15.24% despite operational improvement [2]. Forward P/E of 33.79 prices in significant earnings improvement that depends on the $2.4B contract executing without cost overruns — a challenging assumption given B&W's history of thin margins on large-scale industrial projects. The average daily volume decline from 5.2M to 3.6M shares suggests some trading interest has faded after the initial AI data center euphoria.
