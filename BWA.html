Company Profile
Consumer Discretionary ·Auto Parts & Equipment ·NYSE ·United States
BorgWarner Inc. is a global product leader in innovative and sustainable mobility solutions for the vehicle market, with operations in 22 countries. Products include turbochargers, e-motors, power electronics, battery systems, and thermal management solutions for passenger cars, commercial vehicles, and off-highway equipment. The company is expanding into AI data center power generation through its TurboCell turbine technology. Q2 2026 revenue of $3.65B beat estimates with adjusted operating margin rising 100bps to 11.3%, and management raised full-year adjusted EPS guidance to $5.05-$5.30. Stock up 39.6% YTD under new CEO Joseph Fadool.

Investment Thesis
BorgWarner delivered strong Q2 2026 results with $3.65B revenue beating estimates, adjusted operating margin rising 100bps to 11.3%, and management raising full-year adjusted EPS guidance to $5.05-$5.30 — all while the stock has rallied 39.6% YTD to $65.10 [1]. At 12.88x forward earnings with PEG of 1.03 and D/E of 70%, BWA trades at a reasonable valuation for a diversified powertrain supplier with an emerging AI data center power opportunity through TurboCell targeting 2027 production [3]. The 12 new product awards in Q1 2026 provide multi-year revenue visibility, FY2026 FCF guidance of $900M-$1.1B supports continued capital return ($185M in Q1 alone), and the Inspire Impact Score of 65 (ALIGNED) with zero negative attributions and 19 positive ESG attributes makes BWA one of the highest Inspire-rated stocks in the automotive sector [1]. CEO Fadool's operational discipline is expanding margins despite a challenging global auto production environment.
The bear case centers on structural automotive headwinds and transformation execution risk. FY2026 revenue guidance of $14.0-14.3B implies flat to negative growth versus FY2025's $14.3B as weighted light vehicle production is expected down 3% to flat, and organic revenue declined 4.2% in Q1 [1][2]. GAAP profit margin of 2.89% TTM reflects restructuring charges, Battery Energy Systems segment underperformance, and the inherent margin dilution of transitioning from mature ICE products to early-stage electrified components. ROE of 8.06%, while improved, remains well below the 15% target. The TurboCell AI data center power initiative is entirely unproven — manufacturing turbine generators for static power is a fundamental departure from automotive-scale production, and delays past the 2027 timeline would damage credibility [3]. The legacy ICE turbocharger business faces secular decline as EV adoption accelerates, creating a race to replace declining revenue with new electrified and industrial streams before the ICE decline accelerates beyond management's ability to offset.
Key Catalysts
  1. TurboCell turbine generator production launch in 2027 for AI data center power, opening a new addressable market and validating BorgWarner's industrial diversification beyond declining ICE automotive [3]
  2. Continued adjusted operating margin expansion toward 11%+ as restructuring benefits, cost discipline, and favorable product mix shift toward higher-margin ePropulsion components compound [1]
  3. FY2026 FCF guidance of $900M-$1.1B supports accelerated capital return through buybacks and potential dividend increases, with $185M returned in Q1 alone and stock up 39.6% YTD demonstrating shareholder value creation [1]
Key Risks
  1. EV adoption accelerating faster than expected could compress turbocharger and ICE powertrain demand — still the majority of BWA revenue — before eMobility and TurboCell revenues scale to offset the decline
  2. TurboCell data center power initiative faces material execution risk as manufacturing turbine generators for static power is a fundamental departure from automotive-scale production with no proven track record [3]
  3. GAAP profit margin of 2.89% and revenue guidance of $14.0-14.3B (flat to down) signal that the operational transformation, while improving margins, has not yet restored top-line growth or GAAP profitability to levels commensurate with the stock's 39.6% YTD rally [1][2]
