Company Profile
Financial Services ·Insurance Brokers ·NASDAQ ·United States
The Baldwin Insurance Group is an independent insurance distribution firm providing risk management solutions across three segments: Insurance Advisory Solutions (private risk, commercial risk, employee benefits), Underwriting, Capacity & Technology Solutions (technology-enabled MGA platform), and Mainstreet Insurance Solutions (personal and commercial insurance). Formerly BRP Group, renamed May 2024. Q2 2026 revenue grew 30% YoY to $492.9M with adjusted EBITDA of $116.7M (23.7% margin). Full-year 2026 guidance of $2.01B-$2.05B. The company announced an expanded enterprise AI partnership with Anthropic in May 2026 and is executing a '$3B/30 Catalyst' transformation program targeting 30% margins. Market cap ~$3.1B.

Investment Thesis
Baldwin Insurance Group at 13.1x forward earnings is executing an aggressive platform build that could inflect to profitability and organic growth acceleration in 2026-2027. Q2 2026 delivered $492.9M revenue (+30% YoY) with adjusted EBITDA of $116.7M (+37%, 23.7% margin — a 110bps expansion), and the company raised full-year guidance to $2.01B-$2.05B revenue [1]. Integration of the January 2026 CAC/OBE/Capstone acquisitions is running ahead of schedule with $34M of $43M synergies already actioned. The Anthropic AI enterprise partnership positions Baldwin as a technology-forward distributor, with the '$3B/30 Catalyst' program targeting 30% EBITDA margins through AI-enabled process redesign [2]. At $32.49, the stock has rallied 23% from July lows, reflecting market recognition of the earnings inflection — forward EPS of $2.48 implies a meaningful swing from the trailing loss of -$1.05.
The bear case centers on organic growth weakness, leverage, and governance risk. Organic revenue growth remains a meager 2% in both Q1 and Q2 2026, meaning nearly all reported growth is acquisition-driven — if deal flow slows or synergies disappoint, the growth narrative collapses [1]. GAAP profitability remains negative (trailing profit margin -4.7%, ROE -10.7%) despite $1.74B in revenue, with heavy amortization and integration costs masking underlying economics. D/E of 180.5% with $2.57B in debt against $184M cash creates refinancing risk and interest rate sensitivity [4]. The dual-class share structure concentrating 70%+ voting power with founders, combined with go-private discussions reported in mid-2026, creates governance risk for minority Class A shareholders who could face a take-private at an unattractive price [3]. Insurance brokerage faces real AI disintermediation risk in personal lines and small commercial segments, even as Baldwin invests in AI offensively through the Anthropic partnership. Net insider selling in 2026 raises questions about management conviction at current prices.
Key Catalysts
  1. Organic revenue growth acceleration toward management's double-digit exit-rate target for 2026, with remaining $9M of CAC synergies and Anthropic AI-driven productivity gains providing levers for margin expansion toward the 30% EBITDA target [1][2]
  2. GAAP profitability inflection — forward EPS of $2.48 vs trailing loss of -$1.05 suggests 2026/2027 will mark the transition from GAAP-unprofitable roll-up to profitable platform, which could trigger multiple re-rating [4]

Key Risks
  1. Organic revenue growth of only 2% in both Q1 and Q2 2026 exposes heavy reliance on acquisitions for topline growth — the $2.57B debt-funded roll-up strategy becomes unsustainable if organic growth cannot accelerate and new deal flow slows [1]
  2. D/E of 180.5% with $2.57B total debt and -4.7% GAAP profit margin creates financial fragility — integration stumbles, rising rates, or a recession could stress the balance sheet before the platform reaches self-sustaining profitability [4]
