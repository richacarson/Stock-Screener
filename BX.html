Blackstone is the world's largest alternative asset management firm with approximately $1.27T in total AUM across private equity, real estate, credit & insurance, and hedge fund solutions. Co-founded by Stephen Schwarzman in 1985, Blackstone has grown from a boutique advisory firm into a global financial powerhouse. H1 2026 revenue reached $8.7B (+24% YoY), with Q2 distributable earnings of $2.0B ($1.52/sh, +26% YoY) and fee-related earnings of $1.8B (+22%). Investment Thesis
Blackstone at $109.98 (14.8x forward earnings) offers exposure to the world's dominant alternative asset platform at a significant discount to its August 2026 levels — H1 2026 revenue grew 24% YoY to $8.7B, Q2 distributable earnings rose 26% to $2.0B ($1.52/share), and fee-related earnings grew 22% to $1.8B [1]. The 4.76% dividend yield provides meaningful income, and the Forward P/E of 14.8x represents a rare sub-15x entry point for a platform generating $2B+ in quarterly distributable earnings. The firm's early positioning in AI infrastructure ($50B+ data center portfolio) provides a differentiated growth vector, while perpetual capital vehicles (BREIT, BCRED, insurance) reduce earnings cyclicality with stable management fees that grow with AUM [1][2]. Management expects a return to double-digit base fee growth by 2027.
The bear case centers on distribution sustainability and cyclical exposure. The 111% payout ratio reflects BX's variable distribution model tied to realizations rather than stable cash flows — dividends fell 33% in 2023 ($3.32 vs $4.94 in 2022) and have achieved only 2 consecutive years of growth since, demonstrating the income stream's unreliability for dividend-focused investors [1]. The stock trades 15% below its 50-day SMA of $129.89, reflecting market concern about near-term realization activity and growth sustainability at $1.27T AUM. A Semafor report noted Blackstone limited withdrawals from a flagship credit fund earlier in 2026 over exposure to indebted tech companies, raising credit quality questions [2]. Fossil fuel investment disclosure gaps and Schwarzman's $27M+ political donations create ESG credibility concerns with sustainability-mandated LPs who have conditioned capital commitments on ESG standards [4]. At $82.6B market cap, BX requires sustained AUM growth and realization velocity to justify the premium over tangible book value.

Key Risks

  2. Credit fund withdrawal restrictions and leveraged tech exposure — Blackstone limited redemptions from a flagship credit fund in 2026 over indebted tech company exposure, raising liquidity and credit quality concerns during elevated interest rates [2]
  3. ESG and reputational headwinds — incomplete fossil fuel disclosure, Schwarzman's political donations conflicting with ESG commitments, and anti-ESG backlash creating operational risk with sustainability-mandated LPs who have conditioned capital commitments on ESG standards [4]
