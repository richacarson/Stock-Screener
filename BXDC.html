Company Profile
Real Estate ·REIT - Data Center ·NYSE ·United States
Blackstone Digital Infrastructure Trust Inc. is a newly organized REIT focused on acquiring and owning mission-critical data center assets that power the modern digital economy. The company targets newly-constructed, income-generating, stabilized data center properties leased to investment-grade hyperscale tenants on long-term contracts. IPO'd in May 2026 at $20/share, raising $1.75B. Externally managed by an affiliate of Blackstone, the world's largest alternative asset manager and largest financial investor in data center and digital infrastructure assets globally.

Investment Thesis
BXDC offers public market access to Blackstone's premier data center investment franchise at a critical inflection point for AI infrastructure demand. The REIT targets stabilized, newly-constructed data centers leased to investment-grade hyperscale tenants (Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Meta) on long-term contracts with 2-3% annual rent escalations, providing visibility into stable cash flow generation [1][4]. Blackstone has identified approximately $25B in near-term acquisition opportunities across key U.S. data center markets, and the parent firm's $1.35T AUM and 6+ year track record in digital infrastructure (QTS, AirTrunk, Digital Realty JV) provides unmatched deal sourcing and execution capability [2]. Expected acquisition yields of 5.75-7% on stabilized assets, combined with contractual rent growth, position BXDC to deliver attractive total returns as the data center market scales toward what Blackstone projects as a $1T total addressable market.
The primary concern is BXDC's complete lack of operating history as a public company — there are no reported financials, no proven dividend track record, and no visibility into actual portfolio performance [1]. The external management structure means Blackstone earns fees regardless of BXDC's stock price performance, creating potential misalignment with public shareholders. At ~$20.87 per share (near IPO price of $20), the market is pricing in execution risk despite Blackstone's strong brand. Competition from established data center REITs (Equinix at 50+ facilities, Digital Realty with $6B+ revenue) means BXDC must build scale rapidly to achieve comparable operating efficiencies. The concentrated bet on hyperscale AI demand creates binary risk — any slowdown in AI infrastructure spending would directly impact BXDC's acquisition pipeline and asset valuations [5].
Key Catalysts
  1. First quarterly earnings report expected Q3 2026 providing initial visibility into portfolio performance, rental income, and occupancy metrics for acquired data center assets
  2. Deployment of $1.75B IPO proceeds into identified $25B pipeline of stabilized data center opportunities across Northern Virginia, Ohio, Phoenix, Maryland, and Austin markets
  3. Initiation of dividend payments as acquired assets begin generating stabilized rental income, potentially establishing BXDC as an income vehicle for AI infrastructure exposure
Key Risks
  1. Zero operating history — no reported revenue, earnings, or dividend track record to validate the investment thesis; infrastructure score cannot be mechanically calculated
  2. External management structure creates potential fee misalignment: Blackstone earns management fees regardless of BXDC stock performance, and potential conflicts arise if Blackstone allocates best deals to its private funds over the public REIT
  3. Concentrated hyperscale AI demand risk — any deceleration in cloud provider CapEx spending or shift in data center technology (edge computing, quantum) could impair acquisition pipeline and asset valuations
