Company Profile
Communication Services ·Internet Content & Information ·NASDAQ ·China
Kanzhun Limited operates BOSS Zhipin, China's largest online recruitment platform with 70.2 million monthly active users and 7.1 million paid enterprise customers. The platform uses AI-powered recommendation algorithms and its proprietary Nanbeige large language model to connect job seekers with employers through a direct real-time chat model, covering blue-collar, white-collar, and professional positions. Q2 2026 revenue reached RMB2.40B ($354M), up 14.1% YoY. The company commits at least 50% of prior-year adjusted net income to dividends and buybacks ($400M authorization). Founded in 2014 by CEO Peng Zhao, headquartered in Beijing, China. Market cap ~$8.0B.
4,884 Employees ·ir.zhipin.com

Investment Thesis
Kanzhun operates China's dominant recruitment platform (70.2M MAU, 7.1M paid enterprise customers) with exceptional and improving financials — Q2 2026 revenue of RMB2.40B ($354M) grew 14.1% YoY (accelerating from Q1's 7.6%), operating income surged 32.6%, and the company maintains a 52.85% profit margin on 85.91% gross margins [2][5]. At 12.50x forward P/E with a PEG of 1.33, BZ trades at a deep China discount for a profitable, growing tech platform with a $3.05B net cash fortress balance sheet [5]. The Nanbeige 4.1-3B model (ranked #1 globally among sub-4B parameter models) and DeepSeek-R1 integration power AI-enhanced recruitment features [4]. Management commits at least 50% of adjusted net income through buybacks ($400M authorization) and dividends (0.94% yield), providing shareholder return visibility [2]. Q3 guidance of 11.4-15.6% revenue growth signals sustained momentum. The stock has recovered 32% from July lows, but remains well below its 2021 IPO levels.
The bear case centres on China macro risk, regulatory uncertainty, and the structural China discount. As a Chinese ADR on NASDAQ (also HKEX: 2076), Kanzhun faces VIE structure risk, potential HFCAA delisting threats, and regulatory unpredictability from PRC authorities — the company was subject to a cybersecurity review and new user suspension shortly after its 2021 IPO [4]. China's challenging economic environment pressures employer recruitment spending, and while Q2 growth accelerated to 14.1%, this is below the 20%+ 5-year CAGR, suggesting growth rate normalization as the platform matures. The 120% YoY Q1 net income growth is not sustainable and reflects low prior-year comparables. AI is both enabler and potential disruptor — generalist AI tools could eventually bypass specialized recruitment platforms if employers use ChatGPT/DeepSeek to source candidates directly. International expansion remains limited, keeping BZ dependent on the Chinese labour market. Despite exceptional fundamentals, the China discount may persist indefinitely given U.S.-China geopolitical tensions, and the Inspire Impact Score of 0 (MIXED) provides no faith-alignment catalyst.
Key Catalysts
  1. Revenue growth re-acceleration from Q1's 7.6% to Q2's 14.1% with Q3 guidance of 11.4-15.6% demonstrates sustained momentum that could narrow the China discount at 12.50x forward P/E [2]
  2. Nanbeige LLM and DeepSeek-R1 AI integration driving operating leverage — operating income grew 32.6% on 14.1% revenue growth in Q2, with further margin expansion possible as AI chatbot for blue-collar batch hiring scales [2][4]
  3. Capital return program targeting 50%+ of adjusted net income through $400M buyback authorization and newly initiated dividends provides downside protection and signals management confidence in cash generation durability [2]
Key Risks
  1. China macro and regulatory risk — VIE structure, HFCAA delisting threat, PRC government cybersecurity scrutiny, and potential regulatory intervention could destroy shareholder value regardless of fundamentals; the 2021 cybersecurity review demonstrates this risk is real, not theoretical [3][4]
  2. Revenue growth normalization — Q2's 14.1% growth is well below the 20%+ 5-year CAGR, and continued Chinese economic weakness could further pressure employer recruitment spending, particularly in the blue-collar and SME segments that are BZ's core market [2]
  3. AI platform disruption — generalist AI tools and larger Chinese tech platforms (Alibaba, ByteDance, Tencent) could disintermediate specialized recruitment by enabling direct employer-candidate AI matching without BOSS Zhipin's intermediation [4]
