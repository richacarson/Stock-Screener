Company Profile
Communication Services ·Internet Content & Information ·NASDAQ ·China
Kanzhun Limited operates BOSS Zhipin, China's largest mobile online recruitment platform, which connects job seekers and employers through a direct real-time chat model powered by AI recommendation and its proprietary Nanbeige recruitment large language model. Q2 2026 revenue was about RMB2.40B (+14.1% YoY), income from operations rose 32.6% to RMB863.2M, average monthly active users reached 70.2M (+10.4%), and paid enterprise customers reached 7.2M (+10.8%). Q3 2026 revenue guidance is RMB2.41-2.50B (+11.4% to 15.6%). FY2025 revenue was RMB8.27B with net income of RMB2.74B. The company holds roughly RMB21.0B in cash (per Yahoo Finance) against minimal debt, declared an annual dividend of about $230M, and completed more than $300M of buybacks year to date. Founded in 2014 by CEO Jonathan Peng Zhao; headquartered in Beijing.

Finite vs Infinite Game — Sinek (25%)
Mindset: FINITE Overall: 4 /10
Through Sinek's lens, Kanzhun is a well-run, founder-led operator still playing a mostly finite game. It survived a year-long 2021-2022 registration ban by rectifying its data practices and kept investing roughly 20% of revenue in R&D. But its stated purpose is about efficiency, not an ideal future state, and there is little public evidence of a Just Cause that would outlast its founder.

Investment Thesis
Kanzhun is a high-quality cash machine trading at a value multiple. BOSS Zhipin grew revenue 14.1% in Q2 2026 to RMB2.40B, with operating income up 32.6%, and guides to 11-16% growth in Q3 [2]. Revenue has compounded at 33.6% a year since 2020, ROE is 23.9%, debt is essentially zero, and the stock trades at 11x forward earnings with a ~3% dividend yield plus active buybacks [5]. The proprietary Nanbeige LLM and AI agents are driving record retention and a 60% efficiency gain, and AI-related job postings more than doubled in Q1 2026, so AI so far adds to the platform's demand [3][4].
The bear case starts with China. The 2021 CAC review froze new registrations for about a year and triggered shareholder suits, which shows how quickly regulators can halt growth [7]. Growth has slowed from 30%+ to low-teens, job-seeker paid services are shrinking (RMB14.6M vs RMB24.8M), and a weak Chinese labor market or AI-driven white-collar hiring cuts could cap enterprise spend [2][4]. The TTM 52.8% profit margin is flattered by investment income, and the 5-year PEG of 1.56 shows growth now priced near fair value. ADR and VIE structure risk remains for U.S. holders.
Key Catalysts
  1. Q3 2026 results against RMB2.41-2.50B guidance (+11.4% to 15.6%); an upside print would confirm the Q2 growth re-acceleration [2]
  2. Monetization of AI agents and the results-oriented recruitment model built on the proprietary Nanbeige LLM, alongside AI-related job-posting revenue that more than doubled in Q1 2026 [3][4]
  3. Continued capital returns: an annual dividend of ~$230M and more than $300M of 2026 buybacks against a ~$7.2B market cap [2]
Key Risks
  1. Chinese regulatory intervention: the 2021-2022 CAC cybersecurity review halted new user registration for about a year [7]
  2. AI-driven reductions in white-collar hiring volume shrinking employer recruiting budgets, which management acknowledges as a gradual labor-market effect [4]
  3. Growth deceleration and macro weakness in China's job market, with job-seeker value-added services already down ~41% YoY in Q2 2026 [2]

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience MODERATE
6
AI Risk Protection (5/9): Online recruitment is exposed to AI. AI matching agents could commoditize job-board intermediation, and AI-driven automation could shrink white-collar hiring volume, which CEO Zhao has acknowledged as a gradual labor-market effect [3][4]. BOSS Zhipin's defense is its two-sided network of 70.2M MAUs and 7.2M paying employers, plus its own recruitment LLM. Caixin's data shows software-engineer postings still grew 10.9% in early 2026, so AI has not yet dented hiring [2][4]. AI Opportunity (1/1): AI-related job-posting revenue more than doubled in Q1 2026, and AI agents are shipping to both sides of the platform; management says AI's opportunities outweigh its risks for its model [3][4].
