Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Cabaletta Bio, Inc. is a clinical-stage biotechnology company focused on the discovery and development of engineered T cell therapies for patients with B cell-mediated autoimmune diseases. The company's lead product candidate, rese-cel, is a CD19-targeted chimeric autoantibody receptor T cell (CAAR-T) therapy being evaluated in clinical trials for systemic sclerosis, lupus, and other autoimmune conditions. Founded in 2017, headquartered in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.
200 Employees ·www.cabalettabio.com

Investment Thesis
Cabaletta Bio is pioneering CAAR-T cell therapy for autoimmune diseases, targeting a potentially transformative approach to systemic sclerosis, lupus, and other B cell-mediated conditions where conventional treatments are inadequate. The $150M capital raise in May 2026 extends runway into mid-2027, and the Cellares commercial supply agreement positions the company for potential scalable manufacturing if rese-cel achieves clinical success [1][3]. FDA clearance for automated CAR-T manufacturing represents a regulatory milestone that could reduce manufacturing costs and improve consistency [4]. At a $501M market cap with $266M in projected cash, the stock trades near cash value.
The primary risk is binary clinical outcome — rese-cel is unproven at scale, and the grade 3 ICANS event in a systemic sclerosis patient demonstrated the safety risks inherent in CAR-T therapy for autoimmune patients who are often medically fragile [5]. The company burns approximately $44M per quarter with zero revenue, creating an inevitable need for additional dilutive capital raises beyond mid-2027 [1]. The stock has declined from higher levels to $3.08, reflecting market skepticism about the timeline to commercialization and the competitive landscape as larger companies (Bristol Myers Squibb, Kyverna) pursue similar autoimmune CAR-T approaches with greater resources. Cabaletta has no revenue, no approved products, and no clear path to profitability within the next 3-5 years.
Key Catalysts
  1. Positive rese-cel clinical trial data readouts in systemic sclerosis and lupus that demonstrate durable remission without the safety concerns flagged by the ICANS event [5]
  2. FDA regulatory milestones including potential Breakthrough Therapy Designation for rese-cel in one or more autoimmune indications, which would accelerate the development timeline
  3. Strategic partnership or licensing deal with a larger pharmaceutical company that validates the CAAR-T platform and provides non-dilutive funding beyond the current mid-2027 runway [3]
Key Risks
  1. Binary clinical risk — rese-cel failure in pivotal trials would eliminate the company's primary value driver, and the grade 3 ICANS event signals potential safety headwinds for CAR-T in autoimmune populations [5]
  2. Cash burn of ~$44M per quarter with zero revenue requires additional dilutive capital raises beyond mid-2027, further compressing per-share value for existing shareholders [1]
  3. Competitive pressure from larger, better-funded companies (Bristol Myers Squibb, Kyverna Therapeutics) pursuing CAR-T for autoimmune diseases with more resources and established commercial infrastructure
