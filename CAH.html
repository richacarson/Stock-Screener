Company Profile
Healthcare ·Medical Distribution ·NYSE ·United States
Cardinal Health is one of the 'Big Three' U.S. pharmaceutical and medical product distributors, serving over 90% of U.S. hospitals and approximately 40,000 pharmacies. The company operates through three segments: Pharmaceutical (branded and specialty drug distribution), Global Medical Products Distribution (GMPD — medical, surgical and laboratory products), and Other (at-Home Solutions, Nuclear & Precision Health Solutions, OptiFreight Logistics). FY2026 revenue reached $254.2B (+14% YoY) with non-GAAP EPS of $11.26 (+37%), driven by branded and specialty pharmaceutical demand and the MSO platform buildout.

Investment Thesis
Cardinal Health delivered strong FY2026 results with revenue of $254.2B (+14% YoY) and non-GAAP EPS of $11.26 (+37%), driven by branded and specialty pharmaceutical demand, MSO platform acquisitions, and the GMPD turnaround that produced 91% segment profit growth [1]. The Pharmaceutical segment generated $2.8B in profit (+23%), demonstrating the earnings power of scale in drug distribution. At 16.7x forward earnings, the stock trades at a reasonable valuation, and FY2027 guidance implies continued momentum. The nuclear pharmacy and at-Home Solutions businesses provide diversification, and the MSO buildout positions Cardinal Health in the high-growth specialty pharmacy market [1].
The bear case centers on structural margin limitations and balance sheet fragility. Profit margin of 0.67% means even minor pricing pressure materially impacts earnings — a 10bps change on $254B revenue equals $254M [4]. Negative shareholders' equity from cumulative buybacks and the $6.6B opioid settlement creates no equity cushion during downturns, and debt refinancing occurs on less favorable terms [3]. The GMPD segment remains a 'work in progress' per management and is structurally lower-margin than pharmaceutical distribution [1]. Drug pricing reform (GLP-1 negotiations, PBM transparency legislation) could compress branded drug revenue driving the pharmaceutical segment. The Inspire Impact score of -83 (MISALIGNED) due to abortifacient distribution and activism stances may concern values-aligned investors.
