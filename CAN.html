Company Profile
Technology ·Computer Hardware ·NASDAQ ·Singapore
Canaan Inc. designs and manufactures ASIC (Application-Specific Integrated Circuit) chips for Bitcoin mining under the Avalon brand. Founded by CEO Nangeng Zhang, Canaan shipped the world's first commercial ASIC miner in January 2013. The company operates three business lines: mining machine sales, self-mining operations (holding BTC in treasury), and consumer mining products (Bitcoin mining heaters launched at CES 2025). Canaan discontinued its AI semiconductor unit in June 2025 to concentrate on crypto infrastructure. FY2025 revenue reached $529.7M (+97% YoY) on record computing power sales, but the business has collapsed in 2026 — Q1 revenue fell to $62.7M, Q2 to $31.9M, and Q3 2026 guidance is $11-15M amid Bitcoin price weakness and tightening market demand.

Investment Thesis
Canaan at $0.35/share and $256M market cap represents a high-risk Bitcoin cycle play with genuine technology pioneering credentials — the company shipped the world's first ASIC miner in 2013, holds BTC in treasury, and delivered FY2025 revenue of $529.7M (+97% YoY) during the crypto bull market [1]. The decision to exit AI semiconductors and concentrate on crypto mining demonstrates strategic clarity, and the low D/E of 24.1% limits near-term financial distress risk [2]. The company ended Q2 2026 with $66M in cash (up from $43M in Q1), showing improving liquidity management even amid collapsing revenue. At current prices, any Bitcoin price recovery above $80K would likely reignite mining equipment demand and drive a sharp revenue rebound, similar to the FY2024-FY2025 cycle.
