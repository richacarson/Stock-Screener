Company Profile
Technology ·Computer Hardware - Semiconductors ·NASDAQ ·China
Canaan Inc. is a technology company primarily focused on ASIC (Application-Specific Integrated Circuit) high-performance computing chip design and manufacturing for Bitcoin mining under the Avalon brand. Founded by Nangeng Zhang, Canaan shipped the world's first ASIC Bitcoin miner in 2013 and remains a leading mining equipment provider. The company also operates self-mining operations, holding 1,808 BTC and 3,952 ETH in treasury as of Q1 2026. FY2025 revenue of $529.7M (+97% YoY) was driven by record computing power sales of 14.6 EH/s in Q4 2025, but Q1 2026 revenue declined to $62.7M with an $88.7M net loss amid Bitcoin price weakness. Canaan exited its non-core AI semiconductor business in 2026 to focus exclusively on crypto mining. Transferred listing to Nasdaq Capital Market effective July 1, 2026. Market cap approximately $154M.

Investment Thesis
Canaan at $0.21/share and $154M market cap represents a high-risk Bitcoin cycle play with genuine technology leadership — the company shipped the world's first ASIC miner and delivered record Q4 2025 computing power sales of 14.6 EH/s, driving FY2025 revenue to $529.7M (+97% YoY) [4]. The decision to exit AI semiconductors and concentrate resources on crypto mining demonstrates strategic clarity [5]. The crypto treasury of 1,808 BTC and 3,952 ETH provides direct Bitcoin price exposure alongside the equipment business. Installed hashrate growth of 34.6% YoY and production of ~90 BTC/month from self-mining creates a dual revenue model. Low debt (estimated D/E ~27%) limits financial distress risk, and analyst consensus Strong Buy with a $1.72 target implies 732% upside. The Inspire Impact Score of 0 (MIXED) creates no faith-based restrictions.
The bear case is severe: Canaan has lost $874M over the last three fiscal years (FY2023-FY2025) while generating $1,011M in cumulative revenue, producing an aggregate profit margin of -86% [2]. The stock has fallen 91% from its 52-week high of $2.22 to $0.21, and the Nasdaq Capital Market transfer (from Global Market) signals potential listing compliance issues. Q1 2026 revenue collapsed to $62.7M from Q4 2025's $196.3M (a 68% sequential decline), demonstrating the extreme revenue cyclicality tied to Bitcoin price and halving economics [1]. The 5-year revenue CAGR of -9.0% reflects structural decline from the $773M FY2021 peak. As a Chinese-domiciled company competing against Bitmain (60-80% market share), Canaan faces both competitive disadvantage and geopolitical risk (U.S.-China semiconductor restrictions, sanctions risk). The sub-$1 stock price and $154M market cap create micro-cap liquidity risk — daily dollar volume barely exceeds the $1M liquidity floor. Bitcoin mining economics are inherently deflationary post-halving, and any sustained BTC price decline below $50K could render Canaan's mining operations unprofitable.
