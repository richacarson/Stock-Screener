Company Profile
Financial Services ·Capital Markets ·NYSE ·United States
Cango Inc. operates a Bitcoin mining business with operations across North America, the Middle East, South America, and East Africa, alongside a small online international used car export business through AutoCango.com. Originally a Chinese automotive transaction service platform, Cango divested its PRC business for $352M in late 2024 and pivoted to crypto mining. Q1 2026 revenue was $102M (primarily bitcoin mining) with a $261.1M net loss; Q2 2026 revenue fell 50% to $50.8M with an $81.6M net loss. The company is now pursuing a further pivot to AI compute infrastructure. Headquartered in Dallas, Texas.
96 Employees ·ir.cangoonline.com

Investment Thesis
Cango offers a highly speculative option on Bitcoin mining economics and an emerging AI compute pivot at a deeply distressed valuation. The stock trades at $2.37 post-reverse-split with a ~$97M market cap [4]. Mining operations reduced average cash cost per Bitcoin by 5% sequentially to $73,313 in Q2 2026, and the AI compute pivot — with CTO Jack Jin and plans for GPU node deployment — could provide higher-margin revenue if executed [2]. Q2 2026 showed improved cost discipline with the net loss narrowing from $261.1M (Q1) to $81.6M as the company phased out older S19 miners and transitioned some capacity to a hosted leasing model [1]. At this distressed valuation, any Bitcoin price rally, successful AI compute commercialization, or cost normalization could drive outsized percentage returns.
