Company Profile
Industrials ·Building Products & Equipment ·OTC ·South Korea
Captivision Inc. develops, manufactures, installs, and sells G-Glass, an architectural media glass that transforms building facades into transparent media screens with real-time live stream capability. Founded in 2005 and headquartered in Pyeongtaek-si, South Korea, the company has deployed G-Glass in hundreds of locations globally including sports stadiums, entertainment venues, casinos, airports, and convention centers. Captivision was delisted from Nasdaq in April 2026 after failing to meet compliance deadlines for audited financial results, and now trades on OTC Pink. The company announced a strategic pivot to acquire Montana Goldfields, a subsidiary valued at $750M, to create a Nasdaq-listed U.S. mining company.
