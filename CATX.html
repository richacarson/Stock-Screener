Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NYSE American ·United States
Perspective Therapeutics is a clinical-stage radiopharmaceutical company developing image-guided targeted alpha-particle therapies for cancer, built on the alpha-emitting isotope Lead-212 paired with Pb-203 imaging. Lead programs are [212Pb]VMT-alpha-NET (bamzireotide navoxetan) for SSTR2-expressing neuroendocrine tumors, which reported a 72% 18-month landmark progression-free survival rate and no dose-limiting toxicities at ESMO 2026 (Oct 23, 2026), with a pivotal Phase 3 targeted for late 2026; VMT01 (lapemelanotide zapixetar) for MC1R-positive melanoma; and PSV359 for FAP-positive solid tumors, which received FDA Fast Track designation (Oct 8, 2026) and has a clinical supply collaboration with Merck for Keytruda combinations. The company had ~$271M in cash and short-term investments at March 31, 2026, with runway into late 2027, after a February 2026 offering at $3.79/share. Formerly IsoRay (brachytherapy). Shares at $1.15 sit far below the 50-day average of $2.57. Headquartered in Seattle.

Investment Thesis
Perspective is a high-risk, high-reward bet on Lead-212 targeted alpha therapy. At ESMO 2026, VMT-alpha-NET showed a 72% 18-month landmark PFS across three cohorts with no dose-limiting toxicities, and a pivotal Phase 3 is targeted for late 2026 [3][4]. PSV359 earned FDA Fast Track on October 8, 2026 and a Merck Keytruda combination collaboration, and VMT01 melanoma data is due at SITC in November [4]. Sell-side support is strong: 11 brokers average 'Buy', BTIG has a $14 target, and William Blair initiated with an $11.45 value estimate [4]. At $1.15 the market cap is only ~$131M, against ~$237M in cash per Yahoo Finance [5].
The bear case is that the stock has collapsed from $3.79 (February 2026 offering price) to $1.15, less than half its 50-day average of $2.57. The available sources did not identify a specific cause for the October slide [1][5]. ROE is -39.6%, cash burn is ~$70M a year, and runway extends only into late 2027, so funding a pivotal Phase 3 will likely require more dilutive capital at depressed prices. Pb-212 supply chains and manufacturing scale are unproven, and large pharma radioligand competitors are far better funded. Liquidity is only ~2x the IOWN $1M daily floor.
