Company Profile
Consumer Cyclical ·Restaurants ·NYSE ·United States
CAVA Group owns and operates fast-casual Mediterranean restaurants under the CAVA brand in the United States and sells dips and spreads through grocery retail. Founded in 2006 and headquartered in Washington, D.C., CAVA IPO'd in June 2023 and converted the Zoe's Kitchen chain acquired in 2018. Q2 2026 revenue rose 31.3% to $365.4M, with same-restaurant sales up 9.0% and traffic up 5.3%. FY2026 guidance (reaffirmed) calls for 4.5-6.5% same-restaurant sales growth and 75-77 net new restaurants, with adjusted EBITDA guidance raised in May to $181-191M. TTM revenue is $1.37B. FY2025 revenue was $1.18B, up from $500M in FY2021.
13,480 Employees ·cava.com

Investment Thesis
CAVA is one of the few restaurant chains growing traffic and units at the same time. Q2 2026 revenue rose 31.3% to $365.4M, with same-restaurant sales up 9.0% on 5.3% traffic growth [1]. Management reaffirmed 75-77 net new restaurants and 4.5-6.5% same-restaurant sales growth for FY2026 and raised adjusted EBITDA guidance to $181-191M [1]. Revenue has compounded at ~24% a year since 2021, D/E is a modest 61.9%, and Connected Kitchen AI tools are rolling out to raise unit productivity [2][5].
The bear case is valuation and governance. At 79x forward earnings with a Yahoo PEG of 5.1, the stock prices in years of flawless execution, and reaffirmed same-store guidance implies a second-half slowdown from the 9-10% pace of the first half [1]. Insiders and founding backers sold an alleged ~$2.2B of stock, now the subject of a July 2026 Delaware derivative suit, and a 2022 PFAS packaging class action remains a reputational overhang [4]. Profit margin is just 4.8%, ROE is 8.3%, and free cash flow is roughly breakeven per Yahoo Finance as the company funds rapid unit growth [5].
Key Catalysts
  1. Q3 2026 results: whether same-restaurant sales hold near the 9% Q2 pace or decelerate toward the 4.5-6.5% full-year guidance [1]
  2. Delivery of 75-77 net new restaurants in FY2026 and new-market openings extending national scale [1]
  3. Wider rollout of Connected Kitchen generative-AI and kitchen display systems (currently live in 4 and in test at 25 restaurants) lifting restaurant-level margins [2]
Key Risks
  1. Valuation compression: Forward P/E of 79x and PEG of 5.1 leave little room for any same-store slowdown [5]
  2. Governance overhang from the July 2026 Delaware derivative suit alleging ~$2.2B of insider sales at inflated prices [4]
  3. Consumer-spending pressure on fast casual, plus the pending PFAS packaging class action affecting brand perception [4]
