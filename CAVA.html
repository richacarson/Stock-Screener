Company Profile
Consumer Cyclical ·Restaurants ·NYSE ·United States
CAVA Group, Inc. owns and operates a chain of fast-casual Mediterranean restaurants under the CAVA brand in the United States. The company serves customizable bowls, pitas, and salads made with chef-crafted dips and spreads, and sells a line of dips and spreads through grocery retail channels. Founded in 2006, IPO'd in June 2023 at $22/share, headquartered in Washington, D.C. CAVA operates over 367 restaurants with an expansion target of 1,000 locations by 2032.

Investment Thesis
CAVA delivered a strong Q1 FY2026 with $434.4M revenue (up 32.2% YoY), $23.6M net income, adjusted EBITDA growth of 37.6% to $61.7M, and restaurant-level profit margin of 25.1% [1]. The company crossed $1 billion in trailing twelve-month revenue and is accelerating unit growth with 92 net new restaurant openings. Digital revenue mix of 39.9% demonstrates strong omnichannel execution. With a clear path to 1,000 locations by 2032 and the Mediterranean fast-casual category still in early innings of national penetration, CAVA has significant runway for sustained 20%+ revenue growth. The company's commitment to 75th-95th percentile wages and mission-driven culture creates a differentiated employer brand in a tight labor market, with D/E of just 60% reflecting a conservative balance sheet [5].
The primary concern is extreme valuation: at 142x trailing earnings, 144x forward earnings, and a PEG ratio of 5.89, CAVA is priced for perfection with virtually no margin of safety [5]. Net profit margin of 5.4% and ROE of 8.64% are thin for a stock commanding this premium — any compression from rising food costs, wage inflation, or new market entry challenges could trigger severe multiple contraction. The aggressive expansion plan to 1,000 locations by 2032 assumes consistently successful new market entries, but CAVA has yet to prove it can maintain unit economics as it expands beyond core East Coast markets into less familiar geographies. Insiders have been net sellers in 2026, with $3.1M in sales reported in February [4]. Same-store sales guidance of 3-5% for 2026 represents significant deceleration from prior periods, and the stock has declined 19% from its 52-week high of approximately $101.

  3. Insider selling by multiple executives in early 2026 totaling $3.1M+ and deceleration of same-store sales guidance to 3-5% signal potential caution from management about near-term growth trajectory

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience MODERATE
7
AI Risk Protection (7/9): CAVA's core business — operating physical restaurants, preparing fresh Mediterranean food, and delivering in-person hospitality — is structurally protected from AI disruption [2]. Restaurants require physical labor for food preparation, customer service, and facility management that AI cannot substitute. The fast-casual dining experience depends on human interaction, ingredient freshness, and physical ambiance beyond AI's reach. However, CAVA faces some indirect AI exposure: AI-powered ordering platforms and food delivery aggregators could reduce the differentiation of the in-store experience, and AI-driven competitive intelligence could accelerate rival expansion strategies. AI Opportunity (0/1): While CAVA has deployed AI operationally through camera vision for food prep monitoring, labor forecasting, and Connected Kitchen predictive scheduling, these are internal efficiency tools generating cost savings rather than AI products or services generating external revenue [2]. No quantifiable AI-specific revenue has been reported.
