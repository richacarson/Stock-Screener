Company Profile
Financial Services ·Insurance - Property & Casualty ·NYSE ·Switzerland
Chubb Limited is the world's largest publicly traded property and casualty insurer, operating in 54 countries with six segments: North America Commercial P&C, North America Personal P&C, North America Agricultural, Overseas General Insurance, Global Reinsurance, and Life Insurance. Formerly ACE Limited, the company acquired The Chubb Corporation in 2016 for $28.3B. Headquartered in Zurich, Switzerland.

Investment Thesis
Chubb is the world's largest publicly traded P&C insurer, delivering record FY2025 net income of $10.31B (up 11.2%) with a record P&C combined ratio of 81.2%, followed by Q2 2026 core operating EPS of $7.26 (up 18.2%) and Q1 2026 EPS of $6.82 (up 85.2%) [1][2]. At 12.4x trailing earnings and approximately 10.5x forward earnings with a PEG of 0.53, Chubb is deeply undervalued for a company compounding earnings at 20%+ with 17.3% profit margins and a conservative 27% D/E ratio [5]. CEO Greenberg's 22-year track record, the AI-driven transformation (Chubb Digital $1.4B premium, up 27%), and consistent underwriting discipline across cycles position Chubb as a best-in-class compounder. Warren Buffett's Berkshire Hathaway as a major shareholder provides additional validation of the long-term value proposition.
The bear case centers on catastrophe exposure and the cyclical nature of insurance pricing. As the world's largest P&C insurer, Chubb carries significant exposure to catastrophic events — hurricanes, wildfires, earthquakes — that could cause multi-billion dollar loss quarters and compress earnings unpredictably [1]. Q2 2026 premium growth of only 3.6% (vs 10.7% in Q1) signals potential deceleration as the hard pricing cycle matures, and the insurance industry faces a potential soft market where competitors chase volume with lower pricing. The planned 20% workforce reduction through AI transformation carries execution risk — operational disruption, talent flight, and regulatory scrutiny during the multi-year transition [3]. Conservative advocacy groups have targeted Chubb's ESG and climate policies as 'radical woke ideology,' while environmental groups push for more aggressive climate commitments, creating reputational crossfire from both political flanks [4]. ROE of 14.34% remains just below the 15% target, suggesting structural capital efficiency constraints in the insurance model.

  1. Catastrophe loss exposure — as the world's largest P&C insurer, Chubb faces material earnings volatility from hurricanes, wildfires, and climate-related catastrophic events that could cause multi-billion dollar loss quarters [1]
  2. Insurance pricing cycle deceleration — Q2 2026 premium growth of 3.6% (vs 10.7% in Q1) signals potential soft market transition where competitors undercut pricing, pressuring premium growth trajectory [1]
