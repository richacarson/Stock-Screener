Company Profile
Financial Services ·Banks - Regional ·NASDAQ ·United States
Commerce Bancshares is the bank holding company for Commerce Bank, providing retail, mortgage banking, corporate, investment, trust, and asset management products and services. Operating through Retail Banking, Commercial, and Wealth segments, the company serves individuals and businesses primarily across Missouri, Kansas, Illinois, Oklahoma, and Colorado. Founded in 1865, Commerce Bank has operated for over 160 years with 57 consecutive years of dividend increases — among the longest streaks in the banking industry. Q2 2026 EPS of $1.10 with expanding NIM and strong credit quality. Market cap approximately $8.2B.

Commerce Bancshares is among the most conservatively managed regional banks in the country, delivering Q2 2026 EPS of $1.10 with NIM expanding to 3.77%, trust fees growing 10.3% YoY to $62.2M, excellent credit quality, and return on assets of 1.84% [1]. The bear case centers on limited growth potential and structural challenges facing mid-size regional banks. Regional banks face secular pressure from AI-enabled fintech competitors and digital banking platforms — McKinsey warns that 'gen AI agents threaten retail banks' customer relationships' — and Commerce Bank's moderate scale limits its ability to invest in AI defenses at the pace of money-center banks [3]. The stock has underperformed its sector, and the 5yr revenue CAGR of ~5% caps the long-term growth trajectory.
