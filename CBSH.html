Company Profile
Financial Services ·Banks - Regional ·NASDAQ ·United States
Commerce Bancshares is the bank holding company for Commerce Bank, providing retail, mortgage banking, corporate, investment, trust, and asset management products and services. Operating through Retail Banking, Commercial, and Wealth segments, the company serves individuals and businesses primarily across Missouri, Kansas, Illinois, Oklahoma, and Colorado. Founded in 1865, Commerce Bank has operated for over 160 years with 57 consecutive years of dividend increases — among the longest streaks in the banking industry.

Investment Thesis
Commerce Bancshares delivered strong Q2 2026 results — $501.3M revenue (up 11.9% YoY), $159.8M net income (up 4.8%), and net interest margin expanding to 3.77% with fee revenue growing $8M driven by trust, bank card, and deposit businesses [1]. At 13.5x forward earnings with 31.6% profit margin, 14.7% ROE, ROA of 1.84%, and $4.12B cash against $2.45B debt, CBSH is among the most conservatively managed regional banks in the country [4]. The crown jewel is the 57-year consecutive dividend growth streak — extended with a 7% boost — reflecting extraordinary capital allocation discipline across multiple economic cycles. The Kemper family's multi-generational ownership and the 160+ year operating history provide institutional stability that is genuinely rare in banking.
The bear case centers on limited growth potential and the structural challenges facing mid-size regional banks. Q2 EPS of $1.10 was barely up from $1.09 despite 11.9% revenue growth, suggesting share dilution from the FineMark acquisition is absorbing revenue gains [1]. PEG of 2.14 reflects the market's view that EPS growth is fundamentally constrained — 3-year EPS growth forecast of only 4.8% caps upside potential [4]. The dividend yield of 1.86% has fallen below 2% due to stock price appreciation, offering modest income for a bank with 57 years of dividend growth. Regional banks face secular pressure from AI-enabled fintech competitors and digital banking platforms that threaten retail customer relationships — McKinsey warns that 'gen AI agents threaten retail banks' customer relationships.' Commerce Bank's moderate scale ($8.5B market cap) limits its ability to invest in AI defenses at the pace of money-center banks. The stock's 52-week return of only 1.64% reflects the market's view of CBSH as a stable hold rather than a growth story [4].
