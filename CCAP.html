Company Profile
Financial Services ·Asset Management ·NASDAQ ·United States
Crescent Capital BDC is an externally managed business development company focused on originating and investing in the debt of private U.S. middle market companies. The company is managed by Crescent Capital Group LP, a specialty credit firm founded in 1991 with expertise in private credit. CCAP provides senior secured loans, unitranche facilities, and subordinated debt to portfolio companies. CEO Jason Breaux (since 2015) and President Henry Chung were elevated to the board in May 2026. The company pays a quarterly dividend currently at $0.34/share (reduced from $0.42 in mid-2026) with a 15.6% forward yield, but faces headwinds from declining interest income as rates fall, rising non-accruals particularly in healthcare, and NAV erosion — net income turned negative in H1 2026 with losses of $18.8M.

Net income has turned negative in H1 2026 with $18.8M in losses driven by rising non-accruals, particularly in healthcare portfolio companies [1]. NAV has declined persistently, and the dividend was cut 19% from $0.42 to $0.34 per quarter — further cuts may follow if NII continues declining with falling interest rates. Oppenheimer downgraded CCAP to Perform, cutting the target from $19 to $16, and Seeking Alpha analysts note 'additional downside risks remain' [1][3]. Interest income is structurally declining as rates fall (-15.5% YoY revenue growth), compressing NII and reducing dividend coverage. Payout ratio exceeds 100% on a forward basis, meaning the dividend may not be fully covered by earnings. The stock is down 38% from its 52-week high of $15.77 to $9.78, reflecting persistent NAV erosion and credit deterioration. D/E of 141% is elevated, and ROE has turned negative (-0.46%), indicating the company is currently destroying equity value [4]. The 0.55x book value discount may be warranted rather than a buying opportunity if non-accruals continue rising.
Key Catalysts
