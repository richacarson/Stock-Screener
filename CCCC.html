Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
C4 Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company advancing targeted protein degradation (TPD) science, using its TORPEDO platform to develop orally bioavailable degrader medicines for cancer targets of high unmet need. Headquartered in Watertown, Massachusetts, the company's lead candidate cemsidomide is in Phase 2 development for multiple myeloma, alongside CFT1946 for BRAF V600X solid tumors and CFT8919 for EGFR-mutant non-small cell lung cancer. A new April 2026 Roche collaboration for degrader-antibody conjugates (DACs) added more than $1.0 billion in potential milestones. Q2 2026 cash position of $300.4M provides a runway through end of 2028.

Investment Thesis
C4 Therapeutics is a clinical-stage targeted protein degradation company with a differentiated TORPEDO platform advancing three named oncology programs — cemsidomide (Phase 2 MOMENTUM in multiple myeloma plus a Phase 1b elranatamab combination), CFT1946 (BRAF V600X solid tumors), and CFT8919 (EGFR-mutant NSCLC) [1]. The April 2026 Roche DAC collaboration ($20M upfront, $1.0B+ milestones) validates the platform's applicability to a new modality and adds a potential value-creating catalyst beyond the core pipeline. With $300.4M in cash as of Q2 2026 — up from $268.3M in Q1 reflecting the Roche payment — and management guidance of a runway through end of 2028, C4 has multiple years to generate clinical readouts without imminent financing need [1]. A conservative D/E of 25% and a strong analyst consensus (7 buy, 0 sell, average target $13.33 vs $3.76 current price) reflect confidence in the science.
The bear case centers on binary clinical risk, thin organizational stability, and pre-commercial fundamentals. Q2 2026 revenue was just $6.6M against a $23.6M net loss ($0.18/share), and the mechanical infrastructure score of 3 (WEAK) reflects deeply negative ROE (-48.35%) and profit margin (-380%) typical of pre-commercial biotech — the formula does not capture the genuine balance-sheet cushion from the $300.4M cash position [1]. Three of four non-CEO C-suite roles have turned over in the past three years (CSO, CFO, CMO), raising organizational continuity concerns heading into pivotal cemsidomide readouts, and no insider buying was identified [2]. Cemsidomide's Phase 2 outcome remains the primary binary event — a negative readout would eliminate the core investment thesis with no approved products to fall back on. The company operates in a competitive and rapidly evolving field with Arvinas, Kymera, and major pharma in-licensing protein degradation programs.
