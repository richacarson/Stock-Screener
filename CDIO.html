Company Profile
Healthcare ·Diagnostics & Research (Biotechnology) ·NASDAQ ·United States
Cardio Diagnostics Holdings is a Chicago-based micro-cap that develops AI-driven epigenetic and genetic blood tests for cardiovascular disease, led by its flagship Epi+Gen CHD test, which estimates a patient's three-year risk of a coronary heart disease event, and its PrecisionCHD test. The company was founded in 2017 on technology co-invented at the University of Iowa by CEO Meesha Dogan. Commercial traction is still minimal: trailing-twelve-month revenue through June 2026 was about $14,450 against a net loss of about $6.5M. The stock trades near $1.75 with a market cap of about $5.2M.
15 Employees ·www.cdio.ai

Investment Thesis
The bull case rests on a credible scientific idea: an AI-interpreted epigenetic blood test, Epi+Gen CHD, that estimates three-year heart-attack risk and could fit into preventive cardiology and employer wellness programs [4]. The balance sheet is debt-light, with $5.59M of cash against $370K of total debt and $7.43M of equity at Q2 2026 [1]. The company is still onboarding new providers after the Family Medicine Specialists initiative ended and is pursuing an India partnership to scale access [1][6]. At a $5.2M market cap, any meaningful reimbursement win or channel partnership would be large relative to the company's size [3].
The bear case is overwhelming. TTM revenue is about $14,450 against a net loss of about $6.47M, and FY2025 revenue fell 57.5% to $14,825 [1][2]. Five years after its 2022 Nasdaq listing, the product shows no commercial traction. Quarterly cash burn of $1.5M to $1.8M against $5.59M of cash leaves roughly 3 to 4 quarters of runway. The share count path (26.9M weighted shares in FY2024, about 1.75M after an apparent reverse split, then 2.96M by Q2 2026) points to ongoing dilution [1][2]. Average daily volume of about 36,000 shares, roughly $63K a day, puts the stock far below IOWN's $1M liquidity floor, so it cannot be traded responsibly at portfolio scale [3].

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience MODERATE
6
AI Risk Protection (5/9): The product is a CLIA-lab blood assay that reads DNA methylation and genotype data, a physical and regulated process that a software model cannot perform on its own [4]. The bigger threat is substitution. Cardiovascular risk scoring is a crowded field for AI, including AI-ECG and AI-CT plaque analytics from larger, better-funded players, and those tools could make a stand-alone epigenetic blood test unnecessary. With almost no revenue base, Cardio Diagnostics has little protection if clinicians adopt them. No analyst coverage was found that addresses this risk directly, which is itself a negative for a $5M company. AI Opportunity (1/1): All of the company's revenue, though trivial at about $14K TTM, comes from tests it markets as AI-driven [1][4].
