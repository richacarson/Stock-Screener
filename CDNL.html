Cardinal Infrastructure Group Inc. (formerly Civil Infrastructure Group Inc.) is a full-service civil contracting company providing site development and infrastructure services across the southeastern United States. Services include wet utility installations (water, sewer, stormwater), grading, site clearing, erosion control, drilling/blasting, paving, and related services. Founded in 2013 by CEO Jeremy Spivey, headquartered in Raleigh, NC, and IPO'd December 2025. Q2 2026 revenue surged 114% YoY to a record $226.9M with backlog of $866M (+35%), though adjusted EBITDA margin compressed to 12.4% from 18.6% YoY, triggering a 36%+ single-day stock decline and securities investigations [1][4].

AI Resilience LOW RISK

AI Risk Protection (9/9): Civil contracting — installing water/sewer/stormwater systems, grading, site clearing, drilling/blasting, paving — is among the most AI-immune business models in the economy [1]. Every revenue dollar requires physical crews, heavy equipment, dirt, and concrete at specific locations. AI cannot grade a construction site, install underground utilities, or blast rock formations. The work is deeply regulated by state and municipal codes, requires on-site inspections, and involves site-specific conditions that resist automation. Cardinal's self-performing model with specialized equipment fleets and 1,480 employees makes it structurally impossible for software to substitute. AI data center construction actually increases demand for the site preparation and utility infrastructure services Cardinal provides.
