Company Profile
Technology ·Electronic Design Automation ·NASDAQ ·United States
Cadence Design Systems is a leading provider of electronic design automation (EDA) software, intellectual property (IP), and system design and analysis solutions. The company operates in a duopoly with Synopsys in EDA, enabling the design of semiconductors, printed circuit boards, and systems. Expanding into computational biology and system analysis through acquisitions including the Hexagon D&E business. Led by CEO Dr. Anirudh Devgan since 2021. Headquartered in San Jose, California.
13,800 Employees ·www.cadence.com

Investment Thesis
Cadence is the premier AI beneficiary in enterprise software, operating in an EDA duopoly with Synopsys where AI drives demand acceleration rather than disruption. Q1 2026 delivered $1.474 billion revenue (up 19% YoY) with every segment growing double digits: Core EDA up 18%, IP up 22%, and System Design & Analysis up 18% [1]. Management raised FY2026 guidance to $6.125-6.225 billion (approximately 17% growth) backed by a record $8.0 billion backlog providing exceptional multi-year revenue visibility [1]. Non-GAAP operating margins of 44.7% and ROE of 20.66% reflect a world-class software business model with significant operating leverage. The ChipStack AI Super Agent (10X productivity for chip design) and expansion into computational biology and system analysis under Dr. Devgan's leadership broaden the TAM beyond traditional EDA.
The primary concern is valuation. At 44.68x forward earnings and a PEG ratio of 3.18 (above the 3.0 floor), Cadence is priced for perfection in a market that demands sustained high-teens growth indefinitely [5]. The $106 billion market cap requires continued AI-driven semiconductor design demand acceleration -- any deceleration in customer design starts or a shift in AI chip investment priorities could compress the multiple significantly. The Hexagon D&E acquisition is dilutive to 2026 earnings and creates integration risk for a company that has historically grown organically [1]. CEO Devgan sold $19.2 million in shares in 2026, and while pre-planned under 10b5-1, the consistent insider selling at all-time highs warrants monitoring [5]. Cadence's success is increasingly tied to the AI infrastructure buildout cycle -- if semiconductor capex normalizes or AI chip design complexity plateaus, the premium growth narrative could unwind rapidly.

  3. Cadence's growth narrative is increasingly correlated with the AI infrastructure buildout cycle -- any normalization in semiconductor capex or AI chip design starts could break the demand acceleration thesis
