Company Profile
Real Estate ·REIT - Office ·NYSE ·United States
COPT Defense Properties is an S&P MidCap 400 REIT focused on owning, operating, and developing properties proximate to key U.S. Government defense installations. The Defense/IT Portfolio of 202 properties encompasses 23.3 million square feet at 96.4% occupancy, leased primarily to the U.S. Government, defense contractors, and intelligence agencies engaged in mission-critical national security activities. The company also operates 30+ data centers and is developing a 1 GW data center campus in Iowa.

Investment Thesis
COPT Defense Properties occupies a rare structural advantage at the intersection of defense spending and AI infrastructure demand. The 96.4% Defense/IT occupancy, 84% tenant retention, and 91% data center renewal rent increases demonstrate monopolistic pricing power in a supply-constrained niche [1][2]. Q2 2026 delivered the 24th consecutive quarter of YoY FFO growth at $0.71/share (+4.4%), and management raised full-year FFO guidance to $2.78 midpoint with same-property cash NOI growth lifted to 4.0% [1]. The $440M development pipeline (73% pre-leased) and Iowa 1 GW data center campus for a Fortune 100 cloud tenant provide multi-year revenue visibility. Net Debt/EBITDA of 6.0x and 82% fixed-rate debt at 3.8% weighted average rate reflect prudent balance sheet management for a REIT.
The bear case centers on valuation compression and structural REIT constraints. At 27.1x forward GAAP P/E (or 13.8x P/FFO), the stock prices in steady but modest growth — FFO per share guidance of $2.78 implies only 2.2% growth over 2025, limiting upside surprise potential [5]. D/E of 163% and GAAP payout ratio of ~91% leave minimal retained cash for self-funded development, requiring ongoing capital market access in a potentially rising rate environment. Only 4 consecutive years of dividend growth means the income story has not been proven through a full economic cycle. Political risk is real — shifts in defense spending priorities, base realignment decisions (BRAC), or prolonged government shutdowns could disrupt the concentrated defense tenant base. The data center opportunity, while significant, faces competition from dedicated data center REITs (Equinix, Digital Realty) with deeper expertise and global scale.
Key Catalysts
  1. Iowa 1 GW data center campus development for Fortune 100 cloud tenant could generate substantial development fees and rental income, with AI-driven demand supporting premium rents and full occupancy [2]
  2. Rising U.S. defense spending and geopolitical tensions increasing demand for mission-critical secure facilities, directly benefiting COPT's 96.4% occupied portfolio near military and intelligence installations [1]
  3. Continued FFO guidance raises (now 24 consecutive quarters of YoY FFO growth) could support multiple expansion from 13.8x P/FFO as the market recognizes COPT's dual defense/data center growth profile [1]
Key Risks
  1. Shifts in U.S. defense spending priorities, base realignment decisions (BRAC), or prolonged government shutdowns could disrupt the concentrated defense tenant base that generates 90%+ of rental revenue [1]
  2. D/E of 163%, GAAP payout ratio of ~91%, and 2.2% FFO per share growth leave limited financial flexibility — the company depends on capital market access for development funding while FFO growth trails peers [5]
  3. Competition from dedicated data center REITs (Equinix, Digital Realty) with deeper expertise, global scale, and lower cost of capital could limit COPT's data center pricing power outside its defense-adjacent niche [2]
