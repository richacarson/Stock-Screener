Company Profile
Materials ·Specialty Chemicals ·NYSE ·United States
Celanese Corporation is a global leader in specialty materials and chemical manufacturing, producing engineered polymers, acetyl intermediates, and advanced materials used across automotive, electronics, medical devices, consumer goods, and industrial applications. The company completed the transformative $11B acquisition of DuPont's Mobility & Materials (M&M) business in 2022, significantly expanding its engineered materials portfolio. Q2 2026 revenue of $2.75B (+8.7% YoY) with adjusted EPS of $2.45. The company is aggressively deleveraging from $11.4B net debt, targeting 3x net debt-to-EBITDA. CEO Scott Richardson took over January 2025 after nearly two decades at the company. Market cap ~$4.9B.
11,434 Employees ·www.celanese.com
