Company Profile
Utilities ·Utilities - Independent Power Producers ·NASDAQ ·United States
Constellation Energy is the largest U.S. producer of carbon-free energy and, after closing its $16.4B Calpine acquisition in January 2026, the nation's largest electricity producer, operating the largest U.S. nuclear fleet plus Calpine's natural gas and geothermal assets. Q2 2026 reportable segment revenues were $7.24B (+40.7% YoY, including $2.15B from Calpine). Adjusted operating EPS was $2.55 and GAAP EPS $1.42, and full-year adjusted operating EPS guidance was raised to $11.50-12.50. In Q2 the company signed 920 MW of new long-term PPAs with investment-grade customers, including Walmart. The Crane Clean Energy Center (former Three Mile Island Unit 1) restart under a 20-year Microsoft PPA remains on track for 2H 2027, backed by a $1B DOE loan, NRC fuel license approval and FERC approval of capacity-rights transfer. TTM revenue is $31.27B.

Restarting a retired reactor at the Three Mile Island site under a 20-year contract, and making the $16.4B Calpine acquisition, are bets measured in decades, and four straight years of Great Place to Work certification suggest a culture that supports them. Investment Thesis
Constellation is the scale leader in firm, carbon-free power just as AI data-center demand reshapes the U.S. grid. Q2 2026 segment revenue grew 40.7% to $7.24B with Calpine contributing $2.15B, adjusted EPS of $2.55 drove a $0.50 guidance raise to $11.50-12.50, and 920 MW of new long-term PPAs were signed [1][2]. The Crane restart under a 20-year Microsoft PPA has its NRC fuel license, FERC waiver and $1B DOE loan and is on track for 2H 2027 [2]. At 19.6x forward earnings with 15% ROE and 23% revenue growth, the valuation is reasonable for an AI-infrastructure enabler [4].
The bear case starts with GAAP earnings, which are moving the other way: quarterly EPS fell 46.8% YoY, FY2025 GAAP EPS dropped to $7.40 from $11.89, and Yahoo's PEG of 3.74 shows weak trailing growth support [4]. Growth is increasingly coming from Calpine integration rather than organic nuclear performance, unplanned nuclear outages hit Q2, and the stock has fallen sharply from its peak [1]. Political risk is rising: PJM capacity price collars, calls for emergency auctions, and a PJM analysis suggesting Crane might not connect until 2031 could all cap economics [2][3]. Free cash flow was -$6.6B TTM per Yahoo Finance after the Calpine deal, and insiders are net sellers [3][4].
