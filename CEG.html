Company Profile
Utilities ·Utilities - Independent Power Producers ·NASDAQ ·United States
Constellation Energy is the largest producer of carbon-free energy and the nation's largest electricity producer, operating approximately 60 GW of generating capacity following the $16.4B Calpine acquisition completed January 2026. The fleet consists of 21 nuclear reactors across 12 facilities plus natural gas, wind, solar, and geothermal assets from Calpine. The company serves 2.5 million customers and is restarting the Crane Clean Energy Center (Three Mile Island Unit 1) under a 20-year Microsoft PPA to power AI data centers, with restart now expected as early as 2027 (accelerated from 2028). Q2 2026 delivered revenue of $7.5B and adjusted EPS of $2.55 (beating the $2.36 estimate), with guidance raised to $11.50-$12.50 for FY2026. TTM revenue reached $31.27B with $3.47B in profits. Ranked #1 on Barron's Most Sustainable U.S. Companies for 2026.

Investment Thesis
Constellation Energy is the defining AI-era energy infrastructure play -- the nation's largest electricity producer with 60 GW of capacity and the largest nuclear fleet serving hyperscaler data center demand [1][2]. Q2 2026 delivered $7.5B revenue and adjusted EPS of $2.55 (beating $2.36), leading management to raise FY2026 guidance to $11.50-$12.50 from $11.00-$12.00 [1]. The company signed approximately 920 MW of new long-term nuclear PPAs with investment-grade customers in Q2. The Crane Clean Energy Center restart has been accelerated to potentially 2027 (from original 2028 target), with $1B DOE loan guarantee, FERC waiver, and NRC draft environmental finding all secured [3]. TTM revenue reached $31.27B with $3.47B in profits. Ranked #1 on Barron's Most Sustainable U.S. Companies for 2026. Free cash flow projected at $8.4B in 2026-2027 rising to $11.5-$13.0B by 2028-2029 [1].
The bear case centers on valuation expansion and execution complexity. At 23x forward earnings with a PEG of ~1.5, the stock has re-rated significantly from the $239 lows but remains 35% below the $412.70 peak [5]. D/E has risen to 76% with $24.7B in total debt reflecting Calpine integration financing, creating interest rate sensitivity for this capital-intensive business. CEO Dominguez sold $25.3M in shares and other executives sold $7-12M in early 2026 [4]. The Calpine natural gas fleet introduces carbon-intensity that creates ESG complexity despite the #1 Barron's sustainability ranking. Profit margin of 11.09% remains modest for a stock commanding growth-stock multiples. While guidance was raised, the NRC environmental assessment for Crane remains a key milestone -- any delays could push back the 2027 restart timeline and its associated 835 MW of contracted Microsoft PPA revenue [3].
