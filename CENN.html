Company Profile
Industrials ·Auto Manufacturers - Specialty ·NASDAQ ·United States
Cenntro Inc. (formerly Cenntro Electric Group) designs, develops, manufactures, and sells light-to-medium-duty commercial electric vehicles and related energy solutions. Products include the Metro cargo van, Logistar delivery vehicles, and energy storage systems serving last-mile delivery, utility, and material handling markets globally. The company executed a 1-for-60 reverse stock split in April 2026 to maintain NASDAQ listing compliance. Founded in 2013, headquartered in Freehold, New Jersey.

Investment Thesis
Cenntro occupies the commercial EV space targeting last-mile delivery and utility vehicle markets with its Metro and Logistar platforms, a market segment with legitimate long-term growth tailwinds as fleet operators electrify [3]. Founder and CEO Peter Wang was recognized by Goldman Sachs as one of the 'Most Intriguing Entrepreneurs' and brings deep automotive industry experience to the company's technology vision [3]. The debt-to-equity ratio of 25% is modest, and the company has not accumulated the crippling leverage seen in many failed EV startups. The Inspire Insight score of 55 (ALIGNED) reflects genuinely positive ESG attributes including energy management, product sustainability, and ethical supply chain practices.
Every fundamental metric signals acute financial distress. Revenue collapsed to $1.21 million in Q1 2026, down 43.4% YoY, with FY2025 revenue of just $18.1 million representing a 42.2% decline [1]. The company is burning cash at an alarming rate with a net loss of $73 million in FY2025 -- losses that exceed revenue by a factor of four, producing a -403.7% net profit margin [1]. ROE of -181.2% reflects shareholder value destruction at an extreme rate. The 1-for-60 reverse stock split in April 2026 was required to maintain NASDAQ listing compliance, and the authorization of up to 3 billion common shares signals likely massive dilutive financing ahead [2]. With a market cap under $10 million, cash of just $3.61 million, and average daily dollar volume of approximately $80,768, the stock is essentially uninvestable for any institutional strategy. The stock has declined 91.6% over the past 52 weeks [4].

  3. Regulatory tailwinds from zero-emission vehicle mandates in key markets (California, EU) that could drive fleet operator adoption of commercial EVs
