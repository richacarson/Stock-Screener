Company Profile
Technology ·Security & Surveillance Technology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Cemtrex is a diversified technology company operating through two primary segments: Security (Vicon Industries, specializing in AI-powered video surveillance cameras and systems) and Industrial (Advanced Industrial Services, providing environmental monitoring instruments, emissions controls, and plant engineering services). Under CEO Saagar Govil since 2011, the company has grown from $3M to ~$76M in TTM revenue through organic growth and acquisitions including Invocon and Plant Engineering Services. Vicon's award-winning NEXT modular camera system features AI gun detection and edge AI processing. Market cap is approximately $3-5M, placing it firmly in nano-cap territory with a 94% stock price decline over the past 52 weeks.
