Company Profile

The Carlyle Group Inc. is a global investment firm that deploys private capital across three business segments: Global Private Equity, Global Credit, and Global Investment Solutions (AlpInvest). The firm manages $475 billion in total AUM across 595 investment vehicles.

Investment Thesis
Carlyle Group offers exposure to a diversified alternative asset management platform with $475 billion in AUM and strong fundraising momentum. The firm's strategic pivot toward credit and insurance solutions is building a more durable, fee-based revenue model that reduces dependence on volatile performance fees [1].
However, the stock faces headwinds from elevated leverage (295% D/E), modest profitability metrics (7.7% ROE, 6.2% PM), and lumpy earnings driven by realization cycles. At a forward P/E of 14.3x with a 3.3% dividend yield, valuation is fair but not compelling relative to the business's cyclicality. The MISALIGNED faith alignment score may concern values-based investors [2].
Key Catalysts
  1. Continued AUM growth toward management's $500B+ target, driving fee-related earnings expansion [1]
  2. Acceleration in realization activity as portfolio companies mature and exit windows improve
  3. Potential re-rating as credit and insurance segments increase recurring revenue mix
Key Risks
  1. Rising interest rates or economic slowdown could impair portfolio company valuations and slow fundraising
  2. Fee compression from increased competition in alternative asset management and the rise of AI-driven investment platforms
  3. Key-person risk and talent retention challenges in a competitive market for investment professionals [2]
