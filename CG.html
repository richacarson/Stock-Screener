Investment Thesis
Carlyle trades at 8.3x forward earnings and a 0.66 PEG with a 3.41% yield, implying the market discounts its earnings recovery [2]. Q2 2026 distributable earnings rose to $472.3M ($1.07/share after tax vs $0.91) and fee-related earnings reached $357.7M at a 47% margin, driven by higher capital-markets fees, and FRE is up 4% year to date [1]. The dividend grew from $1.00 in 2020 to $1.40 in 2025 (7.0% CAGR) and has been paid every year since the 2012 IPO [2]. Recovering private-equity exits would lift realized performance revenues sharply.
The bear case is earnings volatility and governance. Q1 2026 distributable earnings fell 44% to $149.9M as realized net performance revenues dropped $106.8M, TTM revenue is down 32.9% and the GAAP payout ratio is 144% [1][2]. Carlyle has lagged larger rivals in scale and growth, the dividend was flat in 2025, and D/E is 198% [2]. The 2022 leadership crisis and subsequent cultural reset, plus ESG-reporting criticism around NGP Energy emissions, weigh on qualitative scores [4][5].
