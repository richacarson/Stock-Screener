Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Gold ·NYSE ·Canada
Centerra Gold Inc. is a Canadian gold mining company operating the Mt. Milligan copper-gold mine in British Columbia and the Oksut gold mine in Turkey. The company lost its flagship Kumtor mine in Kyrgyzstan through a contested nationalization in 2021, settling with the Kyrgyz government in 2022. Centerra has since rebuilt, delivering Q2 2026 revenue of $442.7M (+54% YoY) with net earnings of $72.1M and adjusted EBITDA of $163M. H1 2026 revenue surged 58% to $927.4M with net earnings of $151.5M and adjusted EBITDA up 101% to $327.3M. The company holds $552M in cash against only $43.7M in debt, maintaining a fortress balance sheet.
