Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Gold ·NYSE ·Canada
Centerra Gold is a Canadian-based gold and copper mining company operating the Mount Milligan gold-copper mine in British Columbia and the Oksut gold mine in Turkiye. The company also holds the Kemess copper-gold development project and molybdenum operations (Thompson Creek, Endako). FY2025 revenue reached $1.38B with net income of $584M, and Q1 2026 revenue surged 61.8% YoY to $484.7M. Proven and probable gold reserves grew 58% to 5.5M ounces in 2025.

Investment Thesis
Centerra Gold is a mid-tier gold-copper miner delivering exceptional financial results — Q1 2026 revenue surged 61.8% to $484.7M with net earnings up 160% YoY, driven by gold prices near all-time highs and strong copper demand from AI infrastructure buildout [1]. At 5.5x trailing P/E and 8.3x forward P/E with a near-zero-debt balance sheet (D/E 2%), 40% profit margins, and 34% ROE, the stock trades at a significant discount to peers despite superior margins and growth [6]. The Kemess project ($1.1B NPV, 16% IRR) provides a transformative self-funded growth catalyst, and the 58% increase in proven and probable reserves to 5.5M ounces demonstrates operational excellence [2].
The bear case centers on commodity price dependence and operational concentration. Gold and copper prices are cyclical — a meaningful pullback from current highs would compress margins and free cash flow despite the strong balance sheet. Centerra's Oksut mine in Turkiye carries geopolitical risk, and the company bears reputational scars from the Kumtor seizure. The Kemess project requires significant development capital over several years with permitting, construction, and cost overrun risks typical of large greenfield mining projects. The recent COO departure introduces leadership transition risk at the operational level. Net insider selling signals that management may view current valuations as stretched [5]. Additionally, Centerra's 1.17% dividend yield is too low to provide meaningful downside protection [6].
Key Catalysts
  1. Gold and copper price strength — elevated commodity prices provide significant margin tailwinds, with AI data center buildout driving sustained copper demand that benefits Mount Milligan [1]
  2. Kemess project development — $1.1B NPV and 16% IRR provide a transformative growth asset that could significantly expand Centerra's production profile over 3-5 years, funded without dilution [1]
  3. Reserve growth momentum — 58% increase in proven and probable gold reserves to 5.5M ounces in 2025 extends mine life and demonstrates exploration success [2]
Key Risks
  1. Commodity price cyclicality — a correction in gold or copper prices from current highs would compress margins on a business with 100% commodity exposure [6]
  2. Geopolitical risk at Oksut (Turkiye) and reputational Kumtor overhang — operational disruption from political instability or regulatory changes in foreign jurisdictions
  3. Kemess execution risk — multi-year development requiring significant capital with permitting, construction, and cost overrun risks; COO departure adds operational leadership uncertainty
