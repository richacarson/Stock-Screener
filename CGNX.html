Company Profile
Technology ·Scientific & Technical Instruments ·NASDAQ ·United States
Cognex Corporation is the world's leading provider of machine vision products and solutions for factory automation, logistics, and quality inspection. Products include vision systems, vision sensors, barcode readers, and deep learning-based vision software used across automotive, electronics, consumer products, logistics, and packaging industries. Under new CEO Matt Moschner (appointed July 2025), the company is pursuing three strategic priorities: AI Leadership in industrial machine vision, Customer Centricity, and Market Expansion. Cognex reported record Q2 2026 revenue of $291M (+17% YoY) and Q1 revenue of $268M (+24% YoY), with adjusted EBITDA margins expanding for seven consecutive quarters.

Investment Thesis
Cognex at 37x forward earnings is a recovery play in the global leader of AI-powered machine vision, an industry growing at 10-11% CAGR through-cycle. Q2 2026 record revenue of $291M (+17% YoY) and Q1 revenue of $268M (+24% YoY) demonstrate the cyclical recovery is firmly underway, with adjusted EBITDA margins expanding for seven consecutive quarters to 26.9% [1][2]. The company's fortress balance sheet ($622M cash, no debt) and R&D intensity of 14-16% of revenue fund sustained innovation in deep learning vision tools that expand the addressable market. Under new CEO Matt Moschner, the AI Leadership strategy positions Cognex to capture share in a market where AI-based vision adoption is still in early innings, and the doubling of the served customer base is a credible multi-year growth pathway [3].
The bear case centers on valuation, competitive risk, and cyclicality. Forward P/E of 37x and PEG of approximately 1.85 price in significant earnings recovery — any deceleration from the current 20%+ revenue growth trajectory or margin expansion stall could trigger meaningful multiple compression, particularly with the trailing 5-year revenue CAGR of only 4% and a 5-year EPS CAGR of -7.8% reflecting the 2023 downturn [4]. Keyence remains a formidable competitor with deeper resources, and general-purpose AI vision from NVIDIA, Google, and Amazon could eventually commoditize simpler inspection tasks, pressuring Cognex's ASPs. The machine vision industry is inherently cyclical, tied to capital spending in automotive, semiconductor, and consumer electronics — all of which can reverse sharply during economic downturns. ROE of just 7.21% reflects conservative capital allocation that may not adequately reward shareholders despite the large cash position. The Inspire Impact Score of 60 (ALIGNED) with zero negative attributions is a positive for faith-based investors.

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
8
AI Risk Protection (7/9): Cognex is not disrupted by AI — it IS the AI. The company's core products embed deep learning and machine learning algorithms directly into industrial-grade hardware for edge deployment, creating a vertically integrated moat that general-purpose AI providers cannot easily replicate [3]. The combination of ruggedized hardware (IP-rated cameras, specialized lighting, industrial I/O), proprietary vision algorithms trained on millions of industrial images, and deep domain expertise in manufacturing processes creates barriers that cloud-based AI vision from Google, NVIDIA, or startups cannot overcome without equivalent industrial validation. However, the 2/9 deduction reflects real competitive risk: general-purpose vision AI is improving rapidly, and large platform companies could eventually offer 'good enough' solutions that commoditize simpler inspection tasks. Keyence continues to invest aggressively in competing vision products. AI Opportunity (1/1): AI is the core growth driver, not a supplement. CEO Moschner's #1 strategic priority is AI Leadership, and new products incorporating advanced deep learning for defect detection, assembly verification, and logistics sorting are the primary revenue growth engine [1][3]. Edge Learning technology lowers deployment barriers by enabling customers to train vision models with minimal sample images, expanding the addressable market.
