Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Infrastructure ·ASE ·Hong Kong
ChowChow Cloud International Holdings Limited provides cloud solutions across Hong Kong, Singapore, the Philippines, Indonesia, Taiwan, and Australia. The company offers digital transformation consulting, AI-powered proactive cloud managed services via its Sereno Cloud App360 AI and Data Science Platform, and IT infrastructure solutions including on-premises private cloud and public cloud integrations. Founded in 2014, the company completed its US IPO on NYSE American in September 2025 at $4.00 per share, raised $14.85M in a January 2026 unit financing, and currently trades at $0.56 — down 86% from IPO price with a market cap of approximately $20M.

Investment Thesis
ChowChow Cloud delivered strong H1 2025 results with revenue surging 81.3% YoY to HK$178.2M (~US$22.8M) and net income of HK$12.5M (~US$1.6M), driven by three new cloud CDN and server farm customers [1]. TTM revenue has grown 38.2% to approximately $33M, the company raised $14.85M in January 2026 unit financing extending its cash runway, and it reported $26.1M cash as of June 30, 2026 — enough to fund operations into Q4 2027 [1][3]. The asset-light consulting model maintains minimal debt (D/E ~7%), and the Sereno Cloud App360 AI platform provides differentiation in Asia-Pacific markets where cloud adoption is accelerating across the Philippines, Indonesia, Taiwan, and Australia.
The bear case is severe. ChowChow Cloud is a $20M market cap company with only 22 employees, trading at $0.56 — down 86% from its $4.00 IPO price in September 2025 — with a securities fraud class-action lawsuit alleging undisclosed market manipulation during the IPO period [2][4]. The stock has no analyst coverage, no earnings estimates, and TTM financials show a net loss of approximately $3M with a negative profit margin of -9.2% despite revenue growth [3]. Customer concentration risk is extreme — three customers drove 46.9% of H1 2025 revenue, creating binary revenue risk if any relationship is lost. The company competes against hyperscalers (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud) and hundreds of regional cloud providers with vastly greater resources, and AI automation tools from these platforms increasingly reduce the need for third-party cloud consultants. At this market cap and share price, the stock carries extreme liquidity risk and potential NYSE American delisting exposure [3][4].
