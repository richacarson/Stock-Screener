Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Coherus Oncology (formerly Coherus BioSciences) is a commercial-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on immuno-oncology. Its lead product LOQTORZI (toripalimab) is a PD-1 inhibitor approved for nasopharyngeal carcinoma. The pipeline includes Casdozokitug (IL-27 antagonist) and CHS-114 (CCR8 antibody). The company divested its UDENYCA biosimilar franchise in late 2024 for up to $558.4M, completing its transformation to an innovative oncology company in May 2025.
147 Employees ·www.coherus.com

Investment Thesis
Coherus Oncology is a high-risk turnaround story trading at $1.50 with a $231M market cap. The lead clinical asset Casdozokitug demonstrated 38% ORR and 17.2% complete response rate in Phase 2 HCC, suggesting potential clinical differentiation in a large addressable market [1]. LOQTORZI revenue is growing — Q3 2025 showed 92% YoY increase, and management targets $40-50M in annual LOQTORZI revenue with peak revenues by 2028 [1][2]. Post-UDENYCA divestiture proceeds and the $50.1M equity raise supplement $167M in cash as of Q1 2026. At this market cap, success in Casdozokitug Phase 3 would represent substantial upside.
The bear case is severe and immediate. Revenue collapsed 84% YoY to $42.2M, the company is burning approximately $265M annually in free cash flow, and cash of $167M provides less than one year of runway at current rates [2][5]. The $50.1M equity raise dilutes existing shareholders and signals financial desperation. LOQTORZI competes in the PD-1 space against Keytruda ($25B+ annually) and Opdivo, making commercial scale extremely difficult for a 147-person company. Casdozokitug remains in mid-stage development with significant clinical and regulatory risk — Phase 3 failure would be existential. The stock has declined from $1.88 to $1.50 since April, and further dilutive capital raises are likely. Cash runway creates a hard deadline for clinical or commercial milestones that is measured in months, not years.
Key Catalysts
  1. Casdozokitug Phase 2/3 progression in hepatocellular carcinoma — 38% ORR and 17.2% complete response rate represent potentially best-in-class efficacy data that could attract partnership interest [1]
  2. LOQTORZI commercial ramp targeting $40-50M annual revenue with 92% YoY growth trends, and potential label expansions into lung and skin cancers [1][2]
  3. Clinical collaboration with Cancer Research Institute for LOQTORZI + ENB-003 combination in ovarian cancer could expand the immuno-oncology pipeline and attract partner interest [4]
Key Risks
  1. Cash burn of ~$265M annually against $167M cash (Q1 2026) creates sub-one-year survival window — further dilutive capital raises or partnership deals are required to avoid existential crisis [2][5]
  2. LOQTORZI faces overwhelming competition from Keytruda ($25B+) and Opdivo in the PD-1 space, and $42M FY2025 revenue suggests limited commercial traction against entrenched competitors [1]
  3. Casdozokitug Phase 3 clinical risk — HCC trials have a high failure rate, and negative data would eliminate the primary value driver for the company at a time when cash runway is critically short [1]
