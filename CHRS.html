Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Coherus Oncology (formerly Coherus BioSciences, renamed May 2025) is a commercial-stage oncology company focused on developing and commercializing novel cancer therapies. Key commercial product is LOQTORZI (toripalimab), a PD-1 checkpoint inhibitor approved for nasopharyngeal carcinoma, with growing revenue up 61% YoY in Q1 2026. Pipeline includes casdozokitug (anti-IL-27 antibody) showing 38% overall response rate in hepatocellular carcinoma and tagmokitug across multiple solid tumor indications. The company divested its UDENYCA (pegfilgrastim biosimilar) franchise to Intas Pharmaceuticals for $560M in 2024-2025 to fund its strategic pivot to oncology. Cash position of $105.3M as of June 2026 with approximately 12 months of runway.

Investment Thesis
Coherus Oncology at $1.64 and $190M market cap is a high-risk, high-reward oncology bet with a promising but early-stage pipeline. LOQTORZI revenue is growing rapidly — up 61% YoY in Q1 2026 with Q2 revenue of $14.3M (+40% YoY) — and the casdozokitug anti-IL-27 data (38% ORR in HCC triple therapy) represents a genuinely differentiated mechanism of action in a large cancer indication [1][3]. The company has cleaned up its balance sheet through the $560M UDENYCA divestiture, reducing debt by 90% to $38.8M, and the ALIGNED Inspire Impact Score of 42 with zero negative attributions is a positive for faith-based investors.
The bear case is severe and centers on existential financing risk. Cash of $105.3M against a burn rate of approximately $67M in H1 2026 leaves roughly 12 months of runway — insufficient to fund the pipeline through pivotal trial readouts without additional capital raises that will likely be heavily dilutive at the current micro-cap valuation [4]. LOQTORZI's $57M annualized revenue is a rounding error versus Keytruda's $25B+, and penetrating the highly competitive PD-1 market in larger indications (HCC, lung) requires commercial infrastructure spending that the company cannot afford. The stock trades at $1.64, putting it at risk of NASDAQ delisting if it falls below $1.00 for an extended period. Forward P/E and PEG ratios are not calculable for a company losing $0.22 per share per quarter. The company does not own the underlying LOQTORZI molecule (licensed from Junshi Biosciences), limiting strategic flexibility. While casdozokitug's HCC data is promising, it is Phase 2 data in a small trial, and biotech history is littered with promising early data that failed in pivotal trials.
