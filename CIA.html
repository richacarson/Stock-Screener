Company Profile
Financial Services ·Insurance - Life ·NYSE ·United States
Citizens, Inc. is an insurance holding company headquartered in Austin, Texas, operating through two segments: Life Insurance (domestic whole and term life policies) and Home Service Insurance (international dollar-denominated life insurance sold primarily in Latin America). Q1 FY2026 total revenues were $59.7M (+7% YoY) with $2.3M net income, but Q2 FY2026 revenues declined to $60.4M from $65.1M year-ago with a $0.4M net loss, driven by weaker persistency in the domestic segment and lapsed international policies. The company has achieved 15 consecutive quarters of first-year premium growth, indicating distribution traction despite overall revenue headwinds.
