Company Profile
Technology ·Digital Infrastructure ·NASDAQ ·United States
Cipher Digital (formerly Cipher Mining) is a digital infrastructure company transitioning from Bitcoin mining to AI/HPC data center operations. The company has 907 megawatts of operating and contracted capacity and secured approximately $11.4 billion in contracted revenue over initial lease terms of 10-15 years, anchored by a 15-year, 300 MW direct lease with Amazon Web Services worth $5.5 billion. CEO Tyler Page characterizes 2026 as the 'year of execution,' shifting from an aspirational developer to a vertically integrated platform. The company renamed from Cipher Mining to Cipher Digital in February 2026 to reflect its strategic pivot. Q2 2026 revenue of $24.8M missed estimates by $8.3M as legacy mining winds down ahead of data center revenue ramp in H2 2026.

Investment Thesis
Cipher Digital offers a compelling optionality play on AI infrastructure demand — the company has secured $11.4B in contracted revenue over 10-15 year lease terms, anchored by a $5.5B AWS deal for 300 MW of dedicated AI/HPC capacity [1]. The 907 MW of operating and contracted capacity provides near-term revenue visibility, while the 4.4 GW future pipeline positions the company for significant growth in 2027-2029. The $810M Stingray bond offering at a 6% coupon (the company's lowest rate) signals improving capital market access, and $870M in available liquidity provides runway for near-term buildouts [2]. If management executes on data center delivery timelines, the stock's $6.78B market cap against $11.4B in contracted revenue suggests meaningful upside.
The bear case is substantial and immediate. Cipher is currently generating sharply declining revenue ($24.8M in Q2 vs. $60M in Q4 2025) as mining winds down, with no data center rental payments until H2 2026, creating a dangerous revenue gap [1][2]. The balance sheet carries $5.59B in debt against a deeply negative ROE (-167.48%) and $1.12B in TTM net losses, leaving virtually no margin for execution delays. CEO Tyler Page and multiple directors have been net sellers of stock throughout 2026, with no insider purchases recorded — a troubling signal for a company asking shareholders to fund a multi-billion-dollar transformation [4]. Competition is intense from larger, better-capitalized data center operators (Equinix, Digital Realty) and other Bitcoin miners making identical pivots (Core Scientific, Hut 8). The $150.5M non-cash warrant remeasurement loss in Q2 highlights the dilutive financing structure. Hedge fund manager Leopold Aschenbrenner's exit from his CIFR position adds to concerns about the transition timeline. This is a high-conviction bet on execution that offers significant upside if management delivers, but the Infrastructure score of 0 reflects a company with no current financial foundation.
