Company Profile
Industrials ·Marine Shipping ·NASDAQ ·Greece
C3is Inc. provides international seaborne transportation services, owning and operating drybulk carriers and tankers that transport iron ore, coal, grains, crude oil, and petroleum products. Founded in 2021 as a spinoff from Imperial Petroleum and headquartered in Athens, Greece, the company has expanded to six vessels including three Handysize drybulk carriers, one Aframax tanker, and two MR product tankers. C3is reported H1 2026 voyage revenues of $35.6M (up 83% YoY) with net income of $13.2M, driven by daily TCE rates of $40,260 in Q2 (up 144.5% YoY). Market cap is approximately $1.6M with zero bank debt and $27M cash. The company executed a 1:7 reverse stock split in April 2026 and a 1:40 reverse split in August 2026.
