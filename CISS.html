Company Profile
Industrials ·Marine Shipping ·NASDAQ ·Greece
C3is Inc. provides international seaborne transportation services, owning and operating drybulk carriers and tankers that transport iron ore, coal, grains, and crude oil. Founded in 2021 as a spinoff from Imperial Petroleum and headquartered in Athens, Greece, the company operates three Handysize drybulk carriers and one Aframax tanker, with two MR product tankers expected for delivery in 2026 to bring the fleet to six vessels. Market cap is approximately $930K with zero bank debt.
