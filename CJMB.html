Company Profile
Industrials ·Integrated Freight & Logistics ·NASDAQ ·United States
Callan JMB Inc. is a Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Business providing thermal management logistics solutions to the life sciences industry. The company specializes in frozen shipping for personalized medicine, cell therapies, stem cells, vaccines, diagnostic materials, and biopharmaceuticals. It also provides emergency preparedness services including managing medical stockpiles and responding to state/local emergencies, specialty temperature-regulating packaging, and proprietary 21CFR Part 11 compliant monitoring technology. Founded in 2006, headquartered in Spring Branch, Texas, and IPO'd on NASDAQ in February 2025.
