Company Profile
Industrials ·Integrated Freight & Logistics ·NASDAQ ·United States
Callan JMB Inc. is a Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Business operating across two segments: cold chain logistics for life sciences (through subsidiary Coldchain Technology Services) and oil & gas exploration (through subsidiary Callan Power). The cold chain segment provides thermal management logistics, frozen shipping for personalized medicine, cell therapies, vaccines, and biopharmaceuticals, along with emergency preparedness services. In August 2026, Callan Power signed a definitive agreement to acquire Williston Basin oil & gas assets from Reger Oil, with Northern Oil and Gas founder Michael Reger ($2.9B NOG) named as President. The company also signed a $50-75M manufacturing agreement with Attune Biotech and partnered with Alabama State University for pharmaceutical supply chain resilience. Market cap approximately $8.1M. Revenue TTM ~$5.7M with net loss of ~$8.0M.
