Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Specialty Chemicals ·NASDAQ ·United States
Calumet manufactures, formulates, and markets specialty branded products and renewable fuels across North America. Founded in 1919 and headquartered in Indianapolis, the company operates through Specialty Products and Solutions (solvents, waxes, lubricating oils), Performance Brands (Royal Purple, Bel-Ray, TruFuel), and Montana/Renewables (renewable diesel, sustainable aviation fuel, renewable naphtha). Converted from MLP to C-corporation in 2024 and secured a $1.44B DOE loan for SAF expansion. MaxSAF 150 commenced production in May 2026. FY2025 revenue was $4.14B with a net loss of $33.8M, but 2026 EBITDA guidance of $525-550M reflects significant earnings acceleration. Market cap ~$5.2B.
1,540 Employees ·www.calumet.com

The $1.44B DOE loan funds the SAF expansion but adds to the debt load ($825M+ drawn). Government incentive dependency is the existential risk: 45Z Clean Fuel Production Credits and Blenders Tax Credits provide the margin premium that makes SAF production economically viable, and any policy reversal would collapse the economics [1].
