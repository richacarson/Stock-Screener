Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas Refining & Marketing ·NASDAQ ·United States
Clean Energy Fuels Corp. is the largest provider of renewable natural gas (RNG), compressed natural gas (CNG), and liquefied natural gas (LNG) as alternative vehicle fuels in North America, operating 600+ fueling stations serving medium and heavy-duty vehicle fleets. The company also develops, owns, and operates dairy and livestock waste RNG production projects. Clean Energy reported Q2 2026 revenue of $106.4M (+3.7% YoY) with 63.2 million RNG gallons sold, though the company remains unprofitable with a net loss of $14.9M for the quarter. Market cap is approximately $364M.

Investment Thesis
Clean Energy Fuels offers exposure to the renewable natural gas transportation market at a $364M market cap — a fraction of the value of its 600+ station network and RNG production assets. Q1 2026 revenue of $117.6M (+13.3% YoY) beat analyst expectations of $100.5M, with 67.4 million RNG gallons sold demonstrating growing demand for decarbonized heavy-duty trucking fuel [1]. The company's dairy waste-to-RNG production model creates a circular economy value chain, converting agricultural methane (a potent greenhouse gas) into transportation fuel while generating environmental credits. The Inspire Impact Score of 72 (ALIGNED) with no negative attributions makes CLNE one of the most faith-aligned energy companies available, and growing regulatory mandates for fleet decarbonization could accelerate station utilization.
The bear case is dominated by persistent unprofitability and existential business model questions. Clean Energy has been operating for 25 years without achieving sustained profitability — Q2 2026 net loss was $14.9M on $106.4M revenue, with a -21.27% profit margin and -16.4% ROE that score 0 in the infrastructure framework [2][4]. The stock at $1.65 trades near multi-year lows with a $364M market cap, and the PEG ratio of 4.72 suggests even modest earnings expectations are overvalued. Revenue growth of just 3.7% YoY is inadequate to demonstrate the scalability needed to achieve profitability. The broader EV adoption trend threatens to strand RNG infrastructure as heavy-duty electric trucks from Tesla, Daimler, and others enter the market. With only 503 employees and thin daily liquidity of ~$3.2M, institutional investment is constrained, and any capital raises would be highly dilutive at current share prices.

  2. Electric vehicle displacement risk as heavy-duty battery-electric and hydrogen fuel cell trucks from Tesla Semi, Daimler eCascadia, and Volvo VNR Electric enter the market, potentially stranding RNG fueling infrastructure [4]

AI Risk Protection (8/9): Clean Energy's core business — operating 600+ physical fueling stations, producing RNG from dairy waste, and transporting fuel to heavy-duty vehicle fleets — involves physical-world infrastructure that AI structurally cannot displace [1][2]. Fueling stations require physical construction, maintenance, and fuel logistics. RNG production from organic waste involves biological processes, physical equipment, and environmental permitting. Heavy-duty trucking customers need physical fuel delivered to physical locations. AI may even increase demand for data center power and associated transportation logistics. The primary risk to CLNE is not AI but rather EV adoption displacing natural gas vehicles — a market shift rather than an AI disruption dynamic.
