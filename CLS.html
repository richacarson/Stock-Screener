Company Profile
Technology ·Electronic Components ·NYSE ·Canada
Celestica is a Toronto-headquartered design, manufacturing, hardware platform and supply chain solutions provider. It operates through two segments: Connectivity & Cloud Solutions (CCS), which designs and manufactures custom AI servers, rack-scale AI platforms (Helios with AMD), networking switches, and storage hardware for hyperscalers (81% of revenue), and Advanced Technology Solutions (ATS), serving aerospace & defense, industrial, capital equipment, and HealthTech end markets. Q2 2026 revenue reached $4.70B (+62% YoY) with full-year guidance raised to $20.5B (+65% YoY) and adjusted EPS of $11.30 (+87% YoY). Market cap exceeds $54B.

Investment Thesis
Celestica at 37.6x forward earnings is a transformed company delivering extraordinary growth — Q2 2026 revenue of $4.70B (+62% YoY) with full-year guidance raised to $20.5B (+65%) and adjusted EPS of $11.30 (+87%) confirm Celestica as a primary beneficiary of the hyperscaler AI infrastructure buildout [1]. CCS segment surged 84% YoY to $3.8B, now comprising 81% of total revenue, driven by rack-scale AI platforms, GPU networking, and liquid cooling demand. Adjusted operating margin reached a record 8.2%, up from 7.4% a year ago, demonstrating operating leverage as the mix shifts toward higher-value design-led work. ROE of 52.45%, D/E of just 45%, and a forward PEG of ~0.61 indicate growth is both capital-efficient and reasonably priced relative to the earnings trajectory [5]. Free cash flow guidance raised to $600M, and management guides for 2027 growth to accelerate beyond 2026 rates, suggesting years of runway remain in the AI infrastructure cycle.
The bear case centers on customer concentration, cycle risk, and valuation vulnerability. Celestica's CCS revenue is concentrated in a small number of hyperscaler customers, and any rationalization of AI capex spending — as briefly feared during the DeepSeek episode — could cause sharp revenue declines in an EMS business model with limited pricing power [2]. The stock has appreciated 127% over the past year to $484, pushing the trailing P/E to ~48x and forward P/E to 37.6x — premium multiples that leave limited margin of safety and require sustained 50%+ revenue growth to justify. Profit margins of 6.95% remain structurally lower than pure-play OEMs, constraining earnings leverage relative to revenue growth. Net insider selling of $63M+ in recent months signals management is diversifying rather than deploying personal capital at current prices [5]. If hyperscaler customers choose to insource AI hardware design — as some have done with custom silicon — Celestica's competitive position could erode, and the CEO transition (Mionis to board chair) introduces execution risk during the most critical growth phase.
Key Catalysts
  1. 2027 revenue and EPS growth guided to accelerate beyond 2026's 65%/87% rates as hyperscaler AI infrastructure spending continues to expand, with Celestica's Helios platform and 800G/1.6T networking solutions capturing increased wallet share [1]
  2. Operating margin expansion from 7.4% to record 8.2% as product mix shifts toward higher-value design-led AI platforms and liquid cooling solutions, with further upside as proprietary content increases [1]
  3. Free cash flow of $600M (raised guidance) enabling capital return and continued investment in next-generation AI hardware manufacturing capacity to support the multi-year hyperscaler buildout cycle [1]
Key Risks
  1. Hyperscaler customer concentration — a handful of large cloud providers account for the majority of CCS revenue, and any capex rationalization, spending pause, or insourcing decision could cause disproportionate revenue decline in an EMS model with limited pricing power [2]
  2. AI infrastructure spending cycle risk — 65% revenue growth is tied to a secular buildout that could decelerate if AI model efficiency gains reduce compute requirements or if economic returns on AI investments disappoint enterprise customers [2]
  3. Valuation compression risk with trailing P/E ~48x and forward P/E 37.6x after a 127% 52-week gain — the stock is priced for sustained hyper-growth, and any deceleration or margin disappointment could trigger significant multiple contraction [5]
