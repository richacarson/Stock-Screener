Company Profile
Technology ·Bitcoin Mining / Digital Infrastructure ·NASDAQ ·United States
CleanSpark is a US-based digital infrastructure company operating the largest fleet of Bitcoin mining machines among public US miners, with 50 EH/s operational hashrate representing approximately 5.8% of global Bitcoin network hashrate. The company reported TTM revenue of $785M and is pivoting beyond pure-play Bitcoin mining toward high-performance compute (HPC) and AI data center development, acquiring 270+ acres in Austin County and up to 447 acres in Brazoria County, Texas, targeting 890+ MW of aggregate capacity for AI and HPC workloads by 2027. Q3 FY2026 (ended June 2026) revenue was $138M, down 31% YoY, as the April 2024 Bitcoin halving continues to compress per-block mining rewards. CEO Matt Schultz (co-founder) returned in August 2025 following the resignation of Zachary Bradford.

Investment Thesis
CleanSpark at 18.05x forward earnings is the largest US public Bitcoin miner by operational hashrate (50 EH/s, 5.8% of global network) with a credible AI data center pivot that could rerate the stock from Bitcoin miner to digital infrastructure platform [1][3]. TTM revenue of $785M and the company's differentiated low-cost energy strategy (5.2 cents/kWh, 90%+ clean energy) provide operational resilience. The AI and HPC pivot is concrete — 890+ MW of Texas capacity under development with $1.15B in convertible note financing, targeting H1 2027 energization — and positions CleanSpark as a vertically integrated infrastructure provider serving both Bitcoin network security and AI workload demand [3]. BTC holdings provide balance sheet optionality if Bitcoin prices recover toward previous highs, and the 5-year revenue CAGR of 110% (from $39M to $766M) demonstrates execution capability in rapidly scaling physical infrastructure.
The risks are substantial and immediate. Q3 FY2026 revenue declined 31% YoY to $138M as the April 2024 Bitcoin halving continues to compress per-block mining rewards, and H1 FY2026 reported a $757M net loss including large unrealized BTC fair value adjustments [1][6]. Revenue has declined for multiple consecutive quarters on a YoY basis, and the company is consuming cash. The $1.15B convertible note creates refinancing risk if the HPC pivot takes longer than expected or if BTC prices decline further. Glassdoor reviews describe a toxic work environment with 'revolving door' turnover, and the August 2025 CEO resignation introduces execution risk during the company's most critical strategic transition [4]. The AI data center market is increasingly competitive — CoreWeave, Equinix, Digital Realty, and hyperscalers are all building capacity — and CleanSpark's ability to compete as a late entrant without deep data center operating expertise is unproven. The Inspire Impact Score of 63 (ALIGNED) is a positive, but the speculative nature of both Bitcoin mining economics and the AI pivot make CLSK a high-conviction bet on dual commodity cycles (BTC + AI compute) rather than a stable growth investment.
Key Catalysts
  1. Bitcoin price recovery toward $100K+ would restore gross margins above 50% and dramatically reduce reported losses, given the mining model's operating leverage on 50 EH/s of installed hashrate capacity [1]
  2. First AI and HPC data center tenant announcements with multi-year power purchase agreements at the Texas campuses would validate the infrastructure pivot thesis and de-risk the $1.15B capital deployment [3]
  3. Hashrate growth continuing toward 60+ EH/s combined with network difficulty stabilization would improve per-coin economics and protect mining revenue, with monthly BTC production running at 650-670 BTC [2]
Key Risks
  1. Bitcoin price volatility is the primary earnings driver — a sustained decline below $60K would compress margins materially and accelerate cash burn against the $1.15B convertible debt load, creating potential liquidity pressure before AI revenue commences [1][6]
  2. AI data center pivot execution risk — Texas campus energization delays, failure to sign anchor tenants before 2027, or intensifying competition from CoreWeave, Equinix, and hyperscaler-owned capacity could leave CleanSpark with stranded infrastructure capital at elevated leverage [3]
  3. Organizational instability — CEO resignation, leadership shakeup, and deeply negative Glassdoor reviews describing authoritarian culture and high turnover raise questions about execution capability during the most critical strategic transition in the company's history [4]
