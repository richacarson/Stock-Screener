Company Profile
Healthcare ·Medical Care Facilities ·NasdaqGS ·United Kingdom
COMPASS Pathways plc is a mental health care biotechnology company developing COMP360, an investigational synthetic psilocybin therapy for treatment-resistant depression (TRD). COMP360 has received FDA Breakthrough Therapy designation and completed two pivotal Phase 3 trials with positive results. The company has initiated a rolling NDA submission and holds a Commissioner's National Priority Voucher enabling accelerated FDA review. Also developing a psilocybin program for PTSD. Founded in 2016, headquartered in London, United Kingdom.
156 Employees ·www.compasspathways.com

Investment Thesis
COMPASS Pathways is at the most critical inflection point in its history — both Phase 3 trials for COMP360 psilocybin therapy in treatment-resistant depression met primary endpoints, the rolling NDA submission is underway, and the Commissioner's National Priority Voucher could compress FDA review to 1-2 months after final submission [1][2][3]. Commercial launch readiness is targeted for end of 2026 with launch expected H1 2027. The addressable market is enormous — approximately 100 million people worldwide suffer from TRD with inadequate treatment options. With $466M in cash providing runway into 2028 and the White House Executive Order accelerating psychedelic research, the regulatory tailwind is unprecedented [3]. At $13.47 per share with 4.8M average daily volume, the stock is liquid and positioned for significant upside on approval.
The risk profile is binary and extreme. FDA approval is not guaranteed despite positive Phase 3 results — the FDA rejected Lykos Therapeutics' MDMA therapy for PTSD in 2024, demonstrating that positive trials do not guarantee approval for psychedelic compounds [4]. COMP360 requires a complex REMS program (certified prescribers, trained facilitators, 6-hour supervised sessions) that creates significant commercialization challenges and limits the addressable prescriber base. The company generates zero revenue, has -68.48% ROE, and will continue to burn cash through launch. DEA rescheduling from Schedule I to Schedule II is a prerequisite for commercial distribution, and the timeline remains uncertain. Payer reimbursement for a novel psychedelic therapy is unproven, and the per-session cost (including facilitator time) could limit adoption. Pre-revenue biotechs are inherently speculative — the stock could decline 50%+ on any negative regulatory signal.
Key Catalysts
  1. Completion of rolling NDA submission and FDA acceptance, potentially followed by ultra-accelerated review (1-2 months) under the Commissioner's National Priority Voucher
  2. DEA rescheduling of psilocybin from Schedule I to Schedule II, clearing the regulatory path for commercial distribution of COMP360
  3. Commercial launch in H1 2027 with initial revenue generation, validating the REMS delivery model and payer reimbursement pathway for psychedelic-assisted therapy
Key Risks
  1. FDA could reject or require additional studies despite positive Phase 3 data — the 2024 MDMA rejection demonstrates that psychedelic therapies face heightened regulatory scrutiny beyond standard drug approval processes
  2. Complex REMS requirements (certified prescribers, trained facilitators, 6-hour sessions) create significant commercialization friction and limit the addressable prescriber base, potentially capping revenue potential
  3. Pre-revenue biotech with zero revenue, -68.48% ROE, and binary outcomes — the stock is entirely dependent on regulatory and commercial milestones with no revenue floor if approval is delayed or denied
