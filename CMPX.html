Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Compass Therapeutics, Inc. is a clinical-stage oncology-focused biopharmaceutical company developing proprietary antibody-based therapeutics to treat cancer. The company's scientific focus is on the relationship between angiogenesis, the immune system, and tumor growth. Its pipeline includes tovecimig (CTX-009), a DLL4/VEGF-A bispecific antibody in Phase 2/3 for biliary tract cancer, CTX-471 (CD137 agonist), CTX-8371 (PD-1 x PD-L1 bispecific), and CTX-10726 (PD-1 x VEGF-A bispecific). Founded in 2014, headquartered in Cambridge, Massachusetts.

Investment Thesis
Compass Therapeutics sits at a potential inflection point with tovecimig's positive Phase 2/3 COMPANION-002 results in biliary tract cancer, demonstrating statistically significant overall survival and progression-free survival benefits [2]. A BLA filing is planned for late 2026 with potential FDA approval in H2 2027, which would transform Compass from a zero-revenue clinical-stage company to a commercial oncology franchise. The pipeline extends beyond tovecimig with three additional clinical-stage antibody programs (CTX-471, CTX-8371, CTX-10726), each targeting distinct biological pathways. The balance sheet is strong with $195M in cash providing runway into 2028, and the $403M market cap offers significant upside if tovecimig reaches approval [1].
The primary risk is binary clinical and regulatory execution. Despite positive Phase 2/3 data, the BLA filing has not yet occurred, and FDA review timelines could extend into 2028 or later. The $2.19 share price reflects deep market skepticism -- seven analysts have a consensus Strong Buy with an average price target of $14.57, implying the market is discounting a significant probability of failure [3]. Cash burn of approximately $66.5M annually means the $195M runway, while adequate, leaves limited margin for delays or additional trials. As a pre-revenue biotech with no commercial infrastructure, Compass would need to build or partner for sales capabilities, adding execution risk and cost. The stock trades at just 2x cash, suggesting the market assigns minimal value to the pipeline beyond the balance sheet [1][3].
