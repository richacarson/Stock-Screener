Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Compass Therapeutics, Inc. is a clinical-stage oncology-focused biopharmaceutical company developing proprietary antibody-based therapeutics to treat cancer. The company's scientific focus is on the relationship between angiogenesis, the immune system, and tumor growth. Lead asset tovecimig (CTX-009), a DLL4/VEGF-A bispecific antibody, met its primary endpoint in the Phase 2/3 COMPANION-002 study for biliary tract cancer with ORR of 18.0% vs 5.3% for control (p=0.0228). Pipeline includes CTX-8371 (PD-1 x PD-L1 bispecific) and CTX-10726 (PD-1 x VEGF-A bispecific). Led by CEO Thomas Schuetz, MD, PhD since May 2024. Cash of $180M at Q2 2026 with runway into 2028. Market cap approximately $416M.
30 Employees ·www.compasstherapeutics.com

Investment Thesis
Compass Therapeutics sits at a potential inflection point with tovecimig's positive Phase 2/3 COMPANION-002 results in biliary tract cancer, demonstrating a statistically significant ORR of 18.0% vs 5.3% for control (p=0.0228) with overall survival and progression-free survival benefits [1][2]. FDA feedback on confirmatory study design is expected, potentially leading to regulatory submission and transforming Compass from a zero-revenue clinical-stage company to a commercial oncology franchise. The pipeline extends beyond tovecimig with CTX-8371 showing responses in heavily pre-treated TNBC, NSCLC, and Hodgkin lymphoma patients, and CTX-10726 entering Phase 1 [1]. The balance sheet provides $180M in cash with runway into 2028. The Inspire Impact Score of 31 (ALIGNED) across 5 positive dimensions makes this attractive for faith-based oncology investors. At $2.31 per share, the stock trades near cash value, suggesting the market assigns minimal pipeline premium.
The primary risk is binary clinical and regulatory execution. Despite positive Phase 2/3 data showing ORR of 18.0%, the absolute response rate is modest and the BLA pathway requires FDA alignment on confirmatory study design [2]. Cash burn is accelerating — Q2 2026 net loss of $25.2M exceeded Q1's $18.3M, implying an annualized burn rate of ~$87M that reduces the $180M cash position to only ~2 years of runway [1]. If FDA requires a larger confirmatory trial, additional capital raises would be necessary, diluting the existing ~180M shares outstanding. As a pre-revenue biotech with approximately 30 employees and no commercial infrastructure, Compass would need to build or partner for sales capabilities, adding execution risk and cost. The competitive landscape is intensifying as larger players (Amgen, Roche, AstraZeneca) advance their own bispecific antibody programs with vastly greater resources. Liquidity is thin at ~$1.67M daily trading value, barely clearing the $1M floor and limiting institutional participation [5].
Key Catalysts
  1. FDA feedback on tovecimig confirmatory study design expected by Labor Day 2026, potentially clarifying the regulatory pathway toward BLA submission and commercial-stage transformation [1][2]
