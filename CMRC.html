Company Profile

Commerce.com, Inc. (formerly BigCommerce Holdings) provides an AI-driven commerce ecosystem through its SaaS platform enabling merchants to orchestrate digital commerce experiences across owned and third-party channels for B2B, B2C, and small-business use cases. The company unifies three products — BigCommerce, Feedonomics, and Makeswift — under the Commerce.com brand, positioning for an agentic-commerce era. Headquartered in Austin, Texas.

Investment Thesis
Commerce.com is on track for its first full year of GAAP profitability, with net losses narrowing from -50.1% profit margin in 2022 to -1.67% on a trailing-twelve-month basis and a Forward P/E of just 4.94x and FY2025 PEG of 0.77 — both metrics well inside IOWN's target range and suggesting the market is not pricing in the earnings inflection already underway [1][4]. The 2025 rebrand to Commerce.com, unifying BigCommerce, Feedonomics, and Makeswift around "agentic commerce," targets a defensible complexity/enterprise niche that CEO Travis Hess has explicitly chosen not to contest with Shopify head-on, and new AI-driven catalogue, search, and payments features give the platform fresh product surface area [1][2][3].
The bear case centers on decelerating growth and demand softness: revenue growth has fallen from 26.9% (2022) to just 2.8% (2025), and 2026 full-year guidance was cut by roughly $18M mid-year on weaker B2C replatforming demand, raising doubts about whether the rebrand can reaccelerate the top line [1]. Debt/equity of roughly 307% and return on equity of -12.70% show the balance sheet and profitability are still recovering rather than healthy, and CEO Hess's wholesale replacement of the senior leadership team introduces execution risk during a critical repositioning [1][2]. E-commerce platforms broadly face rising substitution risk from agentic AI shopping tools, and if Commerce.com's complexity-focused niche fails to differentiate meaningfully from larger, better-capitalized rivals, the current cheap valuation could prove to be a value trap rather than a turnaround opportunity.
Key Catalysts
  1. Achieving full-year GAAP profitability in 2026 for the first time in company history, which could rerate the stock off its deeply discounted forward multiple
  2. AI-driven commerce features (Feedonomics AI, Makeswift AI tools, new payments options) gaining measurable merchant adoption and reaccelerating revenue growth above the current 2.8% pace
  3. Stabilization or reacceleration of B2C replatforming demand following the mid-2026 guidance cut
Key Risks
  1. Revenue growth has decelerated to 2.8% YoY with 2026 guidance already cut once, raising doubts about the rebrand's ability to restore double-digit growth
  2. Extensive leadership team turnover under CEO Hess creates execution risk during a critical strategic repositioning
  3. Agentic AI shopping tools and AI-native commerce platforms pose a structural long-term substitution risk to traditional SaaS storefront platforms, including Commerce.com's core subscription business
