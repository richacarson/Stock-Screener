Company Profile
Utilities ·Utilities - Regulated Electric ·NYSE ·United States
CMS Energy Corporation operates as an energy company primarily through its subsidiary, Consumers Energy, which provides regulated electric and natural gas service to 6.8 million residents in Michigan. The company also operates NorthStar Clean Energy, an independent power generation business focused on renewable energy.

Investment Thesis
CMS Energy offers a compelling combination of regulated utility stability and clean energy growth. The company raised its 2026 EPS guidance to $3.83-$3.90, supported by strong Q1 results ($1.13 adjusted EPS, up 10.8% YoY) and outperformance from NorthStar Clean Energy [1]. The 6-8% long-term EPS growth target, underpinned by a robust capital investment program in grid modernization and renewable energy, provides visibility into sustainable earnings growth.
The stock trades at a forward P/E of 19.3x, which is fair for a high-quality regulated utility with above-average growth. The 2.6% dividend yield is modest but supported by a conservative 51% payout ratio with room for continued dividend growth. Michigan's regulatory environment remains constructive, and growing data center demand could provide incremental load growth [2]. The ALIGNED faith alignment score adds appeal for values-based investors focused on clean energy.
