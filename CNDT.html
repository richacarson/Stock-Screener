Company Profile
Technology ·Information Technology Services ·NASDAQ ·United States
Conduent Incorporated provides business process solutions and digital interaction services to government, healthcare, transportation, and commercial clients worldwide. Spun off from Xerox Holdings in 2017, the company operates through Government Services (tolling, benefits administration, payment services), Transportation (traffic management, transit fare collection, road usage charging), and Commercial Industries (business process outsourcing, customer experience management). Revenue has declined for six consecutive years from $4.16B (FY2020) to FY2026 guidance of $2.15-2.25B. CEO Cliff Skelton leads the turnaround effort. Headquartered in Florham Park, New Jersey.
