Investment Thesis
CN Energy Group's investment case rests entirely on optionality in a deeply distressed situation — a $2.7M market cap company with two speculative pivots: the Pathenbot intelligent robotics platform (CES 2026 award winner targeting North American SME automation) and a nascent oil trading business through the $2M Blessing Logistics acquisition [2][4].
