Company Profile
Industrials ·Railroads ·NYSE ·Canada
Canadian National Railway operates the largest rail network in Canada and the only transcontinental network in North America, spanning approximately 20,000 route miles connecting the Atlantic and Pacific oceans through Canada and the U.S. Midwest. CN transports petroleum, grain, forest products, metals, automotive, and intermodal freight across three coasts.

Investment Thesis
Canadian National Railway is a best-in-class infrastructure franchise operating the only transcontinental rail network in North America, delivering accelerating results with Q2 2026 revenue of C$4.753B (up 11% YoY), EPS of C$2.06 (up 10%), and H1 2026 free cash flow of C$1.842B up 19% [1]. Management raised 2026 guidance to low single-digit RTM growth (from flat) and mid-to-high single-digit EPS growth, citing stronger business momentum from grain, energy products, and the Union Pacific strategic agreement [1]. The 28-year consecutive dividend growth streak with 8.8% 5yr CAGR and manageable 47% payout ratio provides reliable income, while the Google Cloud AI partnership delivers measurable operational improvements including 3% fuel efficiency gains [2][5].
The bear case centers on valuation expansion and structural growth constraints. Forward P/E has expanded to 23.2x from 17.0x as the stock rallied 18% from $109 to $130, compressing the margin of safety for a company guiding mid-to-high single-digit EPS growth [5]. PEG ratio of ~3.0x suggests the stock is fully valued relative to growth expectations. The 5yr revenue CAGR of ~2% reflects the mature nature of railroad freight, and even the improved Q2 2026 growth of 11% may be cyclical rather than structural. D/E of 106% with C$22.3B total debt against limited cash creates refinancing risk, while tariff uncertainty remains — CN pulled its 2024-2026 outlook previously amid trade policy disruptions that could reduce cross-border freight volumes [1][3]. Climate transition costs — battery-electric locomotives, emission reduction targets — represent significant capital deployment with uncertain returns, and railroad labor disruptions remain a recurring threat [3].

  2. Tariff and trade policy uncertainty — CN previously pulled its 2024-2026 outlook amid tariff disruptions, and cross-border freight volumes remain vulnerable to North American trade policy shifts [1]
  3. D/E of 106% with C$22.3B total debt and capital-intensive sustainability investments create ongoing financing needs, while railroad labor disruptions and precision scheduled railroading tensions remain structural risks [1][3]

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
8
AI Risk Protection (7/9): Railroads are structurally immune to AI displacement — 20,000 miles of physical track, rolling stock, and logistics infrastructure cannot be digitized or replaced by software [1]. CN's transcontinental network is a natural monopoly with barriers to entry that no AI can replicate. AI-driven economic growth (data center construction requiring power infrastructure, increased manufacturing) drives higher freight volumes rather than displacing them [2]. However, trucking competitors may adopt AI-driven optimization faster due to shorter fleet replacement cycles (3-4 years vs rail's 18.4-year average), creating incremental competitive pressure on intermodal market share [2]. AI Opportunity (1/1): CN's Google Cloud partnership generates measurable value — Q2 2026 showed 3% fuel efficiency improvement, Automated Inspection Portals with AI-powered defect detection reduce unplanned downtime, and network optimization algorithms improve asset utilization across the entire 20,000-mile network [2].
