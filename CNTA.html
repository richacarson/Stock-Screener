Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United Kingdom
Centessa Pharmaceuticals plc was a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on orexin-based neuroscience therapeutics. Its lead program ORX750 (cleminorexton) is an orexin receptor 2 agonist for narcolepsy type 1 and 2 and idiopathic hypersomnia. ACQUIRED by Eli Lilly for $38/share cash plus up to $9/share in contingent value rights (~$7.8B total). Acquisition closed June 24, 2026; ADSs delisted from NASDAQ.

Investment Thesis
Centessa Pharmaceuticals was acquired by Eli Lilly for $38/share in cash plus up to $9/share in contingent value rights (approximately $7.8B total value), with the deal closing on June 24, 2026 [2][5]. Shareholders voted overwhelmingly in favor (126.7M for vs. 23K against). The acquisition validated Centessa's orexin pivot — ORX750 (cleminorexton) demonstrated 87% weekly cataplexy rate reduction in Phase 2a NT1 patients, positioning it as a potential best-in-class treatment for narcolepsy and idiopathic hypersomnia [1]. The pipeline also includes ORX142 and ORX489 for neurodegenerative and neuropsychiatric indications, expanding the orexin platform's addressable market.
The investment opportunity has concluded. CNTA ADSs were delisted from NASDAQ following the June 24, 2026 acquisition closing, and the company is now a wholly owned Eli Lilly subsidiary [5]. Shareholders who held through the acquisition received $38/share in cash, with contingent value rights of up to $9/share tied to future milestones. For historical context, the stock appreciated 183% from its pre-announcement levels, validating the orexin pivot thesis. Remaining risks are limited to CVR milestone achievement under Lilly's ownership — ORX750 Phase 3 data and FDA approval timing will determine whether CVR holders receive the additional $9/share [2]. The securities litigation history over lixivaptan and ZF874 disclosures was resolved as part of the acquisition process.
