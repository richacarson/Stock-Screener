Company Profile
Healthcare ·Medical Devices ·NASDAQ ·United States
Co-Diagnostics, Inc. is a molecular diagnostics company that designs, manufactures, and markets PCR-based reagents, tests, and instruments for infectious disease detection, including COVID-19, influenza, tuberculosis, hepatitis B and C, malaria, dengue, HPV, chikungunya, and Zika virus. Its Co-Dx PCR platform targets point-of-care and at-home testing settings. The company is advancing an upper respiratory multiplex test toward FDA submission targeted for Q3 2026 and, through its CoSara joint venture in India, secured a CDSCO manufacturing license for the PCR Pro instrument with an expanded $13B addressable South Asia market and targeted TB-test commercialization by Q3 2026 [1][2]. Founded in 2013 and headquartered in Salt Lake City, Utah, under Co-Founder, Chairman and CEO Dwight Egan.
