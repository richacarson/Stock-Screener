Company Profile
Healthcare ·Medical Devices ·NASDAQ ·United States
Co-Diagnostics, Inc. is a molecular diagnostics company that designs, manufactures, and markets PCR-based reagents, tests, and instruments for infectious disease detection, including COVID-19, influenza, tuberculosis, hepatitis B and C, malaria, dengue, HPV, chikungunya, and Zika virus. Its Co-Dx PCR platform targets point-of-care and at-home testing settings. Founded in 2013 and headquartered in Salt Lake City, Utah, the company also operates the CoSara joint venture pursuing PCR Pro manufacturing and TB testing opportunities in India and South Asia. Trades on NASDAQ under ticker CODX.

Investment Thesis
Co-Diagnostics is a sub-$10 million market cap molecular diagnostics company attempting to pivot from its 2021 COVID-19 testing windfall (peak revenue $97.9M) toward a point-of-care PCR platform strategy anchored by two named, dated catalysts: a Dual 510(k)/CLIA Waiver FDA submission for an upper respiratory (Flu A/Flu B/RSV) multiplex test targeted for Q3 2026, backed by a completed 1,400-patient clinical study and over 10,000 test-cup runs, and a CoSara joint-venture push into a $13B South Asian TB testing opportunity following a new CDSCO manufacturing license, timed to updated WHO TB testing guidance [1][2][5][6]. The balance sheet carries essentially no debt (D/E of 0.13%), giving the company some optionality if either catalyst converts to revenue [3].
The bear case dominates the current picture: TTM revenue of just $718,166 is down 79.5% year-over-year and roughly 99% from the 2021 peak, against a $48.5M TTM net loss and an Altman Z-Score of -9.03 signaling severe financial distress [2][3]. The FY2025 Form 10-K discloses substantial doubt about the company's ability to continue as a going concern, and cash fell from $11.9M at year-end 2025 to $8.2M at Q1 2026 — a burn rate implying roughly two to three quarters of runway without additional financing, most likely via further dilutive equity raises under the company's ATM program [2][4]. With FDA clearance and India commercialization both still pending as of mid-2026 and a history of shifting timelines against entrenched multi-billion-dollar rivals, CODX is a binary, high-risk speculative position rather than a fundamentally sound holding [1][5][6][9].
