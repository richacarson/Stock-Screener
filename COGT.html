Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Cogent Biosciences is a late-clinical-stage biotechnology company developing precision therapies for genetically defined diseases. Lead candidate bezuclastinib is a selective KIT inhibitor with two NDAs under FDA review (NonAdvSM and 2nd-line GIST under RTOR) and a third NDA (AdvSM) planned for H1 2026, with commercial launch expected H2 2026. Pipeline includes CGT4859 (FGFR2/3 inhibitor in Phase 1/2) and additional early-stage programs.

Investment Thesis
Cogent Biosciences has materially de-risked since the NDA submission milestone, with two bezuclastinib NDAs now under FDA review — SUMMIT for NonAdvSM and PEAK for 2nd-line GIST under Real-Time Oncology Review (RTOR) — and a third APEX NDA for AdvSM on track for H1 2026 [1]. The PEAK trial's mPFS of 16.5 vs 9.2 months (HR=0.50; p<0.0001) represents a highly compelling efficacy signal for what would be the first new second-line GIST therapy in over 20 years [1]. APEX response rates of 57% (mIWG) and 80% (PPR) in AdvSM position bezuclastinib as a potential best-in-class KIT inhibitor. At $38.85/share with a $6.64B market cap, the stock has appreciated 7% from April levels, reflecting growing confidence in regulatory success. The $866.4M cash position provides runway into 2028 including commercialization without dilution [3]. CEO Robbins' Array Biopharma background (built and sold to Pfizer for $11.4B) provides directly relevant commercial launch experience.
The bear case remains substantial for a pre-revenue company valued at $6.64B. At $38.85/share, the market prices in successful FDA approval, commercial launch, and significant peak revenue — articles questioning the 'pricey valuation' after NDA filing reflect market skepticism about whether the clinical data justifies the premium [4]. Q1 2026 operating expenses of $103.6M ($75.4M R&D + $28.2M G&A, up 38% YoY) imply annual cash burn accelerating to ~$400M as commercial infrastructure scales [3]. The $866.4M cash runway depletes by late 2028 without meaningful revenue, and any FDA delay or Complete Response Letter would trigger severe downside. Competition from Blueprint Medicines' avapritinib in the KIT inhibitor space could limit market share. Cogent has zero commercial track record — building a rare disease sales force, payer access programs, and distribution network simultaneously creates significant execution risk. Drug pricing scrutiny for rare disease treatments could compress commercial margins [1].
