Company Profile
Technology ·Semiconductors & Photonics ·NYSE ·United States
Coherent Corp. is a vertically integrated photonics and materials company providing optical interconnects, lasers, and engineered materials for AI data centers, telecommunications, industrial, and electronics markets. Formed through the II-VI/Coherent merger (completed 2022), the company has been transformed under CEO Jim Anderson (since June 2024) into the 'AI Photonics' leader. FY2026 (ending June 2026) delivered record revenue of $7.0B (+28% pro forma), with Q4 revenue of $2.05B (+34% YoY) driven by explosive AI data center demand for 800G and 1.6T optical transceivers [1]. Stock has risen approximately 350% since Anderson's appointment. Market cap approximately $57B.

Investment Thesis
Coherent at $290 and 0.89x PEG is the premier pure-play on AI infrastructure scaling, delivering record FY2026 revenue of $7.0B (+28% pro forma) with non-GAAP EPS growth of 59% and an accelerating trajectory — Q4 revenue of $2.05B (+34%) with guidance for $3B+ quarterly revenue by end of FY2027 [1]. The company's vertical integration from indium phosphide substrates through finished 800G/1.6T optical transceivers creates a manufacturing moat no competitor can easily replicate, while the technology roadmap through 3.2T provides multi-year visibility into product leadership. CEO Jim Anderson's 350% stock appreciation since June 2024 reflects best-in-class execution, cultural transformation from conservative to speed-oriented, and strategic repositioning as the 'AI Photonics' leader [2]. With D/E at just 31%, the balance sheet supports both the capacity expansion needed for the AI buildout and continued margin improvement from operating leverage.
The bear case centers on valuation and cyclicality risk. Forward P/E of 39x prices in sustained hypergrowth, and any deceleration in AI infrastructure spending — whether from hyperscaler capex pauses, technology transitions between transceiver generations, or macroeconomic slowdown — could trigger meaningful multiple compression. The semiconductor/photonics industry is inherently cyclical, and the current AI buildout may represent a peak demand period that normalizes over time. GAAP profitability remains modest (7.1% net margin, 4.72% ROE) as the company invests heavily in capacity and R&D, meaning earnings quality depends on the growth trajectory continuing [4]. The company's reliance on a few hyperscaler customers creates customer concentration risk — any single customer pulling back orders could materially impact revenue. Free cash flow growth was -88.78% TTM as capital expenditures ramped for capacity expansion [4]. The Inspire Impact Score of 55 (ALIGNED) supports faith-based allocation, but position sizing should reflect the valuation premium and cyclical nature of the business.

  3. Capacity expansion execution risk with $3B quarterly revenue target requiring significant capital investment — free cash flow turned deeply negative (-88.78% TTM) as capex ramped, and any demand shortfall against expanded capacity would compress margins [4]
