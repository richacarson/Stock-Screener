Company Profile
Technology ·Scientific & Technical Instruments ·NYSE ·United States
Coherent Corp. designs, manufactures, and markets engineered materials, optoelectronic components, and optical networking products for datacenter, industrial, and communications markets worldwide. Formed through the 2022 merger of II-VI and legacy Coherent, the company operates primarily through Networking, Materials, and Lasers segments, generating FY2026 revenue of $7.12B (up 22.5% YoY) with Q4 FY2026 revenue of $2.05B (up 33.8% YoY). Headquartered in Saxonburg, Pennsylvania, Coherent is the leading supplier of optical transceivers for AI datacenter interconnects, with NVIDIA investing $2B in the company.

Investment Thesis
Coherent at $278.70 with a $55B market cap is the premier pure-play on AI datacenter optical infrastructure, delivering FY2026 revenue of $7.12B (up 22.5% YoY) with Q4 accelerating to $2.05B (+33.8% YoY) and non-GAAP EPS of $1.74 beating consensus by $0.31 [1]. The company is benefiting from a structural tailwind as every new AI GPU cluster requires proportionally more optical interconnects — datacenter revenue grew 37% YoY in Q3 to $1.4B, and NVIDIA's $2B strategic investment validates Coherent's position as essential AI infrastructure [2][4]. Management is quadrupling capacity at Sherman, TX to meet demand, and the company enters FY2027 with 'exceptional customer demand, expanding production capacity, and multiple new growth platforms beginning to ramp' [1]. The balance sheet has deleveraged aggressively post-merger to 31.6% D/E, and forward P/E of 19.8x prices the stock reasonably for 30%+ revenue growth.
The bear case centers on execution risk and cyclicality. ROE of only 8.0% reflects the massive goodwill and intangibles ($12B+ equity base) from the II-VI merger, meaning the company must grow into its balance sheet — profit margins of 11.3% remain well below photonics peers and need significant expansion to justify the $55B market cap [1]. The Industrial segment ($444M Q3 revenue, down from $529M YoY) is in secular decline, and the company is increasingly dependent on a single customer vertical (AI hyperscalers) that could face a capex slowdown [2]. The 1.6T transceiver market is attracting aggressive Chinese competition from companies like InnoLight, potentially compressing ASPs and margins. Trailing P/E of 67.6x leaves minimal margin of safety if datacenter growth decelerates, and the stock sits 8% below its 50-day SMA of $303.73, suggesting near-term momentum has faded. CEO Anderson's cultural transformation is still early, and the company hasn't been tested through a downturn under his leadership [3].
Key Catalysts
  1. AI datacenter optical interconnect demand accelerating — Q4 FY2026 revenue grew 33.8% YoY to $2.05B with management citing 'exceptional customer demand' entering FY2027, as every new AI GPU cluster requires proportionally more optical transceivers [1][2]
  2. NVIDIA's $2B strategic investment validates Coherent as essential AI infrastructure and deepens the partnership for next-generation transceiver co-development, with capacity quadrupling at Sherman, TX to meet demand [3][4]
  3. Margin expansion opportunity as the company scales past II-VI merger integration costs — current 11.3% profit margin has significant room to improve toward photonics peer levels of 15-20% as datacenter revenue mix increases and manufacturing scale improves [1]
Key Risks
  1. Customer concentration in AI hyperscalers creates binary risk — a capex slowdown from Meta, Google, Microsoft, or Amazon would directly impact Coherent's datacenter revenue, which now represents the vast majority of growth and profitability [1][2]
  2. Chinese optical transceiver competitors (InnoLight, Eoptolink) are ramping 1.6T products at lower price points, threatening ASP compression in Coherent's highest-growth segment [4]
  3. ROE of 8.0% on a $12B+ equity base from the II-VI merger means the company must grow into its balance sheet — if revenue growth slows before margins expand, the stock's 67.6x trailing P/E becomes unsustainable [1]
