Company Profile
Financial Services ·Banks - Regional ·NASDAQ ·United States
Columbia Banking System, Inc. is the holding company for Columbia Bank, a Washington state-chartered full-service commercial bank with approximately $66 billion in total assets following transformative mergers with Umpqua Holdings (2023) and Pacific Premier Bancorp. Columbia operates locations throughout Washington, Oregon, Idaho, California, Nevada, and Arizona, providing commercial lending, consumer banking, and wealth management services. Headquartered in Tacoma, Washington.

Investment Thesis
Columbia Banking System delivered strong Q1 2026 results with $677M total revenue, $192M net income ($0.66 EPS, up 61% YoY), 10% ROE, and 3.96% net interest margin, reflecting the full benefit of transformative M&A that grew the bank to $66B in assets [1]. At approximately 10x forward earnings with a 4.98% dividend yield, COLB offers compelling income value for dividend investors, supported by 23 consecutive years of dividend payment, strong regulatory capitalization (CET1 11.5%), and new loan origination volume up 38% YoY to $1.2B [1]. The $200M share repurchase in Q1 2026 at an average price of $30.74 signals management confidence in intrinsic value [1].
The bear case centers on integration execution and credit quality risks. The rapid M&A-driven growth has created a $66B institution that must integrate multiple bank cultures and technology platforms, with efficiency ratio of 58% leaving room for improvement but also reflecting integration costs [1]. Non-performing assets of $264M (0.40% of total assets) and net charge-offs of 0.30% are manageable but bear monitoring as the commercial real estate portfolio seasons in a higher-rate environment. The 5-year revenue CAGR of ~43% is entirely M&A-driven, masking organic growth that is likely low-single-digit [5]. Dividend growth has been modest — only 4 consecutive years of increases at 3.9% 5-year CAGR, below inflation-adjusted targets — and the 57.2% payout ratio limits reinvestment flexibility compared to more capital-efficient peers [5]. ROE of 10% is respectable but below the 15% target, reflecting the goodwill-laden balance sheet from acquisitions.
Key Catalysts
  1. Continued post-merger integration driving operating leverage — efficiency ratio improvement from 58% toward peer levels of 50-55% would meaningfully expand net income and ROE [1]
  2. Net interest margin expansion if the Fed holds rates steady, benefiting Columbia's asset-sensitive balance sheet with $47.7B in loans repricing at current rates [1]
  3. New loan origination volume growth (up 38% YoY to $1.2B in Q1 2026) driving organic earning asset expansion beyond the M&A-driven growth trajectory [1]
Key Risks
  1. Commercial real estate concentration risk as the combined loan portfolio seasons in a higher-rate environment, with non-performing assets of $264M and net charge-offs at 0.30% potentially rising [1]
  2. Integration execution risk from rapid M&A-driven growth — combining multiple bank cultures, technology platforms, and customer relationships across six western states creates operational complexity [1]
  3. Organic revenue growth likely low-single-digit beneath the M&A-inflated 40% headline number, raising questions about whether the franchise can generate sustainable growth once the acquisition pipeline slows [5]
