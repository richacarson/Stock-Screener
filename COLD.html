Company Profile
Real Estate ·REIT - Industrial ·NYSE ·United States
Americold is the largest publicly traded temperature-controlled warehouse REIT globally, operating 224 facilities across North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, and South America totaling approximately 1.4 billion refrigerated cubic feet. The company reported Q1 2026 revenue of $629.9M (flat YoY) and Q2 2026 revenue of $662.9M, with FY2026 Core EBITDA guidance of $570M-$620M. Founded in 1903 and headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia, the company serves food producers, processors, distributors, and retailers with cold storage and transportation solutions.
12,690 Employees ·www.americold.com

Investment Thesis
Americold at $13.82 with a $3.96B market cap offers the highest dividend yield in the screen at 6.66% backed by the essential nature of temperature-controlled food logistics — cold storage infrastructure cannot be replicated by software, and food supply chains require physical refrigerated facilities regardless of economic cycle [1][4]. The company's 224 global facilities with 1.4B cubic feet of capacity represent a significant physical moat, Q2 2026 revenue of $662.9M beat estimates by 6.6%, and FY2026 Core EBITDA guidance of $570M-$620M implies improving operational efficiency under new CEO Robert Chambers [2][5]. AFFO of $81.9M in Q1 2026 confirms the business generates real cash flow despite GAAP losses driven by REIT depreciation.
The bear case is significant: revenue has declined from $2.91B (2022) to $2.60B (2025), AFFO fell 14.7% YoY in Q1 2026 to $0.29/share, and forward EPS is -$0.21 indicating continued GAAP losses [1][3]. D/E of 188.6% with $4.6B in debt against just $40M cash creates balance sheet vulnerability if interest rates remain elevated. Lineage Logistics, backed by private equity, is investing aggressively in automation and could pressure occupancy rates and pricing. The dividend has barely grown — $0.23/quarter since Q1 2025 after years at $0.22 — and the 6.66% yield reflects price deterioration (stock down from $28 in 2022) rather than income growth. Project Orion ERP migration poses execution risk and management distraction [3][5].

AI Resilience LOW RISK
8
AI Risk Protection (8/9): Temperature-controlled warehousing is a physical-world infrastructure business that AI structurally cannot displace — refrigerated facilities require real buildings, refrigeration equipment, and physical handling of perishable goods [4]. AI-driven demand for data centers and computing infrastructure has no direct substitution effect on cold storage; if anything, supply chain digitization increases demand for sophisticated cold chain logistics. Americold's 1.4 billion cubic feet of refrigerated capacity and 224 global facilities represent a physical moat that no software can replicate. The primary competitive risk comes from Lineage Logistics and other operators investing in superior warehouse automation, but this is an efficiency competition, not an AI substitution threat [3]. AI Opportunity (0/1): While Americold has deployed AI internally for energy management and identified 400+ AI use cases, it generates no quantifiable revenue from AI products or services — AI is used as an operational efficiency tool, not a revenue stream [3].
