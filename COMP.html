Company Profile
Real Estate ·Real Estate Services ·NYSE ·United States
Compass, Inc. operates an end-to-end technology platform for residential real estate brokerage in the United States. Following its January 2026 acquisition of Anywhere Real Estate, Compass became the nation's largest residential brokerage, operating brands including Compass, Century 21, Coldwell Banker, and Sotheby's International Realty across a combined agent network exceeding 200,000. Q2 2026 revenue reached a record $4.3 billion, more than double the prior-year period, as the company actioned its full $300 million first-year cost synergy target ahead of schedule.

Investment Thesis
Compass delivered a record Q2 2026 with revenue of $4.3 billion, Adjusted EBITDA nearly tripling to $363 million, and net income of $92 million alongside $180 million of free cash flow, as the company actioned its full $300 million first-year Anywhere cost-synergy target ahead of schedule [1][2]. The combined platform is now the undisputed #1 U.S. residential brokerage with 200,000+ agents across Compass, Century 21, Coldwell Banker, and Sotheby's International Realty, and pro forma organic revenue growth of 14.3% shows the underlying business gaining share even excluding the M&A step-up [1]. Title and escrow attach continues to scale (+7.6% pro forma), pointing toward a higher-margin services mix as integration matures.
The bear case is substantial. Despite the revenue scale-up, profitability remains razor-thin — ROE of 3.63% and profit margin of 0.64% reflect a business generating almost no economic profit on $7B+ of TTM revenue, and Forward P/E of 157x prices in years of flawless synergy execution that has not yet been proven at run-rate [7]. The New York Attorney General opened an active antitrust investigation in June 2026 into whether the Anywhere consolidation harmed competition in luxury markets, with investigators contacting rival NYC brokerages — a probe that could force divestitures or operational restrictions and has already driven double-digit single-day stock declines [5]. Structural AI- and settlement-driven commission compression continues industry-wide, and CEO Reffkin's continued net insider selling ($202M over the trailing 12 months, zero purchases) offers no offsetting signal of conviction at current prices [6].
Key Catalysts
  1. Full run-rate realization of Anywhere merger synergies beyond the $300M first-year target, pushing Adjusted EBITDA margins higher and narrowing the gap between reported and adjusted profitability
  2. Title and escrow cross-sell continuing to scale (already +7.6% pro forma in Q2 2026) as Compass drives higher-margin integrated services attach across the combined 200,000+ agent network
  3. Favorable resolution or narrowing of the New York Attorney General antitrust inquiry, removing a regulatory overhang that has weighed on the stock since June 2026
Key Risks
  1. New York Attorney General antitrust investigation into the Anywhere acquisition's impact on luxury-market competition could result in forced divestitures, consent decrees, or prolonged legal costs that undermine the merger thesis
  2. Structural margin compression from NAR commission-settlement dynamics and AI-driven disintermediation (Zillow, Redfin) keeps net margins near zero even as reported revenue scales, with Forward P/E of 157x leaving no room for execution missteps
  3. Continued net insider selling by CEO Reffkin (six sales, zero purchases, ~$202M over 12 months) removes a potential conviction signal just as the company asks the market to underwrite a multi-year integration and synergy story
