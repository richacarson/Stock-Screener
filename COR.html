Company Profile
Healthcare ·Medical Distribution ·NYSE ·United States
Cencora, Inc. (formerly AmerisourceBergen) is one of the 'Big Three' U.S. pharmaceutical distributors, sourcing and distributing branded, generic, and specialty pharmaceuticals to acute care hospitals, retail pharmacies, mail order pharmacies, and specialty providers. The company also provides global commercialization services, specialty logistics, and animal health distribution through its International Healthcare Solutions segment. Cencora reported Q3 FY2026 revenue of $84.8B (+5.1% YoY) with adjusted EPS of $4.48 (+12%) and raised FY2026 EPS guidance to $17.75-$17.95, driven by specialty distribution and GLP-1 product sales exceeding $2.3B in the quarter. Founded in 1871 and headquartered in Conshohocken, Pennsylvania.
47,000 Employees ·www.cencora.com

Investment Thesis
Cencora at 15.84x forward earnings with a PEG of 0.66 offers rare growth-at-a-reasonable-price in healthcare distribution — the company raised FY2026 adjusted EPS guidance to $17.75-$17.95 with 12% EPS growth and 13-14% adjusted operating income growth, driven by specialty distribution and GLP-1 product volumes exceeding $2.3B quarterly [1]. The Big Three distribution oligopoly creates a structural moat with massive scale barriers to entry, regulatory requirements (DEA licensing, cold chain compliance), and deeply integrated customer relationships. Revenue of $84.8B quarterly positions Cencora to capture secular growth in pharmaceutical spending (specialty drugs, biosimilars, GLP-1 class expansion). The 96.87% ROE, while amplified by leverage, demonstrates efficient capital deployment, and the 0.66 PEG ratio suggests the market undervalues the earnings growth trajectory.
The bear case centers on extreme leverage, razor-thin margins, and structural business model limitations. D/E of 446.52% is far above the 300% floor, reflecting a capital structure that leaves minimal room for error — any disruption to pharmaceutical volume or pricing could rapidly impair debt service capacity [5]. Profit margin of just 0.79% means Cencora earns less than one cent on every dollar of revenue, and even small pricing pressure from manufacturer consolidation or generic competition compresses earnings disproportionately. The Inspire Impact Score of -75 (MISALIGNED) due to Abortifacients and LGBT Activism attributions will exclude COR from many faith-based portfolios. The opioid distribution history creates ongoing legal and reputational overhang. Revenue growth of 5.1% YoY is pedestrian for a $60B market cap company, and the Big Three distribution model faces long-term risk from manufacturer direct-to-patient models, specialty pharmacy vertical integration, and Amazon Pharmacy's entry into drug distribution [1][5].

AI Risk Protection (7/9): Pharmaceutical distribution is fundamentally a physical-world logistics operation — warehousing, cold chain management, regulatory compliance (DEA Schedule II handling), and last-mile delivery to hospitals and pharmacies require extensive physical infrastructure that AI cannot displace [1]. The Big Three distribution oligopoly (McKesson, Cencora, Cardinal Health) is protected by massive scale requirements, regulatory barriers, and deeply integrated customer relationships with switching costs. However, AI could optimize supply chain logistics and potentially disintermediate some value-added services (analytics, market access consulting) that Cencora provides to pharmaceutical manufacturers. AI Opportunity (0/1): Cencora has deployed an AI voice agent for internal insurance verification, but this is a cost-reduction tool, not a revenue-generating AI product; no meaningful AI-derived revenue has been reported [3].
