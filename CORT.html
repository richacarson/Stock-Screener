Company Profile
Healthcare ·Specialty Pharmaceuticals ·NASDAQ ·United States
Corcept Therapeutics Incorporated is a commercial-stage pharmaceutical company focused on discovering and developing drugs that modulate the effects of the stress hormone cortisol. The company markets Korlym (mifepristone), the only FDA-approved treatment for Cushing's syndrome, and Lifyorli (relacorilant), approved March 2026 for platinum-resistant ovarian cancer. Lifyorli generated $47.6M in its first full quarter (Q2 2026) with 1,300+ patients on treatment. Led by co-founder CEO Joseph Belanoff, M.D. (27-year tenure), the company raised 2026 revenue guidance to $1.1-1.2B and holds $545M in cash with zero debt. Founded in 1998, headquartered in Menlo Park, California. Market cap approximately $13B.
