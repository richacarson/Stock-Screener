Company Profile
Industrials ·Railroads ·NYSE ·Canada
Canadian Pacific Kansas City (CPKC) is the first and only transnational Class I railroad linking Canada, the United States, and Mexico via a single-line network of approximately 20,000 route miles. Formed by the April 2023 combination of Canadian Pacific and Kansas City Southern ($31B), CPKC is led by President and CEO Keith Creel (13+ year tenure). Q2 2026 revenues of C$4.2B (+13% YoY) with core adjusted EPS of C$1.27 (+13% YoY), rebounding strongly from Q1 weakness. Grain revenue surged 24% and free cash flow doubled to C$1B. Market cap ~$80B.

Investment Thesis
Canadian Pacific Kansas City is the only tri-national Class I railroad in North America, an unreplicable franchise created by the $31B combination of CP and Kansas City Southern in April 2023 [3]. Q2 2026 demonstrated strong recovery with C$4.2B revenue (+13% YoY), grain revenue surging 24%, and free cash flow doubling to C$1B — rebounding decisively from Q1's disappointing -2% revenue miss [2]. Core adjusted EPS of C$1.27 (+13% YoY) with guidance for accelerated volume and earnings growth in H2 2026. At $89 with Forward PE of 22.2x and D/E of 50%, CPKC is priced as a premium compounder with improving fundamentals and durable cash flows from the only single-line Canada-US-Mexico rail network [6]. Keith Creel's 13-year tenure and proven execution (Q4 2025 record 55.9% OR, $250M automotive synergies delivered 3 years early) validate leadership quality [5].
The bear case centers on growth sustainability, operational risks, and premium valuation. Despite Q2's strong rebound, a 53% spike in fuel costs pressured the operating ratio to 64.6% (up 90 bps YoY), showing cost vulnerability in an asset-heavy business [2]. The November 2025 Kamloops derailment (70,000 litres of jet fuel into Kamloops Lake on First Nations territory) creates environmental, reputational, and litigation risk that undermines the sustainability narrative [4]. The 2024 nationwide labor dispute, which required government intervention to resolve, signals underlying workforce tension despite subsequent contract extensions through 2027. CPKC's tri-national franchise is directly exposed to US-Mexico trade policy and tariff volatility — any disruption to nearshoring flows or cross-border volumes would compress the growth premium at the current 22.2x forward PE. ROE of 8.64% is modest for a premium-valued franchise, constrained by the elevated equity base from the KCS acquisition, and the 0.74% dividend yield offers minimal income support during drawdowns [6].
Key Catalysts
  1. Accelerated H2 2026 volume and earnings growth guided by management, building on Q2's 13% revenue growth and doubled free cash flow, driven by continued integration synergies and nearshoring-driven Mexico-US-Canada freight volumes [2]
  2. Nearshoring thesis uniquely captured by CPKC's single-line tri-national network, with $250M automotive synergies already delivered three years ahead of schedule and additional cross-border volume opportunities as manufacturing shifts toward Mexico [3]
  3. Operating ratio improvement trajectory toward industry-best levels (Q4 2025 record 55.9%) as precision scheduled railroading disciplines scale across the combined network, driving margin expansion and EPS growth [1]
Key Risks
  1. Fuel cost volatility — Q2 2026 saw a 53% spike in fuel costs that pressured operating ratio to 64.6%, demonstrating CPKC's exposure to energy input costs in an asset-heavy, capital-intensive business model [2]
  2. Environmental and reputational risk from the November 2025 Kamloops derailment (70,000 litres of jet fuel on First Nations territory) with potential litigation costs and regulatory scrutiny that could constrain operations [4]
  3. US-Mexico trade and tariff volatility directly exposing the tri-national franchise to cross-border volume disruption — any nearshoring reversal or tariff escalation would compress volumes and the growth premium at 22.2x forward PE [6]

AI Risk Protection (8/9): Railroads are structurally immune to AI disruption — AI cannot replicate 20,000 route miles of track, locomotives, terminals, and cross-border operating rights regulated by the STB, Transport Canada, and Mexico's SCT [3]. Freight rail's energy and cost advantage over trucking (4x more fuel-efficient) strengthens as supply chains prioritize decarbonization, and CPKC's tri-national single-line network is a physically unreplicable asset. AI enhances railroad operations through predictive maintenance and network optimization rather than threatening them.
