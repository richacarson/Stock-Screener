Company Profile
Industrials ·Railroads ·NYSE ·Canada
Canadian Pacific Kansas City (CPKC) is the first and only transnational Class I railroad linking Canada, the United States, and Mexico via a single-line network of approximately 20,000 route miles. Formed by the April 2023 combination of Canadian Pacific and Kansas City Southern ($31B), CPKC is led by President and CEO Keith Creel (13+ year tenure). Market cap $80.1B.

Investment Thesis
Canadian Pacific Kansas City is the only tri-national Class I railroad in North America, an unreplicable franchise created by the $31B combination of CP and Kansas City Southern in April 2023 [3]. FY2025 delivered C$15.1B revenue (+4% YoY) with core adjusted operating ratio improving 140 bps to an industry-best 59.9%, and Q4 2025 achieved a record 55.9% OR [1]. 2026 guidance targets mid-single-digit volume growth and low-double-digit core adjusted EPS growth to C$4.61, supported by a 17.5% quarterly dividend increase and a new 5% share repurchase program [1]. At $90.12 with a Forward PE of 24.3x and PEG of 1.42, CPKC is priced as a premium compounder with D/E of just 51%, profit margin of 27.2%, and durable cash flows from the only single-line Canada-US-Mexico rail network [6]. Keith Creel's 13-year tenure and two Railroad Innovator Awards validate leadership quality [5].
The bear case centers on growth deceleration and operational risks. Q1 2026 missed expectations with revenue of C$3.7B (down 2% YoY) and core adjusted EPS of C$1.04 vs C$1.08 consensus, with operating ratio deteriorating 50 bps to 63.0% from fuel and FX headwinds [2]. Technology changeover-related service disruptions have caused elevated delays and congestion across parts of the network. The November 2025 Kamloops derailment that leaked 70,000 litres of jet fuel into Kamloops Lake on First Nations territory creates environmental, reputational, and litigation risk [4]. CPKC's tri-national franchise is directly exposed to US-Mexico trade policy and tariff volatility — any disruption to nearshoring flows or cross-border volumes would compress the growth premium. ROE of 8.43% is modest for a premium-valued franchise, and the 0.77% dividend yield offers minimal income support during drawdowns.
Key Catalysts
  1. 2026 low-double-digit core adjusted EPS growth to C$4.61 target, driven by continued operating ratio improvement from KCS integration synergies and mid-single-digit volume recovery [1]
  2. Nearshoring-driven Mexico-US-Canada freight volumes uniquely routed on the single-line CPKC network, with $250M automotive synergies already delivered three years ahead of schedule [3]
  3. New 5% share repurchase program and 17.5% quarterly dividend increase to C$0.268/share demonstrating capital return discipline alongside organic growth [1]
Key Risks
  1. Q1 2026 miss (C$1.04 EPS vs C$1.08 consensus, 63.0% OR up 50bps) and technology-related service disruptions signal integration execution risk and growth deceleration from FY2025 levels [2]
  2. November 2025 Kamloops derailment with 70,000 litre jet fuel leak on First Nations territory creates environmental, reputational, and litigation risk that undermines the sustainability narrative [4]
  3. US-Mexico trade and tariff volatility directly exposing the tri-national franchise to cross-border volume disruption — any nearshoring reversal would compress the growth premium at the current 24.3x forward PE [6]

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
8
AI Risk Protection (8/9): Railroads are structurally immune to AI disruption — AI cannot replicate 20,000 route miles of track, locomotives, terminals, and cross-border operating rights regulated by the STB, Transport Canada, and Mexico's SCT [3]. Freight rail's energy and cost advantage over trucking strengthens as supply chains prioritize decarbonization, and CPKC's tri-national single-line network is a physically unreplicable asset. AI actually enhances railroad operations through predictive maintenance and network optimization. AI Opportunity (0/1): CPKC deploys AI internally for bearing failure prediction (90% reduction), waterbody monitoring (1.2M waterbodies scanned), and network optimization [3], but does not generate direct revenue from AI products or services sold to external customers.
