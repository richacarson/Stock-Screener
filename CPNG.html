Investment Thesis
Coupang dominates South Korean e-commerce with 24.7M+ active customers and an unmatched end-to-end logistics network enabling Rocket Delivery to 99% of the country [1]. Q2 2026 revenue of $8.9B grew 10% in constant currency despite the lingering data breach impact, and Q3 2026 guidance of 8-9% FX-neutral growth suggests stabilization [2]. The Product Commerce segment generates $7.4B quarterly with improving unit economics, and Taiwan expansion continues to deliver triple-digit growth as Coupang replicates its logistics playbook in a new market. The 5-year revenue CAGR of ~20% demonstrates sustained platform growth, and once the $1.1B breach costs are absorbed, operating margins should recover toward the 3-5% range demonstrated pre-breach. The Inspire Impact Score of 36 (ALIGNED) is a positive for faith-based investors.
The bear case is significant and multi-dimensional. The November 2025 data breach of 33.7M users has caused sustained financial damage — $1.1B in breach-related costs, Q1 2026 net loss of $266M (swinging from $107M profit), and Forward P/E ballooning to 71.42x as near-term earnings expectations collapse [1][3]. Revenue growth has decelerated sharply from 18-20% in 2025 to 4% reported in Q2 2026, with currency headwinds (weak Korean won) compressing US-dollar reported results. Profit margin of just 1.16% and ROE of 8.23% are far below targets. Developing Offerings will lose $950M-$1B in 2026, creating sustained cash drain [2]. The ESG profile is deeply troubled: multiple warehouse worker deaths, employment blacklists affecting 16,450+ workers, union suppression, and founder-CEO governance failures create regulatory and reputational risks that could intensify as Korean regulators scrutinize platform labor practices [5]. D/E of 104% limits financial flexibility for a company with razor-thin margins.
