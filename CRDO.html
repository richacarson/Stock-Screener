Company Profile
Technology ·Semiconductors ·NASDAQ ·Cayman Islands
Credo Technology designs high-speed connectivity for AI and cloud data centers: active electrical cables (AECs), optical PAM4 DSPs, line-card PHYs, SerDes chiplets and SerDes IP licensing, built on proprietary SerDes and DSP technology. FY2026 (ended May 2, 2026) revenue tripled to $1.34B from $437M with GAAP net income of $472M. Q1 FY2027 revenue was $479M (+115% YoY) and Q2 guidance is $525-535M, with optical revenue targeted above $600M for FY2027. Founded in 2008 and headquartered in San Jose with a Cayman Islands domicile, Credo has a market cap of about $28B after the stock fell to $150.99, well below its 52-week high of $308.67.

Investment Thesis
Credo is a direct play on AI data-center connectivity. FY2026 revenue tripled to $1.34B with GAAP net income of $472M, Q1 FY2027 revenue grew 114.7% to $479M with non-GAAP EPS of $1.20 versus $0.52, and Q2 guidance of $525-535M implies continued sequential growth [1][3]. Optical revenue is targeted above $600M for FY2027, adding a second growth engine beyond copper AECs [1]. After a roughly 50% decline from the $308.67 high, the stock trades at 15.5x forward earnings with an FMP forward PEG of 0.57, on a balance sheet with almost no debt (D/E 0.96%) and a 33.8% net margin [2][8].
The bear case is concentration and cyclicality. About 90% of revenue reportedly comes from the top 10 customers, so one hyperscaler pause or design loss would hit hard, and the stock's 5-year beta of about 3.2 amplifies any slowdown in AI capex [5][6][8]. GAAP gross margin fell sequentially in Q1 FY2027 and guidance of 62.9-64.9% shows continued pressure, inventory has grown to about $208M, and 200G-per-lane products depend on constrained 3nm capacity [1][5][6]. Insiders keep selling (123 disposals, no purchases over 90 days), and larger rivals such as Broadcom and Marvell have far more R&D resources [6].
Key Catalysts
  1. Q2 FY2027 results against $525-535M revenue guidance and gross margin of 62.9-64.9% GAAP / 67-69% non-GAAP [1]
  2. Optical portfolio ramp toward the more-than-$600M FY2027 target, broadening Credo beyond copper AECs [1]
  3. Ramp of 200G-per-lane 1.6T solutions as hyperscalers move to next-generation AI fabrics, with management comfortable on capacity through 2027 [5]
Key Risks
  1. Customer concentration, with about 90% of revenue reportedly from the top 10 customers and two above 10% each, exposing Credo to any hyperscaler capex pause or design-out [6]
  2. Gross-margin compression (GAAP gross margin guided to 62.9-64.9%) and inventory of about $208M, up from $26M two years earlier [1][6]
  3. High volatility (beta about 3.2) and persistent insider selling, after a fall of about 50% from the $308.67 52-week high [6][8]
