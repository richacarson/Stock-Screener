Company Profile

Smart Powerr Corp. (formerly China Recycling Energy Corporation) is a Nevada-incorporated holding company transitioning from legacy waste gas/waste heat/biomass power generation (Build-Operate-Transfer model for Chinese industrial clients) into an energy storage integrated solutions provider. In 2026 the company partnered with Shidai Huazhi (Jiangsu) Energy Technology, led by former CATL general manager Huang Shilin, to co-develop ultra-fast EV charging stations, intelligent AC-DC microgrids, virtual power plant aggregation, and V2G grid-balancing technology using digital-twin 'energy brain' management software [2]. The company executed a 1-for-10 reverse stock split effective June 16, 2026 to regain Nasdaq minimum bid-price compliance, but shares have since fallen back below $1, and the stock is down roughly 98.5% over the past 52 weeks [1][3]. Headquartered in Xi'an, China and founded in 1980.

Investment Thesis
Smart Powerr is attempting a genuine strategic pivot from its legacy waste-to-energy recycling business into China's fast-growing energy storage, EV charging, and microgrid infrastructure market, anchored by a 2026 partnership with Shidai Huazhi, led by former CATL general manager Huang Shilin [2]. The collaboration targets ultra-fast charging stations with integrated storage, AC-DC hybrid intelligent microgrids designed for over 30% adjustable resource response by 2026, virtual power plant aggregation, and V2G grid-balancing technology, with each charging station projected to save over 1 million yuan annually and cut 1,000+ tons of carbon emissions — positioning the company, at least directionally, within China's 'dual carbon' policy tailwind [2]. Q1 2026 revenue of $108,353 was up sharply from $20,625 a year earlier off a tiny base, and gross margin of 50% suggests the underlying maintenance-contract business is not structurally unprofitable at the unit level [1].
The bear case is overwhelming. Smart Powerr's average daily dollar volume of roughly $3,666 is a small fraction of this framework's $1M liquidity floor, making the stock effectively untradeable at any institutional scale and triggering an automatic Infrastructure score of 0 [1][4]. The company already underwent a 1-for-10 reverse stock split in June 2026 specifically to cure a Nasdaq minimum bid-price deficiency, and the stock has since fallen back under $1 and is down approximately 98.5% over the past 52 weeks — a strong signal of continued delisting risk [1][3]. Revenue remains negligible in absolute terms (TTM ~$350,237) relative to the scale of ambition in the Shidai Huazhi partnership, and the market's own reaction to that partnership announcement (the stock fell 15.35% on the news) suggests investors are skeptical it will translate into near-term financial results [2]. Combined with a history of shifting business models (waste recycling to energy storage to EV charging) without sustained capital or revenue traction, this remains a highly speculative, distressed micro-cap unsuitable for IOWN's liquidity and durability standards.
