Smart Powerr Corp. (formerly China Recycling Energy Corporation, renamed in 2022) is a Nevada-incorporated holding company that develops waste energy recycling projects for energy-intensive industrial clients in China using a Build-Operate-Transfer model, including waste gas, waste heat, and biomass power generation. The company is headquartered in Xi'an, China and was founded in 1980.
