Company Profile

Creative Realities, Inc. provides end-to-end digital signage solutions including proprietary CMS software, hardware deployment, content management, and digital media advertising. The company serves retailers, QSR chains, sports venues, and entertainment companies with contextually relevant digital experiences. Following the 2025 acquisition of Cineplex Digital Media (CDM), the company is transitioning to a software-first platform powered by data, analytics, and AI.

Investment Thesis
Creative Realities is executing an aggressive acquisition-driven transformation from niche digital signage installer to integrated digital media platform, with the 2025 CDM acquisition driving Q1 2026 revenue up 68% YoY to $16.35M and management targeting $100M+ full-year 2026 revenue with high-teens adjusted EBITDA margin [1]. The software-first SaaS transition under new CXO Jackie Walker, combined with digital media advertising capabilities from CDM, creates a path toward higher-margin recurring revenue and potentially meaningful scale in the growing retail media network space.
The bull case requires overlooking severe financial stress: $70.1M total debt against $13.1M equity (537% D/E), going concern doubt in the Q1 2026 10-Q, negative working capital of -$9.5M, and -$7.5M TTM free cash flow [1]. Average daily dollar volume of just $347K makes this essentially uninvestable for any institutional strategy and creates significant liquidity risk for individual holders. The company must achieve rapid CDM integration synergies, expand SaaS margins, and refinance its debt structure to survive — any execution miss on the $100M revenue target or margin expansion could force dilutive capital raises or worse. At $4.09 per share with negative earnings, the stock is a speculative turnaround bet rather than an investment.
