Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·Switzerland
CRISPR Therapeutics AG is a gene-editing biotechnology company developing CRISPR/Cas9-based medicines for serious diseases. Its lead product, CASGEVY (developed with partner Vertex Pharmaceuticals), is the world's first approved CRISPR-based therapy, treating sickle cell disease and transfusion-dependent beta thalassemia. The pipeline spans oncology (allogeneic CAR-T), cardiovascular disease (in-vivo gene editing candidates CTX310 and CTX320), autoimmune conditions, and regenerative medicine. Founded in 2013, headquartered in Zug, Switzerland, with primary R&D operations in Boston, Massachusetts.

Investment Thesis
CRISPR Therapeutics is the category creator in CRISPR-based medicine, with CASGEVY — the world's first approved CRISPR therapy — showing accelerating real-world uptake: more than 500 patients have initiated treatment globally and patient initiations and first cell collections nearly tripled in 2025 versus 2024, with continued momentum into 2026 [1]. The company holds $2.44B in cash and marketable securities as of Q1 2026 (up from $1.98B at year-end, boosted by a $585.4M convertible notes raise), giving it a multi-year runway to fund a deep, diversified pipeline spanning cardiovascular disease (CTX310, CTX320), oncology, and autoimmune indications without near-term financing pressure [1][2]. AI Resilience is a genuine strength (8/10, LOW RISK) — CASGEVY's regulatory and manufacturing moat is structurally immune to AI substitution, and the mission has been directly validated by regulatory approval rather than aspiration alone [1][3].
The bear case is that CRISPR Therapeutics' financial-statement mechanics remain those of a pre-commercial-scale biotech: TTM revenue collapsed 89.1% and the 5-year revenue CAGR is -71.8%, reflecting the lumpy, milestone-driven nature of collaboration-revenue accounting (a large one-time Vertex payment in 2021 makes recent periods look like a steep decline even as CASGEVY's actual patient uptake accelerates) [1][6]. ROE of -34.8% and a deeply negative net margin mean Infrastructure — the mechanically calculated financial dimension of this framework — scores just 1/10, and profitability at scale remains multiple years away [6]. CASGEVY's addressable patient population (sickle cell disease and beta thalassemia) is inherently limited and each treatment requires complex, costly cell-collection and manufacturing logistics that could cap the pace of commercial ramp regardless of demand. No positive employee-retention or worthy-rivals evidence was identified, leaving open questions about organizational resilience as the company scales from a single-product story to a multi-program commercial-stage biotech.
Key Catalysts
  1. Continued CASGEVY patient-initiation acceleration — initiations and first cell collections nearly tripled in 2025 versus 2024 with momentum continuing into 2026, directly driving the underlying commercial ramp that lumpy GAAP revenue accounting currently obscures [1]
  2. CTX310/CTX320 in-vivo cardiovascular gene-editing data — durable LDL/triglyceride or Lp(a) reduction readouts would validate the in-vivo platform and unlock a dramatically larger addressable population (40M+ Americans) than CASGEVY's hemoglobinopathy indications [2]
  3. Continued deployment of the $2.44B cash position into pipeline expansion (oncology, autoimmune) without near-term dilutive financing, following the $585.4M convertible notes raise completed in Q1 2026 [1]
