Investment Thesis
CrowdStrike is executing a clean beat-and-raise cycle two years after its 2024 outage: fiscal Q2 2027 revenue rose 26% to $1.47B, ARR reached $5.84B, and record net new ARR of ~$333M grew 51% and beat the high end of guidance by more than $45M [1]. Management raised FY2027 net new ARR guidance to $1.350-1.359B (+34%) and revenue to $5.99-6.01B, expects at least a 30% free cash flow margin, and exited the quarter with a record pipeline [1]. The balance sheet holds $5.01B of cash against $820M of debt, and AI agents expand the attack surface CrowdStrike is built to defend, with Charlotte AI AgentWorks, Falcon IQ and the Agentic Identity Provider positioning the company for the category Kurtz expects to outgrow endpoint [2][4][5].
The bear case is valuation and quality of earnings. At ~155x forward earnings and a 6.7 PEG, with a GAAP profit margin under 1% and ROE of 1.5%, the shares discount years of flawless execution, and any ARR deceleration could compress the multiple sharply [2]. Insiders have made roughly 2,100 sales and no open-market purchases in 2026 [7]. Governance overhangs include a reported DOJ/SEC inquiry into a $32M Carahsoft deal and possible 'pre-booking', even though a shareholder fraud suit over the 2024 outage was dismissed in January 2026 [8]. The Inspire Impact Score of -49 (MISALIGNED) may limit allocation for faith-based mandates.
