Company Profile

Canadian Solar is a vertically integrated manufacturer of solar photovoltaic modules, inverters, and energy storage solutions, operating through CSI Solar (module and storage manufacturing) and Recurrent Energy (global project development). New CEO Colin Parkin was appointed May 14, 2026, with founder Shawn Qu transitioning to Executive Chairman and CTO.
