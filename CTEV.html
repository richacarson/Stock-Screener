Company Profile
Healthcare ·Health Information Services ·NYSE ·United States
Claritev Corporation (formerly MultiPlan Corporation) provides data analytics and technology-enabled cost management, payment, and revenue integrity solutions to the U.S. healthcare industry. The company offers claims intelligence, network solutions, payment and revenue integrity services, and analytics products including reference-based pricing, Vistara, CompleteVue, PlanOptix, and BenInsights. Rebranded from MultiPlan in February 2025 as part of a strategic transformation. Serves national and regional insurers, Blue Cross Blue Shield plans, TPAs, and government payers. Q2 2026 revenue reached $257.5M (+6.6% YoY) with full-year 2026 guidance raised to $985M-$1B [1].

Investment Thesis
Claritev at 4.2x forward earnings offers compelling turnaround optionality if the digital transformation executes — Q2 2026 revenue of $257.5M (+6.6% YoY) and raised full-year guidance of $985M-$1B demonstrate improving commercial traction, while bookings of $70M through H1 2026 (targeting $100M, +50% YoY) signal accelerating demand for AI-enhanced analytics products [1]. EBITDA margins of 61-62% on guided revenue provide substantial cash flow generation potential, and forward EPS of $7.33 implies a dramatic earnings inflection from the current trailing loss of -$17.00 per share. The stock has rallied from ~$12 lows to $30.81, reflecting growing confidence in the transformation under CEO Travis Dalton [3].
The bear case is dominated by balance sheet risk — $4.69B in total debt against negative equity creates a capital structure that could constrain growth investment and amplify any revenue shortfall [1]. Annual interest expense exceeding $400M consumes over 40% of revenue and produces chronic GAAP net losses despite strong operating cash flow of $133M. Revenue has declined from $1.08B (2022) to $965M (2025), and the current 6.6% growth rate must accelerate meaningfully to service the debt load. The healthcare payment integrity market is increasingly competitive with well-capitalized competitors (Optum, Cotiviti) investing heavily in AI-powered solutions [3].
