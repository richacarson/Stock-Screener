Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Infrastructure ·NASDAQ ·United States
Cantaloupe, Inc. was a digital payments and software services company providing technology solutions for the self-service commerce market, including micro-payment processing, self-checkout kiosks, mobile ordering, connected POS systems, and enterprise cloud software. The company processed over 1 billion transactions annually across more than 1.3 million active locations. Cantaloupe completed its merger with 365 Retail Markets (a Providence Equity portfolio company) at $11.20 per share in May 2026 and has been delisted from Nasdaq. The stock is no longer publicly traded.

Investment Thesis
POSITION CLOSED — MERGER COMPLETED. Cantaloupe completed its merger with 365 Retail Markets (backed by Providence Equity Partners) at $11.20 per share in cash in May 2026, and the stock has been delisted from Nasdaq [3]. Shareholders received $11.20 per share, representing a 34% premium to the unaffected price. The combined entity creates a self-service commerce leader with Cantaloupe's 1.3M+ active payment devices and 365 Retail Markets' micro-market and food service technology platform. The business fundamentals were healthy at the time of acquisition — Q1 FY2026 revenue of $78.7M grew 4.3% YoY with transaction fees up 9.2% and subscription fees up 7.2% [1].
The $11.20 per share deal price valued Cantaloupe at approximately $848M, or roughly 2.7x trailing revenue — a modest premium that reflected the company's growth deceleration (revenue growth slowed from 6.8% to 4.3% YoY), the extreme Forward PEG of 8.69 signaling limited near-term earnings growth, and governance concerns from the 2023 SEC revenue inflation fine [1][5]. Equipment sales declined 22.3% in Q1 FY2026 as prior-year demand spikes normalized. For IOWN investors who held CTLP, the position has been fully liquidated at $11.20/share and capital should be redeployed into active IOWN holdings.
