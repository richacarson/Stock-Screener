Company Profile
Real Estate ·REIT - Healthcare Facilities ·NYSE ·United States
CareTrust REIT is a self-administered, publicly-traded real estate investment trust focused on the ownership, acquisition, financing, and leasing of skilled nursing, seniors housing, and other healthcare-related properties. The company generates revenue through triple-net lease arrangements with healthcare operators. Under CEO David Sedgwick (since January 2022), CareTrust has aggressively scaled its portfolio with $1.5B in year-to-date 2026 investments at an 8.7% blended yield. Q2 2026 revenues surged 43% YoY to $161.3M and normalized FFO grew 44% to $119.7M ($0.51/share). Full-year 2026 normalized FFO guidance of $2.03-2.06/share. The company recently increased its quarterly dividend 16% to $0.39/share.
80 Employees ·www.caretrustreit.com

Investment Thesis
CareTrust REIT is an aggressively growing healthcare REIT with exceptional momentum — Q2 2026 revenue up 43% YoY, normalized FFO up 44% to $0.51/share, and $1.5B deployed YTD at an 8.7% blended yield that exceeds the company's cost of capital [1]. The demographic tailwind of aging baby boomers provides a durable demand runway for skilled nursing and seniors housing facilities. Infrastructure scores 8/10 driven by exceptionally low leverage (22% D/E), industry-leading 64% profit margin from the triple-net lease model, and strong growth metrics [3]. AI Resilience scores 9/10 — physical healthcare real estate is structurally immune to AI disruption [1]. The 3.86% dividend yield with 8% five-year growth CAGR and a recent 16% increase to $0.39/quarter provides attractive and growing income. The Inspire Impact Score of 37 (ALIGNED) with zero negative attributions is favorable for faith-based allocation.
The bear case centers on valuation, acquisition-dependent growth, and REIT structural risks. Forward P/E of 24.22x prices in substantial continued growth — any slowdown in acquisition activity or tightening of healthcare REIT cap rates could compress the premium multiple. Revenue growth is entirely acquisition-driven rather than organic; same-property revenue growth is minimal under triple-net leases with fixed escalators. ROE of only 8.07% reflects the capital-intensive nature of real estate ownership — shareholders are paying a premium for growth funded by equity issuance and debt. The GAAP payout ratio of ~95% leaves minimal retained earnings for self-funded growth, requiring ongoing access to capital markets. The 13 analyst consensus 'Strong Buy' and $45.15 price target suggest limited near-term upside from current levels of $40.38 [3]. Insider selling ($1.04M over 3 years) without any insider purchases raises questions about management's personal conviction in the stock at current prices. The company's growth is dependent on continued access to attractively-priced capital — any credit tightening or interest rate spike could choke the acquisition pipeline.
