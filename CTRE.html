Company Profile
Real Estate ·REIT - Healthcare Facilities ·NYSE ·United States
CareTrust REIT, Inc. is a healthcare-focused real estate investment trust that acquires, finances, and owns skilled nursing facilities, assisted living communities, and other senior housing properties. Spun off from The Ensign Group in 2014, the company grew its market cap from $2B to over $8B in two years through disciplined acquisitions, expanded into the UK care home market in 2025, and entered the SHOP (senior housing operating portfolio) segment.

Investment Thesis
CareTrust REIT delivered a transformational 2025, completing $1.80B of investments that drove FFO per share growth of 17% to $1.76, net income growth of 96%, and a 16.4% dividend increase to $0.39/quarter [1][2]. The company raised 2026 guidance with midpoints implying ~15% Normalized FFO growth, achieved an investment-grade credit rating from Moody's, and maintains ultra-low leverage at 0.6x Net Debt/EBITDA. With 12 consecutive years of dividend payments, 10 years of consecutive growth, and a yield of 3.82%, CareTrust offers a rare combination of growth and income in the healthcare REIT space. The operator-first underwriting model backed by 60+ years of Ensign Group heritage creates a differentiated competitive position in skilled nursing and assisted living acquisitions.
The primary concern is valuation stretch and dilution. At 26.9x forward P/E with a PEG ratio of 4.88, CareTrust is priced for continued rapid growth, leaving no margin for acquisition pace deceleration [4]. The 26.4% YoY share dilution used to fund the $1.80B investment program erodes per-share value, and the 92% payout ratio leaves minimal FFO cushion if occupancy or rent collection falters. Concentration in skilled nursing facilities (~60% of portfolio) exposes CareTrust to Medicaid/Medicare reimbursement cuts, state-level staffing mandates, and post-COVID regulatory scrutiny. International expansion into the UK adds currency risk, unfamiliar regulatory environments, and operational complexity that the lean corporate team has not previously navigated [1][2].
Key Catalysts
  1. Continued $1B+ annual acquisition pipeline targeting healthcare properties with immediate accretive returns, supported by investment-grade credit and 0.6x leverage capacity
  2. UK care home market expansion opening a multi-billion dollar growth runway with attractive cap rates relative to the mature US market
  3. SHOP segment operations delivering operator-level returns that exceed traditional triple-net lease yields, diversifying the revenue and earnings model
Key Risks
  1. Forward P/E of 26.9x and PEG of 4.88 embed aggressive growth expectations — any acquisition pace deceleration or integration stumbles could trigger significant multiple compression
  2. 26.4% YoY share dilution to fund acquisitions erodes per-share value and signals dependence on equity capital markets that may not remain favorable
  3. Skilled nursing concentration exposes CareTrust to Medicaid/Medicare reimbursement policy changes, state staffing mandates, and regulatory scrutiny that could impair operator ability to pay rent

AI Resilience LOW RISK

AI Risk Protection (8/9): Healthcare real estate is structurally immune to AI disruption. Skilled nursing facilities, assisted living communities, and senior housing require physical buildings, in-person caregiving, and licensed clinical operations that AI cannot substitute [1]. AI-driven healthcare improvements could increase demand for post-acute care facilities by extending lifespans and creating more patients requiring skilled nursing. CareTrust's core competency — underwriting healthcare operators and matching them with properties — involves relationship-based judgment that resists automation. The triple-net lease model further insulates CareTrust, as operators bear operating risk while CareTrust owns physical assets. AI Opportunity (0/1): No AI-related revenue. CareTrust does not develop or deploy AI products; its business is owning and financing physical healthcare properties [5].
