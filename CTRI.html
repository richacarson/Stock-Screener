Company Profile
Industrials ·Utility Infrastructure Services ·NYSE ·United States
Centuri Holdings, Inc. is a strategic utility infrastructure services company that partners with regulated utilities to build and maintain energy networks across the United States and Canada. Operating through four segments — US Gas, US Electric, Canadian Gas, and Canadian Electric — the company provides installation, maintenance, replacement, and repair services for gas and electric distribution systems, as well as renewable energy infrastructure. Spun off from Southwest Gas Holdings and IPO'd in April 2024.

Investment Thesis
Centuri is riding the most significant utility infrastructure spending cycle in decades, driven by grid modernization, renewable energy integration, and AI-fueled data center power demand [2][5]. FY2025 revenue hit a record $3.0B (+13%), with Q1 2026 accelerating to 31% growth ($723M) and gross profit surging 76% [1][2]. The $6.5B record backlog provides approximately 8 quarters of revenue visibility, and the $13B bidding pipeline offers substantial upside. Management's 'Vision One Centuri' plan targets 10-15% revenue CAGR through 2029 with gross margins expanding to 8.7-9.7%, and the Forward PEG of 0.74 suggests the market has not fully priced the growth trajectory. Adjusted EBITDA guidance for 2026 of $280-310M represents meaningful profit expansion from the 2025 base.
The bear case centers on profitability, leverage, and governance risk. Centuri's 0.98% net profit margin and 4.41% ROE are among the lowest in the industrial sector — even at $3B+ revenue, the company barely earns money [3]. D/E of 108% with $941M total debt creates meaningful financial risk if the utility spending cycle decelerates. The trailing P/E of 82.54x reflects the depressed current earnings, but even the forward P/E of 36.58x is demanding for a construction services company. Securities fraud investigations initiated in August 2024 remain a governance overhang. CEO Christian Brown has been in role only seven months (as of July 2026), limiting confidence in execution under new leadership. The company does not pay a dividend, offering shareholders no income return while waiting for margin expansion to materialize.
Key Catalysts
  1. Record $6.5B backlog and $13B bidding pipeline converting to revenue at accelerating rates, with 2026 guidance of $3.24-3.54B representing 8-18% growth
  2. AI-driven data center expansion requiring massive utility infrastructure investment, creating a secular multi-year demand tailwind for Centuri's electric and gas services
  3. Gross margin expansion toward the 8.7-9.7% Vision One Centuri target would transform bottom-line profitability from the current sub-1% net margin
Key Risks
  1. Sub-1% net profit margin and 4.41% ROE mean the company barely earns money despite $3B+ revenue — any project cost overrun or pricing pressure could push earnings negative
  2. D/E of 108% with $941M total debt creates financial fragility; rising interest rates or slowing utility CapEx could squeeze both margins and the balance sheet simultaneously
  3. Securities fraud investigations (August 2024) and new CEO (December 2024) create governance and execution uncertainty during a critical growth phase
