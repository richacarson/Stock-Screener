Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Cue Biopharma, Inc. is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing a novel class of injectable biologic therapeutics to selectively engage and modulate targeted, disease-relevant T cells. Its proprietary Immuno-STAT platform supports programs including CUE-101 (HPV16+ head and neck cancer), CUE-401 (autoimmune via Treg induction), and CUE-501 (autoimmune via B cell depletion) through collaborations with Boehringer Ingelheim and Ono Pharmaceutical. Market cap approximately $107M.
29 Employees ·www.cuebiopharma.com

Investment Thesis
Cue Biopharma's Q1 2026 results mark a pivotal inflection — revenue surged to $5.7M from $0.4M a year prior, driven by the Boehringer Ingelheim collaboration that validated the proprietary Immuno-STAT platform with a $7.5M milestone payment [1]. CUE-101 Phase 1b demonstrated a confirmed 50% ORR with pembrolizumab in HPV16+ head and neck cancer with median OS of 32.7 months — clinically meaningful results for a 29-person company [1]. The Boehringer partnership carries up to $337.5M in potential milestones, and the $30M private placement plus new CEO Shao-Lee Lin (ex-Acelyrin founder, prior AbbVie) provide capital and leadership runway [1][3]. Net loss narrowed 58% YoY with EPS improving from -$4.95 to -$1.08, signaling improving capital efficiency.
Leadership instability is the defining risk — CUE has burned through three CEOs in ten months (Azam resigned March 2026 after joining September 2025, interim Kellmeyer, now Lin), creating strategic whiplash at a critical clinical stage [3]. The company remains deeply unprofitable with -138.5% ROE, -59.7% profit margin, and approximately $24.6M annual operating cash burn [2]. At $107M market cap with no approved products and full clinical-stage binary risk, a single trial failure could eliminate most of the equity value. The $5.7M quarterly revenue is entirely collaboration-dependent milestone payments rather than commercial product revenue, and future milestones are contingent on successful advancement of partnered programs [1]. The reverse stock split masks underlying share price weakness, and additional dilutive equity raises are likely within 12-18 months given the cash burn trajectory.
