Company Profile
Utilities ·Renewable Electricity ·NYSE ·United States
Clearway Energy is one of the largest owners of clean energy generation assets in the United States, with approximately 9 GW of installed wind, solar, and energy storage capacity plus roughly 2.5 GW of natural gas generation. The company operates as a yieldco, distributing cash available for distribution (CAFD) to shareholders through quarterly dividends. Sponsored by Clearway Energy Group (majority-owned by Global Infrastructure Partners). Q2 2026 revenue grew 22.7% to $481M with Adjusted EBITDA of $409M. Plans $3B in growth investments 2026-2029. Headquartered in Princeton, New Jersey.
60 Employees ·www.clearwayenergy.com

Investment Thesis
Clearway Energy delivered a strong Q2 2026 with revenue up 22.7% to $481M, Adjusted EBITDA of $409M (up 19.2%), and adjusted EPS of $1.00 dramatically beating the $0.38 consensus [1]. At $29.21 with a forward P/E of 17.84 and ~6.5% dividend yield, the stock offers attractive income with growth optionality. The AI data center power opportunity is transformative — 2 GW of AI-linked PPAs including 1.17 GW with Google provide multi-decade contracted revenue visibility, and the $3B growth investment plan spanning 2026-2029 targets wind repowering, battery storage, and data center power solutions [3]. The 8-year dividend track record with 6.5% 5-year growth CAGR supports the income thesis, and the Inspire score of 75 (ALIGNED) makes this one of the strongest faith-aligned holdings in the renewable energy space [4].
The bear case centers on financial structure and near-term headwinds. The 2026 CAFD guidance was lowered to $430-470M from $470-510M due to ENSO weather patterns, highlighting the inherent meteorological variability of renewable energy cash flows [1]. D/E of 177% and payout ratio of 221% (typical for yieldcos but structurally elevated) create refinancing risk if interest rates remain high. ROE of -3.2% reflects the yieldco's depreciation-heavy structure that depresses reported earnings even as cash generation remains robust [5]. Stock price declined from $32.65 to $29.21 since August despite strong Q2 results, suggesting the market is pricing in CAFD guidance risk. Potential changes to clean energy tax credits under evolving federal energy policy could reduce economic returns on new acquisitions and slow portfolio growth.

AI Risk Protection (8/9): Clearway's core business — owning and operating physical power generation infrastructure — is structurally immune to AI disruption and actively benefits from it [3]. AI data centers are among the most electricity-intensive facilities ever built, and explosive growth in AI workloads is creating unprecedented demand for clean power. Clearway has signed 1.17 GW of PPAs with Google specifically for AI data center demand and is developing integrated power campuses for hyperscale customers [3]. The 'AI power bottleneck' is a long-term structural tailwind. No pathway exists for AI to substitute physical electricity generation. The only risk vector is potential policy changes reducing renewable energy incentives, which AI cannot cause but could impact the economics of new project development. AI Opportunity (1/1): The 2 GW of AI-linked PPAs represent contracted, quantifiable AI-driven revenue over multi-decade terms, directly tying Clearway's growth to AI infrastructure expansion [3].
