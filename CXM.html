Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NYSE ·United States
Sprinklr provides a unified AI-powered customer experience management (CXM) platform enabling enterprises to manage customer interactions across 30+ digital channels including social media, messaging, email, and voice. Products include AI agents resolving up to 40% of inquiries, Sprinklr Digital Twin (AI brand representation), contact center intelligence, and generative AI-native service SKUs. Serves large enterprises with subscription SaaS model. Founded in 2009 by Ragy Thomas (Chairman), with CEO Rory Read (ex-Dell) appointed November 2024.

Investment Thesis
Sprinklr delivered FY2026 revenue of $857M (7.6% growth) with Q1 FY2027 beating estimates at $219.5M, suggesting the 1% FY27 guidance was conservative and growth is stabilizing under CEO Rory Read [1][5]. The balance sheet is a fortress — $474M cash, $48M debt, net cash of $426M — against a market cap of $1.17B, meaning 36% of market cap is backed by cash. AI products are demonstrably gaining traction: gen AI SKUs grew 50% YoY, AI agents resolve 40% of inquiries, and Sprinklr Digital Twin is shipping [2][3]. Non-GAAP operating margin expanded from 11% to 17% in FY2026, and best renewal rates in four quarters signal improving customer retention.
The bear case is substantial. Revenue growth decelerated to 7.6% in FY2026 with FY27 initially guided to only ~1%, reflecting competitive pressure from Salesforce Agentforce, Microsoft Copilot, and AI-native startups directly targeting Sprinklr's CXM workflow [1][3]. GAAP profit margin of just 2.7% (impacted by one-time loss) reveals fragile profitability beneath non-GAAP adjustments. Cultural instability is a persistent concern — Glassdoor 3.4/5 with 'toxic work culture' commentary, 'leadership exits,' and strategy described as 'pivoting back and forth' under the new CEO [4]. Net dollar expansion of 102% barely exceeds breakeven, suggesting limited wallet-share growth within existing accounts. The 17-year-old platform carries technical debt that may limit AI integration speed versus purpose-built competitors, and the company's $1.17B market cap leaves it vulnerable to M&A at a discount.
Key Catalysts
  1. Q1 FY2027 revenue beat ($219.5M vs consensus $215.9M) suggests FY27 growth could exceed the conservative ~1% guide, potentially rerating the stock if sustained acceleration materializes
  2. AI agents resolving 40% of inquiries and gen AI SKUs growing 50% YoY could drive net dollar expansion above 110% as enterprise adoption deepens through 2026-2027
  3. Net cash position of $426M (36% of market cap) plus $200M buyback authorization provides downside protection and optionality for accretive M&A or capital return at depressed valuations
Key Risks
  1. Salesforce Agentforce, Microsoft Copilot, and AI-native startups directly target Sprinklr's value proposition — competitive moat is narrowing as hyperscalers deploy AI agents on platforms with larger installed bases
  2. Cultural instability and leadership exits under new CEO threaten execution of the AI-driven turnaround in a market where engineering talent retention is critical for product differentiation
  3. GAAP profit margin of 2.7% and ROE of 7% reveal that the business generates inadequate returns on equity despite $857M revenue, risking value-trap status if growth doesn't reaccelerate
