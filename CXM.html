Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NYSE ·United States
Sprinklr provides a unified AI-powered customer experience management (CXM) platform enabling enterprises to manage customer interactions across 30+ digital channels including social media, messaging, email, and voice. Products include AI agents resolving up to 40% of inquiries, Sprinklr Digital Twin (AI brand representation), contact center intelligence, and generative AI-native service SKUs. Q2 FY2027 total revenue reached $213.7M (up 1% YoY) with subscription revenue growth of 3%, non-GAAP operating margin of 15%, and AI-native SKU ARR growing 40% YoY. Founded in 2009 by Ragy Thomas (Chairman), with CEO Rory Read (ex-Dell) appointed November 2024.

Investment Thesis
Sprinklr's Q2 FY2027 results show AI product traction beneath the flat headline: AI-native SKU ARR grew 40% YoY, NAR (net new ARR) surged 50%, completed transactions increased 30%, and four $1M+ ARR deals closed [1]. The company raised subscription revenue guidance to $782.5-$784.5M, suggesting the AI pivot is gaining commercial traction even as total revenue grew only 1% [1]. The balance sheet is a fortress — $474M cash, $48M debt, net cash of $426M — against a market cap of ~$1.4B, meaning ~30% of market cap is backed by cash [4]. Non-GAAP operating margin of 15% and $79M H1 FCF demonstrate operational discipline. The stock at $6.24 may offer asymmetric upside if AI products accelerate total revenue growth back to the mid-single digits.
The bear case remains substantial. Total revenue growth decelerated to 1% YoY in Q2 FY2027, reflecting competitive pressure from Salesforce Agentforce, Microsoft Copilot, and AI-native startups directly targeting Sprinklr's CXM workflow [1]. GAAP profit margin of just 2.7% and ROE of ~7% reveal that the business generates inadequate returns on equity despite $857M revenue — the company risks value-trap status if AI product growth doesn't translate into total revenue acceleration [4]. Higher data and hosting costs from AI capabilities are pressuring margins and offsetting pricing gains. Cultural instability persists — Glassdoor 3.4/5 with 'toxic work culture' commentary, 'leadership exits,' and strategy described as 'pivoting back and forth' under CEO Read [3]. The 17-year-old platform carries technical debt that may limit AI integration speed versus purpose-built competitors, and the $1.4B market cap leaves it vulnerable to M&A at a discount that may not reward current shareholders.
Key Catalysts
  1. AI-native SKU ARR growth of 40% YoY and NAR growth of 50% could translate into total revenue reacceleration in H2 FY2027 if enterprise adoption of AI agents and Digital Twin deepens beyond the four $1M+ deals closed in Q2 [1]
  2. Raised subscription guidance to $782.5-$784.5M signals management confidence in the AI product pivot; any upside revision to total revenue guidance would catalyze a re-rating [1]
  3. Net cash position of $426M (~30% of market cap) plus $200M buyback authorization provides downside protection and optionality for accretive M&A or capital return at depressed valuations [4]
Key Risks
  1. Salesforce Agentforce, Microsoft Copilot, and AI-native startups directly target Sprinklr's value proposition with larger installed bases — total revenue growth of only 1% suggests competitive moat is narrowing despite strong AI product metrics [1]
  2. Cultural instability and leadership exits under CEO Read threaten execution of the AI-driven turnaround in a market where engineering talent retention is critical for product differentiation [3]
  3. Higher data and hosting costs from AI capabilities are pressuring margins — non-GAAP operating margin of 15% may not improve if AI costs scale faster than AI revenue, risking a permanent profitability ceiling [1]
