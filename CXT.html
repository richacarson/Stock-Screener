Company Profile
Industrials ·Specialty Industrial Machinery ·NYSE ·United States
Crane NXT is an industrial technology company providing solutions to secure, detect, and authenticate critical assets. The company operates through Crane Payment Innovations (CPI) for payment and vending automation technology, and Security and Authentication Technologies for banknote security features, brand protection, and digital content authentication. Separated from Crane Holdings in 2023, Crane NXT has since acquired OpSec Security, De La Rue's authentication business, and Antares Vision (closed March 2026). Q2 2026 sales were $493.2M (+22% YoY), with FY2026 guidance of 15-17% sales growth and raised adjusted EPS guidance of $4.22-$4.42. Market cap approximately $3.1B.
4,800 Employees ·www.cranenxt.com

Investment Thesis
Crane NXT is a compelling value play in industrial security technology, trading at 10.59x forward earnings with 20%+ revenue growth and raised adjusted EPS guidance of $4.22-$4.42 for FY2026 [1][4]. Q2 2026 delivered $493.2M in sales (+22% YoY) with improved net income of $35.4M (+42% YoY in Q2), demonstrating acquisition contributions are materializing. The transformation from a banknote security and payment systems company into a comprehensive authentication platform — through OpSec, De La Rue, and Antares Vision acquisitions — creates a differentiated offering spanning physical currency security, digital brand protection, and pharmaceutical track-and-trace [1]. D/E improved to 90% from 121%, and ROE reached 13.59% as integration progresses. The Inspire Insight score of 37 (ALIGNED) with 13 positive ESG attributions reflects responsible practices, and the business provides genuine societal value by combating counterfeiting and ensuring product authenticity.
The bear case centers on integration complexity, cultural dysfunction, and the GAAP-vs-adjusted earnings gap. Three major acquisitions in under three years create significant execution risk — H1 2026 net income declined to $41.8M from $46.6M despite 20% revenue growth, as acquisition costs and amortization suppressed reported earnings [1]. The wide gap between implied GAAP EPS (~$1.44 annualized H1) and adjusted EPS guidance ($4.22-$4.42) warrants scrutiny of the adjustments. Glassdoor rating of 1.9/5 with 'annual layoffs' and 'lack of vision' is deeply concerning — cultural dysfunction could impair retention of key technical talent during critical integration phases and undermine the very capabilities being acquired [2]. Profit margin of 7.84% reflects ongoing acquisition dilution, and margin recovery depends on achieving synergies across three simultaneous integrations. Organic growth of only 3% in Q2 2026 (versus 22% total) reveals heavy reliance on acquired revenue. The dividend yield of 1.4% does not qualify for the Dividend sleeve, limiting appeal for income-oriented investors despite 33 consecutive years of payments.

AI Resilience MODERATE

AI Risk Protection (6/9): Crane NXT operates in security and authentication — physical banknote security features, vending/payment hardware, and brand protection. The physical hardware components (payment systems, micro-optic security features on currency) are structurally immune to AI displacement — central banks will continue requiring physical security features on banknotes, and vending machines require physical coin/bill handling [1]. The Antares Vision pharmaceutical track-and-trace business operates in a heavily regulated space with high barriers to AI disruption. However, the digital authentication and brand protection segments face mixed AI dynamics: AI enhances counterfeit detection (enabling Crane NXT), but AI-generated counterfeits and deepfakes also increase the threat landscape. Overall, the core business is protected by physical infrastructure requirements, regulatory moats, and switching costs. AI Opportunity (1/1): Crane NXT's Security and Authentication Technologies segment uses machine learning for digital content authentication across online marketplaces and social media platforms [2]. The Antares Vision acquisition adds AI-powered inspection and track-and-trace capabilities in pharmaceutical manufacturing.
