Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Cyclerion Therapeutics, Inc. is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on developing soluble guanylate cyclase (sGC) stimulators for the treatment of serious neuropsychiatric disorders. Spun off from Ironwood Pharmaceuticals in 2019 and headquartered in Cambridge, Massachusetts, the company has a limited pipeline centered on sGC pharmacology for CNS conditions. CEO Regina Graul, Ph.D., was promoted to CEO from President. FY2025 revenue was $2.1M with a net loss of -$3.5M (EPS -$1.11). Q1 2026 revenue was $875K against $1.88M in total expenses. Market cap is approximately $15.7M with the stock trading around $4.25. The company has entered into an exclusive negotiation period and binding equity investment agreement with Korsana Biosciences.

The company's clinical-stage pipeline targets serious CNS conditions with high unmet medical need. The Inspire Impact Score of 44 (ALIGNED) with no negative attributions reflects responsible governance practices.
