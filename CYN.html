Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NASDAQ ·United States
Cyngn develops and deploys autonomous vehicle technology for industrial organizations including manufacturers and logistics companies. Its DriveMod platform enables autonomous tuggers and forklifts for material handling in warehouses, manufacturing, and distribution environments. The Enterprise Autonomy Suite (EAS) software provides fleet management, autonomous navigation, and Cyngn Insight data analytics. Customers include John Deere, Rivian, G&J Pepsi, Coats, and WEG electric motors. NVIDIA partnership for next-gen robotics AI. H1 2026 revenue of $249K (vs. $80.9K prior year). Founded in 2013, headquartered in Menlo Park, California. Executed a 1-for-150 reverse stock split in February 2025.
62 Employees ·www.cyngn.com

Investment Thesis
Cyngn is a speculative early-stage autonomous vehicle company showing accelerating commercial traction — H1 2026 revenue of $249K tripled from $80.9K in H1 2025, autonomous missions increased 127% YoY in Q1, and the company expanded deployments into agriculture (Vann Family Orchards) and manufacturing (WEG electric motors) alongside existing customers John Deere, Rivian, G&J Pepsi, and Coats [1][2]. The DriveMod platform addresses a genuine industrial need for automation amid chronic labor shortages and safety concerns. Trading at $1.24 with $44.4M in cash and no debt, the downside is partially protected by a cash floor significantly above the market cap [4]. The NVIDIA partnership for next-gen robotics AI provides technology credibility. If the EAS subscription model scales to meaningful revenue, the asymmetric upside is significant.
The bear case is existential. With $249K half-year revenue and approximately $23.5M annual net loss, Cyngn is consuming cash roughly 100x faster than it generates revenue [1][4]. The 1-for-150 reverse stock split in February 2025 reflects extreme share price distress, and ongoing dilutive equity offerings continue to erode per-share value — the stock hit a 52-week low of $1.03 in July 2026. After 13 years of development, the company has yet to demonstrate commercial viability at any meaningful scale. Competition from Amazon Robotics, Locus Robotics, NVIDIA's Isaac platform, and dozens of well-funded autonomous vehicle startups could make Cyngn's technology obsolete before reaching profitability. Daily dollar volume of $218K — down from $404K at the prior screen — fails the IOWN liquidity floor decisively, making the stock untradeable for institutional investors. The market cap below the cash balance suggests negative implied value for the technology platform.
