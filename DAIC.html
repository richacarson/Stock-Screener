Company Profile
Technology ·Information Technology Services ·NASDAQ ·United States
CID HoldCo, Inc., operating as Dot Ai, is a Las Vegas-based micro-cap that went public via SPAC in June 2025 selling IoT asset-tracking hardware and AI-enhanced analytics software, and is attempting a shift to subscription revenue. In October 2026 it closed a ~$65M all-stock acquisition of Envoy Technologies, an all-electric car-sharing amenity provider, from BladeRanger, partly to restore Nasdaq's stockholders' equity requirement; Envoy's $12.5M convertible note held by Blink Charging remains outstanding. The company faces a Nasdaq delisting determination (hearing pending), executed a 1-for-25 reverse split in May 2026, reported Q2 2026 revenue of only $12,398 (-90% YoY), and disclosed a secured-loan default that could lead to foreclosure on a material portion of its assets. Market cap is roughly $2.5-2.7M at $1.05/share.

Investment Thesis
The only bull case is an option on a turnaround. Management reaffirmed 2026 guidance of $6.0-7.5M revenue and $12-15M bookings, implying a steep H2 ramp from H1 revenue of roughly $25K, and the October 2026 closing of the ~$65M Envoy acquisition adds an operating EV car-sharing business at residential, hotel and campus properties where Dot Ai's tracking technology could be applied [1][2]. If Nasdaq's Hearings Panel accepts the equity-compliance case and guidance is met, a ~$2.5M market cap leaves extreme upside on any stabilization.
The bear case is existential. Nasdaq staff has determined to delist the stock on market-value grounds and added a delinquent-filing basis, the company is in default on a secured loan (about $1.06M owed as of August 12, 2026) with foreclosure expected to transfer a material portion of its assets, and Envoy brings a $12.5M convertible note held by Blink Charging [2][3]. Revenue fell 90% YoY in Q2 2026, equity is negative, and the 1-for-25 reverse split plus all-stock deal issuance at a $6 reference price against a ~$1 share price point to continued heavy dilution [3][5].
Key Catalysts
  1. Nasdaq Hearings Panel decision on whether the Envoy acquisition satisfies the $2.5M stockholders' equity requirement and resolves the market-value and delinquent-filing deficiencies [2][3]
  2. Delivery of the back-end-weighted 2026 guidance ($6.0-7.5M revenue, $12-15M bookings) in the Q3 and Q4 reports [1]
  3. Resolution of the LHT I, LLC secured-loan default without foreclosure on core operations [3]
Key Risks
  1. Delisting from Nasdaq following the August 6, 2026 staff determination and the added delinquent-filing basis, which would severely impair liquidity [3]
  2. Foreclosure under the secured-loan default, which the company expects would transfer a material portion of its operations and assets [3]
  3. Further dilution from Series C convertible preferred issued in the Envoy deal and the outstanding $12.5M Blink Charging convertible note [2]
