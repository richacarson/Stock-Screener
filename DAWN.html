Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NMS ·United States
Day One Biopharmaceuticals was a clinical-stage oncology company focused on developing and commercializing therapies for genomically defined cancers, including pediatric patients. The company's lead product, Ojemda (tovorafenib), was the first approved RAF inhibitor for pediatric low-grade glioma, generating $155.4M in net product revenue in 2025 with 2026 guidance of $225-250M. Day One was acquired by Servier for $21.50 per share ($2.5B enterprise value) in Q2 2026 and subsequently delisted from Nasdaq. This report reflects the company's pre-acquisition profile as of early 2026. Founded in 2018 and formerly headquartered in Brisbane, California.
