Company Profile
Financial Services ·Asset Management ·NYSE ·United States
DigitalBridge Group, Inc. is a global alternative asset manager dedicated exclusively to digital infrastructure investments, including data centers, cell towers, fiber networks, small cells, and edge infrastructure. The firm manages approximately $40.2B in fee-earning equity under management (FEEUM) as of June 30, 2026. Founded by Marc Ganzi, DigitalBridge sits at the convergence of power and data infrastructure. Q2 2026 reported total revenue of $508.7M with GAAP EPS of $1.15 and net income of $212.7M. In December 2025, SoftBank Group announced a definitive agreement to acquire DigitalBridge for $16.00 per share (~$4.0B enterprise value), with stockholder approval received April 23, 2026 and closing expected H2 2026.

Investment Thesis
DigitalBridge is a uniquely positioned pure-play digital infrastructure asset manager with FEEUM growing 22% to $40.2B as of June 2026, managing portfolio companies at the nexus of AI infrastructure demand [1]. Q2 2026 delivered total revenue of $508.7M with GAAP net income of $212.7M ($1.15 EPS), driven by investment realizations as the digital infrastructure market matures [4]. The SoftBank acquisition at $16.00/share (stockholder approved April 2026, closing expected H2 2026) validates the thesis that AI infrastructure is the defining asset class of the decade — SoftBank explicitly cited DigitalBridge's capabilities for the projected $6.7T AI infrastructure market by 2030 [2]. Marc Ganzi's convergence framework (power + data, estimating 38GW of AI demand) provides a differentiated investment lens. The Inspire Impact Score of 56 (ALIGNED) with zero negative attributions positions DBRG favorably for faith-based investors.
The primary consideration is that DBRG is now a merger arbitrage situation with limited upside. The stock trades at ~$15.80 against the $16.00 acquisition price, offering minimal return beyond the ~1.3% spread to close [6]. If the SoftBank deal were to break, standalone valuation at 37x forward earnings is elevated for an asset manager with ~3.6% ROE and FRE margin compression to 30% (down from 38% as compensation costs rose 19% YoY in Q2 2026) [4]. The fee revenue of $88.1M in Q2 declined 17% YoY even as FEEUM grew, signaling a disconnect between asset growth and near-term monetization. Post-acquisition, DigitalBridge will be privately held under SoftBank, eliminating the public equity investment opportunity. For current holders, the calculus is simple: hold for the ~$0.20/share merger spread or redeploy capital. GAAP financials remain complex — the $508.7M in total revenue includes investment income and carried interest that obscure underlying fee economics.
