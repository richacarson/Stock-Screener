Company Profile
Financial Services ·Asset Management ·NYSE ·United States
DigitalBridge is a leading global alternative asset manager dedicated exclusively to digital infrastructure, managing $40.2B in fee-earning equity under management (FEEUM) invested across data centers, cell towers, fiber networks, small cells, and edge infrastructure. FY2025 fee revenue grew 14% to $375M with FRE of $142M (+33%), and Q1 2026 fee revenue was $87.3M. Founded as Colony Capital in 1991, the company transformed into a pure-play digital infrastructure investor under CEO Marc Ganzi and is headquartered in Boca Raton, Florida. A proposed SoftBank acquisition is pending, limiting forward guidance disclosure.
303 Employees ·www.digitalbridge.com

Investment Thesis
DigitalBridge at $16.00 with a $3.0B market cap offers pure-play exposure to the AI infrastructure supercycle through a leading alternative asset manager with $40.2B FEEUM invested across data centers, towers, and fiber — the physical foundation of every AI workload [3][4]. Fee revenue grew 14% to $375M in FY2025 with FRE surging 33% to $142M, demonstrating the operating leverage of the fee-based model as institutional capital flows into AI infrastructure. The asset-light balance sheet ($508M cash, $320M debt, 12% D/E) and 54% profit margins reflect a premium business model. The Colony-to-DigitalBridge transformation was completed years before the AI boom validated the thesis, demonstrating prescient strategic positioning by CEO Marc Ganzi [3]. The net zero by 2030 portfolio commitment aligns with both ESG mandates and the growing institutional focus on sustainable infrastructure [5].
The bear case is dominated by the pending SoftBank acquisition, which creates binary outcome risk and limits independent analysis of DigitalBridge's standalone value. Forward P/E of 40.0 and PEG of 3.06 reflect acquisition pricing rather than organic fundamentals, and the company declined to provide guidance or host an earnings call with Q2 2026 results because of the transaction [1][2]. FEEUM declined from $41.0B (Dec 2025) to $40.2B (June 2026), raising questions about whether the fundraising cycle has peaked. The 303-employee organization is dependent on a small number of key individuals, and Marc Ganzi's post-acquisition role is uncertain. GAAP revenue metrics are distorted by the Colony Capital transformation — reported revenue fell from $608M to $71M over three years — making fundamental analysis difficult with standard financial tools. If the SoftBank deal falls through, the stock could face significant downside as the acquisition premium unwinds [2].

  1. AI-driven data center construction supercycle — hyperscalers are deploying $200B+ annually in data center capex, creating massive demand for exactly the infrastructure DigitalBridge invests in (data centers, fiber, towers, edge computing) [3][4]
