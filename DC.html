Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Gold Mining ·NYSE American ·United States
Dakota Gold Corp. is a gold exploration and development company focused on revitalizing the historic Homestake District in Lead, South Dakota. The company holds gold mineral properties surrounding the legendary Homestake Mine, which produced over 40 million ounces of gold during its 125-year operating history. Primary projects include the Richmond Hill Oxide Heap Leach Gold Project with an after-tax NPV of up to $3.7B and IRR of 107% at current gold prices, and the Maitland Gold Project. Production targeted for late 2029. Founded in 2017, headquartered in Lead, South Dakota.

Investment Thesis
Dakota Gold offers speculative upside tied to the Richmond Hill gold project, which shows exceptional economics at current gold prices (NPV $3.7B, IRR 107%, AISC ~$1,050/oz) [2]. The debt-free balance sheet ($106.9M cash, virtually zero debt) provides substantial runway through the pre-feasibility study and beyond. The company benefits from private-land positioning in a proven historic mining district that produced over 40 million ounces, and the 6-analyst consensus of 'Strong Buy' with $12.00 price target implies 188% upside from current levels [8]. Gold prices near all-time highs provide a favorable macro backdrop.
However, this remains a pre-revenue exploration play with significant execution risk across permitting, construction, and eventual production ramp -- production is not expected until late 2029 at earliest [2]. Net insider selling of over $1.8M in 2026, led by CEO Quartermain's $1.7M sale, undermines confidence in management's conviction at current prices [7]. The $557M market cap prices in significant optionality for an asset that has not yet been permitted or built. Short interest of 5.46% signals meaningful market skepticism. This is a speculative holding with binary outcomes -- substantial upside if Richmond Hill reaches production on time and on budget, but significant capital risk if permitting, environmental challenges, or gold price declines derail the project.
