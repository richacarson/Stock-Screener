Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Specialty Chemicals ·NYSE ·United States
DuPont de Nemours, Inc. provides technology-based materials and solutions through two segments: Healthcare & Water Technologies and Diversified Industrials. Products include Tyvek protective materials, FILMTEC water filtration membranes, semiconductor fabrication materials, and engineered adhesives and lubricants. The company generated TTM revenue of approximately $7.0B with a 0.8% GAAP profit margin depressed by restructuring charges related to the planned three-way corporate separation; adjusted operating EBITDA margin is approximately 24%. Q2 2026 net sales rose 4% to $1.8B with adjusted EPS of $1.88, beating consensus, and full-year 2026 guidance was raised to $7.16-7.19B revenue and $7.17-7.32 adjusted EPS [1][2].

Investment Thesis
DuPont at $133 with an $18B market cap is a specialty materials company in transformation — the planned three-way separation into Electronics, Water, and New DuPont aims to unlock value from businesses generating $7.0B in combined revenue with a 24% adjusted EBITDA margin [1][2]. Q2 2026 delivered 4% organic sales growth with adjusted EPS of $1.88 beating consensus, and management raised full-year guidance to $7.17-7.32 adjusted EPS, roughly triple trailing GAAP EPS of $3.72 as restructuring charges normalize [1][2]. The semiconductor materials franchise is a direct AI beneficiary — every advanced chip requires DuPont photoresists, CMP slurries, and interconnect materials — while the Water business addresses growing global scarcity. At 16.6x forward earnings with near-zero net debt (D/E 22.9%), DuPont offers reasonable value for a company whose earnings are inflecting sharply higher [5].
The bear case centers on execution risk and depressed trailing financials. GAAP profit margin of just 0.8% and ROE of 1.8% reflect significant restructuring charges, and while management guides to normalized earnings of $7.17-7.32 per share, the three-way separation introduces operational complexity, management distraction, and potential dis-synergies that could delay earnings normalization [3]. Revenue growth of just 4% is modest for a company valued at 16.6x forward earnings, and the estimated 5-year revenue CAGR of ~2% (complicated by corporate restructuring) suggests limited organic growth momentum [5]. DuPont's environmental legacy — 103 Superfund sites and ongoing PFAS litigation — poses tail risk for the post-separation entities, particularly as regulatory scrutiny of forever chemicals intensifies globally [4]. The dividend yield of 1.81% provides minimal income support, and the 81.5% GAAP payout ratio (artificially inflated by depressed earnings) could face pressure if the separation does not unlock the projected cost savings.
Key Catalysts
  1. Three-way corporate separation expected to unlock significant value by creating focused pure-play companies in Electronics, Water, and Industrials — each targeting distinct investor bases with clearer growth narratives and potentially higher valuation multiples [3]
  2. AI-driven semiconductor super cycle directly benefits DuPont's Electronics business as accelerating fab construction and advanced node migration at TSMC, Samsung, and Intel drive demand for photoresists, CMP slurries, and interconnect materials [3]
  3. Earnings normalization from $3.72 GAAP trailing EPS to $7.17-7.32 adjusted EPS guidance as restructuring charges fall away, representing potential 90%+ earnings growth that the 16.6x forward multiple does not fully reflect [1][2]
Key Risks
  1. Three-way separation execution risk — managing simultaneous separations of Electronics and Water businesses while running ongoing operations creates management distraction, potential dis-synergies, and stranded costs that could delay earnings normalization [3]
  2. Environmental and PFAS litigation tail risk — legacy of 103 Superfund sites and ongoing PFAS-related lawsuits could result in material settlements or remediation costs for post-separation entities as global regulatory scrutiny of forever chemicals intensifies [4]
  3. Modest organic growth of 4% YoY with ~2% estimated 5-year revenue CAGR suggests DuPont's end markets are mature, and the company depends on the separation to drive re-rating rather than fundamental growth acceleration [1][2][5]
