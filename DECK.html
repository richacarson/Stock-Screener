Company Profile
Consumer Cyclical ·Footwear & Accessories ·NYSE ·United States
Deckers Outdoor Corporation designs, markets, and distributes footwear, apparel, and accessories through its portfolio of brands including HOKA (performance running and lifestyle), UGG (premium sheepskin and comfort footwear), and Teva (outdoor sandals). HOKA has emerged as the company's primary growth engine, growing 15.9% in FY2026 to become nearly half of total revenue. The company reported record FY2026 revenue of $5.47B (+9.8% YoY) with $1.024B net income, zero debt, and $1.9B cash. Deckers operates a direct-to-consumer model alongside wholesale distribution, with international sales growing 15%.

Investment Thesis
Deckers at 11.24x forward earnings offers a compelling value entry into two category-defining footwear brands. HOKA delivered 15.9% revenue growth in FY2026 and continues to gain share in performance running, lifestyle, and hiking categories, while UGG proved its durability with 8.2% growth [1]. The company generated over $1B in free cash flow on $5.47B revenue, carries zero financial debt, and holds $1.9B in cash — a fortress balance sheet enabling $1.075B in buybacks during FY2026. ROE of 42.56% and profit margin of 18.36% reflect efficient brand-driven economics. FY2027 guidance of $5.86-5.91B (high-single-digit growth) with $7.30-7.45 EPS provides earnings visibility, and international sales growth of 15% signals significant runway in underpenetrated markets.
The bear case centers on decelerating growth, concentration risk, and limited innovation breadth. Revenue growth decelerated from double digits to 9.8% in FY2026, and FY2027 guidance implies further moderation to high-single-digit rates, suggesting HOKA's hypergrowth phase may be plateauing [1]. HOKA and UGG together represent virtually all of the company's revenue — if either brand faces a fashion or performance cycle downturn, the entire company suffers. The stock's 52-week decline reflects market concerns about growth sustainability. The athletic footwear market is intensely competitive, with On Running and New Balance also gaining share, creating pressure on HOKA's growth trajectory. Gross margin guidance of ~56.5% for FY2027 (down from 57.7%) signals potential margin compression from product mix shifts or tariff exposure. An Inspiration score of 3 reflects the absence of a deeply distinctive purpose beyond brand-building, and no AI deployment limits future innovation potential.
Key Catalysts
  1. HOKA's continued international expansion with 15% international sales growth indicating significant market share opportunity in Europe and Asia-Pacific, where brand awareness lags the United States [1]
  2. Direct-to-consumer channel expansion driving higher margins — DTC sales grew 8.1% in Q3 FY2026, and increasing the DTC mix would meaningfully expand operating margins beyond the 23.1% FY2026 level [1]
  3. FY2027 revenue guidance of $5.86-5.91B with EPS of $7.30-7.45 suggests continued profitable growth, and the $1.9B cash position enables aggressive buybacks that compound per-share earnings [1]
Key Risks
  1. Revenue growth deceleration from 9.8% in FY2026 to high-single-digit FY2027 guidance signals HOKA's hypergrowth phase may be plateauing, and the stock's decline from highs reflects market skepticism about sustained growth [1]
  2. Brand concentration risk — HOKA and UGG represent virtually all revenue, and any fashion cycle downturn, product quality issue, or competitive share loss in either brand would directly compress earnings [1]
  3. FY2027 gross margin guidance of ~56.5% (down 120bps from FY2026's 57.7%) indicates emerging margin headwinds from product mix, input costs, or tariff exposure that could pressure profitability as revenue growth slows [1]
