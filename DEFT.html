Company Profile
Financial Services ·Capital Markets ·NCM ·Canada
DeFi Technologies Inc. is a Canadian technology company focused on digital asset exchange-traded products (ETPs) and decentralized finance infrastructure. Through its subsidiary Valour, the company offers synthetic ETPs that track DeFi protocols and digital assets on European exchanges. It also operates DeFi Alpha, a specialized trading desk focused on arbitrage across the crypto ecosystem. The company generated TTM revenue of approximately CAD 61M with a 38.5% trailing profit margin, though revenue declined 40.7% YoY amid weak crypto market conditions and the departure of its CEO in November 2025 [1][2].
