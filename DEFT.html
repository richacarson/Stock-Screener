Company Profile
Financial Services ·Capital Markets ·NCM ·Canada
DeFi Technologies is a Canadian financial technology company that bridges traditional capital markets and decentralized finance through its subsidiaries. The Valour unit issues exchange-traded products (ETPs) tracking digital assets on European exchanges, Stillman Digital operates a trading desk focused on crypto arbitrage and OTC liquidity, and the company makes early-stage venture investments in the digital asset ecosystem. Formerly known as Valour Inc., the company was rebranded in July 2023. Revenue is highly volatile, swinging with crypto markets — TTM revenue of $61.2M includes quarters ranging from -$41.8M to $130.4M [1][3].
