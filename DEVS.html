Company Profile
Industrials ·Carbon Credits & Environmental Services ·NASDAQ ·Canada
DevvStream Corp. is a technology-based carbon credit streaming company that develops and monetizes environmental assets through partnerships with governments and corporations worldwide. The company co-develops carbon offset credit generating projects and provides capital to producers in exchange for a guaranteed portion of carbon credits. In 2026, DevvStream pivoted toward a digital-asset treasury strategy including Bitcoin and Solana staking, while facing NASDAQ delisting risk due to compliance failures. Q3 FY2026 revenue was $720 with a net loss of $6.2M.

Investment Thesis
DevvStream's Inspire Impact Score of 47 (ALIGNED) and environmental sustainability mission through carbon credit development represent the company's primary value proposition for faith-aligned investors. The voluntary carbon credit market is projected to grow significantly as corporations and governments pursue net-zero commitments, and DevvStream's streaming model — providing capital to project developers in exchange for a portion of generated credits — could theoretically scale if executed [4]. The $6M equity investment from EEME Energy provides near-term runway.
The bear case is overwhelming. DevvStream generated just $720 in Q3 FY2026 revenue (down 92.9% YoY) with a $6.2M quarterly net loss, implying the carbon credit business has essentially failed commercially [1]. The company faces NASDAQ delisting due to compliance failures and is transitioning to OTC trading as of June 2026 [3]. The strategic pivot to cryptocurrency treasury operations (Bitcoin and Solana staking with ~12,185 SOL staked) represents an abandonment of the core environmental thesis and introduces volatile crypto exposure to an already distressed company [1]. With an $8.24M market cap, approximately 35M shares outstanding at $0.24, and no path to profitability, DevvStream poses extreme capital loss risk. The company's survival beyond 12-18 months is uncertain without significant additional financing.
