Company Profile
Financial Services ·Capital Markets / Cryptocurrency ·NASDAQ ·United States
DeFi Development Corp. (formerly Janover Inc.) is a Solana treasury company that purchases, holds, and compounds SOL holdings through staking and operating owned validators. The Digital Asset Treasury segment constitutes the core business; the legacy Real Estate Platform segment (connecting commercial mortgage borrowers with lenders) was wound down as of Q2 2026. Acquired by former Kraken executives in April 2025, rebranded from Janover. Headquartered in Boca Raton, Florida.

Investment Thesis
DeFi Development Corp offers leveraged public-market exposure to Solana through a treasury vehicle holding approximately 2.3M SOL and SOL equivalents as of May 2026. SOL-per-share grew 108% over the trailing 12 months to 0.0670, TTM revenue reached $7.53M (up 313% YoY) from staking and validator operations, and FY2026 consensus revenue estimates of $18.7M imply continued rapid growth [1][2]. Cash and investments of $57.1M provide operational runway into 2027. The former-Kraken leadership team brings deep crypto operational expertise, and the company's validator infrastructure and DeFi protocol deployments differentiate it from passive SOL holding strategies [3].
The bear case is severe and multi-dimensional. DFDV is a micro-cap ($93M market cap) with no demonstrated path to profitability — Q1 2026 showed a net loss of $(3.18) per share on just $2.66M revenue, driven by unrealized SOL price fluctuations that make earnings completely unpredictable [1]. The 87% correlation to SOL price means shareholders could buy SOL directly with lower fees and zero dilution risk. Authorized share increases and equity-line draws create persistent dilution overhang that can erode SPS gains even when SOL appreciates. Management tenure averages just 1.2 years, Blake Janover's departure raises continuity concerns, and the wind-down of all legacy businesses leaves DFDV as a pure-play SOL bet with no revenue diversification and no defensive characteristics in a crypto downturn [4]. The stock has fallen from a 52-week high of $33.75 to $3.21, an 90% decline.
Key Catalysts
  1. SOL price appreciation above $200 would dramatically increase NAV and potentially attract institutional investors seeking regulated public-market Solana exposure through a NASDAQ-listed vehicle
  2. Validator infrastructure and DeFi yield compounding achieving the 0.075 SPS target would validate the treasury management strategy and differentiate from simple buy-and-hold crypto funds [2]
  3. Potential Solana ETF approval or broader institutional adoption of SOL could drive a re-rating premium for the only pure-play public Solana treasury vehicle on NASDAQ [3]
Key Risks
  1. Extreme concentration in a single volatile digital asset (SOL) with 87% price volatility means a Solana-specific event — protocol exploit, regulatory action, or competitive displacement by another L1 — could destroy the majority of shareholder value
  2. Persistent share dilution through equity lines, convertible debt, and RSU grants erodes SOL-per-share gains, potentially leaving common shareholders worse off than direct SOL holders paying zero management fees [4]
  3. Micro-cap status ($93M market cap), leadership instability (1.2-year average tenure), 90% decline from 52-week high, and complete dependence on crypto market conditions create existential risk if equity capital markets close during a prolonged SOL downturn [1]
