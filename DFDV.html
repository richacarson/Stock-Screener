Company Profile
Financial Services ·Capital Markets / Cryptocurrency ·NASDAQ ·United States
DeFi Development Corp. (formerly Janover Inc.) is a Solana treasury company that purchases, holds, and compounds SOL holdings through staking and operating owned validators. The Digital Asset Treasury segment constitutes the core business; the legacy Real Estate Platform segment was wound down as of Q2 2026. The company holds approximately 2.29M SOL with SOL-per-share of 0.0670, having achieved 108% SPS growth over the trailing year. Acquired by former Kraken executives in April 2025, rebranded from Janover, and sought to raise over $1B in securities for additional Solana acquisition. Headquartered in Boca Raton, Florida.

Investment Thesis
DeFi Development Corp offers leveraged public-market exposure to Solana through a treasury vehicle holding approximately 2.29M SOL with 108% SOL-per-share growth over the trailing year [2]. Q1 2026 revenue reached $2.66M from staking and validator operations, with FY2025 showing 442% revenue growth and TTM revenue of approximately $15M [1][5]. The stock has recovered 56% from its July lows ($3.21 to $5.00), suggesting improved sentiment as Solana strengthens. The former-Kraken leadership team brings deep crypto operational expertise, the company's validator infrastructure provides structural yield above passive SOL holding, and the $1B securities offering would dramatically scale the treasury if executed [3]. At $163M market cap, DFDV provides the only pure-play public Solana treasury exposure on NASDAQ.
The bear case is severe and multi-dimensional. DFDV reported Q1 2026 EPS of -$3.18 on just $2.66M revenue, driven by massive unrealized losses on SOL holdings that make earnings completely unpredictable [1]. Book value is negative (-$0.39/share) and total debt of $120.6M against only $6.3M cash creates a deeply inverted balance sheet. The stock remains 73% below its 52-week high of $18.28, and the 87% correlation to SOL price means shareholders could buy SOL directly with lower fees and zero dilution risk. The authorized $1B securities offering creates persistent dilution overhang that can erode SPS gains even when SOL appreciates. Management tenure averages just 1.2 years, headcount dropped from 20 to 16, Blake Janover's departure ($692.5K severance) raises continuity concerns, and the complete wind-down of all legacy businesses leaves DFDV as a pure-play SOL bet with no revenue diversification and no defensive characteristics in a crypto downturn [4]. The company's Inspire Impact Score of 0 (MIXED) reflects negligible ESG profile with no positive or negative attributions.
