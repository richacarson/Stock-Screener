Company Profile
Industrials ·Aerospace & Defense ·NASDAQ ·United States
T3 Defense Inc. is a federated holding company acquiring and operating small defense and aerospace businesses (Rimon, ITS, Tiltan, Star 26 Capital, Nimbus Drones, Project 35) spanning AI for defense, UAV/counter-UAS systems, 3D mapping/surveillance, and command-and-control solutions. The company is the latest identity of a corporate shell that began as Brilliant Acquisition Corp, became fintech/forex company Nukkleus Inc. (NUKK), and rebranded to T3 Defense (DFNS) on February 9, 2026 [1][3]. Six-month 2026 revenue was $7.6M (versus $0 in the prior-year period, reflecting the pivot from fintech to defense), with a $109.6M net loss attributable to stockholders driven largely by non-cash warrant-liability losses, and the company executed a 1-for-125 reverse split effective July 20, 2026 to regain Nasdaq compliance [1][3].

Investment Thesis
T3 Defense offers speculative exposure to a hot theme — small-cap defense/AI/counter-UAS consolidation — with a stated FY2026 revenue guide of $26M, a disclosed $12.1M backlog, and $12.0M of incoming RFPs across newly-acquired subsidiaries (Rimon, ITS, Tiltan, Nimbus Drones, Project 35) benefiting from rising global defense and counter-drone spending [1]. The core AI-for-defense and UAS positioning sits in a segment structurally immune to AI disruption and benefiting from elevated geopolitical demand. CEO Menny Shalom has converted debt into equity rather than cashing out, which management may frame as alignment, though the mechanism (note exchange from deal financing) is a weak signal of genuine conviction [4].
The bear case dominates here: this is the same corporate shell (via CIK) that has been a SPAC, then blockchain/forex fintech Nukkleus, and is now T3 Defense within roughly 30 months, executing two reverse splits (most recently 1-for-125 in July 2026) and repeated dilutive share issuance — a pattern independent research firm Fugazi Research explicitly compares to the company's prior playbook and calls 'fundamentally uninvestable at any price above zero' [3]. Trailing-twelve-month net loss of $144.95M against a $16.37M market cap, negative operating cash flow of $13.30M, and an Altman Z-Score of -1.68 indicate severe financial distress that a Return on Equity of -275.59% only partially captures. Fugazi Research further alleges goodwill ballooned from $7.6M to $100M in a single quarter through paper-funded acquisitions rather than organic cash flow, and CEO Shalom's divided attention across multiple other companies (Motomova, Hold Me Ltd, Wayerz, Mipso) is flagged in the company's own filings as a conflict-of-interest risk. Backlog-to-revenue visibility is thin (~1.9 quarters against FY2026 guidance) and neither funded-backlog status nor a TTM book-to-bill ratio is disclosed, leaving the revenue growth story dependent on continued acquisitions rather than demonstrated organic demand.
