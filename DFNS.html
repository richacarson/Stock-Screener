T3 Defense Inc. (formerly Nukkleus Inc., rebranded February 2026) is a federated holding company acquiring and operating mission-critical defense businesses embedded in long-cycle U.S. and Israeli national security programs, spanning drones and counter-UAV systems (Project35, Nimbus), AI-driven GPS-denied navigation and simulation (Tiltan), advanced electro-mechanical manufacturing (ITS, Positech), and AI perimeter-security software (Zorronet). Headquartered in New York with significant operations tied to Israel through its portfolio companies.

Investment Thesis
T3 Defense has executed one of the most complete business-model transformations in the current screening batch, exiting a legacy fintech business entirely to assemble a defense-industrial roll-up (Star26, Tiltan, Nimbus, ITS, Positech, and a newly acquired 60% stake in Project35) serving credentialed customers including Israel Aerospace Industries, Rafael, Elbit Systems, and the Israel Ministry of Defense [1][2][3]. The portfolio ships real AI-enabled products — Project35's HY-380 aerial interceptor completed successful live-fire field trials in 2026 using a dual-sensor AI tracking and guidance system — giving the company a maximum AI Resilience score (10/10) as both structurally immune to AI disruption and an active beneficiary of rising demand for AI-enabled autonomous defense systems [1][2]. Management reaffirmed FY2026 revenue guidance of $26M against a $12.1M backlog and $12.0M of incoming RFPs, providing near-term visibility into a business riding a genuine geopolitical tailwind of rising global defense spending [1].
The bear case is severe and centers on going-concern-level financial distress that the mechanical Infrastructure score only partially captures. The stock has fallen roughly 95.7% year-to-date to $0.042, forcing a 1-for-125 reverse stock split effective July 20, 2026 to regain Nasdaq's minimum $1.00 bid-price compliance, and reported workforce fell from 112 employees entering Q2 2026 to as few as 18 by mid-July [4][5]. TTM net loss of $59.44M against just $3.65M of TTM revenue reflects a company still absorbing the costs of a rapid, debt-and-equity-funded acquisition spree, and most Infrastructure metrics (ROE, revenue growth YoY, 5yr CAGR) had to be excluded from this screen entirely as not meaningful given the wholesale business transformation — meaning the score of 5/10 rests on very thin data. The company's Infinite Game evidence is similarly weak: no Just Cause has yet been tested by time, and the reported headcount collapse directly contradicts the trusting-teams standard this framework requires for a higher score.
