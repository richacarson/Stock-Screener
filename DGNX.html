Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NASDAQ ·United Kingdom / Hong Kong
Diginex Limited provides sustainability RegTech software and advisory services, including the diginexESG reporting platform, diginexLUMEN supply-chain risk assessment, diginexAPPRISE worker-voice data collection, and diginexCLIMATE carbon accounting [6]. Since its January 2025 Nasdaq IPO the company has pursued an acquisition-led transformation (Plan A, Matter, The Remedy Project) and in April 2026 agreed an all-share deal valued at ~US$1.5B for customer-engagement AI company Resulticks, amended on August 14, 2026 with targeted completion of October 30, 2026 subject to shareholder, Nasdaq and regulatory approvals [3]. FY2026 (ended March 31, 2026) revenue was $3.6M against a net loss of $25.5M [1][6]. The share price has collapsed from a 52-week high of $196 (split-adjusted) to ~$1.40, a ~$70M market cap [6].

Investment Thesis
The bull case rests on transformation: FY2026 revenue grew 77% to $3.6M with no interest-bearing debt, and if the Resulticks combination closes, management targets roughly $280M of combined revenue by 2027 from an AI-enabled customer-engagement and sustainability-data platform [1][3]. At ~$70M market cap and ~0.3x book, any successful integration would be a large re-rating from a depressed base [6].
The bear case is severe. FY2026 net loss of $25.5M was roughly 7x revenue, TTM net margin is about -1,137%, ROE is -250%, and cash was only $4.9M at year-end, so the business depends on continued equity issuance [1][2][6]. The stock has fallen from a split-adjusted high of $196 to $1.40, law firms are reviewing the Resulticks valuation, and three-month average volume of ~613K shares (~$0.86M/day) breaches IOWN's $1M liquidity floor [4][6].
Key Catalysts
  1. Completion of the ~$1.5B all-share Resulticks combination (targeted October 30, 2026, subject to shareholder, Nasdaq and regulatory approval), which would consolidate an established AI customer-engagement revenue base [3]
  2. Cross-selling Plan A carbon accounting and diginexLUMEN supply-chain tools into EU CSRD/CSDDD compliance demand as acquired businesses integrate [1][7]
  3. Disclosure of FY2027 interim results showing whether acquired revenue is growing organically and whether G&A ($28.5M in FY2026) can be cut [2]
Key Risks
  1. Liquidity and financing: $4.9M of cash against a ~$25M annual net loss implies near-term dilution; average dollar volume of ~$0.86M/day fails IOWN's $1M liquidity floor [1][6]
  2. Deal risk: the Resulticks transaction may not close, and law-firm investigations into the deal valuation plus a change of CEO on completion create governance and litigation overhang [3][4]
  3. Core ESG-reporting software is exposed to AI automation and to regulatory rollback of ESG disclosure mandates, undermining the original product's demand [7]
