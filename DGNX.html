Company Profile
Technology ·ESG & Sustainability Software ·NASDAQ ·Hong Kong
Diginex Limited is an ESG RegTech holding company that provides AI-driven environmental, social, and governance reporting solutions, advisory services, and compliance platforms. Products include diginexESG (cloud-based ESG reporting), diginexLUMEN (supply chain risk assessments), and diginexAPPRISE (multilingual worker condition data collection). Following acquisitions of PlanA.earth, Matter DK, and The Remedy Project, Diginex is consolidating into an integrated sustainability data platform serving banks, asset managers, and corporates. Market cap approximately $33M.
27 Employees ·www.diginex.com

Investment Thesis
Diginex has assembled a differentiated ESG compliance platform through three acquisitions (PlanA.earth, Matter DK, Remedy Project) and is pursuing a transformative $1.5B acquisition of AI-driven enterprise platform Resulticks, which would bring ~$150M revenue with 32% EBITDA margins and ~70% annual growth [2][3]. Revenue momentum is exceptional at 203% TTM growth, the Abu Dhabi Sustainable Finance Declaration and $40M Resulticks reseller agreement signal institutional credibility, and accelerating global ESG mandates (EU CSRD, ISSB IFRS S1/S2) provide substantial regulatory tailwinds for automated compliance infrastructure [2].
The investment case is overwhelmed by structural and execution risks. The $1.5B Resulticks acquisition was structured with shares at $72 versus the current $1.17 stock price — a 98.4% disconnect that renders the deal implausible as originally structured [3]. The standalone company has only 27 employees, $3.57M TTM revenue, and is burning cash at an accelerating rate ($5.81M net loss in H1 2026 alone), creating going-concern risk within 12-18 months without additional capital raises [1]. The stock has declined approximately 99% from its 52-week high of $318.84, and three strategic pivots in five years (blockchain to ESG to AI enterprise data) suggest a company chasing narratives rather than building durable competitive advantages. Competitive displacement from well-capitalized ESG platform incumbents deploying AI at far greater scale remains a persistent threat.
Key Catalysts
  1. Successful closing and integration of the Resulticks acquisition in some restructured form, which could transform Diginex from a $3.57M revenue ESG tool provider into a meaningful enterprise data platform
  2. Revenue realization from the Abu Dhabi Sustainable Finance Declaration partnership and $40M Resulticks reseller agreement, potentially representing transformative recurring revenue relative to the current base
  3. Accelerating global ESG regulatory mandates (EU CSRD, ISSB IFRS S1/S2) driving enterprise demand for automated AI-powered compliance infrastructure
Key Risks
  1. The $1.5B Resulticks acquisition is implausible at the current $1.17 stock price versus the $72/share deal structure — deal collapse or extreme dilutive restructuring would undermine the entire growth thesis
  2. Severe going-concern risk as the company burns $5.81M in H1 2026 alone on $3.57M TTM revenue, with only $3.1M cash remaining and likely need for dilutive capital raises within 12 months
  3. Competitive displacement from MSCI, Workiva, Salesforce, and S&P Global deploying AI-augmented ESG modules into existing enterprise subscriptions at marginal cost, threatening Diginex's 27-employee standalone platform
