Diginex is a sustainability RegTech company that provides end-to-end SaaS solutions for ESG reporting, carbon accounting, supply chain risk assessment, and regulatory compliance across 19 global frameworks. FY2026 marked a transformative year as the company integrated three acquisitions — Plan A (carbon accounting), Matter (ESG data extraction), and The Remedy Project (supply chain transparency) — growing revenue 77% to $3.6M. New CEO Lubomila Jordanova (Plan A founder, appointed January 2026) leads the combined platform. The company remains debt-free with $4.9M in cash but reported a net loss of $31.1M including a $7.0M goodwill impairment on Matter.

Investment Thesis
Diginex at a $49M market cap is a high-risk, early-stage bet on the intersection of sustainability regulation and AI-driven compliance technology — the company has assembled a differentiated platform spanning ESG reporting (19 global frameworks), carbon accounting (Plan A), AI-powered data extraction (Matter, 80% automation), and supply chain transparency (Remedy Project) [1][2]. Revenue grew 77% to $3.6M in FY2026, and the debt-free balance sheet ($4.9M cash, zero interest-bearing debt) provides a clean financial foundation. CEO Jordanova brings authentic sustainability conviction as the founder of Plan A, and regulatory tailwinds (EU CSRD, SEC climate rules, ISSB standards) are driving mandatory ESG reporting for thousands of companies globally, creating a growing addressable market [2][3]. The 2M-share average daily volume provides reasonable liquidity for a micro-cap.
The bear case is severe: Diginex lost $31.1M on $3.6M in revenue in FY2026 — losing $8.64 for every dollar earned — with an adjusted EBITDA loss of $13.0M reflecting operating costs far exceeding revenue even before one-time charges [1]. The $7.0M goodwill impairment on Matter (acquired just months prior) raises serious questions about acquisition integration and due diligence. The stock has declined 96% in the past 52 weeks, destroying shareholder value through what amounts to an equity-funded platform build. The ESG software market is highly competitive, with well-funded rivals (Watershed raised $100M+ from Sequoia, Persefoni backed by major VCs) that have larger sales teams, more customers, and deeper pockets for AI R&D. The company's $4.9M cash balance provides limited runway given the $13M annual adjusted EBITDA loss — a capital raise appears likely within 12 months at severely diluted prices [1]. Revenue growth of 77% is largely acquisition-driven ($1.2M from acquired companies) rather than organic, and the organic growth rate is unclear. ESG regulatory momentum could stall under changing political environments, removing the tailwind. The Inspire Impact Score of 0 (MIXED) reflects limited ESG data available for this small company.
