Investment Thesis
Digi Power X offers leveraged exposure to AI infrastructure demand with a transformational catalyst: the $1.1B 10-year Cerebras colocation agreement provides contracted revenue visibility that dwarfs the company's current $410M market cap [1][2]. First AI revenue is live since May 2026 from NeoCloudz, with $125M cash, zero debt, and $45M already deployed at the Alabama facility. The $35M NVIDIA Vera Rubin commitment (Q1 2027) and $250-300M 2027 revenue target provide a clear execution roadmap. Hans Vestberg's involvement through US Data Centers Inc. adds enterprise credibility, and the ARMS 200 modular Tier III platform is purpose-built for high-density GPU deployments. At ~$410M market cap against $1.1B in contracted backlog, the risk/reward is highly asymmetric if execution succeeds.
The bear case is dominated by execution risk and competitive positioning. Q1 2026 revenue declined 27% to $6.8M as legacy mining winds down, net loss was $4.7M, and the AI business has only weeks of revenue history [1]. The $1.1B Cerebras deal requires building out 40MW of data center capacity — a massive undertaking for a 50-person company that was mining Bitcoin 18 months ago. CoreWeave ($35B+ valuation), Core Scientific, IREN, and Applied Digital all have larger capacity pipelines, deeper operational experience, and more customer diversification [2]. Environmental and community risks are material — the North Tonawanda class action lawsuit, noise violations, and Earthjustice environmental challenge create legal liabilities and reputational damage that could impede future site development [4]. The crypto-to-AI pivot itself raises strategic credibility questions: would this company pivot again if AI economics deteriorated? Market cap of $410M on $6.8M quarterly revenue represents extreme speculation on 2027 execution.
Key Catalysts
  1. $1.1B Cerebras colocation deal revenue ramp — full deployment targeted Q1 2027 with annual contract value of approximately $110M, transforming the revenue profile from $27M legacy to $250-300M target
  2. $35M NVIDIA Vera Rubin platform deployment in Q1 2027 expanding NeoCloudz GPU-as-a-Service capacity, positioning DGXX for next-generation AI compute demand beyond initial Cerebras contract
  3. Additional enterprise colocation contracts — a second anchor tenant beyond Cerebras would validate the platform and materially de-risk the $250-300M 2027 revenue target
Key Risks
  1. Execution risk concentrated in a 50-person company building 40MW of AI data center capacity for the first time — any delay in Cerebras deployment beyond Q1 2027 would damage credibility and burn cash against the $125M position
  2. North Tonawanda class action lawsuit and Earthjustice environmental challenge create legal liabilities, potential remediation costs, and reputational damage that could impede future site development approvals
  3. Single-customer concentration — the $1.1B Cerebras deal dominates the backlog, and any Cerebras operational, financial, or strategic issue (they are pre-IPO) would materially impact DGXX's entire revenue outlook
