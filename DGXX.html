Company Profile
Technology ·Data Center Infrastructure ·NASDAQ ·United States
Digi Power X Inc. (formerly Digihost Technology Inc., renamed March 2025) is an energy infrastructure company developing AI data centers to drive the expansion of energy assets in the United States. The company is pivoting from legacy cryptocurrency mining operations to an AI infrastructure platform built around modular Tier III AI data centers. DGXX has announced $1.1B in contracted AI infrastructure revenue and is developing capacity across Alabama, New York, and North Carolina with approximately 400 MW of planned capacity. Hans Vestberg, former Verizon CEO, serves as Co-Founder and Senior Advisor to US Data Centers, a subsidiary. The company reported Q2 2026 revenue of $6.63M (down 18% YoY) and a net loss of $14.4M.

Investment Thesis
Digi Power X offers asymmetric upside exposure to the AI infrastructure buildout — the company has secured $1.1B in contracted AI infrastructure revenue and is developing 400 MW of AI data center capacity across Alabama, New York, and North Carolina, with Hans Vestberg (former Verizon CEO) as Co-Founder and Senior Advisor to the subsidiary [2]. FY2027 revenue targets of $250-300M represent a potential 8-10x increase from current TTM revenue of $31.4M. The company holds $128M in cash and generated its first AI compute revenue in Q2 2026, marking the beginning of the monetization phase [1]. The $382M market cap prices the stock at a fraction of contracted revenue, suggesting significant upside if execution materializes.
The bear case is dominated by execution risk and financial fragility. Digi Power X is deeply unprofitable with a net loss of $36M on $31.4M TTM revenue, ROE of -24.71%, and profit margin of -114.76% [1]. The $1.1B in contracted revenue is a forward pipeline, not recognized revenue — converting contracts to operational data centers requires massive capital expenditure, successful construction, power procurement, and customer onboarding, all of which face significant execution risk. Legacy crypto mining revenue is declining (-7.61% in FY2025, -18% in Q2 2026), and the AI compute business is not yet generating meaningful revenue. The stock trades at $3.75, down 59% from its 52-week high of $9.20, reflecting market skepticism about execution. The company competes against hyperscalers and established data center operators with vastly more capital, experience, and customer relationships. An overall IOWN score of 35 (SELL) reflects a speculative pre-revenue transformation play with no established track record, no purpose beyond opportunism, and deep financial weakness.
