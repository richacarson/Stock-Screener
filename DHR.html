Company Profile
Healthcare ·Diagnostics & Research ·NYSE ·United States
Danaher Corporation is a global life sciences and diagnostics innovator operating through three segments: Biotechnology (Cytiva, Pall), Life Sciences (Beckman Coulter Life Sciences, Leica Microsystems, SCIEX), and Diagnostics (Beckman Coulter Diagnostics, Cepheid, Radiometer). The company completed the Veralto spinoff in 2023 to become a pure-play life sciences platform. Q2 2026 revenue of $6.3B (+5.5% YoY) with net earnings of $870M (+60% YoY) and raised FY2026 adjusted EPS guidance to $8.45-$8.60. Bioprocessing orders growing mid-teens. Founded in 1984 by Steven and Mitchell Rales, headquartered in Washington, D.C. Market cap of approximately $160B.

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
8
AI Risk Protection (7/9): Danaher's core business — physical life sciences instrumentation, bioprocessing consumables, and diagnostic equipment — is inherently protected from AI substitution [1]. Bioprocessing (Cytiva) requires physical bioreactors, chromatography columns, and filtration systems to manufacture biologic drugs; AI cannot replace the physical production of monoclonal antibodies or cell therapies. Diagnostics (Beckman Coulter, Cepheid) require physical sample processing and regulatory-cleared hardware. The company benefits from installed base consumable pull-through that creates recurring revenue moats. AI could theoretically accelerate drug discovery (reducing the number of experiments needed), but this would reduce reagent consumption only at the margins while expanding the overall addressable market as more therapies reach clinical trials. No analysts have flagged AI as a displacement risk to Danaher's business lines. AI Opportunity (1/1): Danaher has appointed a Chief Technology and AI Officer (Martin Stumpe, formerly Google Cancer Pathology) with a mandate to integrate AI across R&D, drug discovery, and diagnostics [2]. The company's instrumentation generates massive datasets that are valuable for AI training, creating potential for AI-enhanced products and services. Danaher's platforms are tools used in AI-driven drug discovery workflows, making the company an AI enabler.
