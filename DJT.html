Company Profile

Trump Media & Technology Group Corp. operates the Truth Social social media platform, the Truth+ streaming service, and the Truth.Fi financial services brand. The company went public via SPAC merger in March 2024. Headquartered in Sarasota, Florida. Interim CEO Kevin McGurn replaced former CEO Devin Nunes in April 2026. Q2 2026 revenue reached $1.7M (+89% YoY, +92% sequentially) but net loss widened to $238.1M, driven primarily by $116.6M in digital asset losses. TTM revenue of $4.52M against TTM net losses of $1.30B. Pursuing a planned merger with TAE Technologies (fusion energy company) targeting Q4 2026. Also launched Truth API, a paid B2B data feed selling early access to Truth Social posts for $100K/month.

Investment Thesis
Trump Media's Q2 2026 revenue of $1.7M (+89% YoY, +92% sequentially) represents the strongest quarter in company history, driven by barter advertising, Truth+ subscriptions, and Truth.Fi management fees [1]. The Truth API launch ($100K/month B2B data feeds) and TAE Technologies fusion merger (targeting Q4 2026) represent potential catalysts that could expand revenue streams and redefine the company's business model. Donald Trump's ~53% ownership provides alignment between the largest shareholder and the brand's core asset [7]. Interim CEO Kevin McGurn brings media industry experience (former Hulu/Vevo) that could improve Truth+ monetization. D/E of 41.4% is manageable, and the company still holds meaningful cash and digital asset reserves.
The bear case remains overwhelming by fundamental metrics. TTM revenue of $4.52M against an approximately $2B market cap implies a ~440x price-to-sales ratio — extreme overvaluation by any measure [4]. TTM net losses of $1.30B vastly exceed revenue, with Q2 2026 alone losing $238.1M including $116.6M in digital asset losses from a 13% bitcoin decline [1]. The 99.8% miss on 2021 revenue projections ($1.843B projected vs. $3.68M actual for FY2025) destroys management credibility. The Truth API's paid early access to Trump's potentially market-moving posts has drawn a congressional investigation by Ranking Member Raskin and SEC scrutiny — a former SEC official called it potentially running afoul of insider trading laws [2]. The TAE Technologies fusion merger, if completed, transforms TMTG into a capital-intensive energy company with no near-term commercial revenue. The collapsed crypto partnership and $116.6M in digital asset losses expose the company to volatile markets. The stock trades as a political sentiment vehicle rather than a business — a structural risk that no fundamental analysis can mitigate. Key-man dependency on Donald Trump means any political, legal, or health event could trigger catastrophic decline.
