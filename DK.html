Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas Refining & Marketing ·NYSE ·United States
Delek US Holdings is a downstream energy company that operates petroleum refineries, markets fuels, and manages logistics assets. The Refining segment operates four refineries with approximately 302,000 barrels per day of crude oil throughput capacity in Tyler, TX; El Dorado, AR; Big Spring, TX; and Krotz Springs, LA. The Logistics segment operates through Delek Logistics Partners, managing pipelines, storage tanks, and wholesale marketing with expanding Permian gathering and blending services. H1 2026 revenue surged 47.8% to $4.09B with net income of $180.1M driven by improved crack spreads. Headquartered in Brentwood, Tennessee.

Investment Thesis
Delek US has undergone a dramatic financial transformation in 2026 — H1 revenue surged 47.8% to $4.09B, net income reached $180.1M (vs. loss of $90.1M in H1 2025), and EPS of $2.71 (vs. loss of $1.76) reflect the powerful operating leverage in its four-refinery, 302,000 bpd platform when crack spreads are favorable [1][6]. Forward P/E of 10.4x and PEG of 0.38 indicate deep value on a forward basis. Free cash flow of $497M provides a 10% FCF yield on the $5.0B market cap, funding the $1.02 dividend (1.25% yield) and share repurchases. The Big Spring turnaround completion and Logistics expansion into Permian gathering/blending services demonstrate operational execution. Physical refining assets are irreplaceable infrastructure with high barriers to new entry, and DK is structurally immune to AI disruption [6].
Delek US is fundamentally a commodity business with extreme earnings volatility driven by crack spreads outside management's control — FY2025 revenue declined 9.5% and prior-year TTM net profit margin was negative at -0.48% before the current favorable cycle [1][6]. The D/E ratio of 812% reflects dangerous leverage that amplifies both upside and downside; $3.43B in total debt against ~$186M equity and only $629M cash creates significant refinancing risk if margins compress. Glassdoor reviews reveal serious cultural problems — 2.6/5 culture rating, 'run by a few, for a few' mentality, significant executive turnover, and diversity concerns — that undermine organizational stability [3]. The Innovation (1/10) and Inspiration (1/10) scores are among the lowest in the IOWN universe — this is a company with no competitive moat beyond physical assets and no purpose beyond processing crude. The S&P ESG score of 20 (vs. peers at 47-60) reflects lagging sustainability practices [5]. A sustained crack spread downturn below $10/bbl would rapidly compress earnings and test the leveraged balance sheet.
