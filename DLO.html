Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Infrastructure ·NASDAQ ·Uruguay
DLocal is an emerging markets payments technology platform that enables global enterprise merchants to accept and disburse payments in over 40 countries across Africa, Asia, and Latin America. The company processes local payment methods including cards, bank transfers, cash, and alternative payment methods, serving commerce, streaming, ride-hailing, financial services, and gaming industries. Founded in 2016, headquartered in Montevideo, Uruguay.

Investment Thesis
DLocal delivered strong Q1 2026 results with $335.9M revenue (up 55% YoY), TPV surpassing $14B for the first time (up 73% YoY), and record gross profit of $119M [1]. Six consecutive quarters of 50%+ TPV growth demonstrate accelerating network effects across 40+ emerging markets. At forward P/E of 13.5x and PEG of 0.65, DLocal is one of the most attractively valued high-growth fintechs in the market — a fraction of peers like Adyen (40x+) despite superior revenue growth [5]. D/E of just 20%, ROE of 35%, and Russell 2000 inclusion in 2026 provide financial strength and increasing institutional demand.
The bear case centers on margin compression and governance risk. Gross profit margin contracted 4pp to 35% in Q1 2026 as geographic mix shifts toward lower-take-rate regions, and net income growth of only 11% YoY on 55% revenue growth signals that operating leverage is not yet materializing [1]. Recurring governance controversies — Muddy Waters fraud allegations (2022), Argentine government investigation (2023), Hollenden Square money laundering report (2025) — create a permanent credibility discount even though independent investigations found allegations 'not substantiated' [4]. Employee sentiment is weak (Glassdoor 3.1-3.3) with complaints of micromanagement and rigid culture, suggesting organizational growing pains during rapid scaling [3]. Emerging market concentration creates currency, regulatory, and political risks across 40+ jurisdictions that could simultaneously impact multiple revenue streams.
