Company Profile
Real Estate ·REIT - Specialty (Data Centers) ·NYSE ·United States
Digital Realty Trust is a global data center REIT that supports the full spectrum of colocation, interconnection, and hyperscale data center solutions through its PlatformDIGITAL offering. The company owns and operates data centers across more than 50 metros worldwide, connecting enterprises, cloud providers, and hyperscalers to power cloud computing and AI workloads. Headquartered in Austin, Texas, and led by CEO Andy Power.

Investment Thesis
Digital Realty is a structural beneficiary of the AI infrastructure buildout, posting record Q1 2026 leasing bookings of $707M (21% AI-oriented in the 0-1MW segment) and raising full-year 2026 core FFO guidance to $8.00-$8.10 per share alongside revenue guidance of $6.65-6.75B [1]. Revenue grew 16.1% YoY in Q1 2026 with core FFO up 15.3%, and the company's direct liquid cooling rollout and Innovation Lab position it to capture AI-driven rack-density demand that legacy colocation infrastructure cannot serve [1][2]. As essential physical infrastructure for cloud and AI workloads, DLR carries effectively zero AI substitution risk — it is a beneficiary of the same trend that threatens other industries.
The core risk is valuation and capital discipline: at a forward P/E of 82.9 and PEG above 11, the stock is priced for flawless execution of a multi-year AI capacity buildout, and one narrative estimate places fair value as low as $110 versus the ~$174 trading price [4]. GAAP profitability remains volatile (EPS swung from -81% to +254% growth across recent years) and ROE of 5.7% is well below target, reflecting REIT depreciation accounting and reliance on continued capital raises to fund growth [1][2]. The dividend has been flat since late 2021, and rising debt to fund AI-ready capacity expansion, combined with intense hyperscaler concentration risk, could pressure returns if AI capex growth decelerates faster than expected [4].
Key Catalysts
  1. Continued AI-driven hyperscaler leasing following the record $707M Q1 2026 bookings, with 21% of new deals explicitly AI-oriented and potential to accelerate further as inference workloads scale
  2. Direct liquid cooling (DLC) rollout and Innovation Lab (DRIL) capturing next-generation high-density AI rack demand that legacy air-cooled data centers cannot serve
  3. Potential resumption of dividend growth if FFO growth (guided 15%+ in Q1 2026) continues, following roughly four years of a flat payout
Key Risks
  1. Forward P/E of 82.9 and PEG above 11 leave minimal margin of safety; a slowdown in AI capex growth among hyperscaler customers could trigger significant multiple compression
  2. Elevated leverage and continued heavy capital investment to fund AI-ready capacity expansion increase execution and refinancing risk in a higher-for-longer rate environment
  3. GAAP payout ratio of 130% (versus a healthier ~61% on an FFO basis) signals reliance on FFO growth and capital markets access rather than current GAAP earnings to sustain the dividend
