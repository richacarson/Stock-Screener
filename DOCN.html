Investment Thesis
DigitalOcean is now an AI infrastructure growth story with real profitability. Q2 2026 revenue rose ~29% YoY to ~$281M with adjusted EPS of $0.45 against a $0.26 consensus, and Q3 guidance of $304-307M was above the $294M consensus [1]. FY2026 revenue is guided to ~$1.17-1.18B (+30-31%), with growth of at least 35% exiting Q4 [1]. AI customer ARR of $234M (+212%) is mostly inference rather than commoditized bare-metal GPU rental, and the 6,000+ Inference Engine customers extend DigitalOcean's strength with developers into agentic AI [2]. The stock has fallen to $89.79 from a 52-week high of $187.50, so much of the earlier froth is gone, and the Inspire score of 42 (ALIGNED, no negatives) supports faith-aligned ownership [4].
The bear case is valuation, leverage and cash conversion. At 49x forward earnings and ~10x sales, the stock prices in sustained 30%+ growth [4][5]. Capex now absorbs nearly all operating cash flow (TTM P/FCF ~710x), and D/E of 212% leaves little cushion if GPU utilization or pricing weakens [5]. Consensus EPS is expected to decline (yfinance earnings growth -24.5%) as the FY2025 tax benefit laps, and insiders sold ~3.85M shares in Q2 2026 alone [4][6]. The stock fell ~6.5% even after the Q2 beat-and-raise, showing how sensitive it is to any deceleration [1].
Key Catalysts
  1. Q3 2026 results versus $304-307M revenue guidance, and confirmation of the 35%+ exit-rate growth target for Q4 2026 [1]
  2. Inference Engine scaling — 6,000+ customers within months of its April 2026 launch, with inference services growing ~800% YoY, driving higher-margin AI ARR [2]
  3. New commercial leadership (CRO, CMO hired June 2026) moving up-market to larger AI-native customers and raising net revenue retention [2]
Key Risks
  1. GPU capex intensity — near-zero TTM free cash flow means a slowdown in AI demand or GPU price deflation would quickly pressure returns on billions in deployed capacity [5]
  2. Leverage — D/E of 212% raises refinancing risk if the equity multiple keeps compressing from the $187.50 52-week high [4]
  3. Competitive squeeze — hyperscalers and GPU neoclouds compete aggressively on inference pricing, which could erode DigitalOcean's simplicity premium with SMB and developer customers [2]
