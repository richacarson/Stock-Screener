DigitalOcean Holdings, Inc. operates a cloud computing platform designed for developers, startups, and small-to-medium businesses. In April 2026 the company unveiled its AI-Native Cloud platform at the Deploy 2026 conference — the first cloud built end-to-end for the inference and agentic AI era — spanning five layers covering infrastructure, core cloud, inference, data and managed agents. The platform includes an Inference Engine with serverless endpoints and an intelligent model router. DigitalOcean reported approximately $802M in trailing twelve-month revenue with 12.8% YoY growth.

Investment Thesis
DigitalOcean at $131.37 with 42.2% ROE and 12.8% revenue growth represents a high-quality cloud infrastructure company successfully pivoting into the AI era [2]. The April 2026 launch of the AI-Native Cloud platform — the first cloud built end-to-end for inference and agentic workloads — positions DOCN as the go-to AI infrastructure provider for the SMB and developer market that hyperscalers underserve [1]. Production workloads at Higgsfield AI, Hippocratic AI, and others validate early traction. The Inference Engine with intelligent model routing addresses a genuine pain point in agentic AI deployment. With $802M in revenue and accelerating growth, DOCN offers leveraged exposure to AI workload proliferation at a forward PEG of 1.78.
The bear case centers on valuation and competitive intensity. Forward P/E of 43.2x prices in significant AI-driven revenue acceleration that has not yet materialized in reported financials [2]. Profit margin of 13.2% is below cloud industry leaders, and scaling AI inference infrastructure requires substantial capital expenditure that could pressure margins further. AWS, Azure, and GCP all offer inference APIs and model routing — DigitalOcean's differentiation rests on simplicity and SMB focus, which larger competitors could replicate. The company's ~1,100-person workforce is lean for the ambition of building a five-layer AI cloud platform, raising execution risk. Revenue of $802M remains a fraction of hyperscaler cloud businesses, and customer churn in the SMB segment has historically been a structural challenge for DigitalOcean.
