Company Profile
Healthcare ·Health Information Services ·NYSE ·United States
Doximity operates the largest professional network for U.S. physicians and offers clinical workflow tools: secure messaging, the Dialer telehealth app, e-fax, DoxGPT clinical AI search and Doximity Scribe ambient documentation. Most of its revenue comes from marketing and hiring solutions sold to pharmaceutical manufacturers and health systems. FY2026 (March) revenue was $644.9M (+13%) with net income of $196.1M. In fiscal Q1 2027 (June quarter), revenue grew 7% to $156.6M while adjusted EPS fell 19% on heavier AI investment; FY2027 revenue guidance was raised to $671-$681M (~5% growth). Co-founded in 2010 by CEO Jeff Tangney; headquartered in San Francisco; market cap ~$5.8B.

Investment Thesis
Doximity is a debt-free, very high-margin network (88% gross margin, 25% net margin, 17% ROE) whose free physician tools give pharma and health systems the most efficient channel to U.S. prescribers [6][10]. Clinical AI (Scribe, DoxGPT, AI Search) deepens engagement and has started to monetize, and the raised FY2027 revenue guide of $671-$681M shows the core is holding [1].
Growth has slowed sharply: about 5% guided for FY2027 and ~1% for fiscal Q2, with EPS declining on AI spend, as OpenEvidence competes for the same physician attention with an ad-funded AI model [1][5]. Litigation (OpenEvidence suits, securities and derivative cases) adds noise [8]. We rate DOCS WATCH: the stock has fallen from a $67 52-week high to ~$33, but we want evidence that AI Search monetization re-accelerates growth before upgrading [10].
