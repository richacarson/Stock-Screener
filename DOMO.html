Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NASDAQ ·United States
Domo is a cloud-based business intelligence platform providing data integration, visualization, analytics, and AI-powered agent capabilities. The platform connects to hundreds of data sources and enables real-time dashboards, reporting, and AI agent workflows through its MCP Server and Agent Catalyst products. Founded in 2010 by Josh James (who previously founded Omniture, sold to Adobe for $1.8B), Domo went public in 2018 and is currently exploring strategic alternatives to maximize shareholder value.

Investment Thesis
Domo's primary investment thesis rests on a potential acquisition at a premium to the current ~$156M market cap. The board concluded in February 2026 that pursuing a strategic transaction represents the best path to maximize shareholder value, and is in advanced negotiations regarding a potential transaction [6]. At 0.49x Price/Sales with 74.88% gross margins and $437.9M subscription RPO (+8% YoY), the platform could be attractive to a strategic acquirer seeking BI/analytics capabilities enhanced by the new AI agent framework and MCP Server [1][3].
However, independent of an acquisition, the fundamental picture is deeply challenging. Revenue has flatlined at ~$319M for three consecutive fiscal years, GAAP losses continue ($55.5M TTM), and AI-native tools are commoditizing the traditional BI market that generates Domo's revenue [5]. The $148.4M total debt against $39.1M cash creates balance sheet pressure with no visible path to standalone profitability. The ISS Governance QualityScore of 10 (worst possible), dual-class voting control by Josh James, and heavy insider selling (14 of 15 recent transactions) signal significant governance risk [6][9]. The stock has declined 81% from $18.49 to $3.46, reflecting the market's deep skepticism about standalone viability. If the strategic alternatives process fails to produce a transaction, the stock faces meaningful downside risk.
