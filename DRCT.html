Company Profile
Communication Services ·Advertising Technology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Direct Digital Holdings is an end-to-end, AI-powered advertising technology and media solutions provider headquartered in Houston, Texas. The company operates through buy-side (Orange 142, a digital marketing agency) and sell-side advertising segments, serving middle-market and enterprise clients across energy, healthcare, travel, financial services, and other verticals. DRCT reported FY2025 revenue of $34.7M (down 44% YoY), a net loss of $27.7M, and executed a 55-to-1 reverse stock split. The company launched Ignition+, an AI-enabled programmatic media solution, in March 2026 and faces Nasdaq compliance challenges with an Altman Z-Score of -5.28.
