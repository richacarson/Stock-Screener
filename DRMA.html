Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Dermata Therapeutics is a micro-cap company that pivoted from clinical-stage pharmaceutical development to a direct-to-consumer skincare brand under the Tome label. Despite XYNGARI (DMT310) meeting all three Phase 3 co-primary endpoints in the STAR-1 acne trial, the company withdrew its IND and redirected toward consumer skincare. Dermata reported zero revenue through Q2 2026, with the Tome Foundational Treatment commercial launch set for August 25, 2026 [1]. CEO Gerry Proehl (founder, former Santarus CEO who led its $2.6B sale to Salix) leads a team of approximately 25. Market cap is roughly $5M with 4 million shares outstanding. The company carries a going concern warning with $4.4M cash as of June 30, 2026, and a Q2 net loss of $3.0M [1].
