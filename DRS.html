Company Profile
Industrials ·Aerospace & Defense ·NASDAQ ·United States
Leonardo DRS is a defense electronics and systems company providing advanced sensing, network computing, force protection, and electric power and propulsion technologies to the U.S. military and allies. A subsidiary of Italian defense conglomerate Leonardo S.p.A., the company went public in 2022. New CEO John Baylouny took over in January 2026 after William Lynn's 14-year tenure. Q2 2026 revenue reached $913M (+10% YoY) with 33% EBITDA growth, led by tactical radars, infrared sensing, electric power/propulsion, and force protection programs. FY2026 organic revenue growth guided to 7-9%.

Investment Thesis
Leonardo DRS at 25.64x forward earnings offers exposure to the accelerating defense modernization cycle with a technology-focused portfolio positioned at the intersection of AI, autonomous systems, and next-generation military hardware. Q2 2026 revenue grew 10% YoY to $913M with 33% EBITDA growth, demonstrating improving profitability on a nearly unlevered balance sheet (D/E 9.53%) [2][4]. The SAGEcore AI platform and next-gen AI processor position DRS as a key enabler of defense AI adoption — a secular growth driver as militaries worldwide invest in counter-UAS, electronic warfare, and autonomous systems [3]. FY2026 guidance raised across all metrics with 7-9% organic revenue growth and improving margins, supported by strong demand across tactical radars, infrared sensing, and electric power/propulsion programs.
The bear case centers on valuation, margin profile, and parent company control. Forward P/E of 25.64x prices in significant growth expectations for a company with only 11.92% ROE and 8.52% profit margins — well below defense primes like Lockheed (14%+ margins) and Northrop (12%+) [4]. As a ~72% subsidiary of Leonardo S.p.A., DRS's strategic autonomy is constrained, and the parent's capital allocation priorities may not always align with DRS minority shareholders' interests. The CEO transition to John Baylouny (January 2026) introduces execution risk during the company's most important technology-investment cycle. Revenue concentration in U.S. defense creates exposure to potential defense budget negotiations and government shutdown risks. The stock has declined ~9% from its 50-day SMA of $41.44 to $37.62, suggesting near-term investor skepticism about the growth trajectory.
Key Catalysts
  1. SAGEcore AI platform and next-gen AI processor adoption across multiple defense programs could accelerate revenue growth beyond the 7-9% organic guidance as military AI spending scales globally [3]
  2. EBITDA margins expanding 33% YoY in Q2 2026 on 10% revenue growth — operating leverage on the existing technology platform could drive significant earnings acceleration as revenue scales [1][2]
  3. Global defense spending increases driven by geopolitical tensions (Ukraine, Taiwan Strait, Middle East) expanding Leonardo DRS's addressable market for tactical radars, force protection, and electric power systems
Key Risks
  1. Leonardo S.p.A. parent company control (~72% ownership) constrains strategic autonomy and creates potential minority shareholder misalignment — parent could redirect technology investments or limit capital returns to public shareholders
  2. CEO transition from 14-year veteran William Lynn to John Baylouny in January 2026 introduces execution risk during critical technology investment cycle — new CEO's strategic priorities have not yet been fully articulated [3]
  3. Forward P/E of 25.64x requires sustained high-single-digit revenue growth and margin expansion to justify — any deceleration in defense AI spending or program delays could trigger multiple compression from current premium valuation [4]
