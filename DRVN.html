Company Profile
Consumer Cyclical ·Auto & Truck Dealerships ·NASDAQ ·United States
Driven Brands is the largest automotive services company in North America, operating over 5,100 locations across 13 countries under brands including Take 5 Oil Change, Meineke, MAACO, CARSTAR, and Auto Glass Now. The company provides paint, collision, glass, repair, oil change, and maintenance services through franchise, company-operated, and independently-operated locations. Q1 2026 revenue was $484.4M (+8% YoY) with adjusted EPS of $0.30 beating expectations. The company faces ongoing securities fraud litigation related to 2023-2025 financial restatements.

Investment Thesis
Driven Brands is the largest automotive services platform in North America with 5,100+ locations, and Q1 2026 demonstrated the underlying business is executing: revenue grew 8% to $484.4M, adjusted EPS of $0.30 beat expectations by $0.02, and Take 5 Oil Change delivered 4.5% same-store sales growth with 5% store count expansion [1]. The non-discretionary nature of vehicle maintenance (12.6-year average US vehicle age), asset-light franchise model, and deeply discounted Forward P/E of 11.79x with estimated PEG of 0.65 offer compelling value if the company can resolve its governance issues [5]. The company is actively deleveraging, reducing net leverage from 3.5x to 3.2x with a year-end 3.0x target, and guiding for full-year revenue of $1.95-$2.05B with adjusted EBITDA of $430-$460M [1]. AI immunity (score 8) provides structural resilience.
The bear case is substantial and centers on governance failure. Driven Brands disclosed in February 2026 that it must restate financial statements for FY2023-2024 and quarterly results through September 2025 due to pervasive accounting errors including revenue overstatements, expense understatements, and unreconciled cash balances — the stock dropped approximately 40% on the disclosure [4]. Multiple securities fraud class action lawsuits have been filed covering the May 2023 to February 2026 period, creating material litigation liability. D/E of 347% with $2.76B total debt against $162M cash represents dangerously high leverage, and GAAP metrics remain ugly: ROE of -27.3% and profit margin of -8.12% from impairment charges and restatement-related costs [5]. The Inspire score of just 17 (MIXED) with only 'Hiring Ethics' as a positive attribution reflects minimal faith-alignment appeal. The departing CEO's $3.4M stock sale and ongoing insider selling undermine confidence. Until restated financials are filed and SEC scrutiny concludes, fundamental uncertainty about the true financial picture persists.
