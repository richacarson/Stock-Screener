Company Profile
Technology ·Education Technology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Duolingo is the world's most popular language learning platform with 56.5 million daily active users and 137.8 million monthly active users as of Q1 2026. Founded by Luis von Ahn (co-creator of CAPTCHA and reCAPTCHA), the company operates a freemium model offering language courses in 40+ languages, plus Duolingo Math, Duolingo Music, and Duolingo ABC. The company crossed $1B in annual revenue for the first time in 2025, growing 38.7% YoY, and reported Q1 2026 revenue of $292M (+27% YoY) with 12.5M paid subscribers (+21%). Duolingo has never laid off a full-time employee in its history. The company is deliberately prioritizing DAU growth in 2026, targeting 100M DAU by 2028.

Investment Thesis
Duolingo at 18.42x trailing earnings (58.71x forward) is a mission-driven growth compounder with exceptional unit economics deliberately sacrificing near-term earnings for long-term user growth. Revenue grew 38.7% in 2025 to cross $1B for the first time, and Q1 2026 continued at 27% growth with $292M revenue, while free cash flow margin reached 50.6% ($147.8M) and gross margin expanded to 73% as AI costs declined [1]. The platform's 56.5M DAU (+21%), 12.5M paid subscribers (+21%), and target of 100M DAU by 2028 represent a massive user growth flywheel. D/E of just 6%, ROE of 34.42%, and profit margin of 35.87% reflect best-in-class financial health. Innovation score of 9 and Inspiration score of 7 — among the highest in the IOWN universe — recognize Duolingo's rare combination of genuine purpose, AI-native execution, and category-defining market position. The company has never laid off an employee in 17 years.
The bear case centers on severe AI disruption risk, extreme valuation compression, and the deliberate earnings sacrifice. The stock declined approximately 70-80% from its May 2025 peak of $540 to current levels, with the market explicitly pricing in AI substitution risk — AI chatbots can teach languages conversationally, and AI translation tools reduce the necessity of language learning [2][3]. Forward P/E of 58.71x reflects a forward EPS of only ~$2.63 versus trailing $8.39, as the company absorbs $50M+ in foregone bookings in 2026. The 3-year EPS growth forecast of -9.51% means the company is intentionally shrinking near-term profitability. Full-year 2026 revenue guidance of $1.205B represents just 16% growth — a sharp deceleration from 2025's 38.7%. Revenue composition risk exists as Duolingo English Test (-6%) and in-app purchases (-11%) declined in Q1 2026, concentrating growth in subscriptions [1]. The Inspire Impact Score of -49 (MISALIGNED) may limit allocation for faith-based investors. An AI Resilience score of 5 (MODERATE) — the lowest dimension score — reflects the genuine existential question of whether AI tutors will eventually erode Duolingo's value proposition despite the company's own aggressive AI adoption.
