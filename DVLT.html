Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Infrastructure ·NASDAQ ·United States
Datavault AI Inc., formerly WiSA Technologies, is a data sciences technology company focused on AI-driven data valuation, monetization, credentialing, digital engagement, and real-world asset (RWA) tokenization. The company operates through two divisions: a Data Sciences Division offering DataValue, DataScore, and Information Data Exchange platforms, and an Acoustic Sciences Division (WiSA, ADIO, Sumerian wireless audio technologies) being evaluated for a potential spin-off. CEO Nathaniel Bradley took the helm in December 2024 following the company's acquisition of Data Vault Holdings assets. FY2025 revenue reached $39.1M (+1,362% YoY), with Q4 2025 marking the company's first profitable quarter, though FY2025 net loss was -$79.0M. Market cap approximately $262M.

Investment Thesis
Datavault AI represents a high-risk speculative bet on the convergence of AI-driven data monetization and RWA tokenization. The bull case rests on explosive revenue growth — FY2025 revenue of $39.1M (+1,362% YoY) with 78% gross margins demonstrated demand for the DataValue and DataScore platforms, and Q4 2025 delivered the company's first-ever profitable quarter ($4.2M operating profit) [1]. Management's $200M FY2026 revenue target implies ~400% growth, the forward P/E of 7.66 suggests analysts believe profitability is achievable at scale, and the minimal D/E of 4.6% with $237.97M shareholders' equity provides a capital base for expansion [2]. The potential Acoustic Sciences spin-off could unlock value in the WiSA patent portfolio [4].
The bear case is severe and multifaceted. The company lost $79.0M on $39.1M revenue in FY2025 (profit margin -202%), and Q1 2026 losses of -$53.1M suggest the Q4 profitability was anomalous rather than a turning point [1][2]. Cash reserves of only $2.0M against -$24.0M annual FCF burn create existential liquidity risk absent continued equity dilution (855M shares already outstanding). The $200M FY2026 revenue target requires a 5x scale-up from a company that has never sustained profitability. FY2025 revenue was concentrated in Q4 ($33.8M of $39.1M total), raising questions about revenue recognition quality and sustainability. The stock has declined from $4.10 to $0.31 over the past year, a 92% drawdown reflecting deep market skepticism [2]. The data monetization and RWA tokenization markets are nascent and fiercely competitive, with well-funded AI platforms capable of replicating Datavault's tools.
