Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Silver ·NYSE American ·Canada
Dolly Varden Silver Corporation is a mineral exploration company focused on advancing its 100% held Kitsault Valley Project in the Golden Triangle of British Columbia, Canada. The project hosts a combination of high-grade silver and gold deposits, including the historic Dolly Varden, Torbit, and Homestake Silver mines. Under CEO Shawn Khunkhun (since 2020), the company grew market cap from $20M to ~$260M, raised over $185M in equity, and completed a 56,131-meter drill program in 2025. Dolly Varden shareholders approved a merger of equals with Contango ORE Inc. in March 2026. The company is pre-revenue with $44.6M cash and no debt.
