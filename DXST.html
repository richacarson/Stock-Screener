Company Profile
Industrials ·Environmental Services ·NASDAQ ·China
Decent Holding Inc. provides industrial wastewater treatment services, ecological river restoration, and microbial products for water quality enhancement in China. The company expanded into AI-powered digital health through its Suncare senior care platform, reaching approximately 480 community service locations and 150,000 paid members by June 2026. Founded in 2022, headquartered in Yantai, China. IPO January 2025. Market cap approximately $5.6M. Completed a 1-for-25 reverse stock split in March 2026. The Schall Law Firm announced a securities fraud investigation in April 2026.
