Company Profile
Financial Services ·Investment Management ·NYSE ·United States
Destiny Tech100 Inc. is a non-diversified, closed-end management investment company that gives public market investors indirect access to private, venture-backed technology companies before they IPO. As of March 31, 2026 its largest disclosed positions were Anthropic (18.1%), SpaceX (14.5%), OpenAI (5.8%), and Databricks (2.5%). Externally managed, the fund began trading on the NYSE in March 2024 and transitioned its investment adviser from Destiny Advisors LLC to DX Advisors LLC effective April 29, 2026.

Investment Thesis
DXYZ offers rare, exchange-listed retail exposure to some of the most sought-after private AI and space companies — Anthropic (18.1%), SpaceX (14.5%), OpenAI (5.8%), and Databricks (2.5%) — assets that are otherwise inaccessible to non-accredited investors [1]. A February 2026 SPV top-up added further Anthropic exposure, and potential SpaceX or Databricks IPOs in 2026 could crystallize public valuations for the fund's largest holdings, which is the primary bull case for owning the wrapper today rather than waiting for a listing [1][3].
The bear case dominates. DXYZ is not an operating business — it is a fee-extracting access vehicle with a documented history of trading at extreme (formerly 2,000%+) premiums to its own disclosed NAV, and while that premium has compressed materially by August 2026, the fund's own website NAV of $24.56 is explicitly described as using stale valuations, meaning investors still cannot reliably know what they are paying for [3]. A 2.50% annual fee on gross assets, an active at-the-market share-sale program that dilutes existing holders and has been cited as pressuring the share price, and an April 2026 adviser transition from Destiny Advisors LLC to DX Advisors LLC add governance and cost uncertainty on top of the inherent illiquidity and opacity of the underlying private holdings [2][3]. With no operating revenue, no employees, and no scoreable financial infrastructure, this is a speculative wrapper on volatile private marks, not an IOWN-aligned compounder.
Key Catalysts
  1. A SpaceX, Databricks, or OpenAI IPO in 2026 would convert DXYZ's largest illiquid private holdings into transparently priced public equity, removing valuation uncertainty
  2. Continued markups of Anthropic (18.1% of portfolio, recently expanded via SPV) as the AI investment cycle continues to push private AI valuations higher
  3. Further compression of the historical NAV premium toward parity could make the fund a fairer-priced proxy than in 2024-2025, if the manager curbs additional dilutive at-the-market issuance
Key Risks
  1. No operating revenue or scoreable financial infrastructure — DXYZ's value is entirely dependent on subjective, infrequently updated private-company marks that can move sharply on sentiment alone
  2. Ongoing at-the-market share issuance dilutes existing holders and has been directly cited by analysts as pressuring the stock, while a 2.50% asset-based management fee compounds against shareholder returns every year
  3. Governance uncertainty from the April 2026 adviser transition (Destiny Advisors LLC to DX Advisors LLC) and a persistent gap between disclosed NAV and market price make it difficult to know what investors are actually paying for
