Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Dyne Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biotechnology company focused on developing targeted therapeutics for patients with genetically driven neuromuscular diseases. The company's proprietary FORCE platform enables targeted delivery of oligonucleotide therapeutics to muscle and the central nervous system. Lead candidates include z-rostudirsen (DYN-251) for myotonic dystrophy type 1 (DM1) with a BLA submission targeted for Q2 2026 and potential launch in Q1 2027, and z-basivarsen for facioscapulohumeral muscular dystrophy (FSHD) advancing toward Phase 3. CEO John Cox joined in March 2024 from Bioverativ (Sanofi). Founded in 2018, headquartered in Waltham, Massachusetts.

Competition from Sarepta's established DMD franchise, Avidity Biosciences, and potential gene therapy entrants creates commercial risk even upon approval.
