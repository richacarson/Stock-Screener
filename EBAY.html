Investment Thesis
eBay's Q2 2026 results demolished the bear thesis of growth deceleration — GMV and revenue both surged 14% (vs. Q2 guidance of 8-10%), with strategic priorities driving 70%+ of GMV and each growing 20%+ [1]. U.S. GMV leaped 24%, validating the focused categories strategy domestically. Non-GAAP operating income grew 16% to $893M with gross margins expanding to 74.1%. Full-year guidance was raised to 11-12% FX-neutral revenue growth, signaling durable acceleration. At 18.2x forward earnings, eBay trades at a meaningful discount to e-commerce peers despite accelerating growth, 42.9% ROE, and 71.8% gross margins [6]. The completed Depop acquisition (July 2026) adds Gen Z exposure, the AI Activate programme with OpenAI enhances seller productivity, and the $1.24/share dividend (1.11% yield) plus aggressive buybacks return significant capital to shareholders.
The primary risk is whether Q2's 14% growth represents a new baseline or a peak before lapping dynamics normalize performance. Management itself guides Q3 deceleration due to lapping the Klarna partnership and prior marketing gains, with Depop adding 3-4 points of operating income headwind [1]. The 5-year revenue CAGR of approximately 2% reminds that eBay has been essentially flat over a multi-year period, and the current upturn could prove cyclical rather than structural. Agentic AI commerce from Perplexity, OpenAI, and Google poses a structural threat to marketplace intermediation — eBay's proactive banning of AI shopping agents is a defensive move that acknowledges the company cannot compete with autonomous commerce agents [2]. D/E of 163% from aggressive buybacks creates balance sheet risk if marketplace economics deteriorate. The Inspire Impact Score of -100 (MISALIGNED) eliminates allocation for faith-based mandates, and CEO Iannone's $8.4M insider selling in April-May 2026 does not inspire confidence despite his retained $57M position [5].

  2. Agentic AI commerce from Perplexity, OpenAI, and Google threatens marketplace intermediation by enabling autonomous cross-platform shopping — eBay's defensive banning of AI agents may prove insufficient if the industry standard shifts toward AI-mediated commerce [2]

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience MODERATE

AI Risk Protection (5/9): eBay faces real but manageable AI disruption risk. Agentic commerce — where AI shopping agents autonomously search, compare, and purchase across platforms — threatens eBay's marketplace intermediation model, and eBay's proactive banning of autonomous AI agents in August 2026 acknowledges this threat directly [2]. However, Q2 2026 results demonstrate meaningful defensive moats: strategic priorities (collectibles, luxury, motors, sneakers) now drive 70%+ of GMV, each growing 20%+, and this unique, non-commoditized inventory is structurally harder for AI agents to intermediate than standardized products [1]. U.S. GMV surging 24% suggests eBay's focused category strategy creates defensibility that AI cannot easily replicate. Physical goods still require shipping, trust, and authentication. The main vulnerability remains commodity-adjacent categories where AI shopping tools could bypass eBay entirely. AI Opportunity (1/1): eBay generates meaningful AI-embedded value through AI-powered listing tools, smart search with personalized recommendations, the AI Activate programme with OpenAI for small sellers, and AI-driven authentication and fraud detection [2]. These tools are shipping, measurably improving the platform, and contributing to the 14% GMV growth acceleration.
