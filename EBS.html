Company Profile
Healthcare ·Drug Manufacturers - Specialty & Generic ·NYSE ·United States
Emergent BioSolutions is a life sciences company focused on providing medical countermeasures (MCM) to address chemical, biological, radiological, and nuclear (CBRN) threats, as well as emerging infectious diseases and opioid overdose. Key products include CYFENDUS and BioThrax (anthrax vaccines), ACAM2000 (smallpox vaccine), and NARCAN nasal spray (naloxone for opioid overdose). H1 2026 revenue was $390M (up 7.4% YoY), but full-year guidance was revised down to $645-675M due to NARCAN pricing/volume pressure. The company took a $191M non-cash NARCAN intangible asset impairment in Q2 2026.

Investment Thesis
Emergent BioSolutions at 6.3x forward earnings with a $338M market cap offers deep-value exposure to U.S. government biodefense spending, which benefits from bipartisan support and rising geopolitical threat awareness [1][4]. Q2 2026 revenue surged 66% YoY to $234.3M driven by accelerated MCM deliveries, and Q1 2026 revenue of $156.1M exceeded guidance with a positive EPS of $0.07, suggesting operational stabilization [1]. The company's sole-source anthrax vaccine position (CYFENDUS/BioThrax), ACAM2000 smallpox vaccine, and broad MCM portfolio provide durable government revenue streams with high switching costs. The Inspire Impact Score of 29 (ALIGNED) with 15 positive ESG attributions and zero negative marks reflects the company's public health mission.
The bear case centers on deteriorating financials and franchise erosion. Full-year 2026 guidance was cut to $645-675M from $720-760M, driven by sustained pricing and volume pressure in the NARCAN commercial business, which also triggered a $191M non-cash intangible asset impairment [1]. Profit margin of -23.0% and ROE of -40.3% demonstrate the company is not currently profitable, with D/E of 172.3% adding balance sheet strain [4]. The COVID-era manufacturing quality failures at Bayview led to FDA Warning Letters and destroyed hundreds of millions in vaccine doses, permanently damaging the company's reputation as a reliable manufacturer [3]. Generic naloxone competition from Teva and Hikma is structurally eroding NARCAN's pricing power and market share. The company's dependence on lumpy U.S. government procurement cycles creates revenue volatility, as the 30% Q1 YoY revenue decline and 66% Q2 surge demonstrate [1].
