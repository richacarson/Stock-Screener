Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas Integrated ·NYSE ·Colombia
Ecopetrol S.A. is Colombia's majority state-owned integrated oil and gas company, founded in 1948, operating Exploration & Production, Transportation & Logistics (pipelines) and Refining & Petrochemicals segments; its ADRs trade on the NYSE [7]. With Brent averaging ~$97/bbl, Q2 2026 revenue rose 35.5% YoY to COP 40.2T, EBITDA reached COP 17.7T (44% margin) and net income attributable to owners more than tripled to COP 6.1T [1]. Leadership has been unstable: President Ricardo Roa took leave in April 2026, acting president Juan Carlos Hurtado retired on September 15, 2026, and CFO Alfonso Camilo Barco is now acting CEO [3]. Q3 2026 results are due November 10, 2026 [2]. Market cap ~$35B.

Investment Thesis
Ecopetrol is a cash machine when oil is high: Q2 2026 revenue rose 35.5% to COP 40.2T, EBITDA hit COP 17.7T at a 44% margin and net income attributable to owners jumped to COP 6.1T from COP 1.8T a year earlier, with Brent averaging ~$97/bbl [1]. The ADR trades at 8.5x forward earnings and ~4.5x EV/EBITDA, yields ~3.8% with a ~52% payout, and generates ROE near 16% [7].
The bear case is political and commodity risk. FY2025 revenue fell 16% to COP 111.5T and net income to COP 8.4T as oil weakened, showing how quickly earnings reverse; S&P cut the company to BB-, and D/E of ~96% (123% on FMP's measure) limits flexibility [3][7]. Governance is fragile — three leaders in 2026 — and the 'Iguana Papers' allegations of concealed contamination expose the company to regulatory and SEC scrutiny [3][5]. The stock has already nearly doubled from its $9.10 52-week low [7].
Key Catalysts
  1. Q3 2026 results on November 10, 2026, which should again reflect elevated Brent (~$97/bbl in Q2) and a large YoY earnings increase [1][2]
  2. Appointment of a permanent CEO after the 2026 presidential selection process, potentially stabilizing governance [3]
  3. Reopened technical discussion of unconventional (fracking) development and asset reviews that could expand reserves life [4]
Key Risks
  1. Oil-price reversal: FY2025 showed revenue falling 16% and net income to COP 8.4T when crude weakened; Q2 2026 gains are price-driven [1][7]
  2. Governance and political interference — three leaders in 2026, majority state ownership and an S&P downgrade to BB- [3]
  3. Environmental and legal exposure from the EIA/Earthworks 'Crude Lies' report alleging concealed contamination and a whistleblower request for SEC investigation [5]
