Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas Integrated ·NYSE ·Colombia
Ecopetrol S.A. is the largest integrated energy company in Colombia and one of the principal integrated energy companies in the Americas. The company is responsible for more than 60% of Colombia's hydrocarbon production and operates across exploration, production, transportation, refining, and marketing of oil, gas, and petrochemical products. Majority state-owned (88.5% by the Colombian government), Ecopetrol produced 725,000 boe/d in Q1 2026 and operates the Barrancabermeja and Cartagena refineries. Market cap approximately $30B.
18,903 Employees ·www.ecopetrol.com.co

Investment Thesis
Ecopetrol offers attractive valuation at 9.12x forward earnings with a 4.42% dividend yield, backed by Colombia's dominant energy infrastructure position producing 725,000 boe/d [1][3]. Q1 2026 showed operational improvement with EBITDA margin expanding to 47%, refining margins surging 60% YoY to $17.3/boe, and downstream EBITDA contribution rising from 4% to 14%, demonstrating effective cost execution and business mix diversification [1]. The payout ratio has moderated to 51%, providing better dividend sustainability than the prior year's 96% level. Capex guidance of $5.4-6.7B with execution trending toward the high end signals confidence in the production outlook, and the Copoazu-1 offshore gas discovery adds reserve visibility.
The primary risks are structural. Revenue declined 8.7% in Q1 2026, earnings fell 7.7%, and 5-year dividend growth CAGR is -15%, reflecting an industry facing secular headwinds from energy transition alongside commodity price cyclicality [1][4]. The Colombian government's 88.5% ownership stake creates political risk around capital allocation — the government depends on Ecopetrol dividends as fiscal revenue, constraining the company's ability to reinvest for long-term competitiveness. Sustainalytics rates Ecopetrol's ESG controversy level as 'Significant,' and the 8% energy transition capex allocation is modest relative to the magnitude of the long-term energy transition challenge. At current levels, Ecopetrol offers income value but lacks the growth profile and strategic flexibility for a BUY recommendation.
