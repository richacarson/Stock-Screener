Company Profile
Healthcare ·Medical Devices ·NASDAQ ·France
EDAP TMS is a French medical technology company and the global leader in High-Intensity Focused Ultrasound (HIFU) technology for the non-invasive treatment of prostate cancer. The company's flagship Focal One robotic HIFU platform enables urologists to perform tissue-sparing focal therapy guided by real-time MRI-ultrasound fusion imaging. Founded in 1979 and headquartered in Vaulx-en-Velin, France, EDAP has strategically pivoted from legacy extracorporeal shockwave lithotripsy (ESWL) to focus entirely on HIFU, divesting non-core businesses. The stock now trades under ticker FOCL on NASDAQ. Revenue TTM is $79.3M with Q1 2026 record HIFU revenue of $11.6M (+78% YoY) and 2026 guidance of $72-80M total revenue.
