Company Profile
Healthcare ·Medical Devices ·NASDAQ ·France
EDAP TMS S.A., rebranded as FocalTherics and trading under ticker FOCL since June 2026, is a medical technology company that develops, manufactures, and markets the Focal One robotic HIFU (High-Intensity Focused Ultrasound) system for non-invasive treatment of prostate cancer. In May 2026, the company divested its legacy ESWL lithotripsy and Distribution segments to become a pure-play focal therapy company. H1 2026 revenue from continuing operations was $24.8M, up 54.9% YoY, with record Q2 HIFU system sales of 13 units generating $13.2M revenue. Full-year 2026 HIFU revenue guidance is $50-54M, representing 34-45% growth. The company remains unprofitable with a Q2 2026 net loss of $14.4M from continuing operations. CEO Ryan Rhodes leads from Vaulx-en-Velin, France.
289 Employees ·investor.focalone.com

Investment Thesis
EDAP TMS (FocalTherics) at $4.30 is a high-conviction, high-risk bet on robotic focal therapy becoming the standard of care for localized prostate cancer. The Focal One HIFU system offers a compelling clinical value proposition — non-invasive, organ-preserving treatment with significantly fewer side effects than radical prostatectomy — and the company's H1 2026 revenue growth of 54.9% from continuing operations with record Q2 system sales of 13 units validates accelerating market adoption [1]. The strategic pivot to pure-play focal therapy, the Telix Pharmaceuticals collaboration on PSMA PET imaging integration, and the FDA clearance of Focal One i with AI-driven algorithm support create a focused growth platform with a clear clinical mission [2]. Full-year HIFU guidance of $50-54M represents 34-45% growth.
The bear case is existential for a $203M market cap company burning cash at a significant rate. Q2 2026 net loss from continuing operations was $14.4M against just $13.2M in revenue, and the company expects continued operating losses for the foreseeable future [1]. The stock fails IOWN's liquidity floor with daily dollar volume of only $493K, making it impractical to build or exit positions without market impact. D/E has risen to ~143% as the company took on debt to fund growth, and equity has deteriorated from $76.8M (2022) to $19.4M (2025) due to accumulated losses. The HIFU market remains niche — prostate cancer treatment is dominated by active surveillance, surgery, and radiation, and Focal One must win adoption urologist by urologist. Revenue concentration in a single product platform creates zero margin for execution errors. The company missed Q2 2026 consensus revenue by 8.58%, raising questions about the durability of the growth trajectory.
