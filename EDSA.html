Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NCM ·Canada
Edesa Biotech is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing therapies for inflammatory and immune-related diseases. The pipeline includes EB05 (paridiprubart), a monoclonal anti-TLR4 antibody in Phase 3 for acute respiratory distress syndrome; EB01, a topical non-steroidal cream that completed Phase 3 for chronic allergic contact dermatitis; and EB07, a monoclonal antibody in Phase 2 for pulmonary fibrosis. Founded in 2015 and headquartered in Markham, Canada, Edesa has 17 employees, zero revenue, a market cap of $38M, and approximately $10.3M in cash with $8.9M annual operating cash burn [1].
