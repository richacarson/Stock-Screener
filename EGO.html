Company Profile
Materials ·Gold Mining ·NYSE ·Canada
Eldorado Gold is a Canadian-based mid-tier gold mining company with operations and development projects in Canada, Greece, and Turkey. Key assets include the Lamaque mine (Quebec), Kisladag mine (Turkey), Olympias mine (Greece), and the Skouries copper-gold project (Greece) nearing commercial production in Q4 2026. The company also operates McIlvenna Bay, a copper-zinc mine in Saskatchewan producing its first concentrates. Q1 2026 revenue surged to $532M (+50% YoY) driven by an average realized gold price of $4,891/oz, with net earnings of $136M ($0.69/share). FY2025 annual revenue was $1.8B (+37.5% YoY).

Investment Thesis
Eldorado Gold at 7.30x forward earnings is one of the cheapest growth stories in the gold mining sector — FY2025 revenue grew 37.5% to $1.8B, Q1 2026 revenue surged 50% to $532M on $4,891/oz realized gold prices, and EPS nearly doubled to $0.69/share from $0.35 a year ago [1]. The balance sheet is fortress-grade with $630M cash and D/E of just 25.7%, providing both defensive resilience and offensive optionality. Two major growth catalysts are imminent: Skouries (copper-gold) reaches commercial production in Q4 2026 after a decade of development, adding 140,000+ oz/year gold equivalent, and McIlvenna Bay is producing first copper-zinc concentrates, diversifying the revenue base beyond gold [1]. At PEG 0.61, the market is pricing in minimal growth despite 490,000 oz annual gold production guidance with line-of-sight expansion to 600,000+ oz. Profit margin of 30.36% reflects the operating leverage of gold mining at current prices.
The bear case centers on gold price sensitivity, geopolitical concentration risk, and limited innovation. Eldorado's financial performance is overwhelmingly driven by the gold price — at $4,891/oz realized in Q1 2026, margins are exceptional, but a reversion to $2,000-2,500/oz would compress profit margins dramatically and potentially render Skouries economics less attractive than projected [1]. Geographic risk remains elevated: Greece (Skouries, Olympias) has a documented history of permit revocations, community opposition, and government confrontation, and Turkey (Kisladag) carries political and currency risk [3]. The CEO transition with George Burns retiring September 30, 2026 introduces leadership uncertainty during a critical growth phase (Skouries commissioning). Innovation and Inspiration scores are low (3/10 and 1/10) reflecting that mining is a physical extraction business with limited differentiation — Eldorado's purpose begins and ends with extracting resources profitably. The Inspire Impact Score of 0 (MIXED) is neutral for faith-based investors, with no positive or negative attributions identified. ROE of 11.28% below the 15% target reflects the capital-intensive nature of mining that limits return on equity even in high-margin environments.
Key Catalysts
  1. Skouries copper-gold project reaching commercial production in Q4 2026 after a decade of development, adding 140,000+ oz/year gold equivalent production and diversifying revenue with significant copper byproduct credits [1]
  2. Gold price environment above $4,800/oz driving exceptional margins — Q1 2026 realized $4,891/oz with 30%+ profit margins, and every $100/oz increase translates to approximately $50M in additional annual free cash flow at current production levels [1]

  2. Greek operational risk remains elevated despite Skouries progress — the decade of permit revocations, community protests (10,000+ in Thessaloniki), and government confrontations demonstrates a jurisdiction where political risk can stall projects for years [3]
  3. CEO George Burns retiring September 30, 2026 during the most critical operational period (Skouries commissioning, McIlvenna Bay ramp-up) introduces leadership transition risk, with new management untested through a growth execution phase [2]
