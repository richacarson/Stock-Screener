E-Home Household Service Holdings Limited is a China-based integrated home services provider operating through four segments: Appliance Installation and Maintenance, Housekeeping Services, Senior Care Services, and Educational Consulting Services. Under Chairman and CEO Wenshan Xie, the company has aggressively expanded into AI-enabled services — launching AI intelligent customer service, AI robotic cleaning equipment, and acquiring an AI access control and smart community services company (Fuzhou Yunding). Despite $263.7M in cash and $322.2M in shareholders' equity, the stock trades at a market cap of only ~$4M, reflecting extreme market skepticism about the accessibility of capital in this Chinese micro-cap ADR.
