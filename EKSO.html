Investment Thesis
The former Ekso Bionics shell is now ChronoScale, an independent, Nasdaq-listed (CHRN) GPU-based AI compute platform generating approximately $84.4M in twelve-month revenue, majority-owned (~97%) by Applied Digital, and led by a newly assembled executive team including a 13-year Tesla infrastructure veteran as CTO [1][2]. As an AI-infrastructure pure play, it scores at the top of the AI Resilience dimension (9/10) — the business cannot be disrupted by AI because it is literally selling AI compute capacity, and management has articulated a disciplined 'execution over access' growth philosophy for scaling GPU deployments [1].
The investment case is overwhelmed by extreme, evidence-based risk at this stage. ChronoScale posted an $72.7M net loss against $84.4M revenue (-86.2% profit margin), has essentially no track record as an independent public company (two and a half months old as of this screen), derives revenue from a single disclosed customer (Together AI), and trades at a reported 35.2x price/sales multiple with 'limited financial disclosure' per sell-side coverage [2]. With Applied Digital retaining ~97% ownership, the public float is roughly 4.3M shares against 365,553 average daily volume — a turnover ratio consistent with the stock's observed ±19% two-day swings and $2.91-$30.00 fifty-two-week range. The Infrastructure score is a mechanical 0 (only profit margin was reliably available, and it sits at the floor), and the Infinite Game score of 1/10 reflects a leadership team and organizational structure too new to evaluate for durable purpose. The Inspire Insight faith-alignment score of +53 reflects the legacy exoskeleton medical-device business and should not be read as a signal about the current AI-infrastructure entity.
