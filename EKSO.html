Company Profile

The public company formerly known as Ekso Bionics Holdings (exoskeleton robotics) ceased to exist as an operating exoskeleton business on May 5, 2026, when Applied Digital contributed its GPU cloud business to the Ekso shell in exchange for ~97% ownership, renaming the entity ChronoScale Holdings Corporation (NASDAQ: CHRN). ChronoScale operates as a GPU-based accelerated compute platform for AI training, inference, and HPC workloads. FY2026 (ended May 31, 2026) revenue was $71.6M (-15% YoY) with a net loss of $50.3M, driven by the loss of a major customer in December 2024. Revenue is concentrated with a single customer (Together AI). A two-year partnership with Microsoft to deploy ~50 MW of AI compute (NVIDIA GB300, liquid cooling) was secured, subject to financing and milestone conditions.

Investment Thesis
ChronoScale (formerly Ekso Bionics, now trading as CHRN) is an independent, Nasdaq-listed GPU-based AI compute platform generating $71.6M in FY2026 revenue, majority-owned (~97%) by Applied Digital, and led by a newly assembled team including a 13-year Tesla infrastructure veteran as CTO [1][2]. As an AI-infrastructure pure play, it scores at the top of the AI Resilience dimension (9/10) — the business cannot be disrupted by AI because it is literally selling AI compute capacity. The two-year Microsoft partnership to deploy ~50 MW of NVIDIA GB300 compute with liquid cooling represents a potential transformative customer diversification catalyst that would address the single greatest business risk [2]. At ~$26 per share, the stock offers venture-scale upside if the Microsoft deal closes and ChronoScale establishes itself as a viable GPU-cloud competitor alongside CoreWeave, Lambda, and the hyperscalers.
The investment case remains overwhelmed by extreme, evidence-based risk. FY2026 revenue of $71.6M declined 15% YoY after losing a major customer in December 2024, and the company posted a $50.3M net loss (-70.3% margin) — worse than the -86.2% margin estimated in the prior screen when using 12-month revenue [2]. Revenue remains concentrated with a single customer (Together AI), and the transformative Microsoft partnership is explicitly 'subject to financing and milestone conditions,' meaning ChronoScale may lack the capital to execute [2]. With Applied Digital holding ~97% ownership, the public float is approximately 4.3M shares, creating extreme volatility (52-week range $2.91-$30.00). The Infrastructure score is a mechanical 1/10 (two available metrics, both weak), the Infinite Game score of 1/10 reflects a leadership team too new to evaluate for durable purpose, and the Inspire Impact Score of +53 reflects the legacy exoskeleton business — not the current AI-infrastructure entity. This is a speculative, pre-profitability AI infrastructure play unsuitable for the IOWN framework's emphasis on financial durability and organizational purpose.
