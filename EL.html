Company Profile
Consumer Defensive ·Household & Personal Products ·NYSE ·United States
Estée Lauder develops, markets and sells prestige skin care, makeup, fragrance and hair care through brands including Estée Lauder, Clinique, M·A·C, La Mer, Jo Malone London, Aveda and The Ordinary, plus licensed fashion fragrances such as Tommy Hilfiger and Michael Kors [3]. Under CEO Stéphane de La Faverie's 'Beauty Reimagined' turnaround, fiscal 2026 (ended June 30, 2026) net sales rose about 5% to $15.05B, organic growth was about 3%, and adjusted EPS rose 66% to $2.51. GAAP net income was $182M after a $1.13B loss in FY2025 [1][3]. FY2027 guidance calls for 3-5% organic growth and EPS of $3.10-3.35 [2].

Finite vs Infinite Game — Sinek (25%)
Mindset: MIXED Overall: 5 /10
Viewed through Sinek's lens, Estée Lauder is an 80-year-old family-controlled institution in mid-recovery. The heritage gives it a real sense of continuity, and the new CEO talks in infinite-game language: transformation is 'how we operate', not a project [6]. The recent record is more mixed. Revenue is still below FY2021, and the quarterly dividend was cut from $0.66 to $0.35 in late 2024 [3]. Leadership then spent two years on a profit-recovery turnaround rather than on advancing a cause. The test is whether 'Beauty Reimagined' is a Just Cause or a restructuring slogan.

Investment Thesis
Bull case: the 'Beauty Reimagined' turnaround is visibly working. FY2026 sales returned to roughly 5% growth, adjusted EPS jumped 66% to $2.51, fragrance grew about 10% organically, and mainland China grew about 9% [1]. Management guides FY2027 EPS to $3.10-3.35, and consensus sees EPS of $3.94 in FY2028 and $4.53 in FY2029 as margins rebuild, giving a credible path to earnings recovery from a trough GAAP margin of 1.2% [2][3].
Bear case: at $108.81 the stock already trades at 32.8x FY2027 consensus EPS, with a forward PEG of 5.9, so much of the recovery is priced in [3]. The balance sheet carries D/E of 243% and negative tangible book value. Revenue remains below FY2021 levels, makeup is flat, hair care is declining, travel-retail weakness persists, and FY2027 organic growth guidance is only 3-5% [1][2][3]. Insiders have made no open-market purchases in the last four quarters [3].
Key Catalysts
  1. Q1 FY2027 results confirming the 3-5% organic growth guide and margin expansion toward the $3.10-3.35 EPS range [2]
  2. Continued mainland China recovery (about +9% organic in FY2026) and stabilization of Asia travel retail [1]
  3. Fragrance momentum (about +10% organic in FY2026) and AI-enabled product development, where the formula navigator delivers 20% efficiency gains [1][5]
Key Risks
  1. A valuation of 32.8x forward earnings leaves little room for a miss in a turnaround that has not yet restored FY2021 revenue levels [3]
  2. High leverage (D/E of 243%) and restructuring charges that keep GAAP margins near 1% [3]
  3. Channel disruption from AI shopping agents and continued share loss among younger online consumers [4][5]
