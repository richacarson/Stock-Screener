Estee Lauder's 'Beauty Reimagined' turnaround delivered a strong FY2026 — full-year net sales rose 5% to $15.0B, gross margin expanded 150bps to 75.5%, operating margin improved 320bps to 11.2%, and adjusted EPS of $2.51 represented 66% growth, with Q4 beating consensus by 22% ($0.39 vs $0.32) [1]. FY2027 guidance was raised to $3.10-$3.35 EPS with 3-5% organic growth and 12.7-13.5% operating margins, signaling continued margin recovery toward historical 15%+ levels. Jo Malone London and TOM FORD joined the billion-dollar brand club, Mainland China grew 9% organically, and the AI deployment (240+ GPTs, 31% media ROI improvement, 300bp manufacturing margin enhancement) is industry-leading [1][2]. At forward PEG of ~0.75 against 36% 3-year EPS growth, the turnaround valuation is compelling. The bear case centers on structural challenges, leverage, and the distance still to cover. Revenue of $15.0B remains ~$2.7B below the FY2022 peak of $17.7B, with 5yr revenue CAGR of -1.5% reflecting permanent market share losses to indie brands and social commerce, not just cyclical weakness [1][4]. GAAP financials show ROE of -20.73% and net margin of -1.67%, with D/E of 242% and $6.81B long-term debt constraining strategic flexibility — the 47% dividend cut to $0.35/quarter confirmed the financial strain [4]. The PRGP elimination of 9,000-10,000 positions risks destroying institutional knowledge while Glassdoor reviews show 57% recommendation rate and 'morale at an all-time low' [2]. The operating margin target of 12.7-13.5% for FY2027 remains below the historical 15%+ peak, and the path from adjusted profitability to GAAP profitability remains uncertain given cumulative restructuring charges of $1.5-1.7B. Tariff exposure and social commerce disruption from TikTok Shop and indie brands create ongoing headwinds.
