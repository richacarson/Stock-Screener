Company Profile
Healthcare ·Drug Manufacturers - Specialty & Generic ·NYQ ·United States
Elanco Animal Health innovates, develops, manufactures, and markets products for pets and farm animals worldwide. The company operates Pet Health and Farm Animal segments, with key franchises in parasiticides (Credelio, Seresto), dermatology (Zenrelia), and medicated feed additives (Rumensin). Q2 2026 revenue of $1,368M grew 10% YoY driven by Pet Health (+12%) and Cattle (+17%), with full-year guidance raised to $5.09-$5.14B. Founded in 1954 as a division of Eli Lilly, Elanco was spun off in 2018 and is headquartered in Indianapolis, Indiana.

Investment Thesis
Elanco at $23.55 with an $11.8B market cap is a turnaround story showing accelerating momentum — Q2 2026 revenue of $1,368M grew 10% YoY with Pet Health up 12% and Cattle up 17%, full-year guidance was raised to $5.09-$5.14B revenue and $1.10-$1.16 adjusted EPS, and adjusted EBITDA margin expanded to 21.2% [1]. The Zenrelia and Credelio Quattro product launches are driving the Pet Health acceleration, while the AHV International acquisition adds international farm animal scale. At 18.3x forward earnings, the stock is reasonably valued if the company can sustain high-single-digit organic growth and continue margin expansion toward peer Zoetis' profitability levels. Net leverage of 3.1x is improving toward the 3.0x year-end target, and free cash flow of $827M provides growing financial flexibility [1][4].
The bear case centers on structural profitability challenges and valuation risk. Despite 10% revenue growth, Elanco still generates GAAP losses with negative ROE (-2.98%) and negative profit margins (-3.96%), weighed down by $4.2B in debt from the Bayer acquisition and heavy intangible amortization [4]. The PEG ratio of 4.04 suggests the market already prices in substantial growth improvement that may not materialize — Elanco's 5-year revenue CAGR of just 3% shows the Bayer acquisition added scale but not sustained organic growth. Zoetis, the industry leader, operates at 2x Elanco's revenue with 30%+ profit margins versus Elanco's losses, raising the question of whether Elanco can ever close the profitability gap. Pet health spending is cyclical and could decelerate in a consumer downturn, while the Seresto flea collar franchise faces ongoing generic competition.
