Company Profile
Real Estate ·REIT - Residential ·NYSE ·United States
Elme Communities (formerly Washington Real Estate Investment Trust) was a multifamily REIT operating approximately 8,900 apartment homes in the Washington, DC metro and Atlanta/Sunbelt regions. The company is in active liquidation following shareholder approval of a Plan of Sale and Liquidation in October 2025, with an initial $14.67/share distribution paid in January 2026. The $280M Riverside Apartments sale collapsed in June 2026, causing ELME to withdraw all remaining distribution estimates. The stock trades at $1.67 with a market cap of $148M and only 255 employees managing the wind-down.
