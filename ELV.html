Company Profile
Healthcare ·Managed Care ·NYSE ·United States
Elevance Health (formerly Anthem) is one of the largest health insurance companies in the United States, serving over 112 million people through its Health Benefits and Carelon segments. The company operates as a Blue Cross Blue Shield licensee in 14 states and provides a range of managed care, pharmacy benefits, and health services. Carelon, the company's health services platform, provides behavioral health, pharmacy, and care delivery solutions. Founded in 1944, headquartered in Indianapolis, Indiana.

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
8
AI Risk Protection (7/9): Health insurance is well-protected from AI displacement by deep regulatory moats (state insurance regulations, CMS requirements, BCBS licensure), massive proprietary claims data, provider network contracts requiring physical-world relationships, and complex compliance requirements that create enormous switching costs [3]. AI cannot substitute the core insurance underwriting, risk management, and member service functions. Elevance's Chief Digital Information Officer has drawn a clear line against using AI to deny claims -- denials remain under human review [2]. However, AI could pressure administrative margins by enabling more efficient competitors, and AI-powered care navigation tools could theoretically reduce the intermediary role of managed care over the long term. Analysts are not flagging AI as a major disruption risk for Elevance. AI Opportunity (1/1): Elevance has a dedicated Chief AI Officer (Shawn Wang, 8-year tenure) and is actively deploying AI across claims processing, prior authorization approvals, fraud detection, care management, and member personalization, generating measurable operational improvements [2]. The Responsible AI program aligned with the NIST AI Risk Management Framework demonstrates systematic AI integration.
