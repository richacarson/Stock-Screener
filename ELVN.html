Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Enliven Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company discovering and developing small-molecule kinase inhibitors for cancer. Its lead program, ELVN-001, targets chronic myeloid leukemia (CML) in heavily pretreated patients and showed a 52% cumulative major molecular response (MMR) rate in the Phase 1b ENABLE trial. A Phase 3 pivotal trial (ENABLE-2) is planned for H2 2026. Its second program, ELVN-002, is in Phase 1 for HER2-positive and HER2-mutant solid tumors. Richard Fair was appointed CEO in December 2025, with co-founder Sam Kintz transitioning to Head of Pipeline. The company reported a full-year 2025 net loss of $103.7M with cash of $462.6M providing runway into H1 2029.

Investment Thesis
Enliven is a clinical-stage oncology biotech advancing two active programs: ELVN-001, which showed a 52% cumulative major molecular response rate in heavily pretreated, asciminib-experienced CML patients and is heading into a Phase 3 pivotal trial (ENABLE-2) in H2 2026, and ELVN-002, in Phase 1 combination cohorts for HER2+ breast and lung cancer [1]. The balance sheet is solid for a clinical-stage name — $462.6M cash and virtually zero debt (D/E 0.17%) provide a runway into H1 2029, removing near-term financing risk [1][4]. The December 2025 appointment of Richard Fair as CEO — a 25-year industry veteran with Roche/Genentech commercial leadership experience — positions the company for the commercial phase ahead of potential ELVN-001 approval [2].
The risk profile remains dominated by binary clinical outcomes. ELVN-001's Phase 3 result is the single most important event for the stock, and a disappointing readout would remove the primary valuation support for the $3.28B market cap — the stock has already declined from $60+ to $44.75, reflecting growing investor caution about Phase 3 risk [4]. Governance signals are mixed-to-negative on inspiration metrics: insiders own just 3.9% of the company, Chairman Richard Heyman recently sold $348K of stock (the largest sale in the past year), and OrbiMed has been trimming its position — none of which builds confidence even against a strong cash position [2]. Sustainalytics rates Enliven's ESG management as weak. The competitive landscape in CML (Novartis's asciminib/Scemblix) and in HER2-mutant tumors is well established, meaning ELVN-001 and ELVN-002 must displace entrenched standards of care rather than fill an empty niche.
Key Catalysts
  1. ENABLE-2 Phase 3 pivotal trial initiation for ELVN-001 in H2 2026, the single most significant near-term value driver for the $3.28B market cap [1]
  2. Continued Phase 1b data maturation from the ENABLE trial (52% cumulative MMR in asciminib-experienced patients) strengthening the eventual NDA package [1]
  3. ELVN-002 Phase 1 combination-cohort readouts in HER2+ metastatic breast cancer and NSCLC providing a second, less binary source of pipeline value [1]
Key Risks
  1. Binary Phase 3 risk for ELVN-001 remains the dominant driver of the $3.28B market cap; a disappointing ENABLE-2 readout would remove the primary valuation support and the stock has already declined ~25% from recent highs [4]
  2. Insider selling (Chairman Heyman's $348K sale, OrbiMed trimming its stake) with no offsetting insider buying in the past 12 months, despite management's public conviction in the pipeline [2]
  3. Entrenched competition from Novartis's asciminib/Scemblix in CML and established HER2-targeted therapies in solid tumors means both pipeline assets must displace, not merely supplement, current standards of care [1]
