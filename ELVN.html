Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Enliven Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company discovering and developing small-molecule kinase inhibitors for cancer. Its lead program, ELVN-001, targets chronic myeloid leukemia (CML) in heavily pretreated patients and is advancing toward a Phase 3 pivotal trial (ENABLE-2) in H2 2026. Its second program, ELVN-002, is in Phase 1 development for HER2-positive and HER2-mutant solid tumors, including combination cohorts in breast and lung cancer. Headquartered in Burlingame, California.

Investment Thesis
Enliven is a clinical-stage oncology biotech advancing two active programs: ELVN-001, which showed a 52% cumulative major molecular response rate in heavily pretreated, asciminib-experienced CML patients and is heading into a Phase 3 pivotal trial (ENABLE-2) in H2 2026, and ELVN-002, in Phase 1 combination cohorts for HER2+ breast and lung cancer [1]. The balance sheet is exceptional for a clinical-stage name — $895.16M cash and zero debt provide a runway into 2030, removing near-term financing risk and giving management room to fund both programs without dilution in the immediate term [3].
The risk profile remains dominated by binary clinical outcomes. ELVN-001's Phase 3 result is the single most important event for the stock, and a disappointing readout would remove the primary valuation support for the $4.21B market cap. Governance signals are mixed-to-negative on inspiration metrics: insiders own just 3.9% of the company, Independent Chairman Richard Heyman recently sold $348K of stock (the largest sale in the past year), and OrbiMed has been trimming its position — none of which is a confidence-building signal even against a strong cash position [2]. The competitive landscape in CML (Novartis's asciminib/Scemblix) and in HER2-mutant tumors is well established, meaning ELVN-001 and ELVN-002 must displace entrenched standards of care rather than fill an empty niche.
Key Catalysts
  1. ENABLE-2 Phase 3 pivotal trial initiation for ELVN-001 in H2 2026, the single most significant near-term value driver
  2. Continued Phase 1b data maturation from the ENABLE trial (52% cumulative MMR in asciminib-experienced patients) strengthening the eventual NDA package
  3. ELVN-002 Phase 1 combination-cohort readouts in HER2+ metastatic breast cancer and NSCLC providing a second, less binary source of pipeline value
Key Risks
  1. Binary Phase 3 risk for ELVN-001 remains the dominant driver of the $4.21B market cap; a disappointing ENABLE-2 readout would remove the primary valuation support
  2. Insider selling (Chairman Heyman's $348K sale, OrbiMed trimming its stake) with no offsetting insider buying in the past 12 months, despite management's public conviction in the pipeline
  3. Entrenched competition from Novartis's asciminib/Scemblix in CML and established HER2-targeted therapies in solid tumors means both pipeline assets must displace, not merely supplement, current standards of care
