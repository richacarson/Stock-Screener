Empery Digital Inc. (formerly Volcon Inc.) originally manufactured all-electric off-road motorcycles and utility terrain vehicles. Under CEO John Kim (appointed early 2024), the company pivoted to a Bitcoin treasury strategy and is winding down its powersports operations. In 2026, Empery expanded into AI data center investments through a partnership with Hunt Properties. The company is embroiled in a proxy fight with ATG Capital, with a Delaware court finding breach of fiduciary duties. Q1 2026 net loss was $85.4M (driven by $78.4M digital asset losses), and TTM revenue is approximately $628K.

Investment Thesis
Empery Digital's low D/E ratio of 49% (reflecting CEO John Kim's 2024 debt restructuring), forward P/E of 18.8x, and strategic pivot toward AI data center infrastructure through the Hunt Properties partnership represent the bull case [2][4]. If AI data center investments generate returns, the company's small $84.8M market cap provides leverage to upside. The Inspire Impact Score of 48 (ALIGNED) with 10 positive ESG attributions and zero negative marks — largely inherited from the original Volcon EV business — is favorable for faith-aligned mandates. Kim's track record building Super73 (acquired by Tiger Global) and U-Life (acquired by LG Electronics) provides some entrepreneurial credibility [2].
The bear case is severe. Empery Digital generated only $628K in TTM revenue (down 76.4% YoY) while posting an $85.4M net loss in Q1 2026 alone, driven by $78.4M in digital asset trading losses [1]. The company's strategy has changed twice in under a year, from EVs to Bitcoin treasury to AI data centers, with no commercial traction in any direction. A Delaware court found that the board breached its fiduciary duties, and the proxy fight with ATG Capital (backed by a 9.8% shareholder) alleges 'reckless behavior and poor governance' including employees day-trading 'tens or hundreds of millions of dollars of bitcoin derivatives' [3]. The estimated $22.5M in proxy fight costs alone exceeds 26% of the company's market cap. ROE of -427% and profit margin at 0% (excluding massive digital asset losses) demonstrate no path to profitability. The AI data center pivot has zero revenue, zero established assets, and zero competitive moats against well-capitalized incumbents like Equinix, Digital Realty, and QTS.
