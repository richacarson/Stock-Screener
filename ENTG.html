Company Profile
Technology ·Semiconductor Equipment & Materials ·NASDAQ ·United States
Entegris, Inc. develops, manufactures, and supplies advanced materials, specialty chemicals, and equipment solutions for the semiconductor and other high-technology industries. Products include CMP consumables, selective etch and deposition materials, filtration systems, and fluid handling components critical to leading-edge chip manufacturing at 3nm and 2nm nodes. The 2022 CMC Materials acquisition for ~$6B transformed Entegris into a comprehensive materials platform. CEO David Reeder succeeded Bertrand Loy (who retired July 31, 2026). Q2 2026 achieved record performance with 11% YoY revenue growth, highest adjusted gross margin since early 2022, and $120M free cash flow (14% of sales).

Investment Thesis
Entegris is a mission-critical materials supplier to the world's leading semiconductor manufacturers, providing CMP consumables, deposition materials, and filtration systems that become more valuable as chip geometries shrink to 3nm and 2nm nodes [1][2]. Q2 2026 confirmed the semiconductor recovery thesis — 11% YoY revenue growth with double-digit expansion in both unit-driven and CapEx-driven businesses, highest adjusted gross margin since early 2022, and $120M free cash flow (14% of sales) [2]. The company repaid $200M in debt, reducing net leverage to 3.4x with a path below 3x by year-end, addressing the primary CMC acquisition overhang. Every advanced AI chip requires Entegris materials, creating a structural volume multiplier as AI capex drives fab utilization above 85%. The 5yr revenue CAGR of ~10% demonstrates secular growth, and portfolio simplification is improving margin trajectory. 12-18 month chip qualification cycles create switching costs that protect market share.
Valuation remains demanding at ~32x forward earnings with a PEG of ~1.6, implying the market already prices substantial growth recovery — any semiconductor cycle deceleration could trigger multiple compression [3]. ROE of 7.67% is still well below the 15% target, reflecting the dilutive $6B CMC acquisition that left D/E at 86% despite $200M in Q2 debt repayment [3]. Profit margin of 9.18% is improving but remains below pre-acquisition levels. The CEO transition from 13-year veteran Bertrand Loy (retired July 31, 2026) to finance-background David Reeder adds execution uncertainty — Glassdoor reviews citing 'management by fear' and 'too finance-heavy' leadership suggest cultural challenges that Reeder's CFO background may not address [4][5]. US-China semiconductor export restrictions could reduce the addressable market if advanced materials face the same controls being applied to chipmaking equipment. The semiconductor industry remains cyclical, and while Q2 was strong, the recovery's durability through 2027 is uncertain.
Key Catalysts
  1. AI-driven semiconductor manufacturing ramp accelerating unit demand for advanced materials — Q2 2026 delivered double-digit growth in both unit-driven and CapEx-driven businesses, with record quarters in liquid filtration and advanced planarization [2]
  2. Deleveraging momentum with $200M debt repayment in Q2, net leverage falling to 3.4x with a path below 3x by year-end, which should improve ROE from the depressed 7.67% and unlock margin expansion toward pre-acquisition levels [2]
  3. Portfolio simplification and optimization actions driving highest adjusted gross margin since early 2022, with continued margin expansion potential as CMC integration synergies are fully realized [2]
Key Risks
  1. Forward P/E of ~32x leaves limited margin for error — any semiconductor cycle deceleration or guidance miss could trigger severe multiple compression given the high-expectations valuation [3]
  2. CEO transition risk as David Reeder (finance-background executive, former CFO) replaces 13-year veteran Bertrand Loy, with Glassdoor reviews citing 'management by fear' suggesting cultural challenges that may impair innovation-focused execution [4][5]
  3. US-China semiconductor export restrictions could reduce Entegris's addressable market if advanced materials face the same controls being applied to chipmaking equipment, particularly for Chinese fab customers at leading-edge nodes
