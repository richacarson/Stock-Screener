Company Profile
Technology ·Semiconductor Equipment & Materials ·NASDAQ ·United States
Entegris, Inc. develops, manufactures, and supplies advanced materials, specialty chemicals, and equipment solutions for the semiconductor and other high-technology industries. Products include CMP (chemical mechanical planarization) consumables, selective etch and deposition materials, filtration systems, and fluid handling components. The 2022 acquisition of CMC Materials for approximately $6B transformed Entegris into a comprehensive materials platform serving the world's leading chipmakers including TSMC, Intel, and Samsung.

Investment Thesis
Entegris is a mission-critical materials supplier to the world's leading semiconductor manufacturers, providing CMP consumables, selective etch chemicals, and deposition materials that become more valuable as chip geometries shrink to 3nm and 2nm nodes [1][5]. The CMC Materials acquisition created a comprehensive portfolio with high switching costs (12-18 month chip qualification cycles), and Q1 2026 showed 5% revenue growth driven by AI-related demand recovery [1]. The 5-year revenue CAGR of 11.45% demonstrates consistent secular growth, and Entegris benefits from one of the strongest tailwinds in technology — every advanced AI chip requires more materials per wafer, creating a structural volume multiplier. Strengthening order patterns across the portfolio suggest the semiconductor cycle trough is past, with consensus forecasts projecting 9.09% revenue CAGR over the next three years [4].
Valuation is demanding at 37.9x forward earnings and 83.9x trailing P/E, with a PEG of 1.68 implying the market already prices in substantial growth recovery [4]. Revenue declined in 2024 and 2025 (-1.38% YoY) as the semiconductor industry cycled through a trough, and profit margin remains compressed at 8.18% — well below pre-acquisition levels — reflecting CMC integration costs and $3.81B in acquisition debt (D/E of 93%) [4]. ROE of 6.79% is dramatically below the 15% target, reflecting the dilutive effect of the CMC deal. Glassdoor reviews citing 'management by fear' raise organizational culture concerns, and the CEO transition from 13-year veteran Loy to finance-background Reeder adds execution uncertainty [5]. Geopolitical tensions (US-China semiconductor restrictions) could reduce the addressable market if Chinese fabs face further export controls on advanced materials.
Key Catalysts
  1. AI-driven semiconductor manufacturing ramp accelerating unit demand for advanced materials, particularly at TSMC 3nm/2nm nodes where materials content per wafer increases structurally
  2. CMC Materials integration synergies fully realized through 2026-2027, expanding profit margins toward pre-acquisition levels and improving ROE from the depressed 6.79%
  3. Semiconductor cycle recovery with strengthening order patterns across the portfolio as AI capex by hyperscalers drives fab utilization above 85%
Key Risks
  1. Forward P/E of 37.9x and trailing P/E of 83.9x leave zero margin for error — any semiconductor cycle deceleration or guidance miss could trigger severe multiple compression
  2. $3.81B debt from CMC acquisition constrains financial flexibility with D/E of 93% and depressed ROE of 6.79%, creating vulnerability if the recovery is slower than expected
  3. US-China semiconductor export restrictions could reduce Entegris's addressable market if advanced materials face the same controls being applied to chipmaking equipment
