Company Profile
Healthcare ·Drug Manufacturers - Specialty & Generic ·NASDAQ ·United States
Evolus, Inc. is a performance beauty company operating in the cash-pay aesthetic market. Its flagship product Jeuveau is a proprietary 900 kilodalton purified botulinum toxin type A formulation for glabellar lines, competing directly with Allergan's Botox. The company also markets Evolysse, a collection of injectable hyaluronic acid dermal fillers, across the U.S., Canada, Europe, and Australia. Founded in 2012, headquartered in Newport Beach, California.
334 Employees ·www.evolus.com

Investment Thesis
Evolus delivered Q1 2026 revenue of $73.1M (7% YoY growth) with positive adjusted EBITDA of $0.6M for the second consecutive quarter, marking a decisive turn toward profitability [1]. Jeuveau's 14% U.S. market share with six consecutive years of above-market growth, combined with the Evolysse filler expansion and pending Sculpt approval, creates multiple revenue drivers toward the reaffirmed 2026 guidance of $327-337M [1]. International markets are 'nearly doubling' in several geographies, with the UK noted for rapid growth and European share gains. At 1.3x price/sales with $330M+ in guided revenue, the stock trades at a significant discount to aesthetics peers on a growth-adjusted basis. Long-term 2028 targets of $450-500M revenue with 13-15% EBITDA margins represent significant upside from current levels [1].
The bear case centers on Evolus's narrow competitive position challenging deeply entrenched incumbents and stretched valuation for a pre-GAAP-profit company. Allergan's Botox (60%+ share) benefits from massive brand recognition, physician familiarity, and a full aesthetic portfolio that creates bundling advantages Evolus cannot match. Forward P/E of 54x on nascent earnings leaves minimal margin of safety. Profit margins remain negative at -14.4%, and the transition to sustained profitability depends on expense discipline while simultaneously investing in Evolysse and international expansion. Net insider selling by CEO Moatazedi ($65K at $4.75) and insider Avelar ($147K at $4.89) at depressed prices signals limited conviction from those with the most information [5]. The cash-pay aesthetic market is cyclically sensitive to consumer confidence — any macro weakness could slow elective procedure volumes that drive Jeuveau demand.
