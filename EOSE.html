Company Profile
Industrials ·Electrical Equipment & Parts ·NASDAQ ·United States
Eos Energy Enterprises designs, develops, manufactures, and markets zinc-based battery energy storage systems for utility-scale, microgrid, and commercial/industrial applications. The company's Znyth technology offers a non-lithium alternative for 3- to 12-hour discharge duration. Products include the Z3 battery module, Eos Cube, and DawnOS AI-enabled analytics platform. Q2 2026 delivered record revenue of $68.8M (+351% YoY) with H1 2026 revenue of $125.7M exceeding all of FY2025. Record backlog of $807M and $24.6B commercial pipeline. FY2026 guidance tightened to $300-350M. Manufacturing consolidated at Thorn Hill facility. Targeting positive adjusted gross margin by Q4 2026. Market cap approximately $1.2B.

If Eos cannot demonstrate positive gross margin by Q4 2026 as guided, the ability to sustain operations may be fundamentally impaired.
