Company Profile
Technology ·Information Technology Services ·NYSE ·United States
EPAM Systems provides digital platform engineering and software development services worldwide, spanning cloud, data/AI, customer experience, and cybersecurity consulting, including a collaboration with Wiz on AI-powered cloud risk remediation. The company fully exited Russia and Belarus following the 2022 invasion of Ukraine, relocating roughly 9,000 employees at significant cost. New CEO Balazs Fejes (effective September 2025) is repositioning EPAM as an 'AI-Native' enterprise engineering partner after 2025 revenue reaccelerated to $5.46 billion (+15.4% YoY), though 2026 guidance points to a sharp deceleration to 4.0-6.5% growth.

Investment Thesis
EPAM's 2025 results show a real recovery from its post-Russia stagnation, with revenue reaccelerating to $5.46B (+15.4% YoY) and a nearly debt-free balance sheet (D/E of 4.3%) funding a $223.5M share buyback [1]. A Forward PEG of 0.57 and Forward P/E of just 7.70 suggest the market is still pricing EPAM for the 2022-2024 growth trough rather than crediting the recovery, leaving room for multiple expansion if the AI-Native repositioning under new CEO Balazs Fejes gains traction with enterprise clients [2][3][6]. The 2022 Russia/Belarus exit, while costly and initially criticized, demonstrated that leadership will absorb real financial pain to act on stated values — a data point in favor of organizational resilience.
The bear case is that EPAM's core staff-augmentation and custom-development model is structurally exposed to AI-driven disruption: 2026 guidance of only 4.0-6.5% revenue growth (with organic constant-currency growth as low as 2.5%) represents a sharp deceleration from 15.4% in 2025, and no specific AI-revenue figure has been disclosed to confirm the AI-Native pivot is offsetting commoditization of lower-value coding and testing work [1][2]. Profit margin of 7.15% and ROE of 11.18% remain well below IOWN targets, and Inspiration evidence is thin — no charitable program, retention, or insider-buying data was found beyond the qualitative Glassdoor-driven Trusting Teams score. A reacceleration in 2026 organic growth, or a disclosed AI-revenue metric, would be the clearest signal the pivot is working; absent that, EPAM remains a WATCH.
