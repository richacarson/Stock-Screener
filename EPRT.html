Company Profile
Real Estate ·REIT - Diversified (Net Lease) ·NYSE ·United States
Essential Properties Realty Trust is an internally managed net lease REIT purpose-built in 2016 to focus on middle-market, service-oriented and experience-based tenants — restaurants, car washes, automotive services, medical and dental clinics, early childhood education, entertainment, and health/fitness — via sale-leaseback transactions, a niche often overlooked by larger net lease peers focused on investment-grade tenants [3]. Q1 2026 revenue was $158.8M (+22.7% YoY) and the company raised full-year 2026 AFFO guidance to $2.01-$2.05/share alongside investment volume guidance of $1.2-$1.5B, with 99.6% portfolio occupancy and 3.5x rent coverage [1].

Investment Thesis
Essential Properties has built a differentiated net lease niche around middle-market, service-oriented and experience-based tenants (car washes, restaurants, medical/dental, early childhood education) that are structurally immune to both e-commerce and AI disruption, growing revenue from $164M to $561M over five years (a ~28% CAGR) while maintaining 99.6% occupancy and 3.5x rent coverage [1][2]. The company raised full-year 2026 AFFO guidance to $2.01-$2.05/share and investment volume guidance to $1.2-$1.5B, and D/E of 66% and profit margin of 43.5% reflect a disciplined, well-capitalized balance sheet for continued sale-leaseback growth. The 4.4% dividend yield is backed by 6 consecutive years of increases (9 raises in 5 years) since the November 2019 IPO.
The bear case centers on thin qualitative evidence of durable competitive advantage or organizational depth: with only ~61 employees, EPRT's underwriting and portfolio-management bandwidth is a key-person and process risk as the company scales investment volume toward $1.5B annually [1]. Recent insider activity has skewed toward selling — the CFO sold $186,530 of stock in March 2026 and another insider filed to sell $709,576 in July 2026 — offering no offsetting signal of open-market conviction buying. The 98% payout ratio leaves little dividend-coverage cushion if same-store rent growth (currently a modest 1.5%) or occupancy (99.6%) were to deteriorate, and a Forward P/E of 21.2x/PEG of 3.2 prices in continued double-digit growth that depends on capital markets access to fund the acquisition-driven model.
Key Catalysts
  1. Continued execution against raised 2026 guidance ($2.01-$2.05 AFFO/share, $1.2-1.5B investment volume) as the middle-market sale-leaseback niche remains underserved by larger net lease peers [1]
  2. Portfolio diversification into additional service-oriented and experience-based categories, further insulating the REIT from both e-commerce and AI-driven disruption
  3. Potential re-rating toward larger net lease peers' multiples if EPRT continues to compound revenue and AFFO at a premium growth rate to Realty Income and NNN
Key Risks
  1. Concentration in non-investment-grade, middle-market tenants carries higher credit risk than investment-grade-focused peers if a economic downturn pressures smaller operators' ability to pay rent
  2. Thin organizational bandwidth (~61 employees) underwriting a growing, capital-intensive acquisition pipeline ($1.2-1.5B guided for 2026) concentrates execution risk in a small team [1]
  3. 98% payout ratio leaves minimal dividend-coverage cushion, and continued growth depends on favorable access to equity and debt capital markets to fund the sale-leaseback acquisition model
