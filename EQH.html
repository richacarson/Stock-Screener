Company Profile
Financial Services ·Insurance - Life ·NYSE ·United States
Equitable Holdings, Inc. is a diversified financial services company offering life insurance, annuities, retirement solutions, and wealth management products. The company operates through four segments: Individual Retirement, Group Retirement, Investment Management, and Protection Solutions. Formerly known as AXA Financial, Equitable completed its IPO from AXA Group in 2018. In March 2026, Equitable announced a $22 billion all-stock merger with Corebridge Financial that would create a combined company with $1.5 trillion in assets under management and over 12 million clients. Q1 2026 non-GAAP operating EPS of $1.68 (excluding notable items) grew 25% YoY.

Investment Thesis
Equitable Holdings at 5.6x forward earnings with 51.3% ROE, 25% YoY EPS growth, and a 3.4% dividend yield offers deep value in a structurally improving financial services franchise [2]. The $22 billion Corebridge merger would create a $1.5 trillion AUM combined company with 12 million clients and meaningful cost synergies [1]. Q1 2026 results demonstrated strong organic momentum with $1.3B of net inflows in Retirement and $2.0B in Wealth Management advisory, while management reaffirmed $1.8B cash generation guidance. The deeply discounted valuation reflects market skepticism about merger execution rather than fundamental weakness.
The bear case centers on merger integration risk and structural insurance industry challenges. The $22B Corebridge merger is the largest insurance combination in years and carries substantial execution risk — integrating two large insurance organizations across technology platforms, product suites, and distribution channels could take 3-5 years and distract management from organic growth [1]. Equitable's profit margin of 10.6% is below target, and insurance companies carry structural leverage that depresses the Infrastructure score. Revenue has been volatile due to mark-to-market accounting on investment portfolios, making earnings quality difficult to assess. The short dividend history since the 2018 IPO provides limited track record for income investors. Additionally, AI-powered robo-advisors and digital insurance platforms represent an emerging competitive threat in the wealth management and retirement advice segments that Equitable serves [2].
