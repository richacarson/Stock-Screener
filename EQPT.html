Company Profile
Industrials ·Trading Companies & Distributors ·NASDAQ ·United States
EquipmentShare.com is a nationwide construction technology and equipment solutions provider offering equipment rental, sales, and technology services across 407 operational locations. The company's proprietary T3 platform provides fleet management, telematics, AI-powered safety cameras, and jobsite productivity tools for contractors and builders. Founded in 2014 by brothers Jabbok and Willy Schlacks, IPO completed January 23, 2026 on NASDAQ, headquartered in Columbia, Missouri.

Investment Thesis
EquipmentShare offers compelling exposure to the digitization of the massive and still-analog US construction equipment rental market through a proprietary technology platform (T3) and exceptional revenue execution (38% YoY growth to $989M quarterly, $4.65B TTM) [1][2]. The company raised full-year 2026 revenue guidance to $5.15-$5.58B and adjusted Core EBITDA guidance to $1.88-$2.00B, demonstrating continued momentum. Adjusted Core EBITDA of $399M in Q1 2026 (+38% YoY) with mature rental location adjusted EBITDA margin of 55% highlights strong unit economics [1]. The $500M buyback authorization and 12-analyst 'Buy' consensus with $34.50 average price target (115% upside) reflect institutional confidence in the growth trajectory [6].
Significant risks cloud the thesis. The Umibozu Research short report published June 24, 2026 alleges founder self-dealing through undisclosed related parties (EZ Equipment Zone, Bevel Financial, Armada Fleet Management), claiming at least $77M in fees were extracted through the OWN Program while concealing these relationships from IPO investors [7]. Multiple securities law firms (Kirby McInerney, Pomerantz, Johnson Fistel, Lowey Dannenberg) have initiated investigations [8]. D/E of 268% with $4.21B total debt against $409M cash creates meaningful leverage risk in a cyclical construction industry. The stock has fallen 34% from its January 2026 IPO price of $24.50 to $16.06. Founders controlling ~81% of voting power through a dual-class structure limits governance accountability. Net profit margin of 0.47% leaves virtually no margin of safety, and free cash flow remains deeply negative (-$746M TTM) due to heavy fleet investment [6].

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
9
AI Risk Protection (8/9): Equipment rental for construction is a fundamentally physical-world operation -- heavy machinery must be physically delivered, maintained, operated, and serviced on job sites across 407 locations nationwide [3][4]. AI cannot substitute the core service: getting a bulldozer, crane, or excavator to a construction site and keeping it running. The business requires physical inventory, fleet logistics, on-the-ground service personnel, and customer relationships that operate entirely outside AI's substitution frontier. Construction industry digitization is still early, creating runway for technology adoption rather than threat. AI Opportunity (1/1): EquipmentShare is actively deploying AI as a competitive advantage through its T3 platform -- AI-powered safety cameras, telematics-based predictive maintenance, and data analytics for fleet optimization streaming billions of data points daily [3][4]. The company positions itself as a technology leader in a traditionally analog industry, earning 'Fleet Management Technology Company of the Year' recognition [3].
