Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Gold ·AMEX ·Canada
Equinox Gold Corp. is a Canadian gold mining company that explores, develops, and operates mineral properties across the Americas. Following transformative acquisitions of Calibre Mining (2025) and Orla Mining (2026), and the divestiture of Brazilian operations for $1.015B, EQX is scaling a long-life North American gold platform. The company produced 197,628 ounces in Q1 2026 with 2026 guidance of 700,000-800,000+ ounces and generated TTM revenue of approximately $2.9B with a 28.1% profit margin. Headquartered in Vancouver, Canada.

Investment Thesis
Equinox Gold at $11.12 offers a deeply discounted entry into a rapidly scaling North American gold platform — Forward P/E of 7.5x implies minimal premium for a producer guiding 700,000-800,000+ oz in 2026 against gold prices above $2,500/oz. The portfolio transformation is transformative: Calibre (2025) and Orla (2026) acquisitions doubled the production base, while divestiture of all Brazilian assets for $1.015B slashed debt to just $77M net (excluding convertibles), creating a clean balance sheet with D/E of 13% [1]. Q1 2026 delivered $862M revenue, $527M adjusted EBITDA, and $310M net income on 197,628 oz produced, with the quarterly dividend raised 50% following the Orla merger. Valentine Mine and Greenstone ramp-ups provide organic growth visibility alongside exploration upside from the AI-identified Minotaur target [2].
The bear case centers on integration risk and leadership instability. Two CEO transitions in two years (Smith to Hall to Simpson) during the simultaneous integration of Calibre and Orla create execution risk across a company operating mines in Canada, the U.S., Mexico, and Nicaragua [2]. ROE of just 8.5% reflects the dilutive impact of acquisition-funded share issuance — shares outstanding of 1.17B have roughly tripled through serial M&A, and further dilution is possible if convertible debentures convert. The ESG track record carries scars: Los Filos in Mexico suffered repeated community blockades over disputed social collaboration agreements, and the Aurizona dam failure in Brazil (now divested) raised environmental concerns [3]. At 2.5x book value, the stock prices in successful integration that history suggests is not guaranteed, and gold price sensitivity means any sustained pullback from $2,500+/oz levels would compress margins rapidly given all-in sustaining costs. The 0.47% dividend yield provides negligible income protection.
