Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Gold ·NYSE ·Canada
Equinox Gold Corp. is a mid-tier gold mining company with operations across the Americas. Following the completed $1.97B Calibre Mining acquisition (2025) and the Orla Mining merger (Q2 2026), Equinox has transformed into a major gold producer targeting 870,000-920,000 ounces consolidated in 2026 (approximately 1.1M oz pro forma). Key assets include the Valentine Gold Mine and Greenstone Mine in Canada, Musselwhite (acquired via Orla), Mesquite in California, and Camino Rojo in Mexico. The company divested its Brazil operations for $1.8B, pivoting to a North America-focused strategy. Market cap of $13.5B. Headquartered in Vancouver, Canada.

Investment Thesis
Equinox Gold at 6.77x forward earnings offers leveraged gold exposure with a conservative balance sheet (D/E 13%, $1.2B available liquidity) and transformational growth catalysts [1]. The completed Orla merger creates a 1.1M oz/year Americas-focused producer with H1 2026 revenue of $1.63B, net income of $540.7M, and adjusted EBITDA of $885.5M at average realized gold price of $4,256/oz. Q2 mine-site free cash flow of $223.7M supports the 50% dividend increase and $436M Valentine Phase 2 expansion targeting 223,000 oz/year from late 2028 [1]. The AI-supported Minotaur Zone discovery (2.68 g/t over 32m, 700m+ strike length) provides near-mill exploration upside with 15,000-20,000 metres of dedicated drilling planned [2]. Profit margin of 28.09% at AISC of $1,900-$2,000/oz demonstrates operational efficiency, and Ross Beaty's 3.5% ownership aligns founder interests with shareholders.
The bear case centers on gold price sensitivity, integration complexity, and leadership instability. AISC guidance of $1,900-$2,000/oz means a return to $2,500/oz gold would compress margins severely, potentially halting growth investment and dividend programs [1]. The company is simultaneously integrating Orla Mining (Musselwhite, Camino Rojo, South Railroad) while ramping Valentine Phase 1 and sanctioning Phase 2 — execution risk across multiple complex projects is elevated. ROE of 8.47% remains well below the 15% target despite gold above $4,200/oz, reflecting the dilutive cost of equity-financed acquisitions (shares outstanding up 75% YoY to 1.17B). Four CEO transitions in nine years, with Darren Hall now transitioning to Jason Simpson, creates leadership continuity risk at a critical integration period [2]. The trailing P/E of 11.85x versus forward P/E of 6.77x implies the market expects a significant earnings inflection — if Valentine or newly acquired assets underperform, the trajectory could disappoint.
