Company Profile
Industrials ·Marine Shipping ·NASDAQ ·Greece
Euroseas Ltd. provides ocean-going transportation services internationally, owning and operating a fleet of 21 containerships (15 Feeder and 6 Intermediate) with a combined capacity of approximately 61,144 TEU. The company reported Q2 2026 net revenues of $56.5M with net income of $33.2M ($4.74 EPS) and adjusted EBITDA of $40.1M. LTM revenue stands at $226.6M with a contracted revenue backlog of $650M providing fleet coverage through 2028. Four newbuilding contracts are on order and a JV with NRP investors for a 4,484 TEU vessel due 2028. Market cap is approximately $529M.

Investment Thesis
Euroseas at 4.26x forward earnings is one of the cheapest equities in the market, generating 59.98% profit margins, 29.2% ROE, and LTM free cash flow of $89.3M on a $529M market cap [2]. Q2 2026 delivered $56.5M revenue with $33.2M net income ($4.74 EPS), continuing the strong profitability trend [1]. The $650M contracted revenue backlog with 87% of 2026 covered at $30,700/day and 71% of 2027 at $31,900/day provides exceptional earnings visibility for a shipping company. D/E of 39.0% reflects conservative leverage, and the 4.27% dividend yield with only 15.64% payout ratio leaves substantial room for dividend increases. Four newbuildings on order plus a JV vessel expand capacity for the next cycle while the Inspire Insight score of 42 (ALIGNED) reflects responsible operations.
The bear case centers on peak-cycle risk and structural shipping volatility. The deeply discounted Forward P/E of 4.26 reflects the market's conviction that current earnings are near-peak — analysts forecast 2026 revenue of $225.6M, a slight decline from 2025's $234.4M, as Red Sea disruption tailwinds fade and new vessel supply enters the global fleet [2]. Container shipping is inherently cyclical, and Euroseas' extraordinary 59.98% margins could compress toward historical averages of 20-30% in a rate downturn. The company's micro-cap scale (7.06M shares outstanding) and average daily volume of only 45,650 shares ($3.4M turnover) create liquidity risk for larger positions [2]. The external management structure through related-party Eurobulk Ltd. introduces governance opacity typical of Greek shipping companies [3]. The newbuilding program adds execution risk if deliveries coincide with a weaker market. Innovation (4/10) and Inspiration (3/10) scores reflect a company focused on financial returns rather than building enduring competitive advantages beyond the current cycle.
