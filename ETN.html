Company Profile
Industrials ·Specialty Industrial Machinery ·NYSE ·Ireland
Eaton Corporation is a diversified power management company operating through Electrical Americas, Electrical Global, Aerospace, Vehicle, and eMobility segments. Founded in 1911, the company provides electrical components, power distribution systems, aerospace fuel and motion control, and vehicle drivetrain products. Eaton serves customers in 180 countries and is increasingly positioned as critical infrastructure for AI data centers and electrification. Q2 2026 delivered record sales of $8.53B (+21% YoY, 14% organic + 7% acquisitions) with adjusted EPS of $3.15. Full-year 2026 guidance was raised to $13.05-$13.50 adjusted EPS with organic growth of 9-11%.

Eaton delivered record Q2 2026 with $8.53B revenue (+21% YoY), adjusted EPS of $3.15, and raised full-year guidance to $13.05-$13.50 — the company is firing on all cylinders across its electrification and data center strategy [1][2]. The $11B+ acquisition program (Boyd Thermal liquid cooling, Fibrebond power distribution, Ultra PCS, Resilient Power) positions Eaton as the comprehensive infrastructure provider for the AI data center buildout. ROE of 19.68%, TTM free cash flow of $3.93B, 100 consecutive years of dividend payments, and 17 consecutive years of dividend growth combine growth with financial stability [3]. Operating cash flow generation supports continued deleveraging from the acquisition debt. The consensus Buy rating with $475.57 average price target implies 22% upside from current levels.
The primary risk is valuation. At 31.36x forward earnings and PEG of 2.57, Eaton trades at a significant premium to industrial peers, pricing in the electrification supercycle with minimal margin for execution missteps [5]. The $11B+ acquisition program increased D/E to 105% from 55%, creating leverage risk if the AI data center buildout decelerates or integration costs exceed expectations [2]. Profit margin compressed to 12.75% from 14.9% due to acquisition integration costs, and the path to the 24.1-24.5% segment margin target requires flawless execution across four recently acquired businesses. Glassdoor reviews citing mandatory unpaid leave and employees feeling 'easily replaceable' create cultural risk beneath the strong brand — sustainability targets and Most Loved Workplace awards may not reflect the lived experience [3]. Concentration risk exists if hyperscaler AI capex moderates — the electrical backlog surge is driven by a handful of large data center customers.
