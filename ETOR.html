Company Profile
Financial Services ·Capital Markets ·NASDAQ ·Israel
eToro Group Ltd. operates a multi-asset social trading and investing platform enabling retail investors to trade equities, crypto assets, commodities, currencies, and options. The company pioneered social investing through CopyTrader and Smart Portfolios, and offers the AI companion Tori, AI Agent Portfolios, and eToro Academy. eToro serves 4.28 million funded accounts with assets under administration across 100+ countries. FY2025 net contribution was $868M (+10% YoY) with net income of $216M (+12%). Founded in 2007 in Israel, eToro completed its Nasdaq IPO in May 2025. Market cap ~$2.3B.
1,520 Employees ·www.etoro.com

Investment Thesis
eToro at 9.4x forward earnings is a founder-led fintech trading at a significant discount to peers with accelerating profitability — Q1 2026 net income of $82M (+37% YoY) and Q2 2026 net income of $53M (+77% YoY) demonstrate sustained earnings growth [1]. The balance sheet is fortress-like with $946M cash against just $35M debt (D/E 2.57%), and funded accounts grew 18% to 4.28M. CEO Assia's 19-year track record of prescient technology bets positions the company well for the AI transformation of retail investing, with Tori AI companion, Agent Portfolios, and the public API creating an 'AI-first' social investing platform [2]. The stock's 20% decline from August 2026 highs creates a more attractive entry point, with the price-to-net-cash ratio implying the market values the operating business at just $1.3B — roughly 1.5x net contribution.
The bear case centers on revenue cyclicality, AI disruption risk, and crypto dependency. Total revenue declined -24.6% as crypto trading volume normalized from the 2024-2025 boom, and 93% of FY2025 total revenue was crypto pass-through — the underlying commission business (net contribution $868M) grew only 10%, a modest pace for this valuation thesis [1]. Profit margin of 2.20% on total revenue (distorted by crypto pass-through) leaves thin margin for error, and if crypto winter deepens, both trading volumes and account growth could stall. eToro's CopyTrader differentiation faces direct AI disruption as every brokerage can now offer personalized AI investment advice, potentially commoditizing social trading [2]. The stock is down significantly from its IPO pricing, suggesting institutional skepticism about the platform's staying power. Glassdoor 3.3/5 rating suggests culture challenges that could constrain talent retention in competitive fintech markets. Israel headquarters creates geopolitical risk amid regional tensions.
