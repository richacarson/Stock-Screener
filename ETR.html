Company Profile
Utilities ·Utilities - Regulated Electric ·NYSE ·United States
Entergy Corporation is an integrated energy company engaged in electric power production, transmission, and retail distribution operations serving approximately 3 million customers across Arkansas, Louisiana, Mississippi, and Texas. Founded in 1913, headquartered in New Orleans. Entergy is a primary beneficiary of AI data center load growth with multi-GW hyperscale agreements signed with AWS, Google, and Meta across the Gulf South region.

Investment Thesis
Entergy is the premier regulated utility play on AI data center load growth in the Gulf South. Q1 2026 delivered $3.19B revenue (beating $2.89B consensus) with industrial sales surging 15% YoY — well above 10% full-year guidance — driven by hyperscale data center load additions [1]. The company affirmed 2026 adjusted EPS guidance of $4.25-$4.45 and targets 8-9% EPS CAGR through 2028, underpinned by 3.5 GW of signed electric service agreements and $10B+ Meta Louisiana deal with 5 GW scaling potential [1][3]. CEO Drew Marsh disclosed 5-10 GW of additional data center opportunities under discussion, implying years of capacity deployment ahead [2]. The 12-year dividend growth streak with 5.5% CAGR and 2.18% yield provides income support while the rate base expands at an accelerated pace.
Primary concerns center on valuation and execution risk. At 24x forward earnings, Entergy trades at a significant premium to the regulated utility sector (historical 16-18x), pricing in data center growth that must materialize on schedule. D/E of 182% reflects aggressive capital deployment — the multi-billion dollar infrastructure buildout for hyperscale facilities creates vulnerability if projects face regulatory delays, cost overruns, or if AI capex cycles slow [4]. Customer concentration on Meta is material: contract renegotiation or cancellation would directly pressure EPS growth. The Sierra Club downgrade of Entergy Louisiana's clean energy grade (B to C) for over-reliance on 2,200 MW of new gas generation reveals tension between AI growth strategy and sustainability commitments, creating potential regulatory and reputational headwinds [5]. ROE of 10.77% remains below targets, reflecting regulated return constraints that may not improve despite volume growth.
Key Catalysts
  1. Conversion of 5-10 GW disclosed data center pipeline into signed electric service agreements, extending 8-9% EPS CAGR visibility beyond 2028 with potential multiple expansion [2][3]
  2. Meta Louisiana facility scaling toward 5 GW capacity with additional $2B customer savings and multi-decade revenue visibility for Entergy Louisiana [3]
  3. Constructive rate case outcomes in Louisiana and Mississippi supporting accelerated capital deployment for data center infrastructure and renewable generation [1]
Key Risks
  1. Forward P/E of 24x represents a 40%+ premium to historical regulated utility multiples — any slowdown in hyperscaler capex or contract delays would compress the multiple toward the sector average of 16-18x
  2. Customer concentration on Meta and hyperscale operators creates binary risk: the $10B+ Meta deal and related data center agreements represent the majority of Entergy's above-market growth — a demand slowdown or renegotiation would eliminate the growth premium [2][3]
  3. Environmental backlash against 2,200 MW of new gas generation for AI data centers (Sierra Club grade downgrade from B to C) could create regulatory friction in rate cases and increase permitting risk for future gas projects [5]
