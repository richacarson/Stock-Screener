Company Profile
Consumer Discretionary ·Internet Retail ·NYSE ·United States
Etsy, Inc. operates the world's largest marketplace for unique, handmade, and vintage items, connecting active buyers with millions of creative sellers. The platform generates revenue through marketplace fees, services revenue (Etsy Ads, Etsy Payments), and seller tools. Under new CEO Kruti Patel Goyal (succeeding Josh Silverman in late 2025), the company completed the sale of Depop to eBay for $1.2B in July 2026 and announced 12% workforce reduction (220 jobs) in August 2026. Q2 2026 GMS grew 7.5% YoY to $2.6B with revenue of $668M (+6.2% YoY). Founded in 2005 and headquartered in Brooklyn, New York. Market cap of $5.75B.

Investment Thesis
Etsy is showing genuine inflection in Q2 2026 with GMS growth accelerating to 7.5% YoY ($2.6B) and revenue of $668M (+6.2% YoY), with marketplace revenue up 8.4% and services revenue up 11.2% — three consecutive quarters of YoY GMS growth [5]. At 12.39x forward P/E and a PEG ratio of 1.00, the stock trades at a meaningful discount to platform economics. The completed Depop sale to eBay for $1.2B in July 2026 simplifies the business, adds significant cash to the $1.43B already on hand, and allows new CEO Kruti Patel Goyal to focus on the core Etsy marketplace [1]. Adjusted EBITDA margin of 29.2% with guidance tightened upward to 29-30% demonstrates operating leverage. The 12% workforce reduction (220 jobs) in August 2026 signals commitment to operating efficiency and faster execution under restructured product and engineering leadership [6].
The bear case centers on structural growth limitations and competitive threats. Revenue growth has slowed from 35% in 2021 to 3.75% TTM as the COVID-era e-commerce pull-forward faded, and the Depop sale at $1.2B — a $400M loss from the $1.6B acquisition price — represents a strategic misstep [1]. Net profit margin of 9.81% is modest for a marketplace platform, and GAAP Q2 2026 EPS was negative ($-0.36) due to Depop-related write-downs [5]. AI presents a double-edged sword: while Etsy uses AI for search improvement, AI-generated products increasingly threaten the 'handmade' value proposition — new CEO Goyal faces seller backlash over AI art masquerading as handmade, and AI shopping assistants could bypass marketplace intermediaries entirely [2]. The negative shareholders' equity from buybacks and $3.08B in total debt create financial fragility despite the Depop cash infusion. The 12% layoff, while framed as 'leaning in,' reduces organizational capacity for executing the AI transformation simultaneously with a CEO transition [6]. AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience MODERATE
5
AI Risk Protection (5/9): Etsy faces meaningful AI disruption risk across multiple vectors [2]. AI-generated products (art, designs, personalized items) could flood the marketplace and dilute the 'human-made' value proposition that differentiates Etsy from Amazon — the company has already struggled with AI-generated listings masquerading as handmade goods. AI shopping assistants (ChatGPT, Google Gemini) could disintermediate marketplaces by connecting buyers directly with independent sellers, bypassing Etsy's take rate. Generative AI enables competitors to build handmade-focused marketplaces more quickly and cheaply. However, Etsy has meaningful moats: 86.6M active buyers and millions of sellers create strong two-sided network effects, the brand is deeply associated with 'unique and human' commerce, and physical goods (handmade crafts, vintage items) inherently require human creation. The company's challenge is policing AI-generated content while leveraging AI for discovery and personalization. AI Opportunity (0/1): Etsy uses AI internally for search, recommendations, and complementary product discovery, but does not generate quantifiable revenue from AI products or services sold to customers [2].
