Company Profile
Healthcare ·Health Information Services ·NYSE ·United States
Evolent Health, based in Arlington, Virginia, provides specialty care management for health plans, mainly in oncology and cardiology, through fee-based technology and services and risk-bearing 'Performance Suite' arrangements that aim to lower the total cost of care [4]. CEO Seth Blackley is building the company around AI-assisted prior authorization through its Auth Intelligence platform, which came from the 2024 Machinify deal [2]. Q2 2026 revenue was $652.5M (+46.9% YoY), and management raised FY2026 revenue guidance to $2.6-2.7B with Adjusted EBITDA of $120-135M [1]. Q3 2026 results are scheduled for November 5, 2026 [1].

Investment Thesis
Evolent at $4.45 trades at ~0.24x sales and 11x forward earnings even though revenue is accelerating: Q2 2026 revenue of $652.5M grew 46.9% YoY and beat the $601.9M consensus, and FY2026 revenue guidance was raised to $2.6-2.7B with Adjusted EBITDA of $120-135M [1][5]. Auth Intelligence gives Evolent a real AI cost lever. More than a third of formerly manual reviews now run through AI, AI approvals take minutes instead of days, and the long-term target is ~80% auto-approval, which could widen margins on fixed-fee contracts [2]. Q3 consensus revenue of ~$725-730M versus $480M a year ago points to continued strong growth [1].
The bear case is balance-sheet and business-model risk. FY2025 ended with a $535M net loss (ROE -67%, margin -24%), D/E is 253%, and tangible book value is negative (-$7.69/share) [4][5]. Risk-bearing Performance Suite contracts expose Evolent to cost-trend spikes in oncology and cardiology, and management already assumes a 40% decline in exchange membership in 2026 [2]. Prior authorization faces regulatory and political pressure, and payers could take AI authorization in-house. The company has a history of activist involvement and strategic reviews, plus a securities class action tied to the Passport Health Plan partnership [3]. For faith-aligned investors, the Inspire Impact Score of -67 (MISALIGNED, citing abortion and LGBT activism) is a major negative.
Key Catalysts
  1. Q3 2026 results on November 5, 2026 versus ~$725-730M revenue consensus and the raised $2.6-2.7B FY2026 revenue guide [1]
  2. Auth Intelligence scaling toward the ~80% auto-approval goal, building on Aetna and Highmark launches and lowering clinical-review cost per case [2]
  3. Deleveraging as Adjusted EBITDA of $120-135M and positive operating cash flow are directed at the 253% D/E balance sheet [1][5]
Key Risks
  1. Medical-cost risk in Performance Suite contracts — oncology drug and cardiology cost inflation can turn capitated contracts unprofitable, as the FY2025 $535M net loss showed [5]
  2. Regulatory backlash against prior authorization and AI in coverage decisions could limit the automation thesis or force costly human-review requirements [2]
  3. Leverage and negative tangible book value (-$7.69/share) leave little margin for error if contract losses or membership declines (40% assumed exchange drop) are worse than expected [2][5]

AI Risk Protection (5/9): Evolent's core service, clinical review of specialty prior-authorization requests for health plans, is exactly the kind of rules-plus-judgment workflow AI is automating.
