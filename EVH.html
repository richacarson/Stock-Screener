Company Profile
Healthcare ·Health Information Services ·NYSE ·United States
Evolent Health provides value-based specialty care solutions and population health services to health plans and provider organizations across the United States. The company offers specialty care management in oncology, cardiology, and musculoskeletal markets, along with administrative services including pharmacy benefits management, risk management, analytics, and its proprietary Identifi technology platform. Following the strategic divestiture of its primary care business (Evolent Care Partners) to Privia Health, the company has narrowed its focus to specialty care management.
4,800 Employees ·www.evolenthealth.com

Investment Thesis
Evolent Health's $500M+ newly-contracted annualized revenue launching in 2026 and narrowing Q1 2026 net loss (-$26.6M, EPS improving to -$0.24) suggest the specialty care management strategy is gaining commercial traction [1]. Revenue of $2.1B growing 10.3% YoY demonstrates market demand for outsourced specialty care management in oncology, cardiology, and MSK. The strategic divestiture of Evolent Care Partners to Privia Health simplifies the business model and allows management to concentrate resources on the higher-margin specialty care opportunity. At $5.74 per share, a successful path to profitability could deliver significant upside from current depressed levels.
The bear case is substantial. Evolent remains deeply unprofitable with -28.1% profit margins, -94.5% ROE, and D/E of 187% — financial metrics that reflect a company burning cash to acquire revenue [2]. The $5.74 stock price signals market skepticism about the path to profitability. Large payer competitors (Optum, Evernorth) are building in-house specialty care management capabilities that could reduce demand for Evolent's services. The healthcare regulatory environment remains volatile, with ACA policy changes and Medicaid redeterminations creating headwinds for value-based care adoption. The company's heavy debt load limits financial flexibility and increases vulnerability to any revenue disruption. Without a clear timeline to sustained positive earnings, EVH remains a speculative position.
