Company Profile
Healthcare ·Health Information Services ·NYSE ·United States
Evolent Health provides value-based specialty care solutions and population health services to health plans and provider organizations across the United States. The company offers specialty care management in oncology, cardiology, and musculoskeletal markets, along with administrative services and its proprietary Identifi technology platform. Q2 2026 revenue surged 46.9% YoY to $652.5M, with raised full-year guidance of $2.6-2.7B. A new oncology Performance Suite partnership covering 1.5 million lives across 11 states is expected to generate ~$300M in annualized revenue. Oncology now represents 65% of revenue, up from 36% in 2025.
