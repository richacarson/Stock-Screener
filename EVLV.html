Company Profile
Industrials ·Security & Protection Services ·NASDAQ ·United States
Evolv Technologies develops and deploys AI-powered touchless weapons detection systems for high-traffic public venues. Its flagship Evolv Express uses sensor fusion and machine learning to detect concealed weapons without requiring visitors to stop or empty pockets. The company serves 1,200+ customers across education, healthcare, sports, entertainment, and government sectors and has screened over 4 billion people. Evolv went public via SPAC in 2021 and has undergone significant governance upheaval, including termination of founding CEO Peter George in October 2024 following an SEC investigation into accounting misconduct. New CEO John Kedzierski took over in December 2024. H1 2026 revenue reached $90.1M (+40% YoY) with full-year guidance raised to $180-185M.

Investment Thesis
Evolv is a turnaround story in a compelling end market — AI-powered weapons detection at public venues addresses a genuine societal need, and the operational recovery under CEO John Kedzierski is delivering accelerating results. H1 2026 revenue of $90.1M (+40% YoY) with raised full-year guidance of $180-185M and ARR of $148-150M demonstrate the core business model works when executed with integrity [1][2]. The transition to 50% pure subscription for new deployments strengthens recurring revenue quality, adjusted EBITDA guidance of $15-16M signals a path toward profitability, and Q2 2026 delivered a record quarter for new customer additions. The addressable market — schools, hospitals, corporate campuses, entertainment venues — remains massively underpenetrated, with school safety mandates in multiple states creating a secular demand tailwind.
The bear case centers on credibility risk, unproven profitability, and regulatory overhang. The SEC investigation into accounting misconduct (eight quarters of manipulated revenue, Q2 2022-Q2 2024), FTC probe into AI efficacy claims, and multiple investor lawsuits remain unresolved — adverse outcomes could result in restatements, fines, marketing restrictions, or criminal referrals [4]. The BBC investigation disputing Evolv's weapons detection accuracy raises fundamental questions about the product's value proposition — if the AI does not reliably detect concealed weapons, the entire business model collapses. Net profit margin of -10.8% in Q1 2026 (improving from -22.7% in FY2025) means the company still burns cash despite rapid revenue growth, and the path to sustainable GAAP profitability is uncertain. At $5.18 and ~$930M market cap, the stock prices in significant future growth that assumes resolution of all regulatory and credibility challenges. Competition from established security companies integrating AI into proven platforms could compress margins and cap market share upside.
