Company Profile
Industrials ·Security & Protection Services ·NASDAQ ·United States
Evolv Technologies develops, manufactures, and sells AI-powered security screening systems for high-traffic venues. Its flagship Evolv Express uses advanced sensors and machine learning to detect weapons in unstructured people flows without requiring individuals to stop, remove items, or empty pockets. The company serves 1,200+ customers across education, healthcare, sports, entertainment, and government sectors and has scanned over 4 billion people since 2019.

Investment Thesis
Evolv Technologies is the category leader in AI-powered touchless weapon detection, serving 1,200+ customers and accelerating revenue growth to 45% YoY in Q1 2026 ($46.3M) with ending ARR of $127.3M [1]. Management raised full-year 2026 guidance to $175-180M revenue (20-23% growth) and reaffirmed ARR of $145-150M, while achieving adjusted EBITDA of $3.9M (8.5% margin) in Q1 [1]. The transition to ~50% pure subscription model strengthens recurring revenue quality and operating leverage. With $49.1M cash, improving unit economics, and expanding into bag screening (eXpedite) and analytics (Insights), Evolv is approaching a profitability inflection point in a growing market driven by school safety mandates and venue security regulations. The technology has screened 4+ billion people, creating a proprietary data moat for AI algorithm improvement.
The bear case centers on profitability uncertainty and leadership instability. Despite 45% revenue growth in Q1 2026, Evolv posted a $5.0M GAAP net loss and ROE remains deeply negative — the path to GAAP profitability remains unclear beyond adjusted EBITDA positivity [1]. The forced CEO termination in October 2024 for cultural reasons, followed by two leadership changes in three months, creates organizational risk and execution uncertainty under new CEO John Kedzierski (7 months tenure) [3]. The $1.1B market cap implies ~6x forward revenue, which demands sustained 20%+ growth that could be challenged by traditional security companies (Smiths Detection, Garrett, CEIA) adding AI capabilities to their established customer relationships. Evolv's technology has faced scrutiny over detection accuracy, and any high-profile weapon incident at a protected venue would be catastrophic for the brand. Stock-based compensation remains elevated, diluting shareholder value. Glassdoor rating of 3.1/5 with 33% employee recommendation rate signals cultural dysfunction that could impair talent retention and product development.
Key Catalysts
  1. Q1 2026 revenue acceleration to 45% YoY with raised full-year guidance to $175-180M and reaffirmed year-end ARR of $145-150M, demonstrating sustained commercial momentum [1]
  2. School safety mandate legislation in multiple U.S. states could accelerate education sector adoption, Evolv's fastest-growing vertical, with additional government funding for security screening
  3. Path to full-year GAAP profitability as subscription revenue scales and operating leverage improves from the Q1 2026 adjusted EBITDA of $3.9M (8.5% margin) [1]
Key Risks
  1. Leadership instability — three CEO changes in 14 months (George terminated, Ellenbogen interim, Kedzierski appointed December 2024) creates execution risk during a critical scaling phase, compounded by 3.1/5 Glassdoor rating [3]
  2. Detection accuracy scrutiny — any high-profile weapon incident at an Evolv-protected venue would severely damage brand credibility and could trigger customer defections in a trust-dependent market
  3. GAAP profitability remains elusive despite adjusted EBITDA turning positive, with Q1 2026 net loss of $5.0M and elevated stock-based compensation continuing to dilute shareholder value [1]

AI Risk Protection (7/9): Evolv IS an AI company — its entire product line is built on proprietary AI/ML weapon detection algorithms trained on millions of scans across 1,200+ customer sites [1][2]. AI does not threaten this business; AI IS the business.
