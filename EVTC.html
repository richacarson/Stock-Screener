Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Infrastructure ·NYQ ·United States
EVERTEC is a leading full-service transaction processing and financial technology company in Latin America, Puerto Rico, and the Caribbean. The company provides payment processing, merchant acquiring, business process management, core banking, and IT services across 26 countries, processing approximately 10 billion transactions annually. Headquartered in San Juan, Puerto Rico, EVERTEC transformed from a Caribbean-focused processor into a pan-Latin American platform through its 2023 acquisition of Sinqia. Q2 2026 revenue grew 20% YoY to $274.8M with adjusted EPS of $1.05, and management raised full-year revenue growth guidance to 14.5%-15.6% on a constant currency basis. Stock trades at $30.89 with a market cap of $1.85B.

Investment Thesis
EVERTEC at $30.89 with a forward P/E of 7.06x represents a deeply discounted entry into the leading Latin American payment technology platform, with forward EPS of $4.38 implying 184% earnings recovery from GAAP-depressed trailing EPS of $1.54 (impaired by Q2 acquisition charges) [1][4]. The Sinqia-fueled transformation is delivering — Q2 2026 revenue grew 20% YoY to $274.8M with adjusted EPS of $1.05 (+18%), the Latin America segment surged 52% YoY, and management raised full-year constant currency growth guidance to 14.5%-15.6% [1][2]. The estimated 5-year revenue CAGR of 17% demonstrates that the acquisition thesis is translating into sustained growth, while the ATH debit network monopoly in Puerto Rico provides a stable revenue base. The Inspire Impact Score of 48 (ALIGNED) with 9 positive attributions and zero negatives makes EVERTEC attractive for faith-based portfolios. CEO Schuessler's 11-year tenure with recent internal promotions to COO, CFO, and CIO signals organizational maturity and succession readiness.
The bear case centers on GAAP profitability weakness, leverage risk, and competitive pressures. Trailing GAAP EPS of $1.54 versus adjusted EPS guidance of $3.94-$4.04 reveals a massive gap between reported and adjusted profitability — Q2 2026 GAAP net income was just $5.4M ($0.09/share) versus $40.5M a year earlier, driven by impairment charges, higher interest expense, and integration costs [1]. D/E of 184% reflects acquisition-related leverage that must be managed down while maintaining growth investment, and interest expense is a material drag on earnings. Profit margin of 9.8% is below best-in-class payment processors, reflecting the combined effects of acquisition amortization and the lower-margin profile of some LatAm operations. Revenue growth partially benefits from currency tailwinds ($9.1M FX boost in Q2 from Brazilian real strength), and constant currency growth of 16% is strong but lower than reported 20% [1]. Latin American operations carry inherent political, regulatory, and currency risk across 26 jurisdictions. The Business Solutions segment revenue declined due to the 10% discount to Popular that took effect in Q4 2025, pressuring the company's most stable revenue line [1]. Competition from global fintech platforms (Stripe, Adyen, dLocal) and local digital wallet providers could compress processing margins in EVERTEC's fastest-growing markets.

AI Risk Protection (7/9): EVERTEC's core business — payment processing infrastructure, merchant acquiring, core banking systems, and transaction processing across Latin America — is structurally protected from AI disruption by high switching costs, regulatory compliance requirements, and deep integration into clients' financial operations [1]. Payment technology requires physical POS terminal infrastructure, the ATH debit network in Puerto Rico, banking relationships, and government-regulated processing licenses across 26 countries that AI cannot replicate or displace. The Sinqia acquisition adds regulatory knowledge across 26 Latin American jurisdictions as an additional defensive layer [2]. However, AI-native fintech competitors (Stripe, Adyen, dLocal) could theoretically compress margins in commoditized processing segments, and large global payment networks increasingly deploy AI to optimize their competing offerings. AI Opportunity (0/1): EVERTEC has acknowledged internal AI efficiency gains but has not yet generated quantifiable AI-related revenue, with management explicitly deferring AI financial contributions to its 2027 outlook [2].
