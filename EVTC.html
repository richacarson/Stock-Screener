Company Profile
Technology ·Information Technology Services ·NYSE ·Puerto Rico
EVERTEC is a leading full-service transaction processing company in Latin America and the Caribbean, providing payment technology, merchant acquiring, business process management, and IT services. Headquartered in San Juan, Puerto Rico, the company transformed from a Caribbean-focused processor into a pan-Latin American platform through its 2023 acquisition of Sinqia, now serving clients across 26 countries. Q2 2026 revenue grew 20% YoY to $275M, with the Latin America segment surging 52%, and management raised full-year 2026 revenue growth guidance to 16.5%-17.5%. The company serves approximately 2.8 million merchants and processes billions of transactions annually.
4,600 Employees ·www.evertecinc.com

Investment Thesis
EVERTEC at 8.08x forward earnings represents a compelling value play on Latin American payment technology growth, with the Sinqia-fueled transformation delivering Q2 2026 revenue growth of 20% YoY and adjusted EBITDA margins expanding 320 basis points to 30.3% [1]. The company's pan-LatAm platform now spans 26 countries with major institutional wins including Transbank in Chile and Clip in Mexico, validating the acquisition thesis [2]. ROE of 23.64% and an estimated 5-year revenue CAGR of 17% demonstrate that the growth is both profitable and sustainable, while the Forward PEG of 1.02 suggests the market has not yet fully priced in the company's accelerating growth trajectory [4]. Management's confidence is underscored by raising full-year 2026 revenue growth guidance to 16.5%-17.5% and expanding the share repurchase program.
The bear case centers on integration execution risk, currency volatility, and competitive pressure. D/E of 155% reflects acquisition-related leverage that must be managed down while maintaining growth investment — the tax and JV impacts that pressured Q2 2026 GAAP earnings illustrate the gap between adjusted and reported profitability [1]. Latin American operations carry inherent FX risk across multiple volatile currencies (BRL, CLP, MXN, COP), and political or regulatory changes in any of 26 countries could disrupt operations. The payment processing industry faces secular competitive pressure from global fintech players (Stripe, Adyen) and local digital wallets, though EVERTEC's deep institutional relationships and regulatory compliance infrastructure provide meaningful defensive moats. Net profit margin of approximately 13% is below best-in-class payment processors, reflecting acquisition-related amortization and the lower-margin profile of some LatAm operations.
