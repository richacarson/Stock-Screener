Company Profile
Industrials ·Aerospace & Defense ·NYSE ·United Kingdom
Vertical Aerospace Ltd. is a pre-revenue British aerospace company designing, manufacturing, and certifying the VX4, a zero-operating-emission electric vertical takeoff and landing (eVTOL) aircraft for the advanced air mobility market. The company holds roughly 1,500 pre-orders from customers including American Airlines, Avolon, Bristow, GOL, and Japan Airlines, but pushed its VX4 type-certification target back a year to 2029 and disclosed material 'going concern' doubts, with cash burn far outpacing its roughly $85-117 million cash position and shareholders approving a tenfold increase in authorized share capital to fund future dilutive raises.

Investment Thesis
Vertical Aerospace offers pure-play, high-beta exposure to the eVTOL/urban air mobility category, with roughly 1,500 pre-orders from credible aviation customers (American Airlines, Avolon, Bristow, GOL, Japan Airlines) and genuine 2026 technical progress — piloted transition flights of two full-scale VX4 aircraft and a Farnborough Airshow demonstration [1][2]. The August 2026 appointment of former Airbus Commercial Aircraft CEO Fabrice Bregier as chair, explicitly to convert technical progress and UK Ministry of Defence interest into a scaled commercial and defense business, is a credibility upgrade for a company that had lacked heavyweight aerospace-programme leadership at the board level [2]. AI structurally cannot disrupt Vertical's core certification and manufacturing activity, so if the company survives its funding gap, its business model itself is not at risk from AI substitution.
The bear case is overwhelming on fundamentals: Vertical has zero revenue, negative stockholders' equity (-$121.4M), and management has disclosed 'material uncertainty' about its ability to continue as a going concern, with roughly $85-117M of cash against projected 12-month operating cash outflows of $175-235M [1][4]. The VX4 type-certification timeline has already slipped a year to 2029, and shareholders have approved increasing authorized share capital tenfold (200 million to 1 billion shares), setting up dilution estimated at 43-52% in future capital raises; multiple securities-fraud and shareholder investigations add further legal overhang [4]. With Infrastructure scoring 0 due to negative equity and no revenue, and Innovation and Inspiration both scoring in the WEAK range for lack of disclosed evidence, Vertical remains a speculative, story-driven holding unsuitable for the core IOWN screen until it either reaches a funded path to certification or demonstrates a credible strategic partnership or acquisition.
