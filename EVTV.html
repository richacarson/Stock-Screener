Azio AI Holdings, Inc. (formerly Envirotech Vehicles, Inc.) is a technology infrastructure company focused on developing, owning, and operating artificial intelligence data centers, enterprise GPU compute infrastructure, digital power solutions, and digital asset mining operations. The company completed its merger with Azio AI ahead of schedule in 2026, transitioning from its original electric vehicle manufacturing business. CEO Chris Young and President Simon Yu now lead the combined entity. The development footprint exceeds 548 acres with potential to support up to 500 MW of AI infrastructure capacity. The company secured a $107M contract from a Southeast Asian government and a $27.9M AI infrastructure agreement with Power Champion (scalable to $100M). In July 2026, the company changed its name from Envirotech Vehicles to Azio AI Holdings and is transitioning its ticker from EVTV to AZIO. FY2025 revenue was $5.94M (+218% YoY) with a net loss of -$39.13M. Market cap approximately $20M.

Investment Thesis
Azio AI Holdings offers a speculative play on AI infrastructure at a micro-cap valuation ($20M market cap). The company completed its transformative merger with Azio AI, securing $134M+ in contracts including a $107M Southeast Asian government deal and a $27.9M Power Champion agreement scalable to $100M [2]. The 548-acre development campus with potential for 500 MW of AI infrastructure capacity positions the company in the rapidly growing AI data center market. Revenue grew 218% to $5.94M in FY2025, and the new leadership team (CEO Chris Young, President Simon Yu) brings AI infrastructure expertise [2]. The Inspire Impact Score of 62 (ALIGNED) reflects the company's legacy environmental attributions from its EV manufacturing era. At 0.46x price-to-book, the stock is deeply discounted relative to the potential value of its infrastructure assets and contract pipeline.
The bear case is overwhelming. Azio AI Holdings lost $39.13M on $5.94M revenue in FY2025, producing a profit margin of -659% [1]. The company had only $359K cash at FY2025 year-end and faces NASDAQ compliance issues related to stockholders' equity deficiency below the $2.5M minimum. Revenue TTM has actually declined to $3.70M as the legacy EV business winds down and AI infrastructure contracts have not yet generated meaningful revenue [3]. The $107M and $27.9M contracts represent customer deposits and agreements, not delivered infrastructure or recognized revenue — execution risk is extreme for a company that has never built or operated AI infrastructure at scale. The market is dominated by well-capitalized competitors (Equinix, CoreWeave, hyperscalers) against which a $20M micro-cap has no competitive advantage. The stock has declined from $5.07 to $1.54 (-70%) over the past year, reflecting persistent market skepticism despite the contract announcements [3]. Daily trading volume of approximately $1.1M is barely above the liquidity floor.
