Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Silver ·NYSE ·Canada
Endeavour Silver Corp is a mid-tier precious metals mining company with operations in Mexico, Peru, Chile, and the United States. The company transformed from a two-mine operator into a three-mine producer following the commissioning of the Terronera mine in October 2025 and the Kolpa acquisition in May 2025. Q2 2026 delivered record results: revenue of US$212.1M (up 149% YoY), net earnings of US$66.5M (vs. net loss of $20.5M a year earlier), and mine operating cash flow of $99.9M (up 336%). Production guidance for 2026 is 8.3-8.9M oz silver and 46K-48K oz gold, representing approximately 30% growth. Headquartered in Vancouver, Canada.

Investment Thesis
Endeavour Silver is in a dramatic profitability inflection driven by the successful commissioning of Terronera and the Kolpa acquisition. Q2 2026 delivered record results: revenue of $212.1M (up 149% YoY), net earnings of $66.5M (vs. net loss of $20.5M a year ago), and mine operating cash flow of $99.9M (up 336%) [1]. Production reached 1.94M oz silver (up 31%) and 10,474 oz gold (up 35%) with guidance for 8.3-8.9M oz silver in 2026. At a forward P/E of just 7.50x, the stock is deeply discounted relative to the emerging profitability as Terronera ramps to full capacity. D/E of 38.42% is conservative for a miner that just completed a major mine build. Silver's dual role as precious metal and critical industrial input for solar, electronics, and AI infrastructure creates favorable long-term demand dynamics. Management's courageous $350M+ Terronera investment is now delivering returns, validating the transformation strategy.
The bear case acknowledges that the profitability inflection is real but not yet reflected in TTM metrics — ROE of -3.88% and profit margin of -3.47% still include loss-making quarters from the pre-Terronera period [5]. AISC must continue declining from elevated levels as Terronera reaches steady-state production for the profitability thesis to sustain. The $350M convertible notes issued in December 2025 introduce dilution risk if the stock price exceeds the conversion threshold and add interest expense during the ramp-up phase. Mining execution risk remains significant — underground operations in Mexico and Peru carry operational, regulatory, and geopolitical risks, and Q2 adjusted EPS of $0.15 fell short of the $0.19 consensus estimate despite record revenue [1]. Silver price volatility is amplified through EXK's high beta, meaning a correction below $30/oz would compress margins and delay the earnings recovery.
