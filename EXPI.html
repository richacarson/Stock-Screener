Company Profile
Real Estate ·Real Estate Services ·NASDAQ ·United States
AGNT, Inc. (renamed from eXp World Holdings in June 2026) operates the cloud-based eXp Realty brokerage across North American Realty, International Realty, and Other Affiliated Services segments, serving agents in the Americas, Europe, the Middle East, Asia-Pacific, and South Africa with no physical branch offices. In 2026 the company acquired the NextHome franchise network (500+ franchisees, ~4,900 agents), added a stated agent focus under new CEO Leo Pareja, and continues to operate FrameVR.io and the SUCCESS-branded media and education properties. Founded in 2008 and headquartered in Bellingham, Washington, under Founder/Chairman Glenn Sanford.

Investment Thesis
AGNT (formerly eXp World Holdings) generated $4.96B in TTM revenue (+8.1% YoY) with zero total debt and $111.16M cash, and the 2026 NextHome acquisition adds 500+ franchisees and ~4,900 agents to the network alongside 27% YoY international revenue growth to $40.2M [1]. Q1 2026 revenue beat estimates and operating losses improved 15% YoY on cost-saving initiatives, and management's FY2026 guidance of $4.85-5.15B suggests the business is stabilizing after the 2021 housing-boom peak. The 4.84% dividend yield and zero-debt balance sheet provide a income floor for patient holders willing to look through near-term losses.
The bear case is severe. The stock trades at $4.13, down roughly 90% from its 2021 peak, and the company has posted a net loss for four consecutive years (2022 TTM decline through 2026) with ROE of -0.61% and profit margin of -0.35% — a Forward P/E of 413x reflects how close to breakeven, not how attractive, the stock is [1][3]. The dividend has been flat since August 2023 (payout ratio is undefined against negative earnings, funded from cash rather than profit) and is vulnerable to a cut if losses persist. An unresolved Delaware Court of Chancery derivative lawsuit over board oversight of alleged workplace sexual misconduct creates open-ended governance and reputational risk, and AI-powered valuation and transaction tools threaten the thin-margin, high-agent-count brokerage model that AGNT depends on for scale [2][3].
Key Catalysts
  1. NextHome franchise integration adding ~4,900 agents and a second, less commission-compression-exposed revenue stream (franchise fees) starting FY2026
  2. Return toward breakeven if FY2026 guidance of $4.85-5.15B in revenue is achieved alongside continued 15%+ YoY operating-loss improvement from cost cuts
  3. International expansion (27% YoY growth to $40.2M) diversifying revenue away from the commission-compressed U.S. market
Key Risks
  1. Four consecutive years of net losses despite nearly $5B in revenue raise structural questions about whether the cloud-brokerage/revenue-share model can generate sustainable operating profit
  2. Unresolved Delaware Court of Chancery derivative lawsuit over alleged workplace sexual misconduct creates open financial and reputational liability
  3. AI-powered property search, valuation, and transaction platforms threaten to disintermediate the traditional agent model that AGNT's 85,000+ agent network depends on, compounding post-NAR-settlement commission compression
