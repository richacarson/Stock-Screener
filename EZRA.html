Company Profile
Financial Services ·Insurance Brokers ·NASDAQ ·United States
Reliance Global Group is a micro-cap InsurTech company headquartered in Lakewood, New Jersey, that acquires and operates wholesale and retail insurance agencies under its RELI Exchange brand and the direct-to-consumer 5MinuteInsure.com platform, serving 46 states through 30+ carriers. CEO Ezra Beyman is repositioning the company as an 'AI-native' insurance platform, having recently hired a CTO from Coinbase/Capital One and launched an AI agent that automates back-office insurance tasks. The company executed a 1-for-40 reverse stock split in May 2026 to regain Nasdaq listing compliance and continues to post net losses and declining consolidated revenue following divestitures of non-core agencies.

Investment Thesis
Reliance Global Group is a speculative micro-cap attempting to reposition itself as an AI-native InsurTech platform. The July 2026 launch of a functioning AI agent for insurance back-office automation, combined with the hiring of a CTO from Coinbase and Capital One and a newly formed AI product team, gives the company a credible (if unproven) technology story [2]. Underlying organic metrics at RELI Exchange are genuinely positive — health policies written up 72% YoY and P&C premium up 36% YoY, with the broker network nearly quintupling to ~300 agency partners since 2022 — suggesting the core distribution platform has real product-market traction even as the consolidated entity shrinks [1].
The bear case dominates here. Reliance triggers IOWN's hard liquidity floor — average daily dollar volume of roughly $94,500 is nowhere near the $1,000,000 threshold, meaning Infrastructure scores zero regardless of any other metric [4]. The company executed a 1-for-40 reverse stock split in May 2026 just to maintain Nasdaq's minimum listing price, and the stock has since fallen back to roughly $0.162, suggesting the reverse split did not resolve underlying investor concerns and that a new $50M equity line of credit is diluting shareholders further [4][5]. Consolidated commission income fell 31.7% YoY to $2.1M in Q2 2026 with a $1.99M net loss against just $6.65M of stockholders' equity, leaving very little balance-sheet cushion [1]. Combined with no dividend, no insider buying, and no meaningful market share, Reliance is a high-risk, pre-profitability turnaround story that does not meet IOWN's bar for capital allocation regardless of the genuine AI narrative.

  3. Persistent net losses ($1.99M in Q2 2026 alone) against a thin equity base (~$6.65M) leave minimal room for execution missteps in the AI pivot before additional dilutive or distressed financing becomes necessary [1]
