Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Infrastructure ·NASDAQ ·United States
FatPipe is a pioneer in enterprise-class, application-aware, secure software-defined wide area network (SD-WAN) solutions for enterprises, service providers, government organizations, and middle-market companies. Founded in 2002 and led by CEO Dr. Ragula Bhaskar for 24+ years, the company completed its Nasdaq IPO in April 2025. FY2026 (ended March 2026) revenue was $19.21M (+18% YoY) with net income of $5.0M (+154%). Q4 FY2026 revenue surged 90% YoY to $7.2M. The company is expanding into cybersecurity and targeting a total addressable market expected to exceed $17.6B by 2030.

Investment Thesis
FatPipe at 10.14x forward earnings is a high-growth micro-cap with exceptional financial metrics — 26.80% profit margin, 23.26% ROE, 20.81% debt-to-equity, and 27.8% revenue growth — that would score strongly on Infrastructure if not for the liquidity floor [4]. FY2026 revenue grew 18% to $19.21M with Q4 surging 90% YoY to $7.2M, and Q1 FY2027 continued the momentum at 28% growth [1][2]. The company was an SD-WAN pioneer before the category existed, and recurring revenue (Monthly Recurring Billings up 56% YoY in Q4) is building a more predictable revenue base. The $17.6B+ TAM expected by 2030 provides significant runway if FatPipe can capture even a fraction of market share. CEO Dr. Bhaskar's 24.5-year tenure provides founder-operator alignment with long-term shareholders [3].
The bear case is dominated by illiquidity, scale disadvantage, and competitive intensity. Average daily dollar volume of ~$492K falls below the $1M IOWN liquidity floor, making position entry and exit impractical at institutional scale — the stock would need to roughly double its trading volume to become investable [4]. FatPipe's $74M market cap and $19M revenue are dwarfed by SD-WAN competitors: Cisco ($55B+ revenue), Fortinet ($6B+), Palo Alto Networks ($8B+), and VMware (now Broadcom). These competitors can outspend FatPipe on R&D by orders of magnitude and bundle SD-WAN with broader security and networking platforms. AI-enhanced networking from major competitors (Cisco AI Network Analytics, Juniper Mist AI) could commoditize basic SD-WAN functionality. The company's Nasdaq listing is less than 18 months old, providing minimal public market track record. The Inspire Impact Score of 0 (MIXED) reflects insufficient ESG data for a newly public micro-cap rather than active concerns. Q4 revenue concentration ($7.2M of $19.2M annual) suggests revenue lumpiness that could lead to volatile future quarters.
