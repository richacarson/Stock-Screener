FBS Global Limited is a Singapore-based green building contractor and interior fit-out specialist providing construction and engineering services including supply of building materials and precast concrete components, recycling of construction and industrial wastes, and pavement consultancy. The company undertakes interior fit-out works for institutional, residential, commercial, and industrial building projects. FY2025 revenue was S$25.86M (+87% YoY) with a net loss of S$2.04M. The stock trades near all-time lows at ~$0.60, received a Nasdaq deficiency notice in April 2026 for failing the $1.00 minimum bid price requirement, and has until October 2026 to regain compliance. Founded 1996, headquartered in Singapore.
