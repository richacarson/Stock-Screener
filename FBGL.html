FibroBiologics is a clinical-stage biotechnology company developing fibroblast cell-based therapies and fibroblast-derived materials for chronic diseases. The pipeline includes CYWC628 (wound healing, Phase 1/2 in Australia), with preclinical programs in psoriasis, multiple sclerosis, burn treatment, and disc degeneration. The company holds 270+ US and international patents. Following a 1-for-20 reverse stock split in April 2026 to maintain Nasdaq compliance, the stock trades near penny-stock levels. Founded by CEO Pete O'Heeron, headquartered in Houston, Texas.
