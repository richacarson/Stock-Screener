Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
FibroBiologics is a clinical-stage biotechnology company developing fibroblast-based cell therapies and fibroblast-derived materials for chronic diseases including diabetic foot ulcers, multiple sclerosis, degenerative disc disease, psoriasis, and longevity-related indications. The company holds 270+ US and international patents. Lead program CYWC628 achieved first patient dosing in a Phase 1/2 trial for diabetic foot ulcers in Q2 2026, with interim results expected 2H 2026 and primary safety/efficacy data by year-end. The company strengthened its balance sheet with $9M private placement and $3M public offering. Q2 2026 cash was $3.5M. Founded by CEO Pete O'Heeron, headquartered in Houston, Texas.
