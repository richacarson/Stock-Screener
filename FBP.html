Company Profile
Financials ·Banks - Regional ·NYSE ·United States
First BanCorp is a diversified financial holding company headquartered in San Juan, Puerto Rico. Through its subsidiary FirstBank, it provides personal, commercial, and corporate banking services across Puerto Rico, the U.S. Virgin Islands, and Florida. Founded in 1948, the company operates through six segments including Commercial and Corporate Banking, Consumer Banking, Mortgage Banking, Treasury and Investments, United States Operations, and Virgin Islands Operations.

Investment Thesis
First BanCorp is a high-quality regional bank delivering accelerating earnings growth from a dominant Puerto Rico franchise with Florida expansion optionality [1]. Q2 2026 net income of $96.1M ($0.62/share) grew 24% YoY with a 39% net margin, and the bank reached record pretax preprovision income [1]. The balance sheet is exceptionally clean (D/E 10%, ROE 18.03%) with conservative dividend coverage (33.76% payout ratio) [3]. The stock hit a new 52-week high on July 28, 2026, reflecting market recognition of the earnings quality. At 12.12x trailing earnings with a 2.8% dividend yield growing at ~32% CAGR, FBP offers a compelling income-plus-growth profile. Puerto Rico's economic stabilization and AI-driven data center investment on the island could provide additional tailwinds for commercial lending demand.
The bear case focuses on revenue growth limitations, geographic concentration, and valuation after a significant run-up. Revenue grew only 4.35% in 2025 and the 5-year CAGR is just 3.5%, reflecting the mature nature of Puerto Rico's banking market [4]. Revenue declined 22% from $1.12B (2023) to $877M (2024) before partially recovering, demonstrating sensitivity to interest rate dynamics. The stock's new 52-week high and 12x earnings leave limited margin of safety — analyst consensus projects a bearish outlook despite the 39% net margin [3]. Net insider selling of $1.9M across 22 transactions in the past year is a concerning signal from management [3]. The 6-year dividend track record, while growing rapidly, lacks the institutional depth of established dividend aristocrats. Puerto Rico and USVI operations remain vulnerable to catastrophic hurricanes and the territory's ongoing fiscal challenges.
Key Catalysts
  1. Continued 20%+ EPS growth from expanding net interest margin and commercial loan demand in Puerto Rico and Florida, building on Q2 2026's record pretax preprovision income
  2. Dividend growth continuation — recent increase to $0.18/quarter ($0.72 annualized in 2025, up from $0.12 in 2019) with a 33.76% payout ratio providing significant room for further increases
  3. Puerto Rico economic stabilization and potential AI data center investment on the island driving commercial lending demand and deposit growth
Key Risks
  1. Revenue growth of only 3.5% 5yr CAGR reflects the structural growth ceiling of Puerto Rico's banking market — the island's population has declined ~15% since 2010, limiting long-term deposit and lending growth
  2. Net insider selling of $1.9M across 22 transactions with zero insider buys in the past year signals management may view the stock as fully valued at current levels
  3. Hurricane exposure in Puerto Rico and USVI creates tail risk — a Category 5 storm could simultaneously impact loan quality, property values, and the bank's own infrastructure
