ENvue Medical, Inc. (formerly NanoVibronix, Inc.) is a micro-cap medical device company focused on non-invasive biological response-activating devices targeting biofilm prevention, pain therapy, wound healing, and enteral feeding tube placement. Its product portfolio includes UroShield (ultrasound-based catheter biofilm prevention), PainShield (therapeutic ultrasound pain device), and the FDA-cleared ENvue electromagnetic feeding tube guidance system. Renamed in December 2025 after acquiring ENvue Medical, the company has 12 employees and generated just $1.9M in TTM revenue with negative margins and going concern disclosures. Headquartered in Tyler, Texas.
