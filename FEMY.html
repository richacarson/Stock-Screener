Company Profile
Healthcare ·Medical Devices ·NMS ·United States
Femasys Inc. is a medical technology company focused on women's reproductive health, developing and commercializing FemVue (saline-air device for fallopian tube assessment), FemaSeed (directed insemination device), and FemBloc (permanent birth control candidate in clinical development). Founded in 2004 by CEO Kathy Lee-Sepsick and headquartered in Suwanee, Georgia, the company generated H1 2026 revenue of just $756,716 with a net loss of $3.6M and an accumulated deficit of $149.4M. Femasys holds over 200 global patents and raised $30M in an August 2026 private placement to fund continued operations.

Investment Thesis
Femasys at $2.05 with a $17M market cap is a speculative micro-cap medical device company with a genuine healthcare mission but extreme financial risk. The FemBloc permanent birth control candidate addresses a real market gap created by Essure's withdrawal, and the August 2026 private placement raised $30M (potentially $90M if warrants are exercised), providing runway to advance clinical development [2]. The company's 200+ patent portfolio covering women's health devices, combined with in-house manufacturing capability and CE Mark/Health Canada approval for FemVue MINI, represents intellectual property value that exceeds the current market capitalization [3].
The bear case is overwhelming for any position-sizing beyond speculative. H1 2026 revenue was just $756,716 with a net loss of $3.6M, implying the company burns approximately $7M+ annually against negligible commercial revenue [1][2]. The accumulated deficit of $149.4M and pre-raise cash balance of $1.44M triggered going concern warnings, and Femasys only recently regained Nasdaq listing compliance. FemBloc FDA approval timing is uncertain, commercial scaling of FemVue and FemaSeed has failed to gain traction after years on the market (Q2 2026 sales fell 18.9% YoY), and the company will likely require additional dilutive capital raises before reaching breakeven. The stock's $17M market cap and 711K average daily volume create severe liquidity risk for any institutional position.
Key Catalysts
  1. FemBloc FDA approval for permanent birth control could open a significant market opportunity in the post-Essure landscape, with no direct non-surgical competitor currently available in the U.S. market [3]
  2. August 2026 private placement of $30M extends operating runway, with potential $90M total if warrants are fully exercised for cash, funding clinical development and commercial expansion [2]
  3. CE Mark and Health Canada approval for FemVue MINI provides international revenue diversification opportunity with a compact, lower-cost device targeting broader OB/GYN office adoption
Key Risks
  1. Going concern doubt with $1.44M pre-raise cash and $149.4M accumulated deficit — company has never achieved profitability in 22 years of operation and burns ~$7M+ annually against sub-$2M revenue [1][2]
  2. FemBloc FDA regulatory risk is existential — single-product dependency for future viability, with uncertain clinical trial outcomes and no guaranteed timeline for approval or market authorization
  3. Commercial traction failure — FemVue and FemaSeed have been on the market for years but Q2 2026 sales fell 18.9% YoY to just $332K, suggesting the company cannot scale sales even with approved products [2]
