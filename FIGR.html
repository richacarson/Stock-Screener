Company Profile
Financial Services ·Capital Markets ·NMS ·United States
Figure Technology Solutions is a blockchain-based fintech founded in 2018 by former SoFi CEO Mike Cagney, offering technology-enabled home equity lending (HELOC, cash-out refinance, DSCR loans) paired with a capital-markets distribution marketplace (Figure Connect), plus an emerging digital-asset exchange offering interest-bearing stablecoin deposits and blockchain-based direct stock lending. The company IPO'd in September 2025 raising $663M under CEO Michael Tannenbaum (former Brex COO) and CFO Macrina Kgil (former Blockchain.com CFO). Q2 2026 revenue reached approximately $199M with record profitability and adjusted EBITDA margin above 50%, while consumer loan marketplace volume grew 132% YoY. TTM revenue is approximately $619M with 38% profit margins.
602 Employees ·www.figure.com

Investment Thesis
Figure at $27.13 trades at 14.4x forward earnings — down 34% from August's $41.14 — despite accelerating fundamentals: TTM revenue of ~$619M with 120.8% YoY growth, 38.3% profit margins, 26.1% ROE, and Q2 2026 delivering record profitability with EBITDA margin above 50% on consumer loan marketplace volume of $4.3B (+132% YoY) [1]. The company continues broadening its addressable market — stablecoin deposits, institutional capital-markets distribution via Figure Connect, and a January 2026 direct stock-lending platform — all aimed at a stated $2T consumer-credit opportunity [2]. The valuation compression from the 52-week high of $78 creates a potentially attractive entry point for a company with best-in-class fintech growth metrics, and the Provenance blockchain provides a defensible technology moat that traditional lenders cannot easily replicate.
The bear case centers on youth, cash burn, key-person risk, and the 65% drawdown from highs. Figure IPO'd barely a year ago and has not been tested through a full credit cycle; free cash flow remains deeply negative (-$254M) and operating cash flow is -$86M even as GAAP profitability looks strong, meaning the business still funds scaling from IPO proceeds rather than internal cash generation [1]. The 34% decline since August and 65% decline from the $78 high suggest the market is repricing either growth sustainability or credit risk concerns. Founder and executive chairman Mike Cagney's prior SoFi tenure ended amid reports of a toxic internal culture, and while he has spoken candidly about applying those lessons at Figure, governance risk at a founder-controlled fintech scaling this fast should not be dismissed [4]. Regulatory scrutiny of AI-driven underwriting for fair-lending compliance is an emerging risk as regulators focus on algorithm-driven credit decisions, and the company's blockchain-centric approach faces uncertainty around evolving crypto/digital-asset regulation [2].

Key Risks
  1. Persistently negative free cash flow (-$254M) and operating cash flow (-$86M) despite reported GAAP profitability, raising questions about durability of unit economics if growth normalizes or credit losses spike in a downturn [1]
  2. Regulatory risk around AI-driven underwriting and fair-lending compliance, plus evolving crypto/digital-asset regulation that could constrain the blockchain-centric business model [2]
  3. Founder/key-person concentration risk given Mike Cagney's central executive chairman role and his prior culture-related departure from SoFi, combined with Figure's short public operating history and unproven performance through a credit cycle [4]
