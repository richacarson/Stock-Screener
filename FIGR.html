Company Profile
Financial Services ·Capital Markets ·NASDAQ ·United States
Figure Technology Solutions is a blockchain-based fintech founded in 2018 by former SoFi CEO Mike Cagney, offering technology-enabled home equity lending (HELOC, cash-out refinance, DSCR loans) paired with a capital-markets distribution marketplace, plus an emerging digital-asset exchange offering interest-bearing stablecoin deposits and blockchain-based direct stock lending. The company IPO'd in September 2025 raising $663M and has since posted three consecutive quarters of triple-digit loan-volume growth. Q2 2026 adjusted net revenue climbed 95% YoY to $218M with adjusted EBITDA up 126% to $119M (55% margin) as consumer loan marketplace volume reached $4.3B, up 132% YoY [1].
602 Employees ·www.figure.com

Investment Thesis
Figure is one of the fastest-growing fintechs in the market: Q2 2026 adjusted net revenue rose 95% YoY to $218M, adjusted EBITDA rose 126% to $119M at a 55% margin, and net income more than doubled to $87M, while consumer loan marketplace volume hit $4.3B (+132% YoY) for a third consecutive quarter of triple-digit growth [1]. ROE of 26.1% and profit margin of 38.3% reflect genuinely strong unit economics for a blockchain-native lending platform, and the company continues to broaden its addressable market — stablecoin deposits, institutional capital-markets distribution via Figure Connect, and a January 2026 direct stock-lending platform — all aimed at a stated $2T consumer-credit opportunity [2]. Wall Street has responded in kind, raising full-year 2026 revenue estimates from $725M to $820M over the past 90 days.
The bear case centers on youth, cash burn, and key-person risk. Figure IPO'd less than a year ago and has not been tested through a full credit cycle; free cash flow remains deeply negative (-$254M) and operating cash flow is -$86M even as GAAP profitability looks strong, meaning the business is still funding its blockchain infrastructure and marketplace scaling from its IPO proceeds rather than internal cash generation. Debt-to-equity of 104% is elevated for a company this early in its lifecycle. Founder and executive chairman Mike Cagney's prior tenure at SoFi ended amid reports of a toxic internal culture, and while he has spoken candidly about applying those lessons at Figure, governance and culture risk at a founder-controlled fintech scaling this fast should not be dismissed. Regulatory scrutiny of AI-driven underwriting for fair-lending compliance is also an emerging risk as regulators increasingly focus on algorithm-driven credit decisions [2].
Key Catalysts
  1. Continued triple-digit loan marketplace volume growth ($4.3B in Q2 2026, +132% YoY) as Figure scales beyond HELOCs into DSCR loans, cash-out refinance, and institutional distribution via Figure Connect [1]
  2. Expansion of the January 2026 blockchain-based direct stock-lending platform and stablecoin interest-bearing deposit exchange into meaningful new revenue lines beyond core lending [2]
  3. Full-year 2026 revenue guidance raised from $725M to $820M by consensus analysts over the trailing 90 days, reflecting upward estimate revisions as growth continues to beat expectations [1]
Key Risks
  1. Persistently negative free cash flow (-$254M) and operating cash flow (-$86M) despite reported GAAP profitability, raising questions about the durability of unit economics as growth normalizes
  2. Regulatory risk around AI-driven underwriting and fair-lending compliance as state and federal regulators increase scrutiny of algorithm-based credit decisions [2]
  3. Founder/key-person concentration risk given Mike Cagney's central role and his prior culture-related departure from SoFi, combined with Figure's short public operating history and unproven performance through a credit downturn
