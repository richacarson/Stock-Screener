Company Profile
Consumer Cyclical ·Apparel Manufacturing ·NYSE ·United States
FIGS, Inc. is a digitally native, direct-to-consumer healthcare apparel and lifestyle brand founded in 2013 by Heather Hasson and Trina Spear. The company designs and sells technically advanced scrubwear, lab coats, outerwear, underscrubs, and lifestyle apparel for healthcare professionals. FIGS serves over 3 million active customers across the U.S. and international markets, operating exclusively through its e-commerce platform.

Investment Thesis
FIGS is accelerating out of its 2023-2024 growth trough with Q1 2026 revenue up 28% YoY to $159.9M, surpassing 3 million active customers for the first time while expanding average order value to $124 (+4.2%) and revenue per active customer to $220 (+5.8%) [1]. International revenue grew 49.9% and now represents 17.7% of total revenue, providing a long runway for geographic expansion. The balance sheet is pristine with $277M in cash and investments against near-zero debt, and the company raised full-year 2026 guidance to 14-16% revenue growth with adjusted EBITDA margin of 13.0-13.2%, demonstrating improving unit economics [1].
The bear case centers on valuation and structural profitability concerns. At 35.6x forward earnings with only 6.1% TTM net margin, FIGS is priced as a high-growth tech company but generates apparel-industry margins [3]. The 19.8% after-hours stock decline following the Q1 2026 earnings beat suggests the market questions whether premium DTC apparel can sustain 20%+ growth without significant margin expansion [1]. Operating expenses grew 22.6% in Q1 and operating cash flow was negative $3.2M despite the strong revenue quarter [1]. The healthcare apparel TAM faces competition from well-capitalized entrants, and the DTC-only model limits distribution reach compared to omnichannel competitors.
Key Catalysts
  1. International expansion accelerating at 49.9% YoY growth, now 17.7% of revenue, with significant headroom in underpenetrated markets across Europe, Asia, and Latin America
  2. Non-scrubwear categories growing 31.3% YoY, expanding the addressable market and increasing average order value through cross-selling to the 3M active customer base
  3. Margin inflection as adjusted EBITDA margin expands from 7.3% in Q1 2025 to 8.7% in Q1 2026, with full-year guidance of 13.0-13.2% signaling transition from growth investment to profitable scaling
Key Risks
  1. Premium valuation at 35.6x forward earnings leaves minimal margin of safety for a company with 6.1% TTM net margin and negative Q1 operating cash flow, creating significant downside risk on any growth deceleration
  2. DTC-only distribution model limits addressable market penetration compared to omnichannel competitors and creates vulnerability to rising digital customer acquisition costs
  3. Glassdoor reviews citing high management turnover and leadership quality concerns could undermine the mission-driven culture that differentiates FIGS from commodity scrub brands
