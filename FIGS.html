Company Profile
Consumer Cyclical ·Apparel Manufacturing ·NYSE ·United States
FIGS, Inc. is a digitally native, direct-to-consumer healthcare apparel and lifestyle brand co-founded by Heather Hasson and Trina Spear. It designs and sells premium scrubwear, lab coats, outerwear, underscrubs and lifestyle apparel for healthcare professionals. FY2025 revenue was $631.1M with net income of $34.3M, and Q2 2026 revenue rose 28.8% YoY to $196.6M with net income of $28.4M [1][4]. Management raised FY2026 guidance to ~20% revenue growth with a 14.8-15.0% adjusted EBITDA margin [2]. The stock trades at $21.31 (near its $22.31 52-week high, up from a $7.21 low) for a ~$3.5B market cap [4].

Investment Thesis
FIGS has inflected from its 2023-2024 trough into high-growth, profitable expansion: Q2 2026 revenue rose 28.8% to $196.6M and net income reached $28.4M, up from $7.1M a year earlier, while management raised FY2026 guidance to ~20% growth and a 14.8-15.0% adjusted EBITDA margin [1][2][4]. The balance sheet is clean (D/E 13%, current ratio 5.0), TTM free cash flow per share is $0.58, and a 19.1% five-year revenue CAGR scores well against IOWN targets [4]. The core product is physical, giving FIGS solid AI resilience, and the Inspire score of 17 carries no negative attributions.
The bear case is valuation and durability. At $21.31 the stock trades at 57x trailing and ~57x FY2027 consensus EPS, after roughly tripling from its $7.21 52-week low, while consensus expects EPS of only $0.366 in 2026 and $0.403 in 2028, implying little further earnings growth [4]. Investing.com's call summary indicates growth decelerating to ~20% in Q3 and ~10% in Q4 2026 (unverified against company materials) [2], TTM net margin of 8.7% remains well below the 20% target, and inventory runs ~195 days [4]. Governance and culture questions raised by Spruce Point in 2022 have faded but were never independently resolved [5].
Key Catalysts
  1. Q3 2026 results (early November) will test whether the raised ~20% FY2026 revenue growth and 14.8-15.0% adjusted EBITDA margin guidance hold as comparisons get tougher [2]
  2. Continued margin expansion: Q2 2026 net income of $28.4M on $196.6M revenue (14.4% margin) versus $7.1M on $152.6M a year earlier shows operating leverage in the DTC model [1][4]
  3. Non-scrub categories and international expansion extend runway beyond the core U.S. scrubwear market, supported by a debt-light balance sheet and $0.58/share TTM free cash flow [1][4]
Key Risks
  1. Valuation: ~57x trailing and forward earnings with consensus EPS growth of only ~5% annually from FY2026 to FY2028 leaves the stock exposed to multiple compression if growth slows as guided [4]
  2. Reported deceleration toward ~10% growth in Q4 2026 (per Investing.com call summary) could break the momentum narrative after the stock's near-tripling from its 52-week low [2][4]
  3. Inventory of ~195 days and DTC-only distribution expose FIGS to markdown risk and rising customer acquisition costs if well-capitalized apparel brands push into premium medical apparel [4]
