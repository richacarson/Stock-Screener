Company Profile
Financial Services ·Credit Services ·NYQ ·China
FinVolution Group operates an AI-powered online consumer finance platform connecting borrowers with institutional lenders in China, Indonesia, the Philippines, Pakistan, and Australia. The company processes loan transactions through its ppdai.com platform and AdaKami (Indonesia), JuanHand (Philippines), and Fundo (Australia) brands. TTM revenue was approximately RMB13.2B with 14.5% profit margin, as the company navigates regulatory tightening in China while growing overseas revenue 18% YoY to RMB930M in Q2 2026. Formerly PPDAI Group, founded in 2007 and headquartered in Shanghai.
3,869 Employees ·ir.finvgroup.com

FinVolution at $2.90 with a $685M market cap is a deeply discounted AI-native fintech trading at 2.7x trailing and 3.7x forward earnings, offering a 10.55% dividend yield backed by a 29.2% payout ratio and $6.4B in cash against only $1.6B debt [1]. The LEGO+ global platform powers AI-driven credit scoring across five international markets, with overseas revenue growing 18% YoY to RMB930M in Q2 2026, providing a diversification path as international operations approach 30% of total revenue [1][2]. The bear case is dominated by China regulatory risk and declining domestic fundamentals. Domestic transaction volume fell 19.3% YoY in Q2 2026 as regulatory tightening — including new fee-disclosure rules effective August 2026 — compresses the core business, with full-year 2026 revenue guidance of RMB11.5-12.9B (management expects the lower end) representing a mid-single-digit decline from FY2025's RMB13.6B [1][3]. An isolated credit incident unsettled the industry and raised concerns among funding partners about platform compliance, highlighting the fragility of the institutional-funding model. The 90-day+ delinquency ratio of 2.10% bears watching in a tightening credit environment. VIE structure risk for a Chinese company listed in the U.S. continues to weigh on valuation, and the extreme P/E discount (2.7x trailing) suggests the market prices in significant probability of permanent capital impairment from regulatory intervention, capital controls, or delisting risk [1][3].
