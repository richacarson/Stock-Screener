Company Profile
Consumer Cyclical ·Specialty Retail ·NASDAQ ·United States
Five Below is a specialty value retailer targeting tweens, teens, and beyond with an extreme-value assortment priced mostly $1-$5 (and 'Five Beyond' items at $6-$25) across categories including candy, beauty, accessories, tech, toys, sports, and seasonal items. Founded in 2002 and headquartered in Philadelphia, the company operates approximately 1,750+ stores across 44 states. Under CEO Winnie Park (appointed 2024), Five Below has executed a major turnaround with FY2025 net sales of $4.76B (+22.9% YoY) and comp sales growth of 12.8%. H1 FY2026 net sales are up 27.5% with 18.3% comps, and management raised full-year guidance to $5.63-5.71B revenue with $10.07 adjusted EPS.
7,800 Employees ·www.fivebelow.com

Investment Thesis
Five Below at 20.12x forward earnings and 0.98 PEG represents a rare combination of strong growth and reasonable valuation in specialty retail. The turnaround under CEO Winnie Park has been exceptional — FY2025 comps of +12.8% accelerated to +18.3% in H1 FY2026, with management raising full-year guidance to $5.63-5.71B revenue and $10.07 adjusted EPS [1]. ROE of 28.25%, revenue growth of 22.9% YoY, and an estimated 5-year CAGR of ~16% reflect best-in-class unit economics for a store concept with a 3,500+ location long-term target (currently ~1,750). The Five Beyond expansion ($6-$25 items) has materially improved average ticket without cannibalizing core $1-$5 traffic. As a physical retailer with extreme-value pricing, AI disruption risk is negligible.
The primary risks are execution and cyclicality. D/E of 82.3% is above the 70% target, reflecting debt-funded store expansion that must generate returns. Profit margin of 11.65% is below average for growth companies, leaving limited buffer if comps decelerate. The extraordinary EPS growth of 418% YoY reflects recovery from a depressed base, not sustainable earnings acceleration. Consumer discretionary spending is cyclical — any recession would pressure the teen/tween demographic that represents Five Below's core customer, and comp sales could reverse rapidly as seen in the negative comp period prior to Park's arrival. The Five Beyond concept, while successful, risks brand dilution if price creep continues beyond $25. Store expansion from 1,750 to 3,500+ requires continued execution in increasingly competitive retail real estate markets.
Key Catalysts
  1. Continued comp sales momentum — H1 FY2026 comps of +18.3% with raised full-year guidance to $5.63-5.71B revenue and $10.07 adjusted EPS demonstrates the Park turnaround has durable structural drivers, not just easy comparisons [1]
  2. Store expansion runway from ~1,750 current stores toward 3,500+ long-term target represents near-doubling of the footprint with proven unit economics and 228 net new stores opened in FY2025 [1]
  3. Five Beyond price expansion ($6-$25) continues to improve average ticket and unit economics while attracting older customers, broadening the addressable market beyond the teen/tween core
Key Risks
  1. Consumer discretionary cyclicality — Five Below's teen/tween customer base is highly sensitive to economic downturns, and comp sales could reverse rapidly as seen in the negative comp period before the Park turnaround [1]
  2. Store expansion execution risk — growing from 1,750 to 3,500+ locations requires sustained capital investment (D/E already at 82.3%) in increasingly competitive retail real estate markets with uncertain returns on marginal stores
  3. Profit margin compression risk at 11.65% — tariffs on Chinese imports, rising wages, and potential shrinkage increases could pressure the already-thin margin structure, with limited pricing power at extreme-value price points
