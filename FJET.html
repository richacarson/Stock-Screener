Company Profile
Industrials ·Aerospace & Defense ·NYSE American ·United States
Starfighters Space operates the world's only commercial fleet of flight-ready MACH 2+ supersonic aircraft, consisting of seven F-104 Starfighter jets based at NASA's Kennedy Space Center in Florida and Midland International Air & Space Port in Texas. The company pursues a two-track strategy: hypersonic flight-testing and R&D services for government and commercial clients, plus the STARLAUNCH air-launch platform targeting suborbital and eventually orbital launch capabilities. IPO closed December 2025 at $3.59/share raising $40M. Added to the Russell 3000 Index effective June 2026. Still pre-revenue with Q1 2026 net loss of $4.27M.
