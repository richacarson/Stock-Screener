Company Profile
Technology ·Electronic Manufacturing Services ·NASDAQ ·United States
Flex Ltd. is one of the world's largest contract manufacturers, providing design, manufacturing, and supply chain services through its Integrated Technology Solutions (ITS), Regulated Manufacturing Solutions (RMS), and Cloud and Power Infrastructure (CPI) segments. Q2 FY2027 (reported October 28, 2026) net sales of roughly $8.5B beat the $7.95-8.25B guide, and Q3 was guided to $8.87-9.17B with adjusted EPS of $1.19-1.25. Flex plans a tax-free spin-off of its fast-growing CPI data-center power and cooling business in Q1 calendar 2027, with CPI revenue growth targeted at 65-75% in FY2027. FY2026 revenue was $27.91B.

Investment Thesis
Flex is a well-run contract manufacturer whose growth engine, the CPI data-center power and cooling business, is scaling rapidly with AI capex: Q1 FY2027 CPI revenue grew 35% to $2.2B and Q2 total sales of about $8.5B beat guidance, with FY2027 revenue guidance raised to $33.7-35.2B [1][2]. At 11x forward earnings and a 0.94 PEG, the stock prices little of that growth after a sharp drawdown from its 50-day average near $106 [3].
The Q1 CY2027 CPI spin-off should give investors a pure-play AI infrastructure company targeting 65-75% FY2027 growth, while RemainCo keeps the lower-margin ITS and RMS businesses [2][7]. The IOWN score is held back by weak Inspiration (no distinctive mission, net insider selling) and a finite Infinite Game profile, so the score stays at HOLD despite strong Infrastructure and AI Resilience.
Key Catalysts
  1. CPI spin-off completion in Q1 calendar 2027, creating a pure-play AI data-center power and cooling company [7]
  2. Q3 FY2027 guidance of $8.87-9.17B revenue and $1.19-1.25 adjusted EPS, implying continued acceleration [1]
  3. CPI growth framework of 65-75% in FY2027 and ~80% in FY2028 as hyperscaler capex expands [2]
Key Risks
  1. Spin-off execution, dis-synergies, and one-time costs that already cut FY2027 FCF conversion guidance to ~40% [2]
  2. Hyperscaler capex slowdown or customer concentration in data-center programs [5]
  3. Thin 3.35% net margin leaves little cushion for tariffs, pricing pressure, or labor disputes such as the Malaysia union allegations [3][11]
