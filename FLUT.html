Company Profile
Consumer Cyclical ·Gambling ·NYSE ·Ireland
Flutter Entertainment plc is the world's largest online sports betting and iGaming operator, with brands including FanDuel, PokerStars, Paddy Power, Betfair, Sky Betting & Gaming, and Sisal. FY2025 revenue was $16.38B. In August 2026 Flutter cut its FY2026 revenue guidance midpoint to $17.91B from $18.305B, driven by the US business, and Q2 2026 revenue rose just 3% to $4.33B. Long-time CEO Peter Jackson was succeeded by International CEO Dan Taylor on October 1, 2026, as prediction markets pressure US sports betting share; shares fell about 65% over the past year.

A CEO change in response to a stock collapse and new competitors shows reactive rather than purpose-led leadership.

Investment Thesis
Flutter is the global scale leader in online betting, trading at about 12x forward earnings with a 0.19 PEG after a roughly 65% share-price collapse [3][6]. If new CEO Dan Taylor stabilizes US share and FanDuel margins recover from World Cup marketing and tax pressure, earnings could rebound sharply from 2025's GAAP loss [1][4].
Revenue growth has stalled (Q2 2026 +3%), FY2026 guidance was cut to a $17.91B midpoint, and prediction markets are an unresolved structural threat to US sportsbook economics [1][5]. Combined with a gambling business model, repeated UK problem-gambling penalties, weak Inspiration, and a finite Infinite Game profile, the IOWN score lands at WATCH.
Key Catalysts
  1. Q3 2026 results and Taylor's first strategic update as CEO, including a plan for prediction markets [5][6]
  2. Recovery of FanDuel US share and margins after World Cup-driven marketing spend [1]
