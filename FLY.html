Company Profile
Industrials ·Aerospace & Defense ·NASDAQ ·United States
Firefly Aerospace Inc. is a space and defense technology company offering launch (Alpha rocket), lunar landers (Blue Ghost), spacecraft solutions, and in-space services. Q2 2026 revenue rose 657% to $117.7M, beating the $88.7M consensus, driven largely by an acquired business in Spacecraft Solutions; first-half 2026 revenue was $198.6M versus $71.4M a year earlier. Management guided FY2026 revenue to $420-450M in March 2026 and reported backlog of about $1.3B at Q1 2026. CEO Jason Kim, formerly CEO of Boeing's Millennium Space Systems, has led the company since October 2024. Q3 2026 results had not been reported as of the screen date.

Investment Thesis
Firefly is one of a handful of US companies with launch, lunar-landing, and spacecraft capability, and revenue is scaling fast: Q2 2026 revenue rose 657% to $117.7M and FY2026 guidance is $420-450M, supported by a backlog of about $1.3-1.5B [1][2]. Defense demand for responsive space and a nearly debt-free balance sheet (D/E 4.4%) give it room to keep investing [2].
Profitability is distant (2025 net loss of $298M, R&D at 125% of revenue), four of six Alpha flights have failed, and much of 2026 growth comes from an acquisition rather than organic launch execution [1][4][6]. Weak Inspiration, a low Infrastructure score, and a finite Infinite Game profile hold the IOWN score at WATCH despite strong AI Resilience.
Key Catalysts
  1. Q3 2026 earnings and any update to the $420-450M FY2026 revenue guidance [1]
  2. Successful Alpha return-to-flight missions that restore launch credibility after the 2025 failures [6]
  3. Further defense and NASA awards building on the ~$1.3-1.5B backlog [1]
Key Risks

  2. Securities class action alleging the company overstated Alpha readiness and spacecraft demand at its IPO [6]
  3. Heavy cash burn (2025 net loss $298M) and dependence on government contract timing [4]
