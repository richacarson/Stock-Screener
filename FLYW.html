Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Infrastructure ·NASDAQ ·United States
Flywire is a vertical-specific payments platform enabling complex, high-value transactions in education, healthcare, travel, and B2B industries. The platform integrates payment processing with industry-specific software workflows, invoicing, settlement, and reconciliation across 240+ countries and 140+ currencies, processing $11.4 billion in total payment volume in Q1 2026. Founded in 2009, headquartered in Boston, Massachusetts.
1,460 Employees ·www.flywire.com

Investment Thesis
Flywire delivered a breakout Q1 2026 with revenue surging 41% YoY to $188.1M and GAAP net income of $12.5M, obliterating the $0.03 EPS consensus with $0.10 actual [1]. Management raised FY2026 guidance to 18-24% FX-neutral revenue growth with continued margin expansion, and total payment volume grew 36.5% to $11.4B [1]. The company offers an attractive combination of 40%+ revenue growth, a pristine balance sheet ($355M cash, near-zero debt), and a forward PEG of 0.40 that dramatically undervalues the growth trajectory [4]. Vertical integration into education registrars, healthcare billing systems, and travel booking engines creates switching costs that horizontal payment processors cannot easily replicate. At $15.89, the stock trades at roughly 2.5x forward revenue despite accelerating growth and improving profitability.
The bear case centers on the sustainability of growth and margin fragility. Profit margin of 4.45% remains thin — net income is still modest relative to revenue, and any growth deceleration would pressure the path to meaningful profitability [4]. The FY2026 guidance of 18-24% revenue growth represents significant deceleration from Q1's 41%, with management noting 3-4 points from B2B/healthcare ramps that may not recur [1]. Competition from well-capitalized horizontal processors (Stripe, Adyen, PayPal) creates ongoing pricing pressure, and AI-driven fintech disruption could commoditize some of Flywire's software workflow functions over time [2]. International education payments — still the core vertical — face risks from geopolitical tensions, visa policy changes, and enrollment volatility affecting student mobility. ROE of 3.69% reflects that this business has not yet proven it can generate returns on equity commensurate with its valuation premium [4]. Mixed Glassdoor reviews and leadership turnover concerns (CFO change, People team culture issues) could signal retention challenges at a company dependent on engineering talent [2].

AI Risk Protection (6/9): Flywire's core business involves deep vertical integration into education registrar systems, healthcare billing platforms, and travel booking engines across 240+ countries with 140+ currencies — a regulatory and banking infrastructure complexity that AI cannot easily replicate [1]. Cross-border payment processing requires local payment method integration, currency management, and compliance with jurisdiction-specific regulations that create structural switching costs. AI Opportunity (1/1): Flywire actively deploys AI for payment security, fraud detection, and predictive analytics under an AI-first CTO mandate, and was featured by PCI Security Standards Council as an AI innovator [2]. The company is generating measurable operational value from AI integration across its platform.
