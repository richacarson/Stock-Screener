flyExclusive, Inc. is a vertically integrated private jet operator offering jet charter, fractional ownership, jet club membership, aircraft management, and MRO maintenance services. Founded in 2015 by pilot-CEO Jim Segrave and based in Kinston, North Carolina, the company operates approximately 90 light, mid, and super-midsize jets across North America. Just achieved first positive adjusted EBITDA in Q1 2026.

The operational transformation is real — core fleet utilization improved 15%, dispatch availability rose 760 bps, and the company grew flight hours 7% on a 7% smaller fleet after eliminating 31 non-performing aircraft. Founder-CEO Segrave's vision of 'best operator, not biggest' is being validated by metrics: quality over quantity is driving profitability that growth-at-any-cost never delivered.
