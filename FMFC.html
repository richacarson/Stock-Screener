Company Profile
Consumer Discretionary ·Footwear & Accessories ·NASDAQ ·Cambodia
Kandal M Venture Limited is a Cambodia-based holding company that manufactures, trades in, and sells affordable luxury leather goods through its subsidiary FMF Manufacturing Co., Ltd. Products include handbags (shoulder bags, crossbody bags, tote bags, backpacks, satchels) and smaller leather goods such as wallets, sold to customers in the United States, Europe, Canada, Japan, and internationally. IPO'd on Nasdaq in June 2025 at $4.00/share with a $12M offering.
