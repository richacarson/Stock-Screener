Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Gold ·NYSE ·Canada
Franco-Nevada is the world's largest gold-focused royalty and streaming company, holding a diversified portfolio of over 400 mineral and energy assets that entitle it to a share of production or revenue without operating mines itself. The capital-light model delivered record H1 2026 revenue of $1,231.6M (+67% YoY) with Q2 revenue of $580.9M (+57% YoY) on 132,405 gold-equivalent ounces sold, and record H1 net income of $822.6M (+80%) [1]. The company is debt-free with $4.3B of available capital and trades at a market cap of approximately $52B.

Investment Thesis
Franco-Nevada is the blue-chip gold royalty franchise: a capital-light, ~400-asset portfolio that converts record gold and silver prices into industry-leading margins (65.7% net profit margin) with zero debt and $4.3B of available capital [1][4]. H1 2026 delivered record results — revenue of $1,231.6M (+67% YoY), net income of $822.6M (+80%), and Q2 adjusted EBITDA of $529.7M (+45%) at a 23.2% subsea... 91% margin [1]. The phased restart of Cobre Panama offers a large, discrete catalyst worth 150,000-175,000 GEOs per year if fully resumed. The model is maximally resilient to AI disruption and benefits indirectly from AI-driven copper and electrification demand. The 19th consecutive annual dividend increase (latest +16% to $0.44/quarter) validates the compounder thesis [4].
The rating is HOLD, not BUY, on valuation: at $271.77 and a forward P/E of 28.38x, Franco-Nevada has re-rated substantially from the ~$211 level earlier this year [4]. Much of the gold price upside is already embedded, and a pullback in gold from elevated levels would compress growth optics rapidly. The 0.65% dividend yield provides minimal downside cushion at these prices. The portfolio is diversified but remains gold-price- and Cobre-Panama-outcome-dependent — political/permitting risk in Panama is outside management's control [1]. The estimated PEG of ~1.9 suggests the stock is fairly valued rather than cheap relative to normalized growth expectations. Franco-Nevada is a high-quality compounder to own on pullbacks rather than chase at all-time-high prices.
