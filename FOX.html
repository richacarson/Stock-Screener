Fox Corporation is a news, sports, and entertainment company operating through Cable Network Programming (Fox News, Fox Business, Fox Sports) and Television (FOX broadcast network, Tubi streaming service, owned-and-operated TV stations). FY2026 revenue reached $17.13B (+5% YoY), with Q4 advertising surging 78% to $1.92B driven by FIFA World Cup coverage and Tubi digital growth [1]. Tubi reached 110 million MAUs, achieved profitability each quarter of FY2026 with 35% revenue growth and 17% lift in viewing time [1]. The Murdoch family controls the company through dual-class share structure. Fox settled the Dominion Voting Systems defamation lawsuit for $787.5M in 2023, the largest defamation settlement in U.S. history [3].

Investment Thesis
Fox Corporation delivered FY2026 revenue of $17.13B with Q4 advertising surging 78% on World Cup coverage, while Tubi reached 110M MAUs and achieved consistent quarterly profitability with 35% revenue growth [1]. The stock trades at 10.64x forward earnings — a significant discount to the market and media peers, pricing in structural cord-cutting headwinds despite Fox's demonstrated ability to monetize live events and digital streaming. Balance sheet is solid with D/E of 64.1% and $4.20B in cash [4]. FY2027 benefits from the 2026 U.S. midterm political advertising cycle ($11B+ industry estimate), the NFL's continued ratings strength, and Tubi's scaling digital ad revenue. Operating margin of 25.2% demonstrates content leverage, and the 14.6% payout ratio provides substantial dividend growth capacity [1][4].
The bear case combines structural media decline with governance risk. Fox's dual-class share structure gives the Murdoch family controlling power despite owning a minority economic interest — limiting minority shareholder influence over capital allocation and strategic direction [4]. The $787.5M Dominion settlement exposed reputational and legal risks from content decisions, and additional litigation (Smartmatic lawsuit) remains pending, creating further legal liability exposure [3]. Revenue growth is heavily event-dependent: FY2026's 28.1% Q4 surge was driven by the one-time World Cup; underlying organic growth is modest (~5% full-year). Cord-cutting continues to erode the Cable Network Programming segment's affiliate fee base, with cable subscribers declining 5-7% annually industry-wide. Glassdoor culture ratings of 2.9/5 and descriptions of 'toxic culture' suggest internal organizational risk that could impair talent retention and innovation [3]. Profit margin of 9.84% and single-digit underlying revenue growth limit the company's earnings power despite the optically cheap valuation. Fox's 0.95% dividend yield and lack of PEG ratio visibility make it difficult to justify as either an income or growth holding.
Key Catalysts
  1. 2026 U.S. midterm political advertising cycle with $11B+ estimated industry spend should disproportionately benefit Fox News and FOX broadcast stations, driving a strong FY2027 advertising revenue surge [1]
  2. Tubi's continued scaling toward 150M+ MAUs and growing ARPU would validate Fox's digital transformation thesis and could re-rate the stock closer to streaming/digital media multiples rather than legacy cable discount [1]
  3. NFL and premium sports rights renewals demonstrate Fox Sports' irreplaceable content value; any new rights acquisitions (NBA, college football) would expand the live sports moat that protects against cord-cutting [1]
Key Risks
  1. Smartmatic $2.7B defamation lawsuit remains pending — an adverse outcome or large settlement following the $787.5M Dominion precedent could materially impair shareholder value and further damage Fox's brand reputation [3]
  2. Structural cord-cutting declining cable subscribers 5-7% annually erodes the Cable Network Programming segment's affiliate fee revenue base — the segment that generates the majority of Fox's profits [1]
  3. Murdoch family dual-class control creates permanent governance risk — Lachlan Murdoch's strategic decisions, including content editorial direction and capital allocation, cannot be meaningfully influenced by minority shareholders despite controlling economic interest [3][4]
