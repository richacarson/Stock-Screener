Company Profile
Communication Services ·Entertainment / Media ·NASDAQ ·United States
Fox Corporation operates as a news, sports, and entertainment company in the United States. Segments include Cable Network Programming (Fox News, Fox Business, Fox Sports), Television (Fox Broadcasting Company, Fox Sports), and Other/Corporate. Key properties include Fox News Channel (dominant cable news), Fox Sports (NFL, MLB, NASCAR rights), Fox Broadcasting, and Tubi AVOD streaming platform. Recently acquired Roku to expand streaming technology and hardware capabilities. Controlled by the Murdoch family. Spun off from 21st Century Fox in 2019, headquartered in New York.

Investment Thesis
Fox Corporation delivered Q3 FY2026 adjusted EBITDA of $954M (+11% YoY) on $3.99B revenue, demonstrating earnings power even without the Super Bowl tailwind that boosted Q2 to $5.18B [1][2]. At a forward P/E of 11.34 and D/E of 67%, Fox trades at a meaningful discount to media peers while holding premium sports rights (NFL, MLB, NASCAR), operating the dominant cable news network, and building Tubi into a top AVOD platform [6]. The Roku acquisition adds streaming hardware and technology capabilities, and AI deployment across content analysis and ad targeting positions Fox ahead of traditional media competitors in digital monetization [3]. Distribution revenue grew 3% with 5% Cable Programming growth, providing stable recurring revenue alongside advertising upside.
The primary concern is Fox's finite corporate character and governance risk. The $787.5M Dominion settlement (2023) exposed institutional willingness to sacrifice editorial integrity for ratings, creating ongoing litigation and reputational overhang [5]. Profit margin of 4.2% is thin for a company with $56 per share stock price, and the forward PEG of 6.82 suggests the market sees limited EPS growth potential relative to the P/E multiple [6]. The Roku integration carries execution risk and adds debt to the balance sheet. Insider selling of $38M net in the last 12 months, including board member Paul Ryan's complete liquidation, signals tepid management conviction [6]. Secular cord-cutting pressures continue to erode the traditional cable bundle that underpins Fox News and Fox Sports distribution economics, and the Murdoch family's controlling interest limits minority shareholder influence over strategic decisions.
Key Catalysts
  1. Roku integration delivering cross-platform advertising synergies and expanding Tubi's distribution reach through Roku's 80M+ active accounts, potentially accelerating AVOD revenue growth
  2. AI-native advertising platform driving higher CPMs through real-time content analysis and contextual targeting, positioning Fox ahead of traditional media peers in digital monetization
  3. Upcoming NFL and MLB broadcast seasons in FY2027 providing premium live content that commands the highest ad pricing and serves as Tubi funnel for younger demographics
Key Risks
  1. Forward PEG of 6.82 signals that the market sees limited earnings growth despite the low forward P/E of 11.34, suggesting the current valuation already reflects the bull case
  2. Dominion settlement ($787.5M) and ongoing Smartmatic litigation create continuing legal and reputational risk that could result in additional material financial penalties
  3. Secular cord-cutting eroding cable bundle economics that underpin Fox News and Fox Sports distribution revenue, with 5% Cable Programming growth insufficient to offset long-term subscriber losses
