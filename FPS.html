Company Profile
Industrials ·Electrical Equipment & Parts ·NYSE ·United States
Forgent Power Solutions, Inc. designs and manufactures electrical distribution equipment used in data centers, power grids, renewable energy facilities, and industrial applications. Products include switchgear, busway, power distribution units, and related electrical infrastructure that keeps critical servers and industrial operations online. Formed from a combination of four legacy electrical equipment companies by Platinum Equity, IPO'd in February 2026 at $27/share raising $1.5B. Headquartered in Maple Grove, Minnesota.

Investment Thesis
Forgent Power Solutions is riding the most powerful demand cycle in electrical equipment history, with Q3 FY2026 revenue surging 103% YoY to $379M, record bookings of $867M (+308%), and an all-time-high backlog of $1.98B (+157%) [1]. The company raised FY2026 guidance to $1,350-$1,390M revenue (+82%) and $310-$320M adjusted EBITDA (+86%), with Q4 guidance of $392-$432M [1]. Every AI data center buildout requires electrical distribution equipment, and Forgent's U.S. manufacturing base positions it to capture the domestic infrastructure build. At $50.54 per share ($15.4B market cap), the stock has nearly doubled from its $27 IPO price in just five months, reflecting market recognition of the AI infrastructure thesis [2][3].
The primary concern is that Forgent is a PE-assembled rollup priced for perfection. At a forward P/E of 40.13 on trailing profit margin of just 2.17%, the stock embeds expectations of sustained triple-digit growth that may not survive a normalization of data center construction timelines [3]. D/E of 121% and $707.6M debt against $93.8M cash create balance sheet risk if demand moderates [3]. The company has no track record as a public entity — it IPO'd five months ago — and the four legacy businesses have never operated as a unified platform through a demand cycle. PE sponsors sold 39.4M shares at IPO, and additional secondary offerings could pressure the stock [2]. The 22.4% adjusted EBITDA margin, while improving, is below mature industrial peers, and the trailing P/E of 400.81 underscores how early the earnings ramp truly is. If the AI data center buildout decelerates or shifts to geographies where Forgent lacks manufacturing presence, the growth thesis collapses rapidly.
Key Catalysts
  1. Continued record bookings from hyperscaler data center buildouts driving backlog above $2B with book-to-bill ratio sustaining above 2.0x, providing multi-year revenue visibility
  2. Adjusted EBITDA margin expansion toward 25%+ in Q4 FY2026 as the company scales production and integrates the four legacy businesses into a unified operating platform
  3. Potential inclusion in the S&P 500 as market cap approaches $20B, which would trigger passive index fund buying and broaden the institutional shareholder base
Key Risks
  1. Forward P/E of 40.13 on 2.17% trailing profit margin prices in sustained hypergrowth — any deceleration in AI data center construction timelines would trigger severe multiple compression
  2. D/E of 121% with $707.6M debt creates balance sheet vulnerability if demand moderates; the company has no track record managing through a demand downturn as a unified entity
  3. PE-assembled rollup of four legacy companies operating as a unified platform for less than one year carries integration risk — culture, systems, and operational harmonization remain unproven at scale
