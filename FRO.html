Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas Midstream (Crude Tankers) ·NYSE ·Cyprus
Frontline plc is one of the world's largest listed crude oil tanker owners, operating VLCC, Suezmax, and LR2/Aframax tankers. Tanker rates surged in 2026: Q1 VLCC earnings averaged $103,500/day, and Q2 2026 net profit was a record $659.2M ($2.96/share). Frontline declared a $2.61 Q2 dividend plus a $0.80 special dividend from VLCC sale proceeds, while selling 10 older vessels and adding ECO VLCC newbuilds. CEO Lars Barstad has led the company since 2020; John Fredriksen is the controlling shareholder. Q3 2026 results are scheduled for November 30, 2026.

Investment Thesis
Frontline is a high-quality way to own the crude tanker upcycle: record Q2 2026 net profit of $659.2M, VLCC rates above $100,000/day, and a modern ECO fleet support a 9.5% yield and a forward P/E near 9 [1][2][3]. The Q2 payout of $2.61 plus a $0.80 special dividend shows the variable dividend passes earnings directly to shareholders [1].
Tanker earnings are highly cyclical, and the dividend has swung from nothing in 2021 to $0.38 in 2025 to $5.99 so far in 2026, so income is unreliable [3]. Management has warned that full-quarter Q3 spot rates will come in below contracted levels [2]. Strong Infrastructure and AI Resilience are offset by minimal Innovation, Inspiration, and Infinite Game scores, leaving FRO at WATCH.
