Company Profile
Consumer Defensive ·Packaged Foods ·NASDAQ ·United States
Freshpet is the leading manufacturer of fresh, refrigerated pet food sold through a proprietary network of Freshpet Fridges in retail stores across the United States, Canada, and Europe. The company pioneered the fresh pet food category and crossed $1 billion in annual net sales in 2025, offering natural, minimally processed meals and treats for dogs and cats. Q2 2026 net sales of $305.6M grew 15.5% YoY with net income of $19.5M and adjusted gross margin of 48.6%. Freshpet Kitchens facilities in Bethlehem, PA and Ennis, TX support capacity expansion. CEO Billy Cyr has led since 2016.
1,288 Employees ·www.freshpet.com

Investment Thesis
Freshpet delivered a strong Q2 2026 with $305.6M net sales (up 15.5% YoY) and adjusted gross margin of 48.6%, demonstrating both growth acceleration (up from 13.1% in Q1) and continued margin expansion [2]. The company is the dominant and essentially sole large-scale player in the fresh refrigerated pet food category it created, with a proprietary Freshpet Fridge distribution network providing a structural competitive moat. Profit margins of 17.3% are expanding rapidly as manufacturing capacity utilization improves at Freshpet Kitchens facilities [5]. The 5-year revenue CAGR of 22.6% from ~$425M to over $1.1B reflects the secular pet humanization trend — owners treating pets as family and seeking premium nutrition. D/E of 39.9%, ROE of 17.6%, and $351M cash provide financial flexibility [5]. The Inspire score of 60 (ALIGNED) with 14 positive attributions and zero negatives makes FRPT attractive for faith-based growth investors [4].
The bear case centers on growth deceleration, competitive entry, and valuation. Revenue growth guidance of 8-11% for FY2026 marks a sharp slowdown from the 27% growth rate in FY2024, and while Q2 2026 showed 15.5% growth, full-year guidance remains conservative [1][2]. The forward P/E of 31.27x at 8-11% guided growth gives a PEG of 2.46 — expensive relative to the growth rate, and any further deceleration could trigger meaningful multiple compression. Competition is intensifying as Mars Petcare, Nestle Purina, and General Mills enter the fresh/refrigerated pet food segment with greater scale, distribution reach, and marketing budgets [1]. Freshpet's 1,288-employee workforce and $2.95B market cap leave it vulnerable as a potential acquisition target or competitive casualty if large CPG companies commit resources to the category. The forward P/E of 31.27x is roughly double the trailing P/E of 15.90x, suggesting the market expects earnings to decline or investment spending to increase in coming quarters. The company has no dividend to cushion downside [5].

  3. Small-cap vulnerability with 1,288 employees and $2.95B market cap — dependence on protein ingredient costs, refrigerated supply chain logistics, and consumer spending on premium pet food creates concentration risk without dividend cushion [5]
