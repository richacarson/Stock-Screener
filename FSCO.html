Company Profile
Financial Services ·Asset Management ·NYSE ·United States
FS Credit Opportunities Corp. is a business development company / closed-end fixed income fund launched by Franklin Square Capital Partners (now Future Standard) in January 2013 and managed by FS Global Advisor, LLC. The fund invests across public and private global credit markets — secured and unsecured loans, bonds, event-driven credit, special situations, and private capital solutions — seeking premium returns from complexity and illiquidity, with a focus on Europe and the United States. The fund holds approximately $2.2 billion in assets under management.

Investment Thesis
FS Credit Opportunities Corp offers a high 15.6% distribution yield on a $2.2B portfolio of non-traditional public and private credit, with monthly distributions maintained without interruption since the fund's 2013 inception and a FY2025 NAV total return of 10.89% [1][3]. The fund's differentiated focus on event-driven credit, special situations, and private capital solutions provides exposure to complexity/illiquidity return premiums generally unavailable to retail fixed-income investors, and the July 2026 distribution has been described by management as fully covered by net investment income [3].
The bear case is structural. FSCO does not fit the IOWN operating-company framework well — it has no product innovation, no distinctive mission, and virtually no conventional financial metrics (D/E, ROE, revenue growth, and Forward P/E are all unavailable), which mechanically caps the Infrastructure, Innovation, and Inspiration scores. The payout ratio of 104.9% exceeds net investment income, and management has already cut the monthly distribution once in 2026 'in response to declining interest rates,' breaking a growth streak that had taken the payout from $0.04 to $0.06/month since 2022 [3][4]. YTD 2026 NAV total return of only 1.7% through March, alongside a -16.1% market-price return over the same period, signals the fund is trading at a widening discount to NAV, consistent with market skepticism about distribution sustainability and credit quality in a fund that generates only modest new-money NAV appreciation.
Key Catalysts
  1. Stabilization of the monthly distribution at the current $0.0583/share level with continued full coverage by net investment income, which could narrow the fund's discount to NAV
  2. A steepening or higher-for-longer rate environment reducing pressure on floating-rate credit income relative to the rate-driven cut already taken in 2026
  3. Event-driven credit opportunities (M&A, special situations) widening in a more active corporate-transaction environment, playing to the fund's stated differentiated strategy
