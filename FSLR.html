Company Profile
Technology ·Solar ·NASDAQ ·United States
First Solar, Inc. is the leading American manufacturer of photovoltaic solar modules using proprietary cadmium telluride (CadTel) thin-film semiconductor technology. The company designs, manufactures, and sells solar modules to developers, system integrators, and utilities globally. First Solar operates the largest solar technology manufacturing and R&D footprint in the Western Hemisphere, with three fully vertically integrated manufacturing facilities in Ohio, one in Alabama, and a new $1.1B AI-enabled facility in Louisiana. The company expects to employ over 5,500 people in the US by end of 2026, with approximately $4.5B invested in American manufacturing and R&D since 2019. Market cap approximately $23.75B.

Investment Thesis
First Solar is executing at an elite level with Q1 2026 delivering record revenue of $1.04B (up 24% YoY), 47% gross margins (up 600bps YoY), and EPS of $3.22 (up 65% YoY) [1]. The balance sheet is fortress-grade with $2.43B cash, just 6% debt-to-equity, and 30.73% profit margins that rival software companies despite being a manufacturer [4]. The stock trades at a compelling 11.48x forward P/E and 0.56 PEG ratio, significantly below fair value for a company growing revenue at 27% with best-in-class margins. The AI data center buildout creates a structural demand tailwind, with CEO Widmar positioning First Solar as essential infrastructure for America's AI dominance [3]. Full year 2026 guidance of $4.9-5.2B in sales provides clear visibility.
The primary risk is policy dependence: First Solar's domestic manufacturing premium is significantly supported by IRA tax credits (Section 45X advanced manufacturing production credits), and any legislative modification or repeal would materially impact profitability [1]. The stock's 52-week range of $159.85-$320.95 reflects extreme volatility tied to political sentiment around clean energy policy. Chinese solar manufacturers continue to expand capacity aggressively, maintaining 80%+ global market share with dramatically lower production costs, creating persistent pricing pressure in international markets. First Solar's CadTel technology, while differentiated, represents less than 5% of global solar module production, making the company a niche player dependent on trade protection and IRA incentives to compete. Full year 2026 revenue guidance of $4.9-5.2B implies a potential deceleration from the 27% TTM growth rate, and any tariff policy reversal could immediately compress margins.
Key Catalysts
  1. AI data center buildout driving massive electricity demand growth, with solar as the lowest-cost generation source in most US markets, creating a structural multi-year demand tailwind for First Solar modules
  2. Full ramp-up of the $1.1B AI-enabled Louisiana manufacturing facility adding significant production capacity and further reducing per-unit costs through AI-optimized manufacturing processes
  3. Continuation of IRA Section 45X manufacturing credits and US tariff protection against Chinese solar imports, maintaining First Solar's domestic manufacturing cost advantage
Key Risks
  1. Legislative modification or repeal of IRA Section 45X advanced manufacturing production credits would materially reduce profitability, as domestic manufacturing premiums are significantly supported by tax incentives
  2. Chinese solar manufacturers with 80%+ global market share and dramatically lower production costs continue aggressive capacity expansion, creating persistent pricing pressure in international markets
  3. Stock has exhibited extreme volatility (52-week range $159.85-$320.95) driven by political sentiment around clean energy policy, tariff uncertainty, and trade tensions that could trigger significant multiple compression
