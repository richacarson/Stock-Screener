Investment Thesis
Fastly is executing an accelerating pivot from commodity CDN to AI-enabling edge infrastructure, with Q2 2026 delivering record revenue of $183.3M (+23% YoY, accelerating from Q1's 20%), record GAAP gross margin of 63.3%, and NRR of 117% — the highest in three years [1]. AI-driven traffic is growing 6.5x faster than human traffic on Fastly's network, providing a structural demand tailwind. Security revenue surged 43% YoY to $41.7M (23% of total), diversifying beyond CDN. Raised 2026 guidance to $732-746M revenue with $88-96M non-GAAP operating income reflects management confidence. The Experian AI Agent Trust partnership and C++ SDK for AI workloads position Fastly as critical agentic AI infrastructure. At $4.77B market cap with $50.7M positive free cash flow, the business model is inflecting toward sustainability.
The bear case centers on valuation risk and persistent GAAP unprofitability. At 58.96x forward earnings and -11.80% GAAP profit margin, the stock prices in a profitability trajectory not yet achieved on a GAAP basis — the 313% 52-week surge has pushed the stock above the $27 analyst consensus target [3]. Heavy stock-based compensation dilutes per-share economics and contributes to the negative 8.39% ROE. Insider selling is notable — 80 sells vs. zero buys over 12 months, including CEO Compton ($371K in August), President Lovett ($741K in June), and Director Loop ($220K in August) [3]. The Altman Z-Score of 1.91 indicates increased bankruptcy risk despite positive operating cash flow. Competition from better-capitalized hyperscalers (AWS, Google Cloud) and faster-growing Cloudflare creates ongoing pricing pressure. Customer concentration risk persists — any deceleration in enterprise AI spending could slow RPO-to-revenue conversion. The March 2026 convertible note maturity created a governance-related overhang [3].
