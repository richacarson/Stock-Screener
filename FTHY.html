First Trust High Yield Opportunities 2027 Term Fund is a diversified, closed-end management investment company that seeks current income by investing primarily in high-yield corporate debt securities. Managed by First Trust Advisors L.P. (Wheaton, Illinois), the fund converted from a perpetual structure to a term fund in August 2024, with mandatory tender offer and liquidation targeted for December 2027. The fund pays monthly distributions and trades on the NYSE at approximately $12.82 with net assets of roughly $471M.

Investment Thesis
FTHY at $12.82 offers an 11.7% distribution yield with a defined December 2027 termination date that provides a natural catalyst for discount narrowing — as the liquidation date approaches, the market price should converge toward NAV, delivering both income and capital appreciation for investors who buy at a discount [1]. The fund's high-yield corporate bond portfolio benefits from a resilient credit environment, with the trailing 1-year total return of 7.75% demonstrating adequate performance. Monthly distributions provide consistent income, and the 15-month runway to termination limits duration risk.
The bear case is structural. The payout ratio of approximately 217% indicates the fund is distributing more than its net investment income, likely returning capital to shareholders — a warning sign that the yield is partially illusory. The 52-week range of $12.54-$14.42 suggests price volatility inconsistent with a near-term liquidating vehicle, and the thin daily dollar volume of $1.37M limits exit flexibility.
