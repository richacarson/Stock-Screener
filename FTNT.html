Company Profile
Technology ·Cybersecurity / Software - Infrastructure ·NASDAQ ·United States
Fortinet, Inc. is a global cybersecurity company providing network security solutions including next-generation firewalls (FortiGate), secure SD-WAN, SASE, zero-trust network access, and AI-powered threat detection through its Fortinet Security Fabric platform. Founded in 2000 by Ken Xie and Michael Xie, the company holds 500+ AI patents — more than any other cybersecurity firm — and serves 775,000+ customers globally. Q2 2026 revenue grew 26% YoY to $2.05B with product revenue surging 52%, billings up 33% to $2.37B, and non-GAAP EPS of $0.90 (+41%). Full-year 2026 revenue guidance raised to $8.02-8.18B (19% growth). Market cap approximately $116B.
14,800 Employees ·www.fortinet.com

Investment Thesis
Fortinet is delivering accelerating growth with Q2 2026 revenue up 26% YoY to $2.05B, product revenue surging 52% to $773M, and billings growing 33% to $2.37B — all metrics accelerating from Q1 [1]. Management raised full-year 2026 revenue guidance to $8.02-8.18B (19% growth), reflecting confidence in the FortiGate refresh cycle and customer investment in AI security. With 500+ AI patents (the most in cybersecurity), FortiAI embedded across 12+ products, and partnerships with Anthropic and OpenAI, Fortinet is positioned as a primary beneficiary of the dual AI tailwind: AI-generated threats creating demand and AI-powered solutions commanding premium pricing [2]. Profit margin of 28.2%, ROE of 117%, and 5-year revenue CAGR of 21.6% demonstrate world-class profitability and growth durability. The Inspire Impact Score of 67 (ALIGNED) is favorable for faith-based investors.
The bear case is valuation-focused: at 46.6x forward earnings and a PEG of 3.03 (above the 3.0 floor), Fortinet is priced for perfection — the stock has appreciated over 109% in the past year and trades near its 52-week high of $172.09 [4]. Any deceleration in billings growth from 33% or revenue growth from 26% could trigger significant multiple compression. The trailing P/E of 57.7x leaves minimal margin of safety. Platform competition from Microsoft Security Copilot, CrowdStrike Falcon, and Palo Alto Prisma creates pricing pressure as security functionality gets bundled into broader cloud platforms. The founder-CEO's centralized decision-making style, consistently noted across Glassdoor reviews, could become a scalability limitation as the company grows beyond 14,800 employees [2]. Fortinet pays no dividend despite generating massive free cash flow, returning capital exclusively through buybacks that primarily benefit from the elevated valuation.
