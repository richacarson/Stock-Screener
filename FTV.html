Company Profile
Technology ·Scientific & Technical Instruments ·NYSE ·United States
Fortive Corporation is an industrial technology company providing essential instruments, software, and services through two segments: Intelligent Operating Solutions (Fluke, Accruent, Gordian, ServiceChannel — Q3 2026 revenue $767M, +9.1% core) and Advanced Healthcare Solutions (ASP sterilization, Censis, Provation — Q3 revenue $340M, +3.1% core). Q3 2026 revenue was $1.11B (+7.8% reported, +7.2% core) with adjusted EPS of $0.77 (+13%), and management raised FY2026 adjusted EPS guidance to $3.10-$3.14 (~15% growth). Spun off from Danaher in 2016, Fortive completed the Ralliant (Precision Technologies) separation in June 2025, when Olumide Soroye succeeded Jim Lico as CEO; the 'new Fortive' has ~10,000 employees and has repurchased ~13% of its shares since the separation.

Investment Thesis
Fortive's 'new Fortive' is accelerating: Q3 2026 revenue rose 7.8% to $1.11B (7.2% core) with adjusted EPS up 13% to $0.77, IOS grew 9.1% core at a 35.8% adjusted EBITDA margin, and management raised FY2026 adjusted EPS guidance to $3.10-$3.14, ~15% growth [1]. TTM free cash flow of $1.06B (~5.5% FCF yield on a $19.4B market cap) funds aggressive buybacks — ~$2.3B, or ~13% of shares, since the Ralliant separation — while D/E stays a manageable ~60% [1][6]. At ~19.7x forward earnings, the stock trades in line with industrial-technology peers despite a 63% gross margin and growing recurring software mix.
The bear case centers on growth quality and returns on capital. Advanced Healthcare Solutions grew just 3.1% core in Q3, and the restated continuing-operations revenue base grew only ~3% annually from 2023 to 2025, so the acceleration must persist to justify the multiple [1][2]. ROE of 10.6% and negative tangible book value reflect more than $6B of acquisition goodwill, and insiders have been net sellers with no open-market purchases in the past year [6]. The Inspire Impact Score of -49 (MISALIGNED, LGBT Activism) limits suitability for faith-based mandates.
Key Catalysts
  1. Core growth acceleration: IOS core growth of 9.1% in Q3 2026 and raised FY2026 adjusted EPS guidance of $3.10-$3.14 (~15% growth) could drive estimate revisions into 2027 [1]
  2. Capital return: ~$300M of Q3 buybacks (~2% of diluted shares) and $1.06B TTM FCF support continued double-digit EPS growth even with mid-single-digit revenue growth [1]
  3. AI-enabled productivity under CEO Soroye — 20% engineering productivity gains from AI tools — could expand margins across Fluke, Accruent and Gordian software [3]
Key Risks
  1. Healthcare softness: AHS core growth of only 3.1% and an 11.2% GAAP operating margin in Q3 2026 could weigh on the consolidated growth story if hospital capital budgets tighten [1]
  2. Acquisition-heavy balance sheet: negative tangible book value and 10.6% ROE mean any impairment or poor integration of software acquisitions would hit returns [6]
  3. Software exposure to AI-native competitors in facilities maintenance (ServiceChannel) and construction cost data (Gordian) could erode pricing over time
