Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Specialty Chemicals ·NYSE ·United States
H.B. Fuller Company formulates, manufactures, and markets adhesives, sealants, coatings, polymers, tapes, encapsulants, and other specialty chemical products worldwide. It operates through three segments: Hygiene, Health and Consumable Adhesives; Engineering Adhesives; and Construction Adhesives. The company serves packaging, electronics, clean energy, aerospace, automotive, and construction markets across North America, Latin America, Europe, and Asia Pacific. Founded in 1887 and headquartered in Saint Paul, Minnesota.

Investment Thesis
H.B. The Engineering Adhesives segment positions the company at the intersection of secular growth themes (EVs, clean energy, aerospace) while the Hygiene/Health segment provides defensive stability. FY2026 guidance of $4.70-$4.85 adjusted EPS and $655-$670M EBITDA implies continued margin expansion. The 35-year dividend growth streak and 1.94% yield provide a modest but reliable income component.
The bear case centers on flat-to-declining revenue over the last four years ($3.75B FY2022 to $3.47B FY2025), suggesting volume headwinds that pricing alone cannot permanently offset [2][6]. Thin profit margins of 5.6% leave little room for error, and total debt of $2.19B against $97M cash represents meaningful leverage for a $2.7B market cap company. ROE of 9.6% is below the cost of capital for many investors, indicating the company is not generating excess returns on its asset base. The stock trades 26% below its 52-week high of $68.63, reflecting market concerns about organic growth sustainability and raw material cost pressure in the specialty chemicals industry [2].
