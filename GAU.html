Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Gold ·NYSE American ·Canada
Galiano Gold Inc. (formerly Asanko Gold) is a Vancouver-based gold producer whose principal asset is the Asanko Gold Mine on Ghana's Asankrangwa Gold Belt, comprising the Nkran, Esaase and Abore open-pit deposits and a central processing plant. After consolidating ownership by acquiring Gold Fields' JV stake in 2024, Galiano produced 69,138 oz in H1 2026 and maintains FY2026 guidance of 140,000-160,000 oz at AISC of $2,300-$2,600/oz. Q2 2026 revenue was $156.6M (+61% YoY) with net income of $61.9M ($0.24/share); the company has no debt and $105.9M of total cash including $25.9M restricted under a disputed garnishee order. Q3 2026 results are due November 5, 2026.

Investment Thesis
Galiano is a high-leverage play on gold prices with a clean balance sheet: Q2 2026 revenue rose 61% to $156.6M on a 34% higher gold price and 20% more ounces sold, net income reached $61.9M ($0.24/share), and H1 production of 69,138 oz sits at the top of the indicative range with FY2026 guidance of 140-160 koz maintained [1]. The company carries no debt and $105.9M of total cash, and management says that from 2027 results will 'fully participate in the gold price' as hedges roll off [1][2]. At ~2.5x forward earnings and a 0.45 PEG, the stock prices in substantial operational or jurisdictional risk, leaving upside if the Q1 2027 reserve update extends mine life [6].
The risks are concentrated. Galiano is a single-asset producer in Ghana, where a new sliding-scale royalty pushed Q2 AISC up 10% to $2,473/oz, a September 2025 clash at Esaase left one person dead and suspended operations, and $25.9M of cash is restricted under a disputed garnishee order [1][4]. Hedging losses reduced the Q2 realized price by ~$580/oz, and growth depends on Nkran Cut 3 stripping and drilling conversion that have not yet been translated into reserves [1]. A gold price reversal would compress margins quickly given AISC above $2,400/oz.
Key Catalysts
  1. Q3 2026 results on November 5, 2026, with management guiding to stronger H2 production as Nkran ore availability increases [1][2]
  2. Hedge book roll-off from 2027, allowing full participation in gold prices that averaged $4,432/oz before hedging losses in Q2 2026 [1][2]
  3. Q1 2027 Mineral Resource and Reserve update incorporating 33,700m of Esaase infill and Abore drilling that intersected mineralization 180m below the existing resource [1]
Key Risks
  1. Ghana jurisdiction risk: the new sliding-scale royalty raised AISC 10% YoY, and a $25.9M garnishee order has restricted cash pending court resolution [1]
  2. Social license: the September 2025 Esaase clash between community members and military security caused a death and suspended mining at the deposit [4]
  3. Gold price and cost exposure: AISC of $2,300-$2,600/oz guidance leaves a single-asset producer vulnerable to a gold price decline or pit-sequencing delays at Nkran Cut 3 [1]
