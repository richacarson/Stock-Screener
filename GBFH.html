Company Profile
Financial Services ·Banks - Regional ·NASDAQ ·United States
GBank Financial Holdings is a Las Vegas-based bank holding company for GBank, providing commercial and consumer banking services in Nevada. Founded in 2007, the company serves small to medium-sized businesses, high net worth individuals, professionals, and investors through SBA lending, commercial real estate loans, business lines of credit, and digital banking services. Q2 2026 revenue was $21.95M with net income of $5.5M ($0.38 EPS), and the company recently appointed Jeff Newgard as President and CEO of GBank in June 2026 while Ed Nigro continues as Executive Chairman.
