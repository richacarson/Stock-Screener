Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Infrastructure ·NYSE ·United States
GoDaddy Inc. is the world's largest domain registrar and a leading web presence platform serving over 21 million customers globally. The company provides domain registration, web hosting, website building tools, e-commerce solutions, and marketing services primarily to small businesses and entrepreneurs. Under CEO Aman Bhutani (since 2019), GoDaddy is pivoting toward an AI-first platform through its GoDaddy Airo product, which automates business creation from domain to website to marketing. Q2 2026 revenue grew 7% YoY to $1.3B with operating income up 29%, and free cash flow is guided at ~$1.8B for FY2026. Full-year revenue guidance is $5.215-$5.255B.

Investment Thesis
GoDaddy at 12.47x forward earnings with a PEG ratio of 0.63 offers attractive valuation for a high-quality cash flow compounder — FY2026 free cash flow is guided at ~$1.8B against a ~$15B market cap, implying a ~12% FCF yield [1][2]. The Q2 2026 results demonstrated accelerating profitability with operating income up 29% YoY and net income up 20% on 7% revenue growth, showing strong operating leverage as the business matures [1]. The Airo AI platform, with $50M annualized bookings growing 5x, could become a meaningful growth vector as AI-powered business creation expands the addressable market beyond traditional website builders [3]. GoDaddy's 21 million customer base provides distribution scale that AI-native competitors lack, and the shift toward Applications & Commerce positions the company for higher ARPU from each customer relationship.
The bear case centers on AI disruption to the core business, extreme leverage, and cultural deterioration. GoDaddy's D/E ratio of ~1622% reflects a capital structure optimized for financial engineering rather than business resilience — near-zero equity provides no cushion against revenue disruption [5]. AI website builders from Wix, Squarespace, and emerging AI-native competitors threaten to commoditize web presence creation, potentially compressing GoDaddy's pricing power in its core hosting and website builder segments [3]. Revenue growth of 6% reflects a mature business that may struggle to accelerate even with AI investments — the 5-year CAGR of 6.5% shows no meaningful inflection despite significant strategic pivots. Internal culture concerns — layoffs, outsourcing, benefit reductions creating a 'culture of fear' — risk undermining the organizational capability needed to execute the AI transformation [3].
