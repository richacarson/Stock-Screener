Company Profile
Healthcare ·Medical - Devices ·NASDAQ ·United States
GE HealthCare Technologies, spun off from General Electric in January 2023 and headquartered in Chicago, makes medical imaging systems (CT, MR, molecular imaging, X-ray), ultrasound, patient care solutions (monitoring, anesthesia, diagnostic cardiology) and pharmaceutical diagnostics (contrast media and radiopharmaceuticals) [5]. Q3 2026 revenue rose 7.2% (6.2% organic) to $5.5B, adjusted EPS rose to $1.34 from $1.07, and management raised FY2026 guidance to ~4% organic growth and $4.95-5.05 adjusted EPS [1]. The stock trades at $76.57 (52-week range $58.75-$89.77) for a ~$34.6B market cap and pays a token $0.145/yr dividend (~0.2% yield) [5].

Investment Thesis
GEHC delivered a clean Q3 2026: revenue up 7.2% (6.2% organic) to $5.5B with every segment growing, adjusted EBIT margin up 180bps to 16.6%, adjusted EPS of $1.34 versus $1.07, and FY2026 guidance raised to ~4% organic growth and $4.95-5.05 adjusted EPS [1]. A $23.7B backlog (~4.3 quarters of revenue) and 1.03x book-to-bill support near-term visibility, and at ~13.9x FY2027 consensus EPS of $5.50 the valuation is undemanding for a business whose physical, FDA-regulated products are structurally protected from AI substitution [1][5]. ROE of 16.1% and FMP forward PEG of 1.97 round out a solid financial profile [5].
The bear case is slow structural growth and leverage. Revenue compounded only 3.7% a year from 2020 to 2025, TTM net margin is 8.5%, and D/E of ~90% with negative tangible book value reflects spin-off debt and goodwill [5]. Organic orders grew just 4.0% in Q3 and backlog slipped from $23.9B to $23.7B, signalling that the strong Q2 order intake has normalized [1]. China procurement and tariff exposure remain swing factors for imaging, and the Inspire Impact Score of -73 (MISALIGNED: embryonic stem cells, IVF) is a meaningful concern for faith-based investors even though it carries no weight in the score.
Key Catalysts
  1. Raised FY2026 outlook: organic revenue growth ~4% (from 3-4%), adjusted EPS $4.95-5.05 (from $4.80-5.00) and adjusted EBIT margin of 15.5-15.7% after Q3's 16.6% margin [1]
  2. Pharmaceutical Diagnostics momentum, with Q3 2026 revenue up 13% to $846M as radiopharmaceutical and contrast demand grows alongside imaging volumes [1]
  3. Cloud-first AI imaging platform strategy outlined at JPM 2026, aiming to orchestrate AI across enterprise imaging and deepen the installed-base moat [2]
Key Risks
  1. Low structural growth: 2020-2025 revenue CAGR of 3.7% and Q3 2026 organic orders growth of only 4.0% leave little room for execution slips [5][1]
  2. Leverage from the spin: D/E ~90%, net debt/EBITDA ~2.4x and negative tangible book value constrain flexibility if hospital capital spending weakens [5]
  3. Backlog eased from $23.9B to $23.7B with book-to-bill near 1.0x, and China/tariff exposure in imaging could pressure orders and margins [1]
