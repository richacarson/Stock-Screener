Company Profile
Financial Services ·Capital Markets ·NASDAQ ·United States
Gemini Space Station operates the Gemini cryptocurrency exchange platform, offering trading, custody, derivatives, prediction markets, credit card, and payments infrastructure for digital assets. Founded by Cameron and Tyler Winklevoss in 2014 and IPO'd in September 2025 at $28 per share, the company has since exited international markets (UK, EU, Australia), cut 25% of staff, lost CFO/COO/CLO simultaneously in February 2026, and is pivoting toward CFTC-regulated prediction markets and derivatives. Q2 2026 revenue was $45.5M (+37% YoY) but the company remains deeply unprofitable with H1 2026 net loss of $216.7M.

Investment Thesis
Gemini's bull case rests on its pivot from a declining crypto exchange to a US-focused regulated markets company. Q2 2026 services revenue surged 149% YoY to $23.5M and total revenue grew 37% to $45.5M, demonstrating traction in prediction markets and derivatives under the new CFTC DCO license [1]. H1 2026 net loss narrowed to $216.7M from $282.5M in the prior-year period. The Winklevoss twins' $100M personal investment at $14/share signals founder conviction [2]. At $565M market cap with $189M cash, the valuation implies modest expectations if the prediction markets pivot gains traction and a crypto market recovery reignites exchange volume.
The bear case is severe. GEMI has collapsed ~85% from its $28 IPO price, with trailing profit margin of -250% on $207M revenue and operating cash flow of -$305M [1]. Exchange revenue — still the core business — fell 38% YoY in Q2 as Gemini loses share to Coinbase and larger competitors. Three C-suite executives (CFO, COO, CLO) departed simultaneously in February 2026, followed by 25% staff cuts and exits from UK, EU, and Australia [2]. Full-year 2026 revenue estimates have been cut from $300M to $230M. A securities class action alleges the company misled IPO investors about its strategic direction. Short interest stands at 18.4% of float. Glassdoor reviews describing 'insane bro culture' and 'mass confusion and tribalism' suggest internal dysfunction that undermines execution [2]. With $500M debt and $189M cash against continued cash burn, the runway is limited without additional dilutive capital raises.
