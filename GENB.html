Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Generate Biomedicines is a clinical-stage therapeutics company pioneering Generative Biology — using machine learning to design novel protein therapeutics from scratch rather than optimizing existing molecules. Founded in 2018 as a Flagship Pioneering company and IPO'd in March 2026 (raising ~$400M), the company operates the Generate Platform, integrating computational innovation with scalable biohardware. Lead asset GB-0895 (long-acting anti-TSLP for severe asthma) is in Phase 3 SOLAIRIA trials. Partnerships with Novartis and Amgen generated $31.9M in collaboration revenue in 2025. The company holds $457M in cash and estimates runway into H1 2028. Market cap is approximately $1.9B.
312 Employees ·generatebiomedicines.com

Investment Thesis
Generate Biomedicines at $15.15 per share and $1.9B market cap represents a pure-play bet on AI-driven drug discovery, the highest-potential application of machine learning in healthcare. The Generate Platform's ability to design novel proteins from scratch (not just optimize existing molecules) is genuinely differentiated, validated by major pharma partnerships — Novartis ($25.1M in 2025 revenue) and Amgen [1]. Lead asset GB-0895 (anti-TSLP for severe asthma) in Phase 3 SOLAIRIA trials addresses a large market where long-acting biologics command premium pricing. With $457M in cash providing runway into H1 2028, the company has time to generate clinical data that could unlock significant value. AI Resilience is near-perfect at 9/10 — you cannot be disrupted by the technology you are building.
The bear case is the binary risk inherent in clinical-stage biotechs. GB-0895 Phase 3 success is not guaranteed, and failure would eliminate the company's most advanced clinical asset and potentially its market-supporting narrative. Revenue of $26.5M TTM is entirely collaboration-dependent (Novartis and Amgen milestones), declined 37.7% YoY due to milestone timing, and provides no visibility into sustainable revenue [1]. The company is deeply unprofitable with significant cash burn — even with $457M post-IPO, runway extends only to H1 2028, after which additional financing would likely dilute existing shareholders. Infrastructure scores only 3/10 due to minimal available financial metrics for a pre-profit company. The AI drug discovery space is increasingly crowded with Recursion, Insilico, Absci, and major pharma in-house AI efforts competing for the same opportunity.
