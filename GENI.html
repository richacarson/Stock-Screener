Company Profile
Communication Services ·Internet Content & Information ·NYSE ·United Kingdom
Genius Sports is a London-headquartered B2B provider of official sports data, AI-powered betting technology, and media technology to sports leagues, sportsbooks, and broadcasters worldwide. The company holds exclusive official data rights with major leagues including the NFL, NBA, WNBA, and Premier League (through 2029), and its GeniusIQ platform ingests real-time tracking, video, and stats feeds to power automated officiating, personalized broadcasting, and live in-play betting markets. Genius closed its $1.2B Legend acquisition on April 30, 2026, expanding into prediction markets and live sports advertising, and raised full-year 2026 revenue guidance twice to $1.005-$1.025B with Adjusted EBITDA of $285-$295M (~28.6% margin) [1].

Investment Thesis
Genius Sports at $6.58 with a $1.76B market cap controls the technological chokepoint of the sports data ecosystem — exclusive official data rights with the NFL, NBA, WNBA, and Premier League (through 2029), proprietary tracking technology that captures 10,000 body points at 200Hz versus 26 for the nearest rival, and 98% coverage of legalized US sports betting [3][4]. Q2 2026 revenue surged 64.7% YoY to $196M (beating guidance of $185M), the Media business exploded 193% YoY with 174 new customers, and the company raised full-year 2026 guidance to $1.005-$1.025B revenue with ~28.6% Adjusted EBITDA margin following the $1.2B Legend acquisition [1]. At 6.15x forward earnings with forward EPS estimates of $1.07, the stock trades at a fraction of its historical valuation as the profitability inflection approaches, and coordinated insider buying by the CEO, Chairman, and Legend founder (1.4M shares in February 2026) signals leadership conviction [8][9].
The bear case centers on persistent GAAP unprofitability and legal overhang. Despite 64.7% revenue growth, Q2 2026 produced a $77M net loss driven by non-recurring transaction costs, ROE remains -25.87%, and profit margin sits at -23.0% — the company has never posted a GAAP profit [1][9]. D/E of 120% reflects real leverage from the Legend acquisition, and any integration stumble could pressure the balance sheet. A March 2026 product liability lawsuit alleges Genius data products power 'engineered for addiction' microbetting alongside DraftKings, FanDuel, and the NFL, creating regulatory risk that could restrict the exact real-time betting data products driving growth [6]. An antitrust complaint co-names Genius and Sportradar for allegedly tying exclusive data rights to proprietary technology, threatening the exclusivity moat [10]. A CasinoBeats report also raised questions about undisclosed microbetting commission structures, which if confirmed could reframe the revenue quality narrative [6].
