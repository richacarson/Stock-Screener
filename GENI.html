Company Profile
Technology ·Sports Data & Media Technology (B2B) ·NYSE ·United Kingdom
Genius Sports is a London-headquartered B2B provider of official sports data, betting technology, and media technology to sports leagues, sportsbooks, and broadcasters worldwide. The company holds exclusive official data rights with major leagues including the NFL, NBA, WNBA, and Premier League (through 2029), and its GeniusIQ platform ingests real-time tracking, video, and stats feeds to power automated officiating, personalized broadcasting, and live in-play betting markets. Genius closed its $1.2 billion acquisition of Legend on April 30, 2026, expanding into prediction markets and live sports advertising, and raised full-year 2026 revenue guidance to $1.005-$1.025 billion.

Investment Thesis
Genius Sports controls the technological chokepoint of the sports data ecosystem — exclusive official data rights with the NFL, NBA, WNBA, and Premier League (through 2029), a tracking technology moat (10,000 body points at 200Hz vs. 26 points for the nearest rival), and 98% coverage of the legalized US sports betting market with NFL data [3][4]. FY2025 revenue grew 31% to $669.5M, and the $1.2B Legend acquisition, which closed April 30, 2026, pushes full-year 2026 guidance to $1.005-$1.025B in revenue with adjusted EBITDA margin targeted at 28%, up from 23% pre-acquisition [1]. Q2 2026 revenue rose 64.7% YoY, and insider buying by the CEO and Chairman in February 2026 signals leadership conviction in the growth trajectory.
The bear case centers on unresolved unprofitability and legal overhang: GAAP losses widened even as revenue grew in Q2 2026, ROE (-25.87%) and profit margin (-22.99%) remain deeply negative, and a Forward P/E of 145.75 prices in years of flawless execution [1][9]. A March 2026 product liability lawsuit alleges Genius Sports' data products help power 'engineered for addiction' microbetting alongside DraftKings, FanDuel, and the NFL — litigation risk that could result in regulatory restrictions on the exact real-time betting data products driving growth [5]. Genius is also a co-defendant with Sportradar in an antitrust complaint alleging anticompetitive tying of exclusive data rights, and D/E of 120% reflects real leverage taken on to fund the Legend deal. Any regulatory clampdown on in-play microbetting — the fastest-growing segment the company itself is powering — would directly threaten the growth thesis.
