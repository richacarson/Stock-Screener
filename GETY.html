Company Profile

Getty Images Holdings is one of the world's leading visual content creators and marketplaces, offering stock photos, editorial photography, video, and music through its Getty Images, iStock, and Unsplash brands. The company serves businesses, media organizations, and creative professionals globally. The $3.7 billion Shutterstock merger was terminated in July 2026 after the UK CMA blocked the deal, costing $100M+ in professional fees and financing costs. The stock has collapsed to $0.24 amid AI disruption, accelerating subscriber losses (active subscribers down 25% YoY to 240K), and a $1.98B debt burden. Q2 2026 revenue declined 2.5% YoY to $229.1M.

Investment Thesis
Getty Images owns the world's most valuable editorial content library and retains high Premium Access annual renewal rates for enterprise media customers, representing irreplaceable live-event photography and news content that AI cannot generate [1]. The NVIDIA partnership for licensed AI content generation positions Getty as the copyright-safe alternative to unlicensed generative AI tools, offering indemnification that risk-averse enterprise customers value [6]. Annual subscription revenue grew to 58.8% of total revenue in Q2 2026 (up from 53.5% YoY), demonstrating some success in shifting toward recurring revenue [2]. At $0.24, the stock trades at approximately 0.1x trailing revenue, implying the market has priced in near-total equity value destruction — any stabilization of the business or successful AI monetization could drive significant upside from this depressed base.
The bear case is overwhelming. Active annual subscribers declined 25% YoY to 240,000 in Q2 2026, subscription retention fell from 93.4% to 88.4%, and revenue declined 2.5% — the AI disruption thesis is no longer theoretical, it is materializing in accelerating customer losses [2]. The $1.98B debt burden at 362% D/E with only $96.6M cash creates severe financial distress risk; net debt of -$4.50/share far exceeds the $0.24 equity value, meaning the equity is effectively an out-of-the-money option on the debt being serviced [3]. The Shutterstock merger termination after $100M+ in fees eliminated the primary strategic response to AI disruption, leaving Getty as a standalone entity in a structurally declining market [5]. Search-engine AI overviews are eroding new-customer acquisition, and generative AI tools from Midjourney, DALL-E, and Adobe Firefly produce commercial-quality images at a fraction of Getty's licensing fees. Revenue guidance of $948-988M for 2026 implies contraction from 2025's $981M. The Inspire Impact Score of -62 (MISALIGNED) further limits investor interest from faith-based allocators.
