Company Profile
Industrials ·Security & Protection Services ·NASDAQ ·Thailand
Guardforce AI Co., Limited is an AI-driven technology company with over 43 years of history in secured logistics and retail services across Thailand and the Asia Pacific region. The company operates through two segments: legacy Secured Logistics (86.6% of revenue, primarily cash-in-transit and ATM management) and AI, Robotics-as-a-Service & Smart Solutions (13.4% of revenue, including AI-enabled robots, the DVGO AI agent platform, and the Intelligent Cloud Platform). In March 2026, Guardforce acquired pediatric AI therapy provider MGAI.

Investment Thesis
Guardforce AI is executing a genuine but high-risk pivot from a 43-year-old Thai secured logistics company ($35.2M revenue, 86.6% from legacy cash management) toward an AI and robotics platform [1]. FY2025 showed encouraging signals: the AI/RaaS segment grew 15.3% to $4.7M, R&D spending surged 115%, gross profit rose 7.5% to $5.3M, and the net loss narrowed 10.1% to $5.3M [1]. The March 2026 MGAI acquisition adds pediatric AI therapy capabilities, and the DVGO AI agent platform represents a nascent enterprise product [3]. CEO Olivia Wang's 7-year tenure provides strategic continuity, and the legacy business generates 99.96% recurring revenue providing transformation runway. The executive team shakeup raises organizational stability concerns during a critical transformation period [2]. Speculative upside exists if the AI pivot gains traction and the company achieves profitability, but fundamental quality does not support IOWN ownership at current scale and liquidity.
