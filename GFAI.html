Company Profile
Technology ·AI & Robotics / Security Services ·NASDAQ ·Thailand
Guardforce AI Co., Limited is an Asia-Pacific technology company evolving from legacy secured logistics (cash-in-transit, ATM management) into AI-driven solutions including robotics, smart retail, healthcare AI, and automated security. Led by CEO and Chairman Olivia Wang, the company reported FY2025 revenue of $35.23M (+8.0% YoY from continuing operations) with the AI, RaaS & Smart Solutions segment growing 15.3% to represent 13.4% of revenue. The company acquired MGAI to integrate an LLM-based speech therapy platform serving 110,000+ patients. Net loss narrowed to $5.29M with R&D investment up 115.4%. Market cap is approximately $10.8 million with cash of $23.45M ($0.94/share), exceeding the entire market capitalization.
