Company Profile
Industrials ·Specialty Industrial Machinery ·NYSE ·United States
Graco Inc. designs, manufactures, and markets systems and equipment used to move, measure, mix, control, dispense, and spray fluid and powder materials. Operating through Contractor, Industrial, and Expansion Markets segments, products include paint sprayers, fluid dispensing systems, lubrication equipment, and semiconductor/EV battery manufacturing pumps. Founded in 1926 and headquartered in Minneapolis, Graco reported Q2 2026 record sales of $591M (+3% YoY) with net earnings of $145M ($0.87 EPS, +14% YoY).

Investment Thesis
Graco offers a rare combination of industrial-quality economics with best-in-class financial strength — D/E of 2.1%, $508M net cash, 23.5% profit margins, 21.2% ROE, and $537M in free cash flow on $2.27B revenue [5]. Q2 2026 delivered record sales of $591M with 17% adjusted EPS growth driven by margin expansion and operational discipline even on modest 3% organic growth [1]. The company's 100-year track record of navigating industrial cycles, combined with exposure to secular growth markets (semiconductor manufacturing, EV battery production, infrastructure spending), positions Graco for durable long-term compounding. The acquisition pipeline targets growth acceleration, and the Inspire-ALIGNED score of +52 makes this one of the strongest faith-aligned industrials in the IOWN universe.
The bear case centers on growth stagnation and valuation premium. At 22x forward earnings and a 2.33x PEG ratio, Graco is priced as a premium compounder, but 3.3% organic revenue growth and low-single-digit guidance for FY2026 provide minimal growth to support the multiple [5]. The 5-year revenue CAGR of ~6% includes COVID recovery and may overstate sustainable organic growth, which has been closer to 3-5% in normalized years. The stock trades 19% below its 52-week high of $95.69, reflecting market skepticism about the growth trajectory. The 1.54% dividend yield is below the 2% threshold for dividend-focused investors, and while the 25-year dividend growth streak is impressive, the payout provides limited total return at current prices. Glassdoor reviews suggesting cultural issues under CEO Sheahan and a 3.6 culture rating raise concerns about talent retention in a competitive industrial labor market [3].
Key Catalysts
  1. Acquisition strategy targeting growth markets — semiconductor dispensing, EV battery manufacturing, and industrial automation — could accelerate revenue growth beyond organic low-single-digit guidance [1]
  2. Infrastructure spending tailwinds from federal programs (IIJA, CHIPS Act) driving demand for Graco's construction contractor sprayers and industrial dispensing equipment across U.S. manufacturing reshoring projects
  3. Margin expansion continuing through operational efficiency and pricing power — Q2 2026 delivered 17% EPS growth on only 3% revenue growth, demonstrating the leverage in Graco's business model [1]
Key Risks
  1. Organic revenue growth of 3.3% YoY and low-single-digit FY2026 guidance may not support the 22x forward P/E and 2.33x PEG ratio if growth fails to accelerate, risking multiple compression from the current $77 price level [5]
  2. Construction end-market exposure creates cyclical vulnerability — rising interest rates, housing slowdown, or commercial construction declines could pressure the Contractor segment which represents the largest revenue contributor [1]
  3. Glassdoor culture ratings of 3.6 and employee reviews citing disconnected leadership could impair talent attraction and retention in the competitive industrial engineering labor market, potentially slowing innovation and operational execution [3]
