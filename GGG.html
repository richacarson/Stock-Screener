Company Profile
Industrials ·Specialty Industrial Machinery ·NYSE ·United States
Graco Inc. designs, manufactures, and markets fluid handling equipment and systems for industrial, commercial, and consumer applications worldwide. Operates through three segments: Contractor (spray and painting equipment for professional and residential use), Industrial (pumps, valves, meters, and systems for manufacturing, lubrication, and finishing applications), and Expansion Markets (equipment for semiconductor, oil/gas, and infrastructure). Founded in 1926, headquartered in Minneapolis, Minnesota.
4,400 Employees ·www.graco.com

Investment Thesis
Graco delivered record Q2 2026 results with $591M revenue and $145M net income ($0.87 EPS, up 14% YoY), demonstrating the durability of its fluid handling franchise across Contractor, Industrial, and Expansion Markets segments [1]. The balance sheet is exceptional — D/E of just 2%, $712M cash, $631M TTM free cash flow — supporting 20 consecutive years of dividend growth, aggressive buybacks ($315M in Q2 alone), and strategic M&A including the Valco Melton acquisition targeting high-growth packaging dispense [1][3]. Gross margins of 53.6% and profit margins of 23.5% reflect pricing power and operational efficiency. New growth vectors in semiconductor manufacturing and data center infrastructure position Graco to benefit from AI-driven capex cycles.
The primary concern is valuation relative to Graco's modest organic growth profile. At 24.8x forward earnings and a PEG of 2.48, the stock is priced for mid-single-digit earnings growth, yet full-year guidance calls for only low-single-digit organic revenue growth [1]. Q1 2026 missed consensus estimates (EPS $0.66 vs $0.74 expected), revealing vulnerability to volume softness and tariff-related cost pressures [2]. The company's generic mission and lack of distinctive purpose limit its ability to attract purpose-driven talent and investors. Insider selling outpacing buying (6 sales vs 1 purchase in 6 months) raises questions about management conviction at current valuations [3]. International exposure (~35% of revenue) creates currency and geopolitical risk, particularly in China where automotive demand remains weak.
Key Catalysts
  1. Valco Melton acquisition adding high-growth corrugated packaging dispense platform with 50%+ gross margins, expanding Graco's total addressable market
  2. Semiconductor manufacturing investments in Asia Pacific and data center infrastructure applications creating new revenue streams tied to AI capex supercycle
  3. Backlog up 28% YTD with six-week booking trends up 14%, providing near-term revenue visibility and pricing power through 1.5-2% realization cadence
Key Risks
  1. PEG of 2.48 on low-single-digit organic growth creates significant multiple compression risk if the industrial cycle turns or tariff headwinds compress margins beyond the Q1 2026 60bps gross margin decline
  2. Heavy reliance on construction and industrial end-markets makes revenue cyclically sensitive to housing starts, non-residential construction spending, and manufacturing PMI
  3. Net insider selling (6 sales vs 1 purchase in 6 months) and generic corporate mission limit the stock's appeal to quality-focused and purpose-driven investors

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
8
AI Risk Protection (8/9): Graco's core business — designing and manufacturing physical fluid handling equipment including pumps, sprayers, valves, and meters — is structurally immune to AI substitution [4]. AI cannot spray paint, lubricate machinery, or dispense adhesives. The business requires deep mechanical engineering expertise, precision manufacturing, and a global distribution network that AI has no path to replicate. Graco's 10-K acknowledges generative AI as a general business risk but analysts rate AI margin pressure at only 3/10, confirming minimal near-term disruption threat [3]. Increased semiconductor manufacturing and data center construction driven by AI demand actually expands Graco's addressable market in the Expansion Markets segment [1]. AI Opportunity (0/1): No AI-powered products generating revenue. Graco uses AI/data analytics internally for customer service optimization but reports no quantified AI-specific revenue contribution [3].
