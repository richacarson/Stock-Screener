Company Profile
Technology ·Communication Equipment ·NASDAQ ·Israel
Gilat Satellite Networks Ltd. is a global provider of satellite-based broadband communications solutions, serving defense, homeland security, in-flight connectivity (IFC), and enterprise markets. The SkyEdge platform powers fixed and mobile satellite networks worldwide, and the DataPath subsidiary provides ruggedized SATCOM terminals for U.S. and allied military forces. In June 2026, Gilat announced the $157.5M acquisition of Comtech's Satellite & Space Communications segment, which will push combined annual revenue above $700M and more than double defense revenues. Founded 1987, headquartered in Petah Tikva, Israel. Market cap approximately $766M.

Investment Thesis
Gilat is executing a transformative growth strategy anchored by the $157.5M Comtech Satellite & Space acquisition, which will push combined revenue above $700M and more than double defense revenues [4]. Q2 2026 delivered $122.7M revenue (+17% YoY) with adjusted EBITDA of $15.4M, and 2026 guidance of $500-520M implies continued double-digit organic growth [1]. At $9.94 with a forward P/E of 12.97 — down sharply from 38.1 — the stock now trades at a compelling valuation for a defense-exposed technology company with AI differentiation and 22% 5yr revenue CAGR. The defense SATCOM market is experiencing secular growth driven by satellite modernization programs and contested environment communications needs, with DataPath securing $11M in new U.S. DoD orders and a $16M European MoD contract [3]. AI-powered network management and interference detection differentiate the platform [5]. The Inspire Impact Score of 46 (ALIGNED) with 11 positive attributions reflects strong ESG positioning.
The primary risk is integration execution and the balance sheet impact of the Comtech acquisition. The deal consumes virtually all of Gilat's $159M cash, leaving minimal margin for integration missteps [4]. Comtech's satellite segment generated only 8.6% EBITDA margins ($16.8M on $195.2M TTM revenue), which Gilat must improve to justify the purchase price. The stock's 52% decline from $20.93 to $9.94 reflects market concern about cash depletion, integration risk, and whether the combined entity can achieve targeted synergies [4]. The forward PEG of 4.99 indicates earnings growth is not keeping pace with the elevated trailing multiple. Net profit margin of 6.2% remains thin, and ROE of 7.0% is well below the 15% target for strong infrastructure scoring. CFIUS review for an Israeli company acquiring U.S. defense assets introduces regulatory uncertainty. Additionally, Gilat's reliance on defense contracts creates lumpy revenue patterns that can cause quarterly volatility [1].
