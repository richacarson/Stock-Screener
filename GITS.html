Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NASDAQ ·South Korea
Global Interactive Technologies, Inc. operates and develops Faning, a digital fan engagement platform designed to support online fan communities centered on K-pop and modern Korean culture. The platform provides content discovery, multilingual communication, user-generated content sharing, and digital engagement experiences. Founded in 2021 and headquartered in Seoul, South Korea, the company had annual revenue of approximately $1,900 in FY2025 and $96 in Q1 2026, with 3.67M shares outstanding after a 1-for-20 reverse stock split. The company has disclosed substantial doubt about its ability to continue as a going concern.
12 Employees ·www.faning.io

Investment Thesis
Global Interactive Technologies is a pre-revenue micro-cap operating the Faning digital fan engagement platform for K-pop and Korean culture communities. The company generated $1,900 in FY2025 revenue and $96 in Q1 2026 revenue with 12 employees, and has disclosed substantial doubt about its ability to continue as a going concern [2]. The K-pop fandom market is large and growing, but GITS has failed to capture any meaningful share despite years of development.
This stock is uninvestable under the IOWN framework. The company has no revenue base, no competitive differentiation against dominant platforms like Weverse, recurring Nasdaq compliance failures, and a market cap of $7.86M that precludes institutional participation [1][4]. The 1-for-20 reverse stock split was a cosmetic measure to avoid delisting rather than a signal of fundamental improvement. The only speculative case rests on the music IP partnerships pipeline, but without demonstrated platform traction or a path to monetization, these agreements lack commercial credibility. The SELL rating reflects a company that does not meet minimum standards for investment consideration across any IOWN dimension.
Key Catalysts
  1. Successful commercialization of music IP partnerships with key Korean artists could provide the content differentiation needed to drive meaningful user acquisition on the Faning platform
  2. A strategic partnership with or acquisition by a larger K-pop entertainment company (HYBE, SM Entertainment, JYP) could provide the resources and distribution needed to scale the platform
  3. Breakthrough user growth in Southeast Asian or Latin American K-pop fan markets — underserved regions with rapidly growing fandom — could validate the platform concept
Key Risks
  1. Going concern doubt disclosed in SEC filings with near-zero revenue and cumulative losses creates existential risk — the company may not survive long enough to commercialize its platform [2]
  2. Nasdaq delisting risk from recurring compliance failures (three delinquency notices in 2026 alone) would eliminate what little liquidity and visibility the stock has [4]
  3. The dominant position of HYBE's Weverse (100M+ users) and global social media platforms makes it nearly impossible for a 12-person startup to achieve meaningful market penetration in fan engagement
