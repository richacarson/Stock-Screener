Company Profile
Technology ·Information Technology Services ·NYSE ·Luxembourg
Globant is a digitally native technology services company specializing in AI-powered digital transformation, software engineering, and design. Founded in Argentina by co-founder and CEO Martin Migoya, the company operates across 30+ countries and serves clients including Google, Disney, and Electronic Arts. Globant pioneered AI Pods — subscription-based AI-native delivery teams generating $32.8M ARR with a $352M pipeline. The company's 'Be Kind' cultural framework integrates DEI, climate action, and ethics in AI.

Investment Thesis
Globant is a founder-led, culturally distinctive technology services company navigating the most significant industry disruption since offshore outsourcing, with early signs of adaptation through AI Pods ($32.8M ARR, $352M pipeline) and a $125M buyback signaling confidence [1][2]. Q1 2026 delivered $607.1M revenue exceeding guidance, adjusted operating margin of 14.1%, and free cash flow of $36.1M — the first positive Q1 FCF since 2019 [2]. At forward P/E of 5.21 and PEG of 0.85, the market has priced in severe AI disruption, creating significant upside if the transformation succeeds [5]. The Be Kind culture, 28,510 employees across 30+ countries, and two-decade track record under founder Migoya provide organizational depth uncommon in IT services [3]. Inspire Impact Score of 58 (ALIGNED) with zero negative attributions and 17 positive categories is the strongest faith alignment in its peer group.
The bear case centers on existential AI disruption risk. AI coding assistants and agents are directly threatening Globant's core business of providing outsourced software developers, and the stock's steep decline reflects genuine market concern that IT consulting may be structurally impaired [3][4]. Revenue growth collapsed from 37% in 2022 to essentially flat (1.6% in 2025, -0.7% in Q1 2026), and 2026 guidance of 0.2-2.2% suggests a plateau rather than inflection [1]. AI Pods generated $32.8M ARR — impressive growth but less than 1.5% of total revenue — raising questions about the pace of transformation relative to the speed of disruption [2]. GAAP profit margin of 4.46% and ROE of 5.20% are thin, and the 9.4% workforce decline in 2025 signals structural adjustment rather than growth-driven optimization [5]. Competition from Accenture, Infosys, and AI-native startups with deeper resources could outpace Globant's pivot.
Key Catalysts
  1. AI Pods ARR scaling from $32.8M toward the $60-100M 2026 target with $352M pipeline, proving the subscription-based AI-native model can replace traditional labor-scaled delivery [2]
  2. Revenue growth re-acceleration beyond the 0.2-2.2% 2026 guidance range as AI transformation gains traction with enterprise clients and the $125M buyback signals floor support [1][2]

Key Risks

  2. Revenue stagnation — Q1 2026 growth of -0.7% and 2026 guidance of 0.2-2.2% suggest the business has plateaued, with AI Pods not yet compensating for legacy delivery headwinds [1][2]
  3. Competitive pressure from larger IT services firms (Accenture, Infosys) and AI-native startups with deeper resources for the enterprise AI transformation wave [3]
