Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NASDAQ ·United States
Gloo Holdings, Inc. operates a technology platform for the faith and flourishing ecosystem, connecting churches, nonprofits, and network providers with data, media, marketing, and values-aligned AI tools. The platform includes Gloo Workspace, a subscription-based suite of AI-powered tools for churches, and Gloo 360, a solution for network capability providers. Led by CEO Scott Beck since 2013 and Executive Chair Pat Gelsinger (former Intel CEO), Gloo serves over 100,000 faith, ministry, and nonprofit leaders. FY2025 revenue (ended January 31, 2026) was $94.7M, up 308% YoY, with FY2026 guidance raised to $195M. The company has accumulated $438M in total deficit since founding.
700 Employees ·gloo.com

Investment Thesis
Gloo Holdings is a category-defining faith-tech platform delivering 308% revenue growth to $94.7M in FY2025, with FY2026 guidance raised to $195M [1]. The company occupies a unique position at the intersection of AI and the faith ecosystem, serving 100,000+ churches and nonprofits with values-based AI tools through Gloo Workspace [2]. Leadership is exceptionally credible: CEO Scott Beck (13-year tenure) provides mission continuity while Executive Chair Pat Gelsinger (former Intel CEO) brings world-class technology expertise and personal faith conviction [2].
The investment case hinges on whether explosive revenue growth can translate to profitability before the company's financial resources are exhausted. The $438M cumulative deficit, negative equity of $58.1M, and only $15.1M in cash create existential urgency despite the revenue trajectory [3]. The stock's 67% decline from its $9.98 all-time high reflects market skepticism about the path to sustainability. Under the IOWN framework, Gloo scores exceptionally well on innovation, inspiration, and AI resilience — the faith-tech mission is genuine, the AI strategy is differentiated, and the market position is dominant within its niche. However, the liquidity floor failure (daily dollar volume ~$543K) automatically zeros infrastructure scoring, capping the overall score. A HOLD recommendation at 61 reflects the tension between a uniquely positioned, mission-driven AI company and severe financial fragility that makes the stock unsuitable for immediate purchase under the framework.
Key Catalysts
  1. Achievement of sustained profitability would validate the business model after $438M in cumulative investment and could trigger a significant re-rating, as the FY2026 guidance of $195M revenue approaches scale where operating leverage should emerge
  2. Deepening AI capabilities under Pat Gelsinger's technology leadership could expand the platform's value proposition and pricing power, particularly as faith organizations increasingly adopt AI tools for content creation and community engagement
  3. Expansion beyond the U.S. church market into global faith communities and adjacent nonprofit sectors could dramatically expand the addressable market while leveraging the existing platform infrastructure
Key Risks

  2. The faith-tech market's willingness to pay premium SaaS pricing remains unproven at scale — churches and nonprofits are historically cost-sensitive, and achieving $195M in FY2026 revenue requires sustained growth from organizations with limited technology budgets
  3. General-purpose AI platforms from Google, Microsoft, and OpenAI could develop faith-specific features or integrations that undermine Gloo's differentiation, as the 'values-based AI' moat depends on faith communities valuing alignment over capability
