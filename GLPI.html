Company Profile
Real Estate ·REIT - Specialty ·NASDAQ ·United States
Gaming and Leisure Properties, Inc. is a self-administered Pennsylvania REIT spun off from Penn National Gaming in 2013. GLPI acquires, finances, and owns real estate properties leased to gaming operators under triple-net lease agreements. The portfolio includes 71 gaming and related facilities across 21 states, making it the largest pure-play gaming REIT. Tenants include Penn Entertainment, Caesars Entertainment, Boyd Gaming, Bally's Corporation, and The Cordish Companies. Q2 2026 revenue reached a record $430.5M with net income of $228.4M, and full-year AFFO guidance was raised to $4.10-$4.12 per share. The dividend was lifted 5.1% to $3.28 annualized.

Investment Thesis
GLPI offers a compelling income play with a 7.46% dividend yield (raised 5.1% in 2026) backed by triple-net lease agreements across 71 gaming properties in 21 states with zero rent defaults since inception [1]. Q2 2026 delivered record revenue of $430.5M and net income of $228.4M, with H1 2026 revenue growing 7.2% YoY to $850.5M. AFFO guidance was raised to $4.10-$4.12/share ($1.22B), and the stock trades at just 10.6x P/AFFO — a significant discount to net-lease REIT peers averaging 14-16x [1]. ROE of 17.62%, profit margin of 53%, and forward P/E of 15.86x represent attractive fundamentals for a REIT with 11 consecutive years of dividend growth. The $1.8B development pipeline at blended cap rates above 8% provides multi-year accretive growth visibility, while rent coverage ratios averaging 2x+ ensure income stability.
The primary risk is tenant credit quality deterioration in a recession — casino revenue is discretionary spending, and Bally's Corporation carries elevated leverage with only 2.20x lease coverage ratio, the thinnest in the portfolio [1]. D/E of 181% and net debt/EBITDA of ~5.0x limit acquisition flexibility without dilutive equity issuance. The GAAP payout ratio of ~99% constrains dividend growth to ~5% annually (below the 6% IOWN target), making the 7.5% yield dependent on stable interest rate expectations — if rates remain elevated, income investors may find comparable yields in lower-risk fixed income. Net insider selling across management in recent quarters signals lack of conviction at current valuation [2]. The 5-year dividend growth CAGR of 4.95% trails inflation-adjusted growth expectations. The Inspire Impact Score of -1 (MIXED) with a 'Gambling' negative attribution may limit faith-based investor participation regardless of financial fundamentals.

AI Risk Protection (8/9): GLPI's business is structurally immune to AI disruption. The company owns physical casino real estate leased under long-term triple-net agreements with built-in escalators — AI cannot replace physical casino buildings, land, or the regulatory gaming licenses across 21 states that underpin these properties [1]. Casino gaming has irreducible physical-world components (slot machines, table games, hospitality, entertainment) that AI augments rather than replaces. Rent coverage ratios averaging 2x+ across major tenants (Boyd 2.47x, Cordish PA Live! 2.55x, Bally's 2.20x) provide substantial buffer against any demand shifts [1]. The indirect risk of AI-powered online gambling cannibalizing physical casino attendance is mitigated by historical complementarity between online and land-based gaming. AI Opportunity (0/1): GLPI does not generate quantifiable revenue from AI products or services. While tenant operators may deploy AI-driven player analytics, GLPI as a property landlord has no direct AI revenue stream to cite.
