Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·Denmark
Genmab A/S is a Danish biotechnology company specializing in antibody-based therapeutics for cancer and other diseases. The company has eight approved antibody medicines on the market, including DARZALEX (multiple myeloma, via J&J), Kesimpta (multiple sclerosis, via Novartis), EPKINLY/TEPKINLY (lymphoma), and Tivdak (cervical cancer). Genmab's proprietary DuoBody bispecific technology platform and extensive partnership network with J&J, AbbVie, BioNTech, and Novo Nordisk drive its royalty-heavy revenue model.

Investment Thesis
Genmab is a premier antibody engineering platform company generating $3.72B in 2025 revenue (up 19% YoY), anchored by DARZALEX royalties (~$2.5B from $14.3B J&J net sales) and rapidly scaling proprietary products EPKINLY ($468M, +67%) and Tivdak [1]. Q1 2026 showed accelerating momentum with revenue of $896M (+25% YoY), royalties up 26% to $742M, and proprietary sales up 43% to $176M [2]. The 2026 guidance of $4.1-4.4B appears conservative relative to Q1 run-rate. At 16.95x forward P/E with ~21% profit margins and a deep late-stage pipeline ($5B+ potential), Genmab is attractively valued for a high-growth biotech with proven commercial execution and a durable DuoBody technology moat [5].
The bear case centers on DARZALEX concentration risk and margin compression. DARZALEX royalties represent approximately 67% of 2025 revenue, creating significant dependency on a single J&J-controlled product that will eventually face biosimilar competition [1]. Operating expenses are guided to increase 23-32% in 2026 ($2.7-2.9B vs $2.2B) as Genmab invests in late-stage trials and commercial launch readiness, compressing Q1 2026 profit margin to 5.9% from 27% a year earlier [2]. PEG ratio of 1.89 suggests the stock is not cheap relative to expected growth. The transition from royalty-dependent to self-commercializing company carries execution risk in building sales forces and market access capabilities across multiple geographies simultaneously.
