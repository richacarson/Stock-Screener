Company Profile
Healthcare ·Medical Devices ·NYSE ·United States
Globus Medical develops and commercializes healthcare solutions for musculoskeletal disorders, offering spine implants, orthopedic trauma products, hip and knee arthroplasty systems, and the ExcelsiusGPS robotic guidance and navigation platform. Founded in 2003 by David Paul and headquartered in Audubon, Pennsylvania, the company completed its transformative merger with NuVasive in 2023 and acquired Nevro (neuromodulation) in 2025, creating a ~$3B musculoskeletal technology platform.
7,500 Employees ·www.globusmedical.com

Investment Thesis
Globus Medical is building the most comprehensive musculoskeletal technology platform in medtech, with Q1 2026 revenue of $759.9M (up 27% YoY, 13.2% organic excluding Nevro) and GAAP EPS of $0.90 (up 66.6%) [1]. US Spine delivered its third consecutive quarter of 10%+ growth, and the ExcelsiusGPS robotic system achieved record placements with 59% YoY growth in Q4 2025, creating a recurring revenue flywheel that locks in surgeons to the Globus ecosystem [1][4]. With a virtually debt-free balance sheet (D/E 3%), adjusted gross margins exceeding 69%, and analyst consensus price target of $112.83 (33% upside from current $80), the financial profile is strong [5]. FY2025 full-year results of $2.94B revenue (up 16.7%) with non-GAAP EPS of $3.98 (up 30.8%) demonstrate the combined platform's earnings power [2].
The bear case centers on leadership instability, culture risk, and valuation. Three CEO changes since 2024 (Demski, Scavilla, Pfeil) signal organizational dysfunction that could disrupt NuVasive and Nevro integration execution and surgeon relationship continuity [3]. Glassdoor reviews describing the company as 'run like a dictatorship, all led by fear' with 'profits over patients' suggest a toxic culture that threatens talent retention and potentially patient safety — validated by the FDA warning letter for failing to investigate misplaced screw complaints. The PEG ratio of 2.11 implies the market is pricing in sustained high growth, but the 5yr revenue CAGR of ~25% is overwhelmingly acquisition-driven, and organic growth of ~10-13% is respectable but much less impressive. Forward P/E of 20.2x leaves little margin of safety if integration synergies disappoint or competitors like Medtronic (Hugo), Stryker (Mako), and Intuitive Surgical scale their robotic platforms against ExcelsiusGPS.

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
8
AI Risk Protection (7/9): Surgical implant manufacturing and robotic-assisted surgery require physical-world precision, FDA regulatory approval, surgeon training, and hospital procurement processes that AI cannot displace [1][4]. Globus's spine implants are literally placed inside human bodies — this is fundamentally immune to AI substitution. The ExcelsiusGPS robotic platform creates ecosystem lock-in through surgeon training and per-procedure consumable revenue. However, AI could modestly reduce surgical volume over time through improved AI diagnostics, non-surgical treatment recommendations, or AI-guided physical therapy that prevents some patients from reaching surgery. Competitors could also develop superior AI surgical planning algorithms. AI Opportunity (1/1): ExcelsiusGPS is an AI-enabled robotic navigation platform that directly generates revenue through system sales (record units in Q4 2025), rentals creating recurring revenue, and per-procedure consumables — AI and robotics are core to the business model and the Surgimap AI surgical planning platform is a shipping product [1][4].
