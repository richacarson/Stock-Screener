Company Profile
Consumer Discretionary ·Auto Parts & Equipment ·NASDAQ ·United States
Gentex Corporation is a global technology and electronics manufacturer founded in 1974, headquartered in Zeeland, Michigan. The company is the dominant global supplier of automatic-dimming rearview mirrors and has expanded into ADAS components, camera systems, displays, vehicle-to-home automation, premium audio (VOXX), aerospace, biometrics, and fire protection. CEO Steve Downing leads the company. Q1 2026 revenue was $675.4M (+17.1% YoY) and Q2 2026 was $651.3M (-1% YoY) with net income of $114.7M (+19%). FY2026 revenue guidance is $2.65-2.75B with FY2027 outlook of $2.80-2.90B. Market cap is approximately $5.0 billion.

Investment Thesis
Gentex at 10.30x forward earnings is a deeply undervalued industrial compounder with a dominant market position (90%+ global share in auto-dimming mirrors), a fortress balance sheet (D/E near 0%, $175M cash vs. $10.81M debt), and accelerating diversification into premium audio, aerospace, biometrics, and ADAS [1][4]. Q1 2026 revenue surged 17.1% YoY to $675.4M, and Q2 net income grew 19% with gross margin expanding to 37%. The FY2026 guidance of $2.65-2.75B with FY2027 outlook of $2.80-2.90B provides multi-year growth visibility. EPS growth of approximately 20% YoY, combined with aggressive share repurchases ($66M in Q2 alone) and a well-covered 2.06% dividend (25.4% payout ratio), creates a compelling total return profile. The Inspire Impact Score of 49 (ALIGNED) with 13 positive attributions and zero negative makes this an attractive holding for faith-based investors.
The bear case centers on automotive cyclicality, autonomous driving disruption risk, and margin sustainability. Q2 2026 automotive revenue declined 3% YoY due to weakness in Europe, Japan, Korea, and China, reminding investors that Gentex remains heavily exposed to global auto production volumes [1]. The gross margin expansion to 37% in Q2 was partially boosted by $18M in IEEPA tariff reimbursements — a non-recurring item that flatters underlying profitability. The longer-term risk is that Level 4+ autonomous vehicles could eliminate the need for human-facing mirrors entirely, threatening Gentex's core product category, though this timeline extends well beyond the 3-5 year investment horizon. Profit margin of 14.75% remains below the 20% target, reflecting the capital-intensive nature of precision manufacturing. The 8% blended revenue growth (combining the strong Q1 and weak Q2) sits below the 10% target, and sustained deceleration could limit multiple expansion from the current bargain-basement 10.30x forward P/E.

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
7
AI Risk Protection (6/9): Gentex's core auto-dimming mirror technology relies on proprietary electrochromic chemistry and precision manufacturing that AI cannot replicate — this is physical science, not software [2]. The company's dominant 90%+ market share creates switching costs (OEM qualification, supply chain integration) that provide structural protection. AI Opportunity (1/1): Gentex's camera systems, driver monitoring technology, and ADAS components directly benefit from the AI-driven autonomous vehicle trend. Premium audio (+16% growth) and aerospace/biometrics expansions provide additional AI-adjacent revenue streams [1].
