Alphabet at $340 with 22.6x forward earnings is the most complete AI platform in the world — Q2 2026 revenue of $119.8B (+24% YoY) with Google Cloud surging 82% to $24.8B and a $514B cloud backlog demonstrate massive returns on the $195-205B annual capex investment [1]. The PEG of 1.22 represents one of the best growth-at-reasonable-price opportunities among mega-caps, with ROE of 48.7% and D/E of 18.9% reflecting fortress-grade capital efficiency. The 12th consecutive quarter of double-digit revenue growth, Search maintaining 90% share while integrating AI Overviews, and Gemini powering products for billions of users create a compounding AI flywheel. Google Cloud's trajectory from $6B quarterly revenue in 2023 to $24.8B in Q2 2026 is one of the fastest enterprise businesses ever built [1].
The bear case centers on capex sustainability, antitrust risk, and AI competition. The $44.9B Q2 2026 capex (+100% YoY) with guidance of $195-205B for the full year is a historic commitment — any evidence of diminishing AI returns could trigger a de-rating [1]. The DOJ choice screen mandate could accelerate share loss to AI-native search alternatives (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude) that bypass the traditional search paradigm entirely [3]. The TTM profit margin of 54.8% is inflated by non-operating investment gains, and normalized operating margin of 34% faces pressure from the capex surge. Google's dependence on Search advertising (53% of total revenue) creates concentration risk if AI disrupts the search-and-click model. The Inspire Impact Score of -93 (MISALIGNED) severely limits allocation for faith-based investors. CEO Pichai's acknowledgment that AI will 'transition certain jobs' underscores the societal and regulatory risk surrounding Alphabet's dominant AI position [2].
