Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Gossamer Bio is a San Diego-based clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company whose lead asset is seralutinib, an inhaled PDGFR/CSF1R/c-KIT inhibitor for pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH). The company's PROSERA Phase 3 trial missed its primary clinical endpoint, prompting former partner Chiesi to terminate their collaboration and return full worldwide rights to Gossamer. The company has since cut roughly half its workforce, is pursuing an NDA submission in September 2026 based on a statistically significant CT imaging substudy, and is operating with less than one year of cash runway.

Investment Thesis
Gossamer retains one credible path forward: seralutinib's PROSERA CT imaging substudy showed statistically significant signals across the arterial, venous, and parenchymal vasculature that correlated with clinical outcomes, giving the company enough FDA engagement (a secured Pre-NDA Type B meeting) to target an NDA submission in September 2026 [1]. Regaining full worldwide development and commercial rights from Chiesi, plus a $5M termination payment, simplifies the capital structure and removes a 50/50 U.S. profit share that would have diluted any eventual approval's economics [2]. A successful, even narrow, FDA approval for a rare-disease indication with limited competition could produce outsized returns from the current $75M market cap.
The bear case is severe and immediate. The pivotal Phase 3 trial missed its primary endpoint, which is why Chiesi walked away from the partnership altogether — the company is now pursuing approval on a secondary imaging analysis, a materially higher-risk regulatory path than a clean primary-endpoint win [1][2]. Cash collapsed from $99.2M to $57M in a single quarter (a $42.2M burn), implying well under one year of runway without additional financing, and the company already cut roughly half its workforce in March 2026 [2][3]. The stock has fallen 92% over the past 52 weeks to $0.1542 and trades as a de facto binary regulatory-outcome bet; further dilution via equity raise is highly likely given the cash position, and profit margin of -235% reflects a company with essentially no sustainable revenue base. Every excellence sub-score (Innovation 3, Inspiration 2, Infrastructure 0) reflects a business in genuine distress, not merely undervalued.
