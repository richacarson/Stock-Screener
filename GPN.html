Company Profile
Financial Services ·Specialty Business Services ·NYSE ·United States
Global Payments is a leading pure-play commerce solutions provider delivering payments technology and software to approximately 4 million merchant locations and 1,300+ financial institutions globally. Following the transformative Worldpay acquisition (closed January 2026) and divestiture of Issuer Solutions to FIS, the company repositioned as a focused merchant acquiring platform. Q2 2026 GAAP revenue of $3.32B (+68.6% YoY reported, ~4% normalized) with adjusted EPS of $3.46 (+12% YoY) and 42.0% adjusted operating margin. FY2026 guidance: adjusted EPS $13.60-$13.80. Headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia.

Investment Thesis
Global Payments at 6.2x forward adjusted earnings and PEG of 0.30 is among the cheapest large-cap payment processors globally, reflecting extreme market skepticism about Worldpay integration execution [6]. Q2 2026 delivered adjusted EPS of $3.46 (+12% YoY) with a 42.0% adjusted operating margin on normalized constant-currency adjusted net revenue growth of ~4% [1]. FY2026 guidance of adjusted EPS $13.60-$13.80 implies continued double-digit earnings growth driven by Worldpay synergy capture and margin expansion [2]. The company processes ~$2.5T+ in annual payment volume with deeply embedded merchant relationships across 4 million locations, creating recurring revenue with high switching costs. At $91.05 with $822M in trailing free cash flow, the stock prices in near-zero value for the Worldpay franchise, offering significant upside if integration milestones are met and the multiple normalizes toward payment processor peer averages of 15-20x forward earnings.
The bear case centers on execution risk, leverage, and competitive erosion. The $22B+ debt load (98% D/E, 5.2x Debt/EBITDA) constrains financial flexibility and creates vulnerability to rising interest costs [6]. GAAP results are severely distorted -- Q2 2026 GAAP EPS was just $0.05 (down from $0.99) due to Worldpay acquisition amortization, and trailing GAAP profit margin is -9.15% [1]. The company lowered its FY2026 revenue growth outlook from 5.5% to 4-5% normalized, citing a 'persistent and larger-than-anticipated' Middle East conflict headwind on the travel portfolio (~100 bps impact), revealing sensitivity to geopolitical disruption [2]. Normalized organic growth of 4% trails faster-growing AI-native competitors Adyen (20%+), Stripe, and Block, raising questions about structural share loss in high-growth e-commerce segments. Glassdoor reviews flagging McKinsey-led layoffs of 2,700 employees, poor leadership, and internal politics suggest cultural challenges that could impair the already complex Worldpay integration [4].
