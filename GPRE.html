Company Profile
Energy ·Ethanol & Biofuels ·NASDAQ ·United States
Green Plains Inc. produces and markets low-carbon ethanol, ultra-high protein feed ingredients (Sequence), distillers grains, and renewable corn oil. Operates eight ethanol production facilities at 97% utilization, with a strategic transformation from commodity ethanol to a value-added biorefinery platform leveraging 45Z clean fuel production tax credits. New CEO Chris Osowski (since August 2025) is leading an operational turnaround. Q2 2026 showed $67.1M net income vs. a $72.2M loss a year earlier, with Adj EBITDA reaching $93.3M. SBTi-approved carbon reduction targets.

Investment Thesis
Green Plains has executed a dramatic profitability turnaround — Q2 2026 net income of $67.1M ($0.83 EPS) compared to a ($72.2M) loss a year earlier, with Adj EBITDA surging to $93.3M from $16.4M [1]. The biorefinery transformation is delivering: 45Z clean fuel production tax credits are generating an estimated $200-$225M in EBITDA for FY2026, and the Sequence ultra-high protein feed ingredient provides margin diversification beyond commodity ethanol. The stock trades at just 8.95x forward earnings with D/E of 63% (below the 70% target), ROE of 15.47% (above target), and a PEG of 1.16 [4]. SBTi-approved carbon reduction targets and an Inspire Impact Score of 57 (ALIGNED) with 15 positive attributions support faith-based investment criteria. Plant utilization of 97% across eight facilities demonstrates operational execution under CEO Osowski.
The bear case centers on policy dependency, revenue decline, and organizational challenges. Revenue declined 19.3% YoY to $446.2M due to facility divestitures, and the profitability turnaround is substantially policy-dependent — the 45Z clean fuel production tax credits could be reduced, restructured, or eliminated by future legislation, collapsing the earnings foundation [1]. Ethanol crush margins remain highly cyclical — the swing from ($72.2M) loss to $67.1M profit in one year demonstrates extreme earnings volatility that makes sustainable profitability uncertain. Profit margin of 6.78% is thin for an industrial company and leaves limited buffer for margin compression. Glassdoor ratings of 3.0/5 (culture 2.5/5) with polarized reviews suggest inconsistent employee experience that could impede execution on the biorefinery transformation [2]. The stock has declined from $16.52 to $15.06 despite improved fundamentals, suggesting market skepticism about earnings sustainability. Revenue 5yr CAGR is likely negative due to facility divestitures, reflecting a structurally shrinking revenue base.
Key Catalysts
  1. Continued 45Z clean fuel production tax credit economics generating $200-$225M in FY2026 EBITDA — if policy stability extends through 2027+, this creates a recurring high-margin earnings stream that could normalize profitability at 8.95x forward P/E [1]
  2. Sequence ultra-high protein feed ingredient scaling across all eight facilities, commanding premium pricing over commodity distillers grains and further diversifying the revenue mix away from policy-dependent economics
  3. Carbon capture and sequestration (CCS) program advancing toward commercial operation, potentially generating additional carbon credit revenue and further reducing carbon intensity to maximize 45Z credit value [1]
Key Risks
  1. Tax credit dependency — a substantial portion of 2026 profitability derives from 45Z production tax credits ($200-$225M EBITDA guidance), and any change in U.S. renewable fuel policy (expiration, reduction, tighter qualification standards) would collapse the earnings foundation [1]
  2. Revenue in structural decline at -19.3% YoY ($446.2M vs $552.8M) due to facility divestitures, and 5yr revenue CAGR is likely deeply negative as the company shrank from $3B+ to ~$2B in annual revenue — the company must prove it can grow revenue from the restructured base
  3. Organizational instability — Glassdoor 3.0/5 stars with culture at 2.5/5, CEO change in August 2025, facility closures reducing workforce to 642, and polarized employee reviews signal execution risk on the biorefinery transformation strategy [2]
