Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NYSE ·United States
Grindr Inc. operates the world's largest social networking and dating platform for the LGBTQ+ community, serving approximately 15 million monthly active users globally. The platform generates revenue through premium subscriptions (Grindr Unlimited, XTRA, and the upcoming Edge tier) and advertising. CEO George Arison, appointed Chairman in June 2026, has positioned Grindr as an AI-native company with a lean, high-velocity operating model. Went public via SPAC merger in November 2022. Q2 2026 revenue of $138M (+33% YoY) with full-year 2026 guidance of $540M. Headquartered in West Hollywood, California.
172 Employees ·www.grindr.com

Finite vs Infinite Game — Sinek (25%)
Mindset: FINITE Overall: 3 /10
Grindr under CEO George Arison operates with impressive operational velocity and strategic clarity, but the company's approach to its workforce, user data, and competitive positioning reflects finite game thinking. The 2023 RTO mandate that cost 45% of staff prioritized short-term operational efficiency over organizational trust. Repeated data privacy violations — sharing HIV status, location tracking for advertising, and the $35M UK settlement in September 2026 — demonstrate willingness to monetize user vulnerability in ways that contradict the community-safety mission [3]. Arison's aggressive monetization strategy delivers outstanding financial results but raises questions about whether the company plays to serve its community or to extract maximum value from a captive audience.
Just Cause

Just Cause (1/3): Grindr's stated purpose of connecting the global LGBTQ+ community addresses a genuine human need — millions of LGBTQ+ individuals, especially in hostile environments, rely on the platform for safe community access [1]. Investment Thesis
Grindr delivered strong Q2 2026 results — $138M revenue (+33% YoY), $58M adjusted EBITDA (42% margin), and raised full-year guidance to $540M revenue and $232M adjusted EBITDA [1]. At 20x forward P/E with a PEG of ~0.8, the stock is attractively priced relative to its 30%+ growth trajectory. Grindr's monopoly position in LGBTQ+ social networking (15M MAU with no close competitor) provides durable pricing power and network effects. CEO Arison's AI-native operating model delivers exceptional capital efficiency — $540M guided revenue from just 172 employees implies ~$3.1M revenue per employee [1][2]. Advertising revenue growth of 68% YoY in Q1 2026 ($23M) represents a largely untapped second revenue engine alongside subscriptions.
Grindr's growth story carries significant IOWN framework risks beyond pure financials. The ~852% D/E ratio ($400M+ debt against thin book equity) reflects SPAC-era leverage that constrains balance sheet flexibility [4]. Repeated data privacy violations — including the Norwegian EUR 6M+ fine, ongoing location data controversies, and a fresh $35M UK settlement in September 2026 for sharing private data of thousands of users — expose the company to escalating regulatory and reputational risk globally [3]. The 45% workforce exodus from the 2023 RTO mandate created cultural debt reflected in Glassdoor ratings of 3.1/5 for culture and only 45% recommending the company [2]. Revenue growth is moderating (38% Q1 → 33% Q2) as pricing changes anniversary, and growth was primarily pricing-driven rather than user growth-driven.
