Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Gold ·NYSE American ·Canada
Gold Royalty Corp. is a precious metals-focused royalty and streaming company that provides creative financing solutions to the metals and mining industry. Founded in 2020 by David Garofalo (former CEO of Goldcorp), the company has assembled a portfolio of over 240 royalties and streams primarily in low-risk jurisdictions across Canada, the United States, Mexico, and Brazil. GROY operates a capital-light business model by providing upfront financing to mining operators in exchange for royalty interests, generating revenue without bearing ongoing operating costs.

Investment Thesis
Gold Royalty is an early-stage royalty aggregator positioned to benefit from record gold prices, a rapidly maturing portfolio, and a 2030 GEO outlook of 28,000-34,000 ounces representing nearly 5x current production — all from existing assets without requiring additional capital [1]. Q1 2026 delivered record revenue of $7.2M (+130% YoY), record Adjusted EBITDA of $7.0M (+318%), and the company is on track for 7,500-9,300 GEOs in 2026, confirming the business is approaching the operational leverage inflection characteristic of successful royalty model maturation [1]. With zero debt, a $150M undrawn credit facility, and 7 out of 7 analysts rating the stock a Buy with a $6.00 consensus price target (107% upside from $2.90), GROY offers leveraged exposure to gold prices via a capital-light, fortress-balance-sheet platform [4].
The primary risk is the time horizon required for development-stage royalties to reach production. While Q1 2026 turned GAAP-profitable ($1.8M net income), sustained profitability depends on continued mine ramp-ups at Cote Gold, Borborema, Pedra Branca, and Cozamin [1]. At a Forward P/E of 35.4x, the stock prices in substantial future growth — a gold price correction or development delays at key royalty assets could sharply compress the valuation. The December 2025 $103.5M equity raise at $4.00/share has diluted existing shareholders (254M shares outstanding), and GoldMining Inc.'s 15% ownership position creates potential overhang risk. The royalty model's capital-light advantages are real but GROY remains a junior player at $738M market cap versus Franco-Nevada's $25B+, requiring continued accretive deal sourcing to justify its growth premium.

AI Resilience LOW RISK

AI Risk Protection (8/9): Gold Royalty's business model is IMMUNE to AI disruption. The company earns contractual royalties from physical gold, silver, copper, and PGM mining operations — AI cannot replace the extraction of minerals from the earth or displace the legal/financial construct of a royalty interest [1][3]. The royalty model adds a second layer of insulation: GROY doesn't operate mines, it collects contractual payments from operators, making it doubly insulated from any AI-related operational disruption. Furthermore, AI-driven productivity improvements at partner mines would be net positive for GROY by improving project economics and mine life without any cost exposure to GROY [1].
