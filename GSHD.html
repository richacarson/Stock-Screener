Company Profile
Financial Services ·Insurance Brokers ·NASDAQ ·United States
Goosehead Insurance, Inc. operates as one of the largest personal lines insurance agencies in the United States, using a technology-enabled franchise model to help clients shop across 180+ insurance carriers for homeowners, auto, flood, umbrella, and specialty lines. Founded in 2003, the company distributes through corporate agents and franchise agencies, leveraging its proprietary Digital Agent platform, AI-powered Lily customer service tool, and Salesforce Einstein 1 integration. Q2 2026 total revenues were $113.4M (+21% YoY) with net income of $17.0M, policies in force of 2.1M (+15%), and total written premiums of $1.34B (+14%). Market cap is approximately $2.51B.
1,600 Employees ·www.goosehead.com
