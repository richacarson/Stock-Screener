Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Other Industrial Metals & Mining ·NASDAQ ·United Kingdom
Ferroglobe PLC produces and sells silicon metal, silicon-based ferroalloys, and manganese-based ferroalloys globally. The company supplies silicon metal to aluminum, silicone chemical, solar, and semiconductor industries, and operates quartz mines, coal mines, and a hydroelectric power plant across the U.S., Europe, South Africa, and Canada. CEO Marco Levi has led since 2020. Q2 2026 revenue of $378.6M topped expectations, and management guides FY2026 revenue of $1.5-1.7B, implying ~20% growth.

Investment Thesis
Ferroglobe is a deep-value cyclical turnaround play trading at $3.98 with forward P/E of 9.59x and improving profitability trajectory. Q2 2026 revenue of $378.6M topped consensus estimates with silicon metal revenue surging 25.8% sequentially, and management guides FY2026 revenue of $1.5-1.7B implying ~20% growth driven by trade protections and alloy demand recovery [1][2]. The turnaround thesis rests on three structural catalysts: the 10-year French energy contract reducing costs and enabling year-round production; EU safeguard duties and U.S. anti-dumping rulings protecting margins from Chinese import competition; and growing demand for high-purity silicon from semiconductor, solar, and EV battery industries benefiting from AI infrastructure buildout [3]. ROE improved from -15.3% to -5.99% and profit margin from -8.5% to -2.96%, indicating the turnaround is progressing. Chairman and director insider purchases at $3.86 signal management confidence [5].
The bear case centers on persistent losses, commodity cyclicality, and trade protection dependence. Despite improvement, profit margin remains negative at -2.96% and ROE at -5.99% — the company is still losing money, and the turnaround depends partly on commodity price recovery outside management's control [5]. Revenue peaked at $2.3B in 2022 and the TTM figure of $1.37B reflects extreme cyclical volatility that the 5yr CAGR of ~5% obscures. Cash position deteriorated from $134M to $93M against $251M debt, and while operating cash flow of $48M is positive, the margin of safety is thin [5]. The stock's 25% recovery from $3.18 to $3.98 prices in some turnaround improvement but at $744M market cap, the upside depends on margin recovery that may take 2-3 more quarters to materialize. Environmental regulatory risk is elevated given smelting operations, and trade protections (EU safeguards, anti-dumping orders) can be reversed or circumvented by Chinese producers through third-country routing [4].
