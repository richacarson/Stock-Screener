Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
GT Biopharma, Inc. is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on developing immuno-oncology therapeutics using its proprietary TriKE (Tri-specific Killer Engager) natural killer cell platform. Lead program GTB-3650 targets CD33-expressing blood cancers (acute myeloid leukemia) in Phase 1 trials. Second candidate GTB-5550 targets B7-H3-expressing solid tumors with first patient dosed in May 2026. The company completed a reverse stock split and recently deployed AI-based technologies for drug discovery acceleration. Only 1 full-time employee (CEO Michael Breen), operating through contractors and CROs. Cash of $4.96M with $0 total debt. Market cap approximately $12.9M.
