Company Profile
Industrials ·Specialty Industrial Machinery ·NYSE ·United States
Gates Industrial Corporation plc is a global manufacturer of application-specific fluid power and power transmission solutions. The company operates through two segments: Power Transmission (belts, tensioners) and Fluid Power (hoses, connectors, couplings). Gates serves automotive aftermarket, industrial, and emerging data center cooling markets across 30+ countries. Founded in 1911, incorporated in England and Wales, headquartered in Denver, Colorado. Q2 2026 net sales reached $941.6M (+6.6% YoY) with raised full-year guidance, and TTM revenue of approximately $3.5B.

Investment Thesis
Gates Industrial at 17.38x forward earnings offers reasonable value for a global industrial leader executing a multi-year pivot toward secular growth markets. Q2 2026 revenue of $941.6M (+6.6% YoY) with record adjusted EBITDA of $211.4M (22.5% margin) prompted management to raise full-year guidance across sales, profitability, and EPS ($1.62-1.70) [1]. Personal mobility sustained ~25% core growth in Q2 and commercial on-highway grew 20%+, validating the EV content strategy [1]. Data center cooling (targeting $100-200M by 2028) provides a new growth vector tied to AI infrastructure buildout, with the Data Master MegaFlex product line engineered for direct-to-chip cooling [2]. The balance sheet is conservative at 65% D/E with $812M cash, and the stock trades below its 52-week high of $30.34 [4].
The core concern is that Gates remains a low-margin industrial with GAAP profit margin of 7.30% and ROE of 7.88%, well below industrial peers, suggesting the company is not generating adequate returns on its asset base despite cost-optimization efforts [4]. The 5-year revenue CAGR of approximately 2.5% — with revenue oscillating between $3.39-3.57B since 2021 — shows that growth initiatives have not yet overcome cyclical headwinds in legacy automotive and industrial markets. Under Blackstone ownership since 2014, talent flight on the innovation team threatens the R&D pipeline that must deliver differentiated cooling solutions against established competitors like Parker Hannifin and Continental [3]. Full-year 2026 core sales guidance of only 2.5-4.5% provides limited upside visibility, and the trailing PE of 29.14x prices in more recovery than TTM earnings currently justify [4]. The data center opportunity, while exciting, remains a small fraction of the $3.5B revenue base and requires successful execution at scale.
