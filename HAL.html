Investment Thesis
Halliburton at $31.85 with 11.0x forward earnings and a PEG of 0.66 offers deep value exposure to a recovering international oilfield services cycle — Q2 2026 revenue of $5.7B (+4% YoY) featured the highest Q2 international revenue in over a decade at $3.4B (+6% YoY), driven by Latin America strength and Middle East activity [1]. Completion and Production revenue grew 6% sequentially with operating income up 8%, signaling improving profitability. Free cash flow supports a 2.1% dividend yield with a conservative 35.6% payout ratio. The company's AI-powered digital platforms (DecisionSpace 365, Prodigi, ADNOC partnership) create service differentiation and margin expansion opportunities [2]. The Inspire Impact Score of 38 (ALIGNED) makes Halliburton one of the better-scoring energy companies for faith-based investors.
The bear case centers on cycle risk, thin margins, and organizational culture. Profit margin of 7.2% and ROE of 14.9% are structurally low for oilfield services and leave minimal buffer in a downturn — any OPEC+ production increases or oil price weakness could compress margins further [1]. TTM revenue of ~$22.2B is flat to slightly down from 2024 levels (~$22.9B), and the 3.7% quarterly growth rate is decelerating from 39%+ seen in 2022, suggesting diminishing returns from the post-COVID recovery cycle. Glassdoor reviews describing 'quiet compliance' culture and the company's disclosures of high turnover risk signal organizational dysfunction that could impair execution and innovation [2]. The 5-year dividend growth CAGR of ~16.6% is misleading — it reflects recovery from a near-zero post-2020 cut base rather than genuine compounding, and the payout has been flat since 2024. Management expects only mid- to high single-digit revenue growth for 2026, well below the pace needed to drive meaningful multiple expansion.

  1. International revenue growth acceleration — Q2 2026 international revenue of $3.4B (+6% YoY) was the highest Q2 figure in over a decade, with management guiding for mid- to high single-digit 2026 revenue growth driven by Latin America and recovering Middle East activity [1]
  2. AI and digital services margin expansion — DecisionSpace 365, Prodigi, and the ADNOC AI-enabled drilling partnership position Halliburton to command premium pricing in technology-differentiated oilfield services as the industry shifts toward automation [2]

  2. Workplace culture and talent retention — Glassdoor's 67% recommendation rate with reports of 'quiet compliance,' toxic management, and merit-disconnected advancement suggest organizational dysfunction that could impair execution in an increasingly AI-competitive labor market [2]
  3. Oilfield services cycle maturation — TTM revenue of ~$22.2B is flat to down from 2024, and management's mid- to high single-digit growth guidance suggests the post-COVID super-cycle has peaked, with decelerating returns limiting upside [1]
