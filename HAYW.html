Company Profile
Industrials ·Pool & Spa Equipment ·NYSE ·United States
Hayward Holdings is a global designer, manufacturer, and marketer of pool and outdoor living equipment including pumps, filters, heaters, chlorinators, cleaners, LED lighting, and connected automation systems (OmniHub, OmniPL controllers). The company serves the residential and commercial pool aftermarket (85% of North American net sales) and new construction market. Under CEO Kevin Holleran (since 2019), Hayward has invested in connected pool technology and IoT-enabled equipment. Q2 2026 revenue was $318.4M (+6% YoY) with full-year guidance of ~5% revenue growth. Market cap is approximately $2.8B.

Investment Thesis
Hayward at 14.2x forward earnings offers a reasonably valued play on the secular pool aftermarket replacement cycle. Aftermarket sales represent 85% of North American revenue, providing recurring demand from the installed base of ~5.7 million in-ground pools in the US regardless of new construction trends [1]. Q2 2026 adjusted EBITDA of $92.7M (+5% YoY) on $318.4M revenue demonstrates stable profitability, and the connected pool technology platform (OmniHub, SmartPower) drives positive price/mix as customers upgrade from analog to IoT-enabled equipment. D/E of 63% with no near-term debt maturities (2028 extensions) provides balance sheet stability, and analyst consensus of $17.93 price target implies 37% upside from the current $13.07 [2]. The 7.8% TTM revenue growth marks a return to organic growth after the post-COVID normalization trough, with the full-year guidance of ~5% providing a conservative floor.
The bear case centers on low growth, new construction exposure, and valuation relative to growth rate. Revenue growth of 5-8% with an EPS growth forecast of only ~12% per year over the next three years is modest for a stock trading at 14.2x forward earnings with a PEG of 1.14 -- this is not a growth compounder but a slow-growth industrial with cyclical exposure to housing starts. The 5-year revenue CAGR of ~1.5% reveals that the post-COVID normalization erased most of the pandemic-era gains, and the installed pool base is mature rather than growing rapidly. The 15% of revenue from new construction is directly tied to housing activity, which remains constrained by elevated mortgage rates. Glassdoor culture rating of 2.7/5 (15% below manufacturing peers) raises concerns about talent retention and organizational execution -- the leadership and accountability gaps described by employees could impair innovation and operational improvement [4]. ROE of 10.41% is below the 15% target, suggesting the capital structure is not generating adequate returns for shareholders.   3. Organizational culture challenges -- Glassdoor 2.7/5 rating with complaints about leadership accountability and executive inaction could impair the company's ability to execute on connected technology strategy and attract engineering talent [4]

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
8
AI Risk Protection (8/9): Pool equipment manufacturing is a physical-world business that AI structurally cannot displace. Pumps, filters, heaters, chlorinators, and automation controllers are physical products requiring manufacturing, distribution, installation by licensed contractors, and ongoing aftermarket service [1]. The aftermarket represents 85% of North American net sales -- installed pools require replacement equipment regardless of AI advancement. The business is protected by plumber/contractor relationships, local distribution networks, and building code compliance requirements that AI cannot substitute. The connected pool technology layer (OmniHub, smart pumps) adds a digital interface but the underlying value is physical equipment performance and reliability. AI could theoretically enhance predictive maintenance or competitive smart home integration, but the core revenue stream from physical equipment is immune to AI substitution. AI Opportunity (0/1): Hayward generates no revenue from AI products or services. The Head of Data, Analytics & AI position suggests internal investment, but no AI-generated revenue or quantified cost savings have been disclosed.
