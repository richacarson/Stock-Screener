Company Profile
Financial Services ·Banks - Regional ·NASDAQ ·United States
Huntington Bancshares is a $225 billion regional bank holding company headquartered in Columbus, Ohio, operating primarily across the Midwest and Southeast through approximately 1,000 branches. The company provides commercial, consumer, and mortgage banking services, and has recently transformed its scale through the acquisitions of TCF Financial (2021), Cadence Bank, and Veritex, expanding from $80B to $225B in assets. CEO Steve Steinour has led the company since 2009. Q2 2026 delivered record results: net income of $727M (+36% YoY), revenue of $2.857B, NII of $2.1B (+40%), and ROTCE of 15.1% (17.5% adjusted). The company employs 20,424 people and pays a $0.62 annual dividend (3.49% yield). Market cap approximately $35.9B.
