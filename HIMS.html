Investment Thesis
Hims & Hers continues to post exceptional top-line growth — Q2 2026 revenue rose 38% YoY to $753M with nearly 2.9M subscribers, and management raised full-year guidance to $3.1-3.3B [1]. The Novo Nordisk partnership, Wegovy pill distribution, and Eucalyptus acquisition (driving 17x international revenue growth) give the growth story multiple legs beyond U.S. telehealth. A 5-year revenue CAGR of ~71% reflects genuine platform scaling, and the company generates $849M in free cash flow despite GAAP losses, suggesting operational leverage is beginning to emerge. At $29.00 per share with a forward P/E of 39x, the valuation has compressed significantly from the $65 52-week high, creating a more reasonable entry if growth sustains.
The bear case is serious and multi-layered. The FTC, joined by Utah and California, sued Hims & Hers in July 2026 alleging deceptive billing practices, unauthorized data sharing with Meta and Snap, and unclear subscription disclosure — a direct regulatory threat to the core business model [5]. Every profitability metric hits its Infrastructure scoring floor: D/E of 477%, ROE of -32%, profit margin of -5.5%, reflecting a company not yet sustainably profitable despite $2.58B TTM revenue [4]. Glassdoor reviews reveal a low-trust culture with 29% CEO approval and 'toxic' work environment descriptions, suggesting organizational fragility beneath the growth surface [3]. The forced exit from compounded semaglutide shows the GLP-1 franchise remains exposed to regulatory risk. The stock trades 56% below its 52-week high of $65, and the Inspire Impact Score of -62 (MISALIGNED, Abortifacients) excludes it from faith-based portfolios.

AI Risk Protection (6/9): Hims & Hers' core business — licensed clinicians prescribing regulated medications through a state-by-state compliance framework — has structural protection from pure AI substitution, since prescribing authority cannot be automated end-to-end by AI under current regulations [1].
