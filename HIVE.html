Company Profile
Financial Services ·Capital Markets ·NCM ·United States
HIVE Digital Technologies operates green energy-powered data centers for Bitcoin mining and GPU-based high-performance computing (HPC) across Sweden, Canada, and Paraguay. The company mined 2,885 Bitcoin in FY2026 (ended March 2026) and is expanding its BUZZ HPC platform to provide GPU-as-a-Service for AI workloads. FY2026 total revenue reached $297.8M (+158% YoY), with $278.3M from mining and $19.5M from HPC services. Headquartered in San Antonio, Texas, HIVE operates with 29 employees and targets $750M ARR by end of calendar 2026.

Investment Thesis
HIVE at $2.56 with a $702M market cap is a high-growth Bitcoin miner and emerging AI infrastructure provider that delivered FY2026 revenue of $297.8M (+158% YoY), mined 2,885 BTC, and generated $72.9M adjusted EBITDA (24% margin) [1]. The BUZZ HPC platform — providing GPU-as-a-Service from green energy-powered tier-3 data centers — represents a high-value diversification from Bitcoin mining's cyclicality, with $19.5M in FY2026 HPC revenue and management targeting $750M ARR by end of 2026, including $225M from HPC/AI services [1][2]. The green energy positioning (100% renewable sourcing from hydro and geothermal) differentiates HIVE from competitors and provides structural cost advantages in energy-intensive operations.
The bear case centers on Bitcoin price dependency and execution risk. Despite the HPC pivot, mining still generates 93% of revenue ($278M of $298M), making HIVE's financials hostage to Bitcoin price swings — Q4 FY2026 mining revenue dropped 23.9% sequentially on a 27% Bitcoin price decline [1]. GAAP profit margin of -98.5% reflects massive depreciation and impairment charges on mining equipment whose useful life compresses with each technology upgrade cycle. The $750M ARR target by end of 2026 appears extremely aggressive given current run rates ($298M FY2026 revenue), and the HPC business must scale from $19.5M to $225M — an 11x increase — to hit its component of that target. D/E of 88.6% with $374M debt and only $219M cash creates leverage risk in a downturn. The stock's beta of 3.55 reflects extreme Bitcoin-correlated volatility that will amplify any correction in crypto markets [4].
Key Catalysts
  1. BUZZ HPC scaling from $19.5M to $100M+ in GPU-as-a-Service revenue as enterprise AI demand accelerates, providing recurring contract-based revenue that diversifies away from Bitcoin price volatility [1][2]
  2. Green energy-powered operations provide structural cost advantages and ESG differentiation as AI data center energy consumption faces increasing regulatory scrutiny, positioning HIVE favorably with enterprise customers and ESG-conscious investors [3]
  3. Continued Bitcoin hashrate expansion and potential for higher BTC prices would amplify mining revenue — the fourfold hashrate increase in FY2026 demonstrates operational scaling capability [1]
Key Risks
  1. Bitcoin price dependency with 93% of revenue from mining — a sustained BTC decline below $50K would severely compress margins and force capital allocation trade-offs between mining equipment upgrades and HPC investment [1]
  2. Aggressive $750M ARR target by end of 2026 including $225M from HPC requires 11x scaling of a $19.5M business in under a year, creating execution risk and potential credibility damage if missed [1][2]
  3. GAAP net losses driven by equipment depreciation and impairments obscure cash generation metrics, and beta of 3.55 means the stock will amplify any broader market or crypto selloff, creating extreme portfolio volatility [4]
