Investment Thesis
Hecla at $17.20 is the largest U.S. silver producer with record momentum: Q1 2026 revenue exceeded $411M (+100% YoY) and Q2 sales were $333.9M (+52%), adjusted EBITDA more than doubled to $199M, and the company ended Q2 debt-free with $483M in cash [1]. Forward P/E of 16.7, ROE of 22% and exposure to silver demand from AI, solar and connectivity infrastructure make it a levered way to own the cycle [2].
The bear case is that earnings are a function of silver prices: Q2 income fell to $118M from $165M in Q1 and revenue dropped sequentially from $411M to $334M, evidence of volatility [1]. Mine-level operating risk (shafts, safety, permitting for Libby and Keno Hill), mixed employee sentiment (43% recommend), and cyber exposure amplified by generative AI add idiosyncratic risk [2]. Shares trade below the 50-day average of $18.25.
Key Catalysts
  1. Silver price strength supported by AI, solar and connectivity demand lifting realized margins [2]
  2. Debt-free balance sheet with $483M cash funding organic growth at Keno Hill, Lucky Friday and Libby exploration [1]
  3. Continued emissions reductions (30% below 2019) and Indigenous community partnerships improving social license [3]
Key Risks
  1. Silver and gold price volatility: sequential Q2 revenue decline of about 19% and income decline of about 28% versus Q1 2026 [1]
  2. Operational and permitting risk at underground mines and the Libby project in Montana [2]
  3. Weak employee sentiment (3.2/5 Glassdoor, 43% recommend) and cybersecurity risk amplified by generative AI [2]
