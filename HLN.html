Company Profile
Healthcare ·Drug Manufacturers - Specialty & Generic ·NYSE ·United Kingdom
Haleon is the world's largest pure-play consumer healthcare company, spun off from GSK in July 2022. The company markets leading brands across oral health (Sensodyne, parodontax), OTC pain relief (Advil, Voltaren), vitamins/minerals/supplements (Centrum, Caltrate), respiratory health (Theraflu, Robitussin), and digestive health (Tums, ENO). H1 2026 reported revenue increased 2.2% to GBP 5,602M with organic growth of 2.6%, accelerating to 3.1% in Q2 from 2.2% in Q1. Adjusted operating profit grew 8.2% at constant currency with 140bps of gross margin expansion. FY2026 guidance targets 3-5% organic revenue growth and high-single-digit adjusted operating profit growth. The company serves 1.4 billion consumers across 170+ markets.
24,535 Employees ·www.haleon.com

Investment Thesis
Haleon at 16.55x forward earnings offers a defensive consumer healthcare franchise with global #1 positions in therapeutic oral health (Sensodyne) and strong brands across OTC pain relief (Advil, Voltaren) and vitamins (Centrum) [1][4]. H1 2026 demonstrated improving momentum: Q2 organic revenue growth accelerated to 3.1% from Q1's 2.2%, gross margin expanded 140bps, adjusted operating profit grew 8.2% at constant currency, and adjusted EPS increased 12.0% [1]. The five-year Microsoft AI collaboration positions Haleon to drive ongoing productivity gains across consumer insights, R&D, supply chain, and commercial operations [2]. The 'Win as One' strategy targeting one billion additional consumers by 2030 provides a clear growth framework. The conservative 52% D/E and GBP 769M H1 free cash flow support both the 1.92% dividend yield and share buyback programs. AI resilience is exceptionally strong — physical consumer health products with regulatory moats and brand trust face virtually no AI substitution risk.
The bear case centers on tepid organic growth and limited corporate track record. H1 2026 organic revenue growth of 2.6% — while improving sequentially — remains at the low end of the 3-5% FY2026 guidance range, and the medium-term 4-6% organic growth target reflects a low-single-digit grower trading at 16.55x earnings [1]. ROE of 10.27% is below the 15% target, reflecting the goodwill-heavy balance sheet inherited from GSK rather than efficient capital deployment [4]. With only four years of independence, Haleon lacks a proven track record of navigating economic downturns, competitive disruptions, or leadership transitions as a standalone entity. The Zantac litigation cloud — while Haleon has rejected indemnification claims as inapplicable since it never marketed Zantac in the US or Canada — creates legal tail risk if GSK or Pfizer settlement frameworks are challenged [3]. Private label competition in OTC healthcare is intensifying as retailers develop higher-quality alternatives.
