Investment Thesis
HPE at $69.33 with 14.8x forward earnings and a PEG of 0.55 offers compelling value for an enterprise IT company undergoing transformative growth — Q3 FY2026 delivered record revenue of $12.2B (+34% YoY) with EPS topping $1.11 for the first time, driving raised FY26 guidance to 34-37% revenue growth and $3.75-$3.85 non-GAAP EPS [1]. The Juniper integration is firing on all cylinders with Networking segment growth guided to 73-74%, while GreenLake Intelligence launched the industry's first agentic AI operations framework. GAAP operating profit growth is guided at 1,070-1,105%, reflecting massive margin expansion as integration charges normalize. The $14B Juniper deal creates a differentiated edge-to-cloud platform combining compute, networking, and AI infrastructure positioned for the enterprise AI buildout cycle [2].
The bear case centers on the sustainability of inorganic growth, margin normalization, and competitive intensity. The 34% revenue growth and 410% EPS growth are heavily driven by the Juniper acquisition — organic growth rates are meaningfully lower, and comparisons will become more challenging as the deal anniversaries lap [1]. Trailing GAAP PE of 35.7x is elevated versus the 14.8x forward, reflecting the gap between current profitability and management's optimistic non-GAAP projections. Profit margin of 6.7% and ROE of 10.9% remain below technology sector targets despite improvement from 4.0% and 6.3%, indicating structural margin constraints in enterprise hardware. HPE competes with well-capitalized Dell Technologies, Cisco Systems, and hyperscale cloud providers with deeper pockets and larger installed bases. The 5-year revenue CAGR of only 7% including Juniper reveals how modest the pre-acquisition organic growth trajectory was. The Inspire Impact Score of -67 (MISALIGNED) limits allocation for faith-based investors [3].
