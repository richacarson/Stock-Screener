Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas E&P ·NASDAQ ·United States
HighPeak Energy is an independent crude oil and natural gas exploration and production company focused on the acquisition, development, and exploitation of unconventional reserves in the Midland Basin in West Texas. Founded in 2019 by Jack Hightower and now led by CEO Mike Hollis (former Diamondback Energy COO), the company operates approximately 50 employees and reported Q2 2026 production averaging 45.3 MBoe/d (64% oil, 83% liquids). Revenue forecast for full-year 2026 has been raised to $938.7M, with a capital budget of $255-285M.

Investment Thesis
HighPeak Energy's bull case rests on its operational turnaround and commodity leverage — Q2 2026 delivered revenue of $272.4M (+26% YoY), net income of $74.7M (+216% YoY), and EPS of $0.60 on production of 45.3 MBoe/d (7% above guidance midpoint, 64% oil) [1]. The full-year 2026 revenue forecast has been raised to $938.7M, capital spending is declining in H2 2026 ($255-285M budget), and free cash flow generation is underway. CEO Mike Hollis brings proven operational expertise from building Diamondback Energy into a premier Permian operator [2]. At 0.64x price-to-book, the stock trades at a deep discount to tangible asset value, offering potential re-rating as profitability stabilizes.
The bear case reflects fundamental challenges in valuation, profitability trajectory, and commodity exposure. Trailing ROE of -5.54% and profit margin of -10.28% demonstrate that the company has not yet achieved sustained profitability — Q2 2026 was strong, but the trailing twelve-month data includes loss-making quarters that may recur if commodity prices soften [3]. Forward EPS consensus remains negative (-$0.29), suggesting analysts expect continued losses despite the recent quarter's improvement. D/E of 76.68% with $1.19B in debt creates leverage risk in a declining commodity price environment. The stock has plunged from higher levels to $7.79, trading well below book value, which often signals market skepticism about asset quality or management execution. The $0.08 annual dividend ($0.02/quarter, approximately 1% yield) is negligible and has been inconsistent, undermining any income thesis [3]. As a single-basin commodity producer with 50 employees and no competitive moat, HighPeak is fully exposed to oil price volatility with minimal strategic differentiation.
