Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas E&P ·NASDAQ ·United States
HighPeak Energy, Inc. is an independent crude oil and natural gas company focused on the acquisition, development, exploration, and exploitation of unconventional crude oil and natural gas reserves in the Midland Basin of West Texas, part of the Permian Basin. The company produced 48.3 MBoe/d in FY2025 (68% crude oil, 85% liquids) and held 174 MMBoe of proved reserves. Founder and CEO Jack Hightower retired in September 2025; Michael L. Hollis succeeded him as Interim CEO. Insiders own approximately 78.9% of outstanding shares. Headquartered in Fort Worth, Texas.
·www.highpeakenergy.com

Investment Thesis
HighPeak Energy offers deep value exposure to Permian Basin oil with an extraordinarily aligned management team — 78.9% insider ownership and the former CEO's $2.8M personal stock purchase at $15.45 signal conviction that the stock is undervalued relative to reserve value [4]. The company holds 174 MMBoe of proved reserves, produced 48.3 MBoe/d in FY2025 (68% crude oil), and trades at an enterprise value of approximately $2.1B — roughly $12/Boe of proved reserves [1][2]. The forward P/E of 13.49 and D/E of 63% suggest reasonable valuation and manageable leverage. The 2026 pivot to free cash flow generation and debt reduction through a single-rig program is a prudent response to commodity price volatility.
The core risk is declining production and revenue in a commodity business with no pricing power. Revenue has fallen from $1.11B in 2023 to a TTM of approximately $882M, a decline of roughly 21% driven by both lower oil prices and deliberate capex reduction [1][2]. FY2025 net income of only $19M on $1B+ in assets represents thin profitability that could turn to losses in a sustained commodity downturn. The $1.02B debt burden, while manageable at 63% D/E, requires sustained production and commodity prices to service. The CEO transition from founder Hightower to Interim CEO Hollis introduces leadership uncertainty — 'Interim' suggests the board is still searching for permanent leadership [3]. The stock's 47% decline over the past 52 weeks reflects market skepticism about the production trajectory, and Sustainalytics rates ESG management as weak [5].
Key Catalysts
  1. Successful free cash flow generation in 2026 enabling meaningful debt reduction from the $1.02B current balance, potentially improving the equity story and re-rating the stock
  2. Oil price recovery above $80/bbl would dramatically improve margins and cash flow on the 41-44 MBoe/d guided production, with the 68% crude oil mix providing strong leverage to WTI
  3. Permanent CEO appointment and strategic clarity on capital allocation — whether to remain a single-basin operator, pursue acquisitions, or consider a sale to a larger Permian producer
Key Risks
  1. Sustained oil price weakness below $65/bbl could push the company toward losses given the thin FY2025 net income of $19M and $1.02B debt burden requiring ongoing servicing
  2. Declining production from 48.3 MBoe/d to guided 41-44 MBoe/d with 50% capex cuts signals depletion of current well inventory economics — if base decline rates accelerate, the single-rig program may not maintain even the lower production target
  3. CEO transition risk with Michael Hollis serving as Interim CEO since September 2025 — 11 months without a permanent appointment suggests board indecision, and any misstep in capital allocation or operational execution during this period could erode the reserve value thesis

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
8
AI Risk Protection (8/9): IMMUNE. Oil and gas extraction from the Permian Basin is a fundamentally physical operation — drilling wells, hydraulic fracturing, producing and transporting hydrocarbons — that AI structurally cannot substitute [1]. The geological expertise, physical infrastructure (drilling rigs, completion crews, pipelines, processing facilities), and regulatory compliance required for E&P operations are entirely beyond AI's reach. AI is more likely to increase energy demand — data centers powering AI workloads are projected to consume enormous amounts of electricity, some of which will be generated from natural gas, potentially benefiting producers like HighPeak. AI could also modestly improve operational efficiency through predictive maintenance, reservoir modeling, and completion optimization, but this is incremental, not transformative. AI Opportunity (0/1): No AI revenue. HighPeak is a pure-play oil and gas producer with no AI-related products or services.
