Company Profile
Real Estate ·REIT - Healthcare Facilities ·NYSE ·United States
Healthcare Realty Trust is a real estate investment trust (REIT) focused on owning, managing, and developing outpatient medical properties. The company owns approximately 660 properties totaling over 40 million square feet across major healthcare markets, primarily leased to hospital systems, physician groups, and other healthcare providers. Formed through the 2022 merger of Healthcare Trust and Physicians Realty Trust.

Investment Thesis
Healthcare Realty delivered a breakout Q1 2026 with all-time record 6.9% same-store cash NOI growth, 92.3% occupancy (up 110 bps YoY), 93.5% tenant retention, 4.2% cash leasing spreads, and 2.0 million sq ft of lease executions — prompting management to raise FY2026 FFO guidance to $1.59-$1.65/share [1]. The stock rallied 6.5% on the results and now trades at $20.61 (P/FFO 14.90x), offering a 4.66% dividend yield with a sustainable 59.3% FFO payout ratio. Healthcare real estate benefits from secular tailwinds — aging U.S. demographics, outpatient shift from inpatient care, hospital system expansion into suburban markets, and physician group consolidation. With ~660 properties across 40M+ sq ft, Healthcare Realty has the scale to attract institutional capital and benefits from GRESB 2 Green Star ESG credentials [3]. The operational momentum suggests the post-merger integration is delivering value.
The bear case centers on leadership uncertainty, dividend deterioration, and REIT-unfriendly rate environment. No permanent CEO has been appointed since Todd Meredith's November 2024 departure — interim CEO Connie Moore is executing well operationally but the strategic vacuum creates risk if she departs [2]. The 22.6% dividend cut in 2025 destroyed income investor confidence, and flat dividend guidance with 0% consecutive years of growth signals no near-term restoration. GAAP metrics remain poor (ROE -4.23%, profit margin -17.34%) due to REIT depreciation accounting, but the D/E of 97% and $4B+ total debt require ongoing capital markets access in an elevated rate environment [4]. Revenue has been flat to declining as asset dispositions offset organic growth, and total portfolio quality varies across 660 properties. The 3-year revenue growth forecast of just 1.43% suggests limited top-line expansion even with strong leasing [4]. Competition from Healthpeak (post-Physicians Realty merger) and Welltower for premium medical office assets continues to intensify.
Key Catalysts
  1. Record operational momentum — 6.9% same-store NOI growth, 92.3% occupancy, and raised FFO guidance to $1.59-$1.65 demonstrate portfolio quality improvement that could drive P/FFO multiple expansion from current 14.9x
  2. Permanent CEO appointment providing strategic clarity and restoring investor confidence, potentially unlocking re-rating to sector-appropriate 16-18x P/FFO multiple
  3. Healthcare demographic tailwinds — aging baby boomer population driving outpatient volume growth in the 660-property, 40M sq ft portfolio with embedded rent escalators and 4.2% cash leasing spreads
Key Risks
  1. Leadership vacancy risk — no permanent CEO 20+ months after Todd Meredith's departure; interim management executing well but strategic direction unclear without permanent leadership
  2. Dividend deterioration — 22.6% cut in 2025, 0% growth since, and negative 5yr CAGR of -5% signal income unreliability that deters REIT income investors seeking growth
  3. Interest rate sensitivity — 97% D/E and $4B+ total debt require ongoing capital markets access; elevated rates compress cap rate spreads and increase refinancing costs

AI adoption in healthcare (telemedicine, AI diagnostics) may modestly reduce some routine visit demand, but outpatient surgical centers, imaging facilities, and specialty care require physical presence.
