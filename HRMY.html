Company Profile
Healthcare ·Specialty Pharmaceuticals ·NASDAQ ·United States
Harmony Biosciences Holdings, Inc. is a commercial-stage pharmaceutical company focused on developing and commercializing therapies for patients living with rare neurological diseases. Lead product WAKIX (pitolisant) is the first and only FDA-approved treatment for both excessive daytime sleepiness and cataplexy in adults with narcolepsy, with recent pediatric cataplexy approval. Q2 2026 delivered record WAKIX revenue of ~$261M (+30% YoY) with net income of $75.4M ($1.28 EPS). Full-year 2026 guidance reaffirmed at $1.0-1.04B. Pipeline expanding with BP-205 (orexin 2 agonist) and clemizole (rare epilepsy). Market cap ~$2.2B.
700 Employees ·www.harmonybiosciences.com

Investment Thesis
Harmony Biosciences delivered record Q2 2026 WAKIX revenue of ~$261M (+30% YoY) with net income of $75.4M ($1.28 diluted EPS, up from $0.68 YoY), demonstrating accelerating growth and improving profitability after the Q1 earnings compression from R&D investment [1]. Full-year 2026 guidance of $1.0-1.04B in net product revenue was reaffirmed, representing the company's first billion-dollar revenue year. The infrastructure profile is exceptional: Forward P/E of 7.57 and Forward PEG of 0.42 suggest significant undervaluation relative to 30% revenue growth, supported by D/E of 19%, net cash of $807M ($962.5M cash vs $155M debt), and ROE of 20.46% [4]. The August 2026 Investor Day outlined a credible pipeline diversification strategy — BP-205 (orexin 2 agonist), clemizole (rare epilepsy), and new WAKIX formulations — that could transform Harmony from a single-product franchise into a broader neuroscience platform [2]. Inspire Impact Score of 42 (ALIGNED) supports faith-based allocation.
The primary risk is single-product concentration. WAKIX generates essentially all of Harmony's revenue, creating binary exposure to patent challenges, generic competition, and any safety or efficacy concerns. Patent protection targeting 2030 provides only four years of exclusivity runway, after which generic competition could significantly compress margins [1]. Pipeline candidates (BP-205, clemizole) remain in early-to-mid stage development with uncertain timelines and outcomes — the transition from single-product to diversified platform is a multi-year endeavor with execution risk. The CFO transition announced in July 2026 adds mild organizational uncertainty [1]. Glassdoor reviews citing 'Very Rigid' culture and 'leadership does not listen' present a cultural risk that could affect talent retention in the competitive biopharma labor market [3]. The stock has declined within a 52-week range of $25-60, and cost of product sold increased to 24.2% of revenue in Q2 due to new royalties and licensing agreements, which may compress margins if sustained [1]. With ~700 employees and $2.2B market cap, Harmony is small enough that a single clinical or regulatory setback could have outsized impact.
