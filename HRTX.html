Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Heron Therapeutics is a commercial-stage biotechnology company focused on acute care and oncology therapeutics using its proprietary Biochronomer drug delivery technology. Key products include ZYNRELEF (non-opioid post-surgical pain), CINVANTI (chemotherapy-induced nausea/vomiting prevention), APONVIE (PONV prevention), and SUSTOL (extended-release anti-emetic). Headquartered in Cary, North Carolina, led by CEO Craig Collard.
128 Employees ·www.herontx.com

Investment Thesis
Heron Therapeutics offers a high-risk turnaround story anchored by the Acute Care franchise growing 32% YoY in Q1 2026, led by ZYNRELEF (+27% to $10.2M) and APONVIE (+50% to $3.4M) [1]. The company reaffirmed 2026 guidance of $173-183M revenue with $10-20M adjusted EBITDA, suggesting a credible path toward profitability after the company achieved its first positive adjusted EBITDA of $14.7M in 2025 [1]. IGNITE 2.0 commercial program expanded target accounts 40%, and sales force expansion in Q3 2026 should accelerate ZYNRELEF penetration [1]. At $162M market cap with $0.86 stock price, the company prices in extreme pessimism — any sustained improvement in profitability could drive significant re-rating.
The bear case is severe and has materially worsened. The Delaware District Court ruled CINVANTI's patents invalid, exposing the company's historically largest revenue contributor to generic competition and causing Q1 2026 total revenue to decline 10.8% YoY despite strong Acute Care growth [4]. D/E of 1,550% with $140.6M debt against $46.6M cash and a $162M market cap creates existential financial risk if the ZYNRELEF growth trajectory cannot offset CINVANTI erosion [5]. Profit margin of -20.5% and persistent unprofitability after 40+ years of operation (cumulative losses exceeding $1B) undermine the turnaround narrative. The $0.86 stock price carries meaningful NASDAQ delisting risk, and daily dollar volume of $1.96M barely clears the IOWN liquidity floor. The company must simultaneously grow ZYNRELEF, manage CINVANTI decline, expand the sales force, and service debt — a multi-front execution challenge for a 128-employee organization.
