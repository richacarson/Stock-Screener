Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Heron Therapeutics is a commercial-stage biotechnology company focused on acute care and oncology therapeutics using its proprietary Biochronomer drug delivery technology. Key products include ZYNRELEF (non-opioid post-surgical pain), CINVANTI (chemotherapy-induced nausea/vomiting prevention), APONVIE (PONV prevention), and SUSTOL (extended-release anti-emetic). The company withdrew its 2026 earnings guidance in August 2026 due to CINVANTI patent risk and initiated a strategic review. Stock has declined 63% since July 2026 to $0.32, breaching the IOWN liquidity floor.
