Company Profile
Healthcare ·Health Information Services ·NASDAQ ·United States
HeartFlow is a medical technology company using AI and computational fluid dynamics to create personalized 3D models of patients' coronary arteries from standard CT angiography scans. Its platform provides non-invasive diagnostic solutions for coronary artery disease (CAD) including RoadMap Analysis, FFRCT Analysis, and the recently launched Plaque Analysis. Over 1,800 institutions globally use HeartFlow's platform, with nearly 650,000 patients managed and a database of 200+ million annotated cardiac images.

Investment Thesis
HeartFlow is a high-growth AI-powered medical technology company that delivered Q1 2026 revenue of $52.6M (41% YoY growth) and raised full-year guidance to $228-232M (29-32% growth), up from the previous $218-222M outlook [1][5]. The company's non-invasive cardiac diagnostic platform is a genuine category creator with 1,800+ institutions globally, 650,000+ patients managed, and a proprietary database of 200M+ annotated images [3]. Gross margins expanded to 80.2% (from 75.1% a year ago), and Plaque Analysis is ramping ahead of schedule with a $19-21M outlook and 57% commercial coverage [1][5]. Cash of $254.9M provides adequate runway, analysts unanimously rate the stock Strong Buy with a $37 average target (46% upside), and the technology addresses the #1 global cause of death [3][4].
The bear case centers on persistent unprofitability and severe cultural dysfunction. HeartFlow's TTM net loss of approximately $112M on $191M revenue yields a -58% profit margin, and after 19 years in business, GAAP profitability remains distant despite improving unit economics [4]. Glassdoor reviews have deteriorated to 2.7/5.0 with only 34% of employees recommending the company — reviews describe low morale, forced culture, management that 'doesn't understand AI/ML,' and concerns that the technology 'requires too much human interaction to scale' [2]. The stock has fallen 38% from its 52-week high of $41.22 to $25.37, and the $2.2B market cap at ~11x forward revenue requires sustained 30%+ growth and a credible profitability timeline [4]. Competition from AI-enabled cardiac imaging entrants (Siemens, Cleerly) and dependence on favorable Medicare reimbursement decisions create ongoing risk [5].
Key Catalysts
  1. Raised FY2026 guidance to $228-232M (29-32% growth) with Plaque Analysis ramping ahead of schedule to $19-21M and 57% commercial coverage, creating a second material revenue pillar [1][5]
  2. Gross margin expansion to 80.2% (from 75.1% YoY) with guidance for ~81% demonstrates improving unit economics and a potential path toward operating leverage as revenue scales [1]
  3. PCI Navigator launch for interventional cardiologists expands the platform's clinical utility deeper into the cardiology workflow, potentially increasing revenue per institution [1]
Key Risks
  1. Persistent unprofitability — TTM net loss of ~$112M with -58% profit margin raises concerns about the timeline to breakeven, and $254.9M cash provides approximately 2-3 years of runway at current burn rate [1][4]
  2. Severe cultural dysfunction with Glassdoor at 2.7/5.0 and 34% recommendation rate could impair talent retention and execution quality at a critical growth stage, particularly given employee concerns that the technology 'requires too much human interaction to scale' [2]
  3. Reimbursement dependency — commercial viability of Plaque Analysis and FFRCT depends on continued favorable Medicare and commercial payer coverage decisions; adverse reimbursement changes could compress the $228-232M revenue outlook [1][5]
