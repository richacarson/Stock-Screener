Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Specialty Chemicals ·NYSE ·United States
Global specialty chemicals company operating through three segments: Polyurethanes (MDI-based products), Performance Products (amines, surfactants), and Advanced Materials (epoxy resins, adhesives). Led by family patriarch Peter R. Huntsman as Chairman, President, and CEO since 2000, the company generated Q2 2026 revenue of $1.663B (+14% YoY) with adjusted EBITDA of $120M but a net loss of $6M. H1 2026 revenue of $3.08B showed sequential improvement from Q1's $53M loss. UK regulators raised benzene emission concerns at the Teesside plant, and a Delaware jury verdict in the Qualcomm-Arm case has no direct impact.

Investment Thesis
Huntsman at $8.24 with a $1.45B market cap offers a deep value proposition at 0.22x TTM revenue, with a 19-year dividend history yielding ~5.2% and an Inspire Impact Score of 54 (ALIGNED) across 14 positive ESG attributions [4]. Q2 2026 results showed meaningful cyclical improvement — revenue of $1.663B grew 14% YoY with adjusted EBITDA of $120M and net loss narrowing to just $6M from Q1's $53M loss — suggesting the worst of the feedstock cost headwinds from benzene and European natural gas volatility may be passing [1]. The portfolio restructuring toward higher-margin Advanced Materials and Polyurethanes segments positions Huntsman to benefit from eventual chemical cycle recovery, and the internal deployment of the Citrine Informatics AI platform for R&D acceleration demonstrates forward-looking capability building [3].
The bear case centers on persistent unprofitability and structural decline. Huntsman has posted net losses in four of the last six quarters, with ROE of -3.8% and profit margin of -3.1% reflecting a company unable to generate positive returns in the current environment [4]. The 5-year revenue CAGR of approximately -3% reveals a secular downtrend from the 2021 commodity supercycle peak. The stock's 73% decline from 52-week highs to $8.24 reflects deep capital market skepticism about the cyclical recovery thesis. The dividend of ~$0.43/year consumes more than forward earnings and may require a cut if GAAP losses persist. UK regulators raised benzene emission concerns at the Teesside plant, potentially exposing the company to fines and capital expenditure for remediation [5]. ESG governance has been criticized by ISS for lagging peers on environmental disclosure and human capital management, creating institutional investor exclusion risk. Average daily volume dropped to 2.87M shares (from 5.75M in August), suggesting declining investor interest at these levels [4][5].

AI Risk Protection (7/9): Huntsman's core business — manufacturing specialty chemicals in physical plants using proprietary chemical processes and formulations — has meaningful defensive moats against AI disruption [1]. AI cannot manufacture MDI, polyurethanes, epoxy resins, or amines; these require physical infrastructure, chemical engineering expertise, and regulatory compliance that AI cannot replicate.
