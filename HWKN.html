Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Specialty Chemicals ·NASDAQ ·United States
Hawkins, Inc. is a specialty chemical distribution and blending company headquartered in Minneapolis, Minnesota, operating through three segments: Water Treatment (largest and fastest-growing), Industrial, and Health & Nutrition. FY2026 (ended March 2026) revenue reached a record ~$1.08B across four quarters of year-over-year growth, with the Water Treatment segment leading at 16-21% YoY sales growth driven by six acquisitions completed during the fiscal year. Net income was $81.5M ($3.91/share) vs $84.3M ($4.03/share) in FY2025, with EPS pressure from ~$5M in incremental amortization and interest costs related to the acquisition program. CEO Patrick H. Hawkins has led the company since March 2011.

Investment Thesis
Hawkins at $130 with a $2.7B market cap is a well-managed specialty chemical distribution platform executing an aggressive but disciplined acquisition strategy that is building national scale, particularly in the essential water treatment segment [1][2]. FY2026 revenue hit a record ~$1.08B with all three segments growing, led by Water Treatment at 16-21% YoY. CEO Patrick Hawkins' 15-year tenure provides leadership continuity, and the company's ROE of 15.6% with D/E of just 47% demonstrates efficient capital deployment and balance sheet discipline even while funding six acquisitions in a single fiscal year. The business is structurally protected from AI disruption — you cannot replace physical chemicals with software — and the water treatment end market benefits from secular tailwinds around aging U.S. water infrastructure, EPA regulatory tightening, and growing municipal water treatment spending.
The WATCH recommendation reflects the stock's premium valuation (33.7x trailing P/E, 27.1x forward P/E) against thin profit margins (7.3%) and a near-term EPS decline (-3.6%) driven by acquisition-related amortization and interest costs [1][2]. While the acquisition strategy is building long-term value, the current earnings profile doesn't yet reflect the full benefit of the platform buildout, and any execution misstep in integrating six acquisitions could pressure margins further. The forward PEG ratio is unavailable due to negative near-term earnings growth, making it difficult to assess whether the premium multiple is justified by future growth acceleration. Revenue Growth 5yr CAGR data is also unavailable, and these two missing metrics (4/10 weight units) may inflate the calculated Infrastructure score of 9. An upgrade to HOLD would require demonstrated margin expansion as acquisition synergies materialize and clear evidence that the platform investment is translating into accelerating EPS growth rather than just top-line scale.
