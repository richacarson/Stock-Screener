Company Profile
Industrials ·Aerospace & Defense ·NYSE ·United States
Howmet Aerospace Inc. is a global leader in advanced engineered solutions for the aerospace and transportation industries. The company provides engine components (titanium and nickel superalloy aerostructures and engine parts), fastening systems, engineered structures, and forged wheels across four segments. Spun off from Arconic in April 2020, headquartered in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. Key customers include GE Aerospace, Pratt & Whitney, Rolls-Royce, Boeing, and Airbus.

Howmet operates with a mix of infinite and finite characteristics. Investment Thesis
Howmet Aerospace delivered a dominant Q1 2026 with $2.31B revenue (+19% YoY), adjusted EBITDA margin expanding 320bps to 32.0%, and adj EPS of $1.22 (+42% YoY) [1]. The company raised full-year 2026 guidance to $9.65B revenue and $4.94 adj EPS, underpinned by 36% spares revenue growth ($520M quarterly run-rate representing 23% of total revenue), 20% commercial aerospace growth, and a 39% surge in gas turbines revenue driven by data center power demand [1]. With $1.75B guided free cash flow, sole-source positions on critical engine programs, and record customer backlogs at Boeing and Airbus providing multi-year visibility, Howmet is one of the highest-quality aerospace compounders in the public markets.
The primary concern is valuation. At 51x forward earnings and a PEG ratio of 2.34, Howmet is priced for perfection despite being an industrial manufacturer, not a technology company [6]. The trailing P/E of 62x is 60% above historical averages and significantly above the aerospace peer average of 43.7x. Net insider selling of $25M in 12 months — including the CHRO disposing of 39% of his direct holdings at $269/share — undermines management conviction at current prices [5]. Revenue growth deceleration from the current 19% pace as aerospace production rate increases normalize could trigger significant multiple compression. Geopolitical risk from the Iranian conflict could disrupt engine spares demand, and any Boeing or Airbus production delays directly impact Howmet's volume trajectory [1].
Key Catalysts
  1. Continued engine spares growth driven by aging commercial fleet (average aircraft age exceeding 14 years) and airline capacity expansion, with spares revenue on a $2B+ annual run-rate trajectory
  2. Gas turbines market acceleration (+39% in Q1) driven by AI data center power demand and energy transition infrastructure, diversifying revenue beyond commercial aerospace dependence
  3. Manufacturing capacity expansion and automation investments enabling EBITDA margin progression beyond the current 32% toward management's long-term 35%+ target
Key Risks
  1. Premium valuation at 51x forward P/E and PEG of 2.34 leaves minimal margin of safety — any revenue miss or growth deceleration from the 19% pace could trigger significant multiple compression [6]
  2. Significant net insider selling ($25M in 12 months) including CHRO disposing of 39% of direct holdings signals potential concern about valuation sustainability at current levels [5]
  3. Concentration risk in commercial aerospace OEM production rates — Boeing 737 MAX delivery challenges, Airbus supply chain constraints, or engine program delays could directly impact volume growth [1]

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
8
AI Risk Protection (8/9): Howmet's core business — precision forging and casting of titanium and nickel superalloy engine components — is structurally immune to AI disruption. Manufacturing jet engine blades to single-crystal metallurgical specifications, managing extreme-temperature alloy chemistry, and passing rigorous FAA/EASA certification processes are physical-world capabilities that AI cannot substitute [1]. Multi-year sole-source positions with engine OEMs create switching costs measured in decades of requalification. AI-driven aerospace design actually increases demand for precision components as engines become more complex. The company's own AI adoption in process optimization largely neutralizes competitive risk from AI-enhanced rival manufacturers [7]. AI Opportunity (0/1): Howmet uses AI internally for manufacturing optimization but does not sell AI products or generate direct AI revenue. The 5-10% fuel efficiency improvement from AI-optimized component design benefits customers rather than constituting Howmet AI revenue [7].
