Company Profile
Consumer Cyclical ·Auto Parts ·NYSE ·United States
Hyliion develops the KARNO Power Module, a fuel-agnostic linear generator that produces electricity from heat rather than combustion, capable of running on 20+ fuels including natural gas, hydrogen, ammonia, propane, and diesel with dynamic switching without shutdown. Founded in 2015 by CEO Thomas Healy and headquartered in Cedar Park, Texas. In Q2 2026 revenue reached $4.94M (up 227% YoY) and the company raised full-year 2026 guidance to $15M from $10M, anchored by a $41.7M U.S. Navy contract and ~750 KARNO cores in executed LOIs representing ~$400M in potential revenue.
113 Employees ·www.hyliion.com

Investment Thesis
Hyliion is a speculative early-stage clean energy company at a genuine revenue inflection — Q2 2026 revenue of $4.94M beat consensus by 130%, growing 227% YoY, and management raised full-year 2026 guidance to $15M from $10M [2]. The $41.7M Navy contract validates KARNO technology for military distributed power, and ~750 LOIs representing ~$400M in potential commercial revenue provide a large addressable pipeline [3]. The fuel-agnostic design (20+ fuels with dynamic switching) offers a uniquely flexible value proposition for data centers, military bases, and industrial applications. Inspire score of 65 (ALIGNED) reflects strong environmental credentials across 11 positive attributions.
The bear case centers on execution risk and cash burn. Cash declined from ~$139M to $64M in approximately six months, implying ~$150M annualized burn against $15M revenue guidance — at current rates, the company has less than one year of runway without additional financing [5]. The $700M market cap trades at ~75× TTM revenue of $9.2M, a valuation built entirely on future conversion of non-binding LOIs. Glassdoor reviews (3.0-3.4/5) flagging favoritism and poor planning raise concerns about organizational capacity to execute a manufacturing ramp [4]. CEO Healy's three product pivots demonstrate flexibility but also suggest the company has not yet found durable product-market fit. WATCH until LOI-to-order conversion accelerates and cash burn trajectory stabilizes.
Key Catalysts
  1. Conversion of the 750-unit KARNO LOI pipeline (~$400M potential) into binding purchase orders would validate commercial demand and dramatically de-risk the revenue trajectory
  2. Additional military contracts beyond the $41.7M Navy deal, with management indicating more anticipated in H2 2026, providing government-backed revenue diversification
  3. Achievement of full 200kW KARNO design power rating and UL certification completion would enable production scale and unlock commercial deployment in 2027
Key Risks
  1. Cash runway of approximately 6-12 months at current burn rate (~$75M/half) with only $64M cash, likely requiring dilutive capital raise that could materially impair existing shareholders
  2. Non-binding LOIs ($400M+ from 750 KARNO cores) may not convert to firm orders — no disclosed commercial purchase orders beyond military contracts, and early adopter deployments remain unproven
  3. Organizational capacity constraints at 113 employees with Glassdoor-flagged culture issues (favoritism, non-merit-based advancement) could bottleneck the manufacturing ramp as military and commercial timelines converge
