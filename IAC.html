Investment Thesis
The renamed People Incorporated (ticker PPLI as of June 4, 2026, still tracked here as IAC) has stripped down to two clear assets: America's largest digital/print publisher, reaching 175M+ monthly consumers with 11 consecutive quarters of digital revenue growth (Q2 2026 Digital revenue +6% to $290M, Digital Adjusted EBITDA +18%), and a 26.1% stake in MGM Resorts that it is attempting to convert into full ownership via an $18 billion, $48.30/share cash bid [1][2][5]. The balance sheet is solid post-restructuring (D/E of 27.6%, $1.1B cash) and Forward P/E of 15.93x is undemanding if the publishing business stabilizes and the MGM stake (or a successful buyout) continues to accrete value — Q2 2026 net earnings were boosted by a $721.7 million unrealized MGM mark-to-market gain alone [1]. Management's $40 million annual cost-savings plan from consolidating corporate functions should improve go-forward margins.
The bear case is direct: Google Search's share of traffic to People Inc. content collapsed from 54% to 24% in two years as AI Overviews intercept queries with zero compensation to the publisher, and management has no clear near-term fix since Google will not let publishers split its search and AI crawlers [5]. Reported profitability is now dominated by non-cash, non-operating swings in the MGM equity stake rather than the operating publishing business, meaning both ROE and net income could reverse sharply if MGM's share price declines or the $18 billion bid is rejected or falls through entirely (its outcome was still pending as of the most recent public reporting) [1][4]. The company just absorbed 77 layoffs and lost its CFO/COO and Chief Legal Officer in the same restructuring window, and the pattern of serial spinoffs/rebrands/bids, while flexible, has not been anchored to a consistent Just Cause, landing the Infinite Game score in FINITE territory.
Key Catalysts
  1. Resolution of the $18 billion, $48.30/share cash bid for the remaining 73.9% of MGM Resorts — a successful deal would convert an equity-method stake into full operating control of a major, cash-generative gaming asset
  2. Continued digital revenue growth streak (11 consecutive quarters) at People Inc. as licensing, events, and AI-related partnership revenue diversify away from Google-search-dependent traffic
  3. Realization of the $40 million in annual run-rate cost savings from consolidating corporate functions, expected to complete by Q1 2027
Key Risks
  1. Structural AI disruption of the core publishing traffic model — Google Search referral share fell from 54% to 24% in two years with no clear resolution path, since Google will not separate its search and AI crawlers
  2. Reported profitability is now dominated by unrealized, non-operating swings in the MGM Resorts equity stake (a $721.7M gain in Q2 2026 alone), creating earnings volatility unrelated to the core publishing business
  3. Execution and morale risk from simultaneous layoffs, a rebrand, and the departure of the CFO/COO and Chief Legal Officer, compounded by uncertainty over whether the MGM bid will be accepted, rejected, or need to be sweetened
