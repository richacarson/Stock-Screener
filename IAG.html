Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Gold ·NYQ ·Canada
IAMGOLD Corporation is a mid-tier gold producer and developer operating in Canada and Burkina Faso. The flagship Cote Gold mine in northeastern Ontario is one of Canada's largest new open-pit gold mines, ramping to nameplate capacity alongside the established Essakane mine in Burkina Faso. Q1 2026 revenue surged to $1.03B (+116% YoY) on attributable production of 183,600 oz, and Q2 2026 delivered $857M revenue on 188,100 oz at an average realized price of $4,384/oz. The company ended Q2 with a net cash position of $501M and total liquidity of $1.35B after repaying all debt.

Investment Thesis
IAMGOLD at $18.40 with a $10.5B market cap is a mid-tier gold producer entering a transformative period — the Cote Gold mine is ramping to nameplate capacity while gold prices above $4,300/oz drive extraordinary financial results [1]. Q1 2026 revenue surged 116% to $1.03B with net earnings of $380M ($0.65/share), and Q2 delivered $857M revenue with mine-site free cash flow of $369M (+169% YoY) [1]. The balance sheet is pristine: all debt repaid, $501M cash, and $1.35B total liquidity [1]. At 7.75x forward earnings with 32% ROE and 31% profit margins, IAMGOLD offers compelling value in the gold sector. Annual production guidance of 720K-820K oz for 2026, with Cote expansion plans providing further upside, positions the company for sustained growth if gold prices remain elevated.
The bear case centers on gold price dependency and concentration risk. Revenue growth of 47.5% and earnings growth of 191% are primarily driven by gold prices averaging $4,384-4,859/oz — any sustained decline below $3,000/oz would compress margins severely, as IAMGOLD's all-in sustaining costs provide limited buffer at lower prices [1]. The yfinance PEG ratio of 13.52 (based on 5-year historical growth) signals that current earnings momentum is abnormal and unsustainable if gold prices normalize. Geographic concentration in two jurisdictions creates binary risk: Cote Gold faces Canadian regulatory and environmental challenges, while Essakane operates in Burkina Faso amid ongoing political instability and security concerns — IAMGOLD confirmed normal operations during the 2022 military transition but long-term sovereign risk persists [4]. The Swiss NGO Action de Careme has criticized the Essakane mine's community impact, and while IAMGOLD disputes the findings, reputational and operational risk in West Africa remains elevated [4]. Three CEO transitions in four years create leadership continuity risk during the critical Cote ramp-up phase [3]. Beta of 2.35 means the stock will amplify any gold selloff.
