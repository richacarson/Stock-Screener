Company Profile
Materials ·Gold ·NYSE American ·United States
i-80 Gold Corp. is a Reno, Nevada-based mining company exploring and advancing a portfolio of gold, silver, and polymetallic deposits in Nevada, including Ruby Hill, Granite Creek, and the Lone Tree autoclave refurbishment project. The company is in an aggressive multi-mine build-out phase — Q2 2026 gold production rose to 11,098 ounces from 4,178 ounces a year earlier — funded by a $464.6 million cash position, with CEO Richard Young (since September 2024) leading the push to become Nevada's next leading mid-tier gold producer.

Investment Thesis
i-80 Gold is a well-capitalized Nevada gold developer ($464.6M cash) executing a multi-mine build-out that lifted quarterly gold production roughly 166% YoY to 11,098 ounces, with Granite Creek continuing to ramp and a Lone Tree refurbishment study showing material margin upside and a short payback period [1]. The Granite Creek open pit PEA shows an after-tax NPV of $421M and 30% IRR at $2,175/oz gold, and the company remains on track for full-year production guidance despite third-party processing delays [1][2]. A filtered tailings system and proactive community investment (the Franco-Nevada-backed Eureka County childcare facility) suggest a management team building durable local relationships as it scales.
The bear case is grounded in the current financial reality: profit margin of -198.82%, ROE of -71.66%, and a net loss that widened to $52.5M in Q2 2026 (from $30.2M a year earlier) reflect a company still years from self-funding production economics, with Q2 revenue actually down 12.6% YoY due to processing bottlenecks at Granite Creek [1]. Debt-to-equity of 174% is elevated for a pre-cash-flow-positive miner, and an Altman Z-Score near 0.2 (per third-party analysis) signals meaningful balance-sheet stress despite the large cash balance being consumed by development capex. Nevada's permitting environment carries above-average execution risk for projects like Lone Tree, and the mechanical Infrastructure score of 2/10 reflects a company where the investment case rests entirely on execution of the development plan rather than current financial durability.
