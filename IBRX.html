Investment Thesis
ImmunityBio at $10.28 and a $10.9B market cap is converting a first-in-class bladder cancer therapy into revenue: Q2 2026 net product revenue was $50.7M (+92% YoY, +15% sequential), first-half revenue $94.8M (+121%), and 2025 revenue grew 700% to $113M [1]. The shares trade well above their $8.00 50-day average, reflecting momentum in the ANKTIVA launch and pipeline expansion.
The bear case is steep: annualized revenue of roughly $200M supports a $10.9B valuation only if ANKTIVA expands far beyond bladder cancer, while the company is unprofitable, has a stockholders' deficit and held only $381M in cash at March 31, 2026 against ongoing burn [1][2]. Glassdoor shows 32% recommend and founder-control concerns, and the Inspire score of -62 reflects embryonic stem cell involvement that conflicts with faith-based mandates [2].
Key Catalysts
  1. Eighth-plus consecutive quarter of sequential ANKTIVA revenue growth, with Q2 2026 at $50.7M [1]
  2. Label and indication expansion plus NK/T-cell pipeline data readouts to widen the addressable market [1]
  3. Capital-markets access and financing improving cash runway beyond $381M [1]
Key Risks
  1. Cash burn and balance-sheet fragility: $381M cash and stockholders' deficit against an unprofitable P&L [1][2]
  2. Valuation of about $10.9B on roughly $200M annualized revenue leaves little room for launch disappointment [1]
  3. Founder control, leadership and morale issues (32% recommend), and AI-related operational and security risks cited in company filings [2]
