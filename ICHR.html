Company Profile
Technology ·Semiconductor Equipment & Materials ·NASDAQ ·United States
Ichor Holdings, Ltd. designs, engineers, and manufactures critical fluid delivery subsystems and precision components for semiconductor capital equipment, aerospace, and defense customers. The company's gas and chemical delivery systems are essential to semiconductor fabrication, with Lam Research and Applied Materials representing 76% of 2025 revenue. Founded in 1999, headquartered in Fremont, California, with manufacturing operations in the US, UK, Singapore, Korea, and Malaysia.

Investment Thesis
Ichor Holdings is entering a strong cyclical recovery with Q2 2026 revenue of $294.8M (+15% sequential), management guiding for Q3 revenue of $315-345M and at least 30% full-year growth, driven by AI-related semiconductor equipment demand [1]. The company returned to quarterly profitability in Q2 for the first time in three years, and the path to 20% gross margins provides significant earnings leverage. At a Forward PEG of 0.38, the stock prices in minimal recovery — analyst consensus targets $95.86 (40% upside), and with 3-year EPS growth forecast of 152%, the valuation appears compelling for a cyclical recovery play in the AI semiconductor supply chain [1][2].
The primary risk is extreme customer concentration: Lam Research and Applied Materials represent 76% of revenue, creating binary dependency on two OEMs' capital spending decisions [2]. The company is currently unprofitable (profit margin -3.97%, ROE -5.17%) with negative free cash flow of -$25.6M TTM, and the Malaysia facility ramp continues to pressure margins with operating cash flow not expected to improve until H1 2027 [1]. The semiconductor equipment cycle is notoriously volatile — Ichor's revenue crashed 37% from $1.28B in 2022 to $811M in 2023, and another cyclical downturn could erase recovery gains. The 5-year revenue CAGR of just 0.7% reflects this boom-bust pattern and raises questions about whether the company generates sustainable value through full cycles.
