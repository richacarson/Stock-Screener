Company Profile
Conglomerates ·Diversified Holding Companies ·NASDAQ ·United States
Icahn Enterprises L.P. is a diversified holding company controlled by activist investor Carl Icahn. The company operates through multiple segments including Investment, Energy (CVR Energy), Automotive (Pep Boys, sold mid-2026), Food Packaging, Real Estate, Home Fashion, and Pharma. Listed as a limited partnership on NASDAQ, IEP is primarily a vehicle for Icahn's activist investing strategy and portfolio management.

Investment Thesis
Icahn Enterprises generated $3.0B in Q2 2026 revenue (up from $2.4B YoY), driven by energy segment commodity price recovery and investment fund activity [1]. The indicative NAV of $2.6B against a market cap of ~$5.2B suggests the market prices in Carl Icahn's activist premium and the 26.8% dividend yield ($2.00/year at $0.50/quarter) [2]. The $700M Pep Boys sale provides near-term liquidity, and CVR Energy's refining and fertilizer operations generate real cash flows tied to physical infrastructure that AI cannot disrupt.
The bear case is overwhelming. IEP reported a $355M net loss in Q2 2026 and $814M net loss in H1 2026, with adjusted EBITDA deteriorating to a $134M loss from positive $40M a year ago [1]. The dividend has been cut 75% from $8.00 to $2.00 annually over 2023-2024, and negative earnings make even the reduced distribution unsustainable — payout ratio is undefined on negative EPS. D/E of 443% ($7.71B debt against $1.74B equity) represents extreme leverage [5]. The 2024 SEC settlement over undisclosed share pledges damaged governance credibility [4]. NAV declined $765M in a single quarter. Five-year revenue CAGR of -4.4% and negative ROE of -17.8% indicate secular value destruction, not temporary weakness. At 86, Carl Icahn's succession risk is existential — Ted Papapostolou's appointment as CEO has not been tested by a market cycle [3].
