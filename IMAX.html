Company Profile
Communication Services ·Entertainment ·NYSE ·Canada
IMAX Corporation operates as a technology platform for immersive entertainment, providing premium large-format cinema experiences through a global network of over 1,700 theater systems in 90+ countries. The company operates through Content Solutions (proprietary DMR film remastering technology) and Technology Products and Services (theater system sales/leases, maintenance, IMAX Enhanced home products). Founded in 1967 in Mississauga, Canada, IMAX achieved best-ever revenue of $410M in FY2025 (+16% YoY) with record adjusted EBITDA margin of 45% and record cash from operations of $127M (+79% YoY) [1]. Q2 2026 revenue reached $103M (+12% YoY) with a 46.6% adjusted EBITDA margin, and FY2026 guidance targets $1.4B in IMAX box office with 160-175 system installations [1].

Investment Thesis
IMAX achieved best-ever revenue of $410M in FY2025 (+16% YoY) with record adjusted EBITDA margin of 45% and record operating cash flow of $127M (+79% YoY), demonstrating the operating leverage of its asset-light technology platform model [1]. Q2 2026 continued the momentum with revenue of $103M (+12% YoY) and 46.6% EBITDA margin. FY2026 guidance of $1.4B in IMAX box office and 160-175 system installations provides strong visibility. The stock trades at a Forward PEG of 0.93 — below 1.0 — indicating the market prices in robust growth at a reasonable valuation, and D/E of 62.7% with $159.9M cash provides balance sheet flexibility [3]. IMAX's global installed base of 1,700+ systems creates a recurring revenue annuity from maintenance, content fees, and per-screen royalties that grows as the network expands. AI integration into DMR remastering and theater operations enhances efficiency, while IMAX Enhanced and StreamSmart extend the brand into home entertainment without cannibalizing the premium theatrical experience [2]. The Inspire Impact score of 48 (ALIGNED) supports values-based investing.
The bear case centers on content dependency, valuation, and structural entertainment industry risks. IMAX box office is heavily concentrated in tentpole blockbusters — a small number of franchise films (Marvel, DC, Star Wars, Avatar) drive a disproportionate share of revenue, making the business vulnerable to Hollywood production pipeline variability and strike disruptions [1]. Trailing P/E of 76.8 is extremely elevated, reflecting the still-recovering earnings base from COVID; while Forward P/E of 26.7 is more reasonable, it remains above the 20 target and prices in continued growth that could disappoint. Q1 2026 revenue declined to $81.4M from $86.7M prior year due to China weakness, highlighting geographic concentration risk [1]. Profit margin of 9.8% is well below the 20% target, and while improving, reflects the capital-intensive nature of theater system manufacturing and maintenance. The long-term risk of declining theatrical attendance — particularly among younger demographics who prefer streaming — could reduce the growth rate of new system installations. Competition from Dolby Cinema and other premium formats is intensifying, potentially compressing IMAX's pricing premium.
Key Catalysts
  1. Global IMAX network expansion with 160-175 system installations guided for FY2026, expanding the recurring revenue base from maintenance, content fees, and per-screen royalties across 1,700+ theaters worldwide [1]
  2. IMAX Enhanced and StreamSmart extending the brand into home entertainment and streaming — $2.5T+ global home entertainment market represents a massive TAM expansion beyond the theatrical installed base [2]
  3. AI-enhanced DMR remastering technology improving content quality and production efficiency, with AI video quality solutions creating new revenue streams from media and entertainment company customers [2]
Key Risks
  1. Content concentration risk — IMAX box office is disproportionately driven by a small number of tentpole franchise films; a weak blockbuster slate or Hollywood production disruptions would directly compress revenue and system utilization [1]
  2. China revenue exposure with Q1 2026 showing weakness due to decreased Chinese box office; regulatory, geopolitical, or economic deterioration in China would impact a significant portion of the global installed base [1]
  3. Secular decline in theatrical attendance, particularly among younger demographics preferring streaming, could reduce the long-term growth rate of new system installations and per-screen revenue contributions [1]
