Company Profile
Communication Services ·Entertainment ·NYSE ·Canada
IMAX Corporation designs, manufactures, and licenses proprietary large-screen theater systems, projection and sound technology to a global network of roughly 1,800 IMAX screens across 90+ countries. Revenue comes primarily from technology and equipment sales/leases to exhibitors plus a share of box office receipts from IMAX-formatted films. Headquartered in New York and Mississauga, Ontario; led by longtime CEO Richard Gelfond.
750 Employees ·www.imax.com

Investment Thesis
IMAX is riding a genuine cyclical and structural recovery: TTM revenue of $416M (+14.8% YoY) with gross margins expanding to 59.6%, Q2 2026 revenue up 12% to $103M and adjusted net income up 66%, driven by 38 system installations — the strongest second quarter in a decade [1][2]. 2026 guidance of ~$1.4B global box office and 160-175 installations gives strong forward revenue visibility, and the local-language content strategy (anchored by the record-breaking 'Demon Slayer: Infinity Castle' release in Japan) is diversifying the business beyond Hollywood blockbuster dependency into a more global, multi-market platform [1][2].
The bear case centers on durability of the premium-format moat: analysts flag that improving at-home entertainment and streaming economics could erode IMAX's differentiation over time, and rising production costs increase the risk that more content bypasses theatrical windows entirely, directly threatening the pipeline IMAX depends on [6]. CEO Gelfond's consistent net insider selling via 10b5-1 plans, combined with Glassdoor reviews describing an inconsistent, political internal culture, tempers conviction on the Inspiration and Trusting Teams dimensions even as the financial recovery looks genuine [3][5].
Key Catalysts
  1. 2026 guidance of 160-175 system installations and ~$1.4B global box office provides multi-quarter revenue visibility off a record installation pace
  2. Local-language content strategy (Japan, China, India) diversifying revenue beyond Hollywood blockbuster dependency, validated by record 'Demon Slayer: Infinity Castle' performance
  3. Continued laser-projection upgrade cycle and mid-40s adjusted EBITDA margin guidance signal further margin expansion as the network modernizes
Key Risks
  1. Streaming and at-home entertainment quality improvements could erode IMAX's premium-format differentiation over a multi-year horizon, per analyst commentary
  2. Heavy reliance on blockbuster content creates exposure if more films bypass theatrical windows in favor of direct-to-streaming release strategies
  3. CEO net insider selling via pre-arranged trading plans and Glassdoor reviews citing inconsistent leadership and a political internal culture weigh on long-term conviction
