Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·Australia
Immutep Limited is a clinical-stage biotechnology company pioneering LAG-3 immunotherapy for cancer and autoimmune diseases. Lead candidate eftilagimod alfa (efti) is a first-in-class MHC Class II agonist. The pivotal TACTI-004 Phase III trial in NSCLC was discontinued for futility in March 2026, triggering an 83% stock crash and multiple securities fraud class action lawsuits. Dual-listed on ASX (IMM) and NASDAQ (IMMP), headquartered in Sydney, Australia.
