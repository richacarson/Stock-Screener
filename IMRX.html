Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Immuneering Corporation is a late-stage oncology company developing Deep Cyclic Inhibitors, a new class of cancer therapies designed to deliver pulsed pathway inhibition faster than tumors can adapt. Lead candidate atebimetinib (IMM-1-104) is an oral, once-daily Deep Cyclic MEK Inhibitor advancing through the pivotal Phase 3 MAPKeeper 301 trial in first-line pancreatic cancer, with Phase 2a data showing 17.3-month median overall survival in 55 patients. Under co-founder CEO Ben Zeskind, PhD, the company reported Q2 2026 net loss of $17.3M with $182.7M in cash, providing runway into 2029. Market cap approximately $327M.

Investment Thesis
Immuneering offers a differentiated oncology bet with its novel Deep Cyclic Inhibitor platform targeting first-line pancreatic cancer, where Phase 2a data showed 17.3-month median overall survival versus a historical benchmark of ~11 months — a potentially transformative improvement in one of the deadliest cancers [1]. The pivotal Phase 3 MAPKeeper 301 trial is now recruiting at 30+ sites globally, with $182.7M in cash providing runway into 2029 and eliminating near-term dilution risk [2]. Five analysts maintain a Strong Buy consensus with a $17.20 median price target representing 230% upside from the current $5.21, and the co-founder-led team brings continuity since 2008. At $327M market cap, a positive Phase 3 readout in pancreatic cancer could re-rate the stock dramatically given the $5B+ addressable market.
The bear case centers on the binary nature of a single-asset clinical-stage biotech. Atebimetinib is Immuneering's only asset in advanced clinical development — a Phase 3 failure would leave the company with cash but no viable near-term pipeline, likely compressing the stock toward cash value [1]. Phase 3 pancreatic cancer trials have a historically high failure rate, and the 17.3-month median OS from Phase 2a, while encouraging, was in only 55 patients — insufficient to predict Phase 3 outcomes with confidence. The $30.8M H1 2026 burn rate will accelerate as the global trial ramps, and achieving 2029 runway assumes no additional spending beyond current plans. Infrastructure scores poorly (3/10) due to the pre-revenue nature of the business — no earnings, no revenue, no margin profile. The company's stock has traded between $2.90 and $10.08 in the last 52 weeks, reflecting the high uncertainty inherent in a binary clinical outcome.
