Integrated Media Technology Limited is a technology investment and distribution company headquartered in Adelaide, Australia. The company operates across disparate business lines including manufacture and sale of nano-coated plates for filters and air purifiers, electronic glass, halal certification and distribution, new energy products, and Credit AI (corporate bond risk analytics via Greifenberg Digital). CEO Con Unerkov has served since December 2024, also acting as interim CFO. Annual revenue of $72,641 (2025) across 5 employees.
