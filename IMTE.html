Company Profile
Technology ·Electronic Components ·NASDAQ ·Australia
Integrated Media Technology Limited is a technology investment and distribution company headquartered in Adelaide, Australia. The company operates across disparate business lines including manufacture and sale of nano-coated plates for filters and air purifiers, electronic glass, halal certification and distribution, new energy products, and operation of Ouction, a digital asset trading marketplace. Executive Chairman & CEO is Xiaodong Zhang (since 2021).
