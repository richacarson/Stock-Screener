Company Profile
Technology ·Quantum Computing & Sensing ·NYSE ·United States
Infleqtion builds neutral-atom quantum computers, quantum sensors (optical clocks, RF receivers, inertial sensors) and software for governments, enterprises and research institutions. Founded in 2007 as ColdQuanta out of the University of Colorado Boulder, it listed on the NYSE in February 2026 via a merger with Churchill Capital Corp X. Q2 2026 revenue was restated to $13.5M (+157% YoY) from an originally reported $12.6M, and full-year 2026 guidance was raised to about $45.1M; the GAAP operating loss was $30.6M. On September 24, 2026 the company reported 30 entangled logical qubits on its Sqale system, with a roadmap to 100 logical qubits by 2028. It holds roughly $582M of cash and investments with no debt. Market cap approximately $2.0B at $8.88.

Investment Thesis
Infleqtion is executing on both revenue and technology: Q2 2026 revenue rose 157% (restated) to $13.5M, FY2026 guidance was lifted to ~$45.1M, it delivered 30 logical qubits in September 2026 on schedule, and it has a $100M CHIPS Act LOI [1][4][3]. With about $582M of cash and investments and no debt, it is funded for several years, and its sensing products (optical clocks, RF receivers) give it nearer-term commercial revenue than pure quantum-computing peers, alongside NVIDIA ecosystem integration [1][3].
The bear case is valuation and execution: at ~$2.0B market cap the stock trades near 90x TTM sales with negative operating margins, a GAAP operating loss of $30.6M in Q2 versus $10.1M a year earlier, and stock compensation near 92% of revenue [5][1]. The Q2 revenue restatement, net insider selling by the CTO and directors, and a share price that has fallen from the high teens to $8.88 point to fragile sentiment [6][7]. Q2 operating cash flow was also flattered by a $27.4M payroll-tax timing benefit due to reverse in Q3 [1].
Key Catalysts
  1. Q3 2026 results confirming the ~$45.1M FY2026 revenue guidance after the Q2 restatement
  2. Conversion of the $100M CHIPS Act letter of intent into a definitive award
  3. Progress toward 100 logical qubits by 2028 on the Sqale platform
  4. New government and data-center quantum-sensing contracts (optical clocks, RF receivers) and deeper NVIDIA NVQLink integration
Key Risks
  1. Cash burn from GAAP operating losses (~$30.6M per quarter) eroding the ~$582M cash balance and forcing dilution
  2. Further revenue-recognition issues on government contracts after the Q2 2026 restatement
  3. Continued insider and early-investor selling following the SPAC lock-up
  4. Competition from private neutral-atom players and superconducting/trapped-ion rivals for logical-qubit leadership
