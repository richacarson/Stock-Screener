Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NASDAQ ·United States
Intapp provides vertical cloud software for the global professional and capital markets — law firms, accounting firms, private equity, investment banking, and consulting. The platform covers deal management (DealCloud), risk and compliance, time tracking, and firm operations. FY2026 (ending June 2026) revenue reached $577.8M (+15% YoY) with SaaS revenue of $422.8M (+27%) and Cloud ARR of $495.7M. CEO John Hall has led the company since 2004. Intapp is actively deploying AI through its Celeste agentic AI platform and 70+ DealCloud AI features. Market cap is approximately $2.8B.

Investment Thesis
Intapp occupies a strong vertical niche as the leading cloud platform for professional and capital markets firms, with SaaS revenue growing 27% YoY to $422.8M and Cloud ARR of $495.7M in FY2026 [1]. The forward P/E of 18.8x and estimated PEG of ~0.94 represent attractive valuation for a company with 15% total revenue growth accelerating through AI integration. The Celeste agentic AI platform positions Intapp as an AI enabler for its clients rather than a disruption victim, potentially accelerating adoption and expanding ARPU across the ~$10B addressable market. CEO John Hall's 22-year tenure provides strategic continuity, and FY2027 guidance of $656.5-$660.5M (+14% growth) with Non-GAAP EPS of $1.58-$1.62 (+24.4%) shows improving profitability trajectory [1]. Free cash flow of $144.7M demonstrates strong underlying cash economics despite GAAP losses.
The bear case centers on GAAP profitability and organizational execution. Intapp remains GAAP-unprofitable with a -$41.3M net loss in FY2026, -7.2% profit margin, and negative ROE of -9.9%, which creates vulnerability if growth decelerates or market sentiment shifts against unprofitable software companies [4]. Stock-based compensation inflates non-GAAP metrics and dilutes shareholders. Glassdoor reviews (culture 2.8/5, career 3.0/5) suggest organizational stress from rapid transformation that could impair execution of the AI strategy [3]. The professional services end-market faces its own AI disruption — if AI fundamentally restructures how law firms, accounting firms, and PE firms operate, demand for Intapp's specific workflow tools could shift in unpredictable ways. At $2.8B market cap, the stock prices in continued 15%+ growth and successful AI monetization that remains unproven at scale.

  1. Celeste agentic AI platform driving ARPU expansion across existing customer base as professional services firms adopt AI-powered deal management, risk, and compliance workflows — 70+ AI features already shipped with more on the roadmap [2]

Key Risks

  3. Organizational execution risk highlighted by Glassdoor ratings (culture 2.8/5) and employee descriptions of 'shifting priorities, thin communication, and process friction amid rapid change' during a critical AI transformation period [3]
