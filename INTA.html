Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NASDAQ ·United States
Intapp, Inc. provides AI-powered cloud software solutions for professional and financial services firms, including law firms, accounting firms, investment banks, and private capital managers. Products include DealCloud (deal and relationship management), compliance and conflicts management, time and billing, and the AI-native Celeste agentic platform. Founded in 2000, headquartered in Palo Alto, California. Market cap approximately $1.9B.

Investment Thesis
Intapp is executing a successful cloud transformation with SaaS revenue growing 27% YoY to $107.9M in Q3 FY2026 and cloud ARR reaching $459.3M, while the Celeste AI platform is an early differentiator contributing 15%+ of net new bookings [1][2]. FY2026 guidance of $574-575M total revenue with non-GAAP operating income of $102-104M demonstrates improving unit economics. The virtually debt-free balance sheet (D/E 5%) provides financial flexibility, and the 849K average daily volume at $32.69/share provides adequate liquidity. Analyst consensus is Buy with an average target of $48.63, implying 49% upside.
The bear case centers on persistent GAAP unprofitability (-4.4% net margin, -5.4% ROE) after 25 years of operation, with no clear timeline to GAAP breakeven — the stock trades on non-GAAP metrics and narrative rather than demonstrated earnings power [4]. Cultural deterioration is a material risk: Glassdoor dropped to 3.3/5 with employees describing morale as 'awful' and talent attrition accelerating at a critical moment in the AI platform scaling effort [3]. The narrow vertical focus on professional services limits the total addressable market relative to horizontal SaaS peers, and Microsoft Copilot for M365 (which Intapp integrates with) could cannibalize workflow value if Microsoft builds professional services features natively. The stock has declined significantly from highs, reflecting market skepticism about the profitability inflection thesis.
Key Catalysts
  1. Celeste AI platform scaling beyond 15% of net new bookings as agentic capabilities expand across the 145+ partner ecosystem and Azure Marketplace co-selling channel [1][2]
  2. GAAP profitability inflection as SaaS mix shift drives gross margin expansion (non-GAAP already 78.8%) and operating leverage materializes at scale
  3. Cross-sell and upsell within existing top-100 law firm and private capital client base as cloud migration accelerates from legacy on-premises installed base
Key Risks
  1. Persistent GAAP losses and negative ROE (-5.4%) after 25 years of operation risk further multiple compression if SaaS growth decelerates or the path to profitability extends [4]
  2. Glassdoor-documented cultural deterioration (3.3/5, morale described as 'awful', talent attrition) could undermine execution on the Celeste AI roadmap at a critical scaling moment [3]
  3. Microsoft Copilot platform risk — Intapp's collaboration products depend on M365 integration, and Microsoft could subsume professional services workflow features natively, compressing Intapp's addressable market
