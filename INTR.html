Company Profile
Financial Services ·Digital Banking ·NASDAQ ·Brazil
Inter & Co, Inc. is Brazil's largest digital bank and a leading Latin American fintech, operating a financial super app that provides banking, payments, investments, insurance, and marketplace services to 45.3 million clients. Founded in 1994 as Intermedium, the company was transformed into a 100% digital bank under CEO Joao Vitor Menin beginning in 2015, pioneering the first digital checking account in Latin America. Listed on NASDAQ in 2022. Q2 2026 delivered record net income of R$421M (+34% YoY) with ROE reaching 16.3%, marking 13 consecutive quarters of net income growth. Total assets surpassed R$100B for the first time.

Investment Thesis
Inter & Co at 12.70x forward earnings and 0.31 PEG offers a rare combination of hypergrowth and value in emerging market fintech. Q2 2026 delivered record net income of R$421M (+34% YoY) with ROE reaching 16.3% — the 13th consecutive quarter of net income growth — while the client base reached 45.3M and total assets surpassed R$100B for the first time [1]. The credit portfolio expanded 36% YoY (3x the Brazilian market growth rate), demonstrating the super app's ability to cross-sell financial products at scale [2]. At $5.59 per share, the stock trades 46% below the consensus analyst target of $8.75 (10 analysts, 'Buy' average) and near its 52-week low of $5.04, suggesting the market is underpricing the growth trajectory. The 'Rule of 50' framework targets sustainable ROE + growth above 50%, providing a disciplined path to compounding returns.
The bear case centers on emerging market risk, currency exposure, and concentration in a single economy. Inter is denominated in BRL but trades in USD — the stock has declined from $10.36 to $5.59 over the past year despite record operational results, largely due to BRL depreciation against USD and Brazilian macroeconomic uncertainty [4]. The D/E ratio of 148% reflects standard banking leverage but amplifies any credit quality deterioration in the Brazilian consumer portfolio. ROE of 13.27% (trailing) is still below the 15% target despite strong quarterly trajectory, and the company's net interest margin faces pressure from competitive pricing in Brazilian digital banking. The single-founder dependency on CEO Menin represents key-man risk — the company's infinite game characteristics are tied to his leadership. Regulatory risk from Brazil's Central Bank could constrain growth if credit quality deteriorates in the next economic downturn. The Inspire Impact Score of 29 (ALIGNED) provides modest faith-based appeal through financial inclusion.
