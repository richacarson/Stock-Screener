Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NASDAQ ·United States
Intuit is a global financial technology platform behind TurboTax, QuickBooks, Credit Karma, and Mailchimp, serving consumers, small businesses, and accountants with tax, accounting, credit, and marketing tools. FY2026 (ended July 31, 2026) revenue rose 14% to $21.45B with GAAP diluted EPS of $16.46. In May 2026, CEO Sasan Goodarzi cut about 17% of the workforce (~3,000 roles) to simplify the organization, and Intuit signed multi-year agreements to embed Anthropic and OpenAI models in its products and make its tools available inside Claude and ChatGPT.

Investment Thesis
Intuit combines elite financials with a valuation reset. FY2026 revenue grew 14% to $21.45B, GAAP diluted EPS rose 20% to $16.46, and the five-year revenue CAGR is 17.4%, with a 21% net margin, 23.6% ROE, and only 44% D/E [1][2][3]. After falling from $682 to $341, the stock trades at 12.7x forward earnings with a 1.08 PEG, while FY2027 guidance still calls for 9-10% revenue growth and Q1 revenue growth of 11% [2][4]. The Anthropic and OpenAI agreements position Intuit's tax and accounting engines as the trusted back end for AI assistants rather than a casualty of them [6].
The bear case is that AI erodes the franchise faster than Intuit can adapt. FY2027 TurboTax growth is guided to only 2-3%, average revenue per TurboTax customer is falling, Mailchimp is weak, and desktop products are declining [4]. The 17% workforce reduction and ~$340M restructuring charge raise execution and morale risk, a 2026 securities class action is pending, and the switch to including stock compensation in non-GAAP EPS makes guidance look lower ($22.88-$23.12 vs. $24.27 in FY2026) [5][6][7].
Key Catalysts
  1. Q1 FY2027 results — guidance of $4.29-$4.31B revenue (+11%) and non-GAAP EPS of $2.44-$2.48; beating it would ease fears of an AI-driven slowdown [4]
  2. Monetization of AI distribution — Anthropic and OpenAI agreements put Intuit's tax, accounting, and marketing tools inside Claude and ChatGPT, opening new customer acquisition channels [6]
  3. Margin expansion from restructuring — the May 2026 removal of ~3,000 coordination-heavy roles should lower operating costs from FY2027 onward [6]
Key Risks
  1. AI commoditization of tax and bookkeeping — TurboTax growth guided to just 2-3% for FY2027 with declining revenue per customer [4]
  2. Organizational disruption — a 17% workforce cut (~3,000 roles) could hurt morale and execution during a critical product transition [6]
  3. Mailchimp and desktop decline — weaker Mailchimp revenue and declining desktop products were key drivers of the FY2027 guidance miss [4]
