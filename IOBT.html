Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NMS ·Denmark
IO Biotech is a clinical-stage immuno-oncology company focused on developing T-cell modulators for cancer treatment. The company's lead candidate, Cylembio (IO102-IO103), a dual-antigen T-cell inducer targeting IDO and PD-L1, narrowly missed its primary endpoint in a pivotal Phase III melanoma trial in August 2025 [3]. Following this setback, IO Biotech announced a strategic review in January 2026, retaining Raymond James as financial advisor to evaluate alternatives including merger, sale, or liquidation [2]. The company had approximately $37.1M in cash reserves as of Q3 2025, with zero revenue and accumulated operating losses exceeding $200M. Headquartered near Copenhagen, Denmark.
