Investment Thesis
IonQ delivered its fifth consecutive record revenue quarter with Q2 2026 revenue of $80.1M (+287% YoY, organic growth 132%), and raised full-year guidance to $280-290M with remaining performance obligations nearly quadrupling to $485M from $122M a year earlier [1]. The SkyWater and Nexus acquisitions create a full-stack quantum platform spanning computing, sensing, networking, and quantum-safe security, with a 256-qubit roadmap providing long-term technology differentiation. CEO de Masi's Senate testimony and government partnerships (DARPA, Space Development Agency) position IonQ at the intersection of quantum computing and national security policy tailwinds. D/E of 1.6% with $2.12B cash provides multi-year runway, and the Inspire Impact Score of +44 (ALIGNED) is a distinguishing positive for faith-based portfolios.
The bear case centers on governance and cash burn rather than the technology roadmap. Operating margin of -408% and ROE of -60.5% reflect a deeply pre-profit company — even with 287% revenue growth, the company posted a $1.87B GAAP net loss in Q2 2026 (driven substantially by $1.65B non-cash warrant fair-value charges, with adjusted EBITDA loss of $120.3M) [1][5]. The insider-selling pattern is the most serious concern: approximately $988M in insider stock sales over five years against just $3.35M in purchases, with Wolfpack Research alleging $396.6M was tied to newly created 10b5-1 plans around the CEO transition, and a securities-fraud investigation remains open [4]. Former CEO Chapman's $37.5M discretionary sale two weeks after resigning compounds governance risk. The stock has declined 48% from its 52-week high of $84.64 to $43.77, and beta of 3.36 amplifies any market downturn. Management acknowledges 'no commercial killer app running at scale on quantum hardware' — any roadmap delay would extend the timeline to profitability significantly.
