Company Profile
Technology ·Computer Hardware ·NYSE ·United States
IonQ is a pure-play quantum computing company using trapped-ion technology to build full-stack quantum systems. Under Chairman and CEO Niccolo de Masi, IonQ has delivered five consecutive quarters of record results, with Q2 2026 revenue surging 287% YoY to $80.1M driven by IonQ Tempo system deployments in South Korea (KISTI), Switzerland (QuantumBasel), and accelerating cloud utilization. The company raised its full-year standalone revenue outlook to $280-290M with ~100% organic growth. IonQ acquired SkyWater Technology for $1.8B to add U.S.-based semiconductor fabrication capabilities. The company plans 256-qubit semiconductor-based systems in H1 2027 and targets 10,000 qubits by 2027. Market cap is approximately $16.2B with a net cash position of $2.06B.

Investment Thesis
IonQ at $42.53 ($16.2B market cap) is the leading pure-play public quantum computing company delivering explosive growth — Q2 2026 revenue of $80.1M (+287% YoY) with five consecutive quarters of record results and raised full-year guidance to $280-290M with ~100% organic growth [1]. The company occupies a rare position: a pre-profitable technology pioneer in a market that could reach $850B+ by 2040 according to McKinsey estimates. The $1.8B SkyWater acquisition creates vertical integration from quantum chip design through fabrication, and the 256-qubit semiconductor-based systems planned for H1 2027 represent a meaningful step toward practical quantum advantage. The pristine balance sheet (D/E 2%, $2.06B net cash) funds years of R&D investment without dilutive financing. The Inspire Impact Score of 44 (ALIGNED) is a positive for faith-based investors. World-record quantum performance and the trapped-ion technology advantage over superconducting competitors position IonQ to lead the quantum computing commercialization wave.
The bear case centers on valuation, profitability, and technology risk. IonQ trades at a price-to-sales ratio of ~66x — among the most extreme in public technology markets — pricing in a quantum computing future that remains unproven at commercial scale [4]. The company posted a $1.9B GAAP net loss in Q2 2026 (including $1.6B in non-cash warrant impact), with adjusted EBITDA of -$120.3M, and profitability is likely years away [1]. Short-seller scrutiny over revenue quality — including allegations that lost government contracts created a '$54.6M black hole' backfilled by acquisitions — undermines the organic growth narrative [3]. IonQ's exclusion from the $2B federal quantum program raises credibility concerns about the company's technology positioning relative to competitors (IBM, Google, Quantinuum). Four acquisitions since mid-2025, high CEO compensation, board turnover, and significant shareholder dilution are governance red flags [3]. Most critically, the quantum computing industry has not yet demonstrated practical quantum advantage — the ability to solve commercially relevant problems faster than classical computers. If quantum advantage proves elusive or arrives later than expected, the $16.2B valuation has no fundamental support.

AI Risk Protection (8/9): IonQ's trapped-ion quantum computing technology operates on fundamentally different physical principles than classical computing or AI — quantum mechanics (superposition, entanglement) cannot be replicated or disrupted by AI systems [2]. The company builds physical quantum hardware (ion traps, laser systems, cryogenic infrastructure) that AI structurally cannot substitute. If anything, the growth of AI increases demand for quantum computing: as classical AI approaches theoretical limits in optimization, drug discovery, and cryptography, quantum computing becomes the next frontier. IonQ's quantum security capabilities (quantum-resistant encryption, quantum key distribution) become more valuable as AI makes classical encryption more vulnerable. The score reaches 8 rather than 9 because the quantum computing market is still nascent — if quantum computing fails to achieve practical quantum advantage at commercial scale, the entire business thesis evaporates regardless of AI dynamics.
