Company Profile
Communication Services ·Telecom Services ·NASDAQ ·Singapore
iOThree Limited is a Singapore-based provider of maritime digital technologies, satellite connectivity, and AI-powered analytics for the maritime industry. Operating through Satellite Connectivity and Digitalization segments, the company offers its JARVISS edge computing platform, V.Suite digital solutions (V.SIGHT AI surveillance, V.IoT shipboard monitoring, V.SECURE cybersecurity, V.WEATHER route optimization), and F.R.I.D.A.Y. planned maintenance system for carbon reporting. FY2026 (ended March 2026) revenue grew 40% YoY to US$14.7M with gross profit surging 68.4% to $3.2M, though the company posted a net loss of $1.2M. Incorporated in 2019, listed on NASDAQ.
