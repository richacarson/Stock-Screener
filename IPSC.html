Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Century Therapeutics, Inc. is a clinical-stage biotechnology company developing induced pluripotent stem cell (iPSC)-derived cell therapies for cancer and autoimmune diseases. The company's proprietary Allo-Evasion platform enables the creation of allogeneic (off-the-shelf) cell therapies designed to evade immune rejection. Key pipeline programs include CNTY-813 (iPSC-derived islet therapy for type 1 diabetes) and CNTY-308 (CD19-targeted CAR-iT cell therapy). Founded in 2018, headquartered in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.

Investment Thesis
Century Therapeutics is a platform-stage biotech with genuinely novel iPSC-derived cell therapy technology (Allo-Evasion 5.0) and two advancing programs: CNTY-813 (type 1 diabetes curative islet therapy, IND Q4 2026) and CNTY-308 (CD19 CAR-iT, entering clinic 2026) [1]. FY2025 collaboration revenue of $109.2M validated the platform's commercial appeal to pharma partners, and the net loss narrowed 92% to just $9.6M [1]. The $217M cash position provides runway into Q1 2029, and the January 2026 $135M private placement (oversubscribed, led by TCGX and RA Capital) signals institutional confidence. Analyst price target of $4.75 implies 111% upside from the current $2.25 share price.
The bear case is substantial for this speculative biotech. The stock has lost 89% of its value since the 2021 IPO ($21 to $2.25), destroying shareholder value over five years with no approved products to show for $400M+ in cumulative spending [3]. Near-complete C-suite turnover (CEO, CFO, CSO all replaced within 18 months) signals organizational instability in a 78-person company [2]. Both pipeline programs are pre-clinical or very early clinical stage — CNTY-813's IND isn't even filed yet, and initial clinical data won't arrive until 2H 2027, meaning at least 18 months of binary clinical risk with no revenue-generating products. The iPSC cell therapy space is crowded (Fate Therapeutics, Allogene, CRISPR Therapeutics, larger pharma entrants) with no clear technological winner. The $217M cash runway to Q1 2029 could require additional dilutive financing if programs experience delays. Collaboration revenue of $109.2M is lumpy and partnership-dependent, not recurring product revenue.

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience MODERATE
7
AI Risk Protection (7/9): Cell therapy development requires physical manufacturing of living cells from iPSC differentiation, GMP production, clinical trial execution with physical patient delivery, and FDA regulatory approval — none of which AI can substitute [2]. Century's Allo-Evasion platform involves complex biological engineering (stem cell programming, immune evasion, cell differentiation) that depends on laboratory expertise and physical processes. The regulated nature of cell therapy (BLA approval, manufacturing facility certification, cold chain logistics) creates additional structural barriers to AI disruption. However, AI-powered drug discovery could enable competitors to more efficiently identify better cell engineering approaches or alternative modalities that compete with iPSC-derived therapies. Larger pharma companies with AI capabilities could accelerate their own cell therapy programs, potentially outpacing Century's resource-constrained development. AI Opportunity (0/1): Century does not generate revenue from AI products or services. No AI-related commercial activity has been disclosed.
