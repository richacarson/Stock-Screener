Company Profile
Materials ·Other Industrial Metals & Mining ·NASDAQ ·United States
IperionX Limited is a titanium and critical minerals company developing an all-American titanium supply chain. The company operates a titanium powder production facility in Utah producing angular and spherical titanium metal powders using its patented HAMR (Hydrogen Assisted Metallothermic Reduction) technology, and is advancing the Titan Critical Minerals Project in Tennessee containing titanium, zircon, and rare earth mineral resources. Incorporated in Australia, headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina. Dual-listed on NASDAQ and ASX.

Investment Thesis
IperionX represents a high-risk, high-potential bet on domestic titanium supply chain independence. The company's patented HAMR technology enables low-cost, low-carbon titanium production with UL-validated 100% recycled content, and the Titan Critical Minerals Project DFS confirms a large-scale, technically robust resource base in Tennessee [1][3]. With a clean balance sheet ($54.8M cash, D/E of 4%, current ratio of 6.99) and analyst price targets averaging $55.33 (nearly 2x current price), the market sees significant upside if the company can commercialize its technology at scale [2]. US government critical minerals policy and defense sector titanium demand provide potential tailwinds.
The bear case centers on execution risk inherent in a pre-revenue company burning $18M+ annually in free cash flow with less than one year of cash runway at current rates [1]. Revenue remains sub-$1M despite years of development, and the path to commercial-scale production requires substantial additional capital raises that will dilute existing shareholders. The global titanium market is dominated by established producers with decades of operational experience, and HAMR technology has not yet been proven at commercial scale. At a ~$960M market cap with no revenue, the stock is priced entirely on potential — any delay in commercialization timelines or difficulty scaling the HAMR process could result in significant downside [2].
Key Catalysts
  1. Titan Critical Minerals Project construction financing and commencement of site development, which would validate the DFS projections and de-risk the production timeline
  2. Commercial-scale titanium powder contracts with aerospace or defense customers, providing first meaningful revenue and validating the HAMR technology at production volumes
  3. US critical minerals policy support including potential DoD or DOE funding for domestic titanium production capacity, reducing capital raise requirements
Key Risks
  1. Less than one year of cash runway at current burn rate of $18M+ annually requires near-term capital raises that will dilute existing shareholders and may be difficult in challenging capital markets
  2. HAMR technology has not been proven at commercial scale — failure to scale from pilot production to full-capacity manufacturing would undermine the entire investment thesis
  3. Global titanium market dominated by VSMPO-AVISMA (Russia) and Chinese producers with decades of operational experience and established customer relationships, creating significant competitive barriers to entry
