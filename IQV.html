Company Profile
Healthcare ·Diagnostics & Research ·NYSE ·United States
IQVIA Holdings is the world's largest contract research organization (CRO) and healthcare data analytics company, formed from the 2016 merger of IMS Health and Quintiles Transnational. The company operates through R&D Solutions (clinical trial management, decentralized trials, regulatory services) and Commercial Solutions (real-world data analytics, patient engagement, technology platforms), serving 10,000+ life sciences customers across 100+ countries. IQVIA manages a contracted R&D backlog of $34.2B and processes one of the largest proprietary healthcare datasets globally. Q2 2026 revenue reached $4.37B (+8.7% YoY) with raised FY2026 guidance of $17.3-17.5B and adjusted diluted EPS of $12.80-$13.00.

Investment Thesis
IQVIA delivered Q2 2026 revenue of $4.37B (+8.7% YoY) with adjusted diluted EPS of $3.15 (+12.1%), raising FY2026 guidance to $17.3-17.5B revenue and $12.80-$13.00 EPS [1]. R&D Solutions net new bookings surged to $3.15B (+19% YoY) at a 1.22x book-to-bill ratio, signaling accelerating demand that should translate to revenue acceleration in 2027-2028 as the $34.2B contracted backlog converts [1]. Commercial Solutions grew 8.6% to $1.79B on AI-enabled analytics and patient engagement demand tied to the IQVIA.ai platform, and R&D Solutions grew 8.8% to $2.58B [1]. At 19.2x forward earnings with the stock recovering from a 36% drawdown, the risk/reward improves if IQVIA proves that AI is a net opportunity — the company has not lost a single trial to AI tools and is monetizing AI-driven demand directly through the NVIDIA partnership [2].
The bear case centers on a legitimate, analyst-flagged structural risk: roughly 30% of typical clinical trial costs are tied to functions IQVIA and peer CROs perform (data management, statistical analysis, medical writing), and large pharma sponsors are investing in AI infrastructure that could gradually bring these in-house, eroding the outsourcing model [2]. Elevated leverage at 258% D/E and 3.59x net debt/EBITDA with $16B+ in total debt leaves limited financial flexibility if pricing pressure intensifies or a recession curtails pharma R&D budgets [1]. GAAP profit margin of 8.10% is structurally compressed by $3B+ in acquisition-related intangible amortization, creating earnings quality questions. Heavy net insider selling by CEO Bousbib (~$20.6M over the trailing period, zero purchases) sends a mixed signal on leadership conviction [3]. Glassdoor employee reviews consistently cite toxic culture, favoritism, and quiet-firing practices — organizational dysfunction that risks talent retention in a competitive life sciences labor market [5]. The Inspire Impact Score of 48 (ALIGNED) is a positive for faith-based allocation.
