Company Profile
Industrials ·Specialty Industrial Machinery ·NYSE ·United States
Ingersoll Rand Inc. is a diversified industrial company providing mission-critical flow creation and life science and industrial solutions worldwide. It operates through two segments: Industrial Technologies & Services (compressors, blowers, vacuum systems, air treatment and power tools) and Precision & Science Technologies (pumps, fluid management, dosing and life sciences equipment). Formed from the 2020 combination of Gardner Denver and Ingersoll-Rand's Industrial segment, the company grows through its IRX execution system and a steady cadence of bolt-on acquisitions.

Investment Thesis
Ingersoll Rand is a high-quality industrial compounder with a clean balance sheet (46% D/E, 1.6x net leverage) and 28% adjusted EBITDA margins [1][7]. Q3 2026 orders surged 21% (18% organic), and management raised full-year revenue growth guidance to 5.5%-7.0% with adjusted EPS of $3.58-$3.70 excluding tariff refunds [1].
The score is capped by an acquisition-led model with little measurable AI or R&D differentiation, a generic purpose, and a forward PEG of 2.55 that leaves limited valuation upside [8]. The order acceleration needs to convert into organic revenue growth above the 2%-3.5% guided range before the shares look compelling on this framework.
Key Catalysts
  1. Q3 2026 orders up 21% (18% organic) with a 1.17x IT&S book-to-bill point to accelerating organic revenue in 2027 [1]
  2. $2B added to the buyback authorization plus $3.9B of liquidity to fund continued bolt-on M&A such as Lone Star Blower and the pending US pump maker [1]
  3. Life Sciences orders and revenue growing low double digits, shifting mix toward higher-margin P&ST businesses [1]
Key Risks
  1. TTM earnings are flattered by one-time ILC Dover recoveries and IEEPA tariff refunds; underlying adjusted EBITDA excluding refunds grew only 1% in Q3 [1]
  2. Organic revenue growth guided at just 2%-3.5% for 2026, leaving growth dependent on acquisitions and integration execution [1]
  3. Forward PEG of 2.55 and a 20x forward P/E leave little margin for an industrial slowdown [7][8]
