IREN Limited (formerly Iris Energy) operates as a vertically integrated AI cloud services platform, owning and operating data centers, GPU/CPU compute infrastructure, and managed services software. The company completed its pivot from Bitcoin mining to AI infrastructure, with AI cloud revenue surpassing Bitcoin mining revenue for the first time in Q4 FY2026 ($70.5M vs $66.7M). Anchored by a $9.7B Microsoft cloud contract and an NVIDIA strategic partnership for up to 5GW of AI infrastructure, IREN targets $4B+ in annualized run-rate revenue.
