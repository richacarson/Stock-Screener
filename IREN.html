Company Profile
Technology ·Data Center Infrastructure / Bitcoin Mining ·NASDAQ ·Australia
IREN Limited (formerly Iris Energy) is a vertically integrated data center business operating a dual-engine model: Bitcoin mining (50 EH/s capacity) and AI Cloud services, powered by 100% renewable energy. Founded in 2018 by Australian co-CEOs Daniel and William Roberts, the company operates a 3GW grid-connected power portfolio across data center sites in British Columbia, Texas, and other locations. IREN went public on NASDAQ in November 2021 and rebranded from Iris Energy in 2024. In Q1 FY26 (ending September 2025), IREN reported record revenue of $240.3M (+355% YoY), with Bitcoin mining contributing $232.9M and AI Cloud Services $7.3M. The company has secured a $9.7B Microsoft contract and targets $3.4B in AI Cloud annualized run-rate revenue by end of 2026 through deployment of 140,000 GPUs. Market cap is approximately $13.8B.

Investment Thesis
IREN at $38.74 represents a high-growth data center infrastructure play uniquely positioned at the intersection of Bitcoin mining and AI compute demand. Revenue has grown from $270M (FY2024) to $501M (FY2025) to $757M TTM, driven by a 355% YoY surge in Q1 FY26 to $240.3M [1]. The $9.7B Microsoft contract anchors AI Cloud revenue visibility, with $1.9B expected ARR contribution and total AI Cloud ARR targeting $3.4B by end of 2026 across 140,000 GPUs [2]. The 3GW grid-connected power portfolio — powered entirely by renewable energy — provides a structural advantage as data center power availability becomes the key bottleneck for AI infrastructure expansion. Adjusted EBITDA margin of 38% in Q1 FY26 demonstrates the operating leverage inherent in the infrastructure model, while $1.8B in cash provides runway for continued expansion [1].
The bear case centers on execution risk, leverage, and Bitcoin volatility. D/E of 149% reflects aggressive debt financing including $964.2M in convertible notes and a $1.0B zero-coupon issuance, creating significant financial risk if revenue ramps slower than planned [1][4]. The company's trailing P/E of 71x and P/S of 18.3x price in substantial growth — any shortfall in the $3.4B ARR target could trigger severe multiple compression. Bitcoin mining still contributes $232.9M of $240.3M in Q1 FY26 revenue, creating dependence on volatile cryptocurrency prices [1]. Co-CEO William Roberts' sale of ~$33M in shares raises questions about insider conviction at current valuations [4]. The 26.22% short interest reflects significant market skepticism. Operational risk is concentrated in executing a massive GPU deployment while simultaneously maintaining profitable mining operations — a dual challenge that few companies have successfully managed. The Altman Z-score of 1.42 suggests elevated financial risk despite strong revenue growth.
Key Catalysts
  1. AI Cloud revenue acceleration toward $3.4B ARR target as 140,000 GPU deployment progresses, with the $9.7B Microsoft contract providing revenue visibility and validation for additional enterprise customer acquisition [2]
  2. Bitcoin halving cycle dynamics and potential Bitcoin price appreciation could significantly boost mining profitability, with IREN's 50 EH/s capacity and 100% renewable energy cost structure providing superior unit economics vs. fossil-fuel-powered competitors [1]

Key Risks
  1. Execution risk on the $3.4B AI Cloud ARR target requires successfully deploying 140,000 GPUs, scaling enterprise sales, and maintaining data center uptime — any material shortfall would undermine the growth narrative supporting the 71x trailing P/E [1][2]
  2. Bitcoin price decline or mining difficulty increases could compress mining margins, which still represent the majority of revenue ($232.9M of $240.3M in Q1 FY26), creating earnings volatility that AI cloud revenue has not yet offset [1]
  3. Leverage risk from $3.96B in total debt against $2.21B in cash, with the 149% D/E ratio and 1.42 Altman Z-score indicating that a prolonged downturn in either Bitcoin or AI infrastructure demand could create liquidity pressure [1][4]
