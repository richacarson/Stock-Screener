Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Gold ·NYSE American ·Canada
Integra Resources Corp. is a precious metals producer and project developer focused on gold and silver mining in the Great Basin of the Western United States. Principal assets include the operating Florida Canyon gold mine in Nevada (70,927 oz gold produced in 2025) and the development-stage DeLamar gold-silver project in Idaho with an NPV of $774M at $3,000 gold and $1.9B at current gold prices. Founded in 2017 by George Salamis, headquartered in Vancouver, Canada.

Investment Thesis
Integra Resources is a high-leverage gold play with compelling optionality at current prices. Florida Canyon produced 70,927 oz in 2025 generating $243.9M in revenue, while Q1 2026 showed dramatically improving profitability — $12.5M net earnings on $61.7M revenue with $4,854/oz average realized gold price versus $3,310/oz AISC [1]. The DeLamar project offers transformative upside: the December 2025 feasibility study shows 106,000 gold-equivalent oz annual production over 10 years with $1.9B NPV at current gold prices and 97% IRR [4]. At a forward P/E of 6.40 and $458M market cap against DeLamar NPV alone of nearly $2B at spot, the market appears to discount the development pipeline significantly. Best-in-class insider ownership at 40% with C$40M invested ensures management-shareholder alignment [4].
The risks reflect Integra's junior miner status and development-stage challenges. Florida Canyon AISC of $3,310/oz is in the top quartile of the gold mining cost curve — if gold prices revert toward $2,500-3,000/oz, the mine becomes marginal to unprofitable [1]. DeLamar's $774M NPV at $3,000 gold depends on NEPA permitting (Record of Decision targeted Q3 2027) and requires substantial pre-production capital of $73-82M allocated for 2026 alone, with total development capital not yet fully quantified [1][4]. The $61M bought deal offering in Q1 2026 diluted existing shareholders, and additional equity raises will likely be needed to fund DeLamar construction. TTM profit margin of 3.75% is thin, and the stock has declined 54% from its 52-week high of $4.87 to $2.26, reflecting gold miner sentiment headwinds despite record gold prices [3].
Key Catalysts
  1. DeLamar project receiving a favorable Environmental Impact Statement and Record of Decision from the Bureau of Land Management, targeted for Q3 2027, unlocking construction and financing
  2. Continued gold price strength above $4,000/oz driving Florida Canyon profitability expansion — Q1 2026 operating margin of 40% (up from 27% in Q1 2025) demonstrates exceptional leverage to gold price
  3. 50,000-meter drill program results extending Florida Canyon mine life and potentially expanding DeLamar resources beyond the current 1.3 million oz gold reserve base
Key Risks
  1. Florida Canyon AISC of $3,310/oz places the mine in the top quartile of the global cost curve — a gold price correction to $3,000/oz would compress margins to near-zero and potentially make the operation unprofitable
  2. DeLamar permitting risk — NEPA process and Environmental Impact Statement could face delays, opposition, or unfavorable conditions that push the Record of Decision beyond Q3 2027 or impose costly requirements
  3. Additional equity dilution likely required to fund DeLamar pre-production capital and construction, with the Q1 2026 bought deal already diluting shareholders by issuing shares at depressed valuations
