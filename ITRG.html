Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Gold Mining ·NYSE American ·Canada
Integra Resources Corp. is a U.S.-focused gold mining company headquartered in Vancouver, Canada. Its principal operating asset is the Florida Canyon Mine in Pershing County, Nevada, which achieved its first full year of production in 2025, selling 70,927 ounces of gold at an average realized price of $3,411/oz and generating $243.9M in revenue. The company also holds the DeLamar Project in southwestern Idaho (past-producing, in reclamation/redevelopment) and the Nevada North Project (Wildcat and Mountain View deposits in northwestern Nevada). Q2 2026 delivered record throughput with 16,379 oz gold produced (+30% QoQ). Founded in 2017 by CEO George Salamis, who previously co-led the sale of Integra Gold to Eldorado Gold for C$590M.

Investment Thesis
Integra Resources is an emerging gold producer hitting its stride at a time of historically elevated gold prices. Florida Canyon Mine delivered 70,927 oz in its first full year of production (2025) at $3,411/oz average and is accelerating — Q2 2026 record throughput of 16,379 oz (+30% QoQ) with mine life extended to 8 years and reserves up 74% [1][2]. The company has $111M in cash, minimal debt (D/E 11.69%), and a $566M market cap, trading at just 2.0x trailing revenue with multiple development catalysts (DeLamar redevelopment, Nevada North Wildcat NEPA approval). Mine operating margins of 33-40% demonstrate the Florida Canyon deposit can generate meaningful cash flow at current gold prices [1]. If gold remains above $3,000/oz, the company's multi-asset platform provides organic production growth without dilution.
The bear case centers on thin net profitability, execution risk, and gold price dependency. Despite 33-40% mine operating margins, net profit margin is only 4.1% ($10.68M on $258M TTM revenue) due to corporate costs, depreciation, and development spending, producing an ROE of just ~2.4% and a trailing P/E of 70.5x [4]. All-in sustaining costs of $3,300-3,500/oz are dangerously close to current gold prices — a 10-15% gold price correction would push the mine into cash flow breakeven or worse [1]. The 704% YoY revenue growth is a one-time artifact of the exploration-to-production transition, not a sustainable growth rate — production guidance for 2026 implies essentially flat revenue sequentially. DeLamar and Nevada North require additional capital and multi-year development timelines with uncertain returns. Founder-CEO George Salamis' track record of building mining companies for sale (Integra Gold sold for C$590M) raises questions about long-term ownership commitment. The Inspire Impact Score of 0 (MIXED) provides no faith-based differentiation.

AI Resilience LOW RISK

AI Risk Protection (9/9): Gold mining is structurally immune to AI disruption. Extracting gold from ore deposits requires physical mining operations — drilling, blasting, hauling, heap leaching, processing — that AI cannot replicate, substitute, or displace [2]. Gold is a physical commodity whose value derives from millennia of monetary and cultural significance, not from services that technology can replace. Mine permitting, environmental compliance, and community relations require human judgment in heavily regulated processes. No analyst has identified AI as a risk factor for gold miners. Furthermore, AI may modestly increase demand for gold through economic uncertainty and monetary hedging behavior as AI-driven productivity gains reshape labor markets. AI can enhance mining efficiency (autonomous haul trucks, predictive maintenance, geological modeling) but these are enablers, not threats.
