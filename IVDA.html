Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Infrastructure ·NASDAQ ·United States
Iveda Solutions, Inc. provides AI-driven video surveillance solutions and Internet of Things (IoT) platforms for smart city and commercial applications. Core products include IvedaAI intelligent video search using deep learning and Vision Language Models (VLMs), IvedaEMS energy management system, smart sensors, gateways, and trackers. The company operates primarily through its Taiwan subsidiary (~85% of revenue). Founded in 2003 by CEO David Ly, Iveda has $5.28M in TTM revenue, a $3.8M market cap, and raised $1.6M through a February 2026 equity offering.

Investment Thesis
Iveda Solutions operates in the growing AI video surveillance and IoT market, with a proprietary IvedaAI platform integrating deep learning, LLMs, and Vision Language Models for intelligent video search [2]. Founder-CEO David Ly has led the company for 23 years, and the AI-enabled product suite (video analytics, energy management, smart sensors) addresses genuine smart city and commercial security needs. The February 2026 equity raise brought cash to $5.7M, providing a runway for operations, and gross profit improvement in H1 2026 (to $0.93M from $0.73M year-ago) suggests progress on higher-margin contract mix [1]. The Inspire Impact Score of 55 (ALIGNED) is attractive for faith-based investors.
The fundamental challenge is that Iveda has failed to achieve meaningful scale or profitability after 23 years of operation. H1 2026 revenue of $2.30M declined -24% YoY due to reduced equipment sales, with TTM revenue of only $5.28M and a -$3.2M net loss [1]. ROE of -87.80%, profit margin of -60.57%, and a $3.8M market cap classify this as a nano-cap with extreme execution risk [3][4]. Revenue is dangerously concentrated — approximately 85% comes from the Taiwan subsidiary and 63% from just four customers. The February 2026 dilutive equity offering at $0.35/share signals ongoing survival financing. Daily traded value of approximately $195K renders the stock effectively illiquid and untradeable for institutional investors [3]. Competition from well-capitalized AI surveillance players (Verkada, Avigilon, Hikvision) and cloud AI providers (AWS, Google, Microsoft) with orders of magnitude more resources makes sustainable competitive advantage unlikely at Iveda's scale.
