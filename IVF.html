Company Profile
Healthcare ·Medical Devices & Fertility Services ·NASDAQ ·United States
INVO Fertility is a healthcare services company dedicated to expanding access to assisted reproductive technology (ART) care through its proprietary INVOcell device for intravaginal culture (IVC) and a growing platform of owned and operated fertility clinics. The company reported Q2 2026 revenue of $2.18M (+17% YoY) and clinic-level adjusted EBITDA of $333K. However, going-concern risk persists with H1 2026 net loss of $4.65M from continuing operations. A 1-for-5 reverse stock split was executed in March 2026. The Birmingham clinic acquisition contributed a $2.5M non-cash gain in Q2 2026. Market cap approximately $2.7M.
