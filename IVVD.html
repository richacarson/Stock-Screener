Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Invivyd is a commercial-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on developing and commercializing monoclonal antibody therapies for infectious diseases. Its proprietary INVYMAB platform combines viral surveillance and predictive modeling with advanced antibody engineering. Lead product PEMGARDA is authorized for COVID-19 pre-exposure prophylaxis in immunocompromised patients, generating $14.3M in Q2 2026 revenue (+21% YoY). Pipeline includes VYD2311 (COVID-19 Phase 3), VBY329 (RSV), and VMS063 (measles). Marc Elia appointed CEO in September 2026. Market cap approximately $280M.

Investment Thesis
Invivyd offers a binary risk-reward profile centered on two Phase 3 data readouts expected in Q3 2026. PEMGARDA revenue of $14.3M in Q2 2026 (+21% YoY) validates commercial demand for monoclonal antibody viral protection in immunocompromised patients, with TTM revenue of approximately $55.9M [1]. VYD2311's DECLARATION pivotal trial and LIBERTY Phase 3 trial both approaching top-line data represent transformative catalysts that could support a BLA filing and unlock a significantly larger addressable market. The INVYMAB platform's expansion into RSV and measles demonstrates pipeline optionality beyond COVID-19 dependence [2]. Cash of $160.1M provides runway through data readouts. At ~$280M market cap, meaningful pipeline success is not fully priced in.
The bear case is formidable. Invivyd remains deeply unprofitable with TTM net loss of $77.6M against $55.9M in revenue, and the $160.1M cash position provides runway only through Q3 2027, creating near-term dilution risk regardless of trial outcomes [1]. The September 2026 CEO transition to Marc Elia — the third leadership change — introduces uncertainty at the most critical juncture of Phase 3 data readouts [3]. The COVID-19 antibody market has a history of rapid obsolescence as the virus mutates, with prior competitors (Regeneron's REGEN-COV, Lilly's bamlanivimab) seeing products rendered ineffective by viral evolution. At $1.38 per share, the stock reflects micro-cap risk with high volatility, limited institutional ownership, and a PEG/forward PE that cannot be calculated due to negative earnings.
