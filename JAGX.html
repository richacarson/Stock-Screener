Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Jaguar Health is a commercial-stage pharmaceutical company focused on developing and commercializing plant-based medicines derived from the crofelemer compound. Lead product Mytesi is the only FDA-approved oral plant-based prescription medicine, indicated for HIV-associated diarrhea. In January 2026, the company licensed U.S. rights for Mytesi and Canalevia-CA1 to Woodward Specialty/Future Pak and is now pursuing rare disease indications (intestinal failure) for crofelemer. Three reverse stock splits since 2024 (1:60, 1:25, 1:35) and negative equity underscore severe financial distress. Market cap approximately $4M with stock down 98.6% over 52 weeks.

Investment Thesis
Jaguar Health's sole investment merit lies in its unique asset: crofelemer is the only FDA-approved oral plant-based prescription medicine, with an uncontested rare disease opportunity in intestinal failure where no approved therapies exist. Investigator-sponsored MVID data showing up to 37% reduction in parenteral support is clinically meaningful in this ultrarare population [3]. The January 2026 licensing agreement with Woodward Specialty eliminates U.S. commercialization burn while generating license revenue ($19.1M recognized in Q1 2026), allowing the company to focus scarce resources on the higher-value rare disease pipeline [1]. If crofelemer achieves FDA approval for IF, orphan drug exclusivity could provide pricing power disproportionate to the company's current $4M market cap.
