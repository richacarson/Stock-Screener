Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Jaguar Health is a commercial-stage pharmaceutical company focused on developing and commercializing plant-based medicines derived from the crofelemer compound. Lead product Mytesi is the only FDA-approved treatment for HIV-associated diarrhea. The company licensed U.S. rights for Mytesi and Canalevia-CA1 to Future Pak and is pursuing rare disease indications (intestinal failure) for crofelemer. Three reverse stock splits since 2024 (1:60, 1:25, 1:35) and negative equity underscore severe financial distress.
