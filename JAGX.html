Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Jaguar Health is a commercial-stage pharmaceutical company focused on plant-based, sustainably sourced medicines derived from the crofelemer compound harvested from the Amazon rainforest. Lead product Mytesi is FDA-approved for HIV-associated diarrhea; in early 2026 the company licensed U.S. commercial rights to Future Pak and is now concentrating on rare intestinal-failure indications (MVID and short bowel syndrome) through its Napo Pharmaceuticals subsidiary. The company has executed repeated reverse stock splits, most recently 1-for-15 effective September 17, 2026, to maintain its Nasdaq listing, and has a market capitalization of roughly $4M.

Investment Thesis
Jaguar's only real asset is crofelemer, an FDA-approved botanical drug now being developed for ultrarare intestinal-failure indications (MVID, SBS) where approved options are scarce [1]. The Future Pak license brought in $19.1M of license revenue in Q1 2026 and shifts U.S. commercialization costs off Jaguar's books [3].
Financial distress dominates the investment case. Q2 2026 revenue fell 59%, FY2025 net loss was $53.4M on $11.5M revenue, D/E is 253%, and the September 2026 1-for-15 reverse split was done expressly to keep the Nasdaq listing [1][2][5]. A pipeline success would need to arrive before the next financing round dilutes shareholders further.
Key Catalysts
  1. Regulatory progress for crofelemer powder in MVID or SBS intestinal failure, which could unlock orphan-drug value [1]
  2. Additional license or milestone payments from the Future Pak agreement following the $19.1M recognized in Q1 2026 [3]
  3. Restored compliance with Nasdaq listing standards after the September 17, 2026 reverse split [1]
Key Risks
  1. Ongoing dilution and potential Nasdaq delisting; the company has relied on serial reverse splits to stay listed [1]
  2. Product revenue is collapsing: Q2 2026 total revenue was ~$1.2M, down 59% YoY [2]
  3. Trading liquidity is thin; recent daily traded value of ~$1.1M sits near IOWN's $1M liquidity floor [7]
