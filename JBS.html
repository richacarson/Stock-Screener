Company Profile
Consumer Defensive ·Farm Products ·NYSE ·Brazil
JBS N.V. is the world's largest protein company by revenue, processing beef, poultry, pork, and prepared foods across five continents. Key subsidiaries include JBS USA Beef, JBS USA Pork, Pilgrim's Pride Corporation (poultry), JBS Australia, and Seara (Brazilian branded foods). Redomiciled to the Netherlands and listed on NYSE in 2024-2025. Q1 2026 consolidated revenue was $21.6B (+10.66% YoY). Q2 2026 reported $23.9B in sales with record performance at JBS Beef North America and JBS Brazil, though net loss attributable to JBS was $102M. CEO Wesley Batista Filho succeeded Gilberto Tomazoni as global CEO.
283,000 Employees ·jbsfoodsgroup.com

Investment Thesis
JBS is the world's largest protein company with TTM revenue of $88.3B, a diversified multi-protein, multi-geography platform (beef, poultry, pork, prepared foods across five continents) that provides resilience through commodity cycles [1]. Q1 2026 revenue of $21.6B grew 10.66% YoY, and Q2 2026 delivered record results at JBS Beef North America and JBS Brazil with 18% Seara sales growth. At a forward P/E of 8.45, JBS trades at a significant discount to US food peers like Tyson Foods (15-20x forward). Infrastructure scores 8/10 driven by strong ROE (22.1%), attractive valuation, and constructive dividend metrics (payout ratio 32%, 10+ consecutive years paid). The NYSE listing improves access to US institutional capital and should gradually narrow the governance discount [4].
The bear case is dominated by governance and ESG risk that suppresses the valuation multiple. The Batista family founders paid $256M in US DOJ/SEC fines for corruption and bribery [3]. Global Witness linked JBS to over 80,000 football fields of Amazon deforestation, and 22 institutional investors have divested from JBS since 2021 citing biodiversity and governance concerns [3]. The Q2 2026 net loss of $102M (vs $528M profit year-ago) and IFRS adjusted EBITDA decline of 18% signal emerging margin pressure across the protein complex [1]. D/E of 268% reflects an aggressively leveraged balance sheet with minimal safety margin during commodity downturns. Profit margin of 2.45% is inherently thin and vulnerable to input cost volatility (cattle/feed prices), labor inflation, and potential tariff disruption. The new CEO Wesley Batista Filho — son of convicted co-founder Wesley Batista — raises fresh governance questions rather than resolving legacy concerns [4]. The Inspire Impact Score of 0 (MIXED) and ongoing ESG fund exclusions limit the investable universe for JBS shares.

AI Resilience LOW RISK

AI Risk Protection (8/9 — IMMUNE): JBS's core business — slaughtering, processing, and distributing physical animal protein — is structurally immune to AI disruption. Meat processing requires massive physical infrastructure (processing plants, cold chain logistics, global distribution), regulatory compliance (USDA/FDA inspection), and workforce management across 283,000 employees handling perishable biological materials [1][2]. AI cannot substitute for any of these physical operations. AI increases demand for food processing automation as the industry faces chronic labor shortages, making AI an enabler rather than a disruptor. The only modest exposure is alternative proteins — if AI-designed plant-based or cell-cultured proteins achieve cost parity, they could erode demand for traditional animal protein, but this remains a distant and uncertain threat.
