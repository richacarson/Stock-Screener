Company Profile
Industrials ·Specialty Industrial Machinery ·NASDAQ ·Singapore
JE Cleantech Holdings Limited designs, develops, manufactures, and sells cleaning systems for industrial end-use applications in Singapore, Malaysia, and internationally. Products include aqueous washing systems, plating and cleaning systems, train cleaning systems, and filtration units, as well as precision cleaning systems for semiconductor manufacturing. The company also operates centralized dishwashing and cleaning services for food and beverage establishments. Founded in 1999, headquartered in Singapore.
