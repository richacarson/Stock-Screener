Company Profile
Consumer Defensive ·Education & Training Services ·NasdaqCM ·China
JIADE Limited is a Chengdu, China-based company incorporated in 2023 that provides education supporting services to adult education institutions through software platforms and auxiliary solutions. Services include exam administration, teaching support, pre-enrollment guidance, entrance exam training, and offline tutoring. The company completed a 1-for-25 reverse stock split in March 2026 to regain Nasdaq compliance and has entered a strategic MOU with South Korea's Chinalink Education Group for AI-powered cross-border education services.

Investment Thesis
JIADE Limited presents a speculative micro-cap opportunity in Chinese adult education support services with exceptional revenue growth of 66.2% YoY to RMB 25.70 million ($3.67M) in fiscal 2025 [1]. The strategic cooperation MOU with South Korea's Chinalink Education Group opens a potential pathway into AI-empowered and cross-border education services, including AI teacher systems and multilingual curriculum development [2]. The balance sheet carries minimal leverage at 14.2% D/E, and cash grew 400% to RMB 19.77M with working capital tripling to RMB 66.16M, providing near-term liquidity for operations and investment [1].
The risks overwhelmingly dominate the thesis. JIADE is deeply unprofitable with a -41.23% profit margin and -10.96% ROE, burning cash from operations while funding growth through dilutive share offerings at $2.40/share [1][3]. The 1-for-25 reverse stock split in March 2026 to regain Nasdaq minimum bid compliance is a severe warning sign -- companies that require 25:1 reverse splits typically face continued downward pressure. Trading was halted in June 2026 for additional information requested by Nasdaq, raising questions about disclosure adequacy and potential delisting risk [4]. The company operates in China's heavily regulated education sector, where government crackdowns (e.g., the 2021 double-reduction policy) have destroyed billions in market value for larger peers. Revenue of $3.67M is negligible, the Chinalink MOU is aspirational with no shipped products, and the company's core tutoring and test prep services are directly exposed to AI substitution from well-funded competitors. Insider alignment is questionable given dilutive capital raises and the absence of insider buying.
Key Catalysts
  1. Chinalink Education Group partnership progresses from MOU to shipped AI-powered education products, potentially opening South Korea cross-border market and diversifying revenue beyond domestic Chinese adult education
  2. Achieved profitability or meaningful reduction in net losses as revenue scales beyond the current $3.67M base, with acquisitions and organic growth driving operating leverage
  3. Successful resolution of Nasdaq trading halt with satisfactory information provided, followed by sustained compliance with minimum bid price and listing requirements
Key Risks
  1. Nasdaq delisting risk remains acute -- trading was halted in June 2026 for additional information, following a 1-for-25 reverse stock split executed specifically to regain minimum bid compliance after falling below $1.00 for 30 consecutive days
  2. Core education support services (tutoring, exam prep, teaching support) face direct AI substitution risk from well-funded competitors deploying AI tutoring platforms at scale, while JIADE has no AI products shipping
  3. Chinese regulatory risk is severe -- the government's 2021 double-reduction crackdown on education companies destroyed hundreds of billions in market value, and JIADE's adult education niche could face similar policy intervention at any time
