Company Profile
Industrials ·Building Products & Equipment ·NYSE ·United States
JELD-WEN Holding, Inc. is one of the world's largest manufacturers of doors and windows, operating approximately 60 manufacturing facilities across North America, Europe, and Australia. The company serves residential new construction, repair and remodel, and commercial markets through brands including JELD-WEN, LaCantina, VPI Quality Windows, and Swedoor. Facing significant financial distress with a market cap of $144M against $3.16B TTM revenue, deep operating losses, and D/E of 1,481%. Founded in 1960, headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina.

Investment Thesis
JELD-WEN's Q1 2026 net loss narrowed 60% YoY to $76.8M, and management raised revenue guidance to $3.05-$3.2B from $2.95-$3.1B, reflecting pricing actions and a modest FX tailwind of approximately $50M [1]. At a market cap of just $144M against $3.16B TTM revenue, the stock is priced for severe distress — any stabilization in margins toward the guided $100-$150M adjusted EBITDA would represent a meaningful recovery from the equity's perspective. The housing repair and remodel market should eventually cyclically recover, and JELD-WEN's 60-factory global footprint provides operating leverage when volumes return.

The -16.09% profit margin and -$509M TTM net loss indicate the business cannot cover its fixed costs at current volumes. Revenue has declined approximately 5.9% annually over five years with no catalyst for reversal — management guides for continued 3-6% core revenue decline in 2026 [1]. Adjusted EBITDA guidance of $100-$150M is unchanged despite the revenue guidance increase, suggesting cost savings are being offset by volume pressure. Glassdoor reviews revealing only 26% employee recommendation and 2.4/5 culture rating indicate a disengaged workforce that will struggle to execute any turnaround [2]. The stock at $1.41 faces potential delisting risk and further equity dilution.
