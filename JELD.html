Company Profile
Industrials ·Building Products & Equipment ·NYSE ·United States
JELD-WEN Holding is one of the world's largest manufacturers of doors and windows, operating approximately 60 manufacturing facilities across North America, Europe, and Australia. Serves residential new construction, repair and remodel, and commercial markets through brands including JELD-WEN, LaCantina, VPI Quality Windows, and Swedoor. Under CEO Bill Christensen, the company is in a turnaround effort — Q2 2026 marked the first year-over-year adjusted EBITDA growth in ten quarters (+8.3% to $42.3M). Revenue guidance raised to $3.1-3.2B with EBITDA of $120-150M. Market cap ~$160M, headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina.

Investment Thesis
JELD-WEN's Q2 2026 delivered the first year-over-year adjusted EBITDA growth in ten quarters (+8.3% to $42.3M), and management raised the low end of full-year guidance to revenue of $3.1-3.2B (from $3.05B) and EBITDA of $120-150M (from $100M), signaling the turnaround is gaining traction [1]. At a market cap of $160M against $3.15B TTM revenue and guided EBITDA of $120-150M, the stock prices in extreme distress — any margin normalization toward historical levels would represent massive equity upside. The stock has recovered 30% from $1.41 to $1.84 since the prior screen. The -16.43% profit margin and -228.66% ROE confirm the business cannot cover its fixed costs at current volumes. Revenue has contracted approximately 30% from $4.5B in 2022 to $3.15B, with management still guiding for 2-5% core revenue decline in 2026 [1]. Headcount reduction from 18,000 to 13,900 demonstrates the severity of restructuring, and the company's organizational stability during this period is questionable. A macroeconomic slowdown or housing market weakness would compound the volume pressure, potentially pushing the company toward restructuring. The stock at $1.84 faces potential delisting risk if it cannot sustain above $1.00 compliance thresholds.
