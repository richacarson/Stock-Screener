707 Cayman Holdings Limited sells and distributes fashion apparel products including denim, athleisure, sweater, leather, outerwear, knitwear, and pet lines to mid-sized brand owners and apparel companies. The company reported FY2025 revenue of approximately HK$106.9M ($13.7M USD) with an operating loss of HK$40.1M and a -124.8% profit margin [2]. Founded in 2021 and headquartered in San Po Kong, Hong Kong, 707 Cayman IPO'd on NASDAQ in 2026 and has a micro-cap market capitalization of approximately $2.4M following a July 2026 share consolidation [3].
