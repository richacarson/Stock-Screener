707 Cayman Holdings is a Hong Kong-based holding company that sells and distributes fashion apparel products including denim, athleisure, knitwear, leather outerwear, and pet lines in Hong Kong, Western Europe, North America, and the Middle East. The company IPO'd on Nasdaq in June 2025 at a pre-split equivalent of $80.00 per share, and operates as a subsidiary of JME International Holdings Limited. It executed a 1:12 reverse stock split on July 14, 2026, following a prior 20-for-1 reverse split.
