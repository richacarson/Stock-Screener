Company Profile
Healthcare ·Drug Manufacturers - General ·NYQ ·United States
Johnson & Johnson is a global healthcare company operating through two segments: Innovative Medicine (pharmaceuticals) and MedTech (medical devices and surgical technology). Following the 2023 Kenvue spinoff of its consumer health division, JNJ is focused on prescription pharmaceuticals including blockbuster oncology drug DARZALEX, immunology franchise TREMFYA, and surgical robotics platform OTTAVA [1][2]. The company reported FY2025 revenue of $94.2B with a 21.5% profit margin and 25.7% ROE, and targets $100B+ revenue in FY2026 with Q2 2026 revenue of $25.3B (+6.8% YoY) [1][2]. Founded in 1886 and headquartered in New Brunswick, New Jersey, JNJ is a Dividend King with 63 consecutive years of dividend increases.

Investment Thesis
Johnson & Johnson at $256.48 with a $618B market cap is a premier global healthcare franchise delivering accelerating revenue growth — Q1 2026 revenue rose 9.9% to $24.1B and Q2 reached $25.3B (+6.8%), with management raising FY2026 guidance to $100.8-101.4B in revenue and $11.60-11.75 in adjusted EPS [1][2]. The Kenvue spinoff has created a focused Innovative Medicine + MedTech company with 21.5% profit margins, 25.7% ROE, and a fortress balance sheet (D/E 57.7%) generating $27.6B annual operating cash flow. DARZALEX is growing 17.3% in oncology, the $14B R&D pipeline includes 100+ programs with multiple near-term catalysts (NIPOCALIMAB, OTTAVA robotic surgery), and the company's Dividend King status with 63 consecutive years of increases provides reliable income with a 2.09% yield [1][2]. JNJ's near-total immunity to AI disruption and the Credo-driven culture provide rare defensive characteristics for a company also growing revenue at mid-to-high single digits.
The bear case centers on valuation, litigation, and near-term headwinds. At 21.2x forward earnings and a PEG of 2.71, JNJ trades at a premium that leaves limited margin of safety — the stock has gained approximately 50% over the past year, pricing in much of the post-Kenvue improvement [1][2]. The talc litigation remains a significant overhang with 38,000+ plaintiffs and cumulative claims exceeding $50B; the proposed $9B settlement over 25 years was rejected in bankruptcy court, and ongoing litigation could produce material adverse judgments [4]. Biosimilar erosion of STELARA and REMICADE continues to pressure the immunology franchise, and MedTech faces approximately $500M in tariff headwinds for FY2026. The payout ratio of 60.7% and dividend growth CAGR of 4.8% (below the 6% target for 2x-inflation growth) suggest dividend growth is decelerating as the company balances shareholder returns with R&D investment and litigation reserves. EPS growth of -0.9% in the latest period reflects the drag from biosimilar competition and one-time items offsetting top-line gains [2][4].

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
9
AI Risk Protection (8/9): JNJ's core business is structurally immune to AI disruption. Drug manufacturing requires physical production facilities, GMP compliance, and FDA-regulated processes that AI cannot substitute [1]. Medical devices (surgical instruments, orthopedic implants, contact lenses) are physical products requiring precision manufacturing and regulatory clearance. Clinical trials, the backbone of pharmaceutical innovation, require human subjects testing under FDA oversight that cannot be replaced by AI simulation. AI actually INCREASES demand for JNJ's MedTech products — robotic surgery (OTTAVA) is an AI-enhanced offering that strengthens the business. FDA regulatory barriers create massive switching costs and competitive moats that AI cannot circumvent [1][2]. The only exposure is indirect: AI could accelerate competitors' drug discovery, potentially reducing JNJ's first-mover advantage in some therapeutic areas. AI Opportunity (1/1): JNJ is actively deploying AI across drug discovery, clinical trial optimization, supply chain management, and surgical robotics [3]. The OTTAVA robotic surgery platform integrates AI for surgical guidance, and the company's AI Council coordinates enterprise-wide AI deployment. JNJ's scale and data assets from millions of patient interactions create a significant AI opportunity in precision medicine [2][3].
