Company Profile
Industrials ·Staffing & Employment Services ·NYSE American ·United States
GEE Group Inc. is a provider of specialized staffing solutions including professional contract staffing and direct hire placement services, primarily in IT, engineering, healthcare, and finance/accounting sectors. Founded in 1962 and headquartered in Jacksonville, Florida, the company operates through multiple brands. GEE Group reported Q3 FY2026 (ended June 30, 2026) revenue of $20.8M (-15% YoY) with year-to-date revenue of $60.8M (-17%), as contract staffing revenues declined 20-21% partly due to AI replacing certain contract staffing jobs. Direct hire placement revenue grew 16% YoY to $3.8M in Q3. The company faces activist pressure from Star Equity Holdings (5.4% stake) urging a strategic review. Market cap approximately $25M.

CEO Dewan's track record of 100+ staffing acquisitions suggests the capability for roll-up strategy if market conditions stabilize [3]. AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience HIGH RISK
2
AI Risk Protection (2/9): The staffing and recruitment industry is among the most vulnerable to AI disruption, and GEE Group's own management has acknowledged this on earnings calls — 'certain contract staffing jobs being replaced by artificial intelligence' directly contributed to the 20-21% decline in contract staffing revenues [1][2]. AI tools are already automating core staffing functions: candidate sourcing (LinkedIn Recruiter AI, HireVue), resume screening (AI matching algorithms), initial candidate interviews (AI chatbots), and skills assessment. GEE Group's contract staffing segment — which represents ~84% of revenue — is particularly exposed because AI reduces the need for human intermediaries in filling temporary positions. The company's pivot toward direct hire placements and high-end IT staffing for AI roles is a defensive response, but these segments are also facing pressure from AI-powered recruiting platforms that can match senior candidates without staffing intermediaries. The business model of charging markups on temporary labor is structurally threatened by AI platforms that connect employers directly with candidates. AI Opportunity (0/1): GEE Group does not generate any quantifiable revenue from AI products or services. Management has discussed incorporating AI into recruiting tools but has not reported specific AI-driven revenue contributions.
