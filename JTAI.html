Company Profile
Technology ·Software / Data Center Infrastructure ·NASDAQ ·United States
Jet.AI Inc. is a micro-cap company pivoting from AI-powered private aviation software to AI data center infrastructure. Founded in 2018 by Mike Winston and currently led by CEO George Murnane, the company developed CharterGPT (AI-powered natural language jet booking), Reroute AI (aircraft repositioning optimization), and DynoFlight (emissions tracking API). In July 2026, stockholders approved the divestiture of the aviation business to flyExclusive, pivoting the company toward a 50MW data center project. JTAI has undergone two massive reverse stock splits — 1:225 in November 2024 and 1:200 in April 2026 — to maintain NASDAQ listing compliance. With a market cap of $2.64M, 1.42M shares outstanding, and an Altman Z-score of -0.58 indicating elevated bankruptcy risk, the company is in severe financial distress. Q1 2026 EPS of -$6.68 missed estimates by 234%. The 52-week stock price decline of 99.7% reflects the market's assessment of the company's viability.
