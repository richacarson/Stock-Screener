Jet.AI Inc. is a micro-cap company transitioning from a legacy private aviation platform to AI data center infrastructure development, with shovel-ready and JV-backed projects across Manitoba, the Maritimes, the Midwest, and Nevada (Moapa). The company has completed two reverse stock splits since late 2024 to maintain Nasdaq compliance and, in July 2026, announced a letter of intent for a new merger and related spin-off of its data center business into a separate private operating company valued at roughly $320 million enterprise value.

Investment Thesis
Jet.AI's bull case rests entirely on optionality — the company holds $10.61M in cash with no debt, has secured power and permitting milestones for its Convergence Compute joint venture data center sites (Manitoba, Maritimes, Midwest), and in July 2026 announced a letter of intent for a merger that would value the combined entity at roughly $320M enterprise value, with approximately $20M allocated to existing Jet.AI shareholders [1][2]. If the merger closes and any data center project reaches operational status, current shareholders could see meaningful value creation relative to the company's ~$2.4M market cap.
The bear case is overwhelming. TTM revenue of $7.38M is down 45.9% YoY, operating losses reached -$9.97M, and the stock has fallen 99.73% over the past year, forcing two reverse stock splits (1-for-225 in 2024, 1-for-200 in April 2026) just to maintain Nasdaq compliance [4][5]. Average daily dollar volume of roughly $552,590 falls below the $1M liquidity floor, triggering an automatic zero on the Infrastructure sub-score regardless of any other metric. The company has no operational data centers, no data center revenue, and an interim executive team (Executive Chairman as interim CEO, interim CFO) reshuffling amid the pivot [2]. The July 2026 LOI to merge and spin off the very data center business Jet.AI has spent over a year assembling introduces further uncertainty about what, if anything, current shareholders will retain. This is a speculative, highly illiquid micro-cap unsuitable for IOWN allocation.
