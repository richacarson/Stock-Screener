Company Profile
Consumer Discretionary ·New Energy Vehicles ·NASDAQ ·China
Jiuzi Holdings, Inc. is a China-based micro-cap company originally engaged in franchising and operating new energy vehicle (NEV) retail stores under the 'Jiuzi' brand, selling battery-operated electric vehicles and plug-in electric vehicles in third- and fourth-tier Chinese cities. The company has pivoted toward cryptocurrency treasury management (deploying up to $1 billion into select cryptocurrencies) and AI technology partnerships. Q2 2026 revenue surged to $3.6M (+630% YoY) but operating losses persisted at $5.0M. Founded in 2019, incorporated in Cayman Islands, headquartered in Hangzhou, China. Market cap approximately $2.7M with accumulated deficit of $88M.
