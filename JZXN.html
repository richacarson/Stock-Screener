Company Profile
Consumer Cyclical ·Auto & Truck Dealerships ·NASDAQ ·China
Jiuzi Holdings, Inc. is a Cayman Islands-incorporated micro-cap headquartered in Hangzhou, China, originally engaged in franchising and operating 'Jiuzi'-branded new energy vehicle (NEV) retail stores in lower-tier Chinese cities. Since 2025 it has pivoted toward a board-authorized crypto-asset treasury policy of up to $1 billion and an AI intelligent imaging and data platform, under CEO and Chairman Hongye Zhang (appointed May 2026). The company remains deeply loss-making with minimal cash, and FY2025 (ended October 31, 2025) revenue was $2.88M.
33 Employees ·www.jiuziholdings.com
