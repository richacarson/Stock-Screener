Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
KALA BIO was a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on rare and severe eye diseases using its mesenchymal stem cell secretome (MSC-S) platform. The company's lead candidate KPI-012 failed its Phase 2b CHASE trial for persistent corneal epithelial defect (PCED) in September 2025, and KALA has since ceased clinical development of KPI-012 and its MSC-S platform. The company has pivoted to an AI platform called Researgency.ai. A 1-for-50 reverse stock split was executed in May 2026. CEO Mark Iwicki resigned in February 2025, replaced by interim CEO Todd Bazemore and subsequently by David E. Lazar as permanent CEO. KALA secured a $6M investment alongside the CEO transition. Market cap approximately $14M.
