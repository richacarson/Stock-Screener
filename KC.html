Company Profile
Technology ·Cloud Computing ·NASDAQ ·China
Kingsoft Cloud Holdings provides cloud computing services in China, including public cloud (compute, storage, delivery), enterprise cloud, and AI cloud services. A subsidiary of Kingsoft Corporation with strategic backing from the Xiaomi/Lei Jun ecosystem, the company serves gaming, video, public services, healthcare, and financial services industries. AI cloud has become the primary revenue driver, accounting for 56% of public cloud revenue in Q2 2026. Q2 2026 delivered record quarterly revenue of RMB 3,072M (+31% YoY) and the company's first operating profit (4% adjusted operating margin). Full-year CapEx base case of RMB 15B for AI infrastructure.
14,394 Employees ·www.ksyun.com

Investment Thesis
Kingsoft Cloud is executing a rapid AI cloud transformation, with Q2 2026 delivering record revenue of RMB 3,072M (+31% YoY), AI cloud billings surging 82% YoY to 56% of public cloud revenue, and the company's first-ever operating profit at 4% adjusted operating margin [1]. H1 2026 revenue of RMB 5.78B grew 33.7% YoY while losses narrowed 43.4%, demonstrating the profitability inflection that distinguishes KC from typical growth-stage cloud companies. The captive Xiaomi/Kingsoft ecosystem provides a built-in customer base that reduces go-to-market friction, and the RMB 15B CapEx base case for AI infrastructure signals management confidence in sustained demand [1]. At ~$3.0B market cap with 31% growth and improving margins, the valuation is modest for a Chinese AI infrastructure play at the profitability inflection point.
The bear case centers on competition, leverage, and China risk. Kingsoft Cloud remains a mid-tier player competing against Alibaba Cloud, Tencent Cloud, and Huawei Cloud — each with 10-50x greater scale, GPU procurement capacity, and financial resources — creating persistent pricing pressure [1]. D/E of 123.9% with RMB 11.0B debt and negative free cash flow of -RMB 1.8B reflect the capital-intensive reality of AI infrastructure buildout; the RMB 15B CapEx commitment will further strain the balance sheet [4]. TTM profit margin remains negative at -5.46% and ROE at -7.61%, meaning the Q2 operating profit has not yet demonstrated sustainability across a full year [4]. As a Chinese ADR on NASDAQ, KC faces VIE structure risk, potential HFCAA delisting concerns, and US semiconductor export controls that could constrain GPU access critical for AI cloud expansion.
