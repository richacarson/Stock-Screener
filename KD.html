Company Profile
Technology ·Information Technology Services ·NYSE ·United States
Kyndryl Holdings operates as the world's largest IT infrastructure services provider, spun off from IBM in November 2021. The company offers cloud services, core enterprise services, application/data/AI services, digital workplace services, security and resiliency services, and network/edge services across financial, healthcare, public sector, and technology industries. FY2026 (ended March 2026) revenue was approximately $15.1B with a 0.6% profit margin. Headquartered in New York, KD posted Q4 FY2026 revenue flat on a reported basis with improving profitability and $406M in free cash flow.

Investment Thesis
Kyndryl at $13.21 with a $2.9B market cap is a deep-value turnaround play trading at just 5.3x forward earnings as the world's largest IT infrastructure services provider pivots from declining IBM legacy contracts toward higher-growth cloud, AI, and consulting services. Hyperscaler-related revenue reached $1.9B in FY2026 growing 59% YoY, Kyndryl Consult delivered its third consecutive year of double-digit growth, and free cash flow reached $406M as profitability improvements accelerated [1]. FY2027 guidance targets $600-700M in adjusted pretax income, representing significant margin expansion from the current 0.6% profit margin. The Kyndryl Bridge AI platform provides a vehicle for modernizing client infrastructure and extracting higher-value recurring revenue from the 4,000+ enterprise client base.
The bear case is severe — Kyndryl is a declining-revenue business burdened by $4.9B in debt (422% D/E) with razor-thin margins and no clear path to sustained organic growth. FY2027 revenue guidance of flat-to-down-2% in constant currency means the top line continues shrinking even after four years of managed decline from $17B to $15.1B, and the 3.5-point IBM relationship headwind persists [1]. The IT infrastructure services model faces structural AI disruption as AIOps, automated remediation, and cloud-native self-healing capabilities reduce demand for human-managed services — Kyndryl's core value proposition. With 72,000 employees and a services-heavy cost structure, margin expansion depends on workforce reduction that conflicts with client service quality. The stock has a market cap of just $2.9B against $15.1B in revenue, reflecting the market's deep skepticism about the turnaround thesis, and no dividend provides zero downside income protection [1][3].
Key Catalysts
  1. Hyperscaler-related revenue growing 59% YoY to $1.9B positions Kyndryl as a beneficiary of enterprise cloud migration and AI infrastructure buildout, with a large addressable market in the 4,000+ enterprise client base [1]

  3. Kyndryl Bridge AI platform provides a differentiated infrastructure management offering that could improve client retention and enable premium pricing as enterprises modernize their IT environments [1]
Key Risks

  2. D/E of 422% and $4.9B in total debt against $2.9B market cap creates significant financial risk if the profitability turnaround stalls, with limited balance sheet flexibility to invest in growth or weather a downturn [1]
  3. Structural AI disruption risk to managed IT infrastructure services as AIOps, automated remediation, and cloud-native self-healing capabilities reduce enterprise demand for human-managed infrastructure, threatening Kyndryl's labor-intensive business model [1][2]

AI Risk Protection (4/9): Kyndryl's core business — managed IT infrastructure services including monitoring, maintenance, and incident response — faces real AI substitution risk as AIOps platforms, automated remediation tools, and self-healing infrastructure increasingly reduce the need for human-managed services [1][2].
