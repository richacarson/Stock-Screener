Company Profile
Technology ·Information Technology Services ·NYSE ·United States
Kyndryl Holdings, Inc. is the world's largest IT infrastructure services provider, spun off from IBM in November 2021. The company offers cloud, core enterprise, application/data/AI, digital workplace, security and resiliency, and network services across more than 60 countries under CEO Martin Schroeter. Fiscal Q1 2027 (quarter ended June 2026) revenue was roughly flat to slightly down YoY at $3.6-3.8B, with $14.2B in trailing-12-month signings and hyperscaler-related revenue up 48% to over $530M. The company is still working through a governance crisis that began in February 2026, when it disclosed material weaknesses in internal controls, an SEC inquiry, and the abrupt departures of its CFO, General Counsel, and Comptroller, triggering a 55% single-day stock decline and a securities fraud class action.

Investment Thesis
Kyndryl trades at a Forward P/E of just 5.23x, a valuation that reflects deep market skepticism following the February 2026 governance crisis, and the operational story underneath has real positives: $14.2B in trailing-12-month signings, hyperscaler-related revenue up 48% to over $530M, Kyndryl Consult up 14%, and U.S. revenue up 5% all point to a business successfully repositioning around AI-native managed services and hyperscaler partnerships [1]. Kyndryl Bridge's AI-driven change-risk prediction (60-90% reduction in change failure rates, 180M automated actions/month) is a genuinely differentiated technical capability, and the company's employee-facing culture credentials (#1 Most Loved Workplace 2026, EcoVadis gold ESG rating) are unusually strong for the sector [2][3]. If the governance remediation holds and revenue stabilizes, the current valuation offers meaningful re-rating potential.
The bear case is dominated by the unresolved governance crisis. In February 2026, Kyndryl disclosed material weaknesses in internal controls spanning fiscal year 2025 and the first half of fiscal 2026, explicitly citing "information and communication" and "tone at the top" issues, alongside an SEC Division of Enforcement inquiry into its adjusted free cash flow disclosures — triggering a 55% single-day stock decline, the abrupt simultaneous departure of the CFO, General Counsel, and Comptroller, and a securities fraud class action alleging 18 months of misleading statements [3]. D/E of 422.45% is well above the 300% floor, scoring zero in the Infrastructure calculation, and profit margin of just 0.58% (further compressed by $152M in workforce rebalancing charges) shows a business still near breakeven [1][4]. Revenue declined 3.3% YoY on the headline figure, and the core managed-infrastructure business faces a structural tension: the same AI-driven automation Kyndryl is building into its own platform reduces the billable technician-labor hours its traditional pricing model depends on. Until permanent CFO and General Counsel appointments are finalized and the SEC inquiry resolves, the stock carries meaningful binary governance risk on top of ordinary IT-services execution risk.

  2. D/E of 422.45% leaves Kyndryl with limited balance-sheet flexibility to absorb further governance-related costs, legal settlements, or a prolonged revenue downturn [4]
