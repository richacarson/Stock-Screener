Company Profile
Industrials ·Aerospace & Defense ·NASDAQ ·United States
Nauticus Robotics develops electric autonomous robotic solutions for subsea applications, including the Aquanaut autonomous underwater vehicle, the Olympic Arm all-electric manipulator, and the ToolKITT autonomy software platform for ocean sensing, manipulation, and survey. The company is a micro-cap, pre-scale business headquartered in Webster, Texas, pivoting toward software commercialization, defense/government contracts, and international expansion amid severe revenue headwinds in its core offshore energy market.
