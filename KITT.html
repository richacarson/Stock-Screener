Company Profile
Industrials ·Aerospace & Defense ·NASDAQ ·United States
Nauticus Robotics develops electric autonomous robotic solutions for subsea applications, including the Aquanaut autonomous underwater vehicle, Olympic Arm all-electric manipulator, and ToolKITT autonomy software platform. The company serves ocean sensing, subsea inspection, intervention, and defense markets with a Leidos strategic alliance. Founded in 2014 and headquartered in Webster, Texas, Nauticus posted H1 2026 revenue of $1.05M (down 53% YoY) with a $20.4M net loss and $2.0M in cash as of June 30, 2026, with substantial doubt about going-concern status.
