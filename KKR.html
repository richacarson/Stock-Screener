Investment Thesis
KKR at $86 with a $77B market cap is one of the world's premier alternative asset managers executing on all cylinders — Q2 2026 adjusted net income reached $1.49B ($1.63/share, +40% YoY) driven by strong realizations of $1.29B and fee-related earnings of $1.21B [1]. AUM reached $796B (+16% YoY) with $28B in new capital raised in Q1 alone, and the Global Atlantic insurance platform provides a permanent, low-cost capital base that differentiates KKR from peers. At an estimated 13.2x forward adjusted earnings, KKR trades at a meaningful discount to Blackstone (~20x) despite comparable growth dynamics, and the firm's infrastructure investing leadership positions it to capture the $5T+ global infrastructure investment opportunity. The 4-for-1 stock split and S&P 500 inclusion have broadened the shareholder base.
The bear case centers on cyclicality and execution complexity. KKR's earnings are sensitive to realization activity — Q2's strong results were driven by $1.29B in asset sales that may not be sustainable in a slower M&A environment [1]. The Global Atlantic integration adds insurance regulation risk and balance sheet complexity that makes KKR harder to value than pure-play asset managers. Elevated interest rates compress exit multiples for private equity portfolio companies, potentially reducing future carried interest. KKR's $796B AUM is approaching scale where deployment becomes harder — finding attractive deals at this size requires increasingly large transactions with fewer potential targets. The Inspire Impact Score of -53 (MISALIGNED) with negative attributions for Alcohol and LGBT Activism may exclude KKR from faith-based investment mandates. Glassdoor reviews flagging internal technology adoption challenges and political culture suggest organizational friction that could impair long-term competitiveness [3]. Competition from Blackstone ($1.1T AUM), Apollo, and Carlyle for institutional capital and deal flow creates persistent pressure on management fees and fund terms.

  2. AI infrastructure investment demand creating multi-decade capital deployment opportunity in data centers, power infrastructure, and semiconductor supply chains where KKR has established investment leadership [1]
