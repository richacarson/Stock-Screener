Company Profile
Financial Services ·Asset Management ·NYSE ·United States
KKR is a leading global investment firm managing approximately $796B in assets across private equity, credit, real assets, infrastructure, and insurance (via Global Atlantic). Founded in 1976, the firm is led by co-CEOs Scott Nuttall and Joe Bae. KKR reported record Q2 2026 results with revenue of $5.73B, adjusted net income per share of $1.63 (+38% YoY), and fee-related earnings per share of $1.32 (+34% YoY) with a 70% FRE margin. In June 2026, KKR launched Helix Digital Infrastructure with NVIDIA, Kuwait Investment Authority, and Vistra, committing $10B+ to AI data center and power infrastructure. The firm raised a record $19.2B infrastructure fund in 2026.

Investment Thesis
KKR at 16.18x forward earnings with a PEG of 0.78 offers one of the most attractive valuations in large-cap alternatives, pricing in only modest upside from a platform generating record results — Q2 2026 adjusted net income per share of $1.63 (+38% YoY), fee-related earnings per share of $1.32 (+34%), AUM of $796B (+16% YoY), and a 70% FRE margin [1]. The firm's strategic positioning in AI infrastructure ($10B+ Helix Digital with NVIDIA, $50B CyrusOne partnership, $19.2B record infrastructure fund) creates a differentiated growth engine as global AI capex accelerates [2]. The $46M in insider buying by both co-CEOs and three directors in early 2026 provides extraordinary conviction backing. Global Atlantic's permanent capital base provides stable insurance-linked earnings, and the target of $1T AUM by 2030 offers clear growth visibility.
The bear case centers on earnings quality, cultural execution, and macro sensitivity. KKR's ROE of 7.28% and profit margin of 12.20% are well below targets, reflecting the asset-heavy Global Atlantic insurance balance sheet diluting the higher-margin management company [4]. Revenue and carried interest are inherently volatile — Q2 2026 revenue of $5.73B is strong but quarter-to-quarter swings from mark-to-market movements and lumpy realizations make earnings difficult to predict. Glassdoor reviews describing 'toxic culture shift' in the technology division raise concerns about KKR's ability to build the digital capabilities needed for a technology-driven future. ESG credibility risk is material — KKR's stated ESG commitments conflict with investments in the Port Arthur LNG terminal and Coastal GasLink Pipeline, resulting in an MSCI ESG rating of only BB [3]. The Inspire Impact Score of -53 (MISALIGNED) limits faith-based investor demand. A broader market downturn or private credit deterioration would compress both management fees (via AUM declines) and carried interest (via lower realizations), amplified by the 70% D/E leverage.
