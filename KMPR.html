Company Profile
Financial Services ·Insurance - Property & Casualty ·NYSE ·United States
Kemper Corporation is a Chicago-based insurance holding company providing specialty property and casualty insurance (primarily non-standard personal auto), life insurance, and related financial services. The company serves underserved and specialty markets through independent agents and direct channels. Q2 2026 adjusted EPS of $0.45 beat consensus by $0.11, but a $464.8M non-cash goodwill impairment in the specialty auto segment resulted in a GAAP net loss of $7.90/share. Revenue declining as personal auto volumes contract, with earned premiums down $119M YoY in Q2.

Investment Thesis
Kemper presents a deep value contrarian case with Forward P/E of 5.91x, PEG of 0.75, and a 5.12% dividend yield supported by 37 consecutive years of payments [6]. Seven insider purchases totaling $1.5M with zero insider sells signal genuine leadership conviction in the turnaround [5]. Forward EPS estimates of ~$4.23 imply the adjusted operating business is far healthier than GAAP figures distorted by the one-time $464.8M goodwill impairment, and the Q2 2026 adjusted EPS of $0.45 beating consensus by $0.11 suggests operational stabilization [1]. The life insurance segment provides diversified earnings support, and analysts project 115% earnings growth for 2027 with revenue estimates averaging $4.88B [6].
The primary risk is that Kemper's specialty auto turnaround fails. The $464.8M goodwill impairment signals the specialty auto franchise has materially less value than previously carried, and earned premiums declined $119.2M YoY in Q2 2026 as personal auto volumes contract [1]. Revenue has declined from a 2022 peak of $5.45B to a current run-rate below $4.4B. Short interest of 10.0% reflects bearish institutional sentiment [6]. ROE of -19.7% and profit margin of -10.8% — even distorted by the impairment — underscore operational challenges. AI-enabled insurtech competitors could disrupt non-standard auto through algorithmic underwriting, accelerating adverse selection against Kemper's traditional agency model [3]. The $1.28 annual dividend against negative GAAP earnings requires adjusted operating income recovery toward $3+ EPS within 12-18 months, or the 37-year payment streak faces pressure.
