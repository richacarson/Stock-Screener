Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NYSE American ·Singapore
KNOREX is a Singapore-based, founder-led (CEO Justin Choo) provider of AI-driven cross-channel advertising technology. Its flagship KNOREX XPO platform is an AI-driven advertising management and execution (AMX) cloud platform unifying campaigns across search, social, CTV/OTT, video, audio, display, native, and DOOH channels [1]. The company completed its $12M IPO on NYSE American in September 2025. FY2025 revenue fell 44% to $6.0M from $10.8M in FY2024, driven primarily by the loss of a major automotive client tied to U.S. import tariffs (excluding that client, revenue grew 21%) [1]. As of December 31, 2025, the company reported a stockholders' deficit of $(6.5)M and has posted net losses in each of its last four fiscal years.

Investment Thesis
KNOREX's AI-driven KNOREX XPO advertising platform is a genuine, revenue-generating product unifying campaign management across search, social, CTV/OTT, and display channels, and excluding the loss of a single large automotive client (attributed to U.S. import tariffs), underlying revenue grew 21% in FY2025 [1]. Management points to 2026 as a year of accelerated growth as pipeline opportunities convert following the company's September 2025 IPO, and the core AI product is, by itself, the company's entire go-to-market engine rather than an emerging side bet.
The bear case dominates this name. KNOREX trades at $0.31 with average daily dollar turnover below IOWN's $1M liquidity floor, automatically zeroing the Infrastructure score. FY2025 revenue fell 44% to $6.0M, the company reported a stockholders' deficit of $(6.5)M, received a NYSE American continued-listing non-compliance notice tied to insufficient stockholders' equity, and its FY2025 annual report carried a going-concern qualification from its auditor [1]. The company must submit a compliance plan to NYSE American by September 20, 2026 and regain compliance by February 2028 or face delisting proceedings. As a micro-cap adtech vendor competing against Google, Meta, and The Trade Desk with no scale advantage, KNOREX faces both existential financial risk and competitive AI disruption risk simultaneously, warranting a SELL.
