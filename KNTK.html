Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas Midstream ·NYSE ·United States
Kinetik Holdings is a fully integrated midstream company operating in the Texas Delaware Basin (Permian Basin). Through its Midstream Logistics and Pipeline Transportation segments, Kinetik provides gathering, compression, processing, stabilization, treating, storage, and transportation services for natural gas, natural gas liquids, and crude oil. The company also offers water gathering and disposal services. Formed through the 2022 combination of Altus Midstream and BCP Raptor, Kinetik processed 1.74 Bcf/d of natural gas in Q2 2026 and reported record quarterly adjusted EBITDA of $281M. Market cap of approximately $9B.

Investment Thesis
Kinetik Holdings is a well-managed Delaware Basin midstream operator delivering record financial performance — Q2 2026 revenue of $581.4M (+36% YoY), adjusted EBITDA of $281M (up from $207M), and EPS of $0.64 (up from $0.33) [1]. The company raised full-year 2026 guidance after the record quarter, signaling continued growth driven by Permian Basin production expansion and favorable commodity conditions. At 18.3x trailing earnings with a 6.1% dividend yield, Kinetik offers an attractive combination of growth and income for midstream investors. The 4.9/5 Glassdoor rating with 98% recommend reflects an exceptional culture under CEO Jamie Welch that is rare in the energy sector [3]. The Inspire Impact Score of 53 (ALIGNED) adds faith-based investor appeal.
The bear case centers on financial structure risk, commodity exposure, and valuation. D/E of 136.6% and total debt of ~$4B reflect the capital-intensive nature of midstream infrastructure, but leave limited financial flexibility if natural gas prices decline or Permian Basin production growth slows [5]. The payout ratio of 111.2% on GAAP earnings means the dividend exceeds net income — while distributable cash flow may support the distribution, this metric signals distribution sustainability risk. Forward P/E of 24.3x is above the value target for an infrastructure company, and PEG of 3.49 suggests the current growth rate is already priced in. Kinetik's 5-year dividend growth CAGR of only ~1.6% (from $3.00 to $3.24 annually) lags inflation and makes the yield-only proposition less compelling on a real-return basis. The company is only 4 years old with limited track record through a commodity downturn, and concentrated Delaware Basin exposure creates geographic risk [1][5].
Key Catalysts
  1. Continued Permian Basin production growth driving higher throughput volumes — Kinetik processed 1.74 Bcf/d in Q2 2026 and has capacity expansion projects that could drive volume growth as Delaware Basin activity intensifies [1]
  2. Raised full-year 2026 adjusted EBITDA guidance signals management confidence in sustainable growth trajectory and potential for further upward revisions if commodity conditions remain favorable [1][2]

Key Risks
  1. Payout ratio of 111.2% on GAAP earnings raises dividend sustainability concerns — if distributable cash flow deteriorates or commodity prices decline, the $3.24/year distribution ($0.81/quarter) could face a cut, which would compress the stock significantly given the 6.1% yield support [5]
  2. Concentrated geographic exposure to the Delaware Basin means Kinetik's growth is entirely dependent on Permian Basin production activity — a slowdown in drilling (driven by lower oil/gas prices or regulatory changes) would directly impact throughput volumes [1]
  3. D/E of 136.6% with ~$4B total debt creates interest rate sensitivity and limits financial flexibility in a downturn — midstream companies with excessive leverage have historically underperformed during commodity cycles [5]
