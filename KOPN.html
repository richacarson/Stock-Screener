Company Profile
Technology ·Semiconductors & Semiconductor Equipment ·NASDAQ ·United States
Kopin Corporation is a 40-year-old microdisplay and application-specific optical solutions provider for defense, AI infrastructure, enterprise, and consumer markets. Products include micro-OLED and MicroLED displays for thermal weapon sights, pilot helmet-mounted display systems, armored vehicle targeting, and drone vision systems. Defense applications generated 74% of FY2025 revenue. The company reported Q2 2026 revenue of $12.7M (+51% YoY), swung to GAAP profitability, and received $45M in orders YTD 2026. Kopin is expanding into AI infrastructure through the Fabric.AI Neural I/O collaboration for MicroLED optical interconnects, with a joint steering committee launched August 2026. Headquartered in Westborough, Massachusetts.
200 Employees ·www.kopin.com

Investment Thesis
Kopin is a 40-year defense microdisplay specialist transforming into an optical solutions platform with a compelling AI infrastructure catalyst. Q2 2026 revenue surged 51% YoY to $12.7M with gross profit of $6.1M (48% margin), swinging to GAAP profitability and validating the growth inflection [6]. The company received $45M in orders YTD 2026, and full-year guidance of $52-60M implies 30-50% annual growth. The Fabric.AI collaboration ($15M initial order, exclusive MicroLED manufacturing rights, 19.9% equity stake) opens a potentially transformative AI data center revenue stream, with a joint steering committee launched August 2026 under CEO Murray signaling accelerating commitment [5]. Defense backlog expanded significantly with a $21.5M thermal imaging follow-on, $3.2M Sentinel FPV, $5M+ European HMD awards, and first DarkWAVE order [2]. Management targets $100M revenue by 2028.
The bear case centers on execution risk at speculative valuation. With $4.28 stock price and ~200M shares outstanding, the ~$860M market cap trades at roughly 15x 2026 revenue guidance midpoint — pricing in flawless execution on both defense backlog conversion and the speculative Fabric.AI opportunity [7]. Profit margin of 4.9% TTM remains thin, and while Q2 showed improvement, sustained GAAP profitability has not been demonstrated across a full cycle. The Fabric.AI opportunity relies on early-stage MicroLED optical interconnect technology competing against established silicon photonics from well-funded competitors like Broadcom and Intel. Five-year revenue CAGR of only ~5% (from $45.67M in 2021 to ~$56M guided for 2026) shows that the growth inflection is very recent and not yet validated over a multi-year period [7]. The company's 40-year history includes multiple periods of financial distress — FY2024 saw a $43.9M net loss — suggesting execution risk is non-trivial for a 200-person company attempting to serve both defense and AI markets simultaneously.
Key Catalysts
  1. Q2 2026 revenue inflection (+51% YoY to $12.7M) validates the growth story — H2 2026 backlog conversion from $45M YTD orders should accelerate toward the $52-60M annual guidance, with management targeting GAAP profitability by Q4 2026 [6]
  2. Fabric.AI Neural I/O partnership deepening — August 2026 joint steering committee launch signals transition from development to commercialization, with potential to scale from $15M to $25-50M in 2027 revenue [5]
  3. Defense backlog acceleration from $21.5M thermal imaging follow-on, Sentinel FPV drone program, European HMD expansion (DarkWAVE/THEON), and in-house US OLED manufacturing strengthening sole-source competitive position [2]
Key Risks
  1. Speculative valuation at ~15x forward revenue on a barely profitable company — any miss on $52-60M FY2026 guidance or slowdown in Fabric.AI commercialization could produce severe drawdown [7]
  2. Sustained profitability unproven — FY2024 posted a $43.9M net loss and TTM profit margin is only 4.9%; the Q2 2026 profitability swing must be maintained through multiple quarters to validate the business model at scale
  3. Fabric.AI MicroLED optical interconnects compete against mature silicon photonics technologies from well-capitalized incumbents (Broadcom, Intel), and the $15M initial order may not represent sustainable long-term demand in a rapidly evolving AI infrastructure market [2]
