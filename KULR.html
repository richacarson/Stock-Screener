Company Profile
Technology ·Electronic Components ·NYSE American ·United States
KULR Technology Group designs and builds advanced battery systems and thermal management solutions for autonomous platforms, digital infrastructure, e-mobility, and space applications. The company leverages NASA-derived carbon fiber thermal management technology, offering battery production, thermal runaway shield technology, and the KULR ONE battery platform. KULR maintains a Bitcoin treasury strategy with approximately 1,085 BTC in holdings. Q1 2026 revenue was $4.8M (+98% YoY) with gross margin expanding to 29% from 8%, but the stock has declined over 50% from mid-2026 highs amid continued operating losses.
