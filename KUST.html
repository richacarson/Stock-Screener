Kustom Entertainment (formerly Digital Ally, DGLY) is a micro-cap live event production and online ticketing company operating through its Country Stampede Music Festival (entering its 30th year) and TicketSmarter.com platform. The company rebranded in January 2026, executed a 1-for-5 reverse stock split, and divested its legacy video solutions segment. In September 2026, Kustom announced a $112M acquisition of TFL, LLC (Tickets For Less) to build an integrated end-to-end live entertainment ecosystem. Market cap approximately $1-6M with extreme volatility.
