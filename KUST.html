Kustom Entertainment (formerly Digital Ally, DGLY) is a micro-cap live event production and online ticketing company operating through its Country Stampede Music Festival (entering its 30th year) and TicketSmarter.com platform. The company rebranded in January 2026, executed a 1-for-5 reverse stock split, and is divesting its legacy video solutions segment (58 patents) to Cycurion for $6-8.5M. CEO Stanton Ross leads the pivot to focus exclusively on live entertainment and ticketing markets.
