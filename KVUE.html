Investment Thesis
Kenvue at $17.20 trades at 13.9x forward earnings with a ~4.8% dividend yield, a 15.6% ROE and 81.6% D/E. It delivered three consecutive quarters of net and organic sales growth, with Q2 2026 net sales +3.0%, adjusted EPS +7% to $0.31, and Q1 adjusted EPS +33% to $0.32 [1]. Leading brands such as Tylenol, Listerine and Neutrogena give durable defensive cash flow.
Growth is modest (organic +0.7% in Q1, +1.6% in Q2) and the pending Kimberly-Clark transaction means no forward guidance and deal-completion uncertainty [1]. Employee reviews cite 3+ restructurings in two years and a 61% recommend rate, and the Moderate controversy rating (Sustainalytics 2 of 5) reflects product-liability exposure [2][3]. Profit margin of 10.8% trails the 20% target.
