Company Profile
Technology ·Software — Application ·NYSE ·United States
Klaviyo provides a B2C customer relationship management (CRM) platform enabling businesses to leverage owned marketing channels (email, SMS, push notifications) through data-driven automation and AI-powered personalization. The platform serves over 167,000 customers globally with deep e-commerce integrations, notably as Shopify's recommended email marketing partner. Led by co-CEOs Andrew Bialecki (co-founder, AI/product) and Chano Fernández (ex-Workday, go-to-market). Q2 2026 revenue surged 26% to $370.6M, with full-year guidance raised to $1.53B. Market cap approximately $5.1B.

Investment Thesis
Klaviyo delivered strong Q2 2026 results — $370.6M revenue (26% YoY growth), raised full-year guidance to $1.526-$1.534B (24% growth), and landed its largest-ever deal (an 8-figure multiproduct enterprise contract) with $50K+ ARR customers growing 36% YoY to ~40% of total ARR [1][2]. Non-GAAP operating income reached $50.9M (13.7% margin) and trailing twelve-month free cash flow was $236M (17% margin), demonstrating the SaaS model's inherent operating leverage as the company approaches GAAP profitability. Klaviyo's position as Shopify's recommended email partner, combined with 40+ AI features built since 2017 and the co-CEO restructuring for AI-first development, creates a defensible moat in B2C marketing automation [3]. The balance sheet is clean with $867M net cash, and at 22.4x forward earnings the stock is reasonably valued for a 24%+ growth company.
The bear case centers on growth deceleration, GAAP losses, and emerging risks. Revenue growth has decelerated from 32% (FY2025) to 26% (Q2 2026) to 21.5-22.5% (Q3 2026 guided), and non-GAAP gross margin compressed from 76.4% to 73.4% due to SMS carrier fee growth outpacing the broader business [2]. Full-year non-GAAP operating income guidance was reduced by $10M at midpoint due to Agency acquisition costs and continued investment, creating tension between growth investment and profitability [2]. The company remains GAAP unprofitable with ROE of -0.78% and net margin of -0.66% [4]. A California class action lawsuit alleges surreptitious data collection violating CIPA, and a password-sharing security misconfiguration from 2024-2025 exposed customer sign-up credentials to third-party advertisers — concerning for a company that handles sensitive marketing data [4]. Heavy insider selling including co-CEO Bialecki's 212K-share sale adds to uncertainty. Competitive pressure from Salesforce, HubSpot, and Intuit investing heavily in AI marketing could erode Klaviyo's differentiation.
Key Catalysts
  1. Enterprise expansion accelerating — largest-ever 8-figure multiproduct deal, $50K+ ARR customers growing 36% YoY to ~40% of total ARR, demonstrating Klaviyo's platform can move beyond SMB into higher-value contracts [2]
  2. AI-first product roadmap under co-CEO Bialecki could unlock new revenue streams through Co-Pilot and Autonomous Execution features, differentiating from competitors still adding AI as an afterthought [3]
  3. Path to GAAP profitability visible — non-GAAP operating margin of 13.7%, TTM free cash flow of $236M (17% margin), and improving unit economics suggest GAAP breakeven is approaching as revenue scales past $1.5B [2]
Key Risks
  1. Revenue growth decelerating from 32% (FY25) to 26% (Q2) to 21.5-22.5% (Q3 guided), with non-GAAP gross margin compressing from 76.4% to 73.4% as SMS carrier costs grow faster than the overall business [2]
  2. Data privacy and security liabilities — California class action lawsuit alleging CIPA violations and a 2024-2025 password-sharing misconfiguration exposing customer credentials to third-party advertisers undermine trust in a data-centric platform [4]
  3. Heavy Shopify ecosystem dependence — any deterioration in the Shopify partnership or Shopify's growth trajectory would disproportionately impact Klaviyo's customer acquisition, and persistent GAAP losses (ROE -0.78%, margin -0.66%) leave no profitability buffer [4]
