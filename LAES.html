Company Profile
Technology ·Semiconductors ·NMS ·Switzerland
SEALSQ Corp designs, manufactures, and sells tamper-resistant secure microcontrollers, post-quantum cryptography (PQC) chips, and digital identity infrastructure for IoT devices, critical infrastructure, and government/defense applications. The QS7001 is the first hardware-embedded post-quantum secure chip, integrating NIST-standardized ML-KEM and ML-DSA algorithms directly in silicon. Spun off from WISeKey in December 2023, the company reported finalized H1 2026 revenue of $11.2M (+131% YoY), reaffirmed FY2026 guidance of $27-36M (50-100% growth), and held ~$486M in cash with a >$225M pipeline through 2029.
185 Employees ·www.sealsq.com

Investment Thesis
SEALSQ at $1.81 with a $406M market cap is a first-mover in the emerging post-quantum cryptography semiconductor market, with the QS7001 being the industry's first hardware-embedded PQC chip integrating NIST-standardized algorithms directly in silicon [2]. Finalized H1 2026 revenue surged 131% YoY to $11.2M with 48% gross margins, and management reaffirmed FY2026 guidance of $27-36M (50-100% growth) backed by a >$225M pipeline through 2029 with $100M+ in post-quantum projects [1]. With ~$486M cash, minimal debt (D/E 1.27%), and regulatory tailwinds from France's ANSSI PQC mandate and US CNSA 2.0 requirements, SEALSQ has the financial runway and market positioning to capture the hardware PQC buildout. The IC'Alps acquisition added ~100 ASIC engineers and contributed $2.5M in H1 revenue [1].
The bear case is dominated by dilution, valuation, and execution risk. The stock has declined 77% from its 52-week high of $7.75 to $1.81, reflecting market skepticism about the path to profitability [3]. The H1 2026 net loss widened to $27.8M from $20M a year earlier despite revenue more than doubling, as operating costs rose faster than revenue, producing a -170% net margin [1]. Shares outstanding have grown dramatically through aggressive equity raises, massively diluting existing shareholders. Revenue has been erratic — $30M (FY2023) to $11M (FY2024) to $18M (FY2025) — though H1 2026 shows accelerating traction [1]. Net insider selling by multiple executives signals limited conviction at current levels [3]. Competition from semiconductor giants NXP, Infineon, and STMicroelectronics with established manufacturing scale and customer relationships poses a serious threat once NIST PQC standards fully mature and larger players integrate PQC into existing product lines.

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience MODERATE
7
AI Risk Protection (7/9): SEALSQ manufactures physical semiconductor chips — tamper-resistant secure microcontrollers and PQC processors — that AI software structurally cannot displace [2]. AI systems themselves need hardware security; as AI deployments scale into Physical AI, robotics, and autonomous systems, demand for hardware roots of trust increases. The defense and government partnerships (US DoD via Trusted Semiconductor Solutions, ANSSI compliance) add regulatory moats. AI proliferation means more attack surface requiring hardware-level security. The primary exposure is that AI could accelerate competitor chip design timelines, and larger competitors (NXP, Infineon, STMicroelectronics) with established manufacturing scale could deploy PQC capabilities faster. AI Opportunity (0/1): While SEALSQ has appointed a dedicated Group AI Officer and is developing AI-integrated chipsets at the SEALQuantum lab, no AI products are currently generating quantifiable revenue. The company's PQC chips secure AI infrastructure but are not themselves AI products [2].
