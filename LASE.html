Company Profile
Industrials ·Specialty Industrial Machinery ·NASDAQ ·United States
Laser Photonics Corporation designs, develops, and manufactures industrial laser systems for surface cleaning, rust removal, de-painting, and corrosion control. The company targets disruption of the $46 billion sandblasting and abrasives market with safer, more environmentally friendly laser-based alternatives, serving defense, medical, energy, and industrial sectors. Products include the CleanTech laser cleaning line and MarkStar laser marking systems. Q2 2026 showed sequential recovery with revenue of $1.8M (+97% QoQ) and gross margin returning to positive 8.4%, though the stock has declined to $0.78 amid continued operating losses.

Investment Thesis
Laser Photonics offers high-risk exposure to the disruption of the $46B sandblasting and abrasives market with cleaner, safer laser-based surface cleaning technology. Q2 2026 showed sequential recovery with revenue of $1.8M (+97% QoQ) and gross margin returning to positive 8.4% after the disastrous Q1 [1][2]. Deferred revenue of approximately $4.2M provides near-term order visibility, and the LSAD defense project validates the military market opportunity. Manufacturing consolidation is expected to yield $1M in annual savings, and at $0.78/share and $47M market cap, the stock offers significant upside if the company can stabilize revenue and narrow losses in a massive addressable market.
The bear case is severe. Trailing revenue growth is -30.5% as H1 2026 revenue of approximately $2.7M fell significantly below H1 2025 levels, erasing FY2025's acquisition-driven 144% surge [1][5]. ROE of -1180.9% reflects massive equity destruction, and the balance sheet has just $650K cash against $8.9M debt, making dilutive equity raises likely [1]. The stock at $0.78 faces acute NASDAQ minimum bid delisting risk (sub-$1.00 for 30+ consecutive days triggers a notice). CEO Wayne Tupuola is on health leave, Glassdoor reviews allege shadow governance and stock-price-focused PR strategy over genuine commercial execution [4]. No analyst coverage or institutional validation exists. The company burned approximately $6.2M in net losses in H1 2026, and without a capital raise, the runway is measured in months. The path from micro-cap laser equipment maker to scaled industrial disruptor requires capital and execution credibility that management has not yet demonstrated.
