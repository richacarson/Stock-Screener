Company Profile
Technology ·Semiconductors ·NASDAQ ·United States
nLIGHT designs, develops, manufactures, and sells high-power semiconductor and fiber lasers for aerospace and defense, industrial, and microfabrication applications. The company is vertically integrated from semiconductor chips to complete laser weapon systems, including the HADES directed energy program. Primary manufacturing in Vancouver and Camas, Washington, and Hillsboro, Oregon, plus facilities in Finland and China. Raised $200M in follow-on offering in 2025 to fund U.S. manufacturing expansion.
800 Employees ·www.nlight.net

Investment Thesis
nLIGHT is a vertically integrated laser technology company in the early innings of a transformative defense pivot, delivering Q1 2026 revenue of $80.2M (+55% YoY) with record A&D product revenue of $33.1M (doubled YoY) and the company's first GAAP-profitable quarter ($0.6M net income, $0.20 EPS vs. consensus $0.09) [1]. Gross margins expanded to 30%+ from 17% two years ago, and the company enters Q2 2026 guiding $75-81M revenue with $8-12M adjusted EBITDA [1]. The $162M funded backlog, $200M in fresh capital for U.S. manufacturing expansion, and showcased 70kW laser weapon systems position nLIGHT as the rare pure-play on directed energy — a defense capability with rapidly growing demand as the U.S. military prioritizes laser weapons for drone and missile defense [3]. Founder-CEO Keeney's 25-year leadership and analyst Strong Buy consensus with $90.71 target (+30% upside) underscore conviction in the long-term trajectory [4].
The bear case centers on extreme valuation and execution risk. At forward P/E of 149.74x and $3.95B market cap on just $290M TTM revenue, nLIGHT is priced for near-flawless execution [4]. The company only just achieved GAAP profitability in Q1 2026 ($0.6M net income on $80.2M revenue), and TTM margins remain negative at -5.1%. The exit from cutting and welding creates a $25-30M revenue headwind in 2026 that could mask underlying defense growth [1]. Heavy insider selling — CEO Keeney $7.2M, CFO Corso $1.55M, Director Link $1.6M in Q1 2026 alone — signals management views the current stock price as full [4]. The $162M funded backlog is 'essentially flat year over year,' and the company acknowledges needing 'additional contracts beyond current backlog' for sustained growth, introducing execution variability if defense prototype awards are delayed [2]. Defense contracts are lumpy and subject to Congressional appropriation cycles. Average daily volume declined to 737K shares from 1.77M, potentially reflecting reduced institutional interest at elevated prices.
