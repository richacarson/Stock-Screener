Investment Thesis
Laureate Education is a well-executed for-profit higher education platform focused on Latin America, delivering Q1 2026 revenue of $272.6M (up 15% reported) with new enrollments up 9% (13% Peru, 4% Mexico) and raised FY2026 adjusted EPS guidance to $2.00-$2.08 [1][2]. FY2026 revenue guidance of $1.89-1.905B with adjusted EBITDA of $583-593M implies continued double-digit growth and margin expansion. Financial metrics are strong: 17.24x trailing P/E, 15.87x forward P/E, 28.82% ROE, 69% D/E, and 16.1% profit margin — a rare combination of growth and value [2]. The expanded share buyback program demonstrates capital allocation discipline. Educational access in Latin America is a secular growth driver with demographic tailwinds, and the Inspire Impact Score of +49 (ALIGNED) with zero negative attributions reflects strong faith compatibility.
The primary risks are AI disruption to higher education, geographic concentration, and currency exposure. AI-powered learning platforms and generative AI tutoring tools increasingly offer personalized education at lower cost, threatening the traditional university value proposition — and Laureate has not publicly articulated an AI strategy [3]. Q1 2026 revenue grew only 1% on a constant currency basis (compared to 15% reported), with approximately $9M of intra-year calendar timing unfavorably affecting results — highlighting the FX sensitivity of the business [1]. Mexico intake volume growth softened to 4% versus 13% in Peru, suggesting uneven market dynamics. The for-profit education model carries inherent regulatory and reputational risk — any government action restricting for-profit education in Peru or Mexico would be devastating. At the current stock price of $36.45 near the 52-week high of $40.75, limited near-term upside may be available despite strong fundamentals.

  2. FX and geographic concentration risk — Q1 2026 revenue grew only 1% constant currency vs 15% reported; ~5 percentage points of full-year growth driven by currency; Mexico enrollment softening to 4% vs 13% Peru growth
  3. For-profit education regulatory risk — government actions restricting or penalizing for-profit education in Peru or Mexico could severely impact enrollment, pricing power, and the company's social license to operate

AI Risk Protection (5/9): Laureate faces meaningful AI disruption risk as AI-powered learning platforms (Khan Academy, Coursera, Duolingo), generative AI tutoring tools (ChatGPT, Claude), and credential devaluation increasingly challenge the traditional university value proposition [3]. The company has not publicly articulated an AI adoption strategy, which represents both a missed opportunity and a risk.
