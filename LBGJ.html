Company Profile
Industrials ·Specialty Industrial Machinery ·NASDAQ ·China
Li Bang International Corporation Inc. designs, develops, produces, and sells stainless-steel commercial kitchen equipment under the Li Bang brand in China. Founded in 1992 and incorporated in the Cayman Islands in 2021, the company offers steaming, cooking, baking, frying, disinfection, and refrigeration equipment to hotels, schools, hospitals, institutions, and enterprises. Based in Jiangyin, China, Li Bang is majority-controlled by CEO and Chairman Feng Huang through Maple Huang Holdings Limited (~75.3% of voting power). The company IPO'd on Nasdaq in 2022 at $4 and has since collapsed 99.98% from its October 2024 ATH of $550 to approximately $0.03. Market cap stands at roughly $109K. FY2025 revenue was $11.11M (+2.89% YoY) with a net loss of $1.01M. A planned 1-for-100 reverse stock split was announced to regain Nasdaq minimum bid compliance.

Investment Thesis
Li Bang International is a micro-cap Chinese commercial kitchen equipment manufacturer with FY2025 revenue of $11.11M (+2.89% YoY), a net loss of $1.01M, and a market capitalization of approximately $109K [1][2]. The company's growth thesis rests entirely on two uncertain initiatives: the Yufengyuan food distribution acquisition (projected ~$22M revenue contribution) and the Phase II intelligent kitchen facility (RMB 200M investment, operations expected mid-2026). CEO Feng Huang's 75.3% ownership stake provides founder-owner alignment, and the physical manufacturing business offers moderate protection from AI disruption. The stock's AI resilience score of 5 reflects the reality that stainless-steel kitchen equipment cannot be replaced by software.
The bear case is existential and overwhelming. LBGJ has collapsed 99.98% from its October 2024 ATH of $550 to $0.03, reducing market cap to approximately $109K — less than the cost of a mid-range car [1]. The planned 1-for-100 reverse split is a cosmetic measure to avoid Nasdaq delisting, not a solution to fundamental business problems. Profit margins are -9.1%, ROE is -16.8%, D/E is 136.3%, and the workforce has contracted from ~250 to 102 employees [1][2]. The market's 83.82% single-day decline on the Yufengyuan acquisition announcement signals profound skepticism about management's strategic judgment. There is no analyst coverage, no institutional ownership of substance, and the extreme share price history ($0.02-$550 range) indicates speculative manipulation rather than legitimate price discovery. This is not an investable security for any portfolio with fiduciary standards. IOWN should not hold or consider this stock under any circumstances.
