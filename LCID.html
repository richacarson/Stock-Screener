Company Profile
Consumer Cyclical ·Auto Manufacturers ·NMS ·United States
Lucid Group designs, develops, manufactures, and sells luxury electric vehicles, EV powertrains, and battery systems. The company produces the Lucid Air sedan (class-leading 520-mile range) and Lucid Gravity SUV, with proprietary in-house 900V battery and powertrain technology. Approximately 60%+ owned by Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund. CEO Silvio Napoli (ex-Schindler Group, appointed April 2026) is executing a major restructuring — 20% U.S. workforce reduction, C-suite overhaul, single-shift production, and $1.4B cash flow improvement program. Q3 2026 produced 2,954 vehicles and delivered 3,806 (drawing down inventory), with 2026 production guidance suspended. FY2025 revenue was $1.35B (+68% YoY) with 15,841 deliveries.

Lucid at $3.32 with a $1.3B market cap offers a deeply depressed valuation on premium EV technology. FY2025 revenue grew 68% to $1.35B with 15,841 deliveries, and Q1-Q2 2026 showed continued growth with Q2 revenue reaching ~$405M (+44% sequential) [1]. CEO Napoli has no automotive experience and has eliminated the COO role, overhauled the C-suite, and reduced the workforce 20%, introducing massive execution risk in a brutally competitive market where Tesla, BYD, and legacy OEMs have overwhelming scale advantages.
