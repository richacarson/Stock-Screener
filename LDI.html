Company Profile
Financial Services ·Mortgage Finance ·NYSE ·United States
loanDepot originates, finances, sells and services residential mortgage loans, including conventional agency-conforming, prime jumbo, FHA/VA and home equity products such as the 5X5 HomeLoan. It also provides title, escrow, settlement, real estate and insurance services. Founder and controlling shareholder Anthony Hsieh returned as permanent CEO in July 2025 to restore profitability, refocus on consumer-direct lending and accelerate AI adoption. The shares traded at about $0.58 on November 1, 2026, below the NYSE's $1 continued-listing price standard.

Investment Thesis
loanDepot is a leveraged bet on a mortgage-rate recovery: Q2 2026 revenue rose 18% QoQ to $337.3M on $8.0B of originations, unit volume grew 25%, and management guided expenses lower in Q3 [1]. Founder Anthony Hsieh's return and an AI-driven productivity agenda aim to close the gap to profitability [4].
On IOWN's framework the balance sheet and track record dominate: D/E above 1,800%, a -26.7% ROE, revenue down from $4.34B in FY2020 to $1.54B in FY2025, and a share price of ~$0.58 that sits below the NYSE's $1 standard [2][6]. AI is more likely to compress loan-officer economics than to protect them, and litigation and cybersecurity-incident investigations add tail risk [5].
Key Catalysts
  1. Lower mortgage rates driving refinance volume; Q3 2026 origination guidance of $6.25B-$8.25B [1]
  2. Expense reductions from debt repurchases and productivity initiatives guided for Q3 2026 [1]
  3. AI-driven efficiency in consumer-direct lending under the rebuilt leadership team [4]
Key Risks
  1. Share price of ~$0.58 is below the NYSE $1 continued-listing standard, raising the risk of a reverse split or delisting [6]
  2. Extreme leverage (D/E ~1,807%) and continued net losses (FY2025 net loss of $62.6M) [2][6]
  3. Pending shareholder suits, a former COO's allegations, a Truth in Lending Act class action and state investigations into the cybersecurity incident [5]
