Company Profile
Communication Services ·Advertising Agencies ·NMS ·United States
Liftoff Mobile provides mobile advertising, monetization, and performance marketing solutions through its AI-powered Cortex platform, which uses neural network technology to optimize advertiser performance. The company operates a demand-side platform (DSP) for programmatic user acquisition and a supply-side platform (SSP) for app monetization, serving mobile advertisers and publishers across gaming, e-commerce, fintech, and sports betting verticals. Founded in 2011, merged with Vungle in 2021, and headquartered in Redwood City, California.

Investment Thesis
Liftoff at $15.41 with a $2.6B market cap is an AI-native mobile advertising platform trading at 11.1x forward earnings despite 35% revenue growth and 60% adjusted EBITDA margins. Q2 2026 revenue reached $220M (up 35% YoY), marking the 11th consecutive quarter of growth, with trailing 12-month free cash flow of $184M up 142% YoY [1]. The Cortex neural network platform drives measurable advertiser performance at scale, and the unified DSP+SSP architecture creates a flywheel where demand-side and supply-side data reinforce targeting accuracy. FY2026 guidance of $870-880M revenue (27-28% growth) is conservative given the Q2 beat, and the expansion into sports betting and prediction market verticals (boosted by FIFA World Cup) opens new growth vectors. The company generates real profits and cash flow — adjusted EBITDA of $132M (60% margin) in Q2 suggests the trailing P/E of 61.6x is artificially inflated by $45M in non-cash IPO costs.
The bear case centers on competitive concentration risk and margin sustainability. Liftoff competes directly against tech giants — Google, Meta, and Apple — whose AI advertising capabilities, first-party data advantages, and platform control dwarf Liftoff's resources, and any further privacy changes (following Apple's ATT disruption) could structurally impair the independent mobile ad tech value proposition [1]. Q2 2026 revenue benefited from FIFA World Cup-driven sports betting spend, a non-recurring event that inflates the growth trajectory, and management acknowledged timing quirks in Q2 margin strength [1]. The reported net loss of $4M (including $45M in non-cash IPO expenses) obscures whether the underlying business generates sustainable GAAP profitability, and D/E and ROE data are unavailable, limiting visibility into capital structure and returns on equity. The stock has declined 18% from its 50-day SMA of $18.88, suggesting institutional skepticism about growth sustainability beyond the World Cup boost.

AI Risk Protection (6/9): Liftoff's core business IS AI — the Cortex neural network platform processes billions of ad impressions using machine learning, making the company an AI-native operator rather than a business threatened by AI adoption [1][2].
