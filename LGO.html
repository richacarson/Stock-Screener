Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Other Industrial Metals & Mining ·NASDAQ ·Canada
Largo Inc. is a vertically integrated vanadium producer, mining vanadium pentoxide from its Maracas Menchen Mine in Bahia, Brazil and producing premium VPURE vanadium products for steel, aerospace, defense, chemical, and energy storage applications. The company also produces ilmenite concentrate and has developed VCHARGE vanadium redox flow battery technology through its Storion Energy joint venture with Stryten Energy for long-duration clean energy storage. Market cap approximately $63M.

Investment Thesis
Largo is a vertically integrated pure-play vanadium producer with a 31-year mine life, positioned at the intersection of critical minerals supply and the emerging long-duration energy storage market [1][2][3]. Q1 2026 production surged 102% YoY to 2,616 tonnes with full-year guidance of 10,500-12,000 tonnes representing 15-31% growth, while vanadium prices are recovering — European V2O5 at $5.69/lb (up 8% YoY) and US FeV prices up 56% [1]. At $63M market cap and approximately 0.4x trailing price-to-sales, the stock is priced for distress despite rising commodity prices, production growth, and the Storion Energy JV with Stryten for battery manufacturing. New co-CEO leadership (Arias with 30 years experience, Tellechea with operational expertise) provides turnaround potential.
The bear case is severe and has driven a 49% stock price decline since April 2026. Revenue has collapsed from $229M (FY2022) to $109M TTM, profit margin is -60.3%, and cumulative losses over three years exceed $100M [1][6]. Q1 2026 still produced a $4.7M net loss and -$4.3M adjusted EBITDA despite the production surge, meaning the company is not yet cash-flow positive even with rising vanadium prices. D/E of 69.7% against approximately $107M debt with limited cash creates liquidity risk if losses continue — the company may need to raise capital, diluting the existing $63M market cap. U.S. tariffs on Brazilian vanadium products are creating additional margin compression. The VCHARGE battery business, while strategically compelling, generates zero revenue and requires significant additional capital investment. Daily dollar volume of approximately $1.1M barely clears the $1M liquidity floor, creating exit risk [6].
Key Catalysts
  1. Vanadium price recovery with US FeV prices up 56% YoY and production ramping to 10,500-12,000 tonnes in 2026, providing dual operating leverage on both price and volume as the company approaches profitability breakeven [1]
  2. Storion Energy JV with Stryten Energy for VRFB battery manufacturing and 48 MWh project commercialization, positioning Largo to capture higher-margin downstream value from its own vanadium supply [3]
  3. New co-CEO leadership (Arias and Tellechea) could accelerate operational turnaround and strategic execution after the company's prolonged loss-making period [2]
Key Risks
  1. Vanadium price dependency — revenue has declined 52% from the 2022 peak and the company has no pricing power, with commodity prices driven by Chinese production decisions and global steel market cycles outside Largo's control [1]
  2. Liquidity risk with D/E of 69.7%, approximately $107M debt, negative free cash flow, and cash reserves under pressure — if the production ramp does not restore profitability, the company may face dilutive capital raises against its $63M market cap [6]
  3. U.S. tariffs on Brazilian vanadium exports are compressing margins in a period when the company cannot afford additional cost headwinds, potentially delaying the path to breakeven [1]
