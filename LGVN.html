Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Longeveron is a clinical-stage biotechnology company developing laromestrocel (LOMECEL-B), an allogeneic mesenchymal stem cell therapy sourced from young, healthy donor bone marrow, for aging-related and life-threatening conditions. The company is running pivotal Phase 2b trials across hypoplastic left heart syndrome (HLHS), Alzheimer's disease, and pediatric dilated cardiomyopathy — an unusually broad pivotal-stage pipeline for a company of its size — while facing a going-concern qualification and a shrinking cash runway. Founded in 2014 and headquartered in Miami, Florida.
