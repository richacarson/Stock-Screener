Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NCM ·United States
Longeveron is a clinical-stage biotechnology company developing laromestrocel (LOMECEL-B), an allogeneic mesenchymal stem cell therapy sourced from young, healthy donor bone marrow, for aging-related and life-threatening conditions. The company is running pivotal trials for hypoplastic left heart syndrome (HLHS), Alzheimer's disease, and pediatric dilated cardiomyopathy with five distinct FDA designations. Founded in 2014 and headquartered in Miami, Florida, LGVN reported Q2 2026 revenue of $287K (down 9.2% YoY) with a net loss of $6.1M and cash of $10.1M expected to fund operations into Q4 2026.
