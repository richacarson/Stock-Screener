Company Profile
Technology ·Machine Vision & Industrial Automation ·NASDAQ ·China
Lianhe Sowell International Group Ltd. provides machine vision products and solutions across manufacturing, transportation, security, and building management industries in China. The company offers industrial machine vision products, AI-powered spray painting robots, intelligent building and transportation solutions, and electronic customs clearance systems. Founded in 2007 and headquartered in Shenzhen, China. FY2026 (ended March 2026) revenue was $43.27M (+18% YoY), but the company reported a $5.7M net loss as operating expenses surged 148%. The stock declined 93%+ over the past 52 weeks, executed a 1:16 reverse split in June 2026, and is pricing a dilutive $11M offering at $1.44/unit in September 2026. Market cap approximately $13.4M.
