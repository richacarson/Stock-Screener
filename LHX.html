Company Profile
Industrials ·Aerospace & Defense ·NYSE ·United States
L3Harris Technologies is a global aerospace and defense technology company providing mission-critical solutions across three segments: Space & Mission Systems (satellite and missile defense), Communications & Spectrum Dominance (tactical radios, broadband, night vision), and Missile Solutions (propulsion, hypersonics). Formed through the 2019 merger of L3 Technologies and Harris Corporation with the 2023 Aerojet Rocketdyne acquisition adding vertical propulsion integration. Q2 2026 revenue reached $5.88B with record backlog of $42B. Headquartered in Melbourne, Florida.

Investment Thesis
L3Harris at 18.26x forward earnings offers compelling defense exposure at a meaningful discount — the stock's 30% decline from ~$350 to $247 has created a valuation entry point well below the 20x forward P/E target while fundamentals continue strengthening [1][4]. Q2 2026 delivered $5.88B revenue (+8.4% YoY), EPS of $3.13 (+28.3%), and $7.3B in orders (1.2x book-to-bill), driving record backlog to $42B (~7.3 quarters of revenue visibility) [1]. Management raised FY2026 guidance to $23.2-23.7B revenue and $11.80-$12.00 EPS with ~$3.0B FCF. International sales surged over 20% as NATO and allied defense spending accelerates, expanding the addressable market beyond US DoD [1]. The Palantir strategic partnership and Golden Dome missile defense program position L3Harris to capture AI-enabled defense modernization, and the $42B backlog provides multi-year revenue visibility.
The bear case centers on decelerating growth and structural profitability challenges. Revenue growth moderated from 12% in Q1 to 8.4% in Q2 2026, and ROE of 9.34% remains well below the 15% target, weighed down by acquisition goodwill from L3, Harris, and Aerojet — any write-down could materially impact returns [4]. Profit margin of 8.17% is below defense peer averages, suggesting the Aerojet integration has not yet delivered expected synergies. The 30% stock price decline may reflect market concern about defense budget sustainability given US fiscal deficit pressures and potential spending caps [1]. Glassdoor reviews describing 'toxic culture' and leadership disconnection in a company requiring high-security-clearance engineering talent could undermine long-term execution [2]. Dividend yield of 1.91% is just below the 2% threshold for income investors despite 23 consecutive years of growth and 44+ years of payments.
