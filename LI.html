Company Profile
Consumer Cyclical ·Auto Manufacturers ·NASDAQ ·China
Li Auto Inc. designs, develops, manufactures, and sells premium smart electric vehicles in China. The company pioneered extended-range electric vehicle (EREV) technology in China, offering the L6, L7, L8, and L9 SUV models alongside the MEGA BEV MPV. Founded in 2015 by Li Xiang, the company has 517 retail stores across 160 cities and operates 4,057 charging stations. Q2 2026 deliveries reached 98,330 vehicles with revenue of RMB 25.7B ($3.8B). CEO Li Xiang is pivoting the company toward AI with half of 10B yuan R&D budget devoted to autonomous driving and robotics. Market cap approximately $12.7B.
