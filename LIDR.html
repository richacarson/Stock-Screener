Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Infrastructure ·NasdaqCM ·United States
AEye, Inc. designs, develops, and commercializes software-defined adaptive LiDAR sensing solutions for autonomous driving, advanced driver-assistance systems (ADAS), smart infrastructure, and industrial applications. The company's proprietary iDAR (Intelligent Detection and Ranging) technology combines software-definable hardware with AI-driven perception to enable safer, more efficient sensing at distances up to 1.5 kilometers. Headquartered in Dublin, California.
56 Employees ·www.aeye.ai

Investment Thesis
AEye represents a high-risk, high-potential bet on the convergence of AI and physical sensing. The company's iDAR technology is genuinely differentiated — software-defined adaptive LiDAR with AI-driven perception at distances up to 1.5 kilometers in a smartphone-sized form factor [2]. The NVIDIA DRIVE AGX integration validates the technology at the platform level, and the EAC 2026 award in Shanghai demonstrates global recognition [2]. Customer count has grown 31% to 21 revenue-generating accounts, the commercial pipeline expanded 40%, and management expects a meaningful revenue inflection in H2 2026 [1]. With $77.2M in cash and targeted annual burn of $30-35M, the company has operational runway well into 2028, providing time for the autonomous vehicle and infrastructure markets to mature [1]. The Inspire Insight score of +54 (ALIGNED) is favorable for faith-based investors.
However, the fundamental challenge is that AEye is essentially a pre-revenue company trading at $1.34/share. Q1 2026 revenue of $101,000 missed estimates by 47%, and full-year 2025 revenue was only $233,000 — these are negligible amounts for a public company [1]. ROE of -75.18% reflects ongoing equity destruction, and the -2.03 Forward P/E confirms the market expects continued losses. The LiDAR industry faces a potential existential threat from Tesla's Pure Vision camera-only approach, which argues LiDAR is unnecessary for autonomous driving. Even within LiDAR, competitors Luminar, Hesai, and Ouster have larger revenue bases and customer deployments. AEye's 56 employees and minimal revenue create concentration risk — the company's fate depends on a small number of program wins converting in H2 2026. If the expected revenue inflection fails to materialize, the $77.2M cash position represents a declining option value rather than a growth runway [1][3].

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience DEVELOPING
6
AI Risk Protection (5/9): AEye's business is fundamentally an AI company — its iDAR technology uses artificial intelligence to dynamically control LiDAR scan patterns and prioritize sensing in real-time [2]. AI is not a threat to the core product but rather IS the core product. However, this creates a different risk: AI advances by competitors could render AEye's specific approach obsolete. Tesla's camera-only Pure Vision approach represents an existential AI-driven competitive threat that could eliminate the need for LiDAR entirely if proven safe and effective at scale. Additionally, larger LiDAR competitors (Luminar, Hesai) have more resources to invest in AI-driven perception improvements. AEye's software-defined architecture provides some protection, as the ability to update and improve sensor behavior through software updates creates a longer product lifecycle than hardware-only competitors. The physical sensor hardware itself requires manufacturing capabilities that create modest barriers. AI Opportunity (1/1): The NVIDIA DRIVE AGX integration validates AEye's AI-driven approach at the platform level, making Apollo part of the industry's dominant autonomous computing ecosystem [2]. The expansion into intelligent transportation systems (OPTIS with Michigan DOT) and smart infrastructure applications demonstrates AI-enabled use cases beyond automotive that could diversify revenue streams. AEye's technology is positioned at the intersection of Physical AI and mobility, where AI capability is the primary value driver.
