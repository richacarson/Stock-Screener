Company Profile
Financial Services ·Asset Management & Business Development Companies ·NASDAQ ·United States
Chicago Atlantic BDC is a specialty finance business development company regulated under the Investment Company Act of 1940, focused on originating direct loans to privately held middle-market cannabis companies. The company manages a $334.8M investment portfolio across 37 companies with a weighted average yield on debt investments of 16.0% and zero non-accrual loans. Externally managed by Chicago Atlantic Group, LIEN shares leadership with sister company REFI and has announced an all-stock merger with REFI expected to close Q4 2026. Q2 2026 net investment income was $7.7M ($0.34/share) with NAV of $13.26/share. Headquartered in Miami Beach, Florida.

Investment Thesis
LIEN offers a 14.41% dividend yield backed by a 100% clean cannabis lending portfolio — zero non-accrual loans across 37 companies — with a weighted average yield on debt investments of 16.0% and ultra-conservative 0.09x leverage [1]. The stock trades at $9.48 versus NAV of $13.26, a 29% discount to net asset value that provides meaningful downside protection. Forward P/E of 6.01x and price-to-book of 0.72x reflect extreme value. The planned all-stock merger with REFI (expected Q4 2026) would create a larger combined cannabis lending platform with ~$790M in total portfolio value, potentially improving liquidity and analyst coverage. Director Gordon Scott's $421K insider purchase in August 2026 at $9.53-$9.57 signals management conviction at current levels [3].
The bear case is dominated by illiquidity and structural fragility. Average daily dollar volume of ~$663K triggers the IOWN liquidity floor, making meaningful position construction and exit impossible without significant market impact [4]. Q2 2026 NII declined 23% sequentially from Q1 ($7.7M vs $10.0M) and the portfolio contracted from $364M to $335M as origination slowed, suggesting the cannabis lending market is tightening. The company's entire business model depends on a regulatory anomaly — federal cannabis prohibition that prevents bank competition — and any move toward federal legalization or rescheduling would invite traditional lenders at dramatically lower yields, compressing LIEN's 16% weighted average yield toward conventional middle-market rates of 8-10% [2]. The $500M shelf registration filed in Q2 2026 introduces dilution risk. NAV per share declined from $13.33 to $13.26 in one quarter, and with 100% payout of NII, the company has no earnings retention to grow NAV. Cannabis industry credit risk remains elevated as operator profitability compresses from state-level oversupply and price deflation.
