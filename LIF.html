Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NASDAQ ·United States
Life360 is the leading family safety and location-sharing platform with 97.8 million monthly active users globally and 3.0 million paying circles. The app provides real-time location sharing, driving safety alerts, crash detection with emergency dispatch, and family coordination tools. Revenue is generated through subscriptions and a growing advertising platform powered by the Nativo acquisition. Founded in 2008, headquartered in San Francisco, California. Dual-listed on NASDAQ (LIF) and ASX (360).

Investment Thesis
Life360 delivered a record Q1 2026 with $143.1M revenue (up 38% YoY), 97.8M MAUs (+17%), and 3.0M paying circles (+27%), prompting raised FY2026 guidance to $650-685M revenue (33-40% growth) and $130-140M adjusted EBITDA (~20% margin) [2]. The company has achieved category dominance in family safety with no direct competitor approaching its scale, and the advertising platform (expected $98-115M in FY2026) provides a high-margin second growth vector following the Nativo acquisition. D/E of 52% and $457M cash position provide balance sheet strength, while ROE of 30.7% and revenue growth exceeding 30% demonstrate capital efficiency at scale. At 31.5x forward P/E with 33%+ revenue growth and strong unit economics (ARPPC up 7% YoY), Life360 offers growth at a reasonable price [5].
The bear case centers on platform dependency, earnings quality, and cultural execution risks. Apple and Google control iOS and Android — native family safety features (Find My, Family Link) could marginalize Life360's core offering without warning [4]. The 28.2% GAAP profit margin is misleading: TTM EBITDA is only $26.2M against $149.2M net income, indicating significant non-operating gains are inflating reported profitability — the ~20% adjusted EBITDA margin is more representative of sustainable economics [5]. Glassdoor reviews describe 'constant restructuring,' a fear-based AI transformation where employees race to automate to avoid being fired, and leadership 'enamored with AI buzzwords' without effective adoption [4]. Average daily volume has declined to 679K shares (from ~1.36M earlier), creating growing liquidity risk. The stock is down 35% from its 52-week high, and the $4.1B market cap requires sustained 30%+ growth to justify the forward multiple [5].
Key Catalysts
  1. FY2026 revenue guidance raised to $650-685M (33-40% growth) driven by subscription ARPPC expansion and advertising platform scaling toward $98-115M, with Q1 2026 momentum suggesting upside to guidance [2]
  2. MAU trajectory toward 115M+ users in 2026 with continued international expansion, deepening the network effect moat as each new family member strengthens switching costs
  3. Adjusted EBITDA margin expansion from ~20% toward 25%+ as the advertising business scales and AI-driven operational efficiencies materialize, demonstrating the platform's operating leverage [2]
Key Risks
  1. Platform dependency on Apple iOS and Google Android — native family safety features could disintermediate Life360's core location-sharing functionality, and App Store policy changes could restrict data collection essential to the advertising business [4]
  2. GAAP earnings quality is questionable: TTM net income of $149M exceeds TTM EBITDA of $26M by 5.7x, suggesting non-operating gains inflate reported profitability — actual operating margins are closer to 20% adjusted EBITDA [5]
  3. Cultural deterioration under CEO Antonoff's AI-native restructuring, with Glassdoor reviews describing fear-based dynamics, constant layoffs, and ineffective AI adoption that could drive talent flight during a critical scaling period [4]
