Liminatus Pharma, Inc. is a pre-clinical stage biopharmaceutical company developing IBA101, a next-generation CD47-blocking humanized monoclonal antibody for advanced solid cancers including non-small cell lung cancer. The company completed an all-stock merger with InnocsAI on July 6, 2026, acquiring a CAR-T cell therapy pipeline at a transaction value of approximately $320M. Transferred from Nasdaq Global Market to Nasdaq Capital Market on August 4, 2026, with bid price compliance extension through September 3, 2026. Founded in 2018, based in Cerritos, California.

Investment Thesis
Liminatus holds a pre-clinical oncology pipeline anchored by IBA101 (next-generation CD47 antibody) and now CAR-T cell therapy assets acquired via the InnocsAI merger that closed July 6, 2026 [2]. The second-generation CD47 approach is engineered to avoid hematological toxicities that hampered first-generation programs. At a $6.3M market cap, any clinical validation could represent significant upside from a de minimis base. The InnocsAI assets add pipeline breadth with contingent value rights for 20% of future net proceeds from strategic transactions.
The overwhelming weight of evidence points to extreme risk across every dimension. The company has 1 employee, zero revenue, accumulated deficit exceeding $40M, D/E of 480%, book value of $0.01/share, and TTM net loss of $12.52M [1]. The InnocsAI merger was structured at $0.20/share (below current $0.14 market price) and closed before stockholder approval, raising governance concerns. Massive share dilution from the merger, prior equity offerings, and the Clear Street settlement have destroyed per-share economics [2]. Nasdaq transferred the stock to Capital Market tier on August 4, 2026 with bid price compliance deadline of September 3, 2026 — delisting is imminent without a reverse stock split or sustained price recovery. The Phase 1 IBA101 trial has not commenced dosing 5 months after announcement. Major pharma competitors abandoned similar CD47 programs after investing exponentially more, and the CAR-T assets' development stage and competitive positioning are unclear. No analyst coverage exists [1].
