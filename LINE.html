Company Profile

Lineage is the world's largest global temperature-controlled warehouse REIT with over 500 strategically located facilities totaling approximately 88 million square feet and 3.1 billion cubic feet of capacity across 19 countries in North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific. Purpose: eliminate food waste and help feed the world. IPO'd in July 2024 as the largest REIT IPO in history ($4.5B raised at $78/share). Q2 2026 revenue of $1.36B, with EPS of $0.76 significantly beating the $0.22 loss consensus. 24 facilities under construction. Market cap ~$8.4B.

Investment Thesis
Lineage is the undisputed global leader in temperature-controlled warehousing with 500+ facilities, ~88M sq ft, and $5.37B in trailing revenue — a dominant scale position in critical food supply chain infrastructure [1]. Q2 2026 delivered a significant positive surprise with EPS of $0.76 beating the $0.22 loss consensus and revenue of $1.36B beating estimates, suggesting operational improvement from the $50M cost reduction plan [3]. At $36.81 (down 53% from the $78 IPO price), the stock trades at approximately 12x guided AFFO ($2.75-3.00/share), a reasonable valuation for industrial REIT assets generating 5.73% dividend yield. The 24 facilities under construction provide capacity expansion, and structural tailwinds from e-commerce grocery, pharmaceutical cold chain, and global protein consumption support long-term demand for temperature-controlled infrastructure. The net zero 2040 commitment and 'eliminate food waste' mission align with faith-based investment criteria (Inspire score 34, ALIGNED).
The bear case is substantial. Lineage is unprofitable on a GAAP basis with -3.1% net margin and -2.0% ROE, driven by $8.5B+ in total debt and massive depreciation from aggressive acquisition accounting [6]. Revenue growth was essentially flat at 0.8% YoY, reflecting occupancy headwinds, lower trade volumes from tariff/trade uncertainty, and throughput pressure — with only 1-2% net pricing increases expected for 2026 [3]. The 53% decline from the $78 IPO price reflects the market's reassessment of growth assumptions, and post-IPO PE selling pressure from Bay Grove Capital may continue to weigh on the stock. D/E of 95% creates significant leverage risk in a rising rate environment. The GAAP-based mechanical scoring framework penalizes REITs inherently, producing an Infrastructure score of 3 that understates the operational cash flow quality (AFFO-based). However, the flat revenue trajectory and massive debt load are genuine concerns regardless of accounting framework. The dividend of $2.11/year (5.73% yield) may face pressure if occupancy does not recover.
