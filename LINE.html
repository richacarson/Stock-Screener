Lineage, Inc. is the world's largest global temperature-controlled warehouse REIT with a network of over 500 strategically located facilities totaling approximately 88 million square feet and 3.1 billion cubic feet of capacity across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific. IPO'd in July 2024 as the largest REIT IPO in history. Q1 2026 revenue was $1.297B with Adjusted EBITDA of $314M and AFFO of $0.78 per share.

Investment Thesis
Lineage is the undisputed global leader in temperature-controlled warehousing with 500+ facilities, ~88M sq ft, and $5.36B in annual revenue — a dominant scale position in critical food supply chain infrastructure [1]. Q1 2026 Adjusted EBITDA of $314M grew 3.3% YoY with AFFO of $0.78/share, and management reaffirmed full-year guidance of $1.25-1.3B EBITDA and $2.75-3.00 AFFO/share [3]. The 5.2% dividend yield provides meaningful income, and structural tailwinds from e-commerce grocery, pharmaceutical cold chain, and global protein consumption growth support long-term demand. The $50M+ cost reduction plan should drive margin improvement, and the AFFO-based valuation of ~14x is reasonable relative to industrial REIT peers.
The bear case is significant. Lineage is unprofitable on a GAAP basis with -1.9% net margin and negative ROE, driven by $8.49B in total debt and massive depreciation charges from aggressive acquisition accounting [6]. Revenue growth was essentially flat at 0.3% in 2025, reflecting occupancy headwinds, lower trade volumes from tariff/trade uncertainty, and throughput pressure [3]. The company faces a warehouse fire controversy in Los Angeles and a lawsuit from over 100 residents near a Benton County facility alleging environmental damage [5]. Post-IPO lock-up dynamics and private equity selling pressure could weigh on the stock. The recent 1% dividend increase signals limited distribution growth capacity given the leveraged balance sheet and flat revenue trajectory.
Key Catalysts
  1. Occupancy and pricing stabilization as trade volumes recover from tariff-induced headwinds driving revenue growth back toward the 4.5% annual forecast and supporting EBITDA margin expansion
  2. The $50M+ administrative cost reduction plan with approximately half realized in 2026 directly improving AFFO and supporting dividend growth acceleration
  3. Growing pharmaceutical cold chain requirements and e-commerce grocery penetration driving structural demand for temperature-controlled warehouse capacity in Lineage's existing network
Key Risks
  1. Revenue growth stalled at 0.3% with occupancy and throughput pressured by lower trade volumes — any further deterioration could force dividend reduction given the $8.49B debt load and negative GAAP profitability
  2. Warehouse fire liabilities in Los Angeles and Benton County with 100+ lawsuits alleging environmental damage and negligence creating potential for significant legal costs and reputational harm
  3. Post-IPO private equity selling pressure from Bay Grove Capital and limited dividend growth capacity constrain total return potential while the stock trades at premium AFFO multiples relative to peers with better growth profiles
