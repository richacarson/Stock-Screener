Lockheed Martin Corporation is the world's largest defense contractor, operating across four segments: Aeronautics (F-35, F-16, C-130J), Missiles and Fire Control (THAAD, Javelin, HIMARS), Rotary and Mission Systems (Sikorsky, Aegis), and Space (Orion, GPS satellites). Investment Thesis
Lockheed Martin is the world's largest defense contractor executing at the highest level in its history — Q2 2026 sales grew 9% YoY with operating profit surging $858M, MFC delivered 19% sales growth and 24% profit growth, and the company accumulated a record $230.4B backlog (up $64B from a year earlier) with an extraordinary 3.2x Q2 book-to-bill ratio [1][2]. This backlog provides 5-7 years of government-backed cash flow visibility, underpinning the 23-year consecutive dividend growth streak (2.27% yield, $13.80 annual). The F-35 remains the world's only operational 5th-gen fighter available to allies, with production volumes driving Aeronautics growth, while the $35B THAAD contract and AI-powered autonomous systems (Lamprey, DARPA AIR) position the company at the center of the global defense spending super-cycle [2][3]. At 19.66x forward earnings, LMT trades at a reasonable premium for the highest-quality franchise in defense.
The bear case centers on profitability constraints, leverage, and the structural limitations of defense contracting. Profit margin of 8.16%, while improved from 6.69%, reflects the cost-plus contract model that caps upside in an era of accelerating revenue growth [4]. D/E of 234%, though improved from 339%, remains elevated with significant debt from acquisitions and share buybacks. The payout ratio has risen to 74.32%, limiting room for aggressive dividend acceleration or debt reduction. Revenue growth has been structurally modest (3% 5-year CAGR) despite the current acceleration, and Q1 2026 experienced negative free cash flow of -$291M from ERP implementation issues that could recur during the multi-year 1LMX system migration [1]. The stock has rallied 37.5% over the past year to $598, compressing the margin of safety.
