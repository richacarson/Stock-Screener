(formerly Renovaro Inc.) is a pre-clinical stage biotechnology company developing dendritic cell therapeutic vaccines (RENB-DC11 for pancreatic tumors, RENB-DC20 for breast cancer) and an AI-powered multi-cancer early detection platform. The company rebranded from Renovaro in August 2025, executed a 1-for-8 reverse stock split in May 2026, and has a market capitalization of approximately $4.9M with 29 employees.
