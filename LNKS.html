Linkers Industries Limited, through its subsidiary TEM Electronics (M) Sdn. Bhd., manufactures wire and cable harnesses, connectors and assemblies for home-appliance, industrial and automotive OEMs, with customers in Malaysia, Thailand, Switzerland, the United States and elsewhere. Founded in 1995, it reported FY2026 (June year-end) revenue of about 22.6M (Malaysian ringgit per company reporting). Yahoo Finance records a 1-for-250 share consolidation in April 2026, after repeated Nasdaq minimum-bid deficiency notices; market capitalization is below $1M.

Investment Thesis
The only constructive points are a three-decade operating history at TEM Electronics and a balance sheet with more cash (18.9M) than debt (8.1M) after recent offerings [6]. Revenue edged up 2.9% in FY2026 [6], and any recovery in appliance or automotive harness demand could improve utilization at its Malaysian plants.
The bear case dominates. Revenue has fallen from 32.5M in FY2022 to 22.6M, the profit margin is -33.7% [6], and a reported net loss of RM7.6M in FY2026 was flattered by a RM26.6M non-cash warrant gain [2]. After a 1-for-250 reverse split in April 2026 the shares are back at $0.59, giving a market cap under $1M [6]. Nasdaq rules limit further reverse splits after a cumulative 250:1 ratio [4], so delisting risk is acute. About 85% of revenue is concentrated among a few customers [2], and median daily trading of ~$21K makes the stock effectively untradeable for IOWN [6].
Key Catalysts
  1. Restoring the $1 minimum bid without another reverse split, which Nasdaq rules constrain after a cumulative 250:1 ratio [4]
  2. New OEM wins that reduce the ~85% revenue concentration [2]
  3. Using the net-cash balance sheet (18.9M cash vs 8.1M debt) to reach breakeven [6]
Key Risks
  1. Nasdaq delisting: repeated bid-price deficiencies since March 2025 and limits on further reverse splits [3][4]
  2. Continued dilution from offerings and warrants, with the share price back below $1 after a 1-for-250 consolidation [4][6]
  3. Customer concentration of ~85% of revenue and rising cash burn [2]
  4. Revenue down ~30% from FY2022 levels with a -33.7% profit margin [6]
