Company Profile
Healthcare ·Drug Manufacturers - Specialty & Generic ·NGM ·United States
Lantheus Holdings develops, manufactures, and commercializes diagnostic and therapeutic radiopharmaceuticals and AI-powered imaging solutions. Lead product PYLARIFY (piflufolastat F18) is the first and only FDA-approved PSMA PET imaging agent for prostate cancer. Legacy franchise DEFINITY is the leading echocardiography ultrasound enhancer. The company operates under its 'Find, Fight and Follow' mission. Q2 2026 revenue of $388.2M (+2.7% YoY) with FY2026 adjusted EPS guidance of $5.00-$5.25. Agreed to be acquired by Curium for $102.50/share plus up to $12.00 CVRs (~$8B total). Founded in 1956 and headquartered in Bedford, Massachusetts.
1,193 Employees ·www.lantheus.com

Investment Thesis
Lantheus has executed one of the most impressive business transformations in healthcare, growing revenue from ~$425M in 2021 to $1.56B TTM through the PYLARIFY launch, which created a near-monopoly in FDA-approved PSMA PET imaging for prostate cancer [1][2]. At $99.65, the stock trades with the Curium acquisition floor at $102.50/share plus up to $12.00 CVRs (total ~$8B enterprise value). The company generates 22.2% ROE and maintains conservative leverage (D/E 47.6%) with $593M cash against $625M debt [6]. Forward P/E of 15.6x, PYLARIFY TRUVU launching Q4 2026, and a deep pipeline (PNT2002, PNT2003, MK-6240, flurpiridaz) with CVR milestones targeting >$950M prostate diagnostics and >$300M neurology diagnostics by 2030 provide multiple growth vectors [2][4].
The primary near-term concern is PYLARIFY pricing erosion and growth deceleration. Q1 and Q2 2026 saw modest revenue growth of 1.2% and 2.7% YoY respectively, as ~12% price declines offset volume growth in the core franchise [1]. FY2026 adjusted EPS guidance of $5.00-$5.25 implies stable but unexciting earnings growth. The Curium acquisition at $102.50/share, while providing downside certainty, may undervalue the long-term theragnostics opportunity, and the deal caps upside at $114.50/share including CVRs [4]. CEO Markison's retirement after 13 years, replaced by Mary Anne Heino in January 2026, creates leadership discontinuity during a critical integration period. Glassdoor culture ratings have declined 28% to 2.6/5, well below the pharmaceutical industry average, signaling organizational stress from the transformation and pending acquisition [3]. Animal testing involvement noted in ESG assessments adds ESG risk for screened investors [5].

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
9
AI Risk Protection (8/9): Lantheus's core business — manufacturing and distributing radioactive diagnostic agents and therapeutic radiopharmaceuticals — is structurally immune to AI disruption [3]. PYLARIFY requires physical injection of a radioactive tracer (piflufolastat F18) into patients for PSMA PET imaging; no AI system can replicate the physics of positron emission tomography or the molecular targeting of PSMA-expressing cells. Manufacturing requires cyclotrons, radioisotope handling expertise, and cGMP compliance at specialized nuclear facilities — capabilities that are capital-intensive, heavily regulated, and fundamentally physical. AI actually increases the value of PYLARIFY scans by enabling better image interpretation and clinical decision-making. AI Opportunity (1/1): PYLARIFY AI and aPROMISE are FDA-cleared AI medical device software products generating licensing and service revenue while reinforcing adoption of the Lantheus imaging ecosystem. Automated Bone Scan Index uses artificial neural networks for prostate cancer bone metastasis assessment [3].
