Company Profile
Healthcare ·Drug Manufacturers - Specialty & Generic ·NASDAQ ·United States
Lantheus Holdings develops, manufactures, and commercializes diagnostic and therapeutic radiopharmaceuticals and imaging agents. Lead product PYLARIFY (piflufolastat F18) is the first and only FDA-approved PSMA PET imaging agent for prostate cancer. Legacy franchise DEFINITY is used in echocardiography. The company operates under its 'Find, Fight and Follow' mission — finding disease with diagnostic imaging, fighting disease with targeted radiotherapy, and following disease progression. Agreed to be acquired by Curium for $102.50/share plus up to $12.00 CVRs (total ~$8B), announced August 3, 2026.
1,193 Employees ·www.lantheus.com

Investment Thesis
Lantheus has executed one of the most impressive business transformations in healthcare, growing revenue from ~$425M in 2021 to $1.56B TTM through the PYLARIFY launch, which created a near-monopoly in FDA-approved PSMA PET imaging for prostate cancer [1][2]. The company generates $388M free cash flow with a healthy 22.2% ROE, 48% D/E, and forward P/E of 18.14, reflecting strong financial fundamentals [6]. The Curium acquisition at $102.50/share plus up to $12.00 CVRs validates the franchise value at up to ~$8B. PYLARIFY TRUVU (new formulation) launching Q4 2026, expanding neurology diagnostics, and therapeutic radiopharmaceutical pipeline provide multiple growth vectors, with CVR milestones targeting >$950M prostate cancer diagnostics and >$300M neurology diagnostics by 2030 [4].
The primary near-term concern is PYLARIFY pricing pressure — Q1 and Q2 2026 saw 6.5% and 4.1% PYLARIFY revenue declines respectively as ~12% price erosion offset 5.8% volume growth, compressing margins on the core franchise [1]. Revenue growth has decelerated sharply from the PYLARIFY ramp phase to just 2.3% TTM, and the full-year 2026 guidance of $1.4-1.45B implies mid-single-digit growth at best. The Curium acquisition at $102.50/share, while offering certainty, may undervalue the long-term potential of the theragnostics pipeline — the CVRs provide upside but cap the total return at $114.50/share [4]. CEO Markison's retirement after 13 years creates leadership uncertainty during a critical transition period, and integration with Curium introduces execution risk on a combined $8B+ enterprise.
Key Catalysts
  1. PYLARIFY TRUVU (new formulation) phased launch in Q4 2026 could reinvigorate the franchise by improving manufacturing efficiency and potentially stabilizing pricing dynamics
  2. Curium acquisition completing in H1 2027 at $102.50/share provides a definitive floor, with CVRs offering up to $12.00 additional per share tied to prostate cancer diagnostics exceeding $950M and neurology exceeding $300M by 2030
  3. Expansion into therapeutic radiopharmaceuticals through Evergreen Theragnostics acquisition, advancing the 'Fight' pillar of the Find-Fight-Follow mission into a multi-billion dollar theragnostics market
Key Risks
  1. PYLARIFY pricing erosion of ~12% is only partially offset by 5.8% volume growth, creating a structural revenue headwind that could compress margins if pricing pressure accelerates from payer negotiations or competitor entry
  2. CEO Brian Markison's retirement after 13 years creates leadership continuity risk during the Curium integration — the transformation architect is departing as the company enters its most complex organizational change
  3. Curium acquisition at $102.50 + CVRs caps shareholder upside and subjects the deal to antitrust scrutiny and regulatory approval risk, with an Outside Date extendable to November 2027 creating prolonged uncertainty

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
9
AI Risk Protection (8/9): Lantheus's core business — manufacturing and distributing radioactive diagnostic agents and therapeutic radiopharmaceuticals — is structurally immune to AI disruption [3]. PYLARIFY requires physical injection of a radioactive tracer (piflufolastat F18) into patients for PSMA PET imaging; no AI system can replicate the physics of positron emission tomography or the molecular targeting of PSMA-expressing cells. Manufacturing requires cyclotrons, radioisotope handling expertise, and cGMP compliance at specialized nuclear facilities — capabilities that are capital-intensive, heavily regulated, and fundamentally physical. AI actually increases the value of PYLARIFY scans by enabling better image interpretation and clinical decision-making [3]. Supply chain concentration in limited radioisotope suppliers is a business risk but not an AI disruption risk. AI Opportunity (1/1): AI-integrated image analysis software for PSMA PET imaging creates licensing and service revenue while reinforcing adoption of the Lantheus imaging ecosystem, demonstrating meaningful AI-derived revenue contribution [3].
