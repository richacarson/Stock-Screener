Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Other Precious Metals & Mining ·NYSE American ·United States
Comstock Inc. is a systemic decarbonization company transitioning from legacy Nevada mining operations to clean technology platforms including solar panel recycling (Comstock Metals), renewable fuels production (Bioleum Corporation), and AI-driven materials discovery (GenMat). The company's first industry-scale solar panel recycling facility in northern Nevada is designed to handle 3.3 million panels (100,000 tons) annually, with operations beginning Q2 2026. Market cap of approximately $298M with 75.9M shares outstanding.
45 Employees ·www.comstock.inc

Investment Thesis
Comstock represents a speculative bet on the intersection of solar panel recycling, renewable fuels, and AI-driven materials discovery — secular clean technology themes with strong tailwinds. The company's first industry-scale solar recycling facility (3.3M panels, 100K tons annual capacity) is beginning operations in Q2 2026, with a second Nevada site selected at $14M capex each [1][2]. Bioleum renewable fuels technology claims >100 gallons per ton feedstock-to-fuel yield versus industry standard 40-60 gallons, targeting eventual standalone public company outcome. The $57.5M oversubscribed equity raise (net $53.1M) brought 30+ new institutional investors and provides $53M cash at March 31, 2026 [2]. CEO De Gasperis' 15+ year tenure provides strategic continuity. The Inspire Impact Score of +55 (ALIGNED) with zero negative attributions and eight positive ESG factors makes this one of the most faith-compatible investments in the clean technology space.
The bear case is substantial. Revenue remains negligible at $1.6M trailing twelve months with a -2,778% net profit margin and persistent operating losses exceeding $13M annually [4]. The $297M market cap values the company at approximately 185x trailing revenue, requiring extraordinary revenue ramp-up to justify the valuation. The $57.5M equity raise diluted existing shareholders to 75.9M shares outstanding [2]. Solar panel recycling economics are unproven at commercial scale — processing costs, metals recovery yields, and end-market pricing for recovered materials all carry significant uncertainty. A 45-employee company attempting multi-segment growth across solar recycling, renewable fuels, AI materials discovery, and mining asset monetization is stretched extremely thin operationally. Active monetization of legacy mining assets with definitive agreements expected Q3 2026 represents potential one-time proceeds but does not validate the recurring business model. Suitable only for highly risk-tolerant investors with multi-year time horizons who believe in the decarbonization thesis.

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
7
AI Risk Protection (7/9): Comstock's core business segments involve physical-world industrial operations that AI cannot substitute. Solar panel recycling requires physical dismantling, sorting, and materials recovery — processes involving manual labor and specialized equipment that are structurally immune to digital disruption [1]. Bioleum renewable fuels production is chemical engineering that requires physical biomass processing. Mining asset monetization involves physical land and mineral rights. The company's physical-world operations would likely benefit from AI optimization (yield improvement, process efficiency) rather than face AI substitution risk. The GenMat AI materials discovery platform could face competitive pressure from AI-powered research tools at larger companies, but this is a secondary business line. AI Opportunity (0/1): While GenMat uses physics-based AI for materials discovery and CEO De Gasperis has stated 'anyone not integrating AI into their core competencies will likely be disrupted or replaced,' no specific AI-attributable revenue figures have been disclosed [3].
