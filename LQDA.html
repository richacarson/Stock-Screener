Company Profile
Healthcare ·Drug Manufacturers - Specialty & Generic ·NASDAQ ·United States
Liquidia Corporation is a biopharmaceutical company focused on developing and commercializing inhaled therapies for patients with pulmonary hypertension. Its lead product, YUTREPIA, is a dry-powder inhaled formulation of treprostinil approved for PAH and PH-ILD, leveraging Liquidia's proprietary PRINT technology for precision drug particle engineering. Q1 2026 net product sales hit $132.9M (up 4,162% YoY), with annualized net revenue run rate exceeding $500M in less than one year on market [1]. Analysts forecast $705.3M in 2026 revenue with clear line of sight to $1B+ by 2027 [2]. The company also develops L606, a liposomal treprostinil formulation in Phase III trials.
216 Employees ·www.liquidia.com

Investment Thesis
Liquidia represents a rare biotech success story transitioning from pre-revenue to profitable compounder at compelling valuation. Q1 2026 net product sales of $132.9M (up 4,162% YoY) demonstrate accelerating YUTREPIA adoption, with annualized run rate exceeding $500M in under a year on market [1][2]. The company delivered its third consecutive quarter of profitability with a 30.7% profit margin, $284M cash, and growing free cash flow of $74.3M [4]. At 11.56x forward earnings on analyst estimates of $705.3M in 2026 revenue ($3.26 EPS), Liquidia offers attractive growth-adjusted valuation with management visibility to $1B+ in net revenue by 2027 [2]. The recent appeals court victory overturning a district court patent ruling strengthens the IP position against United Therapeutics [3]. D/E has improved from 170% to 93.7% as the equity base rebuilds through profitability.
The primary risk is single-product concentration — YUTREPIA generates essentially all revenue, creating binary risk from any regulatory, manufacturing, or competitive setback [3]. The stock has declined from ~$80 to $65.31, but even at the lower price, significant growth is priced in — any deceleration in YUTREPIA adoption or pricing pressure from United Therapeutics' established Tyvaso franchise could compress the multiple. The L606 pipeline program (Phase III) is years from potential commercial contribution, leaving the revenue base undiversified. Profit margin declined from 40% to 30.7% quarter-over-quarter, potentially reflecting sales force expansion costs and increasing commercial spend [4]. At 216 employees, the company faces execution risk in scaling commercial operations to support a $1B revenue target. The Inspire Impact Score of 17 (MIXED) is neutral for faith-based investors.

  3. Patent litigation risk persists despite the recent appeals court victory — United Therapeutics has deeper legal and financial resources and may pursue further challenges to YUTREPIA's IP position [3]
