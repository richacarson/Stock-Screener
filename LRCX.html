Investment Thesis
Lam Research at $260.81 (21.56x forward earnings) is a premium semiconductor equipment compounder riding the largest WFE investment cycle in history — FY2026 delivered record revenue of $23.23B (+26% YoY) with record 52% gross margins, followed by a September quarter of $8.22B that beat estimates and implies a $33B+ annual run rate, a dramatic step-change from the current fiscal year [1]. The AI buildout is structurally expanding Lam's addressable market: HBM requires 3-4x more etch/deposition steps, GAA transistors increase process complexity, and advanced packaging adds entirely new equipment layers. D/E of 33% with low leverage provides balance sheet optionality, while the 20% payout ratio and 15% 5-year dividend growth CAGR ensure reliable income compounding. The stock's 17% pullback from $315 to $261 has compressed the forward P/E from 36.6x to 21.6x, dramatically improving the risk-reward as earnings catch up to the lower price [1][2].
The bear case centers on cyclicality and concentration risk. The semiconductor equipment industry is inherently cyclical, and the current AI investment boom may prove front-loaded, with customer capacity additions outpacing end-market demand — WFE spending has historically experienced 20-30% peak-to-trough declines [1]. The 17% stock decline from $315 may reflect early signs of investor concern about sustainability of the AI capex cycle. U.S.-China export restrictions limit Lam's access to Chinese customers (historically 25-30% of industry revenue), and further tightening could accelerate Chinese domestic equipment development from NAURA and AMEC [3].
