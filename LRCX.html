Company Profile
Technology ·Semiconductor Equipment & Materials ·NASDAQ ·United States
Lam Research designs, manufactures, and services semiconductor processing equipment used in the fabrication of integrated circuits. The company is a global leader in etch, deposition, and clean technologies — nearly every advanced chip is built using Lam equipment. FY2026 (ending June 2026) delivered record revenue of $23.23B (+26% YoY), record EPS of $5.82 (+41%), and record Q4 gross margin of 52%. CEO Tim Archer restructured leadership in February 2026, elevating Sesha Varadarajan to COO. September quarter guidance of $8.1B (+20% sequential) signals continued acceleration driven by AI semiconductor investment.

Investment Thesis
Lam Research at 36.55x forward earnings is a premium semiconductor equipment compounder riding the largest WFE investment cycle in history — FY2026 delivered record revenue of $23.23B (+26% YoY) with record gross margins of 52% and EPS of $5.82 (+41%), while September quarter guidance of $8.1B implies a $32B+ annual run rate, a step-change from the current fiscal year [1]. The AI buildout is structurally expanding Lam's addressable market: HBM production requires 3-4x more etch/deposition steps, GAA transistors increase process complexity, and advanced packaging adds entirely new equipment layers. D/E of 35% with $5.6B in cash provides balance sheet optionality, while the 18.5% payout ratio and 14.9% 5-year dividend growth CAGR ensure reliable income growth. Management's description of FY2027 as 'extraordinary' and long-term margin targets of mid-50s% gross and mid-40s% operating margins suggest the earnings trajectory is still early-cycle.
The bear case centers on valuation compression and cyclicality. Forward P/E of 36.55x prices in sustained acceleration — any deceleration in WFE spending or customer pushouts could trigger meaningful multiple compression, as occurred in the FY2023-2024 downturn when revenue declined 14% from FY2022 peaks [4]. The semiconductor equipment industry is inherently cyclical, and the current AI investment boom may prove front-loaded, with customer capacity additions outpacing end-market demand. U.S.-China export restrictions limit Lam's access to Chinese customers (historically 25-30% of industry revenue), and further tightening could accelerate Chinese domestic equipment development [3].
