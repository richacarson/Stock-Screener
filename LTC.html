Company Profile
Real Estate ·REIT - Healthcare Facilities ·NYSE ·United States
LTC Properties, Inc. is a real estate investment trust investing in seniors housing and health care properties through its senior housing operating portfolio (SHOP), triple-net leases, joint ventures, and structured finance solutions. The portfolio includes nearly 190 properties across the United States, approximately 70% seniors housing and 30% skilled nursing. The company is executing a transformative pivot from a passive triple-net lease REIT to an active SHOP operator, with $353M in SHOP acquisitions completed in 2025 and $400-800M targeted for 2026 [1]. SHOP as a percentage of gross investments is projected to grow from 29% to 45% by year-end 2026. Founded in 1992, headquartered in Westlake Village, California.

Investment Thesis
LTC Properties is executing a bold strategic transformation from triple-net lease REIT to active SHOP operator, capitalizing on what management calls the coming 'Golden Age' of senior living driven by baby boomer demographics [1]. Revenue grew 65.5% YoY (TTM) to $340M driven by SHOP resident fees, with earnings growth of 75% and Q4 2025 earnings up 96.4% YoY [3]. The company completed $353M in SHOP acquisitions in 2025 with 22% NOI growth, and targets $400-800M for 2026 to bring SHOP to 45% of investments and 40% of NOI [1]. The 5.36% dividend yield, monthly payment schedule, and 34-year consecutive payment track record (since 1992) provide reliable income, while the SHOP growth pivot offers upside potential. The Inspire Impact Score of 41 (ALIGNED) with seven positive attributions makes LTC an attractive option for faith-based investors in the senior care sector.
The primary risk is execution on the SHOP transformation — LTC is fundamentally changing its business model from passive rent collection to active facility operations. Forward P/E of 27.0x is elevated relative to the triple-net REIT universe, reflecting growth expectations that must be delivered [3]. The payout ratio of 81.4% leaves limited cushion for distribution safety if SHOP execution falters, and the dividend has been flat at $2.28/year for five consecutive years (2021-2025), producing 0% dividend growth CAGR — a significant drag on the Infrastructure score. Healthcare REIT operational risks including labor shortages, occupancy sensitivity, and regulatory changes are amplified in the SHOP model where LTC bears operating risk rather than passing it to tenants. The company's small scale (~190 properties, 25 employees) compared to Welltower limits negotiating leverage [1][2].

  1. SHOP portfolio reaching 45% of gross investments by year-end 2026 through $400-800M in projected acquisitions, transforming the revenue mix toward higher-growth operating income from resident fees [1]

  3. Small scale relative to Welltower and Ventas limits negotiating leverage and portfolio diversification — concentrated SHOP bets in specific geographies create outsized single-market exposure [1]
