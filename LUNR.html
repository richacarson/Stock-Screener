Investment Thesis
Intuitive Machines occupies a unique position as the most prolific commercial lunar landing company and an emerging space prime contractor, with Q2 2026 delivering record revenue of $206.2M (4x+ YoY) and a record $1.8B backlog [1]. Revenue diversification is accelerating — national security jumped from 3% to 30% of Q2 revenue in one year, with the July AMDT-3 Golden Dome award for 18 satellites bringing total IM 300-series spacecraft under contract past 70 units. Gross margin inflected dramatically from negative to 17.4% in Q2, generating $35.9M in positive gross profit [1]. The backlog provides multi-year revenue visibility: 25-30% converting in 2026, 35-40% in 2027. Management reaffirmed $900M-$1B 2026 revenue guidance with positive adjusted EBITDA expected. The Inspire Insight score of 37 (ALIGNED) and the company's role in returning America to the Moon and supporting national defense infrastructure make this an attractive values-aligned holding.
The bear case centers on extreme execution risk: the nearly 5x revenue growth guidance depends on successful Lanteris integration and large contract deliveries, and LUNR stock has declined ~13% recently after mission setbacks raised concerns about future revenue potential [4]. The company remains deeply unprofitable with -32.7% profit margin and -30.3% ROE, and adjusted EBITDA was still negative $14M in Q2 despite revenue improvement [1]. Founder Ghaffarian's continued share sales ($1.74M in August 2026, prior sales of several million) signal insider monetization rather than accumulation [4]. Balance sheet fragility persists with $455M total debt against $232M cash, and further dilution is possible. Government budget risk is real — NASA and DoD contracts are subject to congressional appropriation, and the IDIQ ceiling values ($6.2B, $151B) represent maximum potential rather than guaranteed revenue. The IM-3 lunar mission planned for late 2026 carries meaningful execution risk given the complexity of lunar operations.
