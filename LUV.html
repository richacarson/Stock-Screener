Investment Thesis
Southwest Airlines offers a compelling transformation-driven earnings inflection at an attractive valuation. Q2 2026 delivered record operating revenues of $8.4B (+16.4% YoY) with adjusted EPS of $0.94, the first full quarter where all major commercial initiatives — assigned seating, premium options, bag fees, and expanded distribution — contributed throughout [1]. At 12.9x forward P/E and PEG of 0.33, the stock is priced below legacy carrier multiples despite management guiding adjusted EPS of $3.25-$4.25 for 2026. Adjusted operating margin expanded 3.3 percentage points YoY to 6.7% in Q2 despite an $889M increase in nominal fuel costs, demonstrating the revenue power of the transformation. The AWS partnership for AI and cloud modernization, $1.7B technology investment, and Starlink Wi-Fi rollout on 300+ aircraft by year-end provide additional operational efficiency and customer experience drivers [3]. Q3 2026 guidance of 17.5%-19.5% unit revenue growth signals continued momentum.
The bear case centers on execution risk in the most complex transformation in airline history. The Q3 2026 EPS guidance of $0.50-$0.75 implies a wide range reflecting seasonal fuel cost uncertainty and the ongoing transformation rollout [1]. Abandoning 54-year traditions (open seating, free bags) risks alienating loyal customers, and the plus-size seating policy controversy has generated public backlash, highlighting social governance risk [2]. Profit margin of 2.8% remains thin even as margins improve, leaving minimal buffer for execution stumbles or fuel price spikes. Boeing 737 MAX delivery delays could constrain fleet growth, and Southwest's all-Boeing fleet creates single-supplier concentration risk. The first-ever management layoffs and activist investor involvement create organizational tension during a period requiring exceptional execution. ESG bonus metrics for 2025-2026 executive compensation have drawn attention as a governance concern, and the Inspire Insight score of -49 (MISALIGNED) creates headwinds for values-aligned investors.
