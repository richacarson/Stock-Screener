Company Profile
Financial Services ·Credit Services ·NASDAQ ·China
LexinFintech Holdings Ltd. is a Chinese fintech company providing online consumer finance services primarily to young adults through its Fenqile installment e-commerce platform and technology-empowerment services to financial institution partners. The AI-driven risk management engine processes 98% of loan applications automatically. Q1 2026 revenue of RMB3.31B (+6.6% YoY) but Q2 saw net profit down 36% YoY despite 19% revenue growth, and management warns Q3 2026 loan originations will decrease significantly with a potential net loss. Stock has fallen 75%+ over the past year to $0.75 per ADR amid regulatory tightening and credit concerns. Market cap ~$123M.

The risks are formidable and justify the extreme valuation discount. The $123M market cap and sub-$1 stock price place the ADR in penny stock territory with limited institutional ownership and analyst coverage.
