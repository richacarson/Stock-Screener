Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Chemicals ·NYQ ·United States
LSB Industries manufactures and sells nitrogen-based chemical products for agricultural, industrial, and energy transition markets. Primary products include ammonia, urea ammonium nitrate (UAN), ammonium nitrate, and nitric acid produced at three facilities in Arkansas, Oklahoma, and Alabama. FY2026 TTM revenue is approximately $658M with trailing 12-month adjusted EBITDA of $200M and net debt leverage of 1.1x. The company is developing carbon capture capabilities at its El Dorado facility. Founded in 1968, headquartered in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma.

Investment Thesis
LSB Industries at $9.76 is executing a compelling operational turnaround with Q1 2026 EPS of $0.27 (145% above consensus) and Q2 adjusted EBITDA of $53M (up 40% YoY), generating $200M trailing 12-month EBITDA on improved plant reliability and disciplined commercial execution [1][2]. At Forward P/E of 9.57 with PEG of 0.42, $220M cash, and net leverage of just 1.1x, the stock is deeply undervalued relative to forward earnings power. The El Dorado carbon capture project (Q1 2027, $25-30M annual earnings) and planned ammonia expansion ($20M incremental EBITDA) provide visible earnings growth catalysts totaling $50M in additional annual EBITDA. Favorable market conditions — tight global nitrogen supply, strong ammonium nitrate demand for mining/construction, and emerging demand from AI data center infrastructure — support pricing above mid-cycle levels [1][2].
The bear case centers on commodity cyclicality and thin margins. LSB's revenue collapsed from $902M in 2022 to $522M in 2024 as nitrogen prices normalized, demonstrating the earnings volatility inherent in a business with limited pricing power [1]. Net profit margin of 5.6% is thin and highly sensitive to natural gas input costs — management noted risks from natural gas price spikes driven by 'Gulf Coast industrial demand, AI data centers, and power generation' [2]. The stock's decline from $17.22 to $9.76 (43% drawdown from 52-week high) despite improving fundamentals suggests the market questions the durability of the current upcycle. Glassdoor ratings of 35% recommend and 2.7/5 culture score indicate organizational challenges in a business where plant safety and operational reliability depend on workforce engagement. Carbon capture execution risk is real — El Dorado is LSB's first CCS initiative with no proven operational track record [1][2].
Key Catalysts

  2. Natural gas input cost exposure — AI data center and LNG export demand for natural gas could spike feedstock costs, compressing margins in a business where gas represents the largest cost component [2]
  3. Organizational culture challenges with Glassdoor ratings of 35% recommend and 2.7/5 culture score, creating operational risk at chemical plants where safety and reliability depend on workforce engagement and trust [2]
