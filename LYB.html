Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Specialty Chemicals ·NYQ ·Netherlands
LyondellBasell Industries is one of the world's largest plastics, chemicals, and refining companies — the largest producer of polypropylene and a top-three polyethylene producer. Operating across Olefins and Polyolefins (Americas and Europe/Asia), Intermediates and Derivatives, Advanced Polymer Solutions, and Technology segments. CEO Peter Vanacker is executing a strategic transformation: the Houston refinery was shut down in Q1 2025, European O&P assets were divested in Q2 2026 (at a $734M loss), and the company cut its dividend by 50% in early 2026. Q1 2026 EBITDA was $568M ($615M adjusted) as petrochemical margins recovered from Middle East conflict supply disruptions removing ~6M tons of polyethylene capacity (20-25% of regional supply), not expected to restart before 2027.
