Company Profile
Industrials ·Specialty Business Services ·NASDAQ ·United States
LegalZoom.com, Inc. is an online platform providing legal, compliance and tax solutions primarily to small businesses and consumers in the United States, including business formation, registered agent and compliance subscriptions, estate-planning documents and access to independent attorneys. Founded in 1999 and headquartered in Glendale, California, it generated $756M of revenue in FY2025. Jeff Stibel, a ~5% holder and board chair, became CEO in July 2024.

Investment Thesis
LegalZoom is cheap on cash flow: ~$125M of free cash flow on a ~$1.15B market cap, $167M of cash against $15M of debt, and a forward P/E of 8.3 [8]. Subscriptions — now about two-thirds of revenue — grew 11% in Q2 2026 to $133.4M, and adjusted EBITDA guidance of $190–195M implies 12% growth and a 24% margin despite lower revenue guidance [1]. A ~5% owner-CEO has an incentive to drive per-share value [6].
The core risk is that generative AI is dismantling LegalZoom's customer-acquisition engine and commoditizing its documents. FY2026 revenue guidance has fallen from $805–825M to $795–805M, Q3 guidance implies just 2% growth, and transaction revenue is shrinking [1]. GAAP margins are only 2.1% and FMP's forward PEG is 5.0 [8][9]. The 13% layoff signals cost defense rather than offense, and recurring UPL litigation remains a background legal risk [7].
Key Catalysts
  1. Q3 2026 results versus $192–196M revenue guidance (2% growth at midpoint) and evidence that subscription growth (+11% in Q2) is holding [1]
  2. Delivery of the $190–195M adjusted EBITDA outlook after the 13% workforce reduction [1]
  3. Growth in traffic referred by AI assistants, which management called limited but expected to grow [4]
  4. Capital return funded by ~$125M annual free cash flow and a net-cash balance sheet [8]
Key Risks
  1. Customer discovery shifting from Google search to AI assistants, already cited for the 2026 guidance cut [1]
  2. Commoditization of LLC formation and estate documents as AI tools generate them free or at low cost
  3. Transaction revenue declining (-1% in Q2 2026) faster than subscriptions can offset [1]
  4. Ongoing unauthorized-practice-of-law litigation, including the 2024 New Jersey class action sent to arbitration in 2025 [7]
