Company Profile
Industrials ·Staffing & Employment Services ·NYSE ·United States
ManpowerGroup is a global workforce solutions company operating under the Manpower (temporary and permanent staffing), Experis (IT professional resourcing), and Talent Solutions (RPO and managed services) brands across 75 countries. Founded in 1948 in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, the company serves as a labor market intermediary, connecting workers with employers across industrial, administrative, and technology sectors. Q2 2026 revenue was $4.9B (+8% YoY) with net earnings of $1.13/share. The company is executing a $200M cost savings transformation program and sold its Jefferson Wells U.S. business for $100M. However, trailing profit margin of 0.6% and a 53% dividend cut in 2025 reflect the severe earnings compression in global staffing markets. Market cap of approximately $2.7B.

Investment Thesis
ManpowerGroup at 11.8x forward earnings offers deep value optionality on a global staffing market recovery, with Q2 2026 revenue of $4.9B (+8% YoY) and EPS swinging to $1.13 from a loss of $1.44 a year earlier [1]. The $200M cost savings program by 2028 and $100M Jefferson Wells sale demonstrate management focus on margin improvement, and forward PEG of 0.94 suggests the market is pricing in modest growth at a reasonable multiple [6]. D/E of 67.4% is conservative, providing balance sheet flexibility. With 30+ years of consecutive dividend payments and a 2.5% yield, the company maintains income appeal despite the 2025 cut. Strong revenue growth was observed in the United States, Latin America, and select European markets (Italy, Spain, Poland) suggesting geographic diversification is providing growth pockets [1][2].
The bear case is substantial, driven by secular AI disruption risk and deteriorating profitability. ManpowerGroup's core business of staffing intermediation is among the most AI-exposed sectors — AI-powered recruitment platforms can automate candidate matching, screening, and workforce management at a fraction of the cost of human staffing agencies [4]. Trailing profit margin of just 0.6% indicates razor-thin economics that leave no buffer against competitive pricing pressure. ROE of 5.1% is a third of the 15% target, reflecting the low-capital-efficiency reality of the staffing model. Revenue declined from $19.8B (2022) to $17.9B (2025) before the current recovery, and the 5-year revenue CAGR of approximately -2% indicates structural contraction rather than cyclical weakness [6]. The 53% dividend cut in 2025 (from $3.08/yr to $1.44/yr) destroyed the income thesis for existing holders and signals management's pessimism about near-term earnings power. The Inspire Impact Score of -49 (MISALIGNED) limits allocation for faith-based investors. At $57.37, the stock has recovered 128% from its 52-week low of $25.15, pricing in much of the recovery already.

AI Resilience HIGH RISK

AI Risk Protection (3/9): Staffing and recruitment are among the industries most directly threatened by AI disruption. ManpowerGroup's core value proposition — matching workers with employers, screening candidates, and managing temporary staffing relationships — is exactly what AI-powered platforms do with increasing effectiveness [4]. LinkedIn's AI recruiter, HireVue's automated interviews, Greenhouse's AI screening, and dozens of AI staffing startups are reducing the need for human recruitment intermediaries. ManpowerGroup's own Chief Innovation Officer publicly acknowledges AI as fundamentally transforming the staffing industry, describing an 'augmentation' strategy that implicitly concedes AI's disruptive power [4]. The Experis IT staffing segment is particularly exposed as AI-powered tech talent platforms proliferate. The Manpower light industrial staffing segment has somewhat more physical-world protection (placing workers in warehouses, factories), but even here, workforce management platforms increasingly automate scheduling, matching, and compliance. The $200M cost savings program may itself be a response to AI-driven margin compression in the industry [1]. AI Opportunity (0/1): No AI revenue.
