Company Profile

MARA Holdings, Inc. (formerly Marathon Digital Holdings) is one of the world's largest publicly traded bitcoin miners by hash rate (72.2 EH/s as of Q1 2026) and a major corporate holder of bitcoin. The company is executing a strategic pivot from pure bitcoin mining toward AI/HPC data center infrastructure, with CEO Fred Thiel stating AI compute generates more revenue per electron than mining. Q2 2026 revenue of $174.9M declined 26.7% YoY due to a ~28% lower average Bitcoin price, with a net loss of $611.3M. First-half 2026 revenue was $349.5M versus $452.4M a year earlier. Market cap approximately $3.0B.
