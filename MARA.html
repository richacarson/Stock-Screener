Company Profile
Technology ·Bitcoin Mining / Digital Infrastructure ·NASDAQ ·United States
MARA Holdings, Inc. (formerly Marathon Digital Holdings) is one of the world's largest publicly traded bitcoin miners by hash rate (70.3 EH/s) and the second-largest corporate holder of bitcoin. The company is executing a strategic pivot from pure bitcoin mining toward diversified digital infrastructure, having acquired Long Ridge Energy & Power to expand into AI/HPC data center hosting, partnered with Starwood Digital Ventures for AI facilities, and launched the Exaion subsidiary for sovereign cloud services. Q2 2026 revenue of $174.9M declined 26.7% YoY due to lower bitcoin prices, while full-year 2026 revenue is expected at approximately $810M. Market cap approximately $4.4B.

Investment Thesis
MARA is executing a differentiated pivot from pure bitcoin mining to a diversified digital infrastructure platform, leveraging 1.1+ GW of energized power, land, and data center assets across four continents to serve both crypto and AI workloads [2]. The Long Ridge Energy acquisition ($1.5B EV) is expected to add $144M in annualized EBITDA and positions MARA as a rare public company with the power infrastructure AI customers need. Bitcoin holdings of approximately $2.1B as of June 2026 provide a leveraged option on BTC price appreciation. The Inspire Impact Score of 43 (ALIGNED) and renewable energy commitments (landfill methane mining, wind farm migration, Masdar City immersion plant) make MARA one of the more ESG-constructive crypto companies [3].
The bear case is severe: Q2 2026 revenue declined 26.7% YoY to $174.9M, Q1 2026 produced a $1.3B net loss, and profit margin of -430% reflects a business that has never generated sustained GAAP profitability [1][4]. The $2.46B in debt (primarily convertible notes) against $513.65M cash creates significant balance sheet stress if bitcoin enters a prolonged bear market. The AI infrastructure pivot is entirely pre-revenue and competes against hyperscalers (AWS, Azure, GCP) and established operators (Equinix, Digital Realty) with dramatically greater scale and customer relationships. Revenue concentration in bitcoin mining means MARA's financial performance remains a leveraged derivative of BTC price — the company has no proven ability to generate profits independent of crypto market conditions. CEO insider selling in January 2026 adds a negative signal.
