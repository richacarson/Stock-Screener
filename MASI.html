Company Profile
Healthcare ·Medical Devices ·NASDAQ ·United States
Masimo Corporation developed, manufactured, and marketed noninvasive patient monitoring technologies, including pulse oximetry (SET), brain monitoring (SedLine), hemoglobin monitoring (SpHb), and hospital automation solutions. SET technology is used in hospitals, surgical centers, and home care settings worldwide. ACQUIRED: Danaher Corporation completed its cash acquisition of Masimo at $180/share (~$9.9B enterprise value) in June 2026. The stock was delisted from NASDAQ on June 10, 2026 and is no longer publicly traded. Founder/CEO Joe Kiani was ousted in September 2024 following a proxy fight with activist investor Politan Capital.
