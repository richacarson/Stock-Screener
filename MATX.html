Company Profile
Industrials ·Marine Shipping / Logistics ·NYSE ·United States
Matson, Inc. is a 144-year-old ocean transportation and logistics company providing container shipping services primarily in the Pacific trade lanes. The company operates Matson Navigation (Hawaii, Alaska, Guam, China-Long Beach Express), Matson Logistics (supply chain services), and Matson Terminals (Hawaii terminal operations). Protected by the Jones Act in domestic trade lanes. Q2 2026 delivered $969.4M revenue (+16.7% YoY), operating income of $158.9M (+40.6%), and EPS of $4.27. Management expects full-year 2026 operating income to exceed 2025's $499.8M, with Q3 OI approximately 45% above Q3 2025. Market cap approximately $7.0B.

AI Resilience LOW RISK

AI Risk Protection (6/9): Ocean shipping is fundamentally physical — moving containerized cargo across the Pacific requires vessels, ports, crews, fuel, and infrastructure that AI cannot substitute [1]. Matson's Jones Act protection in Hawaii and Alaska trade lanes adds a regulatory moat entirely independent of technology disruption. The expedited China-Long Beach Express service depends on vessel speed and reliability, not information processing. AI could optimize routing, fuel consumption, and yard operations at the margin, but the core physical service is immune to displacement. The Logistics segment ($202M Q2 revenue, growing 30.4% YoY) has some exposure — AI-powered freight matching platforms and autonomous logistics could eventually pressure supply chain brokerage margins. AI Opportunity (1/1): AI-powered logistics optimization, smart container tracking, predictive maintenance on vessels, and demand forecasting could improve Matson's operational efficiency and service quality, though no specific AI investments have been disclosed.
