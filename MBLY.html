Company Profile
Technology ·Auto Parts ·NASDAQ ·Israel
Mobileye Global Inc. develops and deploys advanced driver-assistance systems (ADAS) and autonomous driving technologies. Its EyeQ system-on-chip platform powers ADAS features in over 200 million vehicles from more than 50 OEMs worldwide. Products include SuperVision (hands-free highway driving), Mobileye Chauffeur (eyes-off L4 autonomy), and the Mobileye Drive self-driving system. Founded in 1999 by Prof. Amnon Shashua in Jerusalem, Shashua announced in July 2026 that he will step down as CEO once a successor is appointed. The company is pivoting to become a vertically integrated robotaxi operator and acquired Mentee Robotics for humanoid robotics. Intel holds approximately 88% economic stake with 97% voting control. Q2 CY2026 revenue was $508M with adjusted operating margin of 31%. Full-year 2026 guidance: $1.97-$2.02B revenue, $365-$425M adjusted operating income.
4,200 Employees ·www.mobileye.com

Investment Thesis
Mobileye is the dominant ADAS technology provider with EyeQ chips deployed in 200M+ vehicles across 50+ OEMs, a $24.5B future revenue pipeline, and a clear technology roadmap from L2 ADAS through L4 autonomous driving [1][2]. Q1 2026 delivered 27% revenue growth to $558M, Q2 achieved 31% adjusted operating margin with adjusted operating profit up 46% YoY, and management raised full-year 2026 guidance to $1.97-$2.02B revenue with adjusted operating income of $365-$425M — an 88% midpoint increase driven by the R&D Law benefit [1]. The balance sheet is fortress-grade — zero debt against $1.34B cash — and Forward PEG of 0.30 indicates the stock is deeply undervalued relative to projected growth [4]. The expansion into vertically integrated robotaxis (Orlando deployment on track for late 2026) and Physical AI via Mentee Robotics positions Mobileye for revenue streams beyond traditional Tier-1 supply. At ~$10 per share, significant execution risk appears priced in.
The bear case centers on profitability, leadership transition, and competitive intensity. GAAP financials remain negative with -20.7% net margin and -3.3% ROE, distorted by impairment charges and intangible amortization but reflecting the reality that GAAP profitability remains elusive despite strong adjusted metrics [4]. Founder CEO Amnon Shashua announced in July 2026 that he will step down once a successor is appointed — a critical leadership transition at the exact moment the company is pivoting from technology supplier to integrated robotaxi operator, entering humanoid robotics, and navigating intensifying competition [3]. Tesla FSD is scaling rapidly with a pure vision approach that could prove technologically superior, Waymo has demonstrated commercial L4 robotaxi service at scale, and Nvidia's DRIVE platform enables OEMs to reduce Mobileye dependence. Intel's 97% voting control creates persistent overhang — Intel's own financial difficulties could force strategic decisions misaligned with Mobileye's long-term vision, including further share sales. Q2 2026 revenue was flat YoY at $508M, suggesting the Q1 growth surge may not be sustainable [1].
