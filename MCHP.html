Investment Thesis
Microchip Technology is accelerating its cyclical recovery, with Q1 FY2027 revenue of $1.485B beating consensus (+38% YoY, +13.2% sequentially) and non-GAAP EPS of $0.76 up 181% YoY [1]. Data Center Solutions is projected to reach $1B in CY2026 (up 69% from $591M in CY2025) with industry-first 3nm Gen6 PCIe Switch and G6 retimers securing design wins with tier-one cloud providers [1]. The estimated PEG ratio of ~0.49 at ~21x forward non-GAAP P/E suggests the market has not fully priced the earnings recovery trajectory toward historical $4-5+ EPS levels. Q2 FY2027 guided up 8% sequentially (~$1.603B, +41% YoY) with gross margins of 66-67% above the 65% long-term model. The 14-year dividend growth streak, 141 consecutive quarters of non-GAAP profitability, and Sanghi's proven turnaround leadership provide downside protection. The bear case centers on COO departure risk and incomplete recovery.
