Company Profile
Financial Services ·Financial Data & Stock Exchanges ·NYSE ·United States
Moody's Corporation operates as an integrated risk assessment firm through two segments: Moody's Analytics (MA) and Moody's Investors Service (MIS). MA provides credit research, analytics, data sets, and cloud-based SaaS solutions for banking, insurance, and KYC workflows. MIS publishes credit ratings and assessment services on corporate, financial institution, governmental, and structured finance obligations. Q2 2026 delivered exceptional results with $2.185B revenue (+15% YoY), $5.03 diluted EPS (+57%), and 44% operating margins. Founded in 1900 and headquartered in New York.

Investment Thesis
Moody's is a durable franchise operating in an oligopolistic market with only three major global credit rating agencies. Q2 2026 delivered exceptional results — $2.185B revenue (+15% YoY), diluted EPS of $5.03 (+57%), and adjusted EPS of $4.68 beating consensus by $0.40 [2]. Full-year 2026 guidance was narrowed to $16.50-$17.00 adjusted EPS with 52-53% adjusted operating margins. The company's early AI adoption — Moody's Copilot across 14,000 employees, featured as an HBR case study — positions it to drive margin expansion and differentiate its analytics offerings [4]. The appointment of Christina Kosmowski as MA CEO to lead AI-enabled innovation underscores the strategic commitment [3]. With $2.5B in planned buybacks, 17 consecutive years of dividend increases, and a regulatory moat that AI cannot replicate, Moody's remains a high-quality compounder.
The bear case centers on premium valuation for a maturing growth story. At 37.2x trailing P/E, 30.3x forward P/E, and PEG of 2.48, investors pay heavily for the regulatory moat and recurring revenue model — any miss on the high-single-digit growth guidance would trigger meaningful multiple compression [1]. D/E of 222% leaves limited buffer if capital markets activity declines during a downturn, and the MIS segment's dependence on bond issuance volumes creates cyclical earnings volatility. Significant insider selling — $6.65M from 6 insiders with zero buys in the last 12 months — suggests leadership views the stock as fully valued at current levels [6]. The 5-year revenue CAGR of 7.5% is modest for a 37x earnings multiple, and the recent strength in Q2 was partially driven by a divestiture gain that may not recur. AI research from firms like scuttleblurb has explored potential 'limits of AI disruption' for Moody's and S&P, suggesting the market may not be fully pricing disruption risk to the analytics segment [3].
Key Catalysts
  1. Continued AI monetization through Moody's Copilot, AI-enhanced analytics, and the new MA CEO Kosmowski's mandate to accelerate AI innovation — could drive MA segment growth above the current 8% ARR trajectory [3][4]
  2. Capital markets recovery driving increased bond issuance volumes, boosting the higher-margin MIS segment which generates outsized operating leverage on incremental revenue [2]
  3. Margin expansion toward the 52-53% adjusted operating margin target as subscription mix increases and AI-driven automation reduces per-unit delivery costs — Q2 2026 operating income of $1.046B demonstrates the model's leverage [2]
Key Risks
  1. Premium valuation risk at 37.2x trailing P/E and 2.48x PEG with significant insider selling — any deceleration below the guided high-single-digit revenue growth or margin expansion targets could trigger a meaningful multiple compression from current elevated levels [1][6]
  2. Regulatory disruption to the NRSRO oligopoly — any move by global regulators to reduce reliance on credit ratings or introduce additional designated agencies would erode Moody's pricing power and competitive moat
  3. Capital markets cyclicality in the MIS segment, where a sustained downturn in bond issuance from rising rates, recession, or credit market dislocation could compress the highest-margin revenue stream and reverse the Q2 2026 acceleration

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK

AI Risk Protection (7/9): Moody's core ratings business is deeply protected by regulatory moats — it is one of only three NRSROs, and credit ratings are legally required for bond issuance, securitization, and bank capital calculations [1]. AI cannot replicate the regulatory designation, issuer relationships, or the 126-year trust foundation. The analytics segment benefits from proprietary data covering 300M+ entities. Fresh research argues Moody's ratings moat is 'relatively insulated from AI,' and CEO Fauber addressed the AI disruption debate at the Bernstein Strategic Decisions Conference, positioning Moody's as 'the disruptor rather than the disrupted' [3]. Some exposure exists in research functions where AI could reduce analyst headcount at client firms, but Moody's is positioned as the AI tool provider. AI Opportunity (1/1): Meaningful AI revenue generation through AI-enhanced analytics products, Moody's Copilot for internal productivity, and AI-powered KYC/risk assessment solutions sold to financial institutions.
