Company Profile
Industrials ·Aerospace & Defense ·NYSE ·Canada
MDA Space Ltd. is Canada's largest space technology developer and manufacturer, founded in 1969. The company operates through three business areas: Satellite Systems (communications and Earth observation satellites), Robotics & Space Operations (Canadarm technology for NASA and ISS, next-generation SKYMAKER platform), and Geointelligence (satellite imagery and analytics). MDA built the original Canadarm, Canadarm2, and Dextre, and is developing AI-enabled Canadarm3 for the Lunar Gateway. Listed on NYSE following a March 2026 US IPO, MDA is a mission partner to NASA, CSA, and commercial space operators worldwide.

Investment Thesis
MDA Space delivered a strong Q2 2026 with revenue of $498.6M (up 33.6% YoY), adjusted EBITDA of $96.3M (up 26.2%), and a record backlog of $4.003B with Q2 bookings of $808.9M [1]. Revenue guidance was raised to $1.8-1.9B for fiscal 2026, representing ~13% growth at the midpoint from the already-elevated 2025 base of $1.633B [1][3]. The company has transformed from a $808M revenue business in 2023 to a near-$2B run-rate in two years, driven by the Telesat Lightspeed constellation, Canadarm3 for Lunar Gateway, and expanding US defense contracts. With $1.1B in total liquidity, D/E of just 21%, and a $4B backlog providing 8 quarters of revenue visibility, MDA has a strong financial foundation for sustained growth.
The valuation is aggressive at 38.6x forward earnings and 78.6x trailing P/E, requiring sustained 30%+ revenue growth to justify the premium [5]. Profit margin of just 2.24% and ROE of 6.87% indicate the company is still in heavy investment mode — capex of $225-275M will keep free cash flow neutral-to-negative in 2026 [1]. The March 2026 US IPO diluted shares outstanding, compressing EPS growth (-9.5% diluted EPS YoY in Q2 despite 34% revenue growth). Significant insider selling ($4.6M net over 12 months, $11.9M in the last 90 days) signals potential overvaluation concerns, particularly from Director John Risley's $10.5M+ in sales [4]. The $4B backlog declined $565M YoY despite strong bookings, suggesting accelerating revenue conversion that could pressure future backlog. Concentration risk exists in the Telesat Lightspeed and Canadarm3 contracts — delays or cancellations in either program would materially impact revenue trajectory.
