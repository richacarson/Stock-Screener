Company Profile
Utilities ·Utilities - Regulated Gas ·NYSE ·United States
MDU Resources Group is a regulated energy delivery company serving approximately 1.2 million customers across 8 states through its electric and natural gas utility subsidiaries (Montana-Dakota Utilities, Cascade Natural Gas, Intermountain Gas, Great Plains Natural Gas) and its pipeline and midstream operations (WBI Energy). The company completed a multi-year strategic transformation in 2023-2024 by spinning off Knife River Corporation (construction materials) and Everus Construction Group (construction services) to become a pure-play regulated energy delivery platform. MDU reported Q2 2026 revenue of $375.2M (+6.8% YoY) and reaffirmed 2026 EPS guidance of $0.93-$1.00 with $560M in planned capital investment.

Investment Thesis
MDU Resources at 22.68x forward earnings offers a post-transformation regulated utility with strong income characteristics — 88 consecutive years of dividends, a conservative 36% payout ratio with room for increases, and 2.58% current yield supported by $560M annual capital investment driving 1-2% customer growth and 6-8% EPS growth targets [1]. The Q2 2026 revenue of $375.2M (+6.8% YoY) and net income of $21.3M (+55% YoY) demonstrate the focused utility model is delivering improving financial performance. The $3.1B five-year capital plan and Bakken East pipeline project provide visible growth catalysts, while the regulated rate-of-return model offers earnings stability through economic cycles. Meaningful insider buying ($660.9K with zero selling) signals management conviction in the post-spinoff strategy [5].
The bear case centers on the post-spinoff growing pains, modest returns, and utility sector valuation headwinds. ROE of 7.01% is well below the 15% target, reflecting regulated return constraints that limit upside even in the best-case scenario. The 5yr revenue CAGR of only 2.1% signals a slow-growth business, and Forward P/E of 22.68 is elevated for a utility with single-digit earnings growth — peer regulated utilities trade at 15-18x forward earnings, suggesting potential overvaluation. The dividend growth trajectory was reset by the 2023-2024 spinoffs, and consecutive growth years stand at only 2 despite the 88-year payment streak. Rising interest rates compress utility valuations by increasing the attractiveness of fixed-income alternatives to dividend stocks. The Bakken East pipeline faces regulatory and environmental approval risks that could delay or derail the project. Q1 2026 revenue declined 10.2% YoY, suggesting seasonal volatility that may mask underlying growth challenges.
Key Catalysts
  1. Bakken East natural gas pipeline project advancing through regulatory approvals, providing a visible long-term growth catalyst with specific construction timelines and revenue contribution potential for the 2027-2029 period [1]
  2. $3.1B capital investment plan for 2026-2030 driving 1-2% annual customer growth and rate base expansion across 8-state service territory, supporting the 6-8% long-term EPS growth objective [1]

Key Risks

  2. Post-spinoff revenue base of $1.88B TTM is significantly smaller and less diversified than pre-transformation MDU, concentrating risk in regulated utility operations vulnerable to weather patterns, rate case outcomes, and natural gas price volatility [1]
  3. Forward P/E of 22.68 represents a premium to regulated utility peers (15-18x typical) that may not be justified by 6-8% earnings growth, creating downside risk if the interest rate environment remains elevated or growth disappoints [5]
