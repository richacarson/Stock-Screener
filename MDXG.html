Company Profile
Healthcare ·Medical Devices ·NASDAQ ·United States
MiMedx Group is a regenerative and therapeutic biologics company focused on developing and marketing placental tissue allografts for surgical, wound care, and regenerative medicine applications. Core products include EPIFIX (for chronic wounds) and AmnioFix (for surgical applications), derived from donated human amniotic tissue. The company reported Q2 2026 revenue of $64.4M, down 35% YoY driven by a 61% collapse in wound care revenue following Medicare reimbursement changes, partially offset by 15% surgical segment growth. MiMedx announced a $350M acquisition of Sanara MedTech to expand into collagen, irrigation, and bone fixation, targeting combined 2027 revenue exceeding $400M. Headquartered in Marietta, Georgia.
700 Employees ·www.mimedx.com

Investment Thesis
MiMedx is executing a strategic pivot from its collapsing wound care business toward higher-growth surgical biologics, with the surgical segment delivering 15% YoY growth and 21% domestic particulate growth in Q2 2026 [1]. The transformative $350M Sanara MedTech acquisition will create a combined company exceeding $400M revenue with 20%+ adjusted EBITDA margins in 2027, adding complementary collagen, irrigation, and bone fixation technologies [5]. Sequential revenue growth of 9% from Q1 to Q2 suggests early wound care stabilization. The balance sheet remains conservatively managed with D/E of just 8% pre-acquisition, and Forward P/E of 23.4x implies analysts expect meaningful earnings recovery. The Inspire Impact Score of 29 (ALIGNED) reflects the company's regenerative medicine contributions to patient welfare.
The bear case is severe: wound care revenue collapsed 61% YoY in Q2 2026 following Medicare reimbursement changes, dragging total revenue down 35% to $64.4M and compressing profit margin to 1.76% [1][6]. Trailing P/E of 116.8x reflects near-total earnings erosion, and ROE has declined from 16.6% to 2.9%. The Sanara acquisition introduces $300M in new debt, integration risk, and management distraction during the most challenging period in the company's operating history [5]. The governance legacy is deeply concerning — former CEO and COO were convicted of securities fraud, the company settled $6.5M in False Claims Act allegations with the VA, and ongoing litigation over predatory sales practices and Axiofill FDA compliance creates persistent legal tail risk [4]. Glassdoor's 3.2/5 rating signals cultural challenges that could impair Sanara integration. The stock at $4.67 prices in meaningful recovery but the margin of safety is thin.
