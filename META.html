Meta at 20.86x forward earnings with a PEG of 0.96 is executing a generational AI infrastructure buildout while delivering 28% revenue growth to $228B+ TTM [1]. The $130-$145B 2026 CapEx represents ~57% of trailing revenue — unprecedented capital intensity for a digital advertising business. Q2 2026 showed the strain: net income fell 14% to $15.8B and operating margin compressed from 43% to 31% as $31.1B in quarterly CapEx flowed through [1]. If AI infrastructure spending does not generate proportional returns, the capital destruction would be massive. The ~8,000 workforce reduction in 2026 (after 21,000 in 2022-2023) raises questions about organizational stability even as the company 'quietly rebuilds' management [2]. Regulatory risks escalate: EU fines exceeding EUR 2B cumulatively, FTC antitrust appeal, shareholder proposals on AI data usage, human rights, child safety all rejected at the May 2026 meeting [3]. Zuckerberg's dual-class share structure means the $130-$145B CapEx is effectively one person's judgment call.
