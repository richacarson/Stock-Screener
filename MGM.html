Company Profile
Consumer Cyclical ·Resorts & Casinos ·NYQ ·United States
MGM Resorts International operates as a global gaming and entertainment company through four segments: Las Vegas Strip Resorts, Regional Operations, MGM China, and MGM Digital. The company operates casino resorts offering gaming, hotel, convention, dining, entertainment, and retail amenities, plus online sports betting and iGaming through BetMGM (JV with Entain). Q2 2026 consolidated revenue was $4.5B (+1% YoY) with net income of $292M versus $49M a year earlier, while Las Vegas Strip revenue rose 3% to $2.2B and MGM Digital revenue surged 20% to $196M. The company is subject to a $48.30/share takeover offer from Barry Diller's People Inc.

Investment Thesis
MGM at $28.47 with a $7.3B market cap trades at a significant discount to the pending $48.30/share takeover offer from Barry Diller's People Inc., representing 70% potential upside if the deal closes. The stock's forward P/E of 15.2x and PEG of 0.36 reflect deep value even without deal completion, supported by ROE of 18.9% and a recovering earnings trajectory — Q2 2026 net income surged to $292M from $49M a year earlier [1]. Las Vegas Strip revenue grew 3% to $2.2B with EBITDAR of $735M (+3%), while MGM Digital revenue surged 20% to $196M, demonstrating the BetMGM platform's growth trajectory [1]. Operating cash flow of $2.5B annually funds both property reinvestment and shareholder returns.
The bear case centers on extreme leverage and decelerating growth. D/E of 893% reflects $30.2B in total debt against just $2.8B cash, a capital structure dependent on continued EBITDA generation and refinancing access [3]. Consolidated revenue grew only 1% YoY in Q2 2026 with adjusted EBITDA declining to $610M from $648M, and adjusted EPS of $0.59 missed estimates versus $0.79 in the prior year, suggesting operational momentum is fading [1]. MGM China EBITDAR fell 15% as Macau recovery stalls, Regional operations face structural headwinds from online gaming cannibalization, and BetMGM's digital segment remains EBITDAR-negative due to elevated marketing costs and gaming taxes. Profit margin of 2.4% leaves zero buffer for a consumer spending slowdown, and the sale-leaseback structure with VICI Properties creates $2B+ in annual rent obligations that function as effectively mandatory debt service. The takeover offer creates binary risk — if the deal fails, the stock could trade materially lower given the operational challenges.
