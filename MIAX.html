Company Profile
Financial Services ·Capital Markets ·NYSE ·United States
Miami International Holdings operates regulated financial exchanges across options, equities, futures, and international listings. The company's platforms include MIAX Options, MIAX Pearl, MIAX Emerald, and MIAX Sapphire for options; MIAX Pearl Equities for U.S. equities; MIAX Futures for derivatives; and the Bermuda Stock Exchange (BSX) and The International Stock Exchange (TISE) for international listings. IPO'd on NYSE in August 2025.

Investment Thesis
Miami International Holdings delivered record Q1 2026 results with net revenue of $128.6M (+40% YoY), adjusted EBITDA of $66.1M (+66% YoY), and adjusted EPS of $0.42 that crushed analyst estimates of $0.27 by 56% [1]. The company commands 17.3% of the U.S. multi-listed options market with 10.9M contracts ADV (+27% YoY), operates with virtually zero debt ($1.5M against $595.9M cash), and generates $145M TTM free cash flow. The exchange operator model benefits from operating leverage — as volumes increase, incremental margins expand dramatically, evidenced by 800bps adjusted EBITDA margin expansion to 51% in Q1 2026 [1]. Analyst consensus projects FY2026 EPS of $1.66, implying significant earnings normalization from the IPO-distorted FY2025.
The primary concern is valuation: at 28.4x forward earnings and $4.5B market cap, MIAX is priced for sustained high-growth execution despite only three quarters as a public company [4][5]. FY2025 GAAP net income was -$70M due to $107.7M in IPO-related debt extinguishment charges, and GAAP profit margin remains only 8.65% TTM. The options exchange market is fiercely competitive — CBOE (~33%), NYSE (~20%), and Nasdaq (~19%) have vastly greater resources and institutional relationships. MIAX's market share gains from 16.0% to 17.3% are impressive but the pace may plateau as incumbents respond with pricing and technology investment. Exchange volumes are cyclical and correlated with market volatility — the elevated volatility driving record Q1 2026 volumes may not persist. CEO insider selling of $9.7M in April 2026, just months after the IPO, introduces overhang risk [4].
Key Catalysts
  1. U.S. options market share trajectory toward 20%+ as MIAX Sapphire ramps and retail options participation continues secular growth, with each percentage point worth approximately $30M+ in incremental annual revenue [1]
  2. GAAP profitability normalization as one-time IPO charges roll off, with analyst consensus projecting FY2026 EPS of $1.66 and underlying adjusted EBITDA margins above 50% [1]
  3. Multi-asset-class diversification with equities, futures, and international listings (BSX, TISE) reducing dependence on the U.S. options market and opening new revenue streams [2]
Key Risks
  1. Premium valuation at 28.4x forward P/E for a recently IPO'd exchange operator with limited public company earnings history, leaving minimal margin of safety against execution disappointment [4][5]
  2. Options market volumes are cyclical and correlated with volatility — the record Q1 2026 volumes may reflect a transient volatility spike rather than a sustainable growth trajectory
  3. Incumbent exchanges (CBOE, Nasdaq, NYSE) with vastly greater resources may respond to MIAX's share gains with aggressive pricing, technology investment, or regulatory lobbying that compresses margins [1]

AI Risk Protection (7/9): MIAX's core exchange infrastructure is deeply protected by regulatory moats — SEC-registered national securities exchange licenses require extensive compliance frameworks that AI cannot replicate or bypass [2]. Network effects (liquidity attracts liquidity), high switching costs for integrated market participants, and physical data center infrastructure create additional structural defenses. Exchange operations involve market surveillance, clearinghouse connectivity, and regulatory reporting that require institutional capabilities far beyond AI's reach. AI-driven algorithmic trading actually increases exchange volumes rather than displacing them. The regulatory barrier alone provides a near-permanent moat against AI disruption [2].
