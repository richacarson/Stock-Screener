Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·Canada
Milestone Pharmaceuticals is a biopharmaceutical company focused on cardiovascular medicines. Its lead product, CARDAMYST (etripamil) nasal spray, received FDA approval in January 2026 as the first and only self-administered treatment for paroxysmal supraventricular tachycardia (PSVT), enabling patients to manage acute cardiac episodes at home rather than visiting the emergency room. The EMA accepted the European MAA, and additional indications including atrial fibrillation with rapid ventricular rate (AFib-RVR) are in Phase II development. Headquartered in Montreal, Canada, with approximately 124.5 million shares outstanding.

Investment Thesis
Milestone Pharmaceuticals achieved a transformative milestone with the January 2026 FDA approval of CARDAMYST (etripamil) nasal spray, the first and only self-administered treatment for acute PSVT episodes affecting 2M+ Americans [1][2]. Early commercial traction is accelerating — approximately 600 prescriptions filled through April 2026 with ~400 unique prescribers, Express Scripts national formulary coverage secured (25%+ of commercially insured lives), and H1 2026 preliminary revenue of ~$5.0M with Q2 2026 revenue up 21% YoY [1][3]. The $184.2M cash position provides 18-24 months of launch runway [1]. CEO Joe Oliveto's ~9-year tenure and July 2025 insider purchase at $1.50 signal deep conviction [3]. Analyst consensus price target of $6.33-7.67 implies significant upside from the ~$1.40 current price. The AFib-RVR Phase II pipeline and European MAA acceptance provide material optionality beyond PSVT [2].
The bear case centers on commercial execution and ongoing dilution. The company is deeply unprofitable with Q1 2026 net loss of $26.1M against $2.16M revenue, and FY2025 net loss of $63.1M [1][2]. Negative shareholders' equity of -$6.1M reflects years of accumulated deficits. FY2026 revenue guidance of $9.8-10.2M requires significant acceleration from H1 2026 pace to hit the high end [3]. The recent $52.5M equity raise and history of dilutive financings mean further dilution is likely if commercialization expenses exceed revenue growth [1]. Market adoption depends on physician awareness expansion from ~400 unique prescribers, payer coverage beyond 25% of commercially insured lives, and patient willingness to self-administer during cardiac episodes. The PSVT addressable market, while underserved, may prove smaller than bull-case estimates, and single-product concentration creates binary risk.
Key Catalysts
  1. CARDAMYST commercial uptake acceleration through 2026-2027 — Q2 2026 revenue of $2.8M (+21% YoY) suggests improving trajectory; each additional major PBM formulary win materially expands the accessible patient population beyond current 25% coverage [1][3]
  2. EMA decision on the European etripamil MAA, opening a second major commercial market and supporting partnership or royalty economics that could diversify the revenue base [2]
  3. Phase II readouts for etripamil in atrial fibrillation with rapid ventricular rate (AFib-RVR), which would multiply the addressable market well beyond the PSVT niche [2]
Key Risks
  1. Deeply unprofitable operations with Q1 2026 net loss of $26.1M against $2.16M revenue create significant cash burn — the $184.2M cash position provides approximately 6-8 quarters of runway at current burn rates before additional dilutive capital raises become necessary [1]
  2. Single-product concentration risk — CARDAMYST represents 100% of current and near-term revenue, making Milestone vulnerable to any safety signal, competitive entry, or slower-than-expected physician adoption [2]
  3. Stock price of ~$1.40 and market cap of ~$175M create micro-cap volatility risk with limited institutional ownership — the stock is one negative clinical or commercial data point away from significant downside [5]
