Company Profile
Technology ·Scientific & Technical Instruments ·NASDAQ ·United States
MKS Inc. (formerly MKS Instruments) provides foundational technology solutions for semiconductor manufacturing, electronics and packaging, and specialty industrial applications. Operating through Vacuum Solutions (VSD), Photonics Solutions (PSD), and Material Solutions (MSD) segments, the company's products touch 85% of semiconductor manufacturing steps and 70% of advanced packaging manufacturing steps. Q2 2026 delivered record revenue of $1.25B (+28% YoY) with adjusted EPS of $3.30 (+86% YoY). Founded in 1961, headquartered in Andover, Massachusetts.

Investment Thesis
MKS at 14.28x forward earnings is a 'picks-and-shovels' AI semiconductor play at a significant discount — the stock's 21% decline from $320 to $252 after Q3 guidance disappointed has created an attractive entry point for a company touching 85% of chip manufacturing steps [1][4]. Q2 2026 was a breakout quarter: record revenue of $1.25B (+28% YoY), adjusted EPS of $3.30 (+86% YoY, beating estimates by 11%), semiconductor revenue up 28% to $554M, and electronics & packaging surging 43% to $381M — all driven by AI-accelerated demand [1]. ROE has crossed above 15% as earnings recover, D/E is declining toward the 2.5x leverage target, and profit margin is improving toward normalization as Atotech integration matures. The Inspire Impact Score of 58 (ALIGNED) enables faith-based allocation. With estimated forward PEG near 1.0 and structural AI tailwinds, MKS offers semiconductor equipment exposure at a value multiple.
The bear case centers on semiconductor cyclicality, elevated leverage, and sequential growth deceleration. Q3 2026 guidance of $1.08B revenue and $2.40 EPS represents a 14% sequential decline from Q2's $1.25B, suggesting the record quarter included pull-forward demand or one-time items — this disappointment drove the stock's 21% decline [1]. D/E of 140% and ~$4B acquisition debt suppress profit margin to 10.1% versus the 20% target and constrain R&D flexibility versus less-leveraged competitors Advanced Energy and Coherent [4]. The 2023 goodwill write-down of $1.84B demonstrates how quickly semiconductor equipment narratives reverse when capex cycles turn, and trailing P/E of 40x reflects the gap between current and normalized earnings. China semiconductor revenue creates geopolitical exposure to export controls and tariffs. Revenue growth of 28.3% is cyclically elevated and will inevitably normalize — the 5yr CAGR of ~8% better represents sustainable growth. The sequential guidance step-down requires monitoring whether it reflects normal seasonality or demand softening.
