Company Profile
Consumer Cyclical ·Resorts & Casinos ·NASDAQ ·Hong Kong
Melco Resorts & Entertainment operates integrated casino resorts in Macau (City of Dreams, Studio City, Altira, Mocha Clubs), the Philippines (City of Dreams Manila), and Cyprus (City of Dreams Mediterranean). Controlled by Chairman and CEO Lawrence Ho, the company is one of only six licensed Macau casino concessionaires, focused on premium-mass gaming and luxury hospitality.

Investment Thesis
Melco delivered Q1 2026 revenue of $1.37B (up 11% YoY) with operating income of $179M and Adjusted Property EBITDA of $381M (+12%), confirming the Macau premium-mass recovery trajectory [1]. TTM revenue of $5.30B with net income of $229M (+277% YoY) shows the earnings inflection is underway. At a forward P/E of 8.69 and PEG of 0.18, the stock is deeply discounted — 12 analysts rate it 'Buy' with a $7.87 consensus price target representing 52% upside from $5.19 [5]. The REM luxury hotel opening at City of Dreams Macau in Q3 2026 and Cyprus ramp provide incremental growth catalysts. Lawrence Ho's founder-led operating model brings long-term alignment with shareholders.
The bear case is severe. Total debt of $6.94B against negative book equity produces a D/E of -561% and an Altman Z-Score of 0.49, indicating substantial bankruptcy risk if the Macau recovery stalls [3][5]. Profit margin of 4.33% is razor-thin for the leverage carried, and ROE is distorted by negative equity. The stock has declined 49% from its 52-week high of $10.15 to $5.19, reflecting market skepticism about the deleveraging timeline [5]. China regulatory risk remains omnipresent — further crackdowns on cross-border capital flows, junket operations, or currency controls could impair Macau GGR at any time. The Glassdoor rating of 2.7/5 and 'Gambling' Inspire attribution signal fundamental concerns about the business model's social impact and organizational health [2][4].
