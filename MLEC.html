Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·Cayman Islands
Moolec Science SA is a science-based ingredient company that uses molecular farming to produce real animal proteins in plants, creating food ingredients with the cost structure of plant-based solutions and the taste and functionality of animal-based products. Flagship products include Piggy Sooy (porcine myoglobin in soybeans) and GLASO (GLA-rich safflower oil). Following a June 2025 business combination, the company expanded across food ingredients, ag-biologicals, precision fermentation (Mycofood/Eternal brand), biomaterials, and R&D services through Agrality and Synbio Powerlabs platforms. Controlled by Agriculture Investment Group Corp. (AIGC) with 65.1% stake.
75 Employees ·www.moolecscience.com
