Company Profile
Consumer Defensive ·Agricultural / Molecular Farming ·NASDAQ ·Luxembourg
MLECW represents publicly traded warrants of Moolec Science SA, a Luxembourg-headquartered science-based ingredient company that produces real animal proteins in plants through proprietary Molecular Farming technology, aiming to upgrade the taste, nutrition, and affordability of alternative-protein products [1]. Moolec became the first Molecular Farming food-tech company to trade publicly (Nasdaq: MLEC) in early 2023 and has since merged with Bioceres Group affiliates (Gentle Technologies, Nutrecon) in June 2025. The underlying common stock trades near $0.02-$5 depending on the period referenced and completed a 1-for-10 reverse split in May 2025; the company received a Nasdaq delisting determination in November 2025 for trading below the $1.00 minimum bid price requirement and is appealing to a Nasdaq Hearings Panel [2].

Investment Thesis
Moolec's underlying Molecular Farming technology -- producing real animal proteins inside plants -- is a genuinely novel platform addressing legitimate global food security and sustainability needs, and the company reported completion of the first phase of GLASO industrialization following a record 2025 growing campaign, with expansion planned into energy applications for 2026 [1]. The June 2025 merger with Bioceres Group affiliates brought additional scale, agricultural expertise, and capital access that could help fund the multi-year path to commercial-scale production.
The bear case is overwhelming for the warrant specifically. MLECW is a warrant on an underlying common stock (MLEC) that received a Nasdaq delisting determination in November 2025 for trading below the $1.00 minimum bid price for 30 consecutive days, has completed a 1-for-10 reverse split, and carries negative EBITDA of roughly -$7.9M against minimal revenue (~$5.6M FY2024) [2]. Trading near $0.02 with effectively no reliable liquidity, MLECW fails IOWN's liquidity floor outright, and the warrant's value is entirely contingent on the underlying stock recovering well above its strike price before expiration -- a low-probability outcome given the company's ongoing delisting appeal and cash burn. This is a speculative, highly dilutive security unsuitable for the IOWN framework at this time.
