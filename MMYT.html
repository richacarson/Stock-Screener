Investment Thesis
MakeMyTrip is India's dominant online travel platform with record FY2026 gross bookings of $10.4B across the world's fastest-growing major travel market [1]. Full-year IFRS revenue reached $1.04B with EBIT-equivalent operating profit rising 30.1% to $156M, demonstrating improving unit economics as the platform scales [1]. Myra conversational AI generated 200,000+ bookings per quarter, validating the company's AI strategy as a competitive differentiator rather than a disruption vulnerability [2]. India's online travel penetration below 40% provides a secular growth runway, and the 5-year revenue CAGR of 13.84% understates the company's booking volume momentum (34% CAGR over four years). The PEG ratio of 0.84 suggests attractive growth-adjusted valuation.
The bear case centers on profitability quality, valuation complexity, and structural disruption risk. Despite operating profit growth of 30.1%, GAAP net income declined to $51.8M in FY2026, reflecting the burden of $1.41B in debt against $765M cash and significant non-operating charges [1][5]. The trailing GAAP P/E of 99x is extremely elevated, and Q4 revenue of $250.12M missed estimates by 11%, indicating potential growth deceleration [1]. Generative AI poses the most significant structural threat — Google Gemini, ChatGPT, and similar tools are becoming capable of end-to-end trip planning that could disintermediate OTA platforms [2]. Competition from Booking.com's India push, Flipkart's Cleartrip, and Google's travel products intensifies pricing pressure. Currency risk (INR/USD) adds structural volatility for US-listed shareholders, and India's geopolitical tensions (India-Pakistan crisis in Q4 impacted travel volumes) create episodic demand risk [1].

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience MODERATE
5
AI Risk Protection (4/9): MakeMyTrip faces meaningful AI disruption risk. Generative AI platforms (Google Gemini, ChatGPT, Perplexity) are increasingly capable of end-to-end trip planning, price comparison, and booking — functions that constitute MakeMyTrip's core value proposition [2]. If consumers begin using AI assistants to plan and book travel directly, OTA intermediaries face structural disintermediation. MakeMyTrip's defense lies in its proprietary hotel and bus supply network (5,000+ cities), payment integrations (India-specific UPI, wallets), post-booking service infrastructure, and regional language support that AI tools cannot yet replicate at scale. Indian market specifics — fragmented supply, diverse languages, emerging travel demographics — provide meaningful geographic protection. AI Opportunity (1/1): Myra conversational AI generated 200,000+ bookings in Q3 FY2026, demonstrating that MakeMyTrip is actively deploying AI to strengthen its competitive position rather than being passively disrupted [2]. ML-powered personalization drives higher conversion rates across the platform.
