Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NGM ·United States
MannKind Corporation is a biopharmaceutical company focused on transforming chronic disease care through inhaled and injectable therapies. Commercial products include Afrezza (inhaled insulin for diabetes), Furoscix (subcutaneous furosemide for heart failure/CKD edema, acquired via scPharma October 2025), V-Go (mechanical insulin delivery), and Tyvaso DPI (inhaled powder for pulmonary hypertension, manufactured for United Therapeutics). Q2 2026 revenue reached $109.4M (+43% YoY) driven primarily by Furoscix contribution, with H1 2026 revenue of $199.5M (+29%). The company operates at a GAAP net loss with a pipeline including MNKD-201 (inhaled nintedanib), MNKD-701 (bumetanide), and ROSE-010 Technosphere for weight management.

Investment Thesis
MannKind at $3.09 is a specialty biopharmaceutical company inflecting toward profitability with $393.6M in TTM revenue growing 43% YoY, driven by the Furoscix acquisition ($110-120M 2026 guidance) and Tyvaso DPI royalties (~$32M/quarter) alongside the stable Afrezza franchise [1]. The company's Technosphere drug delivery platform provides a unique technology moat enabling inhaled formulations of traditionally injectable drugs, with a pipeline spanning IPF (MNKD-201), fluid overload (MNKD-701), and weight management (ROSE-010) that could each represent significant market opportunities. Forward P/E of 22.9x on $0.14 forward EPS suggests the market expects a profitability inflection, and the $1B market cap provides substantial upside if the platform delivers additional approved products.
The bear case centers on persistent unprofitability and execution risk. Despite $394M in TTM revenue, MannKind posted a $19M Q2 2026 GAAP net loss with profit margin of -11.1%, and gross margins compressed from 79% to 66% as the lower-margin Furoscix product became a larger revenue contributor [1][3]. The PEG of 4.39 indicates the stock is overvalued relative to growth, and $434M in total debt against $111M cash creates balance sheet pressure. Afrezza revenue declined 7% YoY in Q2 and V-Go dropped 33%, indicating the core legacy products are deteriorating while the company depends increasingly on acquired products and manufacturing partnerships. The pipeline carries typical biotech binary risk — MNKD-201, MNKD-701, and ROSE-010 are all early-stage with no guarantee of FDA approval. The weight management application (Rose Pharma licensing) enters an intensely competitive market dominated by GLP-1 agonists from Novo Nordisk and Eli Lilly.
