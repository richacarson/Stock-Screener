Investment Thesis
Mobix at $0.84 is guiding fiscal Q4 2026 revenue to $1.4M-$1.8M, about 100% sequential growth with substantially higher gross margin, driven by defense and aerospace EMI Solutions and RaGE Systems, with U.S. Navy and $1M+ threat-detection orders supporting the pivot [1][2]. A small revenue base means upside to defense wins can be large in percentage terms.
The FY2025 10-K flags substantial doubt about going-concern with a $37.7M operating loss; TTM revenue growth is -66.4% and ROE is -1,122% [2]. Market cap is ~$15M and ongoing equity raises (a $6.0M offering) dilute holders, and a lawsuit against former investors adds distraction [3]. Competition in interconnects is intense and fragmented.
Key Catalysts
  1. Defense, aerospace and security demand driving Q4 FY2026 revenue above $1.6M [1]
  2. AI drone intelligence platform and threat-detection sensor orders exceeding $1M [2]
  3. Gross margin expansion from higher production volume and mix [1]
Key Risks
  1. Going-concern doubt and need for additional capital with a $37.7M annual operating loss [2]
  2. Dilution from repeated equity offerings, ~$15M market cap [3]
  3. Competition from larger interconnect vendors and execution risk in the defense pivot [2]
