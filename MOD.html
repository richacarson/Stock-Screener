Investment Thesis
Modine Manufacturing is executing a generational strategic pivot from legacy auto parts thermal management to high-growth AI data center cooling, with FY2026 delivering record results: $3.18B revenue (+23%), adjusted EBITDA of $471M, and adjusted EPS of $5.02 [1]. Data Center cooling revenue surged 90% in FY2026 as hyperscaler AI capex accelerated, and FY2027 guidance of 20-35% revenue growth with $650-680M adjusted EBITDA signals continued momentum [1]. The company IS AI infrastructure — high-density AI chips generate significantly more heat, directly driving demand for Modine's advanced cooling solutions including liquid cooling and the patent-pending Cooling AI Control System [2]. Three strategic acquisitions in FY2026 and the planned spin-off of Performance Technologies position Modine as a pure-play Climate Solutions company. AI Resilience score of 10 reflects maximum protection from and maximum benefit from AI adoption. The Inspire Impact Score of 63 (ALIGNED) with 13 positive ESG attributions confirms the 'Engineering a Cleaner, Healthier World' mission is recognized by faith-aligned screening.
The bear case centers on valuation and execution risk. At 25.8x forward P/E with only 3.4% net profit margin, the stock prices in substantial growth that must materialize — any deceleration in data center buildout or market share loss to Vertiv and Schneider Electric would compress the multiple significantly [4]. GAAP earnings of $121.5M declined 34% YoY despite record revenue, reflecting acquisition integration costs, capacity expansion expenses, and startup inefficiencies as Modine scales aggressively [1]. Gross margin fell to 22.5% in Q4, down 320 basis points YoY, demonstrating that rapid expansion creates profitability headwinds [2]. The planned spin-off of the automotive business creates organizational risk during a critical growth phase, potentially disrupting employee retention and operational focus. Revenue growth is heavily concentrated in data center cooling — a single end market dependent on continued hyperscaler AI capex, which could slow if AI model efficiency improves faster than expected or macroeconomic conditions deteriorate. PEG of 1.71 is above the 1.0 target, suggesting the growth premium has already been partially priced in.
