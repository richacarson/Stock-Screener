Company Profile
Technology ·Cloud Computing — AI Infrastructure ·NASDAQ ·United States
Corvex, Inc. (formerly Movano Inc.) is an AI cloud computing company specializing in GPU-accelerated infrastructure for AI workloads. The company was formed through an all-stock reverse merger completed on March 19, 2026, in which Movano (a failing health wearable company) merged with privately-held Corvex, with the combined entity renamed Corvex and retaining the NASDAQ ticker MOVE. The company operates 'AI Factories' — managed Kubernetes services designed for NVIDIA HGX B200 and H200 GPU systems — offering compute, confidential computing, and inference services through its Amplified AI Cloud platform.

Investment Thesis
Corvex offers speculative exposure to the AI infrastructure build-out through a micro-cap pure-play on GPU-accelerated cloud computing. The company operates managed Kubernetes services for NVIDIA HGX B200 and H200 systems, targeting enterprises needing high-performance AI compute with confidential computing capabilities [1][2]. Q1 2026 generated $510K in revenue from just 12 days of post-merger operations, and $4.4M in deferred revenue suggests a growing pipeline of contracted AI compute capacity. Co-founder Seth Demsey brings decades of experience from NASA, Microsoft, Google, and AOL/Yahoo with dozens of patents in HPC and distributed systems. If Corvex can execute on scaling its AI Factory model, the current ~$480M market cap could be a fraction of the addressable opportunity in the $100B+ AI cloud infrastructure market.
The bear case is severe and multi-dimensional. Corvex is a startup with $510K in quarterly revenue competing against CoreWeave ($35B+ valuation), Lambda Labs, and hyperscalers with billions in GPU infrastructure investment — the competitive disadvantage is existential [2]. The company was formed through a reverse merger that diluted original Movano shareholders to <5% of the combined entity, prompting fiduciary duty investigations by multiple law firms [3]. The stock trades with extreme volatility ($14-$24 intraday range on August 4) and marginal liquidity (~46K shares/day = ~$1M daily dollar volume), making it unsuitable for any position size. At ~$480M market cap on ~$2M annualized revenue, the stock trades at ~240x revenue with no path to profitability articulated. The $40.2M raised through the merger provides limited runway for a capital-intensive GPU cloud business that must compete on infrastructure scale against companies with 100x or more capital access.
Key Catalysts
  1. Conversion of $4.4M deferred revenue to recognized revenue in Q2-Q3 2026, demonstrating demand traction and potentially achieving $5-10M quarterly revenue run-rate
  2. Enterprise customer announcements or strategic partnerships with GPU manufacturers (NVIDIA) or cloud infrastructure players that validate the Amplified AI Cloud platform
  3. Successful scaling of AI Factory managed Kubernetes deployments on NVIDIA B200/H200 systems, establishing Corvex as a credible niche player in confidential AI compute
Key Risks
  1. Existential competitive risk: hyperscalers (AWS, Azure, GCP) and well-capitalized GPU cloud peers (CoreWeave at $35B+) have 100x+ more capital to deploy on GPU infrastructure, and can match any niche offering Corvex develops
  2. Extreme liquidity risk with average daily dollar volume of ~$1M, barely above the IOWN liquidity floor — any moderate selling pressure could cause catastrophic price declines as evidenced by the $14-$24 intraday range on August 4
  3. Reverse merger governance concerns: original Movano shareholders diluted to <5% of combined entity, fiduciary duty investigations underway, and the company has no track record of public company governance or financial reporting as Corvex
