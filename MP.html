Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Other Industrial Metals & Mining ·NYSE ·United States
MP Materials operates the only active rare earth mine and processing facility in the United States at Mountain Pass, California, producing neodymium-praseodymium (NdPr) oxide and metals critical for permanent magnets used in EVs, wind turbines, robotics, and defense applications. The company is building the $1.25B 10X rare earth magnet manufacturing campus in North Texas, targeting operations in 2028 to complete its vertical integration from mine to finished magnet. H1 2026 revenue reached $199.1M (up 68% YoY) with NdPr production up 51% and NdPr sales up 122% as the company transitions from Chinese concentrate sales to domestic value-added processing.

The company's willingness to transform its entire business model from a concentrate exporter to an integrated manufacturer, inspired by China's rare earth industry capability rather than seeking to 'beat' China, demonstrates the rare combination of existential flexibility, courage, and worthy rival recognition that Sinek identifies as hallmarks of infinite game players.

Investment Thesis
MP Materials at $41.61 with a $7.4B market cap is America's only integrated rare earth producer, executing a transformative vertical integration strategy from mine to finished magnet that positions it as critical infrastructure for the electrification, defense, and AI hardware supply chains [1][3]. H1 2026 revenue of $199.1M surged 68% YoY as NdPr production grew 51% and NdPr sales volumes jumped 122%, with the Department of Defense Price Protection Agreement contributing $42.3M in Q1 alone [1]. The $1.25B 10X magnet manufacturing campus in North Texas (2028 operations) will complete the mine-to-magnet value chain using proprietary GBD technology, with Apple committing $500M and GM signing offtake agreements that provide revenue visibility [3][5]. National security tailwinds — Chinese rare earth export restrictions, US critical minerals policy, and DoD procurement mandates — create structural demand protection unavailable to commodity miners.
The bear case centers on execution risk, profitability timeline, and commodity price exposure. MP remains unprofitable with H1 2026 net loss of $28.3M and -14.6% profit margin as the company funds the $1.25B 10X campus construction and NdPr production ramp simultaneously [1]. Forward P/E of 51.6x prices in significant earnings growth that depends on 10X successfully achieving commercial operations in 2028 — a complex manufacturing project with no precedent in the US for decades. The stock has declined from ~$51 SMA to $41.61 (-19%), and Seeking Alpha analysis warns that 'much has already been priced in' [5]. Rare earth prices remain volatile and subject to Chinese supply manipulation; a sustained rare earth price decline could pressure margins before 10X generates offsetting magnet revenue. The DoD Price Protection Agreement ($42.3M in Q1 2026) masks underlying profitability, and its eventual expiration would require organic earnings to fill the gap [1]. Revenue concentration risk is significant — Apple and a few large customers represent a substantial portion of the forward order book, and any relationship disruption would be material [3][5].
