Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas Midstream ·NYSE ·United States
MPLX LP is a midstream energy master limited partnership that owns and operates crude oil and natural gas gathering, processing, transportation, and logistics infrastructure across the United States. Operating through Crude Oil and Products Logistics and Natural Gas and NGL Services segments, MPLX serves as the midstream arm of Marathon Petroleum Corporation. The company operates pipelines, terminals, storage caverns, processing plants, and an inland marine fleet.

Investment Thesis
MPLX LP is a premier midstream MLP offering a 7.61% distribution yield with 12.5% annual growth committed for 2026-2027, supported by 1.3x distribution coverage and $5.8B in distributable cash flow [1][2][5]. Full-year 2025 produced $11.82B in revenue, $4.9B in net income, and $7.0B in adjusted EBITDA, with Q1 2026 EBITDA of $1.7B and management expecting growth to exceed 2025 levels in the second half of the year [1][2]. At 12.32x forward P/E with 44% net profit margin, MPLX is attractively valued as an essential infrastructure business benefiting from AI-driven power demand — the MARA Holdings partnership targets 400 MW expanding to 1.5 GW of gas-fired power for data centers [3][5]. The $2.4B organic growth capital plan provides multi-year distribution growth visibility through Harmon Creek III, Titan expansion, and Permian pipeline projects [1].
The bear case centers on energy transition risk, leverage, and MLP structural limitations. D/E of 176.5% reflects $25.6B in total debt against $14.5B in equity, creating refinancing risk if interest rates remain elevated [5]. The 80% payout ratio and 1.3x distribution coverage in Q1 2026 leave limited margin of safety if throughput volumes decline. Revenue five-year CAGR of just 2.9% suggests the business grows slowly absent acquisitions, and Q1 2026 net income of $912M declined from $1,126M in the prior year due to derivatives, interest expense, and non-recurring items [1][5]. As an MLP, MPLX issues K-1 tax forms, limiting the investor base. Energy transition and regulatory risk create long-term demand uncertainty for fossil fuel infrastructure, and pipeline permitting faces ongoing environmental scrutiny [4]. The MPC general partner's 64% economic interest could lead to decisions prioritizing refining operations over MPLX unitholder distributions.

AI Resilience LOW RISK
9
AI Risk Protection (8/9): MPLX's core business — owning and operating physical midstream energy infrastructure (pipelines, processing plants, terminals, marine vessels) — is structurally immune to AI disruption [2]. AI cannot replace pipelines, fractionation plants, or storage caverns. The business is further protected by regulatory requirements, long-term contracts, and right-of-way assets that are nearly impossible to replicate. AI actually increases demand for MPLX's infrastructure: AI data centers require massive natural gas-fired power generation, which drives throughput volumes through MPLX's gathering and processing systems. Management expects year-over-year EBITDA growth in 2026 to exceed 2025, with data center demand as a growing tailwind [1]. AI Opportunity (1/1): The MARA Holdings collaboration directly monetizes AI demand by supplying natural gas for 400 MW (expanding to 1.5 GW) of gas-fired power generation for data centers and AI workloads in West Texas [3].
