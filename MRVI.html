Company Profile
Healthcare ·Life Sciences Tools & Services ·NASDAQ ·United States
Maravai LifeSciences Holdings, Inc. is a global provider of nucleic acid products and services supporting the development and manufacture of novel therapeutics, diagnostics, and vaccines. Operates through TriLink BioTechnologies (CleanCap mRNA capping technology and custom nucleic acids) and Cygnus Technologies (biologics safety testing) segments. Q2 2026 revenue of $51.4M (+9% YoY) with adjusted gross margin expanding 1,600bps to 58.9%. FY2026 guidance of $205-215M revenue and $33-35M adjusted EBITDA. Founded in 2014, headquartered in San Diego, California, with 415 employees.

Management raised FY2026 EBITDA guidance to $33-35M (from $30-32M) while reiterating $205-215M revenue guidance. TriLink's CleanCap technology retains a leading niche position with 11.5% revenue growth driven by discovery mRNA and GMP consumables demand [1]. The Officinae Bio acquisition adds AI-enabled DNA/RNA synthesis capabilities. CEO Brust's $809K personal share purchase at $3.24 (stock now $7.17, +121%) signals meaningful leadership conviction [6]. D/E of 61.3% is well-managed, and the margin trajectory suggests the worst of the post-COVID restructuring is behind the company.

Forward EPS of -$0.11 means the company is still expected to lose money, with no clear timeline to GAAP profitability [7]. The company competes against much larger, better-capitalized players (Thermo Fisher, Danaher) in nucleic acid production, with only 415 employees and limited resources for R&D investment. The 5-year revenue CAGR of -24% scores 0 with 2x weight in the infrastructure calculation, dragging the mechanical score down. At $7.17 per share with $2.66B market cap on $210M in revenue, the stock requires significant faith in a multi-year recovery trajectory that has not yet demonstrated scale or profitability.
