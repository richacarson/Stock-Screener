At 21.89x forward earnings — down from 25.57x recently — with 40.3% profit margins, 34.0% ROE, and 17.7% revenue growth, valuation has become more attractive for a company compounding at this quality and scale [1][6].
The bear case centers on capital intensity, organizational disruption, and environmental footprint. Internally, Nadella has dismantled the decades-old Senior Leadership Team, demoted veteran executives to individual-contributor roles, and layered a rigid return-to-office mandate atop 15,000+ prior-year layoffs — organizational churn that could erode execution quality during a critical AI competitive window [2]. Total emissions rose 25% in FY2025 as the AI buildout accelerated, with reports of paused carbon removal purchases and the company named by a UN Special Rapporteur regarding operations in conflict zones, creating mounting ESG pressure [4][5].
