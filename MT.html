Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Steel ·NYSE ·Luxembourg
ArcelorMittal S.A. is the world's largest steel producer, operating in over 60 countries with approximately 125,000 employees. The company produces flat and long steel products, tubular products, and mines iron ore and coking coal across Brazil, Liberia, Mexico, South Africa, India, and Canada. Under CEO Aditya Mittal, ArcelorMittal is pursuing decarbonization through EAF expansion (25% of global production) and the XCarb green steel initiative, while partnering with AWS for industrial automation and AI-driven optimization. H1 2026 revenue grew 4.9% to $32.2B, with EBITDA up 8.8% to $3.74B, though net profit declined sharply to $1.26B from $2.6B YoY [1]. The company expects world ex-China apparent steel demand to grow +2% in FY2026.

Investment Thesis
ArcelorMittal offers deep cyclical value as the world's largest steel producer, trading at 9.79x forward earnings with an exceptional PEG of 0.42 and a fortress balance sheet (D/E 25.4%, $4.8B cash) [4]. H1 2026 demonstrated operational improvement with revenue up 4.9% to $32.2B and EBITDA up 8.8% to $3.74B, while recently completed projects are expected to add $1.6B of EBITDA potential ($0.7B in 2026, $0.9B from 2027+) [1]. The strategic collaboration with AWS for AI-driven industrial automation and edge technologies positions ArcelorMittal to capture manufacturing efficiencies across its global operations [2]. The AI infrastructure build-out theme (data centers, power grids) is creating incremental steel demand. ArcelorMittal's vertical integration (mining + steelmaking), EAF expansion to 25% of production, and XCarb green steel initiative provide differentiated positioning as customers demand lower-carbon materials [3].
The bear case centers on commodity cyclicality, margin compression, and capital allocation concerns. H1 2026 net profit dropped sharply to $1.26B from $2.6B despite revenue growth, with profit margin at 2.88% and ROE of 3.28% reflecting the structural challenge of generating attractive returns in commodity steel [1][4]. Revenue has declined from $79.8B (2022) to $62.8B TTM, with a negative 5-year CAGR from peak. Free cash flow turned negative (-$779M), impacted by elevated capex and working capital. The delayed European green steel FIDs undermine the decarbonization thesis and raise questions about 2030 climate target achievability. Chinese steel overcapacity and global trade policy uncertainty create persistent pricing pressure. The Inspire Impact Score of 0 (MIXED) with no positive or negative attributions reflects a neutral faith-alignment profile. Dividend yield of only 0.82% provides limited income support, and the company suspended dividends in 2019-2020, demonstrating cyclical vulnerability of shareholder returns.

AI Risk Protection (8/9): ArcelorMittal's core business — mining iron ore, smelting steel in blast furnaces and EAFs, rolling and finishing steel products, and distributing through global logistics — is essentially immune to AI substitution [1]. Steel production requires massive physical infrastructure (blast furnaces, rolling mills, mines), complex metallurgical processes, and global supply chain operations that AI cannot replicate. AI may increase demand for steel through data center construction (AI infrastructure), grid modernization, and manufacturing expansion. ArcelorMittal's 100-person AI team and AWS collaboration demonstrate that AI enhances rather than threatens the business by optimizing production processes and accelerating product development [2]. The only risk dimension is that AI could help smaller competitors optimize more efficiently, but ArcelorMittal's scale advantages (55.6Mt production, 125,000 employees) are structural.
