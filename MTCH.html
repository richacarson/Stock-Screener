Company Profile

Match Group operates the world's largest portfolio of online dating platforms including Tinder, Hinge, Match, Meetic, OkCupid, Pairs, and BLK. Q2 2026 revenue of $853M declined 1% YoY as total payers fell 6% to 13.3M, partially offset by 6% RPP growth to $21.13. Tinder revenue of $457M declined 1%, while Hinge grew 22%. Investment Thesis
Match Group at 9.6x forward P/E is one of the cheapest profitable internet companies, generating improving profitability — Q2 2026 net income surged 36% to $171M with EBITDA up 14% to $331M on a 39% EBITDA margin [1]. Forward PEG of 0.35 indicates substantial earnings growth is priced affordably. Hinge continues its growth trajectory at 22% YoY, pursuing $1B revenue by 2027, while Tinder's DAU decline narrowed to -4% (best in 10 quarters), suggesting the product turnaround may be approaching an inflection point [1]. The Overtone AI dating venture positions the company to participate in AI-native social connection [2]. Revenue per payer growth of 6% to $21.13 demonstrates monetization improvement even as payers decline. Free cash flow of $931M on $3.5B revenue provides substantial capital return capacity. At this valuation, stabilization of Tinder user metrics or successful Hinge international expansion would drive meaningful multiple expansion from the currently depressed ~10x versus internet peer averages of 20-25x.
The bear case centers on accelerating user erosion and structural risks. Q2 2026 revenue declined 1% YoY to $853M — a reversal from Q1's +4% — and Q3 is guided down 2-3%, suggesting the turnaround is losing momentum [1]. Total payers declined 6% YoY to 13.3M, indicating structural user erosion. The January 2026 data breach (ShinyHunters claiming 10M+ records) and FTC enforcement against OkCupid create material legal liability and reputational damage [3][4]. AI companion chatbots represent a genuine substitution threat for younger users who increasingly form connections with AI rather than human partners. The negative equity position with $3.67B debt provides no financial cushion if the Tinder turnaround fails or legal settlements materialize at scale [5]. Significant C-suite instability — COO departed, CTO departed within months — undermines strategic execution confidence. The Inspire Impact Score of -67 (MISALIGNED) limits faith-based allocation. Q3 guidance of -2 to -3% suggests 2026 may end with negative growth despite Hinge's strength — Tinder's $1.8B+ scale means even modest declines overwhelm Hinge's 22% growth.
