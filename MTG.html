Company Profile
Financial Services ·Mortgage Finance ·NYSE ·United States
MGIC Investment Corporation, through its subsidiary Mortgage Guaranty Insurance Corporation, provides private mortgage insurance and other mortgage credit risk management solutions in the United States. The company insures mortgage default risk for lenders on residential first mortgages with loan-to-value ratios exceeding 80%, enabling homebuyers to purchase homes with less than 20% down payments.
900 Employees ·www.mgic.com

Existential Flexibility
4
MGIC is a pure-play mortgage insurer with virtually no diversification beyond its core PMI business. The company has not pursued adjacent products like title insurance, home warranty, or mortgage origination technology. This focus delivers efficiency but creates existential concentration risk tied to housing cycles and regulatory changes [4]. The near-death experience of 2008-2012 did not trigger a strategic pivot.
Courage to Lead
5
MGIC survived the 2007-2012 housing crisis when the company faced potential insolvency, suspended dividends, and rebuilt capital over a decade — a demonstration of resilience under existential pressure [2]. Dividend reinstatement in 2019 and aggressive share buybacks demonstrate confidence. However, the recovery was survival-driven rather than proactive strategic courage, and the company has not taken bold bets to redefine its industry.

Investment Thesis
MGIC at 8.97x forward earnings with a 59.6% profit margin and 12% D/E ratio offers deep value in a regulated mortgage insurance franchise with structural demand from the conventional mortgage market [2][5]. The 17.2% payout ratio provides massive headroom for dividend growth — the quarterly dividend has grown from $0.10 to $0.15 over three years. Aggressive share buybacks further enhance per-share returns, and the company posted record NIW growth in Q1 2026 despite a challenging rate environment.
The bear case centers on cyclical housing risk and business model concentration. Revenue declined 1.2% YoY with the mortgage market facing sustained high rates that suppress origination volumes [6]. MGIC's business is entirely dependent on new mortgage originations, and any prolonged housing downturn would directly impact insurance-in-force and premiums written. The company has no product diversification beyond private mortgage insurance, making it fully exposed to GSE policy changes that could alter the PMI requirement. ROE of 14.4% is adequate but not exceptional, and the innovation profile reflects a company optimizing rather than reinventing its business model.
Key Catalysts
  1. Federal Reserve rate cuts driving mortgage rates below 6% would significantly boost origination volumes, directly increasing new insurance written and reversing the revenue decline trend
  2. Continued aggressive dividend growth from the 17.2% payout ratio — management has tripled the quarterly dividend from $0.05 to $0.15 since 2022, with substantial room for further increases [6]
  3. AI-driven underwriting efficiency gains improving loss ratios and operating leverage, combined with structural housing supply shortage sustaining MI demand
Key Risks
  1. Sustained high mortgage rates above 6.5% suppressing origination volumes — every 50bp increase in rates reduces purchase applications by an estimated 5-8%, directly impacting new insurance written
  2. GSE reform or policy changes reducing or eliminating the private mortgage insurance requirement for conventional loans, which would be an existential threat to the single-product business model
  3. Rising unemployment or housing price correction increasing loss incidence and severity on the insurance-in-force portfolio, echoing the 2008-2012 crisis that nearly destroyed the company [4]
