Company Profile
Technology ·Semiconductors ·NASDAQ ·United States
MACOM Technology Solutions designs and manufactures analog semiconductor solutions for wireless and wireline applications across the RF, microwave, millimeter wave, and lightwave spectrum. Founded in 1950 and headquartered in Lowell, Massachusetts, the company serves data center, industrial and defense, and telecom markets with integrated circuits, multi-chip modules, diodes, amplifiers, switches, and subsystems used in 5G/6G infrastructure, radar, electronic warfare, fiber optics, and AI data center interconnects. Q3 FY2026 revenue reached $342.2M (+35.8% YoY) with Q4 guided at $415-425M. Data center revenue growth raised to 60%+ driven by 1.6T optical demand.

Investment Thesis
MACOM Technology Solutions is a direct beneficiary of the AI infrastructure buildout, with Q3 FY2026 delivering $342.2M revenue (+35.8% YoY) beating guidance and Q4 guided at $415-425M with adj EPS of $1.97-$2.03 [1]. Management raised data center revenue growth guidance from 35-40% to 60%+, driven by 1.6T optical demand from hyperscaler customers. The financial profile has improved dramatically: forward P/E compressed from 58.7x to 32.87x as forward EPS estimates surged to $8.49, ROE crossed the 15% target to 17.2%, and profit margin reached 20.7% with adjusted gross margins approaching 60% [8]. The balance sheet is conservatively managed with D/E of only 26.9%, and the PEG of 2.05 reflects the growth premium. Strategic investments in IQE compound semiconductors, STMicroelectronics GaN partnership, and Hot Via chip-scale packaging strengthen the long-term technology position [3]. AI Resilience score of 10 reflects maximum immunity — MACOM's physical semiconductor products become more, not less, valuable as AI scales.
The bear case centers on valuation premium and cyclical risk. Trailing P/E of 88.6x reflects the gap between current and forward earnings — the $8.49 forward EPS estimate requires FY2026 to close strong and FY2027 to sustain momentum, and any semiconductor cycle downturn could compress both earnings and multiples simultaneously [8]. PEG of 2.05 remains above the 1.0 target, and the forward P/E of 32.87x still carries a significant premium to semiconductor peers. Heavy insider selling over the past 12 months by major shareholders and executives suggests insiders view current prices as stretched [7]. The low Inspiration score of 2 (WEAK) reflects no articulated purpose beyond product excellence, leadership culture concerns on Glassdoor, and net insider selling — raising questions about long-term organizational resilience. Geopolitical risks including AI chip export controls, trade tensions with China (a significant MACOM market), and supply chain dependencies in compound semiconductors (GaN, InP) create additional headwinds [3]. The Infinite Game score of 4 (FINITE) suggests the company optimizes for growth execution rather than enduring purpose.
