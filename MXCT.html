Company Profile
Healthcare ·Medical Instruments & Supplies ·NASDAQ ·United States
MaxCyte is a cell engineering company that provides Flow Electroporation technology and instruments enabling researchers and biopharmaceutical companies to transfect cells for drug discovery, development, and manufacturing of cell-based therapies including CAR-T, gene editing, and other advanced cell therapies. The company's ExPERT platform is used by over 150 cell therapy developers worldwide, and its Flow Electroporation technology was instrumental in developing CASGEVY, the first FDA-approved non-viral cell therapy. MaxCyte also offers SeQure DX gene editing services and Strategic Platform License (SPL) agreements. Q2 2026 revenue was $7.3M (-15% YoY) with FY2026 guidance of $30-32M. Headquartered in Gaithersburg, Maryland.

AI Resilience MODERATE

AI Risk Protection (7/9): MaxCyte's core business — manufacturing and selling physical electroporation instruments for cell engineering — involves proprietary hardware and biological processes that AI structurally cannot replace [3]. The transfection of cells requires physical electrical pulses to create temporary pores in cell membranes, governed by physics and biology rather than data processing. FDA regulatory requirements for GMP manufacturing equipment create additional barriers to substitution. AI may enhance cell therapy development workflows (target identification, protocol optimization), but the physical electroporation step remains essential. The modest risk is that AI-designed alternative delivery mechanisms could eventually compete, but this is speculative and long-term. AI Opportunity (0/1): MaxCyte does not generate revenue from AI products or services. The company's value proposition is entirely biological/physical [3].
