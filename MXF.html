Company Profile
Financial Services ·Asset Management ·NYSE ·United States
The Mexico Fund, Inc. is a closed-end equity mutual fund that invests primarily in public equity markets of Mexico. Managed by Impulsora del Fondo Mexico, S.C., the fund provides U.S. investors access to Mexican equities listed on the Mexican Stock Exchange. Founded in 1981 and domiciled in Maryland, the fund trades on the NYSE at a persistent discount to NAV.

Investment Thesis
MXF offers concentrated exposure to Mexican equities at a ~12% discount to NAV with a 6.9% dividend yield supported by 31 consecutive years of distributions [1][2]. The fund benefits from Mexico's nearshoring tailwinds as global supply chains diversify from China, driving investment into Mexican manufacturing and infrastructure. NAV returned 24.73% over the trailing year through April 2026, outperforming the broader emerging markets index [1]. Alberto Osorio's 35-year tenure with the fund adviser provides continuity and local market expertise that passive vehicles cannot replicate.
The fund fails IOWN's liquidity floor with average daily dollar volume of just $664K, making meaningful position sizing and exit impossible without market impact [2]. The persistent NAV discount (11-12%) reflects structural issues in the closed-end fund format that management has not addressed in 45 years, while lower-cost alternatives like EWW ETF offer Mexican equity exposure at a fraction of the expense ratio [3]. Saba Capital's 8.35% activist stake creates governance uncertainty and potential for disruptive proxy fights [4]. Revenue declined 7.3% YoY, and the fund's lack of any strategic evolution or competitive response to the passive investing revolution suggests a business model in secular decline.
