Company Profile

Myriad Genetics is a molecular diagnostics and precision medicine company offering tests for oncology (hereditary cancer, tumor profiling, MRD), women's health (prenatal screening, carrier screening), and pharmacogenomics (GeneSight psychotropic medication guidance). The company is pursuing a Cancer Care Continuum strategy but faces execution challenges — Q2 FY2026 revenue declined 11% YoY to $190.7M with average revenue per test falling 9%. Founded in 1991, headquartered in Salt Lake City, Utah. Market cap has fallen to ~$363M.

Investment Thesis
Myriad Genetics is a deep-value turnaround play in precision medicine trading at just $3.79 per share (~$363M market cap) — near its all-time low — with AI-enabled product launches that could reignite growth if execution improves. The Prolaris + AI Test (prostate cancer), Precise MRD (breast cancer, launched March 2026), and GeneSight (pharmacogenomics, +24% YoY in Q1) represent a differentiated pipeline targeting a $10B+ oncology diagnostics market [2][3]. CEO Sam Raha (ex-Agilent) and CTO Raj Jampa are deploying efficiency initiatives while maintaining the Cancer Care Continuum strategy. The balance sheet provides transformation runway with D/E of just 32.8% [6]. Reaffirmed FY2026 guidance of $860-880M revenue and $37-49M adjusted EBITDA suggests management sees a path to profitability that, if achieved, could meaningfully re-rate the stock from current distressed levels.
The bear case is severe and growing. Q2 FY2026 revenue of $190.7M declined 11% YoY with a devastating 9% decrease in average revenue per test, signaling competitive pricing commoditization that threatens the entire business model [1][2]. The stock has collapsed 37% from $6.05 to $3.79 since the previous screen as the market loses confidence in the turnaround. Four consecutive years of net losses with -41.3% profit margin and -23.5% ROE demonstrate fundamental execution failure [6]. At $363M market cap, Myriad is a micro-cap subject to extreme volatility and potential delisting risk if the decline continues. AI-powered competitors (Guardant, Tempus, Foundation Medicine) are building next-generation platforms that could permanently erode Myriad's position. The Inspire Impact Score of 47 (ALIGNED) is the one bright spot for faith-based investors, but financial deterioration dominates the investment case. The FY2026 guidance of $860-880M looks increasingly at risk given H1 revenue of ~$391M requires a significant H2 acceleration to $469M+.
