Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Other Industrial Metals & Mining ·NYQ ·Canada
Northern Dynasty Minerals is a single-asset mineral exploration company holding 100% interest in the Pebble copper-gold-molybdenum-silver-rhenium deposit in southwest Alaska, one of the world's largest undeveloped copper-gold resources (1,840 mineral claims covering ~274 square miles). The EPA veto under Section 404(c) of the Clean Water Act is being challenged in Alaska Federal District Court, with oral arguments held June 25, 2026 and a judicial decision pending. The company generates zero revenue, reported a 2025 net loss of C$104M, and survives on royalty agreements and periodic financing. Market cap approximately $630M at $1.12 per share.

Investment Thesis
Northern Dynasty represents a highly speculative binary option on the Pebble Mine's judicial outcome, with the deposit containing an estimated 57B lbs copper and 71M oz gold in one of the world's largest undeveloped resources [1]. Oral arguments were held on June 25, 2026 in Alaska Federal District Court, and the matter is now 'ripe for decision,' creating a near-term catalyst for resolution [2]. Copper's critical role in AI data center infrastructure and the energy transition provides a secular demand tailwind for the underlying commodity, and the current administration's executive orders on domestic critical mineral production provide a supportive political backdrop. At $1.12 and ~$630M market cap, the stock is deeply discounted relative to the resource's in-ground value.
The bear case remains overwhelming. The stock has declined from $1.62 to $1.12 (-31%) since the previous screen, with the 52-week range reflecting extreme volatility driven entirely by legal speculation [4]. ROE deteriorated sharply to -261.3%, reflecting accelerating capital destruction, and D/E increased from 5% to 30.2% as the company took on additional financing [4]. The DOJ filed its brief supporting the EPA veto in February 2026, and the outcome of the pending judicial decision is highly uncertain — even a favorable ruling would restart years of permitting, environmental review, and partner recruitment before any production could begin [2]. The company has never generated revenue, has no disclosed employees, and would need $5-10B+ in construction capital it cannot independently raise. Four global mining partners have abandoned the project due to environmental and reputational risk, and over 80% of Bristol Bay residents oppose development that threatens the world's largest sockeye salmon fishery [3]. The ~$630M market cap is entirely speculative — there is no operating business to value, and additional dilutive financing will be required to maintain corporate operations.
