Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Other Industrial Metals & Mining ·NYSE American ·Canada
Northern Dynasty Minerals is a single-asset mineral exploration company holding 100% interest in the Pebble copper-gold-molybdenum-silver-rhenium deposit in southwest Alaska, one of the world's largest undeveloped copper-gold resources (1,840 mineral claims covering ~274 square miles). The EPA veto under Section 404(c) of the Clean Water Act is being challenged in Alaska Federal District Court, with oral arguments held on June 25, 2026 and a judicial decision now pending. The company generates zero revenue and survives on royalty agreements and periodic financing. Market cap approximately $910 million.

Investment Thesis

Oral arguments were held on June 25, 2026 in Alaska Federal District Court, and the matter is now 'ripe for decision,' creating a near-term catalyst for resolution [2]. The bear case remains overwhelming. The company has never generated revenue, has no employees, and would need $5-10B+ in construction capital it cannot independently raise. Four global mining partners have abandoned the project due to environmental and reputational risk, and over 80% of Bristol Bay residents oppose development that threatens the world's largest sockeye salmon fishery [3]. The $910M market cap is entirely speculative — there is no operating business to value.
