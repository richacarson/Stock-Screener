Company Profile
Financial Services ·Capital Markets ·NASDAQ ·United States
Nakamoto Inc. (formerly KindlyMD) is a Bitcoin operating company chaired and led by CEO David Bailey, holding more than 5,000 BTC on its balance sheet alongside operating businesses acquired in a $107M all-stock deal: BTC Inc. (Bitcoin Magazine, the Bitcoin Conference) and UTXO Management (asset management) [1]. Since converting from a healthcare company via merger with Nakamoto Holdings in 2025, the stock has fallen roughly 99% from its May 2025 all-time high amid a 1-for-40 reverse split, forced Bitcoin sales to fund operations, and a $210M Bitcoin-backed loan from Kraken [1][2].

Investment Thesis
Nakamoto offers leveraged, operating-company exposure to Bitcoin: more than 5,000 BTC on the balance sheet plus operating businesses in Bitcoin media (Bitcoin Magazine, the Bitcoin Conference) and asset management (UTXO), acquired for $107M in an all-stock deal to diversify beyond a passive treasury model [1]. Q2 2026 marked the company's first positive adjusted operating income since becoming a Bitcoin operating company, and CEO David Bailey's personal $209,989 share purchase in May 2026 signals insider conviction after the stock's steep decline [1].
The bear case is severe: the stock has fallen roughly 99% from its May 2025 all-time high, required a 1-for-40 reverse split, and the company has sold Bitcoin (including 284 BTC for $20M in Q1 2026) to fund working capital — a troubling pattern for a company whose core thesis is accumulating, not liquidating, Bitcoin [1]. A $210M Bitcoin-backed loan from Kraken creates forced-liquidation risk if Bitcoin's price falls further, and ROE of roughly -343% reflects the scale of losses, including goodwill impairment, already absorbed. CEO Bailey's own public letter telling shareholders 'I encourage you to exit' is a remarkable admission that even leadership sees limited near-term upside, and the retained Chief Medical Officer role from the company's prior healthcare identity has become a public symbol of governance drift. This is a high-risk, self-acknowledged turnaround situation, not a stable holding.
Key Catalysts
  1. Sustained positive adjusted operating income following the Q2 2026 milestone, if the BTC Inc. and UTXO Management acquisitions continue to generate recurring media and asset-management revenue independent of Bitcoin price swings [1]
  2. Stabilization of the Bitcoin derivatives yield strategy (which generated ~43 BTC in premiums in Q2 2026) as a way to grow holdings without further outright Bitcoin sales [1]
  3. Successful launch of the Intelligence 2027 event franchise, if it establishes Nakamoto as a credible player at the Bitcoin/AI intersection beyond a single conference brand [2]
Key Risks
  1. The $210M Bitcoin-backed loan from Kraken creates forced-liquidation risk that could compound losses if Bitcoin's price declines further, directly threatening the company's core treasury thesis [2]
  2. Continued need to sell Bitcoin holdings to fund working capital (284 BTC sold for $20M in Q1 2026 alone) undermines the accumulation thesis that Bitcoin treasury investors expect [1]
  3. CEO David Bailey's own public 'I encourage you to exit' letter and the resulting 50% share price plunge reflect a fundamental crisis of confidence that has not yet been resolved [3]
