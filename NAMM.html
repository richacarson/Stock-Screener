Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Gold ·NASDAQ ·Cayman Islands
Namib Minerals operates as a gold producer in Africa, owning and operating How Mine, Mazowe Mine, and Redwing Mine in Zimbabwe. The company was incorporated in 2024 as a subsidiary of The Southern SelliBen Trust and is based in Grand Cayman, Cayman Islands. FY2025 revenue was $82.6M with approximately 25,000 oz gold production. Market cap has contracted to $76.5M as the stock fell 81% from its 52-week high of $7.42 to $1.37. New CEO Tulani Sikwila was appointed in March 2026.

AI Risk Protection (8/9): Gold mining is an inherently physical business — extracting, processing, and refining gold ore requires heavy machinery, geological expertise, and on-the-ground operations that cannot be replaced or disrupted by artificial intelligence [1]. AI cannot mine gold, and there is no foreseeable scenario in which software substitutes for the physical extraction of precious metals. The gold product itself is a commodity with value derived from scarcity and physical properties, not from information content that AI could replicate. AI-driven automation could modestly improve exploration efficiency and processing yields, but this represents incremental optimization rather than existential disruption. Operating in Zimbabwe's challenging environment adds a layer of human judgment and relationship management that AI cannot replicate [3].
