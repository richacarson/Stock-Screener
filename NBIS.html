Investment Thesis
Nebius is one of the purest AI infrastructure plays in public markets, building full-stack GPU cloud platforms for the global AI industry with extraordinary growth momentum — Q2 2026 revenue of $582M (+454% YoY), H1 2026 revenue of $981.3M, and a target of $7-9B annualized by end of 2026 [1]. The $40B+ contracted revenue from Microsoft and Meta provides exceptional forward visibility, and NVIDIA's strategic $700M investment validates the platform's quality and positions Nebius as a preferred GPU cloud partner [2]. Adjusted EBITDA flipped positive at $236.2M in Q2 2026, with management targeting 40% EBITDA margins in 2026 — suggesting a path from growth-at-all-costs to profitable scale. The company's Finland data center featuring innovative free cooling and heat recovery systems achieves among Europe's best energy efficiency metrics [3]. Inspire Impact Score of 38 (ALIGNED) with zero negative attributions supports faith-based allocation.
The bear case centers on extreme valuation, execution risk, and the sustainability of AI infrastructure spending. At $66B market cap on trailing revenue of ~$1.5B annualized and negative forward EPS (-$3.49), the stock prices in flawless execution toward $7-9B annualized revenue [4]. Capital intensity is staggering — $2.1B in Q4 2025 capex alone — and the $775M secured debt financing adds leverage risk if AI infrastructure demand decelerates. Customer concentration in Microsoft and Meta creates dependency risk similar to enterprise cloud concentration. The competitive landscape includes AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, and CoreWeave, all backed by vastly larger balance sheets. Nebius's Russian heritage and Yandex legacy introduce geopolitical and reputational complexity, including potential regulatory scrutiny and investor concern about governance in the Dutch holding structure [2][3]. Profit margin of 3.13% and ROE of 0.60% reflect a company that is investing far ahead of profitability, and any slowdown in hyperscaler AI capex — or a shift to custom silicon reducing GPU cloud demand — could strand billions in infrastructure investment.

Key Risks

  3. Customer concentration in Microsoft and Meta for the majority of $40B+ contracted revenue replicates enterprise dependency risk; loss of either relationship or renegotiation of contract terms could materially impact growth trajectory [1]
