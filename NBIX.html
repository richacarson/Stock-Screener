Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Neurocrine Biosciences is a neuroscience-focused biopharmaceutical company that discovers, develops, and markets treatments for neurological, neuroendocrine, and neuropsychiatric disorders. Lead products include INGREZZA (valbenazine) for tardive dyskinesia, CRENESSITY (crinecerfont) for congenital adrenal hyperplasia, and VYKAT XR. The company completed the acquisition of Soleno Therapeutics in May 2026, adding DCCR for Prader-Willi syndrome. FY2025 net product sales totaled $2.83B (+22% YoY), and Q2 2026 revenue grew 39% YoY to $959M. Founded in 1992, headquartered in San Diego. Market cap approximately $14.1B.

Investment Thesis
Neurocrine Biosciences offers a rare combination of high growth at deeply discounted valuation — Q2 2026 revenue of $959M (39% YoY), Forward P/E of 11.56x, PEG of 0.37, and ROE of 22.09% on a fortress balance sheet with D/E of just 12.5% [1][4]. The profit margin has expanded to 20.91%, demonstrating operating leverage as CRENESSITY scales and INGREZZA continues growing at ~20% YoY. Management raised FY2026 INGREZZA guidance to $2,825-2,875M and completed the Soleno acquisition to add a third commercial franchise [2]. The pipeline includes luvbaloxamide for schizophrenia (Phase 3) and DCCR for Prader-Willi syndrome, providing next-generation growth catalysts beyond the current product portfolio.
The primary risk is concentration — INGREZZA still represents approximately 75% of revenue with patent expiration approaching in the early 2030s, and any competitive threat, generic entry, or pricing pressure would have outsized impact on earnings power [1]. CRENESSITY's long-term peak sales remain uncertain as the congenital adrenal hyperplasia market is small (~30,000 US patients). The stock at $138.40 has underperformed the broader biotech index despite strong fundamentals, suggesting the market is discounting pipeline risk and the INGREZZA patent cliff [4]. Significant insider selling ($62.4M in recent quarters), while likely routine compensation-driven liquidation, creates negative optics. The Soleno acquisition adds execution risk and integration complexity for a company that has historically been an organic growth story. The Inspire Impact Score of 48 (ALIGNED) is a positive for faith-based investors [5].
