Company Profile
Consumer Cyclical ·Apparel Manufacturing ·NasdaqCM ·Hong Kong
Neo-Concept International Group Holdings Limited is a one-stop apparel solution services provider based in Hong Kong, offering market trend analysis, product design and development, raw material sourcing, production and quality control, and logistics management. The company serves brand owners, apparel sourcing agents, and online fashion retailers in European and North American markets, and sells owned-brand knitwear under 'Les100Ciels' and 'SIU' brands through retail stores in the UK and UAE and e-commerce. Full-year 2025 revenue declined 41.8% to HK$137.3M (~$17.6M USD) as China-USA tariff disruptions devastated the private-label business. The company raised $8.1M through a dilutive public offering in February 2026.

Investment Thesis
Neo-Concept International is a micro-cap apparel company attempting a strategic pivot from private-label sourcing to owned-brand retail through Les100Ciels and SIU brand acquisitions [4]. Owned-brand revenue grew 138.8% in H1 2025, and net income turned positive at HK$2.0M (~$0.3M USD) versus HK$1.4M in the prior period [1]. The company's commitment to sustainable fashion — traceable cashmere sourcing, clean manufacturing processes, ethical supply chain documentation — differentiates it modestly in the premium knitwear niche [6]. If the owned-brand channel scales successfully and tariff headwinds stabilize, the current micro-cap valuation could offer asymmetric upside.
The fundamental problem is that Neo-Concept's business has collapsed — full-year 2025 revenue declined 41.8% to HK$137.3M (~$17.6M USD) as China-USA tariff policies devastated the private-label business, and H1 2025 delivered just $7.7M USD in total revenue [1][5]. With a 0.24% profit margin, 0.58% ROE, and trailing P/E of 151.6, the company generates virtually no economic value for shareholders. The dilutive February 2026 public offering at $0.5454/share — raising only $8.1M — suggests the company needed emergency capital to continue operations [7]. Board instability (director resignation March 2026), no analyst coverage, no institutional sponsorship, no Forward P/E or PEG data, and HK$82% D/E on a rapidly declining revenue base make this effectively uninvestable for most capital allocators [3][8]. The owned-brand pivot is unproven at scale, and the company competes against vastly larger, better-capitalized global apparel companies. Further tariff escalation could eliminate the remaining private-label business entirely.
Key Catalysts
  1. Les100Ciels and SIU owned-brand retail scaling in UK, UAE, and e-commerce channels, building on the 138.8% H1 2025 growth that partially offsets private-label collapse

Key Risks

  2. Profit margin of 0.24% and ROE of 0.58% leave zero buffer against any revenue shortfall or cost increase — the company is one bad quarter away from net losses
  3. Dilutive February 2026 offering at $0.5454/share, board instability (director resignation), no analyst coverage, no Forward P/E, and micro-cap scale create extreme information asymmetry and governance risk for outside investors
