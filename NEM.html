Company Profile

Newmont Corporation is the world's largest gold producer, also exploring for copper, silver, lead, zinc, and other metals. The company operates across 12 countries including the United States, Australia, Ghana, Peru, Canada, and Papua New Guinea. Founded in 1916 and headquartered in Denver, Colorado. Q2 2026 revenue reached $6.12B (+15% YoY) with record $2.2B quarterly free cash flow and $3.8B adjusted EBITDA, driven by an average realized gold price of $4,414/oz (+33% YoY). Investment Thesis
Newmont is the world's largest gold producer delivering record financial performance on the back of historic gold prices — Q2 2026 revenue of $6.12B (+15% YoY), adjusted EPS of $2.10 (+47% YoY), and record $2.2B quarterly free cash flow with $3.8B adjusted EBITDA, powered by a $4,414/oz average realized gold price (+33% YoY) [1]. The balance sheet is a fortress with D/E of just 15.8%, net cash position of $3.83B, and ROE of 22.2% [4]. At a forward P/E of 11.93x, the stock appears significantly undervalued relative to earnings power with 23 analysts rating it Buy and a $131.39 consensus target (+9% upside). The 2023 Newcrest acquisition created an unmatched global portfolio of tier-one gold and copper assets, and AI Resilience at 8/10 reflects gold mining's structural immunity to AI disruption while AI-driven energy demand supports gold's safe-haven premium. The Inspire Insight score of 52 (ALIGNED) with 17 positive attributions and zero negatives, combined with #1 DJSI ranking for 8 consecutive years, confirms strong faith and ESG alignment [3].
The bear case centers on gold price dependency, production declines, and organizational instability. Newmont's exceptional financials are primarily driven by gold price appreciation rather than operational volume growth — Q2 2026 gold production of 1.29M oz declined 12.5% YoY, and a sustained gold price correction would compress revenue, margins, and FCF simultaneously [1][4]. The simultaneous replacement of CFO, COO, and CTO in 2026 represents a wholesale leadership reset that creates execution risk during a critical period of Newcrest integration [2]. Glassdoor reviews citing 'constant layoffs,' 'no job security,' and 'growing disconnect between corporate and ground level' suggest cultural challenges that could impact talent retention — particularly in the 'global war for talent' CEO Palmer has identified around data science and AI expertise [2]. The dividend has been cut from $2.20 to $1.04 annualized over prior years, undermining income investor confidence despite record cash flows. Mining companies inherently face geopolitical, regulatory, and environmental risks across 12-country operations, and Sustainalytics' 'Significant Controversy Level' rating and unmet human rights targets create ESG headline risk despite the strong DJSI ranking [3].
