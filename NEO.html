Company Profile
Healthcare ·Diagnostics & Research ·NASDAQ ·United States
NeoGenomics, Inc. operates a network of cancer-focused testing laboratories in the United States and the United Kingdom. The company provides cytogenetics, FISH, flow cytometry, immunohistochemistry, molecular testing, and next-generation sequencing (NGS) services to hospitals, academic centers, oncologists, pharmaceutical companies, and clinical laboratories. Founded in 2001, headquartered in Fort Myers, Florida.

Investment Thesis
NeoGenomics offers pure-play exposure to the precision oncology diagnostics market, with Q1 2026 revenue of $187M growing 11% YoY driven by accelerating NGS growth of 26% that now represents a third of clinical revenue [1]. Full-year 2026 guidance was raised to $797-803M with adjusted EBITDA of $55-57M (27-31% YoY growth), representing a clear profitability inflection trajectory [1]. The RaDaR ST launch and PanTracer MolDx approval add high-margin, clinically differentiated products to the portfolio, while average unit price increases of 8% demonstrate pricing power [1]. The June 2026 convertible note offering priced at a 35% premium signals strong institutional demand [8].
The bear case centers on persistent unprofitability and execution risk. Despite $187M quarterly revenue and improving loss trajectory (net loss decreased 34% YoY in Q1), NeoGenomics still generates negative ROE of -13% and negative profit margin — the path to GAAP profitability remains unproven [1][7]. CEO Tony Zook was appointed only in April 2025 and faces Glassdoor culture concerns with employees citing 'resistance to innovation' and favoritism in career advancement [3]. AI-powered pathology from Paige AI, PathAI, and Google Health poses medium-term disruption risk to NeoGenomics' image-based diagnostics business lines. The stock's 25% appreciation since April to $10.45 has stretched the forward valuation, while competition from Quest, Labcorp, and Foundation Medicine with vastly greater scale could compress margins as NGS testing commoditizes.

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience MODERATE
5
AI Risk Protection (4/9): Cancer diagnostics faces meaningful AI exposure. AI-powered pathology and diagnostic tools from companies like Paige AI, PathAI, and Google Health are demonstrating ability to read tissue slides, identify genetic markers, and interpret molecular data — core activities NeoGenomics performs with human pathologists and technicians [3]. While regulatory barriers (CLIA certification, FDA approval) provide near-term protection, AI could partially replace anatomic pathology workflows over 3-5 years. NeoGenomics' NGS testing (now 33% of clinical revenue, growing 26% [1]) is more defensible since it involves complex wet-lab chemistry that AI cannot replicate, but image-based diagnostics (IHC, cytogenetics) face real substitution risk as AI pathology matures. AI Opportunity (1/1): NeoGenomics deploys AI internally through its digital strategy initiative, leveraging automation for lab workflow efficiency, and NGS bioinformatics pipelines use ML for variant calling and interpretation — generating measurable operational value even if not separately reported as revenue [3].
