Company Profile
Healthcare ·Medical Devices ·NASDAQ ·United States
Neogen Corporation develops, manufactures, and markets products dedicated to food and animal safety worldwide. The Food Safety segment (about 73% of Q1 FY2027 revenue) offers diagnostic test kits and culture media for pathogen, allergen, mycotoxin, and environmental monitoring, anchored by the Petrifilm platform acquired with 3M's Food Safety business in 2022. The Animal Safety segment provides animal genomics, veterinary instruments, and biosecurity products. CEO Mike Nassif (appointed August 2025, formerly of Siemens Healthineers) is leading a turnaround focused on execution discipline, organic innovation, and margin expansion.

Investment Thesis
Neogen's turnaround is gaining traction. Q1 FY2027 revenue rose 6.5% to $222.8M with core growth of 8.1%, Food Safety grew 7.4% to $163.2M, Animal Safety returned to growth (+4.2% to $59.6M), and adjusted EBITDA margin expanded to 18.7% from 17.0% [1]. Management raised FY2027 guidance to $885-890M revenue and $181-183M adjusted EBITDA, and the October 2026 Investor Day laid out a plan for growth, innovation, and margin expansion through FY2031 [1][2]. The balance sheet is conservative for a recovering business (D/E 37%, current ratio 3.9x), and the stock trades at 26x forward earnings and roughly 1.2x book value [4]. Food-safety testing is a recurring, regulation-driven consumables business with secular tailwinds from tightening global food standards.
The bear case is that GAAP profitability remains elusive: Neogen posted a $11.9M net loss in Q1 FY2027, TTM profit margin is -6.3%, and ROE is -2.7%, with $57.6M of FY2026 interest expense absorbing much of the operating improvement [1][3][4]. Roughly 3 points of Q1 core growth came from prior-year distributor inventory adjustments and order timing, so underlying growth is closer to 5% [1]. Tangible book value is negative after the 3M merger, R&D intensity is only 2.1% of revenue, and the executive team is newly assembled, leaving execution risk on the FY2031 targets [3][4].
Key Catalysts
  1. Raised FY2027 guidance of $885-890M revenue and $181-183M adjusted EBITDA; continued margin expansion toward 20%+ would support a return to GAAP profitability [1]
  2. Execution on the FY2031 targets laid out at the October 2026 Investor Day, including the first organic innovation pipeline under Nassif [2]
  3. Debt paydown reducing the $57.6M annual interest burden, which would flow directly to GAAP EPS [3]
Key Risks
  1. Roughly 3 points of Q1 FY2027 core growth were timing-related (distributor inventory and order timing), so growth could decelerate in coming quarters [1]
  2. Persistent GAAP losses (TTM profit margin -6.3%, ROE -2.7%) and negative tangible book value after the 3M merger and $1.09B FY2025 write-down [3][4]
  3. Low R&D intensity (2.1% of revenue) leaves Neogen vulnerable to molecular and rapid-testing platforms from better-funded diagnostics competitors [3]
