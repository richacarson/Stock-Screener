Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas Equipment & Services ·NASDAQ ·United States
National Energy Services Reunited is the largest MENA-headquartered oilfield services company publicly listed on NASDAQ. Founded in 2017 as a SPAC by former Schlumberger executive Sherif Foda, NESR provides production services (hydraulic fracturing, coiled tubing, cementing, stimulation, water management), drilling and evaluation services, and environmental/decarbonization services (NEDA) across 16 countries in the Middle East and North Africa. NESR reported record Q2 2026 revenue of $520.8M (+59% YoY) with net income of $44.0M, surpassing its $2B annualized revenue run rate target ahead of schedule. Market cap approximately $3.2 billion.

Investment Thesis
NESR delivered record Q2 2026 revenue of $520.8M (+59% YoY) with net income nearly tripling to $44.0M, while adjusted EBITDA reached $106.2M at a 20.4% margin — expanding 150bps sequentially [1]. The company surpassed its $2B annualized revenue run rate target ahead of schedule, driven by the Jafurah unconventional gas contract ramp in Saudi Arabia and strong demand across Oman and Egypt. The balance sheet is conservative (D/E 29.3%, $175M cash vs $305M debt) and the stock trades at just 11.6x forward earnings ($2.76 forward EPS) despite 59% revenue growth and 190% quarterly earnings growth. Management is initiating capital returns with a $0.10/share quarterly dividend starting Q4 2026 and a $50M buyback program, signaling confidence in sustainable free cash flow of $137.6M [1]. NESR's positioning as the dominant MENA-native energy services provider gives it preferential access to Saudi Aramco, ADNOC, and other national oil company spending programs.
The bear case centers on margin quality, insider selling, and customer concentration. Profit margin of 5.8% and ROE of 9.4% are well below IOWN targets, indicating that impressive topline growth is not translating into commensurate shareholder returns — the oilfield services business is inherently capital-intensive and low-margin even during upcycles [1]. Director Al-Nowais liquidated $51.4M in shares in June 2026 (89% of his holdings) following 1.5M+ additional shares sold in May 2026, signaling that a key insider believes current prices reflect full value after the stock's 223% move from $9.92 to $32.09 over 52 weeks. Geographic concentration in MENA creates severe customer risk — Saudi Aramco spending cuts, OPEC+ production curtailments, or sustained oil below $60/barrel would compress the entire revenue base. At a trailing P/E of 35.7x (inflated by the rapid earnings growth ramp), any execution miss on Jafurah or new contract wins could sharply re-rate the stock. The SPAC origin and relatively short public history leave limited track record for navigating a full commodity cycle downturn.
