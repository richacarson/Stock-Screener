Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas Midstream ·NASDAQ ·United States
New Fortress Energy is an integrated gas-to-power energy infrastructure company providing LNG terminals, floating storage and regasification units (FSRUs), LNG carriers, and power generation across the Caribbean, Mexico, and Brazil. The company completed a 1:30 reverse stock split in 2025 amid severe financial distress. Q2 2026 revenue of $312.5M missed consensus of $511.6M, with EPS of -$1.30. FY2025 revenue was $1.50B (-36% YoY). The company carries $8.57B in debt against $190M cash with negative shareholders' equity of -$55M and has flagged going concern risk. FLNG 1 liquefaction came online and Barcarena terminal became operational in April 2026. Market cap ~$38M.
