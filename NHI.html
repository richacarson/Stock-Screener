Company Profile
Real Estate ·REIT - Healthcare Facilities ·NYSE ·United States
National Health Investors is a self-managed real estate investment trust specializing in sale-leaseback, joint ventures, and mortgage and mezzanine financing of senior housing and medical facility investments. The portfolio includes independent living, assisted living and memory care communities, entrance-fee retirement communities, senior living campuses, skilled nursing facilities, and specialty hospitals. NHI is executing a strategic pivot to its SHOP (Senior Housing Operating Portfolio) platform, which expanded 137% YoY to ~$850M representing 24% of total investment. In Q2 2026, NHI reported revenue of $121.3M and diluted EPS of $1.15 (+45.6% YoY), appointed its first COO (Chris Maingot), and completed $117.4M in property dispositions across seven properties.
