Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Nektar Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on immunotherapy, developing rezpegaldesleukin as its lead regulatory T-cell stimulator for atopic dermatitis (Phase 3 ZENITH-AD), alopecia areata, and Type 1 diabetes. The pipeline includes NKTR-0165 (ulcerative colitis, vitiligo, MS), NKTR-0166 (autoimmune), NKTR-422 (fibrosis), and NKTR-255 (solid tumors). Founded in 1990 and headquartered in San Francisco with 63 employees, the company reported H1 2026 revenue of $21.0M (primarily royalties) and a net loss of $85.5M, with $1.02B in cash and investments as of June 2026.

Investment Thesis
Nektar offers asymmetric biotech upside with rezpegaldesleukin targeting the $20B+ atopic dermatitis market dominated by Dupixent ($13B+ annual sales). The Phase 2b RADICAL trial delivered statistically significant results across primary and secondary endpoints, triggering a 287% stock surge and analyst price target increases to $153 [1][3]. The $1.02B cash position (quadrupled from $245.8M in December 2025) provides multi-year runway to fund the Phase 3 ZENITH-AD program launched July 2026 without near-term dilution risk. The $90M jury verdict against Eli Lilly for breaching the rezpegaldesleukin license confirms the IP's commercial value and provides additional capital [3].
The bear case centers on binary clinical risk and governance concerns. Nektar has a history of late-stage failures — bempegaldesleukin collapsed in 2022, and the REZOLVE-AA alopecia areata trial was compromised by four patients with major eligibility violations, triggering securities fraud litigation alleging management concealed the issues for ten months [3]. Drug safety concerns about rezpegaldesleukin dropout rates could signal tolerability problems that worsen in Phase 3. With forward EPS of -$10.84, H1 2026 net loss of $85.5M, and annual burn exceeding $250M, the $2.1B market cap is entirely dependent on Phase 3 success [1]. Short interest at 18.5% reflects significant skepticism, and insider selling (CEO Robin sold 20K shares in June 2026) rather than buying signals limited management conviction at current prices [4]. The stock has declined 45% from its 52-week high of $109 to $60.40.

  1. Phase 3 ZENITH-AD program (initiated July 2026) for rezpegaldesleukin in atopic dermatitis represents the primary value-creation catalyst — positive data could support regulatory filing against the $20B+ AD market [1]

Key Risks
  1. Binary Phase 3 risk — bempegaldesleukin failed late-stage previously, REZOLVE-AA was compromised by enrollment violations, and drug safety concerns about rezpegaldesleukin dropout rates could surface in larger ZENITH-AD trials [3]
  2. Active securities fraud class action and insider trading lawsuits create material litigation, financial, and reputational overhang regardless of clinical outcomes — the allegations of concealed trial violations for ten months undermine management credibility [3]
  3. Annual cash burn exceeding $250M with only $55M in declining royalty revenue means the $1.02B cash position provides approximately 3-4 years of runway — a Phase 3 failure would leave the company with no near-term revenue path and a depleting balance sheet [1][4]
