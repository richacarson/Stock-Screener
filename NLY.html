Investment Thesis
Annaly Capital Management at 7.56x forward earnings with a 12.84% dividend yield, 27 consecutive years of payments, and improving earnings trajectory offers deep income value as the largest mortgage REIT [4]. Q2 2026 delivered EAD of $0.79/share (beating estimates by 4.6%), book value per share of $20.15 (+1.7% QoQ), and 5.5% economic return — the ninth consecutive quarter of earnings exceeding the dividend [1]. The dividend raise to $0.75/quarter signals management confidence in sustained spread economics. Record $7.1B in residential credit loan purchases diversifies the portfolio beyond pure agency MBS, adding higher-yielding assets that improve return on equity. ROE of ~18.4% exceeds targets, and the Inspire Insight score of 54 (ALIGNED) with 9 positive attributions and zero negatives confirms clean ESG practices suitable for faith-based investors [3].
The bear case centers on structural leverage risk, dividend history, and interest rate sensitivity. D/E of ~733% — while structural for mortgage REITs using 7.3x GAAP leverage — means Annaly is a leveraged bet on interest rate spreads, and any yield curve flattening or spike in short-term funding costs could rapidly compress net interest income [4]. Average daily volume has declined from ~6.8M to ~4.1M shares, reducing liquidity from its prior levels. The dividend has been cut repeatedly over the past decade with a 5-year growth CAGR of -5.6%, and the 90% payout ratio leaves minimal cushion — while the current $0.75/quarter is covered by $0.79 EAD, that 105.3% coverage ratio provides a razor-thin margin for error [1]. The stock trades at $23.37, 5% below its 52-week high of $24.52, suggesting the market views the current recovery as mature. NLY's total return history — price appreciation plus dividends — has typically underperformed simpler fixed-income alternatives over full rate cycles. With $100B+ in mortgage assets funded primarily through overnight and short-term repo, any disruption to funding markets could create forced selling at distressed prices.
