Investment Thesis
Nokia at $10.60 is a turnaround with a credible AI tailwind: Q2 2026 net sales rose 9% to EUR 4.88B, gross margin expanded 70bp to 46%, operating margin rose 70bp to 9%, and AI and Cloud sales more than doubled (+105%) [1]. Network Infrastructure is guided +12-14% for 2026, a EUR 19.5B backlog (57% recognized within a year) gives visibility, and a balance sheet with D/E of 15.8% leaves room to invest [1].
The bear case is that profitability is thin and valuation already prices the turnaround: ROE is 3.5%, profit margin 3.5%, trailing EPS fell 98% and forward P/E is 21.9x [1]. The 5-year revenue trend is negative, mobile networks remains a drag, backlog slipped from EUR 20.0B to EUR 19.5B in 2025, and Nokia competes against larger AI-networking incumbents [1]. Strategy and org changes under a new CEO create execution risk, and the Inspire score of 0 gives no faith signal either way [2].
Key Catalysts
  1. AI and Cloud customer sales (+105% in Q2 2026) scaling as hyperscalers buy optical and IP networking, supported by Infinera integration [1]
  2. Network Infrastructure growth of 12-14% in 2026 with IP and Optical +18-20%, driving operating margin expansion beyond 9% [1]
  3. Conversion of the EUR 19.5B order backlog, with 57% scheduled for recognition within a year [1]
Key Risks
  1. Thin profitability: ROE 3.5% and 3.5% net margin leave little cushion if mobile networks demand or operator capex weakens [1]
  2. Execution risk from a new CEO, new AI division, Infinera integration and ongoing reorganizations, with talent competition in AI [2]
  3. Competition from Huawei, Ericsson, Ciena, Cisco and Nvidia-aligned vendors in AI networking, plus a 5-year revenue CAGR that is still negative [1][2]
