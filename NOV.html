Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas Equipment & Services ·NYSE ·United States
NOV Inc. designs, manufactures, and sells equipment and technology used in oil and gas drilling and production operations worldwide. The company operates through two segments: Energy Equipment (rig systems, marine and offshore equipment, wellbore technologies) and Energy Products and Services (completion tools, tubulars, fiberglass systems, and process and flow technologies). Expanding into low-carbon solutions including electrified drilling rigs, offshore wind installation vessels, and carbon capture technology.

Investment Thesis
NOV offers deep value characteristics with a forward P/E of 18.9 and conservative 37% D/E ratio, backed by a $4.08B capital equipment backlog providing revenue visibility [1][3]. Q2 2026 demonstrated margin expansion with adjusted EBITDA of $283M (13.3% of sales), a record level driven by operational excellence, favorable pricing, and cost reductions. EPS growth of 35% YoY signals that the earnings power of the business is recovering even as top-line revenue remains flat. The energy transition portfolio ($339M in low-carbon solutions revenue) positions NOV for secular demand growth in offshore wind and carbon capture [5]. Share buybacks ($63M in Q2) and a 2.14% dividend yield provide capital return while waiting for the oilfield cycle to turn.
The primary bear case is cyclical revenue stagnation and deteriorating order momentum. Q2 2026 revenue of $2.13B declined 2% YoY, and the capital equipment book-to-bill ratio has fallen to 74% — well below 1.0 for two consecutive quarters, signaling backlog depletion ahead [1][2]. The $4.08B backlog is down $220M from the year-ago level, with Q3 guidance of flat to 2% revenue growth offering little near-term upside. Profit margin of just 1.10% and ROE of 1.54% indicate the company struggles to generate adequate returns on its large asset base [3]. The 168% payout ratio is unsustainable if earnings don't recover, and the Altman Z-Score of 1.97 flags elevated financial stress. NOV remains deeply exposed to oil price cyclicality and the secular decline thesis for fossil fuel demand, with low-carbon solutions still representing a small fraction of total revenue.

AI Risk Protection (8/9): NOV manufactures physical drilling equipment — rig systems, drill pipe, subsea flexible pipe, valves, and completion tools — in operations involving extreme temperatures, pressures, and hostile offshore environments [1][2]. This deeply physical manufacturing and field service business is structurally immune to AI substitution. AI cannot replace the fabrication of a 30,000-foot drill string, the assembly of a semi-submersible rig, or the deployment of subsea equipment in 10,000 feet of water. Regulatory requirements (API standards, BSEE compliance) add additional moats. AI may actually increase demand for NOV's monitoring and predictive analytics services as operators seek to optimize equipment utilization and reduce downtime. AI Opportunity (0/1): NOV deploys ML-based predictive analytics internally through its industrial data platform, but this does not generate separately quantifiable AI revenue. The company uses AI to enhance existing services rather than selling AI products directly [4].
