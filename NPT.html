Texxon Holding at $2.57 with a $57M market cap is a micro-cap Chinese chemical trading and plastics manufacturing company that grew revenue 18.5% to $797M in FY2025, driven by an 88.5% surge in plastic particle sales to $272M as the new Henan polystyrene factory expanded production capacity [2][4]. The company completed key safety and regulatory milestones for the 600,000-ton polystyrene factory in January 2026, providing vertical integration from pure commodity trading into manufacturing with better potential margins. The stock trades at extremely low multiples relative to revenue, reflecting the deep discount applied to small Chinese companies listed on U.S. exchanges.
The bear case dominates: H1 FY2026 revenue collapsed 35.8% to $327M as basic chemical sales cratered 64.1% due to weak Chinese industrial demand, swinging to a $1.0M net loss from $2.3M profit [1]. Key Catalysts
  1. Henan polystyrene factory reaching full capacity — the 600,000-ton annual production facility commenced operations in June 2026 and could improve margins by capturing manufacturing value versus pure commodity trading [4]
