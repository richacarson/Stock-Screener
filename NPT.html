Company Profile
Industrials ·Specialty Chemicals ·NASDAQ ·China
Texxon Holding Limited is a supply chain management company based in Shanghai, China, operating through two segments: Supply Chain Trading and Plastic Manufacturing. The company distributes basic chemicals including alcohols, aromatic chemical raw materials, and plastic particles used in pipelines, packaging, and agricultural membranes. Texxon IPO'd on the Nasdaq Capital Market in October 2025 and commissioned a 600,000-ton annual capacity polystyrene manufacturing facility in Henan province in June 2026.
