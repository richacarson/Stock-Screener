Company Profile
Industrials ·Electrical Equipment & Parts ·NYSE ·United States
Energy Vault Holdings develops, deploys, and operates utility-scale energy storage solutions through three proprietary platforms: B-Vault (electrochemical battery for short-duration storage), G-Vault (gravity energy storage for long-duration), and H-Vault (hydrogen or hybrid storage). The company also offers the Vault-Bidder AI-powered software platform for dispatch and revenue optimization. Q2 2026 revenue doubled YoY to $17.4M, backlog surged 107% to approximately $2B, and full-year 2026 guidance was raised to $270-310M.
350 Employees ·www.energyvault.com

Investment Thesis
Energy Vault is positioned at the intersection of two mega-trends — the global renewable energy transition and the AI data center boom — with a unique multi-technology storage platform (battery, gravity, hydrogen, AI software) that can serve diverse customer needs [1][4]. The company's backlog surged 107% YoY to approximately $2B, with its largest single contract (1.25 GW, $500-600M) validating the platform approach. Full-year 2026 revenue guidance was raised to $270-310M with GAAP gross margins expanding to 20-25% [1][2]. Cash increased for six consecutive quarters to $148M, up 26% sequentially and over 150% YoY, demonstrating improving capital efficiency [2]. MW capacity under management doubled to over 1 GW, marking the strategic shift from project developer to energy infrastructure platform provider [1].
The bear case centers on persistent unprofitability — FY2025 net loss was $103.6M on $203.7M revenue, with net profit margin of -53% and an alarming debt-to-equity ratio of 563% [6]. The stock price of $3.75 (down from higher levels) reflects market skepticism about the path to profitability despite impressive backlog growth. Revenue recognition is lumpy and project-dependent — Q2 2026 revenue of $17.4M seems low relative to $270-310M full-year guidance, suggesting heavy back-half weighting and execution risk [1][2]. Competition from Tesla Energy, Fluence, and other well-capitalized storage providers could compress margins. The gravity storage technology (G-Vault) that originally differentiated Energy Vault has been largely supplanted by the battery platform, raising questions about whether the remaining differentiation (AI software, multi-technology flexibility) creates durable competitive advantage [6].
