Company Profile
Utilities ·Utilities - Renewable ·NYSE ·United States
Energy Vault Holdings develops and deploys utility-scale energy storage solutions including battery (B-Vault), gravity (G-Vault), and hybrid (H-Vault) systems, complemented by AI-powered energy management software (Vault-OS, Vault-Bidder). The company has pivoted from its original gravity-based storage concept to a build-own-operate infrastructure platform combining energy storage, power generation, and AI compute infrastructure. Founded in 2017, headquartered in Westlake Village, California.
142 Employees ·www.energyvault.com

Investment Thesis
Energy Vault delivered transformative FY2025 results with revenue surging 340%+ to $203.7M from $46.2M in FY2024, driven by a rapidly scaling build-own-operate infrastructure model now exceeding 1 GW of contracted capacity with $180M+ in expected annual recurring EBITDA [1][2]. The $1.35B contract backlog (up 300%+ YoY and 42% sequentially in Q1 2026) provides exceptional forward visibility, and 2026 guidance of $225-300M implies continued 30%+ growth. CEO Piconi's expansion into AI compute infrastructure positions Energy Vault at the intersection of two secular tailwinds — renewable energy storage and AI data center buildout. Multiple insiders purchased shares in Q1 2026, signaling management conviction at current prices [6].
The fundamental risk is persistent unprofitability with a $103.6M net loss in FY2025, ROE of -136%, and D/E of 323% reflecting minimal book equity against $94.6M in total debt [2][7]. The $58.3M cash position requires continued project financing, convertible notes, and potentially dilutive capital raises to fund the capital-intensive Own & Operate model. Gross margins of 23.6% are improving but remain thin. The stock's 376% one-year return prices in significant growth expectations — any delay in backlog conversion, project financing challenges, or margin compression could trigger sharp downside. Only 142 employees managing a $1.35B backlog and 1 GW+ portfolio creates material organizational scaling risk, and intense competition from Tesla Megapack, Fluence, and BYD with far greater resources threatens market share.

  1. Conversion of $1.35B contract backlog into recognized revenue, with 2026 guidance of $225-300M and the Own & Operate portfolio approaching scale for recurring EBITDA generation at $180M+ annual run rate
  2. AI compute infrastructure platform expansion capturing hyperscale data center demand for co-located energy storage and power solutions, with dedicated leadership appointed in May 2026
  3. Path to profitability through gross margin expansion from 23.6% toward 25%+ guidance and operating leverage as fixed costs spread across a growing revenue base
Key Risks
  1. Deeply unprofitable with -$103.6M net loss in FY2025, -136% ROE, and D/E of 323% — the company must continuously secure project financing and may require further dilutive equity raises to fund its capital-intensive model
  2. Backlog-to-revenue ratio of ~25 quarters signals strong demand but extreme execution risk — permitting delays, supply chain disruptions, or construction cost overruns on a $1.35B backlog managed by 142 employees could erode margins
  3. Intense competition from Tesla Megapack, Fluence, and BYD in the commoditized battery storage market, where Energy Vault lacks the scale, manufacturing capability, and balance sheet depth of established players
