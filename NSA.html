Company Profile
Real Estate ·REIT - Industrial ·NYSE ·United States
National Storage Affiliates Trust is a self-storage REIT headquartered in Greenwood Village, Colorado, that grew via its distinctive PRO (Participating Regional Operator) partnership model to become one of the largest self-storage owners and operators in the U.S. On March 16, 2026, NSA agreed to an all-stock acquisition by Public Storage valued at approximately $10.5B enterprise value (0.14 Public Storage shares per NSA share). Shareholders approved the transaction on July 14, 2026 (99.9% of votes cast), and closing is expected on or about July 22, 2026 — three days after this screen date — after which NSA will cease to trade as an independent company.

Investment Thesis
IMPORTANT CONTEXT: National Storage Affiliates is in the final days of being acquired by Public Storage in an all-stock transaction (0.14 PSA shares per NSA share, ~$10.5B enterprise value). Shareholders approved the deal on July 14, 2026 with 99.9% of votes cast in favor, and closing is scheduled for on or about July 22, 2026 — three days after this screen date [2]. NSA now trades at $45.02, essentially identical to the implied deal value (0.14 x Public Storage's $314.68 close = $44.06), confirming the market prices this as a near-certain close. A special prorated stub dividend of $0.0336/share was declared for the July 1-21 period immediately before consummation [6]. Independently, the underlying business shows early signs of stabilization — Q1 2026 revenue grew 1.5% YoY to $185.4M and Core FFO per share rose 5.6%, reversing the negative same-store trends of 2024-2025 — but this fundamental recovery is now irrelevant to NSA shareholders, who will receive Public Storage stock rather than continued NSA equity [1].
Because the deal is all but certain to close within days, NSA is not a fundamentally-driven investment opportunity at this point — it is a merger-arbitrage position with a near-zero remaining spread, and any 'upside' or 'downside' from here will track Public Storage's own share price rather than NSA's operating performance. New negative evidence should also weigh on how the deal is understood: Glassdoor reviews describe a low-trust culture with high manager turnover and a CEO reviewers say does not welcome feedback, which stands in contrast to the polished leadership-continuity narrative NSA has presented publicly. There is no case for initiating a new position in NSA at this stage; existing holders are simply waiting for the stock-for-stock conversion to complete.
