Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NGM ·United States
Intellia Therapeutics is a clinical-stage genome editing company focused on developing potentially curative therapeutics using CRISPR/Cas9 technology. Co-founded by Nobel laureate Jennifer Doudna, the company pioneers in vivo gene editing — delivering CRISPR directly into the body to permanently edit disease-associated genes with a single treatment course. Lead programs target hereditary angioedema (lonvo-z, BLA acceptance expected H2 2026) and hereditary transthyretin amyloidosis (nex-z, Phase 3 enrollment resuming). Q2 2026 collaboration revenue of $7.7M with cash of approximately $628M providing runway into 2028 [1].

Investment Thesis
Intellia at $11.65 with a $1.63B market cap represents a high-conviction bet on curative gene editing with near-term regulatory catalysts approaching inflection. The Phase 3 HAELO trial for lonvo-z demonstrated 87% reduction in HAE attacks versus placebo with no serious adverse events, and the BLA is under FDA review with acceptance expected H2 2026 and potential U.S. launch H1 2027 — positioning lonvo-z as the first in vivo CRISPR therapy to reach market [1]. The FDA fully released clinical holds on both MAGNITUDE trials, and nex-z enrollment is targeting completion H2 2026 for ATTR amyloidosis. Cash of approximately $628M provides runway into 2028, sufficient to fund the lonvo-z launch and advance nex-z through Phase 3 [1]. At $1.63B market cap and significant short interest, the stock prices in substantial skepticism, creating asymmetric upside if lonvo-z achieves approval and the in vivo CRISPR platform validates for multiple indications.
The bear case is substantial clinical, regulatory, and commercial risk concentrated in an unprofitable pre-commercial company. The patient death during the Magnitude trial raised genuine safety concerns about in vivo CRISPR gene editing that damaged investor confidence — while the clinical hold was released, any additional serious adverse events could permanently impair the platform's regulatory path [1]. Revenue of just $7.7M in Q2 2026 (down 46% YoY) reflects near-total dependence on lumpy collaboration revenue with no commercial products generating sales. Annual cash burn of approximately $250M against $628M in cash creates a ~2.5-year runway, but the workforce reduction from ~650 to ~377 employees signals financial pressure and triggered a shareholder investigation by Bragar Eagel & Squire related to the NTLA-3001 cessation [3]. The HAE market is already served by existing prophylactic therapies (Takhzyro, lanadelumab), meaning lonvo-z faces commercial adoption challenges even if approved. ROE of -55.4% and deeply negative operating margins reflect the financial reality of a company years from profitability.

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience MODERATE
7
AI Risk Protection (7/9): Gene therapy is a deeply physical, biological process protected by multiple structural moats against AI disruption. CRISPR-based therapeutics require proprietary lipid nanoparticle delivery systems, GMP manufacturing of guide RNAs, clinical trial expertise in rare diseases, and FDA regulatory approval processes that AI cannot replicate or substitute [2]. The fundamental biology of editing genes inside a patient's body involves physical manufacturing and biological interactions entirely outside the digital domain. However, AI is accelerating drug discovery and could enable competitors to develop rival gene editing approaches faster, potentially reducing the value of Intellia's early-mover advantage. AI-powered computational biology tools could identify better guide RNA sequences or delivery mechanisms. AI Opportunity (0/1): No separately quantifiable AI revenue. Intellia may use computational tools in R&D but does not sell AI products or services.
