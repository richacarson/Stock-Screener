Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Agricultural Inputs ·NYSE ·Canada
Nutrien, headquartered in Saskatoon, Canada, provides crop inputs and services through four segments: Retail (Nutrien Ag Solutions, which sells crop nutrients, crop protection, seed and proprietary products and offers agronomy and financing), Potash, Nitrogen and Phosphate. It was formed in 2018 by the merger of PotashCorp and Agrium. Revenue was $26.9B in 2025 with net income of $2.27B. In 2026 the company posted record potash volumes and raised potash sales guidance to 14.2-14.8 million tonnes. CEO Ken Seitz has led Nutrien since 2022 and is focused on cost reduction, capital discipline and share repurchases.

Investment Thesis
Nutrien is the low-cost leader in potash, a nutrient with no substitute, and it pairs that with the largest grower-facing ag retail network. First-half 2026 adjusted EBITDA rose 6% to $3.5B on record potash volumes and proprietary-product margins, and management raised 2026 potash volume guidance to 14.2-14.8Mt while calling potash fundamentals constructive and nitrogen tight [1]. 2025 net income more than tripled to $2.27B on $26.9B of revenue [4]. At $69.42 the stock trades at 13.7x forward earnings with a 3.17% yield, a 44% payout ratio and 49% D/E [6]. The dividend has risen every year since 2019 and buybacks are accelerating [1][7].
The bear case is commodity cyclicality and modest structural returns. Revenue swung from $20.9B (2020) to $37.9B (2022) and back to $26.0B (2024), and net income ranged from $459M to $7.66B over the same period [4]. ROE of 9.4% and an 8.7% profit margin show that through-cycle returns sit below IOWN's targets, and the 3.9% five-year dividend CAGR trails inflation-plus [6][7]. Trade policy is a live risk, including the December 2025 US tariff threats on Canadian fertilizer, as are geopolitical swings in Belarusian and Russian potash supply [5]. The stock sits about 19% below its $85.36 52-week high on softer fertilizer sentiment [6].
Key Catalysts
  1. Q3 2026 results (early November): record potash volumes against raised 14.2-14.8Mt guidance and a tight nitrogen market in the second half of 2026 could drive estimate revisions [1]
  2. Lower capital spending plus an accelerated share repurchase pace, which together with the $2.20 annual dividend return a growing share of free cash flow to shareholders [1]
  3. Growth in Retail proprietary products and digital agronomy toward Retail adjusted EBITDA guidance of $1.75-1.95B, which reduces dependence on commodity nutrient prices [1][3]
Key Risks
  1. Potash and nitrogen price volatility: net income has ranged from $459M to $7.66B over five years, and a renewed price slump would compress margins and slow dividend growth [4]
  2. US-Canada trade friction, including threatened tariffs on Canadian fertilizer (December 2025), given that the US is Nutrien's largest market [5]
  3. Operational safety and environmental exposure at underground potash mines and nitrogen plants, illustrated by the May 2024 fatality-related mine suspension [5]
