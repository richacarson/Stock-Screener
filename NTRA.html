Company Profile
Healthcare ·Diagnostics & Research ·NASDAQ ·United States
Natera is a diagnostics company specializing in cell-free DNA (cfDNA) testing across oncology, women's health, and organ health. Its flagship products include Signatera (personalized ctDNA test for cancer MRD assessment with >$1B annualized revenue and >90% MRD market share), Panorama (non-invasive prenatal testing), Horizon (carrier screening), Prospera (organ transplant rejection monitoring), and newer launches including Altera (comprehensive genomic profiling) and Empower (hereditary cancer screening). The company processed over 1 million tests in Q1 2026 and raised 2026 revenue guidance to $2.74-$2.82B.
6,500 Employees ·www.natera.com

Investment Thesis
Natera is the dominant player in cell-free DNA diagnostics with Q1 2026 revenue of $697M (up 39% YoY), Signatera achieving >$1B annualized revenue with >90% MRD market share, and raised 2026 guidance of $2.74-$2.82B [1]. Oncology test volumes surged 54.4% YoY to 258,900 in Q1 2026, and NCCN endorsement of ctDNA-MRD testing for bladder cancer expands the addressable market [3]. Gross margins of 64-66% and management's expectation of positive net cash inflow in 2026 suggest a profitability inflection is approaching. The conservative balance sheet (D/E 16%) and deep regulatory moats from CLIA certifications, clinical validation studies, and Medicare coverage create durable competitive advantages that AI advances enhance rather than threaten.
The bear case centers on persistent unprofitability and valuation risk. Q1 2026 net loss was $85.1M despite $697M revenue, with operating expenses of $538.6M driven by aggressive R&D and SGA spending [1]. The ~$38B market cap implies ~14x 2026 revenue guidance for a company that hasn't demonstrated sustained GAAP profitability. Significant insider selling ($17.18M by director Botha, $1.1M by CEO Chapman, $595K by insider Brophy) raises questions about leadership's conviction at current valuations [4]. Medicare reimbursement risk remains the single largest threat — any adverse CMS coverage decisions for Signatera could materially impair the oncology growth thesis. Competition from Guardant Health's Shield tests and Grail's Galleri continues to intensify in the liquid biopsy space.
Key Catalysts
  1. NCCN endorsement of ctDNA-MRD testing for bladder cancer and Japan regulatory approval for colorectal cancer expand Signatera's addressable market into new tumor types with institutional backing [3]
  2. Profitability inflection — management guides 64-66% gross margins and positive net cash inflow for 2026, with operating leverage on the $2.74-$2.82B revenue base potentially driving GAAP profitability in 2027 [1]
  3. Signatera's >$1B annualized revenue with >90% MRD share and 54.4% oncology volume growth creates a compounding data moat that widens competitive advantages with each test processed [1]
Key Risks
  1. Medicare reimbursement concentration — adverse CMS coverage decisions for Signatera in any cancer indication could materially impact revenue growth, with the oncology franchise dependent on continued coverage expansion [1]
  2. Valuation risk at ~14x 2026 revenue with no GAAP profitability — any deceleration in growth or margin expansion could trigger significant multiple compression from the ~$38B market cap [5]
  3. Significant insider selling across the C-suite in 2026 ($17.18M by director Botha, $1.1M by CEO Chapman) may signal leadership concerns about sustainability of current valuation levels [4]
