Company Profile

Netskope is a cloud security provider whose unified Netskope One platform delivers data protection, secure access, threat prevention, and networking across SaaS, web, hybrid IT, and AI workloads (SSE/SASE). Founded in 2012 by CEO Sanjay Beri and headquartered in Santa Clara, California, Netskope went public on Nasdaq in September 2025. ARR reached $899M (+27% YoY) as of July 31, 2026.

Investment Thesis
Netskope is a scaled, fast-growing cloud security platform riding the convergence of SSE/SASE and the need to secure enterprise AI adoption. Q2 FY2027 revenue grew 29% to $220.5M and ARR rose 27% to $899M, with the company beating guidance on every metric; FY2026 revenue was $709.0M (+32%) [1][2]. FY2027 revenue guidance of $870-876M implies continued ~23%+ growth, gross margin expanded to 68% in FY2026, and the balance sheet carries $697M of working capital with a 2.0x current ratio [1][3][6]. A strong culture (Glassdoor 4.3, 91% CEO approval) and founder leadership support execution, and the stock has more than tripled off its 52-week low of $7.67 [5][7].
The bear case is valuation and profitability. At roughly $10B market cap the stock trades at about 13x EV/sales with a forward P/E of 875, while TTM profit margin is -91.8%, ROE is -435%, and stock-based compensation equals 72% of revenue [6][7]. Free cash flow remains negative (Q2 FY2027: -$29.8M), and ARR growth is decelerating from 31% at FY2026 year-end to 29% in Q1 and 27% in Q2 FY2027 [1][2]. Larger platforms bundle SSE into broader security suites, and Netskope's beta of about 3.0 implies severe drawdown risk if growth slows [7].
Key Catalysts
  1. Sustained ARR growth from $899M toward $1B+, with demand from securing generative-AI adoption across regulated industries [1][4]
  2. Delivery on FY2027 revenue guidance of $870-876M and progress toward positive free cash flow [1]
  3. Declining stock-based compensation as a share of revenue after the IPO year, narrowing GAAP losses [3][6]
Key Risks
  1. Extreme valuation (forward P/E ~875, ~13x EV/sales) with ARR growth decelerating from 31% to 27% over three quarters [1][2][6]
  2. Deep GAAP losses (FY2026 net loss $679M, profit margin -91.8%) and stock comp at 72% of revenue diluting shareholders [3][6]
  3. Competitive pressure from larger security platforms bundling SSE/SASE, plus ongoing IP disputes with rivals such as Fortinet [8]
