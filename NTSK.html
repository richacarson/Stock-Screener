Netskope, Inc. is a global cybersecurity leader providing a cloud-native Secure Access Service Edge (SASE) platform for data protection, threat prevention, and zero trust network access. The company's Netskope One platform converges security and networking into a single solution, powered by its proprietary NewEdge backbone network spanning 75+ regions. FY2026 revenue was $709M (up 32% YoY), ARR reached $845M (up 29%), and GAAP gross margin expanded to 68%. Founded in 2012 by CEO Sanjay Beri.

Investment Thesis
Netskope is a category leader in SASE — the fastest-growing segment of cybersecurity — with a platform uniquely positioned to benefit from both cloud adoption and the AI revolution [4][7]. FY2026 revenue of $709M (up 32% YoY), ARR of $845M (up 29%), and gross retention rates at historical highs demonstrate strong product-market fit and customer stickiness [1]. The company's proprietary NewEdge backbone and AI-native security capabilities create meaningful competitive differentiation against Zscaler, Palo Alto Networks, and Cloudflare. With 18 analysts rating the stock 'Buy' and an average price target of $17.64 (15% upside from $15.32), the market sees near-term appreciation potential [6]. The structural tailwind of AI adoption — every organization deploying AI needs to secure the data and workloads — provides a multi-year growth runway.
The bear case centers on severe unprofitability: FY2026 net loss was $679.4M on $709M revenue, representing a -96% net profit margin that is 91.6% worse than FY2025 [1][6]. Debt-to-equity of 370.6% ($721M debt vs $194.5M equity) creates financing risk, and the company's ROE of -349.2% ranks among the worst in its peer group [6]. The recently-public stock faces lock-up expiration and potential insider selling pressure. Competition is intense — Zscaler has first-mover advantage in cloud security, Palo Alto Networks has the broadest security portfolio, and Cloudflare has infrastructure advantages — and any deceleration in the 30%+ revenue growth rate could severely compress the stock's valuation. Path to profitability remains uncertain, with operating margin at -54.8% and heavy ongoing investment in the NewEdge backbone and AI capabilities [6].
