Investment Thesis
Nu Holdings at 19.23x forward earnings with a 0.81 PEG ratio is a rare combination of high-growth and reasonable valuation — Q1 2026 delivered record $5.0B revenue (+41% YoY), $871M net income, and 4M net new customers bringing the total to 135M+ across three countries [1]. The financial profile is exceptional: 30% ROE, debt-free balance sheet with $15.9B cash, and Forward P/E below 20x for a company growing revenue 41% and earnings 35.5%. Mexico reaching operational break-even with 15M customers validates the cross-border replication model and unlocks a second major growth engine. Average revenue per active customer continues rising sequentially to $16, and the credit portfolio grew 40% YoY to $37.2B. The AI-driven banking strategy ('Three Act Strategy') positions Nubank to leverage its 135M-customer data moat for personalized financial products, while the Inspire Impact Score of 31 (ALIGNED) reflects strong ethical practices including financial inclusion [4].
The bear case centers on credit risk, geographic concentration, and competitive threats. Q1 2026 EPS of $0.18 missed the $0.20 consensus by 10%, driven by elevated credit provisions as the $37.2B loan portfolio grows in countries with volatile macroeconomic conditions [1]. Brazil represents ~85% of the customer base — any Brazilian recession, currency devaluation, or regulatory tightening (the Central Bank has been raising rates) could significantly impair credit quality. The 40% YoY credit portfolio growth in a higher-rate environment raises questions about underwriting discipline, particularly for Nubank's underserved customer base with limited credit history. Competition is intensifying from both traditional banks investing in digital and other fintechs (PicPay, Mercado Pago, Inter) that replicate Nubank's playbook. The profit margin of ~18% (using total revenue) is below the 20% infrastructure target, reflecting the capital-intensive nature of lending. The Three Act Strategy's AI vision is ambitious but unproven — no quantified AI revenue has been reported, and the mid-2026 financial planning tools have yet to demonstrate product-market fit.
Key Catalysts
  1. Mexico growth acceleration following Q1 2026 operational break-even with 15M customers — replicating Brazil's trajectory could add $3-5B in annual revenue at maturity, validating the cross-border digital banking model [1]
  2. Credit portfolio expansion to $37.2B (+40% YoY) with improving unit economics as average revenue per active customer grows sequentially, driving operating leverage on the zero-branch cost structure [1]

Key Risks
  1. Credit quality deterioration as the $37.2B loan portfolio grows 40% YoY amid Brazilian Central Bank rate hikes and volatile Latin American macroeconomic conditions — elevated provisions already caused Q1 2026 EPS to miss consensus by 10% [1]
  2. Geographic concentration with ~85% of customers in Brazil exposes Nubank to single-country regulatory risk, currency devaluation (BRL), and potential government intervention in digital banking regulation [1]
  3. Intensifying competition from traditional banks investing in digital transformation (Itau, Bradesco) and rival fintechs (Mercado Pago, PicPay, Inter) that replicate the zero-fee digital banking model, potentially compressing margins and increasing customer acquisition costs [2]
