Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Steel ·NYSE ·United States
Nucor Corporation is the largest steel producer in North America, operating through three segments: Steel Mills, Steel Products, and Raw Materials. The company pioneered electric arc furnace (EAF) steelmaking using recycled scrap and has expanded into data center infrastructure, Econiq low-carbon steel, solar torque tubes, and insulated metal panels. Founded in 1905 and headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina.

Investment Thesis
Nucor is North America's largest steelmaker in a powerful cyclical recovery — Q1 2026 revenue of $9.5B (up 21.3% YoY) and EPS of $3.23 (vs $0.67 a year ago) with Q2 guidance of $4.50-$4.60, projecting annualized EPS of ~$16-17 [1][5]. At 13.52x forward P/E and 0.59 PEG ratio, the stock is attractively valued relative to the earnings trajectory. D/E of 32% is best-in-class, and the 53 consecutive years of dividend growth represent the longest active streak in the steel industry [5]. Nucor's integrated data center infrastructure division directly serves the AI buildout, while Econiq low-carbon steel certification across 12 mills positions the company for the sustainability premium in construction and manufacturing. The legendary Iverson-Topalian culture of no layoffs, profit-sharing, and decentralized leadership creates a structural competitive advantage that is virtually impossible for competitors to replicate [2].
The bear case centers on steel cyclicality and valuation sensitivity. The trailing P/E of 24.54x remains elevated due to the depressed 2024-2025 earnings base, and any global economic slowdown or steel pricing weakness would compress the cyclical recovery that drives the attractive forward metrics [5]. Profit margin of 6.82% and ROE of 12.29% remain below targets despite the recovery — normalized steel margins of 5-7% at mid-cycle produce returns that may not justify the current 70%+ stock price appreciation over the past year. Trade policy uncertainty, including potential tariff changes and retaliatory measures, could disrupt North American steel supply/demand dynamics [1]. Beta of 1.92 means Nucor amplifies market downside significantly in risk-off environments. The 5-year revenue CAGR of 10.1% benefits from the 2020 COVID trough as a base year — excluding this anomaly, steel revenue growth has been volatile and mid-single-digits at best. The 0.90% dividend yield, while growing at 6.3% 5-year CAGR, provides minimal current income [5].
