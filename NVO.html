Company Profile
Healthcare ·Drug Manufacturers - General ·NYSE ·Denmark
Novo Nordisk A/S is a global pharmaceutical company focused on diabetes care, obesity treatment, and rare diseases. The world leader in insulin and GLP-1 receptor agonists, with blockbuster products Ozempic (diabetes) and Wegovy (obesity). Q2 2026 results beat estimates with revenue of $12.1B and adjusted sales up 7% at constant exchange rates, and management raised full-year 2026 guidance to 0% to -6% CER (from -12% to -4% previously) as oral Wegovy surpassed 5 million U.S. prescriptions since launch. Now led by permanent CEO Maziar Mike Doustdar following the 2025 leadership transition. Founded in 1923 and headquartered in Bagsvaerd, Denmark.

Investment Thesis
Novo Nordisk beat Q2 2026 estimates ($12.1B revenue vs. $10.94B expected, adjusted EPS of $0.95 vs. $0.78 expected) and raised full-year 2026 guidance to 0% to -6% CER, a meaningful improvement from the prior -12% to -4% range [1]. Oral Wegovy has surpassed 5 million U.S. prescriptions since its January 2026 launch, and International Operations obesity franchise revenue grew 37% in Q2, validating the injectable-to-oral transition at scale [1]. At 14.93x forward earnings with 59.82% ROE, 35.35% profit margin, and a 2.80% dividend yield backed by 26+ consecutive years of DKK dividend increases (International Dividend Aristocrat status), the stock remains attractively priced relative to its cash-generative franchise [3][4]. Leadership stability under permanent CEO Mike Doustdar, combined with a newly formalized OpenAI partnership aimed at compressing drug development timelines, strengthens the long-term innovation case.
The bear case still centers on competitive and pricing pressure. Eli Lilly's tirzepatide franchise continues to match semaglutide efficacy and take share, and Amgen, Pfizer, and Viking Therapeutics are advancing their own GLP-1 competitors [1]. US pricing pressure remains structural, with Wegovy injectable US sales down 22% at constant exchange rates in Q2 even as the oral pill offsets the decline [1]. The December 2024 CagriSema Phase 3 disappointment still shadows next-generation pipeline confidence, and TTM revenue growth of just 6.6% — well below the double-digit growth of prior years — confirms the deceleration story is real, not fully resolved. The Inspire Impact Score of -83 (MISALIGNED) may limit allocation for faith-based investors despite otherwise strong fundamentals.
Key Catalysts
  1. Oral Wegovy scaling beyond 5 million prescriptions toward broader payer coverage and international rollout, continuing to offset injectable Wegovy US softness [1]
  2. CagriSema Phase 3 cardiovascular outcomes data expected through 2026-2027, which could validate the combination therapy and restore confidence dented by the December 2024 efficacy miss
  3. OpenAI partnership-driven acceleration of the drug discovery pipeline, potentially compressing next-generation candidate timelines and reinforcing Novo's R&D cost advantage [2]
Key Risks
  1. Continued Wegovy injectable US erosion (-22% CER in Q2 2026) if oral pill uptake and international growth cannot fully offset the decline [1]
  2. Intensifying GLP-1 competition from Eli Lilly (tirzepatide), Amgen, and Viking Therapeutics could further compress semaglutide market share and pricing power
  3. TTM revenue growth of only 6.6% confirms the post-2024 deceleration remains a live risk, and any further guidance disappointment could reverse the recent multiple re-rating
