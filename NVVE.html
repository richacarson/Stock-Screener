Company Profile
Technology ·Electrical Equipment & Parts ·OTC ·United States
Nuvve Holding Corp. is a vehicle-to-grid (V2G) technology company that develops and deploys bidirectional charging solutions, enabling electric vehicles to store and discharge energy back to the grid through its proprietary GIVe software platform. The company operates V2G charging infrastructure for commercial fleets, school buses, and stationary batteries, managing 29.9 MW as of Q2 2026. Revenue surged 268% YoY to $1.23M in Q2 2026, but the company remains deeply unprofitable with a $7.0M net loss and negative gross margins of -14.5%. Nuvve was delisted from Nasdaq in July 2026 for failing to meet bid price, periodic reporting, and stockholders' equity requirements, and now trades on OTC Pink. Cash stood at just $0.5M against $19.4M in liabilities and a $7.5M stockholders' deficit. Market cap approximately $1.3M.

Investment Thesis
Nuvve is a pure-play vehicle-to-grid technology pioneer with a genuine competitive advantage in bidirectional charging and grid services. Q2 2026 revenue surged 268% YoY to $1.23M, net losses narrowed 46% to $7.0M, and backlog grew to $5.3M — the company's 'strongest pipeline in history' [1]. Megawatts under management grew 16.8% to 29.9 MW, demonstrating expanding commercial deployments. The integration of Astrea AI into the FleetBox platform adds differentiation in charging optimization [2]. As V2G technology moves from pilot to commercial adoption — driven by regulatory mandates (California's V2G building code requirements), utility demand response programs, and fleet electrification — Nuvve's early-mover advantage in the software stack could position it as an acquisition target for larger energy or EV infrastructure companies.
The bear case is existential survival risk, not mere underperformance. Nuvve has $0.5M in cash against $19.4M in liabilities and a $7.5M stockholders' deficit, creating immediate going-concern risk that could force liquidation or highly dilutive emergency financing [1][3]. Nasdaq delisted the stock in July 2026, triggering automatic termination of the $25M equity facility with Five Narrow Lane/Hailstone Peak — eliminating the company's primary capital access mechanism at the moment it needs funding most [3]. Gross margins remain negative at -14.5%, meaning every dollar of revenue costs more than a dollar to deliver, and the company has never achieved profitability at any level [1]. The 1-for-18 reverse split and move to OTC Pink markets severely limit institutional investor access and trading liquidity, with daily trading value of approximately $19,000. Even with 268% revenue growth, the base is $1.23M quarterly — irrelevant scale for a company burning $7M per quarter. The Inspire Impact Score of 58 (ALIGNED) is the sole positive screen for faith-based investors, but the SELL recommendation reflects that the stock is uninvestable due to near-zero liquidity, negative equity, and existential bankruptcy risk.
