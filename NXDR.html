Company Profile
Communication Services ·Internet Content & Information ·NASDAQ ·United States
Nextdoor (San Francisco; founded 2008 by Nirav Tolia) runs a verified-address neighborhood social network connecting residents with each other and with local businesses, monetized mainly through advertising [3]. Tolia returned as CEO, president and chair in 2024 after Sarah Friar stepped down [5]. Q2 2026 revenue was ~$75M (+15% YoY, above the $71-73M outlook), platform weekly active users hit a record 22.9M (+5%), self-serve revenue grew 32% to ~67% of revenue, net loss narrowed to $2M and Adjusted EBITDA was $10M (13% margin); full-year 2026 guidance was raised to low-teens revenue growth and ~10% Adjusted EBITDA margin [1]. Cash and marketable securities were ~$378M [1].

Investment Thesis
Nextdoor is inflecting: Q2 2026 revenue of ~$75M grew 15% and beat guidance, WAU hit a record 22.9M, self-serve revenue grew 32%, and Adjusted EBITDA swung to $10M (13% margin, +~$12M YoY), prompting a raised outlook of low-teens growth and ~10% EBITDA margin for 2026 [1]. With ~$378M of cash and securities against an ~$890M market cap and virtually no debt, the balance sheet gives the turnaround runway, and consensus models positive EPS by 2029 [1][4].
The bear case is that Nextdoor remains GAAP-unprofitable (TTM net margin -11.1%, ROE -7.3%), stock-based compensation is ~22% of revenue, and WAU growth of 5% is modest for a social network [4][1]. Digital advertising is exposed to AI-driven search and assistants, and the platform carries reputational risk from racial-profiling and moderation controversies [7]. On the IOWN framework, weak Inspiration (2/10) and a FINITE Infinite Game score (4/10) hold the overall at 44, a WATCH.
Key Catalysts
  1. Q3 2026 results against guidance of $76-78M revenue and $6.5-8M Adjusted EBITDA, with continued sequential WAU growth [1]
  2. Self-serve advertising scaling (+32% YoY, ~67% of revenue), lifting revenue per user and margins toward the ~10% full-year Adjusted EBITDA target [1]
  3. Path to GAAP profitability: Q2 2026 net loss narrowed to $2M from $15M; consensus shows positive EPS by 2029 [1][4]
Key Risks
  1. AI-driven search and assistants intercepting local-recommendation intent that drives Nextdoor's advertising value
  2. Recurring content-moderation and racial-profiling controversies that could hurt user trust and advertiser demand [7]
  3. Dilution: stock-based compensation at ~22% of revenue against a ~$2.30 share price [4]
