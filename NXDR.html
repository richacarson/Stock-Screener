Company Profile
Communication Services ·Internet Content & Information ·NYSE ·United States
Nextdoor Holdings, Inc. operates the essential neighborhood network connecting neighbors, businesses, and public agencies. The platform enables hyperlocal communication including neighborhood discussions, local business recommendations, safety alerts, and community events, serving 110 million verified neighbors across 350,000 neighborhoods. The company monetizes through AI-powered local business advertising, self-serve ad tools, and sponsored content. Q2 2026 was a landmark quarter with record revenue of $75M (+15% YoY), record adjusted EBITDA of $10M, and near GAAP breakeven with a net loss of only $2M. Formerly traded as KIND, the ticker changed to NXDR on NYSE in July 2025. Founded in 2011, headquartered in San Francisco, California.

Investment Thesis
Nextdoor's Q2 2026 was a landmark quarter validating the turnaround under co-founder CEO Tolia: revenue of $75M (+15% YoY) beat guidance, adjusted EBITDA reached a record $10M (16-point YoY margin improvement), GAAP net loss narrowed to just $2M (vs. $15M a year ago), and Platform WAU hit a record 22.9M [1]. The 110M+ verified neighbor network creates a structural moat in hyperlocal social networking with no competitor at scale, and the $378M cash position with zero debt provides ample runway [1]. AI-powered ad optimizations and the AI Ask feature are driving advertising efficiency improvements, and the company raised full-year guidance for both revenue and adjusted EBITDA margins. If Nextdoor achieves GAAP profitability while maintaining 15%+ revenue growth, the stock at $2.49 (market cap ~$1B) offers significant upside relative to the 110M-user platform value.
The bear case centers on chronic monetization failure and competitive disruption. Despite 110M+ verified neighbors, Nextdoor generates only ~$300M annualized revenue — approximately $2.73 per user, dramatically below social media peers (Meta averages $47+ per user). Five years after the 2021 SPAC IPO at $4.3B, the market cap has declined to approximately $1B, reflecting persistent inability to convert community engagement into advertiser spending at scale [4]. The workforce reduction from ~650 to 463 employees, while improving efficiency, suggests the company has limited organic growth potential. AI disruption risk remains real — Google AI, ChatGPT, and other tools are increasingly capable of providing hyperlocal information, potentially eroding the platform's differentiation for both users and advertisers. AI Risk Protection (5/9): Nextdoor's hyperlocal social network faces moderate AI disruption risk from multiple vectors.
