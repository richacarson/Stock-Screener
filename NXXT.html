Company Profile
Utilities ·Utilities - Renewable ·NASDAQ ·United States
NextNRG is an AI-driven energy company operating a mobile fuel delivery platform alongside wireless EV charging, solar, battery storage, and smart microgrid solutions. The company integrates artificial intelligence and machine learning with utility infrastructure through its AI-optimized Utility Operating System and Dashboard. Revenue is generated from mobile fuel delivery, solar power purchase agreements, SaaS energy management software, and hardware sales. Founded in 2016, headquartered in Miami, Florida. Market cap has fallen to ~$23M with the stock declining approximately 69% over the past 12 months.
