Company Profile
Utilities ·Utilities - Renewable ·NASDAQ ·United States
NextNRG is an AI-driven energy company operating a mobile fuel delivery platform alongside emerging wireless EV charging, solar, battery storage, and smart microgrid solutions. The company integrates artificial intelligence and machine learning with utility infrastructure to create a unified platform for modern energy management. Revenue is generated from mobile fuel delivery (on-demand and subscription), solar power purchase agreements, SaaS energy management software, and hardware sales. Founded in 2016, headquartered in Miami, Florida.
