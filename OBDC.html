Company Profile
Financial Services ·Asset Management - BDC ·NYSE ·United States
Blue Owl Capital Corporation is a publicly traded business development company (BDC) externally managed by Blue Owl Capital Advisors, providing direct lending primarily to U.S. middle-market companies with a portfolio of approximately $19B at fair value. Formed through the January 2025 merger of OBDC and OBDE, the combined entity is one of the largest publicly traded BDCs. Q2 2026 total investment income was $401.3M with GAAP NII of $0.36/share, NAV of $14.26/share, and non-accruals at 2.8% of portfolio cost [1].

Investment Thesis
OBDC at $10.35 with a $5.1B market cap trades at a 27% discount to NAV of $14.26/share, offering a 12.75% annualized dividend yield backed by Q2 2026 GAAP NII of $0.36/share that covers the $0.33 total quarterly distribution with $0.29/share of spillover income providing additional cushion [1][3]. The January 2025 OBDE merger created one of the largest publicly traded BDCs with a $19B+ portfolio at fair value, and the Blue Owl direct lending platform benefits from strong origination relationships, low average LTV of 30%, and an annualized net realized loss rate of just 8 basis points [2]. Total investment income of $401.3M in Q2 2026 beat the $394M estimate, demonstrating resilient portfolio performance despite the falling rate environment.
The bear case centers on secular NII headwinds and portfolio credit risk. Federal funds rate cuts are compressing floating-rate portfolio yields, driving the base dividend cut from approximately $0.35 to $0.31/share in Q1 2026 with further reductions possible if rates continue declining [3]. Non-accruals rose to 2.8% of portfolio cost in Q2 2026 from 2.0% in Q1, indicating deteriorating credit quality in a subset of borrowers [1]. The 18% software borrower exposure faces AI disruption risk that analysts and investors have flagged as a structural concern, with the stock trading below its IPO price [2][4]. The externally managed structure means shareholders bear management fees regardless of performance, and the persistent 27% discount to NAV suggests the market has structurally repriced the risk-reward profile of leveraged direct lending in a declining rate environment.
Key Catalysts
  1. Wide NAV discount of 27% ($10.35 vs $14.26 NAV) provides significant margin of safety if credit performance stabilizes and the BDC sector recovers toward historical averages of 0.95-1.05x NAV [1]
  2. Spillover income of $0.29/share provides 93% coverage of the base quarterly dividend independently, suggesting the $0.31/share distribution is sustainable even if NII declines further from rate cuts [1][3]
  3. Blue Owl's origination platform with low 30% average LTV and 8bps annualized net realized loss rate provides downside protection in a potential credit cycle, with the merged OBDC/OBDE entity offering scale advantages in deal sourcing [2]
Key Risks
  1. Continued Federal Reserve rate cuts will compress floating-rate portfolio yields — each 100bps cut reduces annualized NII by approximately $0.10-0.12/share, potentially forcing additional base dividend reductions below the current $0.31/share and driving further share price declines [3]
  2. Non-accruals rising from 2.0% to 2.8% of portfolio cost in a single quarter suggests a potential credit deterioration cycle, and the 18% software borrower concentration faces AI disruption risk that could impair credit quality across multiple names simultaneously [1][4]
  3. The externally managed BDC structure creates a permanent fee drag with management and incentive fees paid to Blue Owl Capital Advisors regardless of stock price performance, misaligning incentives between the adviser and public shareholders in a persistent NAV discount environment
