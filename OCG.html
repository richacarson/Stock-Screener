Company Profile
Consumer Cyclical ·Internet Retail ·NASDAQ ·Hong Kong
Oriental Culture Holding operates an online platform facilitating e-commerce trading of artwork, collectibles, and commodities primarily in China and Hong Kong. On July 27, 2026, the company sold 100% of its core subsidiary (China International Assets Equity of Artworks Exchange Limited) for HK$1M and announced a strategic pivot to platform support services for third-party trading platforms. The company previously executed a 1-for-220 reverse stock split and paid a special cash dividend of $0.15/share (9.80% yield). Active traders on the platform collapsed from 15,124 to 4,504 following a prior investigation that materially harmed customer confidence, though charges against key shareholders were dropped in May 2025. Market cap is $2.97M against $35.34M in cash.
13 Employees ·www.ocgroup.hk
