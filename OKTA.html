Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Infrastructure ·NASDAQ ·United States
Okta is the leading independent identity and access management platform, providing cloud-based solutions that secure every identity — from workforce employees to customers to AI agents. Co-founded by CEO Todd McKinnon in 2009, the company's product suite includes Workforce Identity Cloud, Customer Identity Cloud (via Auth0 acquisition), and Agentic Identity capabilities. Q2 FY2027 (July 2026) revenue was $805M (+11% YoY) with non-GAAP EPS of $1.05, beating estimates and sending the stock up 20%. FY2027 guidance raised to $3.22-3.23B revenue. RPO of $4.86B (+17% YoY). Market cap approximately $27B.

Investment Thesis
Okta at $166 with 11% revenue growth, $4.86B RPO growing 17% YoY, and Forrester Wave leadership positions it as the essential identity infrastructure layer for the agentic AI era [1][4]. Q2 FY2027 delivered a convincing beat — revenue $805M vs. $795M expected, non-GAAP EPS $1.05 vs. $0.97 expected — with AI agent security specifically cited as a growth driver as companies racing to deploy agentic AI turn to Okta for identity tools [1]. FY2027 guidance raised to $3.22-3.23B with EPS of $3.90-3.94, while GAAP operating margins expanded 770 bps to 13%, demonstrating operating leverage is materializing. The fortress balance sheet ($2.59B cash, D/E 6%) provides strategic flexibility. The independence thesis differentiates from Microsoft Entra in a multi-cloud world where enterprises want vendor-neutral identity layers.
The bear case centers on elevated valuation, competitive pressure, and cultural challenges. Forward P/E of approximately 42x at $166 prices in sustained growth acceleration, but revenue growth decelerated from 20%+ to 11% YoY, and FY2027 guidance implies only 11% full-year growth — any further deceleration could trigger meaningful multiple compression [1][4]. ROE of 8% and GAAP profit margin of 14% remain well below enterprise SaaS peers at scale, leaving the profitability story unfinished. Microsoft Entra's bundling with Azure and M365 creates persistent competitive pressure that has not abated. The October 2023 support system breach (134 customers affected) damaged brand trust for an identity security company, and Glassdoor reviews citing frequent RIFs and micromanagement suggest internal cultural problems that could impair innovation velocity [2][3]. The Inspire Impact Score of -49 (MISALIGNED) limits allocation for faith-based investors. PEG of approximately 2.0 provides moderate but not compelling valuation support for the current growth rate.
