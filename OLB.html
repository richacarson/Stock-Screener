The OLB Group, Inc. provides integrated financial and transaction processing services for small and mid-sized merchants in the United States. The company operates through Fintech Services and Bitcoin Mining segments, offering CrowdPay crowdfunding, OmniSoft cloud business management, eVance payment processing, and SecurePay payment gateway solutions for over 10,300 merchants in 130+ industries. Revenue declined to $8.68M in FY2025 (-32.4% YoY) with a net loss of $5.9M. Market cap approximately $4.4M.
