Olenox Industries Inc. (formerly Safe & Green Holdings, renamed January 2026) is a micro-cap industrial holding company focused on acquiring, operating, and scaling businesses across energy, infrastructure, and digital markets. Core operations include oil and gas field services in Texas; the legacy modular construction subsidiary SG Echo filed Chapter 11 in April 2026. The company has executed two reverse stock splits (1-for-64 in 2025, 1-for-10 in May 2026) and entered a non-binding LOI with CS Digital Ventures (Bitcoin mining). Going concern flagged by auditor.

Investment Thesis
Olenox Industries is a micro-cap turnaround story with TTM revenue recovering to $5.0M as O&G field services in Texas gain traction, up from the $3.4M trough [1]. The non-binding LOI with CS Digital Ventures (Bitcoin mining platform with $20.6M estimated 2025 revenue) could be transformative if completed and integrated, and the Machfu IoT acquisition provides recurring revenue potential from industrial sensor gateways [1][2]. CEO McLaren's 30+ years of energy industry experience provides domain expertise, and at ~$5.5M market cap, the stock is priced below liquidation value, creating asymmetric upside if the turnaround executes.
The bear case is overwhelming and represents the dominant probability. TTM net losses of -$17.0M dwarf $5.0M in revenue (-340% profit margin), the auditor flagged going concern with only $427K cash at year-end 2025, and the modular construction subsidiary SG Echo filed Chapter 11 in April 2026 triggering ~$4.0M debt default [3]. The stock has declined 98.68% over the past year despite two reverse stock splits (cumulative 1-for-640), and the company twice failed to achieve annual meeting quorum — a severe governance red flag indicating complete shareholder disengagement [3]. The CS Digital Ventures LOI is non-binding and speculative, the company has changed its name three times and pivoted across five unrelated business lines, and there is no analyst coverage, no forward estimates, and no institutional ownership of any significance. The stock fails the IOWN liquidity floor at ~$599K daily dollar volume, making it untradeable for any portfolio of meaningful size [4].
