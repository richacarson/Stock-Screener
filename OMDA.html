Company Profile
Healthcare ·Health Information Services ·NASDAQ ·United States
Omada Health is a virtual care platform for chronic condition management, offering programs for prediabetes, diabetes, hypertension, musculoskeletal conditions, weight management, and GLP-1 support. The company pairs human health coaches with AI-driven tools and behavioral science to help members build sustainable health habits. Omada serves employers, health plans, and health systems through a direct sales force and channel partners. FY2025 revenue reached $260M (+53% YoY) with 886,000 members. Founded in 2011 by Sean Duffy, IPO'd in 2024, headquartered in South San Francisco. Market cap approximately $1.25B.
946 Employees ·www.omadahealth.com

AI Risk Protection (5/9): Omada's core virtual care model — pairing human coaches with technology — faces real AI substitution risk from AI-first competitors that could offer cheaper, fully automated chronic disease management without human coaches [2].
