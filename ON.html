Company Profile

ON Semiconductor Corporation (onsemi) provides intelligent sensing and power solutions across automotive, industrial, AI data center, computing, and mobile end markets. The company specializes in silicon carbide (SiC) and gallium nitride (GaN) power semiconductors, CMOS image sensors, and analog/mixed-signal ICs. Q2 2026 revenue of $1.60B (+9% YoY) beat estimates, with AI data center revenue growing 30%+ sequentially and on track to more than double in 2026. Non-GAAP gross margin expanded to 39.3%, and FCF surged to $425.4M (4x YoY). Stock price $83.38.

Investment Thesis
Onsemi is executing a textbook cyclical recovery with accelerating AI exposure. Q2 2026 revenue of $1.60B (+9% YoY) beat estimates, non-GAAP EPS of $0.74 exceeded consensus by 3-4%, and FCF surged to $425.4M — roughly 4x year-ago levels [1]. AI data center revenue grew 30%+ sequentially and is on track to more than double in 2026, with Q3 guidance of $1.65-1.75B above consensus suggesting continued momentum [1]. Non-GAAP gross margin expanded to 39.3%, marking four consecutive quarters of improvement as manufacturing utilization recovers. The PEG ratio of 0.26 indicates expected earnings growth dramatically exceeds the valuation multiple — the most compelling growth-at-a-reasonable-price metric in the semiconductor space [4]. Leadership in SiC power semiconductors positions onsemi at the intersection of automotive electrification and AI infrastructure power management, two secular megatrends. The Inspire Insight score of 71 (ALIGNED) with 20 positive attributions and zero negatives, plus EcoVadis top 1% and MSCI 'A' ESG ratings, confirms strong values alignment [3].
The bear case centers on cyclical uncertainty, cultural risk, and valuation reset. The stock has fallen 31% from its June high of $121.62 to $83.38, resetting the valuation but also signaling market concern about the durability of the recovery [4]. Trailing P/E of 53.1x still reflects the cyclical earnings trough, and profit margin of 10.17% is recovering but remains well below historical peaks and the 20% target [4]. Glassdoor data reveals a concerning cultural environment — only 55% of employees recommend onsemi, culture rated 3.1/5, with reviews citing 'constant layoffs' and 'toxic work environment' — scoring Inspiration at just 3/10 and suggesting the strategic transformation has extracted significant human cost [5]. Chinese SiC competitors (BYD Semiconductor, StarPower) are expanding capacity rapidly and could compress pricing in the automotive SiC market [1]. Automotive concentration at 50%+ of revenue creates vulnerability to EV adoption rate moderation, while the 5-year revenue CAGR of approximately 2% reflects the depth of the cyclical hole onsemi is climbing out of. Rare earth element sourcing transparency has been flagged as an ESG gap [3].
