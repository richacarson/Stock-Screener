ON Semiconductor Corporation (onsemi) provides intelligent sensing and power solutions across automotive, industrial, AI data center, computing, and mobile end markets. The company specializes in silicon carbide (SiC) and gallium nitride (GaN) power semiconductors, CMOS image sensors, and analog/mixed-signal ICs. Q2 2026 revenue of $1.604B (+9% YoY) beat estimates, with AI data center revenue on track to more than double in 2026. Q3 guidance of $1.65-1.75B signals continued acceleration. Forward PEG of 0.18 reflects expected earnings recovery from the cyclical trough. Stock price $77.53, market cap approximately $30B.

Investment Thesis
Onsemi is executing a textbook cyclical recovery with accelerating AI exposure at a compelling valuation. Q2 2026 revenue of $1.604B (+9% YoY) beat estimates, with Q3 guidance of $1.65-1.75B above consensus signaling continued momentum [1]. AI data center SiC revenue is on track to more than double in 2026, growing nearly 60% YoY, with the CFO noting EPS grew faster than sales driven by gross margin expansion [1][3]. Forward PEG of 0.18 and forward P/E of 17.12 represent the most compelling growth-at-a-reasonable-price metrics in the semiconductor space, reflecting expected earnings recovery from $1.55 TTM to $4.53 forward EPS [4]. Leadership in SiC power semiconductors positions onsemi at the intersection of automotive electrification and AI infrastructure power management, two secular megatrends. The Inspire Insight score of 71 (ALIGNED) with 20 positive attributions and zero negatives, plus EcoVadis top 1% and MSCI 'A' ESG ratings, confirms strong values alignment [3].
The bear case centers on cyclical uncertainty and incomplete margin recovery. The stock at $77.53 has pulled back from its June high, resetting the valuation but also signaling market concern about the durability of the recovery [4]. Trailing P/E of 50x still reflects the cyclical earnings trough — current ROE of 8.3% and profit margin of 10.2% remain well below historical peaks and the 20% target, and the 5-year revenue CAGR of approximately 2.6% reflects the depth of the cyclical hole onsemi is climbing out of [4]. Chinese SiC competitors (BYD Semiconductor, StarPower) are expanding capacity rapidly and could compress pricing in the automotive SiC market [1]. Automotive concentration at ~50% of revenue creates vulnerability to EV adoption rate moderation, while the company's transformation under El-Khoury has not yet been stress-tested through a genuine industry downturn or leadership transition. Rare earth element sourcing transparency has been flagged as an ESG gap by external assessors [3].
