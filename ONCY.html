Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Oncolytics Biotech Inc. is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing pelareorep, an intravenously delivered oncolytic reovirus immunotherapy for the treatment of various solid tumor cancers. Lead indications include metastatic pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (Phase 3, FDA Fast Track designation), HR+/HER2- metastatic breast cancer (>60% response rate), colorectal cancer, and anal cancer. Founded in 1998, redomiciled from Canada to Nevada in 2026. New CEO Jared Kelly, architect of the $2B Ambrx-J&J acquisition, was appointed to accelerate partnering and commercialization strategy.

AI Resilience LOW RISK

AI Risk Protection (8/9): Oncolytics' core business — developing a biological immunotherapy (pelareorep oncolytic virus) for solid tumor cancers — is structurally immune to AI disruption. Drug development requires physical-world wet lab research, biological manufacturing of viral therapies, clinical trials with human patients, and FDA regulatory pathways that AI fundamentally cannot substitute [1]. The oncolytic virus mechanism of action requires biological production and clinical administration that exists entirely in the physical domain. AI cannot replace clinical trial enrollment, patient dosing, or regulatory approval processes. AI may modestly accelerate drug discovery timelines and improve clinical trial design (patient selection, biomarker analysis), but these are efficiency tools that benefit rather than threaten the business model. AI Opportunity (0/1): Oncolytics does not generate any revenue, let alone AI-specific revenue. While the broader pharmaceutical industry adopts AI for clinical trial optimization and drug discovery, Oncolytics itself has not reported quantifiable AI-related revenue or products.
