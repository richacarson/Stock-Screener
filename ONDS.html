Investment Thesis
Ondas at $7.24 (about $4.2B market cap) is among the fastest-growing defense-tech names: Q2 2026 revenue of $83.8M was up 13x YoY and 67% sequentially, 2026 guidance was raised to $525-550M, and backlog rose to ~$613M ($757M pro forma) from $68M at year-end 2025 [1]. Order capture of ~$175M in Q2 and $105M in early Q3 supports a book-to-bill above 2x, balance sheet leverage is minimal (D/E 2.6%) and AI-enabled counter-UAS and ISR demand is structural [1].
The bear case rests on quality of growth and governance: J Capital Research (February 2026) alleged acquisition-driven growth, heavy share issuance (~$829M raised in 2025) and overpaid deals, short interest exceeded 44% of float, and CEO Brock sold about $4.6M in December 2025 [3]. Forward earnings are negative (forward P/E -362), the 96% reported margin reflects non-operating items, and the H2 2026 ramp to $525-550M requires revenue to roughly double again from Q2 levels [1]. The stock sits below its 50-day average ($8.00) and the Inspire score of 42 (Aligned) carries no scoring weight.
Key Catalysts
  1. Execution on the raised 2026 revenue guide of $525-550M as the $613M backlog converts, with DZYNE and Cyberhawk adding $144M of pro forma backlog [1]
  2. Continued counter-UAS, ISR and autonomous-systems orders from defense and public-safety customers ($175M in Q2, $105M in early Q3) [1]
  3. Path to operating profitability as scale arrives, which would validate the acquisition platform and shrink dilution risk [1]
Key Risks
  1. Dilution and acquisition-integration risk: ~$829M raised in 2025 and serial deals flagged by J Capital Research as overpriced [3]
  2. Governance and sentiment: short interest above 44% of float, fails-to-deliver controversy and CEO selling of ~$4.6M [3]
  3. Back-half revenue ramp risk: guidance of $525-550M requires revenue to roughly double from the $83.8M Q2 run rate, and negative forward EPS leaves little valuation support [1]
