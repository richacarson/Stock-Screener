Company Profile
Healthcare ·Health Information Services ·NASDAQ ·United States
OneMedNet Corporation provides regulatory-grade, AI-ready Real-World Data (RWD) solutions for life sciences, medical device, and healthcare AI organizations through its iRWD platform. The federated platform leverages AI tools to search, de-identify, and curate clinical imaging datasets at scale, spanning over 1,400 healthcare systems, 121 million clinical exams, and 31 million unique patients. The company partners with Palantir Technologies for AI platform capabilities. In July 2026, OneMedNet secured an $11.5M contract to supply iRWD platform data, and YTD 2026 bookings exceeded $3M — surpassing total 2025 bookings. Revenue TTM is approximately $1.46M. Founded in 2017, headquartered in Minneapolis, Minnesota.
25 Employees ·www.onemednet.com
