Company Profile
Healthcare ·Health Information Services ·NASDAQ ·United States
OneMedNet Corporation provides regulatory-grade, AI-ready Real-World Data (RWD) solutions for life sciences, medical device, and healthcare AI organizations through its iRWD platform. The federated platform leverages AI tools to search, de-identify, and curate clinical imaging datasets at scale, spanning over 1,400 healthcare systems, 121 million clinical exams, and 31 million unique patients. The company partners with Palantir Technologies for AI platform capabilities. Founded in 2017, headquartered in Minneapolis, Minnesota.

Investment Thesis
OneMedNet occupies a strategic position in the AI healthcare data value chain, providing the regulatory-grade medical imaging data that AI models and life sciences researchers increasingly demand. The iRWD platform's federated network of 1,400+ healthcare systems, 121 million clinical exams, and 31 million unique patients creates a data asset that is difficult to replicate due to HIPAA compliance requirements and the physical-world origin of clinical imaging [3]. The Palantir AIP partnership provides world-class AI infrastructure, and the $3M in new bookings secured in June 2026 alone — surpassing all of 2025 — suggests inflection-point commercial traction. The 329% RWD revenue growth in 2025 and MedCase expansion into the $1.5B data annotation market provide multiple growth vectors. CEO Aaron Green's healthcare IT background (Change Healthcare/Optum Insight) brings enterprise sales credibility.
The bear case is overwhelming at current scale. OneMedNet generates only $643K-$1.32M in TTM revenue with losses of $3.35M-$10.1M, producing deeply negative margins on a $39.68M market cap [4]. At $0.62/share, the stock is firmly in penny stock territory with daily dollar volume of only ~$279K, making it untradeable for institutional investors and triggering the IOWN liquidity floor that zeros out the infrastructure score. The company has been public since 2023 without achieving meaningful revenue scale, and the gap between the impressive network size (1,400+ health systems, 31M patients) and negligible commercial revenue ($1.32M) raises fundamental questions about whether the data can be monetized at scale. Dilution risk is high — with 54.43M shares outstanding and ongoing losses, additional capital raises are likely and would further impair per-share economics. Competition from IQVIA, Flatiron Health, Tempus, and health system-built data platforms could marginalize OneMedNet before it achieves escape velocity.
Key Catalysts
  1. Conversion of the $3M June 2026 bookings surge into recognized revenue, validating that the iRWD platform has crossed the product-market fit threshold and can sustain commercial momentum
  2. Palantir AIP-powered multimodal subscription search launch, which could transform OneMedNet from a project-based data provider into a recurring-revenue SaaS platform with higher margins
  3. Strategic partnership or acquisition by a larger health data company (IQVIA, Tempus, Veracyte) seeking regulatory-grade medical imaging RWD capabilities — OneMedNet's network is more valuable as a component of a larger platform
