Company Profile
Healthcare ·Medical - Healthcare Information Services ·NASDAQ ·United States
OneMedNet curates de-identified clinical imaging real-world data (iRWD) from a network of hospital and academic research providers and sells it to life sciences companies and AI developers for training and validating medical imaging models. Based in Eden Prairie, Minnesota, the micro-cap ($25M market cap) generated FY2025 revenue of $1.36M (+111%) on a $2.8M net loss, and H1 2026 revenue of $388K (+33%) with a widened $4.6M net loss. The balance sheet showed stockholders' deficit of $3.5M and $358K of cash at June 30, 2026.

Investment Thesis
OneMedNet sits on a genuine secular tailwind: medical imaging AI developers need large volumes of curated, de-identified real-world imaging data, and the company's provider network and AI-assisted curation (claimed 5x efficiency gains) are built to supply it [3][4]. Revenue more than doubled in FY2025 to $1.36M and H1 2026 revenue rose 33% to $388K, with Q2 2026 revenue of $292K up ~88% YoY as data delivery revenue grew [1][2]. At a ~$25M market cap, a few large data contracts could move results materially.
The bear case is existential. Stockholders' equity was -$3.5M and cash only $358K against $4.7M of current liabilities at June 30, 2026, and H1 2026 net loss widened to $4.6M versus net income of $1.1M a year earlier [1][2], implying continued dilutive financing (multiple 2026 Form 424B3 filings) [2]. Revenue is tiny and lumpy (Q1 2026 fell ~30% YoY), gross profit has been negative in every year since 2021 [1], and average daily dollar volume of ~$223K is far below IOWN's $1M liquidity floor [5].
Key Catalysts
  1. Large data-delivery contracts with pharma or imaging-AI developers under the unified RWD platform, building on the ~88% YoY Q2 2026 revenue increase [2]
  2. Expansion of the provider network (management cites four new strategic partners nearly doubling the clinical data network) increasing data supply for AI training deals [4]
  3. Q3 2026 results (expected around Nov 13, 2026) showing whether data-delivery growth can offset lost BEAM subscription revenue [2]
Key Risks
  1. Going-concern-level liquidity: $358K cash and a $3.5M stockholders' deficit at June 30, 2026 make further heavy dilution highly likely [1]
  2. Persistent negative gross margins (FY2025 gross profit -$0.5M on $1.36M revenue) suggest the data-curation model does not yet cover its direct costs [1]
  3. Disintermediation by larger RWD aggregators, synthetic data, or health systems licensing imaging data directly to AI developers, plus extreme illiquidity (~$223K average daily dollar volume) [5]
