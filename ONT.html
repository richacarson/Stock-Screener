Onterris, Inc. (formerly Montrose Environmental Group, rebranded April 2026, ticker changed to ONT from MEG in May 2026) operates as a global environmental solutions company providing assessment, permitting, emergency response, environmental testing, laboratory services, and remediation solutions. The company serves oil and gas, utilities, government entities, industrial manufacturing, and renewable energy industries across the United States, Australia, Canada, and internationally. Q2 2026 revenue was $186.7M with EBITDA margin expanding to 17.1%. Management lowered FY2026 guidance to $740-790M revenue and $117-120M Adj EBITDA, citing weaker pass-through, emergency response revenue, and regulatory waivers delaying air-testing work.

Investment Thesis
Onterris at $20.95 is an environmental services platform positioned to benefit from secular regulatory tailwinds including PFAS remediation mandates, methane emission controls, and tightening environmental standards. The Q2 2026 adjusted EBITDA margin expanded to 17.1% (from 16.9% YoY), demonstrating ongoing cost optimization even as revenue declined due to non-recurring prior-year emergency response revenue [2]. Insider buying is exceptionally strong — Director Peter Graham invested $519K in 30,000 shares and CFO Allan Dicks purchased $100K in 6,535 shares at lower prices, with zero insider sales in three months [5]. The SBTi-validated net-zero goals and patent-pending VeriPlume Capture System for methane emissions position the company at the intersection of environmental regulation and clean technology [3][4]. The bear case centers on the guidance cut, thin profitability, and acquisition integration challenges. Trailing PE of 160x and forward PE of 72.96 reflect near-zero GAAP earnings — the company has not demonstrated it can convert $800M+ in revenue into meaningful bottom-line profitability, and forward EPS estimates collapsed from $1.74 to ~$0.29 after the guidance cut [5]. D/E of 88% reflects acquisition-related leverage with $388.5M in debt and only $11M in cash, constraining financial flexibility. The environmental services market is highly fragmented, and Onterris's acquisition-driven growth model is increasingly difficult to sustain as integration costs persist and organic growth remains elusive — Q2 2026 revenue declined $47.9M YoY when a single prior-year emergency event is removed from comparisons [2].
