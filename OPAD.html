Company Profile
Real Estate ·Real Estate Services ·NYSE ·United States
Offerpad Solutions operates a technology-enabled real estate platform offering cash home purchases (iBuying), renovation services, brokerage services, and ancillary products including mortgage, title insurance, and escrow. The company has pivoted from pure iBuying toward a multi-solution platform with AI-driven pricing (Scout, HENRY), Direct+ partnerships, and its HomePro agent program. CEO Brian Bair declared the 'rebuilding phase is largely behind us' after Q2 2026 showed gross margin of 9.2% — the highest since Q3 2023 — though revenue of $77.7M was down 52% YoY with 295 transactions [1]. Headquartered in Tempe, Arizona.
140 Employees ·www.offerpad.com

Investment Thesis
Offerpad is a deeply distressed iBuyer attempting a pivot to an AI-driven multi-solution real estate platform. Q2 2026 showed sequential improvement with gross margin rising to 9.2% — the highest since Q3 2023 — and CEO Bair declaring the 'rebuilding phase is largely behind us' with the 'buying engine back on' [1]. The HENRY AI platform for property inspection and Scout for pricing optimization are live and attributed to conversion improvements [2]. Management targets 1,000 quarterly transactions and positive adjusted EBITDA by year-end 2026. At a $22M market cap, the stock offers significant upside if the pivot succeeds and the housing market normalizes.
The bear case is severe and dominant. Revenue collapsed from $3.95B (2022) to $77.7M in Q2 2026 (down 52% YoY), and the company achieved only 295 transactions against the 1,000 quarterly target needed for EBITDA breakeven [1]. The 1:20 reverse stock split addressed NYSE delisting risk but crushed trading volume — the stock now fails IOWN's $1M daily liquidity floor with approximately $422K daily dollar volume [4]. D/E of 206%, ROE of -103.57%, and continued net losses suggest potential need for additional capital raises at highly dilutive terms. Gross margin of 9.2% is razor-thin for home sales, and competition from Opendoor's larger scale, traditional brokerages adopting AI technology, and Zillow's data advantages threatens Offerpad's survival as a going concern. With 140 employees and a $22M market cap, the company lacks the resources to compete in a capital-intensive industry [4].

AI Risk Protection (4/9): Offerpad's core iBuying business relies on algorithmic pricing — a function where AI is both an enabler and competitive threat.
