Company Profile

Ocean Power Technologies is a maritime domain awareness company providing DaaS (Data as a Service), RaaS (Robotics as a Service), and PaaS (Power as a Service) solutions for defense, security, offshore energy, and marine research markets. Products include PowerBuoy (wave-powered sensor and power platform), WAM-V (autonomous unmanned surface vehicle), and Merrows (command and control system). FY2026 (ending April 2026) revenue was $3.74M (-36% YoY) with a net loss of $48.9M. Record backlog of $20.1M including the largest contract in company history — a $6.5M multi-PowerBuoy maritime domain awareness program for the US Coast Guard. Founded in 1984. The company has initiated a review of strategic alternatives. Market cap ~$9.2M.

Investment Thesis
OPT offers a highly speculative play on the growing maritime domain awareness market, with the $6.5M US Coast Guard contract representing a potential inflection point for the company's defense business. Record backlog of $20.1M — more than 5x FY2026 revenue of $3.74M — provides the strongest forward revenue visibility in the company's 42-year history [1]. The PowerBuoy and WAM-V platforms serve genuine needs in coastal defense, offshore energy monitoring, and ocean research that are growing as nations invest in maritime security and offshore wind infrastructure. The Mythos AI partnership for autonomous vessel capabilities positions OPT at the intersection of maritime hardware and AI-driven autonomy [2]. At $9.2M market cap, the stock trades below the value of its backlog, creating potential asset-value upside if contracts convert to revenue. Inspire Impact Score of 54 (ALIGNED) with zero negative attributions and strong environmental sustainability profile supports faith-based consideration for highly risk-tolerant investors [3].

The stock is profoundly illiquid at $292K daily dollar volume, well below the $1M liquidity floor, making position sizing impossible and exit risk extreme. The company is actively reviewing strategic alternatives, which typically signals potential sale, merger, restructuring, or dissolution [2]. Forward EPS of -$2.23 implies continued losses with no path to profitability visible in analyst estimates. ROE of -319% and market cap of $9.2M reflect a company that has destroyed far more capital than it has created over four decades [4]. The $20.1M backlog, while a record, depends on successful contract execution by a 65-person company — any delivery delays, technology failures, or customer cancellations could eliminate the only bull case catalyst. The stock price of $1.02 risks NASDAQ minimum bid price delisting requirements, adding regulatory overhang to the existential financial risk.
