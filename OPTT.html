Company Profile

Ocean Power Technologies is a maritime domain awareness company providing autonomous offshore solutions through its AI-capable Merrows PowerBuoy wave energy converters, WAM-V autonomous surface vessels, and subsea monitoring platforms. The company serves defense and security, offshore energy, and maritime surveillance markets. FY2026 marked a transformative year with record $19.8M backlog, the largest deployment contract in company history ($6.5M U.S. Coast Guard program), and acquisition of strategic subsea technology. Founded 1984, headquartered in Monroe Township, New Jersey.

Investment Thesis
Ocean Power Technologies reached a commercial inflection in FY2026, with record $19.8M backlog (up 58% YoY), the company's largest-ever deployment contract ($6.5M U.S. Coast Guard program), and acquisition of strategic subsea technology expanding operational capabilities [1][3]. The AI-capable Merrows PowerBuoy is now shipping to defense customers in the Middle East and U.S., validating the pivot from wave energy to autonomous maritime domain awareness [2]. A $142.3M sales pipeline and revenue forecast to grow 43% annually over the next three years suggest accelerating commercial traction. The balance sheet is conservatively managed with D/E of 43%, and the faith alignment score of 54 (ALIGNED) with zero negative attributions makes OPTT a clean screen for values-aligned investors.
The fundamental challenge remains the massive gap between backlog/pipeline promises and commercial reality — FY2026 revenue was only $4.1M (down 30.5% YoY) against a $43.7M net loss, yielding a catastrophic -1065% profit margin [1][5]. After 42 years of operation, OPTT has never achieved profitability, and the $50M market cap at $0.20 per share reflects perennial micro-cap risk. The stock barely clears the $1M daily dollar volume liquidity floor ($1.16M), making institutional investment impractical. Dilution risk is ongoing — the company must raise capital to fund operations, threatening existing shareholders at the current penny-stock price. The $19.8M backlog-to-$4.1M-revenue ratio of 4.8 years is extremely high, potentially signaling capacity constraints or execution risk in converting backlog to recognized revenue at scale.
