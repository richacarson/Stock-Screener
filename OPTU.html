Investment Thesis
Optimum Communications is a deeply distressed broadband incumbent whose financial situation has deteriorated further since the Altice USA era. Q1 2026 revenue of $2.07B declined 4.0% YoY with a catastrophic -$6.11 EPS loss and -54.55% net profit margin [1]. The company carries approximately $26B in debt against negative stockholders' equity of -$5.2B, and is actively litigating against its own creditors in an unprecedented antitrust lawsuit [3]. The stock trades at $1.23 — down from $30+ in 2021 — reflecting market pricing of severe restructuring risk. Lightpath fiber B2B and Optimum Mobile provide pockets of growth, but they are too small to offset the structural decline in legacy cable subscribers facing fiber overbuilders and fixed wireless competition [1].
The bull case requires a successful debt restructuring that preserves equity value, stabilization of broadband subscriber trends through the convergence-led pricing strategy, and Lightpath/mobile growth accelerating to offset cable decline. AI-driven data consumption growth is a secular tailwind for broadband pipes. However, Patrick Drahi's controversial asset transfers — moving assets beyond creditors' reach — have escalated the creditor conflict to active litigation, raising restructuring risk that could wipe out the equity entirely [3]. FY2026 consensus projects revenue declining to $8.16B with losses deepening to -$6.53 EPS. Glassdoor sentiment (44% recommend, 2.9/5 culture) and multiple recent leadership appointments signal ongoing organizational instability. Against fiber overbuilders with superior unit economics and fixed wireless competitors with zero last-mile capex, OPTU's structural disadvantages are deepening. This is a highly leveraged equity stub on a telecom business in secular decline — SELL.
Key Catalysts
  1. Successful debt restructuring or refinancing that reduces the $26B burden and removes near-term balance-sheet risk without wiping out equity holders
  2. Lightpath fiber B2B revenue acceleration and Optimum Mobile subscriber growth offsetting legacy cable broadband subscriber losses
  3. Convergence-led pricing and packaging stabilizing broadband net adds and ARPU, reversing the multi-year revenue decline trajectory
Key Risks
  1. Equity wipeout risk from debt restructuring — with negative $5.2B stockholders' equity and $26B debt, a bankruptcy filing or adverse creditor settlement could eliminate all equity value
  2. Escalating creditor conflict — controversial asset transfers and antitrust lawsuit against Apollo, Ares, BlackRock, and other major creditors have created unprecedented legal complexity that could delay any resolution and increase costs
  3. Structural competitive decline — fiber overbuilders (Verizon Fios, AT&T Fiber) and fixed wireless entrants (T-Mobile, Starlink) continue eroding broadband share in OPTU's 21-state footprint while the company lacks capital to accelerate its own fiber buildout
