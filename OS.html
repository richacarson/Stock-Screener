Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NASDAQ ·United States
OneStream, Inc. provides a cloud-native Digital Finance Cloud platform that unifies core financial and operational processes — financial close, consolidation, planning, forecasting, and reporting — with embedded AI and machine learning for the Office of the CFO. The company serves over 1,800 customers including 18% of the Fortune 500 across approximately 1,600 employees. OneStream IPO'd on NASDAQ in July 2024 but was taken private by Hg in an all-cash acquisition at $24.00/share ($6.4B equity value) that closed on April 1, 2026. The stock is no longer publicly traded.
