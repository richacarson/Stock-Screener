Investment Thesis
Oscar Health has executed a remarkable profitability inflection in 2026 — H1 generated $1.1B in earnings from operations and $1B in net income, prompting management to raise full-year EFO guidance by $250M to $500-700M [1]. Q2 2026 revenue of $4.9B (+70% YoY) and membership of 2.96M (+46% YoY) demonstrate both top-line momentum and pricing discipline, with MLR improving 12 percentage points YoY to 79.2% in Q2 [1]. The Oswell AI agent and 21+ AI use cases are driving measurable operational efficiency gains that translate directly to margin improvement [2]. At ~15x trailing earnings and an estimated PEG of 0.5, the stock is reasonably valued for a company growing revenue 60%+ annually with demonstrated profitability. The digital-first platform creates a structural cost advantage over legacy carriers, and expansion into small group, Medicare Advantage, and +Oscar licensing extends the growth runway. Inspire ALIGNED score (31) is favorable for faith-based investors.
The primary risk is earnings sustainability. The H1 2026 results benefited from favorable prior period reserve development (one-time tailwind), and the full-year MLR guidance of 81.5-82.5% implies significant margin compression in H2 2026 relative to Q2's 79.2% [1]. The EFO guidance of $500-700M for the full year, despite H1 already delivering $1.1B, suggests management expects H2 earnings to be materially lower — or is being extremely conservative. At $32 per share (up 15% from the prior screen), the stock has re-rated to price in some profitability improvement. Oscar's heavy concentration in ACA marketplace plans (2.96M members) exposes the company to policy risk — changes in ACA subsidies or exchange regulations could materially impact membership dynamics [1]. Glassdoor reviews reveal cultural challenges including concerns about leadership qualification and strategic direction changes, which could impair execution as the company scales to $19B in revenue [2]. Competition from UnitedHealth, Elevance, and CVS/Aetna intensifies as incumbents accelerate digital transformation. The 7.4% profit margin, while a breakthrough, remains thin relative to mature insurers.
