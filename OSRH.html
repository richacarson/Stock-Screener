OSR Health, Inc. (formerly OSR Holdings, Inc., renamed June 2026) is a global healthcare holding company operating across immuno-oncology, regenerative biologics, and medical device technologies. Key assets include Vaximm AG (oral T-cell immunotherapy platform with VXM01 targeting glioblastoma, recently out-licensed with up to $815M in potential milestones), Woori IO (noninvasive glucose monitoring platform with Samsung collaboration), and South Korean subsidiary operations in regenerative medicine. Went public via SPAC business combination in February 2025.
