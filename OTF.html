Company Profile
Financials ·Business Development Company ·NYSE ·United States
Blue Owl Technology Finance Corp. is a specialty finance company focused on originating and managing a diversified portfolio of debt and equity investments in U.S. technology-related companies, with a strategic focus on software. Externally managed by Blue Owl Technology Credit Advisors, LLC, an affiliate of Blue Owl Capital Inc. (NYSE: OWL). OTF is the largest tech-focused business development company (BDC) with a portfolio of $14.1 billion across 203 companies as of March 2026. Listed on NYSE in May 2025 after operating as a non-traded BDC since 2018.

Investment Thesis
Blue Owl Technology Finance offers a compelling yield of 15.4% (including special dividends) supported by a $14.1 billion portfolio of 203 technology-focused companies with minimal credit losses — non-accruals represent just 0.1% of fair value [1]. The Q1 2026 earnings of $0.37 GAAP NII per share and $0.50 per share of spillover income provide a cushion for the $0.40 quarterly distribution. Blue Owl Capital's platform provides institutional-quality deal origination, with $1.7 billion in new commitments during Q1 2026 [1]. The tech-focused strategy positions OTF to benefit from secular demand for private credit among software and technology companies, while the 0.85x debt-to-equity leverage ratio provides room for portfolio growth.
The primary concern is the significant 37% discount to NAV — OTF trades at $10.39 versus $16.49 book value — which signals market skepticism about the portfolio's mark-to-market valuations or the sustainability of current distribution levels [1]. The payout ratio exceeded current-quarter NII at 108%, requiring drawdown of accumulated spillover income to fund distributions. Q1 2026 revenue of $325.9 million missed consensus estimates of $340.1 million, contributing to the stock's underperformance [1]. External management creates structural conflicts — management fees are based on assets under management, incentivizing growth over risk discipline. As a newly public BDC (NYSE-listed since May 2025), OTF lacks the multi-cycle track record that income investors typically require. Rising interest rates support spread income, but a credit deterioration cycle in the technology sector could rapidly erode portfolio values and force distribution cuts.
Key Catalysts
  1. Portfolio yield stabilization and potential distribution increases as spillover income builds, supported by $1.7B quarterly new investment commitments and expanding tech credit demand
  2. Narrowing of the 37% NAV discount as the company builds a public market track record and the $200M share repurchase program absorbs shares below book value
  3. Continued credit quality demonstrated by non-accruals at 0.1% of fair value, which should build investor confidence in the durability of the 15%+ distribution yield
Key Risks
  1. Payout ratio of 108% exceeds current-quarter NII, relying on accumulated spillover income — a credit deterioration cycle or rising non-accruals could force distribution cuts, which would compress the stock price further below NAV
  2. External management fee structure creates misaligned incentives — management fees based on AUM incentivize portfolio growth over credit discipline, and the 37% NAV discount suggests the market has concerns about portfolio valuations
  3. Newly public BDC with limited public market track record — the transition from non-traded to NYSE-listed in May 2025 means OTF has not yet navigated a full credit cycle as a public company, creating uncertainty about performance under stress
