Company Profile
Financial Services ·Asset Management ·NYQ ·United States
Blue Owl Capital is an alternative asset manager with $319B in AUM as of Q2 2026 (up 12% YoY), operating across three platforms: GP Strategic Capital (minority stakes in other asset managers and professional sports franchises), Direct Lending (private credit to middle-market companies), and Real Estate (triple net lease and credit). Co-founded by Doug Ostrover and Marc Lipschultz, the firm went public via SPAC in May 2021 and scaled AUM from $94B to $319B in five years. Q2 2026 revenue was $753.1M (+7% YoY) with fee-related earnings margin of 58.5% and distributable earnings of $0.22/share.

Investment Thesis
Blue Owl Capital at $8.95 (9.08x forward earnings, PEG 0.15) offers a high-yield entry (10.17%) into alternative asset management with $319B AUM growing 12% YoY and fee-related earnings margin of 58.5% [1]. The GP Strategic Capital business — minority stakes in other asset managers and professional sports franchises — generates permanent capital fees with minimal redemption risk, creating a uniquely durable revenue stream. Q2 2026 revenue of $753.1M beat estimates, and $29.9B in AUM not yet paying fees represents approximately $349M in expected annual management fees once deployed. The founder-led structure with all four co-founders active and heavily invested ensures alignment. The Inspire Impact Score of 29 (ALIGNED) with zero negative attributions supports faith-based allocation.
The bear case centers on GAAP distortions, growth deceleration, and credit cycle risk. GAAP profit margin of 2.7%, ROE of 5.7%, and payout ratio exceeding 100% dramatically understate Blue Owl's economic profitability but create a low infrastructure score (6) that penalizes the stock in quantitative screens [1]. Revenue growth decelerated from 25%+ in 2023 to 7.1% in Q2 2026, raising questions about whether the rapid scaling phase is ending as AUM approaches $320B. The distributable earnings payout ratio of approximately 105% leaves minimal cushion — any shortfall in fee revenue would pressure the $0.91 annual dividend, which would be devastating for a stock trading primarily on yield. The direct lending portfolio has never been tested through a full credit cycle as a public company, and Lipschultz's admission that Blue Owl is 'working down' AI-vulnerable software exposure ahead of 2028 refinancings acknowledges real portfolio risk [2]. Innovation and Inspiration scores are structurally low (3 and 2) because IOWN's framework rewards product innovation and purpose-driven leadership that financial firms rarely possess [3].
