Pan American Silver Corp. is the world's largest primary silver producer, operating mines across Chile, Peru, Brazil, Mexico, Canada, Argentina, Bolivia, and Guatemala. Cash and short-term investments totaled $1.8B with total liquidity of $3.2B. Founded in 1979, headquartered in Vancouver, Canada.

AI Risk Protection (9/9): Precious metals mining is an inherently physical, capital-intensive, and geologically complex activity that AI cannot displace [1].
