Ping An Biomedical Co., Ltd., formerly Majestic Ideal Holdings Ltd. (name changed September 2025), provides supply chain management services in the apparel industry in China, Hong Kong, Cambodia, and Europe. Services include market trend analysis, product design and development, raw material sourcing, production and quality control, and logistics management.
