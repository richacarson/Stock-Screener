Ping An Biomedical Co., Ltd. (formerly Majestic Ideal Holdings Ltd., renamed September 2025) provides apparel supply chain management services — trend analysis, product design, raw material sourcing, production and quality control, and logistics — in China, Hong Kong, Cambodia and Europe, and manufactures and trades yarns and textiles. Management is attempting a pivot toward healthcare and biotechnology, including a planned $2.5M oncology patent acquisition. The company effected a 1-for-40 reverse stock split on October 23, 2026 to address a Nasdaq minimum bid price deficiency. Not affiliated with Ping An Insurance.

Investment Thesis
The only bull case is optionality: management has rebranded toward biomedicine, plans a $2.5M oncology patent acquisition, has built an equity investment portfolio reported at RMB 41.2M, and carries low leverage (13% debt-to-equity) [1][2][3]. If the 1-for-40 reverse split restores Nasdaq compliance and a healthcare asset gains traction, the ~$16M market cap could rerate.
The bear case dominates: core revenue fell 47% year-over-year in Q1 2026 after a 61% decline in FY2025, 70% of revenue comes from three customers, and reported profitability depends on non-operating items such as credit-loss reversals and revaluation gains rather than operations [2]. The entire senior leadership turned over in October 2025, the company has 7 employees, and its filings warn that a price at or below $0.10 for 10 days could trigger immediate suspension [4][5]. Daily dollar volume of ~$841K is below IOWN's liquidity floor, making the stock uninvestable regardless of fundamentals [1].
