Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NMS ·United States
Parabilis Medicines (formerly FogPharma) is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing stabilized helical peptide therapeutics through its Helicon discovery platform, which integrates AI and physics-based methods. The platform targets traditionally undruggable intracellular protein-protein interactions for cancer treatment. Lead program FOG-001 (zolucatetide) is the first and only direct inhibitor of the beta-catenin:TCF interaction. The company completed an upsized IPO in June 2026 raising $745M (38.5M shares at $20). Stock price $27.48, market cap approximately $3.4B.

Investment Thesis
Parabilis Medicines represents a high-conviction bet on a genuinely novel drug modality — stabilized helical peptides targeting undruggable intracellular protein-protein interactions. FOG-001 (zolucatetide) validated the platform with 100% tumor reduction in desmoid patients and 80% ORR at ESMO 2025, and is advancing toward Phase III [1]. The upsized $745M IPO in June 2026 (with 10x oversubscription and Fidelity, GV, and RA Capital participation) provides strong institutional validation and extends cash runway to H2 2029 with $1.12B in total cash [1][4]. CEO Mammen brings transformative pharma leadership from J&J and pioneered AI integration in drug discovery [2]. The Helicon platform generates multiple therapeutic modalities (functional inhibitors, protein degraders, radiopharmaceuticals) from a single engine, creating optionality rare among clinical-stage biotechs.
The bear case centers on binary clinical risk, valuation, and execution uncertainty. Parabilis has TTM revenue of just $148K and annual net losses exceeding $200M, with the $3.4B market cap resting entirely on pipeline optionality — failure of zolucatetide would undermine the Helicon platform thesis and could collapse the valuation [1][4]. The stock has declined 28% from the post-IPO high of $38 to $27.48, reflecting market reassessment of the premium paid for unproven science. Competition from larger AI-native drug discovery platforms (Recursion, Insilico Medicine) and established peptide players could erode first-mover advantage. The Inspire Impact Score of 0 (MIXED) with no positive or negative attributions reflects limited ESG data coverage for a newly public company. As a pre-revenue biotech with no commercial track record, investors are paying for scientific optionality with minimal margin of safety at current prices.

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience MODERATE
7
AI Risk Protection (7/9): Biotech drug development is deeply protected from AI disruption — it requires physical wet-lab experimentation, clinical trials with human patients, FDA regulatory approval, and pharmaceutical manufacturing, all of which AI cannot replace end-to-end [2]. The regulatory moat alone (FDA IND/NDA process, GMP manufacturing requirements, clinical trial oversight) creates structural barriers that software cannot bypass. Parabilis's proprietary helical peptide chemistry provides a scientific moat that purely computational approaches cannot easily replicate. However, AI is transforming drug discovery economics by accelerating target identification and molecular design, creating competitive pressure for companies that fail to adopt AI tools — larger AI-native discovery platforms (Recursion, Insilico) could outcompete in speed [2]. AI Opportunity (0/1): While Parabilis uses AI in its Helicon discovery platform and the nference partnership applies machine learning for biomarker identification, the company does not generate quantifiable revenue from AI-related products or services [1]. AI is a tool for drug discovery, not a revenue stream — TTM revenue of $148K is from grants/licensing, not AI products.
