Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NYSE ·United States
Procore Technologies is the leading cloud-based construction management platform, connecting project stakeholders across owners, general contractors, and specialty contractors. The platform manages project financials, quality, safety, resources, and documents across the construction lifecycle. Founder CEO Tooey Courtemanche stepped down in November 2025, replaced by former Ansys CEO Ajei Gopal. Q2 2026 revenue was $375.2M (+15.8% YoY) with 21.5% non-GAAP operating margin (800 bps expansion), and full-year 2026 guidance was raised to $1.51-$1.514B revenue with 18.5-19% non-GAAP operating margin. Market cap is approximately $8.4B.
3,200 Employees ·www.procore.com

Investment Thesis
Procore at approximately 31x forward non-GAAP earnings is the dominant construction management platform delivering both growth and margin expansion — Q2 2026 revenue of $375.2M (+15.8% YoY) with 21.5% non-GAAP operating margin (800 bps expansion) demonstrates the operating leverage of its SaaS model [1]. The raised full-year guidance of $1.51-$1.514B revenue with 18.5-19% non-GAAP margin, plus a 2027 target of 25% margin, shows a clear path to Rule of 40+ economics. The 5-year revenue CAGR of ~24% reflects consistent penetration of a massive TAM — the global construction industry is $13T+ and digital adoption remains in early innings. Procore Helix and AI features (Copilot, Agents) create an upsell catalyst that deepens the platform moat with proprietary construction data [3]. The Inspire Impact Score of 62 (ALIGNED) with zero negative attributions makes PCOR suitable for faith-aligned portfolios. The balance sheet is pristine with D/E of just 5%, providing strategic flexibility for acquisitions or buybacks.
The bear case centers on valuation, growth deceleration, and execution risk. At ~31x forward non-GAAP P/E, the stock prices in substantial continued growth — but revenue growth has decelerated from 21% in 2024 to 15% in 2025 to a guided 14.5% in 2026, raising questions about how much incremental growth remains from the existing construction TAM [1][2]. GAAP profitability remains negative (profit margin -2.73%) due to heavy stock-based compensation, and the ROE of -3.12% means the company is not yet generating positive returns on equity for shareholders [5]. The CEO transition from mission-driven founder Courtemanche to professional manager Gopal introduces execution risk — Gopal must maintain product innovation velocity while driving margin expansion, and his prior company Ansys had a different industry context. Concerns about President Steve Davis's background at failed AI-health startup Babylon Health raise questions about AI governance [3]. Construction is a cyclical industry sensitive to interest rates and housing starts — a macro downturn could pressure growth rates and ARR retention. The company faces growing competition from Autodesk Construction Cloud, Oracle Primavera, and Trimble, all investing aggressively in cloud and AI capabilities.
Key Catalysts
  1. Non-GAAP operating margin expansion from 19% (2026 guide) to 25% (2027 target) represents nearly 1,100 bps of improvement versus FY2025, demonstrating the SaaS operating leverage thesis and potentially driving re-rating from growth-only to growth-plus-profitability valuation [1]
  2. Procore Helix AI platform adoption (Copilot, Agents) could accelerate revenue growth by increasing platform value, driving upsell within existing accounts, and strengthening competitive moats through proprietary construction intelligence [3]
  3. International expansion into underpenetrated construction markets outside the US represents a greenfield growth opportunity that could re-accelerate the 5-year revenue CAGR above 20%
Key Risks
  1. Revenue growth deceleration from 24% CAGR to 14.5% guided 2026 growth could compress the valuation multiple if the market perceives Procore as approaching growth maturity before achieving scale-commensurate profitability [1][2]
  2. CEO transition risk as Ajei Gopal (six months into tenure) must simultaneously drive AI innovation, margin expansion, and cultural continuity — a complex mandate for a new leader in a mission-driven organization [3]
  3. Construction sector cyclicality exposes Procore to macro-driven spending pullbacks — elevated interest rates and potential housing slowdowns could pressure customer spending and new logo acquisition

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
7
AI Risk Protection (6/9): Construction management is structurally protected from AI disruption because the industry involves physical project execution, complex multi-party coordination, regulatory compliance, and deep domain workflows that cannot be replicated by general-purpose AI models [1]. Procore's platform moat includes proprietary construction data, workflow integrations, and network effects across project stakeholders — switching costs that AI entrants would struggle to overcome. The fragmented, relationship-driven nature of construction creates barriers to AI-native disruption. However, AI-powered point solutions (schedule optimization, cost estimation, document analysis) could commoditize specific Procore features if the company fails to integrate AI faster than competitors. New CEO Ajei Gopal's technology background creates execution risk if AI strategy is mismanaged, and questions about President Steve Davis's prior role at failed AI-health startup Babylon Health have raised stakeholder concerns about AI governance [3]. AI Opportunity (1/1): Procore Helix, Copilot, and Agents demonstrate that AI is an enabler, not a threat, to Procore's business — embedding AI intelligence into the construction platform increases customer value, drives upsell, and strengthens the competitive moat [3]. Construction generates massive unstructured data (documents, photos, plans, communications) that AI can uniquely process, creating opportunities for predictive analytics, automated safety monitoring, and intelligent project recommendations.
