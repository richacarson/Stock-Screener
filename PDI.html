Company Profile
Financial Services ·Closed-End Fund - Debt ·NYSE ·United States
PIMCO Dynamic Income Fund (PDI) is a closed-end fixed income fund launched in 2012 and managed by Pacific Investment Management Company LLC. The fund invests across global fixed income markets including non-agency residential and commercial mortgage-backed securities, investment-grade and high-yield corporate bonds, emerging markets debt, and sovereign bonds. PDI employs significant leverage (approximately 40-50%) to enhance yield and pays monthly distributions. The annual distribution is $2.65 per share, producing a 15.79% yield at current prices. As a CEF, standard operating company metrics (revenue, R&D, employees) do not apply.

The IOWN framework is designed to evaluate operating companies, not fund vehicles, and PDI's scores reflect this structural mismatch rather than a judgment on the fund's income generation capability. The overall score of 25 results from a closed-end fund having no innovation, no mission, no infrastructure in the corporate sense, and no infinite-game characteristics — dimensions that simply do not apply to a managed portfolio of bonds [2]. The real risks for PDI investors are: (1) leverage of 40-50% amplifies both returns and losses, creating significant NAV volatility during fixed income sell-offs; (2) the 14.2% distribution yield may include return of capital components that erode NAV over time if net investment income does not fully cover distributions; (3) the CEF trades at a discount or premium to NAV, adding a layer of market sentiment risk beyond the underlying portfolio [1]. YTD market return of +1.0% versus NAV return of +0.6% suggests the premium has expanded modestly, which could compress returns going forward.
