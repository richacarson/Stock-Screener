Shell Company (Defunct) ·SPAC Rights — Merger Completed ·NASDAQ (Delisted) ·Cayman Islands
PELIR was the Nasdaq ticker for the rights issued by Pelican Acquisition Corporation, a blank-check SPAC. Each right entitled the holder to receive one-tenth of one ordinary share upon completion of a business combination. Pelican's shareholders approved the merger with Greenland Exploration Limited and March GL Company on March 19, 2026, and the combination closed on March 25, 2026, forming Greenland Energy Company. Nasdaq filed a Form 25-NSE the same day removing PELIR (along with PELI ordinary shares and PELIU units) from listing. PELIR no longer exists as an independently tradable security — any residual rights converted into shares of the successor, which now trades under ticker GLND at $4.49 as of September 2026 (down 80% from its $23 52-week high).
