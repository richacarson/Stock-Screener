Company Profile
Consumer Cyclical ·Gambling ·NASDAQ ·United States
PENN Entertainment operates 43+ gaming and racing properties across North America under brands including Hollywood Casino, Ameristar, and L'Auberge. The company also operates theScore Bet sports betting platform, standalone iCasino (Hollywood iCasino), and theScore media app. PENN's $2B+ ESPN Bet partnership (2023) was terminated in November 2025, with customers migrated to theScore Bet in December 2025. Headquartered in Wyomissing, Pennsylvania with $7.2B TTM revenue and approximately $2.3B market cap.

Investment Thesis
PENN Entertainment returned to GAAP profitability in Q2 2026 with $32.6M net income (vs. -$18.3M loss in Q2 2025) and consolidated Adjusted EBITDA surging 32% to $312.6M, marking a meaningful inflection point in the post-ESPN Bet transition [1]. The Interactive segment generated $349.4M revenue with EBITDA loss narrowing to just -$9.5M (vs. -$89M prior year), positioning the division for imminent profitability that could unlock $100M+ in annual EBITDA [1]. Hollywood iCasino delivered record quarterly revenues, Ontario showed World Cup-driven momentum, and the Alberta launch on July 13 expands the platform footprint [1]. Retail operations set record quarterly revenues with strong regional casino performance across food, beverage, and hotel segments. At $17.74, the stock trades at just 0.33x sales and 10.6x forward earnings, deeply discounted to gaming peers, with CEO Snowden's $1M+ in personal share purchases at $14-15 providing a floor of insider conviction [3][7].
The bear case is dominated by PENN's $11.27B debt load against $686.6M cash, creating a D/E ratio of 617% that constrains strategic flexibility and generates significant interest expense [3]. TTM net margin of -12.7% reflects ongoing costs from the failed ESPN partnership, digital investments, and casino renovations that have only begun to translate to bottom-line profitability in Q2 2026. The retreat from ESPN Bet after $2B+ commitment and less than two years of operation raises lasting questions about management's strategic judgment, and theScore Bet must now rebuild brand awareness from scratch against DraftKings and FanDuel's structural advantages. Revenue growth of 5.2% YoY is modest for a company carrying this level of leverage — the debt-to-equity ratio far exceeds the 300% infrastructure floor. The 'Gambling' negative attribution in the Inspire score (-1) reflects the fundamental ethical tension in a business model that profits from consumer losses, and regulatory risk from potential online gambling restrictions, increased gaming taxes, or responsible gambling mandates could compress margins [6].
