AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience MODERATE
7
AI Risk Protection (7/9): Pharmaceutical development is structurally protected from AI disruption — the core business requires physical clinical trials (Phase I-III spanning years), FDA regulatory approval, large-scale GMP manufacturing of complex biological and chemical compounds, and global distribution infrastructure that AI cannot replace [2]. Pfizer's moats include 175+ years of regulatory relationships, manufacturing expertise, and patent portfolios. AI enhances drug discovery (Pfizer has demonstrated 80-90% time reduction in computational screening) but does not eliminate the years-long development, testing, and approval process that follows. The primary AI exposure is indirect: AI could empower smaller biotech competitors to identify drug targets faster, increasing competitive pressure on pipeline investments. However, capital requirements for late-stage trials ($1B+ per drug) and global manufacturing create barriers that AI alone cannot overcome. AI Opportunity (0/1): Pfizer uses AI extensively for internal drug discovery and manufacturing efficiency (20% throughput increase, 80-90% computational time savings) but does not sell AI products or services [2]. The revenue comes from drugs developed using AI tools, not from AI itself.
