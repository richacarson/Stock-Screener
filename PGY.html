Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Infrastructure (AI Credit Decisioning) ·NASDAQ ·Israel
Pagaya Technologies, co-founded in 2016 by CEO Gal Krubiner, runs an AI-driven credit decisioning network that helps U.S. banks, fintechs and auto lenders approve borrowers they would otherwise decline, funding the resulting loans through asset-backed securitizations. FY2025 revenue was $1.26B (+26%) with GAAP net income of $81M, its first profitable year. Q2 2026 revenue rose 19% to $387M on $3.5B of network volume (+33%), GAAP net income nearly tripled to $45M, and full-year 2026 guidance was raised to revenue of $1.425-1.525B and GAAP net income of $155-180M.

Investment Thesis
Pagaya has turned the corner to consistent GAAP profits: Q2 2026 revenue rose 19% to $387M, network volume grew 33% to $3.5B, GAAP net income nearly tripled to $45M, and 2026 guidance was raised to $155-180M GAAP net income and $460-490M adjusted EBITDA [2]. Revenue compounded at ~69% annually from 2020 to 2025 [1], auto lending is a powerful new engine (+~140% in Q2) [2], and the stock trades at ~5x forward earnings with a forward PEG of 0.15 [6][1].
The bear case is credit-cycle and trust risk. Pagaya's network is built on borrowers that traditional models decline, so a rise in consumer delinquencies would hit both its securitization funding and its retained risk. Q3 2026 revenue guidance of $370-390M sits below the ~$398M consensus [2], FRLPC take rate of 4.2% of volume signals fee pressure [2], and D/E is ~134% [6]. Iceberg Research's 2025 allegations about risk retention and executive conduct triggered law-firm investigations [5], and the company carries a 2024 net loss of $401M as a reminder of how quickly results can swing [1].
Key Catalysts
  1. Q3 2026 results (expected November 2026) against guidance of $42-52M GAAP net income and $3.425-3.625B network volume [2]
  2. Continued auto-lending expansion across ~30,000 dealerships driving network-volume growth above 30% [2]
  3. Delivering the raised FY2026 targets ($1.425-1.525B revenue, $155-180M GAAP net income) to support re-rating from ~5x forward earnings [2][6]
Key Risks
  1. Consumer credit deterioration among near-prime borrowers impairing securitization demand and retained risk positions [2]
  2. Governance overhang from Iceberg Research's 2025 allegations of risk-shifting to investors and executive irregularities [5]
  3. Take-rate compression (FRLPC 4.2% of network volume) and AI-underwriting commoditization by banks and rivals like Upstart [2]
