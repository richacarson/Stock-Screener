Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Phio Pharmaceuticals Corp. is a clinical-stage biotechnology company developing immuno-oncology therapeutics on its proprietary INTASYL self-delivering RNAi platform, co-founded by Nobel laureate Craig Mello. Its lead compound, PH-762, is an intratumoral INTASYL compound that silences PD-1 and completed a 22-patient Phase 1b trial in March 2026; PH-894 targets BRD4. The company has no product revenue, and reports 6 full-time employees.

Investment Thesis
Phio offers high-risk optionality on a differentiated RNAi platform co-founded by Nobel laureate Craig Mello: INTASYL compounds self-deliver into tumors without lipid nanoparticles, and PH-762 (PD-1 silencing) completed a 22-patient Phase 1b study in March 2026 [2][3]. An FDA meeting to define next steps was planned for Q3 2026, and management says cash of $13.0M funds operations into the second half of 2027 [2]. With a market cap near $10M, positive regulatory feedback or a partnership could rerate the shares [1].
The bear case is severe: Phio has no revenue, cash fell from $21.0M to $13.0M in six months, the Q1 2026 net loss doubled to $4.0M, and the April 2026 ATM of up to $6.36M (about 5.0M shares versus 11.6M outstanding) implies heavy dilution [2][4]. The stock at $0.86 is below Nasdaq's $1 minimum bid, and past filings warn that cumulative reverse splits may eliminate the usual grace period [1][4]. Daily dollar volume of ~$109K is far below IOWN's liquidity floor [1].
Key Catalysts
  1. FDA feedback on the PH-762 development path following the Q3 2026 meeting request
  2. Partnership or licensing deal for the INTASYL platform that brings non-dilutive capital
  3. Advancement of PH-894 (BRD4 silencing) toward the clinic
Key Risks
  1. Cash runway only into 2H 2027 with ongoing ATM dilution (up to ~5.0M new shares vs. 11.6M outstanding)
  2. Nasdaq delisting risk with the share price below $1.00 and limited reverse-split flexibility
  3. Clinical or regulatory setback for PH-762, the company's only clinical-stage asset
  4. Competition from well-funded RNAi and checkpoint-inhibitor developers using AI-driven design
  5. Illiquidity: ~$109K average daily dollar volume, far below IOWN's $1M floor
