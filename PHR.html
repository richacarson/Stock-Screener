Company Profile
Healthcare ·Health Information Services ·NYSE ·United States
Phreesia is a healthcare SaaS company providing patient intake, payments, and engagement solutions to healthcare organizations. The platform processes approximately 180 million patient visits and over $4.8 billion in patient payments annually. Revenue comes from three segments: subscription services, network solutions (life sciences), and payment processing. Co-founded in 2005 by CEO Chaim Indig. The company achieved its first full year of GAAP profitability in FY2026 ($480.6M revenue, $2.3M net income). Q2 FY2027 revenue was $129.5M (+10% YoY), but the company lowered its FY2027 revenue outlook citing reduced pharmaceutical manufacturer spending, triggering a securities fraud class action lawsuit.

Investment Thesis
Phreesia at 6.25x forward earnings offers a deep value entry into a 21-year co-founder-led healthcare SaaS platform processing 180M patient visits and $4.8B in payments annually, with improving profitability — FY2026 marked the first GAAP-positive year ($2.3M net income, adjusted EBITDA $29.1M growing toward FY2027 guidance of $125-135M), and forward EPS of $1.61 implies dramatic margin expansion [1]. The stock has been severely punished (down ~67% from highs) following the FY2027 revenue guidance cut and securities fraud lawsuit, creating a potential asymmetric entry if pharma advertising revenue stabilizes and the core subscription/payments business sustains double-digit growth. D/E of 18.3% with $72.9M cash provides balance sheet cushion, and the $160M AccessOne acquisition extends the platform into patient financing. Revenue per client growing 8% YoY indicates deepening wallet share and embedded switching costs.
The bear case centers on credibility damage, cultural dysfunction, and competitive threats. The securities fraud class action (covering May 2025 to March 2026) alleging false and misleading statements about growth prospects creates legal overhang and shatters management credibility with investors [3]. Revenue growth decelerated from a 25% 5-year CAGR to 10% in Q2 FY2027, with the pharmaceutical advertising segment (~20% of revenue) facing reduced spending commitments and deteriorating visibility [1][3]. The ConnectOnCall ransomware breach (Feb-May 2024, 10+ month notification delay) exposed patient data and raises serious questions about data security competence in a HIPAA-regulated business [3]. Glassdoor reviews (2.8/5 culture, 2.5/5 career opportunities) describe toxic management and CEO dysfunction, creating talent retention risk [2]. GAAP profit margin of 2.1% leaves virtually no margin for error, and EHR vendors (Epic, Oracle Health) increasingly bundle native intake and payment functionality that could commoditize Phreesia's standalone proposition. AI Risk Protection (6/9): Phreesia's core business involves deeply integrated workflows with EHR systems, insurance verification, and payment processing infrastructure that create meaningful switching costs [1]. HIPAA regulatory requirements, PCI compliance for payments, and complex insurance workflows add protective barriers that AI startups cannot easily bypass.
