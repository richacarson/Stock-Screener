Company Profile

Plug Power designs, develops, and sells hydrogen fuel cell systems and green hydrogen infrastructure. Core products include GenDrive (PEM fuel cell systems for material handling EVs), GenFuel (liquid hydrogen fueling and delivery), GenEco electrolyzers for clean hydrogen production, and cryogenic equipment for gas distribution. TTM revenue of $744M with a -221% profit margin as the company pursues profitability — targeting positive EBITDAS by Q4 2026. Q1 2026 revenue grew 22% YoY to $163.5M. Founded in 1997, headquartered in Slingerlands, New York.

Investment Thesis
Plug Power at $1.89 is a turnaround play in the green hydrogen economy showing early signs of stabilization — Q1 2026 revenue grew 22% YoY to $163.5M with a 71% improvement in gross margins, the electrolyzer platform surged 343%, and management raised full-year guidance to 15-16% revenue growth with a path to positive EBITDAS by Q4 2026 [1][2]. New CEO Jose Luis Crespo brings 12 years of Plug Power experience as Chief Revenue Officer and President, having helped scale revenue from $20M to $700M+, and his focus on operational discipline and profitability addresses the company's most critical weakness. The hydrogen market opportunity remains large if adoption accelerates, with GenDrive deployments at Amazon and Walmart providing a sticky installed base and electrolyzers opening a higher-value revenue stream [1].
The bear case is severe: Plug Power has destroyed billions in shareholder value over 29 years without ever achieving profitability, and the stock has collapsed from $75 in 2021 to $1.89 today — a 97.5% decline [1]. At -$482M in annual operating cash flow with only $172M in cash against $1.04B in debt, the balance sheet is precarious and likely requires additional dilutive capital raises. Profit margin of -220.6% means every dollar of revenue costs $3.21 to generate, and the path to positive EBITDAS by Q4 2026 assumes margin improvement that has been promised but never delivered at scale. DOE funding cuts under the current administration threaten hydrogen subsidies that Plug Power's economics depend on, while battery-electric alternatives continue gaining cost advantages in material handling and transportation — the very markets Plug Power targets [3]. The CEO transition introduces execution risk at a critical juncture, and 49% Glassdoor recommendation suggests organizational culture challenges that could impede the turnaround.
