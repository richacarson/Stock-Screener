Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·AMEX ·Israel
Protalix BioTherapeutics develops, produces, and commercializes recombinant therapeutic proteins using its proprietary ProCellEx plant cell-based protein expression system — the first plant cell-based technology to receive FDA approval. Key products include Elelyso for Gaucher disease (partnered with Pfizer) and Elfabrio for Fabry disease (partnered with Chiesi), with pipeline candidates PRX-115 (gout) and PRX-119 (rare renal diseases). Q2 2026 revenue from selling goods reached $19.8M (+29% YoY) driven by Elfabrio, with YTD net income of $22.1M. Headquartered in Carmiel, Israel.
226 Employees ·www.protalix.com

Investment Thesis
Protalix BioTherapeutics is transitioning from a milestone-dependent biotech to a sustainably profitable rare disease company. Q2 2026 revenue from selling goods reached $19.8M (+29% YoY from $15.4M), demonstrating genuine Elfabrio demand growth beyond one-time milestones, with YTD net income of $22.1M and FY2026 guidance reaffirmed at $78-83M total revenue ($33-35M in Elfabrio product sales) [1]. At $2.75 and just 4.4x forward P/E with zero traditional debt (D/E 12.9%), ROE of 31.2%, and profit margin of 23.5%, PLX offers deep value for a profitable biotech with a validated manufacturing platform. Cash of $40.7M provides runway through 2028 without dilution. PRX-115 Phase II for uncontrolled gout (data H2 2027) could unlock a significantly larger addressable market than Fabry disease. CEO Bashan's personal share purchase at $1.81 signals insider conviction [3].
The bear case centers on competitive threats, milestone dependency, and micro-cap constraints. Oral Fabry disease treatments under development offer superior patient convenience over IV-administered enzyme replacement therapy, potentially compressing Elfabrio's addressable market over time [1]. Total H1 2026 revenue of $53.6M includes a $25M one-time Chiesi milestone — excluding it, organic H1 product revenue of ~$28.6M annualizes to ~$57M, roughly flat to FY2025's $51.8M. At $2.75 per share and $222M market cap, the stock is a micro-cap with declining daily liquidity (704K avg volume, $1.9M daily — barely above the $1M floor), limiting institutional participation and creating vulnerability to liquidity shocks. Cash decreased from $51M to $40.7M in Q2, and while the runway extends to 2028, any pipeline setback would erode the financial cushion. Dependence on three commercial partners (Chiesi, Pfizer, Fiocruz) concentrates revenue risk, and Israel-based manufacturing creates geopolitical supply chain exposure [1][2].

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience MODERATE
7
AI Risk Protection (7/9): Protalix's core business — manufacturing recombinant therapeutic proteins using proprietary plant cell-based expression systems — is a physical, wet-lab operation that AI cannot substitute [2]. Enzyme replacement therapy for rare diseases requires biological manufacturing in living plant cell culture systems, FDA/EMA regulatory approval, and extensive clinical validation that create substantial barriers to disruption. However, AI-driven drug design is accelerating competitor development of alternative therapies (gene therapy, substrate reduction, small molecule and oral approaches) for Gaucher and Fabry disease that could structurally compress Elfabrio and Elelyso's addressable markets. The threat is indirect — AI won't replace Protalix's manufacturing but may help competitors create superior treatments faster. AI Opportunity (0/1): Protalix does not generate revenue from AI products or services; the company's technology platform is biological, not computational.
