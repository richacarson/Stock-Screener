Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·AMEX ·Israel
Protalix BioTherapeutics develops, produces, and commercializes recombinant therapeutic proteins using its proprietary ProCellEx plant cell-based protein expression system — the first plant cell-based technology to receive FDA approval. Key products include Elelyso for Gaucher disease (partnered with Pfizer) and Elfabrio for Fabry disease (partnered with Chiesi), with pipeline candidates PRX-115 (gout) and PRX-119 (rare renal diseases). Headquartered in Carmiel, Israel.
226 Employees ·www.protalix.com

Investment Thesis
Protalix BioTherapeutics delivered a breakout Q1 2026 — revenue of $33.75M and net income of $18.32M ($0.22 EPS, up 233.8% YoY), driven by the $25M Chiesi milestone payment for Elfabrio's EU every-4-weeks dosing approval [1]. Cash improved to $51M, extending the runway through 2028 without dilution. The company reaffirmed FY2026 guidance of $78-83M (48-57% growth), and at just ~6.6x forward P/E with zero traditional debt (D/E 17.3%), the stock offers deep value if the pipeline delivers. PRX-115 for uncontrolled gout (Phase II data expected H2 2027) addresses a larger market than Gaucher or Fabry, and the ProCellEx plant cell platform provides sustainable manufacturing cost advantages [1][2]. CEO Bashan's personal share purchases signal insider alignment [4].
The bear case is that Q1 2026's profitability is an illusion — the $25M Chiesi milestone is one-time, and underlying product revenue declined from $10M to $7.4M as Pfizer reduced Elelyso purchases [1]. Excluding milestones, FY2026 organic product revenue guidance is $53-58M, roughly flat to FY2025's $51.8M — the business is not growing organically. Elfabrio faces existential competitive pressure from oral Fabry treatments that offer superior patient convenience over IV enzyme replacement therapy [3]. At $2.32 per share and $171M market cap, the stock is a micro-cap with inherent liquidity and institutional investment constraints. The company's 226-person headcount and $19.6M R&D budget are small by industry standards, limiting the pace of pipeline development. Dependence on three partners (Chiesi, Pfizer, Fiocruz) for revenue creates concentration risk, and any disruption to the Israel-based manufacturing operations poses geopolitical supply chain risk [1][5].
Key Catalysts
  1. PRX-115 Phase II RELEASE trial data for uncontrolled gout expected H2 2027 — a positive readout could validate a new revenue stream in a significantly larger addressable market than Gaucher or Fabry disease [1]
  2. FY2026 revenue guidance of $78-83M driven by $25M milestone receipt and growing Elfabrio sales ($33-35M), potentially demonstrating the company's transition from loss-making to sustained profitability [1]
  3. Cash position of $51M provides runway through 2028 without dilution, giving PRX-115 time to generate clinical data that could attract partnership interest or re-rate the stock from its current micro-cap valuation
Key Risks
  1. Oral Fabry disease treatments threatening Elfabrio's enzyme replacement therapy market — patient preference for oral convenience could structurally compress the addressable market for IV-administered biologics [3]
  2. Underlying product revenue declining ($7.4M vs $10M in Q1 YoY) despite headline growth from milestone payments — the core franchise may be eroding under competitive pressure [1]
  3. Micro-cap constraints at $171M market cap and $2.32 share price limit institutional participation and create execution risk for a 226-person company dependent on three commercial partners for revenue [4]

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience MODERATE
7
AI Risk Protection (7/9): Protalix's core business — manufacturing recombinant therapeutic proteins using proprietary plant cell-based expression systems — is a physical, wet-lab operation that AI cannot substitute [2]. Enzyme replacement therapy for rare diseases requires biological manufacturing in living plant cell culture systems, FDA/EMA regulatory approval, and extensive clinical validation that create substantial barriers to disruption. However, AI-driven drug design is accelerating competitor development of alternative therapies (gene therapy, substrate reduction, small molecule approaches) for Gaucher and Fabry disease that could structurally compress Elfabrio and Elelyso's addressable markets. The threat is indirect — AI won't replace Protalix's manufacturing but may help competitors create superior treatments faster. AI Opportunity (0/1): Protalix does not generate revenue from AI products or services; the Secarna collaboration leverages partner AI capabilities rather than Protalix-developed technology.
