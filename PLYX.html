Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Polaryx Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biotechnology company focused on the discovery, development, and commercialization of novel, disease-modifying therapies for rare, pediatric lysosomal storage disorders (LSDs). Lead candidate PLX-200 (gemfibrozil) is a PPAR agonist targeting lysosomal dysfunction and impaired autophagy. PLX-200 received FDA Fast Track Designation in February 2026 and FDA safe-to-proceed for the Phase 2 SOTERIA basket trial. The company completed a direct listing on NASDAQ in February 2026 and closed a $10M PIPE financing in May 2026 at ~$4.00/share. Market cap approximately $123M with 11 employees.
