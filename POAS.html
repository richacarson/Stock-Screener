Company Profile
Healthcare ·Medical Devices ·NYSE American ·Singapore
Phaos Technology Holdings (Cayman) Limited, through its Singapore-based subsidiary Phaos Technology Pte. Ltd., manufactures AI-powered advanced microscopy products (Optonano, PT-Industrial, PT-Metrology, PT-Biology, PT-Stereo, PT-Zoom) for manufacturing, biomedical, and research customers. The company completed a NYSE American IPO in November 2025, raising roughly $16M combined, but revenue collapsed from $1.88M (FY2024) to $167,707 (FY2025) to $132,077 (FY2026), with net losses widening to S$6.16M and the company disclosing substantial doubt about its ability to continue as a going concern [1]. CEO Andrew Yeo Eng Sian and COO Tay Beng Boon both resigned December 31, 2025, replaced by interim CEO Gan Hong Loon [2].

Investment Thesis
There is no credible bull case for Phaos Technology at this time. The company's AI-powered microscopy products address genuine markets (manufacturing quality control, biomedical imaging, research), and it does hold a functioning NYSE American listing following its November 2025 IPO that raised approximately $16M in gross proceeds, providing some near-term cash runway [1]. That is the extent of the case for owning the stock.
The bear case is overwhelming: revenue collapsed 91% from $1.88M in FY2024 to $167,707 in FY2025 and fell further to $132,077 in FY2026, while net losses widened to S$6.16M, and the company has explicitly disclosed substantial doubt about its ability to continue as a going concern [1]. Average daily dollar volume of roughly $46,500 is less than 5% of the $1,000,000 liquidity floor this framework requires, meaning the stock is effectively untradeable at any meaningful size and Infrastructure scores 0 by hard rule. The simultaneous resignation of both the CEO and COO on December 31, 2025, followed by a February 2026 disclosure disaffiliating from two related parties (SG AB Venture and TongHuai SG2 Enterprise), points to governance instability layered on top of a collapsing operating business [2][3]. With a market capitalization near $2.8M, negative ROE of -369%, and no analyst coverage, this is a speculative micro-cap with a high probability of continued shareholder value destruction or delisting.
