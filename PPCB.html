Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·Australia
Propanc Biopharma is a pre-revenue clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing PRP, a proenzyme-based therapy targeting advanced solid tumors including pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC), ovarian cancer, and refractory prostate cancer. The company plans to file an Australian Clinical Trial Application in 2026 for a Phase 1b first-in-human study of PRP, with enrollment of up to 50 patients planned for February 2027. Market cap of ~$1.9M with no product revenue. CEO James Nathanielsz also serves as CFO and Executive Chairman. FY2025 net loss of $(58.9M) driven largely by non-cash items on a minimal operating cost base.
