Investment Thesis
PPG's Q2 2026 net sales of $4.495B (+7% YoY, +4% organic) beat consensus by 3.1%, and management reaffirmed FY2026 adjusted EPS guidance of $7.70-$8.10 despite raw material headwinds [1]. As a Dividend King with 55 consecutive years of dividend increases, a 42.35% payout ratio, and a Forward P/E of just 12.76x (PEG of 1.50), PPG offers a reasonably valued, income-generating way to own a global coatings leader with an ROE of 19.30%. Continued capital investment in aerospace capacity ($300M modernization program plus a new $380M Shelby, NC facility) positions PPG to capture commercial aerospace coatings demand as capacity comes online in 2026-2027 [2].
The bear case centers on weak organic growth and margin pressure: the 5-year revenue CAGR of -1.42% shows PPG has not meaningfully grown its top line since the 2021 post-COVID peak, and profit margin of just 9.57% sits well below the 20% target as raw material inflation compressed Q2 2026 EBITDA margin from 18.5% to 17.4% YoY, with Q3 guidance calling for margins flat to down another 100bps [1]. PFAS 'forever chemicals' litigation is a growing tail risk — PPG still has at least three PFAS-family substances in its product portfolio and has already settled a Jersey City chromium contamination case for $5M and been found liable for Clean Water Act violations on the Allegheny River — as chemical-industry PFAS litigation accelerates broadly [3]. The Inspire Impact Score of -49 (MISALIGNED, driven by LGBT Activism) may also limit allocation for faith-based investors despite otherwise strong ESG practices.
Key Catalysts
  1. Aerospace segment capacity investments ($300M modernization plus $380M Shelby, NC facility) coming online in 2026-2027 to meet commercial aerospace coatings and sealants demand [2]
  2. Continued organic growth re-acceleration (Q2 2026: +7% net sales, +4% organic) if raw material cost pressures ease into H2 2026 [1]

Key Risks
  1. PFAS 'forever chemicals' litigation exposure — PPG retains multiple PFAS-family substances in its product portfolio amid accelerating industry-wide chemical litigation [3]
  2. Margin compression from raw material inflation, with Q3 2026 guidance calling for adjusted EBITDA margin flat to down 100bps year over year [1]
  3. Five-year revenue CAGR of -1.42% signals limited organic volume growth, making the equity story dependent on price/mix and margin recovery rather than genuine demand expansion
