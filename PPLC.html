Company Profile
Utilities ·Utilities - Regulated Electric ·NYSE ·United States
PPL Corporation provides electricity and natural gas to approximately 3.6 million customers in the United States through three regulated segments: Kentucky Regulated, Pennsylvania Regulated, and Rhode Island Regulated. The company generates, transmits, and distributes electricity from coal, gas, hydro, and solar sources. Founded in 1920 and headquartered in Allentown, Pennsylvania, PPL is investing in a $23 billion capital plan to modernize its grid and serve growing AI and data center demand. PPLC is the corporate unit ticker; PPL is the common stock. Q2 2026 ongoing EPS was $0.33 versus $0.32 in 2025, and the company reaffirmed full-year 2026 EPS guidance of $1.90-$1.98 with a 6-8% annual EPS growth target through 2029.

Investment Thesis
PPL Corporation at a Forward P/E of ~21x (PPLC unit) is a steady regulated utility delivering consistent earnings growth with a reaffirmed 6-8% annual EPS growth target through 2029 [1]. The $23 billion capital plan positions PPL to benefit from the secular surge in data center power demand driven by AI infrastructure buildout, with the Invitium JV specifically targeting this opportunity. The 2.51% dividend yield (PPLC unit) provides income, and the 75+ year track record of uninterrupted dividend payments offers reliability. Q2 2026 ongoing EPS of $0.33 grew from $0.32 YoY, and first-half 2026 ongoing EPS of $0.96 tracks toward the $1.94 midpoint of full-year guidance [1]. Regulatory wins and constructive rate environments in Pennsylvania, Kentucky, and Rhode Island support the capital plan's cost recovery.
The bear case centers on elevated leverage, below-target profitability, and limited upside beyond the regulated framework. D/E of 135.4% is above the 70% target, reflecting $20.4 billion in total debt needed to finance the capital plan, and ROE of 8.6% is well below the 15% target typical of high-quality compounders [4]. The Glassdoor culture score of 2.6/5 (24% below industry) raises concerns about organizational health and employee retention [3]. The PPLC unit trades at a premium to PPL common stock ($44.30 vs $31.97) with much lower liquidity (183K avg daily volume), creating trading friction. Revenue growth of 4.2% YoY is modest, and the 5yr CAGR of ~5% reflects the slow-growth reality of regulated utilities even as the AI demand narrative drives optimism. The dividend was cut significantly in 2021 during the UK spinoff, and consecutive years of growth is only 5 from the reduced base. The stock trades near its 52-week low ($44.09), suggesting the market is not yet rewarding the data center narrative.
Key Catalysts
  1. AI-driven data center demand could accelerate rate base growth beyond the current $23 billion capital plan, with PPL's Invitium JV specifically targeting hyperscaler power needs in its three-state service territory [1]
  2. Regulatory approvals for grid modernization and renewable integration projects could unlock accelerated capital deployment and earnings growth beginning in 2027, when management expects stronger growth to materialize [1]
  3. Quarterly dividend increase trajectory continues, with the $0.285/quarter rate representing a 9% YoY increase, and the 6-8% EPS growth target supporting continued dividend growth toward restoring the pre-spinoff payout level [1]
Key Risks
  1. Elevated leverage at 135.4% D/E creates refinancing risk in a higher-rate environment, and the $20.4 billion debt load requires continuous regulatory cost recovery to service — any adverse rate case outcomes could pressure earnings and dividend growth [4]
  2. Data center demand outlook may be slower to materialize than expected, as hyperscaler buildout timelines remain uncertain and PPL competes with other utilities for data center location decisions despite regulated territory advantages [1]
  3. The Glassdoor culture rating of 2.6/5 — 24% below industry peers — suggests potential workforce challenges that could impair execution of the $23 billion capital plan requiring thousands of skilled workers over the next several years [3]
