Company Profile
Utilities ·Utilities - Regulated Electric ·NYSE ·United States
PPL Corporation provides electricity and natural gas to approximately 3.6 million customers across Pennsylvania, Kentucky, and Rhode Island through three regulated segments. The company operates 14+ GW of generation capacity including coal, gas, hydro, and solar, and is executing a $23B capital plan through 2029 focused on renewables, grid modernization, and data center infrastructure. PPLC represents PPL Corporation Corporate Units (equity units) combining stock purchase contracts with senior notes. Founded in 1920 and headquartered in Allentown, Pennsylvania.

Investment Thesis
PPL Corporation delivered solid Q1 2026 results — GAAP earnings of $452M ($0.60/share, up 7.1% YoY) on revenue of $2.77B (up 10.8%), with non-GAAP EPS of $0.63 beating expectations [1]. FY2026 guidance of $1.90-$1.98 and 6-8% annual EPS growth through 2029 were reaffirmed, driven by a $23B capital plan at 10.3% annual rate base growth. PPL is a prime beneficiary of the AI electricity supercycle — 'massive' data center interconnection requests across Pennsylvania and Kentucky are accelerating rate base investment, with the company now co-developing generation infrastructure for data centers [4]. At 18.2x forward P/E with a 3.22% dividend yield (42 consecutive years paid, 7.7% recent growth CAGR), PPL offers utility-grade income with above-average growth visibility through data center demand and clean energy transition investment [2].
The bear case centers on cultural execution risk and financial constraints. Glassdoor data reveals 3.1/5 employee satisfaction with only 44% recommendation rate and 39% positive outlook — concerning for a company asking employees to execute a multi-decade transformation [6]. D/E of 131% and negative free cash flow ($-1.89/share) create interest rate sensitivity and limit financial flexibility for the $23B capital plan. The 2022 dividend cut from $1.66 to $0.875 (47% reduction following the UK sale) demonstrates that dividend reliability is not absolute despite 42 years of payments — the 7.7% growth CAGR is inflated by rebuilding from a cut base. ROE of 8.16% is constrained by the regulated return framework where allowed returns are set by regulators, not markets. Regulatory proceedings across three states create ongoing uncertainty on allowed returns, capital recovery, and rate structures — the PA PUC investigated PPL's $356M rate increase request in 2025-2026 [5]. Data center demand execution risk remains — converting interconnection requests into actual load growth requires years of successful grid buildout and permitting.
Key Catalysts
  1. AI-driven data center electricity demand creating unprecedented load growth in PPL's Pennsylvania and Kentucky territories, with the company now co-developing generation infrastructure to capture the full value chain of AI power demand [4]
  2. $23B infrastructure capital plan through 2029 at 10.3% annual rate base growth, providing long-term EPS visibility of 6-8% annually with stronger growth beginning in 2027 [1]
  3. Clean energy transition — 2,000 MW coal retirement by 2034 replaced by renewable generation, positioning PPL for favorable regulatory treatment and ESG-driven capital flows while reducing emissions 70% by 2035 [5]
Key Risks
  1. Employee satisfaction crisis — Glassdoor 3.1/5 with 44% recommend and 39% positive outlook threatens execution of the multi-decade transformation strategy, as disengaged employees cannot deliver the 'Utility of the Future' vision [6]
  2. Negative free cash flow (-$1.89/share) and 131% D/E create interest rate sensitivity and financing risk for the $23B capital plan — rising rates compress utility valuations and increase borrowing costs [2]
  3. Regulatory risk across three state jurisdictions — PA PUC investigated a $356M rate increase request, and allowed ROE decisions directly determine earnings growth potential regardless of data center demand [5]
