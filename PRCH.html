Company Profile
Financial Services ·Insurance - Property & Casualty ·NASDAQ ·United States
Porch Group is a vertical software and insurance platform company serving the homeownership ecosystem. The company operates across Insurance Services ($92.9M Q2 revenue, +38% YoY), Software & Data ($23.1M), and Consumer Services ($18.1M), managing Porch Reciprocal Exchange for homeowners insurance. Under CEO Matt Ehrlichman, Porch transformed from a home services marketplace into an insurance-first platform leveraging proprietary data on 90%+ of U.S. properties. Q2 2026 Porch-owned revenue reached $131.8M (+23% YoY) with adjusted EBITDA of $39.1M (+150% YoY). Market cap approximately $1.8B.

Investment Thesis
Porch Group is executing an accelerating margin inflection, with Q2 2026 Porch-owned revenue of $131.8M (+23% YoY), Insurance Services surging 38% to $92.9M, and adjusted EBITDA leaping 150% to $39.1M [1]. Reciprocal written premium reached $139.8M with 58,700 policies written (+38% YoY), while agency branches expanded 148% and quote volumes jumped 87%, demonstrating compounding distribution scale. The company achieved $5.6M net income attributable to Porch in Q2 versus $2.6M prior year, signaling sustained GAAP profitability. Raised 2026 guidance of $506-517M Porch-owned revenue and $119-125M adjusted EBITDA (+55-63%) provides substantial visibility. The Home Factors data moat covering 90%+ of U.S. properties creates a differentiated underwriting advantage that improves with scale.
The bear case centers on existential balance sheet risk and governance concerns. D/E of 2,092% from $475.1M in convertible notes ($333.3M at 6.75%, $134M at 9.00%) against a stockholders' deficit of $11.8M creates extreme financial fragility — any insurance catastrophe loss, reinsurance repricing, or capital markets disruption could overwhelm the thin equity base [1][4]. Forward P/E of 43.19x prices in substantial earnings growth from a company that posted -103% ROE and -2.6% profit margin on a trailing basis. The DOL's June 2024 finding that Porch subsidiary American Home Protect fired a whistleblower who reported deceptive customer refund practices raises governance red flags [3]. The 2023 Vesttoo letter-of-credit scandal (40%+ stock decline) demonstrated the tail risks embedded in the insurance model. Competition from well-capitalized traditional insurers deploying AI at scale limits long-term pricing power. The Inspire Impact Score of 41 (ALIGNED) supports faith-based allocation but the whistleblower finding creates tension.
