Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas E&P ·NASDAQ ·United States
Prairie Operating Co. is an independent energy company engaged in the acquisition, development, and production of crude oil, natural gas, and natural gas liquids, with assets concentrated in the oil and liquids-rich Niobrara and Codell formations of Colorado's Denver-Julesburg (DJ) Basin. The company scaled rapidly since 2023 through a series of asset acquisitions following a reverse merger. Headquartered in Houston, Texas.

Investment Thesis
Prairie Operating has scaled rapidly since its 2023 pivot into DJ Basin oil and gas, posting Q1 2026 revenue of $83.4M (up over 500% quarter-over-quarter) and Adjusted EBITDA of $37.2M, with 2026 guidance reaffirmed at 25,500-27,500 Boe/d and $240-260M Adjusted EBITDA [1]. The core business is structurally immune to AI disruption, and industry-wide AI adoption in E&P operations (predictive maintenance, drilling optimization) is expected to generate meaningful cost efficiencies across the sector over 2026-2030 [5]. The stock's forward P/E of roughly 1.3x against guided 2026 net income of $55-65M suggests the market is pricing in substantial execution or commodity-price risk relative to the underlying production growth story [1][4].
The bigger concerns are financial and governance quality rather than the commodity thesis. ROE of -49% and profit margin of -37.9% reflect a business still working through the costs of its rapid, debt- and equity-funded acquisition strategy, with D/E at 155% [4]. The June 2026 leadership shakeup — co-founder CEO Matthew Lewis Kaplan-style resignation and president retirement with a reported $4M separation payout — introduces execution uncertainty just as the company works to hit its 2026 production and EBITDA targets under new CEO Gregory Patton [2]. There is no distinctive corporate mission, no community investment record, and no evidence of insider buying to signal management conviction, and at $0.78/share with ~$1.56M in daily dollar volume, the stock sits just above IOWN's liquidity floor with limited cushion [1][4].
