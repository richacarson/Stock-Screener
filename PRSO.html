Company Profile
Technology ·Semiconductors ·NASDAQ ·United States
Peraso Inc. is a fabless semiconductor company specializing in 60 GHz millimeter-wave (mmWave) wireless technology, offering chipsets, antenna modules, software, and intellectual property for high-bandwidth wireless connectivity. The company completed a strategic pivot from legacy memory ICs to pure-play mmWave, now targeting fixed wireless access, defense/drone communications, and Edge AI applications. Q2 2026 revenue was $1.3M (+36% sequentially but -41% YoY), with TTM revenue of $8.37M. Market cap is approximately $10M, headquartered in San Jose, California.
42 Employees ·www.perasoinc.com
