Company Profile
Financial Services ·Insurance - Life ·NYSE ·United States
Prudential Financial, Inc. is a global financial services company providing insurance, investment management, and other financial products and services in the United States, Asia, Europe, and Latin America. Operates through PGIM (one of the world's largest asset managers with $1.43 trillion AUM), US Businesses (individual life, group insurance, retirement solutions), and International Businesses segments. Founded in 1875 and headquartered in Newark, New Jersey.

Investment Thesis
Prudential Financial delivered Q1 2026 adjusted operating income of $1.278B (up from $1.188B YoY) with adjusted EPS of $3.61, while PGIM grew adjusted operating income 22% to $190M as AUM reached $1.43T [1]. At 8.44x forward earnings with a 4.86% dividend yield and 13 consecutive years of dividend growth, PRU offers deep value pricing for a diversified financial franchise with a 150-year operating history. The balance sheet is disciplined at 72.28% D/E, and the PEG ratio of 0.90 suggests earnings growth is priced attractively. TTM net income of $3.47B grew 51% YoY, demonstrating earnings power recovery [5].
The bear case centers on structural growth challenges and near-term earnings headwinds. The $525-575M pretax impact from Prudential of Japan's suspension will meaningfully pressure 2026 adjusted operating income [1]. Revenue growth of 4.08% TTM and an estimated 2% five-year CAGR reflect the mature, low-growth nature of the core insurance franchise. ROE of 11.08% falls below the 15% target, and profit margin of 5.99% is constrained by the insurance business model's inherent cost structure. The CEO transition to Andy Sullivan (March 2025) introduces leadership uncertainty during a period of strategic recalibration. The Inspire Insight score of -49 (MISALIGNED) may concern faith-aligned investors, and the below-target 4.2% five-year dividend growth CAGR (vs. 6% target) suggests limited income growth despite the attractive current yield [5].
