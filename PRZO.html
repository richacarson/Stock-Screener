Company Profile
Industrials ·Aerospace & Defense ·NASDAQ ·Israel
ParaZero Technologies is a micro-cap Israeli aerospace and defense company that designs, develops, and manufactures autonomous parachute safety systems (SafeAir) for commercial drones, counter-UAS defense systems (DefendAir), and precision aerial delivery systems (DropAir). Under CEO Ariel Alon (ex-Elbit Systems, Rafael), the company is pivoting from drone safety components to Tier 1 defense provider with multi-layered counter-drone solutions. H1 2026 revenue of $1.06M (+195% YoY) already surpassed full-year 2025 revenue. Market cap approximately $20.6M.
