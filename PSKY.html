Company Profile
Communication Services ·Entertainment ·NASDAQ ·United States
Paramount Skydance Corporation operates as a media and entertainment company worldwide, formed through the August 2025 merger of Paramount Global, National Amusements, and Skydance Media. It operates through Studios (Paramount Pictures, Skydance), Direct-to-Consumer (Paramount+ with 81.6M subscribers, Pluto TV), and TV Media (CBS, Nickelodeon, MTV, BET) segments. Q2 2026 revenue of $6.91B was driven by DTC +9% and Film +16%, offset by TV Media -9%. CEO David Ellison raised full-year adjusted EBITDA guidance to $3.8-3.9B. Founded 1914, headquartered in New York.

Investment Thesis
Paramount Skydance at $10.07 is a high-risk turnaround play under CEO David Ellison with an improving financial trajectory. Q2 2026 revenue of $6.91B beat estimates, with Film division growing 16% to $1.31B, DTC revenue rising 9% to $2.47B, and Paramount+ reaching 81.6M subscribers [1]. Full-year adjusted EBITDA guidance was raised to $3.8-3.9B, and $3B+ merger synergies are tracking ahead of plan. Forward P/E of 12.28 and PEG of 0.54 offer attractive valuation for the turnaround thesis — the market prices PSKY at just 0.39x revenue and 0.97x book value [4]. Ellison's tech-forward vision, AI-focused leadership hires (CPO from Meta), and Oracle family connection provide differentiated tools for transformation. The iconic content library (CBS, Nickelodeon, MTV, Paramount Pictures) and franchise portfolio (Mission: Impossible, Star Trek, Transformers) are irreplaceable assets with global streaming distribution potential.
The bear case is substantial and dominates the near-term outlook. PSKY carries $16.6B in debt against an $11.3B market cap, ROE is negative (-0.90%), and GAAP profit margin is -2.13% — the company lost $605M over the trailing twelve months [4]. The stock has declined 32% over the past year, reflecting market skepticism about the turnaround. TV Media revenue fell 9% to $3.13B in Q2 as cord-cutting accelerates, and this is the largest segment. Post-merger culture has deteriorated severely: 600 employees exited after the RTO mandate, Glassdoor reviews describe a numbers-driven environment hostile to loyalty and creativity [2]. Entertainment faces existential AI disruption risk as AI-generated content threatens traditional production economics. Revenue growth of 4% (FY2026 guidance to $30B) with essentially 0% 5-year CAGR provides no organic growth cushion. The dividend yield of 1.98% ($0.20/share) is funded from cash flow during GAAP losses. Paramount+ remains subscale relative to Netflix and Disney+ while competing for content spending. At $10.07, PSKY is a binary bet on Ellison's execution — the turnaround requires simultaneous debt reduction, streaming growth, linear TV stabilization, and cultural rebuilding [4].

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience MODERATE
5
AI Risk Protection (4/9): Entertainment faces significant AI disruption risk. AI can generate scripts, visual effects, music, voiceovers, and entire video segments at a fraction of traditional production costs, potentially commoditizing content creation that has been Paramount's core competency for 112 years [2]. AI-generated content from competitors (YouTube, TikTok, AI-native creators) threatens traditional media consumption. The Hollywood strikes of 2023 were explicitly about this existential threat. However, premium live content (CBS Sports, CBS News), iconic franchises (Mission: Impossible, Star Trek), and the irreplaceable cultural resonance of tent-pole films and live events provide defensive moats — AI cannot replicate the Super Bowl or a Tom Cruise action sequence. The 81.6M Paramount+ subscriber base and CBS broadcast network create distribution moats. AI Opportunity (1/1): Ellison's AI strategy is the most concrete in legacy media — CPO Dane Glasgow hired from Meta specifically for AI-powered capabilities, board member with AI ecosystem expertise added, and explicit plans to integrate AI for content personalization, discovery algorithms, production cost reduction, and smarter advertising [2]. The Oracle family connection (Ellison is Larry Ellison's son) provides potential AI infrastructure advantages. While still strategy-phase, the investment in AI leadership is tangible.
