Company Profile
Healthcare ·Diagnostics & Research ·NASDAQ ·United States
Personalis develops, markets, and sells advanced cancer genomic tests and services. Products include NeXT Personal, a tumor-informed liquid biopsy test for detection of minimal residual disease (MRD) and recurrence monitoring in solid tumors, and ImmunoID NeXT, a tissue-based comprehensive genomic profiling service. Tempus AI has agreed to acquire Personalis in a definitive $1.5B transaction at $16.25 per share, expected to close late 2026 or early 2027.
259 Employees ·www.personalis.com

Investment Thesis
Personalis is being acquired by Tempus AI in a definitive $1.5B transaction at $16.25 per share (6% premium to last close, 28% premium to 30-day VWAP), expected to close late 2026 or early 2027 pending regulatory approvals [3]. The deal validates Personalis's NeXT Personal MRD technology as a strategic asset for AI-enabled precision oncology, targeting a $20B MRD market opportunity. Q2 2026 preliminary revenue of $22.4M and 33% quarter-over-quarter clinical test volume growth show improving operational momentum [2]. The combination with Tempus's multimodal data platform and AI capabilities could accelerate commercialization. The clean balance sheet with $233.2M cash and no meaningful debt provides stability during the merger process.
With the Tempus acquisition pending at $16.25/share, the independent investment thesis is effectively replaced by merger arbitrage. The primary question is deal completion risk, not standalone business fundamentals. On the underlying business, challenges remain: Q1 2026 revenue declined 25% YoY to $15.5M, the company loses more than its revenue ($81.3M net loss on $69.6M TTM revenue), and profit margin of -117% shows no path to standalone profitability in the near term [1][6]. CEO Hall and CFO Tachibana sold shares at $13-14 in late June 2026, weeks before the July 20 acquisition announcement at $16.25, raising timing questions [5]. The MRD market is intensely competitive with Natera (Signatera) holding first-mover advantage. If the Tempus deal fails, the stock would likely fall significantly below the $16.25 deal price, exposing shareholders to substantial downside given the -35% ROE and -117% profit margin fundamentals.

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience MODERATE
6
AI Risk Protection (6/9): Personalis's MRD testing requires physical sample processing, wet-lab sequencing, and complex bioinformatics — the physical lab components are structurally protected from AI substitution [4]. Medicare coverage approvals and CLIA/CAP certifications create regulatory moats. The Tempus AI acquisition at $1.5B specifically validates the strategic value of Personalis's genomic data and MRD technology for AI-enabled precision oncology [3]. However, the bioinformatics and variant-calling analysis layer faces competition from AI-native platforms, and larger competitors like Natera and Guardant Health are investing heavily in AI-enhanced algorithms that could erode technical differentiation. AI could lower barriers to entry for computational aspects of cancer genomics. AI Opportunity (0/1): While Personalis uses machine learning in its bioinformatics pipeline and its data is being acquired by Tempus AI specifically for AI integration, the company does not currently generate quantifiable AI-specific revenue from its own operations [4].
