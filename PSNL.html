Company Profile
Healthcare ·Diagnostics & Research ·NASDAQ ·United States
Personalis develops, markets, and sells advanced cancer genomic tests and services. Products include NeXT Personal, a tumor-informed liquid biopsy test for detection of minimal residual disease (MRD) and recurrence monitoring in solid tumors, and ImmunoID NeXT, a tissue-based comprehensive genomic profiling service. Q2 2026 revenue of $22.4M grew 30% YoY with clinical test volume up 199% to 10,384 tests. FY2026 guidance: $78-80M revenue with clinical revenue growing 500%+. Tempus AI has agreed to acquire Personalis in a definitive $1.5B transaction at $16.25 per share, expected to close late 2026 or early 2027. Founded in 2011, headquartered in Fremont, California, with 259 employees.

Investment Thesis
Personalis is being acquired by Tempus AI in a definitive $1.5B transaction at $16.25 per share, expected to close late 2026 or early 2027, making this primarily a merger arbitrage situation [3]. The deal validates Personalis's NeXT Personal MRD technology as a strategic asset for AI-enabled precision oncology, targeting a $20B MRD market opportunity. On the underlying business, Q2 2026 delivered strong results: $22.4M revenue (+30% YoY) with clinical test volume surging 199% to 10,384 tests, and clinical revenue is growing 500%+ annually with Medicare coverage secured for breast and lung cancer surveillance [1]. FY2026 guidance of $78-80M revenue with 43K-45K clinical tests shows the VA-to-clinical pivot is succeeding. D/E of only 16.2% reflects a clean balance sheet. The Inspire Impact Score of 16 (MIXED) and 2 positive attributions (Customer Welfare, Product Access and Affordability) provide neutral faith-based positioning.
The bear case centers on deal completion risk and fundamentally unprofitable standalone operations. At $16.24 (essentially at the $16.25 deal price), there is negligible upside and significant downside if the Tempus acquisition fails — the stock would likely fall sharply given ROE of -50.3%, profit margin of -153.8%, and forward EPS of -$0.94 [6]. The company projects a $105M net loss and $100M cash usage in 2026, meaning it burns more cash annually than its current revenue base. Even with strong clinical growth (199% volume increase), the absolute revenue of $78-80M is small relative to the $1.73B market cap, implying a 22x revenue multiple that only makes sense in the context of the Tempus acquisition premium [1]. Natera's Signatera holds first-mover advantage in MRD testing with broader cancer type coverage and stronger brand recognition among oncologists. CEO and CFO insider selling at $13-14 in late June 2026, weeks before the July 20 acquisition announcement at $16.25, raises corporate governance timing questions [5]. The 5-year revenue CAGR of only 1.4% reflects the difficult transition from VA contracts to clinical diagnostics.

AI Resilience MODERATE

AI Risk Protection (6/9): Personalis's MRD testing requires physical sample processing, wet-lab sequencing, and complex bioinformatics — the physical lab components are structurally protected from AI substitution [4]. Medicare coverage approvals and CLIA/CAP certifications create regulatory moats. The Tempus AI acquisition at $1.5B specifically validates the strategic value of Personalis's genomic data and MRD technology for AI-enabled precision oncology [3]. However, the bioinformatics and variant-calling analysis layer faces competition from AI-native platforms, and larger competitors like Natera and Guardant Health are investing heavily in AI-enhanced algorithms that could erode technical differentiation. AI could lower barriers to entry for computational aspects of cancer genomics while physical sequencing remains protected. AI Opportunity (0/1): While Personalis uses machine learning in its bioinformatics pipeline and its data is being acquired by Tempus AI specifically for AI integration, the company does not currently generate quantifiable AI-specific revenue from its own operations.
