PSQ Holdings operates PublicSquare, a values-aligned fintech platform offering payments processing, buy-now-pay-later solutions via Credova, and credit products for underserved industries. Originally launched as a conservative-aligned online marketplace, the company has pivoted entirely to financial infrastructure and credit services, with publicsquare.com now redirecting to credova.com. The company appointed Dusty Wunderlich (former Credova CEO) as CEO in January 2026 following founder Michael Seifert's departure. Market cap approximately $12.9 million.
