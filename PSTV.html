Plus Therapeutics (rebranding as Cerenome, ticker CNSY effective August 3, 2026) is a clinical-stage pharmaceutical company focused on developing targeted radiotherapeutics for central nervous system cancers. Lead candidate REYOBIQ (rhenium Re186 obisbemeda) is in NIH-supported ReSPECT clinical trials for recurrent glioblastoma, leptomeningeal metastases, and pediatric brain cancer. The CNSide cerebrospinal fluid diagnostic platform has expanded to approximately 126 million covered lives. Q1 2026 revenue was $1.0M with a $6.9M net loss. Market cap is approximately $22M.
