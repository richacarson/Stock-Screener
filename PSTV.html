Plus Therapeutics (rebranding as Cerenome, ticker CNSY effective August 3, 2026) is a clinical-stage pharmaceutical company developing targeted radiotherapeutics for central nervous system cancers. Lead candidate REYOBIQ (rhenium Re186 obisbemeda) is in NIH-supported ReSPECT clinical trials for recurrent glioblastoma, leptomeningeal metastases, and pediatric brain cancer. The CNSide cerebrospinal fluid diagnostic platform has expanded to approximately 126 million covered lives. The company executed a 1-for-25 reverse stock split in April 2026 to maintain NASDAQ listing compliance. Q1 2026 revenue was $1.0M with significant operating losses. Market cap is approximately $22M.
