Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NASDAQ ·United States
PTC Inc. is an industrial software company providing Windchill (product lifecycle management), Creo (CAD), Onshape (cloud-native CAD), and Codebeamer (application lifecycle management) to manufacturers across the Americas, Europe, and Asia Pacific. Under CEO Neil Barua (since February 2024), PTC is executing an 'Intelligent Product Data Foundation' strategy that integrates these platforms to enable AI capabilities across connected product-development data. Q1 FY2026 revenue grew 21% YoY to $685.8M and Q2 FY2026 surged 22% to $774.3M, with FY2026 revenue guided at $2.69-2.75B [1][2]. The company divested Kepware and ThingWorx IoT businesses to sharpen focus on core PLM/CAD/ALM platforms.

Investment Thesis
PTC is delivering strong execution on its 'Intelligent Product Data Foundation' strategy, with Q1 FY2026 revenue up 21% to $685.8M and Q2 up 22% to $774.3M, supported by FY2026 guidance of $2.69-2.75B in revenue and raised ARR growth guidance of ~9.25% [1][2]. The balance sheet is excellent (D/E 46%, ROE 35%, profit margin 41%) and the stock trades at just 15.6x forward earnings — a compelling valuation for a high-quality industrial software franchise. CEO Barua's decision to divest Kepware/ThingWorx to refocus on core PLM/CAD/ALM, with early AI monetization visible in ServiceMax and Onshape Labs, supports a durable, purpose-aligned growth story [3][4].
Near-term noise from the divestitures creates headline risk — yfinance shows -6.8% revenue growth on trailing comparisons that include divested revenue in the prior period, and EPS comparisons are distorted by one-time divestiture gains (Q2 FY2026 GAAP EPS of $5.00 includes a gain) [2]. Forward PEG of approximately 1.0 suggests the market has largely priced in mid-teens growth, leaving limited room for further multiple expansion. PTC's Inspire Impact Score of -49 (MISALIGNED, driven by LGBT Activism attribution) limits fit for faith-aligned mandates despite 19 positive ESG-related attributions. Competitive intensity from Siemens Teamcenter and Dassault Systemes remains a structural overhang on PLM market share.
Key Catalysts
  1. Full-year FY2026 ARR growth tracking above the raised 9.25% guidance as divested-asset noise rolls off and organic subscription momentum continues
  2. AI monetization acceleration across Onshape Labs (generative design, AI-driven engineering workflows) and ServiceMax (field service AI) — concrete revenue-generating features shipping to customers [3][4]
  3. Post-divestiture focus on the unified Intelligent Product Data Foundation should improve cross-sell opportunities across Windchill, Creo, and Codebeamer customers
Key Risks
  1. Divestiture of Kepware/ThingWorx creates revenue comparison headwinds — yfinance trailing growth shows -6.8%, which may concern investors not parsing the organic vs reported distinction
  2. Q2 FY2026 GAAP EPS of $5.00 includes divestiture gains, inflating profit margin to 41.4% — normalized margins are closer to 27-30%, and one-time gains mask underlying profitability trends [2]
  3. Intense competition from Siemens Teamcenter and Dassault Systemes in the core PLM market, with no evidence PTC is gaining share despite strong product execution
