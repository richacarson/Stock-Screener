Company Profile
Consumer Cyclical ·Leisure ·NASDAQ ·United States
Peloton Interactive provides connected fitness products and subscription services including the Bike, Bike+ Pro, Tread, Tread+ Pro, Row+ Pro, and Precor commercial equipment. The company generates recurring revenue through its All-Access membership ($44/month) providing live and on-demand fitness classes. FY2026 (ended June 2026) delivered $2,446M revenue (-1.8% YoY) and the company's first-ever full-year net income of approximately $63M compared to a -$119M net loss in FY2025. Q3 FY2026 revenue reached $631M (+4% QoQ) with Adjusted EBITDA of $126M (+41% YoY). CEO Peter Stern (ex-Apple/Ford) appointed January 2025. Market cap ~$2.2B.
2,199 Employees ·www.onepeloton.com

Investment Thesis
Peloton achieved its first-ever profitable full year in FY2026, generating approximately $63M net income and $354M free cash flow after years of catastrophic losses [1]. CEO Peter Stern has stabilized operations — Adjusted EBITDA grew 41% YoY in Q3 FY2026, margins are improving, and the Pro hardware tier (Bike+ Pro, Tread+ Pro, Row+ Pro) drives higher ASPs. The stock at $4.92 and ~$2.2B market cap trades at 0.9x TTM revenue, pricing in significant downside that may already be reflected. The forward P/E of 20.8x for a company generating $354M free cash flow on $2.4B revenue suggests upside if profitability is sustained. Peloton IQ AI features differentiate the subscription from free alternatives. The 2.8M+ paid connected fitness subscribers provide a recurring revenue base that generates high-margin subscription income [1][4].
The bear case is severe. Revenue has declined 39% from the $4.0B FY2021 peak to $2.4B in FY2026 with no evidence of growth resumption — FY2026 revenue was essentially flat YoY (-1.8%), and the 5-year CAGR is -9.5% [1][4]. Peloton has negative stockholders' equity — $1.71B total debt exceeds total assets minus liabilities — making D/E and ROE undefined and the balance sheet structurally impaired [4]. The 2.58% profit margin on $2.4B revenue generates only $63M net income, which could easily reverse with modest revenue declines or cost increases. Paid connected fitness subscribers are declining, threatening the recurring revenue model. Organizational trust is shattered: 74% workforce reduction, 43% CEO approval on Glassdoor, and culture described as 'degraded immensely' [2]. AI-powered fitness apps threaten the $44/month subscription value proposition with free or near-free alternatives. Insider selling (69 sales, zero purchases) at already-depressed levels signals that even management sees limited upside [4]. Inspire Impact Score of -49 (MISALIGNED) creates faith-alignment concerns.

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience MODERATE
7
AI Risk Protection (6/9): Peloton's core business involves physical fitness hardware (bikes, treads, rowers) paired with live instructor-led content and a social community layer — elements that AI cannot fully replicate [2]. The physical product requires manufacturing, distribution, and in-home installation that are structurally immune to AI disruption. The human instructor relationships, live class energy, and social leaderboard community create switching costs that AI fitness apps cannot easily substitute. However, Peloton's $44/month subscription faces growing pressure from AI-powered fitness alternatives: Apple Fitness+ with AI coaching, free AI workout generators, and ChatGPT-style personalized fitness planning that can replicate the content value proposition at zero marginal cost [2]. The subscription model, which generates the majority of gross profit, is the most AI-exposed component. Analysts believe a 'compelling lane' exists for Peloton within connected fitness, but the AI threat to the content premium is real and growing. AI Opportunity (1/1): Peloton IQ is a shipping AI product — an AI-assisted personal trainer providing form feedback, rep counting, and personalized workout recommendations to paying subscribers [2]. CTO Francis Shanahan is leading the AI integration strategy, making AI a revenue-enhancing feature of the existing subscription.
