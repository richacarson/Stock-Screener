Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas Equipment & Services ·NYSE ·United States
ProPetro Holding Corp. is a Midland, Texas-based oilfield services company providing pressure pumping and other complementary services to upstream oil and gas companies in the Permian Basin. The company operates hydraulic fracturing fleets and has expanded into power generation through its PROPWR business, with a strategic Caterpillar agreement for up to 2.6 GW of power generation capacity by 2032.

Investment Thesis
ProPetro's PROPWR power generation business represents a genuine strategic pivot that could transform the company from a cyclical pressure pumper into a diversified energy services platform. The Caterpillar agreement for up to 2.6 GW of capacity by 2032 provides long-term revenue visibility, and the shift from diesel to electric operations aligns with industry electrification trends [3]. The stock trades at a 52-week low with a $794 million market cap, and the 11-analyst consensus Buy rating with a $18.36 target implies 52% upside. If PROPWR scales successfully while frac activity stabilizes, the company could return to profitability and re-rate significantly.
The bear case is severe: Q1 2026 revenue declined 24.7% year-over-year to $271 million with a net loss of $4 million, and adjusted EBITDA margin compressed to just 13% from 18% the prior quarter [1]. Planned capital expenditures of $540-610 million in 2026 far exceed cash generation, requiring debt or equity financing that risks further dilution. The Permian Basin pressure pumping market faces structural overcapacity, and Exxon/Pioneer's complete divestiture of its 16.6 million share stake at $16.66 removes a key strategic sponsor [4]. The PROPWR buildout is capital-intensive with multi-year payback periods, and any sustained weakness in oil prices would delay the investment thesis.
Key Catalysts
  1. PROPWR power generation business scaling through major contracts and Caterpillar partnership, with potential for the segment to become a meaningful revenue contributor by 2027-2028
  2. Permian Basin frac activity stabilization or recovery driven by oil price improvements or accelerated well completions by major E&P operators
  3. Successful diesel-to-electric fleet conversion reducing operating costs and positioning ProPetro for ESG-conscious E&P customers willing to pay a premium for lower-emission completions
Key Risks
  1. Permian Basin pressure pumping overcapacity with Q1 2026 revenue down 24.7% YoY and adjusted EBITDA margin at only 13% — further deterioration could push the company into sustained losses and force fleet stacking
  2. Capital expenditure plan of $540-610 million in 2026 against Q1 operating cash flow of only $3 million creates significant funding gap requiring additional debt or dilutive equity raises
  3. Exxon/Pioneer's complete divestiture of its ProPetro stake removes a key strategic sponsor and signals skepticism from the company's most informed institutional holder
