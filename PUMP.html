Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas Equipment & Services ·NYSE ·United States
ProPetro is an integrated energy service company based in Midland, Texas, providing hydraulic fracturing, wireline, and complementary energy and power generation services to upstream E&P companies in the Permian Basin. FY2025 revenue of $1.27B declined 12% YoY as U.S. completion activity contracted, with net income collapsing to $1M ($0.01/share) from a net loss of $138M in FY2024. The company is transitioning to electric-powered FORCE fleets through its PROPWR initiative, expected to deliver meaningful earnings in H2 2026. Market cap approximately $1.6B with 122.6M shares outstanding.
1,720 Employees ·www.propetroservices.com

Investment Thesis
ProPetro at $12.80 offers a levered bet on Permian Basin completion activity recovery backed by the cleanest balance sheet in oilfield services — D/E of just 19% versus peers at 50-100%+ [4]. The PROPWR electric fleet initiative is the company's primary earnings catalyst, with management guiding to positive and increasingly meaningful contributions in H2 2026 [1]. At a $1.6B market cap and analyst consensus 'Buy' with a $17.32 average price target (35% upside), the stock prices in significant recovery potential. The 5-year revenue CAGR of 10% demonstrates the company's ability to scale during upcycles, and the Permian Basin's status as the highest-return U.S. oil-producing region provides structural demand support. AI-driven data center power demand could create a secular tailwind for natural gas completions, benefiting Permian-focused service companies.
The bear case is severe and immediate. FY2025 revenue declined 12% to $1.27B with net income of just $1M, and Q1 2026 revenue fell another 24.7% YoY to $270.7M as U.S. completion activity remains depressed [1]. ProPetro is a single-basin operator (Permian) with zero geographic diversification, making it entirely dependent on Permian activity levels. The company generates no meaningful earnings, with analysts forecasting -$0.06 EPS for FY2026, rendering forward P/E incalculable [4]. The exit of Pioneer/Exxon (16.6M shares sold at $16.66) removes a strategic anchor shareholder and signals the largest E&P in the Permian sees limited value in maintaining its ProPetro position [3]. The stock's 52-week range of $4.51-$18.50 reflects extreme volatility typical of a commodity cyclical with near-zero earnings power. Innovation and Inspiration scores of 3 and 2 respectively reflect a company without a distinctive purpose or track record of product-level innovation beyond fleet electrification.
