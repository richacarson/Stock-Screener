Q/C Technologies, Inc. (formerly TNF Pharmaceuticals, formerly MyMD Pharmaceuticals) is a micro-cap shell company that pivoted from clinical-stage pharmaceuticals to quantum-class photonic computing in September 2025. Through an exclusive global licensing agreement with Israel's LightSolver Ltd., the company markets the qc-LPU100 laser processing unit for cryptocurrency and blockchain compute applications while retaining a dormant Isomyosamine autoimmune-disease pharmaceutical asset from its prior identity. With 3 employees, zero product revenue, and a market cap of roughly $7M, Martin Shkreli was appointed Strategic Advisor to the company in 2025.

Investment Thesis
Q/C Technologies offers pure speculative optionality on an unproven laser-based computing accelerator for cryptocurrency infrastructure through its exclusive global license to LightSolver's LPU technology, branded qc-LPU100 [1]. LightSolver's own roadmap targets scaling to 100,000 variables by 2027 and 1,000,000 by 2029, and any independently validated benchmark showing genuine advantages over GPU-based compute could produce outsized percentage moves given the roughly $7M market cap and $0.87 share price. The company carries almost no debt ($371K) and roughly $11M in cash, providing modest runway, and the retained Isomyosamine autoimmune-disease pharmaceutical asset offers a small option value should the quantum-computing pivot fail [2].
The bear case dominates. Q/C Technologies is a 3-employee shell with zero product revenue in either its pharmaceutical or computing identity, a TTM net loss of approximately $16.6M, and an ROE near -108% [2]. The stock has collapsed 89% from its post-reverse-split 52-week high of $8.17 to $0.87, and short interest of roughly 106% of float signals aggressive dilutive-financing dynamics typical of toxic convertible structures. The appointment of Martin Shkreli -- convicted of securities fraud and permanently barred from the securities industry -- as a paid Strategic Advisor is a serious governance red flag regardless of the underlying technology's merit [3]. With two complete business-model pivots in roughly 14 months and no independently validated technology, there is no credible basis for a near-term revenue or earnings inflection, and further dilutive capital raises appear likely to fund operations [4].
Key Catalysts
  1. Independent third-party benchmarking of the qc-LPU100 laser processing unit against GPU-based compute for blockchain/crypto workloads could establish technical credibility currently absent from the story [1]
  2. A first commercial deployment or pilot agreement with a cryptocurrency mining or compute customer would provide the company's first proof-of-concept revenue
  3. LightSolver's published roadmap to scale the underlying LPU architecture to 100,000 variables by 2027 could de-risk the technology's commercial viability if milestones are met [1]
Key Risks
  1. Zero product revenue against a TTM net loss of approximately $16.6M and roughly $11M in cash creates a limited runway that will likely require further dilutive capital raises, and short interest of roughly 106% of float signals market skepticism about the financing structure [2][4]
  2. The qc-LPU100's 'quantum-class' performance claims remain unvalidated by independent benchmarks or peer-reviewed research, and the underlying technology is exclusively licensed from a third party (LightSolver) rather than owned outright [1]
  3. Governance risk from the appointment of Martin Shkreli -- convicted of securities fraud -- as Strategic Advisor, combined with two complete business-model pivots in roughly 14 months, signals a promotional, trend-chasing operating pattern rather than a durable business [3]
