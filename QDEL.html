Company Profile
Healthcare ·Diagnostics & Research ·NASDAQ ·United States
QuidelOrtho Corporation develops, manufactures, and markets diagnostic solutions for healthcare providers worldwide. Formed from the 2022 merger of Quidel Corporation and Ortho Clinical Diagnostics, the company operates across Labs, Point of Care, Immunohematology, Molecular Diagnostics, and Donor Screening segments. Products include immunoassays, blood typing, molecular tests, and rapid point-of-care diagnostics. Market cap approximately $1.1B.

Investment Thesis
QuidelOrtho offers deep value at 8.97x forward earnings on FY2026 adjusted EPS guidance of $1.80-2.00, with the stock trading near its 52-week low of $9.92 at $16.55 [5]. The adjusted EBITDA franchise is real: $615-630M guided for FY2026 on $2.70-2.75B revenue implies a 23% adjusted EBITDA margin [1]. The Immunohematology segment is a bright spot with 8% YoY revenue growth and sticky hospital blood bank relationships. Free cash flow guidance of $100-120M for FY2026 provides some balance sheet repair capacity against $2.46B in long-term debt. If the company stabilizes post-merger integration and respiratory testing volumes normalize, there is meaningful upside from multiple expansion.
The bear case is severe: $1.21B TTM net loss, negative ROE of -50%, D/E of 155%, and an Altman Z-Score of 0.61 that flags genuine bankruptcy risk [5]. Q1 2026 revenue declined 10.5% YoY with Point of Care down 34% on weak respiratory season and China IVD pricing pressures threatening the Labs segment, which represents 57% of revenue [1]. Guidance was lowered across all metrics — revenue, EBITDA, and EPS — suggesting management lacks visibility into recovery timing. The $2.46B debt load against only $140M cash and negative operating cash flow in Q1 creates liquidity risk if revenue continues declining [1]. Glassdoor reviews (2.8/5) indicate organizational dysfunction that could impair the company's ability to execute on R&D and commercial strategies [2]. The Donor Screening business is winding down by mid-2026, removing another revenue stream. At $1.1B market cap, the stock is pricing in meaningful probability of further deterioration or restructuring.
