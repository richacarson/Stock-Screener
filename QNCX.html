Company Profile

Quince Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biotechnology company focused on acquiring, developing, and commercializing therapeutics for patients with debilitating and rare diseases. Following the failure of its Phase 3 NEAT trial for eDSP in ataxia-telangiectasia in January 2026, Quince pivoted by acquiring Orphai Therapeutics with a $150M private placement to fund LAM-001, an inhaled mTOR inhibitor targeting rare lung diseases including PH-ILD. Market cap is approximately $32M with no commercial revenue and 38 employees.

Investment Thesis
Quince Therapeutics at $32.92 is a speculative micro-cap biotech ($32M market cap) that has pivoted from clinical failure to a new pipeline. The Orphai Therapeutics acquisition brought LAM-001, an inhaled mTOR inhibitor showing promising Phase 2a data in PH-ILD, with Oppenheimer initiating coverage at an Outperform rating and $82 price target — implying 150%+ upside if clinical milestones are achieved [3]. The $150M private placement provides capital runway to advance LAM-001 through key milestones expected through 2028. The rare lung disease focus addresses underserved patient populations with potential orphan drug designation benefits.
The bear case is severe. The stock is dangerously illiquid — daily dollar volume of approximately $60K (1,814 shares at $32.92) means institutional investors cannot meaningfully participate, and any selling pressure could cause dramatic price declines. Market cap of $32M with 38 employees and a single early-stage asset creates binary risk: LAM-001 either advances successfully or the company faces a repeat of the eDSP outcome.
