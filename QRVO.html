Company Profile
Technology ·Semiconductors ·NASDAQ ·United States
Qorvo, Inc. develops and commercializes technologies and products for wireless, wired, and power markets worldwide. Operating through three segments — High Performance Analog (HPA), Connectivity and Sensors Group (CSG), and Advanced Cellular Group (ACG) — the company supplies RF solutions, power management, UWB, Wi-Fi, and MEMS-based sensors for smartphones, defense/aerospace, 5G/6G infrastructure, automotive, smart home, and industrial applications. Founded in 1957 and headquartered in Greensboro, North Carolina.

Investment Thesis
Qorvo's margin transformation story is gaining traction: FY2026 non-GAAP gross margin expanded 370bps YoY, and FY2027 Q1 delivered 51.1% GAAP gross margin — the highest in the company's history — driven by the strategic shift from low-margin smartphone RF to higher-margin HPA and defense applications [1][2]. At a Forward P/E of 13.61 and PEG of 0.95, the stock is attractively valued relative to semiconductor peers, suggesting the market has not fully priced in the margin improvement trajectory. The company's GaN technology leadership in defense radar and communications provides a differentiated, high-barrier franchise with growing government spending tailwinds, and FY2027 outlook targets EPS approaching $7.00 with non-GAAP gross margin above 50% [2].
The bear case centers on structural revenue decline: FY2026 full-year revenue of $3.68B declined 1.09% YoY, and FY2027 guidance calls for a mid-single-digit decline further [2]. The 5-year revenue CAGR of -1.8% reflects a company that has shrunk from its $4.65B peak and cannot offset smartphone RF content losses with HPA growth. FY2027 Q1 revenue of $784.8M was lower than FY2026 Q1's $818.8M, demonstrating the top-line decline continues quarter over quarter [2]. Customer concentration remains a risk — lost Apple RF content drove the strategic pivot, and further OEM losses could accelerate the revenue contraction. Net insider selling with zero purchases across 12 transactions in the past year raises questions about management conviction [5]. The Glassdoor rating of 3.5/5 and workforce reduction from 8,600 to 6,200 suggest organizational stress during the restructuring [3].
Key Catalysts
  1. HPA segment growing double digits in FY2027 could become the dominant revenue driver, accelerating the mix shift toward 50%+ gross margins and validating the multi-year strategic transformation [2]
  2. Defense and aerospace spending increases globally, expanding the addressable market for Qorvo's differentiated GaN-based radar and communications solutions [1]
  3. Forward PEG of 0.95 below 1.0 suggests meaningful value upside if FY2027 earnings growth materializes at the guided EPS approaching $7.00, nearly doubling from trailing EPS of $3.54 [2]
Key Risks
  1. FY2027 revenue guided for mid-single-digit decline signals the smartphone RF business is contracting faster than HPA growth can offset, risking a prolonged revenue contraction from the $4.65B peak [2]
  2. Customer concentration and competitive displacement — further smartphone OEM content losses could accelerate revenue decline, while Skyworks, Broadcom, and Qualcomm invest aggressively in adjacent markets [1]
  3. Net insider selling with 12 sell transactions and zero purchases in the past year indicates management may lack near-term conviction despite the margin improvement narrative [5]

AI Resilience MODERATE

AI Risk Protection (7/9): Qorvo's core business — designing and manufacturing RF semiconductor components — involves physical fabrication, proprietary GaN process technology, and deep analog engineering expertise that AI cannot replicate or substitute [1]. RF filter design and power amplifier manufacturing require specialized semiconductor fabs and decades of customer qualification cycles. AI data centers and edge computing may increase demand for RF connectivity components. However, AI-driven chip design tools could reduce engineering differentiation over time, and the smartphone RF market faces ongoing commoditization pressure from well-capitalized competitors. AI Opportunity (0/1): No quantifiable AI-specific revenue. Qorvo's RF components enable connectivity infrastructure that supports AI workloads, but the company does not sell AI products directly or report AI-driven revenue contributions [2].
