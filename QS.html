Company Profile
Consumer Cyclical ·Auto Parts ·NASDAQ ·United States
QuantumScape Corporation develops solid-state lithium-metal battery technology for electric vehicles and emerging applications including AI data center power. Founded in 2010, the company remains pre-commercial-revenue after 16 years of development. Its proprietary ceramic solid-state separator replaces conventional liquid electrolytes in lithium-ion batteries, promising higher energy density, faster charging, and improved safety. The QSE-5 B-sample cell (Raptor) is in pilot production. VW's PowerCo is the anchor OEM partner. Q2 2026 net loss was $98.2M with EPS of -$0.16 (beating -$0.18 estimate). Customer billings in FY2025 totaled $19.5M. Cash and investments of approximately $859M with minimal debt ($67M). Adjusted EBITDA guidance of -$250M to -$275M for FY2026. Market cap ~$2.8B.
700 Employees ·www.quantumscape.com

Investment Thesis
QuantumScape represents a high-conviction bet on the commercialization of solid-state lithium-metal batteries — potentially the most transformative energy storage technology in development. The company's ceramic solid-state separator promises 80% higher energy density, 15-minute fast charging, improved safety (no liquid electrolyte fire risk), and longer cycle life versus conventional lithium-ion [1][2]. VW's PowerCo partnership provides OEM validation and a path to automotive-scale manufacturing. The QSE-5 B-sample cell (Raptor) in pilot production represents a concrete milestone toward commercialization. The emerging AI data center power opportunity (800V DC systems) expands the addressable market beyond EVs [2]. The balance sheet is strong: $859M cash against only $67M debt, with D/E of 6.4%, providing 3+ years of runway at current burn rates [4]. Q2 2026 EPS of -$0.16 beat the -$0.18 consensus, and customer billings of $19.5M in FY2025 signal early customer engagement. Inspire Impact Score of 49 (ALIGNED) supports faith-based allocation.
The bear case is existential. QuantumScape has spent 16 years and approximately $3B+ in cumulative investment without generating commercial product revenue — FY2025 reported $0 in product revenue, and the company guides for $250-275M in adjusted EBITDA losses for FY2026 [1][4]. At $4.57/share and $2.8B market cap, investors are paying for technology that has never been manufactured at scale, in an industry where Toyota, CATL, and Samsung SDI are pursuing competing approaches with vastly larger manufacturing capabilities. Cash runway of approximately 3-3.5 years at current burn ($859M cash, ~$250M annual burn) creates a ticking clock — if commercial production is not achieved before capital runs out, dilutive equity raises or partnership transactions become necessary [4]. Insider selling is overwhelming: 70 sales versus zero purchases, signaling that the people closest to the technology are reducing their exposure [4]. Glassdoor reviews citing 'toxic management' and 'poor safety culture' raise execution risk in a business where manufacturing precision is everything [2]. The AI data center power narrative is speculative — no orders, no prototypes demonstrated for this application, and the pivot may be narrative management rather than genuine market opportunity. Commercial solid-state battery timelines have been repeatedly deferred across the industry.

AI Resilience LOW RISK

AI Risk Protection (8/9): QuantumScape's core business — developing and manufacturing solid-state lithium-metal batteries — involves physical materials science, electrochemistry, and precision manufacturing that AI structurally cannot replicate or displace [2]. Battery production requires physical-world operations: ceramic separator fabrication, cell assembly, and quality testing that exist entirely outside the digital domain. AI cannot substitute for a physical battery. Furthermore, the AI boom is creating new demand vectors for QuantumScape's technology — the company has pivoted its narrative to include AI data center power applications (800V DC batteries for high-density computing), framing solid-state batteries as AI infrastructure rather than purely an EV play [2]. AI data centers require massive energy storage for reliability and power density, potentially opening a large addressable market beyond the original EV thesis. AI is also being used internally to optimize QS's own manufacturing processes, accelerating the path to commercial production [2]. The only risk AI poses to QuantumScape is competitive: AI-driven materials discovery could help rival battery companies (Toyota, CATL, Samsung SDI) accelerate their own solid-state programs. AI Opportunity (0/1): QuantumScape does not generate meaningful revenue from AI products or services. Customer billings of $19.5M represent development-stage samples, not AI revenue. The AI data center power opportunity is speculative and has not generated orders [1].
