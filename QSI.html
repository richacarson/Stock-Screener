Company Profile
Healthcare ·Medical Devices ·NASDAQ ·United States
Quantum-Si develops proprietary single-molecule detection technology for next-generation protein sequencing (NGPS). The company offers Platinum and Platinum Pro instruments, library preparation kits, sequencing kits, and cloud-based AI-powered analysis software for proteomic research, drug discovery, and diagnostics. Founded by Jonathan Rothberg in 2013, the company is preparing its next-generation Proteus platform for launch in Q2 2027, targeting human and non-human proteomics markets exceeding $4B annually.
120 Employees ·www.quantum-si.com

Investment Thesis
Quantum-Si is the pioneer in commercial single-molecule protein sequencing, a potentially transformative technology for proteomics research, drug discovery, and diagnostics [4]. The Proteus platform targets Q2 2027 launch with an identified $4B+ non-human proteomics market opportunity in agricultural, pathogen, and viral surveillance applications [1]. With $111.7M cash providing runway to approximately late 2027, the company has near-term financial stability to execute the Proteus launch without immediate dilution [5]. At $170.7M market cap — roughly 1.5x cash — the market assigns minimal value to the technology and pipeline, meaning any positive Proteus adoption data could trigger significant re-rating. The NVIDIA collaboration for AI-accelerated data processing adds credibility, and founder Rothberg's track record with Ion Torrent (acquired for $725M) provides pedigree in life sciences platform development [4].
The bear case is formidable for a company that has consumed over $500M in capital with $1M in annual revenue. Q2 2026 revenue of $344K (down 42% YoY) and full-year guidance of $1M (down from $3.05M in FY2024) means the company is moving backwards commercially, even if the decline is 'intentional' for the Proteus transition [1]. The Proteus launch delay from end-2026 to Q2 2027 extends the cash burn timeline, and cash declined from $190M to $112M — providing approximately 14 months of runway at current spending of $98M annually. The 20% workforce reduction signals financial distress [1]. The -49% ROE, -6,118% profit margin (estimated), and deeply negative cash flow paint a picture of a company that has not proven product-market fit after 13 years. Proteus at its target price faces a challenging capital equipment environment with NIH funding uncertainty and tariff headwinds. Only ~120 employees after the RIF limits commercial reach, and the stock has declined 75% from its 52-week high of $3.10 to $0.78 [5].
Key Catalysts
  1. Proteus platform launch in Q2 2027 with expanded capability for both human and non-human proteomics — first installations and adoption data could validate the platform and trigger re-rating from near-cash valuation [1]
  2. $4B+ non-human proteomics market opportunity in agricultural, pathogen detection, and viral surveillance applications broadens the addressable market beyond academic research budgets [1]

Key Risks

  2. 20% workforce reduction and $112M declining cash position signal a company in survival mode — if Proteus fails to generate commercial traction by mid-2027, the company faces existential funding risk [1][5]
  3. Unproven market — next-generation protein sequencing has not demonstrated market demand comparable to genomic sequencing, and mass spectrometry incumbents (Thermo Fisher, Bruker) continue advancing competitive approaches

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
8
AI Risk Protection (7/9): Quantum-Si operates at the intersection of semiconductor engineering, molecular biology, and precision instrumentation — developing physical hardware with proprietary chip-based single-molecule photon detection that AI cannot replicate or substitute [4]. The competitive moat is in the physical science of detecting individual protein molecules on a semiconductor chip, not in software. AI is an enabler — the NVIDIA collaboration and AI-powered analysis software strengthen rather than threaten the platform. Regulatory requirements for medical device instrumentation add additional protection. The primary AI-adjacent risk is computational protein prediction (AlphaFold-style models) reducing some experimental sequencing demand, but single-molecule data provides unique information that computational models cannot generate. AI Opportunity (1/1): The company actively generates value from AI through its cloud-based analysis software for automated AI-driven sequencing data interpretation and the NVIDIA collaboration for accelerated protein sequencing data processing [4].
