Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NCM ·United States
Q32 Bio Inc. is a clinical-stage biotechnology company developing novel biologics to restore healthy immune balance in patients with alopecia areata and other autoimmune and inflammatory diseases. Lead candidate bempikibart (ADX-914) is a fully human anti-IL-7 receptor alpha monoclonal antibody that completed Phase 2b with positive SIGNAL-AA trial results, with a registration-directed program expected in H1 2027. The company also develops ADX-096, a C3d mAb-CR1 fusion protein for ophthalmologic indications. Q32 Bio funded operations through a $200M offering in July 2026 and the November 2025 sale of ADX-097 to Horizon Therapeutics. Cash of $106.3M at June 30, 2026 plus the $200M offering provides runway through Phase 3 topline results.
22 Employees ·www.q32bio.com
