Company Profile
Technology ·Scientific & Technical Instruments ·NYSE ·United States
Ralliant Corporation is a global precision technologies company that designs, develops, manufactures, and services precision instruments and highly engineered products. The company operates through two segments: Test & Measurement (Tektronix oscilloscopes, Keithley source measure units, EA Elektro-Automatik power supplies) and Sensors & Safety Systems (Qualitrol grid monitoring, Gems Sensors, Pacific Scientific defense and space products). Spun off from Fortive Corporation in June 2025, headquartered in Raleigh, North Carolina, with over 150 years of operating heritage.

Investment Thesis
Ralliant delivered a strong Q1 2026 with $535 million in revenue (up 11% YoY, 9% organic), prompting management to raise full-year guidance to $2.185-2.245 billion in revenue and $2.53-$2.69 in adjusted EPS [1]. The company is strategically positioned in high-growth end markets — data center infrastructure testing, physical AI applications, critical defense programs, and power grid resilience — where precision measurement and sensing are essential and demand is accelerating. The balance sheet is conservatively leveraged at 39% D/E, the Enterprise Productivity Program targets $50-60 million in annualized savings by 2028, and the expanded $500 million share repurchase program demonstrates capital allocation discipline [1]. With 150+ years of brand heritage through Tektronix and strong category leadership in test and measurement, Ralliant has deep customer trust and technical moats in mission-critical applications.
The primary concern is valuation and profitability transition. At approximately 26x forward adjusted earnings, Ralliant trades at a premium for an industrial company with 8.3% net profit margins [1][5]. The first year of independence brought significant cost headwinds — $14.7 million in quarterly interest expense, higher standalone compensation costs, and incremental public company expenses — that compressed net margins by 500 basis points versus the prior year period [1]. ROE of 7.9% is well below the 15% target, reflecting the newly established capital structure [4]. The stock has rallied 47% from its $46.58 level in April 2026, pricing in much of the growth story already. Revenue growth 5-year CAGR is unavailable given the company's one-year track record as an independent entity, and investors must take on execution risk that management can simultaneously drive organic growth, realize productivity savings, and expand margins while building a new corporate culture from scratch.
Key Catalysts
  1. Data center and AI infrastructure investment cycle driving sustained demand for Tektronix test and measurement platforms as hyperscalers expand capacity through 2027-2028 [1][2]
  2. Enterprise Productivity Program delivering $50-60 million in annualized run-rate savings by 2028, potentially driving adjusted EBITDA margins above the 20% target range [1]
  3. Defense and power grid resilience spending increases providing diversified, counter-cyclical demand growth for the Sensors & Safety Systems segment [2]
Key Risks
  1. At approximately 26x forward adjusted earnings with 8.3% net margins and a 47% stock price rally since April, valuation leaves minimal margin of safety — any deceleration from the 11% revenue growth rate could trigger meaningful multiple compression [1][5]
  2. First-year standalone costs have compressed margins by 500bps, and the path to margin recovery depends on the Enterprise Productivity Program hitting ambitious $50-60M savings targets that have not yet been demonstrated [1]
  3. Limited operating history as an independent company means investors cannot assess management's ability to navigate an economic downturn or cyclical decline in capital spending across key end markets [3]
