Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Rani Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biotherapeutics company developing the RaniPill, a robotic oral-delivery capsule designed to deliver biologics (peptides, proteins, antibodies, oligonucleotides) that today require injection. The platform aims to give patients and healthcare providers an oral alternative to injectable medications, with the current lead program (RT-114, an oral GLP-1/GLP-2 dual agonist) in Phase 1 development with partner ProGen.
