Company Profile
Industrials ·Specialty Business Services ·NYSE ·Canada
RB Global, Inc. (formerly Ritchie Bros. Auctioneers) operates the world's largest commercial asset marketplace, facilitating the buying and selling of heavy equipment, trucks, and other industrial assets through live and online auctions. Following its transformative 2023 merger with IAA (Insurance Auto Auctions), the company expanded into the insurance salvage vehicle market. RB Global also operates SmartEquip (parts procurement) and Rouse Services (data analytics). Headquartered in Burnaby, British Columbia, Canada.

Investment Thesis
RB Global delivered a strong Q1 2026 with revenue of $1.235B (up 11% YoY), net income of $135.6M (up 20%), and gross transaction value of $4.3B (up 13%), prompting management to raise full-year GTV growth guidance to 6-9% and adjusted EBIT guidance to $1.49-1.55B [1][3]. The company's dominant market position as the world's largest commercial asset marketplace, bolstered by the transformative 2023 IAA merger, creates significant competitive moats through network effects, proprietary valuation data, and trust-based buyer-seller relationships. The commercial construction and transportation segment showed particular strength with GTV up 27% in Q1 [1]. Analyst consensus is Strong Buy with an average price target of ~$125, implying ~25% upside.
The primary concern is valuation compression risk — at a trailing P/E of ~49x and forward P/E of 23x, the stock prices in significant earnings growth that could be disrupted by used equipment market cyclicality or post-merger integration challenges. ROE of 7.26% is weak, depressed by the $5B+ in goodwill and intangibles from the IAA acquisition, and it remains unclear when returns on invested capital will normalize [1]. Profit margin of 9.33% is modest for a marketplace/platform business and suggests the company has not yet fully realized operating leverage from the IAA integration. Additionally, the company's reliance on cyclical end-markets (construction, transportation, agriculture) exposes it to macroeconomic downturns that could compress transaction volumes and margins simultaneously.
Key Catalysts
  1. Full realization of IAA merger synergies driving margin expansion as integration completes and duplicate costs are eliminated, with forward EPS implying significant earnings growth over trailing figures
  2. Big Iron Auction Company acquisition (May 2026) opening the U.S. agriculture equipment market, diversifying revenue beyond construction and transportation [3]
  3. Commercial construction and transportation GTV accelerating at 27% YoY in Q1 2026, potentially driving full-year results above the raised 6-9% GTV growth guidance [1]
Key Risks
  1. Used equipment market cyclicality — a construction or transportation downturn could compress GTV and transaction-based revenues, with the company's higher post-merger fixed cost base amplifying margin pressure
  2. ROE of 7.26% reflects $5B+ in acquisition-related goodwill that may face impairment risk if the IAA integration underdelivers on expected synergies and growth targets
  3. Trailing P/E of 49x leaves minimal margin of safety — any miss against the raised FY2026 guidance could trigger significant multiple compression given the premium valuation

AI Resilience MODERATE

AI Risk Protection (7/9): RB Global's core business involves physical-world asset transactions — heavy equipment inspections, vehicle transport logistics, title transfers, and trust-based marketplace interactions that AI structurally cannot substitute [1]. The auction process requires physical presence of assets, complex condition assessments, regulatory compliance across jurisdictions, and buyer-seller trust that depends on decades of marketplace reputation. The insurance salvage business (IAA) involves physical vehicle towing, storage, and regulated title processing. These operations have deep physical and regulatory moats. However, AI could potentially disrupt the company's data analytics and valuation services (Rouse) and enhance competitor marketplace platforms, representing a modest risk to ancillary revenue streams. AI Opportunity (0/1): No quantifiable AI-related revenue has been reported. While RB Global operates sophisticated digital marketplace technology, no AI-specific products, features, or revenue streams have been disclosed in earnings materials.
