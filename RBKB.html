Company Profile
Financial Services ·Banks - Regional ·NASDAQ ·United States
Rhinebeck Bancorp is the holding company for Rhinebeck Bank, a 166-year-old community bank serving New York's Hudson Valley region. The bank offers traditional deposit products, residential mortgage lending, commercial real estate loans, and small business banking. Rhinebeck completed a second-step conversion from mutual to full stock ownership on July 21, 2026, raising $88.8M in gross proceeds at $10/share. Total assets of $1.47B with $926M in loans and $1.28B in deposits. New CEO Matthew Smith (former Chief Digital Banking Officer at Webster Bank) was appointed October 2025, replacing 40-year veteran Michael Quinn. Headquartered in Poughkeepsie, New York.
158 Employees ·www.rhinebeckbank.com

Investment Thesis
Rhinebeck Bancorp operates a conservatively managed 166-year-old community bank with $1.47B in assets, strong asset quality (0.37% NPLs, 227% allowance coverage), and well-above-minimum capital ratios (Tier 1 at 14.61%) [1]. The completed second-step conversion raised $88.8M at $10/share, creating a substantial capital war chest for organic growth, potential acquisitions, and future share repurchases — the stock now trades at $12.99, a 30% gain from the offering price. New CEO Matthew Smith brings digital transformation experience from Webster Bank and Columbia Financial, with an 'open architecture, fast follower' technology strategy and a 50-page strategic plan [2]. Trading at 14.43x trailing earnings and approximately 1.42x book value ($9.16 book per share), the stock offers modest valuation support relative to tangible book.
Growth headwinds are significant. Net interest income grew only 1.2% YoY in Q2 2026, with net interest margin under pressure from competitive deposit pricing [1]. The second-step conversion roughly doubled the share count to ~19.75M shares, diluting forward EPS substantially from the trailing $0.90 figure. The bank's efficiency ratio of 75.84% lags high-performing community banks (target below 60%) and limits earnings leverage. With no dividend, no analyst coverage, and micro-cap status (~$203M market cap), the stock offers limited catalysts for near-term appreciation. Average daily volume of only 134K shares creates meaningful liquidity risk — the $1.74M daily trading value barely clears the $1M liquidity floor, and institutional investors cannot easily build or exit positions. The Inspire Impact Score of 24 (MIXED) is neutral for faith-based investors.
