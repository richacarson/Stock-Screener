Company Profile
Communication Services ·Electronic Gaming & Multimedia ·NYQ ·United States
Roblox Corporation operates an immersive user-generated content platform for social connection and communication, enabling users to explore, create, and share 3D experiences. Revenue is generated primarily through the sale of virtual currency (Robux) used for in-experience purchases and avatar customization, with TTM revenue of $5.7B growing 36% YoY. The platform serves over 90M daily active users worldwide and has paid over $900M to developers through its Developer Exchange program. Founded in 2004 and headquartered in San Mateo, California, with 3,065 employees.

Investment Thesis
Roblox at $48.09 with a $34.4B market cap is the dominant UGC gaming platform executing on monetization scale — Q1 2026 revenue surged 39% to $1.4B with record bookings of $1.7B (up 43%), and TTM revenue reached $5.7B with $2.1B in operating cash flow and $1.5B in free cash flow [1][2]. The platform's 90M+ DAUs and 3M+ developer ecosystem create a powerful network effect, with user growth accelerating in the 17+ demographic and advertising revenue scaling. The Developer Exchange program's $900M+ in creator payouts has built a self-reinforcing content flywheel that competitors cannot easily replicate. With $3.0B in cash against $1.8B in debt and revenue compounding at 24%+ over five years, Roblox has the financial runway to reach sustained profitability while investing in AI-powered creation tools and platform expansion.
The bear case centers on the path to profitability and decelerating bookings growth. Despite $5.7B in TTM revenue, RBLX remains GAAP unprofitable with -17.6% profit margin and -$1.42 EPS, and forward estimates still project losses through FY2027 [1][2]. Q2 2026 bookings growth decelerated sharply to 8% (from 43% in Q1), and Q3 2026 bookings guidance of $1.58B-$1.65B implies a 14-18% YoY decline — raising concerns that the platform's growth engine is stalling. The D/E ratio of 1,423% reflects $1.8B in convertible notes that create dilution risk upon conversion. Child-safety controversies — including a $10M settlement and Inspire's Exploitation/Sexually Explicit flags — pose regulatory and reputational risk [4]. The PEG ratio of 8.18 prices in sustained hypergrowth that bookings trends increasingly undermine, while generative AI tools could eventually reduce the platform's creator lock-in advantage.
Key Catalysts
  1. Advertising platform scaling rapidly as brands discover 90M+ DAU audience — self-serve ads launched in 2025 with early revenue traction, creating a high-margin revenue stream that diversifies beyond Robux transactions [1]
  2. 17+ user demographic growing fastest, expanding total addressable market from children's entertainment to cross-generational social platform — management expects 50%+ of new users to be over 13 within two years [2]
  3. AI-powered creation tools (Roblox Assistant, AI code assist, AI texture generation) lowering development barriers and accelerating content production, potentially driving a new wave of creator-led experiences and platform engagement [3]
Key Risks
  1. Bookings deceleration from 43% YoY in Q1 to 8% in Q2 2026, with Q3 guidance implying outright decline of 14-18% — suggests the post-pandemic engagement surge is fading and revenue recognition tailwinds (27-month deferral) mask weakening demand [2]
  2. Persistent GAAP unprofitability with -17.6% profit margin and no clear timeline to breakeven — $1.8B in convertible notes create dilution overhang, and stock-based compensation of ~$1B annually inflates cash flow metrics relative to true economic profitability [1]
  3. Child safety and content moderation risk — $10M settlement in 2023 and Inspire's Exploitation/Sexually Explicit flags create regulatory vulnerability, particularly as European and U.S. regulators increase scrutiny of children's online platforms [4]

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience MODERATE
6
AI Risk Protection (6/9): Roblox's core platform benefits from deep network effects — 90M+ DAUs and 3M+ developers create a social graph and content ecosystem that AI cannot easily replicate or bypass [1]. The UGC model means AI-generated content enhances rather than threatens the platform, as developers adopt AI tools to create richer experiences faster. However, generative AI poses a medium-term structural risk: AI tools like Unreal Engine 5's AI features and standalone AI game generators could enable creators to build high-quality gaming experiences outside Roblox's walled garden, potentially reducing the platform's moat among sophisticated developers. The CTO acknowledges AI's role in enhancing trust & safety but hasn't addressed competitive substitution risk publicly [3]. AI Opportunity (0/1): While Roblox deploys AI internally for content moderation, creator tools, and recommendation systems, no standalone AI revenue stream has been quantified — AI is an operational enabler rather than a revenue driver.
