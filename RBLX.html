Company Profile
Communication Services ·Electronic Gaming & Multimedia ·NYSE ·United States
Roblox Corporation operates the world's largest user-generated content gaming and social platform, serving over 132 million daily active users globally. Users explore millions of 3D experiences built by a vast developer community using Roblox Studio. CEO David Baszucki has led the company since founding it in 2004, increasingly positioning Roblox as an immersive social media and advertising platform beyond traditional gaming. Q2 2026 revenue surged 36% YoY to $1.5B, but bookings growth stalled at 8% with management guiding Q3 bookings to decline 14-18% YoY — the first negative bookings guidance in company history — as safety changes friction impacts user acquisition. Market cap is approximately $27B.

Investment Thesis
Roblox is the dominant user-generated content gaming platform with 132 million daily active users and TTM revenue of $5.69B (+41% YoY), demonstrating extraordinary top-line momentum and network effects that no competitor has replicated at scale [1]. Q2 2026 revenue surged 36% to $1.5B, and the platform generates $1.64B in free cash flow despite GAAP losses, driven by deferred revenue mechanics from bookings recognized ratably [4]. The Vector AI advertising platform is driving double-digit sequential ad revenue growth, creating a high-margin revenue stream that could accelerate the path to profitability [2]. CEO Baszucki's 22-year founder leadership provides strategic consistency, and generative AI creation tools are expanding the developer ecosystem by enabling anyone to build immersive 3D experiences. The expansion to older demographics (17-24 fastest growing cohort) broadens the addressable market beyond children's gaming.
The bear case is severe and multi-dimensional. Q3 2026 bookings guidance of -14% to -18% YoY represents the first negative bookings guidance in company history, signaling that safety-driven changes are materially impacting user acquisition and engagement [1]. Management has withdrawn full-year guidance due to uncertainty, an ominous signal for a company that has never achieved GAAP profitability in 22 years of operation. D/E of 1423% reflects extreme leverage with $1.84B in debt against minimal equity ($0.21 book value per share), creating financial fragility [4]. Net loss of $1.0B on $5.69B TTM revenue (-17.61% profit margin) raises fundamental questions about whether the business model can ever generate sustainable profits. Child safety controversies are escalating: lawsuits from at least six state AGs allege exploitation and exposure of minors to sexual content, with Louisiana AG calling the platform 'the perfect place for pedophiles' [3]. The Inspire Impact Score of -81 (MISALIGNED) with 'Exploitation' and 'Sexually Explicit' negative attributions makes RBLX unsuitable for faith-aligned portfolios. The stock has declined 67.68% over the past 52 weeks from $117 to $37.93, and further regulatory action could impose additional growth friction.

AI Risk Protection (5/9): AI is predominantly an enabler for Roblox, but the platform faces meaningful AI-driven competitive risk. The value proposition — a user-generated content ecosystem with 132M daily users and millions of creators — is protected by network effects that AI cannot easily replicate [1]. Roblox is actively deploying AI to strengthen its position: Vector AI powers advertising, generative AI tools lower creation barriers, and AI content moderation scales safety [2].
