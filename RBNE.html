Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas Midstream ·NASDAQ ·Cyprus
Robin Energy Ltd. operates as an international ship-owning company providing seaborne energy transportation services worldwide. The company acquires, owns, charters, and operates oceangoing LPG carriers and tanker vessels. Originally operating two LPG carriers and one tanker, the company sold its tanker vessel M/T Wonder Mimosa in April 2026 for an approximate $6.7M gain and announced a spin-off of its tanker segment into AI OKTO CORP. Formed through a spin-off from Toro Corp in April 2025, based in Limassol, Cyprus.

Investment Thesis
Robin Energy is a nano-cap shipping company (~$1.4M market cap) that has secured $8.4M in contracted LPG charter revenue for 2026, with both LPG carriers at 100% utilization generating aggregate monthly charter revenue of $713,000 [3]. The April 2026 sale of the tanker M/T Wonder Mimosa produced an approximate $6.7M gain, demonstrating the company's ability to monetize assets at favorable valuations. The company carries minimal debt with $5.6M in cash, and the two LPG carriers have multi-year charter contracts locked in through early 2027 at rates of $353,000 and $360,000 per month [3]. The AI OKTO spin-off could unlock optionality value if AI-enabled maritime operations gain traction [2].
The bear case is overwhelming. Robin Energy's $1.36M market cap is smaller than the monthly revenue from its two LPG carriers, suggesting the market assigns virtually zero going-concern value to the equity [3]. The stock has declined 98.7% over 12 months, falling from the $374 range to $2.64, likely reflecting dilution from the ATM program ($13.9M raised at $4.31/share) and the July 2026 offering ($3.0M at $4.00/share) that massively expanded the share count [3]. The AI OKTO spin-off further fragments an already tiny company into two even smaller entities, reducing any remaining liquidity. With no analyst coverage, no forward earnings estimates, minimal financial disclosure, and a Cyprus domicile managed by a Greek third-party ship manager, RBNE presents extreme concentration, governance, dilution, and liquidity risks that make it unsuitable for any investment portfolio. The company's revenue depends entirely on two LPG vessels — a single operational incident could be existential.
