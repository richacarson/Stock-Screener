Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Infrastructure ·NYSE ·United States
Rubrik provides cloud data security and cyber resilience solutions including enterprise data protection, threat analytics, security posture management, identity recovery, and cyber recovery. The AI-powered platform (Ruby AI agent, SentryAI, Agent Cloud) helps organizations detect, respond to, and recover from cyberattacks while enabling trusted AI agent deployment. Founded in 2013 by CEO Bipul Sinha, IPO'd April 2024. Q2 FY2027 (ended July 2026) delivered $427.3M revenue (+38% YoY), GAAP EPS of $0.22 (beating -$0.04 consensus by 650%), and subscription ARR of $1.66B (+33% YoY). Raised FY2027 revenue guidance to $1.685-1.693B. Gartner Magic Quadrant Leader for Backup and Data Protection for 7th consecutive year.

Investment Thesis
Rubrik delivered a blowout Q2 FY2027 on August 27, 2026 with $427.3M revenue (+38% YoY), GAAP EPS of $0.22 (beating -$0.04 consensus by 650%), subscription ARR of $1.66B (+33% YoY), and $65.7M free cash flow [1]. Two consecutive quarters of GAAP profitability validate a decade of patient investment. Management raised all FY2027 metrics: $1.685-1.693B revenue, $1.854-1.862B ARR, non-GAAP EPS of $0.47-0.53, and $293-303M FCF [1][4]. Rubrik's position as Gartner MQ Leader for 7th consecutive year, AI-powered cyber resilience platform (Ruby, SentryAI, Agent Cloud for agentic AI governance), and MSCI BBB ESG rating position it in a secular growth market where AI-driven cyberattacks create non-discretionary enterprise spending. Director Mark McLaughlin's $502K insider purchase, net-cash-positive balance sheet ($1.75B cash vs. $1.15B convertible notes), and lead founding partnership in the Cloud Security Alliance's AI Resilience Center signal institution-building [2][4].
The bear case centers on extreme valuation and growth deceleration. At $14.7B market cap and forward P/E of approximately 186x, the stock prices in sustained hyper-growth — any deceleration below 30% revenue growth could trigger severe multiple compression [5]. Revenue growth already decelerated from 48% (FY2026) to 38% (Q2 FY2027), and while management raised guidance, the trajectory implies continued step-down. TTM profit margin of -20.25% reflects heavy stock-based compensation that inflates non-GAAP profitability metrics, though GAAP profitability is now emerging [1]. Company insiders collectively sold $86M more than they bought in the last 12 months, creating selling pressure despite Director McLaughlin's $502K purchase [4]. Competition from Veeam, Cohesity-Veritas, and Commvault remains intense as each invests in AI capabilities. A February 2026 log server breach — while contained — is reputationally damaging for a cybersecurity company [3]. The $1.15B in convertible notes creates potential dilution risk upon conversion.
