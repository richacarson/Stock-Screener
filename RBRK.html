Company Profile

Rubrik provides cloud data security and cyber resilience solutions, including enterprise data protection, data threat analytics, security posture management, identity recovery, and cyber recovery. The AI-powered platform (Ruby AI agent, SentryAI) helps organizations detect, respond to, and recover from cyberattacks. Founded in 2013 by CEO Bipul Sinha, IPO'd April 2024, with $1.57B subscription ARR and 2,805 customers with $100K+ ARR. Investment Thesis
Rubrik delivered a blowout Q1 FY2027 with $387.1M revenue (+39% YoY), GAAP EPS of $0.16 (beating -$0.11 consensus by 245%), and subscription ARR of $1.57B (+32% YoY) — achieving first-ever GAAP profitability [4]. The company raised all FY2027 guided metrics: $1.83B ARR, $1.6B revenue, non-GAAP EPS of $0.07-0.27, and $265-275M FCF [1][4]. Operating cash flow margin of 21% and free cash flow margin of 19% demonstrate operating leverage emerging in the model [4]. Rubrik's position as Gartner MQ Leader for 7th consecutive year, 2,805 customers with $100K+ ARR, and AI-powered cyber resilience platform (Ruby, SentryAI, Identity Resilience) position it in a secular growth market where AI-driven cyberattacks drive non-discretionary enterprise spending. Net-cash-positive balance sheet ($1.68B cash vs. $1.13B convertible notes) and board talent (former CIA CISO, ex-Palo Alto Networks CEO) signal institution-building [1][2].
The bear case centers on extreme valuation and growth deceleration risk. At forward P/E of 282.95 and $17.22B market cap, the stock prices in sustained hyper-growth — any deceleration below 30% revenue growth could trigger severe multiple compression [5]. Revenue growth already decelerated from 48% (FY2026) to 39% (Q1 FY2027), and FY2027 guidance implies ~21% full-year growth, suggesting a meaningful step-down [1][4]. The company remains GAAP unprofitable on a trailing basis (-20.3% profit margin) due to $329.4M in annual stock-based compensation that inflates non-GAAP metrics [1]. A February 2026 log server breach — while contained and transparently handled — is reputationally damaging for a cybersecurity company and follows a 2023 breach from the Clop/GoAnywhere attacks [3]. Competition from Veeam, Cohesity-Veritas, and Commvault remains intense, with each investing heavily in AI and cloud capabilities. As a recently IPO'd company, insider lock-up expirations create potential selling pressure.
Key Catalysts
  1. FY2027 ARR target of $1.83B with $265-275M FCF and first-ever GAAP profitability trajectory could trigger institutional re-rating as Rubrik transitions from growth to profitable growth [1][4]
  2. AI-driven cyberattack escalation creates secular demand tailwind — as nation-state actors weaponize AI, enterprise spending on cyber resilience platforms like Rubrik becomes non-discretionary [2]
  3. Identity Resilience and expanded AI security product suite (Ruby, SentryAI) create multiple growth vectors within existing customer base of 2,805 enterprises with $100K+ ARR [1][2]
Key Risks
  1. Revenue growth deceleration from 48% (FY2026) to guided ~21% (FY2027) at a forward P/E of 282.95 creates severe valuation compression risk — any further slowdown could trigger a significant stock re-rating [4][5]
  2. Competitive intensity — Veeam, Cohesity-Veritas, and Commvault are investing heavily in AI-powered data security, creating pricing pressure and feature parity risk in a market where switching costs exist but are not insurmountable [1]
  3. February 2026 log server breach and 2023 Clop/GoAnywhere breach represent reputational risk for a cybersecurity company, even though both were contained and disclosed transparently [3]
