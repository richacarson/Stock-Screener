Company Profile
Communication Services ·Telecom Services ·NYSE ·Canada
Rogers Communications is one of Canada's Big Three telecommunications companies, providing wireless, cable internet, television, and media services. The company operates Canada's largest cable network and a leading wireless network, along with a Sports and Media segment that includes the Toronto Blue Jays (MLB), Sportsnet, and other media properties. Rogers acquired Shaw Communications for C$26B in 2023, transforming it into Canada's largest cable provider. New CEO Ali El-Haj was appointed in May 2026, bringing 30+ years of international experience from the automotive and manufacturing industries. Q2 2026 delivered total revenue of C$5.62B (+7.7% YoY). Founded 1960, headquartered in Toronto.
