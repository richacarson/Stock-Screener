Company Profile
Healthcare ·Drug Manufacturers - Specialty & Generic ·NYSE ·India
Dr. Reddy's Laboratories Ltd is a global pharmaceutical company headquartered in Hyderabad, India, engaged in the manufacture and sale of generic pharmaceuticals, biosimilars, active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), and specialty drugs. The company operates across North America, Europe, India, and emerging markets, with key therapeutic areas including oncology, gastroenterology, cardiovascular, and pain management. Founded in 1984 by Dr. K. Anji Reddy with the mission of making medicines accessible and affordable. NYSE-listed ADR.

Investment Thesis
Dr. Reddy's achieved record FY2026 adjusted revenue of $3.63B (+4.6% in constant currency), with emerging markets growing 32% and India 19%, demonstrating the geographic diversification thesis despite lenalidomide headwinds [1]. At $12.60 ($10.6B market cap), the stock trades at 23.9x forward earnings — a premium for generic pharma justified by biosimilar pipeline optionality, GLP-1 semaglutide entry, and a 40-year track record under founding family governance. Balance sheet is clean with D/E of 20%, net cash of $112M, and gross margins recovering from the 52.8% Q4 trough as product mix normalizes post-lenalidomide [4]. AI adoption for R&D acceleration and manufacturing optimization positions Dr. Reddy's ahead of generic pharma peers. The 'Good Health Can't Wait' mission and ESG leadership (DJSI recognition, 100% renewable power target) align with long-term institutional investor criteria.
The bear case centers on revenue deceleration, margin compression, and US market challenges. FY2026 USD revenue declined 6.1% as INR depreciation offset constant-currency growth, and Q4 revenue of $849M missed the $893M estimate by 5% as lenalidomide patent expiry compressed sales [1]. Gross margins contracted from 58.5% to 52.8% in FY2026, and EBITDA margin compressed to 22.8%, signaling a lower-margin product mix going forward. The US generic drug market faces persistent pricing pressure from buyers (PBMs, GPOs), and Dr. Reddy's US business showed muted growth. Forward P/E of 23.9x prices in biosimilar and GLP-1 success that is not yet de-risked — execution failure on semaglutide or biosimilar regulatory setbacks could trigger meaningful multiple compression. Revenue forecast of 8.7% annual growth over 3 years is moderate for the valuation premium [4].
