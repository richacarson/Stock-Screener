Company Profile
Real Estate ·Real Estate Services ·NASDAQ ·United States
The Real Brokerage is a real estate technology company operating in the U.S. and Canada with 35,000+ agents. The company combines a cloud-based brokerage model with integrated mortgage, title, and financial technology services. Powered by AI tools including its Leo virtual concierge, Real offers agents a technology-forward platform with competitive economics. In April 2026, Real announced an $880M acquisition of REMAX Holdings to create Real REMAX Group with 180,000+ agents across 120+ countries. Founded in 2014, headquartered in Miami, Florida.

Investment Thesis
The Real Brokerage achieved a landmark milestone in Q2 2026 — its first-ever quarter of positive net income (EPS $0.01) — validating the path from hyper-growth investment phase to profitability [1]. Revenue surged 58.7% YoY to $540.7M in Q2 2026, with adjusted EBITDA of $20.04M and free cash flow margin improving to 7.5% [1]. TTM revenue stands at $2.08B with $72.2M in free cash flow on a debt-free balance sheet with $62.9M cash [2][6]. The $880M REMAX acquisition, expected to close H2 2026, would create Real REMAX Group with 180,000+ agents across 120+ countries and $30M in targeted synergies, transforming Real from a North American challenger into a global technology-enabled real estate platform [3]. AI integration is a genuine competitive advantage: Leo handles all agent support calls 24/7, automated marketing creates content at scale, and operating expense per transaction declined 19% to $508 [2]. At $2.01 per share (~$438M market cap), the stock trades at just 0.2x TTM revenue with analyst consensus of $5.15 (Strong Buy), suggesting significant upside if the company sustains profitability and executes REMAX integration [6].
The bear case centers on integration risk, unproven sustained profitability, and structural industry headwinds. Despite the Q2 2026 milestone, TTM profit margin remains at -0.32% and ROE at -13.05%, meaning sustained profitability is far from established [6]. The $880M REMAX acquisition carries enormous execution risk — integrating a 45-year-old franchise model (8,500 offices, 145,000 agents) with a cloud-native platform across 120 countries is operationally complex, and channel conflict between franchise-owned and company-owned models could alienate agents on both sides [3]. Insiders have been net sellers to the tune of $25M over the past 12 months, with CEO Poleg reducing his direct holdings, signaling potential lack of conviction at current levels [5]. Commission compression from the NAR settlement and AI-powered consumer tools (Zillow, Redfin) remains a structural risk to the entire brokerage industry's revenue pool. The stock has declined 60.9% from its 52-week high of $5.41 with 10.8% short interest, reflecting meaningful market skepticism about the path forward [6]. Agent retention is inherently fragile — 35,000+ independent contractors have zero switching costs and could migrate to the next lowest-cost platform.

  3. Insider selling signal — net insider selling of $25M over the past 12 months, including CEO Poleg reducing direct holdings from 8.04M to 7.29M shares, combined with 10.8% short interest and 60.9% decline from 52-week highs, suggests meaningful skepticism about near-term execution [5][6]

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience MODERATE
5
AI Risk Protection (4/9): Real estate brokerage faces meaningful AI disruption risk. AI-powered consumer tools from Zillow, Redfin, and emerging platforms could increasingly enable buyers and sellers to search, evaluate, and transact properties with less agent involvement [4]. The NAR commission settlement has already weakened the traditional commission structure, and AI could accelerate commission compression by reducing the perceived value of agent services. However, Real's technology-forward model positions it better than traditional brokerages — the company is embracing AI with Leo and automated marketing rather than being disrupted by it. Physical real estate transactions still require human judgment, local market knowledge, and trust relationships that AI cannot fully replicate, but the moat is narrowing as AI capabilities expand. AI Opportunity (1/1): Real generates measurable value from AI through Leo (handling all agent support calls 24/7), AI-driven marketing automation at scale, and operational efficiency improvements. Adjusted operating expense per transaction declined 19% YoY to $508, directly contributing to the company's first positive net income quarter in Q2 2026 [1][2].
