Real REMAX Group Inc. (formerly The Real Brokerage Inc., renamed August 2026 following its acquisition of RE/MAX Holdings) is a real estate technology company operating a tech-native, agent-first brokerage model across the United States and Canada. Founded in 2014 and headquartered in Miami, Florida, the company provides title, mortgage, digital wallet, and franchising/ancillary services alongside its core brokerage platform, reZEN, which uses AI-powered tools to reduce agents' administrative burden.

Investment Thesis
Real REMAX Group is the fastest-growing major U.S. brokerage, having scaled to 35,000+ agents and $2.0 billion of FY2025 revenue (+56%) on a tech-native, low-overhead model, and it just completed a transformative $880 million acquisition of RE/MAX that adds a century-old global franchise brand and ~$190 million of pro forma combined adjusted EBITDA including committed synergies [1][2][8]. The agent-first culture — reinforced by promoting former agents into the Board and C-suite — has driven strong organic recruitment and narrowing losses even as the company continues to invest in AI-enabled tools (reZEN) and ancillary revenue streams (title, mortgage, fintech wallet) [4][5].
The core risk is that Infrastructure metrics remain weak on a mechanical basis: ROE and profit margin are still negative as the company absorbs RE/MAX integration costs and continues to fund rapid agent-count growth, and forward earnings visibility is limited immediately post-acquisition. Investors are effectively underwriting continued execution of the RE/MAX integration and a path to sustained profitability, which is plausible given the trajectory of narrowing losses and rising adjusted EBITDA, but is not yet proven at scale.
Key Catalysts
  1. RE/MAX integration synergies (targeted $30M committed run-rate) flowing through to consolidated adjusted EBITDA over 2026-2027
  2. Continued agent-count growth (+26% YoY) expanding the revenue base and ancillary service attach rates (title, mortgage, wallet)
  3. Path to sustained GAAP profitability as losses have narrowed from prior years toward breakeven
Key Risks
  1. Integration execution risk from absorbing RE/MAX's legacy franchise network and culture into a tech-native brokerage model
  2. Industry-wide commission compression risk from AI-enabled portal search/matching tools and post-NAR-settlement buyer-agent fee pressure
  3. Persistently negative ROE and profit margin under the mechanical Infrastructure scoring, reflecting an unproven path to durable profitability
