Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas E&P ·NYSE American ·United States
Ring Energy is an independent oil and natural gas company operating primarily on the Northwest Shelf and Central Basin Platform in the Permian Basin of West Texas and New Mexico. The company produced 19,990 Boe/d in Q2 2026, with oil comprising approximately 63% of production. Ring completed the Stronghold Oil & Gas acquisition in 2022, significantly expanding its acreage position to approximately 83,550 net developed acres. Market cap is approximately $373M with revolving credit borrowings of $360M and a leverage ratio of 2.02x.

Investment Thesis
Ring Energy at 5.54x forward earnings is a deep-value micro-cap E&P with improving operational momentum — Q2 2026 revenue of $104.7M surged 27% YoY as production reached 19,990 Boe/d and oil prices recovered, driving $64.8M in net income ($0.27/share) and $54.5M in adjusted EBITDA (+42% sequentially) [1]. The company paid down $66M in revolving credit debt during Q2, reducing leverage from 2.42x to 2.02x, with total liquidity of $226.1M providing a cushion against oil price volatility. D/E of 48.28% is conservative for an E&P, and the forward valuation implies the market prices in significant commodity downside [3]. Management's 2027 guidance of ~10% production growth on ~10% less capital spending signals improving capital efficiency as longer lateral wells (>1.5 miles) reduce per-unit costs [1]. Insider buying by both CEO McKinney ($59.5K) and CFO Johl ($277.2K) in June 2026 provides additional conviction at depressed levels [4].
The bear case centers on commodity dependence, impairment risk, and scale disadvantages. Ring Energy's $162.1M ceiling test impairment in Q1 2026 wiped out all prior-year earnings and produced a TTM net loss of $220.6M, demonstrating the company's extreme sensitivity to oil price movements — at sub-$70 WTI, Ring's proved reserves face further write-downs [2]. TTM revenue of $308M is down 3.2% year-over-year despite the Q2 recovery, and adjusted free cash flow of just $4.6M in H1 2026 (down 85% YoY) shows minimal cash generation after capex. The $360M in revolving credit borrowings require continued compliance with a 3.0x leverage covenant, leaving limited margin during prolonged commodity downturns [2]. At ~20,000 Boe/d, Ring is a micro-cap competing against Permian operators with vastly greater scale advantages in drilling costs, midstream access, and capital markets access. The Inspire Impact Score of 56 (ALIGNED) is a positive differentiator for faith-based investors [3].
