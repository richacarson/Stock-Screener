Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Infrastructure ·NASDAQ ·United States
Rekor Systems provides intelligent infrastructure solutions for public safety, urban mobility, and transportation management. Products include Rekor One (roadway intelligence engine), Rekor Scout (public safety ALPR), Rekor Discover (data analytics), and Rekor Command (transportation management). The company leverages AI-powered computer vision and data analytics to monitor roadway conditions and detect incidents in real-time. Headquartered in Columbia, Maryland.

Investment Thesis
Rekor Systems is an AI-powered roadway intelligence company showing modest improvement: Q1 2026 revenue of $10.3M grew 12% YoY with gross margins expanding to 53% from 48%, adjusted EBITDA loss narrowing to $(6.5M) from $(7.4M), and revenue growth across all product lines (Scout, Discover, Command) [1]. The company's AI capabilities have demonstrated real-world impact — 159% higher incident detection in a Texas pilot and 60% efficiency gains — and the shift toward higher-margin software and data-as-a-service revenue provides a credible path to profitability. Management expects cost reductions from Q1 workforce restructuring to be fully reflected in Q2 and beyond [1].
However, Rekor faces existential financial risk that dominates the investment case. The auditor issued a going concern doubt in March 2026, the stock trades at $0.695 near its 52-week low of $0.60 (down 80% from the $3.42 high), and the $96M market cap makes this a nano-cap with severe liquidity constraints — daily dollar volume of $735K fails the IOWN liquidity floor [2]. A 2021 class action lawsuit exposed competitive vulnerabilities, with a short-seller alleging ALPR technology 'outclassed by global competitors' and revenues 'overstated by up to 80%' [4]. CEO turnover (March 2025) and Glassdoor rating of 2.6/5 describe 'volatile' leadership. With D/E of 85%, ROE of -88.3%, and net margin of -60.5%, the balance sheet cannot sustain prolonged losses without further dilutive financing [2].
