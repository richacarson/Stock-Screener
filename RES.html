Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas Equipment & Services ·NYSE ·United States
RPC, Inc. is a diversified oilfield services holding company headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia, providing specialized services and equipment to independent and major oil and gas companies. Operating primarily in the Permian Basin with 10 hydraulic fracturing fleets, the company's Technical Services segment includes pressure pumping, coiled tubing, cementing, downhole tools, and nitrogen services, while the Support Services segment provides equipment rental and service support. Controlled by the Rollins family, RPC generated $1.6B in FY2025 revenue and reported Q1 2026 revenue of $455M (+36.6% YoY) driven by Technical Services outperformance. Q2 2026 revenue was $460.9M with net income recovering to $12.1M. Market cap approximately $1.4B.

Investment Thesis
RPC offers a clean balance sheet (D/E 7%) and deep Permian Basin exposure through 10 hydraulic fracturing fleets at a modest Forward P/E of 19.42x [3]. The Q1 2026 revenue surge of 36.6% YoY to $455M — the fastest growth among oilfield services peers — demonstrates the company's Technical Services segment gaining traction through the Platinum Pressure Pumping acquisition and strong completions activity [1]. Q2 2026 showed continued sequential improvement with revenue of $460.9M and net income recovering to $12.1M from just $0.9M in Q1 [2]. The Rollins family's conservative management style provides downside protection through cycles, and the Inspire Impact Score of 45 (ALIGNED) with zero negative attributions makes RES accessible to faith-based investors seeking energy exposure.
The bear case is compelling: RPC is a small-cap, low-margin oilfield services company operating in a structurally challenging industry with minimal competitive differentiation. Profit margin of 6.46% and ROE of 4.41% are far below investment-grade targets, reflecting the capital-intensive, commoditized nature of pressure pumping [3]. The dividend payout ratio of 109% exceeds earnings, making the $0.16/year payment unsustainable without improvement in profitability — and with zero years of dividend growth and 0% 5yr CAGR, the income proposition is stagnant [2]. The negative PEG ratio of -0.26 signals that analysts expect earnings to decline, potentially compressing the Forward P/E further. RPC faces structural headwinds from digital oilfield adoption, where AI-powered analytics and autonomous equipment could reduce demand for traditional labor-intensive services over time. With no innovation pipeline, no AI strategy, and below-average employee satisfaction (Glassdoor 3.3), the company lacks the capabilities to navigate an evolving energy services landscape [4].
Key Catalysts
  1. Sustained Permian Basin completions activity driving Technical Services revenue above the $460M/quarter run rate, with potential for EBITDA margin recovery from the depressed 11.8% level as acquisition integration matures and pricing stabilizes [1]

Key Risks

  2. Digital oilfield adoption by E&P customers — AI-powered frac optimization, autonomous equipment, predictive maintenance — reducing demand for traditional labor-intensive services and pressuring RPC's revenue per fleet [4]
  3. Dividend sustainability risk with payout ratio at 109% of earnings; continued profitability weakness could force a dividend cut, removing the income thesis and triggering selling from yield-oriented investors [2][3]
