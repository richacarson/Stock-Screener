Company Profile
Industrials ·Industrial Distribution ·NYSE ·United States
Resideo Technologies, Inc. is a global provider of smart home and building technology solutions, spun off from Honeywell in 2018. It operates in two segments: Products & Solutions (P&S), which develops thermostats, security and fire safety products, and connected home devices under the Honeywell Home brand ($706M Q1 2026 revenue); and ADI Global Distribution, a leading wholesale distributor of low-voltage security, fire, AV, and networking products ($1.206B Q1 2026 revenue). The ADI spin-off into an independent public company is expected mid-Q3 to mid-Q4 2026.

Investment Thesis
Resideo delivered strong Q1 2026 results — total revenue of $1.912B (+8% YoY) with adjusted EBITDA of $215M (+28% YoY) and adjusted EPS of $0.65, beating estimates [1]. P&S achieved its 12th consecutive quarter of margin expansion to 25.1% adjusted EBITDA margin, and the company reaffirmed FY2026 guidance of $7.8-7.9B revenue with $935-985M adjusted EBITDA and $3.00-3.20 adjusted EPS [1]. The ADI spin-off (Form 10 filed, expected mid-Q3 to mid-Q4 2026) should unlock sum-of-the-parts value — ADI alone generates ~$5B in revenue while P&S delivers ~$2.8B at 25%+ EBITDA margins. At 11.6x forward earnings, the stock is cheap relative to the underlying earnings power, especially post-Honeywell payment removal of perpetual liability. AI integration into ProSeries positions Resideo for smart home market growth.
The bear case centers on leverage, cash flow, and execution risk. D/E of 121% reflects $3.23B in total debt from the Honeywell payment and ADI acquisition, and Q1 2026 operating cash flow was negative $145M (vs. -$65M prior year), masking cash flow pressure behind strong earnings [1][2]. GAAP ROE of -16.93% and profit margin of -7.05% remain distorted by the Honeywell payment, and investors may underappreciate how long normalization takes. The ADI spin-off introduces execution complexity — separating a ~$5B distribution business involves IT, supply chain, and organizational untangling that could distract management during a critical growth period [3]. Revenue is tied ~50% to home improvement and construction activity that could slow in a weakening housing market, and competitors in smart home (Ring/Amazon, Google Nest, Alarm.com) have larger platforms and deeper pockets for AI development.
Key Catalysts
  1. ADI spin-off completion expected mid-Q3 to mid-Q4 2026, creating two focused public companies and unlocking sum-of-the-parts value — P&S at 25%+ EBITDA margins versus ADI's distribution scale [1][3]
  2. FY2026 adjusted EBITDA guidance of $935M-985M represents 12-18% growth, supported by 12 consecutive quarters of P&S margin expansion and AI-enhanced ProSeries product launches [1]
  3. Post-Honeywell payment, GAAP metrics will normalize throughout 2026, creating potential for multiple re-rating as trailing ROE and profit margin turn positive and institutional investors re-engage [1]
Key Risks
  1. Cash flow pressure: Q1 2026 operating cash flow was -$145M (vs. -$65M prior year), and $3.23B total debt with D/E of 121% requires disciplined deleveraging that limits capital allocation flexibility [1][2]
  2. ADI spin-off execution risk — separation of a ~$5B distribution business involves complex IT, supply chain, and organizational untangling that could introduce operational disruptions and dis-synergies [3]
  3. Housing market sensitivity — approximately 50% of revenue is tied to home improvement and new construction, exposing earnings to macro cyclicality in a potentially weakening residential market [1]
