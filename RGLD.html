Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Gold ·NASDAQ ·United States
Royal Gold is one of the world's leading precious metals royalty and streaming companies, acquiring and managing royalty and stream interests in gold, silver, copper, and other metal mining operations globally. The company does not operate mines — it provides upfront financing to miners in exchange for contractual rights to a percentage of future production at discounted prices. In 2025, Royal Gold completed a transformative $4.1B acquisition of Sandstorm Gold and Horizon Copper and a $1.0B stream at the Kansanshi copper-gold mine, driving record FY2025 revenue of $1.0B (+43% YoY). Q1 2026 delivered record quarterly revenue of $469M (+143% YoY) with earnings of $281M (+148%). Market cap is approximately $21.4B.

Investment Thesis
Royal Gold at 19.62x forward earnings delivers a rare combination of transformative growth and premium business quality — Q1 2026 revenue of $469M (+143% YoY) and earnings of $281M (+148%) demonstrate the immediate accretive impact of the Sandstorm/Horizon acquisition and Kansanshi stream [1]. The 48% profit margin reflects the capital-light royalty/streaming model where revenue flows directly to the bottom line with minimal operating costs. The 180+ royalty and stream portfolio across gold, silver, and copper provides diversification that reduces single-asset risk, and the balance sheet is exceptionally clean at 5.19% D/E. Gold prices above $2,500/oz and structural copper demand from AI data center infrastructure provide sustained commodity tailwinds. The Zacks consensus estimate of $10.26 EPS for FY2026 implies 40% earnings growth.
The bear case centers on commodity price dependency and acquisition integration risk. Royal Gold's revenue is directly linked to gold, silver, and copper prices — a sustained decline in gold below $2,000/oz or copper below $4/lb would materially compress revenue and margins despite the cost-light model [4]. The $4.1B Sandstorm acquisition and $1.0B Kansanshi stream represent significant capital concentration in assets whose value depends on long-term commodity price assumptions; if the current precious metals and copper bull market reverses, the returns on deployed capital could disappoint. ROE of 13.60% is below the 15% target, suggesting the massive equity issuance for the Sandstorm deal has diluted returns on capital in the near term. Forward P/E of 19.62x is fair but not cheap for a commodity-linked business — any commodity downturn would compress multiples quickly. The royalty/streaming model also means Royal Gold has no operational control over the mining assets that generate its revenue, creating execution dependency on third-party operators.
