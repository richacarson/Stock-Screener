Company Profile
Materials ·Gold Streaming & Royalties ·NASDAQ ·United States
Royal Gold, Inc. is a precious metals streaming and royalty company headquartered in Denver, Colorado. Unlike traditional miners, Royal Gold provides upfront capital to mining operators in exchange for the right to purchase a percentage of metal production at below-market prices (streams) or receive a percentage of revenue (royalties). The company holds interests in over 175 properties across the Americas, Australia, and Africa, with key assets including Mount Milligan, Pueblo Viejo, Cortez, and Andacollo. Founded in 1981, Royal Gold operates with approximately 30 employees and is the only precious metals company in the S&P High Yield Dividend Aristocrats Index.

Investment Thesis
Royal Gold delivered record Q1 2026 results with $469M revenue, $294M operating cash flow, and $281M earnings, powered by an 83% adjusted EBITDA margin and realized gold prices near all-time highs [1]. The streaming model provides exceptional operating leverage — costs are largely fixed while revenue scales linearly with commodity prices, resulting in a 48.9% net profit margin and 15% ROE [1][3]. With debt-to-equity of just 23%, a Forward PEG of 0.63, and a pipeline of growth assets including Mount Milligan, Fourmile at Cortez, and expanding copper exposure, Royal Gold offers high-margin precious metals exposure with minimal operational risk. The 24-year dividend growth streak and 20% payout ratio provide income growth headroom [6].
The primary risk is extreme gold price dependency — Royal Gold's record results are driven by gold near $4,800+/oz, and a reversion toward historical averages ($1,500-2,000/oz) would cut revenue by 50-60% given the streaming model's direct commodity exposure [1]. The Forward P/E of 20.64 prices in continued gold strength, and the trailing P/E of ~27 reflects premium valuation relative to the broader market [3]. The company's Innovation (3/10) and Inspiration (3/10) scores are structurally constrained by the streaming business model — with 30 employees and no R&D, Royal Gold cannot innovate or inspire in the way operating companies can. The Infinite Game score of 4 reflects a fundamentally financial purpose that lacks the idealistic cause Sinek requires. Any meaningful gold price correction would expose the fragility behind the currently exceptional financial metrics.
Key Catalysts
  1. Gold price strength driven by central bank buying, geopolitical uncertainty, and de-dollarization trends supporting record-level margins and 83% EBITDA margins on streaming revenue [1]
  2. Copper streaming pipeline expansion benefits from AI data center buildout and electrification demand, diversifying Royal Gold beyond precious metals dependency [2]
  3. Fourmile deposit at Cortez and Mount Milligan extension provide production growth visibility into the late 2020s and 2030s, supporting the 17.2% forward revenue growth forecast [1][2]
Key Risks
  1. Gold price reversion from near-record ~$4,800/oz to historical averages would compress revenue 50-60% and expose the streaming model's direct commodity leverage — there is zero hedging or operational control to mitigate this [1]
  2. Premium valuation (trailing P/E ~27, Forward P/E 20.64) leaves no margin of safety if gold corrects or growth expectations for Fourmile and copper pipeline are delayed [3]
  3. Concentration risk in long-life mines — Mount Milligan (operator Centerra Gold) and Pueblo Viejo (Barrick Gold) represent material revenue contributors, and operational disruptions at either would directly impact Royal Gold's cash flow with no ability to intervene [1][2]
