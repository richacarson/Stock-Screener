Company Profile
Industrials ·Aerospace & Defense ·NYSE ·United States
Sturm, Ruger & Company designs, manufactures, and sells firearms for the commercial sporting market in the United States under the Ruger and Marlin brands. Products include semi-automatic pistols, revolvers, single-shot and autoloading rifles, modern sporting rifles, bolt-action rifles, lever-action rifles, and over-under shotguns. The company also operates a castings and MIM parts segment. Operates manufacturing facilities in Southport, CT; Newport, NH; Mayodan, NC; and Prescott, AZ. Under new CEO Todd Seyfert (appointed March 2025), the company has restructured into autonomous business units and reported its 5th consecutive quarter of sequential and YoY sales growth [1]. Founded in 1949, virtually debt-free with $117M cash and $1.5M in debt.

AI Risk Protection (8/9): Firearms manufacturing is a physical-world business involving precision machining, metallurgy, assembly, quality testing, and distribution through federally licensed dealers — none of which AI can replace or substitute [1]. The industry is deeply regulated by the ATF, requiring physical compliance infrastructure, background check systems, and serialized product tracking that are structurally immune to AI disruption. Consumer demand for firearms is driven by Second Amendment exercise, personal protection, hunting, and sport shooting — fundamental physical activities AI cannot displace. However, AI does not actively increase demand for firearms (unlike energy or semiconductors where AI is a demand driver), limiting the score to 8.
