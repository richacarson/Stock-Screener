Robert Half Inc. is a global staffing and consulting firm specializing in finance, technology, legal, and administrative placement through contract and permanent placement services. The company operates Protiviti, a global consulting subsidiary providing internal audit, technology consulting, risk, and compliance solutions. Q2 2026 revenue of $1.336B showed moderating decline (-2% YoY reported), with talent solutions delivering its third consecutive quarter of sequential growth and permanent placement posting 2.5% adjusted YoY growth. Investment Thesis
Robert Half reported Q2 2026 revenue of $1.336B (down only 2% YoY, improving from -3.8% in Q1), with talent solutions delivering its third consecutive quarter of sequential growth and permanent placement posting 2.5% adjusted YoY growth [1]. At a forward P/E of 16.71, the stock prices in a meaningful earnings recovery that analysts project. The balance sheet is clean (D/E 20%) and the 5.25% dividend yield backed by 23 consecutive years of growth provides substantial income. The stock has recovered 34% from its July lows to $44.97, reflecting improving sentiment on the cyclical recovery thesis. Fortune recognition as Most Innovative Company, Stevie Awards for AI/ML Solution, and the promotion of three senior leaders for AI and digital transformation suggest the company is actively evolving from 'staffing at risk from AI' to 'staffing enabled by AI' [2].
The bear case is significant: profit margin of just 2.5% and payout ratio of 153% are clearly unsustainable — Robert Half is paying more in dividends than it earns, and any further earnings deterioration would force a dividend cut that would devastate the income thesis and stock price [1][5]. Revenue has declined for multiple consecutive quarters against a growing economy, with 5-year revenue CAGR of -3.3% and 5-year EPS declining 14.9% annually, suggesting structural demand erosion rather than purely cyclical weakness. Staffing is directly exposed to AI disruption — automated screening, AI interviewing, and LinkedIn AI matching are reducing demand for traditional recruiter-mediated placement, and the company's own survey found 67% of HR leaders say AI-generated applications are already slowing hiring [2]. While Protiviti consulting provides diversification, the overall profit margin compression from historical 6-8% to 2.5% reflects a business model under pressure. The Inspire score of 54 (ALIGNED) is a positive for faith-based investors, but cannot offset the fundamental earnings deterioration [4].

Key Risks

  2. Structural AI disruption of core staffing model — AI hiring tools, automated screening, and AI agents handling professional tasks could permanently reduce demand for traditional staffing services, and 67% of HR leaders already report AI is changing hiring dynamics [2]
  3. Multi-quarter revenue decline despite a growing economy suggests secular headwinds (5-year revenue CAGR of -3.3%) that a macro recovery alone cannot reverse, calling into question whether profitability will ever return to historical levels [1]
