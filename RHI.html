Company Profile
Industrials ·Staffing & Employment Services ·NYSE ·United States
Robert Half Inc. provides specialized talent solutions (contract and permanent placement in finance & accounting, technology, and administrative roles) and business consulting through its Protiviti subsidiary [10]. Q3 2026 revenue was $1.359B (+0.4% YoY reported), with talent solutions growing 1.6% on an adjusted basis — its first growth since 2022 — while Protiviti revenue fell 2.9% to $483.5M [1][12]. CEO Keith Waddell, with the company since 1986 and CEO since December 2019, maintains a $0.59 quarterly dividend that has exceeded pre-financing cash generation year-to-date [1][5].

Investment Thesis
Robert Half may be at a cyclical trough: Q3 2026 EPS of $0.49 beat the $0.47 consensus and rose 14% — the first year-over-year EPS increase since 2022 — as talent solutions returned to growth (+1.6% adjusted), permanent placement rose 5.6%, and talent-solutions gross margin expanded 0.7 points to 47.4% [1][12]. A debt-light balance sheet (D/E 20%), a 5.4% dividend yield, and Protiviti's guide to flat-to-up 4% in Q4 give operating leverage if white-collar hiring recovers [1][10].
The bear case is structural: revenue fell from $7.24B in 2022 to $5.38B in 2025, the company itself flags AI displacement of clerical and professional roles, and its admin & customer support business is still contracting [1][11]. The dividend is uncovered — $179.8M paid in nine months versus $47.1M of pre-financing cash generation, a 193% payout ratio and cash down to $321M from $464M — so a dividend cut is a real risk, while profit margin is just 2.3% and Protiviti gross margin compressed sharply earlier in 2026 [1][2][10].
Key Catalysts
  1. Sustained recovery in talent solutions after the first growth since 2022 (+1.6% adjusted in Q3 2026), led by permanent placement (+5.6%) and technology contract (+4.6% to $165.1M) [1][12]
  2. Protiviti returning to growth, with Q4 2026 revenue guided flat to up 4% after a -3.3% Q3 that beat the -4% to -8% guide [1]
  3. Operating leverage from a debt-free balance sheet: Q3 operating income excluding trust swings rose 18.5% to $72.6M on flat revenue [1]
Key Risks
  1. AI displacement of finance, accounting and administrative roles — the core of Robert Half's contract and permanent placement franchise — which the company itself lists as a standing risk factor [1]
  2. Dividend sustainability: nine-month dividends of $179.8M against $47.1M of pre-financing cash generation, a 193% payout ratio and the 2026 dividend held flat after 21 years of increases [1][10]
  3. Wage-and-hour class actions alleging recruiter misclassification (Dorff, filed 2018) and unpaid interview time for 212,000+ workers (PAGA, filed 2015) [9]
