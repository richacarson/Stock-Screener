Algorhythm Holdings is a diversified technology holding company whose core asset is SemiCab, an AI-powered logistics platform that optimizes freight movement using ML predictions and predictive optimization to enable fully loaded round trips and reduce empty trucking miles. Formerly The Singing Machine Company (karaoke machines), the firm rebranded in September 2024 following its acquisition of SemiCab in July 2024. Q2 2026 revenue was $3.0M (+161% YoY), but the company reported a net loss of $4.15M and has flagged going concern risk. Market cap is approximately $5.9M.

Investment Thesis
Algorhythm Holdings offers speculative exposure to AI-powered logistics optimization through SemiCab, which has attracted blue-chip enterprise customers including P&G India (contract expanded 32% in Q2), Onida Electronics (new MSA), Coca-Cola India, and Unilever [1][3]. Q2 2026 revenue of $3.0M (+161% YoY, +25% sequentially) demonstrates accelerating customer adoption, and gross margin improved 850 basis points sequentially, suggesting progress toward unit economics [1]. The EPS loss of $0.25 beat the estimated $0.36 loss by 30.6%, showing improving cost discipline. The Inspire Impact Score of 36 (ALIGNED) with no negative attributions makes it clean for faith-based screening, and SemiCab's mission of reducing empty trucking miles aligns with emissions reduction goals.
The investment case is overwhelmed by existential risk factors. The company has a $5.9M market cap, negative gross margins of -26.28% TTM, and management has flagged going concern risk [2][4]. Cash of ~$8M against quarterly net losses of $4.15M provides only 1-2 quarters of runway. D/E of 275% with promissory notes and related party debt signals financial fragility, and the Altman Z-Score of -7.18 indicates severe bankruptcy risk [4]. The stock price has dropped 44% from $0.62 to $0.3444, and daily dollar volume of ~$407K triggers the liquidity floor — the stock is effectively uninvestable for any fund with position-sizing discipline. Insider ownership of only 1.1% suggests management is not meaningfully aligned with shareholders. The potential issuance of 5M shares to settle a claim adds dilution risk. The board's evaluation of a potential energy infrastructure acquisition raises strategic focus concerns — pivoting from karaoke to AI logistics to energy within two years suggests an opportunistic holding company rather than a focused technology builder. Revenue growth, while impressive on a percentage basis, operates on a scale ($8.5M TTM) that is immaterial in the context of the global logistics technology market.
