Company Profile
Technology ·Consumer Electronics ·NASDAQ ·United States
Algorhythm Holdings is an AI technology and consumer electronics holding company whose core asset is SemiCab, an AI-powered logistics platform that uses ML predictions and predictive optimization to orchestrate collaborative freight movement and reduce empty trucking miles. Formerly The Singing Machine Company (karaoke machines since 1982), the firm rebranded in September 2024 following its acquisition of SemiCab. Q2 2026 revenue was $3.0M (+161% YoY, +25% sequentially), reaching a $12M+ annualized run rate, but the company reported a net loss of $4.15M for Q2 and $9.5M for H1 2026 with going concern warnings [1][2]. Market cap is approximately $2.8M.

Investment Thesis
Algorhythm Holdings offers speculative exposure to AI-powered logistics optimization through SemiCab, which has demonstrated rapid revenue acceleration — Q2 2026 revenue of $3.0M (+161% YoY, +25% sequentially) reaching a $12M+ annualized run rate [1][3]. Gross margin improved 850 basis points sequentially, suggesting progress toward unit economics. The platform's AI/ML-driven approach to reducing empty trucking miles addresses a real inefficiency in the freight market, and the Inspire Impact Score of 36 (ALIGNED) with no negative attributions makes it clean for faith-based screening.
The investment case is overwhelmed by existential risk factors. The company has a $2.8M market cap, deeply negative margins (ROE -524%, profit margin -176%), and management has flagged going concern risk [2][4]. H1 2026 net losses of $9.5M against only $5.4M in revenue indicate severe cash burn. D/E of 275% with heavy debt financing signals financial fragility. The stock price has collapsed 97% over the past year to $0.133, and daily dollar volume of approximately $246K triggers the liquidity floor — the stock is effectively uninvestable for any fund with position-sizing discipline. Insider ownership is minimal. The 46-employee company competes against massively better-funded AI logistics platforms with superior data assets, engineering resources, and established customer relationships. Revenue growth, while impressive on a percentage basis, operates at a scale ($3M quarterly) that is immaterial in the context of the global logistics market.
