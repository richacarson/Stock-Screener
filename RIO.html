Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Other Industrial Metals & Mining ·NYSE ·United Kingdom
Rio Tinto is a global diversified miner producing iron ore, copper, aluminium, lithium and other minerals, led since August 2025 by CEO Simon Trott under a 'stronger, sharper, simpler' strategy [3]. In H1 2026 underlying EBITDA rose 28% to $14.8B, copper EBITDA rose 84% to $5.7B, free cash flow rose 75%, and the interim ordinary dividend rose 43% to 211 US cents per share. The company also made first high-grade iron ore sales from Simandou in Guinea in April 2026 [1]. The ADR trades at $83.25, about $135.4B market cap [5].

Investment Thesis
Rio Tinto offers cheap exposure to copper and high-grade iron ore. H1 2026 underlying EBITDA rose 28% to $14.8B, free cash flow rose 75%, copper EBITDA rose 84% to $5.7B, and the interim dividend rose 43% to 211 cents per share at a 50% payout [1]. Simandou began high-grade sales in April with 5-10 Mt guided for 2026, and copper guidance of 800-870 kt is maintained [1]. At 9.8x forward earnings with D/E of 32%, ROE of 19% and a 5.6% yield [5], Rio is one of the cheapest large-cap ways to own AI- and electrification-driven copper demand.
The bear case is commodity cyclicality and the reliability of the payout. Rio's variable dividend fell in 2023 and 2025, and the 5-year dividend CAGR is slightly negative (-0.7%) [5]. Iron ore still dominates earnings and is exposed to Chinese steel demand, which Simandou's added supply could depress. Simandou has shipped only 0.4 Mt so far against a 5-10 Mt target, so execution risk remains [1]. Trott's cost-cutting, divestment and leadership overhaul adds transition risk [3]. Juukan Gorge remains a reputational overhang with Indigenous communities and ESG investors [4].
Key Catalysts
  1. Simandou ramp-up toward the 5-10 Mt 2026 sales guidance (0.4 Mt shipped in H1) [1]
  2. Copper production delivery against 800-870 kt 2026 guidance (442 kt in H1) as Oyu Tolgoi underground ramps up [1]
  3. Execution of the up-to-$5B 2026 asset cash release and 80+ productivity initiatives under the 'stronger, sharper, simpler' plan [1][3]
Key Risks
  1. Iron ore price downturn driven by Chinese steel demand and new Simandou and Guinea supply [1]
  2. Variable dividend policy: annual ADR dividends fell in 2023 and 2025, so income is not reliable [5]
  3. Social-licence and sovereign risk in Guinea, Mongolia and Indigenous lands in Australia, where Juukan Gorge cost Rio its CEO [4]
