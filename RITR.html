Reitar Logtech Holdings provides construction management, engineering design, and asset management services primarily for cold storage facilities, automated warehouses, and logistics infrastructure in Hong Kong. Listed on NASDAQ in 2024 via a Cayman Islands holding structure, the company pursues a 'MIS' strategy (Mergers & Acquisitions, Integration, and Spin-offs). Market cap approximately $40 million.
