Company Profile
Financial Services ·Asset Management ·NYSE ·United States
Raymond James Financial is a diversified financial services holding company providing wealth management, capital markets, asset management, and banking services. The firm operates through Private Client Group, Capital Markets, Asset Management, and Bank segments, serving individual and institutional clients with approximately $1.92 trillion in client assets under administration through ~8,800 financial advisors. Founded in 1962, headquartered in St. Petersburg, Florida.

At 11.95x forward P/E with 17.3% ROE and a conservative 48% D/E ratio, the stock is deeply undervalued relative to its financial performance. Forward PEG of approximately 1.5 reflects analyst expectations of only ~6% annual earnings growth going forward, suggesting the record Q3 FY2026 results may represent a cyclical peak rather than a sustainable growth trajectory [5]. Net insider selling — multiple executives disposing of stock in July 2026 — raises questions about near-term management outlook [4]. AI Resilience MODERATE

AI Risk Protection (6/9): Raymond James' core business — wealth management advisory relationships, investment banking, and commercial lending — has meaningful protection from AI disruption. The advisor-client relationship is fundamentally human and trust-based, regulated by FINRA and SEC compliance requirements, and involves high-value decisions that clients are unlikely to delegate to AI [2].
