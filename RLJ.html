Company Profile
Real Estate ·REIT - Hotel & Motel ·NYSE ·United States
RLJ Lodging Trust is a self-advised, publicly traded REIT that owns 91 premium-branded, rooms-oriented, high-margin, focused-service and compact full-service hotels located in urban demand centers across the United States. Founded in 2011 by Robert L. Johnson, the company reported Q2 2026 comparable hotel revenue of $382M (up 6.8% YoY) with adjusted FFO of $0.52 per diluted share. The company raised full-year 2026 guidance following strong second-quarter results.

Investment Thesis
RLJ Lodging Trust offers a 5.47% dividend yield trading at approximately 8.5x forward FFO ($1.29-$1.45 guidance), a meaningful discount to hotel REIT peers [1][5]. Q2 2026 results demonstrated operational momentum with comparable hotel revenue up 6.8% to $382M, AFFO per share up 8.3% to $0.52, and hotel EBITDA up 7.1% — all outperforming the broader lodging industry [1]. Management raised full-year 2026 guidance following the strong second quarter, and the portfolio's concentration in premium-branded, rooms-oriented properties in urban demand centers provides structural margin advantages [2].
The bear case centers on severe internal organizational dysfunction and structural limitations. Glassdoor reviews describe a toxic culture with 9/10 employees reportedly disapproving of CEO Hale, and the departure of both the CFO and General Counsel alongside ~27% annual attrition from a 75-person team signals systemic leadership issues that could impair strategic execution [3]. D/E of 130% is manageable but leaves limited flexibility for accretive acquisitions in a higher-rate environment. The stock trades 15% below its 52-week high of $12.89 with 10.2% short interest, suggesting market skepticism [5]. The dividend was suspended during COVID (2020-2021) with only 4 consecutive years of payments since resumption, raising reliability concerns for the next cyclical downturn. RevPAR growth guidance of 1.5-3.5% indicates the recovery cycle may be maturing [1].
