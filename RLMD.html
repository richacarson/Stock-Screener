Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Relmada Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biotechnology company developing transformative therapies for oncology and central nervous system conditions. Following the failure and termination of its lead depression drug REL-1017 (esmethadone) in 2024-2025, the company pivoted to NDV-01 (controlled-release intravesical formulation for non-muscle invasive bladder cancer, Phase 2/3) and sepranolone (CNS disorders). Headquartered in New York, NY.
17 Employees ·www.relmada.com

Investment Thesis
Relmada has rebuilt its pipeline following the REL-1017 depression drug failure with two promising clinical programs: NDV-01 targeting non-muscle invasive bladder cancer (Phase 3 RESCUE program launching mid-2026 with FDA-confirmed registrational pathways) and sepranolone for Prader-Willi syndrome (Phase 2 initiating mid-2026) [1]. The company significantly strengthened its financial position with a $160M private placement, ending Q1 2026 with $234M in cash — providing runway through 2029 to fund the NDV-01 Phase 3 program [3]. Both programs address genuine unmet medical needs with limited approved therapies, creating potential for premium pricing and accelerated regulatory pathways. The appointment of CBO Bipin Dalmia (uro-oncology expert) signals commitment to commercial readiness [2].
The bear case is substantial for a pre-revenue biotech with a history of clinical failures. REL-1017 failed in two Phase III trials, destroying significant shareholder value and triggering a securities fraud investigation [1]. Relmada has generated zero revenue in its 20+ year history, with cumulative net losses exceeding $400M. The new pipeline is entirely in-licensed (NDV-01 from Trigone, sepranolone from Asarina Pharma), creating dependency on partners. With only 17 employees, execution capacity for two simultaneous registrational programs is questionable. The ~$670M market cap represents pure clinical trial optionality — binary outcomes on Phase 2/3 results could drive the stock to zero or multiples. Q1 2026 EPS of -$0.22 missed consensus of -$0.19, indicating higher-than-expected cash burn [1]. Even with $234M in cash, Phase 3 oncology trials are expensive and timeline extensions remain a risk.
Key Catalysts
  1. NDV-01 Phase 3 RESCUE program initiation in mid-2026 with two FDA-confirmed registrational pathways for intermediate-risk and BCG-unresponsive bladder cancer [1]
  2. Sepranolone Phase 2 trial in Prader-Willi syndrome initiating mid-2026, targeting a rare disease with no approved treatments and potential orphan drug designation [1]
  3. $234M cash position with runway through 2029 eliminates near-term dilution risk and provides financial stability to complete the NDV-01 Phase 3 program [3]
Key Risks
  1. Clinical trial failure risk — Relmada's track record includes two Phase III failures (REL-1017), and both new programs face binary outcomes with no commercial revenue to cushion negative results [1]
  2. Execution capacity — running two registrational programs simultaneously with only 17 employees creates dependency on CROs and partners, with limited internal oversight capacity [2]
  3. Valuation optionality — the ~$670M market cap on zero revenue is entirely dependent on clinical trial success, with Q1 2026 EPS miss (-$0.22 vs -$0.19 consensus) suggesting burn rate may exceed expectations [1]
