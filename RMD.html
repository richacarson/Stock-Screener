Company Profile
Healthcare ·Medical Instruments & Supplies ·NYSE ·United States
ResMed is a global leader in cloud-connected medical devices and digital health solutions for sleep apnea, COPD, and other chronic respiratory conditions. Products include CPAP/BiPAP devices (AirSense platform), masks, and accessories, complemented by AI-enabled Smart Comfort personalized therapy, digital health platforms (myAir, AirView), and out-of-hospital care software (MatrixCare, Brightree). FY2026 (ended June 30) delivered $5.7B revenue (+10% YoY), EPS of $10.43 (+10%), and gross margin expansion of 290bps. CEO Mick Farrell named Chair of AdvaMed Board. Market cap ~$32B.

Investment Thesis
ResMed is a dominant medical device franchise with an innovation moat that keeps widening — FY2026 delivered $5.7B revenue (+10% YoY), EPS of $10.43 (+10%), and 290bps of gross margin expansion driven by supply chain productivity and the mix shift toward higher-margin digital health [1]. At 16.84x forward earnings with D/E of just 12.54%, ROE of 24.27%, and profit margin of 26.95%, ResMed offers best-in-class medtech fundamentals at a reasonable valuation. The GLP-1 drug threat has been definitively defused after three years of data — patients discontinue weight-loss drugs within a year while maintaining CPAP therapy, and ResMed is leveraging GLP-1 awareness to drive new sleep apnea diagnoses among prescribers [2]. Smart Comfort, the first FDA-cleared AI-enabled CPAP device, leverages 100M+ nights of proprietary sleep data to create a data moat that deepens with scale. The Inspire Impact Score of 54 (ALIGNED) with 14 positive ESG attributions and zero negative flags makes ResMed one of the strongest faith-aligned healthcare investments in the IOWN universe.
The primary risk is growth deceleration and the transition from share-capture to organic growth. FY2027 guidance of 5-7% core revenue growth represents significant deceleration from 10% in FY2026, as post-Philips recall market share gains are largely captured and tougher comparisons emerge [1]. At ~$32B market cap, the stock needs to justify premium medtech multiples without the tailwind of competitor weakness that boosted FY2024-2026 results. The PEG of ~1.68 reflects this deceleration concern. Digital health revenue (MatrixCare, Brightree) faces competitive pressure from larger health IT platforms, and the transition from transactional device sales to recurring SaaS revenue remains incomplete. A potential resurgence of the GLP-1 disruption narrative — if newer, more effective weight-loss drugs demonstrate sustained sleep apnea reduction in long-term clinical trials — could pressure the stock again despite current data being favorable. International expansion, particularly in China and emerging markets, carries regulatory and reimbursement uncertainty.
Key Catalysts
  1. Smart Comfort AI platform broader rollout in FY2027, improving CPAP adherence rates and creating a differentiated patient experience that drives mask replacement revenue and reduces therapy churn [2]
  2. Expansion of sleep apnea diagnosis rates through GLP-1 prescriber education and AI-driven screening tools — with 80% of sleep apnea cases undiagnosed globally, each percentage point improvement in diagnosis represents billions in addressable market expansion
  3. Continued gross margin expansion (290bps FY2026) from supply chain productivity, digital health mix shift, and price/volume leverage in masks and accessories, driving free cash flow growth to support $1.85B+ in annual capital returns
Key Risks
  1. FY2027 revenue growth guidance of 5-7% represents significant deceleration from 10% in FY2026, risking multiple compression if the market rerates ResMed from growth to mature medtech valuation [1]
  2. Post-Philips recall market share gains are largely captured — organic growth must now come from new patient diagnosis, geographic expansion, and digital health adoption rather than competitor weakness
  3. Potential resurgence of GLP-1 disruption narrative if next-generation weight-loss drugs demonstrate sustained sleep apnea reduction in long-term clinical trials, despite current three-year data being favorable for ResMed

The company's 100M+ nights of proprietary sleep data creates an AI moat that competitors cannot replicate.
