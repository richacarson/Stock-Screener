Company Profile
Healthcare ·Medical Devices & Equipment ·NYSE ·United States
ResMed is a global leader in cloud-connected medical devices and digital health solutions for sleep apnea, COPD, and other chronic respiratory conditions. Products include CPAP/BiPAP devices (AirSense platform), masks, and accessories, complemented by digital health platforms (myAir, AirView) and out-of-hospital care software (MatrixCare, Brightree). FY2026 revenue of $5.7B with ~$33B market cap.

Investment Thesis
ResMed delivered strong FY2026 results with $5.7B revenue (10% growth), non-GAAP EPS of $11.17 (17% growth), and $1.6B free cash flow, demonstrating the power of its dominant position in the $30B+ sleep apnea device market [1]. The GLP-1 drug threat has been definitively defused — three years of data show patients discontinue weight-loss drugs within a year while maintaining CPAP therapy, and ResMed is using the awareness to drive new sleep apnea diagnoses among GLP-1 prescribers [3]. The Smart Comfort AI platform, leveraging 100M+ nights of proprietary sleep data, creates a data moat that deepens with scale. Forward P/E of 19.25 is attractive for a medical device leader generating 27.5% net margins and 25.7% ROE with minimal leverage (D/E 13%).
The primary risk is decelerating growth. FY2027 guidance of 5-7% core revenue growth (down from 10% in FY2026) reflects headwinds from the Astral ventilator portfolio and tougher comparisons post-Philips recall benefit [1]. At ~$33B market cap, the stock needs to justify premium medtech multiples without the tailwind of competitor market share capture that boosted FY2024-2026 results. Digital health revenue (MatrixCare, Brightree) faces competitive pressure from larger health IT platforms, and the transition from transactional device sales to recurring SaaS revenue remains incomplete. International expansion, particularly in China and emerging markets, carries regulatory and reimbursement uncertainty that could cap the global addressable market opportunity.
Key Catalysts
  1. Smart Comfort AI platform broader rollout in 2026-2027, improving CPAP adherence rates and creating a differentiated patient experience that drives mask replacement revenue and reduces churn
  2. Expansion of sleep apnea diagnosis rates through GLP-1 prescriber education — with 80% of cases undiagnosed globally, each percentage point improvement represents billions in addressable market
  3. Capital return acceleration with $1.85B+ guided for FY2027 through buybacks and dividends, supported by $1.6B annual free cash flow generation
Key Risks
  1. FY2027 revenue growth guidance of 5-7% represents significant deceleration from 10% in FY2026, risking multiple compression if the market rerates from growth to value
  2. Post-Philips recall market share gains are largely captured — organic growth must now come from new patient diagnosis and geographic expansion rather than competitor weakness
  3. Potential resurgence of GLP-1 disruption narrative if newer, more effective weight-loss drugs demonstrate sustained sleep apnea reduction in long-term clinical trials

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
9
AI Risk Protection (8/9): ResMed's core business — manufacturing FDA-cleared CPAP devices, masks, and accessories — is structurally immune to AI disruption. Physical medical devices require clinical validation, regulatory clearance, manufacturing expertise, and supply chain infrastructure that AI cannot substitute [1][3]. The GLP-1 drug scare (Ozempic/Wegovy potentially reducing sleep apnea prevalence through weight loss) proved unfounded after three years of data — the majority of patients discontinue GLP-1 therapy within one year while remaining good CPAP patients, and ResMed has used the episode to educate physicians about sleep apnea screening [3]. The company's 100M+ nights of proprietary sleep data creates an AI moat that competitors cannot replicate. AI Opportunity (1/1): Smart Comfort is the first FDA-cleared AI-enabled medical device for personalized CPAP therapy, generating meaningful revenue enhancement through improved patient adherence and device attachment rates. The myAir digital health platform uses machine learning for patient engagement across millions of connected devices [3].
