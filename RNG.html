Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NYSE ·United States
RingCentral, Inc. provides cloud-based unified communications as a service (UCaaS) and contact center as a service (CCaaS) solutions for businesses worldwide. The company's flagship RingEX platform integrates phone, messaging, video, and fax across devices, infused with RingSense AI for personalized automation and intelligence. Products include RingCentral MVP, RingCX (AI-powered contact center), and RingCentral Video. Q2 FY2026 revenue was $657M (+5.9% YoY), total ARR reached $2.8B (+7% YoY), and AI ARR exceeds 10% of total and has doubled year-over-year. Named Gartner Magic Quadrant UCaaS Leader for 12th consecutive year. Founded in 1999 by Vlad Shmunis, headquartered in Belmont, California.
7,378 Employees ·www.ringcentral.com

Investment Thesis
RingCentral presents a compelling value case driven by AI monetization momentum and dramatically attractive forward valuation. At a forward P/E of 8.15 and PEG of 0.51, the market appears to price RNG as a declining legacy communications company while ignoring that AI ARR now exceeds 10% of total ARR and is doubling annually [1][4]. Q2 2026 revenue of $657M (+5.9% YoY) with total ARR of $2.8B (+7%) and non-GAAP operating margin expanding to 23.4% demonstrate the business is generating substantial cash flow — management targets $590M FCF in 2026 [1]. The 2026 guidance raise to $2.62-$2.64B revenue and $4.85-$5.01 non-GAAP EPS signals confidence in the trajectory. Founder Vlad Shmunis's direct leadership of the AI-first pivot, supported by Chief AI Officer Dr. Antonio Nucci and President/COO Kira Makagon, adds execution credibility [2]. The stock's 79% rally from July 2026 lows suggests the market is beginning to recognize the value, but forward multiples remain deeply discounted relative to peers.
The bear case is structural: the core UCaaS market is maturing with only 5.9% revenue growth as Microsoft Teams bundling continues to commoditize business communications [1]. Negative stockholders' equity and $1.25B debt load (against only $117M cash) reflect a leveraged balance sheet from years of GAAP losses and aggressive buybacks — the $1.14B net debt position limits strategic flexibility [4]. GAAP profit margin of just 4.27% means the company is only beginning its GAAP profitability journey despite being 27 years old, and the wide gap between trailing P/E (43.57x) and forward P/E (8.15x) relies on aggressive non-GAAP earnings expectations [4]. The organizational culture is strained — Glassdoor reviews cite quarterly layoffs, shifting quotas, and fear-based dynamics that could impair talent retention at a critical AI transformation moment [3]. Competition from Microsoft Teams/Copilot, Zoom AI Companion, and emerging AI-native communication platforms remains intense.
