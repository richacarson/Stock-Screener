Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United Kingdom
Roivant Sciences is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company operating through a decentralized 'Vant' model — a family of independently led companies focused on discovering, developing, and commercializing medicines. Lead asset brepocitinib (TYK2/JAK1 inhibitor) is expected to launch for dermatomyositis by end of September 2026, pending FDA approval. IMVT-1402 (FcRn antibody) is in registrational studies across multiple autoimmune indications. The company received $950M from Moderna in a lipid nanoparticle technology settlement in July 2026. Cash position of approximately $4.7B with only $107M in debt. Market cap is approximately $26.2B.
