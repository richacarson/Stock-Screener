Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United Kingdom
Roivant Sciences is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company operating through a decentralized 'Vant' model — a family of independently led subsidiaries focused on discovering, developing, and commercializing medicines. Lead programs include IMVT-1402 (FcRn antibody) in registrational studies across multiple autoimmune indications and mosliciguat (inhaled sGC activator) for pulmonary hypertension. The company received $950M from Moderna in a lipid nanoparticle technology settlement in 2026 and previously sold Telavant to Roche for $7.1B. Revenue for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 was $1.44M (down 34% YoY), with a net loss of $189.8M. Led by CEO Matt Gline since 2021, Roivant maintains a fortress balance sheet with minimal debt (D/E 2%) and has 9 buy / 2 hold analyst ratings [1][2].
