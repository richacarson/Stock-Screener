Company Profile
Consumer Discretionary ·Casinos & Gaming ·NYSE American ·United States
High Roller Technologies, Inc. is a micro-cap online gaming and prediction markets operator headquartered in Las Vegas, Nevada. Founded in 2021, the company operates premium real-money online casino brands HighRoller.com and Fruta.com, offering over 6,000 games from 90+ providers across multiple international jurisdictions. Under CEO Seth Young (appointed August 2025), the company is executing a strategic pivot from traditional iGaming toward U.S. prediction markets in partnership with Crypto.com, while exiting certain international markets. FY2025 revenue was $20.45M (-11.9% YoY), with Q2 2026 revenue declining 52% YoY to $2.81M as the market exits accelerate. Market cap approximately $68M.

Investment Thesis
High Roller Technologies offers speculative upside through its pivot to U.S. prediction markets with Crypto.com as a high-profile partner, backed by a clean balance sheet ($18M cash, D/E 3%) and manageable near-term cash burn [2][3]. The appointment of a Head of Applied AI and investment in compliance automation position the company for the regulatory complexity of state-by-state prediction markets launches. At $68M market cap, any success in capturing meaningful prediction markets share could generate outsized returns. The company's existing iGaming infrastructure and regulatory experience across multiple jurisdictions provide a foundation for the prediction markets buildout. FY2025 EPS of $0.37 (including discontinued operations) demonstrates the company has generated profit, even if the path to repeating that is uncertain [1].
The bear case is severe: High Roller is a micro-cap ($68M) company with rapidly declining revenue (-52% Q2 2026 YoY), widening operating losses (-$2.5M Q2 operating loss), and a business model in flux [2]. The FY2025 net income of $3.16M was entirely dependent on a $2.47M gain from discontinued operations — the continuing business lost $6.2M at the operating level [1]. The prediction markets pivot is unproven, entering a space dominated by well-capitalized competitors (Kalshi, Polymarket, and potentially DraftKings/FanDuel). The $25M direct offering dilutes existing shareholders while funding an uncertain strategic bet [5]. Liquidity is critically thin — daily dollar volume sits at approximately $1M, right at the IOWN liquidity floor, meaning any sustained volume decline could render the stock uninvestable [4]. The trailing PE of 42.29x prices in growth that has not materialized, and with three of four key growth metrics unavailable, the valuation is difficult to anchor. Management instability (new CEO, COO, and multiple C-suite appointments in under 12 months) raises execution risk for a complex regulatory-heavy market entry [3].
