Investment Thesis
High Roller Technologies is attempting a strategic pivot from pure iGaming to U.S. prediction markets, partnering with Crypto.com and investing in AI capabilities under newly appointed Head of Applied AI Nicholis Muller [2]. The company has $22.5M in cash with no debt after a $25M raise, providing runway for the prediction markets launch. At $69M market cap, successful execution in the rapidly growing prediction markets space could deliver significant upside from a low base.
The bear case is compelling. Revenue declined 14.9% TTM to $18.6M with Q1 2026 showing a 35% YoY decline to just $3.4M and a $3.0M net loss [1]. The company has only 57 employees and a CEO with less than one year of tenure, competing against DraftKings, FanDuel, and Kalshi in prediction markets — companies with hundreds of millions in revenue and massive user bases. The $25M raise diluted shareholders while the prediction markets product has not launched or generated any revenue. NYSE American compliance was only regained in April 2026 after falling below the $4M equity threshold, and the 52-week price range of $1.16 to $33.68 reflects extreme volatility [3]. Average daily traded value of $493K makes the stock illiquid for any institutional investor. The gambling industry faces ongoing regulatory uncertainty in multiple jurisdictions.
