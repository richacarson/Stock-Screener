In June 2026, long-time CEO Corey Thomas transitioned to Executive Chairman focused on AI strategy, with Wael Mohamed assuming CEO responsibilities. Investment Thesis
Rapid7 at 7.52x forward earnings and 0.26x PEG offers deep value in cybersecurity — a sector with durable secular demand driven by escalating AI-generated threats and regulatory compliance requirements. Q2 2026 delivered $31.9M in free cash flow (15.1% margin) and full-year guidance of ~$130M FCF (~15.5% margin) demonstrates the profitability pivot is working, with non-GAAP operating income of $28.9M (13.7% margin) expanding sequentially [2]. The $824M ARR base provides recurring revenue visibility, with detection and response (~55% of ARR) still growing ~5% YoY. At a ~$700M market cap, the ~18.6% FCF yield is compelling if the revenue base stabilizes. Insider buying of $15.1M by CEO Thomas and directors signals leadership conviction [4].
The bear case is severe: ARR declined to $824M from $837M a year ago, and Q3 guidance of ~$812M implies continued contraction through year-end — signaling persistent competitive displacement by CrowdStrike and Palo Alto Networks who grow 15-30% while Rapid7 shrinks [2]. D/E of 552% from $965M in convertible note debt against $670M cash creates refinancing risk if the turnaround stalls [4]. Wholesale C-suite turnover (CEO, CFO, CCO, CPTO replaced within 12 months) and declining Glassdoor ratings (3.4/5) risk talent attrition during the AI-SOC pivot that requires deep technical execution [3]. GAAP profit margin of just 2.61% exposes the gap between non-GAAP presentation and actual profitability — stock-based compensation and amortization consume most of the operating income.
