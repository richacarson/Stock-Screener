Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Cybersecurity ·NASDAQ ·United States
Rapid7, Inc. provides cloud-native cybersecurity solutions including vulnerability management (InsightVM), detection and response (InsightIDR), and managed detection and response (MDR) services through its Command Platform. The company serves over 11,000 customers globally. Founded in 2000, headquartered in Boston, Massachusetts. Recently appointed Wael Mohamed as CEO to drive an AI-powered security operations center (AI SOC) strategy, with the acquisition of Kenzo Security's agentic AI platform.

Investment Thesis
Rapid7 trades at a compelling valuation — forward P/E of 5.9x and PEG of 0.26 — following a 61% stock price decline over 52 weeks that prices in significant pessimism [5]. Q1 2026 showed revenue of $210M beating consensus ($208M) and non-GAAP EPS of $0.36 exceeding the $0.30 estimate, with free cash flow of $33.4M demonstrating strong cash generation even as the top line contracts [1]. The new CEO Wael Mohamed brings 30 years of cybersecurity experience and is driving an AI-SOC strategy centered on the Kenzo Security acquisition (agentic AI platform), positioning Rapid7 to leverage AI as an enabler of autonomous threat detection and response [2]. With $670M in total liquidity and $125-135M in expected FY2026 free cash flow, the balance sheet supports a multi-quarter transformation despite high nominal leverage.
Revenue is contracting — FY2026 guidance of $836-842M represents a 2-3% YoY decline, and ARR of $832M is down 0.6% YoY, indicating persistent competitive displacement by CrowdStrike and Palo Alto Networks who are integrating AI security capabilities faster and at greater scale [1]. D/E of 552% from $965M in convertible notes creates refinancing risk if the turnaround stalls, and GAAP profit margin of only 2.6% reveals that non-GAAP profitability depends on excluding substantial stock-based compensation [5]. Glassdoor ratings declined 10% to 3.4/5 as employees cite cultural disruption from wholesale leadership changes, with reviews noting that 'new senior leadership translated company failures as a People problem' [2]. The CEO transition from long-tenured Corey Thomas to operator Wael Mohamed adds execution risk during the most critical competitive period in cybersecurity as AI reshapes the entire threat landscape.
