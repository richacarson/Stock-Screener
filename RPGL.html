Republic Power Group Limited provides customized enterprise resource planning (ERP) software solutions, consulting and technical support services, and peripheral hardware to corporate clients and government agencies in Singapore and Malaysia. The company integrates AI and IoT capabilities into its ERP solutions and is developing a SaaS subscription-based ERP product. Incorporated in 2015 and headquartered in Singapore, RPGL is a subsidiary of True Sage International Limited.

Republic Power Group is a nano-cap Singapore-based ERP software provider with $3.0M in annual revenue, 8 employees, and a $6M market cap [1][4]. The stock trades at a nominal 1.8x trailing P/E with 12% profit margins and 79% gross margins, suggesting the core ERP consulting business is profitable when projects are active. The company is developing a SaaS subscription-based ERP product expected to launch in Q3 FY2026, which could provide more predictable recurring revenue [1]. The NVT strategic partnership for blockchain-enabled asset tokenization announced in February 2026 represents a speculative growth avenue [3]. AI and IoT integration in ERP solutions positions the company to pursue specialized projects requiring advanced integrated solutions [2].
The bear case is overwhelming for any institutional-quality investor. Republic Power fails the IOWN liquidity floor with daily traded value of approximately $66K-$248K versus the $1M minimum [4]. The company disclosed an accounting discrepancy in November 2025 that triggered a 43% stock decline, received multiple Nasdaq non-compliance notices (bid price deficiency, late filings), and executed two reverse stock splits within four months (1-for-20 in February, 1-for-40 in May 2026) to avoid delisting [3][4]. The subsidiary relationship with True Sage International Limited creates complex governance dynamics for minority shareholders. Market capitalization has collapsed from $20M to approximately $6M, and the stock has lost over 99% of its value from its October 2025 all-time high.
