Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biopharmaceutical Royalties ·NASDAQ ·United States
Royalty Pharma is the largest buyer of biopharmaceutical royalties and a funder of innovation across the biopharma industry. It holds royalties on about 35 marketed therapies and 20 development-stage candidates in rare disease, oncology, neuroscience, infectious disease, hematology and diabetes. Founded in 1996 by CEO Pablo Legorreta, it is based in New York. Q2 2026 Royalty Receipts grew 14% to $768M, and full-year 2026 Portfolio Receipts guidance was raised to $3.40-3.50B [1][6].

Investment Thesis
Royalty Pharma is a highly cash-generative, AI-insulated compounder with diversified exposure to about 35 marketed therapies. Q2 2026 Royalty Receipts rose 14% to $768M, and management raised 2026 Portfolio Receipts guidance for the second time to $3.40-3.50B, driven by Tremfya, Voranigo, Imdelltra and Evrysdi [1][7]. TTM free cash flow of about $3.0B equals a 10% yield on a $29.9B market cap. The stock trades at about 11.6x FY2027 consensus EPS of $5.81, and consensus sees EPS of $7.13 by FY2029 [3][4]. The model carries 89% gross margins and no manufacturing or commercial risk, with royalty growth guided at 7-10% in 2026 [1].
Royalty income runs off as patents expire. 2026 guidance already absorbs Promacta loss of exclusivity, a biosimilar Tysabri launch and Inflation Reduction Act price negotiation, so growth requires continual redeployment into new deals at attractive returns [7]. Leverage is meaningful at a D/E of 131% and net debt of 4.7x EBITDA, and GAAP earnings are volatile: Q2 2026 GAAP EPS was $0.04 versus $1.32 adjusted, and reported revenue of $674M missed some estimates [3][12]. The stock has rallied to the top of its 52-week range ($36.03-$67.71), insiders are steady net sellers, and the new AI program adds cost without proven deal-selection benefit [5][9].
Key Catalysts
  1. Q3 2026 results and a possible third raise to 2026 Portfolio Receipts guidance (currently $3.40-3.50B)
  2. Continued ramp of newer royalty drivers (Voranigo, Imdelltra, Tremfya, Evrysdi) offsetting Promacta and Tysabri erosion
  3. Large new royalty or R&D-funding transactions, which are excluded from guidance and add to growth
Key Risks
  1. Patent cliffs and biosimilar entry (Promacta LOE, Tysabri biosimilar) eroding legacy royalty streams faster than new deals replace them
  2. IRA Medicare price negotiation reducing royalty-bearing net sales on key products
  3. Leverage of about 4.7x net debt to EBITDA and binary clinical risk on development-stage funding commitments
