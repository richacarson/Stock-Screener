Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Royalty Pharma is the world's largest buyer of biopharmaceutical royalties, with a portfolio of royalties on approximately 35 marketed therapies and 20 development-stage candidates spanning rare disease, oncology, neuroscience, infectious disease, hematology, and diabetes. Founded in 1996 by Pablo Legorreta, the company has deployed $30B+ in capital to advance innovation across the drug development ecosystem, collaborating with academic institutions, biotechs, and global pharma companies. Q2 2026 Royalty Receipts grew 14% YoY to $768M driven by Tremfya, Voranigo, Imdelltra, and Evrysdi, and the company raised full-year 2026 guidance for Portfolio Receipts. Market cap ~$27B.

Investment Thesis
Royalty Pharma delivered strong H1 2026 results with Q2 Royalty Receipts growing 14% YoY to $768M driven by Tremfya, Voranigo, Imdelltra, and Evrysdi, prompting the company to raise full-year 2026 guidance for Portfolio Receipts [1]. At a forward P/E of just 9.0x and PEG of 0.72 — remarkably cheap for a business with 32% profit margin and predictable royalty cash flows — the market appears to over-discount patent cliff and biosimilar risks [5]. H1 2026 total income reached $1.30B (up 13% from H1 2025), and Q2 adjusted EBITDA and Portfolio Cash Flow each reached $736M, reflecting the extraordinarily lean operating model where costs run at just 4.8% of Portfolio Receipts [1]. The company's $30B+ lifetime deployment track record under Legorreta's 30-year leadership, combined with the expansion into academic institution partnerships, demonstrates a sustainable deal pipeline.
The bear case centers on growth durability and portfolio concentration risk. While the company raised 2026 guidance, GAAP revenue has been largely flat over the past 5 years (~$2.3-2.4B annually), with the more relevant Portfolio Receipts metric showing lumpy growth driven by milestone payments [1]. The $8.97B total debt load, while manageable against current cash flows, creates refinancing risk if interest rates remain elevated. GAAP earnings are extremely volatile — Q2 2026 net income of only $17.9M vs Q1's $294.7M — driven by mark-to-market adjustments on equity investments that obscure the underlying cash generation [1]. The $115M in net insider selling over the past 12 months signals reduced conviction from those with the deepest portfolio knowledge [4]. Medicare drug price negotiation under the Inflation Reduction Act could reduce future royalty values on negotiated drugs, and biosimilar competition for key therapies creates ongoing revenue replacement risk.

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
8
AI Risk Protection (8/9): Royalty Pharma's core business — owning contractual royalty streams on approved and development-stage biopharmaceutical therapies — is structurally immune to AI disruption. The business involves deeply regulated processes (FDA approval, patent law, pharmaceutical regulation) and legal/contractual ownership of cash flows that AI cannot substitute [2]. Drug development requires clinical trials, physical manufacturing, and regulatory approval that remain firmly in the physical world. Critically, AI-accelerated drug discovery actually INCREASES demand for RPRX's services by expanding the pipeline of drugs reaching approval and creating more royalty acquisition opportunities. The Q2 2026 growth in Tremfya, Voranigo, Imdelltra, and Evrysdi demonstrates how diverse therapeutic areas drive durable royalty streams regardless of AI trends [1]. AI Opportunity (0/1): The appointment of Lucas Glass as Head of AI for deal evaluation and sourcing is promising but pre-revenue — no AI products are shipping or generating quantifiable revenue improvements yet [2].
