Company Profile
Industrials ·Specialty Industrial Machinery ·NYSE ·United States
Regal Rexnord provides sustainable motion control and power management solutions through three segments: Automation & Motion Control (conveyors, aerospace components, precision motion, E-Pod data center power systems), Industrial Powertrain Solutions (bearings, couplings, gearboxes, clutches), and Power Efficiency Solutions (AC/DC motors, electronic drives, HVAC fans and blowers). Formed from the $3.7B merger of Regal Beloit and Rexnord in 2022. Aamir Paul joined as CEO in July 2026 from Schneider Electric. Revenue of ~$6.2B with $735M in E-Pod orders for data center projects. Founded 1955, headquartered in Milwaukee, Wisconsin.
28,700 Employees ·www.regalrexnord.com
