Company Profile
Consumer Cyclical ·Gambling ·NYSE ·United States
Rush Street Interactive, Inc. develops, supplies, and operates regulated online casino (iCasino) and sports betting platforms under the BetRivers and PlaySugarHouse brands across the United States, Canada, and Latin America. Co-founded and led by CEO Richard Schwartz since 2012, the company is headquartered in Chicago, Illinois, and has scaled through North American iCasino share gains and Latin American (Mexico, Colombia) market expansion, on a proprietary in-house technology platform.

Investment Thesis
Rush Street Interactive delivered record Q1 2026 results — revenue of $370.4M (+41% YoY), net income of $26.2M (+134%), and Adjusted EBITDA of $60.2M (+81%) — and raised full-year 2026 guidance to $1.49-1.54B revenue (+31-36%) and $230-250M Adjusted EBITDA (+50-63%), driven by North American iCasino share gains and accelerating Latin American growth (543,000 MAUs, +54% YoY) [1]. The balance sheet is pristine for the sector, with a net cash position of $336.32M and D/E of just 2%, and the company's responsible-gaming leadership (first RG Check accreditation in the US) and deliberate casino-first competitive positioning provide some insulation as state-by-state legalization continues [1][3][6].
Valuation is demanding — a 93x trailing and 49x forward P/E price in years of continued 30%+ growth, and the 2.98% GAAP profit margin remains thin relative to the revenue base despite the strong EBITDA trajectory, leaving limited room for a growth deceleration surprise [6]. CEO Richard Schwartz has been a persistent and sizable net seller of stock — $4.35M in February, $1.99M in April, an additional April sale, and $4.94M in July 2026 alone, with zero offsetting purchases — a pattern that, combined with Colombia's 2025 tax-framework change (an estimated $70M revenue and $25-30M EBITDA hit), tempers the growth narrative [4]. RSI's Inspire Insight faith-alignment score of -1 also reflects a negative 'Gambling' attribution that will disqualify it for many values-based mandates regardless of financial performance.
