Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NMS ·United States
Retension Pharmaceuticals is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing RTN-001, an oral, once-daily phosphodiesterase-5 (PDE-5) inhibitor designed to reduce blood pressure by potentiating nitric oxide signaling. The company targets uncontrolled and resistant hypertension, a condition affecting approximately 20% of the 120 million U.S. hypertension patients. Founded in 2023 and headquartered in Falls Church, Virginia, Retension IPO'd on October 9, 2026 at $12 per share, raising $45M. The company is pre-revenue with a net loss of approximately $12M for the twelve months ended June 30, 2026, and had $13.5M in cash pre-IPO.

Investment Thesis
Retension Pharmaceuticals at $14.07 is a clinical-stage bet on RTN-001, a once-daily oral PDE-5 inhibitor targeting uncontrolled and resistant hypertension affecting approximately 24 million Americans with inadequate current treatment options [3]. The company raised $45M in its October 2026 IPO (upsized from original $36M target), providing approximately 3-4 years of runway at current burn rates, and CEO Eric Keller's immediate post-IPO purchase of $249,600 in shares signals insider conviction [1][4]. The Phase 2b trial initiated October 2025 with data expected H1 2027 represents a near-term catalyst, and the hypertension market's massive addressable population ($20B+ globally) provides significant upside if efficacy and safety data are favorable.
The bear case is binary clinical risk in a single-asset, pre-revenue company with no financial moat. Retension has zero revenue, zero approved products, and its entire $117M market cap rests on one Phase 2b trial outcome that will not read out until H1 2027 [1]. The company's accounting restatement for advance CRO payments in its S-1 filing raises governance questions for a company with only 12 employees [3]. Competition in hypertension is fierce — established players like Novartis, AstraZeneca, and Bayer have extensive cardiovascular portfolios and resources that dwarf Retension's $58M post-IPO cash position. The PDE-5 mechanism is well-explored territory (sildenafil, tadalafil), and RTN-001 must demonstrate differentiation in a crowded mechanistic space. With no forward earnings estimates, zero analyst coverage, and a stock that has traded for only nine days, there is no margin of safety and the risk of total capital loss is material if Phase 2b data disappoint.
