Company Profile
Communication Services ·Internet Content & AI Infrastructure ·NASDAQ ·United States
RUM Group (formerly Rumble Inc.) operates a video-sharing and livestreaming platform positioned as a free-speech alternative to YouTube, serving 56 million monthly active users globally. In June 2026 the company closed its $970M acquisition of Northern Data AG, adding ~22,000 NVIDIA GPUs and European data center capacity, and restructured into two divisions: Rumble (creator/video platform) and Quake AI (AI compute/cloud infrastructure). Q2 2026 revenue reached $40.4M (+61% YoY), driven by the Northern Data consolidation. Founded 2013 by CEO Chris Pavlovski, headquartered in Longboat Key, Florida.

Investment Thesis
RUM Group is transforming from a niche video platform into a hybrid media-and-AI-infrastructure company with tangible assets. Q2 2026 revenue of $40.4M (+61% YoY) demonstrates accelerating top-line momentum from the Northern Data consolidation, and the $270M multiyear Together AI contract for Blackwell B300 GPU cloud capacity provides quantified near-term AI revenue visibility [2][3]. The core video platform's 56M MAUs with ARPU of just $0.40 offers significant monetization upside as Rumble Shorts advertising launches in H2 2026 [1]. A net-cash balance sheet (D/E ~1%, $217M cash) provides flexibility without dilution risk, and the Inspire Impact Score of 26 (ALIGNED) makes RUM one of the few tech-growth names accessible to faith-based investors.
The bear case centers on persistent unprofitability and integration risk. Q1 2026 net loss widened to $30.3M (vs. $2.7M prior year) with sales and marketing up 134%, and profit margin of -106.9% with ROE of -37.1% show no near-term path to breakeven [1]. The 2026 revenue forecast was cut from $460M to ~$240M, suggesting Northern Data integration is proceeding slower than expected [2]. Quake AI must compete against hyperscalers (AWS, Azure, GCP) in AI infrastructure with fewer than 300 employees — a capital-intensive business requiring execution capability that is unproven at this scale. The core video platform's ideological positioning limits its addressable advertising market, and ARPU of $0.40 vs. YouTube's ~$4+ per user suggests structural monetization challenges that the free-speech brand may not overcome even with Shorts launch [1]. Forward P/E is not applicable given negative earnings, making traditional valuation analysis impossible.
Key Catalysts
  1. Quake AI ramping toward Northern Data's raised EUR170-190M FY2026 revenue outlook as the $270M Together AI GPU cloud agreement for Blackwell B300 capacity enters full production [2][3]
  2. Rumble Shorts advertising monetization launching H2 2026 across the 56M MAU base, with potential to lift ARPU from $0.40 toward $1.00+ and dramatically improve platform-level profitability [1]
  3. Continued data center buildout leveraging ~200MW of energized and planned power capacity, positioning Quake AI to capture demand from AI compute-constrained enterprise customers unable to access hyperscaler capacity [2]
Key Risks
  1. Widening losses ($30.3M Q1 2026 net loss vs. $2.7M prior year) with S&M up 134% and no visible timeline to profitability — continued cash burn could force dilutive financing even with the current $217M cash position [1]
  2. Northern Data integration risk: 2026 revenue forecast slashed from $460M to ~$240M, and competing against AWS/Azure/GCP in AI infrastructure with <300 employees requires execution capability that is unproven at scale [2]
  3. Structural advertising ceiling from ideological positioning — major advertisers avoid politically polarizing platforms, capping ARPU and concentrating revenue risk in a narrow base of willing advertisers [1]
