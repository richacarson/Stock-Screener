Company Profile
Technology ·Solar ·NMS ·United States
Sunrun Inc. is the largest residential solar, battery storage, and energy services company in the United States, serving over 900,000 customers through customer agreements (leases and power purchase agreements) and direct system sales. TTM revenue reached $3.5B with 52.8% YoY growth driven primarily by energy storage system sales to a third-party purchaser under a 2025 transaction structure. The company also operates virtual power plant programs providing grid services to utilities. Founded in 2007 and headquartered in San Francisco, California, with 9,059 employees.

Investment Thesis
Sunrun at $7.64 with a $1.8B market cap is the dominant U.S. residential solar platform trading at a distressed valuation — 5.2x trailing earnings and 11.7x forward earnings despite 52.8% revenue growth in Q2 2026 to $870M [1][2]. The company's 900K+ customer fleet is the largest distributed energy asset in the country, increasingly monetizable through VPP programs and grid services as utilities seek flexible capacity. Storage attachment rates continue rising, and the energy storage system sales channel (up 193% in Q2) is diversifying revenue beyond traditional lease economics. With 8.1M+ metric tons of carbon emissions prevented and 30% of executive compensation tied to ESG metrics, Sunrun is positioned to benefit from the long-term secular shift toward distributed solar+storage as electricity demand surges from AI data centers and electrification [4].
The bear case is severe and immediate. Sunrun's balance sheet carries $15.2B in debt against $726M in cash, producing negative shareholder equity and -19.9% ROE — the stock has fallen from $60+ in 2021 to $7.64, destroying 87% of shareholder value [1]. Free cash flow is deeply negative at -$1.97B, and management cut FY2026 cash generation guidance to $200-375M from $250-450M, citing weaker affiliate volumes (down 70%+ YoY in Q2), the Freedom Forever bankruptcy, direct-sales ramp costs, and higher interest rates [2]. The 52.8% revenue growth is misleading — it's driven by a third-party storage sale transaction structure that began in Q3 2025, not organic customer growth. Muddy Waters' short thesis targeting Sunrun's financial model has not been fully resolved. The PEG ratio of 3.07 suggests the market expects growth to decelerate sharply, and with forward EPS of only $0.65 (down 56% from TTM $1.48), earnings are declining even as revenue inflates [2].
Key Catalysts
  1. Virtual power plant programs scaling as utilities seek distributed energy resources for grid reliability — Sunrun's 900K+ customer fleet represents the largest VPP-capable asset in the U.S., with growing revenue from grid services contracts [1]
  2. Storage attachment rates rising as battery costs decline and homeowner demand for energy resilience grows — storage-attached systems generate 40-60% higher lifetime value per customer and drive the energy systems revenue channel [2]
  3. AI data center electricity demand surge increasing national power consumption, benefiting distributed solar+storage as a grid-edge generation source and driving policy support for residential renewables [4]
Key Risks
  1. Balance sheet fragility with $15.2B in debt, negative equity, -$1.97B free cash flow, and cash generation guidance cut to $200-375M — rising interest rates directly compress the spread on solar leases and PPAs that define the business model [1][2]
  2. Revenue growth artificially inflated by third-party storage sale transaction structure (193% jump in energy systems revenue) masking fundamental weakness — affiliate volume declined 70%+ YoY in Q2, and Freedom Forever's bankruptcy removes a major installation partner [2]
  3. Regulatory risk from NEM 3.0-style net metering reforms spreading beyond California, reducing the economic value proposition for residential solar customers and potentially depressing new customer additions [4]
