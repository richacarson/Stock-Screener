Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Revolution Medicines is a precision oncology company that has developed the first approved targeted therapy against RAS-driven cancers, the most frequently mutated oncogene family in human cancers. On August 26, 2026, the FDA approved daraxonrasib (RASONQUE) for metastatic pancreatic adenocarcinoma, making it the first RAS GTPase family inhibitor approved for pancreatic cancer — a drug that doubled survival rates in clinical trials. The company's pipeline also includes zoldonrasib (RAS G12D selective inhibitor) in Phase III clinical trials for non-small cell lung cancer. Founded in 2014, headquartered in Redwood City, California, with a $44.5B market cap and $3.9B cash position.

AI Risk Protection (8/9): Drug development and commercialization are among the most structurally protected businesses from AI disruption — FDA regulatory pathways demand multi-year clinical trials with human subjects, physical drug manufacturing involves complex biochemical processes, and patent/regulatory exclusivity creates legal moats that AI cannot circumvent [4]. Daraxonrasib's FDA approval and manufacturing process are deeply physical-world operations that AI structurally cannot displace. Revolution Medicines' core competence in medicinal chemistry targeting RAS protein conformations requires decades of wet-lab expertise and institutional knowledge. The oncologist-patient treatment decision process involves deeply regulated prescription, insurance, and care coordination workflows that further insulate the business. The only AI-related exposure is that AI-driven drug discovery platforms could accelerate competitors' ability to develop rival RAS-targeting molecules, but RVMD's first-mover advantage with an approved product creates a significant head start.
