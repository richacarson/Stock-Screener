Company Profile
Healthcare ·Diagnostics & Research ·NYSE ·United States
Revvity, Inc. (formerly PerkinElmer, rebranded 2023) provides health sciences solutions, technologies, and services globally. Operates through Life Sciences (genomics, proteomics, drug discovery tools, Signals software) and Diagnostics (reproductive health screening, immunodiagnostics, applied genomics) segments. The company divested PerkinElmer's legacy food quality testing and enterprise services businesses in 2023 to focus exclusively on health sciences from discovery to cure. Q2 2026 revenue of $730M showed 4% pro forma growth, and management raised full-year 2026 guidance to $2.83-$2.86B revenue with $5.30-$5.40 adjusted EPS. Market cap is approximately $15.9B.

Revvity's Q2 2026 showed continued momentum — $730M revenue (4% pro forma growth), adjusted EPS of $1.41, and management raising full-year 2026 guidance to $2.83-$2.86B revenue with 4-5% organic growth and $5.30-$5.40 adjusted EPS [1]. The balance sheet is clean (D/E 46.2%) and free cash flow conversion remains strong at approximately 97%. The Signals software ecosystem expansion with AI platforms (Xynthetica, BioDesign, LabGistics acquired via Virtyx Technologies) positions Revvity at the intersection of life sciences and AI-driven discovery [2]. Inspire score of +57 with zero negative attributions provides strong faith-alignment support [3].
The primary concern is valuation relative to organic growth. At 68.65x trailing GAAP P/E and only 1.3% TTM revenue growth, RVTY requires sustained acceleration to justify the premium multiple [4]. GAAP profitability remains structurally challenged — ROE of 3.2% and profit margin of 8.2% reflect $7.5B of goodwill-related amortization from transformation-era acquisitions, creating a persistent gap between adjusted (approximately $5.35 EPS) and reported ($2.07 EPS) earnings. The China immunodiagnostics divestiture creates near-term EPS headwinds despite improving the portfolio focus. AI platform monetization (Xynthetica, BioDesign, LabGistics) is unproven and faces competition from larger players (Thermo Fisher, Danaher, Benchling) with deeper customer relationships in drug discovery workflows [2]. Glassdoor reviews citing constant layoffs and leadership challenges raise questions about whether the transformation is being executed at the expense of employee morale [2]. The stock has rallied significantly, potentially front-running the recovery and limiting near-term upside.

AI Risk Protection (7/9): Revvity's core business — diagnostic instruments, reagents, genetic screening consumables, and neonatal screening services — involves physical-world products and highly regulated clinical workflows that AI structurally cannot replace [1]. FDA/CE-marked diagnostic assays require extensive clinical validation, physical sample processing, and reagent chemistry that AI cannot substitute. Lab instruments (mass spectrometers, plate readers, automated liquid handling) demand hardware manufacturing capabilities beyond AI's reach. AI enhances data interpretation but cannot displace the underlying physical testing infrastructure. Pharma/biotech customers reported organic growth recovery with AI-enhanced discovery tools expanding Revvity's addressable market rather than cannibalizing it [1]. AI Opportunity (1/1): Xynthetica (AI-models-as-a-service), BioDesign (cloud-native molecular design for biologics), and the Virtyx Technologies acquisition represent concrete AI product development streams targeting the drug discovery workflow, building new revenue opportunities on top of the existing instrument and reagent platform [2].
