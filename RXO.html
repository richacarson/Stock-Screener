Company Profile
Industrials ·Freight & Logistics Services ·NYSE ·United States
RXO, Inc. is a tech-enabled asset-light transportation solutions provider spun off from XPO Logistics in November 2022 and headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina. The company operates one of the largest truck brokerage platforms in North America, complemented by managed transportation and last-mile delivery services. Under CEO Drew Wilkerson, RXO is deploying agentic AI and digital bidding technology to reshape freight brokerage, having automated 500,000+ phone calls in Q2 2026 and launched AI-powered pricing and truck check-in systems. Q2 2026 revenue climbed 25% YoY to $1.77B with spot freight mix surging to 42% of truckload volume (+1,500bps YoY). Management describes the company as 'early in a multiyear recovery.' Market cap approximately $3.7B.

Investment Thesis
RXO is an AI-first freight brokerage at an inflection point — Q2 2026 revenue surged 25% YoY to $1.77B with spot mix climbing to 42% of truckload volume (+1,500bps YoY), driving the highest TL gross profit per load in four years (+11% sequential) [1]. The company's agentic AI technology (500K+ automated phone calls in Q2), AI-powered pricing, and digital bidding platform represent the most aggressive AI deployment in the freight brokerage sector, positioning RXO to capture disproportionate share as the market recovers [3]. Management's view that the company is 'early in a multiyear recovery' suggests substantial earnings upside from normalization — the forward PE of 71x reflects trough-level earnings, not sustainable profitability [2]. The Coyote Logistics acquisition added scale, and the Inspire Impact Score of 42 (ALIGNED) with zero negative attributions makes RXO accessible to faith-based investors seeking logistics exposure [4].
The bear case is significant: RXO remains unprofitable with -6.79% ROE and -1.72% profit margin on $5.74B in LTM revenue, generating $100M in losses [5]. The forward PE of 71.14x is above the 60x floor used in IOWN infrastructure scoring, reflecting trough earnings that may not recover as quickly as bulls expect. Freight brokerage is one of the sectors most directly threatened by AI disruption — the traditional information asymmetry that generates broker margins is being eroded by AI platforms that match freight to capacity more efficiently, as evidenced by C.H. Robinson's stock decline on AI disruption fears [3]. RXO's own AI deployment may not be sufficient to offset industry-wide margin compression if every broker adopts similar technology. The company has limited operating history as a standalone entity (spun off November 2022), making long-term financial predictability low. Debt of 43% D/E is manageable but represents leverage taken on for the Coyote acquisition that must be serviced through a cyclical recovery with uncertain timing. If the freight market recovery stalls — due to economic slowdown, trade policy shifts, or capacity oversupply — RXO's losses could deepen before profitability is achieved [2].

AI Risk Protection (4/9): Freight brokerage is one of the industries most directly exposed to AI disruption. The traditional broker value proposition — matching shipper demand with carrier capacity using information advantages and relationship networks — is precisely what AI platforms can automate more efficiently [3]. C.H. Robinson saw its stock plunge as investors priced in AI-driven margin compression, and headlines like 'AI Disrupts the Freight Industry' signal that analysts view this as real, not theoretical. AI-native competitors (Convoy's technology, Uber Freight, digital-first brokers) are already eroding traditional information asymmetry. However, RXO's scale (top-5 broker, $5.74B revenue), established carrier networks, shipper relationships, and regulatory complexity provide some defensibility that pure technology cannot easily replicate. Physical logistics ultimately requires physical trucks and human drivers, creating a floor beneath the brokerage model. AI Opportunity (1/1): RXO is actively generating revenue from AI deployment — agentic AI automated 500K+ phone calls in Q2 2026, AI-driven pricing is deployed at scale across millions of data points, and AI-powered truck check-in technology serves warehouse customers [3]. CEO Wilkerson has made AI the centerpiece of RXO's competitive strategy, positioning the company as an AI-first broker rather than a traditional one adapting to AI.
