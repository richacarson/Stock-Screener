Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Recursion Pharmaceuticals is a Salt Lake City-based clinical-stage TechBio company operating Recursion OS, an AI-native, end-to-end drug discovery and development platform integrating biology, chemistry, and clinical development. The company has compressed target-identification-to-clinical-entry timelines from an industry-standard 5 years to roughly 18 months and has realized more than $500 million in validated partnership inflows to date from collaborators including Sanofi, Bayer, and a $50 million strategic investment and multi-year foundation-model collaboration with NVIDIA [1][2]. Najat Khan became CEO on January 1, 2026, succeeding co-founder Chris Gibson (now board chair), after the company cut 20% of its workforce in 2025 to narrow pipeline focus and extend its cash runway into Q4 2027 [3].

Investment Thesis
Recursion Pharmaceuticals is one of the clearest AI-native businesses in the coverage universe — Recursion OS has compressed drug discovery timelines from 5 years to 18 months, generated over $500 million in validated partnership inflows from Sanofi and Bayer, and attracted a $50 million NVIDIA strategic investment and foundation-model collaboration [1][2]. New CEO Najat Khan's 2026 mandate — converting AI participation into 'clinical proof and durable value' via five named programs with defined 12-18-month inflection points — gives the story near-term, falsifiable catalysts, and the balance sheet (7% D/E, $654M+ cash, runway into Q4 2027) provides room to execute without near-term financing risk [1][3].
The bear case is severe cash burn and shareholder dilution risk set against a still-tiny revenue base: TTM revenue of $66.41M against a $559.78M net loss (profit margin near -843%) and ROE of -57.16% reflect a company years from self-sustaining economics, and Q1/Q2 2026 revenue came in well below analyst forecasts (-60% and -37% misses respectively), even as YoY growth held at 11% [1]. The 2025 workforce reduction of 20% and NVIDIA's full 13F exit from its equity position (while the commercial collaboration continues) both raise questions about conviction from sophisticated observers, and insiders have sold exclusively — zero buys against 38 sells in the trailing year. RXRX is best understood as a high-optionality bet on AI-native drug discovery reaching clinical proof points over the next 12-18 months, appropriate for a HOLD pending evidence the narrowed pipeline delivers durable value.
