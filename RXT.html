Rackspace Technology is a hybrid cloud and AI solutions company operating through Public Cloud and Private Cloud segments. Q2 2026 revenue was $670M (+0.6% YoY) with Public Cloud at $407M and Private Cloud at $264M. FY2026 guidance is $2.6-2.7B total revenue with $160-170M non-GAAP operating profit.
