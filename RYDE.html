Ryde Group Ltd. is a Singapore-based mobility and quick commerce platform offering ride-hailing, carpooling, and parcel delivery services. Differentiates through a 0% commission model for drivers, positioning as a driver-first alternative to Grab and Gojek in the Singapore market. Founded in 2014 by Terence Zou and listed on NYSE American in March 2024.
