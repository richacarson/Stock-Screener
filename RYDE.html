Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·AMEX ·Singapore
Ryde Group is a Singapore-based technology company operating a ride-hailing, carpooling, and delivery platform. Founded in 2014 by Terence Zou, Ryde became the first Singaporean ride-hailing company listed on NYSE American in 2024. The company differentiates through a 0% commission model for driver-partners and has expanded into electric vehicle (EV) mobility services. Q1 2026 revenue was S$3.77M (US$2.93M, +38% YoY) with a narrowing net loss of S$2.07M. Total assets of S$42.95M and shareholders' equity of S$31.12M as of March 2026. Market cap is approximately $110M.

Investment Thesis

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience HIGH RISK
4
AI Risk Protection (4/9): Ryde's core business — matching riders with driver-partners through a mobile platform — faces significant long-term disruption risk from autonomous vehicles (AVs). If AV technology matures in Southeast Asia, the entire driver-partner model that generates Ryde's revenue could become obsolete [2]. Companies like Waymo, Cruise, and Baidu are already operating commercial robotaxi services in select markets. Singapore, with its well-mapped urban environment and progressive regulatory stance on autonomous transport, could adopt AVs faster than many countries. In the near term (3-5 years), regulatory hurdles, infrastructure requirements, and consumer trust issues provide a buffer, but the structural threat is real. Additionally, Grab's massive data advantage from millions of daily rides in Southeast Asia creates an AI-powered competitive moat in pricing optimization, demand prediction, and driver allocation that Ryde cannot match at its current scale. AI Opportunity (0/1): The automated job matching feature being developed for Q4 2026 rollout is AI-adjacent but not yet generating revenue. Ryde does not sell AI products or services externally.
