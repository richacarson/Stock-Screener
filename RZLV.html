Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Infrastructure ·NASDAQ ·United Kingdom
Rezolve AI PLC is a London-headquartered AI-powered commerce platform providing conversational AI shopping, product discovery, and checkout solutions to enterprise retailers. Founded in 2016 by serial entrepreneur Daniel M. Wagner (who previously founded MAID/Dialog and Powa Technologies), Rezolve went public via SPAC merger and grew primarily through acquisitions of ViSenze and GroupBy. The company reports preliminary H1 2026 revenue of approximately $127M (nearly 20x H1 2025) and has reaffirmed full-year 2026 guidance of $360M. Market cap is approximately $1.09B with 573 employees. A September 2025 short seller report from Fuzzy Panda Research raised serious allegations about revenue quality and CEO history.

Full-year 2026 guidance is $360M, implying H2 revenue of $233M+ (83%+ sequential growth). The bear case is dominated by governance and revenue quality concerns. The report also alleged self-dealing ($93.9M in share-based compensation to Wagner's entity) and questionable partnership economics ($147.7M paid to Microsoft with minimal return). Net loss of $101.4M on $46.8M TTM revenue produces a -216.7% profit margin, and the company remains deeply unprofitable even as revenue scales [2]. The stock has declined 68% from its 52-week high of $8.45 to $2.74, suggesting the market is pricing in significant skepticism despite the Strong Buy consensus. Glassdoor reviews cite leadership misalignment and employees unable to articulate what the company sells.
