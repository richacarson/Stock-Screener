Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Infrastructure ·NASDAQ ·United States
Sabre Corporation operates as a software and technology company for the global travel industry. Through its marketplace, the company connects travel suppliers (airlines, hotels, car rentals) with travel buyers (agencies, corporate travel departments). Sabre divested its Hospitality Solutions division to TPG Capital in 2025, focusing on its core Travel Distribution and Airline IT businesses. The company is undergoing a 'fundamental transition' from a traditional global distribution system (GDS) to an AI-native technology platform under CEO Kurt Ekert, with AI-native APIs, Model Context Protocol (MCP) integration, and agentic AI capabilities.

Investment Thesis
Sabre Corporation offers a deep-value turnaround thesis anchored in its AI-native transformation. The company completed a five-year cloud migration from mainframe to cloud-native architecture and is now building AI-native APIs, MCP integration, and agentic AI capabilities under CEO Kurt Ekert's leadership [2][3]. Q1 2026 revenue grew 8% YoY to $760M with adjusted EBITDA up 21% to $159M, and full-year 2026 EBITDA guidance of $585M demonstrates operational momentum [1]. The trailing PE of 1.61x (distorted by the Hospitality Solutions sale gain) and $2.23 stock price offer asymmetric upside if the AI transformation repositions Sabre as the infrastructure backbone for AI-powered travel planning rather than a disruption victim. The company repaid over $1B of debt in 2025, reducing net debt to $3.74B, and the travel data cloud — among the largest globally — provides a competitive moat that AI competitors need access to rather than replace [2].
The bear case is dominated by existential leverage and AI disruption risk. Total debt of $4.27B against negative equity and a $880M market cap means equity holders are deeply subordinated — any stumble in the recovery could wipe out equity value entirely [6]. The company has issued $500M in new 10.75% notes due 2029 and $150M in exchangeable notes to manage maturities, reflecting ongoing refinancing pressure at high interest rates [5]. Revenue of $2.83B TTM remains 27% below the pre-COVID peak of $3.87B, and Q2 2026 growth decelerated to just 4% YoY with a net loss of $36M [1]. The AI disruption threat is genuine: if AI-powered travel agents bypass the GDS entirely through direct airline/hotel APIs and NDC standards, Sabre's intermediary role becomes structurally impaired. February 2026 layoffs, ESG executive departures, and declining Glassdoor scores (3.8/5, -5% YoY) signal organizational stress [3][4][5]. The Inspire Insight score of -49 (MISALIGNED) limits faith-based investor interest. Forward P/E of 58.36x implies minimal GAAP profitability after massive interest expense consumes the $585M EBITDA.
