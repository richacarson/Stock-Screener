Company Profile
Energy ·Renewable Fuels ·NASDAQ ·United States
XCF Global, Inc. manufactures and sells renewable fuels in the United States, developing and operating clean fuel production facilities to produce sustainable aviation fuel (SAF), renewable diesel, and renewable naphtha. Its flagship asset is New Rise Renewables Reno, a facility with 38 million gallon per year permitted nameplate capacity in Nevada. Following a facility upgrade and restart, the plant is now producing renewable fuels at approximately 40 degrees lower process temperatures. The company signed a Joint Commercialization and Development Agreement in June 2026 with Continual Renewable Ventures for a proposed New Rise ANZ facility in Australia targeting 175 million liters per year [1]. CEO Chris Cooper, formerly President of Neste U.S., was appointed Chairman in May 2026. The company targets 2027 net revenue of $110-120M, EBITDA of $65-70M from 40-43 million gallons production [1]. Stock trades at $0.36, received a Nasdaq noncompliance notice in December 2025 for trading below $1.00.

Investment Thesis
XCF Global offers pure-play exposure to the secular SAF growth opportunity supported by ICAO CORSIA mandates, EU blending requirements, and U.S. energy security priorities. The Reno facility is operational with 38M gallon capacity, initial renewable diesel sales have commenced, and 2027 targets of $110-120M net revenue and $65-70M EBITDA represent transformative upside from the current $20.82M TTM revenue base [1]. The June 2026 Australia JDA adds international expansion optionality. CEO Cooper's background as President of Neste U.S. — the global SAF leader — provides genuine industry credibility [2].
The bear case is overwhelming. D/E of 678% and Altman-Z of -0.80 indicate existential financial risk — the company converted $16.7M in payables to equity to avoid covenant breach, and Nasdaq issued a noncompliance notice for trading below $1.00 since December 2025 [3]. Three C-suite departures in 12 months raise execution credibility concerns for a 53-person company. Forward EPS consensus of -$0.19 for FY2026 confirms the business is not yet self-sustaining. The stock has collapsed from a 52-week high of $45.90 to $0.36, destroying over 99% of shareholder value. The 2027 targets require 5-6x revenue growth in approximately 18 months with no clear path to funding the operational ramp. The current ratio of 0.11 indicates severe near-term liquidity constraints [3]. SELL reflects present financial reality, not long-term market opportunity.
