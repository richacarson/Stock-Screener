Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Infrastructure ·NASDAQ ·United States
SailPoint, Inc. is the leader in identity governance and administration (IGA), providing enterprise software that helps organizations govern digital identities across their IT environments. Founded by Mark McClain, the company was taken private by Thoma Bravo in 2022 for $6.9B, then re-IPO'd in January 2025 raising $1.4B. Its Atlas cloud platform provides unified identity security for human and non-human identities, including AI agents. Q1 FY2027 (ended April 2026) delivered $1.163B ARR (+26% YoY), $781M SaaS ARR (+36% YoY), and $280M revenue (+22% YoY). FY2027 guidance calls for $1.369B ARR, $1.27B revenue, 19% adjusted operating margin, and ~$200M free cash flow.
3,229 Employees ·www.sailpoint.com

Investment Thesis
SailPoint is a rare identity security pure-play at $1.16B ARR scale, positioned at the intersection of cybersecurity's most critical domain and AI's most urgent governance challenge [1][2]. Q1 FY2027 delivered $1.163B ARR (+26% YoY), $781M SaaS ARR (+36%), and 22% revenue growth, with FY2027 guidance calling for $1.369B ARR, $1.27B revenue, 19% adjusted operating margin, and ~$200M FCF [1]. The Atlas platform transformation created a modern multi-tenant architecture enabling rapid innovation [3]. The explosion of AI agents and non-human identities is creating an entirely new market — the Entro acquisition and AWS collaboration position SailPoint as the identity layer for agentic AI [4][5]. Net revenue retention of 114% and 97% gross retention demonstrate best-in-class customer economics.
The primary overhang is Thoma Bravo's 88% ownership, creating persistent secondary offering risk and strategic constraints tied to PE exit timelines [3]. SailPoint remains GAAP unprofitable with -$294M FY2026 losses driven by SBC, and the stock trades below its $23 IPO price at ~$14, reflecting investor skepticism about the PE overhang [2][3]. Forward P/E of ~35 on adjusted earnings requires faith in the profitability transition, while GAAP losses deter value investors. Competition is intensifying as CyberArk expands into identity governance and Microsoft Entra leverages its installed base. The Inspire Impact Score of 0 (MIXED) with no attributions either way provides no differentiator for faith-based investors. Massive insider selling ($1B+ cumulative since 2017 versus essentially zero buying) signals the PE sponsor's exit orientation rather than long-term conviction [7].
