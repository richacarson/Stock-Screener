Company Profile
Industrials ·Aerospace & Defense ·NCM ·United States
Satellogic Inc. is a vertically integrated geospatial analytics company operating a constellation of sub-meter resolution Earth observation satellites. The company generates revenue through two segments: Space Systems (sovereign satellite constellation sales and operations, 55% of Q2 2026 revenue) and Data & Analytics (AI-powered geospatial intelligence, 45% of Q2 2026 revenue). Q2 2026 revenue grew 259% YoY to $15.9M, marking the first quarter of positive operating income. The company holds $112.8M in cash with $80.7M in remaining performance obligations. Founded in 2010 in Montevideo, Uruguay, now legally domiciled in the United States, with 154 employees.

The Q1 2026 net loss of $118.3M (including $113M non-cash fair value charges) illustrates the volatility of GAAP results that can obscure underlying operations. D/E of 284.8% and breakeven profit margin mean the company has no margin of safety — any contract delay or constellation malfunction could deplete the $112.8M cash position. CFO departure in August 2026 introduces financial leadership risk at a critical scaling moment [1]. The negative forward P/E (-171.7x) confirms analysts expect continued losses, and the PEG ratio is incalculable. Space debris risk, launch failure probability, and the capital intensity of constellation replacement create ongoing existential threats unique to satellite operators. Customer concentration in sovereign deals makes revenue lumpy and dependent on government procurement cycles.
