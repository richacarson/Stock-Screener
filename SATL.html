Company Profile
Industrials ·Aerospace & Defense ·NASDAQ ·United States
Satellogic Inc. is a vertically integrated earth observation company that designs, manufactures, and operates a constellation of 54+ sub-meter resolution satellites. The company provides constellation-as-a-service, space system solutions, AI-powered geospatial analytics, and satellite sales to government, defense, and commercial customers globally. Founded in 2010 by Emiliano Kargieman. Satellogic achieved its first-ever positive operating income and adjusted EBITDA in Q2 2026, with revenue surging 259% YoY to $15.9M and remaining performance obligations of $80.7M.
137 Employees ·satellogic.com

Investment Thesis
Satellogic offers pure-play exposure to the rapidly growing sovereign earth observation market with a uniquely vertically integrated model at an inflection point. Q2 2026 marked multiple firsts: revenue surged 259% YoY to $15.9M, the company achieved its first-ever positive operating income ($0.3M), and adjusted EBITDA turned positive at $2.8M [2]. RPO of $80.7M (up from $64.8M in Q1) with $45.8M expected in the next 12 months provides growing revenue visibility, and the nearly $1 billion sovereign pipeline could be transformative if converted [1][2]. The company designs and manufactures satellites at ~$1.3M each — an order of magnitude cheaper than competitors — then layers AI-powered analytics through the Aleph Observer platform [1]. The NextGen platform with 30cm resolution and on-orbit AI analytics, expected operational by 2027, could unlock higher-value defense contracts [3]. With $112.8M in cash and the profitability inflection achieved, the path to sustainable positive operating income is becoming credible.
The bear case centers on the extremely early stage of revenue development and persistent cash burn risk. At $32M TTM revenue, the company remains a micro-revenue space startup that must scale dramatically to justify any meaningful market capitalization [2][4]. ROE of -127.65% and profit margin of -27.01% reflect a business that achieved its first positive operating quarter only in Q2 2026 — sustained profitability is far from assured [4]. The stock has declined 26% from $6.03 to $4.49, reflecting market skepticism about the pace of pipeline conversion. Average daily volume dropped to 1.72M shares (from 11.85M previously), reducing liquidity. The $35M January 2026 offering highlights ongoing dilution risk as the company may require additional capital raises before reaching cash flow self-sufficiency [5]. Competition from better-capitalized Planet Labs (800+ satellites) and Maxar (defense incumbent) could compress pricing. Sovereign contract cycles are long and politically sensitive, creating revenue timing uncertainty. AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
8
AI Risk Protection (7/9): Satellogic's physical satellite infrastructure — designing, manufacturing, launching, and operating 54+ satellites in orbit — creates a structural moat against pure-software AI disruption [1]. Earth observation requires physical assets in space that cannot be replicated by algorithms alone. The vertically integrated model (hardware through analytics) means AI enhances rather than threatens its value proposition. Defense and sovereign customers increasingly require domestic satellite capabilities for intelligence applications, adding regulatory barriers [3]. The NextGen platform with on-orbit AI-enabled analytics demonstrates AI integration directly into the physical asset layer, where processing occurs on the satellite itself rather than relying on ground-based AI that competitors could replicate [3]. The main AI risk is that larger competitors (Planet Labs, Maxar) could deploy superior AI analytics on their imagery, commoditizing the data layer. AI Opportunity (1/1): AI directly amplifies Satellogic's business — the shift from selling raw imagery to AI-processed geospatial intelligence through the Aleph Observer platform creates higher-margin recurring revenue streams [1]. On-orbit AI analytics on the NextGen platform represents a direct AI-enabled product offering that reduces latency and increases intelligence value for defense and sovereign customers.
