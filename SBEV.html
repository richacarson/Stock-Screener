Company Profile
Consumer Defensive ·Beverages - Wineries & Distilleries ·ASE ·United States
Splash Beverage Group, Inc. is a micro-cap company that formerly manufactured and distributed beverages including SALT Tequila, Copa DI Vino wine, and Pulpoloco Sangria. As of mid-2026, the company has essentially ceased beverage operations and announced a strategic pivot toward regulated wellness, cannabinoid, and functional consumer products under new interim CEO Brady Cobb. Founder/CEO Robert Nistico resigned November 2025; president William Meissner resigned June 2026.

Investment Thesis
There is no credible bull case for SBEV. The company has a market cap under $900K, one employee, TTM revenue of $8,684, a net loss of $24.9M, and a stock price of $0.33 — down 97.6% from its 52-week high of $13.40 [1][5]. The only speculative thesis is the cannabinoid/wellness pivot under interim CEO Brady Cobb, including the CannEpil licensing agreement with Argent BioPharma [3]. If Cobb can attract capital and execute the pivot, the sub-$1M market cap provides an extremely low base for percentage returns. The Inspire score of +54 (ALIGNED) with zero negative attributions is a modest positive.
The bear case is overwhelming and existential. SBEV faces NYSE American delisting after failing shareholders' equity listing standards — the company must restore compliance by January 2027 or face delisting to OTC markets [2]. The 2025 audited financials carry a going concern paragraph, questioning the company's ability to continue operating [2]. With essentially zero revenue ($8,684 TTM), no employees beyond the interim CEO, and two CEO-level departures in seven months, the company is functionally a shell [3]. The cannabinoid pivot is entirely unproven — no products are developed, no regulatory approvals obtained, and no manufacturing or distribution infrastructure exists. Any capital raise at sub-$1M market cap would result in catastrophic dilution for existing shareholders. This is not an operating company; it is a speculative vehicle with a high probability of total capital loss.
