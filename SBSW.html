Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Precious Metals Mining ·NYSE ·South Africa
Sibanye Stillwater is the world's largest primary producer of platinum group metals (PGMs), a significant South African gold miner, and an emerging green metals player through its Keliber lithium project in Finland (construction completed 2026) and recycling operations. The company operates deep-level mines in South Africa's Witwatersrand Basin and PGM operations in Rustenburg and Montana (US). Q1 2026 group adjusted EBITDA surged 371% YoY to R19.4B ($1.2B) driven by an 87% increase in 4E PGM basket prices and 49% higher gold prices. CEO transition from founder Neal Froneman to Richard Stewart was completed in September 2025. Revenue consensus for FY2026 is $11.69B.

Investment Thesis
Sibanye Stillwater at a forward P/E of 2.43x — 79% below the metals and mining sector median — is the cheapest major miner globally, and Q1 2026 results demonstrate the explosive operating leverage that commodity price recovery delivers: group adjusted EBITDA surged 371% to R19.4B ($1.2B) as an 87% increase in PGM basket prices dropped through to largely flat all-in sustaining costs [1]. SA gold contributed R4.7B EBITDA (+160%) on 49% higher gold prices, and the recycling/zinc businesses added $127M in EBITDA. The Keliber lithium project completion adds a new revenue stream into the European EV supply chain. With consensus FY2026 revenue of $11.69B and the dividend restored at $0.25/share (2.53% yield), the company offers both deep value and cyclical recovery positioning [4].
The bear case rests on the structural and cyclical risks inherent to deep-level mining in South Africa. ROE remains negative at -10.25% and profit margin is -6.51% on a trailing basis, reflecting the damage from the 2023-2024 PGM price collapse [4]. PGM demand faces long-term structural headwinds from the EV transition — as catalytic converter demand declines, PGM prices may not sustain current levels. South African operations carry sovereign risk including power instability (Eskom load-shedding), labor tensions, and community disputes including forced resettlement controversies [3]. The CEO transition from founder Froneman to Stewart introduces execution uncertainty during a critical recovery period. The highly cyclical nature of mining earnings means the current 2.43x forward P/E could rapidly become meaningless if commodity prices reverse.
