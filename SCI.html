Company Profile
Consumer Discretionary ·Funeral Services ·NYSE ·United States
Service Corporation International is the largest provider of deathcare products and services in North America, operating over 1,900 funeral service locations and 500+ cemeteries across 44 U.S. states, eight Canadian provinces, and the District of Columbia. The company operates under the Dignity Memorial, National Cremation Society, and other brands, offering funeral and cremation services, cemetery property and merchandise, and preneed planning.

Investment Thesis
Service Corporation International delivered Q2 FY2026 revenue of $1,103M (up 4% YoY) with diluted EPS of $0.90 vs. $0.86 prior year, and management raised FY2026 adjusted operating cash flow guidance by $50M to $1,085M midpoint while confirming the $4.20 adjusted EPS midpoint [1][2]. The company benefits from an irreplaceable competitive position — 1,900+ funeral homes and 500+ cemeteries with the Dignity Memorial brand — and a powerful demographic tailwind as 73 million baby boomers enter peak mortality years over the next two decades. Preneed cemetery sales growing high single digits and an $8B trust fund portfolio provide long-term revenue visibility, while the 35% payout ratio and strong FCF ($535M TTM) support continued dividend growth (12 consecutive years) and share buybacks [3].
The primary concern is SCI's aggressive leverage at 345% D/E ($5.3B total debt) with an Altman Z-Score of 0.78, placing it firmly in the distress zone despite consistent cash flow generation [3]. Revenue growth remains pedestrian at 3% YoY — the company is a low-single-digit grower reliant on pricing power and acquisitions rather than organic expansion. Q1 FY2026 saw margin compression with net income declining 5% YoY despite revenue growth, and EPS of $0.97 missed the $1.02 estimate [1]. The 1.69% dividend yield falls short of the 2% threshold for income investors, while the 22x trailing P/E may overvalue a company with sub-5% top-line growth. The funeral industry faces secular headwinds from rising cremation rates (now ~60% of dispositions) which carry lower average revenue per service than traditional burial.
Key Catalysts
  1. Baby boomer mortality wave beginning to accelerate — U.S. deaths projected to increase 1-2% annually through the 2040s, providing a structural volume tailwind for SCI's 2,400 locations
  2. Management guiding double-digit EPS growth in H2 2026 with preneed cemetery sales at mid-to-high single-digit growth and $64M in renewable energy tax credits reducing FY2026 cash tax rate to 15-16%
  3. Continued industry consolidation opportunity — independent funeral homes represent ~85% of the U.S. market, with $75-125M annual acquisition target providing accretive growth
Key Risks
  1. Altman Z-Score of 0.78 and D/E of 345% create meaningful financial distress risk if interest rates remain elevated or if a recession suppresses preneed sales — $5.3B total debt requires consistent refinancing access
  2. Cremation rate approaching 60% and rising compresses average revenue per service, as cremation generates roughly 40% less revenue than traditional burial — pricing power alone may not offset volume mix shift
  3. Q1 FY2026 margin compression (net income -5% YoY) from insurance vendor transition headwinds (200bps funeral margin impact) and rising labor costs may persist longer than management projects
