Company Profile
Healthcare ·Health Information Services ·NASDAQ ·United States
Schrodinger, Inc. develops a physics-based computational platform that enables discovery of novel molecules for drug development and materials applications. Operating through Software and Drug Discovery segments, the company sells simulation software to all top 20 pharmaceutical companies and maintains a proprietary pipeline of clinical-stage drug candidates. Founded in 1990, the company combines physics-based modeling with AI to evaluate millions of molecular candidates, and is headquartered in New York, New York.

Investment Thesis
Schrodinger represents a high-conviction bet on computational drug discovery, with the only platform adopted by all top 20 pharmaceutical companies and a dual revenue engine now accelerating. Q1 2026 showed 12% ACV growth to $28.4M and drug discovery revenue that more than doubled to $22.9M, while total Q1 revenue reached $58.6M [1]. The 2026 guidance of $218-228M ACV (10-15% growth) and $55-65M drug discovery revenue provides visible growth trajectory. The Bunsen agentic AI co-scientist launch positions the company at the intersection of physics and AI in drug discovery [4]. The balance sheet is strong with $395M cash against $109M debt, providing runway to the 2028 EBITDA profitability target. The 5-year revenue CAGR of 17.45% and validated platform (Nimbus/Takeda $6B deal) demonstrate genuine adoption momentum [3][5].
The bear case centers on persistent unprofitability and competitive intensity. Net margins of -40% with no projected profitability until 2028 mean the company burns cash to fund drug discovery programs that carry ~90% clinical failure rates [5]. The software-to-hosted licensing transition is causing near-term revenue volatility, with Q1 2026 software revenue down 21% even as ACV grew 12% [1]. Revenue of $58.6M in Q1 missed the $71.8M consensus estimate, demonstrating continued execution uncertainty. The competitive landscape is intensifying as AlphaFold, generative chemistry, and well-funded AI drug discovery startups (Recursion, Insilico, Isomorphic Labs) challenge Schrodinger's technological differentiation [3]. At ~$1.5B market cap with persistent losses, the company requires either a clinical breakthrough in SGR-1505/SGR-3515 or sustained software acceleration to justify the investment. The stock's 80%+ decline from 2021 highs reflects deep market skepticism about the path to profitability.
