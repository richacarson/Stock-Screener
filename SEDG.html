Company Profile
Technology ·Solar ·NASDAQ ·Israel
SolarEdge Technologies, Inc. designs and manufactures DC-optimized inverter systems, power optimizers, and monitoring solutions for solar photovoltaic installations worldwide. The company is expanding into energy storage, EV charging, and AI data center power solutions through its SolarEdge Nexis platform. Founded in 2006, headquartered in Herzliya, Israel, with manufacturing operations across multiple countries.
3,576 Employees ·www.solaredge.com

Investment Thesis
SolarEdge is in recovery mode after a devastating 2024-2025 downturn that saw revenue collapse from $3B+ to under $1B. Q1 2026 showed strong momentum with $310.5M revenue (up 46% YoY), the sixth consecutive quarter of margin expansion, and Q2 2026 guidance of $325-355M with near-breakeven operating profitability expected at the midpoint [1]. New CEO Shuki Nir is pivoting the company toward profitable growth, capturing global market share through the Nexis platform, and pursuing AI data-center power opportunities that could diversify the revenue base beyond cyclical residential solar. The Inspire Impact score of 58 (ALIGNED) and 12 positive ESG attributions reflect genuine environmental and social value creation [4].
The bear case is substantial: SolarEdge remains deeply unprofitable with -28.6% profit margins, -72.5% ROE, and a forward PEG of 4.61 that implies the market is pricing in a recovery that may not fully materialize [5]. The U.S. market -- 51% of revenue -- declined 20% in Q1 2026 even as the company grew overall, raising questions about geographic sustainability [1]. The solar industry remains cyclical and policy-dependent, with tariff uncertainties and subsidy changes creating ongoing volatility. The company's $404M debt against $542M cash provides limited cushion, and the 3,576-employee workforce has been significantly downsized, potentially losing institutional knowledge. The competitive landscape is intensifying from Chinese manufacturers (Huawei, Sungrow) in Europe and Enphase in the U.S., threatening the margin recovery trajectory.
Key Catalysts
  1. Achievement of breakeven operating profitability in Q2-Q3 2026 would validate the margin recovery narrative and could trigger significant multiple re-rating from the current forward P/E of 36.3x
  2. SolarEdge Nexis platform rollout and AI data-center power roadmap could open a new addressable market beyond residential solar, diversifying revenue and reducing cyclicality
  3. Continued global solar installation growth driven by EU energy independence policies and IRA incentives in the U.S., with SolarEdge positioned to benefit from the industry recovery
Key Risks
  1. U.S. revenue declined 20% in Q1 2026 despite overall 46% growth, suggesting the largest market segment is weakening and the recovery may be geographically uneven
  2. Forward PEG of 4.61 implies the stock is priced for a growth trajectory that requires sustained 40%+ revenue expansion while simultaneously achieving profitability -- a challenging dual mandate
  3. Intensifying competition from Chinese solar inverter manufacturers (Huawei, Sungrow) in the European market could compress margins and limit the profitability recovery
