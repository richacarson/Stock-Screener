Company Profile
Materials ·Packaging & Containers ·NYSE ·United States
Sealed Air is a global packaging company providing food safety and product protection solutions through its Cryovac food packaging and Bubble Wrap protective packaging brands, organized into Food and Protective segments. FY2025 revenue of $5.36B was essentially flat (-0.6% YoY), with earnings of $505.5M (+91% from a depressed FY2024 base). The company has been acquired by Clayton, Dubilier & Rice (CD&R) in an all-cash transaction with an enterprise value of $10.3B. CEO Dustin Semach was appointed in February 2025 after predecessor Patrick Kivits was ousted after less than a year.
