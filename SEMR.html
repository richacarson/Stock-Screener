Company Profile
Technology ·Application Software (SEO / Digital Marketing) — Acquired by Adobe ·Formerly NYSE (delisted April 28, 2026) ·United States
Semrush Holdings operated an online visibility management and content marketing SaaS platform for SEO, paid advertising, content marketing, and competitive research, serving marketers, agencies, and enterprises globally [1]. On April 28, 2026, Adobe completed its acquisition of Semrush for $12.00 per share in an all-cash transaction (~$1.9B equity value, a 77.5% premium to the pre-announcement price), and Semrush is now a wholly owned Adobe subsidiary with its NYSE listing delisted [2]. Founded 2008, headquartered in Boston, Massachusetts.

Investment Thesis
Semrush's standalone investment case is now moot: Adobe completed its $1.9B all-cash acquisition on April 28, 2026 at $12.00 per share (a 77.5% premium to the pre-deal price), and the NYSE listing has been delisted [2]. Prior to the deal, the business case was genuinely strong — FY2025 revenue of $443.6M grew 18% YoY, the company was debt-free with $269.3M in cash, and Q4 2025 net new ARR grew 48% YoY on the strength of an early, successful pivot into Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), tracking brand visibility within AI answer engines ahead of the broader market [3][4].
There is no forward-looking bull case for SEMR as a standalone security: it is no longer listed, no longer tradeable, and has been fully absorbed into Adobe.
