Company Profile
Consumer Services ·Online Gaming & Sports Betting ·NYSE ·Guernsey
Super Group (SGHC) Limited is a global online sports betting and gaming operator headquartered in Guernsey, operating through two flagship brands: Betway (online sports betting and casino) and Spin (multi-brand online casino including Jackpot City). In Q1 2026, the company reorganized reporting segments from brand-based (Betway/Spin) to geography-based (Africa/International), reflecting Africa's growing strategic importance at 44% of reportable segment revenue.

Investment Thesis
Super Group delivered strong Q1 2026 results with $612M revenue (up 18% YoY) and adjusted EBITDA of $152M (25% margin, up 36% YoY), reaffirming full-year guidance of revenue >$2.55B and adjusted EBITDA >$680M [1]. The Africa segment is the growth engine, generating $267M in Q1 revenue (up 33% YoY) and now representing 44% of reportable segment revenue, with the Betway Nigeria expansion creating a large addressable market [5]. The balance sheet is exceptionally clean with D/E of just 12%, and the forward P/E of 17.55 is attractive relative to 21.7% revenue growth and rapidly expanding earnings [2]. AI-driven personalization in bet pricing and dynamic odds is enhancing customer engagement and margin optimization [3]. At $15.51 per share with $5.68B market cap, the stock trades at a reasonable multiple for a high-growth, debt-free online gaming operator.
The primary risks center on regulatory and geographic concentration. Africa contributes 44% of segment revenue but exposes the company to political instability, currency volatility, and regulatory uncertainty across multiple jurisdictions [1][5]. The gambling industry faces ongoing regulatory tightening globally, with potential for increased taxation, advertising restrictions, or outright market closures. Profit margin of just 7% reflects the high cost of customer acquisition and regulatory compliance in online gaming — this is a volume business, not a margin business. The insider selling pattern, particularly from COO Ross, raises governance concerns about management alignment [4]. The trailing P/E of 42x (vs. 17.5x forward) reflects significant expectations for earnings acceleration, creating downside if growth moderates. Faith-based investors should note the core business is online gambling, which carries inherent social responsibility concerns around addiction.
Key Catalysts
  1. Africa segment revenue acceleration driven by Betway Nigeria expansion and mobile penetration growth across Sub-Saharan Africa, with the segment growing 33% YoY in Q1 2026
  2. Earnings acceleration toward the forward P/E of 17.55x, driven by operating leverage on 18%+ revenue growth and adjusted EBITDA margin expansion toward 27%+
  3. AI-driven hyper-personalized bet pricing and dynamic odds engines increasing customer lifetime value and reducing customer acquisition costs across both Africa and International segments
Key Risks
  1. Geographic concentration risk: 44% of segment revenue from African markets exposes the company to political instability, currency devaluation (e.g., Nigerian naira), and regulatory uncertainty that could disrupt operations
  2. Regulatory tightening in key markets through increased taxation, advertising restrictions, or licensing changes could compress margins in a business already operating at 7% net profit margin
  3. Insider selling by senior management, including COO Kirsty Farrah Ross, signals potential concerns about valuation or growth sustainability at the executive level despite strong public guidance
