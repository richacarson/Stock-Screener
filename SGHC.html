Company Profile
Consumer Discretionary ·Casinos & Gaming ·NYSE ·Guernsey
Super Group (SGHC) Limited is a global digital gaming and sports betting company headquartered in Guernsey, operating through its two flagship brands: Betway (single-brand online sports betting and casino) and Spin (multi-brand online casino). Under CEO Neal Menashe, a 23-year company veteran, Super Group serves 6.4 million monthly active customers across 17 countries spanning Europe, Africa, the Americas, and Rest of World. The company signed a landmark deal naming Betway as the principal and exclusive global betting partner of Manchester United. Q1 2026 revenue grew 18% YoY to $612M, followed by record Q2 2026 revenue of $684M (+18% YoY) with Adjusted EBITDA of $204M (30% margin). Full-year 2026 guidance was raised to revenue >$2.6B and EBITDA >$710M. Market cap approximately $7B.

Investment Thesis
Super Group is a rare combination of double-digit growth and reasonable valuation in global iGaming — Q2 2026 delivered record revenue of $684M (+18% YoY) with Adjusted EBITDA of $204M (+30% YoY) at a 30% margin, and management raised full-year guidance to revenue >$2.6B and EBITDA >$710M [1][2]. The Forward P/E of 20.48x for a company growing revenue 18% and EBITDA 30%+ represents a significant discount to U.S.-focused peers like DraftKings (40x+) and Flutter (25x+), driven by Super Group's international focus and gambling sector discount [5]. The Manchester United principal betting partnership is a transformational brand catalyst, positioning Betway alongside the world's most-followed football club across 6.4M monthly active customers in Africa, Europe, and Americas [3]. The balance sheet is fortress-strong: $365M cash, $74M debt, and ROE of 21.38% — a capital-light platform model generating substantial free cash flow that funds expansion without dilution.
The bear case centers on regulatory risk, tax headwinds, and industry concentration. The UK government's increase of Remote Gaming Duty from 21% to 40% (effective April 2026) directly compresses Super Group's highest-margin European market, with management estimating a ~6% reduction in Group Adjusted EBITDA — and further tax increases in other jurisdictions could follow the UK template [4]. Net profit margin of 7.03% is structurally constrained by gaming taxes, regulatory compliance costs, and customer acquisition spending, leaving limited buffer against additional regulatory headwinds [5]. Africa, the company's fastest-growing region, carries political, currency, and regulatory instability risks — the Nigeria expansion in particular faces uncertain enforcement of online gambling regulations and potential policy reversals [3]. The company's dual-brand strategy (Betway sports betting, Spin casino) concentrates risk in online gambling, which faces social license scrutiny globally as governments respond to gambling addiction concerns.
