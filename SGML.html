Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Other Industrial Metals & Mining ·NASDAQ ·Canada
Sigma Lithium is a Canadian-listed lithium producer operating the Grota do Cirilo hard-rock spodumene project in Minas Gerais, Brazil, which it markets as 'Quintuple Zero' green lithium (dry-stacked tailings, hydro power, no hazardous chemicals). First production began in 2023; FY2025 revenue was $110M with a $50M net loss, but 2026 has shown a margin recovery with record Q1 and Q2 2026 results (Q2 revenue ~$55M at a 60% gross margin). Operations paused in July 2026 pending a TAC agreement with state regulators. CEO and co-chair Ana Cabral-Gardner leads the company. Market cap approximately $0.8B at $7.00.

Investment Thesis
Sigma Lithium is a high-beta, single-asset lithium producer with improving unit economics: Q2 2026 delivered record ~$55M revenue at a 60% gross margin and 47% EBITDA margin, and the stock trades at under 4x forward earnings after falling ~70% from its 52-week high of $24.48 [2][3]. If lithium prices keep recovering and Grota do Cirilo restarts under a finalized TAC agreement, consensus forward EPS of $1.83 implies substantial upside to the $11.33 mean target [3].
Against that, the balance sheet is stretched (D/E 337%, $277M debt vs $17M cash), operations are paused for regulatory reasons, Q2 revenue missed consensus ($54.7M vs $70.3M), and ESG and governance controversies are severe [2][3][7][8]. IOWN should treat SGML as a WATCH: the upside depends on regulatory resolution and the commodity cycle rather than durable competitive excellence.
