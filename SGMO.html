Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·OTCQB/OTCID (delisted from Nasdaq) ·United States
Sangamo Therapeutics is a clinical-stage genomic medicine company built around proprietary zinc finger gene editing and epigenetic regulation technology, historically pursuing therapies for hemophilia A, Fabry disease, and neurological disorders through partnerships with large pharma. The company reported Q1 FY2026 revenue of just $1.4M (down from $6.4M a year earlier and 96% below the $35.4M Street estimate), disclosed 'substantial doubt' about its ability to continue as a going concern, and was delisted from Nasdaq to the OTCQB market in May 2026 after failing minimum bid price requirements. Sangamo filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy reorganization on June 23, 2026 and is now running a court-supervised asset sale process, with bids due August 4, 2026 and a sale hearing August 20, 2026 [1][3].

Investment Thesis
There is no credible bull case for SGMO as a going concern. The company filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy on June 23, 2026 after disclosing going-concern doubt, a 96% revenue miss versus estimates ($1.4M vs. $35.4M expected), and a cash runway that would have run out in fiscal Q3 2026 absent emergency financing [1][3]. Shares were delisted from Nasdaq in May 2026 and now trade around $0.145 on the OTCQB/OTCID market under a reorganized ticker. The only remaining value-realization event is the court-supervised sale of Sangamo's zinc finger gene-editing IP and pipeline assets, with bids due August 4, 2026 and a sale hearing scheduled for August 20, 2026 [3].
The bear case is simply the base case: in a Chapter 11 asset sale, common equity holders are typically last in the priority waterfall and frequently receive minimal-to-zero recovery once secured creditors, unsecured creditors, and administrative claims are paid. Nasdaq has already denied Sangamo's relisting bid, and the stock is expected to be formally delisted and deregistered, which will further impair liquidity and price discovery for remaining shareholders [3]. Even Sangamo's underlying gene-editing science, which was genuinely differentiated, could not generate sufficient collaboration/license revenue to sustain the company's cost structure — a cautionary example of a company whose Just Cause could not survive contact with capital markets reality. IOWN screening should treat SGMO as a SELL / avoid regardless of the nominal AI-resilience or mission-quality scores, given the near-certainty of equity impairment through the bankruptcy process.
Key Catalysts

Key Risks

  2. Delisting and deregistration from Nasdaq (already executed, trading now on OTCQB/OTCID) sharply reduces liquidity, institutional eligibility, and price transparency for remaining shares [3]
  3. Revenue base has effectively collapsed — Q1 FY2026 revenue of $1.4M was down 78% YoY and 96% below consensus, showing collaboration/license revenue the company depended on has largely dried up [1]
