Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Gold Mining ·NYSE ·Canada
Skeena Resources Limited (recently rebranded as Skeena Gold + Silver) is a precious metals developer focused on advancing the Eskay Creek Gold-Silver Project, a past-producing mine in British Columbia's Golden Triangle. Eskay Creek is positioned as one of the highest-grade and lowest-cost open-pit precious metals projects globally, with substantial silver by-product production. The company also holds the Snip gold project. Skeena secured environmental assessment approval for Eskay Creek in January 2026 and is transitioning from development to construction phase.

Investment Thesis
Skeena Resources holds one of the highest-grade open-pit gold-silver projects globally in Eskay Creek, which secured full environmental permitting in January 2026 and is now advancing through construction with C$294M in capex deployed in FY2025 [1][4]. At $32.54/share ($4.03B market cap), the stock has rallied 124% over the past year as permitting risk has been eliminated and gold prices have been strong. The company has a relatively clean balance sheet with only C$65M in debt against C$180M equity, though free cash flow is deeply negative (-C$373M TTM) as expected for a mine under construction [4]. The consent-based Tahltan Nation partnership and lowest-quartile GHG design provide social license advantages that de-risk operations. Analyst projections show earnings turning positive with 108.7% annual EPS growth expected as Eskay Creek reaches production.
The bear case centers on execution and financing risk. Skeena has never operated a producing mine at scale, and capital cost overruns are endemic in mining — the C$337M TTM capex spend is accelerating and total project costs may exceed initial estimates [4]. Cash on hand of only C$43M against a major construction program suggests additional equity or debt financing will be needed, diluting existing shareholders. The Forward P/E of 37.56 prices in successful execution with no margin for delays or cost overruns. Gold price sensitivity is extreme — a sustained drop below $1,800/oz would severely impair project economics. The stock's beta of 2.24 reflects high volatility appropriate for a pre-production miner. With zero revenue, negative cash flow, and a single-asset concentration in a remote Canadian location subject to weather, permitting, and Indigenous relations risk, this is a speculative position dependent on flawless execution.
Key Catalysts
  1. Eskay Creek construction milestones and first gold pour timeline — any production acceleration would validate the $4B market cap and potentially trigger re-rating toward producer multiples
  2. Sustained gold price strength above $2,000/oz enhances Eskay Creek's projected economics as one of the lowest-cost open-pit gold mines globally, with high-grade ore providing margin cushion
  3. Completion of the consent-based Tahltan Nation agreement — first of its kind under BC's DRIPA — would set a precedent and further de-risk the project's social license
Key Risks
  1. Capital cost overruns during construction — mining projects frequently exceed initial estimates by 20-40%, and with only C$43M cash remaining, additional dilutive financing is likely required
  2. Single-asset concentration in a remote northern BC location exposes the company to weather delays, supply chain disruptions, and the risk that any Eskay Creek-specific issue (geological, environmental, community relations) impacts the entire enterprise value
  3. Forward P/E of 37.56 and $4B market cap for a company with no revenue and -C$103M net income means any execution delay or gold price weakness could trigger severe multiple compression
