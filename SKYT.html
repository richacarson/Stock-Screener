Company Profile
Technology ·Semiconductors ·NASDAQ ·United States
SkyWater Technology is a U.S.-owned pure-play semiconductor foundry and DMEA-accredited Category 1A Trusted Supplier, offering Technology-as-a-Service development and volume manufacturing across mixed-signal CMOS, MEMS, superconducting ICs, photonics, and advanced packaging. Following the 2025 acquisition of Infineon's Fab 25 in Austin, Texas, SkyWater became the largest pure-play foundry in the United States. SkyWater was acquired by IonQ for $35.00/share in cash and stock (approved by stockholders June 2026) and delisted from NASDAQ on July 31, 2026. Now operates as an IonQ subsidiary.

Investment Thesis
SkyWater delivered record Q2 2026 revenue of $156.4M (+165% YoY), with TTM revenue reaching $638.8M (+120.5% YoY), driven by the Fab 25 acquisition contributing $87.4M in Wafer Services revenue and strong organic growth across defense/aerospace and advanced packaging [1]. The company was acquired by IonQ for $35.00/share (approved June 2026, delisted July 31, 2026) to create what IonQ calls the only vertically integrated full-stack quantum platform company [2]. Pre-delisting fundamentals were exceptional: FY2025 profitability of $118.9M net income (ROE 95.85%), 5yr revenue CAGR of 28.4%, and a unique DMEA Category 1A Trusted Foundry position that no domestic competitor replicates. The semiconductor fabrication business is structurally immune to AI disruption and benefits from accelerating demand for domestic, trusted chip manufacturing tied to AI, defense, and quantum computing buildout.
As a delisted stock (acquired by IonQ, July 31, 2026), SKYT is no longer directly investable. The key risk for former shareholders who received IonQ stock is IonQ's ability to integrate SkyWater's fabrication operations and achieve the projected synergies of the vertically integrated quantum platform. The FTC's second request for additional information during merger review highlighted regulatory scrutiny, and post-merger governance protections for former SkyWater shareholders may be weaker under IonQ's charter [2]. Operationally, D/E of 127% reflects the balance sheet strain from the Fab 25 acquisition, and the extreme ROE of 95.85% is amplified by thin equity from prior-year accumulated losses rather than sustainable capital efficiency [6]. Culturally, Glassdoor scores declining 8% YoY with toxic management allegations and the $97,130 Minnesota environmental fine suggest organizational health issues that the IonQ integration will need to address [4][5].
