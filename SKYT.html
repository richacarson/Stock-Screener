Company Profile
Technology ·Semiconductors ·NASDAQ ·United States
SkyWater Technology is a U.S.-owned pure-play semiconductor foundry and DMEA-accredited Category 1A Trusted Supplier, offering Technology-as-a-Service development and volume manufacturing across mixed-signal CMOS, MEMS, superconducting ICs, photonics, and advanced packaging. Following the 2025 acquisition of Infineon's Fab 25 in Austin, Texas, SkyWater became the largest pure-play foundry in the United States. Headquartered in Bloomington, Minnesota. In January 2026, IonQ announced a definitive agreement to acquire SkyWater for $35.00/share in cash and stock.

Investment Thesis
SkyWater delivered FY2025 revenue growth of 29% to $442.1M and Q1 2026 revenue of $160.7M (up 162% YoY), driven substantially by the Fab 25 acquisition, which contributed $86.3M of Wafer Services revenue in the quarter and made SkyWater the largest pure-play foundry in the U.S. [1][2]. In January 2026, IonQ announced a definitive agreement to acquire SkyWater for $35.00/share in cash and stock (implied equity value ~$1.8B) to create what IonQ calls the only vertically integrated full-stack quantum platform company, and SkyWater stockholders approved the merger in June 2026 [2]. The core semiconductor fabrication business is structurally immune to AI disruption and stands to benefit from growing demand for secure, domestic, trusted chip manufacturing capacity tied to both AI and quantum computing buildout.
The stock currently trades at $31.36, an 11% discount to the $35.00 deal price, reflecting real closing risk: IonQ and SkyWater received a second request for additional information from the FTC as part of merger review, extending the regulatory timeline and introducing uncertainty about whether and when the deal closes [2]. Because forward analyst estimates have largely gone dark pending the acquisition, Forward P/E and PEG are unavailable, meaning the mechanically strong Infrastructure score of 10 leans more heavily on trailing profitability (ROE 95.9%, aided by a thin post-loss equity base) than on a fully validated forward view [4]. Culture signals are mixed — Glassdoor culture-and-values score of 3.1/5 with at least one review alleging leadership suppresses dissent — and TG-style community-investment or insider-buying evidence is absent, keeping Inspiration low despite a genuinely distinctive corporate mission [3][4].
Key Catalysts
  1. Closing of the IonQ acquisition at $35.00/share, which would crystallize the current ~11% spread to shareholders if regulatory review concludes favorably
  2. Continued Fab 25 integration driving toward the FY2026 guidance of at least $600M revenue and $60M adjusted EBITDA
  3. Expanding defense, aerospace, and quantum-computing program wins leveraging SkyWater's unique DMEA Category 1A Trusted Foundry status
Key Risks
  1. FTC issued a second request for additional information on the IonQ-SkyWater merger, extending regulatory review and creating real risk the deal is delayed, restructured, or blocked
  2. Post-merger governance protections for former SkyWater shareholders may be materially weaker under IonQ's corporate charter and bylaws than under SkyWater's current structure
  3. Glassdoor culture-and-values rating of 3.1/5 with reported leadership suppression of dissenting views raises questions about organizational health independent of the pending acquisition
