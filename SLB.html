Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas Equipment & Services ·NYSE ·United States
SLB N.V. (formerly Schlumberger Limited) is the world's largest oilfield services company, operating through four divisions: Digital & Integration, Reservoir Performance, Well Construction, and Production Systems. The company provides technology-driven solutions across the energy lifecycle including drilling, completion, production, carbon management, data center cooling/power solutions, and digital platforms. Founded in 1926, headquartered in Houston, Texas, with 109,000 employees in 100+ countries. Q2 2026 revenue reached $8.97B with Data Center Solutions surging 80% YoY.

Investment Thesis
SLB is the world's largest and most technologically diversified energy services company, with $36.4B TTM revenue, $4.4B free cash flow, and a strategic vision under CEO Le Peuch that is expanding the company's addressable market into AI infrastructure [1][2]. Q2 2026 delivered $8.97B revenue (beating estimates by $215M) and $0.55 EPS (beating by $0.04), with pretax operating margin expanding 138 bps to 15.5% and adjusted EBITDA margin back above 20% [1]. Data Center Solutions emerged as a standout growth engine, surging 80% YoY with a path to $2B+ annualized run rate by 2027 — leveraging SLB's industrial cooling and power management expertise for AI data centers. At 18.5x forward earnings, the stock offers reasonable valuation for a company diversifying from cyclical oilfield services into structural AI infrastructure demand. The 2.28% dividend yield with 50+ year payment history provides income stability.
The bear case centers on cyclical exposure and margin compression. SLB's trailing P/E of 25.5x is elevated for an oilfield services company, and organic revenue growth (excluding ChampionX) has been pressured by Middle East disruptions and international project delays [1]. Profit margin of 8.53% remains well below the 20% target, constrained by the capital-intensive nature of physical services operations. The payout ratio of 57.22% is elevated for a cyclical business — the 2020 dividend cut from $2.00 to $0.50 (75% reduction) demonstrates that payouts are not recession-proof, and the dividend has not recovered to pre-cut levels despite 4 consecutive years of growth. With $12.8B in total debt, a prolonged downturn in global E&P spending could pressure both the balance sheet and shareholder returns. Data Center Solutions, while exciting, is still less than 3% of total revenue and faces competition from established data center infrastructure providers.
Key Catalysts
  1. Data Center Solutions revenue surging 80% YoY with path to $2B+ annualized run rate by 2027, transforming SLB from a pure oilfield services company into an AI infrastructure provider [1]
  2. International E&P spending recovery as Middle East disruptions ease and major NOC projects resume, unlocking pent-up demand across SLB's largest revenue region
  3. ChampionX integration delivering targeted synergies while diversifying revenue toward recurring production chemical streams less sensitive to drilling cycle volatility [1]
Key Risks
  1. Organic oilfield services revenue has been pressured by Middle East disruptions and international softness — sustained activity weakness could compress earnings from the current $2.06 TTM EPS despite acquisition contributions [1]
  2. Profit margin of 8.53% is structurally limited by the capital-intensive, physical nature of oilfield services — even with Data Center Solutions growth, the margin profile is unlikely to converge with technology peers
  3. The 2020 dividend cut (75% from $2.00 to $0.50/year) demonstrated payout vulnerability in commodity downturns — the current 57.22% payout ratio and $12.8B debt load limit cushion if E&P spending enters another downcycle
