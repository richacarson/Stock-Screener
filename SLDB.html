Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Solid Biosciences is a clinical-stage gene therapy company developing precision genetic medicines for neuromuscular and cardiac diseases. Founded in 2013 by Ilan Ganot (whose son has Duchenne muscular dystrophy), the company is led by CEO Bo Cumbo (appointed December 2022). The lead program SGT-003 has dosed 53 participants in the Phase 1/2 INSPIRE DUCHENNE trial and entered Phase 3 (IMPACT DUCHENNE), with an FDA meeting planned for late Q4 2026. The pipeline has expanded to include SGT-212 (Friedreich's ataxia, Phase 1b), SGT-501 (CPVT), and SGT-601 (TNNT2 cardiomyopathy). Cash position of $377.7M as of June 2026 provides runway to mid-2028.
121 Employees ·www.solidbio.com

Investment Thesis
Solid Biosciences at $8.54 offers high-risk clinical-stage gene therapy optionality with multiple near-term catalysts. The lead program SGT-003 has dosed 53 participants in Phase 1/2 INSPIRE DUCHENNE with generally well-tolerated results and advanced to Phase 3 IMPACT DUCHENNE, with an FDA meeting planned for late Q4 2026 that could define the regulatory pathway [1]. Cash of $377.7M provides 2+ years of runway to mid-2028, reducing near-term dilution risk, and the $915M market cap reflects modest expectations for a company with a Phase 3 asset and multiple clinical-stage programs [1].
The bear case is the standard clinical-stage biotech risk profile amplified by gene therapy complexity. SGT-003 faces direct competition from Sarepta's Elevidys, an already-approved Duchenne gene therapy, meaning Solid must demonstrate clinically meaningful superiority to gain market share. Gene therapy manufacturing is notoriously complex and costly, with scale-up risk as programs advance. ROE of -65.5% and profit margin of 0% reflect the pre-revenue reality. Insider selling by CEO Cumbo ($97K) and COO Howton ($43K) in February 2026, while modest amounts, does not signal conviction at current prices [4].
