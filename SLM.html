Company Profile
Financial Services ·Credit Services ·NASDAQ ·United States
SLM Corporation (Sallie Mae) originates and services private education loans to students and their families in the United States. The company also provides retail deposit accounts including high-yield savings, money market accounts, and certificates of deposit. Sallie Mae separated from Navient in 2014 and operates as the largest dedicated private student lender with a $4.86B market cap.

Investment Thesis
SLM Corporation trades at a deeply discounted 9.17x forward earnings with elite profitability — 38.1% net margin, 33.8% ROE, and a 16.5% payout ratio providing massive dividend growth runway [4]. Q1 2026 GAAP EPS of $1.54 (up 10% YoY) prompted management to raise full-year guidance to $3.10-$3.20 per share, supported by $2.9B in loan originations (up 5%) and a 5.29% net interest margin [1]. The strategic launch of Parent Loan products ahead of the federal Grad PLUS phase-out creates a near-term growth catalyst, while the Adtalem partnership expands the addressable market into healthcare student financing [2]. The stock has appreciated from $22.16 to $25.77 since the last screen, reflecting growing recognition of the earnings trajectory.
The bear case centers on competitive disruption and structural leverage. SoFi Technologies commands a $23.55B market cap (nearly 5x SLM's $4.86B) despite lower profitability, reflecting market conviction that AI-driven fintech platforms will erode traditional lending franchises [2]. SLM's D/E ratio of 253% is structurally elevated for a lending business but leaves minimal buffer against credit deterioration. Revenue growth of 7.6% YoY and a modest 2.9% 5-year CAGR suggest limited organic growth. The company's narrow focus on private student lending creates concentration risk tied to higher education enrollment trends, federal student loan policy changes, and employment outcomes for graduates. Historical controversies — discrimination lawsuits, aggressive collection practices, $30M+ in fee refunds — create reputational overhang that may constrain customer acquisition [5].
Key Catalysts
  1. Parent Loan product launch capturing demand from the federal Grad PLUS phase-out beginning July 1, 2026, potentially expanding SLM's addressable market by $8-10B in annual originations
  2. Raised full-year 2026 EPS guidance to $3.10-$3.20 with continued net interest margin expansion above 5% and a 16.5% payout ratio enabling rapid dividend growth from the current $0.52 annual rate
  3. Adtalem Global Education partnership establishing a template for private financing of graduate healthcare education, a high-value market segment with strong repayment economics
Key Risks
  1. SoFi and other AI-driven fintech lenders leveraging alternative data underwriting and superior digital experiences to capture private student loan market share from Sallie Mae's traditional franchise
  2. D/E ratio of 253% leaves limited balance sheet flexibility during credit cycle deterioration, and any increase in student loan delinquencies could pressure earnings and capital ratios disproportionately
  3. Federal student loan policy uncertainty — potential changes to Income-Driven Repayment plans, new direct lending programs, or Grad PLUS reinstatement could reverse the positive demand tailwind from the current phase-out
