Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Infrastructure ·NASDAQ ·Ireland
Brera Holdings PLC, operating as Solmate Infrastructure, abandoned its original social-impact multi-club football ownership business in 2025 to become a Solana-focused digital asset treasury (DAT) and crypto staking infrastructure company based in Abu Dhabi. Following a $300M PIPE backed by the Solana Foundation, ARK Invest, RockawayX, and Pulsar Group, the company accumulates and stakes SOL while retaining a residual football portfolio. The company faces a Nasdaq delisting notice (April 2026) for failing the $1.00 minimum bid price, terminated the proposed RockawayX acquisition over 'material inconsistencies and omissions' in financial representations, and appointed Ron Sade as CEO effective May 2026 after terminating prior CEO Marco Santori [1][2][3].
