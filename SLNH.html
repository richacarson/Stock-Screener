Company Profile
Technology ·Renewable Energy Data Centers ·NASDAQ ·United States
Soluna Holdings operates renewable-powered data centers co-located with wind and solar farms, providing computing infrastructure for AI workloads and cryptocurrency mining. The company monetizes curtailed (wasted) renewable energy through high-performance computing, operating projects including Dorothy (1A, 2, and 3), Sophie, and Kati data center sites. Soluna Cloud hosts energy-intensive AI workloads with clean power and smart infrastructure.
75 Employees ·www.solunacomputing.com

Investment Thesis
Soluna Holdings is positioned at the intersection of two powerful secular trends — AI compute demand and renewable energy transition — with Q2 2026 revenue surging 145% YoY to $15.1M, marking the fifth consecutive quarter of sequential growth [1]. Data hosting revenue (primarily AI workloads) reached 71% of total revenue, up 178% YoY, validating the strategic pivot from crypto mining [2]. The 6.3 GW development pipeline, Dorothy 3 expansion to 300 MW, and $113M cash position provide growth runway [6]. The co-location model at stranded renewable energy sites offers a differentiated cost advantage — monetizing curtailed clean energy that would otherwise go to waste. CEO Belizaire's consistent vision of 'making renewable energy a global superpower' provides strategic coherence to the growth story [3].
The bear case is significant and immediate. Despite 145% revenue growth, Q2 net loss widened to $20.7M and gross profit declined even as revenue surged — operating leverage has not materialized [1][7]. Profit margin of approximately -190% on TTM revenue of $33M demonstrates the company is far from breakeven. Share count more than doubled through aggressive equity financing, creating severe dilution that overwhelms revenue growth on a per-share basis [7]. The $10.5B-valued 6.3 GW pipeline remains aspirational — converting development pipeline to operational data centers requires massive capital investment and execution capability that Soluna has not yet demonstrated at scale. Nasdaq noncompliance risk (previously flagged for listing standards), reliance on capital markets for survival, and competition from hyperscalers with vastly greater resources present material threats to the investment thesis.
Key Catalysts
  1. Dorothy 3 ramp to 300 MW capacity driving step-change in revenue and potentially first operating leverage as the largest project in the portfolio generates hosting revenue at scale [6]
  2. 6.3 GW development pipeline conversion — even a small fraction of this pipeline reaching operational status would dramatically expand Soluna's revenue base and validate the stranded renewables + AI data center model [1]
  3. AI data center demand acceleration as hyperscaler capacity constraints drive enterprise AI workloads to alternative hosting providers with clean power credentials [4]
Key Risks
  1. Severe dilution risk — share count more than doubled through equity financing in recent quarters, and continued capital-intensive expansion will require additional raises that could further erode per-share economics [7]
  2. Gross profit declined even as Q2 revenue surged 145%, suggesting cost structure challenges and raising questions about whether the co-location model achieves operating leverage at current scale [1]
  3. Pipeline-to-delivery execution risk — the 6.3 GW development pipeline requires billions in capital investment and construction capability that Soluna has not yet demonstrated, with hyperscalers offering competing renewable-powered data center solutions at vastly greater scale
