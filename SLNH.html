Company Profile
Technology ·Data Centers / Digital Infrastructure ·NASDAQ ·United States
Soluna Holdings develops and operates modular data centers co-located with wind, solar, and hydro sites, converting otherwise-curtailed renewable power into computing capacity for Bitcoin mining hosting, proprietary mining, and AI/high-performance computing workloads. Projects include Dorothy (Texas), Sophie (Kentucky), and Kati (Texas). Formerly Mechanical Technology Inc. (founded 1961), it renamed in November 2021. John Belizaire is CEO.
51 Employees ·www.solunacomputing.com

Investment Thesis
Soluna is growing quickly from a small base: Q2 2026 revenue rose 145% to $15.1M, TTM revenue reached $42.1M, and consensus expects about $61.8M of 2026 revenue [1][2]. The company vertically integrated the 50 MW Dorothy 1 site, Kati 1 reached first gross profit, a Metrobloks JV targets the Kati 2 AI data center, and the development pipeline expanded to roughly 6.3 GW [1]. With over $113M of cash against $39M of debt (D/E 18.6%), Soluna has runway to convert renewable-sited land and power into AI hosting capacity, a model that AI power demand directly favors [8].
The bear case is profitability and dilution. Net losses were $53.4M in 2025 and $63.3M in 2024, Q2 2026 net loss widened to about $20.7-22.6M, gross profit fell to $0.8M on ramp and maintenance costs, and free cash flow is about -$40M [1][8]. Q2 revenue missed the $15.7M consensus. Growth is funded through ATM equity sales, and the share price has fallen to $0.65 from a 50-day average of $1.03, raising Nasdaq minimum-bid risk. Sustainalytics rates ESG risk 'High' at 31.4, and Bitcoin price swings still drive much of revenue [5].
Key Catalysts
  1. Signing a creditworthy AI/HPC tenant for Kati 2 under the Metrobloks joint venture, converting pipeline into contracted AI revenue [1]
  2. Q3 2026 results (consensus about $18.1M revenue) showing gross margin recovery after Dorothy 1 integration and Kati 1 profitability [1][2]
  3. Monetizing part of the ~6.3 GW renewable-sited pipeline through JV partnerships that reduce Soluna's equity funding needs [1]
Key Risks
  1. Ongoing dilution from ATM equity sales to fund losses (free cash flow about -$40M) with the stock below $1 and at risk of a Nasdaq bid-price notice [8]
  2. Bitcoin price and network-hashrate swings that compress hosting and mining economics, which still make up most revenue [1]
  3. Execution risk in converting renewable-sited Bitcoin infrastructure to AI-grade data centers, highlighted by the $28.6M loss on the terminated HPE contract [5]
