Company Profile
Financial Services ·Insurance Brokers ·NYSE ·United States
SelectQuote is a direct-to-consumer insurance distribution company specializing in Medicare Advantage, Medicare Supplement, life, and auto/home insurance policies. The company also operates SelectRx, a pharmacy services segment, and Healthcare Services including in-home care. SelectQuote connects consumers with insurance carriers through a technology-enabled platform staffed by licensed agents. Founded in 1985 and headquartered in Overland Park, Kansas.
4,269 Employees ·www.selectquote.com

Investment Thesis
SelectQuote delivered a strong Q3 FY2026 beat with revenue of $431M (up 6% YoY), adjusted EBITDA of $45M (up 18%), and EPS of $0.11 vs $0.02 consensus — a 450% beat that drove a 47.5% stock price surge [1]. Management reaffirmed full-year guidance, and the Senior segment posted strong 32% EBITDA margins. At approximately 3.75x forward P/E, the stock offers extreme deep value with a $1.64B revenue base. The Healthcare Services segment ($199M/quarter, up 5%) now represents nearly half of revenue, diversifying beyond the insurance distribution model. The company is generating positive operating cash flow with expectations to build meaningful cash generation in FY2027 and beyond [1]. The 5-year revenue CAGR of ~14% demonstrates consistent growth momentum.
The bear case is dominated by legal and regulatory risk. The U.S. Department of Justice filed a False Claims Act complaint against SelectQuote for alleged misrepresentations in Medicare Advantage sales practices, triggering a securities class action lawsuit and the stock's 68% decline over the past year [3]. The stock at $1.20 is priced for distress — either the DOJ case resolves favorably and the stock re-rates significantly, or penalties and behavioral remediation permanently impair the core business model. Total debt of $405M against only $30M cash creates a net leverage position of $375M — nearly 2x the $204M market cap — meaning any revenue disruption from DOJ penalties or carrier relationship damage could stress the balance sheet. Profit margin of just 1.3% leaves zero room for legal settlements, fines, or operational remediation costs. Q3 revenue growth decelerated to 6% from 12% in Q2, raising questions about whether the growth trajectory is plateauing even before DOJ-related headwinds materialize [1].
Key Catalysts
  1. DOJ False Claims Act case resolution — a favorable outcome or manageable settlement could remove the primary overhang and trigger significant stock re-rating from the current ~3.75x forward PE [3]
  2. Healthcare Services segment approaching $800M annual revenue run-rate with improving contribution margins, reducing dependency on the higher-risk insurance distribution model [1]
  3. Operating cash flow inflection with management guiding to significant year-over-year improvement in FY2026 and further improvement in FY2027, supporting debt reduction [1]
Key Risks
  1. DOJ False Claims Act complaint alleging systemic misrepresentation in Medicare Advantage sales — potential penalties, behavioral remediation, and reputational damage could permanently impair the core Senior segment business model [3]
  2. Leverage risk — $405M total debt against $30M cash ($375M net debt) represents nearly 2x the $204M market cap, creating existential risk if revenue declines or legal penalties are substantial [4]
  3. Revenue growth deceleration from 12% (Q2) to 6% (Q3) and carrier concentration risk — a single carrier's $40M budget cut previously forced a guidance reduction, demonstrating vulnerability to partner decisions [1]
