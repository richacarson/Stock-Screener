Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
SELLAS Life Sciences Group, Inc. is a late-stage clinical biopharmaceutical company focused on the development of novel cancer therapeutics. Lead candidate galinpepimut-S (GPS) is a peptide immunotherapy targeting the WT1 antigen in a pivotal Phase 3 REGAL trial for AML maintenance therapy, which reportedly reached its pre-specified 80th event trigger in August 2026, though the company has not confirmed nor released results [1][4]. SLS009 (tambiciclib), a CDK9 inhibitor, has Phase 2 topline data expected Q4 2026. Market cap $2.35B with $138.3M cash and 13 employees. Pomerantz Law Firm is investigating potential securities fraud related to trial disclosure timing [4].

Investment Thesis
SELLAS Life Sciences is a high-risk, high-reward clinical-stage biotech with its pivotal Phase 3 REGAL trial of galinpepimut-S (GPS) in AML maintenance reportedly at the pre-specified 80th event trigger for final efficacy analysis — the most material near-term binary catalyst [1][4]. The company has $138.3M in cash providing approximately 5-6 years of runway at the current ~$36M annual burn rate, substantially reducing near-term dilution risk [1]. GPS targets WT1-positive AML maintenance with no approved immunotherapy competitor, and SLS009 (tambiciclib) Phase 2 topline data in newly diagnosed AML is expected Q4 2026 [1]. Updated executive severance terms and Merck partnership add buyout speculation to the narrative. The 3D Medicines licensing agreement provides $191.5M in potential milestone payments. If REGAL data is positive and the disclosure delay resolves favorably, the $2.35B market cap could have significant upside.
The bear case is severe and has worsened since August. The Pomerantz Law Firm is investigating potential securities fraud related to the company's handling of the REGAL trial's 80th event — reports suggest the pre-specified trigger was reached but the company has not confirmed the milestone or released data, creating a disclosure gap that has drawn legal scrutiny and a 13% stock decline [4]. SELLAS is a pre-revenue company with 13 employees, zero commercialization infrastructure, and a binary outcome trial that will determine its viability. Q2 2026 net loss widened to $9.6M from $6.6M YoY, with R&D spending up 62% to $6.3M [1]. If REGAL fails, the stock faces catastrophic downside — SLS009 alone cannot justify the $2.35B market cap. ROE of -39.63% and zero revenue mean investors are buying pure optionality. The 2024 departures of the CCO and General Counsel, combined with the securities fraud investigation, raise fundamental governance concerns at the most critical juncture in the company's history.
