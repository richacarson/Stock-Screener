Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Building Materials ·NASDAQ ·United States
Smith-Midland Corporation invents, develops, manufactures, markets, leases, licenses, sells, and installs precast concrete products and systems for the construction, highway, utility, and farming industries. Key proprietary products include J-J Hooks highway safety barriers, SlenderWall lightweight architectural panels, Easi-Set and Easi-Span precast buildings, utility vaults, SoftSound noise-absorbing soundwalls, and Beach Prisms erosion control modules. Founded in 1960 and headquartered in Midland, Virginia with 179 employees, Smith-Midland posted FY2025 record revenue of $93.4M but H1 2026 revenue declined to $45.0M due to the loss of a non-recurring special barrier project, while maintaining a backlog of $57.4M as of August 2026.
