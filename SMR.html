Company Profile
Industrials ·Electrical Equipment & Parts ·NYSE ·United States
NuScale Power is the first and only company to receive U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) Standard Design Approval for a small modular reactor (SMR), positioning it as the global leader in advanced nuclear technology. The company's VOYGR SMR power plant design uses a scalable modular approach with individual 77 MWe modules that can be combined into multi-module configurations for flexible deployment. Under CEO John Hopkins (since 2012), NuScale went public via SPAC merger in 2022 and is pursuing international deployment opportunities after the cancellation of the Idaho Carbon Free Power Project (CFPP) in 2023. The company reported FY2026 estimated revenue of ~$36-40M with TTM net losses of approximately $386M, and holds $1.9B in liquidity. NuScale is actively targeting AI data center power supply as a growth market for its clean baseload nuclear technology.

Investment Thesis
NuScale Power at $8.92 ($3.25B market cap) is the highest-conviction bet on nuclear energy for the AI era — the first and only NRC-approved SMR design gives the company a regulatory moat that competitors cannot replicate in less than 5-7 years [2]. The AI data center buildout creates secular demand for clean baseload electricity that nuclear is uniquely positioned to provide — 24/7 carbon-free power co-located with data centers, solving the intermittency problem that limits solar and wind.
