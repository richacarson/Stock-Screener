Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NYSE ·United States
SmartRent is a proptech platform providing enterprise smart home and smart building solutions for the multifamily housing industry. The company offers purpose-built hardware (smart locks, sensors, thermostats) and an integrated SaaS platform enabling property managers to automate operations, optimize energy usage, and enhance resident experiences across millions of installed devices. Founded in 2017, headquartered in Scottsdale, AZ, the company is executing its Vision 2028 strategy to transition from hardware-centric revenue to an AI-powered property intelligence platform.
