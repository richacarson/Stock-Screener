Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NYSE ·Israel
Similarweb Ltd. provides digital data and analytics solutions enabling businesses to benchmark performance against competitors, analyze web traffic, understand digital consumer behavior, and optimize marketing strategies. The company serves marketers, analysts, and investors through its web intelligence, shopper intelligence, and GenAI data platforms. In Q2 2026, Similarweb achieved its first-ever GAAP operating profit, with AI-related revenue reaching 13% of total revenue, and raised full-year guidance to $340-380M. Headquartered in Israel with approximately 1,000 employees across 9 global offices.

Large language models and AI-powered research tools (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) are increasingly capable of providing competitive intelligence that partially overlaps with Similarweb's value proposition [3].
