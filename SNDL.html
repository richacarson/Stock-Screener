Company Profile
Consumer Defensive ·Beverages - Wineries & Distilleries ·NASDAQ ·Canada
SNDL Inc. is a vertically integrated cannabis company operating in Canada across cannabis cultivation, distribution, and retail, as well as liquor retail through Wine and Beyond, Ace Liquor, and Liquor Depot banners. Formerly known as Sundial Growers, the company rebranded in July 2022 and has diversified from pure-play cannabis cultivation into multi-format retail. Self-described as the largest private-sector liquor and cannabis retailer in Canada.

Investment Thesis
SNDL offers a speculative value proposition as one of Canada's largest vertically integrated cannabis and liquor retailers with an exceptionally clean balance sheet — C$213.4M in cash with no traditional debt at the end of Q1 2026 [1]. The company's diversified retail platform spans ~340 cannabis and liquor stores, and international cannabis sales surged 94% YoY in Q1, demonstrating growth potential beyond the saturated Canadian domestic market. Management anticipates cannabis market improvement in H2 2026 and has identified C$20M+ in profit enhancement initiatives. The stock trades at just $1.39 with a $360M market cap, providing asymmetric upside if the company achieves sustainable profitability or benefits from Canadian cannabis market rationalization [3].
The bear case is substantial. SNDL remains unprofitable with negative ROE of -1.41% and profit margin of -1.7%, meaning shareholders continue to lose money on every dollar of equity invested [3]. Q1 2026 revenue declined 4.4% YoY to C$195.9M with cannabis segment revenue falling 3.8% and operating loss widening 20% — the opposite direction of progress [1]. The Canadian cannabis market is structurally oversupplied with intense price competition and regulatory burden, and SNDL has not achieved GAAP profitability despite years of operation and serial acquisitions. Glassdoor rating of 2.8/5 signals cultural challenges that impede execution. At $1.39, the stock remains near penny-stock territory with limited institutional interest and potential NASDAQ delisting risk if it falls below $1.00. The Inspire score of -38 (MISALIGNED) reflects cannabis cultivation and THC retail negative attributions [3].
