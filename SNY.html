Company Profile
Healthcare ·Drug Manufacturers - General ·NASDAQ ·France
Sanofi is a Paris-based global biopharmaceutical company focused on immunology, rare diseases, neurology and vaccines, anchored by Dupixent (EUR 15.0B of 9M 2026 sales, +34.5%). After spinning off its Opella consumer-health business, Sanofi reported Q3 2026 sales of EUR 14.4B (+14.3% CER) and raised 2026 guidance to low-teens sales growth and mid-teens business EPS growth. Belen Garijo, formerly CEO of Merck KGaA, became CEO on April 29, 2026, after the board declined to renew Paul Hudson's mandate in February 2026. Market cap approximately $108B.

Investment Thesis
Sanofi offers a 5.5% yield with 31 consecutive years of euro dividend increases, a strong balance sheet (D/E 34%) and a forward P/E of only 8.5, while Dupixent (+34.8% in Q3 2026) and new launches (+31.4%) drive low-teens sales growth and mid-teens business EPS growth under raised 2026 guidance [1][2][8]. Q3 2026 free cash flow rose 9.2% to EUR 3.27B, and the EUR 1B buyback was completed in April 2026 [1].
The central debate is the Dupixent loss of exclusivity: the new CEO explicitly prioritizes protecting earnings through it, while pipeline setbacks such as the EUR 952M amlitelimab impairment raise doubts about replacement growth [1][4]. Trailing IFRS metrics (ROE 5.7%, payout 127.5%) understate the business because of impairments and amortization, but they still constrain the mechanical Infrastructure score. Overall SNY is a HOLD for IOWN; note the MISALIGNED faith-alignment flag.
