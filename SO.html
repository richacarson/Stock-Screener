Company Profile
Utilities ·Utilities - Regulated Electric ·NYSE ·United States
Southern Company is the largest electric utility in the United States by customer count, serving 9 million customers through its regulated subsidiaries (Georgia Power, Alabama Power, Mississippi Power) and Southern Company Gas. The company completed Vogtle Units 3 and 4, the first new U.S. nuclear reactors in over 30 years, at a cost exceeding $30B. Under CEO Chris Womack (35+ year company veteran), Southern Company is positioning as a critical infrastructure provider for AI data centers, with 10 GW of power contracts secured. Q2 2026 adj EPS was $1.13 (+22.8% YoY), and management guided full-year 2026 adj EPS at or near the top of the $4.50-$4.60 range, with 8-9% EPS growth projected through 2028.

Investment Thesis
Southern Company at ~20x forward earnings offers a defensive, income-generating position with a powerful AI tailwind. The 10 GW data center power pipeline transforms the company from a slow-growth utility into an infrastructure beneficiary of the AI buildout, with load growth in the Southeast U.S. accelerating faster than at any point in decades [3]. Adj EPS guidance of $4.50-$4.60 for 2026 with 8-9% growth through 2028 provides earnings visibility, while the $3.04 annual dividend (3.30% yield, 25 consecutive years of increases) offers reliable income [1][2]. The completed Vogtle nuclear units add 2+ GW of carbon-free baseload capacity with 60-80 year asset lives, and the regulated utility model provides earnings stability regardless of economic cycles. Net debt has improved, and the company is approaching Dividend Aristocrat status.
The bear case centers on the capital-intensive balance sheet, slow dividend growth, and regulatory/ESG risk. D/E of 190% is elevated even by utility standards, reflecting the Vogtle cost overruns that added billions in debt [4][5]. The 73% payout ratio limits dividend safety, and 3.4% dividend growth barely outpaces inflation — Southern Company will never be a dividend growth compounder. Forward P/E of 20.99x is above the utility sector average, pricing in the AI data center thesis with limited margin of safety if load growth disappoints. CEO Womack's public advocacy for extending coal plant lifetimes creates ESG and regulatory risk — environmental groups and state regulators may push back, and the stance contradicts clean energy commitments [3]. The Inspire Impact Score of -49 (MISALIGNED) limits the investor base for faith-focused allocators. Rate case outcomes in Georgia and Alabama remain a perennial risk for regulated earnings.
