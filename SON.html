Company Profile
Consumer Cyclical ·Packaging & Containers ·NYSE ·United States
Sonoco Products Company designs, manufactures, and sells engineered and sustainable packaging products through two segments: Consumer Packaging (rigid paper, steel, and plastic containers, metal ends, closures) and Industrial Paper Packaging (paperboard tubes, cores, protective packaging, recycled paperboard). Founded in 1899 and headquartered in Hartsville, South Carolina, the company transformed its portfolio through the $3.9B Eviosys acquisition (European metal packaging) in 2024 and ThermoSafe divestiture. TTM revenue of $7.5B with Q2 FY2026 record operating cash flow of $301M (up 56% YoY).

Investment Thesis
Sonoco at $48.50 trades at a deeply discounted 7.7x forward earnings with adjusted EPS guidance of $5.97 for FY2026 and $6.72 for FY2027, implying a 12.5% forward earnings growth rate at a PEG of just 0.21 [1][2]. The 4.45% dividend yield is well-covered at a 33% payout ratio, backed by an extraordinary 100+ consecutive years of payments and 30+ years of consecutive increases — Dividend Aristocrat credentials that provide a durable income floor [5]. Q2 2026 delivered record operating cash flow of $301M (up 56% YoY) and free cash flow of $237M (up 139% YoY), enabling accelerated deleveraging of the $4.8B Eviosys-related debt. ROE of 18.9% demonstrates the business earns well above its cost of capital, and the Eviosys integration is delivering adjusted earnings growth of 10.6% in Q2 despite flat organic revenue.
The bear case centers on acquisition-driven leverage and modest organic growth. D/E of 132% reflects $4.8B in debt from the Eviosys acquisition, leaving minimal balance sheet flexibility if packaging volumes weaken or interest costs rise [5]. Organic revenue is essentially flat at -1.3% YoY (only +2% excluding the ThermoSafe divestiture), raising questions about whether the mature packaging industry can deliver the top-line growth needed to delever the balance sheet over time [1]. GAAP EPS growth of -78.8% in Q2 2026 reflects the lapping of one-time gains in the prior year, creating a misleading trailing PE that may confuse the valuation story. The 5-year dividend growth CAGR of only 3.7% trails inflation, and the slow pace of dividend increases suggests management is prioritizing deleveraging over income growth. Packaging is a mature, cyclical industry exposed to raw material cost volatility (steel, aluminum, paper) and consumer spending sensitivity, while integration complexity across Sonoco's legacy US operations and Eviosys's European platform introduces ongoing execution risk [3][4].

AI Resilience LOW RISK

AI Risk Protection (8/9): Sonoco's core business is manufacturing physical packaging products — composite cans, industrial tubes and cores, metal containers, and protective packaging — that AI structurally cannot substitute [1]. Consumer goods companies will always require physical containers for food, beverage, and personal care products regardless of AI advances. The business involves capital-intensive manufacturing operations across multiple continents, complex supply chains, and deep customer relationships with CPG companies that create significant switching costs. AI may actually increase packaging demand as e-commerce growth accelerates demand for protective and sustainable packaging solutions. AI Opportunity (0/1): Sonoco is deploying AI in manufacturing operations as the 'next leg of operations excellence to drive productivity while lowering costs,' but does not generate quantifiable revenue from AI-related products or services [3]. AI functions as an internal efficiency tool, not a revenue driver.
