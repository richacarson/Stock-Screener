Company Profile
Materials ·Packaging & Containers ·NYSE ·United States
Sonoco Products Company is a global provider of consumer, industrial, healthcare, and protective packaging solutions. Founded in 1899 and headquartered in Hartsville, South Carolina, the company operates through Consumer Packaging and Industrial Paper Packaging segments. Following the $3.9B acquisition of Eviosys (European metal packaging) in 2024 and divestiture of ThermoSafe, Sonoco has transformed into a focused packaging platform with approximately $7.5B in annual revenue.

Investment Thesis
Sonoco trades at an attractive 9.5x forward earnings with adjusted EPS guidance of $5.80-$6.20 for 2026, representing 20% growth ex-divestitures, as the Eviosys integration delivers synergies and the packaging business recovers [1][2]. The 3.83% dividend yield is well-covered at a 36% forward payout ratio, supported by 101 consecutive years of payments and 42+ years of consecutive increases — Dividend Aristocrat credentials that provide income floor. Cash flow surged in Q2 2026, enabling deleveraging of the $5B debt load. ROE of 20.15% demonstrates the business earns well above its cost of capital, and the deep value multiple provides margin of safety against execution risk.
The primary risk is the $5B debt burden (140% D/E) from the Eviosys acquisition, which leaves minimal balance sheet flexibility and creates vulnerability to any earnings miss or rising interest rates [6]. Organic revenue growth is essentially flat at -1% YoY (only +2% excluding divestitures), raising questions about whether the packaging industry can deliver the top-line growth needed to delever [1]. The trailing P/E of 5.5x reflects one-time gains that flatter GAAP earnings and may not recur. Integration risk from Eviosys remains real — Glassdoor reviews flag challenges in corporate communication and departmental collaboration across acquired businesses [5]. Packaging is a mature, cyclical industry exposed to raw material cost volatility and volume sensitivity to consumer spending trends.
Key Catalysts
  1. Eviosys integration synergy realization driving $5.80-$6.20 adjusted EPS in 2026 and accelerating free cash flow generation for debt reduction from the $5B acquisition-related leverage
  2. Packaging volume recovery as consumer spending stabilizes, with Q2 2026 showing 2% organic revenue growth ex-ThermoSafe after multiple quarters of contraction
  3. Potential credit rating upgrade as deleveraging accelerates from strong cash flow generation, reducing interest expense and creating capacity for dividend growth acceleration
Key Risks
  1. Debt-to-equity of 140% and $5B gross debt from the Eviosys acquisition create significant financial risk, with minimal balance sheet flexibility if packaging volumes weaken or interest costs rise
  2. Organic revenue growth of only 2% ex-divestitures suggests limited pricing power in a mature packaging market where consumer brand customers aggressively manage supplier costs
  3. Eviosys integration complexity across European operations introduces execution risk — Glassdoor reviews flag cultural challenges with acquired businesses, and the European metal packaging market faces its own competitive and regulatory pressures
