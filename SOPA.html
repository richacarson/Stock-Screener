Company Profile

Society Pass is a Southeast Asia-focused digital ecosystem company operating across Singapore, Vietnam, Indonesia, the Philippines, and Thailand in digital marketing, lifestyle, beauty, travel, and food & beverage verticals. The company claims 3.3 million registered users and 205,000 registered merchants. On May 12, 2026, Society Pass and its SoPa subsidiary filed Chapter 11 bankruptcy (Houston, Case No. 26-90525), and Nasdaq moved to delist shares on May 21, 2026, suspending trading despite the company's planned appeal. Market cap has collapsed to approximately $1M. The most recent earnings (March 2026) showed revenue of $2.05M and EPS of -$0.15, with TTM revenue of $7.1M and net losses of -$10.2M.

Investment Thesis
There is no bull case for Society Pass at this time. The company filed Chapter 11 bankruptcy on May 12, 2026, Nasdaq suspended trading on May 21, 2026, and the stock trades OTC at approximately $0.37 with a market cap near $1M [1]. TTM revenue of $7.1M against net losses of $10.2M demonstrates a business model that does not work — the company burns $1.43 for every $1 in revenue it generates [2][3]. The most recent quarter showed revenue declining 18% YoY, accelerating the negative trajectory. Even the Inspire Impact Score of 33 (ALIGNED) cannot offset the fundamental business failure.
The risks are existential and immediate. Chapter 11 restructuring may not result in a viable going concern — the company's assets (registered users and merchant relationships across 5 SE Asian countries) have uncertain value in bankruptcy proceedings, and creditor enforcement has been stayed but not eliminated [1]. Nasdaq delisting removes institutional investor access and liquidity, creating a spiral of declining share price and trading volume (daily dollar volume already below $125K). Even if the company emerges from bankruptcy, the competitive landscape in Southeast Asian digital commerce is dominated by Grab, Shopee, and GoTo, all of which have orders-of-magnitude more capital, technology, and market share. The $15.3M cash balance is subject to bankruptcy proceedings and may be consumed by restructuring costs, legal fees, and operating losses before any recovery plan can be executed.
