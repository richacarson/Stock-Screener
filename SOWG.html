Company Profile
Consumer Defensive ·Confectioners ·NASDAQ ·United States
Sow Good Inc. formerly manufactured freeze-dried candy and snack products. In late 2025, the company sold substantially all manufacturing assets to related party Trea Grove, LLC for $1.5M and now operates as a commission-based distributor earning 10% on Trea Grove gross receipts. Activist CEO David Lazar resigned after just three months (March 2026). The company executed a 15:1 reverse stock split in April 2026 to avoid Nasdaq delisting, received a Nasdaq equity deficiency notice, and has disclosed substantial doubt about its ability to continue as a going concern. Market cap approximately $63M.
