Company Profile
Consumer Defensive ·Confectioners ·NASDAQ ·United States
Sow Good Inc. (formerly Black Ridge Oil & Gas) markets freeze-dried candy and snack products under the Sow Good brand. After a boom in the freeze-dried candy category through 2024, the company sold substantially all of its manufacturing assets to a related party in late 2025 and converted to a commission-based distribution-agent model, earning a 10% fee on distributor gross receipts. Activist investor David Lazar took over as Chair and CEO in January 2026 via a $6M private placement, and the company has disclosed going-concern doubt amid a collapse in revenue.
