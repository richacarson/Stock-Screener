Company Profile
Financial Services ·Financial Data & Analytics ·NYSE ·United States
S&P Global provides credit ratings, benchmarks, analytics, and data to global capital and commodity markets. Following the Q2 2026 Mobility spinoff, the company operates through four segments: S&P Global Ratings (credit ratings and research), S&P Global Market Intelligence (financial data, analytics, and AI-powered tools via Kensho), S&P Dow Jones Indices (benchmarks including the S&P 500), and S&P Global Commodity Insights (Platts energy and commodity benchmarks). The 2022 IHS Markit merger ($44B) transformed the company into a comprehensive data analytics platform. Q2 2026 revenue reached $4.15B (+10% YoY) with Ratings posting a record quarter (+17%) and Indices achieving its 13th consecutive record (+20%). CEO Martina Cheung is leading an AI-first transformation through Kensho and agentic solutions. Market cap is approximately $130B.

S&P Global at 23x forward earnings is the preeminent data infrastructure franchise for global capital markets, operating in a government-sanctioned ratings oligopoly and owning the world's most tracked equity benchmark. Q2 2026 delivered $4.15B revenue (+10% YoY) with Ratings posting a record quarter (+17%) and Indices achieving its 13th consecutive record (+20% YoY) [1][2]. Profit margin of 30.54% reflects the high-margin subscription model, EPS grew 26.4% YoY, and the company raised adjusted EPS guidance to $17.50-17.75 for FY2026. The AI transformation through Kensho and the July 2026 Market Intelligence restructuring for agentic solutions positions the company to enhance existing products while creating new revenue streams [4]. The bear case centers on valuation premium and growth deceleration. Forward PEG of 1.85 prices in sustained earnings growth, while full-year guidance of only 5.9-7.9% revenue growth suggests the 10% quarterly pace may decelerate in H2 2026 [1]. ROE of 14.25% narrowly misses the 15% target due to the $27B+ goodwill base from IHS Markit. The Texas Attorney General's investigation into S&P Global's use of ESG factors in credit ratings represents a regulatory risk vector as politicization of ESG could force restructuring of sustainability products [5]. Market Intelligence faces competitive pressure from Bloomberg, FactSet, and AI-native analytics platforms — the July 2026 reorganization with executive changes implicitly acknowledges the segment needs significant evolution [4]. At $130B market cap, S&P Global is priced as a flawless compounder where any execution miss could trigger multiple compression.
