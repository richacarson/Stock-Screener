Company Profile
Industrials ·Engineering & Construction ·NASDAQ ·Singapore
Springview Holdings Ltd, through its wholly owned subsidiary Springview Enterprises Pte. Ltd., designs and constructs residential and commercial buildings in Singapore, offering design, construction, furniture customization, and project management services. Founded in 2002 and led by CEO Zhuo Wang, the company listed on NASDAQ in October 2024 and remains a Nasdaq 'controlled company' majority-owned by Avanta (BVI) Limited. The company achieved bizSAFE Level 4 certification and upgraded to GB1 contractor certification in November 2024.
