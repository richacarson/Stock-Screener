Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NASDAQ ·United States
Sprout Social provides a cloud-based social media management platform that enables organizations to manage, monitor, and analyze their social media presence across channels including Facebook, Instagram, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, TikTok, and Pinterest. The platform offers publishing, analytics, engagement, listening, and advocacy tools for brands and agencies. The company is transitioning upmarket from SMB to enterprise customers, with the $30K+ ARR segment now contributing over 61% of subscription revenue. Q2 2026 revenue grew 10.8% to $123.8M with non-GAAP operating margin expanding 370bps to 12.9%. Full-year 2026 revenue guidance is $493-$495.6M. Market cap is approximately $656M.

Investment Thesis
Sprout Social at 7.0x forward earnings offers an attractive entry point for a mid-cap SaaS platform transitioning to profitability — Q2 2026 revenue grew 10.8% to $123.8M with non-GAAP operating margin expanding 370bps to 12.9%, and the company is guiding to $493-$495.6M full-year revenue [1]. The enterprise pivot is gaining traction with $30K+ ARR customers contributing 61% of subscription revenue (up 20% YoY), CRPO accelerating to 12.4% growth, and total RPO growing 15.5% — indicating the business is building a stickier, higher-value customer base [1]. Forward EPS of $1.54 (vs trailing -$0.48) implies the profitability inflection is imminent, and the PEG ratio of 0.08 reflects the magnitude of the earnings improvement. D/E of 20.1% provides balance sheet flexibility, and the Inspire Impact Score of 46 (ALIGNED) with zero negative attributions supports faith-based investor criteria.
The bear case centers on AI disruption risk, small scale, and organizational instability. Sprout Social's core value proposition — social media management, content scheduling, analytics — is directly threatened by generative AI tools that can perform many of these functions at near-zero cost, and social platforms (Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok) are developing native AI management tools that could disintermediate third-party platforms entirely [2]. At $656M market cap and $482M TTM revenue, Sprout is subscale relative to enterprise software peers and vulnerable to competitive pressure from larger players like Sprinklr and Salesforce Social Studio. The ~20% workforce layoff signals organizational stress, and employee reviews describe leadership disconnect that could undermine the culture needed to retain talent in a competitive SaaS market [2]. Four consecutive years of net losses ($43M in FY2025) mean the profitability inflection remains forward-looking — any revenue deceleration could delay the breakeven timeline and pressure the stock further from its ~80% decline from all-time highs. Revenue growth decelerated from 31% (2023) to 22% (2024) to 13% (2025), raising questions about whether 10.8% Q2 growth represents a floor or continued trajectory decline.

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience MODERATE
5
AI Risk Protection (4/9): Sprout Social's core business — social media management including content scheduling, publishing, analytics, and engagement monitoring — faces meaningful AI disruption risk. Generative AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) can create social media content, draft responses, and analyze sentiment at near-zero marginal cost, potentially commoditizing the content creation and basic analytics functions that form a significant part of Sprout's value proposition [2]. AI-native social media tools could replicate many of Sprout's features as embedded capabilities within social platforms themselves (Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok all developing native AI tools). However, Sprout's enterprise customer relationships, multi-channel integration, compliance workflows, and social listening at scale create switching costs and data moats that basic AI tools cannot easily replicate. The platform's governance, approval workflows, and audit trail features serve regulated industries where generic AI tools are insufficient. AI Opportunity (1/1): Sprout Social is actively embedding AI into its platform — AI-powered content suggestions, automated sentiment analysis, smart inbox prioritization, and GenAI-driven customer engagement features. Q2 2026 referenced 'AI-driven' growth as a factor in enterprise momentum, and the company has invested in AI capabilities that are shipping to customers [1][2].
