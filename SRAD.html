Investment Thesis
Sportradar at 23x forward earnings is a high-growth sports technology platform delivering 19% revenue growth in Q2 2026 (€378M) with full-year guidance of €1.52-1.53B (+19-21% constant currency) and adjusted EBITDA margin expanding to 23.7-24.0% [1]. The company's moat is contractual — exclusive multi-year data rights with all four major North American leagues, ATP, IOC, and FIFA create structural barriers that AI alone cannot overcome. Revenue has nearly tripled from €561M in 2021 to guided €1.53B in 2026, a ~23% CAGR. The IMG ARENA acquisition adds exclusive global sports betting content rights across golf, tennis, and MMA, expanding the addressable market significantly. CEO Koerl's $6.7M+ personal share purchase in May 2026, combined with the $250M accelerated buyback and $1B total repurchase authorization, signals strong management conviction in intrinsic value [4]. Record free cash flow of €167M in FY2025 (+38% in Q1 2026) demonstrates improving cash conversion that should continue as margin expansion accelerates.
The bear case centers on profitability, governance, and market maturation. Net profit margin of just 5.27% and ROE of 7.42% are well below technology company standards, reflecting heavy investment in content rights and IMG ARENA acquisition amortization [5]. The trailing P/E of ~47x demands sustained revenue acceleration that may be difficult as the U.S. sports betting market slows — management noted no major state openings expected and delays in prediction-market deals in their Q2 2026 update [1]. Employee culture is a red flag — Glassdoor reviews describe 'a culture of deference and self-preservation' with accelerating talent drain among high performers, which could undermine technology platform competitiveness at a critical AI integration period [2]. Currency headwinds from EUR/USD translation add revenue volatility (EUR9.3M FX loss in Q1 2026 drove a net loss). The Inspire Impact Score of -1 (MIXED) reflects the Gambling negative attribution, limiting allocation for faith-based investors. Rights cost inflation at league renewals could squeeze margins if Sportradar cannot pass through costs to bookmakers.
Key Catalysts
  1. IMG ARENA content rights monetization ramping through 2026-2027 as exclusive golf, tennis, and MMA data feeds generate incremental revenue and cross-selling synergies across managed trading and advertising platforms drive margin expansion toward the 23.7-24.0% EBITDA guide [1]
  2. AI-native product expansion under CTO/CAIO Behshad Behzadi (ex-Google) — prediction markets, personalized streaming, automated content, and computer vision across 700K+ streamed events, positioning Sportradar at the intersection of sports data and generative AI [2]
  3. $1B total authorized share repurchase program including $250M accelerated buyback, supported by record free cash flow (€167M in FY2025) and pristine balance sheet (D/E 7%, $300M net cash), reducing share count and compounding per-share value [1][4]
Key Risks
  1. Persistently thin profitability (5.27% net margin, 7.42% ROE) at a 47x trailing P/E creates significant downside risk if revenue growth decelerates below the 19-21% guided range, as the valuation embeds no margin for earnings disappointments [5]
  2. Employee culture dysfunction — Glassdoor reviews describe centralized CEO control, accelerating talent drain among high performers, and a culture of self-preservation that could undermine technology platform competitiveness during the critical AI integration period [2]
  3. U.S. sports betting market maturation with no major new state openings expected, combined with regulatory and tax headwinds in international markets and rights cost inflation at league renewals, could compress the secular growth runway [1]
