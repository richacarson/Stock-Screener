Company Profile
Utilities ·Utilities - Diversified ·NYSE ·United States
Sempra is a Fortune 500 energy infrastructure company with approximately $110 billion in assets and 16,800 employees. Operating through regulated utilities SDG&E and SoCalGas in California, Sempra Texas (majority owner of Oncor), and Sempra Infrastructure (LNG and clean energy), the company serves approximately 40 million consumers. FY2025 revenue was $13.7B. Sempra announced a record $65 billion capital plan through 2030 targeting 11% compound rate base growth and 7-9% long-term EPS growth.

Investment Thesis
Sempra offers a compelling blend of defensive utility characteristics and above-average growth potential, anchored by a record $65B capital investment plan through 2030 targeting 11% rate base CAGR and 7-9% EPS growth [7]. The company's regulated utility moat — serving 40 million consumers through SDG&E, SoCalGas, and Oncor — provides highly visible, inflation-protected earnings, while Sempra Infrastructure's LNG development platform adds optionality to the energy export theme [1][3]. Oncor's positioning in Texas to capture data center and electrification-driven demand growth is a differentiated catalyst. With 23 consecutive years of dividend growth, a 2.88% yield, and forward P/E of 17.44, Sempra trades at a discount to utility peers while offering a superior growth profile. CEO Martin's strategic transformation from a regional utility to a national infrastructure platform demonstrates disciplined capital allocation over a multi-decade horizon.
Bear case risks include the elevated trailing P/E of 28.5x, which reflects depressed near-term earnings from regulatory lag and investment cycle timing rather than permanent overvaluation [4]. The 85% payout ratio limits retained earnings for reinvestment, and the $65B capital plan requires significant external financing that could dilute shareholders or pressure credit metrics if interest rates remain elevated [7]. Revenue growth of just 1.6% YoY and 3.8% 5yr CAGR reflects the inherent slow-growth nature of regulated utilities, and ROE of 6.77% trails the 15% target meaningfully [4]. California regulatory risk remains a persistent overhang, with wildfire liability, rate case outcomes, and political uncertainty around energy policy. The Inspire Impact score of -49 (MISALIGNED) may deter faith-aligned investors despite the company's extensive positive ESG attributions across 20 categories [6].
Key Catalysts
  1. Execution on $65B capital plan driving 11% rate base CAGR through 2030, with Oncor capturing accelerating data center and electrification demand in Texas
  2. Port Arthur LNG and Cameron LNG Phase 2 development milestones de-risking Sempra Infrastructure's growth platform and unlocking earnings visibility
  3. Continued dividend growth trajectory — 24th consecutive annual increase expected in 2026 — supported by regulated earnings mix improving to 95% by 2030
Key Risks
  1. California regulatory and wildfire liability risk — rate case outcomes, political intervention, and catastrophic event exposure could impair utility earnings and create capital calls
  2. Elevated 85% payout ratio and massive $65B capital plan require significant external financing; sustained high interest rates could compress returns on invested capital below regulatory assumptions
  3. LNG development timeline and cost overruns — multi-billion-dollar infrastructure projects face construction risk, permitting delays, and potential demand shifts if global LNG markets soften

AI Risk Protection (7/9): Sempra's core business — regulated electricity transmission and distribution, natural gas delivery, and LNG infrastructure — faces virtually zero direct AI substitution risk [6].
