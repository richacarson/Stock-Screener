Company Profile
Consumer Cyclical ·Auto Parts ·NYSE ·United States
Stoneridge designs and manufactures electrical and electronic systems, components, and modules for the automotive, commercial vehicle, off-highway, and agricultural vehicle markets. Its flagship product is MirrorEye, a camera monitor system replacing conventional side mirrors on commercial vehicles, which achieved record Q2 2026 revenue of $37M and surpassed 150,000 units produced globally. The company divested its Control Devices segment to focus on technology-driven products, with 2026 continuing operations revenue guided to $645-670M. Headquartered in Novi, Michigan, with new CEO Natalia Noblet taking the helm in April 2026.
