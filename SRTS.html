Company Profile
Healthcare ·Medical Devices ·NASDAQ ·United States
Sensus Healthcare designs, manufactures, and markets superficial radiation therapy (SRT) systems — primarily the SRT-100 — as non-surgical alternatives for treating non-melanoma skin cancers and keloids. The company reported H1 FY2026 revenue of just $5.7M, down 63.7% from $15.7M in the year-ago period, driven by the loss of its largest historical customer. Q2 2026 included a $5.7M deferred tax asset valuation allowance, widening the net loss to $8.7M ($0.53/share). The company held $15.2M in cash with no debt as of June 30, 2026. CEO Joe Sardano leads from Boca Raton, Florida.
