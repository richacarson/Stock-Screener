SRX Global Inc. (formerly SRx Health Solutions, formerly Better Choice Company) is an AI-driven investment platform focused on generating returns across high-conviction operating companies, strategic assets, and technology-enabled opportunities. The company completed its acquisition of EMJ Crypto Technologies (EMJX) in June 2026 and rebranded from SRx Health Solutions to SRX Global, launching a new AI-driven platform strategy [1]. As of June 30, 2026, the company reported approximately $40M in cash, $15M in short-term investment assets, estimated NAV of ~$60M ($3.07/share on 19.5M post-reverse-split shares), and no debt outstanding [1]. FY2025 annual revenue was $6.53M. The stock has experienced extreme volatility (52-week range $0.96-$46.80) and faces NYSE governance warnings and regulatory scrutiny [2].

Investment Thesis
SRX Global's post-EMJX acquisition balance sheet — $40M cash, $15M short-term investments, no debt, preliminary NAV of $3.07/share against a stock price of ~$1.47 — implies the market values the company at roughly 50% of stated net asset value [1]. If management can successfully deploy capital into high-returning operating companies and demonstrate the EMJX AI-driven allocation framework generates above-market returns, the NAV discount could close or reverse. The leadership team of CEO Kent Cunningham and President Eric Jackson brings public markets and asset management experience. At micro-cap valuations, even modest investment success could generate significant percentage returns.
The bear case is severe. SRX Global has failed at three prior business models (pet food, pharmacy, crypto treasury), generating negative cumulative returns for long-term shareholders. FY2025 revenue was only $6.53M with -50.99% profit margins, and Q2 FY2026 revenue was $3.44M [3]. NYSE governance warnings, a regulatory probe reported in July 2026, and the stock's 80% decline from its 52-week high signal serious institutional concerns [2]. The 'AI-driven platform' positioning has no proven track record — no disclosed investment returns, no AUM growth metrics, and no evidence the EMJX framework generates alpha. The extreme 52-week range ($0.96-$46.80) reflects a penny stock trading vehicle rather than a legitimate investment company. The PE ratio of 0.1 is likely distorted by non-operating gains. For IOWN's framework, every dimension fails: no innovation track record, no mission beyond returns, negative profitability, and serial strategic pivots that characterize a finite game player.
