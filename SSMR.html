Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Silver Mining ·NYSE ·United States
Sunshine Silver Mining & Refining Company is a pre-production silver mining company focused on developing and restarting the historic Sunshine Mine in Idaho's Coeur d'Alene Mining District. The company also operates a silver/copper refinery and an antimony refinery addressing critical mineral supply chain needs. IPO'd in June 2026 at $13.50/share, raising approximately $270M. The Q2 2026 report shows $288.7M cash, $16.7M quarterly net loss, and production restart targeted for late 2028 with base case projections of $268M average annual revenue over a 24-year mine life.

Investment Thesis
Sunshine Silver offers leveraged exposure to silver prices through the highest-grade silver project in the United States, with 300M+ ounces of historical endowment and modern exploration including AI-powered ore modeling [1][2]. The stock has risen 29% from its $13.50 IPO price to $17.45, reflecting growing appreciation for AI-driven silver demand as each AI data center uses substantially more silver than traditional facilities. The $288.5M net cash position (virtually debt-free) provides over 5 years of development runway at current ~$55M annual burn rate, and base case mine economics project $268M average annual revenue with $182M EBITDA over a 24-year mine life. The antimony refinery partnership with Perpetua Resources adds critical mineral optionality with potential US government support [3]. Analyst consensus is Buy with $19.33 price target (11% upside) [5].
The bear case centers on execution risk and extreme pre-revenue valuation. Sunshine Silver reported a $53.6M TTM net loss with only $0.43M in incidental revenue — the $2.5B market cap is built entirely on optionality of future production that remains 2+ years away after 16 years of development [1][5]. The feasibility study is not expected until Q2 2027, and production restart hinges on a positive final investment decision that could be delayed by permitting challenges, geological surprises, or silver price declines. At current burn rates (~$55M/year), the $288M cash pile could require supplemental financing before production generates cash flow, risking dilution. Silver price sensitivity is extreme: base case economics assume $46.36/oz, and a sustained decline below $30/oz could render the project uneconomic. Single-asset concentration in one Idaho mine creates geological, regulatory, and operational risk with zero revenue diversification. The Infrastructure score of 3/10 reflects the fundamental financial weakness of a pre-revenue company with deeply negative ROE and no earnings visibility.

Mining permitting, environmental compliance, and underground operations create deep physical and regulatory moats.
