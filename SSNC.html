Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NASDAQ ·United States
SS&C Technologies provides software and software-enabled services to the asset management, wealth, insurance, banking, and healthcare industries, including fund administration (SS&C GlobeOp), transfer agency and investor services (DST), Advent portfolio software, Calastone fund transaction network, Blue Prism intelligent automation, and healthcare claims and pharmacy administration. Founded in 1986 by CEO Bill Stone and headquartered in Windsor, Connecticut, it serves over 23,000 clients.

Investment Thesis
SS&C is a steady compounder priced like a value stock. Q3 2026 revenue rose 7.8% to $1,690.0M with 5.2% adjusted organic growth, adjusted EPS rose 14.8% to $1.78, and adjusted EBITDA reached a record $674.8M at a 39.9% margin [1]. Full-year guidance calls for $6,736-6,776M of adjusted revenue and $7.11-7.17 of adjusted EPS, and nine-month operating cash flow grew 10.4% to $1,216.3M [1]. At 10.4x forward earnings with a 31% payout ratio, an 11.1% dividend raise to $1.20, and steady buybacks, Infrastructure scores 9/10, and internal AI deployment (3,500 digital workers, 50 agents) supports margin expansion [1][3].
The bear case is leverage, earnings quality, and AI pricing pressure. Gross debt is $7.5B (net leverage 2.63x), $2.0B of notes mature September 2027 and need refinancing, and GAAP Q3 EPS was flat at $0.83 with net income down 5.3% [1]. Organic growth of about 5% depends on labor-heavy services that AI could commoditize, which drove a software-sector selloff in 2026 [3]. Governance is a concern: say-on-pay failed in 2020 and 2022 and CEO pay ran about 65% above peers [6][7], while succession from the 40-year founder remains unresolved.
Key Catalysts
  1. Refinancing the $2.0B of September 2027 senior notes in Q4 2026 on favorable terms, removing a near-term overhang [1]
  2. Q4 2026 adjusted EPS of $1.88-1.94 and continued EBITDA margin gains from internal AI agents and digital workers [1][3]
  3. Commercial traction for agentic AI and tokenization offerings across the 23,000-client base [1][4]
Key Risks
  1. AI-driven pricing compression in labor-intensive fund administration and transfer agency services [3]
  2. Leverage of 2.63x with $7.5B gross debt and acquisition integration risk if growth slows [1]
  3. Founder-CEO succession and governance risk given Bill Stone's tenure and past failed say-on-pay votes [6][7]
