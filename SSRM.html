Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Gold ·NASDAQ ·United States
SSR Mining is a precious metals mining company focused on the Americas, operating the Marigold gold mine in Nevada, the Seabee gold operation in Saskatchewan, the Puna silver-lead-zinc operation in Argentina, and the Cripple Creek & Victor gold mine in Colorado. The company completed its $1.5B sale of the Copler mine in Turkey on June 24, 2026, following the fatal 2024 heap leach pad failure. Third-largest gold producer in the United States. Market cap approximately $6.4B.

Investment Thesis
SSR Mining is a deep-value gold miner trading at 5.86x forward earnings versus peer multiples of 12-14x, offering significant upside if the valuation discount closes [5]. Q1 2026 delivered a strong beat — revenue surged 84% to $581.8M, adjusted EPS of $1.15 exceeded the $0.79 consensus by 46%, and free cash flow nearly tripled to $210.8M [4]. The balance sheet is pristine at 2% D/E with $1.1B in liquidity plus $1.5B in completed Copler sale proceeds, providing extraordinary firepower for acquisitions or returns. The company authorized $500M in additional buybacks (bringing total programs to $800M) and reinstated dividends [4]. As the third-largest US gold producer with 11M gold-equivalent ounce reserves, SSR Mining is well-positioned to benefit from sustained gold price strength.
The bear case centers on the Copler disaster's lasting impact on management credibility and the execution risk of capital redeployment. Nine workers were killed in the February 2024 heap leach pad collapse caused by a liner system design flaw, exposing fundamental safety culture failures [3]. Without Copler, SSR Mining has a smaller production base (450,000-535,000 gold-equivalent ounces for 2026) with limited diversification across Marigold, CCV, Seabee, and Puna [4]. The deep valuation discount to peers may reflect legitimate governance concerns rather than simple mispricing. Capital allocation is the critical risk — the company must redeploy $1.5B+ into value-accretive acquisitions during a gold bull market where asset prices are elevated. Trailing profit margin of 12.17% is below peers, and Glassdoor reviews (3.2/5) citing 'ego battles' and 'micro managing leadership' suggest cultural issues that could impair operational execution [2].
Key Catalysts
  1. Value-accretive deployment of $1.5B+ in Copler sale proceeds and operating cash flow into new mining assets, potentially transforming SSR Mining's production profile and reserve base during a favorable gold price environment
  2. Gold price strength driving analyst EPS estimates of $4+ for 2026, with Q1 adjusted EPS already at $1.15 annualizing to $4.60+ if sustained, representing significant earnings growth [4]
  3. $800M in total buyback authorizations at 5.86x forward earnings providing meaningful per-share value accretion and signaling management confidence in the stock's discount to intrinsic value [4][5]
Key Risks
  1. Post-Copler credibility deficit — the fatal 2024 heap leach pad collapse killed nine workers and exposed safety culture failures, potentially impairing management credibility with investors, regulators, and acquisition targets [3]
  2. Capital redeployment risk — SSR Mining must find and execute value-accretive acquisitions with $1.5B+ during a gold bull market where mining asset prices are elevated, and overpaying would destroy the deep-value thesis
  3. Concentrated production base of 450,000-535,000 gold-equivalent ounces across four mines leaves limited margin for operational setbacks, weather disruptions, or permitting delays at any single operation [4]
