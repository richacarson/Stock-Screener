Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Gold ·NASDAQ ·United States
SSR Mining is a precious metals mining company focused on the Americas, operating the Marigold gold mine in Nevada, the Seabee gold operation in Saskatchewan, the Puna silver-lead-zinc operation in Argentina, and the Cripple Creek & Victor gold mine in Colorado. The company completed its $1.5B sale of the Copler mine in Turkey to Cengiz Holding on June 24, 2026, following the fatal 2024 heap leach pad failure. Third-largest gold producer in the United States. Q2 2026 revenue of $443.8M. Market cap approximately $6.4B.

Investment Thesis
SSR Mining is a deep-value gold miner trading at 7.36x forward earnings versus peer multiples of 12-14x, with improving fundamentals driven by record gold prices and post-Copler operational clarity [5][6]. Q1 2026 delivered a strong beat — revenue surged 84% to $581.8M, adjusted EPS of $1.15 exceeded the $0.79 consensus by 46%, and free cash flow nearly tripled to $210.8M [4]. Q2 2026 followed with $443.8M revenue and $0.47 EPS (+11.9% YoY) [5]. The balance sheet is pristine at 1.82% D/E with $1.5B in completed Copler sale proceeds plus $1.1B in existing liquidity, providing extraordinary firepower for acquisitions. ROE has improved to 17.34% and profit margin to 29.47% as Copler charges clear and gold prices surge [6]. The $500M additional buyback program at 7.36x forward earnings provides meaningful per-share value accretion.
The bear case centers on the Copler disaster's lasting impact on management credibility and the execution risk of capital redeployment. Nine workers were killed in the February 2024 heap leach pad collapse, exposing fundamental safety culture failures that Glassdoor reviews (2.7/5) corroborate with descriptions of 'ego battles' and 'micro managing leadership' [2][3]. Without Copler, SSR Mining has a smaller production base (450,000-535,000 gold-equivalent ounces for 2026) with limited mine diversification [4]. The deep valuation discount to peers may reflect legitimate governance concerns rather than simple mispricing. Capital allocation is the critical risk — the company must redeploy $1.5B+ into value-accretive acquisitions during a gold bull market where mining asset prices are elevated and overpaying would destroy the deep-value thesis. Q2 2026 highlighted 'cost pressures' amid strong cash position, suggesting operational challenges that gold price alone cannot solve [5]. Revenue and earnings are heavily leveraged to gold prices, and any correction in gold (currently at record levels) would compress margins and production economics.
Key Catalysts
  1. Value-accretive deployment of $1.5B+ in Copler sale proceeds into new mining assets could transform SSR Mining's production profile and reserve base, potentially narrowing the peer valuation discount [4][5]
  2. Gold price strength with 44% YTD gains driving analyst EPS estimates higher — Q1 adjusted EPS of $1.15 annualizes to $4.60+, representing significant earnings power at current gold levels [4]
  3. $800M in total buyback authorizations at 7.36x forward earnings providing meaningful per-share value accretion and signaling management confidence in the stock's discount to intrinsic value [4][6]
Key Risks
  1. Post-Copler credibility deficit — the fatal 2024 heap leach pad collapse killed nine workers, exposing safety culture failures confirmed by Glassdoor reviews (2.7/5), potentially impairing management credibility with investors, regulators, and acquisition targets [2][3]
  2. Capital redeployment risk — SSR Mining must find and execute value-accretive acquisitions with $1.5B+ during a gold bull market where mining asset prices are elevated, and overpaying would destroy the deep-value thesis [4]
  3. Gold price concentration risk — all revenue and margin metrics are heavily leveraged to record gold prices (+44% YTD), and any meaningful correction in gold would compress profit margins, reduce FCF, and potentially impair the valuation thesis [4][6]

AI Resilience LOW RISK

AI Risk Protection (8/9): Gold mining is a physical-world operation that AI structurally cannot displace — extracting gold from underground and open-pit mines requires heavy equipment, geological expertise, and physical infrastructure that no AI tool can substitute [1]. SSR Mining's core revenue streams — Marigold heap leach, Seabee underground, CCV open pit, and Puna polymetallic operations — face zero substitution risk from AI. The gold price itself is driven by macroeconomic factors (inflation hedging, central bank buying, geopolitical risk) entirely orthogonal to AI disruption. AI may actually increase demand for precious metals through electronics and data center infrastructure expansion. The only modest risk is that AI-driven exploration tools could help competitors identify deposits more efficiently, but this does not threaten SSR Mining's existing reserves and operations. The company may use basic automation in operations, but no measurable AI revenue contribution is evident [2].
