Company Profile
Financial Services ·Information Technology Services ·NASDAQ ·Brazil
StoneCo is a Brazilian fintech platform that provides payment processing, digital banking, credit, and software solutions to small and medium-sized merchants across Brazil. The company processes payments through its proprietary POS network, offers working capital advances and credit products, and provides enterprise management software. CEO Mateus Schwening was appointed in March 2026 (transitioning from CFO), following the resignation of CEO Pedro Zinner. Q1 2026 revenue reached R$3.58B (+13% YoY) with net income of R$1.78B (+247%), though the net income surge was driven by a one-time tax gain. Q2 2026 EPS was $0.47, beating estimates. The company guided FY2026 adjusted gross profit of R$6.6-7.0B and adj basic EPS of R$10.8-11.4, though credit quality deterioration (90+ day NPLs ~7%, cost of risk 21.9%) is the key concern.

Investment Thesis
StoneCo at 4.92x forward earnings is a deep-value Brazilian fintech trading at a substantial discount to intrinsic worth. ROE of 30.65%, profit margin of 25.37%, and Forward PEG of 0.33 represent exceptional financial metrics for a company growing revenue at 13%+ and guiding for R$6.6-7.0B in FY2026 adjusted gross profit [1][4]. The company's integrated payment-banking-credit platform creates deep merchant relationships with high switching costs, and the Brazilian SMB market remains underpenetrated by digital financial services. Q1 2026 net income of R$1.78B (+247%) demonstrated earnings power, and the special dividend signals management confidence in cash generation [2]. At current levels, the market is pricing in permanent impairment from credit losses and Brazil macro risk, creating asymmetric upside if credit quality stabilizes and Brazil's interest rate cycle turns favorable.
The bear case is credit quality, leadership instability, and Brazil macro risk. Credit metrics deteriorated sharply in recent quarters — 90+ day NPLs near 7%, cost of risk at 21.9%, and provisions rising 50%+ QoQ — raising concerns that StoneCo's credit expansion thesis is fundamentally flawed [1]. As a company increasingly dependent on credit revenue, worsening asset quality could consume the earnings gains from platform expansion. The CEO transition (third change in recent years) and 6 new board directors in 3 years create governance risk and strategic uncertainty — each leader has shifted the company's direction [3]. Brazil's high Selic rate environment compresses fintech margins and increases credit losses, and any escalation in U.S.-Brazil trade tensions or BRL depreciation directly impacts the dollar-denominated stock price. Atmos Capital's complete exit from its $169M position despite a 'Strong Buy' consensus is a concerning signal from a sophisticated local investor.
Key Catalysts
  1. Brazilian interest rate cycle turning — any Selic rate reduction would immediately improve credit margins, reduce NPL formation, and expand fintech demand from SMBs currently priced out of credit markets [1]
  2. H2 2026 improvement driven by TPV growth acceleration and credit revenue expansion, as guided by management, which could demonstrate the platform thesis despite Q1-Q2 headwinds [1]
  3. Valuation re-rating from 4.92x forward earnings toward fintech peer multiples (10-15x) if credit quality stabilizes and the new CEO establishes strategic credibility [4]
Key Risks
  1. Credit quality spiral — 90+ day NPLs near 7% and cost of risk at 21.9% could worsen if Brazil's macro environment deteriorates, potentially requiring large provision increases that eliminate profitability [1]
  2. Leadership instability with the third CEO transition in recent years and 6 new board directors in 3 years creating strategic uncertainty and governance risk that discourages institutional investment [3]
  3. Brazil macro headwinds — elevated Selic rates, BRL depreciation, and potential U.S.-Brazil trade tensions create a hostile operating environment for a fintech dependent on SMB credit demand [1]
