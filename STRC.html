Strategy Inc. (formerly MicroStrategy) operates as the world's largest Bitcoin treasury company and enterprise analytics software provider. Investment Thesis
STRC is Strategy Inc.'s variable rate preferred stock offering approximately 12.2% current yield ($12 annual dividend on $98.51 market price) backed by the capital structure of the world's largest corporate Bitcoin holder (843,775+ BTC) [1][3]. The preferred has recovered from $88.39 to $98.51 since July, now trading at just a 1.5% discount to $100 par, reflecting improved market confidence in dividend sustainability. FY2025 software revenue of $477M grew 3% with accelerating Subscription Services growth from the Strategy ONE AI platform. Insider purchases of $4.2M+ by CEO Le and multiple directors signal management conviction in the capital structure [3]. At 12.2% yield with potential capital appreciation back to par, STRC offers an attractive income instrument for investors comfortable with Bitcoin correlation risk.
The bear case centers on extraordinary leverage and single-asset concentration. STRC's 12% preferred dividend costs approximately $1B+ annually against only $477M in stagnant software revenue — the gap must be funded by capital markets activities that depend on favorable Bitcoin prices and investor appetite for additional equity/debt issuance [1][3]. Trailing EPS of -$99.16 reflects $12.77B+ in Bitcoin mark-to-market GAAP losses, and while forward EPS of $49.49 assumes Bitcoin appreciation, any sustained BTC decline below the company's cost basis could impair dividend coverage. The Glassdoor rating of 2.8/5.0 with reports of toxic leadership culture suggests organizational decay in the software business that provides the only operational revenue [2]. Revenue 5yr CAGR of -1.5% demonstrates the software business is slowly eroding while all strategic focus is on Bitcoin accumulation. Bitcoin's historical 50%+ drawdowns represent the primary existential risk to STRC's dividend and recovery value.
Key Catalysts
  1. Bitcoin price appreciation above Strategy's ~$75K average cost basis would expand the equity cushion protecting STRC's preferred dividend and drive the stock back to or above $100 par value [1]
  2. Strategy ONE cloud migration and Subscription Services growth demonstrate viability of the non-Bitcoin revenue base that ultimately supports STRC's dividend coverage [1]
  3. Continued insider buying by CEO Le and directors ($4.2M+) and STRC's recovery from $88 to $98.51 signal improving market confidence in the capital structure's sustainability [3]
Key Risks
  1. STRC's 12% preferred dividend requires ~$1B+ annually against only $477M in software revenue — the coverage gap depends on capital markets access that a Bitcoin price decline could shut off [1][3]
  2. Organizational culture deterioration at 2.8/5.0 Glassdoor with toxic leadership reports threatens the software business viability that provides Strategy's only operational revenue stream [2]
  3. Bitcoin's historical 50%+ drawdowns represent existential risk — a sustained BTC decline below cost basis could trigger capital structure stress, preferred dividend suspension, and STRC trading at deep discounts to par [1]

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience MODERATE
6
AI Risk Protection (5/9): Strategy Inc.'s dual business creates a split AI risk profile. The Bitcoin treasury — which constitutes over 99% of enterprise value at 843,775+ BTC — is structurally immune to AI disruption; Bitcoin's value proposition as a decentralized store of value is not threatened by artificial intelligence [1]. However, the legacy enterprise analytics business (Strategy ONE, ~$477M annual revenue) faces significant AI disruption pressure from AI-native competitors like Databricks, Snowflake, and Microsoft Copilot-integrated Power BI that are eroding market share in a segment already stagnant. Since the software business generates cash flow needed to service STRC's 12% preferred dividend, its AI vulnerability creates indirect risk for preferred holders. AI Opportunity (1/1): Strategy ONE integrates AI technologies for enterprise analytics with natural-language query capabilities, and Subscription Services growth indicates customer adoption of the AI-enhanced cloud platform [1].
