Starz Entertainment Corp. operates the STARZ premium subscription video programming service in the United States and Canada, offering original series and feature films through direct-to-consumer streaming and wholesale distribution via cable, satellite, and telecom providers. Spun off from Lionsgate in 2025, the company reported a record 12.7M domestic OTT subscribers in Q4 2025, with trailing adjusted OIBDA of $204M. With only 517 employees and annual revenue of approximately $1.25B, Starz is one of the smallest publicly traded premium content platforms. Based in Vancouver, Canada.

Investment Thesis
Starz at $23.79 with a $407M market cap is a speculative small-cap play on premium content streaming at a deeply discounted valuation. The company achieved a record 12.7M domestic OTT subscribers in Q4 2025, generated $204M in trailing adjusted OIBDA, and management guides to positive free cash flow of $80-120M while reducing leverage to approximately 2.7x [1]. The 'Power' franchise remains a valuable content asset driving subscriber retention, and the small employee base of 517 keeps fixed costs manageable. At under $400M enterprise value, Starz could be an attractive acquisition target for a larger streaming platform seeking niche premium content and an established subscriber base [1][3].
The bear case is overwhelming for a standalone investment. Starz carries $787M in debt against only $60M in cash, with a D/E ratio of 266% and deeply negative profit margins of -25% — the company is structurally unprofitable on a GAAP basis [5]. Revenue is declining at -3.7% YoY as cord-cutting accelerates and the streaming market consolidates around larger, better-capitalized competitors [2]. With only 12.7M subscribers and $1.25B in annual revenue, Starz lacks the scale to compete for premium content against Netflix ($40B+ content budget), Disney ($30B+), or Amazon ($15B+). The company stopped reporting subscriber counts, which typically signals deterioration management wants to obscure [1]. Forward EPS of -$4.53 implies continued deep losses, and the stock trades at 1.3x book value with no dividend and no path to profitability visible in analyst estimates. The Lionsgate separation saddled Starz with disproportionate debt that constrains content investment, creating a negative flywheel of declining subscribers, reduced content spend, and further subscriber losses [3][5].
Key Catalysts
  1. Potential acquisition by a larger streaming platform (Netflix, Amazon, Apple, Paramount Skydance) seeking Starz's 12.7M subscriber base and premium content library at an attractive sub-$500M enterprise value [1][3]
  2. Power franchise expansion continuing to drive subscriber retention and engagement, with the brand extending across multiple successful spin-off series that create recurring content value [1]
  3. Leverage reduction to 2.7x as guided by management, combined with $80-120M in projected unlevered free cash flow, could stabilize the balance sheet and reduce the existential debt risk that depresses the stock [1]
Key Risks
  1. Structural unprofitability with -25% profit margins, $787M in debt on $60M cash, and forward EPS of -$4.53 — the company may lack the financial resources to fund content investment necessary to retain subscribers against better-capitalized competitors [5]
  2. Accelerating cord-cutting and streaming consolidation squeezing niche premium providers — Starz's 12.7M subscribers represent a fraction of major platforms, and content budgets cannot compete at scale, creating a negative flywheel of subscriber losses and reduced content quality [1][2]
  3. AI-driven disruption of scripted content production compressing the value of Starz's core programming, while the separated entity lacks access to the Lionsgate-Runway AI partnership that could offset production cost pressures [4]

AI Risk Protection (5/9): Starz occupies an EXPOSED position in AI disruption. The company's core business — producing and licensing premium scripted content — faces real substitution risk as generative AI tools increasingly replicate writing, visual effects, dubbing, and localization at a fraction of traditional production costs [4]. The 2023 Hollywood strikes validated AI displacement as a concrete industry threat. Unlike larger studios with diversified revenue (live sports, news, theme parks), Starz has no AI-immune business lines — its entire value proposition depends on human-created scripted entertainment that faces direct AI competition. However, premium branded content (the 'Power' franchise, original series with A-list talent) retains human creative value that generic AI output cannot replicate, and Starz's wholesale distribution relationships provide some switching-cost protection [1].
