StubHub Holdings operates the world's largest ticketing marketplace for live events, enabling buying and selling of tickets through websites and mobile applications under the StubHub and viagogo brands. The company holds approximately 50% North American secondary ticketing market share and processed $9.2B in gross merchandise sales in 2025. IPO'd in September 2025 at $23.50 per share. Market cap approximately $4.8B.

Investment Thesis
StubHub is the dominant secondary ticketing marketplace with approximately 50% North American market share, 82% gross margins, and accelerating growth. Q1 2026 marked a turning point: revenue grew 12% YoY to $446M (normalizing post-Eras Tour comps), net income flipped to $48M from a -$22.2M loss, adjusted EBITDA margin expanded 400bps to 16%, and free cash flow surged 90% to $290.6M [4]. Management reiterated full-year guidance for $9.9-10.1B GMS and $400-420M adjusted EBITDA [4]. The stock has recovered from $6.10 to $12.86 but remains 45% below its $23.50 IPO price, suggesting further upside if operational momentum continues. New revenue streams from advertising (sponsored listings) and the OpenAI partnership for agentic commerce could provide incremental growth catalysts [5].
The bear case revolves around AI disruption risk, cultural dysfunction, and regulatory headwinds. StubHub's own SEC filing warns of 'Google Zero' risk — AI-driven search evolution and agentic AI could bypass marketplace intermediation, threatening the web traffic and discovery model that drives transactions [2]. Glassdoor's 2.5/5 rating with only 22% of employees recommending the company and 'incredibly toxic' culture reviews suggest deep organizational challenges [2]. The April 2026 FTC settlement ($10M for deceptive fee practices) signals regulatory scrutiny of secondary ticketing that could intensify under the Biden-era transparency rules [6]. GAAP financials remain deeply distorted by the $1.4B IPO SBC charge and $479M valuation allowance, making it difficult to assess true profitability. Forward P/E of 27x (vs 4.7x in April) reduces the deep-value case as the stock has re-rated. ROE remains deeply negative at -109%, and the company has yet to generate a full year of GAAP profitability as a public company.
Key Catalysts
  1. Full-year 2026 adjusted EBITDA of $400-420M (up ~80% YoY) execution would demonstrate the marketplace's operating leverage and justify further re-rating from the current 45% IPO discount [4]
  2. OpenAI/ChatGPT agentic commerce partnership could transform StubHub from a destination marketplace into an embedded ticketing platform within AI assistants, turning the AI disruption risk into an opportunity [5]
  3. Advertising and sponsored listings monetization scaling through 2026 — a high-margin revenue stream that could materially improve unit economics without requiring GMS growth [1]
Key Risks
  1. AI-driven search disruption ('Google Zero') — StubHub's reliance on internet search engines for traffic is a self-acknowledged risk factor as AI assistants and agentic commerce could disintermediate traditional marketplace discovery [2]
  2. Organizational dysfunction — 2.5/5 Glassdoor rating, 22% employee recommendation rate, and 'incredibly toxic' culture reviews create execution risk during a critical post-IPO scaling phase [2]
  3. Regulatory escalation — the $10M FTC settlement for deceptive fee practices signals heightened scrutiny of secondary ticketing; additional enforcement actions under the Unfair or Deceptive Fees Rule could impose transparency requirements that compress take rates [6]

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience MODERATE
5
AI Risk Protection (4/9): StubHub faces meaningful AI disruption risk from agentic commerce and AI-driven search changes. The company's own SEC filing explicitly warns of 'Google Zero' risk — AI-driven search could erode the web traffic that drives ticket discovery [2]. StubHub International CEO Bob Kupbens acknowledged that AI will bring 'new entrants that disrupt innovation, maybe more aggressively than some existing brands' [5]. As AI assistants handle ticket purchasing directly, StubHub's marketplace intermediation could be bypassed. However, ~50% market share, network effects from seller liquidity, and buyer trust provide meaningful defensive moats that AI cannot easily replicate. AI Opportunity (1/1): StubHub generates AI-adjacent value through its OpenAI/ChatGPT partnership for conversational ticket discovery, AI-driven dynamic pricing, and AI-powered fraud detection — these are shipping, revenue-generating AI features that position StubHub as an early mover in agentic commerce for ticketing [2][5].
