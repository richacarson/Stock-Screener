Company Profile
Technology ·Computer Hardware ·NASDAQ ·Ireland
Seagate Technology is one of two vertically integrated hard disk drive manufacturers (alongside Western Digital) and a leader in mass-capacity nearline storage for hyperscale cloud data centers, built on its HAMR-based Mozaic platform. FY2026 (ended July 3, 2026) revenue rose 34% to $12.2B with $3.18B of net income. Fiscal Q1 2027 revenue jumped 61% to $4.23B at a 57.3% GAAP gross margin, and management guided FQ2 to $4.8B. Dave Mosley has been CEO since 2017 and board chair since October 2025. The company is domiciled in Dublin, Ireland. Market cap approximately $148B.

Finite vs Infinite Game — Sinek (25%)
Mindset: MIXED Overall: 6 /10
Seagate shows real infinite-game traits: it kept funding HAMR through the FY2023 downturn ($529M net loss, $797M R&D) and is now harvesting that bet with record margins [1][3]. Its leadership is long-tenured, with Mosley spending nearly three decades at the company before becoming CEO [4]. Sinek would still question the heavy capital-return orientation ($568M returned in FQ1 alone), the securities class action and export-control settlement history, and a purpose framed around products rather than a Just Cause [1][5].

Investment Thesis
Seagate is in a powerful upcycle driven by data-center storage demand: FQ1 2027 revenue grew 61% to $4.23B, GAAP gross margin expanded to 57.3%, free cash flow reached a September-quarter record of $1.48B, and FQ2 guidance calls for $4.8B of revenue and $9.20 non-GAAP EPS [1]. At $644.58 the stock trades at 9.8x forward earnings with a 0.37 PEG, about 44% below its 52-week high of $1,145 [2].
Debt retirement of about $1.95B has cut gross debt to $1.6B and normalized D/E to 77%, removing the balance-sheet drag that weighed on prior screens [1][2]. Risks are the cyclicality of HDD demand (FY2024 revenue was 44% below FY2022), the low 2.7% 5-year revenue CAGR, modest R&D intensity, and the stock's sharp drawdown after the FQ1 print [3][6].
