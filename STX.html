Company Profile
Technology ·Computer Hardware ·NASDAQ ·United States
Seagate Technology is the world's leading manufacturer of hard disk drives (HDDs), forming a duopoly with Western Digital. The company commercialized Heat-Assisted Magnetic Recording (HAMR) technology through its Mozaic platform, enabling next-generation high-capacity storage for AI data centers and cloud infrastructure. FY2026 delivered $12.2B revenue (+34% YoY), record $3.1B FCF, and non-GAAP EPS of $15.58 (+92%). CEO William Davis succeeded Dave Mosley (now Chairman) in January 2025. Headquartered in Dublin, Ireland with primary operations in the U.S. and Singapore. Market cap approximately $186B.

Investment Thesis
Seagate is experiencing a transformative AI-driven growth cycle validated by record FY2026 results — $12.2B revenue (+34% YoY), non-GAAP EPS of $15.58 (+92%), and record $3.1B free cash flow [2]. HAMR technology via the Mozaic platform is now fully commercialized with five major cloud customers, providing a multi-generational product roadmap to 50TB+ drives that cement HDD's cost/TB advantage for AI mass-capacity storage [1]. Data center revenue surged 57% and accounts for ~90% of exabyte shipments as agentic AI, inference workloads, and multimodal applications drive exponential data creation [1][2]. Forward PEG of 0.46 against 73% 3-year EPS growth forecast suggests the stock remains attractively valued despite the massive re-rating [5]. The HDD duopoly with Western Digital provides structural pricing power, and profit margin of 21.6% demonstrates operating leverage.
The bear case centers on cyclicality, valuation, and balance sheet risk. Seagate's revenue collapsed 44% from $11.66B (FY2022) to $6.55B (FY2024), demonstrating the extreme demand cyclicality inherent in HDD manufacturing — FY2026's record year does not eliminate the risk of another severe downturn when AI capex decelerates [5]. The 82x trailing P/E and 35.5x forward P/E at $868 per share prices in substantial continued growth; any revenue deceleration or shift toward flash storage for AI workloads could trigger severe multiple compression given the 2.07 beta. D/E of 382% remains well above the 300% floor, reflecting cumulative buyback-driven equity erosion that leaves minimal balance sheet cushion for the next downturn. The $300M Huawei export control penalty (2023) exposed governance weaknesses in compliance oversight [4]. The CEO transition from 30-year veteran Mosley to William Davis in January 2025 introduces leadership uncertainty. 5-year revenue CAGR of just 0.6% reminds investors that the current boom follows an equally dramatic bust.
