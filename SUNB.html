Company Profile
Industrials ·Rental & Leasing Services ·NYQ ·United States
Sunbelt Rentals Holdings, Inc. is the second-largest equipment rental company in North America, operating 1,250+ locations across the United States, United Kingdom, and Canada under the Sunbelt Rentals brand. The company rents construction, industrial, and general equipment including mobile elevating work platforms, excavators, forklifts, generators, and specialty solutions (power/HVAC, scaffold, trench safety, flooring, temporary structures). Founded in 1947, Sunbelt completed its NYSE listing in March 2026 after separating from UK parent Ashtead Group. Fiscal 2026 (ending April 2026) delivered record total revenue of $11.15B with adjusted EBITDA of $4.68B (41.9% margin) and record free cash flow of $2.1B.

Investment Thesis
Sunbelt Rentals at $76.02 with a $31.2B market cap is a newly independent, high-quality industrial compounder trading at an attractive 15.6x forward earnings with a PEG of just 1.14. Fiscal 2026 (ending April 2026) delivered record total revenue of $11.15B, record free cash flow of $2.1B (up 23%), and adjusted EBITDA of $4.68B (41.9% margin), despite Q4 being burdened by restructuring and NYSE relisting costs [1]. The specialty rental segment grew 15.1% in Q4, and the Reliant Asset Management acquisition expands the modular space offering. FY2027 guidance of 5-8% rental revenue growth and $4.85B-$5.05B adjusted EBITDA positions Sunbelt for continued compounding, and the equipment rental industry benefits from secular tailwinds including infrastructure spending, data center construction, and outsourcing trends favoring rental over ownership.
The bear case centers on cyclical exposure and balance sheet leverage. D/E of 153% is structurally elevated for the asset-heavy rental model, and the $41B+ fleet requires continuous capital reinvestment even in downturns [1]. EBITDA margin compressed 210 basis points YoY in FY2026 as higher ancillary revenues, repair costs, and growth investments weighed on profitability — a trend that could continue if utilization rates soften in a construction slowdown. Revenue growth has decelerated from double-digits to mid-single-digits (FY2025 revenue actually declined slightly from FY2024 before recovering in FY2026), suggesting the post-COVID equipment boom is normalizing. The newly listed company lacks a dividend track record (first dividends paid in FY2026), and the separation from Ashtead Group means Sunbelt must now independently manage its capital markets relationships, tax structure, and governance. A construction recession would expose the operational leverage inherent in a fixed-cost fleet rental model, potentially compressing margins significantly below the 42% EBITDA target [2].
Key Catalysts
  1. Secular equipment rental penetration growth — rental penetration in North America continues to increase as construction companies prefer renting over owning specialty equipment, expanding Sunbelt's $70B+ addressable market with the specialty segment growing 15.1% in Q4 FY2026 [1]
  2. Infrastructure and data center construction spending — federal infrastructure bills, reshoring initiatives, and hyperscaler data center buildouts create multi-year demand tailwinds for heavy equipment, aerial platforms, power generation, and temporary structures
  3. Record free cash flow of $2.1B (up 23%) enables simultaneous debt reduction, fleet investment, tuck-in acquisitions (Reliant Asset Management), and newly initiated dividends as the independent public company matures its capital return framework [1]
Key Risks
  1. Construction cycle downturn — a slowdown in commercial construction or infrastructure spending would reduce equipment utilization rates and rental pricing power, exposing the operational leverage in a fixed-cost fleet model with 153% D/E [1]
  2. EBITDA margin compression — FY2026 adjusted EBITDA margin of 41.9% already declined 210 basis points YoY from higher costs and growth investments; continued margin erosion would pressure the earnings growth story that supports the current 15.6x forward multiple
  3. Newly independent company risk — Sunbelt's March 2026 NYSE listing means the company lacks a track record as a standalone public entity; investor relations, capital allocation discipline, and governance structures are untested without Ashtead Group oversight [2]
