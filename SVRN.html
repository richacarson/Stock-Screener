Company Profile
Industrials ·Marine Shipping ·NASDAQ ·Greece
SVRN, Inc. (formerly OceanPal Inc., legal name change effective August 27, 2026) operates a dual business model: a legacy dry bulk shipping fleet and SovereignAI Services LLC, a digital asset treasury subsidiary holding approximately 55.5 million NEAR Protocol tokens. The company raised $120M through a PIPE to launch SovereignAI, which aims to build confidential AI cloud infrastructure. Changed ticker from OP to SVRN in November 2025. Executed a 1-for-20 reverse stock split in March 2026. Marshall Islands incorporated, Greek operated. Appointed G-20 Group to manage NEAR treasury yield strategy in 2026.

Investment Thesis
The bull case is a deep discount to stated book: SVRN's ~$58M market cap compares with $156.6M of year-end 2025 stockholders' equity, the company carries no debt, its three ships have no drydockings scheduled in 2026, and SovereignAI earns NEAR staking yield ($0.7M in its first two months) [1][5]. A sharp NEAR rally or progress on the confidential AI-cloud plan could re-rate the shares quickly.
The bear case dominates: FY2025 revenue fell 45% to $14.1M with a $65.2M net loss, value hinges on a single volatile token, and short seller Fugazi calls the stock 'uninvestable,' alleging the $120M PIPE included only ~$13.7M of cash with the NEAR Foundation controlling ~71.6% on a fully exercised basis [1][4]. Serial reverse splits, Nasdaq delisting notices, auditor turnover and alleged related-party vessel sales signal severe governance risk [3][4].
Key Catalysts
  1. Sustained NEAR Protocol price appreciation lifting treasury NAV well above the market cap [2]
  2. Delivery of a concrete product or customer for SovereignAI's confidential AI cloud
  3. Resolution of Nasdaq listing compliance following the March 30, 2026 1-for-20 reverse split [3]
Key Risks
  1. Concentration in a single crypto token: NEAR price declines translate directly into mark-to-market losses (FY2025 net loss $65.2M) [1]
  2. Further dilution from warrants and offerings — the company has executed four reverse splits since 2022 and saw share count jump ~9x in three weeks in 2025 [4]
  3. Governance and control risk: alleged related-party vessel sales, auditor resignation, and potential NEAR Foundation control of ~71.6% fully exercised [4]
