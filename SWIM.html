Company Profile
Consumer Cyclical ·Leisure ·NASDAQ ·United States
Latham Group, Inc. is the largest designer, manufacturer, and marketer of in-ground residential swimming pools in North America, Australia, and New Zealand. The company operates through fiberglass pools (growing to ~80% of in-ground sales), vinyl liner pools, and pool covers segments. Headquartered in Latham, New York, IPO'd on NASDAQ in April 2021.

Investment Thesis
Latham Group is executing a compelling turnaround as the dominant North American pool manufacturer, with Q2 2026 revenue growing 14.4% to $197.5M and fiberglass pools reaching ~80% of in-ground sales [1][2]. Consumer leads surging 60% YoY and website traffic up 30% signal pent-up demand recovery from the post-COVID pool normalization trough. Full-year guidance of $600-620M represents steady top-line acceleration, and the Freedom Pools acquisition expands manufacturing capacity for the fiberglass transition. At $7.48 with a PEG of 1.25, the stock offers reasonable growth valuation if earnings normalize — analyst consensus forecasts 58% earnings growth and 6.7% revenue growth annually. Insider buying (Director James Cline purchasing 50K shares at $4.84 in May) signals board-level confidence in the recovery [4].
The primary concern is profitability — annual net margin of 0.9% and ROE of 2.13% indicate Latham has not yet demonstrated the ability to convert revenue growth into meaningful earnings power [5]. Q2 gross margins contracted 160bps to 35.5% due to transitory production costs, and GAAP EPS of $0.11 missed consensus by $0.05, raising questions about cost management during expansion [2]. The trailing P/E of 149.6x reflects how little GAAP earnings the company generates relative to its $880M market cap. Pool demand is inherently cyclical and tied to housing starts, consumer confidence, and discretionary spending — a recession would severely impact new pool installations. Additionally, the company has no meaningful e-commerce channel or digital services layer, and the CEO transition to Sean Gadd in January 2026 introduces execution uncertainty during a critical recovery phase.
Key Catalysts
  1. Fiberglass pool conversion reaching 80%+ of in-ground sales, with higher margins and faster installation driving structural mix improvement and dealer network expansion
  2. Post-COVID pool demand normalization ending as consumer leads surge 60% YoY and sustained double-digit growth in Florida signals durable recovery in the largest pool market
  3. Full-year guidance of $600-620M with management expecting margin pressure to ease in H2, creating potential for earnings inflection as production costs normalize
Key Risks
  1. Annual profit margin of 0.9% and ROE of 2.13% indicate the company has not proven it can convert revenue growth into meaningful shareholder returns, with Q2 EPS missing consensus by $0.05
  2. Pool demand is highly cyclical and discretionary — a consumer spending slowdown or housing market contraction would directly impact new pool installations, which drive 70%+ of revenue
  3. CEO transition to Sean Gadd in January 2026 introduces leadership execution risk during a critical recovery phase, with the previous CEO's strategy still being validated
