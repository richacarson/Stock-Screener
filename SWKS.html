Company Profile
Technology ·Semiconductors ·NASDAQ ·United States
Skyworks Solutions designs, manufactures, and markets analog and mixed-signal semiconductor products for mobile, automotive, broadband, IoT, and industrial applications. The company's front-end modules, amplifiers, filters, and switches are critical components in 5G smartphones, Wi-Fi 7 routers, automotive connectivity, and data center infrastructure. Founded in 1962 and headquartered in Irvine, California, with approximately $9.4B market cap.

Investment Thesis
Skyworks offers deep value in semiconductors with a forward P/E of just 12.89x, a 4.54% dividend yield backed by 12 consecutive years of growth, and a fortress balance sheet with $1.44B cash against $997M debt (D/E of 17.3%) [1][4]. Q2 FY2026 revenue of $943.7M included a landmark multi-generational Android OEM design win expected to generate over $1 billion through 2030, providing long-term revenue visibility beyond the Apple relationship [1]. Broad Markets delivered its 9th consecutive quarter of double-digit YoY growth driven by Wi-Fi 7, data center, and automotive, now approaching 43% of total sales [1]. Q3 FY2026 guidance of $900-950M with Broad Markets at approximately 43% suggests the diversification strategy is accelerating. The non-GAAP operating margin of 20% demonstrates underlying profitability that the GAAP figures (burdened by amortization) obscure [1].
The bear case centers on structural mobile revenue decline and earnings quality concerns. Q2 FY2026 revenue declined 1% YoY and GAAP net income fell 48.2% to $35.6M, with GAAP operating margin at just 4.5% versus 20% non-GAAP — a $147M gap driven by amortization, restructuring, and stock-based compensation [1]. Free cash flow was negative $32M in Q2 due to elevated capex of $82.3M, and operating cash flow plunged 87.7% YoY from $409.5M to $50.3M [1]. The GAAP payout ratio of 117% is unsustainable — the $106.8M quarterly dividend exceeds GAAP earnings, and a dividend cut would remove the yield support thesis and trigger institutional selling [4]. Apple modem internalization remains the paramount risk: Apple designing its own cellular modems could progressively reduce Skyworks' RF content per iPhone, threatening the company's largest customer relationship. The 5yr revenue CAGR near 0% from FY2021 peak of $5.1B suggests the mobile RF market may be structurally declining for discrete component suppliers [4].
Key Catalysts
  1. Multi-generational Android OEM design win generating $1B+ revenue through 2030 diversifies customer concentration away from Apple and validates Skyworks' competitive position in non-Apple RF markets [1]
  2. Broad Markets approaching 43% of revenue with 9 consecutive quarters of double-digit growth, driven by Wi-Fi 7, data center AI infrastructure, and automotive connectivity — potential to become the majority revenue source within 2 years [1]
  3. Forward P/E of 12.89x with 4.54% dividend yield creates compelling value if earnings stabilize around non-GAAP guidance levels, offering 30-50% upside to historical mid-teens P/E multiples [4]
Key Risks
  1. Apple modem internalization — Apple designing its own cellular modems would progressively reduce Skyworks' RF content per iPhone, threatening the company's largest single-customer revenue stream and potentially accelerating mobile revenue decline [1]
  2. Dividend sustainability — GAAP payout ratio of 117% is unsustainable, and negative free cash flow of -$32M in Q2 FY2026 means the $427M annual dividend is being partially funded from the balance sheet rather than operations [1][4]
  3. GAAP vs non-GAAP divergence — the $147M quarterly gap between GAAP and non-GAAP operating income raises questions about underlying earnings quality, as amortization, restructuring, and SBC represent real economic costs [1]
