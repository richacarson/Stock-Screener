Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Coking Coal ·NYSE ·United States
SunCoke Energy is the largest independent producer of high-quality metallurgical coke in the Americas, with over 45 years of production experience using proprietary heat recovery cokemaking technology. The company operates through two segments: Domestic Coke (serving blast furnace steelmakers) and Industrial Services (material handling, scrap processing, and logistics for steel and industrial customers). SunCoke acquired Phoenix Global in 2025 to expand industrial services.

Investment Thesis
SunCoke Energy offers a 5.7% dividend yield at $8.42 (up 28% from April) supported by the largest independent metallurgical coke operation in the Americas and a diversifying Industrial Services segment bolstered by the Phoenix Global acquisition [1][5]. Management guides $18-36M net income and $230-250M adjusted EBITDA for 2026, with Domestic Coke contributing $162-168M and Industrial Services (including a full year of Phoenix) contributing $90-100M EBITDA [1]. The company maintained full-year guidance despite a weak Q1 2026 ($3.4M net loss from weather disruptions), signaling confidence in second-half recovery [4]. The dividend has grown at a 14.9% 5-year CAGR ($0.24 to $0.48 annually), and the stock trades at a forward P/E of 15.83 [5].
The bear case centers on cyclicality, secular decline, and payout sustainability. SunCoke's core coke business is structurally tied to blast furnace steelmaking, which faces long-term decline as EAF now represents over 70% of U.S. steel production [1]. FY2025 revenue declined 5.1% to $1.8B, and Q1 2026 showed a $3.4M net loss versus $19.4M net income a year earlier [4]. The payout ratio of ~90% on forward earnings (estimated from forward P/E of 15.83) leaves minimal margin of safety for the $0.48 annual dividend, and at the midpoint of $18-36M net income guidance, EPS would only be ~$0.32, making the payout ratio exceed 100% [1][5]. Profit margin of 1.31% provides almost no cushion for operational disruptions, and the company remains exposed to steel industry cyclicality, raw material costs, and aging facility risks like the Middletown turbine failure.
Key Catalysts
  1. Phoenix Global integration delivering full-year Industrial Services EBITDA of $90-100M and synergies through 2026, diversifying the revenue base beyond core coke [1]
  2. Second-half 2026 recovery restoring profitability after weather-disrupted Q1, with management maintaining $18-36M net income guidance [4]
  3. Continued dividend growth from 14.9% 5-year CAGR — the 5.7% yield at $8.42 remains attractive if the company achieves the upper end of guidance [5]
Key Risks
  1. Secular decline in blast furnace steelmaking as EAF adoption accelerates past 70% of U.S. production — SunCoke's core coke demand is structurally at risk from this industry transition [1]
  2. Dividend sustainability at risk — payout ratio of ~90% on forward earnings and potentially 150% on midpoint guidance leaves no margin of safety, requiring top-end earnings to cover the $0.48 annual dividend [1][5]
  3. Thin profit margin of 1.31% provides virtually no buffer against operational disruptions, raw material cost spikes, or further weather-related production shortfalls like the Q1 2026 loss [4]
