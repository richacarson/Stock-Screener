Investment Thesis
No longer investable. UHS completed its $5.25/share cash acquisition (~$835M) on August 17, 2026, and TALK was delisted [4][5]. Before the sale, the bull case rested on Payor-driven growth: 2025 revenue rose 22% to $228.9M, 2026 guidance was $275-290M, and the debt-free balance sheet held ~$90M of cash [1][2].
The bear case that produced the sale was thin profitability (TTM net margin under 0.1%), decelerating growth (Q1 2026 +18% vs consensus miss), shrinking R&D, and rising AI-chatbot competition in low-acuity therapy [1][2]. Former holders were cashed out at $5.25. Exposure to this business now exists only through Universal Health Services (UHS).
