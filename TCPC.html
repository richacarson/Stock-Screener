Company Profile
Financial Services ·Asset Management ·NASDAQ ·United States
BlackRock TCP Capital Corp. is an externally managed business development company (BDC) specializing in direct equity and debt investments in middle-market companies, primarily through senior secured loans, mezzanine debt, and junior capital across technology, healthcare, and business services sectors. Managed by BlackRock as part of its US Private Capital platform. CEO Jason Mehring was appointed in November 2024 following the departure of Rajneesh Vig; the company faces a class action lawsuit filed February 2026 alleging materially false statements. Q1 and Q2 2026 NII of $0.22/share each covered the reduced $0.17/quarter dividend. A $523M portfolio sale transaction was announced in Q2 2026 to reshape the investment portfolio. Market cap is approximately $339M.

Investment Thesis

Q1 and Q2 2026 each showed NII of $0.22/share covering the $0.17 quarterly dividend at 129% NII coverage, suggesting the reduced distribution rate may be sustainable [1]. The $523M portfolio sale announced in Q2 2026 should accelerate the portfolio quality improvement and reduce exposure to stressed credits [1].
