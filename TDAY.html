Company Profile
Communication Services ·Publishing ·NYSE ·United States
USA TODAY Co., Inc. (formerly Gannett Co., Inc., renamed October 2025) operates as a media and digital marketing solutions company through three segments: Domestic Gannett Media, Newsquest (UK), and Digital Marketing Solutions. It publishes USA Today and over 200 local daily newspapers, operates digital media brands, and provides digital marketing solutions under the LocaliQ brand. The company is transitioning from legacy print toward digital revenue, which is projected to exceed 50% of total revenue in 2026. Signed a strategic AI content licensing agreement with Perplexity in July 2025.

Finite vs Infinite Game — Sinek (25%)
Mindset: FINITE Overall: 3 /10
USA TODAY Co. exhibits predominantly finite-game thinking — a company fighting for survival through serial cost cuts, layoffs, and financial engineering rather than building capabilities for endurance. The workforce has been halved, newsrooms gutted, and the company's primary growth strategy is reaching revenue 'flatness' — an explicitly finite goal. The Perplexity AI partnership and digital transformation show some adaptive instincts, but execution has been incremental. Sinek would observe that a company cutting its core capability (journalism) to fund quarterly EBITDA targets is playing to survive this quarter, not to keep playing.
Just Cause
4
Just Cause (1/3): Local journalism has an inherent public purpose — informing communities, holding local government accountable, and enabling civic participation. This is a cause worth advancing [2]. However, years of newsroom cuts, centralized content production, and cost-reduction programs have systematically eroded the connection between this purpose and practice. The company's stated 2026 goal of 'digital revenue exceeding 50% of total' is a financial target, not a cause. Leadership has not articulated a viable long-term vision for sustaining local journalism beyond managing its decline [1].

Investment Thesis
USA TODAY Co. trades at $1.22B market cap for a company generating $2.28B in annual revenue, with the stock up 119.8% YoY as the market prices in digital transformation progress [5]. Digital revenue is approaching 50% of total revenue with record Q4 digital subscription ARPU of $9.81, and the company expects adjusted EBITDA growth in H2 2026 and for the full year [1]. The Perplexity AI partnership creates a new revenue-sharing model for AI-era content monetization [2]. At 16.8x forward earnings (using the more optimistic analyst estimates), the stock offers leverage to any stabilization in the revenue trajectory. The $100M cost reduction program is driving margin improvement even as topline contracts.
The bear case is severe for a long-term holder. Revenue has declined every year from $3.21B (2021) to $2.28B TTM — a cumulative 29% contraction with 5yr CAGR of -8.0%, and Q1 2026 revenue of $548.5M continued the decline at -4% YoY [1]. D/E of 733.5% with $1.13B in debt against minimal equity creates existential refinancing risk in 2026-2027 [4][5]. Profit margin of 1.3% and forward P/E of 84.08x leave zero margin of safety — at these earnings levels, the stock is priced for a dramatic earnings inflection that the fundamentals do not support. AI disruption is an existential threat: ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews can deliver news summaries and local information without driving traffic to publisher websites, directly undermining the advertising and subscription model. The workforce has been halved through serial layoffs that have gutted local journalism capacity [3]. With trailing EPS of $0.20 declining -44% YoY, the 119% stock price increase looks disconnected from fundamental reality.

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience HIGH RISK
4
AI Risk Protection (3/9): EXPOSED — Media and publishing is among the most AI-disrupted sectors. AI can generate news summaries, sports scores, weather updates, and commodity content that directly substitutes for much of USA TODAY's output. National news aggregation faces severe AI substitution risk from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Meta AI, which deliver information without requiring users to visit publisher websites [2]. The digital marketing business (LocaliQ) faces competition from AI-powered ad platforms that serve SMBs more efficiently. Print advertising, still a significant revenue stream, is in terminal decline accelerated by AI ad targeting that diverts budgets to digital platforms. Local journalism has moderate structural protection — covering city councils, local courts, and community events requires human presence — but this segment alone cannot sustain the enterprise [1]. AI Opportunity (1/1): The Perplexity AI partnership generates advertising revenue sharing from content licensing, representing a pragmatic monetization of content assets in the AI era [2]. CEO Reed frames AI as an opportunity to 'accelerate' and 'deliver shareholder value,' though specific revenue figures from AI are not disclosed.
