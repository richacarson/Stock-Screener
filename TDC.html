Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Infrastructure ·NYSE ·United States
Teradata Corporation provides cloud analytics and data platform solutions to enterprise customers worldwide. The company's Teradata VantageCloud platform enables organizations to connect and manage data across hybrid and multi-cloud environments, build AI and machine learning models, and derive actionable business insights at scale. Teradata serves large enterprises in financial services, healthcare, manufacturing, telecommunications, and government. Named Ethisphere's World's Most Ethical Company for 16 consecutive years. Founded in 1979, headquartered in San Diego, California.

Teradata exhibits predominantly finite game characteristics. Investment Thesis
Teradata at 10.1x forward earnings offers deep value in enterprise analytics software — Q1 2026 revenue of $444M (+6% YoY) showed acceleration with recurring revenue jumping 12%, and public cloud ARR of $686M growing 13% demonstrates the cloud migration is gaining traction [1][2]. The Q2 2026 raised GAAP EPS guidance to $4.43-$4.51 and adjusted free cash flow of $330-$350M (including $315M SAP settlement benefit) provides significant financial flexibility. The company's 150+ enterprise AI engagements position it as a credible AI-enabled analytics platform for regulated industries where data governance and performance at scale remain critical. At $2.7B market cap with $1.5B in ARR and deep Fortune 500 relationships, Teradata is a potential strategic acquisition target for a larger cloud or data platform company looking for enterprise customer access [1][5].
The fundamental challenge is that Teradata is a legacy data analytics company in secular revenue decline — total revenue has fallen from $1.83B in 2023 to ~$1.69B TTM, a ~-1.5% 5-year CAGR, and FY2026 guidance calls for -2% to flat total revenue [1][2]. Cloud-native competitors Snowflake and Databricks are building AI-native platforms that increasingly serve the same large enterprise customers, and public cloud providers embed analytics directly into their infrastructure. The PEG ratio of 6.0 reflects the market's skepticism about growth — a low P/E alone does not create value without a revenue growth engine. The 9-10% workforce reduction and repeated restructuring cycles create organizational instability that undermines innovation capacity [3]. TTM earnings are inflated by the one-time $315M SAP settlement; normalized non-GAAP EPS guidance of $2.65-$2.73 implies a more modest 11x forward earnings.   2. Snowflake, Databricks, and cloud-native provider offerings building AI-integrated analytics platforms that could accelerate customer migration away from Teradata's hybrid approach, especially as AI capabilities become table stakes [4]
  3. Repeated workforce reductions (9-10% in 2025-2026) and Glassdoor leadership criticism create organizational instability and brain drain, potentially undermining the company's ability to execute its AI and cloud innovation strategy [3]
