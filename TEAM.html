Company Profile
Technology ·Software—Application ·NASDAQ ·United States
Atlassian provides enterprise collaboration and developer tools including Jira (project tracking), Confluence (wiki/documentation), Trello (task management), Bitbucket (code repository), and Loom (video messaging). The company completed a multi-year cloud migration and is embedding AI across its platform through Atlassian Intelligence and the Rovo AI agent. Co-founded in 2002 by Mike Cannon-Brookes and Scott Farquhar, Atlassian reported FY2026 revenue of $6.57B (+26% YoY) with cloud revenue growing 31% in Q4 to $1.21B. The company achieved GAAP profitability in Q4 FY2026 (12% operating margin) but reported a full-year GAAP net loss of $54M. Subscription ARR reached $6.6B and remaining performance obligations grew 44% to $4.82B. Market cap is approximately $42.7B.

Finite vs Infinite Game — Sinek (25%)
Mindset: FINITE Overall: 4 /10
Atlassian shows finite-game characteristics in a moment of competitive crisis. The cloud migration was a genuinely courageous long-term bet, and the co-founders' 20+ year commitment is notable. However, the company's response to AI disruption — layoffs justified by AI efficiency, cultural deterioration reflected in employee sentiment, and a stock decline that has eroded organizational confidence — suggests leadership is prioritizing quarterly survival over cause-driven strategy. The absence of a deeply tested Just Cause and the breakdown in organizational trust place Atlassian in finite territory despite its historically strong culture.

Investment Thesis
Atlassian delivered $6.57B in FY2026 revenue (+26% YoY) with cloud growth accelerating to 31% and subscription ARR reaching $6.6B (+23%), demonstrating the cloud migration is maturing into a durable growth platform [1]. Q4 marked the first GAAP-profitable quarter (12% operating margin), while full-year free cash flow reached $1.32B (20% margin). Remaining performance obligations of $4.82B (+44%) provide strong revenue visibility. The 85.64% gross margin, 37.98% co-founder insider ownership, and dominant position in developer project management (Jira) create structural advantages [4]. Atlassian Intelligence and Rovo AI position the company to defend its franchise in the AI-augmented workflow era, and the PEG ratio of 2.11 suggests reasonable growth-adjusted valuation relative to enterprise SaaS peers.
FY2027 guidance of ~13% total revenue growth represents a sharp deceleration from 26%, suggesting the cloud migration tailwind is fading [1]. The company remains GAAP-unprofitable on a full-year basis (net loss of $54M) despite $6.57B in revenue, with stock-based compensation consuming the gap between GAAP and non-GAAP profitability — ROE of -4.48% and profit margin of -0.82% reflect this disconnect [4]. Generative AI platforms from GitHub Copilot, Notion AI, Linear, and standalone LLMs directly threaten Atlassian's documentation and project management categories, and the stock's 65%+ decline from 2021 highs and 6.23% short interest reflect investor skepticism [2][4]. Glassdoor reviews describing 'values eroded' and 'no job security' suggest cultural deterioration that could impair talent retention during a critical AI transition period. The Inspire Impact Score of -49 (MISALIGNED) limits allocation for faith-based investors [3].
