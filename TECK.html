Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Copper ·NYSE ·Canada
Teck Resources Limited is a Canadian mining company focused on copper and zinc after selling its steelmaking coal business (Elk Valley Resources) to Glencore in 2024. Core assets include the Quebrada Blanca (QB2) copper mine in Chile, Highland Valley Copper in British Columbia, the Antamina joint venture in Peru, the Red Dog zinc mine in Alaska, and the Trail metallurgical operations. Teck and Anglo American agreed in September 2025 to a merger of equals that would form Anglo Teck; shareholders approved the deal in December 2025 and closing awaits remaining regulatory approvals, including China and South Korea.

Investment Thesis
Teck is now a copper-and-zinc business with real operating leverage to copper. Q2 2026 revenue rose to C$3.6B from C$2.0B, adjusted EBITDA rose 204% to C$2.2B, and adjusted EPS reached $1.93 versus $0.38 a year earlier [1][4]. The balance sheet is conservative, with debt-to-equity of 32% and about C$6.7B of cash [13]. The trailing P/E of 11.4 understates earnings power if copper stays tight. The Anglo Teck merger targets about US$800M of annual pre-tax synergies and would combine QB2 with Anglo's Collahuasi next door in Chile [1].
The bear case starts with the commodity cycle. Consensus forward EPS of about $4.01 is roughly 28% below trailing EPS of $5.58, which gives a forward PEG of 2.85 [13]. Teck shareholders are also tied to a merger awaiting Chinese and South Korean approval, which leaves deal-timing and integration risk and could dilute Teck's culture inside a larger, more bureaucratic Anglo Teck [1]. Operational execution at QB2 has needed a formal operations review [6], and R&D has fallen to about 0.3% of revenue.
Key Catalysts
  1. Closing of the Anglo Teck merger after Chinese and South Korean approval, unlocking about US$800M of annual pre-tax synergies including QB2-Collahuasi integration [1]
  2. Continued QB2 throughput and recovery improvements lifting copper volumes toward the top of guidance [1]
  3. Structural copper deficit from AI data centers, grid upgrades and EVs supporting high copper prices and by-product credits [3]
Key Risks
  1. Copper price normalization: consensus already expects forward EPS about 28% below trailing levels [13]
  2. Regulatory delay or added conditions on the Anglo Teck merger from China's SAMR, and integration risk after closing [1]
  3. Operating and environmental incidents at the Chilean and Peruvian assets, such as the reported Andacollo environmental fine, plus tailings and water-permitting exposure [12]
