Company Profile
Technology ·Electronic Components ·NYSE ·Ireland
TE Connectivity plc is the world's largest connector and sensor company. It designs and makes connectivity and sensor solutions for harsh environments in two segments: Transportation Solutions (automotive connectors, electrification components, industrial and commercial transportation, sensors) and Industrial Solutions (digital data networks for AI data centers, aerospace, defense, medical, automation, and energy/grid). Fiscal 2026 sales were about $19.9B, up about 15%, and orders hit a record $22.2B.

Investment Thesis
TE closed fiscal 2026 with record Q4 sales of $5.3B (up 12%), more than 20% adjusted EPS growth, and record full-year orders of $22.2B (up 27%), giving it momentum into a guided 15% sales increase in Q1 FY2027 [1][2]. As the largest connector maker, it sells into AI data centers, grid modernization and vehicle electrification. The balance sheet is strong (41% D/E, 24.6% ROE), and the stock trades at only 14.7x forward earnings with a PEG of 0.95 [12]. The dividend has compounded at about 9% a year with a sub-30% payout ratio.
The main risk is cyclical. Transportation Solutions is still TE's largest segment and depends on global auto production, which faces EV-adoption uncertainty and tariff disruption, including an $87M IEEPA tariff refund obligation in Q4 [3]. TTM book-to-bill of about 1.12 is healthy but not exceptional, and AI data-center orders could prove lumpy if hyperscaler capex pauses. The 16.4% profit margin trails top-tier peers, and the -49 Inspire score is a values flag for faith-based investors.
Key Catalysts
  1. Conversion of record $22.2B FY2026 orders into the guided 15% Q1 FY2027 sales growth and double-digit FY2027 growth [1]
  2. Further AI data-center interconnect orders (Digital Data Networks) and grid/energy connectivity demand [1][5]
  3. Margin expansion and buybacks supporting continued 20%+ adjusted EPS growth at a 14.7x forward P/E [2][12]
Key Risks
  1. Global light-vehicle production slowdown or EV program delays hitting Transportation Solutions, still TE's largest segment
  2. Hyperscaler capex digestion causing AI connectivity orders to decelerate from 2026's elevated levels
  3. Tariff and trade-policy volatility, including IEEPA refund pass-throughs that reduced reported Q4 growth [3]
