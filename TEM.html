Company Profile
Healthcare ·Diagnostics & Research ·NASDAQ ·United States
Tempus AI is a healthcare technology company that has built one of the world's largest libraries of clinical and molecular data, using artificial intelligence to deliver precision medicine at scale. The company operates across two segments: Diagnostics (genomic profiling, molecular diagnostics, MRD testing) generating $261M in Q1 2026, and Data & Applications (AI platform licensing to biopharma for drug discovery) generating $87M in Q1 2026. Founded in 2015 by Eric Lefkofsky after a personal experience with cancer, Tempus IPO'd on NASDAQ in June 2024. Q1 2026 revenue grew 36% to $348.1M, Q2 reached $382.5M (+22%), and the company achieved its first GAAP quarterly profit of $5.6M in Q2 2026. Full-year 2026 guidance is $1.595-$1.605B with ~$65M adjusted EBITDA. Market cap is approximately $12.4B.

Investment Thesis
Tempus AI is the leading AI-native precision medicine platform, with Q1 2026 revenue of $348.1M (+36% YoY) and Q2 of $382.5M (+22%), powered by one of the world's largest clinical-molecular data libraries [1]. The company achieved its first GAAP quarterly profit of $5.6M in Q2 2026, signaling an inflection toward sustainable profitability, with full-year guidance of $1.595-$1.605B revenue and ~$65M adjusted EBITDA [1]. MRD testing volume grew 500% YoY, representing a transformative new capability in cancer monitoring. Collaboration with 19 of the top 20 pharma companies validates the Data & Applications platform at the highest tier of biopharma, with 63.98% gross margins providing the foundation for operating leverage as scale increases [4]. The Inspire Impact Score of 51 (ALIGNED) makes Tempus one of the few high-growth AI companies available to faith-based investors. AI Resilience of 8 reflects that Tempus IS the AI disruption in healthcare — protected by regulatory barriers, proprietary data, and deep domain expertise.
The bear case centers on governance risk and extreme valuation. Spruce Point Capital's short report raised concerns about 'aggressive and suspicious accounting,' CEO cash-out patterns, and circular cash flows between related parties, leading to a securities fraud investigation following a 19% stock decline in May 2025 [3]. At $12.4B market cap against trailing losses of -$254M, with D/E of 270% and ROE of -67.44%, the stock is priced for flawless execution of an unproven profitability trajectory [4]. Revenue growth decelerated sharply from 36% in Q1 to 22% in Q2 2026, raising questions about growth sustainability [1]. The 18.42% short interest — one of the highest among healthcare stocks — reflects deep skepticism. Competition from well-capitalized rivals (Foundation Medicine/Roche, Guardant Health) could pressure margins as genomic testing commoditizes. The average analyst price target of $63.36 sits below the current stock price, suggesting limited upside in consensus estimates [4].
