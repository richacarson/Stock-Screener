Company Profile
Healthcare ·Health Information Services ·NMS ·United States
Tempus AI is a healthcare technology company applying artificial intelligence to precision medicine, operating two primary segments: Diagnostics ($289.3M Q2 2026 revenue, +20% YoY) and Data & Applications ($93.2M Q2 2026 revenue, +28% YoY). Founded in 2015 by Eric Lefkofsky after his wife's breast cancer diagnosis, the company provides NGS diagnostics, AI-powered clinical decision support, de-identified data licensing to biopharma, and clinical trial matching services. TTM revenue is approximately $1.43B with 21.6% YoY growth, and Q2 2026 marked Tempus's first quarter of net profitability ($5.6M net income) [1][2]. The company announced a $1.5-1.7B acquisition of Personalis expected to close in late 2026 or early 2027 [2].

Investment Thesis
Tempus AI at $66.75 with a $12.0B market cap is the leading AI-powered precision medicine platform, achieving a critical inflection point with its first quarter of net profitability ($5.6M) in Q2 2026 on $382.5M revenue (+22% YoY) [1][2]. The Diagnostics segment delivered 31% oncology volume growth while Data & Applications grew 28%, and adjusted EBITDA of $8.0M (up 244% YoY) validates the path to sustained profitability with full-year guidance of ~$65M EBITDA on $1.595-1.605B revenue [2]. The company sits at the intersection of two powerful secular trends — AI adoption and personalized healthcare — with a proprietary moat in the world's largest clinical-molecular data library that deepens with every test processed. The $1.5-1.7B Personalis acquisition expands into MRD testing and whole genome sequencing, and the $820.7M cash position provides ample runway [2].
The bear case centers on valuation risk and execution challenges. At $12B market cap on $1.43B TTM revenue with only $5.6M in quarterly net income, Tempus trades at approximately 8.4x revenue — a premium that requires sustained 20%+ growth and meaningful margin expansion to justify [1]. D/E of 324.6% reflects $1.17B in convertible notes that could dilute equity holders upon conversion, and the Personalis acquisition at $1.5-1.7B adds integration risk while the company is just reaching profitability [2][5]. ROE of -67.4% and cumulative losses exceeding $1.4B mean the balance sheet remains fragile despite cash reserves. CEO Lefkofsky's insider sales, while routine under 10b5-1 plans, are directionally negative at a critical valuation juncture [3]. The precision medicine market faces reimbursement uncertainty as payers scrutinize the cost-effectiveness of comprehensive genomic profiling, and competitor Guardant Health and established lab companies (Quest, LabCorp) are investing heavily in AI-powered diagnostics. Tempus must execute flawlessly on the Personalis integration while simultaneously scaling toward cash flow positivity — a challenging dual mandate [2][5].
