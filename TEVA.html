Company Profile
Healthcare ·Drug Manufacturers - Specialty & Generic ·NYSE ·Israel
Teva Pharmaceutical Industries is the world's largest generic drug manufacturer and a growing innovative pharmaceutical company executing a 'Pivot to Growth' strategy under CEO Richard Francis. The innovative portfolio includes AUSTEDO (tardive dyskinesia/Huntington's, guided $2.45-2.6B for FY2026), AJOVY (migraine prevention, $850-870M), and UZEDY (schizophrenia, $270-290M), which collectively grew 43% YoY in Q2 2026. FY2025 revenue was $17.3B, marking the third consecutive year of growth, though FY2026 guidance of $16.4-16.8B projects a modest decline. Headquartered in Tel Aviv with operations in ~60 countries and approximately 31,000 employees.

Investment Thesis
Teva at 15.42x forward earnings offers reasonable value for a pharmaceutical company executing a genuine business model transformation. The innovative portfolio — AUSTEDO (guided $2.45-2.6B FY2026), AJOVY ($850-870M), and UZEDY ($270-290M) — grew 43% YoY in Q2 2026, demonstrating the Pivot to Growth strategy is delivering [1][2]. ROE of 21.60% exceeds the target, gross margins expanded 80bps to 55.4% as revenue mix shifts toward higher-margin innovative products, and the company has steadily deleveraged from its peak debt levels. The duvakitug partnership with Sanofi (anti-TL1A for IBD) represents a potential blockbuster pipeline opportunity in a large unmet-need category. As the world's largest generic drug manufacturer dispensing ~58B doses annually, Teva has irreplaceable scale in an essential healthcare function.
The bear case centers on legacy burdens, growth sustainability, and valuation deterioration. FY2026 revenue guidance of $16.4-16.8B represents a decline from FY2025's $17.3B as Copaxone generic erosion outpaces innovative product growth [1]. D/E of 206% remains elevated, constraining financial flexibility and preventing dividend reinstatement. The PEG ratio of 3.78 (up from 0.9 in June) signals the market has priced in much of the growth story at $36.77, creating multiple compression risk if the innovative portfolio growth rate decelerates. Profit margin of 9.02% is well below pharma peers, compressed by settlement payments and competitive generics pricing. Sustainalytics assigns a severe controversy rating of 5.0 with ESG scores dramatically lagging peers (Social: 32 vs. 76 average), reflecting opioid settlement legacy and governance concerns [4]. The Inspire Impact Score of -75 (MISALIGNED) limits the investor universe for faith-aligned portfolios. The 5-year revenue CAGR of just 1.28% underscores the prolonged stagnation that the current growth surge has not yet corrected on a trailing basis.

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience MODERATE
7
AI Risk Protection (7/9): Teva's core business — manufacturing and distributing generic and branded pharmaceuticals — is structurally protected from AI disruption. Physical drug manufacturing requires FDA/EMA-regulated facilities, complex chemical/biological processes, global supply chains, and cGMP compliance that AI cannot replace [1]. The generics business is particularly insulated: it competes on manufacturing efficiency, regulatory expertise (ANDA filings), and distribution scale rather than intellectual property that AI could erode. AUSTEDO, AJOVY, and UZEDY are protected by patents and clinical data. However, AI-accelerated drug discovery at competitors could compress Teva's innovative pipeline development timelines, and AI-driven formulation optimization could lower barriers to generic entry over time. Analysts have not flagged AI as a material risk to Teva's business model. AI Opportunity (0/1): Teva operates the Teva Rise innovation platform and experiments with Gen AI in business functions, but generates no quantifiable revenue from AI-powered products or services [3]. No specific AI-driven cost savings have been disclosed.
