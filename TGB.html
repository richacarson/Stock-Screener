Company Profile
Materials ·Copper ·NYSEAMERICAN ·Canada
Taseko Mines Limited (rebranding as Trekor Metals Limited) is a Canadian copper mining company operating the Gibraltar Mine in British Columbia, one of the largest open-pit copper-molybdenum mines in North America, and the newly commissioned Florence Copper in-situ recovery project in Arizona, which delivered its first full quarter of commercial production in Q2 2026. The company also holds development-stage interests in the Yellowhead copper and Aley niobium projects. Founded in 1966, headquartered in Vancouver, Canada.

Investment Thesis
Taseko offers leveraged exposure to the copper cycle at an attractive forward valuation of 13.65x earnings, with a genuine production inflection underway. Q2 2026 delivered record quarterly revenue of CAD 331M, CAD 183M operating cash flow, and CAD 125M adjusted EBITDA, as Florence Copper posted its first full quarter of commercial production (5M+ lbs cathode) alongside steady Gibraltar output (30M lbs in Q1 at $2.63/lb C1 cost) [1][2]. TTM revenue growth of 70.3% and a 5-year revenue CAGR near 23% reflect the Florence ramp layering onto an already-producing Gibraltar asset, with copper prices above CAD 6/lb providing a favorable pricing backdrop linked partly to AI/data-center-driven electrification demand.
The bear case centers on balance-sheet leverage and execution risk. D/E of 89% and thin current profitability (TTM profit margin of 1.59%, ROE of 2.21%) reflect a company still absorbing Florence's construction and ramp costs, leaving it exposed to a sustained copper price pullback. Florence Copper's ISCR technology must continue proving out at full commercial scale — wellfield performance shortfalls or environmental incidents at the Arizona site could delay the ramp. Taseko remains a single-commodity producer with historically high volatility (beta near 2.0), and its trailing P/E of 318.69x underscores how much of the investment case depends on a forward earnings trajectory not yet fully realized. The unresolved history with the Tsilhqot'in Nation over Gibraltar's Fraser River discharge permit also represents an ongoing regulatory and reputational overhang.
Key Catalysts
  1. Florence Copper sustaining its ramp toward 2026 guidance of 30-35M lbs following its first full commercial-production quarter in Q2 2026, validating ISCR technology at scale
  2. Copper prices above CAD 6/lb supported by AI data-center and electrification-driven demand, directly flowing through to record quarterly revenue and EBITDA
  3. Continued deleveraging and margin expansion as Florence reaches steady-state production, following the $500M bond refinancing and Mitsui strategic partnership
Key Risks
  1. D/E of 89% combined with thin current profitability (1.59% margin, 2.21% ROE) leaves the balance sheet vulnerable to a sustained copper price decline
  2. Florence Copper's in-situ recovery technology remains early in commercial-scale operation; wellfield performance issues or environmental incidents could delay the production ramp
  3. Unresolved tension with the Tsilhqot'in Nation over the Gibraltar tailings discharge permit into the Fraser River represents an ongoing regulatory and reputational risk
