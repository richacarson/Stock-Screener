Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Copper ·ASE ·Canada
Trekor Metals Limited (formerly Taseko Mines) is a Canadian mid-tier copper producer operating the Gibraltar open-pit copper-molybdenum mine in south-central British Columbia and the Florence Copper in-situ recovery (ISCR) project in Arizona. Rebranded from Taseko Mines in June 2026. TTM revenue reached approximately $985M driven by record Q2 2026 revenue of $331M as Florence Copper ramped to commercial production alongside Gibraltar's 85,000 tpd throughput, with realized copper prices of $5.74/lb supporting strong operating cash flow of $415M [1][2].

Investment Thesis
Trekor Metals at $7.97 with a $2.9B market cap is a copper growth story entering a transformational earnings inflection. Q2 2026 delivered record revenue of $331M as Florence Copper ramped to commercial production alongside Gibraltar's record throughput, with Q1 2026 generating $237M revenue, $16.8M net income (vs. a loss a year prior), and $93.5M adjusted EBITDA [1][2]. The forward P/E of 10.0x and PEG of 0.33 imply analysts expect earnings to surge from trailing EPS of $0.02 to forward EPS of $0.79 as Florence reaches its 30-35M lb annual target and Gibraltar targets 110-115M lbs. Operating cash flow of $415M TTM provides the financial foundation, and copper's structural demand tailwind from AI data centers, electrification, and energy transition supports pricing above $5.50/lb [3].
The bear case centers on execution risk and commodity cyclicality. Trekor's 1.6% profit margin and 2.2% ROE reflect a company still absorbing Florence Copper's development costs, with -$105M free cash flow indicating the investment-to-harvest transition is incomplete [5]. D/E of 89.4% and $765M total debt against $187M cash create leverage risk if copper prices decline from current elevated levels. Florence Copper's ISCR technology, while promising, remains commercially unproven at sustained full-scale output — any permitting setbacks, aquifer contamination concerns, or production shortfalls would undermine the entire growth thesis. The trailing P/E of 398.5x underscores how dependent the valuation is on forward earnings materializing. Copper prices above $5/lb reflect peak-cycle conditions, and any global economic slowdown could compress margins rapidly for a company with significant fixed obligations and beta of 2.09.
Key Catalysts
  1. Florence Copper ramp-up to 30-35M lbs annual production represents a step-change in earnings power — the project generated $10M EBITDA in its first full production quarter and should approach $150M+ annual EBITDA at full rates with copper above $5/lb [1][2]
  2. Structural copper demand growth from AI data center construction and electrification — S&P Global projects AI alone could boost copper demand 50% by 2040, supporting sustained high prices for Trekor's expanding production base [3]
  3. Forward EPS of $0.79 implies massive earnings growth from the $0.02 trailing figure, with forward P/E of 10x and PEG of 0.33 suggesting significant re-rating upside if the production trajectory materializes through 2027 [5]
Key Risks
  1. Florence Copper ISCR technology remains commercially unproven at sustained full scale — any permitting delays, aquifer issues, or production shortfalls below the 30-35M lb target would remove the primary growth catalyst and strand $400M+ in invested capital [1]
  2. Copper price cyclicality at elevated $5.50+/lb levels — a global recession or China demand slowdown could push copper below $4/lb, compressing margins sharply for a company with only 1.6% profit margin, $765M debt, and beta of 2.09 [5]
  3. Dual-asset management stretch — running Gibraltar at record 85,000 tpd while simultaneously ramping Florence stretches management bandwidth and capital, with negative free cash flow persisting through the transition period [2][5]
