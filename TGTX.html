Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
TG Therapeutics is a fully integrated, commercial-stage biotechnology company focused on the acquisition, development, and commercialization of novel treatments for B-cell diseases. Its lead product, BRIUMVI (ublituximab), is FDA-approved for relapsing forms of multiple sclerosis. Founded in 2011 and led by Chairman, President, and CEO Michael S. Weiss, the company raised FY2026 total revenue guidance to approximately $950M and is targeting a ~$1B annualized U.S. BRIUMVI run rate exiting 2026. Headquartered in New York City.
432 Employees ·www.tgtherapeutics.com

Investment Thesis
TG Therapeutics has accelerated its commercial momentum with Q2 2026 BRIUMVI U.S. net revenue of $227.7M (+70% YoY), driving management to raise full-year 2026 total revenue guidance to approximately $950M and target a ~$1B annualized BRIUMVI run rate exiting 2026 [1][2]. H1 2026 revenue of $445.3M already exceeds FY2024's full-year total, confirming the rapid scaling trajectory. ROE of 100.28%, $612M cash position, and trailing PE of 20.39x on a stock that grew revenue 70% YoY demonstrate that BRIUMVI's commercial execution is translating into real earnings power [5]. The subcutaneous BRIUMVI Phase 3 program offers a credible next leg of growth with multi-billion-dollar potential, and emerging CD19 CAR-T autoimmune disease research provides pipeline optionality. Takeover speculation has intensified, with multiple analyst reports flagging TG as an acquisition target given BRIUMVI's growth trajectory and the company's ~$9B market cap [3].
The offsetting concerns remain cultural and governance quality. Glassdoor's 2.3/5 rating — declining from 2.5 in mid-2026 — with explicit 'toxic culture' complaints is among the lowest in the biotech sector and represents a meaningful organizational health risk as the company scales from 432 employees to support a $1B+ revenue base [4]. Q2 2026 net income compressed to $7.78M (from $28.2M YoY) as R&D and SG&A spending surged for subcutaneous BRIUMVI and pipeline build-out, signaling that the 55-65% profit margins from FY2025 are not sustainable at current investment levels [1]. Forward P/E of 29.47x on a PEG of ~1.5 leaves limited margin for error if BRIUMVI growth decelerates or biosimilar competition emerges post-patent. CEO Weiss holds ~22% of shares but has made no insider purchases in 18 months, and the July 2026 law firm investigation into potential fiduciary duty breaches adds governance overhang [5].
