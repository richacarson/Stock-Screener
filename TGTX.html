Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
TG Therapeutics is a fully integrated, commercial-stage biotechnology company focused on the acquisition, development, and commercialization of novel treatments for B-cell diseases. Its lead product, BRIUMVI (ublituximab), is FDA-approved for relapsing forms of multiple sclerosis. Founded in 2011 and led by Chairman, President, and CEO Michael S. Weiss, the company is headquartered in New York City.

Investment Thesis
TG Therapeutics has completed a dramatic commercial turnaround on the strength of BRIUMVI, with FY2025 revenue up 87.3% to $616.3M and Q1 2026 revenue nearly doubling YoY to $204.9M, prompting management to raise the full-year 2026 total revenue target to approximately $925M and BRIUMVI U.S. net revenue guidance to $885-900M [1][2]. Profitability metrics are exceptional (ROE 112.6%, profit margin 65.95%, diluted EPS growth of 1,747% YoY), and the subcutaneous BRIUMVI program in Phase 3 development offers a credible next leg of growth that management has characterized as multi-billion-dollar potential [2][4]. The core commercial business — physician relationships, infusion administration, and patent-protected biologic manufacturing — is well insulated from AI disruption, with AI showing up only as an early-stage genomic-target-discovery tool rather than a threat.
The offsetting concerns are cultural and governance quality rather than commercial execution. Glassdoor's 2.5/5 rating with explicit 'toxic culture' complaints and protection of a poorly-behaving senior leader stands out even among biotech peers, and a July 2026 law firm investigation into whether officers and directors breached fiduciary duties through potential self-dealing adds a fresh governance overhang on top of the company's history of failed trials and 2017-2019 securities litigation [4][5]. CEO Weiss has made no insider purchases in 18 months despite holding a 22% stake, offering no fresh signal of conviction, and Forward P/E of 31.7x against Forward PEG of 1.53 leaves less margin for error if BRIUMVI growth decelerates or biosimilar competition emerges post-patent [4][5].
Key Catalysts
  1. Subcutaneous BRIUMVI Phase 3 readout and potential approval, which management has framed as multi-billion-dollar incremental opportunity for a self-administered MS treatment
  2. Continued record new patient starts sustaining the raised FY2026 guidance of $885-900M in BRIUMVI U.S. net revenue
  3. Pediatric MS trial progress (ULTIMATE/ACTRIMS) expanding BRIUMVI's addressable patient population
Key Risks
  1. July 2026 law firm investigation into potential fiduciary duty breaches and self-dealing by TG officers and directors introduces governance and litigation overhang
  2. Glassdoor culture rating of 2.5/5 with explicit 'toxic culture' complaints and reported protection of a poorly-performing senior leader signals organizational health risk that could affect execution and retention
  3. Forward P/E of 31.7x prices in continued high growth; any deceleration in BRIUMVI patient starts or emergence of biosimilar competition would pressure the premium valuation
