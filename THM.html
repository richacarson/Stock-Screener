Investment Thesis
International Tower Hill Mines offers leveraged exposure to gold prices through the Livengood Gold Project, one of the largest undeveloped gold deposits in North America with approximately 9 Moz measured & indicated resources [1]. The $115M equity financing closed in January 2026 — including Paulson & Co.'s $40M private placement — provides a multi-year cash runway with $50M earmarked for feasibility studies and $35M for community engagement and permitting [1][2]. At ~$536M market cap and $2.05/share, the stock is a pure gold optionality play that could deliver significant upside if Livengood proves economic with gold prices sustaining above $2,700/oz. The 2025 investigation of antimony critical mineral potential adds strategic diversification optionality.
The bear case is substantial and dominates the risk-reward profile. This is a pre-revenue company that has been in the exploration stage since 1978 — 48 years without generating a dollar of revenue or producing an ounce of gold [1][4]. The $115M raise is entirely dilutive to existing shareholders (37% dilution from the January 2026 offering alone). With 3 employees, negative ROE of -8.2%, and no production timeline or construction decision, THM is a speculative bet on geology and gold prices rather than an investment in a functioning business. The company's survival depends on continued access to capital markets, and Anglogold Ashanti's recent exit from a similar Alaska gold partnership (Novagold's Donlin project) highlights the difficulty of advancing large Alaskan mines to production. Infrastructure scores 0/10 across all available financial metrics — there is no financial foundation to evaluate.
Key Catalysts
  1. Feasibility study completion — the $50M allocated for technical studies from the 2026 equity raise could produce a positive feasibility assessment that de-risks the project and catalyzes a re-rating, particularly with gold above $2,700/oz
  2. Permitting milestones — successful advancement through Alaska state and federal permitting processes, supported by the $35M community engagement allocation, would remove a key development overhang
  3. Antimony critical mineral assessment — if significant antimony resources are confirmed at Livengood, the project gains strategic value as antimony is a critical mineral for defense and technology applications, potentially attracting government support or strategic partners
Key Risks
  1. Permanent capital destruction — 48 years of exploration with zero revenue raises the risk that the Livengood deposit may never reach economic production, in which case the equity is worthless regardless of gold prices
  2. Dilution risk — the $115M equity financing is entirely dilutive, and additional capital raises totaling potentially billions of dollars will be required to fund mine construction, further eroding per-share value
  3. Single-asset concentration — the company's entire value depends on one deposit in one jurisdiction (Alaska), with no diversification to offset project-specific geological, regulatory, or permitting risks
