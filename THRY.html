Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NASDAQ ·United States
Thryv Holdings provides cloud-based software and marketing solutions for small and medium-sized businesses. The company operates two segments: SaaS (CRM, invoicing, scheduling, social media management, and the AI-driven Marketing Center add-on) and Marketing Services (legacy print and digital yellow pages). Thryv is executing a multi-year transformation from its declining legacy Dex Media/Yellow Pages business into a SMB SaaS platform, with SaaS now representing 70% of total revenue. Founded as Dex Media in 2013, rebranded as Thryv in 2019, headquartered in Grapevine, TX.
2,702 Employees ·www.thryv.com

Investment Thesis
Thryv at $3.29 with SaaS revenue now at 70% of total and growing 5% YoY represents a deep-value bet on the completion of the Yellow Pages-to-SaaS transformation [1]. Q1 2026 marked the company's first profitable quarter with net income of $4.5M ($0.10/share), and full-year SaaS guidance of $463M-$471M provides revenue visibility [1]. The hiring of CTO Sean Wechter (AI/infrastructure focus) and CPO Rees Johnson signals renewed product investment, while the Marketing Center AI add-on demonstrates platform relevance in an AI-driven market [2]. At 10.5x trailing earnings with $625M expected revenue, Thryv is priced for failure while the SaaS unit shows durable customer engagement with the SMB installed base.
The bear case is formidable. The stock has declined 70.78% in the past year, reflecting market concerns about AI disruption to mid-market SaaS platforms and execution risk in the transformation [4]. Marketing Services revenue is declining 27.5% annually and will continue to drag total revenue — the 5-year CAGR of -10% shows the legacy business shrinking faster than SaaS grows [1]. D/E of 115% with $258.6M in debt against only $7.95M in cash creates financial fragility during the transition, and the 21% share dilution erodes existing shareholder value [4]. SaaS revenue growth of 5% is well below the growth rates needed to justify a software valuation, and the forward P/E of 35.05x on thin forward earnings offers no margin of safety. The SMB software market is intensely competitive, with AI-native tools from larger players threatening to compress Thryv's pricing power and accelerate churn from the legacy customer base.

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience HIGH RISK
4
AI Risk Protection (3/9): Thryv's SMB SaaS platform — CRM, scheduling, invoicing, social media management, and marketing automation — faces significant AI substitution risk [2]. AI-native tools can replicate individual platform functions at lower cost: AI-powered CRM (HubSpot AI), AI scheduling assistants, automated invoicing, and AI-generated social media content are all actively shipping from larger, better-funded competitors. The stock lost 70%+ over the past year partly due to SaaS sector fears that AI would erode pricing power for mid-market platforms [4]. Analysts have flagged the broader risk that AI makes it easier to replicate what previously required expensive enterprise software. AI Opportunity (1/1): Marketing Center is an AI-driven add-on shipping to SMB customers, and TeamChat includes AI-integrated collaboration features — these represent concrete AI products generating customer value, demonstrating Thryv is actively incorporating AI rather than being purely disrupted by it [2].
