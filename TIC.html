Company Profile
Industrials ·Testing, Inspection, Certification & Engineering Services ·NYSE ·United States
TIC Solutions, Inc. (formerly Acuren Corporation) is a leading provider of tech-enabled Testing, Inspection, Certification and Compliance (TICC), engineering, geospatial, and critical asset integrity services across North America and select international markets. The company was formed through the August 2025 merger of Acuren Corporation and NV5 Global, Inc. ($1.7B acquisition), creating a $2B+ revenue platform serving industrial, infrastructure, energy, utilities, construction, and commercial real estate end markets. Rebranded to TIC Solutions in October 2025. CEO Benjamin Heraud took over from founder Tal Pizzey in March 2026. Q2 2026 revenue was $584.3M with a record combined backlog of $1.18B (+20% YoY).
12,760 Employees ·www.ticsolutions.com

Investment Thesis
TIC Solutions offers a contrarian value opportunity in critical infrastructure services. The August 2025 Acuren-NV5 merger created a market-leading North American TICC and engineering platform with $2B+ annual revenue, and Q2 2026 shows improving execution: revenue of $584.3M, record C&E revenue of $207M (+16.8%), Adjusted EBITDA of $94.8M (+74% YoY), and a record combined backlog of $1.18B up 20% YoY [1][2]. The GAAP net loss narrowed dramatically from $41.5M in Q1 to $13.3M in Q2, with adjusted diluted EPS of $0.14 turning positive. Full-year 2026 guidance reaffirmed at $2.15-2.25B revenue and $330-355M adjusted EBITDA (15-16% margin) [2]. The stock at $9.30 has recovered 23% from its $7.53 July low. Secular tailwinds from aging US infrastructure, energy transition compliance, and industrial safety regulation create a durable demand backdrop for TICC services.
The bear case centers on integration execution risk and current GAAP unprofitability. Despite improving trends, the company remains GAAP unprofitable with negative ROE (-6.3%) and profit margin (-5.8%), reflecting persistent merger-related purchase accounting amortization and integration costs [2]. D/E of 80% indicates meaningful leverage from the $1.7B NV5 merger financing. Revenue growth on a combined basis of only 3.3% in Q2 (decelerating from 4.3% in Q1) suggests limited organic momentum despite the record backlog [2]. The CEO transition from 39-year veteran Tal Pizzey to Ben Heraud in March 2026 introduces leadership uncertainty during the most critical integration phase [3]. Glassdoor reviews citing 'stale' culture and 'obsolete' technology raise concerns about the organization's ability to modernize and attract talent [4]. The stock remains down ~38% from its September 2025 high of $14.94, and with no dividend, negative free cash flow, and no clear timeline to GAAP profitability, TIC Solutions requires patience and conviction that integration synergies will eventually materialize.

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
8
AI Risk Protection (8/9): TIC Solutions' core business — physical inspection, testing, and certification of industrial assets, buildings, and public infrastructure — is structurally immune to AI disruption. The work requires physical presence at industrial sites (pipelines, refineries, power plants, bridges), specialized equipment (ultrasonic testing, radiography, eddy current), and certified human inspectors who must physically assess asset integrity [1]. Regulatory requirements mandate in-person inspection and certification for safety-critical infrastructure across energy, utilities, and construction sectors. AI cannot substitute for a technician performing non-destructive testing on a weld joint at a refinery. The growing infrastructure maintenance backlog (aging US infrastructure, energy transition compliance) actually increases demand for these services. Industry articles note 'resistance to AI adoption often comes from inspectors and assessors who carry personal liability for what they approve' [5]. AI could enhance inspection analysis through computer vision for defect detection, but physical service delivery and regulatory certification requirements remain human-dependent. AI Opportunity (0/1): No evidence of AI-driven revenue generation. The company describes itself as 'tech-enabled' but has not reported AI-specific products or revenue [2].
