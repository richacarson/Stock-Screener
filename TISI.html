Company Profile
Industrials ·Specialty Business Services ·NYSE ·United States
Team, Inc. provides specialty industrial services including mechanical, heat-treating, and inspection services globally through its TEAM360 platform. The company operates in two segments: Inspection & Heat Treating (IHT) and Mechanical Services (MS), serving refining, petrochemical, power generation, aerospace, mining, and pipeline industries across 13 countries. Q1 2026 revenue grew 8.3% to $215.1M while Q2 2026 revenue fell to $228.7M (-7.8% YoY) with net loss of $6.8M. Full-year 2026 guidance reaffirmed at $920-945M revenue and $68-73M Adjusted EBITDA under new CEO Gary Hill (appointed February 2026).

Q1 2026 delivered 8.3% revenue growth to the highest Q1 since 2019. Revenue growth 5yr CAGR of 2.6% barely outpaces inflation, and the company guided to the lower half of ranges for 2026. Until liquidity improves by an order of magnitude and the balance sheet is materially de-risked, Team remains a speculative turnaround play unsuitable for IOWN.

AI Resilience LOW RISK

AI Risk Protection (8/9): Team's core business — on-site industrial inspection, heat treating, leak repair, and mechanical services — is fundamentally physical work that AI cannot replace [2]. Certified inspectors must physically access pipes, pressure vessels, and heat exchangers in refineries and petrochemical plants. NDE certifications (ASME, API codes), hands-on expertise, and presence in hazardous environments are requirements that no AI or robotic system can replicate at scale. Regulatory requirements mandate human-performed inspections for critical infrastructure. AI may enhance inspection accuracy through computer vision-assisted defect detection, but this augments rather than replaces Team's workforce. Scored 8 rather than 9 because incremental automation in NDE data analysis could modestly reduce technician requirements over time. AI Opportunity (0/1): Team does not generate revenue from AI products. The ONEINSIGHT platform is a digital field management tool, not an AI product, and no AI-driven revenue has been quantified [2].
