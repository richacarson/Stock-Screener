Company Profile

Team, Inc. provides specialty industrial services including mechanical, heat-treating, and inspection services globally through its TEAM360 platform. The company operates in two segments: Inspection & Heat Treating (IHT) and Mechanical Services (MS), serving refining, petrochemical, power generation, aerospace, mining, and pipeline industries across 13 countries. Investment Thesis
Team Inc. offers a turnaround narrative backed by essential, AI-immune industrial services with full-year 2026 guidance of $920-945M revenue and $68-73M Adjusted EBITDA [1]. The stock has rallied 37% from $16.68 to $22.80, reflecting improving sentiment as Q1 2026 delivered 8.3% revenue growth and a narrowed net loss. New CEO Gary Hill brings fresh strategic focus on higher-margin aerospace and power generation markets. The company's physical services — inspection, heat treating, and mechanical repair of critical infrastructure — are virtually immune to AI disruption and benefit from aging industrial assets requiring ongoing maintenance. The Inspire Insight score of 49 with 14 positive ESG attributions reflects strong environmental alignment through emissions detection services [3].
The bear case is dominated by a catastrophically overleveraged balance sheet and severe illiquidity. D/E of 4,620% with $376M total debt against only $8.14M stockholders' equity represents existential leverage risk — any revenue shortfall could trigger covenant violations or liquidity crisis [5]. The stock is essentially uninvestable for IOWN due to extreme illiquidity — average daily volume of 29,671 shares produces only $676K in daily dollar volume, below the $1M minimum threshold, making position entry and exit at scale impossible. Q2 2026 deteriorated sharply with revenue falling 7.8% to $228.7M and Adjusted EBITDA plunging 48% to $12.8M as Middle East conflict deferred turnaround activity [1]. Net losses persist ($42.7M TTM, $6.8M in Q2 alone). The $75M preferred stock issuance adds dilution risk to an already complex capital structure. Revenue growth of ~4% at guidance midpoint barely outpaces inflation, and the company guided to the lower half of ranges. Until liquidity improves by an order of magnitude and the balance sheet is materially de-risked, Team remains a speculative turnaround play unsuitable for IOWN.

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
8
AI Risk Protection (8/9): Team's core business — on-site industrial inspection, heat treating, leak repair, and mechanical services — is fundamentally physical work that AI cannot replace [2]. Certified inspectors must physically access pipes, pressure vessels, and heat exchangers in refineries and petrochemical plants. NDE certifications, hands-on expertise, and presence in hazardous environments are requirements that no AI or robotic system can currently replicate at scale. Regulatory requirements (ASME, API, NDE codes) mandate human-performed inspections for critical infrastructure. AI may enhance inspection accuracy through computer vision-assisted defect detection, but this augments rather than replaces Team's workforce. The physical, regulated, and safety-critical nature of these services provides near-total immunity to AI disruption. AI Opportunity (0/1): Team does not generate revenue from AI products. The ONEINSIGHT platform is a digital field management tool, not an AI product, and no AI-driven revenue has been reported [2].
