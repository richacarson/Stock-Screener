Company Profile

Valion Bio, Inc. (formerly Tivic Health Systems; ticker transitioning from TIVC to VBIO) is a late-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on Entolimod, a TLR5 agonist with FDA Fast Track and Orphan Drug designations for acute radiation syndrome (ARS) under the FDA's Animal Rule. The company also operates Velocity Bioworks, a contract development and manufacturing organization (CDMO) acquired in December 2025 for approximately $16.3 million. The company pivoted from its original bioelectronic medicine business (ClearUP nasal device) in late 2025. Market cap under $1 million with going concern warning.

Investment Thesis
Valion Bio's sole investment case rests on Entolimod, a TLR5 agonist with FDA Fast Track and Orphan Drug designations for acute radiation syndrome, which could qualify for Strategic National Stockpile procurement through BARDA — a potential non-dilutive, nine-figure revenue event if the government exercises procurement options [1]. The company completed a 200-fold scale-up of Entolimod at 50L and operates Velocity Bioworks CDMO (acquired December 2025 for $16.3M), providing internal manufacturing capability. The stock trades at 0.07x book value ($0.2854 vs $3.84 book value per share), offering extreme optionality if Entolimod secures government procurement [3].
The bear case is overwhelming. Valion Bio has a market cap of $913,515 — less than the price of a median U.S. home — with going concern doubt disclosed by management, $7.2M cash against $17.03M debt, and quarterly cash burn of $5M, implying less than two quarters of liquidity remaining [1][3]. The BARDA procurement thesis is entirely speculative — no contract has been disclosed, and the government procurement process is notoriously slow and uncertain.
