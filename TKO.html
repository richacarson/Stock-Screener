TKO Group Holdings operates as a premium sports entertainment company through three segments: UFC (mixed martial arts), WWE (professional wrestling), and IMG (sports marketing/events). Formed in 2023 through the merger of UFC and WWE under Endeavor Group, TKO reported Q2 2026 revenue of $1.55B (up 18% YoY) with adjusted EBITDA of $649.9M (up 23% YoY). Full-year 2026 guidance raised to $5.775B-$5.825B revenue.

Investment Thesis
TKO Group Holdings owns two of the most irreplaceable live sports entertainment properties in the world — UFC and WWE — with Q2 2026 revenue of $1.55B growing 18% YoY and adjusted EBITDA of $649.9M at an exceptional 42% margin [1]. UFC's Paramount content distribution deal drove 29% segment revenue growth, and IMG's FIFA World Cup hospitality sales hit records. Full-year guidance raised to $5.775B-$5.825B revenue and $2.275B-$2.305B adjusted EBITDA, reflecting accelerating platform economics. The balance sheet is healthy at 59% D/E, and live sports content commands a scarcity premium in the AI-saturated streaming era — TKO's dual brands are among the last content categories that audiences must consume in real-time [1][5].
The bear case centers on valuation, governance, and structural complexity. At 38.4x forward earnings with GAAP profit margin of just 4.33% and ROE of 7.06%, TKO is priced for flawless execution while significant value leaks through the complex Endeavor ownership structure, non-cash charges, and minority interest flows [5]. The payout ratio of 109% on the dividend is unsustainable without earnings growth. Governance risk remains elevated — Vince McMahon's sex trafficking scandal forced his January 2024 resignation, and the $375M UFC antitrust settlement revealed systemic fighter pay suppression that could resurface as a labor relations or regulatory issue [4]. Key-person dependence on Dana White (UFC) and specific fighter drawing power creates concentration risk. PEG of 1.49 suggests the market no longer prices TKO as a high-growth story at this valuation level.
Key Catalysts
  1. Paramount content distribution deal driving 29% UFC revenue growth with multi-year escalation clauses providing sustained media rights acceleration [1]
  2. Cross-platform UFC-WWE-IMG synergies driving adjusted EBITDA margin expansion to 42% with further improvement potential from shared sponsorship, venue, and distribution efficiencies [1]
  3. Live sports scarcity premium intensifying as streaming platforms compete for must-see real-time content, positioning UFC and WWE as two of the most valuable media rights properties globally
Key Risks
  1. GAAP profit margin of 4.33% and ROE of 7.06% mask significant value leakage through Endeavor ownership complexity, non-cash charges, and minority interest — forward P/E of 38.4x leaves limited margin of safety [5]
  2. Governance risk from Vince McMahon sex trafficking scandal and $375M UFC antitrust fighter pay settlement signal systemic labor exploitation and corporate oversight failures that could resurface [4]
  3. Key-person dependence on Dana White (UFC) and specific fighter drawing power creates concentration risk — loss of major stars or leadership changes could impact event revenue and brand equity

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
9
AI Risk Protection (9/9): Live sports entertainment is arguably the most AI-immune business model in existence — UFC fights require real athletes competing in front of live audiences, and WWE matches demand physical performers creating unreplicable spectacle [1][2]. AI cannot replicate the charisma of a UFC champion, the drama of a WrestleMania main event, or the unpredictability of live competition. Media rights for live sports are the most sought-after content in the streaming era precisely because they are the last content category audiences consume in real-time, immune to AI-generated alternatives. No analyst has flagged AI as a disruption risk to combat sports or professional wrestling. AI-driven content creation elsewhere increases the scarcity value of authentic live entertainment. AI Opportunity (0/1): No quantifiable AI-specific revenue reported. While AI could enhance fan engagement, personalized content delivery, or production workflows, TKO has not disclosed AI-related revenue streams.
