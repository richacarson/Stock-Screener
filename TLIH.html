Company Profile
Industrials ·Industrial Distribution ·NASDAQ ·Singapore
Ten-League International Holdings, founded in 1998 and headquartered in Singapore, sells and rents heavy equipment (foundation, hoist, excavation, and port machinery) and provides engineering consultancy to the port, construction, civil engineering, and underground foundation industries. The company has expanded into electric reach stackers and container handlers with swappable battery technology plus supporting charging infrastructure for port operators. Ten-League went public on Nasdaq on July 8, 2025 and executed a 1-for-10 reverse share split effective May 1, 2026 to attempt to maintain its listing.
