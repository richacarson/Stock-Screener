Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas Midstream ·ASE ·Malaysia
TMD Energy Limited is a Malaysia and Singapore-based marine fuel bunkering services provider operating a fleet of 15 bunkering vessels across 19 Malaysian ports. The company supplies marine gas and fuel oil (HSFO, LSFO, VLSFO) to ships and vessels, and also provides vessel chartering, ship management, and cargo oil services. For the six months ended December 2025, revenue fell 22.5% to $247.6M with a net loss of $8.5M, driven by declining marine fuel demand and a 16% drop in global oil prices [1]. Market cap stands at approximately $13M.
