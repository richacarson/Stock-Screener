Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NGM ·United States
Tango Therapeutics is a precision oncology company developing therapies that exploit synthetic lethality in genetically defined cancers. The company's lead program, vopimetostat (TNG462), is an MTA-cooperative PRMT5 inhibitor targeting MTAP-deleted tumors — a genetic alteration found in approximately 15% of all cancers. Tango has demonstrated a 49% objective response rate in its Phase 1/2 histology-agnostic trial and is pursuing multiple combination strategies with partners including Revolution Medicines and Erasca.
137 Employees ·www.tangotx.com

Investment Thesis
Tango Therapeutics offers compelling exposure to a potentially transformative oncology platform targeting MTAP-deleted cancers through synthetic lethality. Vopimetostat's Phase 1/2 data — 49% ORR and 9.1-month mPFS across multiple tumor types — represents one of the strongest early-stage oncology readouts in recent years and positions Tango as the clinical leader in a space addressing approximately 15% of all cancers [1]. The company's $380M cash position provides 5+ years of runway at current burn rates, and the expanding combination strategy with Revolution Medicines and Erasca could significantly broaden vopimetostat's commercial potential [2][3]. At a $4.87B market cap, the stock prices in meaningful optionality but not full success across all indications.
The bear case centers on significant clinical and competitive risk inherent to a single-product clinical-stage biotech. Vopimetostat remains in Phase 1/2 with no pivotal trial initiated — the path to FDA approval requires years of additional development and substantial capital expenditure beyond current cash reserves [1]. Large pharma competitors (GSK, BMS) are developing competing MTAP-targeted programs with vastly greater resources, manufacturing infrastructure, and commercial reach, threatening Tango's first-mover advantage [2]. Financial fundamentals are deeply negative: -188% profit margins, -38.4% ROE, and -$68M annual free cash flow with only $57M in collaboration revenue provide no margin of safety if clinical setbacks occur [3]. The negative forward P/E makes traditional valuation impossible, and the $4.87B market cap implies the market is pricing in high probability of clinical and commercial success that remains far from guaranteed.
Key Catalysts
  1. Updated vopimetostat Phase 1/2 data readout expected in 2026 from the combination cohorts with Revolution Medicines' daraxonrasib/zoldonrasib (30 patients enrolled as of December 2025), which could demonstrate synergistic activity in MTAP-deleted and RAS-driven cancers [2]
  2. Initiation of the Erasca combination trial (vopimetostat + ERAS-0015 pan-RAS molecular glue) announced March 2026, expanding the combination strategy and potentially validating a novel mechanistic approach [2]
  3. Potential pivotal trial design announcement or FDA breakthrough therapy designation for vopimetostat monotherapy based on the 49% ORR data, which could accelerate the regulatory pathway and de-risk the development timeline [1]
Key Risks
  1. Phase 1/2 data maturation risk — the 49% ORR and 9.1-month mPFS could narrow as the dataset matures, or combination cohorts could show unexpected toxicity, fundamentally undermining the investment thesis [1]
  2. Large pharma competitive threat from GSK, Bristol Myers Squibb, and others developing competing MTAP-targeted programs with greater clinical trial infrastructure, manufacturing capabilities, and commercial reach [2]
  3. Cash runway and dilution risk — with -$68M annual free cash flow and no product revenue, Tango will likely require additional capital raises before any potential approval, diluting existing shareholders and creating financing dependency [3]
