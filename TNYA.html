Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Tenaya Therapeutics, Inc. is a clinical-stage biotechnology company focused on discovering and developing therapies for heart disease through gene therapy, cellular regeneration, and precision medicine platforms. Lead programs include TN-201 for MYBPC3-associated hypertrophic cardiomyopathy (HCM) and TN-401 for PKP2-associated arrhythmogenic right ventricular cardiomyopathy (ARVC). Multi-target research collaboration with Alnylam Pharmaceuticals ($10M upfront, up to $1.1B potential milestones). Founded 2016, headquartered in South San Francisco.
130 Employees ·www.tenayatherapeutics.com

Investment Thesis
Tenaya occupies a unique niche in cardiac gene therapy, targeting genetic heart diseases (HCM, ARVC) with no curative options for patients. TN-201 Phase 1b/2 MyPEAK-1 trial data represents a potentially transformative catalyst — if successful, it could establish the first gene therapy for hypertrophic cardiomyopathy, a disease affecting 1 in 500 people [1]. The March 2026 Alnylam collaboration ($10M upfront, $1.1B potential milestones) validates the platform and provides non-dilutive capital and partnership credibility [1]. Q1 2026 net loss improved to $19.3M ($0.09/share) from $26.9M ($0.24/share) year-ago, showing improving capital efficiency [1]. Eight analysts maintain a 'Strong Buy' consensus with a $4.00 price target (413% upside from $0.78), reflecting confidence in the clinical pipeline [5]. The Alnylam collaboration generated TNYA's first revenue ($1.1M Q2 2026), marking a milestone toward platform validation [2].
The fundamental challenge is binary clinical risk — TNYA is a pre-revenue company burning $19.3M per quarter with approximately $78M cash (as of March 2026), providing roughly 4 quarters of runway [1]. An additional capital raise is likely necessary, and at $0.78 per share (down 67% from the 52-week high of $2.34), dilution will be severe for existing shareholders holding 224M shares outstanding [3]. The entire investment thesis rests on binary clinical trial outcomes — TN-201 MyPEAK-1 data will either validate or substantially impair the platform's value. Gene therapy manufacturing scalability (AAV vector production), payer reimbursement for expensive one-time therapies (gene therapy price points typically exceed $1M), and the long regulatory pathway to BLA approval represent execution risks even in the success scenario. Analyst profitability projections for 2027 ($57.4M net income) are highly contingent on clinical and regulatory success that is far from assured.
Key Catalysts
  1. TN-201 Phase 1b/2 MyPEAK-1 trial data from adult cohorts representing a binary catalyst for the HCM gene therapy program with potential to establish a new therapeutic category for 1 in 500 people [1]
  2. Alnylam Pharmaceuticals collaboration advancing novel cardiovascular gene targets with up to $1.1B in potential milestones, providing non-dilutive funding, platform validation, and first-ever revenue generation [1][2]
  3. TN-301 HDAC6 inhibitor advancing into clinical development for heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF) and Duchenne muscular dystrophy, diversifying the pipeline beyond gene therapy [1]
Key Risks
  1. Cash runway of approximately 4 quarters ($78M at $19.3M/quarter burn rate) necessitates likely capital raise at severely depressed share price ($0.78, down 67% from 52-week high), threatening significant dilution to 224M shares outstanding [1][3]
  2. Binary clinical risk — TN-201 MyPEAK-1 trial results will validate or substantially impair the core gene therapy platform, with no commercial revenue base to sustain operations if clinical programs fail [1]
  3. Gene therapy commercialization execution risk — AAV manufacturing scalability, payer willingness to reimburse $1M+ one-time therapies, and lengthy BLA regulatory pathway create multi-year uncertainty even in the clinical success scenario

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
8
AI Risk Protection (8/9): Gene therapy development for cardiac diseases involves highly specialized biological science, physical manufacturing of viral vectors (AAV), FDA-regulated clinical trials with human patients, and complex delivery procedures that AI structurally cannot replace [1]. The regulatory pathway (IND, Phase 1/2/3, BLA) creates years of process-based moats that no AI system can circumvent. Gene therapy manufacturing requires specialized biological production facilities that are inherently physical. AI may accelerate target identification and clinical trial design, but the core business of developing, manufacturing, and delivering gene therapies is fundamentally biological and regulated. No analyst has flagged AI as a substitution risk for cardiac gene therapy — AI could even increase demand by enabling identification of more patients who could benefit from gene therapy. AI Opportunity (0/1): No AI-powered products or AI-generated revenue. While computational tools may assist in research, no quantified AI revenue contribution exists [1].
