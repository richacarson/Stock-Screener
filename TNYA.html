Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Tenaya Therapeutics, Inc. is a clinical-stage biotechnology company in South San Francisco developing gene therapies that target the genetic drivers of heart disease. Lead programs are TN-201 (MYBPC3-associated hypertrophic cardiomyopathy, MyPEAK-1 trial, EMA PRIME designation) and TN-401 (PKP2-associated arrhythmogenic right ventricular cardiomyopathy, RIDGE-1 trial). The company has a cardiac capsid collaboration with Alnylam. It has no approved products, and its shares moved to the Nasdaq Capital Market in July 2026 after a minimum-bid-price deficiency.

Investment Thesis
Tenaya's two lead gene therapies target genetically defined cardiomyopathies with no disease-modifying treatments. TN-201 for MYBPC3 HCM has EMA PRIME designation and a place in the FDA's Rare Disease Evidence Principles process, with no dose-limiting toxicities. TN-401 for PKP2 ARVC reduced PVCs by a mean of 64% [1][2]. Pivotal-path updates are due in Q4 2026. With a roughly $91M market cap against $78.1M of cash, a positive regulatory read-through or another partnership like the Alnylam deal could re-rate the stock sharply [2][9].
The bear case dominates. Tenaya has no revenue, quarterly losses of about $21.6M before impairments, and cash guided only through Q3 2027. Analysts flag that an equity raise is likely before then, which at $0.40 per share would mean heavy dilution [1][3]. The stock fell below $1 and moved to the Nasdaq Capital Market to stay listed [8]. Daily dollar volume of about $1.1M sits right at IOWN's liquidity floor, and cardiac gene therapy carries significant safety and pivotal-trial design risk.
Key Catalysts
  1. Q4 2026 regulatory update on pivotal trial designs for TN-201 and TN-401 [1]
  2. Additional MyPEAK-1 and RIDGE-1 clinical data showing durable expression and functional benefit [2]
  3. New non-dilutive partnerships extending the Alnylam capsid collaboration model [1]
Key Risks
  1. Cash runway only to Q3 2027, so a highly dilutive equity raise or reverse split is likely [1][3]
  2. Nasdaq delisting risk if the $1.00 minimum bid is not regained during the Capital Market compliance period [8]
  3. Gene-therapy safety events or an FDA requirement for larger, costlier pivotal trials than Tenaya can fund
