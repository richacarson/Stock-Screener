Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Infrastructure ·NYSE ·United States
Toast, Inc. operates a cloud-based digital technology platform purpose-built for the restaurant industry. The platform integrates point-of-sale, payment processing, digital ordering and delivery, payroll and team management, inventory and supply chain tools, marketing, and AI-powered financial technology solutions. Toast serves approximately 180,000 restaurant locations across the United States and internationally. Q2 2026 delivered revenue of $1.91B (+23% YoY), operating income of $152M, Adjusted EBITDA of $221M, and ARR of $2.4B (+25% YoY). Key AI products include ToastIQ, Sous Chef, and AI-powered marketing. Founded in 2011 and headquartered in Boston, Massachusetts.

Investment Thesis
Toast is the dominant platform for restaurant technology, serving ~180,000 locations (+22% YoY) with a comprehensive cloud-based operating system. Q2 2026 delivered revenue of $1.91B (+23% YoY), operating income of $152M, Adjusted EBITDA of $221M (beating estimates by 13.3%), and ARR of $2.4B growing 25% [1]. The company raised full-year 2026 guidance to subscription and fintech gross profit of $2,325-2,355M (23-25% growth) and Adjusted EBITDA of $805-825M. At Forward PEG of 0.70 and Forward P/E of 22.70, Toast offers attractive value relative to its 23%+ growth rate [4]. The debt-free balance sheet ($1.71B cash) and expanding profitability provide financial flexibility. Toast's AI innovation suite — ToastIQ, Sous Chef, AI Marketing Assistant — positions the company to monetize AI across its massive installed base. The Inspire Impact score of 39 (ALIGNED) with 8 positive attributions and zero negatives reflects strong ESG positioning [3].
The bear case focuses on margin trajectory, valuation expansion, and competitive threats. Despite strong Q2 results, the stock remains 24% below its 52-week high of $45.64, reflecting market skepticism about long-term margin potential [4]. Profit margin of 7.14% is thin for a company approaching $8B in annualized revenue, and the revenue mix remains heavily weighted toward lower-margin payment processing (~80% of revenue) rather than higher-margin SaaS subscriptions, creating a structural ceiling on margin expansion. Forward P/E of 22.70 (up from 14.5 in June) suggests the valuation has re-rated significantly — PEG of 0.70 remains attractive but further multiple expansion requires sustained 20%+ growth. The restaurant industry is inherently cyclical — a recession could slow the ~9,500 quarterly net new location additions and increase churn among existing customers facing margin pressure. Block (Square), Fiserv (Clover), and emerging AI-native startups could erode Toast's vertical advantages as AI lowers barriers to building restaurant-specific features through platform unbundling [2]. International expansion remains early stage with unproven unit economics.
Key Catalysts
  1. Raised 2026 guidance to $2,325-2,355M gross profit (23-25% growth) and $805-825M Adjusted EBITDA — continued execution and margin expansion should drive further stock appreciation from the current PEG of 0.70 [1]
  2. AI monetization through ToastIQ, Sous Chef, and AI Marketing Assistant creates upsell opportunities across 180,000 locations — AI features could shift revenue mix toward higher-margin software and accelerate ARR growth beyond the current 25% pace [1][2]
  3. ~9,500 net new locations per quarter with total addressable market of 860,000+ US restaurant locations suggests significant runway for continued 20%+ location growth, plus international expansion in Ireland and India [1]
Key Risks
  1. Profit margin of 7.14% remains thin at ~$8B annualized revenue scale, and the revenue mix is heavily weighted toward lower-margin payment processing (~80%), creating a structural margin ceiling despite improving Adjusted EBITDA [1][4]
  2. Restaurant industry cyclicality: a recession could simultaneously slow net new location additions (~9,500/quarter), increase existing customer churn, and compress per-location spending — Toast's growth narrative would deteriorate rapidly in a downturn [1]
  3. AI-driven unbundling risk: AI enables creation of highly specialized, customizable, low-cost restaurant tools integratable via APIs to lower-cost POS systems, potentially eroding Toast's integrated platform premium and threatening software revenue [2]
