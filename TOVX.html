Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·AMEX ·United States
Theriva Biologics, Inc. is a clinical-stage immuno-oncology company developing oncolytic viruses designed to overcome the protective barrier surrounding solid tumors and selectively kill tumor cells. Its lead candidate, VCN-01, is an oncolytic adenovirus advancing toward a pivotal Phase 3 trial in metastatic pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC), with FDA alignment on trial design and VIRAGE2 Phase 2a dosing underway. Additional programs include VCN-11 (Albumin Shield Technology for systemic delivery), SYN-004 (ribaxamase for aGVHD prevention), and SYN-020 (GI indications). Market cap ~$10M with $14.4M cash. Founded in 2001 and headquartered in Rockville, Maryland.
15 Employees ·therivabio.com

Investment Thesis
Theriva Biologics is a micro-cap clinical-stage biotech with a potentially meaningful oncolytic virus candidate (VCN-01) targeting pancreatic cancer — a $7B+ market with devastating patient outcomes. The company has achieved FDA alignment on a pivotal Phase 3 trial design for VCN-01 in metastatic PDAC, and has dosed first patients in the VIRAGE2 Phase 2a trial to optimize dosing regimens [1]. Both FDA and EMA have agreed to repeated dosing 'macrocycles' of VCN-01 with standard-of-care chemotherapy, a regulatory milestone that de-risks the pivotal trial path. Cash of $14.4M provides runway into early 2027. Q1 2026 net loss narrowed to $2.0M from $4.3M a year earlier, demonstrating improving cash efficiency [1]. The Inspire Impact Score of 28 (ALIGNED) with 5 positive attributions and zero negatives reflects the company's cancer-treatment mission [3].
The bear case is overwhelming for all but the most speculative investors. With a ~$10M market cap, 15 employees, negative ROE of -137.8%, and R&D spending that declined 88% YoY, Theriva lacks the resources to advance its pipeline without significant dilutive financing [1][4]. The stock trades at $0.23 — down 84.8% from its 52-week high of $0.86 — firmly in penny-stock territory with shares outstanding expanding to ~45.9M through repeated equity issuances. The company has changed its name and strategic focus multiple times, and a quorum issue delaying a warrant vote suggests weak institutional backing [3]. Even if VCN-01 shows promise in Phase 2/3 trials, the company would need a well-funded partner or acquirer to bring it to market. The 88% R&D spending cut is particularly concerning, as it suggests the company is prioritizing cash preservation over pipeline advancement. Forward P/E is meaningless for a company with no clear path to profitability in the medium term.
