Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·ASE ·United States
Theriva Biologics is a clinical-stage biotechnology company developing oncolytic virus and microbiome-based therapeutics for cancer and related diseases. Its lead candidate VCN-01 (zabilugene almadenorepvec) is an oncolytic adenovirus in Phase 2 clinical trials for metastatic pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC), with the Phase 2b VIRAGE trial having enrolled 92 patients and FDA end-of-Phase 2 meeting completed in March 2026. The company has 15 employees after a 32% workforce reduction in September 2025 and is headquartered in Rockville, Maryland.

Investment Thesis
Theriva at $0.22 with a $10M market cap is a micro-cap clinical-stage bet on VCN-01, an oncolytic adenovirus targeting metastatic pancreatic cancer — one of the deadliest cancers with ~12% five-year survival and limited treatment options [3]. The Phase 2b VIRAGE trial enrolled 92 patients, the FDA end-of-Phase 2 meeting was completed in March 2026, and the VIRAGE2 repeat-dosing trial dosed its first patient in July 2026, potentially supporting a Phase 3 design [1][3]. With $11.3M in cash and a pipeline including SYN-004 for aGVHD prevention, a positive VIRAGE readout could create asymmetric upside from the current depressed valuation.
The bear case is existential. Theriva has no approved products, no meaningful revenue ($300K trailing), burns $12.7M annually in operating cash, and cut 32% of its workforce in September 2025, leaving only 15 employees [4][5]. The $11.3M cash position provides roughly 10 months of runway at the current burn rate, virtually guaranteeing additional dilutive capital raises that will further erode the share price from its current $0.22 penny-stock level. Average daily dollar volume of just $117K makes the stock effectively untradeable for institutional investors. The oncolytic virus space is competitive, with Amgen's T-VEC and multiple clinical-stage programs from better-funded competitors, and pancreatic cancer trials have a notoriously high failure rate. Forward EPS of -$0.20 against a $0.22 stock price implies the market expects continued deep losses with no near-term path to profitability [2].
