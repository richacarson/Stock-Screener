Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas E&P ·NYSE American ·United States
Trio Petroleum Corp. is a micro-cap oil and gas exploration and development company operating in Monterey County, California (South Salinas Project, 85.75% WI in 9,245 acres) and Uintah County, Utah. Founded in July 2021 and IPO'd in April 2023 at $3.00/share, the company is currently producing approximately 78.7 barrels of oil per day (BOPD). TTM revenue is $695K with a net loss of -$6.48M. Trio has been expanding into Canadian oil and gas assets through an ATM equity program that raised ~$24M. The company has $22M in cash, no long-term debt, and one employee (CEO Robin S. Ross). Market cap: $11M.
