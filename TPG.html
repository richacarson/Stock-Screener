Company Profile
Financial Services ·Asset Management ·NASDAQ ·United States
TPG Inc., founded in 1992 and headquartered in Fort Worth, Texas, is a global alternative asset manager. Its platforms span private equity, growth, impact (Rise), credit and real estate (Angelo Gordon), and market solutions [4]. At Q3 2026, AUM was $345.7B (+21% YoY), fee-paying AUM was $190B (+16%), and dry powder reached a record $86B. Fee-related earnings were $321M at a ~50% margin, and after-tax distributable earnings were $0.65 per share [1][2]. TPG has been public since its January 2022 IPO.

Investment Thesis
TPG is compounding its fee base. Q3 2026 AUM rose 21% to $345.7B, fee-paying AUM grew 16% to $190B, FRE rose ~43% YoY at a ~50% margin, and record dry powder of $86B should convert into management fees as it is deployed [1][2]. At $48 (well off the $70 52-week high) the stock trades at ~13.6x FY2027 consensus EPS, with a 4.65% dividend yield and consensus EPS rising from $2.79 (2026) to $4.28 (2028) [3][7].
The bear case is that GAAP economics are far weaker than distributable earnings. The GAAP net margin is 5.2%, the TTM P/E is 32.8x, and D/E is 168.6% [3]. Q3 fee-related revenue grew only ~1.6% sequentially, and normalized FRE margin was ~47% excluding $62M of catch-up fees [1][2]. Realizations and carried interest depend on exit markets. The firm also faces greenwashing and anti-ESG political risk to fundraising, as flagged in its own 10-K [5].
Key Catalysts
  1. Deployment of the record $86B dry powder converting into fee-paying AUM and carried interest
  2. FRE margin expansion toward management's ~48% 2026 guidance on a normalized basis
  3. Exit-market recovery driving realizations and higher distributable earnings per share
Key Risks
  1. Normalized FRE margin (~47%) below the headline 50%; fee-related revenue growth stalling sequentially
  2. Leverage: GAAP debt-to-equity of 168.6% and payout well above GAAP earnings
  3. Anti-ESG and greenwashing regulatory risk to the impact-investing brand
  4. AI disruption of software/services portfolio companies pressuring fund marks
