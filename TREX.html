Company Profile
Industrials ·Building Products & Equipment ·NYSE ·United States
Trex Company, Inc. is the world's largest manufacturer of wood-alternative composite decking and railing, made from approximately 95% recycled and reclaimed content, including post-consumer polyethylene film. Product lines include Trex Transcend, Trex Signature, Trex Select, and Trex Enhance, along with railing, fencing, and outdoor lighting accessories. Headquartered in Winchester, Virginia, Trex sells primarily through home improvement retailers and a network of specialty distributors and contractors.

Investment Thesis
Trex is the dominant category leader in composite decking with an estimated 50-60% market share, built on a genuinely differentiated, sustainability-driven business model that upcycles recycled plastic film into durable outdoor-living products [5][7]. Q1 2026 results beat expectations with $343 million in net sales and 40.5% gross margin, and preliminary Q2 2026 revenue of approximately $418 million topped both prior guidance ($388-403M) and consensus, prompting a raised full-year 2026 outlook of $1.215-1.250 billion in net sales and $335-350 million in adjusted EBITDA [1]. Zacks Research upgraded the stock to 'Strong-Buy' in July 2026, and 19 analysts carry a consensus 'Buy' rating with a 12-month price target implying meaningful upside from current levels [7].
The bear case centers on valuation versus a stalled growth trajectory: the 5-year revenue CAGR is roughly flat (-0.5%, from $1.197B in FY2021 to $1.174B in FY2025) reflecting a prolonged repair-and-remodel market downturn, yet the stock trades at a Forward PEG of 5.29 and Forward P/E of nearly 26x — a valuation that assumes a growth reacceleration that has not yet shown up in the multi-year numbers [6]. Net income actually declined from $238.4 million in FY2024 to $190.4 million in FY2025 despite share buybacks supporting EPS, and insider ownership of just 0.93% with a recent SVP sale (-3.68% of their position) offers no signal of executive conviction at current prices [6][7]. The stock's 52-week range of $29.77-$68.78 also reflects significant sensitivity to housing and remodeling market sentiment.
