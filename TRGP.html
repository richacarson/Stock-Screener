Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas Midstream ·NYSE ·United States
Targa Resources Corp. is one of the largest independent midstream infrastructure companies in North America, providing gathering, processing, and transportation services for natural gas and natural gas liquids (NGLs). The company operates approximately 45,000 miles of natural gas pipelines and 43 gas processing plants, primarily in the Permian Basin. A FORTUNE 500 and S&P 500 constituent headquartered in Houston, Texas. Q1 2026 delivered record results with net income of $480M (up 141% YoY), and the company raised 2026 EBITDA guidance to $5.4-5.6B.

Investment Thesis
Targa Resources delivered record Q1 2026 results with net income of $480M (up 141% YoY) and raised 2026 EBITDA guidance to $5.4-5.6B, following record FY2025 adjusted EBITDA of $4.96B (up 20%) and net income of $1.85B (up 46%) [1][2]. The company's transformation to 90%+ fee-based revenue provides earnings stability, while the Permian Basin's production growth and surging AI-driven natural gas demand offer structural tailwinds [3]. At a forward PEG of 1.25 and forward P/E of 24.19, Targa trades at a reasonable valuation relative to its earnings growth trajectory. ROE of 70.84% reflects exceptional capital efficiency. The 25% dividend increase to $5.00 annualized underscores management confidence in sustainable cash flow generation. The Inspire score of 41 (ALIGNED) with 8 positive attributions makes Targa suitable for faith-based portfolios [4].
The primary concern is Targa's elevated leverage — a debt-to-equity ratio of 515.79% and $16B+ in total debt creates vulnerability to interest rate shocks and capital market disruptions, even though the predictable fee-based cash flows support the debt load [5]. Revenue growth of only 4.2% YoY masks the fact that EBITDA and earnings growth are primarily driven by new project completions and operating leverage rather than organic throughput expansion. The $4.5B net growth capex program requires continued Permian production growth — any slowdown in E&P activity due to commodity price weakness or regulatory changes could leave Targa with underutilized capacity. Forward P/E of 24.19x has expanded from prior periods, reflecting a premium re-rating that leaves limited margin of safety if execution disappoints. Q2 2026 EPS of $3.56 versus Q2 2025's $2.89 shows strong execution, but the trailing P/E of 27.93x prices in continued acceleration [5]. Environmental regulatory risk from methane emissions rules and potential carbon pricing could increase operating costs, though the AGI technology positions Targa well for stricter emissions regimes [3][4].
