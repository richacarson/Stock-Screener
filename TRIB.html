Company Profile
Healthcare ·Diagnostics & Research ·NASDAQ ·Ireland
Trinity Biotech is a commercial-stage diagnostics company focused on diabetes management, human diagnostics (HIV testing, haemoglobin products), and wearable biosensors. The company executed a 1-for-30 ADS reverse split in July 2026 to regain Nasdaq bid-price compliance. Q1 2026 revenue grew 43% to $10.8M driven by TrinScreen HIV test orders and haemoglobin product sales. In June 2026, Trinity launched Trinovium, a subsidiary leveraging its fluid manufacturing capabilities for AI data center liquid cooling. CEO John Gillard (since December 2023, formerly Google) is leading a transformation plan. Market cap is approximately $7.7M, making it a micro-cap equity.

Investment Thesis
Trinity Biotech's bull case rests on two asymmetric bets: a diagnostics turnaround and an AI infrastructure pivot. Q1 2026 revenue grew 43% to $10.8M with gross margin expanding from 25.2% to 35.4%, demonstrating early traction in the transformation plan [2]. The 2M+ TrinScreen HIV test orders for Q3 2026 provide near-term revenue visibility, and Trinovium positions the company at the intersection of healthcare-grade fluid technology and the multi-billion dollar AI data center cooling market [1]. CEO Gillard's Google infrastructure background is directly relevant to Trinovium's target market. At a $7.7M market cap, the stock is priced for failure — any material Trinovium traction or sustained diagnostics improvement could drive outsized returns from an extremely depressed base.
The bear case is severe and multidimensional. Trinity is a micro-cap equity with a $7.7M market cap that required a 1-for-30 reverse split to maintain Nasdaq listing, carries financial strength rated 1/10, and depends on emergency funding from Perceptive Advisors ($7M in 2026) to sustain operations [2]. The stock is essentially uninvestable for institutional portfolios due to liquidity constraints — estimated daily traded value of approximately $162K means IOWN could not build or exit a meaningful position without moving the stock price materially. Net loss of $4.4M in Q1 2026, while narrowing from $8.8M a year prior, reflects continued cash burn. Trinovium has zero revenue and no announced customer contracts. The diagnostics business competes against Abbott, Roche, and Siemens Healthineers with 1/10,000th their resources. Revenue guidance was lowered from $15.5-16.0M to $11.5-12.5M for a subsequent period, signaling execution challenges [2].
