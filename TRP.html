Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas Midstream ·NYSE ·Canada
TC Energy Corporation operates one of the largest natural gas pipeline networks in North America, spanning approximately 93,000 km of natural gas pipelines and 4,900 MW of power generation. Following the spin-off of its crude oil pipelines business (South Bow Corporation) in October 2024, TC Energy is a pure-play natural gas and power infrastructure company. The NGTL system and Columbia Gas system are core assets. Headquartered in Calgary, Alberta, Canada.

Investment Thesis
TC Energy is a pure-play North American natural gas and power infrastructure company delivering strong operational execution, with Q1 2026 comparable EBITDA rising 14% to C\.1B and the NGTL system setting all-time delivery records of 18.3 Bcf/d [1][2]. The company is a direct beneficiary of AI-driven data center demand growth, which is accelerating natural gas consumption across North America. The 3.65% dividend yield backed by 40+ years of consecutive payments and 24+ years of consecutive growth provides reliable income. Management guided to .6-.8B EBITDA for 2026 with a growth trajectory to .6-.1B by 2028, supported by the newly approved \.5B Appalachia Supply Project [2]. The stock has gained 44% over the past year as the market re-rates the company's AI-era relevance.
The principal risk is TC Energy's heavily leveraged balance sheet with .2B in net debt and D/E of 167%, which constrains financial flexibility and exposes the company to interest rate risk [4]. The payout ratio of 108.6% on a GAAP basis raises sustainability questions, though cash flow coverage is adequate with \.15B in operating cash flow and \.68B FCF. Forward P/E of 26.05 is premium for a utility-like infrastructure business, reflecting the market's enthusiasm for AI-driven energy demand that may be partially priced in after the 44% run-up. Revenue growth is modest at 6.6% YoY, and the 5-year revenue CAGR of approximately 2% reflects the South Bow spin-off impact. The Coastal GasLink cost overruns and broader regulatory uncertainty around pipeline approvals in Canada remain ongoing concerns for future project economics.
Key Catalysts
  1. AI data center buildout driving accelerating natural gas demand across North America, with TC Energy's pipeline systems setting new delivery records as hyperscaler power needs grow exponentially
  2. Appalachia Supply Project (\.5B) expansion of Columbia Gas system providing visible EBITDA growth from secured capacity, with management targeting .6-.1B by 2028
  3. Potential deleveraging from strong free cash flow and completed capital projects improving credit metrics and reducing interest expense burden
Key Risks
  1. Net debt of .2B and D/E of 167% create significant interest rate sensitivity and limit financial flexibility during a capital-intensive growth phase for pipeline expansion
  2. GAAP payout ratio of 108.6% exceeds net income, and while cash flow coverage is adequate, sustained earnings pressure could force a dividend growth slowdown or cut
  3. Regulatory and environmental opposition to pipeline projects in Canada, where Coastal GasLink cost overruns more than doubled the original budget, may repeat on future projects

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
9
AI Risk Protection (8/9): TC Energy's core business — operating physical natural gas pipelines and power generation assets — is structurally immune to AI disruption. Pipelines are irreplaceable physical infrastructure with 50+ year asset lives, regulated rate structures, and no digital substitution path. AI cannot transport natural gas or generate electricity from existing power plants [1]. Moreover, AI is actively increasing demand for TC Energy's services: data center buildout is driving unprecedented electricity demand, much of it gas-fired, with TC Energy's pipeline systems setting 7 new all-time delivery records in Q1 2026, including the NGTL system record of 18.3 Bcf/d [2]. The regulated nature of pipeline tariffs provides additional protection — rate structures are set by regulators, not competitive markets. AI Opportunity (1/1): TC Energy is deploying AI internally through its Moveworks partnership and AWS machine learning integration for pipeline operations, predictive maintenance, and process automation [3]. While not a revenue generator, these AI tools are delivering operational efficiency gains and positioning the company to benefit from AI-driven energy demand growth.
