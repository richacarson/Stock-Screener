Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Trevi Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing Haduvio (oral nalbuphine ER) for the treatment of chronic cough in patients with idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF), non-IPF interstitial lung disease, and refractory chronic cough (RCC). Founded in 2011 by CEO Jennifer Good, the company has no revenue-generating products and is fully funded through its Phase 3 program with a pro forma cash balance of approximately $334M following an April 2026 stock offering, extending its runway through 2030. In Q2 2026, Trevi initiated its first Phase 3 trial of Haduvio in IPF-related chronic cough and a Phase 2b trial in RCC. Market cap approximately $2.1B.

Investment Thesis
Trevi offers a high-risk/high-reward binary bet on Haduvio (nalbuphine ER) in Phase 3 for IPF-related chronic cough — a debilitating condition affecting ~100,000+ patients in the US alone with zero FDA-approved treatments [1][2]. The company is fully funded through 2030 with $334M in cash following the April offering, eliminating near-term dilution risk and providing runway to complete Phase 3 and submit an NDA. CEO Good's 14.5-year founding tenure and the company's $2.1B market cap suggest the market is pricing in meaningful probability of approval. The dual clinical programs (Phase 3 IPF cough + Phase 2b RCC) provide multiple shots on goal, and first-mover advantage in IPF cough treatment could generate blockbuster-level revenue given the unmet medical need and lack of competition.
The bear case is fundamentally about binary clinical risk: Haduvio is a single drug candidate that has not yet demonstrated Phase 3 efficacy, and clinical-stage biotechs fail more often than they succeed. If the Phase 3 trial fails to meet its primary endpoint, the stock could lose 70-80% of its $2.1B market cap overnight — the company has zero revenue, zero other commercial assets, and limited pipeline diversification [1][4]. The Q2 2026 net loss of $17.8M (up from $12.3M in Q2 2025) reflects accelerating cash burn as Phase 3 enrollment scales, and the $334M cash position — while extending runway through 2030 — represents a finite resource consumed at an increasing rate. Forward P/E of -25.8x confirms the market expects losses for years. The stock trades at $14.89, 15% below its 50-day SMA of $17.43, suggesting recent market skepticism [4].   1. Phase 3 trial data readout for Haduvio in IPF-related chronic cough — positive topline results would represent the first FDA-approved treatment for this indication and could trigger a significant re-rating of the $2.1B market cap [1][2]
