Company Profile
Basic Materials ·Gold ·NYSE ·Tanzania
TRX Gold Corporation (formerly Tanzanian Gold Corporation) operates the Buckreef Gold Project in Tanzania's Lake Victoria Goldfields region. The company transitioned from exploration to commercial production and reported YTD FY2026 revenue of $92.0M with gross profit of $54.7M (59% gross margin) and adjusted EBITDA of $54.1M. FY2026 gold production guidance of 25,000-30,000 ounces has been met, with expectations to finish at the top end. CEO Stephen Mullowney has led the company since December 2020, with COO Richard Boffey appointed in December 2024.

Investment Thesis
TRX Gold at $1.05 with a forward P/E of 10.5x offers leveraged exposure to gold prices through a rapidly scaling Tanzanian producer. The company has executed a successful transition from exploration to production, with FY2026 YTD revenue of $92.0M, gross margins of 59%, and adjusted EBITDA margins near 60% — exceptional profitability for a junior miner [1][2]. With gold prices above $4,600/oz and production at the top end of 25,000-30,000 oz guidance, TRX Gold generates meaningful cash flow from its low-cost Buckreef operation. The near-zero debt-to-equity ratio (4.5%) and attractive forward P/E suggest the market is pricing in significant risk that the current gold price environment won't persist.
The bear case centers on single-asset concentration risk and structural limitations. TRX Gold's entire revenue depends on one mine (Buckreef) in one country (Tanzania), creating catastrophic risk from operational disruption, regulatory changes, or resource depletion [3]. Despite strong EBITDA, the company reports negative GAAP net income (-16.7% net margin), indicating that depreciation, amortization, and non-cash charges consume operating profits. The $344M market cap and $1.05 share price make this a highly volatile micro-cap stock unsuitable for conservative portfolios. Gold price sensitivity is extreme — a return to $2,000/oz gold would collapse revenues and likely push the company back to operating losses. The absence of dividend payments, short production track record, and limited mine life visibility further constrain the investment case to speculative gold bulls with high risk tolerance.
Key Catalysts
  1. Gold prices above $4,600/oz drive exceptional margins — Q2 FY2026 realized $4,655/oz with 61% gross margin and 59% adjusted EBITDA margin, creating substantial free cash flow generation [1]

Key Risks

  2. Negative GAAP net margin of -16.7% despite strong EBITDA indicates heavy non-cash charges and raises questions about true economic profitability and capital intensity of the mining operation [5]
  3. Extreme gold price sensitivity — the company's profitability is entirely dependent on elevated gold prices ($4,600+/oz), and a normalization toward historical averages would severely compress margins and cash flow
