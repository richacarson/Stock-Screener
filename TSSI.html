Company Profile
Technology ·Information Technology Services ·NASDAQ ·United States
TSS, Inc. (Total Site Solutions) provides data center infrastructure services including AI rack integration, systems deployment, procurement, and facilities management. Based in Georgetown, Texas, the company builds, integrates, and deploys custom high-performance computing infrastructure for data centers. Q2 2026 revenue of $35.1M reflected an intentional shift from low-margin procurement (-45%) to higher-margin systems integration (+46%) and facilities management (+84%). FY2025 revenue was $245.7M (+66%), and FY2026 adjusted EBITDA guidance is reaffirmed at $20-22M (high end). Market cap approximately $239M.

Investment Thesis
TSS is a direct beneficiary of the AI infrastructure buildout, having grown revenue from $30.6M (FY2022) to $245.7M (FY2025) — a 100%+ 3-year CAGR driven by AI rack integration demand [1]. The company is executing an intentional margin-accretive pivot: Q2 2026 systems integration revenue grew 46% to $13.9M (now 40% of mix vs 8% prior year) and facilities management surged 84%, even as lower-margin procurement was deliberately reduced [2]. Management reaffirmed 2026 EBITDA guidance toward the high end of $20-22M and invested $17M in next-gen AI technology to capture the next wave of demand. At $8.46 with trailing P/E of 16.27, D/E of 48.3%, ROE of 32%, and PEG of 0.19, the valuation is attractive on trailing metrics for a company positioned at the center of AI infrastructure spending.
The bear case centers on revenue volatility, insider selling, and forward earnings compression. Q2 2026 revenue of $35.1M (-20% YoY) and forward P/E of 35.25 (vs trailing 16.27) imply a dramatic earnings decline from $0.52 trailing EPS to $0.24 forward, suggesting the procurement transition will compress near-term profitability [4]. Insider selling of $8M+ across the entire C-suite in 2026 — CEO Dewan ($2.4M), COO Marrott ($598K), CFO Chism ($375K), and Director Woodward ($4.76M) — is a significant red flag for a $239M market cap company, contradicting the reaffirmed guidance narrative [4]. Customer concentration remains the primary structural risk — TSS's growth appears driven by a limited number of OEM relationships where a single contract loss would be material. Larger IT infrastructure companies (Dell, Celestica, Flex) could scale AI rack integration competitively, and TSS's rapid growth may attract new entrants to the space. Headcount declined from ~350 to 286, and the company has not been tested through an AI capex downturn.

  2. Insider selling totaling $8M+ across CEO, COO, CFO, and a major director in 2026 is disproportionate to the $239M market cap and contradicts the positive guidance narrative [4]
