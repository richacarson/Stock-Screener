Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NASDAQ ·United States
ServiceTitan provides an end-to-end cloud-based software platform for the trades industry, managing business workflows including advertising, job scheduling, dispatching, estimates, invoicing, payment processing, and inventory management for home and commercial service contractors across HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and related trades. Founded by Ara Mahdessian and Vahe Kuzoyan in 2008, IPO'd in December 2024, and headquartered in Glendale, California. FY2026 revenue reached $961M (up 24.5% YoY) with ~9,500 active customers and $68.5B gross transaction volume.
5,500 Employees ·www.servicetitan.com

Investment Thesis
ServiceTitan at 33x forward earnings with 20.9% revenue growth and dominant market position in the $1.5T trades vertical offers a compelling growth-at-a-reasonable-price opportunity after the stock's 32% decline from its 50-day SMA of $81 to $55 [5]. FY2026 revenue of $961M (up 24.5% YoY) with trailing revenue surpassing $1B demonstrates durable demand, and ~9,500 active customers with high switching costs provide recurring subscription revenue [1][2]. AI is the company's #1 priority, with Titan Intelligence and Pro Products already generating a 20% conversion increase for contractors — the appointment of a CTPO from Figma and Meta signals accelerating product innovation [3]. The nearly debt-free balance sheet (D/E of 3%) provides financial flexibility, and profit margins improved from -23.9% to -12.1% over the past year, suggesting a path to GAAP profitability.
The bear case centers on persistent GAAP unprofitability and post-IPO valuation compression. ServiceTitan reported a -12.1% profit margin with ROE still negative at -8.3%, and while non-GAAP metrics show improvement, significant stock-based compensation flatters operating performance [5]. At 33x forward earnings, any deceleration in growth below 20% could trigger further multiple compression — the stock has already declined from its IPO highs. The trades vertical, while large, is fragmented and price-sensitive, and economic slowdowns in construction and housing directly impact contractor spending on software. ServiceTitan's own S-1 identified AI from Microsoft and OpenAI as a risk factor, acknowledging that LLMs could potentially commoditize core platform functions [4]. Additionally, Glassdoor's 3.3/5 rating with stark departmental disparities (Sales 99% vs. Customer Success 61%) suggests cultural challenges that could affect retention as stock-based compensation declines in value.
Key Catalysts
  1. AI-powered Pro Products driving ARPU expansion with 20% conversion increase for contractor customers — new CTPO from Figma/Meta mandated to accelerate AI product evolution across the ~9,500 customer base [2][3]
  2. Path to GAAP profitability accelerating as profit margin improved from -23.9% to -12.1% and non-GAAP operating income guided to $48-53M, with potential re-rating from growth-at-all-costs to profitable growth SaaS valuation [1][2]
  3. Commercial and industrial vertical expansion opening significantly larger addressable market beyond core residential trades, with FY2027 revenue guidance of $910-920M implying continued 20%+ growth [2]
Key Risks
  1. Persistent GAAP losses with -12.1% profit margin and -8.3% ROE raise questions about sustainable profitability, especially as stock-based compensation remains a major expense excluded from non-GAAP metrics [5]
  2. Housing and construction cycle sensitivity — trade contractor spending on software is discretionary and correlated with residential and commercial construction activity, creating macroeconomic vulnerability
  3. Post-IPO stock decline from $81 SMA50 to $55 current price and insider selling during lockup expirations create shareholder pressure, while ServiceTitan's own S-1 identified LLM commoditization as a risk factor [4][5]
