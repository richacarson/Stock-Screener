Investment Thesis
Tetra Tech delivered strong Q2 2026 results with core net revenue growing 8% YoY (excluding USAID/DOS headwinds), EBITDA margin expanding 90bps, and raised FY2026 guidance to $4.25-4.40B net revenue [1]. Backlog of $4.28B (up 8% sequentially) provides revenue visibility. ROE of 25.64% and D/E of 59.5% demonstrate financial quality. The company is uniquely positioned for secular growth in water infrastructure (aging US systems), PFAS remediation, and climate resilience, with CEO Batrack's 20+ year tenure providing strategic consistency.
The primary risk is USAID/DOS funding uncertainty — federal government spending changes have created a headwind that masks the strong underlying core growth. At 18.06x forward PE and 2.1 PEG, the stock is not cheap for a consulting firm with 10% profit margins. Revenue declined 4.9% YoY on a reported basis due to government contract headwinds, and visibility on the timing of recovery remains uncertain. The consulting business model has inherent limitations on scalability compared to product companies.
Key Catalysts
  1. Core business growing 8% YoY with EBITDA margin expansion of 90bps — the USAID/DOS headwind will eventually anniversary, revealing stronger underlying growth
  2. PFAS remediation and water infrastructure spending represent multi-decade secular tailwinds with $600B+ in US water infrastructure investment needed
  3. Backlog of $4.28B up 8% sequentially provides visibility and signals continued demand strength across government and commercial clients
Key Risks
  1. USAID/DOS federal spending uncertainty creates a drag on reported revenue growth and may persist depending on government budget priorities
  2. PEG of 2.1 suggests the stock may be fairly valued to slightly overvalued relative to its mid-single-digit growth trajectory
  3. Consulting business model limits scalability — margin expansion is constrained by labor intensity and competitive pricing in government contracts
