Company Profile
Technology ·Electronic Gaming & Multimedia ·NASDAQ ·United States
Take-Two Interactive is a global developer, publisher, and marketer of interactive entertainment operating through Rockstar Games (Grand Theft Auto, Red Dead Redemption), 2K (NBA 2K, WWE 2K), and Zynga (mobile gaming). FY2026 net revenue grew 18% to $6.66 billion on $1.58 billion of Q4 net bookings, and the company guided FY2027 net bookings to $8.0-8.2 billion ahead of Grand Theft Auto VI's November 19, 2026 launch, which analysts project could sell 29-85 million units in its first year [1]. Nearly 10,000 of the company's 12,909 employees work in product development, reflecting the scale of investment behind its flagship titles.

Investment Thesis
Take-Two is entering its most significant catalyst window in company history: Grand Theft Auto VI launches November 19, 2026 with 'unprecedented' preorder demand per Variety, and management has guided FY2027 net bookings to $8.0-8.2 billion, with analyst unit estimates ranging from 29 million (Piper Sandler, conservative) to 85 million (Konvoy, bullish) in the first fiscal year [1]. FY2026 revenue already grew 18% to $6.66 billion ahead of launch, and the 14.56% 5-year revenue CAGR reflects a durable, franchise-driven growth engine spanning Rockstar, 2K, and Zynga's mobile portfolio. Zelnick's public refusal to let generative AI shortcut GTA VI's hand-crafted world-building — while quietly using AI for internal efficiency — reflects a defensible AAA creative moat that most analysts do not view as AI-disruptable [2][3].
The bear case is straightforward: this is currently an unprofitable company (TTM ROE -9.04%, profit margin -4.79%) trading at a 33.61x forward P/E and 111% D/E, pricing in a large fraction of the GTA VI opportunity before a single unit has shipped [4]. MoffettNathanson downgraded the stock to Neutral in 2026 citing industry-wide layoff headlines and 'challenged fundamental economics' in gaming, and Take-Two's own abrupt elimination of its AI research team just weeks after publicly embracing the technology raises real questions about strategic coherence and internal trust [3]. If GTA VI's launch disappoints relative to the wide 29-85 million unit range analysts are debating, or if post-launch bookings decay faster than modeled, the stock's premium valuation offers little downside protection. The Inspire Impact Score of -62 (MISALIGNED), driven by the Sexually Explicit attribution common to open-world Rockstar titles, will exclude this name from faith-based portfolios independent of the GTA VI outcome.
