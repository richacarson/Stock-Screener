Company Profile
Technology ·Solar ·NASDAQ ·Spain
Turbo Energy, S.A. is a Valencia, Spain-based maker of solar energy storage systems. Its main products are the SUNBOX all-in-one inverter-and-battery units for homes and businesses and SUNBOX Industry systems (30 kW-2,000 kW). Both are managed by a cloud platform that uses AI to optimize charging and discharging against consumption, power prices, and weather. The company is majority-owned by Umbrella Solar Investment, listed on Nasdaq in September 2023, and is led by CEO Mariano Soria and Chairman Enrique Selva. H1 2026 revenue rose about 173% to €15.0M, driven by a 366 MWh industrial storage project.

Investment Thesis
Turbo Energy is a micro-cap (about $12M market cap) leveraged to European and U.S. demand for behind-the-meter and industrial storage. H1 2026 revenue rose about 173% to €15.0M, operating income turned positive (a €1.7M improvement), and the net loss narrowed to €65,647 from €1.4M [1]. If the 366 MWh industrial project leads to repeat utility-scale orders, this small revenue base could grow quickly, and the stock trades at about 0.5x TTM sales [2][6].
The risks are severe. Growth depends on one large project, gross margin fell to 16.5% from 24.0%, and revenue has swung widely ($31.1M in 2022, $9.4M in 2024) [1][6]. The company received a Nasdaq minimum stockholders' equity deficiency notice in January 2026 and cured it only through dilutive offerings, and Nasdaq warned that a failure at the next periodic report could still lead to delisting [5]. With a share price below $1, it also faces minimum bid price risk, and daily dollar volume barely clears IOWN's $1M liquidity floor.
Key Catalysts
  1. Follow-on utility or industrial storage awards after delivery of the 366 MWh project, which would show H1 2026 growth is repeatable
  2. U.S. distribution expansion of SUNBOX residential and SUNBOX Industry products
  3. Full-year 2026 results showing sustained positive operating income and stockholders' equity above Nasdaq's $2.5M minimum
Key Risks
  1. Nasdaq listing risk: the stockholders' equity deficiency was cured only in June 2026, Nasdaq warned of delisting if compliance lapses at the next periodic report, and a sub-$1 share price adds minimum bid price exposure
  2. Customer and project concentration: H1 2026 growth came mostly from one 366 MWh industrial project, and gross margin fell 750 bps to 16.5%
  3. Dilution and commoditization: repeated 424B5 equity offerings plus price competition from much larger Chinese battery and inverter makers
