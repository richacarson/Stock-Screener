CrossCountry Mortgage completed its acquisition of Two Harbors on August 25, 2026 at $12.00 per share plus a $0.20326 stub period dividend, and the stock was delisted from the NYSE. Investment Thesis
Two Harbors was acquired by CrossCountry Mortgage on August 25, 2026 at $12.00 per share plus a $0.20326 stub period dividend, and the stock has been delisted from the NYSE — there is no remaining independent equity investment opportunity [3]. The final quarters showed improving fundamentals: Q2 2026 net income of $49.4M and Q1 2026 net income of $32.3M represented a material recovery from the $79.1M net loss in the prior year, with book value stabilizing at $10.68/share [1]. The MSR-focused strategy through RoundPoint provided strategic value to CrossCountry, which gains an integrated servicing platform to complement its origination business.
TWO's independent track record was characterized by financial distress and structural challenges that led to the acquisition outcome. D/E of 379% and ROE of -1.19% reflect the fundamental fragility of leveraged mortgage REIT models in volatile interest rate environments [4]. The dividend was cut multiple times since 2020 (5yr CAGR of -10.7%), undermining income reliability for the Dividend sleeve despite an 11.24% trailing yield. The protracted acquisition process — multiple vote delays, competing UWM bid, stockholder lawsuit — created governance uncertainty. As a wholly-owned subsidiary of CrossCountry Mortgage, TWO's future performance will not be independently reported or investable. The Inspire Impact Score of 45 (ALIGNED) was the strongest faith alignment metric, with positive attributions for Employee Wellbeing, Ethical Supply Chain Management, Hiring Ethics, Product Access and Affordability, and Product Sustainability — but this is now academic given the delisting.
