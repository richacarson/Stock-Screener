Company Profile
Real Estate ·REIT - Mortgage ·NYSE ·United States
Two Harbors Investment Corp. is an MSR-focused real estate investment trust that invests in, finances, and manages mortgage servicing rights, residential mortgage-backed securities, and residential mortgage loans. Through its operational platform RoundPoint Mortgage Servicing LLC, it is one of the largest servicers of conventional loans in the U.S. The company has entered into a definitive merger agreement with CrossCountry Mortgage at $12 per share, with the transaction expected to close in H2 2026.

Investment Thesis
TWO offers a near-term arbitrage opportunity with the stock trading at $12.07 versus the CrossCountry Mortgage $12/share acquisition price, plus an 11.28% dividend yield providing income while investors wait for H2 2026 close [1][3]. Q1 2026 showed material improvement with net income of $32.3M versus a $79.1M net loss a year earlier, and book value of $10.57/share provides a floor [1]. The MSR-focused business model through RoundPoint generates stable servicing fee income, and the competing interest from UWM Holdings suggests strategic value in TWO's servicing platform that exceeds the current offer price.
TWO is a financially distressed mortgage REIT with negative ROE (-29.7%), massive leverage (D/E 478.5%), and declining revenue in an environment that has compressed MSR valuations [1][4]. The dividend has been cut multiple times since 2020, with 5-year growth CAGR of -10.7%, undermining income reliability for the Dividend sleeve [4]. The pending CrossCountry acquisition introduces significant execution risk — the shareholder vote has been delayed four times, a stockholder lawsuit alleges proxy disclosure deficiencies, and UWM's competing bid creates regulatory complications [3]. If the deal fails, the stock could decline toward book value of $10.57, a 12% downside. Revenue is expected to decline by 150% per annum on average over the next two years, making standalone viability questionable.
