Company Profile
Healthcare ·Life Sciences Tools & Services ·NASDAQ ·United States
10x Genomics develops and sells instruments, consumables, and software for single-cell and spatial biology research. Its platforms include Chromium (single-cell gene expression, multiome, and immune profiling), Xenium (in situ spatial transcriptomics), and the newly launched Atera spatial platform which has generated extraordinary customer demand with booked orders exceeding the full-year shipment target of ~40 instruments. Founded in 2012 and headquartered in Pleasanton, California, the company serves academic, pharmaceutical, and biotechnology research customers globally. Cited in 10,000+ research papers. FY2026 revenue guidance raised to $610-630M. Approximately 40-50% of revenue is supported by US government funding (NIH).

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience MODERATE
7
AI Risk Protection (7/9): 10x Genomics' core business — designing, manufacturing, and selling physical laboratory instruments and consumables for single-cell and spatial biology — is structurally protected from AI disruption [1][3]. AI cannot replace the physical sample preparation, tissue analysis, or spatial profiling that Chromium, Xenium, and Atera perform. These instruments generate the raw biological data that AI tools subsequently analyze, making 10x a data generation infrastructure provider rather than AI-vulnerable. AI advances in computational biology actually increase demand for high-quality genomics data — more powerful AI models need more training data from instruments like 10x's platforms. Regulatory requirements for laboratory instruments and high switching costs for validated research workflows provide additional defensive moats. However, AI-driven computational methods could potentially reduce the need for certain wet-lab experiments over time. AI Opportunity (0/1): No AI-specific revenue or products identified. The company's tools generate data for downstream AI analysis but 10x itself does not monetize AI capabilities [3].
