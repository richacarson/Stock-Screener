Company Profile
Industrials ·Specialty Business Services ·NYSE ·United States
UL Solutions is a global safety science and certification company providing testing, inspection, certification (TIC), advisory, and training services across industries. The UL mark appears on over 22 billion products worldwide. Founded in 1894 as Underwriters Laboratories, the company completed its IPO on the NYSE in April 2024. Headquartered in Northbrook, Illinois.

Investment Thesis
UL Solutions is a best-in-class TIC company with a 130-year safety mission and irreplaceable brand recognition (UL mark on 22+ billion products). Q2 2026 revenue grew 5.2% to $816M with 6.6% organic growth and adjusted EBITDA margin expansion of 140 bps to 26.8% [1]. The launch of UL 3115 AI Safety Certification positions the company at the intersection of AI proliferation and regulatory demand, creating a growth vector that should accelerate as governments mandate AI product safety standards. D/E of 53% and ROE of 31% demonstrate strong financial health, and the company's regulatory moat — safety certification requirements are embedded in building codes, insurance standards, and product regulations worldwide — provides exceptional revenue durability.
The primary concern is valuation. At a forward P/E of 37.51 and PEG of 3.13, UL Solutions is priced as a high-growth technology company despite delivering 5-7% organic revenue growth — more consistent with a mature services business [6]. The trailing P/E of 51.38 reflects one-time gains inflating GAAP earnings, but even adjusted metrics show premium pricing relative to the TIC peer group. Net margin of 11.94% reflects the labor-intensive service model, and there is limited visibility into how quickly AI safety certification can scale to move the revenue needle on a $3.1B+ base. As a company public for only 2 years, UL Solutions has limited track record of managing quarterly earnings expectations, and insider selling of $6.8M in the last 12 months does not signal insider conviction at current prices [6].
Key Catalysts
  1. AI safety regulation mandates from the EU AI Act and potential US frameworks could drive demand for UL 3115 certification, converting voluntary adoption into required compliance revenue
  2. Adjusted EBITDA margin expansion continuing above 26.8%, driven by operating leverage on organic growth and efficiency improvements across the 14,851-employee global workforce
  3. ESG advisory and assurance practice expansion as CSRD and SEC climate disclosure rules increase demand for third-party verification services
Key Risks
  1. Forward P/E of 37.51 and PEG of 3.13 price UL Solutions for high-growth execution that 5-7% organic revenue growth may not support — any deceleration in organic growth could trigger meaningful de-rating
  2. AI-driven automation of routine inspection and document review workflows could compress margins in traditional TIC services, as the company itself acknowledges in SEC risk factor disclosures
  3. Two-year public company track record provides limited evidence of management's ability to balance quarterly earnings expectations with long-term mission-driven investment

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience MODERATE
7
AI Risk Protection (6/9): UL Solutions' core TIC business involves physical product testing, on-site inspections, and regulatory certification that require laboratory equipment, trained personnel, and physical access to products — creating meaningful barriers to AI substitution [3]. Regulatory requirements for safety certification ensure continued demand for third-party validation that AI alone cannot provide. However, the company's own SEC filings identify 'potential disruption of industries by technological advances in artificial intelligence' as a risk factor, acknowledging that AI could automate portions of document review, data analysis, and routine inspection workflows currently performed by human analysts [1]. The risk is moderate, not existential — the regulatory moat and physical testing requirements protect the core. AI Opportunity (1/1): UL 3115 AI Safety Certification Services launched in November 2025, creating a new revenue stream from evaluating AI-embedded products. As AI proliferates across consumer electronics, automotive, medical devices, and industrial equipment, demand for AI safety certification should grow proportionally with AI adoption [3].
