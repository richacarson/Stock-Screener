Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Upstream Bio, Inc. is a clinical-stage biotechnology company developing verekitug, a fully human monoclonal antibody targeting the thymic stromal lymphopoietin receptor (TSLPR), for the treatment of severe respiratory disorders. Lead candidate verekitug has completed positive Phase 2 trials in CRSwNP (VIBRANT) and severe asthma (VALIANT), with COPD trial (VENTURE) ongoing. Phase 3 expected Q1 2027. Founded 2021, headquartered in Waltham, Massachusetts.
75 Employees ·upstreambio.com

Investment Thesis
Upstream Bio offers high-risk/high-reward exposure to the anti-TSLPR pathway in respiratory disease, with verekitug having delivered positive Phase 2 results in two of three indications — VIBRANT (CRSwNP) and VALIANT (severe asthma, 56% flare-up reduction) [1]. End-of-Phase 2 FDA meetings are planned for mid-2026 in asthma and CRSwNP, with Phase 3 initiation targeted for Q1 2027. With $294.6M in cash providing runway through 2027, the company has sufficient capital to reach Phase 3 initiation without near-term dilution [1]. The stock has fallen 81% from its 52-week high of $33.68 to $6.26, potentially pricing in excessive pessimism relative to the positive Phase 2 data across multiple large indications. Analyst consensus price target of $38.25 implies 511% upside [3]. Inspire score of +28 (ALIGNED) supports the faith-aligned thesis.
The bear case is substantial and binary. The VALIANT asthma data, while showing a statistically significant 56% flare-up reduction, triggered a 47% stock decline, suggesting the market expected stronger efficacy versus Amgen's tezepelumab (Tezspire) which is already generating $1B+ in annual sales with established physician familiarity [1]. Upstream Bio burns approximately $40M per quarter ($160M annualized) with essentially zero product revenue ($3.32M TTM collaboration income), and its $340M market cap represents the market's assessment that verekitug has a modest probability of commercial success [3]. Cash of $294.6M provides runway only through 2027, meaning Phase 3 results must be strong enough to attract a partner or enable non-dilutive financing. Trading volume has declined significantly, with recent 20-day average volume falling well below 100K shares, creating liquidity risk for IOWN-sized positions.
