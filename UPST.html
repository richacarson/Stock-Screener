Company Profile
Financials ·Financial Services ·NASDAQ ·United States
Upstart Holdings operates a cloud-based AI lending marketplace connecting consumer loan demand with bank and credit union partners. The platform uses proprietary ML models assessing 1,600+ variables to evaluate creditworthiness beyond traditional FICO scores, with over 90% of loans fully automated. Upstart serves personal loans, auto refinancing, home equity, and small business lending through 100+ bank partners. Co-founder Paul Gu became CEO in May 2026, succeeding co-founder Dave Girouard who became Executive Chairman [3]. Q2 2026 revenue reached $364.7M with $4.2B in loan originations, marking the company's return to GAAP profitability for the first time since late 2021 [1]. Market cap approximately $2.5B.

Investment Thesis
Upstart is the premier pure-play AI lending platform, delivering 46% YoY revenue growth in H1 2026 and achieving GAAP profitability in Q2 2026 ($17M net income) for the first time since late 2021, with record contribution profit of $193M (+41% QoQ) [1]. Full-year 2026 guidance of ~$1.4B revenue with 21% adjusted EBITDA margin and 35% CAGR target through 2028 provide exceptional growth visibility. At forward P/E of 13.57x and PEG of 0.31, the stock is dramatically undervalued relative to its growth rate [6]. Auto and home loan originations each grew 300%+ and 250%+ YoY respectively, demonstrating multi-product platform expansion [2]. New CEO Paul Gu's $1.38M insider purchase and $6.4M collective insider buying signal leadership conviction during the transition [3]. The national bank charter application could transform unit economics from marketplace fees to net interest income.
The critical risks center on balance sheet leverage and credit cycle sensitivity. D/E of 270% with approximately $2B in debt creates significant financial fragility if a credit downturn contracts origination volumes — as demonstrated in 2022-2023 when revenue collapsed 40% [6]. The company's high beta (~3.5) and extreme price volatility make it unsuitable for risk-averse investors. Upstart's AI model has not been tested through a severe recession, and expansion into auto and home lending increases cyclical credit exposure. The NAACP LDF monitorship found lower approval rates for Black applicants in earlier model versions, creating ongoing fair-lending regulatory risk despite improvements [5]. Competitive risk from traditional banks building in-house AI underwriting, and from other fintechs targeting the same underserved segments, could compress marketplace take rates. Profit margin of only 4.21% provides minimal cushion if growth decelerates [6].
Key Catalysts
  1. Full-year 2026 revenue guidance of ~$1.4B with 21% adjusted EBITDA margin ($294M), following the return to GAAP profitability in Q2 2026 — validates the AI lending model's earnings power after years of losses [1]
  2. National bank charter approval could transform Upstart from a marketplace (earning ~3-4% take rate fees) to a direct lender (earning net interest income), dramatically expanding unit economics and addressable market
  3. Auto and home lending products growing 300%+ and 250%+ YoY respectively, with $4.2B quarterly originations diversifying revenue beyond personal loans [1][2]
Key Risks
  1. D/E of 270% with ~$2B in debt creates acute balance sheet fragility — revenue collapsed 40% in 2022-2023 during the last credit tightening, demonstrating extreme cyclical sensitivity that could recur in a recession [6]
  2. AI model risk in a severe recession: Upstart's 1,600-variable model has never been tested through a deep downturn, and model failure could cause catastrophic default rates on partner bank portfolios [4]
  3. Fair-lending regulatory risk from the NAACP LDF monitorship findings of disparate impact on Black borrowers in earlier model versions, compounded by the bank charter application inviting additional regulatory scrutiny [5]
