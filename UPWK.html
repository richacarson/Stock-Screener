Company Profile
Communication Services ·Internet Content & Information ·NASDAQ ·United States
Upwork operates one of the world's largest online work marketplaces, connecting businesses with independent professionals and agencies across software development, design, marketing, customer service, data science and AI consulting, with offerings spanning Basic, Business Plus, Enterprise and Payroll tiers. In Q3 2026 revenue fell about 8% YoY to $185.6M and gross services volume (GSV) fell 10.1% to $914.1M as active clients declined 10%, while AI-skilled freelancer GSV reached a ~$450M annualized run rate (+55% YoY) and Business Plus GSV rose 132%. The company repaid $361M of convertible notes in Q3 and guides FY2026 revenue to $742-750M. Market cap approximately $1.2B.

Investment Thesis
Upwork is very cheap for a profitable, cash-generative platform: it trades at about 7.4x forward earnings and roughly 6x EV/EBITDA, generated $53.9M of free cash flow in Q3 2026 at a 34.4% adjusted EBITDA margin, and has a near-debt-free balance sheet with $304M of cash and securities after repaying its convertible notes [1][8][9]. The growth pockets are real: AI-skilled freelancer GSV runs at ~$450M annualized (+55% YoY), and Business Plus GSV grew 132% with 40% of its clients spending on Upwork for the first time [1].
The bear case is structural. GSV fell 10.1% and active clients fell 10% in Q3 2026, Q4 guidance of $169-177M implies a further decline, and FY2026 revenue guidance was cut from $760-790M to $742-750M as AI replaces lower-complexity freelance tasks [1][10]. With FMP's forward PEG above 4 because EPS is expected to fall, the low multiple may be a value trap, and leadership churn (a ~25% layoff and the CFO's departure) adds execution risk [1][3][9].
Key Catalysts
  1. AI-skilled freelancer GSV (~$450M run rate, +55% YoY) growing large enough to offset declines in traditional categories
  2. Business Plus scaling (GSV +132%, active clients +154% YoY) into a durable SMB growth engine
  3. Capital returns from a debt-free balance sheet, with $109.7M repurchased in the first nine months of 2026
Key Risks
  1. Continued AI substitution in writing, design, translation and basic coding categories driving double-digit active-client and GSV declines
  2. Revenue guidance cuts (FY2026 lowered to $742-750M) and Q4 2026 guidance of $169-177M signaling an accelerating top-line decline
  3. Leadership and organizational instability after a ~25% layoff and the CFO transition to Peter Sanborn on January 1, 2027
