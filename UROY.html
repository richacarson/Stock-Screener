Company Profile
Energy ·Uranium ·NASDAQ ·Canada
Uranium Royalty Corp. is the world's only pure-play uranium royalty and streaming company, providing capital to uranium mining operations in exchange for royalty interests on future production. Headquartered in Vancouver, Canada, the company holds a diversified portfolio of royalty and streaming interests across uranium projects in the Americas, Africa, and Australia. In 2026, the company completed a transformative combination with Sweetwater Royalties (US$1.1B attributable equity value) expected to triple EBITDA and diversify into trona and critical minerals [1]. Q3 FY2026 revenue was CA$16.7M with net income of CA$1.96M, marking the company's transition to profitability.

Investment Thesis
Uranium Royalty Corp. offers unique exposure to the nuclear energy renaissance through the only pure-play uranium royalty model, with a pristine balance sheet (zero debt), forward PE of 12.37 reflecting expected earnings transformation from the Sweetwater combination, and structural tailwinds from AI data center power demand driving nuclear energy adoption [1][2]. The Sweetwater deal is expected to triple EBITDA, diversify into trona and critical minerals, and create a $1.9B enterprise value platform. Revenue has grown from near-zero to $40.25M TTM with profit margin reaching 21.53%, and Q3 FY2026 marked the transition to profitability with CA$1.96M in net income [4]. The royalty model provides operational leverage without the cost and execution risk of mine operations.
The bear case centers on execution risk of the Sweetwater combination, which at $1.1B attributable equity value represents a transformative but high-stakes bet for a ~$531M market cap company [1]. The trailing PE of 164.33 highlights that current profitability is minimal, and the forward PE of 12.37 relies heavily on the deal closing and delivering expected synergies — any delay or integration challenge would severely impair the investment thesis. Revenue is expected to decline 89% per annum on average over the next 3 years according to analyst forecasts, suggesting the current revenue level may be unsustainable without the Sweetwater deal [4]. The board lacks experienced directors (5 of 6 joined within the past 3 years), ROE of just 1.29% reflects early-stage economics, and uranium price volatility and nuclear regulatory risk add commodity and political exposure. Insider selling of $77K over the past 3 months with no offsetting purchases provides no insider conviction signal.

AI Resilience LOW RISK

AI Risk Protection (9/9): Uranium royalties are structurally immune to AI disruption. The business involves physical commodity extraction, geological resource development, and contractual royalty streams tied to mine production volumes that AI cannot replicate or substitute [2]. Nuclear power generation requires physical uranium fuel with no technological alternative. Furthermore, AI data center proliferation is a direct tailwind for nuclear energy demand — hyperscale computing requires massive, reliable baseload power that nuclear uniquely provides, increasing uranium demand and potentially elevating royalty revenues [1]. The royalty model's simplicity (contractual cash flows from physical production) makes it one of the most AI-resistant business structures in public markets. The benefit from AI is entirely indirect, through increased electricity demand driving nuclear power expansion and uranium prices.
