Company Profile
Financial Services ·Banks - Regional ·NYSE ·United States
U.S. Bancorp is the parent company of U.S. Bank, the fifth-largest commercial bank in the United States with $680B+ in assets. The company provides a diversified mix of consumer and commercial banking, payments, wealth management, and corporate trust services across 26 states. Under CEO Gunjan Kedia (first woman to lead a top-5 US bank, elevated April 2025), USB posted record Q2 2026 revenue of $7.71B (+10.1% YoY) with adjusted EPS of $1.35 (+21.6%), NII of $4.4B (+7.5%), and fee income up 13.2%. The BTIG acquisition closed June 1, 2026, and full-year guidance was raised to 7-9% revenue growth. Founded in 1863, headquartered in Minneapolis, Minnesota.

Investment Thesis
U.S. Bancorp delivered a record Q2 2026 with revenue of $7.71B (+10.1% YoY), adjusted EPS of $1.35 (+21.6%), NII of $4.4B (+7.5%), and fee income up 13.2%, prompting management to raise full-year guidance to 7-9% revenue growth [1]. The BTIG acquisition closed June 1, adding capital markets capabilities with ~$200M expected quarterly revenue contribution in H2 2026. At 9.8x forward earnings with a 3.80% dividend yield, $2.16 annualized payout, and 40+ consecutive years of dividend payments, USB offers attractive income-plus-growth for a diversified $680B+ banking franchise [5]. The Amazon small business lending partnership launching Q3 2026 ($75-85M projected quarterly revenue) adds a credible new growth vector in embedded finance. CEO Kedia's appointment as the first woman to lead a top-5 US bank and the deliberate succession from Cecere reflect institutional leadership maturity.
The primary risk is USB's position as a mid-tier player in an industry where scale advantages are accelerating — JPMorgan and Bank of America can outspend USB on technology by 5-10x, potentially creating a widening gap in AI-driven efficiency and customer experience [2]. ROE of 12.62% remains below the 15% cost-of-equity benchmark, suggesting Union Bank integration synergies have not fully materialized despite the NIM expansion to 2.79% from 2.66% [5]. Net insider selling exceeded $9M across CEO Kedia and four other executives in 2026, raising questions about management conviction at current levels — all transactions were option exercises and sales with zero open-market purchases [5]. The 5-year dividend growth CAGR of 4.8% barely exceeds inflation, limiting total return for income investors seeking real payout growth. The Inspire Impact Score of -67 (MISALIGNED) due to Abortion Activism and LGBT Activism negative attributions limits allocation for faith-based portfolios despite 15 positive ESG attributions. The stock's 11% decline from $63.81 to $56.89 since August may reflect pre-Q3 earnings positioning and concerns about commercial real estate exposure in the Union Bank portfolio [1].
