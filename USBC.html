USBC, Inc. (formerly Know Labs, Inc.) is a micro-cap company that pivoted in August 2025 from non-invasive health monitoring technology to digital financial services following a $125M strategic acquisition by Greg Kidd's Goldeneye 1995 LLC. The company develops the USBC deposit token — a blockchain-based tokenized deposit embedded with digital identity — and maintains a Bitcoin treasury strategy. It completed divestiture of legacy sensor technology in April 2026 and finalized strategic partnerships with Uphold and Vast Bank.

Investment Thesis
USBC, Inc. is a speculative micro-cap ($132M market cap) that completed its corporate transformation by divesting legacy sensor technology in April 2026 and finalizing strategic partnerships with Uphold and Vast Bank for its tokenized deposit initiative [2][4]. CEO Greg Kidd's $125M acquisition via Goldeneye 1995 LLC and his credentials as former Ripple Chief Risk Officer provide credibility in the digital assets space. The company regained NYSE American listing compliance in March 2026, and the USBC token concept — a blockchain-based tokenized deposit embedded with digital identity — addresses a growing market for regulated digital dollar solutions. D/E of 20.7% with $72.3M in total assets against $12.4M in liabilities suggests manageable financial structure [5].
The bear case remains overwhelming for any fundamental investor. USBC generates zero revenue and lost $27.5M in the trailing twelve months (up from $22.1M in FY2025), with management explicitly stating it expects to continue incurring operating losses for the foreseeable future [5]. The liquidity floor test fails at $151K daily dollar volume ($407K shares x $0.37) — significantly worse than April's $426K — making the stock unsuitable for institutional investment. The company has no commercial product, no customers, and no revenue visibility. R&D spending collapsed and the sensor tech was divested, leaving USBC with no proprietary technology. Share dilution continues with outstanding shares at 384M. The Bitcoin treasury strategy introduces cryptocurrency volatility, and the entire investment thesis depends on the tokenized deposit concept eventually generating revenue — an outcome with zero evidence to support it.
