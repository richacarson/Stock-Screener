USBC, Inc. (formerly Know Labs, Inc.) is a micro-cap company that pivoted in August 2025 from non-invasive health monitoring technology to digital financial services following a $125M strategic acquisition by Greg Kidd's Goldeneye 1995 LLC. The company develops the USBC deposit token — a blockchain-based tokenized deposit embedded with digital identity — and maintains a Bitcoin treasury strategy. It completed divestiture of legacy sensor technology in March 2026 and finalized strategic partnerships with Uphold and Vast Bank. Greg Kidd holds a 92.2% controlling interest. Market cap approximately $198M.

Investment Thesis
USBC, Inc. is a speculative micro-cap ($198M market cap) that completed its corporate transformation by divesting legacy sensor technology in March 2026 and pursuing tokenized deposit solutions through partnerships with Uphold and Vast Bank [2][3]. CEO Greg Kidd's $125M acquisition via Goldeneye 1995 LLC and his background as former Ripple Chief Risk Officer provide credibility in the digital assets space. The USBC token concept — a blockchain-based tokenized deposit embedded with digital identity that pays high-yield rewards — addresses a growing market for regulated digital dollar solutions. Stock price has recovered from lows of $0.29 to $0.507, and D/E of 32.7% indicates manageable leverage structure.
The bear case remains overwhelming for any fundamental investor. USBC generates effectively zero revenue and burns an estimated $25M+ annually, with management explicitly expecting continued operating losses for the foreseeable future [3]. The liquidity floor test fails at $160K daily dollar volume (316K shares x $0.507), making the stock unsuitable for institutional investment. The company has no commercial product, no customers, no revenue visibility, and no proprietary technology after the sensor tech divestiture. With only 16 employees and $3M cash against $15.2M debt, the company is entirely dependent on external financing or Bitcoin treasury appreciation to fund operations [4]. Goldeneye 1995 LLC's 92.2% controlling interest creates total governance concentration, and the share structure makes the stock illiquid. The entire investment thesis depends on the tokenized deposit concept eventually generating revenue — an outcome with zero evidence to support it.
Key Catalysts
  1. Launch of the USBC deposit token as a commercial product through the Uphold and Vast Bank partnerships, generating first revenue and validating the $125M acquisition thesis [2]

  3. Greg Kidd's industry connections from Ripple could accelerate additional banking partnerships for tokenized deposit distribution beyond the initial Uphold/Vast Bank arrangement [1]
Key Risks
  1. Zero revenue with estimated $25M+ annual losses and only 16 employees — a pre-revenue speculative vehicle with no proven business model and management-acknowledged expectation of continued losses [3][4]

  3. No proprietary technology after sensor tech divestiture and 92.2% ownership concentration by Goldeneye 1995 LLC create both IP vulnerability and governance risk — the token concept has no IP protection against well-funded competitors [3]
