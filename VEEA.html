Company Profile
Technology ·Edge Computing & AI Infrastructure ·NASDAQ ·United States
Veea Inc. is an edge computing and Edge AI company offering the VeeaONE hyperconverged platform that integrates connectivity, computing, storage, and cybersecurity at the network edge. Products include SecureConnect (AI-enabled security for SMBs), AirLynx (wireless connectivity), and MetaLynx (immersive technology). Founded in 2014 with 123 patents across edge computing domains, recognized by Gartner for edge computing innovation. Despite 12 years of operation, TTM revenue is only $388K with a $8.7M market cap, placing the company firmly in pre-commercial stage.

Investment Thesis
Veea holds 123 patents across edge computing domains and is positioned in a market projected to grow at 36.9% CAGR through 2030 [3]. The VeeaONE platform's integration of AI, cybersecurity, connectivity, and storage into hyperconverged edge devices addresses a genuine enterprise need — particularly SecureConnect for SMB cybersecurity (43% of cyberattacks target small businesses) [2]. The company recently strengthened its executive team with commercial and operational leaders, signaling intent to accelerate from R&D to revenue [1]. At $8.71M market cap, the stock is priced for potential extinction, meaning any commercial traction could produce outsized percentage returns. Revenue grew 56.6% in 2025 (from $142K to $222K) and appears to be accelerating in 2026 with $388K TTM [3].
The bear case is overwhelming. After 12 years of operation, Veea has generated only $388K in TTM revenue — orders of magnitude below what is needed to sustain a 45-person company [3]. The $15.63M annual net loss is nearly twice the entire $8.71M market cap, indicating the company is burning through capital at an unsustainable rate. The stock has collapsed 91% from its 52-week high of $1.86 to $0.17, and daily dollar volume of ~$75K makes the stock effectively untradeable for any institutional investor. The edge computing market is dominated by well-funded hyperscalers (AWS, Microsoft, NVIDIA) that can deploy edge AI solutions at massively greater scale with existing customer relationships. Veea's patent portfolio has value, but patents without commercial traction are defensive assets, not revenue generators. Continued operation likely requires dilutive capital raises that would further destroy shareholder value. The company fails the IOWN liquidity floor, resulting in an Infrastructure score of 0 — this stock is not investable at current trading volumes.
