Twin Vee PowerCats Co. designs, manufactures, and sells power catamaran boats ranging from 21 to 41 feet, including the Bahama Boat Works brand. Based in Fort Pierce, Florida, Twin Vee specializes in dual-hull catamaran designs known for stability and fuel efficiency. Led by CEO/Chairman Joseph Visconti. The company completed a 1-for-37 reverse stock split in May 2026 to maintain Nasdaq listing compliance [5]. Q1 2026 revenue was $3.96M (+10% YoY) but gross margin fell to 5% with a $2.09M net loss, and management disclosed substantial going concern doubt [1][2]. In July 2026, Twin Vee announced a merger agreement with USFM Corporation to privatize its boating operations [3]. Market cap approximately $7.3M.
