Company Profile
Technology ·Autonomous Retail Technology ·NASDAQ ·United States
VenHub Global, Inc. develops and operates fully autonomous, AI-powered robotic convenience stores (Smart Stores) that operate 24/7 without employees. Stores use two robotic arms to dispense snacks, beverages, and packaged goods, managed remotely via AI-powered inventory management. VenHub targets retail, healthcare, government, military, and sports venue customers with fixed and mobile deployment models. The company went public via direct listing on NASDAQ in January 2026, has 24 pending patent applications, and disclosed substantial doubt about its ability to continue as a going concern. Founded in 2023, headquartered in Las Vegas, Nevada.
27 Employees ·www.venhub.com

At $0.85 per share ($83M market cap), any successful commercial deployment or strategic partnership could generate outsized percentage returns from a deeply depressed base.
