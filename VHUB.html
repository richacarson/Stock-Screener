Company Profile
Technology ·Autonomous Retail Technology ·NASDAQ ·United States
VenHub Global, Inc. develops and operates fully autonomous, AI-powered robotic convenience stores (Smart Stores) that operate 24/7 without employees. The company deploys fixed and mobile retail units using advanced artificial intelligence, smart inventory management, and robotic fulfillment systems. Smart Stores can be built for $250K-$400K and deployed within days. VenHub serves retail businesses, healthcare, government, military, and sports venue customers. Founded in 2023 by Shahan and Shoushana Ohanessian, IPO'd January 2026, headquartered in Las Vegas, Nevada.
50 Employees ·www.venhub.com

Investment Thesis
VenHub Global represents a speculative bet on the autonomous retail revolution, offering AI-powered robotic convenience stores that operate 24/7 without employees at a build cost of $250K-$400K per unit [2]. The company expanded its IP portfolio in March 2026 and appointed experienced advisory board members, including AI and energy sector executives [2][3]. With a $93M market cap and stock price of $1.11 (down 85% from IPO), any successful commercial deployment or partnership could generate significant percentage returns from a deeply depressed base. All-in on a future where retail labor shortages and rising wages make autonomous stores increasingly compelling.
VenHub is a pre-revenue startup with catastrophic financial metrics that fails nearly every IOWN quality screen. Revenue of $864K against a $51M annual loss means the company burns $60 for every $1 earned [1]. The balance sheet shows negative equity ($4.61M assets vs. $12.32M liabilities) and only $89K in cash before the February 2026 dilutive private placement that raised $18.9M [4]. Cash runway extends only into approximately H2 2027, creating existential funding risk. The stock has lost 85% since its January 2026 IPO, and the private placement at $2.45 per share (now trading at $1.11) represents further dilution for existing shareholders. VenHub competes in concept against Amazon Go, backed by trillion-dollar resources, and has no demonstrated competitive moat. This is not an investment -- it is a highly speculative venture bet with a high probability of total capital loss.
