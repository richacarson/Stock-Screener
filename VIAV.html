Company Profile
Technology ·Scientific & Technical Instruments ·NASDAQ ·United States
VIAV Solutions provides network test, measurement, and assurance solutions for communications service providers, enterprises, network equipment manufacturers, government, and avionics. The company operates through two segments: Network Enablement (NSE), which offers fiber optic, wireless, and data center testing equipment, and Optical Security and Performance Products (OSP), which produces anti-counterfeiting technology for currency and government documents plus 3D sensing components. VIAV acquired Spirent's high-speed ethernet, network security, and channel emulation testing business from Keysight for $410M in 2025, expanding its data center and AI infrastructure testing capabilities. FY2026 revenue reached $1.52B (+40% YoY), driven by data center ecosystem and aerospace/defense demand.

Investment Thesis
VIAV is a turnaround story firing on all cylinders — FY2026 revenue surged 40% to $1.52B with Q4 revenue of $443.1M exceeding guidance, non-GAAP operating margin expanded 630bps to 20.6%, and non-GAAP EPS more than doubled to $1.00 from $0.47 [1]. The $410M Spirent acquisition has been successfully integrated, driving NSE segment revenue growth of 69.2% YoY as AI data center buildout creates structural demand for high-speed ethernet testing. The company occupies a strategic position in the AI infrastructure value chain — every hyperscaler data center requires network testing — and secured recognition in NVIDIA's AI reference architecture [2]. Forward P/E of 23.3x on consensus FY2027 EPS of $1.22 represents reasonable valuation for 23% expected revenue growth, and Q1 FY2027 guidance of $450-460M with 27.1% operating margin signals continued momentum.
The bear case centers on GAAP profitability gaps, cultural deterioration, and cyclical risk. Despite strong non-GAAP results, GAAP profit margin remains negative at -2.0% due to heavy acquisition-related amortization, meaning reported earnings materially overstate economic profitability [6]. CEO Khaykin's $22M+ in insider sales during 2026 — including $13.4M in a single day — raises alignment concerns despite the strong operational narrative [5]. Glassdoor reviews describe a deteriorating workplace culture with time-tracking and management turnover, which threatens the talent retention critical for a technology company [4]. The 40% revenue growth is heavily acquisition-inflated; organic growth was more modest and the 5-year revenue CAGR of just 4.8% reflects the deep FY2023-2024 downcycle. Network test equipment demand is cyclical and tied to capex spending by service providers and hyperscalers — any pullback in AI data center investment would disproportionately impact VIAV's growth trajectory. The stock's 244% 52-week price increase prices in significant continued execution.
