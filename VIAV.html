Company Profile
Technology ·Communication Equipment ·NMS ·United States
Viavi Solutions Inc. provides network test, measurement, and assurance solutions through its NSE segment, and optical security and performance products through its OSP segment, serving military, aerospace, telecom, data center, and anti-counterfeiting markets globally. Founded in 1923 (formerly JDS Uniphase), the company posted FY2026 revenue of $1.518B (+40% YoY), with Q4 FY2026 revenue reaching $443.1M (+52.5% YoY) — above the high end of guidance — driven by strong data center and aerospace/defense demand plus the Spirent Communications integration. Non-GAAP operating margin expanded to 24.0% in Q4, up 960bps YoY. The company guided Q1 FY2027 revenue of $450-460M, ahead of consensus.

Investment Thesis
Viavi Solutions at $34.73 with an $8.5B market cap is a technology infrastructure play riding the AI and data center build-out cycle — FY2026 revenue surged 40% to $1.518B, with Q4 revenue of $443.1M (+52.5% YoY) exceeding the high end of guidance, driven by the Spirent integration and strong data center and aerospace/defense demand [1][2]. Non-GAAP operating margin expanded 960bps to 24.0% in Q4, demonstrating improving profitability as scale benefits materialize. The company guided Q1 FY2027 revenue of $450-460M (versus $433M consensus), and management accelerated the $500M quarterly revenue milestone from FY2028 to CY2027, signaling sustained momentum [3]. With a forward P/E of 17.8 and PEG of 1.34, valuation is reasonable for a company with 50%+ revenue growth and a structural position as a picks-and-shovels supplier to AI infrastructure.
The bear case centers on sustainability of growth and integration risk. The 52.5% YoY revenue growth includes the Spirent acquisition, making organic growth rates significantly lower — possibly mid-single-digits based on the pre-acquisition revenue base [1][2]. Trailing GAAP profitability remains negative (ROE -2.7%, profit margin -2.0%), and the stock has declined 42% from its 52-week high of $60.43 to $34.73, suggesting the market sees deceleration ahead. Data center capex cycles are inherently volatile — if hyperscaler AI spending slows or shifts to inference-optimized architectures requiring less network testing, Viavi's growth tailwind could reverse quickly. The OSP segment ($89.2M quarterly) faces more modest growth prospects tied to government anti-counterfeiting contracts and automotive 3D sensing. Competition from Keysight Technologies ($5.6B revenue) in network test provides a well-resourced rival, and Viavi's GAAP losses amid record revenue raise questions about when the Spirent integration costs will fully recede [3][4].
Key Catalysts
  1. AI-driven data center build-out creating sustained demand for network test and measurement equipment — management pulled forward the $500M quarterly revenue milestone to CY2027 from FY2028, implying continued strong order intake from hyperscalers [1][3]
  2. Spirent integration delivering above-guidance financial results with 960bps non-GAAP margin expansion in Q4, suggesting revenue and cost synergies are ahead of plan and GAAP profitability inflection is approaching [1][2]
  3. Forward P/E of 17.8 and PEG of 1.34 represent attractive valuation for a company with structural AI tailwinds, offering potential re-rating if GAAP profitability turns positive and growth sustains in the mid-teens organically [3]
Key Risks
  1. Acquisition-inflated growth rates — the 52.5% YoY revenue growth includes Spirent, and organic growth is likely mid-single-digits; as the acquisition anniversary approaches, reported growth will decelerate materially, potentially disappointing investors expecting continued 40%+ growth [1][2]
  2. Data center capex cycle dependency — hyperscaler AI spending is inherently lumpy, and any slowdown in cloud infrastructure investment would directly reduce demand for Viavi's network test equipment [3]
  3. Persistent GAAP losses despite record revenue (trailing profit margin -2.0%, ROE -2.7%) raise execution concerns about when Spirent integration costs will recede and sustainable profitability will emerge at scale [1][4]
