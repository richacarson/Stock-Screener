Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Vir Biotechnology is a San Francisco-based clinical-stage immunology company developing treatments for chronic hepatitis delta (CHD) and dual-masked T-cell engagers (TCEs) for solid tumors. It previously commercialized the COVID-19 antibody sotrovimab, which generated over $2.7B of revenue in 2021-2022. Its lead program, the elebsiran plus tobevibart combination, achieved undetectable HDV RNA in 88% of Phase 2 SOLSTICE participants at Week 96, and the fully enrolled Phase 3 ECLIPSE program has topline data expected in 1Q 2027. Q2 2026 revenue was $238.9M, mostly from a $240M upfront payment from Astellas for VIR-5500 (PSMA TCE), and cash and investments were about $1.01B. Q3 2026 results are scheduled for November 4, 2026. Market cap approximately $1.9B.

Investment Thesis
Vir has two late-stage value drivers. In chronic hepatitis delta, a severe liver disease with limited options, elebsiran plus tobevibart achieved undetectable HDV RNA in 88% of Phase 2 SOLSTICE patients at Week 96, and fully enrolled Phase 3 ECLIPSE trials are expected to read out in 1Q 2027 [2][4]. Its dual-masked T-cell engager platform received external validation through Astellas' $240M upfront for VIR-5500, which drove Q2 2026 revenue of $238.9M and net income of $80.1M, and about $1.01B of cash funds operations through key readouts [4].
The bear case is binary clinical risk with no commercial revenue base: FY2025 net loss was $438M, the forward P/E is negative, and the 5-year revenue CAGR is negative once the sotrovimab windfall is excluded [1][10]. A negative ECLIPSE readout would remove most of the near-term value, and masked TCEs in HER2 and PSMA face crowded competition. Q3 2026 results (due November 4, 2026) will show cash burn without the one-time Astellas payment [13].
Key Catalysts
  1. Phase 3 ECLIPSE 1, 2 and 3 topline data in chronic hepatitis delta, expected 1Q 2027
  2. Updated VIR-5818 (HER2 dual-masked TCE) Phase 1 dose-escalation data in 2H 2026
  3. Further milestones or partnerships for the PRO-XTEN masked TCE platform following the $240M Astellas VIR-5500 deal
Key Risks
  1. Binary Phase 3 risk: a failed or mixed ECLIPSE readout would undermine the lead hepatitis delta franchise
  2. Cash burn of roughly $400M+ per year without recurring product revenue, risking dilution if the pipeline slips
  3. Competitive pressure in T-cell engagers for PSMA and HER2 targets and from established hepatitis delta therapies
