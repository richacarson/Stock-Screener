Company Profile
Consumer Cyclical ·Furnishings, Fixtures & Appliances ·NASDAQ ·United States
Virco Manufacturing Corporation designs, manufactures, and distributes furniture for the education market, primarily serving preschool through university institutions across the United States. The company operates manufacturing facilities in Torrance, California and Conway, Arkansas, producing classroom desks, chairs, tables, storage, and cafeteria furniture under the Virco and Equipment for Educators brands. Founded in 1950, Virco is the largest domestic manufacturer of furniture for the educational market. FY2026 (Jan 2026) revenue was $199.7M (-25% YoY) with $2.6M net income.
