Company Profile
Consumer Cyclical ·Furnishings, Fixtures & Appliances ·NASDAQ ·United States
Virco Manufacturing Corporation designs, manufactures, and distributes furniture for the education market, primarily serving preschool through university institutions across the United States and Canada. The company operates a vertically integrated manufacturing campus in Conway, Arkansas with over 2 million square feet of production and warehouse space, producing classroom desks, chairs, tables, storage, and cafeteria furniture. Founded in 1950, Virco is the largest domestic manufacturer of furniture for the educational market. FY2026 (Jan 2026) revenue was $199.7M (-25% YoY) with $2.6M net income; H1 FY2027 revenue declined 6% to $118M with backlog up 13% YoY.
