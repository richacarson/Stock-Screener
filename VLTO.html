Company Profile
Industrials ·Specialty Industrial Machinery ·NYSE ·United States
Veralto Corporation is a global leader in essential technology solutions that monitor, enhance, and protect the world's most vital resources. The Water Quality segment (Hach, ChemTreat, Trojan Technologies) provides analytical instruments, chemical treatment solutions, and UV disinfection systems for municipal and industrial water applications. The Product Quality & Innovation segment (Videojet, X-Rite, Pantone, Linx, FOSS) delivers marking, coding, and color management solutions. Spun off from Danaher Corporation in September 2023, headquartered in Waltham, Massachusetts.
16,000 Employees ·www.veralto.com

Investment Thesis
Veralto is a high-quality industrial compounder with category-leading positions in water quality (Hach #1 globally) and product marking/coding (Videojet #1 globally), supported by the Veralto Enterprise System continuous improvement culture inherited from Danaher [2]. FY2025 revenue of $5.50B grew 6.0% with earnings up 12.85% to $940M, and Q1 2026 continued the momentum with 6.7% revenue growth and 13% adjusted EPS growth, prompting a raised full-year outlook [1]. The AAA MSCI ESG rating, 'Safeguarding the World's Most Vital Resources' mission, and AI-enabled food safety acquisitions (TraceGains, GlobalVision) position the company at the intersection of secular growth themes: clean water infrastructure, food safety regulation, and AI-driven quality automation [3][4]. At $91.54 with a forward P/E of 21.11, Veralto offers a quality compounding story with defensive end markets and regulatory tailwinds.
The primary concern is valuation relative to growth. At 21.11x forward earnings and PEG of 2.35, Veralto is priced as a premium quality compounder despite only 6% revenue growth -- the stock needs sustained mid-single-digit organic growth plus margin expansion to justify the multiple [6]. Revenue growth of 6.0% is solid but below the 10% target for top infrastructure scores, and the company has not demonstrated whether it can accelerate organic growth beyond the mid-single-digit range without acquisitions. D/E of 93% is elevated from Danaher separation debt and acquisition financing, limiting financial flexibility. As a newly independent company (since September 2023), Veralto has not been tested through a recession or market downturn as a standalone entity, and the VES culture -- while proven at Danaher -- is still being validated under independent leadership. Insider selling without offsetting purchases raises questions about management's conviction at current valuation levels [6].

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
9
AI Risk Protection (8/9): Veralto's core businesses are fundamentally immune to AI disruption because they operate in the physical world. Water Quality (Hach, ChemTreat, Trojan Technologies) involves physical analytical instruments, chemical treatment of water, and UV disinfection systems -- AI cannot substitute for the physical chemistry of water treatment or the hardware needed to measure water quality parameters [2]. Product Quality & Innovation (Videojet, X-Rite, Linx) involves physical marking, coding, and printing on physical products -- every consumer product needs a physical barcode, date code, or lot number applied by physical equipment. AI cannot print on a cereal box. Pantone's color standard is an industry-standard physical reference that AI tools must reference, not replace. Furthermore, AI-driven automation in manufacturing and food production increases the need for quality monitoring (Hach, FOSS) and marking/coding (Videojet) on automated production lines. Regulatory requirements for water quality testing and product labeling are structural, not discretionary. No analyst has flagged AI as a substitution risk for any of Veralto's business lines. AI Opportunity (1/1): Veralto generates AI-related revenue through its TraceGains platform, which provides AI-enabled packaging and label compliance solutions to food and beverage customers [3]. The AI capabilities enhance the existing product portfolio and create upselling opportunities across the installed base.
