Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Vanda Pharmaceuticals is a biopharmaceutical company focused on developing and commercializing therapies for high unmet medical needs. Commercial portfolio includes Fanapt (schizophrenia/bipolar I), HETLIOZ (non-24-hour sleep-wake disorder), PONVORY (relapsing MS), NEREUS (tradipitant, motion-induced vomiting), and the newly FDA-approved BYSANTI (milsaperidone). Founded in 2003 by CEO Mihael Polymeropoulos, MD, headquartered in Washington, D.C. Market cap approximately $349M.
533 Employees ·www.vandapharma.com

Investment Thesis
Vanda is executing an aggressive commercial transformation with five FDA-approved products, raising FY2026 revenue guidance to $240-290M including $10-30M from newly launched NEREUS [2]. Fanapt posted breakout Q2 2026 growth of 23% YoY to $36M with total prescriptions up 31%, and the BYSANTI approval adds a fifth revenue stream [3]. The tradipitant platform has meaningful upside if the GLP-1 nausea prevention indication succeeds, addressing a multi-billion-dollar market. Minimal debt (D/E 3.0%) provides balance sheet safety, and $170M cash funds operations through at least year-end 2027 [6]. The Inspire Impact score of 42 reflects strong alignment across customer welfare, data security, and ethical practices.
The bear case is severe: Vanda is burning approximately $60M per quarter with Q2 2026 net loss of $62.5M on just $50.5M in product sales, implying only 6-7 quarters of cash runway before dilutive financing becomes necessary [3]. HETLIOZ collapsed 66% YoY to $5.6M in Q2, removing a previously stable revenue pillar faster than new products can compensate [3]. Governance is a material concern — CEO Polymeropoulos has installed three family members in key roles, Glassdoor reviews describe innovation suppression, and insiders sold $2.8M net over the past year including $1.3M by the CEO at $8.17/share while the stock now trades at $5.80 [4][5]. ROE of -60.5% and profit margin of -124% reflect sustained equity erosion, and the 5-year revenue CAGR of -1.5% shows the business has actually shrunk over time despite pipeline investment. At $349M market cap the valuation appears cheap, but the combination of cash burn, governance risk, and execution complexity across five simultaneous commercial products with only 533 employees makes the risk/reward asymmetric to the downside.

AI Risk Protection (7/9): Vanda's core business — FDA-approved small-molecule drugs for CNS and rare diseases — is protected by the most heavily regulated commercial process in the U.S. economy [1].
