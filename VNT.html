Company Profile
Technology ·Scientific & Technical Instruments ·NYSE ·United States
Vontier Corporation is a global mobility ecosystem company spun off from Fortive (originally Danaher) in October 2020. The company operates through three segments: Mobility Technologies (convenience retail operating platforms, point-of-sale/payment, EV charging software), Repair Solutions (Matco Tools automotive diagnostic equipment and tool franchise network), and Environmental and Fueling Solutions (Gilbarco fuel dispensers, Veeder-Root environmental monitoring). Vontier posted FY2025 revenue of $3.08B (+3.2% YoY) with adjusted EPS growth of 11%, and initiated 2026 guidance of $3.10-3.15B with 80bps operating margin expansion. CEO Mark Morelli leads the company from Raleigh, NC.
7,800 Employees ·www.vontier.com

Investment Thesis
Vontier at 8.29x forward earnings and 0.77x PEG offers compelling value for a Danaher-pedigreed industrial technology company executing a software-enabled mobility transformation. FY2025 revenue of $3.08B grew 3.2% with adjusted EPS growth of 11%, and 2026 guidance targets $3.10-3.15B with 80bps operating margin expansion [1]. ROE of 28.9% reflects efficient capital deployment, and the Gilbarco Veeder-Root franchise holds a leading global position in fuel dispensing and environmental monitoring with a massive installed base generating recurring aftermarket revenue. The appointment of a Chief Innovation and Growth Officer in February 2026 to drive Industrial AI and digital modernization signals acceleration of the software pivot [2]. The EV transition, rather than being solely a threat to petroleum fueling, creates new revenue opportunities through EV charging network software and alternative fuel dispensing.
The bear case centers on low organic growth, EV transition headwinds, and leverage. Revenue growth of approximately 3% (with Q2 2026 core sales declining 0.2%) is well below the growth premium implied by Vontier's technology positioning — the company trades more like a mature industrial than a growth platform [1]. D/E of 161% reflects spinoff-related leverage that constrains financial flexibility and limits the capacity for transformative acquisitions or accelerated investment. Trailing EPS declined 68.3% and GAAP earnings have been volatile, making the forward P/E of 8.29x potentially misleading if earnings normalization is less robust than consensus expects [4]. The long-term secular decline of petroleum fueling volumes as the global vehicle fleet electrifies threatens the core Gilbarco revenue base, and the EV charging software business is subscale relative to competitors like ChargePoint, Blink, and major cloud platforms. The Inspire Impact Score of -49 (MISALIGNED) limits faith-based investor allocation. The tiny 0.32% dividend yield provides minimal income support during any share price weakness.
