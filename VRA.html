Company Profile
Consumer Cyclical ·Footwear & Accessories ·NASDAQ ·United States
Vera Bradley, Inc. designs and manufactures women's handbags, luggage, travel items, fashion and home accessories, and gifts. The company operates through Vera Bradley Direct (full-line and outlet stores, e-commerce) and Vera Bradley Indirect (specialty retail, department stores, wholesale) segments. Founded in 1982 in Fort Wayne, Indiana. Sold Pura Vida Bracelets in March 2025. Executing Project Sunshine turnaround under CEO Ian Bickley (appointed July 2025). Q2 FY2027 revenue of $71.7M was the second consecutive quarter of overall revenue growth with DTC sales up 8.0% YoY.
1,360 Employees ·verabradley.com

AI Risk Protection (6/9): Vera Bradley's core business — designing, manufacturing, and retailing physical women's accessories — involves tangible products with tactile and aesthetic qualities that AI cannot directly replicate [1]. Consumers buy handbags, luggage, and home accessories for their physical characteristics, patterns, and brand identity, and the retail store experience provides physical-world defensibility. Vera Bradley's mid-market positioning makes it particularly exposed to AI-enabled low-cost competitors attacking from below, while AI marketing tools favor brands with larger digital audiences.
