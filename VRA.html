Company Profile
Consumer Cyclical ·Footwear & Accessories ·NASDAQ ·United States
Vera Bradley, Inc. designs and manufactures women's handbags, luggage, travel items, fashion and home accessories, and gifts. The company operates through its Vera Bradley Direct segment (full-line and outlet stores, e-commerce) and Vera Bradley Indirect segment (specialty retail, department stores, wholesale). Founded in 1982 in Fort Wayne, Indiana. Sold Pura Vida Bracelets in March 2025. Executing Project Sunshine turnaround under CEO Ian Bickley (appointed July 2025). Q1 FY2027 revenue of $55.7M grew 7.8% — the first growth quarter since Q4 FY2022.
1,360 Employees ·verabradley.com
