Company Profile
Healthcare ·Biotechnology ·NASDAQ ·United States
Verrica Pharmaceuticals is a dermatology therapeutics company focused on developing and commercializing treatments for skin diseases requiring medical intervention. The company's lead product YCANTH (VP-102) is the first and only FDA-approved topical treatment for molluscum contagiosum, a highly contagious and primarily pediatric viral skin disease. YCANTH is a proprietary drug-device combination containing cantharidin administered in-office by healthcare providers. Founded in 2013 by dermatology and health-sector professionals, Verrica has a Phase 3 common warts program with data readout expected mid-2027. Q2 2026 YCANTH dispensed units reached 19,626 (+46.1% YoY). Market cap approximately $93M.

Investment Thesis
Verrica owns the only FDA-approved treatment for molluscum contagiosum — a condition affecting an estimated 6 million Americans annually — with YCANTH dispensed units accelerating 46.1% YoY to 19,626 in Q2 2026, demonstrating strong adoption momentum in a massively underpenetrated market [1]. U.S. YCANTH net product revenue of $5.1M (+12.3% YoY, +18.7% QoQ) shows sequential improvement as the commercial infrastructure matures. The Phase 3 common warts program (data readout mid-2027) could dramatically expand the addressable market for the cantharidin platform into a condition affecting 22% of children [2]. The stock trades at approximately 3x trailing revenue with a market cap of only $93M — offering significant upside if the common warts indication succeeds. The new $27.5M credit facility extends cash runway into 2028, covering the Phase 3 readout without immediate dilutive financing.
The bear case centers on cash burn, liquidity, and binary clinical risk. Verrica posted a $13.2M net loss in Q2 2026 on only $5.9M revenue, and the cash position has declined from $46.3M (FY2024) to approximately $11.2M, leaving the company dependent on the new $27.5M credit facility for survival [4]. Average daily dollar volume of ~$651K is dangerously illiquid — triggering the IOWN liquidity floor — making institutional-sized positions impractical. The Phase 3 common warts trial is a binary event that could destroy or create significant value depending on results; failure would leave the company with only the molluscum indication in a narrow market [2]. Q2 2026 total revenue of $5.9M versus $12.7M a year earlier (inflated by an $8.0M non-recurring milestone) reveals that organic product growth alone cannot yet sustain the company's operating expenses. The CEO change in November 2024 introduces execution uncertainty during the critical early commercialization phase. Profit margin of -102.84% and ROE of -240.35% demonstrate the company remains far from profitability with its current product alone.
