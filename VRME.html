Company Profile
Technology ·Information Technology Services ·NASDAQ ·Cayman Islands
TICKER CHANGE: VerifyMe, Inc. (VRME) completed a reverse merger with Open World Ltd. effective October 1, 2026. It renamed itself OpenWorld, Inc. and now trades on the Nasdaq Capital Market as OPNW, so the VRME symbol no longer trades. Legacy OpenWorld holders own about 85-90% of the combined company and legacy VerifyMe holders about 10%. VerifyMe did a 1-for-10 reverse split on September 29, 2026 and paid a $1.50 special cash dividend around closing. OpenWorld, based in Camana Bay in the Cayman Islands, describes itself as a digital-assets and blockchain company offering real-world-asset tokenization, stablecoin infrastructure, capital-markets advisory and governance structuring for clients in the Gulf, Europe, Australia and Southeast Asia. Matthew Shaw is Chairman and CEO. The legacy VerifyMe brand-protection and PeriShip precision-logistics business was shrinking fast before the merger: Q1 2026 revenue was $1.77M versus $4.46M a year earlier. OPNW shares fell from $5.54 on their first trading day to $0.92 by October 30, 2026.

Investment Thesis
The bull case is purely optionality. OpenWorld enters public markets with a Nasdaq listing, about 13.4M shares outstanding and a roughly $12M market cap [1][2]. It has a visible pipeline: tokenized shares on Figure's OPEN network targeted for Q4 2026, a definitive agreement to acquire Blocksight, a BeyondAI term sheet for a structured IP-backed transaction of up to $130M, and a Saudi RWA tokenization center tied to Vision 2030 [5][6]. If even one large institutional tokenization mandate converts to recurring fees, revenue could re-rate meaningfully against such a small base.
The bear case dominates. This is a reverse merger into a pre-revenue-scale digital-assets startup, and the VRME ticker no longer exists. The legacy VerifyMe business was collapsing, with Q1 2026 revenue down 60% to $1.77M and 2025 revenue of $16.4M down from $25.3M in 2023 [2][3]. OPNW fell from $5.54 on October 1 to $0.92 on October 30, 2026, and at about $283K of daily traded value it fails IOWN's $1M liquidity floor [2]. The $50M GEM equity line points to ongoing dilution, about 76% of shares come out of lock-up in tranches through day 180, and Nasdaq continued-listing compliance is an explicit risk in the S-4 [1][7]. The stock is not investable for IOWN.
