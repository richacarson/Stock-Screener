Company Profile
Industrials ·Electrical Equipment & Parts ·NYSE ·United States
Vertiv designs, manufactures, and services critical digital infrastructure for data centers and communication networks — uninterruptible power supplies, power distribution, thermal management and liquid cooling, racks, and integrated modular systems — branded around a 'grid-to-chip' portfolio for AI-density deployments. Formerly Emerson Network Power, it went public via SPAC in 2020 with Executive Chairman David Cote. Q2 2026 net sales rose 24% with adjusted operating margin of 22.6%, and 2026 sales are guided to ~$14B. CEO Giordano Albertazzi has led since January 2023; headquartered in Westerville, Ohio.

Investment Thesis
Vertiv is the purest scaled play on the physical layer of AI data centers: Q2 2026 net sales rose 24%, adjusted operating margin expanded 410bps to 22.6%, adjusted EPS climbed 60% to $1.52, and full-year guidance was raised to ~$14B of sales (+37%), ~$6.70 of adjusted EPS (+60%), and ~$2.5B of adjusted free cash flow [1]. A record $15.0B binding backlog — up 109% YoY after a 2.9x Q4 2025 book-to-bill — gives 12-18 months of visibility, and the stock trades at a 0.83 PEG despite 44% ROE [3][4][8].
The bear case is cycle and valuation risk: at ~27x forward and ~56x trailing earnings, Vertiv is priced for continuous hyperscaler capex growth, and the Q2 2026 revenue miss on 'timing' shows how shipment slippage moves the stock [2][8]. Competition from Schneider Electric, Eaton, and newer liquid-cooling specialists could pressure pricing, and rapid capacity expansion plus serial acquisitions raise execution and integration risk; outstanding shareholder derivative and customer patent litigation add tail risk [5][7].
Key Catalysts
  1. Conversion of the $15.0B backlog into a strong second half 2026 as new manufacturing capacity comes online, supporting the raised ~$14B sales and $6.70 EPS guidance
  2. Closing of the King Environmental Services acquisition in Q4 2026, extending PurgeRite's liquid-cooling fluid-management services model into EMEA
  3. Next-generation high-density power and liquid-cooling platforms for upcoming GPU rack architectures expanding content per megawatt
Key Risks
  1. Hyperscaler AI capex digestion or order deferrals would hit a business priced at ~27x forward earnings with a backlog concentrated in large data-center customers
  2. Shipment-timing slippage and capacity ramp challenges, as seen in the Q2 2026 revenue miss despite an EPS beat
  3. Intensifying competition from Schneider Electric, Eaton, and liquid-cooling specialists, plus integration risk across multiple 2025-2026 acquisitions
