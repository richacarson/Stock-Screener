Company Profile
Technology ·Semiconductors ·NYSE ·United States
Vishay Intertechnology manufactures discrete semiconductors and passive electronic components used in automotive, industrial, computing, consumer, telecommunications, military, aerospace, and medical applications. Products include MOSFETs, diodes, optoelectronic components, resistors, inductors, and capacitors. The company is executing a 'Vishay 3.0' transformation with SiC wafer production targeting AI server and EV applications. Founded in 1962 and headquartered in Malvern, Pennsylvania.

Investment Thesis
Vishay Intertechnology is a cyclical semiconductor recovery play with AI-adjacent positioning — Q1 2026 revenue of $839.2M (+17% YoY) crushed profit estimates by 66.7%, with Q2 guidance of $875-905M signaling continued acceleration [1]. The Vishay 3.0 transformation is expanding capacity in SiC power semiconductors that serve AI server and EV power delivery, with the company supplying over 80% of the bill of materials for power applications [2]. Full-year 2026 analyst consensus of $3.23B revenue (+8.3%) with $0.77 EPS marks the return to profitability after two loss years. D/E of 53% provides a clean balance sheet for capacity investment. Faith alignment is ALIGNED at +59, attracting values-based capital [4].
The bear case is significant: the stock has surged from $27 to $64 (137% gain), pushing forward P/E to 55.6x — pricing in a dramatic earnings recovery that remains fragile [5]. Two consecutive years of net losses ($31M in 2024, $9M in 2025) with profit margin at 0.07% TTM means profitability has barely returned. Revenue of $3.07B in 2025 remains 12% below the $3.50B 2022 peak, yielding a negative 5yr CAGR of -1.7%. The elevated forward P/E requires sustained recovery — if the semiconductor cycle stalls or AI demand disappoints, the multiple will compress severely. Competition from ON Semiconductor and Infineon with superior SiC solutions threatens Vishay's AI growth narrative [2]. The bottom-5% executive team rating (Comparably 40/100) raises organizational culture concerns. The $0.40 dividend (0.9% yield) has been flat since 2022, eroding income value.
