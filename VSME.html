VS Media Holdings Limited is an investment holding company operating a network of over 1,500 digital creators who produce content for social media platforms primarily in Hong Kong and Taiwan. The company generates revenue through campaign-based marketing services, social commerce, and influencer marketing solutions connecting brands with digital creators. Executed a 1-for-20 reverse stock split in January 2026 to regain Nasdaq minimum bid price compliance. Founded in 2013, headquartered in Kwun Tong, Hong Kong. Market cap approximately $5 million.
