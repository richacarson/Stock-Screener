Company Profile
Communication Services ·Internet Content & Information ·NASDAQ ·Hong Kong
VS Media Holdings operates a network of digital creators who create and upload content to social media platforms including YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok, primarily in Hong Kong and Taiwan. The company provides marketing services for brands including strategy development, creator selection, content creation, and distribution. VS Media went public on NASDAQ in September 2023 and has a market cap of $2.85M. The company announced an AI Smart Living strategic pivot in June 2026 targeting smart home and digital health, but the initiative remains in planning stages with no confirmed revenue or agreements. CEO Nga Fan Wong resigned effective March 2026, and the company has faced Nasdaq minimum bid price compliance issues.
