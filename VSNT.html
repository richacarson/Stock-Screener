Company Profile
Communication Services ·Broadcasting & Cable TV ·NASDAQ ·United States
Versant Media Group is an independent media company spun off from Comcast on January 2, 2026, trading on Nasdaq since January 5, 2026. The company operates a portfolio of cable television networks including USA Network, Bravo, CNBC, MSNBC, E!, Syfy, Oxygen, and Golf Channel, along with digital platforms Fandango and GolfNow. Revenue is derived from linear distribution (pay-TV affiliate fees), advertising, and platform services. Full-year 2025 revenue of $6.69 billion with $2.42 billion adjusted EBITDA.

Investment Thesis
Versant Media trades at a deeply discounted 5.71x forward P/E with a 4.13% dividend yield, $1B buyback authorization, and $2.42B adjusted EBITDA on $6.69B revenue — offering compelling near-term cash returns for income-oriented investors [1][2]. Q1 2026 showed margin resilience with adjusted EBITDA up 5% YoY to $704M despite a 1% revenue decline, and free cash flow of $558M in a single quarter demonstrates exceptional cash generation. Platforms revenue (GolfNow, Fandango) grew 9% to $192M, suggesting digital diversification is gaining early traction [1]. At a $5.14B market cap with $1.86B annual FCF, the stock offers a 36% free cash flow yield.
The bear case is structural and powerful: linear TV distribution revenue ($1.01B in Q1, down 7%) represents approximately 60% of total revenue and is in secular decline as cord-cutting accelerates with no floor in sight — pay-TV subscribers have fallen from over 100M to approximately 60M over the past decade [1][4]. Versant was deliberately created as a spinoff of Comcast's declining assets while Comcast retained the growing Peacock streaming platform, signaling that even the parent company viewed these properties as ex-growth. Full-year 2025 net income fell 31.8% to $930M despite only a 5.3% revenue decline, revealing operating leverage that works in both directions [2]. Without a credible path to replace declining linear revenue through digital transformation, the low multiple may be a value trap rather than a buying opportunity — the company lacks the innovation, mission, and long-term strategic vision that the IOWN framework values most highly.
Key Catalysts
  1. Platforms segment (GolfNow, Fandango) growing 9% YoY to $192M in Q1 2026 represents an emerging diversification engine if management can accelerate digital revenue through acquisitions and organic investment [1]
  2. FCF yield of approximately 36% ($1.86B FCF on $5.14B market cap) supports aggressive capital return through the $1B buyback and 4.13% dividend yield, providing downside support [1][2]
  3. Advertising revenue decline improving from -12% in prior year to -5% in Q1 2026, suggesting potential stabilization as new measurement tools and digital ad formats gain traction [1]
Key Risks
  1. Linear distribution revenue declining 7% YoY in Q1 2026 with no structural floor — continued cord-cutting could accelerate if economic conditions weaken, threatening the 60% of revenue derived from pay-TV affiliate fees [1]
  2. Versant was created through a spinoff precisely because Comcast wanted to separate itself from these declining assets — structural headwinds are baked into the company's DNA and will persist regardless of management execution [4]
  3. Full-year 2025 net income declined 31.8% on only a 5.3% revenue decline, demonstrating negative operating leverage that could compress earnings dramatically if revenue declines accelerate [2]
