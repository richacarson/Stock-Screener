Company Profile
Healthcare ·Medical Devices ·NYSE American ·United States
Catheter Precision designs, manufactures, and sells medical technologies for cardiac electrophysiology (EP). Lead products include the VIVO (View into Ventricular Onset) non-invasive 3D cardiac mapping system for localizing idiopathic ventricular arrhythmias and the LockeT suture retention device for temporary suture management. Headquartered in Fort Mill, South Carolina with 17 employees, the company reported Q1 2026 revenue of $432K (up 200% YoY) but carries a going concern warning with a $530K market capitalization.

Until Catheter Precision demonstrates sustained quarterly revenue above $5M with a visible path to profitability, the stock carries speculative risk inappropriate for a disciplined portfolio.
