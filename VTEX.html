Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NYSE ·Cayman Islands
VTEX provides a SaaS digital commerce platform for enterprise brands and retailers, enabling customers to build online stores, integrate and manage orders across channels, and create marketplaces. The company serves 2,200 B2C and B2B customers including Carrefour, Colgate, Sony, and Whirlpool, operating 3,100 online stores across 44 countries. Founded in 2000 and headquartered in the Cayman Islands, VTEX has repositioned itself as an 'AI-native commerce suite' with VTEX Vision 2026 introducing AI Workspace, Agentic CX Platform, and Retail Media Platform. Full year 2025 revenue was $240.5M (+6.1% YoY), with TTM revenue of $252.6M.
1,139 Employees ·vtex.com

Investment Thesis
VTEX at 13.1x forward earnings offers attractive valuation for a SaaS commerce platform with a nearly debt-free balance sheet ($186M cash, $7.1M debt), expanding margins (non-GAAP operating margins reaching low 20s), and a 2,200-customer enterprise base including Carrefour, Colgate, and Sony across 44 countries [1][3]. The AI-native repositioning with VTEX Vision 2026 — AI Workspace, Agentic CX Platform, and Google Universal Commerce Protocol integration — positions the company at the intersection of enterprise commerce and AI agent workflows [2]. Full year 2025 net income of $20.01M (+26.5% YoY) demonstrates the transition from growth-mode losses to sustained profitability, with Q2 2026 beating EPS consensus at $0.05 vs $0.04 expected [1].
The bear case centers on sharply decelerating revenue growth and competitive pressure from larger, better-resourced platforms. Revenue growth decelerated from 27% in FY2022-2023 to 6.1% in FY2025, and 2026 guidance projects only mid-to-high single-digit subscription revenue growth — a concerning trajectory for a company valued as a growth SaaS platform [1]. Q2 2026 revenue of $64.38M missed the $64.53M consensus, suggesting the growth deceleration has not yet bottomed. VTEX competes against Shopify (dominant in SMB/mid-market), Salesforce Commerce Cloud, and Adobe Commerce — all of which have vastly larger engineering teams and AI research budgets. The small employee base of 1,139 limits the company's ability to innovate across its expanding product surface (commerce, retail media, CX, AI agents simultaneously). The stock's $597M market cap makes it a micro-cap with limited institutional coverage and higher volatility risk.
Key Catalysts
  1. AI-native commerce suite adoption by enterprise customers — VTEX Vision 2026's AI Workspace and Agentic CX Platform could re-accelerate growth if enterprises adopt AI-driven commerce workflows at scale [2]
  2. Margin expansion trajectory — non-GAAP operating margins reaching the low 20s with free cash flow of $41M demonstrate operating leverage that could compound EPS growth even at modest revenue growth rates [1]
  3. Geographic expansion beyond Latin American core into European and North American enterprise markets, where the company's multi-country, multi-currency platform creates differentiation versus domestic-focused competitors [3]
Key Risks
  1. Revenue growth deceleration from 27% to 6% over three years may signal structural competitive erosion rather than a cyclical trough, particularly as Shopify and commercetools expand into VTEX's enterprise segment [1]
  2. Micro-cap status ($597M market cap) with 1,139 employees limits ability to execute across multiple strategic initiatives simultaneously (AI agents, retail media, CX platform, geographic expansion) [4]
  3. Dependence on Latin American markets exposes the company to FX headwinds — the company guided for FX-neutral growth metrics, suggesting currency translation materially impacts reported results [1]
