Company Profile

NCR Voyix provides digital commerce software and hardware for the retail and restaurant industries, including the cloud-native VoyixPOS platform, AI-accelerated commerce applications, and payment processing, following the 2023 separation of NCR Corporation into NCR Voyix (software/retail/restaurant) and NCR Atleos (ATM hardware) [1]. Investment Thesis
NCR Voyix trades at 8.91x forward earnings amid an ongoing turnaround from legacy POS hardware vendor to cloud-native commerce software company. Q1 2026 results showed the net loss from continuing operations narrowing to $2M from $21M a year earlier, Adjusted EBITDA rising to $78M, and recurring revenue growing to $419M from $404M — early signs of stabilization [1]. FY2026 guidance calls for Adjusted free cash flow of $190-220M, implying 40-62% growth versus 2025, and the newly launched cloud-native VoyixPOS platform and AI-accelerated application suite give the company concrete, shipped products to sell into its large enterprise retail/restaurant installed base [1][3]. CEO James Kelly's deliberate cultural reset — ending exec-wing access barriers and personally engaging 72 customer CEOs — signals a genuine attempt to rebuild both internal trust and customer relationships [3].
The bear case is substantial. FY2025 revenue declined 4.65% to roughly $2.69B, profit margin is a thin 2.76%, ROE is just 5.38%, and D/E of 116% reflects a leveraged balance sheet with $1.38B in debt against only $231M cash — a net debt position of roughly $1.15B [4]. Negative free cash flow of -$288M and an Altman Z-score of 1.36 point to elevated financial-distress risk if the software transition stalls [3]. NCR Voyix faces intensifying, analyst-flagged competition from AI-native, cloud-first challengers Toast, Block/Square, and Lightspeed, which can out-innovate the legacy platform, and the company's own AI Risk Protection score of just 4/9 reflects that this is a real, not hypothetical, threat [3]. Pension-related litigation dating to 2015 was only settled in April 2026, an overhang finally resolved but reflective of the company's legacy-liability baggage [4].
Key Catalysts
  1. Continued Adjusted EBITDA and free cash flow improvement toward FY2026 guidance of $190-220M would validate the software-led turnaround and support debt reduction [1]
  2. VoyixPOS and AI-accelerated application suite adoption driving ARR growth beyond the current 2% pace, demonstrating competitive resilience against Toast and Square [3]
  3. Further deleveraging of the $1.38B debt load as free cash flow inflects positive, reducing the Altman Z-score distress signal [3][4]
Key Risks
  1. Elevated financial distress risk — Altman Z-score of 1.36, negative free cash flow of -$288M, and net debt of roughly $1.15B against a low-margin (2.76%) business leave little room for execution missteps [3][4]
  2. AI-native competitors Toast, Square, and Lightspeed are structurally faster-moving and could continue eroding NCR Voyix's enterprise retail/restaurant share despite switching-cost protection [3]
  3. FY2025 revenue decline of 4.65% and ROE of just 5.38% show the software transformation has not yet translated into top-line growth or superior capital returns [4]
