Investment Thesis
NCR Voyix trades at 8.48x forward earnings amid an ongoing turnaround from legacy POS hardware vendor to cloud-native commerce software company. Q1 2026 EPS of $0.10 beat consensus of $0.07, Q2 2026 showed recurring revenue up 3% and adjusted EBITDA up 5%, and platform sites grew 16% YoY — early signs of stabilization under CEO James Kelly [1][2]. FY2026 guidance calls for adjusted EBITDA of $432M–$447M with improving free cash flow, and the cloud-native VoyixPOS platform and AI-accelerated application suite give the company concrete, shipped products to sell into its large enterprise retail/restaurant installed base [1][3]. The deeply discounted forward multiple prices in significant execution risk, creating optionality if the transformation gains traction.
The bear case is substantial. Reported FY2026 revenue guidance of $2.19B–$2.30B reflects a -13% to -18% decline from FY2025 (driven by Japan banking divestiture), profit margin is a thin 2.8%, ROE is just 5.27%, and D/E of 116% reflects $1.31B in debt against only $237M cash [5]. Glassdoor reviews reveal a deeply troubled culture with constant restructuring, leadership churn, and employee burnout — 2.8/5 culture rating — suggesting the organizational foundation for sustainable transformation may be weak [3]. NCR Voyix faces intensifying competition from AI-native, cloud-first challengers Toast, Block/Square, and Lightspeed, which can out-innovate the legacy platform, and the company's AI Risk Protection score of just 4/9 reflects real, not hypothetical, competitive exposure [3].
