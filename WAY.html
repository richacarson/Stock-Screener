Company Profile
Healthcare ·Health Information Services ·NASDAQ ·United States
Waystar Holding Corp. provides cloud-based healthcare payment software that simplifies and automates the revenue cycle for over 30,000 healthcare provider clients. The platform processes billions of dollars in healthcare payments annually, using AI-powered technology (AltitudeAI) for claims management, denial prevention, patient payments, and financial analytics. Completed the $1.25B acquisition of Iodine Software for clinical intelligence AI in 2025. IPO'd on NASDAQ in June 2024 at $21.50/share, raising $968 million. Founded in 2017 by CEO Matt Hawkins, headquartered in Louisville, Kentucky.
1,700 Employees ·www.waystar.com

Investment Thesis
Waystar delivered exceptional Q1 2026 results with revenue up 22% to $313.9M, subscription revenue up 38% to $172.2M, adjusted EBITDA margin of 43%, and net revenue retention of ~111% — demonstrating strong customer stickiness and accelerating growth [1]. AI-powered capabilities contributed to approximately 40% of new bookings, with AltitudeAI targeting the $100B healthcare RCM labor pool for automation [1][2]. Full-year 2026 guidance of $1.274-$1.294B revenue with adjusted EBITDA of $530-$540M and non-GAAP net income of $317-$335M reflects profitable, high-quality growth. At Forward P/E of 11.83 and Forward PEG of 0.66, Waystar appears significantly undervalued relative to its 22% revenue growth rate and expanding margins, particularly compared to healthcare IT peers. The Inspire score of 37 (ALIGNED) and zero negative attributions make WAY compatible with faith-aligned mandates.
Waystar's ROE of only 3.57% reflects the IPO-era balance sheet with $1.49B in total debt (net leverage 2.7x) and a large equity base from the 2024 IPO and Iodine acquisition — GAAP profitability is modest at 10.9% net margin due to amortization of intangibles from pre-IPO PE ownership [5]. Private equity sponsor overhang from EQT, CPPIB, Bain, and Francisco Partners creates ongoing secondary offering risk that will continue pressuring the share price — the stock is essentially flat from the $21.50 IPO price. The healthcare RCM space is intensely competitive with established players (Change Healthcare/Optum, Availity, R1 RCM, Experian Health) and the risk that AI could lower barriers to entry. Organic growth adjusted for the Iodine acquisition may normalize to ~10-12%, materially below the reported 22%, which includes acquisition-driven revenue. The company has only 20 months of public market operating history (IPO June 2024), providing limited visibility into how management navigates economic downturns or healthcare sector disruption [4][5].
