Weibo Corporation operates China's leading microblogging platform, serving as a social media service for public self-expression, content distribution, and social networking. The platform serves over 560 million monthly active users and generates revenue through two segments: Advertising and Marketing Services and Value-Added Services. Q2 2026 revenue reached $453.8M, up 2% YoY. Majority owned by New Wave Holdings (Sina), headquartered in Beijing, China.

Investment Thesis
Weibo trades at 5.0x forward earnings with a 9.4% dividend yield, offering extreme value metrics that reflect the deep China ADR discount applied to a platform with 560M+ MAUs and $2.6B in cash against $1.9B in debt [4]. Q1 2026 revenue grew 6% to $421.3M with advertising revenue accelerating 9% driven by non-Alibaba client diversification and AI-powered ad targeting [1]. Q2 2026 revenue rose 2% to $453.8M, with value-added services surging 19% to $72.9M. Profit margin of 17.8% and price-to-book of 0.39x demonstrate that the market is pricing Weibo well below liquidation value, and the forward PEG of 0.79 suggests adequate growth for the price if the platform can stabilize its competitive position [4].
The SELL recommendation reflects structural headwinds that the valuation discount alone cannot overcome. The estimated 5-year revenue CAGR of -2% represents a platform in secular decline, losing user engagement and advertiser mindshare to ByteDance (Douyin), Xiaohongshu, and Bilibili [1][4]. Q2 2026 advertising revenue declined 1% with management warning of continued ad pressure in H2 2026. GAAP earnings have deteriorated sharply — trailing EPS fell 45.8% YoY driven by impairments and investment losses. Sustainalytics' 'non-compliant' rating on UN human rights principles creates ESG exclusion risk from institutional investors [3]. The VIE structure, concentrated New Wave Holdings ownership, and potential US ADR delisting risk limit minority shareholder rights and exit optionality. Short interest of 18.3% reflects significant bearish positioning [4]. The 9.4% dividend yield may not be sustainable — Weibo already cut its annual dividend from $0.82 to $0.61 per ADS in 2026, a 26% reduction that signals management concern about earnings sustainability.
