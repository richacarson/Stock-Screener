Company Profile
Communication Services ·Entertainment ·NASDAQ ·United States
Warner Bros. Discovery is a global media and entertainment company formed from the April 2022 merger of WarnerMedia and Discovery, Inc. Operates premium content franchises across streaming (Max with 100M+ subscribers), film studios (Warner Bros. Pictures), television networks (HBO, CNN, Discovery Channel, HGTV, TLC), and gaming. Q2 2026 total revenue of $8.72B declined 11% YoY as linear TV advertising fell 22% and theatrical revenue dropped 46%. Streaming revenue topped $3B for the first time with streaming EBITDA of $512M (+63% YoY). Subject to a pending Paramount merger with a $47.5M workforce fund commitment. CEO David Zaslav set to depart. Market cap ~$76B.

The pending Paramount merger provides a potential floor on the stock and could unlock content synergies by combining HBO, Discovery's unscripted library, and Paramount's film/TV franchises. The company's premium IP portfolio (HBO, DC, Harry Potter, CNN) represents irreplaceable branded content, and management stated 2027 could be its 'strongest HBO year yet.' Best-ever retention and lower churn expected in 2026 from content strength and bundling strategies.
