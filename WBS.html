Investment Thesis
There is no longer an investable thesis in WBS. Santander completed the acquisition on August 20, 2026, and each share was converted into $48.75 in cash plus 2.0548 Santander ADSs, worth roughly $78.7-78.9 per share at late-August ADS prices [1]. Before the deal, Webster was a high-quality regional bank. It earned 17% ROTCE in 2025 with 10% EPS growth, 8% loan growth and 6% deposit growth, and its HSA Bank franchise was expected to add $1-2.5B of low-cost deposits over five years [3]. Former holders who kept their Santander ADSs now own a piece of that franchise inside a global bank.
WBS is delisted and cannot be bought, so it fails IOWN's liquidity floor by definition [1]. Anyone wanting exposure to the former Webster franchise would need to evaluate Santander (SAN), a different company with different geographic, regulatory and currency exposures. This report should be retired from the active screen, and any position held through the merger should be reviewed as Santander ADSs.
