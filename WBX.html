Company Profile
Industrials ·Electrical Equipment & Parts ·NYSE ·Spain
Wallbox N.V. designs, manufactures, and sells electric vehicle charging and energy management solutions for residential, semi-public, and public use worldwide. Products include the Pulsar Plus and Pulsar Pro AC home chargers, Quasar bidirectional DC charger (first residential V2G charger), Commander 2 commercial charger, Supernova DC fast charger, and myWallbox energy management platform. Founded in 2015, headquartered in Barcelona, Spain. Listed on NYSE following SPAC merger with Kensington Capital Acquisition Corp. II in October 2021.

Investment Thesis
Wallbox operates in the structurally attractive EV charging infrastructure market, benefiting from accelerating global EV adoption, government charging mandates (EU's Alternative Fuels Infrastructure Regulation, US NEVI program), and the long-term electrification megatrend. The company has a differentiated product portfolio anchored by the Quasar bidirectional DC charger and improving gross margins (37.3% in Q1 2026 vs. 34.2% in FY2024) [1]. CEO Enric Asuncion's $886K personal stock purchase at $2.72/share in June 2026 demonstrates insider conviction, and the €22.5M restructuring agreement with banking partners provides a potential path to financial stabilization [2][4]. Q2 2026 guidance of €33-36M revenue with 38-40% gross margin suggests sequential recovery [1].
However, the investment case is overwhelmed by existential financial risk. Wallbox has negative equity of -€31M, €168.2M in debt, and generated a -€103.2M net loss in FY2025 on only €145.1M of revenue (-71.1% net margin) [1][3]. Q1 2026 revenue declined 21% YoY to €29.7M, and the company received an NYSE non-compliance notice in February 2026 for failing minimum market cap and equity thresholds [2]. The €22.5M restructuring provides only modest liquidity relief relative to the cash burn rate — with just €7.6M cash on hand and -€6.0M quarterly adjusted EBITDA, the runway is dangerously short [1]. The 20-for-1 reverse stock split in July 2025 and subsequent price volatility ($2.72 to $4.46 in days) signal speculative trading rather than fundamental improvement. Average daily dollar volume of ~$853K falls below the $1M IOWN liquidity floor, making the stock effectively untradable for any institutional-scale position [3][6].
