Company Profile

DataMEDS AI, Inc. (formerly Wellgistics Health, Inc., renamed July 21, 2026; ticker changed from WGRX to MEDS on July 22, 2026) is a health information technology and pharmaceutical services company developing the EinsteinRx AI pharmacy dispensing optimization platform and PharmacyChain blockchain-enabled smart contracts system. The company operates pharmacy, wholesale distribution, and technology segments. Q1 2026 revenue was $0.929M with a net operating loss of $7.74M. Under Interim Co-CEO Gerald Commissiong, the company has assembled a new leadership team (CTO Brett Thompson, COO Tony Madsen, CRO Shelley Bailey, CIO Srini Kalla) and is pivoting toward AI-integrated healthcare data and pharmacy technology. Previously known as Danam Health, Inc. Headquartered in West Palm Beach, Florida.
