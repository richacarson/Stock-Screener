Company Profile
Consumer Cyclical ·Lodging ·NYSE ·United States
Wyndham Hotels & Resorts is the world's largest hotel franchising company by number of properties, with approximately 8,300 hotels across approximately 100 countries on six continents. The company operates an asset-light franchise model focused on economy and midscale segments through brands including Super 8, Days Inn, La Quinta, Ramada, and Wyndham. CEO Geoff Ballotti has led the company since its 2018 spin-off from Wyndham Worldwide. Q2 2026 adjusted EBITDA grew 9% YoY to $212M with FY2026 guidance of $735-745M adjusted EBITDA. The company has deployed 250 AI agents handling hundreds of thousands of guest calls.

Investment Thesis
Wyndham offers a compelling franchise compounding story at 12.6x forward earnings with a 0.48 PEG ratio — the world's largest economy hotel franchisor by property count with 8,300+ hotels generating highly predictable fee-based revenue [1][5]. Q2 2026 adjusted EBITDA grew 9% YoY to $212M with FY2026 guidance of $735-745M, and system-wide rooms grew 4% demonstrating continued franchise pipeline momentum [1]. The AI deployment is a genuine differentiator — 250 agents handling 500K+ guest interactions with 25% handle time reduction and higher booking conversion demonstrate measurable ROI [3]. The 2.56% dividend yield has grown at a 14.3% 5yr CAGR, supported by $362M in annual free cash flow. ROE of 39.4% reflects the capital efficiency inherent in the asset-light model. Inspire Impact Score of -1 (MIXED) reflects gambling exposure through casino-adjacent properties.
The bear case centers on structural leverage, slow top-line growth, and franchise model limitations. D/E of 557% is among the highest of any S&P 500 constituent, reflecting an aggressive capital return strategy that leaves minimal balance sheet cushion for an economic downturn [5]. Revenue growth YoY of -5.5% and an estimated 5yr CAGR of only 2% reveal that the franchise model's fee revenue growth is inherently capped by the pace of system expansion and RevPAR improvement — this is a 5-7% organic grower, not a compounder [1]. Profit margin of 14.6% is below the 20% target, constrained by technology investments and marketing spend necessary to compete with Marriott and Hilton's massive loyalty ecosystems. The 60.9% payout ratio leaves limited retained earnings flexibility. A recession that compresses consumer travel spending would disproportionately impact economy hotels, reducing RevPAR and potentially triggering franchisee attrition. AI-powered travel intermediaries (Google AI Mode, conversational assistants) could commoditize hotel brand differentiation over time, pressuring franchise fee economics.

  2. Revenue growth constraint inherent in the franchise model — top-line grew only 2% CAGR over five years, and a recession-driven slowdown in travel demand could push revenue growth negative while fixed debt service costs remain unchanged [1]
  3. AI-powered travel intermediaries (Google AI Mode, conversational booking agents) could commoditize hotel brand differentiation over the medium term, reducing the value proposition of Wyndham's central reservation system and pressuring franchise fee economics [3]

AI Resilience MODERATE

AI Risk Protection (6/9): Wyndham's core business — hotel franchising — involves physical-world hospitality operations that AI cannot replace. Travelers need physical hotel rooms, and the franchise model's value proposition (brand recognition, loyalty program, reservation system, operational support) creates meaningful switching costs for franchisee-owners [1]. The economy and midscale hotel segments serve price-sensitive travelers where the human touch and physical product are central. However, AI threatens the distribution layer: AI-powered travel agents (Google AI Mode, conversational booking interfaces) could commoditize brand differentiation and pressure franchise fees if AI-enabled direct booking reduces the value of Wyndham's central reservation system. Online travel agency disruption via AI intermediaries represents a medium-term structural risk. AI Opportunity (1/1): Wyndham generates quantifiable value from AI — 250 AI agents deployed across 7% of hotels handling over half a million guest interactions with 25% reduction in handle time and higher booking conversion [3]. The Google partnership on agentic AI booking and multi-LLM strategy (Google, ChatGPT, Anthropic) position Wyndham at the forefront of hospitality AI adoption.
