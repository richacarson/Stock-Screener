Company Profile
Financial Services ·Asset Management ·NASDAQ ·United States
WhiteHorse Finance is an externally managed, non-diversified business development company (BDC) that originates and invests in senior secured loans to lower middle market companies with $50M-$350M enterprise value. Managed by an affiliate of H.I.G. Capital, a $67B global alternative investment firm. Q2 2026 NII was $4.7M ($0.217/share), down from Q1's $5.6M ($0.253/share), on total investment income of $14.4M. NAV per share increased to $11.77 from $11.47 driven by net realized/unrealized gains and accretive share repurchases. The stock trades at a ~39% discount to NAV with weighted average portfolio yield of 10.8%.

Investment Thesis
WhiteHorse Finance offers a deeply discounted entry point at 0.61x book value ($7.16 vs. $11.77 NAV) with a forward P/E of 6.79x and a ~14% dividend yield on the $1.00 annualized distribution [1][4]. NAV per share recovered 2.6% sequentially to $11.77 in Q2 2026, driven by $0.265/share in net realized/unrealized gains and $0.06/share accretion from share repurchases. Origination volume is running 40-50% above prior year with improved credit quality — better credits, lower leverage, and better documentation terms — suggesting the portfolio rebuild is underway. The H.I.G. Capital platform ($67B AUM) provides institutional deal sourcing and workout capabilities that smaller BDCs lack, and the 10.8% weighted average portfolio yield provides adequate spread income [1].

The $85M unsecured note maturity in December 2026 creates refinancing risk when the stock trades at a 39% discount to NAV, severely limiting accretive capital options. The externally managed structure means H.I.G. Capital continues collecting base management and incentive fees regardless of shareholder outcomes. With daily dollar volume of approximately $716K — below the $1M IOWN liquidity threshold — this stock is too illiquid for meaningful position sizing [4].
