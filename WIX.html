Company Profile
Technology ·Software—Application ·NASDAQ ·Israel
Wix.com is a cloud-based web development platform that enables users to create, manage, and grow an online presence through intuitive drag-and-drop tools and AI-powered website creation. The company serves over 270 million registered users across 190 countries, offering website building, e-commerce, business management tools, and the recently acquired BASE44 AI-driven business creation platform. Wix reported Q2 2026 revenue of $563.1M (+15% YoY) and total bookings of $569M (+12% YoY), with full-year 2026 guidance targeting low-to-mid-teens revenue growth. Market cap is approximately $4.0B. The company is headquartered in Tel Aviv, Israel and led by co-founder and CEO Avishai Abrahami, who has served since 2010.

Investment Thesis
Wix at approximately 17x forward earnings is a growth-stage platform company executing a high-stakes AI pivot, with Q2 2026 revenue of $563.1M (+15% YoY) and bookings of $569M (+12% YoY) demonstrating continued momentum despite severe AI disruption fears that drove the stock down 71% over the past year [1]. The BASE44 acquisition provides an AI-native business creation platform with surging margins, while Wix Harmony reimagines the core website builder with generative AI. Forward PEG of approximately 1.16 suggests reasonable growth-adjusted valuation. The company's installed base of 270M+ registered users provides distribution advantage that pure AI tools lack, and the full-stack offering (hosting, domains, payments, CRM) creates switching costs that AI generators haven't yet replicated. With $960M in cash and high-teens free cash flow margin guidance for 2026, the company has financial runway to fund the AI transition [1].
The bear case is substantial and centers on existential AI disruption risk. AI website generators (Bolt, v0, standalone coding assistants) can create complete websites from natural language prompts, potentially bypassing the entire website builder category and making Wix's template-based value proposition obsolete [2]. The stock's 71% decline from its 52-week high of $190.93 to recent lows near $43.70 reflects the market pricing meaningful probability of this outcome. Financially, GAAP profitability remains negative with heavy SBC and restructuring charges, negative book equity, and $1.63B in debt creating balance sheet risk [1]. The 61% increase in sales and marketing suggests the company is buying growth rather than earning it organically. Non-GAAP operating margin compressed from 22% to 12% YoY as the company pours capital into AI capabilities, with no guarantee these investments will generate adequate returns. Insider selling across the C-suite — including CEO Abrahami, President Zohar, and CTO Even-Haim — undermines confidence in the long-term outlook [4]. The Inspire Impact Score of 40 (ALIGNED) is a positive for faith-based investors, though the company's financial fragility during the AI transition introduces allocation risk.

AI Resilience MODERATE
5
AI Risk Protection (4/9): Wix's core business — a platform for building websites through templates and drag-and-drop tools — faces direct substitution risk from AI-powered alternatives that can generate complete websites from natural language prompts without requiring any platform interface at all [2]. Multiple analysts have flagged this as an existential challenge, and the stock's 71% decline over the past year reflects the market pricing meaningful AI disruption risk. Competitors including Bolt, v0, and standalone AI coding assistants can replicate Wix's value proposition at lower cost. However, Wix is not passively awaiting disruption — the company is actively pivoting to become an AI-first platform through BASE44 and Wix Harmony, and its installed base of 270M+ registered users provides distribution advantage that pure AI tools lack [1]. The company also benefits from the non-trivial complexity of hosting, domain management, payments, and ongoing site maintenance that AI generators do not yet fully solve. The risk is real but the company is adapting aggressively. AI Opportunity (1/1): BASE44 is generating AI-driven revenue, with Q2 2026 earnings highlighting AI-driven growth acceleration and surging BASE44 margins [1]. Wix Harmony is commercially shipping AI website creation to customers. While specific AI revenue figures are not separately disclosed, management commentary positions AI as a core growth driver.
