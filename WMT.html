Investment Thesis
Walmart at $104.26 is a $827B retailer growing net sales 4-5% with adjusted operating income guided up 7-8.5% for FY27, and global eCommerce growing 23% in Q2 [1]. Scale in grocery, a rising advertising and marketplace mix, and an AI push (OpenAI partnership, associate AI certification) support margin expansion and defensive resilience [2].
The bear case is valuation: forward P/E of 32.3 and a PEG of 4.2 price in much faster profit growth than 7-8.5% operating income guidance, while profit margin is only 3.0%. Trailing earnings growth was -9.1%, the stock sits below its 50-day average ($108.74), and Walmart U.S. comps of 2.6% leave little room for error. Labor intensity (2.1M employees), tariffs and agentic-AI disintermediation are further risks [1][2].
Key Catalysts
  1. eCommerce up 23% globally and advertising scale lifting operating income growth to 7-8.5% [1]
  2. AI-driven efficiency and ChatGPT shopping partnership under new CEO Furner [2]
  3. Share gains in grocery from value-seeking higher-income households [1]
Key Risks
  1. Valuation at 32.3x forward P/E and 4.2 PEG leaves little margin for a growth miss [1]
  2. Thin 3.0% net margin makes earnings sensitive to tariffs, wages and price investment [1]
  3. Agentic AI shopping could shift customer relationships to third-party AI platforms [2]
