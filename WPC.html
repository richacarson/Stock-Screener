Company Profile
Real Estate ·REIT - Diversified ·NYSE ·United States
W. P. Carey Inc. ranks among the largest net lease REITs with a well-diversified portfolio of operationally critical commercial real estate properties covering approximately 183 million square feet. The company invests primarily in single-tenant industrial, warehouse, and retail properties in the U.S. and Europe under long-term net leases with built-in rent escalations. In late 2023, WPC exited its entire office portfolio and reset its dividend to reposition toward higher-quality industrial and essential retail assets.

Investment Thesis
W. P. Carey delivered strong Q2 2026 results with revenue of $461.1M (up 7.0% YoY), AFFO per share of $1.34 (up 4.7%), and adjusted earnings of $0.82 beating estimates by 22% [1][2]. Management raised and narrowed 2026 AFFO guidance to $5.19-$5.27/share, implying 5.2% YoY growth at the midpoint. Portfolio occupancy improved to 98.5% with contractual same-store rent growth of 2.6% providing reliable organic growth [2]. The 4.89% dividend yield — now growing again with $0.93/quarter — offers attractive current income from a portfolio of operationally critical properties with long-duration leases and built-in rent escalations. At $76.50, the stock trades at 14.5x AFFO, a reasonable valuation for a diversified net lease REIT with improving fundamentals.
The bear case centers on the legacy damage from the 2023 dividend reset. The 5-year dividend growth CAGR remains negative at approximately -2.5%, and the GAAP payout ratio of 156.4% (typical for REITs but optically concerning) leaves limited margin if property fundamentals deteriorate [1]. D/E of 104.90% and significant total debt require continued access to capital markets at favorable rates — a risk if interest rates remain elevated or re-accelerate. Revenue growth decelerated from 10.9% in Q1 to 7.0% in Q2, suggesting acquisition-driven growth is normalizing [2]. European exposure (~35% of ABR) introduces currency risk and geopolitical uncertainty. Competition for net lease acquisitions from larger peers like Realty Income compresses cap rates on new investments, and WPC's sub-200 employee count creates key-person risk around specialized deal sourcing expertise.
Key Catalysts
  1. Raised 2026 AFFO guidance to $5.19-$5.27/share with continued acquisition pipeline deployment, providing visible earnings growth and potential for further dividend increases toward pre-reset levels
  2. Contractual same-store rent escalations of 2.6% (constant currency) across the 98.5% occupied portfolio provide organic growth independent of acquisition activity or interest rate environment
  3. Post-office-exit portfolio concentration in industrial (~64% of ABR) and essential retail positions WPC to benefit from nearshoring trends, logistics demand, and potential interest rate cuts that would re-rate REIT valuations
Key Risks
  1. Elevated leverage (D/E 104.90%) with significant total debt exposure to refinancing risk — sustained higher interest rates would compress spreads on new acquisitions and increase borrowing costs on maturing debt
  2. European exposure (~35% of ABR) introduces currency risk, geopolitical uncertainty, and regulatory complexity that US-focused peers avoid entirely
  3. The 2023 dividend reset destroyed WPC's decades-long dividend growth track record — 5-year dividend CAGR remains negative at -2.5%, and rebuilding credibility with income-focused investors requires multiple additional years of consecutive increases
