Company Profile
Healthcare ·Medical Instruments & Supplies ·NYSE ·United States
Warby Parker is a vertically integrated, omnichannel eyewear company that designs and sells prescription glasses, sunglasses and contact lenses and provides eye exams through its retail stores and e-commerce channels. Founded in 2010 by co-CEOs Neil Blumenthal and Dave Gilboa, it is a public benefit corporation and certified B Corp known for its Buy a Pair, Give a Pair program. Q2 2026 net revenue rose 9.8% to $235.5M, and management guided Q3 2026 revenue to $243M-$246M (+10-11%) and full-year 2026 revenue to $959M-$976M. The company has a long-term partnership with Google to develop AI-enabled smart eyewear. Market cap approximately $2.9B.

Investment Thesis
Warby Parker is a steady compounder in a structurally protected category. Revenue grew from $393.7M in 2020 to $871.9M in 2025 (17.2% CAGR), Q2 2026 revenue rose 9.8% to $235.5M, and full-year 2026 guidance of $959M-$976M implies 10-12% growth [1][4]. The business just crossed GAAP profitability ($1.6M net income in 2025, $4.6M in Q2 2026 helped by a tariff refund), so operating leverage on a gross margin of roughly 54% could expand earnings meaningfully as stores mature [1][4]. A clean balance sheet (D/E 64%, about $293M cash against $251M of total debt) funds store growth and the Google AI-eyewear partnership without dilution [6].
The bear case is valuation against thin profits. At 38.8x forward earnings with ROE of 2.1% and a 0.85% profit margin, the stock prices in margin expansion that has not yet arrived, and Q2 2026 revenue came in slightly below consensus [1][6]. Growth has slowed from the high teens into the 10% range, and Q3 2026 guidance of $243M-$246M (+10-11%) leaves little room for error before results due in early November [1]. Insiders have been consistent net sellers with no open-market purchases in 2025-2026 [5], and the AI smart-glasses category is unproven and contested by much larger players. For faith-based investors, the Inspire score of -49 (MISALIGNED, LGBT Activism) is a significant screen-out despite 16 positive attributions.
Key Catalysts
  1. Q3 2026 results against guidance of $243M-$246M revenue and $26M-$28M adjusted EBITDA; a beat would support the full-year $959M-$976M outlook and confirm margin expansion [1]
  2. Commercial launch of the AI smart eyewear developed with Google, which would open a new higher-ticket product category beyond prescription frames [2]
  3. Continued store openings and the build-out of eye exams and contacts, which deepen customer lifetime value and push GAAP profitability above 2025's breakeven level [1][4]
Key Risks
  1. Valuation risk: at 38.8x forward earnings with a 0.85% profit margin, any slowdown below the 10% growth guidance could compress the multiple sharply [1][6]
  2. Competitive pressure from EssilorLuxottica and big-tech smart-glasses entrants could squeeze both the core frame business and the new AI-eyewear category [2]
  3. Tariff exposure on imported frames and lenses; Q2 2026 net income depended on a one-time tariff refund, so underlying profitability is weaker than reported [1]
