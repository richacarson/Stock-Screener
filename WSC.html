Company Profile
Industrials ·Rental & Leasing Services ·NASDAQ ·United States
WillScot Holdings Corporation is the North American leader in modular space and portable storage solutions, leasing mobile offices, portable storage containers, and specialty equipment to construction, education, government, retail, and industrial customers through one of the industry's largest branch networks across the US, Canada, Mexico, and UK. Headquartered in Scottsdale, Arizona, the company is increasingly focused on large-project and data-center vertical demand as a growth driver.

Investment Thesis
WillScot raised its FY2026 outlook to approximately $2.25B revenue and $915M adjusted EBITDA after a Q1 2026 beat ($549M revenue, $211M adjusted EBITDA, $0.21 adjusted EPS vs. $0.16 consensus), driven by accelerating large- and mega-project demand — particularly a data-center vertical management expects to grow 50% in 2026 with the opportunity pipeline up 70% [1]. Management is guiding to a leasing-revenue inflection in the second half of 2026 as this higher-margin project mix builds. Delivery and installation revenue already grew 12% YoY in Q1, and the company's dominant modular-space branch network provides a structurally AI-immune, physical-asset moat.
The balance sheet and governance picture is the primary concern: debt-to-equity of 438% is far outside IOWN's target range, and WillScot posted a full-year FY2025 net loss of -$53.0M (versus +$28.1M in FY2024) despite record revenue, with profit margin and ROE both negative [6]. Culture signals are weak — a 2.6/5 Glassdoor rating (28% below industry average) with reviews citing management that 'suffocated innovation,' compounded by net insider selling from a board director in May 2026 rather than conviction buying [3][5]. With thin Inspiration (1/10) and Infinite Game (2/10) scores reflecting a lack of a differentiated Just Cause or high-trust culture, WillScot screens as an operationally-recovering but governance- and culture-challenged name best suited for a WATCH rather than a buy.
Key Catalysts
  1. H2 2026 leasing revenue inflection as large-project and data-center vertical demand converts from pipeline (up 70%) into activated units, per management guidance
