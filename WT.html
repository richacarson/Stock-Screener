Company Profile
Financial Services ·Asset Management ·NYSE ·United States
WisdomTree is a global asset management firm specializing in exchange-traded funds (ETFs) and exchange-traded products (ETPs), with a focus on smart-beta, factor-based, and thematic investment strategies including AI and digital assets. Founded by CEO Jono Steinberg, who has led the company for 37 years, WisdomTree manages record assets under management of $162.9B following the Atlantic House acquisition. Q2 2026 revenue surged 57.3% YoY to $177.2M, with adjusted EPS of $0.31 beating consensus by 14.8%. The company offers AI-focused and digital asset strategies and has been building WisdomTree Prime, a blockchain-enabled digital finance platform. Market cap approximately $3.6B.

Investment Thesis
WisdomTree at 16.95x forward earnings is attractively valued for an asset manager delivering 57.3% revenue growth and record AUM of $162.9B — Q2 2026 adjusted EPS of $0.31 beat consensus by 14.8%, marking the sixth consecutive quarter of record AUM [1]. The Atlantic House acquisition expands European distribution and is expected to lift revenue yield by 2 basis points, while $5.9B in quarterly net inflows demonstrate organic growth momentum across international equity and fixed income strategies. CEO Jono Steinberg's 37-year tenure provides unparalleled leadership continuity. The WisdomTree Prime digital assets platform and AI-themed ETFs position the company at the intersection of two secular growth trends. The Inspire Impact Score of 52 (ALIGNED) with nine positive attributions and zero negatives makes this highly accessible to faith-based investors.
The bear case centers on leverage, the SEC fine, and sustainability of growth. D/E has surged to 253% as debt rose from $275M to $1.08B, largely financing the Atlantic House acquisition — this creates significant financial risk if markets decline and AUM-linked revenue falls [4]. The trailing P/E of 46.38 suggests current earnings are depressed relative to forward expectations, creating execution risk if the Atlantic House integration underperforms. The $4M SEC fine for ESG fund misstatements (including fossil fuel and tobacco stocks in ESG-marketed ETFs) raises compliance and reputational concerns — the company marketed funds as ESG-compliant that demonstrably were not, eroding investor trust [3]. Profit margin of 11.26% is below industry leaders, and the 57.3% revenue growth is partly acquisition-driven rather than purely organic. The dividend yield of 0.61% with 0% growth over 5 years is negligible and does not provide income support. WisdomTree remains a small player competing against BlackRock, Vanguard, and State Street — firms with massive scale advantages in distribution, technology, and pricing power.

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience MODERATE
6
AI Risk Protection (6/9): WisdomTree's ETF/ETP business model has meaningful structural protections from AI disruption. Passive and factor-based ETFs are already systematic and rules-based — they don't rely on human stock-picking judgment that AI could replace. Regulatory requirements (SEC registration, compliance), distribution relationships (broker-dealer platforms), and brand trust create switching costs [2]. However, AI-driven robo-advisors and algorithmic portfolio construction could put pressure on management fees over time, and AI could commoditize the factor-based and thematic investment strategies that differentiate WisdomTree from larger competitors. New AI-native asset managers could emerge with lower cost structures. The $4M SEC fine for ESG fund misstatements (2024) also highlights compliance risk that AI-powered monitoring could expose more frequently across the industry [3]. AI Opportunity (0/1): WisdomTree offers AI-themed investment products (AI & Innovation ETFs) that provide investor exposure to AI companies, but the firm does not generate revenue from AI technology itself. These are investment products, not AI revenue [2].
