Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas E&P ·NYQ ·United States
W&T Offshore, Inc. is an independent oil and natural gas producer focused exclusively on the Gulf of America, with operations spanning exploration, development, and acquisition of offshore properties. Founded in 1983 by CEO Tracy Krohn, the company produces crude oil, natural gas, and NGLs. Q2 2026 revenue was $162.6M (+33% YoY) with net income of $12.6M and adjusted EBITDA of $54.4M. Production averaged 34,700 Boe/d, at the midpoint of guidance and up 3% from Q2 2025. The company restarted dividends in late 2023 after a 9-year hiatus and has maintained a $0.01/quarter payout. Market cap of $596M on TTM revenue of approximately $562M.
370 Employees ·www.wtoffshore.com
