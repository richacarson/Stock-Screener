Company Profile
Utilities ·Utilities - Regulated Water ·NYSE ·United States
Essential Utilities is the largest publicly traded water utility in the United States, providing regulated water, wastewater, and natural gas services to approximately 5.5 million people across 10 states under the Aqua and Peoples brands. The company operates through two segments: Regulated Water and Regulated Natural Gas (Peoples Gas). Essential is pursuing a transformative merger with American Water (NYSE: AWK), expected to close Q1 2027 with ~95% shareholder approval. Headquartered in Bryn Mawr, Pennsylvania.

Investment Thesis
Essential Utilities reported Q1 2026 revenue of $861.8M (up 10% YoY), driven by regulatory rate recoveries and continued acquisition momentum, while investing $269M in infrastructure toward the $1.7B full-year capital plan [1]. The company's crown jewel is its 80-year dividend payment history with 33 consecutive years of growth and a 6.9% five-year dividend growth CAGR — among the strongest records in the utility sector [4]. At 16.9x forward earnings with a 3.55% yield, Essential offers attractive total return potential through the 5-7% EPS CAGR guidance plus reliable dividend income. The pending American Water merger (expected Q1 2027, ~95% shareholder approval) would create the dominant U.S. water utility with enhanced scale and acquisition capabilities [1]. Physical water and gas infrastructure provides near-perfect AI resilience, and the Inspire score of 46 (ALIGNED) reflects strong environmental and social stewardship with 17 positive attributions [3].
The bear case centers on execution risk, leverage, and modest growth. GAAP EPS fell 23% in Q1 2026 to $0.79 from $1.03 as $16.3M in merger-related costs and higher interest expense on the $500M senior note issuance at 5.125% offset revenue growth [1]. D/E of 123% is already elevated and the American Water merger will add significant additional debt. The payout ratio of 69.47% leaves thinner margin of safety for dividend coverage than peers with sub-50% ratios. ROE of 8.34% is structurally constrained by allowed returns on equity in regulated rate cases — unfavorable regulatory outcomes could compress it further. The 5-7% EPS growth guidance is modest relative to growth alternatives, and the stock's 0.64 beta limits upside in risk-on markets. Integration of American Water's operations across multiple state regulatory jurisdictions carries meaningful execution risk; if the merger fails to close, the company absorbs significant costs with no strategic benefit [4].
