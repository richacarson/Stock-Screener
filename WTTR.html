Company Profile
Energy ·Oil & Gas Equipment & Services ·NYSE ·United States
Select Water Solutions, Inc. provides sustainable water and chemical solutions to the energy industry through three segments: Water Infrastructure (pipelines, recycling facilities, disposal wells), Water Services (transfer, containment, monitoring), and Chemical Technologies (fracturing chemicals, production chemicals). Founded by John D. Schmitz, the company rebranded from Select Energy Services in 2023 to reflect its strategic pivot toward permanent water infrastructure. Headquartered in Houston, Texas.

Investment Thesis
Select Water Solutions occupies a structurally advantaged position in oilfield water management — the fastest-growing cost center for E&P companies, with water volumes per well increasing annually. Q1 2026 delivered record consolidated revenue of $366M with record Water Infrastructure revenue of $97M (up 19% sequentially), and management raised 2026 Water Infrastructure growth guidance to 25-30% [1][2]. The strategic pivot from truck-based services to permanent pipeline infrastructure creates higher-margin, recurring revenue with multi-decade asset lives. A pristine balance sheet (D/E 24.93%), founder-CEO leadership since 2008, and AI-powered technologies (FluidMatch, AquaView) provide differentiation. The stock's 286% five-year appreciation reflects the market's recognition of the infrastructure transition thesis [3].
The bear case centers on energy cycle dependency and valuation risk. Despite the 'water' rebrand, 100% of revenue comes from oil and gas producers, and Q1 2026 consolidated revenue declined 2.3% YoY as Water Services and Chemical Technologies offset Infrastructure gains [1][2]. Net profit margin of 1.90% and ROE of 2.42% indicate Select is not yet earning its cost of capital, and the trailing P/E of 57.19x prices in significant forward margin expansion that may not materialize if oil prices weaken. The dividend payout ratio of 133% (dividends exceeding net income) is unsustainable without EBITDA-based cash flow support, and the 3-year dividend history provides no track record assurance. The $175-225M annual infrastructure CapEx commitment creates capital risk on projects with multi-year payback periods, and average daily volume of only 660K shares limits institutional interest and creates liquidity risk in a downturn.
Key Catalysts
  1. Water Infrastructure segment revenue growth of 25-30% in 2026 driven by pipeline projects already under construction, with Q1's record $97M validating the acceleration from 20-25% prior guidance
  2. Secular increase in water volumes per well completion and Permian Basin operator consolidation creating larger, longer-term water management contracts that favor infrastructure-scale providers
  3. AI data center proliferation driving both energy demand (more drilling, more produced water for Select to manage) and direct water demand (cooling infrastructure), positioning Select as an indirect AI beneficiary
Key Risks
  1. Near-total revenue dependency on oil and gas producers — a sustained oil price decline below $60/bbl would compress drilling activity and water management demand across all three segments regardless of infrastructure investments
  2. Net profit margin of 1.90% and trailing PE of 57x create significant downside risk if the Infrastructure-driven margin expansion thesis disappoints — the valuation leaves no room for execution missteps
  3. Multi-year infrastructure CapEx commitments of $175-225M annually represent substantial capital risk if contracted water volumes underperform due to producer consolidation, basin-level activity declines, or water recycling innovations by competitors
