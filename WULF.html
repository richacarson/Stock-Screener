Investment Thesis
TeraWulf has executed one of the most compelling strategic pivots in the digital infrastructure space — from Bitcoin mining to AI data center hosting — winning a landmark ~$19B Anthropic contract that provides decades of contracted revenue visibility [3]. HPC lease revenue reached $31.9M in Q2 2026 (71% of total), up 52% sequentially, with 102 MW of operational capacity and a pipeline targeting 250-500 MW of new capacity annually [1]. The company's zero-carbon power assets (95% clean energy) and grid-connected Lake Mariner facility provide structural competitive advantages as AI companies increasingly prioritize sustainable, reliable power for compute-intensive workloads. With $3.0B in cash and restricted cash, TeraWulf has the liquidity to fund its expansion without dilutive capital raises. The Inspire Impact Score of 53 (ALIGNED) makes this one of the few pure-play AI infrastructure companies accessible to faith-based investors.
The bear case centers on financial profile and execution risk. The D/E ratio of 3699% reflects extreme leverage relative to a thin equity base, and ROE of -343.69% is driven by massive non-cash warrant fair value adjustments ($939.9M net loss in Q2 alone) that distort the true operating picture [1][5]. Forward P/E of 316.89x prices in future profitability that has not yet materialized — the company is still pre-profit on a GAAP basis. Total revenue of $44.8M actually declined 6% YoY as the bitcoin mining business contracted faster than HPC revenue scaled [1]. The Anthropic contract, while transformative, concentrates customer risk in a single counterparty for the bulk of future revenue. Building 250-500 MW of new capacity annually requires significant capital expenditure with execution risk on timelines, permitting, and grid interconnection. The company has only 141 employees to manage this expansion, creating operational scalability concerns. Analyst estimates are volatile with Q1 2026 EPS missing expectations at -$1.01 vs. -$0.20 estimate [5].
