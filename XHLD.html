Company Profile
Communication Services ·Broadcasting ·NASDAQ ·United States
TEN Holdings provides event planning, production, and broadcasting services in the United States, organizing virtual, hybrid, self-service, and physical events including conferences, product launches, trainings, and shareholder meetings. The company's platforms (Xyvid Pro and TEN Pro) are internet-based broadcast platforms with interactive engagement tools. Controlled by V-Cube Inc. of Japan. Founded in 2011, headquartered in Langhorne, Pennsylvania.
