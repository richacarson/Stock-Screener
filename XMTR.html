Company Profile
Industrials ·Industrial Distribution ·NMS ·United States
Xometry operates an AI-powered global marketplace for on-demand manufacturing, connecting buyers needing custom parts with a network of suppliers offering CNC machining, 3D printing, injection molding, sheet metal fabrication, and other processes. Proprietary AI algorithms provide instant pricing, lead times, and supplier matching. In Q2 2026, Xometry delivered record revenue of $229M (up 41% YoY) with marketplace revenue of $215.4M (up 45% YoY), and raised full-year 2026 revenue guidance to 33-34% growth with adjusted EBITDA of $60-62M.

Investment Thesis
Xometry at $104.23 with a $5.97B market cap delivered a breakout Q2 2026 with record revenue of $229M (41% YoY growth), marketplace revenue of $215.4M (45% YoY, accelerating 500bps from Q1), and adjusted EBITDA of $14.1M versus $3.9M a year earlier [1][2]. Management raised full-year 2026 guidance to 33-34% revenue growth and $60-62M adjusted EBITDA (more than 3x FY2025's $18.5M), with incremental EBITDA margins targeting 20% in H2 2026 [2]. The AI-powered marketplace creates powerful network effects — more buyers attract more suppliers, generating more pricing data that improves AI model accuracy, which drives better margins and more buyers. With 89,557 active buyers and 2,039 enterprise accounts ($50K+ spend, up 23% YoY), Xometry is positioned to become the dominant platform for custom manufacturing procurement globally.
The primary risk is valuation. At 81.2x forward earnings and 7.4x trailing revenue, Xometry is priced for sustained hypergrowth in a company that has never generated a full year of GAAP profit [1][2]. The stock has risen from $91.58 (50-day SMA) to $104.23, creating a high bar for continued performance. The CEO transition from co-founder Randy Altschuler to Sanjeev Singh Sahni in July 2026 introduces execution risk at a critical inflection point — new leadership must maintain growth momentum while driving the final push to sustained GAAP profitability [3]. Profit margin remains negative at -3.8% and ROE at -7.1%, reflecting ongoing losses despite rapid revenue growth. The $339.8M in convertible notes creates refinancing risk and potential dilution. While the path to profitability is becoming clearer with adjusted EBITDA scaling rapidly, any growth deceleration from the current 41% pace could trigger significant multiple compression from current elevated levels.
Key Catalysts
  1. GAAP profitability inflection expected in late 2026 or 2027 as raised adjusted EBITDA guidance of $60-62M (3x+ FY2025) and 20% incremental EBITDA margins in H2 2026 indicate fixed-cost leverage is accelerating toward breakeven [1][2]

  2. CEO transition from co-founder to external executive at a critical growth inflection point creates execution risk around strategic continuity, cultural preservation, and maintaining relationships with key enterprise accounts [3]
  3. Negative profit margin (-3.8%) and negative ROE (-7.1%) mean the company has never generated sustained GAAP profit — the path to profitability depends on continued rapid revenue growth and operating leverage that could be disrupted by competitive pressure or macro slowdown [1]
