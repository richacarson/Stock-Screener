Company Profile
Industrials ·Specialty Industrial Machinery ·NASDAQ ·United States
Xometry, Inc. operates an AI-powered global marketplace for on-demand manufacturing, connecting buyers needing custom parts with a network of suppliers offering CNC machining, 3D printing, injection molding, sheet metal fabrication, and other processes. Proprietary AI algorithms provide instant pricing, lead times, and supplier matching. Founded in 2014, headquartered in Derwood, Maryland. Over 89,000 active buyers and a growing international presence.

Investment Thesis
Xometry delivered a breakout Q2 2026 with record revenue of $229.3M (41% YoY growth), raised full-year 2026 guidance to 33-34% revenue growth and $60-62M adjusted EBITDA, and expanded its active buyer base to 89,557 (up 20% YoY) with enterprise accounts ($50K+ annual spend) growing 23% to 2,039 [1]. The AI-powered marketplace model creates powerful network effects — more buyers attract more suppliers, generating more pricing data that improves AI model accuracy, which drives better margins and more buyers. Operating margin has improved from -26.9% (FY2021) to -3.8% (TTM), with the company approaching GAAP profitability as fixed costs are leveraged across rapidly growing revenue [5]. With a $5.34B market cap on $807.5M TTM revenue and accelerating growth, Xometry is positioned to become the dominant platform for custom manufacturing procurement globally.
The primary risk is valuation. At 98.57x forward earnings and 6.6x trailing revenue, Xometry is priced for perfection in a company that has never generated a full year of GAAP profit [4]. The stock has risen 112% in the past 52 weeks, creating a high bar for continued performance. The CEO transition from co-founder Randy Altschuler to external hire Sanjeev Singh Sahni in July 2026 introduces execution risk at a critical inflection point — new leadership must maintain growth momentum while driving the final push to sustained profitability [3]. Marketplace gross margin of 34.7% declined 70 basis points YoY in Q2 2026 despite AI improvements, suggesting competitive pricing pressure from Protolabs, Fictiv, and other platforms. The $328.7M in convertible notes ($85.5M current) creates refinancing risk, and with only $21.8M in cash (supplemented by $494.9M in marketable securities), the balance sheet depends on securities valuations remaining stable [1].
Key Catalysts
  1. GAAP profitability inflection expected in late 2026 or 2027 as $60-62M adjusted EBITDA guidance implies fixed-cost leverage on 33-34% revenue growth is nearing breakeven on a reported basis
  2. Enterprise account expansion — 2,039 accounts at $50K+ annual spend (up 23% YoY) demonstrates deepening penetration into larger procurement budgets with higher lifetime value and stickier relationships
  3. International expansion into European and Asian manufacturing markets offers a massive total addressable market with limited AI-powered marketplace competition outside the US
Key Risks
  1. Forward P/E of 98.57x leaves no margin of safety — any growth deceleration from the current 41% YoY pace or delay in reaching GAAP profitability could trigger significant multiple compression from the $5.34B market cap
  2. CEO transition from co-founder to external executive at a critical growth inflection point creates execution risk around strategic continuity, cultural preservation, and maintaining relationships with key enterprise accounts
  3. Marketplace gross margin declined 70bps YoY to 34.7% in Q2 2026 despite AI pricing improvements, suggesting competitive pressure from Protolabs and emerging platforms could cap margin expansion even as the company scales

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
8
AI Risk Protection (7/9): Xometry's core business coordinates physical manufacturing — CNC machining, 3D printing, injection molding — which AI cannot substitute. The marketplace model requires a physical supplier network, quality inspection capabilities, and logistics coordination that are structurally beyond AI's reach [1]. Xometry's proprietary pricing data from millions of historical transactions creates a deepening data moat that would take years for competitors to replicate. However, AI tools could eventually enable buyers to build their own supplier networks or automate RFQ processes, partially disintermediating the marketplace. Other AI-native platforms could emerge as competitors. AI Opportunity (1/1): AI is the foundational technology driving Xometry's business — proprietary machine learning models power instant quoting, supplier matching, and dynamic pricing across all manufacturing processes. Q2 2026 marketplace gross margin improvements were directly attributed to AI model enhancements, and 100% of Xometry's $229.3M Q2 revenue flows through AI-powered infrastructure [1].
