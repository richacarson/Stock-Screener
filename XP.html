Investment Thesis
XP Inc. at $29.23 with a $14.8B market cap is Brazil's dominant independent investment platform executing steady diversification into full-service financial services. Q2 2026 net revenue grew 9% YoY to R$4.884B with EBT margin expanding 172bps YoY to 32.0%, while adjusted EPS of R$2.67 beat consensus by 3.1% [1]. The financial profile is compelling: ROE of 22.6%, profit margin of 28.5%, and forward P/E of just 11.88x against double-digit revenue growth. The corporate/institutional segment is scaling rapidly (revenue up 117% YoY to R$606M), diversifying beyond retail brokerage. Combined client assets reached R$2.2T (+17% YoY) with R$28B in net new money, and management maintains double-digit revenue growth guidance for full-year 2026 [1].
The bear case centers on Brazilian macro risk and decelerating earnings growth. Management explicitly acknowledged navigating a 'challenging environment' in 2026, and adjusted net income growth decelerated to just 5% YoY in Q2 despite 9% revenue growth, suggesting margin pressure from new product investments and wider credit spreads [1]. The D/E ratio of 642% reflects financial services leverage but amplifies sensitivity to Brazilian interest rate and credit cycles with Selic rates elevated. Traditional banks (Itaú, Bradesco) are closing the technology gap with massive digital investments, while NuBank has grown to 100M+ customers competing for the same retail base [2]. The founder's dominant shareholding and executive chairman role create governance concentration risk for minority investors. Brazil country risk — BRL/USD currency volatility, political uncertainty, and regulatory changes from CVM and BACEN — adds structural headwinds that US-listed fintech peers do not face.
Key Catalysts
  1. Corporate/institutional segment revenue surging 117% YoY to R$606M in Q2 2026 as investment banking, capital markets, and corporate services diversify beyond retail brokerage dependency [1]
  2. Forward P/E of 11.88x represents significant discount to US fintech peers despite 22.6% ROE and 28.5% profit margins, creating valuation re-rating potential as Brazilian macro stabilizes
  3. Banking license and credit expansion opening a multi-trillion-real addressable market in Brazilian consumer and SME lending, with cross-sell to the existing 4.8M+ active client base and R$2.2T in combined assets [1]
Key Risks
  1. Brazilian macroeconomic volatility — elevated Selic rates compress net interest margins while BRL depreciation erodes USD-denominated returns, with Q2 net income growth decelerating to 5% vs. 9% revenue growth signaling margin pressure [1]
  2. Intensifying competition from BTG Pactual's digital platform, NuBank's 100M+ customer base, and traditional banks' fintech initiatives could pressure XP's fee advantage and client acquisition costs [2]
  3. Founder governance concentration — Guilherme Benchimol's dominant shareholding and executive chairman role create key-person risk and potential minority shareholder alignment concerns [2]
