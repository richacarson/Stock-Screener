Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Infrastructure ·NYSE ·United States
Yext, Inc. operates a digital presence platform that helps businesses manage their online brand visibility across search engines, maps, apps, voice assistants, and AI-powered discovery channels. The platform includes Listings, Pages, Reviews, Social, and AI Search products built on a proprietary Knowledge Graph. The company is pivoting to become the infrastructure layer for agentic marketing in the AI era, with products like Yext Scout for AI search visibility and competitive intelligence. Founded in 2006, headquartered in New York City.
1,120 Employees ·www.yext.com

Investment Thesis
Yext delivered FY2026 revenue of $446.6M with net income of $0.31/share and adjusted EBITDA margin of 24% ($107.3M), demonstrating meaningful profitability improvement even as revenue contracted slightly (-1.4% YoY) [1]. The forward P/E of 7.71 represents a compelling valuation for a SaaS company with $440.8M ARR, especially as Yext pivots to become the infrastructure layer for agentic marketing with products like Yext Scout and an open platform for AI agent development [3][4]. The Knowledge Graph — structured data that AI agents need to discover and trust brand information — becomes more valuable as AI-powered search and discovery channels proliferate. Q1 FY2027 delivered $107.9M revenue with 25% adjusted EBITDA margin, suggesting operational discipline is durable [2]. EPS growth of 240.9% YoY reflects the earnings inflection.
The bear case centers on Yext's declining revenue trajectory (-1.4% YoY) and the risk that the AI pivot is more narrative than substance [1]. ARR declined from $446.5M to $440.8M sequentially from FY2026 to Q1 FY2027, suggesting customer churn is outpacing new AI-driven bookings [2]. The D/E ratio of 917.9% is alarming, though it is largely an artifact of the $140M tender offer that depleted the equity base — the company still generated $26.9M adjusted EBITDA in Q1 FY2027. The fundamental risk is that Google, Meta, and AI-native platforms could internalize the structured data layer, making Yext's Knowledge Graph redundant. With 1,120 employees and a ~$600M market cap, Yext is a small player competing with tech giants for relevance in the AI search ecosystem. The Inspire Impact Score of -49 (MISALIGNED) may deter faith-based investors [5].
Key Catalysts
  1. Agentic marketing platform adoption by enterprise partners could create a new revenue stream and network effects as brands and agencies build AI agents on Yext's infrastructure and Knowledge Graph
  2. Forward P/E of 7.71 implies the market expects minimal growth, creating significant upside if the AI pivot drives even modest revenue acceleration in FY2028
  3. Yext Scout and AI search visibility tools position the company to capture demand from brands needing to manage their presence across AI-powered discovery channels beyond traditional SEO
Key Risks
  1. ARR declined from $446.5M to $440.8M sequentially, indicating the legacy listings business is shrinking faster than AI-driven products can replace the revenue — continued net churn could erode the business before the AI pivot matures
  2. D/E of 917.9% leaves the company financially fragile with minimal equity cushion, making it vulnerable to any operational setback or need for additional capital investment in the AI transition
  3. Google, Meta, and AI-native platforms (Perplexity, OpenAI) could internalize structured data management, commoditizing Yext's Knowledge Graph value proposition and making the company's core product redundant for AI discovery
