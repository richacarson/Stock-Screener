Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NYSE ·United States
Yext is a digital presence platform for multi-location brands, offering AI-powered solutions across Listings, Pages, Reviews, Social, and AI Search built on a proprietary Knowledge Graph. FY2026 (ended January 2026) revenue reached $446.6M (up ~13% YoY) with the company turning profitable at $37.9M net income after a $27.9M loss in FY2025. CEO Michael Walrath leads the company's transition toward AI-driven brand visibility, supported by the Hearsay Systems acquisition for financial services and a $140M tender offer to return capital.

Investment Thesis
Yext's FY2026 inflection to profitability ($37.9M net income vs. prior year $27.9M loss) and 24% adjusted EBITDA margin demonstrate operational discipline after years of SaaS-style growth spending [1]. At a forward P/E of 14.41, the stock trades at a significant discount to the broader SaaS sector, reflecting investor skepticism about the company's ability to sustain growth as AI search disrupts its core listings business [4]. The Knowledge Graph and AI Search platform provide a credible pivot strategy, but execution risk remains high.
The critical question is whether Yext can successfully transition from a listings management vendor — where Google's free Business Profile creates permanent pricing pressure — to an AI-powered digital presence platform where the Knowledge Graph becomes essential infrastructure for how AI systems discover and present business information [2]. The Hearsay acquisition and AI Search launch are positive signals, but the 5.3% forward revenue growth forecast and Q3 FY2026 revenue decline suggest the pivot has not yet gained sufficient traction. The $140M tender offer returns capital but also limits investment capacity for the transformation ahead. At $3.80/share, the risk-reward is balanced: significant upside if the AI pivot succeeds, but meaningful downside if legacy revenue erodes faster than new products scale.
Key Catalysts
  1. AI Search platform adoption could accelerate as enterprises seek alternatives to traditional search for their websites, with the Knowledge Graph providing competitive moat as structured data infrastructure for AI systems [2]
  2. FY2026 profitability inflection ($37.9M net income, 24% EBITDA margin) demonstrates unit economics work at current scale, creating operating leverage for incremental revenue growth [1]
  3. Hearsay Systems acquisition extends AI capabilities into the regulated financial services vertical, opening a $5B+ addressable market for compliance-aware digital communications [2]
Key Risks
  1. Google's AI Overviews, LLM-powered search (ChatGPT, Perplexity), and free Business Profile threaten the core value proposition of managed digital presence, with Q3 FY2026 revenue declining 2% YoY as an early warning signal [1]
  2. Forward revenue growth of 5.3% lags the 13% SaaS industry average, suggesting the AI pivot has not yet translated into demand acceleration despite significant product investment [1]
  3. Stock at $3.80 with $117% D/E ratio and $41M net debt position limits financial flexibility for the sustained investment needed to complete the platform transformation [4]

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience NEUTRAL
5
AI Risk Protection (4/9): Yext faces significant AI disruption risk to its core listings and reviews management business. Google's AI Overviews and Search Generative Experience reduce click-through to traditional business listings, directly undermining the value proposition of Yext's Listings product [1][2]. LLM-powered search engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity) bypass traditional search entirely, potentially making managed digital presence less relevant for consumer discovery. AI-generated content tools could reduce the value of Yext's Pages and Reviews products. The Knowledge Graph provides structured data that feeds AI systems, creating some defensibility, but Google's own structured data capabilities and free Business Profile offering represent a powerful competitor that controls the distribution channel. AI Opportunity (1/1): Yext is pivoting aggressively toward AI — Yext AI Search provides enterprise-grade conversational search for websites, the Hearsay Systems acquisition extends AI into financial services advisor compliance, and the Knowledge Graph is inherently designed to structure data for AI consumption [2]. The company's ability to position itself as the infrastructure layer feeding AI systems (rather than competing with them) determines whether AI is ultimately an existential threat or a growth catalyst.
