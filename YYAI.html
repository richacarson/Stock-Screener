AiRWA Inc. (formerly Connexa Sports Technologies) operates artificial intelligence software providing online matchmaking and dating experiences in Hong Kong, the United States, and the United Kingdom. The company rebranded in October 2025 and pivoted to Web3/blockchain-based real-world asset (RWA) tokenization on Solana. In FY2026, AiRWA acquired Aberfeldy Holdings Limited and its Malaysian subsidiary 26 Rafael Sdn. Bhd. for $140M, adding a staffing and technology services operation generating $25M+ annual revenue. Underwent a 1-for-40 reverse stock split in May 2026.
