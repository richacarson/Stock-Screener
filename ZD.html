Company Profile
Communication Services ·Advertising Agencies ·NASDAQ ·United States
Ziff Davis, Inc. is a digital media and internet company operating technology, gaming, health, shopping, and cybersecurity content properties. Key brands include PCMag, CNET, IGN Entertainment, Mashable, Everyday Health, BabyCenter, RetailMeNot, IPVanish, and Humble Bundle. In 2026, the company completed the $1.2B sale of its Connectivity business (Ookla, Speedtest, Downdetector) to Accenture. Formerly known as j2 Global, rebranded in October 2021. Founded 1927, headquartered in New York.

Investment Thesis
Ziff Davis at 9.5x forward earnings trades at a significant discount to the broader market, and the company's balance sheet is strengthened by $1.6B in cash following the $1.2B Connectivity business sale to Accenture [4]. Q2 2026 adjusted diluted EPS rose 13.2% to $1.03 despite revenue declining 2.7%, demonstrating effective cost management and AI-driven operational efficiency [1]. The price-to-book of 0.89x means the stock trades below tangible asset value. CEO Vivek Shah's 8.7-year tenure and the company's 99-year history of serial reinvention provide evidence of institutional adaptability. Free cash flow of $529M gives significant optionality for acquisitions, buybacks, or new strategic investments [4].
The SELL recommendation reflects structural AI disruption risk to the core digital media business that the valuation discount alone cannot offset. Revenue has declined in Q1 and Q2 2026 with Q3 guidance projecting continued low-to-mid single digit YoY declines, and the estimated 5-year revenue CAGR of approximately 2% confirms secular stagnation [1]. The $1.2B Connectivity sale removed Ziff Davis's most AI-resilient and proprietary assets (Ookla/Speedtest network measurement data), leaving a portfolio of content properties (PCMag, CNET, IGN, Everyday Health, RetailMeNot) that are highly exposed to AI search disruption — Google AI Overviews and AI chatbots directly reduce traffic to content websites. ROE is negative at -1.5% and the trailing P/E is undefined due to GAAP losses [4]. Short interest of 25.7% signals significant bearish institutional conviction. The company pays no dividend and generates no income return for investors waiting for a turnaround. With no clear strategic vision for deploying the Connectivity sale proceeds, Ziff Davis risks becoming a shrinking digital media holding company sitting on depreciating content assets.
