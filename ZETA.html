Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NYSE ·United States
Zeta Global is an AI-powered marketing technology platform that combines the industry's 3rd largest proprietary data set with artificial intelligence to help enterprises acquire, grow, and retain customers. The platform enables personalized marketing at scale through identity resolution, predictive analytics, and AI-driven campaign optimization. Founded by serial entrepreneur David Steinberg (7 companies, 30+ year career), Zeta has achieved 19 consecutive beat-and-raise quarters. Q2 2026 revenue was $443M (+44% YoY) with adjusted EBITDA of $92M (+56% YoY). The company launched Athena by Zeta, an AI agent platform that drove 7X more agent interactions in its first week. Full-year 2026 guidance was raised to $1.818B revenue (+39% YoY). Market cap is approximately $7.3B.

Investment Thesis
Zeta at 26.62x forward earnings with a PEG of 0.78 is a high-growth AI platform achieving 44-50% revenue acceleration with improving profitability — 19 consecutive beat-and-raise quarters and full-year 2026 guidance raised to $1.818B (+39% YoY) [1]. Adjusted EBITDA of $92M in Q2 2026 (+56% YoY) at a 20.7% margin demonstrates operating leverage in the model, and the company achieved its first GAAP net income ($8M) in Q2. The Athena AI agent platform is a product inflection — 7X more agent interactions and 60%+ AI platform usage adoption in week one suggest a step-function improvement in platform capabilities that could drive further customer expansion and ARPU growth [1]. Super-scaled customers at 189 (+19% YoY) with $1.7M ARPU (+21% YoY) show deep enterprise penetration. Free cash flow of $224M TTM provides self-funding growth without dilution. Founder-CEO Steinberg's 30+ year track record of building and scaling technology companies provides execution confidence.
The bear case centers on valuation risk, competitive intensity, and the transition from growth to sustained profitability. Forward P/E of 26.62 prices in substantial continued growth — any deceleration from 44% could trigger meaningful multiple compression, especially with short interest at 10.5% creating potential selling pressure [4]. Zeta competes against Salesforce Marketing Cloud ($5B+ marketing revenue), Adobe Experience Cloud, The Trade Desk, and increasingly Google and Meta's own AI marketing tools — all with larger data sets, broader distribution, and deeper pockets. GAAP profitability remains marginal at -0.14% net margin, with adjusted metrics excluding substantial stock-based compensation. The company's sensitivity to data privacy regulation is a structural risk — Zeta collects sensitive consumer data including race/ethnicity, health conditions, and religion for audience targeting, which faces increasing regulatory scrutiny under evolving privacy frameworks [3]. Revenue growth decelerated from 50% in Q1 to 44% in Q2, and while still exceptional, the trend bears monitoring. The Inspire Impact Score of 39 (ALIGNED) is a positive for faith-based investors, though data practices warrant ongoing monitoring.

AI Resilience (25%)
AI Resilience LOW RISK
7
AI Risk Protection (6/9): Zeta occupies an unusual position — as an AI-native marketing platform, it IS the AI infrastructure rather than a company being disrupted by AI. The proprietary data set (3rd largest in marketing tech) and AI models trained on it create a competitive moat that generic AI tools cannot easily replicate [2]. Customer switching costs are high once enterprises integrate Zeta's data and AI into their marketing workflows, and the 189 super-scaled customers with $1.7M ARPU demonstrate deep platform dependency. However, AI democratization poses a real threat — Google, Meta, Salesforce, and Adobe all deploy AI-powered marketing tools with larger data sets and broader distribution. OpenAI and other foundation model providers could enable startups to build competing AI marketing platforms more cheaply. Zeta's moat is its proprietary data and customer relationships, not its AI models per se, and that moat could narrow as AI commoditizes. AI Opportunity (1/1): Zeta generates 100% of its revenue through AI-powered technology. The Athena platform drove 7X more agent interactions, customers report 600% return on marketing spend, and the AI capabilities are directly responsible for the 44% revenue growth and customer expansion [1].
