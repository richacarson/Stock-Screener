Company Profile
Financial Services ·Regional Banking ·NASDAQ ·United States
Zions Bancorporation, National Association is a regional bank holding company operating as a 'Collection of Great Banks' with local brand names across the Western United States, including Zions Bank, Amegy Bank, California Bank & Trust, National Bank of Arizona, Nevada State Bank, and Vectra Bank Colorado. With approximately $89 billion in total assets, the company provides commercial and retail banking, mortgage lending, and wealth management services through seven separately managed affiliate bank divisions across 11 Western and Southwestern states. Completed a landmark core banking modernization on TCS BaNCS in 2024. Founded in 1873, headquartered in Salt Lake City, Utah.

Investment Thesis
Zions Bancorporation delivered exceptional H1 2026 results: Q1 net earnings of $232M ($1.56 EPS, +38% YoY) and Q2 revenue of $1.1B with diluted EPS of $3.05 (nearly doubled), driven by NIM expansion to 3.27% and 11% customer-related noninterest income growth [1][2]. Core EPS excluding $252M in Visa Class B-1 equity gains still grew 10% to $1.74 in Q2, demonstrating underlying operational strength. At a forward P/E of 10.14 with a 2.57% dividend yield, 28% payout ratio, and 13 consecutive years of dividend increases, the stock is significantly undervalued versus historical banking multiples [6]. The completed TCS BaNCS core modernization provides a structural technology advantage over regional peers. CEO insider buying of $265K in February 2026 signals management conviction [6]. The $89B-asset bank maintains fortress-quality credit metrics with net charge-offs of just 0.03%.
The bear case centers on earnings quality and structural growth limitations. Q2 2026 headline EPS of $3.05 was inflated by $252M in non-recurring Visa equity gains -- core EPS was $1.74, and future quarters will lack this boost [2]. The 5-year dividend growth CAGR of 4.56% trails the 6% target (roughly 2x inflation), and the 5-year revenue CAGR reflects the slow-growth nature of traditional banking. Zions' geographic concentration in Western US markets creates vulnerability to regional economic downturns, particularly in Texas (Amegy Bank) and California (California Bank & Trust) commercial real estate. While AI deployment is progressing internally, no AI products generate external revenue, and fintech competitors continue to encroach on traditional banking fee streams. The explicitly shareholder-value-focused mission and mixed Glassdoor reviews (some describing a 'political' culture) suggest organizational limitations in the inspiration dimension [3][4].
Key Catalysts
  1. Monetization of the TCS BaNCS core platform advantage through faster digital product launches, potentially driving the efficiency ratio below 60% and expanding NIM beyond 3.27% [1][4]
  2. Continued NIM expansion and customer-related noninterest income growth (11% in Q2 2026) as the interest rate environment stabilizes, supporting core earnings growth beyond the Visa one-time gains [2]
  3. 13 consecutive years of dividend increases with a 28% payout ratio provides substantial room for accelerated dividend growth, which could re-rate the stock toward the peer-group average valuation [6]
Key Risks
  1. Geographic concentration in 11 Western/Southwestern US states creates vulnerability to regional economic downturns, particularly in Texas and California commercial real estate markets [4]
  2. 5-year dividend growth CAGR of 4.56% trails the 6% target (2x inflation), and structural slow-growth banking dynamics may limit the ability to accelerate distributions despite the low 28% payout ratio [6]
  3. Q2 2026 headline results masked by $252M Visa gains -- future quarters will normalize, and ROE of 16.4% may not sustain if credit conditions deteriorate from the current 0.03% net charge-off level [1][2]
