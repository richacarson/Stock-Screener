Company Profile
Financial Services ·Regional Banking ·NASDAQ ·United States
Zions Bancorporation, National Association is a regional bank holding company operating as a 'Collection of Great Banks' with local brand names across the Western United States, including Zions Bank, Amegy Bank, California Bank & Trust, National Bank of Arizona, Nevada State Bank, and Vectra Bank Colorado. With approximately $90 billion in total assets, $76.9 billion in deposits, and $61.3 billion in loans, the company provides commercial and retail banking, mortgage lending, and wealth management services. Completed a landmark core banking modernization on TCS BaNCS in 2024. Founded in 1873, headquartered in Salt Lake City, Utah.

Investment Thesis
Zions Bancorporation delivered strong Q1 2026 results with net earnings of $232 million ($1.56 per share), up 37% year-over-year, driven by net interest income expansion to $662 million (NIM of 3.27%) and 10% customer-related noninterest income growth [1]. The bank's completed core modernization on TCS BaNCS gives it a structural technology advantage over regional peers, enabling faster product development and lower operating costs. At a forward P/E of 9.49 with a 2.56% dividend yield and 30% payout ratio, the stock trades at a significant discount to historical banking multiples. The balance sheet is fortress-quality: CET1 of 11.5%, net charge-offs of just 0.03%, and tangible book value per share up 19% to $41.75 [1]. CEO insider buying of $265K in February 2026 signals management conviction [6].
The bear case centers on structural challenges facing regional banking. The 5-year revenue CAGR of just 4% reflects the slow-growth nature of traditional banking, and Zions' Western US geographic concentration creates vulnerability to regional economic downturns [4]. ROE of 14.12% is below the 15% target, reflecting the capital intensity of banking. While Zions is exploring AI and digital innovation, it has not yet monetized these capabilities, and fintech competitors continue to encroach on traditional banking revenue streams. The company's explicitly shareholder-value-focused mission and finite game orientation suggest management may prioritize near-term returns over transformative long-term investments. Dividend growth of approximately 4% annually barely exceeds inflation, lagging more aggressive dividend growers in the sector [5].
Key Catalysts

  2. 5-year revenue CAGR of 4% reflects the structural low-growth challenge facing traditional banking, with fintech competitors capturing an increasing share of payment, lending, and advisory revenue
