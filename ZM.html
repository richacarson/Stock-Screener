Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Application ·NASDAQ ·United States
Zoom Communications positions itself as an 'AI-first, open work platform built for human connection,' spanning Meetings, Zoom Phone, Contact Center and the broader AI-first Customer Experience (CX) portfolio, Team Chat, Docs, Workvivo and AI Companion. Fiscal Q2 2027 (quarter ended July 31, 2026) revenue was $1,277.2M (+4.9% YoY), with Enterprise revenue up 7.8% — its fastest in three years — while Online revenue grew just 0.6%. Management raised FY2027 guidance to $5.085-$5.095B of revenue, $6.08-$6.12 of non-GAAP EPS and $1.78-$1.82B of free cash flow. Founded by CEO Eric Yuan in 2011 and headquartered in San Jose, Zoom holds a strategic investment portfolio that grew to $3.79B and carries essentially no debt.

Investment Thesis
Zoom is a cash machine priced like a value stock: FY2027 guidance calls for $1.78-$1.82B of free cash flow on ~$5.09B of revenue and $6.08-$6.12 of non-GAAP EPS, putting the shares at roughly 15.7x forward earnings with essentially no debt and a $3.79B strategic-investment portfolio that produced $1.61B of gains in Q2 alone [1][2]. Growth is quietly improving where it matters — Enterprise revenue rose 7.8%, its best rate in three years, customers above $100K grew 8.2% to 4,625, and the AI-first CX portfolio is compounding at high-double-digit ARR rates with Zoom Virtual Agent customers up 256% [1]. Management raised full-year guidance and continues to retire shares (3.7M repurchased in Q2, ~$1.3B authorization remaining) [1].
The bear case is that Zoom is a mature, ex-growth franchise whose headline profits flatter reality. Total revenue is growing under 5%, Online revenue just 0.6%, and Enterprise net dollar expansion of 99% means existing customers are not expanding [1]. GAAP net income of $1.54B in Q2 came overwhelmingly from investment gains, so the 65% TTM net margin and single-digit trailing P/E are not repeatable [1][2]. FMP's forward PEG of 2.84 signals little earnings growth, stock-based compensation still runs ~14.6% of revenue, and Microsoft and Google bundle AI-enabled meetings for free inside their suites [2]. Insiders have sold in every recent quarter with no open-market purchases [6], and the Inspire Impact Score of -49 (MISALIGNED, Abortion Activism) limits suitability for faith-based mandates.

Key Risks
  1. Core meetings stagnation: Online revenue +0.6% YoY and Enterprise net dollar expansion of 99% show limited expansion within the installed base as Microsoft Teams and Google Meet bundle AI features [1]
  2. Earnings-quality risk: $1.61B of Q2 investment gains drove GAAP EPS of $5.15 versus $1.55 non-GAAP — mark-downs in the strategic portfolio could produce equally large GAAP losses [1]
  3. Persistent insider selling (23-53 open-market sales per quarter, no purchases) and stock-based compensation near 14.6% of revenue dilute the per-share benefit of buybacks [2][6]

AI Risk Protection (5/9): Zoom's core meetings franchise is exposed — Online revenue grew only 0.6% YoY and Enterprise net dollar expansion sits at 99% [1], while Microsoft and Google bundle AI assistants into Teams and Meet and cross-platform AI notetakers reduce the stickiness of any single meeting tool; AI agents completing work asynchronously could also reduce meeting volume over time.
