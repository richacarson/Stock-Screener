Company Profile

CleanCore Solutions is a micro-cap company undergoing its third strategic pivot in two years. Originally a patented aqueous ozone cleaning technology company, the company adopted a Dogecoin treasury strategy in late 2025 (accumulating 733.1M DOGE), then in June 2026 appointed new CEO Tyler Hassen and announced it would exit both the cleaning business and Dogecoin strategy to focus on building AI data center infrastructure in the United States. Investment Thesis
CleanCore Solutions offers optionality on the AI data center buildout theme under new CEO Tyler Hassen (appointed June 2026), who brings government and energy sector experience from his role as Acting Assistant Secretary at the U.S. Department of the Interior [2]. The company has signed a non-binding LOI for a Midwest data center project and is evaluating additional opportunities across rural and industrial U.S. areas. Revenue grew 80.9% TTM to $3.4M from the cleaning business, and the stock has recovered from $0.22 lows to $0.69, with market cap reaching $153M. The cleaning technology (59% gross margin, 125% Q1 revenue growth) demonstrated the team could build and sell a product, even if the business is being exited [1].
The bear case is overwhelming and multifaceted. The company has pivoted its entire business model three times in two years — cleaning products to Dogecoin treasury (733.1M DOGE) to AI data centers — demonstrating zero strategic consistency [1][2]. Nine-month net losses of $155M against $3.4M TTM revenue indicate catastrophic cash burn, with only $17.1M cash remaining (approximately 4-5 months of runway at Q3 FY2026 burn rate) [2]. The company has no operational data center infrastructure, no data center revenue, no data center engineering team, and is competing against established operators with decades of experience and billions in existing infrastructure. Shares outstanding of 221.94M imply massive dilution from serial equity raises, and additional dilution is inevitable given the cash position [3]. The AI data center LOI is non-binding. At $153M market cap with zero data center revenue, the stock is trading entirely on narrative. IOWN cannot invest in a company with this level of execution uncertainty and strategic instability.

Key Risks

  3. Serial strategic pivots (cleaning → Dogecoin → AI data centers) signal management may abandon the data center strategy if market sentiment shifts, leaving shareholders with further losses and no viable business
