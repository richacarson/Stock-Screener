Company Profile
Technology ·Software - Infrastructure (Cybersecurity) ·NASDAQ ·United States
Zscaler is the pioneer of zero trust security architecture, operating the world's largest inline cloud security platform that processes over 500 billion daily transactions for more than 40% of Fortune 500 companies. Founded in 2007 by CEO Jay Chaudhry, Zscaler's Zero Trust Exchange replaces legacy firewall-based security with cloud-native protection across internet access, private applications, and digital experience. FY2026 (ending July 2026) delivered record revenue of $3.35B (+25% YoY) with 25% ARR growth, non-GAAP gross margin of 80.7%, and record 23% non-GAAP operating margin. The company is expanding aggressively into AI agent security with new tools to prevent AI agent permission abuse and protect against AI-enhanced cyber threats. Market cap is approximately $26.5B.

Investment Thesis
Zscaler is the dominant zero trust security platform serving 40%+ of Fortune 500 companies, with a 5-year revenue CAGR of ~38% reflecting the secular shift from legacy firewalls to cloud-native security [1][2]. FY2026 delivered $3.35B revenue (+25%) with record 23% non-GAAP operating margin, demonstrating that growth and profitability can coexist as the platform scales [1]. The AI agent security opportunity is a massive TAM expansion — as enterprises deploy autonomous AI agents, every agent becomes a new attack surface requiring zero trust security, positioning Zscaler at the center of the next wave of cybersecurity spending [3]. With an Inspire Impact Score of 67 (ALIGNED), Zscaler is one of the highest-rated faith-aligned cybersecurity investments available. No ethical controversies have been identified [5].
At 40.96x forward earnings and 4.38x PEG, Zscaler is priced for perfection in a market where FY2027 guidance of $3.9B (+17%) signals meaningful growth deceleration from 25% [1][4]. The company remains GAAP unprofitable despite $3.35B revenue, with ROE of -3.71% and profit margin of -2.44%, raising questions about when real profitability will materialize as stock-based compensation remains elevated [4]. Competition from Palo Alto Networks (which has its own zero trust platform and 'platformization' strategy) and CrowdStrike is intensifying, directly targeting Zscaler's customer base with integrated security offerings. The concentrated insider selling via 10b5-1 plans (CFO, CPO, and CLO all sold in 2026) is routine but does not signal insider conviction at current prices. Infrastructure scores only 4/10 because the GAAP-based algorithm penalizes stock-based compensation-driven unprofitability, a structural issue for high-growth SaaS companies that the non-GAAP profitability (23% operating margin) does not reflect.

AI Resilience LOW RISK

AI Risk Protection (9/9): Zscaler is maximally immune to AI disruption because AI fundamentally increases demand for cybersecurity infrastructure. AI-powered attacks are becoming more sophisticated — better phishing, faster vulnerability exploitation, automated social engineering — which drives enterprise spending on defense solutions [2][3]. Simultaneously, the explosion of AI agents in enterprises creates an entirely new attack surface requiring zero trust security: 'Yesterday, a user was the weakest link. The platform processes 500B+ daily transactions using AI for threat detection at a scale impossible without AI, making Zscaler both a beneficiary of AI adoption and a provider of AI-powered defense. No analyst has identified AI as a substitution risk for Zscaler; the consensus is that AI increases cybersecurity TAM. AI Opportunity (1/1): Zscaler generates meaningful AI-embedded revenue through its AI-powered security platform and newly launched AI agent security tools [3].
