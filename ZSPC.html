Company Profile
Technology ·Computer Hardware ·OTC ·United States
zSpace provides augmented and virtual reality educational technology solutions for K-12 schools and career and technical education (CTE) markets. Products include the Inspire and Imagine laptop devices that deliver autostereoscopic 3D graphics without eyewear, a tracked stylus for interacting with virtual objects, and a software platform for STEM, health sciences, automotive, and advanced manufacturing applications. Trusted by 3,500+ school districts. Founded in 2006 by CEO Paul Kellenberger (serving since inception). Q2 2026 revenue declined to $5.4M (from $7.5M prior year) impacted by EMEA order delays from the Iran conflict. The Board has initiated a review of strategic alternatives. Previously listed on NASDAQ, now trading on OTC markets at $0.16 per share with ~$5.9M market cap.
