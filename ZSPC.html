The overwhelming bear case: zSpace faces existential financial risk with a going concern warning from both management and auditors [1]. Full-year FY2025 posted a $25.4M net loss on $27.9M revenue (-91% margin), and Q1 FY2026 revenue declined 22% YoY to $5.3M. Cash stands at $2.9M after a $3M emergency capital infusion — without additional funding, the company may not survive 12 months. A 1-for-25 reverse stock split in April 2026 failed to sustain Nasdaq compliance, with the stock at $0.16. Class action lawsuits allege the December 2024 IPO registration statement was materially misleading [3]. Five material weaknesses in internal controls with three unresolved signal fundamental governance failures. The strategic review initiated after Q1 results may lead to a sale, restructuring, or dissolution. The Iran war delayed key Middle East deals. This company is in survival mode — the question is not what the stock is worth, but whether the company will exist in 12 months.
