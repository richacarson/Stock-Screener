Company Profile
Healthcare ·Drug Manufacturers — Animal Health ·NYSE ·United States
Zoetis discovers, develops, manufactures, and commercializes medicines, vaccines, diagnostics, and biodevices for companion animals and livestock. Spun off from Pfizer in 2013, it is the world's largest animal health company, with FY2025 revenue of $9.47B. Key franchises include Simparica Trio parasiticides, Apoquel and Cytopoint dermatology, the Librela and Solensia monoclonal antibody pain treatments, and the AI-powered Vetscan Imagyst diagnostics platform. In August 2026 Zoetis cut FY2026 guidance to $9.12-$9.32B revenue (organic operational growth of -3% to -1%) amid weaker U.S. companion animal demand and intensifying competition.

Investment Thesis
Zoetis is the world's largest animal health company, earning a 27.7% net margin and 64.9% ROE on $9.5B of revenue, yet it trades at just 12.4x forward earnings with a 2.66% yield and a dividend that has grown 20% a year over five years [5]. Q2 2026 still produced $691M of net income ($1.65 per share) despite the downturn, and its AI-powered Vetscan Imagyst diagnostics platform, now expanding into radiology via VitalRADS, adds a recurring, technology-led growth leg [1][4].
The bear case is a franchise under competitive siege: FY2026 guidance was cut to -3% to -1% organic operational growth and adjusted EPS of $6.15-$6.25 after misses in Q1 and Q2, with share losses in Simparica Trio, Apoquel/Cytopoint and Librela [1][3]. Leverage has risen to 294% D/E, and a securities class action (City of Ann Arbor v. Zoetis, S.D.N.Y.) over Librela disclosures remains pending, adding legal and reputational overhang [3][5].
Key Catalysts
  1. Q3 2026 results (early November) showing stabilization in U.S. companion animal clinic visits after Q2's 1% organic operational decline
  2. Growth of the AI diagnostics platform (seven Vetscan Imagyst applications plus VitalRADS radiology) as a new recurring revenue stream
  3. A 13th consecutive dividend increase from the current $2.12 annual rate, supported by a 33.9% payout ratio
Key Risks
  1. Continued share loss in parasiticides, dermatology and OA pain to Elanco and other entrants, extending the 2026 revenue decline
  2. Librela neurological adverse-event label update and the pending securities class action eroding veterinarian trust
  3. D/E near 294% after a 41% debt increase in 2025 limits balance-sheet flexibility if the downturn persists
