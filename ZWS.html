Company Profile
Industrials ·Building Products & Equipment ·NYSE ·United States
Zurn Elkay Water Solutions Corporation is the leading North American manufacturer of sustainable water management solutions, serving commercial, institutional, and residential markets. Formed through the 2022 combination of Zurn (engineered water management) and Elkay Manufacturing (drinking water solutions and stainless steel sinks), the company offers a comprehensive portfolio spanning drainage, hygienic and environmental products, drinking water filtration, and water heaters following the 2026 Intellihot acquisition. Headquartered in Milwaukee, WI, ZWS has more than doubled sales over the past five years under CEO Todd Adams' 17-year tenure.

Investment Thesis
Zurn Elkay at 26.4x forward earnings is a premium water infrastructure compounder delivering 10-11% organic revenue growth, record EBITDA margins of 27.7% in Q2 2026, and $112M in quarterly free cash flow [1][3]. The company occupies a dominant position in sustainable water solutions with secular tailwinds from aging infrastructure replacement, lead pipe remediation mandates, and increasing clean water regulatory requirements. D/E of 30.1% provides balance sheet flexibility for continued tuck-in M&A, and the Intellihot acquisition adds a high-growth water heating platform. CEO Adams' 17-year tenure provides strategic continuity, and the raised full-year outlook signals confidence in sustained execution. The Inspire Impact Score of 55 (ALIGNED) with 13 positive ESG attributions and zero negatives reflects genuine sustainability leadership in a business inherently aligned with clean water access.
The bear case centers on valuation and integration execution risk. Forward P/E of 26.4x prices in substantial growth that must be sustained — any deceleration from the 10-11% organic growth trajectory could trigger multiple compression given the premium. Profit margin of 15.3% remains well below the 20% target despite record EBITDA margins, reflecting the capital-intensive manufacturing business model [5]. Post-merger integration is ongoing with Glassdoor reviews flagging organizational churn and leadership instability at mid-levels. The Intellihot acquisition adds a fourth integration workstream while the Elkay combination is still maturing. Revenue growth benefited from favorable price/mix dynamics and non-residential construction strength — a commercial construction downturn or shift to competitive bidding could pressure both volume and pricing. The 0.86% dividend yield provides minimal downside protection for income-oriented investors.
Key Catalysts
  1. Federal infrastructure spending and lead pipe replacement mandates creating multi-year secular demand tailwind for water management products — estimated $15B+ in lead pipe remediation alone over the next decade [3]
  2. Intellihot integration and cross-selling across the Zurn Elkay distribution network expected to contribute ~$16M in incremental sales for H2 2026 with potential for accelerating growth as commercial relationships mature [1]
  3. Continued margin expansion from Rexnord Business System operational discipline and Elkay synergy capture — Q2 2026 record EBITDA margin of 27.7% suggests pricing power and operating leverage with further upside potential [3]
Key Risks
  1. Premium valuation at 26.4x forward P/E leaves limited margin of safety — organic growth deceleration below 10% or margin miss could trigger meaningful multiple compression given the stock trades at a significant premium to building products peers [5]
  2. Post-merger organizational integration creating cultural friction with Glassdoor reviews citing frequent reorganizations, leadership turnover, and erosion of collaborative culture — sustained employee churn could undermine operational execution [2]
  3. Non-residential construction cycle dependency — approximately 60% of revenue is tied to commercial, institutional, and infrastructure end markets where a construction spending downturn could pressure volumes despite favorable secular trends [3]
